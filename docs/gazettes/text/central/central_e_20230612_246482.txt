INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/4800-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Shri Rewa Gurjar Medical Science Training and Research Centre situated at Patwari Halka 
No. 42, Village Panchamba, Kedwa Road, Bhikangaon Distt. Khargone — 4533l, Madhya Pradesh is 
recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct 
GNM & B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-8/l4800-INC dated 28/09//2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section |4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, क्‍947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24], Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 28/09/2022 was also enclosed to Shri Rewa Gurjar Medical Science Training and Research Centre 
situated at Patwari Halka No. 42, Village Panchamba, Kedwa Road, Bhikangaon Distt. Khargone — 4533l, 
Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Shri Rewa Gurjar Medical Science Training and Research Centre, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Shri 
Rewa Gurjar Medical Science Training and Research Centre has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
7/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies L.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM & B.Sc(N) Shri 
Rewa Gurjar Medical Science Training and Research Centre situated at Patwari Halka No. 42, Village 
Panchamba, Kedwa Road, Bhikangaon Distt. Khargone — 4533l, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
