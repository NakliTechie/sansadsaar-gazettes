MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 11th September, 2023 
S.O. 3978(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37 A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies "Rail Over Rail (ROR) double line 
between Guntakal West — Mallappa Gate (9.64 Km)" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in 
the State of Andhra Pradesh in the Districts of Ananthapuramu and Kurnool for providing National 


Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette.
