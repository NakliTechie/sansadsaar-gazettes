Secunderabad, the 22nd August, 2025 


S.O. 3866(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Doubling between Tirupati — Pakala— Katpadi (04 Km)" 
as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the Districts of Tirupati 
and Chittoor and in the State of Tamil Nadu in the District of Vellore for providing National Infrastructure 
for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
