grounds for so doing, exempt any person from the operation of this rule. 


5. Power to relax. — Where the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient 


so to do, it may, by order, for reasons to be recorded in writing and in consultation with the Union 
Public Service Commission, relax any of the provisions of these rules with respect to any class or 
category of persons. 


Saving. — Nothing in these rules shall affect reservations, relaxation of age-limit and other 
concessions required to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward 
Classes, ex-Servicemen and other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued 
by the Central Government from time to time in this regard.
