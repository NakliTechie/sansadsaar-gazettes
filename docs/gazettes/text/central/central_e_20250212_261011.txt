notification of 333.7396 hectares from the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Gujarat has given its approval to the proposal vide letter 
No. IC/INFRA/SEZ/NOC/260362] dated 2277 March, 2024. After de-notification, the de-notified parcels of 
land would be utilised for industrial purpose and creation of infrastructure which would sub-serve the

of section 3 of the said Act and other related requirements are fulfilled;
