INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 22nd February, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/4396-INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Late K L Pandey School of Nursing situated at Niwali, Vill. Sildad, Teh — Nieali, Distt. Barwani 
— 45666, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses 
Registration Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. [8-8/4396-INC dated /04/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section |4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24l, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated /04/2022 was also enclosed to Late K L Pandey School of Nursing situated at Niwali, Vill. Sildad, 
Teh — Nieali, Distt. Barwani — 45666, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested 
to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Late K L Pandey School of Nursing, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Late K L Pandey School of Nursing 
has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
29/06/202 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Late K L 
Pandey School of Nursing situated at Niwali, Vill. Sildad, Teh - Nieali, Distt. Barwani — 45666, Madhya 
Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State 
government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
