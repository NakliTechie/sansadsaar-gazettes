(Protection) Rules, 986, issued a notification to declare an Eco-Sensitive Zone around Kitam Bird 
Sanctuary, Sikkim in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number 
S.0.267 (E), dated the 27" August, 204; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in 
the public interest to amend the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides 
that whenever it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may 
dispense with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment 
(Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to 
dispense with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment 
(Protection) Rules, 986 for amending the notification number 5.0. S.0.267 (E), dated the 277 August, 
204;

of sub-section (2), and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 
986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the Government of India, 
Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.0.267 (E), dated the 27" August, 204, namely:-
