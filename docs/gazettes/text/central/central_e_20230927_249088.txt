TERMINATION NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th September, 2023 
Case No. CVD-NSR-0/2022 


Subject: Termination of New Shipper Review investigation for determination of individual countervailing/anti- 
subsidy duty rate for M/s LS Metal Vina LLC (Producer from Vietnam) in the case of Anti-Subsidy duties 
imposed on import of Copper Tubes & Pipes” originating in or exported from Vietnam. 

F. No. 07/6/2022-DGTR.—A. Introduction 

l. LS Metal Vina LLC, Vietnam (herein after referred as the “applicant”) has filed an application in 
accordance with Article 9.3 of WTO Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures (hereinafter also 
referred to as "ASCM"), the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Countervailing Duty on 
Subsidized Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 995 as amended from time to time (hereinafter also 
referred to as the 'Rules') and the Custom Tariff Act, [975 as amended from time to time (hereinafter referred to as 


[भाग I—ars |] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


the 'Act') before the Designated Authority (hereinafter also referred to as the "Authority") requesting for 
determination of their individual countervailing/ anti-subsidy duty rates in the matter of anti-subsidy duties levied on 
subsidized imports of Copper Pipes and Tubes, originating in or exported from Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam. 


2. The Designated Authority, on the basis of prima facie evidence submitted by the applicant, issued a public notice 
vide notification no. 070/6/2022-96ाफए dated 30" September 2022, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, initiating the subject investigation. 

B. Withdrawal of the application by Domestic Industry 

3. The applicant vide email dated 22.09.2023, has withdrawn their application stating that there is a lacuna under 

the Indian Countervailing Duty framework in the sense that there are no provisions for a new shipper review, 
due to which it would not be possible to proceed further in the subject investigation. 


4. The applicant has requested the Authority to terminate the present investigation with liberty to the applicant to 
file new application after the amendment to include a NSR provision in the Indian CVD framework. 

C. Examination by the Designated Authority 

5. In view of the request made by the applicant withdrawing its application, the Designated Authority considers 
that the investigation has become infructuous after the withdrawal of the application. 

6. Since the applicant has withdrawn the application, it is considered appropriate not to proceed further with the 
conduct of the investigation. Accordingly, in terms of Rule |4 (a) of the Rules the Authority, hereby terminates 
the New Shipper Review investigation for determination of individual countervailing/anti-subsidy duty rate for 
M/s LS Metal Vina LLC (Producer from Vietnam) in the case of anti-subsidy duties imposed on import of 
Copper Tubes & Pipes” originating in or exported from Vietnam initiated vide notification no. 07/l6/2022- 
DGTR dated 30.09.2022.
