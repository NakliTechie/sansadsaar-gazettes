MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th February, 2025 


S.O. 858(E).— WHEREAS the Central Government is satisfied that circumstances have arisen rendering it 
necessary in the public interest, to relax the restrictions imposed by the Tobacco Board Act, 1975 (4 of 1975); 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government considers it necessary in the public interest to dispose of the excess 
flue cured virginia tobacco of registered growers and flue cured virginia tobacco of unregistered growers at the 
authorised auction platforms of the Tobacco Board in the State of Karnataka; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 30 of the Tobacco 
Board Act, 1975, the Central Government in the public interest hereby relaxes the operation of the provisions of sub- 
section (1) of section 10 read with sub-section (1) of section 14A of the said Act in the State of Karnataka for the 
auctions during the crop season 2024-2025, and permits the sale of excess flue cured virginia tobacco crop of 
the registered growers and unauthorised flue cured virginia tobacco crop of the unregistered growers at the auction 
platforms authorised by the Tobacco Board. 


[F. No. 5/3/2019-EP(Agri-VI)/Pt.] 
RAJESH AGRAWAL, Addl. Secy.

का.आ. 859(अ).--केन्द्रीय सरकार, तंबाकू बोर्ड अधिनियम, 1975 (1975 का 4) की धारा 20 ए द्वारा 


प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, तंबाकू बोर्ड को फसल सीजन 2024-2025 के दौरान कर्नाटक राज्य में पंजीकृत 
उत्पादकों द्वारा उत्पादित अतिरिक्त फ्लू क्यूर्ड वर्जीनिया तम्बाकू और अपंजीकृत उत्पादकों द्वारा उत्पादित 
अप्राधिकृत फ्लू ALS वर्जीनिया तम्बाकू की कर्नाटक राज्य में इसके नीलामी प्लेटफार्मों पर नीलामी में तम्बाकू बोर्ड 
के पंजीकृत व्यापारियों और डीलरों को उक्त अधिनियम के तहत सामान्य लागू सेवा प्रभारों के भुगतान पर खरीद 
की अनुमति देने के लिए प्राधिकृत करती है।

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th February, 2025 

S.O. 859(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 20A of the Tobacco Board Act, 1975 
(4 of 1975), the Central Government hereby authorises the Tobacco Board to allow the registered traders and dealers 
of the Tobacco Board to purchase at their auction platforms in the State of Karnataka for the auctions during crop season 
2024-2025, the excess flue cured virginia tobacco produced by the registered growers and the unauthorised flue cured 
virginia tobacco produced by unregistered growers in the State of Karnataka on the payment of normal applicable service 
charges under the said Act.
