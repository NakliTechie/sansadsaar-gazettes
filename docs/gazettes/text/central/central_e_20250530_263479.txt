recruitment to the Indian Civil Accounts Service in the Department of Expenditure, under the Ministry of Finance, 


namely:-

Definitions.- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires,- 

“appointed day” means the date on which these rules come into force; 

“Commission” means the Union Public Service Commission; 

“Controlling Authority” means the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Expenditure); 


“Departmental Promotion Committee” means a Committee constituted to consider promotions in any grade of 
the Service specified in Schedule I of these rules and the composition of such Committee shall be as specified 
in Schedule III of these rules; 


“Departmental Confirmation Committee” means a committee constituted to consider the probation and 
confirmation in the grade of Junior Time Scale and its composition shall be as specified in Schedule HI of 
these rules; 


“Duty Posts” means permanent post, included in Schedule I of the rules; 


“Examination” means the Civil Services Examination conducted by the Commission for recruitment to the 
Indian Civil Accounts Service and such other Service or Services as may be specified by the Commission from 
time to time; 


“Government” means the Central Government; 
“Grade” means a grade of the service; 


“OBC” shall comprise Other Backward Classes having the same meaning and applicability as laid down in the 
O.M. No. 36012/22/93-Estt. (SCT) dated 08.09.1993, of the DoP&T, as amended from time to time; 


“Regular Service” in relation to any grade means the period or periods of service in that grade rendered after 
selection and appointment on regular basis to that grade and includes any period or periods: 


(i) taken into account for purpose of seniority in the case of those already in service at that time of 
notification of these rules; 


(ii) during which an officer would have held a post in that grade but for being on leave or otherwise 
not being available for holding such posts; 


“Schedule” means a Schedule attached to these rules; 


“Scheduled Castes” and “Scheduled Tribes” shall respectively have the same meanings as in clauses (24) and 
(25) of article 366 of the Constitution; 


“Screening Committee” means a committee constituted as specified in Schedule III of these rules to consider 
placement in the Non-Functional Selection Grade of the Junior Administrative Grade or grant of Non- 
Functional Upgradation (NFU) in any grade; 


“Service” means the Indian Civil Accounts Service, which shall consist of the posts or grades or time scales 
mentioned in Schedule-I to these rules;

3. Constitution of the Service.- (1) The Service shall consist of the following persons, namely:- 


(i) All the existing officers holding appointment on regular basis in the Indian Civil Accounts Service in 
their respective grades on the appointed day; 


(ii) Persons appointed to the Service in accordance with the provisions of these rules. 
(2) All the posts included in the Service shall be classified as Group ‘A’ posts. 


(3) The regular service of the officers referred to in clause (i) of sub-rule (1) before the commencement of these rules 
shall be counted for the purpose of probation, confirmation, qualifying service for promotion and seniority in the 
Service. 


(4) The cadre of Service shall be controlled by the Controlling Authority. 


4. Grades and authorised strength of the Service.- (1) The grades, authorised strength and pay matrix of 
various grades of the duty posts of the Service on the appointed day shall be as specified in the Schedule-I. 


(2) After the appointed day, the authorised strength of the duty posts of various grades of the Service shall be such 
as may, from time to time, be determined by the Government (M/o Finance & D/o Personnel & Training) through 
established procedure. 


(3) The authority competent to make an appointment to a grade may make permanent or temporary additions to 
that grade, through the established procedure determined by the Government (M/o Finance and D/o Personnel & 
Training), as it may deem necessary from time to time subject to any general or special order that may be issued by the 
President. 


5. Method of Recruitment.- (1) Any vacancy in any of the grades specified in Schedule I after the notification 
of these rules shall be filled in the manner hereinafter provided in this rule, namely:- 


(i) By Direct Recruitment — By direct recruitment in the Junior Time Scale on the basis of the Civil Services 
Examination conducted by the Commission in accordance with the rules for said exam notified by the 
Government/ Commission. 50% of the vacancies in the grade of Junior Time Scale shall be filled by 
Direct Recruitment. 


(ii) By promotion of officers from Central Civil Accounts Service — 50% of the vacancies in the grade of 
Junior Time Scale shall be filled by promotion on selection basis from amongst Senior Accounts Officers 
in Group ‘A’ of the Central Civil Accounts Service in level 10 in the pay matrix (Rs. 56100-177500) 
with three years’ regular service in the grade on the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion 
Committee, as specified in Schedule-III. 


(2) Appointments to the posts or grades at the level of Senior Time Scale and above in the Service shall be made 
by promotion in accordance with Schedule-II. 


(3) In the event of non availability of eligible officers by promotion, the Government may, fill the duty posts of 
Junior Administrative Grade and Senior Time Scale grades on deputation basis for specified period, from the officers 
of Ministries/ Departments of the Central Government as per the criteria, eligibility, terms and conditions in accordance 
with Schedule-IT: 

Provided that the period of deputation shall ordinarily be for a period not exceeding three years. 


6. Appointment to the Service.- All appointments to the Service shall be made by the Government as per 
provisions of Scheduled-II. 


7. Probation.- (1) Every officer recruited to the grade of Junior Time Scale by Direct Recruitment shall be on 
probation for a period of two years:- 


1. Provided that the Controlling Authority may extend the period of probation in accordance with the 
instructions issued by the Government from time to time. 


li. Provided further that any decision for extension of a probation period shall be taken ordinarily within eight 
weeks after the expiry of the previous probationary period and communicated in writing to the concerned 
officer together with the reasons for so doing within the said period.

(2) On completion of the period of probation or any extension thereof, officers shall, if considered fit for 
permanent appointment, be retained in their appointments on regular basis and be confirmed in the due course against 
the available substantive vacancies, as the case may be. 


(3) If, during the period of probation or any extension thereof, as the case may be, Government is of the opinion 
that an officer is not fit for permanent appointment, Government may discharge or revert the officer to the post held by 
him prior to his appointment in the service, as the case may be. 


(4) During the period of probation, or any extension thereof, candidates may be required by the Government to 
undergo such courses of training and instructions and to pass examinations, and tests (including examination in Hindi) 
as the Government may deem fit, as a condition to satisfactory completion of the probation. 


(5) During the period of probation, passing of Foundation Course shall be mandatory for probationers. 


(6) As regards other matters relating to probation, the members of the Service will be governed by the instructions 
issued by the Government in this regard from time to time. 


8. Promotion.- (1)(a) The field of selection and eligibility criteria for promotion to various grades of the Service 
shall be as specified in Schedule-II. 


(b) Appointments to the posts of Senior Time Scale and above (except for Non Functional Selection Grade, where 
appointment is made through placement), in the Service shall be made by promotion from amongst the officers in the 
next lower grade with the minimum qualifying service as specified in Column 4 of Schedule-II. 

(2) Appointment of officers to Non-Functional Selection Grade (level-13) shall be made as placement on the basis of 
seniority, subject to suitability on the recommendations of the Committee consulted for this purpose in accordance with 
Schedule-II (A). 


9. Non-Functional Upgradation.- (1) Non-functional upgradation to officers of Indian Civil Accounts Service 
in various grades (upto Higher Administrative Grade Level) of the service, shall be granted to eligible officers on the 
recommendations of the Screening Committee as specified in Schedule III of the rules, whenever an Indian 
Administrative Service Officer of the State or Joint Cadre is posted at the Centre to a particular grade carrying pay ina 
specific level in the pay matrix, the officers belonging to batches of the organised Group 'A' services that are senior by 
two years or more and have not so far been promoted to that particular grade would be granted the same grade on 
non-functional basis from the date of posting of Indian Administrative Service Officers in that particular grade at the 
centre, in terms of provisions of Annexure-I to Department of Personnel and Training Office Memorandum 
No. AB.14017/64/2008-Estt.(RR) dated the 24th April, 2009, as amended from time to time, and on the 
recommendations of the Departmental Screening Committee constituted in accordance with Schedule II. 


(2) As prescribed in the orders and instructions issued on the matter by the Government, all the terms and 
conditions, including prescribed eligibility criteria and promotional norms such as ‘benchmark' for promotion to a 
particular grade of Indian Civil Accounts Service, etc. shall have to be met at the time of screening for grant of higher 
pay-scale. 


(3) The ‘Batch’ of Indian Civil Accounts Service Officers for determining their eligibility for grant of non- 
functional upgradation would be decided as follows:- 


(i) In respect of directly recruited officers in the entry grade (Junior Time Scale), the “Batch' year shall be the year 
following the year in which competitive examination was held through which they were selected. The batch of the 
officer promoted to subsequent grades of the Service would remain the same for the purpose of grant of 
non-functional upgradation in different pay levels, provided the officer is not superseded due to any reason. In 
case an officer is superseded, the officer would be considered along with the 'Batch'’ with which his seniority is 
fixed. 


(ii) In respect of promotee officers inducted into the Service through promotion in the entry grade (Junior Time Scale), 
the batch year shall be the 'Batch' of the directly recruited officers, with whom their seniority is clubbed in the 
Indian Civil Accounts Service. The batch of the officer promoted to subsequent grades of the Service would remain 
the same for grant of non-functional upgradation in different pay levels, provided the officer is not superseded due 
to any reason. In case an officer is superseded, the officer would be considered along with the ‘Batch’ with which 
his seniority is fixed.

10. Seniority.- (1) The determination of seniority of direct recruits and promotees and inter-se seniority between 
direct recruits and promotees shall be governed by the principles provided in the instructions, issued by the Department 
of Personnel and Training on seniority from time to time. 


(2) Senior Accounts Officers on their promotion to Junior Time Scale of the Service will be given two years’ weightage 
in seniority and placed en-bloc below the junior most direct recruit of that year. 


11. Liability for transfer.-Officers of the service shall be liable for transfer anywhere in India and outside. 


12. Disqualifications.- No Person; 
(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or 


(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, shall be eligible 
for appointment to service: 


Provided that the Government may, if, satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal law applicable to 
such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for so doing exempt any person from 
the operation of this rule. 


13. Power to issue certain orders.- The Government may issue orders, not inconsistent with these rules, 
to provide for all matters, for which provision is necessary or expedient for the purpose of giving effect to these rules. 


14. Power to relax.- Where the Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do, it may, by 
order for reasons to be recorded in writing and in consultation with the Union Public Service Commission, relax any of 
the provisions of these rules with respect to any clause or category of persons. 


15. Saving.- Nothing in these rules shall effect reservation, relaxation of age limit and other concessions required 
to be provided for the Schedule Castes, the Schedule Tribes, Other Backward Classes, Ex-servicemen and other special 


categories of persons, in accordance with the orders issued by the Government from time to time in this regard. 


16. Interpretation.- If any question arises as to the interpretation of these rules it shall be referred to the 
Government who shall decide the same in consultation with the Commission. 


17. Residuary matters.- In regard to matters not specially covered by these rules or orders made or issued these 


under or by special orders, the members of the Service shall be governed by the rules, regulations and orders applicable 
to the Central Civil Services in general.
