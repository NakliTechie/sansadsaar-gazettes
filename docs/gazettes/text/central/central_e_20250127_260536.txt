MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2025 


S.O. 48I(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.O. 469 (E), dated the 02707 February 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 
of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building of National Highway No. 39-A (Two Lane 
/Four Lane with Paved shoulder, etc), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 3l9 A 
(Mohania-Ramgarh-Chausa-Buxar) in the stretch of land from Km. 30+660 to km 65+95 in the District of Buxar in 
the state of BIHAR; 

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Dainik Jagran and 
Dainik Bhaskar dated the 6" February 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
