MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Food and Public Distribution) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th June, 2023 


S.O. 2659(E).—In pursuance of the provisions of section 7 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial 
and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 (18 of 2016), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, 
Food and Public Distribution, Department of Food and Public Distribution, vide number S.O. 371(E), dated the 8" 
February, 2017, namely : - 


In the said notification,- 


(a) in paragraph 1, in sub-paragraph (2), for the words, figures and letters “by 30" June, 2023”, the words, 
figures and letters “by 30" September, 2023” shall be substituted; 


(b) in paragraph 2, in sub-paragraph (1), for the words, figures and letters “by 30" June, 2023”, the words, 
figures and letters “by 30" September, 2023” shall be substituted. 


[F. No. 9(2)/2016-PD-II] 
PALKA SAHNI, Jt. Secy.

S.O. 1392 (E), dated the 237 March, 2023.
