MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th April, 2025 


G.S.R. 232(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 18 of the Mines and Minerals 
(Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules further to amend the Mineral Conservation and Development Rules, 2017, namely:— 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Mineral Conservation and Development (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Mineral Conservation and Development Rules, 2017 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 3, 
in sub-rule (1), for clause (e), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: — 


‘(e) "calibrated lump ore” means the sized iron ore lumps obtained after crushing and screening of the 
run-of-mine ore, having size range between 5 mm and 40 mm and minimum Fe grade of 60% and 
above;’. 


3. In the said rules, in Schedule-I, — 


[भाग ए--खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(a) in Form F1 and Form G1, under the heading “**Grades of minerals to be reported in the above tables are as 
given below. (If separate grades are not mentioned below, report the mineral name against grade):”, in the table, 
under the column relating to GRADES, for item number (iv) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall 
be substituted, namely:— 


“(iv) Calibrated Lump Ore (CLO) 
(Quantity already reported in Lumps should not be reported against CLO) 


(a) 60% to below 62% Fe (5-40 mm size CLO) 


(b) 62% to below 65% Fe (5-40 mm size CLO) 


(c) 65% and above Fe (5-40 mm size CLO)”; 


(b) in Form L and Form M, under the heading “#Grades of minerals to be reported in the above tables are as 
given below. (If separate grades are not mentioned below, report the mineral name against grade):”, in the table, 
under the column relating to GRADES, for item number (iv) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall 
be substituted, namely:— 


“(iv) Calibrated Lump Ore (CLO) 
(Quantity already reported in Lumps should not be reported against CLO) 


(a) 60% to below 62% Fe (5-40 mm size CLO) 


(b) 62% to below 65% Fe (5-40 mm size CLO) 


(c) 65% and above Fe (5-40 mm size CLO)”.
