अधिनियम, 2008 (2008 का 11) (जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 20क के खंण्ड (1) 
द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह समाधान हो जाने के पश्चात कि लोक प्रयोजन के लिए, वह भूमि, जिसका 
संक्षिप्त विवरण इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में दिया गया है, महाराष्ट्र राज्य के पालघर जिले में, “मुंबई शहर परिवहन 
परियोजना-॥॥ # के अंतर्गत पश्चिम रेलवे पर वानगाव ई. एम. यु. कारशेड परियोजना” नाम की विशेष रेल परियोजना के 
निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के लिए अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जत करने आशय की घोषणा करती है; 


उक्त भूमि में हितबद्ध कोई व्यक्ति, राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से तीस दिन के भीतर, उक्त 
अधिनियम की धारा 20 घ की उप-धारा (1) के अधीन उपर्युक्त प्रयोजन के लिए ऐसी भूमि के अर्जन और उपयोग के सम्बन्ध 
में आक्षेप कर सकेगा; अन्यथा अधिनियम की धारा 20 (क) के खण्ड 1 के अंतर्गत उल्लेखित प्रयोजन के लिए उक्त जमीन का 
उपयोग किया जाएगा;

Mumbai, the Ist June, 2023 


S.O. 2370(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) and the Railways Act. 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act). 
The Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, declare its intention to acquire land. The land 
brief description of which has been given in the schedule annexed hereto. is required for execution of the Special 
Railway Project, namely, “Construction of Vangaon E.M.U. Carshed on Western Railway under MUTP II A Project” 
in District Palghar in the State of Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section (1) of section 20D of the said Act; Otherwise, such land will be used for the above-mentioned 
purpose under sub-section 1 of Section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Additional Residential Deputy 
Collector- Jawhar, District Collector Office, Palghar, District Palghar, Maharashtra, in writing and shall set out the 
grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or 
by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the 
competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections; 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final; 


Land finally to be acquired from the land notified in this notification will be decided after joint measurement 
of the said land. The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
