MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th October, 2023 


S.O. 4685(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O 200 (E) dated 03 May 2023, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section (i) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 ( 48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Construction (Four lane /winding), Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-66 on the stretch of land from Km. 205.400 to Km. 24.300 (Pedhe Parshuram Section) in the 
District Of Ratnagiri in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Sagar” and “Goa Times” 
both dated 20 June 2023 ; under sub- section (3) of section 3A ofthe said Act; 


And whereas objections have not been received from any person by the competent Authority 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग I—ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now , therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act,the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
