Patna, the 9th June, 2026 
S.O. 30I(E).— Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section (l) of Section 
20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred as the “said Act”), had, through the notification

land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto, situated in Dhanbad District of Jharkhand State, for the purpose of 
execution, maintenance, management and operation of “Dhanbad- Matari 3" line” railway project. 


Substance of the said notification was published in local daily newspapers in accordance with the provisions 
of sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act, and objections were received by the Competent Authority. After 
hearing such objections, the Competent Authority passed orders under the provisions of Section 20D of the said Act and 
submitted its report under sub-section (.) of Section 20E of the said Act. 


Now, therefore, the Central Government, upon receipt of the report from the Competent Authority and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, hereby declares that the land specified 
in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 

And further, in exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, Central 
Government hereby declares that, upon publication of this notification in the Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
