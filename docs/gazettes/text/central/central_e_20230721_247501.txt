MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 20th July, 2023 


S.O. 3257(E)— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 784(E) dated 9" April, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section-3, Sub section (ii) (herein after referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 2008 (lI" of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act). The Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, Mavli junction — Bari Sadri Gauge conversion 
project in the State of Rajasthan. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily local newspapers “Rajasthan 
Patrika” and “Dainik Bhasker’” dated 227" April, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection has been received by the competent authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government herby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
