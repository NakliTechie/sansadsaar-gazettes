MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th December, 2023 


G.S.R. 92(E).—Whereas the draft rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, 97l, was 
published, as required by section 36 of the Insecticides Act, 968 (46 of 968) vide notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (Department of Agriculture and 
Farmers Welfare) number G.S.R. 645(E), dated the I* September, 2023, in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section-3, Sub-section (i), inviting objections and suggestions from all persons 
likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry of a period of thirty days from the date on which the copies 
of the Gazette of India in which the said notification was published, were made available to the public; 


And whereas, copies of the said notification were made available to the public on l“ day of 
September, 2023; 


And whereas, objections or suggestions received from the public in respect of the said draft rules 
have been considered by the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36 of the said Act, the Central 
Government after consultation with the Board hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Insecticides Rules, 97], namely:- 


l.  (() These rules may be called the Insecticides (Seventh Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insecticides Rules, 97], in rule 0, in sub-rule (3A), in clause (iii), after the first proviso, the 
following proviso shall be inserted, namely:— 


“Provided further that he must have passed Class 2" or Higher Secondary Certificate Examination 
from a recognized Board or University.”
