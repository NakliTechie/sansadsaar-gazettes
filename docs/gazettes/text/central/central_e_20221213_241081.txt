परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ., जो भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग ॥, 
खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा महाराष्ट्र राज्य के औरंगाबाद जिले में NH753F के कि.मी. 99.66 से 
कि.मी. 144.75 (उपविभागीय अधिकारी, सिललोड) तक के भूखण्ड पर टोल प्लाजा के निर्माण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और 
प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

2022" को "ARAL SERA" और "30 अगस्त 2022" को "लोकमत टाईम्स" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th December, 2022 


99.0. 5833(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH753F on the stretch of land from Km. 99.66 to Km. 144.75 (sub divisional office, near 
nagar parishad, sillod) in the district of AURANGABAD in the state of MAHARASHTRA; 


[भाग व---खण्ड 3(॥)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Sahyadri Times on dated 
29th August 2022” and “Lokmat Times on dated 30th August 2022” under sub-section (3) of section 3A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by 
the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
