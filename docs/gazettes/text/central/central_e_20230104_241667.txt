MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th January, 2023 
S.O. 29(E).— Whereas, the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (37 of 1967) (hereinafter referred to 


as the said Act) has been enacted to provide for more effective prevention of certain unlawful activities of individuals 
and associations and for dealing with terrorist activities and for matters connected therewith; 


And whereas, clause (a) of sub-section (1) and sub-section (2) of section 35 of the said Act empowers the 
Central Government to add the name of an individual in the Fourth Schedule to the said Act, if it believes that he is 
involved in terrorism; 


And whereas, Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger @ Abu Usman Al-Kashmiri son of Mohd. Abdullah Ahanger born in 
Nawakadal, Srinagar in 1974 presently based in Afghanistan is one of the chief recruiters of Islamic State Jammu and 
Kashmir (ISJK); 


And whereas, the said Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger has close contacts with Al- Qaida and other Global Terrorist 
Groups and is engaged in restarting the Islamic State (IS) channels in India; 


And whereas, the said Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger is working towards providing traction to militancy in Kashmir 
and has started the process of identifying people for inclusion in his Kashmir based network; 


[भाग व---खण्ड 3(1i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, the said Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger was appointed as head of Islamic State (IS) recruitment cell for 
India and had been instrumental in initiating an online India-centric ISIS Propaganda Magazine; 


And whereas, the said Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger is a wanted terrorist in Jammu and Kashmir for more than two 
decades and has started planning terror related strategies in Jammu and Kashmir by building coordination channels 
between various terrorist organisations; 


And whereas, the Central Government believes that Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger @ Abu Usman Al-Kashmiri is 
involved in terrorism and the said Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger @ Abu Usman Al-Kashmiri is to be added as a terrorist 
under the said Act; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (1) of section 35 of the 
Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the 
Fourth Schedule to the said Act, namely:- 


In the Fourth Schedule to the said Act, after serial number 48 and entries relating thereto, the following serial 
number and entries shall be inserted, namely: - 


“49. Aijaz Ahmad Ahanger @ Abu Usman Al-Kashmini ”.
