S.O. 236(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.0.342(E) dated 25.0.2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act) and Amendment to the said notification published vide 5.00 609(E) dated 
22.2.2022 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of 
section 3A of the said Act, the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of National Highway No. 6, in the stretch of Package-II (6-laning of existing 4-lane road from Kalaparru (Design Km. 
050.680) to Chinna Avutupalli (Design Km.l076.480) of NH-5 (new NH-I6) including 6-lane Hanuman Junction 
Bypass from (Existing Km. !055.680 to Km.l060.800) (Design Ch. 0.000 to Ch. 6.720) (Total Design length - 27.400 
Km) in the District of Krishna in the State of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification dated 25.0!.2022 has been published in “Prajasakthi 
(Telugu)” and “The Time of India” both dated 0].02.2022, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
