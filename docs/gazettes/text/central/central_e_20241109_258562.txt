MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Prayagraj, the 7th November, 2024 


S.O. 4865(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the ministry of Railways (North 
Central Railway, Gati Shakti Unit), Notification Number, 8.0. 3585(E), dated the 2l August 2024 published in Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), Notification Number 3269, dated the 22 August 2024 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (I) of section 20A of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (I" of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the Land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project namely “Prayagraj-Kanpur Central- 
Construction of 02 Lane ROB at km: 838/7-9 in lieu of level crossing no. 4 between Bamrauli — Manauri 
Stations” in the District of Prayagraj in the State of Uttar Pradesh; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely, 
“Amar Ujala, Prayagraj & Hindustan Times, Lucknow” dated 25 August 2024 under sub-section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, Six nos. of objection was received and the same has been considered by the Competent 
authority accordingly order has been passed. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent Authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the lands specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the 
schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
