चूंकि, टीवीएन एकेडमी ऑफ हल्थ साइंस के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को राज्य सरकार से कोई अनुशंसा प्राप्त नहीं हुई है। 
यहां तक कि संस्था यानी टीवीएन एकेडमी ऑफ sca साइंस ने भी जारी किए गए नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है। 


वहीं, परिषद्‌ द्वारा इस मामले पर समग्र विचार किया गया है। दिनांक 24.07.2023 की निरीक्षण रिपोर्ट के अवलोकन 
से पता चलता है कि संस्थान में कई मामलों में कमी है, विशेष रूप से वे कमियां जो नैदानिक और शिक्षण सुविधाओं से संबंधित 
हैं। ये गंभीर कमियां हैं जिसका मतलब है कि संस्थान एएनएम पाठ्यक्रम का संचालन करने में असमर्थ है।

2024 से टीवीएन एकेडमी ऑफ sce साइंस, टीवीएम नगर, पोडातुरपेट, जिला तिरुवल्लूर-634208 (तमिलनाडु) से 
एएनएम मान्यता प्राप्त योग्यता धारक प्रत्येक व्यक्ति केवल तमिलनाडु राज्य में पंजीकृत होने का हकदार होगा।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-29/429-INC: — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, TVN Academy of Health Science situated at TVM Nagar Podaturpet, Distt. Thiruvallur 63208 
Tamil Nadu is recognized by State Government of Tamil Nadu and Tamil Nadu Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct 
ANM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-29/429-INC (49626) dated 6/0/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, [947 addressed to the Secretary (Health & FW), Department of Health and Family 
Welfare, Government of Tamil Nadu, 4" Floor, Fort St. George, Secretariat, Chennai 600009, Tamil Nadu was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 6/0/2024 was also enclosed to, TVN Academy of Health Science situated at TVM Nagar 
Podaturpet, Distt. Thiruvallur 63208 Tamil Nadu. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
TVN Academy of Health Science Even the institution TVN Academy of Health Science has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 2/07/2023 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., clinical and 
teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run ANM Course 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section |4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 2/05/2024 
any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM Course from TVN Academy of Health Science situated at 
TVM Nagar Podaturpet, Distt. Thiruvallur 63208 Tamil Nadu shall be entitled to be registered only in the State 
of Tamil Nadu. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
