S.O. 2764(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the 
Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Andal-Sainthia 37१ & 
47 line” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Paschim Bardhaman and Birbhum 
Districts in the state of West Bengal, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
