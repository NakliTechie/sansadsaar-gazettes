S.O. 4588(E).—In pursuance of Section 3 of the National Commission for Women Act, 990 
(20 of 990), the Central Government hereby nominates Dr. Archana Majumdar as Member of the National 
Commission for Women (NCW) for a period of three years from the date of assumption of charge of the post, 
or till the age of 65 years, or until further orders, whichever is the earliest.
