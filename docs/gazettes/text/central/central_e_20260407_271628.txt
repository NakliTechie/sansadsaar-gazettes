Subject: Streamlining of Halal Certification Process for Meat and Meat Products - regarding. 


5.0. 1759(E).— In exercise of powers conferred under Section 3 read with section 5 of the Foreign Trade 
(Development & Regulation) Act 1992, read with Para 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended 
from time to time, the Central Government hereby amends the list of countries provided in Notification No. 34/2024- 
25 dated 01.10.2024 for the export of specified Meat and Meat products. 


2 The list of countries mentioned in Paragraph 1(v) of Notification No. 34/2024-25 is hereby expanded to include 
the following additional countries:

VU. Algeria XIV. =‘ Tunisia 
3. The compliance requirements under the "India Conformity Assessment Scheme (I-CAS)- Halal" for the 


additional countries listed above shall come into effect as follows: 


e For all the above countries except Egypt: Effect after a transition period of two (02) weeks from the date 
of this notification. 


e For Egypt: Effect after a period of six (06) months from the date of this notification, to facilitate system 
readiness and onboarding of certification bodies.

Halal Certification under the i-CAS framework for specified Meat and Meat products.
