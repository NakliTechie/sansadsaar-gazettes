NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th January, 2024 


G.S.R. 34(E).—The following draft rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, 97l, which the Central 
Government proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36 of the Insecticides Act, 968 (46 of 
968), is hereby published, as required by the said section, for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby; 
and notice is hereby given that the said draft rules shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of thirty days from 
the date on which copies of this notification as published in the Gazette of India are made available to the public; 


Objections or suggestions which may be received from any person in respect of the said draft rules before the 
expiry of the aforesaid period of thirty days shall be considered by the Central Government; 


Objections and suggestions in respect to the said draft rules may be forwarded to the Joint Secretary (Plant 
Protection), Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, 
KrishiBhawan, New Delhi-!!000I or through email at jspp-dac @ gov.in. 


Draft Rules 
l. Short title and Commencement: (|) These Rules may be called the Insecticides (First Amendment) Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their final publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Insecticides Rules, 97, in rule 0, in sub-rule (IA), in the first proviso, for the figures, letters and word
