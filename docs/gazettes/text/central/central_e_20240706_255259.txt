Mumbai, the 6th July, 2024 


S.O. 2650(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (I) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for 
execution of the Special Railway Project, namely, Construction of 2 lane Road Over Bridge (ROB) in lieu of LC 
No. 278 at Km 455/20-22 between section Varodara-Geratpur of Vadodara Division on Western Railway in the 
State of Gujarat, hereby declares its intention to acquired such land: 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
Notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section(I) of section 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely, Prant officer Nadiad, 277 floor, 
Jilla seva sadan, Dabhan Road, Nadiad, District Kheda in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner 
and may after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (ID of section 20(D) of the said act shall be 
final.
