Kolkata, the 0 th June, 2024 
S.O. 2226(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Extension of Nimcha UP avoiding line to connect 
UP loop of Kalipahari in Asansol Division of Eastern Railway” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of 


land in Paschim Bardhaman district in the State of West Bengal, for providing national infrastructure for a public 


purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
