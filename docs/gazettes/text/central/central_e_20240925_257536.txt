Highways Act, 1956(48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in 
notification of the government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, S.O. 3408(E) 
dated 27.07.2022, for the work of Widening from two lane to four lane with paved shoulder in km 60/650- 
73/2 and strengthening in km 60/650 - 61/8, 62/8-63/4, 65/2-67/2, 67/6-70/0, 71/2-71/450 and 71/750 — 
73/2 and only wearing course at km.61/8-62/8, 63/4-65/2, 67/2-67/6, 70/0-71/2 including widening of 
minor bridges, construction of additional bridge at km.64/2, widening and reconstruction of culverts, 
construction of retaining walls and drain in built up areas in Musiri - Namakkal road (NH 3818) in the 
district of Namakkal in the state of Tamil Nadu published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
Section 3, Sub-section (II),27.07.2022 namely:- 


In the schedule to the said notification, against serial no. 1 in column 2 for the entry District 
Revenue Officer, Namakkal the entry SDRO (LA), Highways, Salem shall be substituted.
