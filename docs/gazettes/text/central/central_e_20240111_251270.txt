MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th January, 2024 
S.O. 146(E).—Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) and proviso to 
sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (37 of 1967), the Central 
Government declared the Muslim League Jammu Kashmir (Masarat Alam faction), to be an unlawful 


association vide notification number S.O. 5462(E), dated the 27th December, 2023, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 27th December, 2023. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42 of the Unlawful Activities 
(Prevention) Act, 1967, the Central Government hereby directs that all powers exercisable by it under 
section 7 and section 8 of the said Act shall be exercised also by the State Governments and the Union 
Territory Administration in relation to the above said unlawful association.
