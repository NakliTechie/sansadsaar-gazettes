MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 16th January, 2025 


S.O. 297(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railway (North Western Railway Construction), S.O. No. 1059 (A) dated 4 MARCH , 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 5 
MARCH, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (i) of section 
20A of the Railways Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification for execution, maintenance and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely 
"Taranga Hill -Abu Road Via Ambaji (116.65 km ) NEW BG LINE Project District- Mehsana in 
the State of Gujarat. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers 
namely “Divya Bhaskar“and “Sandesh” on date 10 March ,2024 under sub section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection were received by the Competent Authority within due time. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the 
Competent Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise 
of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government 
free from all encumbrances.
