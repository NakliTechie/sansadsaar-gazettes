New Delhi, the l5th September, 2023 


S.O. 423(E).—_WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 5057(E), dated 2l* October, 2022 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I], Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 287 October, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway 
Project, namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor in the District of Gaya in the State of Bihar; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification for inviting objections from public thereby was 
published in daily newspapers namely “Hindustan” and “Prabhat Khabar”, dated I** November, 2022 under 
sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government;
