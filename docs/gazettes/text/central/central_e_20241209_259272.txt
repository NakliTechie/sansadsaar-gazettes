NOTIFICAION 
New Delhi, the 9th December, 2024 


G.S.R. 758(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Central Board 
of Indirect Taxes and Customs, Commissionerates, Lower Division Clerk, Group ‘C’ Post Recruitment Rules, 2022, 
namely:- 


. Short title and commencement.- (.) These rules may be called the Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, 
Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, Commissionerates, Lower Division Clerk, Group ‘C’ Post, Recruitment 
(Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, 
Commissionerates, Lower Division Clerk, Group ‘C’ Post Recruitment Rules, 2022, in rule 5, sub-rule (2) shall be 
omitted.
