Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) 
(Sixth Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


F. No. IBBI/2025-26/GN/REG30.— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (t) of sub- section () of 
section 96 read with section 240 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 206 (3! of 206), the Insolvency and 
Bankruptcy Board of India hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Insolvency and Bankruptcy 
Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) Regulations, 20l6, namely:- 


l. ({) These regulations may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution 
Process for Corporate Persons) (Sixth Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) 
Regulations, 206 (hereinafter referred to as ‘the principal regulations’), regulation 39C shall be omitted. 


3. In the principal regulations, in regulation 39D, 
(i) in clause (a), after the words, number and mark “Companies Act, 203;”, the word “and” shall be inserted. 
(ii) clause (b) shall be omitted. 


4. In the principal regulations, in Form H, in paragraph 5, point b shall be omitted.
