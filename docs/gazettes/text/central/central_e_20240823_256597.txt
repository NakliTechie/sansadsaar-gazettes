MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT 
(Department of Social Justice and Empowerment ) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd August, 2024 


No. N-16/1/2021-PLAN.—As per Government of India’s Resolution No. N-16/5/2021-PLAN 
dated 03.02.2022 regarding National Commission for Safai Karamcharis (NCSK), the President of India is pleased to 
accept the resignation of Shri Karsan D Ghavri from the post of Member of the NCSK with effect from 31.03.2024.
