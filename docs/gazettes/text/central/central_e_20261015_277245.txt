New Delhi, the 15th October, 2026 


G.S.R. 895(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 55 of the Special Economic Zones 
Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006, namely: — 


1. Short title and commencement. - (/) These rules may be called the Special Economic Zones (Fourth 
Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 18, in 
sub-rule (2), after clause (ii), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely: - 


“(ii a) For the purpose of a fund setup in any International Financial Service Centre, the provisional 
offer of space confirmed by the Developer in writing for the fund management entity shall be 
considered as the provisional offer of space. 


Explanation 1.— For the purpose of this clause, (ii b) and proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 22, the 
expressions “fund management entity (FME)” and "fund" shall have the same meanings as 
respectively assigned to them in clauses (n) and (gg) of regulation 2 of the International Financial 
Services Centres Authority Regulations, 2025. 


Explanation 2.— For the purpose of this clause, (ii b) and proviso to sub-rule (4) and sub-rule (6B) 
of rule 19, sub-rule (7) of rule 21B and proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 22, the expression “International 
Financial Service Centre” shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause (g) of section 3 of 
the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (50 of 2019) and rules or regulations 
made thereunder. 


(ii b) For a fund setup in any International Financial Service Centre, the Lease Deed executed by the 
fund management entity of the said fund shall be considered as Lease Deed of the said fund.”. 


3. In the said rules, in rule 19, — 
(i) in sub-rule (4) after third proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 


“Provided also that for a Unit set up in an International Financial Service Centre, grant of 
the first recognition, registration, license or authorisation, by whatever name called, in its final form, 
by the International Financial Services Centres Authority, shall be considered as commencement of 
service activity and the date of commencement shall be the date of grant of such recognition, 
registration, license or authorisation: 


Provided also that the date of commencement determined under the fourth proviso shall be 
the same even if the recognition, registration, license or authorisation issued by the International

determined, ceases to be valid.”; 


(ii) in sub-rule (6B), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 


“Provided that for a Unit set up in an International Financial Service Centre, the renewal of

(i) employment generated;

4. In the said rules, for rule 21B, the following rule shall be substituted, namely: — 


"21B. Units dealing with aircraft or ship leasing activities. — (/) A Unit in an International 
Financial Services Centre, authorised to undertake aircraft or ship leasing or a person intending to 
setup a Unit in an International Financial Services Centre to undertake aircraft or ship leasing activity, 
if allowed by the International Financial Services Centre Authority not to maintain separate office,

leasing activity or Trust and Company Service Provider activity, as may be approved by the 
International Financial Services Centre Authority: 


Provided that utilisation of office space or manpower or both shall be allowed only with 
Trust and Company Service Provider, which is providing or intending to provide their services to the 
person allowed under this sub-rule. 


(2) A person allowed under the sub-rule (/) shall not require a separate provisional offer of space 
and lease deed, and as such the provisional offer of space and lease deed of the Unit with which
