MINISTRY OF FINANCE

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th October, 2023 
No. 59/2023-Customs 


G.S.R. 738(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 25 of the Customs Act, 
1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, 
hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Finance (Department of Revenue), No. 55/2022-Customs, dated the 317 October 2022, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 796(E), dated the 317 October 2022, 


namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Annexure, in the entry against Condition number 5, for the figures, letters and 
words “167 day of October, 2023”, the figures, letters and words eye day of April, 2024” shall be substituted.
