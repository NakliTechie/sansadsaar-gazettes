the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening /four laning etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-56 on the stretch of land from Km 32+250 to Km !79+700 in (Prant Officer, Rajpipla) in the 
District of Narmada in the State of GUJARAT. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification $.0.498 (E) dated 20.0.2022 has been published in “Divya 
Bhaskar "' and "The Indian Express” both dated 08.!2.2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
