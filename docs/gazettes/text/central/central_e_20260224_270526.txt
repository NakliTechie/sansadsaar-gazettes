S.O. 989(E).—Whereas M/s Korba Power Limited (Formerly known as Lanco Amarkantak Power Limited), 
the applicant with its registered office at Flat No.5A, 5th Floor, D.NO.6-3-626/1/601, Parameshwara Apartment, 
Anand Nagar, Khairatabad, Hyderabad-500004 has applied for authorization under Section 164 of the Electricity Act, 
2003 for the transmission scheme, “Construction of dedicated transmission line for unit 3 & 4 (2x660 MW) of Korba 
Power Limited (Formerly known as Lanco Amarkantak Limited) Thermal Power Project at Korba, Chhattisgarh”. 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 11/2/2011-PG (Lanco Amarkantak) 
dated 5" Jul, 2011 had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of 
transmission line included in the transmission scheme, “Construction of dedicated transmission line for unit 3 & 4 
(2x660 MW) of Korba Power Limited (Formerly known as Lanco Amarkantak Limited) Thermal Power Project at 
Korba, Chhattisgarh”. 


M/s Korba Power Limited had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspaper Central 
Chronicle (in English) dated 177 May 2025, The Times of India (in English) dated 177 May 2025, Haribhoomi (in 
Hindi) dated 17" May 2025 along with corrigendum dated 187 Jun 2025, Lok Sadan (in Hindi) dated 17" May, 2025 
along with corrigendum dated 18" Jun 2025 & and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 19" July 2025 for the general 
public to make observations/ representations on the proposed transmission route within two months from the date of 
publication. Subsequently, M/s Korba Power Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 6" Jan 2026 declaring that no 
observation/ representation was received within two months from the date of Publication in the official Gazette of 
Government of India and newspaper publications. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so 
established or maintained for laying of overhead transmission line for M/s Korba Power Limited (Formerly known as 
Lanco Amarkantak Power Limited). The following overhead transmission line is covered under this scheme: -
