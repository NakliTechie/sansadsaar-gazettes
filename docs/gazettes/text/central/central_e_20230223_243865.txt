फा . सं . 18-15 / 3278 आईएनसी . – भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 ( 1947 का XLVIII ) की धारा 14 
के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्द्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है : 
जबकि , श्रुति स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग , मैलूर रोड़ , लिडकर कार्यालय के पास , जिला बिदर - 585403 , कर्नाटक कर्नाटक राज्य 
सरकार और कर्नाटक नर्सिंग काउंसिल द्वारा मान्यताप्राप्त है । 


वहीं , प्रधान सचिव , स्वास्थ्य और परिवार कल्याण विभाग , कमरा नम्बर 105 , प्रथम तल , विकास सौदा , बंगलौर 
560001 , कर्नाटक को भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए एक 
नोटिस सं . 18-15 / 3278 - आईएनसी दिनांक 19/02/2021 जारी किया गया था । नोटिस दिनांक 19/02/2021 की एक 
प्रति श्रुति स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग , मैलूर रोड़ , लिडकर कार्यालय के पास , जिला बिदर - 585403 , कर्नाटक को भी भेजी गई थी । 
जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का 
अनुरोध किया गया था । 


जबकि , श्रुति स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग के मामले में परिषद राज्य सरकार से कोई सिफारिश नहीं मिली है । और श्रुति स्कूल ऑफ 
नर्सिंग ने भी जारी नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-15 / 3278 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 
1947 ( XLVIll of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Shruti School of Nursing situated at Mailoor Road , Near Lidkar Office , Distt . Bidar 585403 
Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Nursing Council to conduct GNM course . 


- 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-15 / 3278 - INC dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of Indian 
Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family Welfare Department , Room No. 105 , 
01st Floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore – 560001 , Karnataka was issued . Copy of the notice dated 19/02/2021 was also 
enclosed to Shruti School of Nursing situated at Mailoor Road , Near Lidkar Office , Distt . Bidar - 585403 
Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council 
as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of Shruti 
School of Nursing . Even the institution Shruti School of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 26/12/2020 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , clinical and 
teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run GNM Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 any 
person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Shruti School of Nursing situated at Mailoor Road , Near 
Lidkar Office , Distt . Bidar- 585403 Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka 
subject to the orders of State government of Karnataka . 


Lt. Col. ( Dr. ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
