S.O. 4221(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 19 of the Telecommunications Act, 
2023 (44 of 2023) read with rule 5 of the Telecommunications (Framework to Notify Standards, Conformity 
Assessment and Certification) Rules, 2025, the Central Government hereby notifies the following revised 
standards for telecommunication equipment with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette, namely: — 


Number and name of revised standards 


TEC 67222606 Essential Requirements 
Session border controller 


TEC 58432606 Essential Requirements 
VHF UHF Radio System Equipment 


2. The existing standards and the above revised standards for essential requirements shall remain in force concurrently 
for a period of ninety days from the date of publication of this notification, after which, the revised standards only shall 
be applicable.
