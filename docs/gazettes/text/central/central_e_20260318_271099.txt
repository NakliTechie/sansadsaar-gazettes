3. आकडा संग्रह की पद्धति 


प्रत्येक सांख्यिकी अधिकारी अपने कार्यक्षेत्र में आने वाले सूचनादाताओं को एक नोटिस जारी करेगा, जिसमें सूचना देने की 
तिथि, वह अधिकारी अथवा कार्यालय जिनको सूचना दी जानी है, वह इकाई अथवा इकाइयाँ जिनके संबंध में सूचना दी 
जानी है, उनका ब्यौरा तथा वह प्रपत्र और विधि जिनके अनुसार सूचना प्रदान की जानी अपेक्षित है, का उल्लेख होगा। इसमें 
इस उद्देश्य के लिए निरूपित तथा सूचनादाताओं के लिए नोटिस में दर्शाई गई निश्चित तिथि तक उपलब्ध कराए जाने वाले 
वेब पोर्टल/अनुप्रयोग के माध्यम से इलेक्ट्रॉनिक रूप में सूचना प्रस्तुत करता भी शामिल होगा।

वेतन नामावली, लेखा परीक्षक की रिपोर्ट अथवा प्रदान की गई सूचना के समर्थन में कोई अन्य दस्तावेज शामिल हैं, का 
निरीक्षण सांख्यिकी अधिकारी अथवा उसके द्वारा अधिकृत किसी व्यक्ति द्वारा किया जा सकता है। 


12. निरीक्षण की विधि 


सांख्यिकी अधिकारी अथवा उसके द्वारा अधिकृत कोई व्यक्ति उद्यम के उन परिसरों में, जहाँ ऐसे अभिलेख रखे जाते हैं, 
व्यवसायिक अभिलेखों तथा अन्य संबंधित अभिलेखों के आधार पर प्रस्तुत की गई सूचना का सत्यापन कर सकता है और 
आवश्यक स्पष्टीकरण उद्यम के स्वामी, अधिभोगी, प्रबंधक या प्रभारी व्यक्ति अथवा इस प्रयोजन के लिए उद्यम द्वारा अधिकृत 
किसी व्यक्ति से प्राप्त कर सकता है।

SCHEDULE-I 
1. Subject and Purpose for Collection of Statistics 


Statistics relating to the growth, composition and structure of corporate entities, namely entities registered 
under the Companies Act, 1956 or the Companies Act, 2013 or the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, 
and belonging to the services sector including trading enterprises, shall be collected through the Annual 
Survey of Incorporated Services Sector Enterprises (ASISSE) - 2026. 


2. Geographical Area for Collection of Statistics 


The Annual Survey of Incorporated Services Sector Enterprises (ASISSE) - 2026 shall be conducted in the 
whole of India. 


3. Method of Data Collection 


A notice shall be issued by each Statistics Officer to informants under his jurisdiction, indicating therein the 
date by which, the officer or office to whom, the unit or units for which, and the format and manner in which 
information shall be required to be furnished, including submission in electronic form through a web-based 
application or portal designed for the purpose and made available to informants up to a certain date, specified 
in the notice. 


4. Nature of Informants from Whom Data May Be Collected 


The informants shall be the owner, occupier, manager or person in charge of corporate entities registered 
under the Companies Act, 1956 or the Companies Act, 2013 or the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, 
and belonging to the services sector including trading enterprises, who shall furnish information pursuant to 
the notice issued by the Statistics Officer. 


5. Consolidated Information 


A Statistics Officer may, by notice, require furnishing of consolidated information in respect of an enterprise 
identified by a specific Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) operating within a State or 
Union Territory, covering the principal place of business and all additional places of business pertaining to 
the selected GSTIN. 


6. Period During Which Collection of Statistics May Be Completed 


The date for submission of information by each informant shall be specified in the notice and shall generally 
fall during the period from April, 2026 to March, 2027. 


7. Reference Period 


Information shall be furnished for the financial year commencing on Ist April, 2024 and ending on 31st 
March, 2025, or for the accounting year of a unit ending on any date between Ist April, 2024 and 31st March, 
2025. 


8. Nature of Information to Be Collected 


The information required shall relate to assets and liabilities; receipts from services; operating and non- 
operating expenses; consumption of goods and services; nature and type of services rendered; distributive 
and marketing expenses; employment and labour cost; working days and hours; absenteeism; labour 
turnover; gross value added; earnings and social security benefits of employees; and operational

characteristics of the enterprise, including activity, ownership, scale of operation, asset base, use of 
Information and Communication Technology (ICT), and adoption of green economy or clean technology 
practices during the accounting period. 


9. Language of the Web Portal 
An informant shall furnish information in web-portal which is bilingual (English or Hindi) in nature. 
10. Obligation of Informant 


The owner, occupier, manager or person in charge of an enterprise shall furnish the information in the 
manner and by the date specified in the notice issued by the concerned Statistics Officer, and shall produce 
such relevant records for inspection and answer such questions relating to the information sought as may be 
required by the Statistics Officer or a person authorised by him. 


11. Nature of Business Records Subject to Inspection 


The business records of an enterprise, including the balance sheet, profit and loss account, muster rolls, 
attendance registers, labour registers, payrolls, auditor’s report, or any other document in support of the 
information furnished, may be inspected by the Statistics Officer or a person authorised by him. 


12. Manner of Inspection 


The Statistics Officer or a person authorised by him may verify the information furnished on the basis of the 
business records and other relevant records of the enterprise at the premises where such records are kept, and 
may seek such clarifications as may be necessary from the owner, occupier, manager or person in charge of 
the enterprise or any person authorised by the enterprise in this behalf. 


13. Restriction on Use of Information 


In accordance with section 9 of the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 read with the applicable provisions of 
the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024, the information collected through the Annual Survey of Incorporated 
Services Sector Enterprises (ASISSE) - 2026 shall be used solely for statistical purposes and shall not be 
published, disclosed or otherwise disseminated in identifiable form.
