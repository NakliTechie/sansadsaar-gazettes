MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th June, 2024 


9.0. 2340(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.O. 3723 (E) dated 2.08.2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3(A) of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 
956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening / six laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of National Highway No.-76 in the stretch of land from Km. 3.800 to Km. l65.625 
(Udaipur-Chittorgarh section) in the district of UDAIPUR in the state of RAJASTHAN 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Rajasthan Patrika” and “Pratah 
Kaal” both dated 28th December 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 9 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
