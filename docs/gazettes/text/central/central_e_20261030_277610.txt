MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th October, 2026 


9.0. 5933(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural 
Gas, Vide S.O. No. 6029(E) dated 23 December 2025, published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette of India No. 5829 dated 26 
December 2025, issued under Sub-Section (l) of Section-3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right 
of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that notification for the purpose of 
laying “Kandla -Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, Section :- Pipavav to IHB Limited Dumad” for the transportation of 
LPG in Tehsil Dhandhuka, District Ahmedabad in the State of Gujarat by IHB Limited; 


And whereas, copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the concerned land owners up to 
20" April, 2026 to 29th April 2026; 


And whereas, Shri Avalabhai Khatarabhai Baria, Competent Authority and Deputy Collector (Retd.), Kandla - 
Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, IHB Limited, Vadodara (Gujarat) has under Sub-Section-(l) of Section-6 of said Act, 
has submitted his report to the Central Government; 


And whereas, the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire Right of User therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section-() of Section-6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this notification is 
hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section-(4) of Section-6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that Right of User in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration, in IHB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


THB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section-!0 of the P&MP Act, 962 
and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would lie against the Central Government on any matter relating to the pipeline.
