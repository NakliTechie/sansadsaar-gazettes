No. PR / 266 / 2014 / DD / 296 / 2014 / DC / 1476 / 2021. - In terms of the provisions of Section 21B ( 3 ) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act , 1949 read with Rule 18 ( 17 ) of the Chartered Accountants ( Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases ) Rules , 2007 , the Disciplinary Committee has held CA. 
Sandhya Jayesh Vasandani ( Membership No. 128973 ) , M I G 386 , K K Nagar , Sector 4 , Ranna Park , 
AHMEDABAD ( Gujarat ) 380061 , guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Items ( 5 ) and ( 7 ) 
of Part I of Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in 
terms of Rule 19 ( 1 ) of the aforesaid Rules , ordered for removal of name of CA. Sandhya Jayesh 
Vasandani ( Membership No. 128973 ) from the Register of Members for a period of 01 ( One ) year and also imposed a 
fine of Rs . 1,00,000 / - ( Rupees One Lakh ) only . In pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and 
in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 2 ) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act , it is hereby notified under 
Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 1988 , that the name of said CA. Sandhya Jayesh 
Vasandani ( Membership No. 128973 ) , shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 
01 ( One ) year with effect from 24th March , 2023 .
