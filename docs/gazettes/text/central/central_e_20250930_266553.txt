G.S.R. 724(E). —In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section 
(2) of section 24 of the Coinage Act, 20ll(/ of 20]), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules, namely:- 


l.Short title and commencement.- (l) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of 
Commemorative Coin on the occasion of 50™ Birth Anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel) Rules, 
2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2.Denomination of coin.- The coin of one hundred fifty rupees denomination shall only be coined at 
the Mint for issue under the authority of the Central Government to commemorate on the occasion of 
50" Birth Anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.
