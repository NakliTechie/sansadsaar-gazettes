MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 
S.O. 4164(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 482 at Km. 
740/6-8 between Bayyavaram - Anakapalle stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State 
of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Anakapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect 


from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
