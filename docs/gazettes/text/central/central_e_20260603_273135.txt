(Terms and Conditions of Service of Employees) Regulations, 2020, namely: - 


l. (a) These regulations may be called the Bureau of Indian Standards (Terms and Conditions of Service of Employees) 
Regulations, 2026.

referred to as the said regulations), the sub clause 4(a) of clause no. |2 shall be substituted as below: 


“the stay accommodation charges and lump sum amount per day shall be at par with the central government 
employees as amended from time to time.” 


Note: The Actual expenditure on staying accommodation subject to the prescribed ceiling rates, shall be admissible on 
production of receipt & the rates are excluding taxes or Government levies, which shall be extra at actuals.
