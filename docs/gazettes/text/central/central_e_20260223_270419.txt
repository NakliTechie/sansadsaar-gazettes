MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 20th February, 2026 


S.O. 956(E).— Whereas, by the notification by Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number S.O.No.5749(E) Dated. |-2-2025. Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II Section 
3 Sub section (ii) issued under sub section (l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
Construction of Road Over Bridge in Lieu of Level Crossing (LC) No.23 in Matmari Village of Raichur Taluka in 
Raichur District, in the state of Karnataka. 


And whereas an extract of this notification was published in the daily newspapers the Samyukta Karnataka 
Dated: 26-2-2025, Deccan Herald Dated: 26-2-2025 and Praja Vani Dated: 27-|2-2025 with the approval of Manoj 
Kumar, Chief Engineer, Construction. South Central Railway, Secunderabad, under sub-section (l); Section 20A of 
this Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (L) of 20 (E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its report to 
the Central Government. 


Now therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands mentioned in 
the Schedule have been annexed. To be obtained here for the purpose mentioned above: 


Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of this Act, the Central Government, hereby declares 
on publication of notification in the Official Gazette, that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central government is from all encumbrances.
