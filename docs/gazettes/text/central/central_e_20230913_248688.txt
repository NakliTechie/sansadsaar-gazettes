Conditions of License 
No cultivator shall be granted license unless he/she satisfies that: 


He/She did not, in the course of actual cultivation, exceed the area licensed for poppy cultivation during the 
crop year 2022-23 beyond the 5% ‘Condonable Limit’ allowed in the licensing policy. 


He/she did not at any time resort to illicit cultivation of opium poppy and was not charged in any competent 
court for any offence under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 985, and the Rules made 
there under. 


He/she did not during the crop year 2022-23 violate any departmental instructions issued by the Central 
Bureau of Narcotics/ Narcotics Commissioner to the cultivators. 


Maximum Area 
All eligible cultivators will be issued license of 0.0 Hectares each. 


Cultivators will be permitted to take on lease, land belonging to others, to make up the licensed area, if they 
so desire. 


Period of license 


Except for the cultivators eligible under 2(i) of this policy License to cultivate opium poppy for production of 
Poppy Straw from which no juice is extracted through lancing will be issued for five crop years from the 
issuance i.e. license issued to cultivators for crop year 2023-24 will remain effective till crop year 2027-28. 


License will remain in force until cultivator 
a. is found involved in illicit activities, 
b. is charge sheeted under NDPS act, 
c. violate the departmental instructions related to opium poppy cultivation. 
d. voluntarily surrender license before CBN 
Forewarning 


Morphine content of Un-lanced Poppy Straw tendered during 2023-24 may become the basis for payment for 
the crop year 2023-24, if the Government decides to do so in this regard. 


Cultivators who fully ploughed back their entire poppy crop for consecutive three crop years would not be 
entitled for license in the next crop year. 


An average yield on 700 kg per hectare of un-lanced poppy capsule alongwith straw should be achieved 
during the crop year 2023-24 to become eligible for a license to cultivate opium poppy in the following year 
ie. 2024-25.
