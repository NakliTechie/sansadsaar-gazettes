MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th October, 2024 
99.0. 4672(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 47!2(E) dated 27th October, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction (Widening/4-Lane etc.) Maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 35A in the stretch of land from Km. 39.00 to Km. 79.200 for Widening to 4 
lane from Km. 39.00 to Km. 79.20 Pkg-HI (Jaunpur — Akbarpur section) and Akbarpur bypass of NH-I35A in the 


State of Uttar Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Janmorcha” and “Times of 


India” both dated 23rd December, 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
