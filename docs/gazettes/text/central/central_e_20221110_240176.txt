S.O. 5230 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 2 ) of section 1 of the Weapons 
of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems ( Prohibition of Unlawful Activities ) Amendment Act ,

which the provisions of the said Act shall come into force .
