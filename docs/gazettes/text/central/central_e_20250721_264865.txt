S.O. 3335(E).— Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of the LPG a pipeline should be laid for implementing “Kandla -Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project”, 
by IHB Limited from Pipavav to Dumad Terminal, in the state of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, it is 
necessary to acquire the right of user (RoU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid, and which 
is described in the Schedule annexed to this notification; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section (l) of Section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein; 


Any person who is interested in the land described in the said Schedule, may submit objection in writing to 
Shri Avalabhai Khatarabhai Baria, Competent Authority and Deputy Collector (Retd), "Kandla Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline 
Project” IHB Limited, Gujarat within twenty-one days from the date on which the copies of this notification issued 
under sub-section (l) of Section 3 of the said Act, as published in the Gazette of India, are made available to the general 
public.
