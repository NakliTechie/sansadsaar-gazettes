Mumbai, the l0th October, 2025 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 467(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 5.00 324 (E) dated llth July 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II,

operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Ahmednagar-Beed-Parli-Vaijnath (26l Km.) New Line 
Maharashtra” in the district of Beed in the State of Maharashtra.

report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
