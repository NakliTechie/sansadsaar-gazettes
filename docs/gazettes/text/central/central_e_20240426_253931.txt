MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th April, 2024 


S.O. 846(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Number S.O. 5!99(E) Dated 07 December 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 248 in the stretch of land from Km. 24.000 to Km. 64.000 (Raebareli- 
Allahabad Section) in the district of Pratapgarh, in the state of UTTAR PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Hindi “Jansatta” and English 
“The Times of India” both dated 30 December 2023, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
