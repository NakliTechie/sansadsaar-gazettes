NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the |2th March, 2026 
Under clause 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 380(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (ll of 2008), the Central 
Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has been 
given in schedule annexed hereto, is required for purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely “Kherwadi Railway station development of circulating area of Goods shed in 
UP direction for holding passengers during Kumbh Mela traffic in 2027” District Nashik in the State of 
Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid 
purpose under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act.

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely, Competent Authority Land 
acquisition and Sub divisional officer, Niphad, Maharashtra in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and 
the Competent Authority shall give the objector the opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal 
practitioner and may, after hearing such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
