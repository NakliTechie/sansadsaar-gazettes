G.S.R. 755(E).—The following draft rules to amend the Compensation to Victims of Hit and Run Motor

the public.

morth@gov.in. Any objections or suggestions which may be received from any person with respect to the said draft 
rules before the expiry of the period aforesaid will be considered by the Central Government.
