MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |6th June, 2026 


S.O. 375(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 
956 (48 of 956), (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for Upgradation to 
two-Lane with paved shoulders of Naliya to Narayan Sarovar Section from Design Km. 220.773 to Km. 288.987 of the 
National Highway २०.4 in Kutch District in the State of Gujarat. hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under —section (l) of section 3C of the said 
Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the 
competent authority shall give the objector and opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and 
may, after hearing all such objection and after making such further enquire, if any, as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be inspected by 
the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
