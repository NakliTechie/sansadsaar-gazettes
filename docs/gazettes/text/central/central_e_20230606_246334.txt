MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Western Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 6th June 2023 


S.O. 2500(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (Railway Board), number, S.O 49 (E) dated the 23rd March 2023 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 24th March 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the 
said notification ) and issued under sub-section () of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Nimach-Ratlam Doubling Project in the 
State of Madhya Pradesh; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“Dainik Bhaskar (Hindi)’, “Raj Express (Hindi)”, “Dashpur Express (Hindi)”, “Malav Darshan (Hindi)”, 
“Free Press Journal (English)” & “Times of India (English) on 30th March 2023 by Additional Collector 
and Competent Authority, Ratlam (M.P.) under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; And 
whereas, no objections have been received by Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
here by declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
