MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th February, 2026 


S.O. 050(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for Building 
(Two/four laning, etc) maintenance, management and operation of Shahpur-Murbad (NH-222)-Mhapsa- 
Karjat-Khopoli NH-4 (Section 3 Patgaon-Khopoli) National Highway 548A in the stretch of land from 
Km. 43/783 to Km. 69/503 in the district of Raigad in the state of Maharashtra. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objections to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section() of section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Sub Divisonal Officer Karjat, 
Division Karjat, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector 
an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either Allow or 
disallow the objections.
