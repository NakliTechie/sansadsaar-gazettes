New Delhi, the Sth May, 2026 
S.O. 223(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.745(E) dated 2/02/2026, published in Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 


Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Construction of Major Bridge on

And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 745 (0) dated 2/02/2026,has been published in 
“Dainik Lokmat” and “The Times of India” Both Dated 5/02/2026 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
