MINISTRY OF POWER 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th October, 2023 


S.O. 4616(E).—in exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section(1) of section 162 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) read with Qualifications, Powers and Functions of Chief Electrical Inspector and Electrical 
Inspectors Rules, 2006 (herein after called the "said Rules") notified on 17.08.2006 and as amended on 16.05.2016, 
the Central Government hereby appoints Shri Rajashekhara P, Manager/RST KMRL as Electrical Inspector for Kochi 
Metro Rail Limited(KMRL), from the date of this Notification till his tenure in KMRL or till further orders, whichever 
is earlier, subject to fulfilment of the conditions as mentioned in the said Rules. 


The above mentioned officer shall exercise the powers and perform his functions in respect of electrical 
works, electrical installations and electrical rolling stock in operation within the areas occupied by the KMRL or in 
respect of works and electrical installations under the control of KMRL/belonging to KMRL as per the procedure 
provided in Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electricity Supply) Regulations, 2010, as 
amended from time to time. 


It shall be ensured that Shri Rajashekhara P. shall not function as the Electrical Inspector in respect of the 
work assigned to him as Manager/RST KMRL. 


The person appointed as Electrical Inspector shall undergo such training as the Central Government may 
consider necessary for the purpose and such training shall be completed to the satisfaction of the Government.
