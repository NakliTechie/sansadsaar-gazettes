NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd April, 2024 
No. 08/2023 
Subject: - Streamlining of Halal Certification Process for Meat and Meat Products. 


S.O. 1773(E).—The Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with Section 5 
of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), as amended, read with Para 1.02 and 
2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, in partial modification of Notification No. 03/2023 dated 6" April, 2023 read 
with Notification No. 41/2023 dated 27" October, 2023, hereby extends the time period, for both accreditation of 
Halal Certification Bodies as well as for registration of export units, for a further period of three months i.e., up to 
47 July, 2024. 


ee Effect of this Notification: 


Time period for accreditation of Halal Certification Bodies and registration of Export Units have been extended by a 
period of three months i.e., up to 47 July 2024.
