NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist January, 2024 


G.S.R. 25(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines [Senior Stores 
Officer, Stores Officer, Assistant Stores Officer and Store Keeper (Technical)] Recruitment Rules, 2019, namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines 
[Senior Stores Officer, Stores Officer, Assistant Stores Officer and Store Keeper (Technical)] Recruitment 
(Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: In the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines [Senior Stores Officer, Stores Officer, Assistant Stores 
Officer and Store Keeper (Technical)] Recruitment Rules, 2019, in the Schedule, against serial number 2 relating to 
the post of Stores Officer, - 


(A) in column (9), for the entry, the entry “Two years for promotee.” shall be substituted; 
(B) in column (11), - 
(i) under the sub-heading “Promotion”, for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be substituted; 


(ii) after sub-heading “Promotion” and the entries relating thereto and before Note, the following Note shall be 
inserted, namely: - 


“Note 1: The eligibility service shall continue to be three years in respect of incumbents holding the feeder post of 
Assistant Stores Officer in level 7 in the pay matrix (Rs. 44900-142400) on regular basis on the date of notification 
of the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines [Senior Stores Officer, Stores Officer, Assistant Stores Officer 
and Store Keeper (Technical)] Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023.”; 


(iii) the “Note” shall be numbered as “Note 2”; 
(iv) under the sub-heading “Deputation (including short term contract)”, - 
(a) inclause (a), - 


(A) in sub-clause (ii), after the words, brackets, letters and figures “pay matrix (Rs. 53100-167800)”, the 
words “with four years in” shall be inserted; 


(B) in sub-clause (111), for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be substituted; 
(b) for clause (b), and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 
(b) possessing the following educational qualifications and experience, - 


(1) Masters Degree with Economics or Commerce or Statistics or Business Studies or Public 
Administration from a recognised University or Institute; 


(1) two years experience in indenting, purchase of technical stores and stores account in a concern of the 
Central Government or State Government or statutory or autonomous organisation or public sector 
undertakings or recognised University or research Institution. 


OR 


(i) Bachelors Degree in Economics or Commerce or Statistics or Business Studies or Public 
Administration as a subject from a recognised University or Institute; 


(ii) Diploma in Materials Management or Warehousing Management or Purchasing or Logistics or 
Public Procurement from recognised University or Institute;
