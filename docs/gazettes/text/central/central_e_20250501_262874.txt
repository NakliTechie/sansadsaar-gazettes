MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th April, 2025 


F. No. RT-2308/04/209-T(Part)—Whereas section 40 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities 
Act, 206 (49 of 20l6) envisages that the Central Government shall, in consultation with the Chief 
Commissioner, formulate rules for persons with disabilities laying down the standards of accessibility for 
the physical environment, transportation, information and communications, including appropriate 
technologies and systems, and other facilities and services provided to the public in urban and rural areas; 


And Whereas, the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways released ‘Accessibility Guidelines for 
Bus Terminals and Bus Stops' which were prepared after detailed consultation with stakeholders 
including consultation with Chief Commissioner of Persons with Disabilities; 


Now, therefore, the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways hereby notifies the ‘Accessibility 
Guidelines for Bus Terminals and Bus Stops' and the same is made available on the web site via link 
https://morth.nic.in/sites/default/files/circulars_document/l-Accesibility %20guidelines_Bus- 


Terminals0O.pdf .
