THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA, NEW DELHI 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th June, 2024 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. PR-229/2014/DD/307/2014/DC/716/2017.—In terms of the provisions of Section 21B(3) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rule 18(17) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held CA. V 
Nagarajan (Membership No. 024450), No. 7 III, 7th Main Road, New Colony, Chromepet, CHENNAI-600 044, 
guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clause (7) of Part I of the Second Schedule to the 
aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in terms of Rule 19(1) of the aforesaid 
Rules, ordered for removal of name of CA. V Nagarajan (Membership No. 024450) from the Register of Members 
for a period of 02 (Two) years. Aggrieved by Order of the Committee, the Respondent preferred an Appeal (no. 
25/ICAI/2021) before the Appellate Authority in terms of Section 22G of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and 
the Appellate Authority by its order dated 11" October, 2023 reduced the period of removal of name of the Appellant 
from 02 (Two) years to 03 (Three) months. In pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Appellate Authority and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified under 
Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 1988, that the name of CA. V Nagarajan (Membership 
No. 024450), shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 03 (Three) months with effect 
from 28" June, 2024. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secretary
