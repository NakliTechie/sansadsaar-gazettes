MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 
(Land and Development Office) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 7th February, 2025 
S.O. 646(E).—In pursuance of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of Section 4 of the Aadhaar 
(Targeted Delivery of Financial and other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 20I6, (as amended), read with Rule 5 
of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020, Ministry 
of Housing and Urban Affairs having been authorized by the Central Government, hereby notifies that the Land and 
Development Office, is allowed to perform Aadhaar Authentication, on voluntary basis, for establishing the identity of 


individuals at the time of applying for process’ related to land under the purview of Land and Development Office on 
its online portal, using Yes/No or/and eK YC authentication facility. 


2. Land and Development Office shall adhere to all provisions of the Act, the rules and regulations made 
thereunder and directions issued by the ‘Unique Identification Authority of India’.
