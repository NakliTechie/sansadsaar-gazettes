MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th July, 2023 


G.S.R. 518(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (f) of sub-section (2) of section 63 read with 
sub-section (4) of section 16 of the Competition Act, 2002 (12 of 2003), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules to amend the Competition Commission of India (Director General) Recruitment Rules, 2009, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Competition Commission of India (Director General) Recruitment 
Amendment Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Competition Commission of India (Director General) Recruitment Rules, 2009 (hereinafter referred to as 
the said rules), after rule 3, the following rule shall be inserted, namely :— 


“3A. Selection Committee and its procedure. —-(1) Selection of the Director General shall be made through the 
Search-cum-Selection Committee consisting of — 


(a) the Chairperson, Competition Commission of India - Chairperson; 
(b) an officer not below the rank of Joint Secretary 
to the Government of India in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs - Member; 
(c) an expert-person of eminence nominated by the - Member. 
Central Government 
(2) For the purpose of selection of the Director General, the Search-cum-Selection Committee shall- 
(a) devise its own procedure; and 
(b) recommend a panel of upto three names. 


(3) The Director General shall be appointed by the Competition Commission of India out of the panel 
recommended by the Search-cum-Selection Committee, after obtaining prior approval of the Central 
Government.”. 


3. In the Schedule to the said rules, - 


(a) for column (4) and the entries relating thereto, the following column and entries shall be substituted, 
namely: - 


“Level in the pay matrix 


(4) 
Level 15 (Rs. 1,82,200-2,24,100).”; 


(b) in column (10), for the words “Two years”, the words “Not applicable” shall be substituted; 
(c) in column (12), for the entries, the following entries shall be substituted, namely: - 
“Officers of the Central Government or State Government,- 
(a) G) holding analogous post on regular basis; or 


(ii) with three years regular service in the post in pay level -14 (Rs. 1,44,200-2, 18,200) in the 
pay matrix or equivalent; and 


(b) should be persons of integrity and outstanding ability and who have experience in 
investigation, and knowledge of accountancy, management, business, public administration, 
international trade, law or economics.”. 


(d) incolumn (13), for the existing entries, the entry “Not applicable” shall be substituted.

MANOJ PANDEY, Jt. Secy.
