MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th June, 2026 


S.O. 3097(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 807(E) Dated: 16/02/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highways No. 146B (Shahganj bypass to Bari section) in the stretch of land from design Km. 102.000 to Km. 142.357 in 
the district of SEHORE in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in News Papers “Navduniya” and “The 
Hitavada” both dated 06/03/2026; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


Whereas no objection under Section 3-C has been made to the Competent Authority within the period specified 
therein; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 5 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely 
in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
