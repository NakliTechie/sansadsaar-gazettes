MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th March, 2026 


S.O. 48(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.5863(E) dated 7 
December 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under 
sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for building maintenance, management and operation of National Highway 66 in 
the stretch of land Km 6.6 to km 205.4 in the District of Ratnagiri in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O.5863(E) dated 7 December 2025, has been 
published in “Dainik Tarun Bharat” and “Dainik Ratnbhumi” Both Dated 09 January 2026 ; under sub- 
section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered 
and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
