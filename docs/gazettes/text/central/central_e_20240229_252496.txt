S.O. 97(E).—Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of the LPG a pipeline should be laid for implementing “Kandla - Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline 
Project” by IHB Limited from Dumad to Bhopal in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, it is 
necessary to acquire the right of user (ROU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid, and 
which is described in the Schedule annexed to this notification. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act,l962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein;
