MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2023 


S.O. 2278(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of the India in the Ministry of Petroleum 
and Natural Gas S.O. No. 4250(E) Dated 2 September 2022 published in the Gazette of the India Gazette No. 4069 
Dated 2 September 2022 and under sub-section () of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that 
notification for the purpose of laying “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project” for the transportation of LPG in 
Tehsil Depalpur , Hatod, Sanwer District Indore In the state of Madhya Pradesh by IHB Limited. 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public up to 2] september 
2022 And whereas Dr. Ram Prakash Tiwari, the Competent Authority has under sub-section (.) of section 6 of the 
said Act submitted report to the Central Government; 


And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY 


[PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the LPG pipeline. 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration in JHB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


THB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section l0 of the P&MP Act, 962 
and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would be against the Central Government on any matter relating to the pipeline.
