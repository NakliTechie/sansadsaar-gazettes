MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th July, 2025 

S.O. 3509(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, 800 Dated:3/02/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 
(48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four- 
laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NHI9D in the stretch of land from Km. 32.2 to 
Km. 66.5 in the district of SAMASTIPUR in the state of BIHAR. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Jagaran” and 
“The Times of India” both dated 2] February’2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered 
and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 30॥)| 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
