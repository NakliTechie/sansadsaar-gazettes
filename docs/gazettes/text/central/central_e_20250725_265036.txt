(Railway Board) number 89, dated the 24.April.2025, published in the Gazzette of India (Extra-Ordinary) Part-II

Road-Anuppur & Raipur-Lakholi sections over SEC Railway to meet 3000MT loading target in the state of Odisha,

published as per section 20A sub section (4) in the daily Newspapers Nav Bharat (Hindi), Dainik Bhaskar (Hindi), 
Times of India (English) on 04/05/2025, and whereas, One objection was received within the set time period and

(.) of section 20E of the said act, the Competent Authority has submitted his report to the central Government. Now, 
therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub 
section () of the section 20E of the said act 989, the central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) 
of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the 
official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free 
from all Encumbrances.
