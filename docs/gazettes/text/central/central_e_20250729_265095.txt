उपर्युक्त संकल्प पत्र में गैर-सरकारी सदस्यों की सूची में क्रम संख्या 3 (पृष्ठ-2 और पृष्ठ-4) पर उल्लिखित श्री मिशन 
रंजन दास जी, संसद सदस्य (राज्य सभा), असम का राज्य सभा में कार्यकाल समाप्त हो जाने के कारण उक्त हिंदी 
सलाहकार समिति से उनकी सदस्यता समाप्त हो गई है। 


अतः उनके स्थान पर संसदीय कार्य मंत्रालय ने श्री मस्तान राव यादव बीडा जी, संसद सदस्य, राज्य सभा को 
नामित किया है, जिन्हें उक्त सलाहकार समिति का गैर-सरकारी सदस्य घोषित किया जाता है।

MINISTRY OF COAL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th July, 2025 


Subject: Declaration of Member of Parliament nominated by the Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs with 
respect to reconstitution of the Hindi Advisory Committee of the Ministry of Coal. 


F.No. E 11016/1/2024-Hindi.—It is hereby informed that in supersession of — Resolution No. E- 
11016/1/2019-Hindi, The Hindi Advisory Committee has been reconstituted by the Ministry of Coal, Government of 
India vide Resolution No. 11016/1/2024-Hindi- 166 on 19" June, 2025. 


In the list of non-official Members mentioned at sr. no. 3 (Page-2 and Page-4) in the aforesaid Resolution, 
Shri Mission Ranjan Das, Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha), Assam has ceased to be a member of the said Hindi 
Advisory Committee due to expiry of his tenure in Rajya Sabha. 


In his place, the Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs has nominated Shri Masthan Rao Yadav Beedha, Member 
of Parliament, Rajya Sabha, who is hereby declared as the Non-Official member of the said Hindi Advisory 
Committee. 


Effect of Notification: Shri Masthan Rao Yadav Beedha, Member of Parliament has been declared as a Non- 
Official member in the Hindi Advisory Committee of the Ministry of Coal.
