राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग Il, खंड 3, उपखंड () में प्रकाशित किया गया था, जिसमें इससे प्रभावित होने की संभावना वाले

MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
(Department of Telecommunications) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th October, 2025 


G.S.R. 740(E).— Whereas, the draft of the Telecommunications (Authorisation for Provision of 
Miscellaneous Telecommunication Services) Rules, 2025 was published, as required by section 56(1) of the 
Telecommunications Act, 2023 (44 of 2023), in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry 
of Communications, vide number G.S.R. 611(E), dated the 9th September, 2025, in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (1), inviting objections and suggestions from all persons 
likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry of the period of thirty days from the date on which the 
copies of the Official Gazette containing the said notification were made available to the public; 


And whereas, the said draft rules were made available to the public on the 10th September, 2025; 


And whereas, considering the requests received from the stakeholders for extending the timeline of 
sending comments on the said draft rules and larger implications of the said rules, the Central Government 
hereby extends the timeline of sending objections or suggestions on the draft rules upto 21.10.2025.
