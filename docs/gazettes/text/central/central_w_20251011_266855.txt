PART I—Sec. 1] THE GAZETTE OF INDIA, OCTOBER 11, 2025 (ASVINA 19, 1947) 517 
PRESIDENT’S SECRETARIAT 
New Delhi, the 15th August 2025 


No. 61-Pres/2025—The President of India is pleased to award Medal for Gallantry (GM) to the under mentioned 
personnel of Madhya Pradesh Police:— 


SI No Name of the Awardee Rank at the time of Action Medal awarded 
1 Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan Sub-Inspector GM 


Statement of service for which the decoration has been awarded 


On 24/6/2003, SHO Ghatigaon Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan along with one Home Gaurd Sainik Seetaram was busy 
in the investigation of crime No. 49/2003 u/s 394 IPC 11/13 MPDPK Act at village Danda Khirk and they received a reliable 
tip from a deployed informer that the notorious absconder Wagqila Gurjar resident of Danda Khirk alongwith three to four 
unknown armed Badmash dressed in military uniform were spotted since last three four days continuously near the temple of 
Hanuman and Siddh Baba in the thick forest, 3/4 KM adjacent to village Danda Khirak are regularly torturing villagers and 
collecting money forcefully for mining contractors situated in nearby forest. To verify and recce the topographical situation, 
SHO Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan along with the informer and associate Sainik Sitaram reached the pointed spot. No sooner 
they were recceing the situation two unknown armed badmash with twelve bore Guns out of which one dressed in military 
uniform came across and at gun point stopped the SHO and his team and asked about their identity. SHO responded to be 
mining officers. Meanwhile one of the armed badmash having suspicion opened fire on SHO Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan in 
which he narrowly escaped and fired in self-defence. The dacoit then scuffled and started wrestling with the SHO and tried to 
overpower him by pulling him down on earth and inflicted injuries by teeth bite on the left scapular region and left cheek and 
on both the hands, he again tried to fire on the SHO who was lying on the ground but Shri Chauhan sparingly came out of the 
clutches of the dacoit. Meanwhile the informer came forward and struck a stone boulder over the head of the dacoit two three 
times, resulting in the dacoit's death. On the other hand, the other dacoit was scuffling with the Home Guard Sainik Sitaram in 
which the dacoit opened fire on the Home Guard Sainik and tried to snatch the service revolver from him in which he rescued 
himself and fired in self-defence but undeterred with the attack, he struck with a stone boulder two three times over the head 
of the dacoit resulting in his death. Two 12 bore single barrel guns with 11 live cartridges and 03 empty cartridges were 
recovered from the deceased dacoits. On further verification, both were identified namely as Shiv Narayan S/o Gendalal Pathak 
Rio Utila and Rameshwar S/o Gendalal Pathak R/o Utila who were absconding from the village and joined hands with 
absconder Waqila Gurjar. Both dacoits committed a murder in village Utila in 1987 in which they were sentenced life 
imprisonment by Session Court, Gwalior and subsequently confirmed by High Court in Dec 2002 but both of them jumped bail 
and absconded. Within a span of six months, they formed a formidable local gang and were operating in areas of P.S. Utila. 
Behat. Ghatigaon, Panihar. Tigra etc. A reward of Rs. 5000/- each was declared by S.P. Gwalior in January 2003. 


In the entire phase of this face to face encounter of about 20 minutes, the role of Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan was of a 
high order in which the dacoits inflicted injuries by teeth bites and through the barrel and butt of the gun on the different part 
of the body of Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan, but undeterred with the attack and at a great personal risk to his life he kept on 
fighting with the absconders and it was due to his personal valour and courage and with a sense of high devotion to duty and 
an extra ordinary leadership that both the dreaded dacoits were killed in this encounter. 


In this operation, Shri Vivek Kumar Chauhan, Sub-Inspector of Madhya Pradesh Police displayed conspicuous 
gallantry, courage and devotion to duty of a higher order. 


The Medal for Gallantry (GM) is conferred under President’s Secretariat Notification No. 85-Pres/2023 
dated 14th October, 2023 and Ministry of Home Affairs’ communication No. 11024/06/2023-PMA dated 16th October, 2023. 
Consequently, this award carries with it the admissible monetary allowance with effect from 24/06/2003.
