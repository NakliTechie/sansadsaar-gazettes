S.O. 5451(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub- section (1) of Section 20A of the Railways Act,

Central Government after being satisfied that for the public purpose the land the brief description of which has been 
given in schedule annexed hereto, is required for purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
the Special Railway Project namely, "Agra Fort-Bandikui Doubling Project” in District Alwar in the state of 
Rajasthan hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar” and Rajasthan Patrika” dated 27.04.2025 under sub section (4) of Section 20A of the said ACT. 


Any whereas, in 01 village objections were received and competent Authority have passed the orders 
accordingly; 

Any whereas, in pursuance of sub section 20E of the said Act, the competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
