9.0. 5336(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 


and Highways,S.O.No 475(E) dtd 2.09.2023, published in the Gazette of India. Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of I956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building Two Lane with paved shoulder, maintenance, 
management and operation of NH-548 H on the stretch of land from Km. 09/00 to Km. 3/00 in the district 
of KOLHAPUR in the state of MAHARASHTRA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Daily Sindhudurg Samachar" 
and "Daily Rashtrageet” both dated 7.0.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
