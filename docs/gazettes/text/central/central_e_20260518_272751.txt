MINISTRY OF COAL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th May, 2026 


G.S.R. 370(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3E read with section 7 of the Coal 
Mines Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1948 (46 of 1948), the Central Government 
hereby makes the following scheme further to amend the Coal Mines Pension Scheme, 1998, namely:- 


1. (1) This scheme may be called the Coal Mines Pension (Amendment) Scheme, 2026. 
(2) It shall come into force with effect from the 8" March, 2024. 


2. In the Coal Mines Pension Scheme, 1998, in paragraph 12, after sub-para (4), the following sub- 
paragraph shall be inserted, namely:- 


“(5) The revised pension amount for pensioners under Coal Mines Family Pension Scheme, 1971 
shall be one thousand Rupee per month”.
