S.O. 6102(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Immigration and 
Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number $.0.3987(E), dated the 1“ September, 2025, namely:-

relating thereto, the following serial numbers and entries shall be inserted, namely: - 


“40. Rajkot Hirasar (Gujarat). 
41. Vadodara (Gujarat)”.
