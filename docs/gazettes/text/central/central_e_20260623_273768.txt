NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd June, 2026 


S.O. 3370(E).— In pursuance of the provisions of section 53 of the Telecommunications Act, 2023 (44 of 
2023) read with rule 82 of the Telecommunications (Authorisation for Provision of Principal Telecommunication 
Services) Rules, 2026, rule 78 of the Telecommunications (Authorisation for Provision of Miscellaneous 
Telecommunication Services) Rules, 2026, rule 62 of the Telecommunications (Authorisation for Captive 
Telecommunication Services) Rules, 2026 and rule 9 of the Telecommunications (Terms and Conditions for 
Migration) Rules, 2026, the Central Government hereby notifies the "Telecom eServices Portal" to enable the digital 


implementation of the provisions of the said Act.
