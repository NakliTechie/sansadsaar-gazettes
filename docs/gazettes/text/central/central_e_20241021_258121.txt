वरिष्ठ चयन ग्रेड (निदेशक) की चयन सूची (एसएसजीएसएल) में अधिकारी को शामिल करने के परिणामस्वरूप, श्री श्री 
निवास शर्मा, (सीएसएल संख्या- 6606) को दिनांक 01.10.2024 (अपराह्न) से नियमित आधार पर निदेशक पद पर

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st October, 2024 


F. No. 1/11/2006-Ad-III (Vol.ID/Pt./1662.—Consequent upon inclusion of officer in the Senior Selection 
Grade (Director) Select List (SSGSL) of Central Secretariat Services for the year 2024 vide DoP&Ts Order 
No. 3/2/2024-CS.1(D) dated 30.09.2024, Shri Shree Niwas Sharma (CSL No. 6606) is hereby appointed on regular 
basis to the post of Director w.e.f. 01.10.2024 (F.N.). In terms of Rule 2 (c) of CCS Rules, approved service in the 
grade of Director will be count from 01.07.2024. 


2. The above inclusion of officer in SSGSL 2024 will remain subject to further orders of the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court of India in the Civil Appeal No. 629 of 2022 arising out of SLP (C) No. 30621/2011 (Shri. Jarnail Singh & 
Ors.V. Lachhmi Narain Gupta & Ors. and other connected matters. 


BISWAJIT DAS, Dy. Director General
