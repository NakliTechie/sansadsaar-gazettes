G.S.R. 3(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section l64 of the Central Goods and Services Tax

. Short title and commencement. -() These rules may be called the Central Goods and Services Tax (Amendment) 
Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the 3“ day of December, 2023. 
2. In the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 207, in rule 80,— 
(a) after sub-rule (A), the following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely:- 


“(]83) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-rule (.), for the financial year 2022-2023, the said annual
