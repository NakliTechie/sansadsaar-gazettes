S.O. 649(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3 of the Jute Packaging 
Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, !987 (0 of 987), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry of Textiles, published in the 


Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (ii) vide number S.O. 5459(E), dated the 26" 
December, 2023, namely:-

2024”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 30" April, 2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier” 
shall be substituted.
