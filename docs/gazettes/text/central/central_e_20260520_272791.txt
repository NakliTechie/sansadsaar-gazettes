अत: अब केंद्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से उक्त रिपोर्ट के प्राप्त होने पर और उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20 ड 
की उपधारा (1) द्वारा प्रदत शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट 
भूमि पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिये अर्जित की जायेगी | 


और, यह कि केंद्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम कि धारा 20 ड कि उपधारा (2) के अनुसरण मे यह घोषणा करती है

केंद्रीय सरकार में अत्यांकित रूप से निहित हो जायेगी |

notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Kalyan-Asangaon 4th line” in the district of Thane in 
the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Loknayak” and 


“Sagar” dated 03.01.2026 under sub section (4) of section 20 A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in vehaloli village no objections were received and the same have been considered by the 
competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
