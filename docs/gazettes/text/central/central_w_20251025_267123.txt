विशेषज्ञ समिति द्वारा किया गया और उसे स्वीकार किया गया। आयोग ने दिनांक 23.07.2025 को हुई अपनी 592वीं बैठक (मद संख्या 2.17)

(B) FUNCTIONS 


The functions of the Committee will be to advise the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas in matters relating to the 
progressive use of Hindi for Official work and allied issues falling within the frame-work of the Official Language policy laid 
down by the Government of India. 

(C) TENURE 

The term of the Committee will be 3 years from the date of its composition, provided that: 


(1) A member of Parliament nominated to the Committee shall cease to be a member of the Committee as soon as 
he ceases to be a member of Parliament. 


(2) Official members of the Committee shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue of which 
they are the members of the Committee. 


(3) Any mid-term vacancy shall be filled up by the concerned member’s successor in office, who shall be a member 
of the Committee for the remaining term of three years. 


(D) TRAVELLING AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


The non-official members of the Committee will be paid travelling and daily allowances for attending the meeting of 
the Committee as per the guidelines contained in Office Memorandum Number II/20034/04/86 O.L.(A-2) dated 22 January, 
1987 of Department of Official Language and at the fixed rates and as per rules amended by the Government of India from 
time to time.
