MINISTRY OF LAW AND JUSTICE

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th September, 2023 
F. No. A-12023(1)/15/2016-Admn.III (LA).—In continuation to the Government of India, 
Ministry of Law and Justice, Department of Legal Affairs’ Notification No. A-12023(1)/15/2016-Admin. 
गा (LA) dated the 07th March, 2023, published in the Gazette of India - Extraordinary, the Central 
Government extends the tenure of Shri G.S. Pannu, Vice-President, Income Tax Appellate Tribunal 
(TAT), as President of the Tribunal in officiating capacity for a further period of six months, with effect 


from 6th of September, 2023, or till the regular President is appointed, or until further orders, whichever is 
the earliest. 


Dr. RAJIV MANI, Addl. Secy.
