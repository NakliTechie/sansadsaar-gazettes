MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd June, 2026 


S.O. 3368(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the 


Telecommunications Act, 2023 (44 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints the 23rd day of June, 2026, as 


the date on which the provisions of sub-section (1) and sub-section (6) of section 3 of the said Act shall come into 
force.
