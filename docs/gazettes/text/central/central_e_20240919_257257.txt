NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th September, 2024 


S.O 4018(E).—Whereas by a notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Housing 
and Urban Affairs vide number S.0.2813 (E), dated the 187 September, 2013, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 197 September, 2013, the Central 
Government added the metro alignment of Mumbai Metro Line-3 in respect of the metropolitan area of 
Mumbai to the Schedule of the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978 (33 of 1978) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act); 


And whereas, on the request of the Government of Maharashtra, the Central Government hereby 
considers it necessary to rename certain metro stations of the said Mumbai Metro Line-3; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 32 of the said 
Act, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the Schedule to the said Act, 
namely:- 


2: In the Schedule to the said Act, for the Mumbai Metro Line-3 (Colaba-Bandra-SEEPZ) in respect 
of the metropolitan area of Mumbai, the following shall be substituted, namely:-
