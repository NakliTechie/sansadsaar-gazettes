S.O. 1509(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Maritime Anti-Piracy Act, 2022 (3 of 


2023), hereinafter referred to as said Act, and all other powers enabling in this behalf, the Central Government hereby 
authorizes — 


(a) the rank of Chief Petty Officer and above of the Indian Navy; and the rank of Adhikari and above of the 
Indian Coast Guard to exercise the powers of arrest as exercisable by a police officer under the Bharatiya Nagarik 
Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023) for the purposes of the said Act; and 


(b) the rank of Sub-Inspector and above, of the following State Governments and Union territory 
Administrations, to exercise the powers of arrest, investigation and prosecution of any person as exercisable by a 
police officer under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023) for the purposes of the said Act:-
