शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, विचारण न्यायालयों में राष्ट्रीय अन्वेषण अभिकरण द्वारा चलाए गए मामलों, विधि द्वारा 
स्थापित पुनरीक्षण अथवा अपील न्यायालयों में उक्त मामलों से सम्बंधित अपीलों, पुनरीक्षाओं अथवा अन्य मामलों में पैरवी 
करने के लिए निम्नलिखित अधिवक्ताओं को नीचे दी गई तालिका में दिए गए विवरण के अनुसार, विशेष लोक अभियोजक

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 22nd February, 2024 
S.O. 820(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 15 of the National 
Investigation Agency Act, 2008 (34 of 2008), read with sub-section (8) of section 24 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973 (2 of 1974), the Central Government had notified the following Advocates as Special Public 
Prosecutors, as per details given in the table below, for conducting the cases instituted by the National Investigation 


Agency in the trial courts, appeals, revisions or other matters arising out of the case in revisional or appellate courts 
established by law; 


S.No. Name of Advocate Notification No. & date State/UT for which notified 
1. Sh. T. Tirounavocarassou S.O. 1551(E) dated 11.07.2012 Puducherry 
2; Sh. Amit M. Nair 9.0. 2672(E) dated 29.06.2021 Gujarat 


And whereas, the Central Government has decided to discontinue the appointment of Sh. T. Tirounavocarassou 
and Sh. Amit M. Nair; 


Now, therefore, the Central Government terminates the appointment of Sh. T. Tirounavocarassou and Sh. Amit 
M. Nair as Special Public Prosecutors with immediate effect.
