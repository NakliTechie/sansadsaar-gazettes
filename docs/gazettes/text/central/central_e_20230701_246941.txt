G.S.R. 473(E). —WHEREAS, the Central Government on being satisfied that the import duty leviable on 
Liquified Propane classifiable under CTH 2711 12 00 and Liquified Butane classifiable under CTH 2711 13 00 of the 
First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975 (51 of 1975), should be increased and that circumstances exist 
which render it necessary to take immediate action. 


NOW, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 8A of the said Customs 
Tariff Act, the Central Government, hereby directs that the First Schedule to the said Customs Tariff Act, shall 
be amended in the following manner, namely:- 


In the First Schedule to the said Customs Tariff Act, in Chapter 27, for the entry in column (4) occurring 
against tariff items 2711 12 00 and 2711 13 00, the entry “15%” shall be substituted 


2. This shall come into force with immediate effect. 
[F. No. 349/01/2023-TRU] 
AMREETA TITUS, Dy. Secy.
