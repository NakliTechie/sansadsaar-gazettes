INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F.No. 18-36 / 12954 - INC . — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Shakuntala Nursing College situated at Shivaji Nagar , Godda , Distt . Godda - 814133 , 
Jharkhand is recognized by State Government of Jharkhand and Jharkhand Nurses Registration Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-36 / 12954 - INC / 12373 dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Chief Secretary , Health and Family Welfare Department , 
Government of Jharkhand , Nepal House , Doranda , Ranchi – 834002 , Jharkhand was issued . Copy of the notice dated 
19/02/2021 was also enclosed to Shakuntala Nursing College situated at Shivaji Nagar , Godda , Distt . Godda – 
814133 , Jharkhand . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Shakuntala Nursing College . Even the institution Shakuntala Nursing College has not responded to the notices 
issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
22/06/2020 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 
i.e. , clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Shakuntala Nursing College situated at 
Shivaji Nagar , Godda , Distt . Godda – 814133 , Jharkhand shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of 
Jharkhand subject to orders of the State government of Jharkhand .
