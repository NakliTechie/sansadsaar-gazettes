S.O. 80I(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O 568 (E) dated the 23 December 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 
3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 27 December 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sun- 
section (l) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Wardha-Nagpur 
4th line in the district of Wardha in the State of Maharashtra.
