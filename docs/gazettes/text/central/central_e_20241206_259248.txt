परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. का.आ. 3236(अ). तारीख 09/08/2024, जो भारत के

दौंड मनमाड ted लाईन के 10 के गेट क्र.06 कि.मी. 284/3-4 उपर राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग संख्या-५॥1-548डी कि.मी 
115.700 से 117.700 तक के लिंपनगाव मे रेलवे उड्डाणपुल के निर्माण (उपविभागीय अधिकारी, श्रागोंदा-पारनेर 
विभाग, अहमदनगर) तक के भूखण्ड पर टोल प्लाजा के निर्माण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से 
उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

को "दैनिक नवमराठा" और "लोकमत STRAT दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

New Delhi, the 6th December, 2024 


9.0. 5276(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 3236(E). Dated:09/08/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
on National Highway No. NH-548D for Construction of Railway Over bridge at LC Gate No.6 at Km 284/3-4 of 
Daund Manmad railway line (DD-MNR) in km 115.700 to 117.700 of NH-548D (Sub Divisional Office, Shrigonda 
Parner Division) in the district ofp AHMEDNAGAR in the state of MAHARASHTRA

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Navmaratha” and “Lokmat 
times” both dated-24.08.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
