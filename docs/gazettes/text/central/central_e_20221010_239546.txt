की एक प्रति सुभवंती स्वास्थ्य शिक्षा संस्थान , रामदेव नगर , महादेवा , सीवान- 841226 , बिहार को भी भेजी गई थी । 
जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का 
अनुरोध किया गया था । 


जबकि , परिषद् को सुभवंती स्वास्थ्य शिक्षा संस्थान के मामले में राज्य सरकार से कोई सिफारिश नहीं मिली है । 
यहां तक कि सुभवंती स्वास्थ्य शिक्षा संस्थान ने भी जारी नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-05 / 10672 - INC . — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Subhwanti Institute of Health Education situated at Ramdeo Nagar , Mahadeva , 
Siwan - 841226 , Bihar is recognized by State Government of Bihar and Bihar Nurses & Registration 
Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-05 / 10672 - INC / 11916 dated 09/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition 
under section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) 
Department of Health , Government of Bihar , Vikas Bhawan , New Secretariat , Bihar was issued . Copy of 
the notice dated 09/02/2021 was also enclosed Subhwanti Institute of Health Education situated at 
Ramdeo Nagar , Mahadeva , Siwan - 841226 , Bihar . In the notice issued , the State Government was 
requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects 
the case of Subhwanti Institute of Health Education . Even the institution Subhwanti Institute of Health 
Education has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection 
under section 13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only 
that the Council would be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct 
nursing courses . Refusal for inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the 
requisite Teaching , Clinical or infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Subhwanti Institute of Health 
Education situated at Ramdeo Nagar , Mahadeva , Siwan - 841226 , Bihar shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Bihar subject to orders of State government of Bihar . 

Lt. Col ( Dr. ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
