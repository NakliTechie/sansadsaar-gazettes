New Delhi, the l6th September, 2025 


G.S.R. 639(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (2) of section 5l read with 
sub-section (3) of section 5 of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority Act, 203 (23 of 203), the 
Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Pension Fund Regulatory and 
Development Authority (Salary and Allowances Payable to, and Other Terms and Conditions of Service of, 


Chairperson and Whole-time Members) Rules, 204, namely:- 


l. () These rules may be called the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (Salary and Allowances 
Payable to, and Other Terms and Conditions of Service of, Chairperson and Whole-time Members) Amendment 
Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (Salary and Allowances Payable to, and Other Terms 
and Conditions of Service of, Chairperson and Whole-time Members) Rules, 204, (hereafter referred to as the said 


rules) in rule 5, - 


(a) for sub-rules (l) and (2), the following sub-rule (5) shall be substituted, namely : - 


“(]) The Chairperson shall have an option to receive pay as admissible to a Secretary to the Government of India 


or a consolidated salary of Rs. 5,62,500 per month without facility of house and car. 


(2) Every whole-time member shall have an option to receive pay as admissible to an Additional Secretary to the 


Government of India or a consolidated salary of Rs. 5,00,000 per month without facility of house and car’; 
(b) after sub-rule (2), the following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely: - 


“(3) In the case of an appointment of a person as the Chairperson or a whole-time member shall have an option 
to receive pay as admissible to a Secretary or Additional Secretary to the Government of India, respectively, who 
has retired from service under the Central Government or the State Government, as the case may be, and who is 
in receipt of, or has received, or has become entitled to receive any retirement benefits by way of pension, 
gratuity, employer’s contribution to the Contributory Provident Fund or other Funds or retirement benefits, the 
pay and allowances of such Chairperson or member shall be reduced by gross amount of pension and pension 
equivalent of gratuity or employer’s contribution to the Contributory Provident Fund or any other form of 
retirement benefits, if any, drawn or to be drawn by him”. 


3. In the said rules, after rule 5, the following rule shall be inserted, namely: - 


“SA. Dearness allowance. - The Chairperson or a whole-time member who has opted pay as admissible to a 
Secretary or Additional Secretary to the Government of India, respectively, shall receive dearness allowance at 


the rates admissible to a Group ‘A’ Officer of the Central Government”. 
4. In the said rules, after rule 6 the following rule shall be inserted, namely : - 


“6A. () In case the Chairperson or a whole-time member opts for Central Government pay structure, it may be 


confirmed from the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs whether General Pool Residential Accommodation 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(i)] 


may be provided and in case of non-allotment of General Pool Residential Accommodation, the HRA may be 


granted as per extant instructions issued by the Central Government in this behalf. 
(2) Charges for water, electricity and fuel consumed in the house shall be borne by the occupant of the house. 


(3) Nothing in this rule shall apply to the Chairperson and a whole-time member who has opted a 
consolidated salary”.
