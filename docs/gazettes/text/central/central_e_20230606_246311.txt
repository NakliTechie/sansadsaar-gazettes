S.O. 2451 (E).— Whereas Numaligarh Refinery Limited, is now proposed to lay Paradip Numaligarh Crude 
Oil Pipeline (PNCPL) for transportation of Crude Oil from Paradip (Odisha) to Numaligarh (Assam). The proposed 
ROU of Paradip Numaligarh Crude Oil Pipeline (PNCPL) traverses in the same route that of Indradhanush Gas Grid 
Limited’s Guwahati-Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL) for 38.38 Km. in Jorhat (Assam) District. 


Numaligarh Refinery Limited proposes to make use of about 38.38 Km. common ROU corridor of 
Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited’s Guwahati - Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL), which was acquired initially 
by Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited published in Central Gazette vide, S.O. 4806(E) Dated 29 December, 2020 
published in the Central Gazette No. 4216 dated 31 December, 2020, S.O. 332(E) Dated 20 January, 2021 published 
in the Central Gazette No. 301 dated 22 January, 2021, S.O. 4395(E) Dated 04 October, 2021 published in the Central 
Gazette No. 4042 dated 22 October, 2021, S.O. 1596(E) Dated 01 April, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 
1533 dated 04 April, 2022, S.O. 2930(E) Dated 27 June, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 2788 dated 29 
June, 2022, 8.00. 3946(E) Dated 12 August, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 3772 dated 23 August, 2022,

9.0. 5413(E) Dated 21 November, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 5185 dated 22 November, 2022. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby directs that the right of user in the lands as specified in the schedule appended to this 
notification for laying the pipeline shall, Jointly vest on the date publication of the declaration, in the Indradhanush 
Gas Grid Limited for laying the Guwahati - Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL) as well as for laying Paradip 
Numaligarh Crude Oil Pipeline (PNCPL) by Numaligarh Refinery Limited for 38.38 Km. in Jorhat (Assam) District 
free from all encumbrances.
