Patna, the 23rd October, 2024 
S.O. 4868(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred under clause 37A of Section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008, General Manager/East Central Railway has approved to declare ““New/ 4th line & 
balance 3rd line between PUNARAKH-BARH-BAKHTIYARPUR (28.22 KM) of East Central 
Railway”, as Special Railway Project. Therefore, “New/ 4thline & balance 3rd line between 


PUNARAKH-BARH-BAKHTIYARPUR (28.22 KM) of East Central Railway” project is declared 
Special Railway Project in the State of Bihar.
