6. Fee for commercial vehicle (excluding vehicles plying under National permit) registered in the district where

7. The driver or owner or a person in charge of a mechanical vehicle which is loaded in excess of permissible 
load specified for its category, shall be liable to pay fee for entering the overload vehicle on the National Highway to 


the fee collecting agency, equal to the fee specified in rule 10 of the said rules. 


8. All definitions including category of mechanical vehicles, its permissible load, category of exempted

9. The fee rates shall be displayed for information of the users in accordance with rules 12 of the said rules.
