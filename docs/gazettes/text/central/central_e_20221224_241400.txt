The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 
24th December, 2022 and is hereby published for general information:— 


THE CONSTITUTION (SCHEDULED CASTES AND SCHEDULED 
TRIBES) ORDERS (SECOND AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022 
No. 20 oF 2022 
[24th December, 2022. | 


An Act further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 950 and 
the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) (Uttar Pradesh) Order, 967. 


BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-third Year of the Republic of India as 
follows:— 


. This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) Short title. 
Orders (Second Amendment) Act, 2022.

2 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA EXTRAORDINARY [Part II— 


2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,— 


(a) "Scheduled Castes Order" means the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) 
Order, 950; 


(b) "Scheduled Tribes Order" means the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) 
(Uttar Pradesh) Order, 967. 


3. The Scheduled Castes Order is hereby amended in the manner and to the extent 
specified in the First Schedule. 


4. The Scheduled Tribes Order is hereby amended in the manner and to the extent 
specified in the Second Schedule.
