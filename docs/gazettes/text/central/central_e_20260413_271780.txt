S.R.O. 3(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Defence, Defence Research and Development 
Organisation Group ‘C’ (Security) posts Recruitment Rules, 2012, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Defence Research and Development Organisation, 
Group ‘C’ (Security) posts Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2026.
