2. उक्त संस्तुत व्यक्तिगत पाटनरोधी शुल्कों के प्रभावी क्रियान्वयन तथा समुचित संग्रहण को सुनिश्चित करने के उद्देश्य 
से, निर्दिष्ट प्राधिकारी द्वारा उक्त अंतिम निष्कर्षों में निम्नलिखित संशोधन किया जाता है। 


3. उक्त अंतिम निष्कर्षों के अनुच्छेद 154 में शुल्क तालिका के तुरंत पश्चात निम्नलिखित अनुच्छेद जोड़ा जाएगा: 


“उपरोक्त शुल्क तालिका में उल्लिखित कंपनियों के लिए निर्दिष्ट व्यक्तिगत शुल्क दरों का लागू होना इस शर्त के अधीन होगा

AMENDMENT NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th February, 2026 


Subject: Amendment to the Final Findings issued in Anti-dumping investigation on the imports of 
“Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET Resin)” originating in or exported from China PR. 


F. No. 6/24/2019-DGTR.— Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act 1975 as amended from time to 
time and the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Antidumping Duty on Dumped 
Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 1995 thereof, the Designated Authority issued the Final 
Findings vide Notification F. No. 6/24/2019-DGTR dated 28th December, 2020, recommending definitive 
anti-dumping duty on the imports of “Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET Resin)” (the subject goods) 
originating in or exported from China PR. 


2. The Designated Authority, in order to ensure the effective application and collection of the 
recommended individual anti-dumping duties, hereby amends the Final Findings. 


3. The following paragraph shall be inserted immediately below the Duty Table in paragraph 154 of 
the said Final Findings: 


“The application of the individual duty rates specified for the companies mentioned in the above duty 
table shall be conditional upon presentation to the customs authorities of a valid commercial invoice, 
on which shall appear a declaration dated and signed by an official of the entity issuing such invoice, 
identified by his/her name and function, drafted as follows: 


‘I, the undersigned, certify that the (volume) of Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET Resin) sold for 
export to India covered by this invoice was manufactured by (company name and address) in China 
PR. I declare that the information provided in this invoice is complete and correct.’ 


If no such invoice is presented, the duty applicable to all other rates shall apply. 


This requirement is without prejudice to the verification procedures independently undertaken by 
the Customs authorities under the applicable customs law and regulations.” 


4. This amendment notification shall be read in conjunction with the Final Findings issued vide 
Notification F. No. 6/24/2019-DGTR dated 28th December, 2020. 


AMITABH KUMAR, Designated Authority
