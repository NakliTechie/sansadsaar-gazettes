New Delhi, the 20th January, 2026 
S.O. 289(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways

NH-32 in the district of TIRUNELVELI in the state of TAMIL NADU, hereby declares its intention to acquire such

being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making

objections.
