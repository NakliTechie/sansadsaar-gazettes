NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 17th April , 2023 

S.O. 1762 ( E ) .— Whereas the Central Government vide notifications of the Government of India in Ministry 
of Labour and Employment numbers S.O. 1262 ( E ) , dated the 17th March , 2023 and S.O. 1263 ( E ) , dated the 
17th March , 2023 , delegated the powers exercisable by it under sub - sections ( 1 ) and ( 2 ) of section 10 of the Industrial 
Disputes Act , 1947 ( 14 of 1947 ) , and section 12 subject to the provisions of sub - sections ( 1 ) and ( 2 ) of section 10 , of 
the said Act , respectively , to the officers specified in the said notifications ; 


And whereas , consequent upon issuance of the said notifications , the notification of the Government of India 
in the Ministry of Labour and Employment number S.O. 1936 ( E ) , dated the 10th June , 2019 has become redundant ; 


Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39 of the Industrial Disputes Act , 1947 ( 14 of 
1947 ) , the Central Government hereby rescinds the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour 
and Employment number S.O. 1936 ( E ) , dated the 10th June , 2019 , except as respects things done or omitted to be 

done before such rescission .
