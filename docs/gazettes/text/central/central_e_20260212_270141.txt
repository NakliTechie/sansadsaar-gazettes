New Delhi, the 11th February, 2026 


S.O. 765(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 3 of the Airports Authority of India 
Act, 1994 (No. 55 of 1994), the Central Government hereby appoints Smt. Nivedita Dubey to the post of 
Member (HR), Airports Authority of India in the scale of pay of Rs.1,80,000-3,40,000/- (IDA) with effect 
from the date of assumption of charge of the post i.e. 30.01.2026 (A/N) till the date of her superannuation 
1.6. 31.03.2030 or until further orders, whichever is the earlier.
