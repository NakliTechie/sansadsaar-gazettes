MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 
(Department of Defence Research and Developmen) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th October, 2026 


S.R.O. 30.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Defence, Defence Research and Development 
Organisation, Store Assistant ‘B’ and Store Assistant ‘A’ (Group ‘C’ Posts), Recruitment Rules, 2022, namely:- 


1 88 (1) Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Defence 
Research and Development Organisation, Store Assistant ‘B’ and Store Assistant ‘A’ (Group ‘C’ Posts), Recruitment 
(Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: In the Ministry of Defence, Defence Research and Development Organisation, Store Assistant ‘B’ and Store 
Assistant ‘A’ (Group ‘C’ Posts), Recruitment Rules, 2022, in the Schedule, under column (7), for the post relating to 
Store Assistant ‘A’ under heading Essential Qualifications for the portion beginning with “(1) 127 class pass from a 
recognised Board” shall be substituted as under:- 


“(1) 12th class or equivalent passed from a recognised Board.”
