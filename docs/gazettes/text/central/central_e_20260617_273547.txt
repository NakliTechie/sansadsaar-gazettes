MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Consumer Affairs) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th June, 2026 


G.S.R. 489(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (1), (0) and (9) of 
sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Government Approved Test Centre) Rules, 2013, namely:- 


1. (1) Short title and commencement.- These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Government Approved Test 
Centre) Second Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Legal Metrology (Government Approved Test Centre) Rules, 2013 in rule 18, for sub-rule (3), the following 
shall be substituted:- 


“(3) A fee of rupees ten thousand shall be payable at the time of renewal of recognition of a Government 
Approved Test Centre for a period of one year in respect of each equipment.” 


[F. No. 1-30/1/2025-W&M] 
ANUPAM MISHRA, Addl. Secy. 


Note: The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide 
number G.S.R. 593 (E), dated the 5th September, 2013 and was last amended vide number G.S.R. 346 (E), 
dated 87 May, 2026.
