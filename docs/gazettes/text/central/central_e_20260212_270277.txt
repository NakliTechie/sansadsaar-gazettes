AIRPORTS AUTHORITY OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th February, 2026 
F. No. A.60011/34/2016/HRPC.— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clause 
(b) of sub-section (2) of section 42 of the Airports Authority of India Act, 1994 (55 of 1994), the Airports Authority 


of India, with the approval of the Central Government, hereby makes the following Regulations to amend the 
Airports Authority of India (Recruitment and Promotion) Regulations, 2020, namely:- 


1. SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT: 


1.1. These may be called the Airports Authority of India (Recruitment and Promotion) Amendment Regulations, 
2026. 


1.2 These shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Airports Authority of India (Recruitment and Promotion) Regulations, 2020 (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Regulations), in regulation 6, in sub-regulation 6.1, in clause (i), the Note shall be renumbered as Note 1, 
thereof and after Note 1, as so renumbered, the following Note shall be inserted, namely: - 


“Note 2: No direct recruitment in Air Traffic Control cadre and Communication Navigation and Surveillance 
cadre in E-3 and E-6 level shall be made.” 


3. In the said Regulations, in regulation 8, - 
(A) for clause (iv), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(iv)(a) For recruitment in Junior Executive (E-1 Level/ Grade) for the discipline where eligibility criteria is 
Engineering graduate including Information Technology discipline where eligibility criteria is Engineering graduate 
or Masters in Computer Application in accordance with Annexure-I of these Regulations, applications shall be invited 
from Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering qualified candidates with score of last three years without fixing any 
minimum qualifying criteria and selection will be made as per merit list prepared based on Graduate Aptitude Test in 
Engineering score of applied candidates. 


(b) In respect of recruitment at Junior Executive (E-1 Level/ Grade) in other disciplines, where eligibility requirement 
is other than the Engineering graduate, selection shall be made on the basis of merit in the online examination 
conducted by Authority.”; 

(B) after clause (v), the following clauses shall be inserted, namely: - 


“(vi) Equal weightage criteria for written examination i.e. 50% for subject related and 50% for general knowledge 
shall be adopted across all disciplines with respect to departmental examinations for the post of Junior Executive (E-1 
Level/Grade). 


(vii) Equal opportunity shall be provided for persons with disabilities in accordance with the law for the time being 
in force.; 


(viii) For direct recruitment to the post of Junior Assistant (Fire Services) (NE-4), the level for written examination 
shall be as per table below: - 


Table

Caste and Scheduled Tribe category candidates and the time duration for written examination shall be two hours.”

“15.7 The number of candidates to be considered for filling up the vacancies by direct recruitment and 25% 
Departmental Examination (DE) quota will normally be five times the number of vacancies to be filled and where the 
numbers of such candidates are less than five times the vacancies to be filled, all the qualified candidates shall be 
considered by the Selection Committee. However, where the vacancies are limited (upto 10) to be filled by direct 
recruitment and the number of candidates who apply and fulfil the prescribed criteria are more, the criteria for calling 
of candidates shall be decided by the Competent Authority. 


15.7.1 To squeeze time line, initially candidates shall be called two times of the number of vacancies and final result 
would be declared. To fill unfilled vacancies, if any, candidates may be called as per requirements from overall five 
times. This shall not be applicable in case of Fire Services and the other recruitments where interviews are mandatory 
after Computer Based Test. Likewise, the number of candidates to be called for document verification will be up to 
five times the number of vacancies. However, to squeeze timeline, initially candidates shall be called two times of the 
number of vacancies. In case vacancies still remain unfilled candidates may be called from overall up to five times 
from number of vacancies as per the requirement, duly following as per the extant instructions.”; 


(C) for sub-regulation 15.8, the following sub-regulation shall be substituted, namely: - 


“15.8 The qualification marks in the written or competitive exam for various posts (DR & DE) shall be 50% marks for

candidates. In respect of candidates with Benchmark Disabilities, irrespective of their category (Unreserved or Other 
Backward Class or Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe), the qualifying marks in written or competitive exam shall be 
40%. This is subjected to fitness of such candidate for appointment to the post (s) in Airports Authority of India.”
