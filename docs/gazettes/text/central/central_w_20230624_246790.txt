No. 26-Pres/2023—The President is pleased to award the Fire Service Medal for Gallantry to the under mentioned 
officials of Jammu & Kashmir Fire Services: — 


i) Sh. Firdous Ahmad Khan 
Selection Grade Fireman 


ii) Sh. Bashir Ahmad Ahanger 
Fireman 


2. Selection Grade Fireman, Sh. Firdous Ahmad Khan and Fireman Sh. Bashir Ahmad Ahanger were working in 
Jammu & Kashmir Fire Services during the year 2021. 


3. On 17.01.2021 at 1707 hrs. a call was received regarding a fire incident at Watal Kadal, Srinagar. The area is 
congested with high population density, and old buildings. Immediately a firefighting team including SG Fireman Firdous 
Ahmad and Bashir Ahmad Ahanger rushed to the spot. The team observed that a number of houses were under fire and LPG 
cylinders were also exploding. The fire was spreading to the adjacent property. Houses in the area were made of timber which 
were highly combustible in fire situation. The team sensed that the whole area would burn into ashes and it would result into a 
major disaster, if the fire was not prevented from spreading to the adjacent residential houses.Both the Firemen without caring 
for their life entered the burning house with a charged fire hose. They controlled the fire and prevented its spread to the adjacent 
houses. After controlling the fire, Sh. Firdous Ahmad Khan and Sh. Bashir Ahmad Ahanger rescued the residents from the 
houses to the place of safety. During the fire and rescue operation, LPG cylinders exploded and the leg and arm of Sh. Firdous 
Ahmad Khan got fractured and Sh. Bashir Ahmad sustained serious burn injuries on his face. Both were unconscious and 
shifted to the Hospital for treatment. 


4. Selection Grade Fireman, Sh. Firdous Ahmad Khan and Fireman Sh. Bashir Ahmad Ahanger thus displayed 
conspicuous gallantry, exemplary courage, professional expertise and devotion to duty of a very high order. 


5. These awards are made for gallantry under Rule 3 (i) of the Rules governing the award of Fire Service Medal for 
Gallantry and consequently carries with it special allowance admissible under rule 5 (a) w.e.f. 17.01.2021.
