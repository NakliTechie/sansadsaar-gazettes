Vijwayawada, the 26th October, 2026 
NOTICE UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT, 989 


S.O. 5809(E).— Whereas, by the notification by Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number S.O.No.793(E). Dated.!2.02.2026 Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II Section 3 
Sub section (ii), dated. 6.02.2026 (herein after referred to as said notification) and issued under sub section (l) of 
section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the Construction of Road Over Bridge in Lieu of Level 
Crossing (LC) No. 278 at Km.386/0-2 between Nidubrolu and Tsunduru Stations in Tsunduru village of Tsunduru 
mandal, Bapatla District in the state of Andhra Pradesh, hereby declares its intension to acquire such land. 


And whereas an extract of this notification was published in the daily newspapers the The Deccan Chronical, 
Sakshi Daily News paper on 25.02.2026 and Andhraprabha on 26.02.2026 with the approval of Manoj Kumar, Chief 
Engineer, Construction, Road Safety Projects, South Central Railway, Secunderabad, under sub -section (); Section 
20A of this Act. 


And Whereas, Competent Authority (LA) & Revenue Divisional Officer, Repalle have not received 
objections for Tsunduru Village of Tsunduru Mandal of Bapatla District of Andhra Pradesh State and passed orders 
U/s.20D(2). 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20(E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands mentioned 
in the Schedule have been annexed to be obtained here for the purpose mentioned above; 


Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of this Act, the Central Government, hereby declares 
on publication of notification in the Official Gazette, that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government is free from all encumbrances.
