NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 24th July, 2026 


5.0. 4197(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies the work of "Kacheguda Yard Remodeling: Increasing the CSRs of line no. 3 
(610 m), line no.4 (579 m) and line no. 5 (585 m) to accommodate full length trains of South Central Railway of 
Secunderabad - Dhone section of Hyderabad Division of South Central Railway " as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in the District of Hyderabad for providing National Infrastructure for a 
public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
