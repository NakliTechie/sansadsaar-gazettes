THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA, NEW DELHI 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th August, 2024 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. PR/G/02/2022/DD/29/2022/DC/682/2022.—In terms of the provisions of Section 2!B(3) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 949 read with Rules 8(7) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held 
CA. Santhosh DN (M. No. 252502), Yeshwanthapura, Malur Taluk, Kolar District, BENGALURU 56330, 
guilty of Professional and Other Misconduct falling within the meaning of Item (7) of Part I of Second Schedule and 
Item (2) of Part IV of the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being 
heard in terms of Rule 9(/) of the aforesaid Rules, ordered for removal of name of CA. Santhosh DN (M. 
No. 252502) from the Register of Members for a period of 03(Three) years and also imposed a fine of Rs. 2,00,000/- 
(Rupees Two Lakhs only) to be paid within 90(ninety) days and in case of default in payment of fine within stipulated 
time his name shall be removed for a further period of 30(Thirty) days. Since the Respondent had failed to pay the 
imposed fine within stipulated time, in pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in exercise 
of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, read with Regulation 8 of the 
Chartered Accountants Regulations, 988, it is hereby notified that the name of CA. Santhosh D N (M. 
No. 252502), shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a consolidated period of 03(Three) years 
and 30 (Thirty) days [03 (Three) years plus additional 30 (Thirty) days] with effect from 30" August, 2024. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secy.
