G.S.R. 52(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Mines and Minerals (Development 
and Regulation) Act, 957 (67 of 957), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend 
the Minerals (Evidence of Mineral Contents) Rules, 20I5, namely:— 


. Short title and commencement.— (|) These rules may be called the Minerals (Evidence of Mineral Contents) 
Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Minerals (Evidence of Mineral Contents) Rules, 20I5 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 2, in 
clause (iii), after the words and brackets “(67 of 957)”, the words “having grade equal to or more than the threshold 
value as specified and notified under the Atomic Minerals Concession Rules, 206” shall be inserted. 


3. In the said rules, in rule 5, — 


(i) in the marginal heading, after the word and figures “section |!”, the words, figures and letter “or section !D” 
shall be inserted. 


(ii) in the opening portion, after the word and figures “section |”, the words, figures and letter “or section |]D” shall 
be inserted. 


4. In the said rules, in rule 7,— 


(i) in sub-rule (.), after the word and figures “section |l”, the word, figures and letter “or section !lD” shall be 
inserted; 


(ii) in sub-rule (A), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:— 


“Provided that in respect of any mineral specified in the Part D of the First Schedule to the Act, the proposal 
shall be submitted to the Central Government.”; 


(iii) in sub-rule (B), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:— 


“Provided that in respect of any mineral specified in Part D of the First Schedule to the Act, in order to 
identify mineral potentiality of a block based on the available geoscience data where resources are yet to be 
established as referred in clause (a) of sub-rule (l), including in any block proposed by any person under the 
sub-rule (A), the Central Government shall place it before a committee consisting of the following members, 
namely :— 


(a) Additional Secretary or Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Mines, Government of India — Chairman; 


(b) Deputy Director General of the State Unit of Geological Survey of India of the State concerned — 
Member; 


(c) Principal Secretary or Secretary in the Mining and Geology Department of State Government (by 
whatever name called) or his representative — Member; 


(c) Director (Technical), Ministry of Mines, Government of India - Member Secretary.”; 


(iv) in sub-rule (ID), after the words “State Government”, occurring at both the places, the words “or the Central 
Government, as the case may be” shall be inserted.
