MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th May, 2025 


S.O. 2411(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building of 
flyover/VUP on Ajmer Nagaur Section of NH-58 (old NH-58) (i) Tehla at km 38/240 (ii) construction of 
Flyover/VUP at Kuchera Bypass at km 116/450 and Development of Junction on NH-89 from 0/00 to 148/250 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation in the District of 
Nagaur in the state of Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Sub Divisional Officer, Riyanbadi 
in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making 
such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections.
