MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Constrution Organization) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the Ist April, 2024 
S.O. 588(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 54(E), dated the 2 January, 2024 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 2 January, 2024 and issued under Sub-section () of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Railway Project, namely, Platform no.02 at Misrauli Railway Station 


in connection with Janghai-Pratapgarh-Amethi (87Km) Doubling Project in the district of Amethi, in the State of 
Uttar Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
"Hindustan" (Hindi) and "Times of India" (English), dated the 8 January, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A 
of the said Act, no objection have been received. 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


[भाग व---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
