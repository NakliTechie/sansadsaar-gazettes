MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |5th September, 2025 
G.S.R. 637(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution of 


India, the President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Indian Railways, (Deputy Chief Accounts Officer 
(Cash and Pay), Chief Cashier and Assistant Chief Cashier) Recruitment Rules, 202l, namely:- 


l. (l) These rules may be called the Indian Railways, (Deputy Chief Accounts Officer (Cash and Pay), Chief 
Cashier and Assistant Chief Cashier) Amendment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Indian Railways, (Deputy Chief Accounts Officer (Cash and Pay), Chief Cashier and Assistant Chief 
Cashier) Recruitment Rules, 202], in the Schedule, for the post of Chief Cashier, in Column (I), under the sub- 
heading ‘Promotion’,- 


(i) for the words, figures, brackets and letters “six years regular service in level-8 in the pay matrix”, the words, 
figures, brackets and letters “eight years regular service in level-8 (Rs. 47600-!500) in pay matrix” shall be 
substituted; 


(ii) the Note shall be renumbered as Note | thereof and after Note | as so renumbered, the following Note shall 
be inserted, namely:- 


“Note 2: The eligibility service for promotion shall continue to be six years for persons holding feeder post of 
Assistant Chief Cashier in level-8 (Rs. 47600-500) in the pay matrix on regular basis on the date of 
modification of these rules.”.

ANVITA SINHA, Executive Director Establishment (Gazetted Cadre)
