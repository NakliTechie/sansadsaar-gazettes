New Delhi, the 9th June, 2025 


S.O. 2745(E)— Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section 
(.) of section 4 of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 957 (67 of 957), the Central 
Government notified M/s. United Exploration India Private Limited under 'Category A Exploration Agencies' 
vide notification number G.S.R. 284(E) dated the 7" April, 2022 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), dated the 7" April, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification); 


2. And whereas, upon completion of its initial accreditation period, M/s. United Exploration India 
Private Limited has obtained re-accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Education and 
Training of the Quality Council of India (QCI-NABET); 


3. Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section () of section 
4 of the said Act and in accordance with the guidelines for notification of accredited private exploration 
agencies issued by the Government of India in the Ministry of Mines vide Order No. 0७. ५७।-|6/]5/202  -५॥॥65 
VI, dated the !2 August, 202] for the purposes of the second proviso to sub-section () of section 4 of the 
said Act, the Central Government hereby extends the period specified in the said notification for a further 
period of three years from the date of expiry of the said notification or till expiry of the re-accreditation 
granted or further order, whichever is earlier. 


4. This notification shall come into force on the date of publication in the official Gazette.
