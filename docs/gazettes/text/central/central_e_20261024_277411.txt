JERC No. 37/2026.—In exercise of power conferred Under Section 91 & 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 and

Staff) Regulations, 2009, and in supersession of the Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (Medical Facility) 
Regulations 2024, the Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (JERC) hereby makes the following Regulations: 


1. Short Title, Extent of Application and Commencement 


i. These Regulations may be called the Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (Medical Facility) Regulations, 
2026. 


ii. The Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (Medical Facility) Regulations, 2024 stands repealed from the date 
of notification of these Regulations. 


iii. These Regulations shall come into effect from the date of publication in the official Gazette. 


2. Definitions 
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires: - 
1. “Commission” means Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (For the State of Goa & UTs). 


ii. “Competent Authority” means the Chairperson of the Commission or any other officer to whom such powers 
may be delegated by the Commission for the purpose of these Regulations. 


iii. “Eligible Persons” includes 
e Incumbents appointed against sanctioned posts, regular employees and deputationists of the Commission. 


e Person who has superannuated (at the age of 60 or as decided by the Central Government and adopted by 
the Commission). 


e Medical facilities shall be allowed to the Chairperson and Member under these regulations after completion 
of their tenure or upon attaining the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier. During their tenure, their medical 
facilities shall be regulated as per Rule 13 of the Ministry of Power Notification dated 19.03.2007. 


e Employees in case of his/her retirement on medical incapacitation before superannuation shall also be 
eligible to avail the benefit under these regulations. Employees in case of his/her voluntary retirement by 
giving notice of not less than three months in writing to the Commission after he/she has rendered at least 
10 years of regular service, in aggregate, in Government / PSUs / Statutory Bodies out of which he/she has 
rendered at least 5 years of regular service in the Commission as on the date of voluntary retirement. 


e Incase of death, irrespective of length of service, the spouse/ dependent family members become eligible 
immediately and may opt for the scheme as per entitlement of the deceased employee. 


iv. Employees who are separated by way of removal or dismissal because of disciplinary proceedings are not 
eligible. 


v. The term 'family' means and includes 


e Husband or wife, as the case may be and other dependant family members. Dependent Family Members 
include: parents (employee can have either his/her parents or his/her parents-in-law as dependents), 
widowed sisters, widowed daughters, minor brothers and minor sister, children and step-children wholly 
dependent upon the Government Servant and who are normally residing with the Government Servant 
(son up to the age of 25 or till his marriage whichever is earlier and daughter till she gets married). In 
addition, ‘dependent’ also includes, dependent divorced /separated daughters (including their dependant 
minor children). 


e The term dependent means that income from all sources, including pension benefit, is less than Rs. 15,000 
per month. However, the spouse of the primary cardholder is an exception to the aforementioned clause.

Outdoor Treatment (OPD) 


The eligible person will be entitled to actual reimbursement of medical expenses for OPD treatment for 
themselves, spouse and their family members with the annual limits based on the last cell of the corresponding 
pay matrix + dearness allowance in which they are /were working, applicable from April to March every 
financial year. The pay scale/pay matrix shall stand revised on implementation of the respective Central Pay 
Commission. 


OPD treatment includes doctor’s consultations, medicine, tests and investigations, daycare procedures, dental 
care, eye care, denture including dental implants, hearing aid (within the overall annual limit) and spectacles 
prescribed by the treating specialist for self and dependent family members. 


This annual limit is exclusive of the expenses incurred on major tests where the cost of such tests is 7,500/- or 
more such as MRI, CT scan, Angiography etc., which will be reimbursable on an actual basis. The said limit 
may be revised by the Commission through a separate order/instructions.

Eligible person and their family members shall be entitled to have ‘dental treatment’ from any dental surgeon/clinic.

Cosmetic dental treatment like teeth whitening, Dental Veneers, Composite Bonding, tooth-coloured, Laser

Reimbursement of expenses for purchase/ replacement/repair/ adjustment of various artificial appliances shall be 
allowed. In the case of diseases like Polio, where an appliance is fitted to a child, which has to be readjusted or 


replaced periodically as the child grows or the affected part improves, reimbursement of cost of boot (shoe) in the

Ambulance Charges 


Ambulance charges for admission to the hospital in case of maternity or serious illness, emergency cases or injury 
shall be reimbursed on actuals. In case public transport such as Taxi has been used to carry the patient in emergency 
or non-availability of Ambulance, actual charges paid shall be reimbursed. 


Medical termination of pregnancy 


The expenditure incurred on medical termination of pregnancy shall be reimbursed in full provided it has been 
performed at a Health Care Centre recognised or approved under the Medical Termination of Pregnancy Act 1971. 


Annual Medical Health check-up 


The eligible person (excluding family) may undergo an annual medical examination in a financial year from the 
empanelled/ nominated health care centre. The expenditure on this will be reimbursed subject to the overall ceiling 
limit as laid down in Regulation 3(i). 


Power to review 


The power to review these Regulations shall vest with the Commission. 


Interpretation 
If any queries is arises on the interpretation of any clause or clauses of these Regulations, it shall be referred to the 
Commission, whose decision shall be final. 


Power to relax and amend 


Where the Chairperson of the Commission is of the view that it is necessary or expedient so to do, he/she may, for 
reasons to be recorded in writing, relax any of the provisions of these Regulations. The Commission reserves the 
right to amend or modify the guidelines of these Regulations in part or in full.
