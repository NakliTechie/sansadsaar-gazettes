G.S.R. 644(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Goods and Services Tax Council Secretariat, Group ‘A’ 
Executive Posts Recruitment Rules, 20l8, namely:- 


. Short title and commencement.- (|) These rules may be called the Goods and Services Tax Council Secretariat, 
Group ‘A’ Executive Posts Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Goods and Services Tax Council Secretariat, Group ‘A’ Executive Posts Recruitment Rules, 208,- 
(a) in rule 7, after the words “the Scheduled Tribes,”, the words “Other Backward Classes,” shall be inserted; 


(b) in the schedule,- 


i. against serial number | relating to the post of Director, in column ((3), for the words “is 
necessary”, the words “is not necessary” shall be substituted; 


ii. against serial number 2 relating to the post of Deputy Secretary, in column (3), for the words “‘is 
necessary”, the words “is not necessary” shall be substituted; 


iii. against serial number 3 relating to the post of Under Secretary, in column (l3), for the words “is 
necessary”, the words “is not necessary” shall be substituted.
