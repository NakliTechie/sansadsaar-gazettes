. Place of Cultivation 
Opium poppy cultivation may be licensed in any tract as may be notified in this behalf by the Central Government. 
2. Eligibility for Cultivation 
Subject to clauses 3 and 7 of this notification, the following cultivators (including legal heirs) shall be eligible 
for a license to cultivate opium poppy for obtaining opium gum through lancing: 


(i) Cultivators who had cultivated opium poppy during the crop year 2023-24 and tendered an average yield of 
Morphine (MQY-M) not less than 4.2 kg per hectare. 


Note- Average qualifying yield of Morphine in opium tendered in kilograms per hectare will be termed as Minimum 

Qualifying Yield (MQY-M) in the notification hereinafter. 

(ii) Cultivators who ploughed back their entire poppy crop, cultivated during the crop year 202-22, 2022-23

this regard, but had not similarly ploughed back their entire poppy crop during 2020-2]. 

(iii) Cultivators whose appeal against refusal of License has been allowed after the last date of settlement in the 
crop year 2023-24. 

(iv) Cultivators who were eligible for a license for the crop year 2023-24, but did not obtain license for any 
reason, or who after having obtained a license, did not actually cultivate opium poppy due to any reason. 

(v) Cultivator who is nominated by deceased eligible cultivator in column No. |] in Form No. | (see rule 7) for 
the crop year 2023-24. 

(vi) One of legal heirs of deceased eligible cultivators as determined by the District Opium Officer after 
following the due process, in the cases where nomination in Form No.| is not made for any reason or 
nomination of person not falling under definition of family members/ blood relatives in Form No. | is made. 

(vii) Cultivators who had cultivated opium poppy for production of Poppy Straw from which no juice is extracted 
through lancing during the crop year 2023-24 and have tendered poppy straw at weighment center with an 
average yield of 900 kg per hectare or more.

No cultivator shall be granted license unless he/she satisfies that: 

(0). मर She did not, in the course of actual cultivation, exceed the area licensed for poppy cultivation during

court for any offence under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 985 and the Rules made 
there under. 

(iii) He/she did not, during the crop year 2023-24, violated any of the departmental instructions issued by the 
Central Bureau of Narcotics/ Narcotics Commissioner to the cultivators.

These General Licensing conditions are without prejudice to the right of the Narcotics Commissioner/ 
Deputy Narcotics Commissioner to issue/ withhold a license whenever it is deemed proper so to do in 
accordance with the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs & Psychotropic Substances Act, 985 and the Rules 
made thereunder. 


The license shall be subject to the condition that any field may be taken over for any research that may be 
conducted by the Government directly or in collaboration with any specialized Institution or Agency for 
obtaining opium gum or poppy straw without extraction of opium. The cultivator whose field is selected for 
research shall be considered for license for the next year, if he/ she has tendered the stipulated MQY-M and 
is otherwise eligible. The area taken over for research will not be taken into account while calculating the 
Average yield. 


For the purpose of making payment of opium tendered by the cultivator, on the basis of analysis by the 
Government Opium and Alkaloid Works, Neemuch or Ghazipur, either on the quantity of opium tendered 
by a farmer calculated at 70° consistency or on the morphine content basis, if government decides to do so. 


In respect of cultivators having opium cultivation license in a particular village but are having residence in 
the adjacent village, such cultivators may be allowed to store opium in their residence, provided that there 
is continuous human settlement between such villages. 


The name of eligible cultivators will be uploaded on CBN website(cbn.nic.in) and/or CBN online portal 
(cbnonline.nic.in) and may be communicated through other means like messages on given mobile number, 
mails etc. The cultivators listed in the eligibility list shall apply through online mode on CBN Online Portal 
for obtaining license within the stipulated timeline. 


Existing licensees shall not be required to submit paper for which information is already with CBN. 
Information will be provided in case of any change from previous year.
