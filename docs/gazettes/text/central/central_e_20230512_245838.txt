INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the lth May, 2023 


F.No. 8-8/l4588-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas APS Academy of Nursing Science situated at Sector 2A, Saket Nagar Distt. Bhopal - 462024, 
Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration 
Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-l8/l4588-INC dated 27/07/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24I, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 27/07/2022 was also enclosed to APS Academy of Nursing Science situated at Sector 2A, Saket Nagar 
Distt. Bhopal - 462024, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of APS Academy of Nursing Science, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution APS Academy of Nursing Science has 
not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
30/2/202] would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 6/l/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) APS Academy of 
Nursing Science situated at Sector 2A, Saket Nagar Distt. Bhopal - 462024, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to 
be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh. 

Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
