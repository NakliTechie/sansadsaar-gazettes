MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 23rd April, 2026 
5.0. 2097(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) Number S.O. 97(A) dated the 28" April 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 28" April 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mahabubnagar — Dhone doubling project in Wanaparthy 


District of Telangana State. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers namely “Mana 
Telangana” “The New India Express” “The Hindu” and “Hindimilap” and “Rehanuma-Urdu” dated |-05-2025 under 
sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in Arepally, Thippadampally Villages of Atmakur Mandal of Wanaparthy District no 
objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been 
passed; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 

Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 


the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to 


shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
