G.S.R. 537(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A of the Government Savings Promotion 
Act, 873 (5 of 873), the Central Government hereby makes the following Scheme further to amend the National 
Savings Scheme Rules, 992, namely:- 


l. (.) These rules may be called the National Savings Scheme (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Savings Scheme Rules, 992, in rule 6, in sub-rule (), for clause (५), the following 
clauses shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(v) For the period commencing from the |‘ day of March, 2003 but before I*' day of October, 2024, the 
interest at the rate of seven-and-a-half per cent per annum shall be allowed for a calendar month on the 
lowest balance at credit of an account between the close of the tenth day and the end of the month and such 
interest shall be calculated and credited to the account at the end of each year. 


(vi) The balances at the credit of the subscribers of the National Savings Scheme under these rules, on or 
after the I*' day of October, 2024, shall bear no interest.
