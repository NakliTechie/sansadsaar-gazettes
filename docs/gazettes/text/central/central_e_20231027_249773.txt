NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th October, 2023 
No. 4 /2023 
Subject: - Streamlining of Halal Certification Process for Meat and Meat Products. 


S.O. 47I7(E).—The Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with Section 5 
of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 992 (No. 22 of 992), as amended, read with Para |.02 and 
2.0l of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, in partial modification of Notification No. 03/2023 dated 6th April, 2023, 
hereby extends the time period, for both accreditation of Halal Certification Bodies as well as for registration of export 
units, for a further period of six months i.e., up to 5" April 2024. 


2s Effect of this Notification: 


Time period for accreditation of Halal Certification Bodies and registration of Export Units have been extended 
by a period of six months i.e., up to 5th April 2024.
