अधिनियम, 1974 (1974 का 6) की धारा 12 की उप-धारा (3 क) के द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, केंद्रीय

हेतु निम्नलिखित विनियम बनाता है, अर्थात्‌:-- 


1. संक्षिप्त नाम और प्रारंभ. - (1) इन विनियमों को केंद्रीय प्रदूषण नियंत्रण बोर्ड (सदस्य सचिव को छोड़कर अन्य 
अधिकारियों और अन्य कर्मचारियों की भर्ती की पद्धति, सेवा की निबंधन और शर्तें) (संशोधन) विनियम, 2025 कहा 
जाएगा। 


(2) ये विनियम राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तिथि से प्रभावी माने जाएंगे।

CENTRAL POLLUTION CONTROL BOARD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th March, 2025 


F. No. AD-11023/2/2021-ADMIN-R-HO-CPCB-HO.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section 
(3A) of section 12 of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (6 of 1974), the Central Pollution 
Control Board, with the previous approval of the Central Government, hereby makes the following regulations further 
to amend the Central Pollution Control Board (Method of Recruitment, Terms and Conditions of Service of Officers 
and other Employees other than Member-Secretary) Regulations, 2021, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These regulations may be called the Central Pollution Control Board 
(Method of Recruitment, Terms and Conditions of Service of Officers and other Employees other than Member- 
Secretary) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) These regulations shall be deemed to have come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Central Pollution Control Board (Method of Recruitment, Terms and Conditions of Service of Officers and 
other Employees other than Member-Secretary) Regulations, 2021 notified vide F. No. AD-11023/2/2021-ADMIN-R- 
HO-CPCB-HO dated 18th November, 2021, the following regulation shall be inserted at S.No. 12, namely:- 


“(12) Relaxation in Upper Age-Limit for CPCB Regular Employees.- 


(1) Age Limit for Direct Recruits for Group A and B posts. - Relaxable for CPCB Regular 
Employees (with three years of continuous service) up to five years for posts which are in same 
line or allied cadres and where a relationship could be established that the service already 
rendered in a particular post will be useful for the efficient discharge of the duties of post. 


(2) Age Limit for Direct Recruits for Group C posts. - Relaxable for CPCB Regular Employees 
(with three years of continuous service) up to forty years for posts which are in the same line or 
allied cadres. 


AMANDEEP GARG, Chairman
