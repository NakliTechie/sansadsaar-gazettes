NOTIFICATION 


New Delhi, the 4th October, 2023 


S.O. 439I(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.949 (8), dated 26" April, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 27" April, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Eastern 
Dedicated Freight Corridor in the District of Ghaziabad in the State of Uttar Pradesh; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification for inviting objections from public thereby was 
published in daily newspapers namely “Hindustan Times” and “Dainik Jagran”, dated 24" May, 2023 under sub- 


section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period;

AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and
