MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th January, 2024 


S.O. 179(E).—Whereas, Tehreek-e-Hurriyat, Jammu and Kashmir has been declared as an unlawful 
association vide notification number S.O. 5532(E), dated the 317 December, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii). 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 5 read with sub-section (1) 
of section 4 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (37 of 1967), the Central Government hereby 
constitutes the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Tribunal consisting of Justice Sachin Datta, Judge, High Court of 
Delhi, for the purpose of adjudicating whether or not there is sufficient cause for declaring the Tehreek-e-Hurriyat, 
Jammu and Kashmir as an unlawful association.
