5.0. 3855(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section | of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (8 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints the date of issue of this 
notification as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to serial number 20 and the 
entries thereto in the Schedule to the said Act, relating to the High Denomination Banknotes (Demonetisation) Act, 
978 (ll of 978), shall come into force.
