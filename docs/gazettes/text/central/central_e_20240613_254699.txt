S.O. 2271(E).—Whereas it appears to Government that it is necessary in the public interest that for

Jharsuguda in the state of Maharashtra, a pipeline should be laid by M/s. GAIL (India) Limited.

Minerals Pipeline (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act 1962 (50 of 1962), Government of India hereby declares 
its intention to acquire the right of user therein;

date on which the copies of the notification issued under sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the said Act, as published in 
the Gazette of India, are made available to the General Public, object in writing to the acquisition of the right of used 
therein or laying of the pipeline under the land to the Competent Authority, State of Maharashtra, M/s. GAIL (India) 
Limited, Gail Bhavan, Plot No. 73, Road No. 3, Sector - 15, Cbd Belapur, Navi Mumbai - 400614.
