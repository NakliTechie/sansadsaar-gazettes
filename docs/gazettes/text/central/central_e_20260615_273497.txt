S.O. 34(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 62700) Dated:06/02/2026, 958(E) Dated:20/04/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part Il, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, 
management and operation of NH35] in the stretch of land from Km. 27 to Km. 54 in the district of AMRELI in the 
state of GUJARAT 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” dated 
4/02/2026 , “Divya Prakash” dated 4/02/2026 , “The Indian Express” dated 02/05/2026 , “AVADH TIMES” dated 
02/05/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
