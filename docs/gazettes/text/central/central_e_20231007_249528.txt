Patna, the 23rd August, 2023 


S.O. 4378(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred under clause 7A of Section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008, as nominated by State Government is hereby authorised the District Land 
Acquisition Officer, Bhojpur as Competent Authority to perform the function of such authority under the 
said Act in the area of “Ara Byepass Line to be connected from Jagjivan Halt (PNBE-DDU Main line) 
to existing Ara-Sasaram line through link cabin (Y- Connectivity at Ara) (3.06 Km) under Danapur 
Division of East Central Railway)”, which is part of special Railway project, in the State of Bihar with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Gazette of India.
