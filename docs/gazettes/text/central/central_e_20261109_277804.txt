MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2026 


S.O. 6103(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Immigration and 
Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.0.3987(E), dated the 17 September, 2025, namely:- 


In the said notification, the following entries shall be omitted: - 
(i) In the Table, in category I. AIRPORTS, the entry against Serial Number 36. 
(ii) In the Table, in category III. LAND PORTS, the entry against Serial Number 9. 


[F. No. 25022/61/2025-Imm] 
NITESH KUMAR VYAS, Addl. Secy. 


Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii), dated the 17 September, 2025 vide number S.O. 3987 (E), dated the 17 September, 2025 and amendment issued 
vide notification 8.0. 3478 (E) dated 29.06.2026 and S.O. 3778 (E) dated 10.07.2026.
