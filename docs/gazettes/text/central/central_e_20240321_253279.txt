एफ.सं. 4(4)-बी(डब्ल्यूएण्डएम)/2018.---भारत सरकार, एतदद्वारा दो सौ छह करोड़ रुपए केवल (206 करोड़ 
रु.) (अंकित) की कुल राशि के लिए दिनांक 29 सितंबर, 2004 की अधिसूचना एफ सं.4(16)डब्ल्यूएण्डएम/2004 के पैरा

विशेष प्रतिभूति, 2024, (जिसे इसमें इसके बाद संक्षेप में “विशेष प्रतिभूति” कहा गया है) के समय पूर्व मोचन को 
अधिसूचित करती है।

MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Economic Affairs) 
(BUDGET DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st March, 2024 


Subject:Premature redemption of a part of outstanding stock of Non-interest bearing Government of India 
IDBI Special Security, 2024. 


F. No. 4(4)-B(W&M)/2018.—Government of India, hereby notifies further premature redemption on 
Non-interest bearing Government of India IDBI Special Security, 2024 (hereinafter briefly described as “the Special 
Security’’) by exercising “call option” at par as stipulated in para 501) of its Notification F.No.4(16)-W&M/2004 dated 
September 29, 2004, for an aggregate amount of Rupees Two Hundred and Six crores only (2206.00 crore) [nominal]. 


2. Date of Premature Redemption 
The date of premature redemption of the Special Security by the Government of India shall be March 31, 2024. 
3. Outstanding Stock 


The outstanding stock of the Special Security shall be Rupees Six Hundred Seventy Two crores and 
Seventy Three lakhs only (672.73 crore) [nominal] after the exercise of call option. 


By order of the President of India,
