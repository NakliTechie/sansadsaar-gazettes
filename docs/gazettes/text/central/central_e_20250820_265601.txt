S.O. 3830(E).— Whereas M/s TP Saurya Ltd, the applicant with its registered office at C/o The Tata Power 
Company Limited, Corporate Centre, 34, Sant Tukaram Road, Carnac Bunder, Mumbai - 400009, Maharashtra, India 
has applied for authorization under Section 64 of the Electricity Act, 2003, for laying of overhead transmission line 
included in the transmission scheme, “Installation of dedicated overhead transmission line providing connectivity to 
M/s TP Saurya Ltd. for its 300 MW solar generation project in Koppal District, Karnataka". 

And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its File No. 25-!7/98/2024-PG dated 4.0.2024. 
had granted prior approval under section 68() of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of overhead transmission line 
included in the transmission scheme, “Installation of dedicated overhead transmission line providing connectivity to 
M/s TP Saurya Ltd. for its 300 MW solar generation project in Koppal District, Karnataka". 

M/s TP Saurya Ltd had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspapers, The New Indian Express 
(in English) dated 0..2024 & Rajasthan Patrika (in Hindi) dated ..2024, Vijay Karnataka (Kannada) dated 
0..2024, Vijayvani (Kannada) dated 0..2024 and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 4.2.2024 for the general 
public to make observations/ representations on the proposed transmission route within two months from the date of 
publication. Subsequently, M/s TP Saurya Ltd has submitted an affidavit dated 07.08.2025 declaring that no observation/ 
representation was received within two months from the date of Publication in the official Gazette of Government of 
India. 

And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 64 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, l885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so established 
or maintained for laying of overhead transmission line for M/s TP Saurya Ltd. The following overhead transmission 
line is covered under this scheme: 

l. M/s TP Saurya Ltd. Pooling station (Village: Kavaloora Talakal, Taluk: Koppal, District: Koppal, Karnataka) 

- Koppal PS CISTS S/s) 220 kV S/c line. 

(line length is approx. 4.29 km, out of which few km of the line from Koppal PS end would be implemented 

on M/c towers) 

The overhead transmission line covered under the above scheme will pass through, over, around and between the 
following villages, towns and cities of Karnataka. 


Villages Tehsil District 


Bannikoppa, Tadakal Koppal 


Now, after careful consideration, Ministry of Power, Government of India, under section l64 of the Electricity Act, 
2003, confers all the powers to M/s TP Saurya Ltd. for laying above overhead transmission line, which telegraph 
authority possesses under the Indian Telegraph Act, !885 with respect to placing of telegraph lines and posts for the 
purposes of a telegraph established or maintained, by Government or to be established or maintained subject to following 
terms and conditions for installing the above mentioned line, namely: 


i. The approval is granted for 25 years. 


ii. The Applicant shall have to seek the consent of the concerned authorities i.e., local bodies, Railways, National 
Highways, State Highways etc. before erection of proposed line. 
ili. The Applicant shall have to follow regulations/ codes of the Appropriate Commission regarding transmission, 
O&M, open access, etc., framed under the Electricity Act, 2003. 
iv. The Applicant shall operate the line after approval of Electrical Inspector / Chief Electrical Inspector of Central 
Government. 
Vv. The approval is subject to compliance of the requirement of the provisions of the Electricity Act, 2003 and the 


rules made there under by the applicant. 


Vi. M/s TP Saurya Ltd. shall have to submit the requisite clearances to Central Electricity Authority after obtaining 
the same from concermed authorities like Civil Aviation, Defense etc., at the time of Electrical Inspection. 


Vil. In case, the route of above overhead lines (or some portion of the route of above overhead line) falls in the 
Great Indian Bustard (GIB) area, the applicant has to comply with the orders of the Hon’ble Supreme Court in 
the petition No.838 of 209 regarding Great Indian Bustard (GIB) case, and the directions of the 
technical/expert committee constituted by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in this regard.
