NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the Ist October, 2025 
NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 20E of THE RAILWAY ACT 989 


5.0. 4539(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, In the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) number 3446 , dated the 30th July 2025, published in the Gazette of India (Extra-Ordinary) Part-II 
section 3 sub section (ii) , hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under subsection (l) of 20A of the railway 
act,l989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act),the central Government declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of Special Railway Project, namely 
“Bilaspur Division — Construction of ROB in lieu of LC No. CG-6 at SRBA Yard” in the state of Chhattisgarh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification Number 3446, dated 30th July, 2025, was published in 
the daily newspapers, Korba Balco Times (Hindi) Korba edition and Loksadan (Hindi) Korba edition on 2.08.2025 
under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objection was received within the set time period. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted his report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section |) of Section 20E of the said Act, 989, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
