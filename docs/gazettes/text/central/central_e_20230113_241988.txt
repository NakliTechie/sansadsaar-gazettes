MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 

( Department of Posts ) 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 13th January , 2023 

G.S.R. 21 ( E ) .– In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution , the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Department of Posts ( Postal Assistant & Sorting Assistant ) 
Recruitment Rules , 2022 , namely : 


( 1 ) These rules may be called the Department of Posts ( Postal Assistant and Sorting Assistant ) Recruitment 
( Amendment ) Rules , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Department of Posts ( Postal Assistant & Sorting Assistant ) Recruitment Rules , 2022 ( hereinafter to be 
called as the said rules ) , in the short title , for the brackets , words and symbol " ( Postal Assistant & Sorting Assistant ) " , 
the brackets and words " ( Postal Assistant and Sorting Assistant ) " shall be substituted . 


3. In the Schedule to the said rules , in the posts against serial number 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 and 5 , in the entries under 
column ( 11 ) , in Part ( B ) , — 


( i ) before Note 1 , the following proviso shall be inserted , namely : — 

“ Provided that for officials holding post in Level 1 , Level 2 and Level 3 of the Pay Matrix , who have 
rendered regular service as Gramin Dak Sevaks on or before the commencement of the Department of 
Posts ( Postal Assistant and Sorting Assistant ) Recruitment Rules , 2022 , the total regular service including 
the service rendered regularly as Gramin Dak Sevaks shall be five years . ” ;
