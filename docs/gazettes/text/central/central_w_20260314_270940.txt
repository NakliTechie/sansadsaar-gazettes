सं. 11034/01/2023-राजभाषा(नीति)--राजभाषा हिन्दी को बढ़ावा देने के उद्देश्य से हिन्दी में मौलिक पुस्तक लेखन को प्रोत्साहित 
करने हेतु राजभाषा विभाग द्वारा जारी संकल्प संख्या 11034/01/2023-राजभाषा(नीति) दिनांक 22.06.2023 का अधिक्रमण करते हुए वर्ष 
2025-26 से हिन्दी में मौलिक पुस्तक लेखन एवं हिन्दी में अनूदित पुस्तक हेतु संशोधित “राजभाषा गौरव पुरस्कार योजना” लागू की जाती है। 
इस योजना के अंतर्गत अब भारत के नागरिकों को निम्नलिखित श्रेणियों और विषयों पर पुरस्कार दिए जाएंगे:-- 


(क) इंजीनियरी, इलेक्ट्रॉनिक्स, कंप्यूटर विज्ञान, भौतिकी, जीव विज्ञान, ऊर्जा, अंतरिक्ष विज्ञान, आयुर्विज्ञान, रसायन विज्ञान, 
सूचना प्रौद्योगिकी, प्रबंधन, मनोविज्ञान तथा समसामयिक विषय जैसे उदारीकरण, भूमंडलीकरण, उपभोक्तावाद, 
मानवाधिकार, प्रदूषण, पर्यावरण विषयों पर हिन्दी में मौलिक पुस्तक लेखन हेतु राजभाषा गौरव पुरस्कार। 


(ख) विधि और पुलिस अनुसंधान, न्‍्यायालयिक विज्ञान, अपराधशास्त्र और पुलिस प्रशासन आदि विषयों पर हिन्दी में मौलिक 
पुस्तक लेखन हेतु राजभाषा गौरव पुरस्कार। 


(ग) संस्कृति, धर्म, कला, धरोहर आदि पर हिन्दी में मौलिक पुस्तक लेखन हेतु राजभाषा गौरव पुरस्कार। 


(घ) "गः' क्षेत्र के लेखक द्वारा हिन्दी में मौलिक पुस्तक लेखन हेतु राजभाषा गौरव पुरस्कार (उपर्युक्त सभी श्रेणियों के विषयों

RESOLUTION 


No.11034/01/2023-0.L.(Policy)—A revised award scheme named ‘Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar Yojna' from the year 
2025-26 is introduced by the Department of Official Language, with an objective to encourage writing books originally in 
Hindi and to promote Official Language, in supersession of earlier Resolution No. 11034/01/2023-O.L.(Policy) dated 
22.06.2023. Under the scheme, the citizens of India will be awarded in the following categories and subjects: — 


(A) Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar for writing original book in Hindi on Engineering, Electronics, Computer 
Science, Physics, Biology, Energy, Space Science, Medicine, Chemistry, Information Technology, 
Management, Psychology and contemporary topics like Liberalisation, Globalization, Consumerism, 
Human Rights, Pollution. 


(B) Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar for writing original books in Hindi on the subjects of Law and Police 
Research, Forensic Science, Criminology and Police Administration etc. 


(C) Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar for writing original book in Hindi on subjects related to Culture, Religion, 
Arts and Heritage etc. 


(D) Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar for writing original books in Hindi by the author of 'C' region (on the subjects 
of all the above categories). 


(E) Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar for books translated in Hindi — For translated books on classics and subjects 
related to culture, art, heritage. 


2. Name: The name of the scheme shall be “Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar Scheme.” 
3. Definitions: In this scheme, unless the context otherwise requires:— 


(1) "Scheme" means :- Books originally written in Hindi on the subjects mentioned in categories (a) to (०) 
above. In addition, "Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar Scheme" for nationally/internationally awarded books on 
subjects like culture, arts, heritage, classic work and translated books of renowned authors, scholars and 
personalities in Hindi. 


(ii) “Original” means:- A book originally written in Hindi and published for the first time. 


(iii) “Translated” means a book translated from Indian languages and English into Hindi or a translated book 
on classic literature. 


(iv) "Book" means "published books". 
(v) "Year" means (a)Scheme year - Financial year (b) Publication year - Calendar Year. 
(vi) A writer from Region C means:— 

(a) The mother tongue of the author should not be Hindi. 


(b) The writer must have completed his/her schooling from the respective Region ‘C’ state. (Region ‘C’ 
- All eight North-Eastern states and Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Goa, Jammu 
& Kashmir, Odisha, Lakshadweep, Pondicherry, and West Bengal.) 


(vii) The subject of translation means:— 


(a) Hindi translation of books and works of famous authors, scholars and personalities on subjects like 
culture, arts, heritage, awarded at national/international level. 


(b) In this category, translated work of books written in the form of departmental manuals, research 
papers written for Ph.D., poetry, novel, story, drama biography, sketch, reportage, travelogue, 
memoir etc. or written as a textbook will not be eligible. 


4. Objectives:— 


(a) Invarious ministries/departments/offices/undertakings/banks etc of the Central Government, official work 
is also undertaken on technical subjects. Due to lack of books on subjects like technical, science, judicial 
science, police, criminology research, police administration, culture, religion, arts, heritage etc and law 
there is a difficulty in increasing the use of Hindi in official work. In addition, due to being less familiar 
with or not familiar with the Hindi vocabulary of these subjects, personnel face difficulties in doing official 
work in Hindi. 


(b) A new category is being included to encourage original writing in Hindi by authors of ‘C’ region.
