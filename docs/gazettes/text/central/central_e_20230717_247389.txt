पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उप-धारा (1) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह समाधान 
हो जाने के पश्चात्‌ किबिहार राज्य मधेपुरा जिले में राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग संख्या-107 के कि.मी. 83.750 से 
कि.मी. 119.100 तक के भू-खण्ड के निर्माण (चौड़ीकरण/4-लेन का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के

अपनी आक्षेप प्रकट कर सकेगा ; 


ऐसी प्रत्येक आपत्ति सक्षम प्राधिकारी, अर्थात्‌ भू-अर्जन पदाधिकारी, मधेपुरा को लिखित रूप मे प्रस्तुत की 
जाएगी और उसमें उसके आधार अधिकथित किए जाएंगे और सक्षम प्राधिकारी, आक्षेपकर्ता को व्यक्तिगत रूप मे या किसी 
विधि व्यवसायी द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आक्षेपों की सुनवाई के पश्चात्‌ तथा ऐसी और जाँच करने के 
पश्चात्‌, यदि कोई हो, जिसे सक्षम प्राधिकारी आवश्यक समझे, आदेश द्वारा या तो आक्षेपों को अनुज्ञात कर सकेगा या 
अननुज्ञात कर सकेगा ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th July, 2023 


S.O. 3182(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 
(48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No-107 in the stretch of 
land from Km. 83.750 to Km. 119.100 (Mahesh Khunt- Sonbarsa Raj- Saharsa-Madhepura-Purnea Section) in the 
district of MADHEPURA in the state of BIHAR, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of section 3C of the 
said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, District Land Acquisition Officer, 
Madhepura, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
