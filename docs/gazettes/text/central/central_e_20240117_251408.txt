G.S.R. 45(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 76 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, further to amend the Electricity Rules, 2005, namely: 


l.  (4) These rules may be called the Electricity (Second Amendment) Rules, 2024.
