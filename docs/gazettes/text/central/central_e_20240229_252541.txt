पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 5.0 3124(5) तारीख 13/07/2023, जो भारत के 
राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा पश्चिम बंगाल राज्य के मालदा जिले में 
NH81 के कि.मी. 19.205 से कि.मी.46.55 तक के भू-खण्ड के निर्माण (चौड़ीकरण/पेव्ड शोल्डर सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का 
बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन 
करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख 06.10.2023 को 
"द टाइम्स ऑफ़ इण्डिया" और "वर्तमान" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ; 


जबकि सक्षम प्राधिकारी को धारा 3-ग के तहत दायर आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई है, जिस पर विचार किया गया है और 
उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th February, 2024 


S.O. 949(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O 3124(E) Dated:13/07/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for "building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH81 in the stretch of land from Km. 19.205 to Km. 46.55 in the district of Malda in 
the state of WEST BENGAL’. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” and “ 
Bartaman” both dated 06.10.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
