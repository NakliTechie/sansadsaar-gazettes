MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th July, 2023 


9.0. 3263(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of the India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas vide 8.0. No. 8i5 (E) Dated-20 April-2023 published in the Gazette of the India Gazette २०. ।733 dated- 
2 April-2023 under sub-section (l) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of 
User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that notification for the purpose 
of laying “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project” for the transportation of LPG in Tehsil-Soraon, District-Prayagraj 
in the state of Uttar Pradesh by IHB Limited. 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public up to 27 April-2023 and 
whereas the Shri Rakesh Kumar Competent Authority has under sub-section (.) of section 6 of the said Act submitted 
report to the Central Government; 


And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said land 
is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this notification is 
hereby acquired for laying the pipeline. 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration in IHB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


IHB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section !0 of the P&MP Act, 962 
and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would be against the Central Government on any matter relating to the pipeline.
