INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-5/3503-INC: — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIUI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order: — 


Whereas, Government of Karnataka and Karnataka State nursing Council had granted recognition to Dr. Ajay 
School of Nursing at Opp. Veerender Patel Layout, University Road, Gulbarga, Gulbarga, Distt. Gulbarga 58504 
Karnataka (“College”) to conduct GNM course. 


Whereas, in terms of Section |3 (.) of the Indian Nursing Council Act ("Act"), the Indian Nursing Council 
("Council") has the power to inspect any Institution recognized as a training institution by State authorities. However, 
when the inspectors appointed by the Council reached the address provided by the College on .0.2023, it was found 
that no college is existing on the said address. 


Whereas, letter no. l8-5/03503-INC dated 2.04.2023 was issued to the College informing them about the 
closure of their application for suitability. Further, a notice no. 8-5/03503-INC (4798) dated 26.2.2023 was issued 
to the Principal Secretary, Health and family welfare department (state government of Karnataka) asking them for their 
remarks/ comments on the issue of no college existing on the address provided. However, no comments were received 
by the Council. 


Whereas, the Executive Committee of the Council had considered the aforesaid matter in its entirety. After 
conducting physical inspections, it was found that no college in the name of Dr. Ajay School of nursing existed on the 
address provided and that obtaining recognition from the state authorities by providing incorrect information amounts 
to a serious deficiency. 


Hence, therefore, the Council in exercise of its power conferred under Section 4 (3)-(b) of the Act declares 
that with effect from 2.05.2024, any person obtaining a qualification of GNM from Dr. Ajay school of Nursing 
allegedly situated at Opp. Veerender Patel Layout, University Road, Gulbarga, Gulbarga, Distt. Gulbarga 58504 
Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
