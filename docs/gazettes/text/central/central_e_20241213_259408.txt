New Delhi, the 2th December, 2024 


S.O. 5382(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) 
Rules, 96l, namely: - 


l. (.) These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and 
Seventy-eighth Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force at once. 


a In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 96!, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE, under the 
heading “MINISTRY OF WOMEN AND CHILD DEVELOPMENT (MAHILA AUR BAL VIKAS 
MANTRALAYA)” — 


(a) Entry [3 relating to “Central Social Welfare Board (CSWB)” shall be omitted; 
(b) Entry 5 relating to “Food and Nutrition Board” shall be omitted; 
(c) Entry 9 relating to “Rashtriya Mahila Kosh” shall be omitted. 


Droupadi Murmu 
President
