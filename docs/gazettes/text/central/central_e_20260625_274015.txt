MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th June, 2026 


S.O. 3405(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 4299(E). Dated:23/09/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of (widening 2 lane/ 4 Lane with paved shoulder etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH160-A in the stretch of land from Km. 156/000 to Km. 217/210 in the 
district of PALGHAR in the state of MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Free Press Journal” dated 
22/11/2025 , “ Navshakti ” dated 22/11/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
