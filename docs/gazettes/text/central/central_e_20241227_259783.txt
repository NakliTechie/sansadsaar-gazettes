अधीन जारी, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण भाग (Il), खंड 3, उप-खंड (ii), तारीख 03 सितम्बर 2024 में प्रकाशित भारत 
सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (रेलवे बोर्ड) की अधिसूचना संख्यांक का.आ. 3749 (अ) तारीख 02 सितम्बर 2024 (जिसे इसमें 
इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है ), द्वारा उक्त अधिसूचना से उपबद्ध अनुसूची मे विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को, महाराष्ट्र 
राज्य के वर्धा जिले में विशेष रेल परियोजना अर्थात वर्धा (सेवाग्राम) - नागपुर चौथी रेलवे लाइन के निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, 
प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की थी | 

और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20(क) की उप धारा (4) के अधीन दैनिक समाचार पत्रों 
अर्थात “दैनिक विधाता” और “दैनिक प्रतापगढ़चे वारे” में तारीख 18 सितम्बर 2024 को प्रकाशित किया गया था। 


और कोई आक्षेप प्राप्त नहीं हुए थे और उन पर विचार किया गया है तथा सक्षम अधिकारी द्वारा तदनुसार आदेश 
पारित कर दिये गए हैं।

Mumbai, the 23rd December, 2024 

S.O. 5617(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 3749 (E) dated the 02 September 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 3 September 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sun-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
Wardha (Sewagram)-Nagpur 4th line in the district of Wardha in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Daink 
Vidhata” And “Dainik Pratapgadche Vare” Dated 18 September 2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority 
accordingly orders have been passed: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government, 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further in pursuance the sub-section (2) of aecction 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in Central Government free from all encumbrances.
