MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2]st June, 2024 
S.O. 246(E).— In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of 
Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962) and in modification of Notification of the Government of India, 
Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas 8.0. No. 560(E) dated 06-02-2024, the Central Government hereby authorizes 
Additional District Magistrate (Judicial) District- Gautambudh Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, in place of Sub Divisional 
Officer (Judicial) Jewar, District-Gautambudh Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, to perform the functions of Competent Authority 
for laying of Piyala (Faridabad, Haryana) to Jewar Airport (Gautambudh Nagar U.P.) ATF Pipeline by Bharat 


Petroleum Corporation Limited under the said Act, within the territory of Uttar Pradesh State. 


This notification will be effective from the date of Issue.
