MINISTRY OF CHEMICALS AND FERTILIZERS 
(Department Of Chemicals And Petrochemicals) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 25th June, 2026 


5.0. 3456(E).— Whereas, the Central Government has published the Linear Alkyl Benzene (Quality Control) 
Order, 2022 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide S.O. 648 (E), dated 5" 
April, 2022 (hereinafter to referred as the said Order) in the public interest under and the powers conferred by section 
6 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 206 ([] of 206); 

And whereas, in view of the exigencies prevailing globally, the Central Government, in consultation with the 
Bureau, has taken cognizance of the supply chain disruption and is satisfied that to ensure availability of the Linear 
Alkyl Benzene, it is necessary and expedient to suspend the operation of the said Order temporarily; 

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section |6 read with sub-section (3) of section 25 of the 
Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 206 ([] of 206), and being of the opinion that it is necessary so to do in the public 
interest, the Central Government, after consultation with the Bureau of Indian Standards, hereby makes the following 
amendment to the said Order, namely :- 

In the Linear Alkyl Benzene (Quality Control) Order, 2022, in paragraph 2, in the proviso, for the 0.07.2026 
to the 30.09.2026 (First July Two Thousand Twenty-Six to Thirty September Two Thousand Twenty-Six only) shall be 
substituted.
