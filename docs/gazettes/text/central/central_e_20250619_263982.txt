2. समिति के कार्य 


इस समिति का कार्य संविधान में राजभाषा संबंधी प्रावधानों, अधिनियमों व नियमों के उपबंधों, केंद्रीय हिंदी समिति 
के निर्णयों और राजभाषा विभाग, गृह मंत्रालय द्वारा सरकारी कामकाज में हिंदी के प्रयोग के संबंध में निर्धारित नीतियों के 
अनुसार जारी निदेशों, अनुदेशों के कार्यान्वयन और कोयला मंत्रालय तथा इसके अधीनस्थ कार्यालयों/स्वायत्तशासी 


निकायों/कंपनियों में हिंदी के प्रगामी प्रयोग को बढ़ाने के बारे में सलाह देना होगा।

The function of this Samiti will be to advise on the implementation of the Official Language provisions in the 
Constitution, provisions of Acts and Rules, decisions of the Kendriya Hindi Samiti and the directives and instructions 
issued by the Department of Official Language, Ministry of Home Affairs as per the policies laid down regarding the 
use of Hindi in official work and to promote the progressive use of Hindi in the Ministry of Coal and its subordinate 
offices/autonomous bodies/companies. 


3. Tenure 
(i) Term of the Samiti will be ordinarily for three years from the date of its constitution, provided. 


(ii) A member of Parliament nominated to the Samiti will cease to be the member of the Samiti as soon as he ceases to 
be member of Parliament ; 


(iii) Ex-officio Members of the Samiti shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue of which, they 
are members of this Samiti. 


(iv) Ifa vacancy arises in the Samiti due to death or resignation from the membership of the Samiti by any member, the 
member so appointed against that vacancy, will hold office for the residual term of three years; and 


(v) In special circumstances, the tenure of the Samiti may be curtailed or extended by Ministry of Coal. 


4 GENERAL 


The Headquarters of the Samiti shall be at New Delhi, but if considered necessary it may hold its meetings at 
any other place also. 


5 T.A. AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


(a) The Members of Parliament nominated in the Samiti, will be paid Travelling Allowance/Daily Allowance as per 
the provisions in the “Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowance & Pension) Act, 1954” and amendments issued 
from time to time and rules made thereunder vide Department of Official Language Office Memorandum 
No. H/20034/04/2005-O.L (Policy-2), dated 03 February, 2006 for participating in meetings of the Samiti. 


(b) Travelling Allowance/Daily Allowance to other non-official members of the Samiti, will be paid as per the 
guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language O.M. No.II/20034/04/86-OL(A-2) dated 22.1.1987 
keeping in view the high level status of the Committee and in accordance with the prescribed rates and rules as 
amended from time to time by the Government of India.
