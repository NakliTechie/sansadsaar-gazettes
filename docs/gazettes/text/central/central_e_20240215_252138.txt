In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons)

regulation shall be inserted, namely: - 


“4D. Operating separate bank account for each real estate project. 


Where the corporate debtor has any real estate project, the interim resolution professional or the resolution 
professional, as the case may be, shall operate a separate bank account for each real estate project.”
