MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2th March, 2024 


S.O. 297(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act,l956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for building (Six-Laning, widening etc) maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No-544 in the 
stretch of the land from Km.236.l35 to Km.264.490 (Vadakkenchery-Thrissur Section) of NH544 in the district 
of Thrissur in the state of Kerala, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, "The Special Deputy 
Collector, LANH(NH-66) Kodungallur '"'in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either, allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 

The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
