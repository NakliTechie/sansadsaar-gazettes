MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l0th August, 2026 


G.S.R. 729(E).— The following draft rules to further amend the All India Tourist Vehicles 
(Permit), Rules, 2023 which the Central Government proposes to make, in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (4) read with sub-section (9) of section 88 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 
988 (59 of 988), is hereby published as required by sub-section (l) of Section 22 of the said Act 
for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby given that the said 
draft rules shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of a period of thirty days from the date 
on which the copies of this notification as published in the Gazette of India, are made available to 
the public. 

Objections or suggestions, if any, may be sent to the Additional Secretary (MVL), Ministry 
of Road Transport and Highways, Transport Bhawan, Parliament Street, New Delhi-!000I, Email: 
comments-morth@gov.in. Any objection(s) or suggestion(s) which may be received from any 
person with respect to the said draft rules before the expiry of the period aforesaid will be considered 
by the Central Government. 


DRAFT RULES 


. Short title and commencement.- (|) These rules may be called the All India Tourist Vehicles 
(Permit) (Second Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force with effect from the date of publication of the Notification in the 
Official Gazette. 


2. In the All India Tourist Vehicles (Permit) Rules, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), 
in rule 3,— 


(i) in the table under sub-rule (3),— 


(a) in column (3) of serial number |, for the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “20,000”, 
the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “l5,000” shall be substituted; 


(b) in column (3) of serial number 2, for the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “30,000” 
the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “22,500” shall be substituted; 


(c) in column (3) of serial number 3, for the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “80,000”, 
the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “60,000” shall be substituted; 


(d) in column (3) of serial number 4, for the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark 
*3,00,000”, the Arabic numerals and punctuation mark “2,25,000” shall be substituted; 


(i) in sub-rule (4), for the words “driven on methanol or ethanol fuel”, the words “exclusively driven 
on hydrogen, methanol, ethanol fuel or any natural gas” shall be substituted. 


3. In the said rules, in rule 4, for the proviso to sub-rule (5), the following provisos shall be 
substituted, namely,—
