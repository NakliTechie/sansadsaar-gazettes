THE NEW DELHI INTERNATIONAL ARBITRATION CENTRE 
( AMENDMENT ) ACT , 2022 

No. 23 OF 2022 


[ 30th December , 2022. ] 

An Act to amend the New Delhi International Arbitration Centre Act , 2019 .

follows : 


1. ( 1 ) This Act may be called the New Delhi International Arbitration Centre Short title and 
( Amendment ) Act , 2022 .
