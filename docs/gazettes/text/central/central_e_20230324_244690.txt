NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 24th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 223 ( E ) —In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section 1 of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act , 2021 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Ports , Shipping 
& Waterways ( PHRD Division ) No.G.S.R.187 ( E ) dated 14th March , 2023 , the Central Government hereby appoints 
Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary / Secretary holding the charge of Transport Department , Government 
of West Bengal , in place of Shri Binod Kumar , IAS , Former Principal Secretary , Transport Department , Government 
of West Bengal , as a representative of State Government on the Board of Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Authority 
under Section 3 ( 1 ) ( c ) ( i ) of Major Port Authorities Act , 2021 with immediate effect and until further orders .
