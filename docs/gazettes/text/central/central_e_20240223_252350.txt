पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 4859(अ) तारीख 8 नवंबर 2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग 1, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गथी, द्वारा तेलंगाना राज्य के कामारेड्डी जिले में (चौड़ीकरण/पेग्ड 
शोल्डर सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का बनाना आदि, के कि.मी. 129.00 से कि.मी. 164.620 आरडीओ-बांसवाडा 3क के तहत, 
(टीए एंड एफएस काम के येल्लारेड्डी-रुद्ररखंड के 106.830 से किमी. 158.496 (मौजूदा चेनेज किमी. 123.500 से

New Delhi, the 23rd February, 2024 


S.O. 831(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.0.4859(E) Dated on 8th November 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and 
operation (widening to two lane with paved shoulder four laning etc., from Km. 129.00 to Km. 164.620 as per 
3a Jurisdiction of RDO-Banswada) {as per the TA&FS the work from Km. 106.830 to Km.158.496 of 
Yellareddy-Rudrur section of Telangana (Existing Chainage from Km 123.500 to Km.176.800 of Medak- 
Yellareddy-Rudrur section of NH-765D Package-ID}. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Velugu (Telugu)” and “The Hindu 
(English)”’ both dated 24 December 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
