New Delhi, the 26th April, 2024 


S.O. 839(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 698(E) dated 4.02.2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part वी, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH-48B (Design Km 0.000 to Km 40.949) in the stretch of land from junction of NH 709E (Rohtak-Bhiwani 
road) near village Ninan-Palwas to village Barasi in Bhiwani district (upto design Km 38.346) in the district of 
BHIWANTI in the state of HARYANA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Times of india" and “Punjab 
Kesari” both dated 29.02.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
