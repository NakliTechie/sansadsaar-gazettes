MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 8th July, 2025 


S.O. 3044(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause 37A of Section 2 of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act. 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of Bridge No. 100 (13 x 30.50m Span Open Web 
Girder) and Bridge (RUB/LHS) No. 99 (1 x 12.20M PSC Slab) including misc. works like pitching, toe wall, floor 
protection, Earthwork in formation, blanketing, trolley refuges, laying and linking of Broad Gauge line, tamping of 
track, destressing of LWRs, transportation of P. way material, supply and Stacking of stone ballast and other misc. 
works required for successful completion of work in connection with construction of new Major Bridge No. 100 on 
diverted alignment on Patan-Bhildi BG section of Ahmedabad Division on Western as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Gujarat, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from 
the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
