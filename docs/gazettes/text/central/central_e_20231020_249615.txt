MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th October, 2023 


9.0. 4602(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 


and Highways, 957 Dated:0/03/208 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH353J in the stretch of land from Km. 35.08 to Km. 62.9 (Katol) in the district of NAGPUR in the state of 
MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Navbharat” and “Lokmat” both 
dated 03/04/208; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
