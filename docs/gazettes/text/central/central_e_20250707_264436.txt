MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th July, 2025 


G.S.R. 454(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section l98 of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage 
of Passengers) Rules, 2000, namely:- 


l. () These rules may be called the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage of Passengers) Amendment 
Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In Chapter VIII of the said rules, in rule 36, in sub-rule (9), for ‘Method C’, the following shall be 
substituted, namely:- 


“Method C: When a trap is used, a train shall not be admitted on to the trapped line unless the trap has 
been closed, so that the train shall not be derailed, if the Loco Pilot overshoots such trap, except as provided 


under clause (3) or clause (4) of rule 3.40 of general rules, [976 or para 7..8 (e)Gii) of Indian Railways 
Signal Engineering Manual.”

NAVEEN GULATI, Member Infrastructure and Ex-Officio Secy.
