New Delhi, the | lth July, 2023 


S.O. 3070(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building(widening/2-Laning with paved shoulder), maintenance, management and operation of the National Highway 
no. 552 Ext. in the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 2+800 km (Mihona Lahar Daboh Bhander Bypass Road) 
(Mihona-Bypass) (Village Banthari, Mihona) 3(A) Additional, in the District- Bhind in the state of MADHYA 
PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Sub-Divisional officer 
(Revenue) and Land Acquisition officer Lahar, District Bhind, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and 
the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal 
practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, eitherallow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (l) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
