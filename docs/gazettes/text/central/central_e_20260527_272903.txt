MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 25th May, 2026 


5.0. 2671(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S O No 360(E), Dated: 22.01.2026 published in Gazette of India vide no 332 Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 23.01.2026 and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely,”Construction of Ghatkesar TSS in Secunderabad- 
Kazipet section for Up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from 1x25KV to 2x25 KV traction system to meet 
3000MT loading target” in Yamnampet &Ghatkesar Village of Ghatkesar Mandal in Medchal- Malakjgiri District in 
the state of Telangana. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ABN Andhra Jyothi” &’”Nameste 
Telangana” in Telugu Newspaper, “The Hindu” &”The New Indian Express” in English Newspapers,” Hindi Milap” in 
Hindi Newspaper and “Siasat” in Urdu Newspaper on 29.01.2026 with the approval of Mothilal Bhookya, Chief 
Engineer, Construction-II, South Central Railway, Secunderabad. 


And whereas Competent Authority for LA Keesara received objections U/s 20D and after completion of 
enquiry disallowed the objections under Sub section (2) of Section 20D of Railways Act 1989 and accordingly orders 
have been passed And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
