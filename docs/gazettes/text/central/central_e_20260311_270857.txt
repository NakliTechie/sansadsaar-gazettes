NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1 1th March, 2026 


S.O. 1283(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways S.0.3722(E).Dated 13/08/2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, Sub- 
Section (1) Issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with pave shoulder/four laning etc.) Maintenance, 
management and operation of Talere-Gaganbawada-Kolhapur Road National Highway 166-G on the stretch of 
land from Ch. 0/00 to 06/00 to Ch. 13/000 to 19/440 in the district of Sindhudurg in the State of Maharashtra.

in "Dainik Pudhari” And “Dainik Prahar” both dated 01/09/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately: 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
