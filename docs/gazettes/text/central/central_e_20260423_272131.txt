New Delhi, the 22nd April, 2026 
S.O. 2065(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by Sub Section (l) of section 3 (A) of the National Highways

published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II , Section3, Sub Section (ii), dated 08" April 2026, and

Panipat to Gorakhpur” shall be substituted.

substituted.
