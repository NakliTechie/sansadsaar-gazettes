NOTIFICATION 
Prayagraj, the 20th February, 2024 
S.O. 936(E).—Whereas,by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Cetral Railway, Construction Department) Notification No. 8.0. 4249, dated 04 October, 2023 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraodinary, Part-II, section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 11 October, 2023 and issued of sub-section (1) of section, 
20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to 


the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of specical Railway project namely, 
“Etawah Byepass” in the District of Etawah in the state of Uttar Pradesh; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers “Hindustan Times” 
dated 19-10-2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section (1) of secton 20E of the said Act, the competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government.

conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the Railways Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby declares that 


the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 


declares that on publiscation of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed
