ORDER 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2025 
S.O. 2902(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3 of the Jute 
Packaging Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 987 (0 of 987), the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry 


of Textiles, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (ii) vide number 
S.O. 830(E), dated the 2274 April, 2025, namely:- 


In the said Order, in the Preamble, in paragraph 4, for the words, figures and letters “up to the 
30" June, 2025”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 30" September, 2025 or until further orders, 
whichever is earlier” shall be substituted.
