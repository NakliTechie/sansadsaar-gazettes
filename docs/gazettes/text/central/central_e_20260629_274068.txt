MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 29th June, 2026 


5.0. 3482(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 955 
(0 of 955), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Fertiliser (Inorganic, 
Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order, 985, namely: — 


हि Short title and commencement. — (/) This Order may be called the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) 
(Control) Seventh Amendment Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2, In the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order, 985 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Order), in clause 2, — 


(a) in sub-clause (h), after the words “Compressed Bio Gas plants”, the words “and Bio Gas plants” shall 
be inserted; 


(b) in sub-clause (००9), after the words “Compressed Bio Gas plants”, the words “and Bio Gas plants” 
shall be inserted. 


3. In the said Order, in clause 8, in sub-clause (3), for the Seventh proviso, the following proviso shall be 
substituted, namely: — 


“Provided also that the manufacturer of Fermented Organic Manure and Liquid Fermented Organic 
Manure, shall not be required to obtain the authorisation letter under sub-clause (3) of clause 8 of this Order for a 
period of ten years from the date of publication of the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Seventh 
Amendment Order, 2026.”.
