विनियामक बोर्ड अधिनियम, 2006 (2006 का अधिनियम 19) की धारा 61 द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, 
पेट्रोलियम और प्राकृतिक गैस विनियामक बोर्ड इसके द्वारा पेट्रोलियम और प्राकृतिक गैस नियामक बोर्ड (परामर्शदाताओं 
की नियुक्ति) विनियम, 2007 में संशोधन करने के लिए निम्नलिखित विनियम बनाता है, अर्थात्‌:-- 


1. संक्षिप्त शीर्षक एवं प्रारंभ:-- 


(1) इन विनियमों को पेट्रोलियम और प्राकृतिक गैस विनियामक बोर्ड (परामर्शदाताओं की नियुक्ति) संशोधन 
विनियम, 2023 कहा जाएगा। 


(2) ये नियम सरकारी राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तारीख से लागू होंगे। 
2. पेट्रोलियम और प्राकृतिक गैस विनियामक बोर्ड (परामर्शदाताओं की नियुक्ति) विनियम, 2007 में: 
(i) विनियम 17 के उप-विनियम (1) के बाद, निम्नलिखित उप-विनियम शामिल किया जाएगा; 
"(1क) व्यक्तिगत परामर्शदाता की नियुक्ति के प्रयोजन के लिए संबंधित सदस्य की सिफारिश पर अध्यक्ष द्वारा

PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS REGULATORY BOARD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, 1°‘ December, 2023 


F. No. PNGRB/Admin/12-HR (05)/2021 (E-3651).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 61 of 
the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006 (Act 19 of 2006), the Petroleum and Natural Gas 
Regulatory Board hereby makes the following regulations, further to amend the Petroleum and Natural Gas 
Regulatory Board (Appointment of Consultants) Regulations, 2007, namely: — 


1. Short title and commencement: — 


(i) These regulations may be called the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Appointment of 
Consultants) Amendment Regulations, 2023. 


(ii) These regulations shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 
2: In the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Appointment of Consultants) Regulations, 2007: 
(i) After sub-regulation (1) of Regulations 17, the following sub-regulation shall be inserted; 


“(1A) For the purpose of the engagement of Individual Consultant, the process shall be initiated by the 
Chairperson on recommendation of the concerned Member”. 


(ii) In clause (b) of sub-regulation (3) of Regulations 17; 
(c) the word ‘five’, shall be substituted by the word ‘six’; 


(9) the words beginning with “Provided that....” and ending with “....... Level V for them”, shall be 
omitted. 


(1) In sub-clause (ii) of proviso to clause (c) sub-regulation (6) of Regulations 17, the year, “2021” shall be 
substituted by the year “2023”. 


(iv) After the proviso to clause (f) of sub-regulation (7) of Regulation 17, the following proviso shall be 
inserted; 


“Provided further that the Board may at its discretion relax the age of applicant for the position of 
Individual Consultant and age for retaining the Individual Consultant” 


(v) In Schedule II; 
(a) For the word “Category”, shall be substituted by the word “Level”; 
(b) For The word ‘Level’ under the head “Category of Individual Consultant’, shall be omitted; 
(c) For the head ‘Preferred experience in years’: 
(i) the words ‘Upto five’, shall be substituted by the numbers “0-3”; 
(ii) the words “More than five to ten”, shall be substituted by the words “Minimum 03”; 
(1) the words “More than ten to fifteen”, shall be substituted by the words “Minimum 06”; 


(iv) the words “More than fifteen to twenty”, shall be substituted by the words “Minimum 
10”;

(d) At the end, the following shall be inserted:
