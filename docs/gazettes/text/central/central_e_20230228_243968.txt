MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th February, 2023 


G.S.R. 40(E).—Whereas the draft rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, 97, was published, as 
required by section 36 of the Insecticides Act, 968 (46 of 968) vide notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) number G.S.R. 
790(E), dated the 25" October, 2022, in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section-3, Sub-section 
(i), inviting objections or suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry of a period of 
thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Gazette of India in which the said notification was published were 
made available to the public; 


And whereas, copies of the said notification were made available to the public on 25" October 2022; 


And whereas, objections or suggestions received from the public in respect of the said draft rules have been 
considered by the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36 of the Insecticides Act, 968 (46 of 968), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, !97, namely :- 


l. (.) These rules may be called the Insecticides (First Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insecticides Rules, 97], in rule 9, for sub-rule (4B), the following shall be substituted namely:— 


[भाग त---खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


“(4B) A person who applies for manufacturing license shall possess or shall employ a person possessing the 
following educational qualifications, namely:- 


(i) Doctorate in Chemistry or Agriculture Chemistry or Agriculture Chemicals or Master of Technology or Master 
of Science in Chemical Engineering or equivalent for manufacture of technical grade insecticides. 


(i) Graduate degree in Agriculture or Science with Chemistry as one of the subject for manufacture of insecticides 
formulations; 


Provided that a person possessing a manufacturing license on the date of commencement of the Insecticides (First 
Amendment) Rules, 2023 shall be allowed a period of four years to comply with the prescribed educational 
qualifications or a period of one year to employ a person possessing the said qualification, whichever is earlier.”
