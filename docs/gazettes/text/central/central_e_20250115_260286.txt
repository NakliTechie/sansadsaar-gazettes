Subject: Addition of new laboratory in Para 4.73 of the Handbook of Procedures, 2023. 

F.No. 0/94/80/258/AM2/PC-4.—In exercise of powers conferred under Paragraph .03 and 
2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign 
Trade hereby makes the following amendment in the Para 4.73 of the Handbook of Procedures 2023: 
A new entry at SI no. 9 in the Para 4.73 of HBoP-2023 is added as under: 
(9) GIA Laboratory, DMCC, Dubai, UAE. 
Effect of the Public Notice: GIA Laboratory, DMCC, Dubai, UAE has been included in the list of 


authorized laboratories under Para 4.73 of the HBoP-2023 for certification / grading of diamonds of 0.25 
carat and above.
