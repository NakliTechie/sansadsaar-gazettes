अपेक्षित है,ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की थी।

किया गया था। और सक्षम प्राधिकारी द्वारा कोई आक्षेप प्राप्त नहीं किया गया। धारा 20ड की उपधारा(1)के अनुसरण में 
सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने अपनी रिपोर्ट केंद्रीय सरकार को प्रस्तुत कर दी है।

NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai , the 28th January 2025 


S.O. 513(E).— Where as by the Notification of the Government of India, in the 
Ministry of Railways (Railway Board), number S.O. 3941(E), dated 13/09/2024 published in Gazette 
of India Extraordinary. Part II, section-3, Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 
(240f1989)(here in after referred to as the said Act),the Central Government, has declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of land to be acquired with or without structure for the 
Special Railway Project, New BG Line project From Dahod - Indore Via, Jhabua, Sardrapur, dhar 
and Pithampur (204.76km) in District Dhar, Tehsil Gandhwani Village Golpura Pada - 
Sultanpur in the state of Madhya Pradesh. And whereas, the substance of the said notification was 
published in daily “Free press Journal” dated 21/09/2024 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 
21/09/2024 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act. And, whereas, nil objection has 
been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise 
of the power conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that the lands specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the 
afore said purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20 (E) of the said Act, the Central 
Government here by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from 
all encumbrances.
