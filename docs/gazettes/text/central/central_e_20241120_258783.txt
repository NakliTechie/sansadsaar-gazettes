G.S.R. 78(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (९) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of

l. Short title and commencement.- (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on 
the occasion of 75 Anniversary of Constitution) Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

under the authority of the Central Government to commemorate on the occasion of 75" Anniversary of 
Constitution.
