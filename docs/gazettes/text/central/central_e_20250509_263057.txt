NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th May, 2025 


S.O. 2103(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 5418(E) Dated: 13/12/2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Subsection (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening /Two lane with paved shoulder/Four laning etc.,) maintenance, 
management and operation of Improvement to 2-lane with paved shoulder of NH-40 (New NH-206) in the stretch of 
land from existing Km. 123.8 to Km. 131.82 (Design Km. 37+550 to Km. 45+760) Design Length 7.76 Km. (Pynursla 
Bypass) in the district of East Khasi Hills in the state of MEGHALAYA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Mawphor (Khasi)” and “The 
Shillong Times (English)” both dated 07th January 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
