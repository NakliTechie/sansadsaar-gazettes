NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 15th April, 2026 


S.O. 1908(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board ) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No. 752(A) dated 13.08.2025 Extra Ordinary- 
part II- Section-3 -Sub-section (ii)) (here in after referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20A 
of the Railway Act 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its 
intention to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 


management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, 
Dabhoi — Samlaya Gauge Conversion Project in District Vadodara in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “the indian 
express” and “ divya bhaskar ” dated 26 August 2025 under subsection (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, objection has been received & resolved by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority Dabhoi - 


Samlaya has submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 


schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 


shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
