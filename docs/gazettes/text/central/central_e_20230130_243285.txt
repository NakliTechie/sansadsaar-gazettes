MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30" January, 2023 


G.S.R. 61(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sections 6, 8 and 25 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 
1986, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the E-Waste (Management) 
Rules, 2022, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called E-Waste (Management) Amendment 
Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force from the 1“ day of April, 2023. 
2. In the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022, - 


(i) in rule 16, in sub-rule (5), for the words *‘in the product user documentation’’, the words “‘as and 
when required by the Central Pollution Control Board’’ shall be substituted; 


(1) in Schedule - II, after $1. No. 35 and entries relating thereto, the following Sl. Nos. and entries shall 
be inserted, namely: - 


7736, Cadmium and lead in Solar panels/cells, solar Photovoltaic panels/cells/modules as listed in 
Schedule - I 


37. Lead in Medical Devices (with the exception of all implanted and infected products) as listed in 
Schedule - [.”’.
