MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 28th March, 2024 


S.O. 586(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.00. l88(E), dated the lith January, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the llth January, 2023 and issued under sub-section (.) of section 20A of 
the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Kadi — Kalol - Katosan Road Gauge 
Conversion Project (Western Railway) in the District of Ahmedabad and Mahesana in the State of Gujarat; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the 
“Sandesh” (Gujarati),“Times Of India’(English), & “Young Leader ” (Hindi) dated 23rd December , 2023 under 
sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, nil objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been passed; 


[भाग ॥---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
