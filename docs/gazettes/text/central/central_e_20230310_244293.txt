MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]0th March, 2023 


S.O. 46(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section of 3A of the National 
Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building (widening/four laning etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. llA (New 
NH-48), in the stretch of land of from km 58.320 to Km 62.500 (Dausa-Manoharpur Section) in the district of Jaipur 
in the State of Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every, such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the Sub Divisional Officer, 
Shahpura, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act Shall is final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
