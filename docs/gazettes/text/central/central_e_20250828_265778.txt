NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2025 


G.S.R. 589(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 14 of the Administrative 
Tribunals Act, 1985 (13 of 1985), the Central Government hereby specifies the 28 August, 2025, as the date on and 
from which the provisions of sub-section (3) of section 14 of the said Act shall apply to the National Green Tribunal, a 
statutory body under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, and for the said purpose, makes the 
following further amendment in the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Personnel, Public 
Grievances and Pensions (Department of Personnel and Training) published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R. 730(E), dated the 2nd May, 1986, namely:— 


In the Schedule to the said notification, after serial number 229 and the entries relating thereto, the following 
serial number and entries shall be inserted, namely:- 


1. 2. 3. 


“230. National Green Tribunal Statutory body under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate 
Change”.
