ORDER 

New Delhi , the 18th November , 2022 

S.O. 5396 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Bureau of Indian 
Standards Act , 2016 ( 11 of 2016 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the 
Methylene Chloride ( Dichloromethane ) ( Quality Control ) Order , 2021 published by the Ministry of 
Chemicals and Fertilizers , Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals , namely : 


In the said order , in paragraph 1 , for sub - paragraph ( 2 ) , the following sub - paragraph shall be 
substituted , namely : 

" ( 2 ) This order shall come into force on the 20th May , 2023. "
