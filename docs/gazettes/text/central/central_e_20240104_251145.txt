MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist January, 2024 


G.S.R. 14(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Regional 
Controller of Mines, Deputy Controller of Mines, Senior Assistant Controller of Mines, Assistant Controller of Mines 
and Assistant Mining Engineer (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2019, namely:-— 


1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, 
Regional Controller of Mines, Deputy Controller of Mines, Senior Assistant Controller of Mines, Assistant Controller 
of Mines and Assistant Mining Engineer (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2 In the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Regional Controller of Mines, Deputy Controller of 
Mines, Senior Assistant Controller of Mines, Assistant Controller of Mines and Assistant Mining Engineer (Group ‘A’ 
and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2019, in the Schedule, against serial number 4 relating to the post of Assistant 
Controller of Mines, - 


(a) in column (9), for the entries, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 
“Two years. 


Note: Direct recruits shall be required to successfully complete a mandatory induction training of at least two 
weeks duration, as prescribed by the competent authority, for completion of probation.”; 


(b) in column (11), - 


(i) | under the sub-heading “Promotion”, for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be 
substituted; 


(ii) after sub-heading “Promotion” and the entries relating thereto and before Note, the following note shall 
be inserted, namely: - 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Note : 


“Note 1: The eligibility service shall continue to be three years in respect of incumbents holding the 
feeder post of Assistant Mining Engineer in level 7 in the pay matrix (Rs. 44900-142400) on regular 
basis on the date of notification of the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Regional Controller 
of Mines, Deputy Controller of Mines, Senior Assistant Controller of Mines, Assistant Controller of 
Mines and Assistant Mining Engineer (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 
2023.”; 


(iii) the “Note” shall be numbered as “Note 2”; 
(iv) “Note 1” shall be renumbered as “Note 3”. 
[F. No. 22/1/2023-M.I1]
