NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 4th August, 2025 


5.0. 3613(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of 2-lane Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 281 at Km. 389/32-34 between Tenali — Nidubrolu stations" as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Guntur for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette.
