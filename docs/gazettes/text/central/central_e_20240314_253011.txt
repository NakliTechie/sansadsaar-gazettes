rules further to amend the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 20l9, namely:- 


l. (!) These rules may be called the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) (Second 
Amendment) Rules, 2024.

“Explanation.- For the purposes of this clause, unit shall include unit that has been partly paid up, which is 
permitted under the regulations framed by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, in consultation with 
Government of India;”.
