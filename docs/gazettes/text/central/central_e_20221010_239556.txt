INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-05 / 11251 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Shri Bhagwat Prasad Singh Memorial , ANM Training School situated at Deo More , GT Road 
( NH - 2 ) PO – Teldiha , PS - Madanpur , Aurangabad 824103 , Bihar is recognized by State Government of Bihar 
and Bihar Nurses & Registration Council . 


- 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-05 / 12251 - INC / 11913 dated 09/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) Department of Health , 
Government of Bihar , Vikas Bhawan , New Secretariat , Bihar was issued . Copy of the notice dated 09/02/2021 was 
also enclosed to Shri Bhagwat Prasad Singh Memorial , ANM Training School situated at Deo More , GT Road 
( NH - 2 ) PO – Teldiha , PS – Madanpur , Aurangabad - 824103 , Bihar . In the notice issued , the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Shri Bhagwat Prasad Singh Memorial , ANM Training School . Even the institution Shri Bhagwat Prasad 
Singh Memorial , ANM Training School has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection under section 
13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only that the Council would 
be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct nursing courses . Refusal for 
inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the requisite Teaching , Clinical or 
infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Shri Bhagwat Prasad Singh Memorial , 
ANM Training School situated at Deo More , GT Road ( NH - 2 ) PO Teldiha , PS Madanpur , Aurangabad - 
824103 , Bihar shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Bihar subject to orders of State government 
of Bihar . 


Lt. Col ( Dr. ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
