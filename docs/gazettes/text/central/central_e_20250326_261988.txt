NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th March, 2025 


S.O. 1402(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, published in the Gazette of India,Extraordinary, Part H, Section3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub- 
section (1) of section3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (here in after referred to as the said Act) 
Vide S.O. No. 121 (E) dated 7th January 2025 the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction (four laning etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of Ring Road (Phase-I) in the stretch of land from Km 0.000 to Km 22.150 in the district 
of BASTI in the state of UTTAR PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Times of India (English)” and 
“Amar Ujala (Hindi)” both dated 28th January 2025 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority not received any objections filed under Section 3-C; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
