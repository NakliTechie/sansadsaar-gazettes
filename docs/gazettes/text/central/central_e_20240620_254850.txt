S.O. 238(E).—Where as by the Notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number 8.0. 3779(E), dated 23th August, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part 
II, section-3, Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (240f!989)(here in after referred to as the 
said Act),the Central Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification 3779(J)date 23 Aug 2023 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of land to 
be acquired with or without structure for the Special Railway Project, New BG Line project From Dahod - Indore 
Via, Jhabua, Sardrapur, dhar and Pithampur (204.76km) in District Dhar, Village NauGaon Bujurg in the state of 
Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily “Free press Journal” dated 
26" August, 2023 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 26th August, 2023 under sub-section (4) of Section 
20A of the said Act. And, whereas, Nil objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power 
conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20 (E) of the said Act, the Central 
Government here by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in 
the Schedule aNnexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
