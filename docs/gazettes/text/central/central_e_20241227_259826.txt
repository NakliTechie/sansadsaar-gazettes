New Delhi, the |9th November, 2024 


F. No. 8-8/4600-INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIHI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Shanti College Of Nursing situated at New Jhansi Khajurao National Highway, Nowgong, 
Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 4720l, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh 
and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-l8/l4600-INC (5284) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Madhya Pradesh Room No. 24, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was 
issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Shanti College Of Nursing situated at New Jhansi 
Khajurao National Highway, Nowgong, Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 4720l, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice 
issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respectsSuitability of 
the nursing institution. 

Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government asrespects the case 


of Shanti College Of Nursing. Even the institution, Shanti College Of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Indian Nursing Council vide itsletter no. 8- 
8/I4600-INC dated 2/04/2023 considered the institution as closed for GNM course as no nursing teaching faculty 
and students were present on the day of inspection. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Shanti CollegeOf Nursing situated at New 
Jhansi Khajurao National Highway, Nowgong, Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 4720, Madhya Pradesh. Shall be 
entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh.
