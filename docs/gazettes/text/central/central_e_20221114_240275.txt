MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd November, 2022 


99.00. 5265(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Petroleum and Natural Gas S.O. No. 3685(E) 13" October 2020, issued under sub-section (1) of section 
3of the petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (50 of 1962), 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), published in the Gazette of India dated the 20" October, 2020 the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule 
appended to that notification for the purpose of laying Augmentation of “Muzaffarpur-Motihari Pipeline 
Project” for the transportation of LPG Products in Block-Motihari, District- East Champaran in the state 
of Bihar by Indian Oil Corporation Limited ; 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the concerned land 
owner by 187 January 2022. 


And whereas Shri Arun Kumar Jha, Competent Authority Patna (Bihar) has under sub-section (1) 
of section 6 of the said Act submitted report to the Central Government ; 


And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that 
the said land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein ; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 6 of the said Act, 
the Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule 
appended to this notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline ; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, 
instead of vesting in the Central Government, vest on the date of publication of the declaration, in Indian 
Oil Corporation Limeted, free from all encumbrances.
