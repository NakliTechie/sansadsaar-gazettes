MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Central Railway (Engineering Department)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Prayagraj, the 14th August, 2025 


S.O. 3857(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Central Railway Engineering Department, notification no. S.O 1846 (E) dated 22 April, 2025 published in Gazette of 
India, extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub section (ii), notification no. 1813 dated 24 April 2025 (hereinafter referred 
to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of clause 20A of section 2 of the Railways Act 1989 (24 
of 1989) and Railways Amendment act 2008 (llof 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the Land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution of special Railway Project namely, “Elimination of manned level crossing No. 413 by providing 
RUB in Manikpur-Naini Railway Stations of Prayagraj Division’, District Rewa in the state of Madhya Pradesh; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily news paper namely, Dainik 
Jagran-Rewa, Patrika -Satna and Times of India Bhopal Dated 01 May, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, no any objection was received, 


And_ whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of said Act, the Competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the Land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
