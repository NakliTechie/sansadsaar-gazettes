2. स्वास्थ्य और परिवार कल्याण मंत्रालय, स्वास्थ्य सेवा महानिदेशालय, केन्द्रीय ओषधि मानक नियंत्रण संगठन, 
चिकित्सा युक्ति ऊर्ध्वाधर में (समूह “क” और “ख” पद) भर्ती नियम 2024 की अनुसूची में :- 


(i) सहायक arate नियंत्रक (|) के पद से संबंधित क्रम संख्या 4 के सामने, स्तंभ (11) में, उपशीर्षक प्रोन्नति के 
अधीन, टिप्पण के स्थान पर निम्नलिखित टिप्पण रखे जाएंगे, अर्थात्‌:- 


“टिप्पण 1: जहां ऐसे कनिष्ठ व्यक्तियों के संबंध में जिन्होंने अपनी aes या पात्रता सेवा पूरी कर ली है, प्रोन्नति के लिए विचार 
किया जा रहा हो, वहाँ उनके ज्येष्ठ व्यक्तियों के संबंध में भी विचार किया जाएगा परंतु यह तब जब कि उसके द्वारा की गई 
ऐसी अर्हक या पात्रता सेवा, अपेक्षित ASH या पात्रता सेवा के आधे से अधिक या दो वर्ष, इनमें से जो भी कम हो, कम न हो 
और उन्होंने अपने ऐसे कानिष्ठ व्यक्तियों के सहित, जिन्होंने ऐसी अर्हक या पात्रता सेवा पहले ही पूरी कर ली है, अगली उच्चतर 
श्रेणी में प्रोन्नति के लिए अपनी परिवीक्षा की अवधि सफलतापूर्वक पूरी कर ली हो।

MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th April, 2025 


G.S.R. 260(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, 
the President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, 
Directorate General of Health Services, Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation, Medical Device 
Vertical (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts), Recruitment Rules, 2024, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) These rules may be called Ministry of Health and Family 
Welfare, Directorate General of Health Services, Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation, Medical 


Device Vertical (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts), Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


[भाग I—ave 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


2. In the Schedule to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Directorate General of Health Services, 
Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation, Medical Device Vertical (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts), 
Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(i) against serial number 4 relating to the post of Assistant Drugs Controller (1), in column (11), under 
the sub-heading promotion, for the Note, the following Notes shall be substituted, namely:- 


“Note 1: Where juniors who have completed their qualifying or eligibility service are being considered for 
promotion, their seniors would also be considered provided they are not short of the requisite qualifying or 
eligibility service by more than half of such qualifying or eligibility service or two years, whichever is less, 
and have successfully completed their probation period for promotion to the next higher grade along with 
their juniors who have already completed such qualifying or eligibility service. 


Note 2: The eligibility service for the incumbents holding the post of Drugs Inspector (Medical Devices) on 
regular basis on the date of commencement of these rules, shall be six years of regular service in the grade.”; 


(ii) against serial number 5 relating to the post of Drugs Inspector (Medical Devices), in column (7), 
under sub-heading “Qualification”, for the words “Bachelors of engineering technology in Bio 
Medical”, the words “Bachelors of engineering or technology in Bio Medical” shall be substituted.
