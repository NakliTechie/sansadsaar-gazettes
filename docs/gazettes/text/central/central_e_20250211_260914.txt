S.O. 707(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section 
(.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, !956 (48 of l956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act),Vide 
S.O. No. 4079(E) dated 9 September, 2024, the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of (Widening two / four laning etc,) 
Maintenance, management and operation of NH73lA (RAM VAN GAMAN PATH) on the stretch of land from Km 
49.55 to 2.950 in the District of Kaushambi in the state of UTTAR PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Aaj ” and “ The Times of India ” 
both dated 6 October, 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
