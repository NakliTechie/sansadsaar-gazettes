AWARD 


By order No. L-31011/7/2006 (IR(B-II)) dated 13.09.2006, the Central Government, Ministry of Labour in 
exercise of the power conferred under section 10(1)(d) and (2A) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 has referred the 
following issues to this Tribunal for adjudication. 


“Whether the action of the management of M/s. ATC (Clearing & Shipping) Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai in imposing 
the punishment of dismissal from services of Shri Jeevan Pawar w.e.f. 21.10.2005 is justified? If not, what relief the 
workman Shri Jeevan Pawar is entitled to?” 


The facts giving rise to the reference in brief are that the concerned workman was appointed as Clerk in the 
establishment of M/s. ATC (Clearing & Shipping) Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai in the year 1981. In due course, he was 
promoted as a Sr. Clerk. 


That sometime in the year 2005, he was placed under suspension for committing misconduct of 
misappropriation of the funds of the Company. Later, he was charge sheeted for committing misconduct under clause 
22(a), 22(d) and 22(i) of Model Standing Order framed under the Industrial Employment Standing Order Act, 1946 
on 24.03.2005 i.e. for inflating bills of mobile phone sets worth Rs.5,800/- and Rs.7150/- as Rs.15,800/- and 
Rs.17,150/-, purchased for official use from Coral Trading Company and M/s. Om Sai Electronics and Gift Articles, 
Santacruz and thereby misappropriating a sum of Rs.20,000/- and which was corroborated by Mr. Salim & Mr. Javed, 
the suppliers of those two mobile sets. 


4042 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : NOVEMBER 11, 2023/KARTIKA 20, 1945 [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Then management initiated departmental enquiry on such charges against the concerned workman. The 
concerned workman fully participated in the domestic enquiry. Inquiry Officer considering both oral and 
documentary evidence which were produced before him, found the concerned workman guilty to the charges. The 
disciplinary authority concurring with the finding of the Inquiry Officer proposed punishment of ‘removal from 
service’. Accordingly, the concerned workman was terminated from the services w.e.f. 21.10.2005. 


Challenging the order of dismissal of service, the concerned workman raised an industrial dispute before the 
appropriate authority and who in turn referred the matter to this tribunal for adjudication. 


Record shows the predecessor of this tribunal passed a preliminary award on 01.02.2013 holding the 
domestic enquiry conducted by the employer / management of M/s. ATC (Clearing & Shipping) Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai 
against the concerned workman not to be fair and proper and findings given by the Inquiry Officer to be totally 
perverse. Therefore, in view of provisions of section 11A of the I.D. Act, extended the management / employer to 
prove the charge against the concerned workman by adducing further evidence. 


The management / employer challenged the preliminary award dated 01.02.2013 before the Hon’ble High 
Court of Bombay and filed the writ petition No. 7386 / 2013. The Hon’ble High Court of Bombay affirmed the 
preliminary award dated 01.02.2013 and dismissed the writ petition. 


The management in order to prove the charge which it has brought against the concerned workman for 
misappropriation of Rs.20,000/- by inflating the bills of two mobile sets examined its following officials / staff:- 


1. Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe, its Manager who was working as Dy. Manager [A/cs. Dept.] in the year 2005, 
2 Shri Girish Achut Gadre, Chief Manager [A/cs.], 

3. Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar, Head of Export Dept. of the management and 

4 Shri Arvind Krishanji Kunte, ex-employee of the company. 


Now, let me see whether the management of the employer has been able to prove the charge that has been 
brought against the concerned workman. 


The charge sheet dated 24.03.2005 shows that while conducting statutory audit by M/s. H.T. Patel and 
Company, Chartered Accountant engaged by the employer for the financial year 2004 — 2005, it was found certain 
mobile phones were purchased for use of the staff and for making gift to the guest of the organization and bills raised 
to be doubtful. On queries, it was reported by Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe that a mobile set worth Rs.15,800/- was 
purchased by the company for use of Mr. Somesh Vaidya, Custom Clerk and invoice for the purchase of such mobile 
was sanctioned by Mr. Navroz, Administrative Manager. Mr. Navroz on queries informed Mr. Moghe mobile set 
worth Rs.5,800/- was actually purchased for use of Mr. S. Vaidya. It was found that said mobile phone was purchased 
from M/s. Coral Trading Company vide Invoice No. 93 EB. 


It has been alleged in the charge sheet, the concerned workman was the one who wrote the price of mobile 
set as Rs.15,800/- in place of Rs.5,800/- and wrote and drew a cheque worth Rs.15,800/- in favour of M/s. Coral 
Trading Company. That management called Mr. Salim and Mr. Javed two representatives of M/s. Coral Trading 
Company to clarify on the matter and who informed Mr. Navroz, that they were influenced by the concerned 
workman to alter the amount by adding certain figures on invoice. Mr. Javed furnished a written statement to that 
effect before Mr. A.K. Kunte, Dy. Manager of the company on 25.02.2005. 


It further shows that another mobile phone set of Nokia was purchased from M/s. Om Sai Electronics and 
Gift Articles, situated at 212, Hanuman Bldg., Vakola, Santacruz [East], Mumbai — 400 055. The said set was 
supplied by Mr. Javed and Mr. Salim through bill No. 394 dated 09.04.2004. The actual price of the said set was 
Rs.7150/- but the concerned workman by influencing Mr. Salim and Mr. Javed altered the bill for Rs.17,150/- and 
managed to issue cheque No. 673059 dated 19.10.2004 drawn on Corporation Bank, Fort branch and thereby 
managed to misappropriate Rs.10,000/- by influencing Mr. Salim and Mr. Javed. 


Therefore, the management considering the above conduct of the concerned workman to be grave in nature 
causing financial loss to the establishment by practicing fraud for his own financial gain charge sheeted him under 
clause 22 (a), 22(d) and 220) of Model Standing Order, framed under the Industrial Employment Standing Order 
Act, 1946 on 24.03.2005. 


Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe in his evidence in chief on affidavit stated that being Dy. Management [A/cs. 
Dept.] he used to work as an Internal Auditor though the company had engaged M/s. H.T. Patel & Co. Chartered 
Accountant as its statutory auditor. That during the internal audit and on scrutiny of mobile purchased bills, he found 
Bill No. 394:93EV:50:46A doubtful as amount mentioned in those bills being altered. He has further stated that the 
amount mentioned in those 4 bills was much more than the actual price of purchased mobile phone sets. He has also 
stated that different pens with different colours of ink being used in the preparation of those 4 bills. He found that 
figure ‘1’ being written in different colour of ink in those bills. He questioned Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and

Mr. Navroz Langrana, the two persons who had sanctioned and approved those bills and who have stated alteration in

But, during cross examination, he has admitted that during the internal audit he did not notice any

He further admitted that no charge was brought against the concerned workman for over writing or causing

Therefore, such evidence of Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe made during cross examination totally discredit 
what he has stated against the concerned workman in his evidence in chief on affidavit. Infact, his admission creates a 
doubt about the allegations and charge that have been brought against the concerned workman and prove the charges 
that have been brought against the concerned workman was / is not proper and supported by any documentary 
evidence. 


Similarly, Shri Girish Achut Gadre, Chief Manager [A/cs.] in his evidence in chief has stated that on 
receiving report from Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe Dy. Manager [A/cs.], he called for comments from Shri Ganesh 
Narayan Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana about the discrepancy in the disputed bills which they had sanctioned 
and approved. He has stated that Mr. Navroz Langrana used to verify the product with the bills and used to hand over 
the set to the concerned workman for whom the mobile phone was ordered. He has also stated that Mr. Navroz 
Langrana informed him many times the column meant for mentioning amounts in words in the invoice / bill used to 
be left blank as price mentioned on the mobile cover box used to match with the amount mentioned in the figure in 
the bills. Mr. Navroz Langrana used to ignore such blank column while sanctioning and approving the bill. 


That he reported such incidence to his senior Mr. Shantanu 5. Bhadkamka, M.D. and who directed him to 
investigate and initiate the disciplinary action against the culprit. 


That he called Mr. Javed, the supplier of the mobile phone and Mr. Javed in presence of Mr. A.K. Kunte, 
Sr. Dy. Manager admitted inflating the bills at the instance of concerned workman and Mr. Javed gave his statement 
in writing. Therefore, he on the basis of the written statement of Mr. Javed, initiated disciplinary action against 
concerned workman as per the directions of Mr. Shantanu s. Bhadkamka, M.D. and placed the workman under 
suspension on 25.02.2005. Then enquiry was started against the workman. Mr. Javed was noticed during the 
departmental enquiry but who refused to appear, rather he brought a false allegation against the company for putting 
pressure on him for giving false statement against the workman in writing. 


He has also stated at the relevant time the concerned workman was working as a Sr. Clerk in A/cs. Dept. and 
whose duty was to make entries of approved bills in books of accounts and on the basis of which payment used to be 
released after his approval. It was the duty of concerned workman to issue cheque to the parties after approval. 
Further, he during his examination in chief under oath before the tribunal he has proved the charge sheet, suspension 
order, disputed bills etc. 


However, during the cross examination he has admitted that he has not charge sheeted or brought any charge 
against the concerned workman for modification and fabrication of disputed mobile bills. Further, he has admitted 
that he acted on the report of Mr. Moghe, Dy. Manager [A/cs.] and who reported that there was alteration and 
modification in the mobile bills. But Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe in his cross examination discussed above admitted 
that he did not notice any discrepancy in the bills and did not find any discrepancy in between written figures and 
amount written in the words in those disputed bills. 


He has further admitted that Mr. Jeevan Pawar, the concerned workman has no authority to sign on the 
cheque for payment of bills. That bills are also produced before the concerned officer who signed the cheque. The 
cheque is prepared and signed after the bill is approved by the concerned officer. He has further admitted in his cross 
examination that Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana were the approving and sanctioning 
authorities of those disputed bills. The concerned workman used to prepare the cheques on the basis of approved bills

signed by Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana. He has further admitted that he and 
Mr. Lagoo, the joint authorized signatories signed on those disputed cheques issued against disputed bills. At the 
time of signing the cheque, the amount mentioned therein was rechecked and re-verified with the relevant invoices. 


Therefore, from cross examination of this witness it is seen that Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and Mr. 
Navroz Langrana were approving and sanctioning authorities of the disputed bills and not the concerned workman. 


He has further admitted during cross examination that Mr. Javed through his letters dated 05.07.2005 and 
13.07.2005 brought allegations against him for coercing him to give statement before Mr. A.K. Kunte, Sr. Dy. 
Manager on 25.02.2005, otherwise his other pending bills would be withheld. Such statement further makes the 
allegation that the concerned workman had influenced Mr. Javed, the vendor to inflate the disputed mobile bills 
appears to be doubtful. 


From the evidence of Shri Girish Achut Gadre, it appears that it is he and Mr. Lagoo were the authorized 
signatories of the cheque and not the concerned workman at the relevant time. Further, it is seen Shri Ganesh Narayan 
Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana were the two officials, who were responsible to approve the bills and check the 
invoices and it was not the concerned workman. So it is not known how this concerned workman who was neither the 
approving authority of the bills nor the signatory of the cheque could manipulate the dispued bills and invoices. 


Thus, from the evidence of Shri Prashant Sharad Moghe and Shri Girish Achut Gadre, it appears that after 
the approval of bills and invoices by Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana, cheque was 
prepared and signed by Shri Girish Achut Gadre and Mr. Lagoo, the joint signatories and who too before putting their 
signatures on the cheques used to verify and check the invoices. 


The management has also examined Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar. He in his evidence in chief on 
affidavit stated that the establishment used to buy mobile sets for official purpose from M/s. Om Sai Electronics and 
Gift Articles, Santacruz [East], Mumbai — 400 055. Mr. Javed, the Proprietor of M/s. Om Sai Electronics and Gift 
Articles, Santacruz, used to personally deliver the mobile set along with bills to him. That Mr. Javed submitted the 
bill for Rs.7150/- for the mobile set. That he took the delivery of mobile set and signed on the bill having an amount 
of Rs.7150/- and at that time Mr. Navroz Langrana was the administration in-charge was present. He never received 
or approved any bill against the said mobile phone for Rs.17150/-. Later he was enquired about the bill by Shri 
Prashant Sharad Moghe and he was shocked to find that the bill was for Rs.17,150/- instead of Rs.7150/-. 


However, during his cross examination he has stated that in the bill there is a column for writing amount in 
words or letters that he did not ask Mr. Javed to fill the said blank column and that he approved the bill leaving the 
said column blank, but at the same time he has admitted that he did mention in his report that there was alteration or 
modification in the bills and did not state that such modification and alteration was done by the concerned workman. 
He has stated it is the duty of the concerned workman to prepare cheque and present the same before the signatories 
for signatures. 


Shri Arvind Krishanji Kunte, ex-employee of the management in his evidence in chief on affidavit stated that 
in his presence Mr. Javed admitted that out of 4 bills submitted by him he has altered 2 bills bearing No. 394 and 
93EV as per the instructions of the concerned workman who was in need of money. That Mr. Javed further admitted 
that he handed over the inflated the amount of Rs.20,000/- against those two disputed bills to concerned workman 
Shri Jeevan Pawar. He has stated that he obtained the statement of Mr. Javed without any coercion and under duress. 


Surprisingly, this witness during his cross examination admitted there is line of signature of the approving 
authority on the amount written in words on those bills. So a question may arise, then how there can be manipulation 
in the column meant for writing the amount in words / letters, when the signature of approving authority appears over 
the amounts written in words. His such evidence further makes the statement given by Mr. Moghe and Mr. Gadre that 
column meant for writing the amount of bill in words were always kept blank doubtful. Further, this witness has 
admitted that Mr. Javed had filed an application alleging he had obtained his statement incriminating the concerned 
workman for inflating the bills under pressure and under coercion. 


It is very interesting to note the management for reason best known to it has failed to produce the main 
witness Mr. Javed on whose statement recorded by Mr. Kunte, the management has acted and put the concerned 
workman under suspension and thereafter charge sheeted him. In order to prove incriminatory statement given by Mr. 
Javed against the concerned workman, the management ought to have produced him and prove his incriminating 
statement given against the workman before the tribunal and which the management has failed to do so. Therefore, in 
the eyes of law the uncorroborated incriminating statement of Mr. Javed is of no value and cannot be used against the 
concerned workman. 


Further, the management for reason best known to it has failed to produce and examine Mr. Navroz 
Langrana, a vital witness to this case. 


Therefore, in view of the evidence of the above four witnesses of the management and as discussed leaves no 
room for doubt the management has failed to bring home charge of manipulation of bills and thereby 
misappropriation of Rs. 20,000/- against the concerned workman beyond preponderance of probability.

Infact, it appears that management of ATC (Clearing & Shipping) Pvt. Ltd. and especially Shri Girish Achut 
Gadre in order to save the high rank officers, the real culprits who were involved or indulged in the scam of looting 
the company financially by preparing inflated bills during audit appears to have made a Sr. Clerk Mr. Pawar a 
scapegoat. The concerned workman appears to have been victimized by the management and a victim of unfair labour 
practices probably to save its official namely Shri Ganesh Narayan Karandikar and Mr. Navroz Langrana, the two 
officials who were responsible in approving those disputed bills and Mr. Girish Achut Gadre and Mr. Lagoo, the two 
signatories of the cheques. 


Therefore, this tribunal holds the management has failed to prove the charge of misappropriation against the 
concerned workman beyond preponderance of probabilities. Consequentially the order of dismissal of Shri Jeevan 
Pawar, Sr. Clerk w.e.f. 21.10.2005 is held to be illegal. 


It has come on record the concerned workman was 63 years old on 06.01.2020, the day he gave evidence 
before this tribunal. Therefore, it appears he has already been superannuated from the service on his attaining the age 
of superannuation either at the age of 58 years or at the age of 60 years, whichever is applicable to him. Therefore, the 
question of his reinstatement in service does not arise, but he is entitled to get full back wages from the day he was 
placed under suspension i.e. on 25.02.2005 till his superannuation from the service. He is further entitled to get all the 
service benefits to which a retired employee of ATC (Clearing and Shipping) Pvt. Ltd. is entitled to i.e. pension, 
gratuity, leave encashment, P.F. and other statutory entitlements. 


Management of ATC (Clearing and Shipping) Pvt. Ltd. is hereby directed to pay all the entire back wages 
along with interest @ 12% p.a. along with pensionary benefits to the concerned workman within six months from the 
date hereof failing which the concerned workman shall be entitled to recover or realise the same as per rules and laws. 


Accordingly, a final award is hereby passed in favour of Shri Jeevan S. Pawar, a Sr. Clerk holding his 
termination to be illegal. Reference No. CGIT-1 / 31 of 2006 is hereby disposed of.
