the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Namaste Telangana (Telugu)” 
and “The Hindu (English)” both dated 20.09.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
