New Delhi, the 3rd September, 2024 


S.O. 3796(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. No. 422 (E) dated 22™ September 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II,

(with provision of new ROB) to Mandal Town connecting National Highway No. 58 (Km 0.300) to NH-48 (Km

settled the same appropriately;

submitted its report to the Central Government;

conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land
