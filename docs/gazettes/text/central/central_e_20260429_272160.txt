NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 23th April, 2026 
Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 227(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (No.] of 2008), the Central Government 
hereby notifies “Proposed construction of 2 lane ROB in lieu of LC-70 at KM 56/6-7 in KWV-MRJ Section” as 
Railway project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Sangli District of 
Maharashtra State, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette.
