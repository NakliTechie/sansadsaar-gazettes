MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 27th July, 2026 


S.O. 4158(E).— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
published in the Gazette of India dated the 29" July, 2025, under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) & the railway (amendment) Act — 2008 (11 of 2008), the intention of the 
Central Government to acquire certain lands for the public purpose of construction of "Luni-Samdari-Bhildi 
Doubling Project (Special Railway Project) in Jodhpur Division of Rajasthan State" was declared; 


And whereas during the course of Joint Measurement Survey (JMS) and verification by the Revenue 
staff of Raniwara Tehsil, it has been observed that certain survey numbers situated in villages Jakhri & 
Raniwara Khurd of Raniwara Tehsil, District Jalore, Rajasthan, required for the said project were not 
included in the aforesaid notification, due to error in digitized maps of villages. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the railway 
(amendment) Act — 2008 (11 of 2008) & the Railways Act, 1989, the Central Government hereby declares 
its intention to acquire the lands specified in the Schedule below for the said public purpose. 


Any person interested in the land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, file objections, if any, to the Competent Authority, namely Sub 
Divisional Officer Raniwara, District Jalore, Rajasthan in writing, as provided under sub-section (1) of 
section 20D of the said Act.
