NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st July, 2023 


S.O. 3314(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 of the National Investigation Act, 2008 (34 
of 2008), the Central Government in supersession of the notification of the Government of India, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (1), vide No. 8.0. 3390 (E) dated the 19" September, 
2019, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the Central Government in 
consultation with the Hon’ble Chief Justice of the High Court of Andhra Pradesh, hereby designates the Court of III 
Additional District Court-cum-Special Judge for trial of SPE and ACB cases, Vishakhapatnam and Court of II 
Additional District Court-cum-Metropolitan Sessions Judge, Vijayawada in State of Andhra Pradesh as the Special 
Courts for the purpose of sub-section (1) of section 11 of the said Act for the trial of Scheduled Offences investigated 
by the National Investigation Agency. 


The jurisdiction of the Special Courts mentioned above in the State of Andhra Pradesh shall be as mentioned 
in the following table:
