NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 25th January , 2023 

F. No. 4-1 / 2018 - Policy - 1. - In supersession of the definition of Broadband issued vide Notification 
No. 4-4 / 2009 - Policy - I dated 18th July , 2013 and after consideration of the recommendation of the Telecom 
Regulatory Authority of India ( TRAI ) , the Central Government hereby revise the definition of Broadband 
as follows : 


" Broadband is a data connection that is able to support interactive services 
including Internet access and has the capability of the minimum download speed of 
2 Mbps to an individual subscriber from the point of presence ( POP ) of the service 
provider intending to provide Broadband service . " 

The notification shall come in to force with immediate effect .
