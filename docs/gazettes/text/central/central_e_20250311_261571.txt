फा. सं. मौ.आ.रा.प्रौ.सं./अधिनियम/01/2025(अ).----मौलाना आज़ाद राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी संस्थान भोपाल का 
बोर्ड राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी, विज्ञान शिक्षा और अनुसंधान संस्थान अधिनियम, 2007 (2007 का 29) की धारा 26 की 
उपधारा (2) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, मौलाना आज़ाद राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी संस्थान भोपाल के कुलाध्यक्ष के 
अनुमोदन से, मौलाना आज़ाद राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी संस्थान भोपाल परिनियम, 2017 में संशोधन करने के लिए निम्नलिखित 
परिनियम बनाता है, अर्थात्‌:-- 


1. (1) इन परिनियमों का संक्षिप्त नाम मौलाना आज़ाद राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी संस्थान भोपाल (संशोधन) 
परिनियम, 2025 है। 


(2) ये मौलाना आज़ाद राष्ट्रीय प्रौद्योगिकी संस्थान भोपाल पर लागू होंगे। 
(3) ये राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तारीख को प्रवृत्त होंगे।

MAULANA AZAD NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY BHOPAL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st February, 2025 
F. No. MANIT/Statutes/01/2025 (E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 
26 of the National Institutes of Technology, Science Education and Research Act, 2007 (29 of 2007), with the 
approval of the Visitor of the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology Bhopal, the Board of the Maulana Azad 


National Institute of Technology Bhopal, hereby makes the following Statutes to amend the Maulana Azad National 
Institute of Technology Bhopal Statutes, 2017, namely:— 


1. (1) These Statutes may be called the Statutes of the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology 
Bhopal (Amendment) Statute, 2025. 


(2) They shall apply to the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology Bhopal. 
(3) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology Bhopal Statutes, 2017 (hereinafter referred to as the 


principal Statutes), in Statute 14, after sub-statute (iv), the following sub-statute shall be inserted, namely— 


“(v) In the event of occurrence of any vacancy in the office of the Chairperson of Board of Governors by reason of 
expiry of his or her tenure, death, resignation or otherwise or in the event of the Chairperson being unable to 
discharge his or her functions owing to absence, illness or any other cause, the Chairperson of an Institute of National 
Importance, may discharge the functions assigned to the Chairperson under Section 16 of the Act, for a period of six 
months or till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever is earlier, with the approval of the Visitor and in 
case of expiry of tenure, the Visitor may extend the term of the incumbent Chairperson for a period of six months or 


till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever is earlier.”
