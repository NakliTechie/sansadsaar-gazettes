Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of the 
notification in the official Gazette, object, under sub-section () of the section 20D of the said Act to the acquisition and use

further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections as per 
Sub-Section (2) of section 20D of the said Act.

Authority under Sub-Section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final.
