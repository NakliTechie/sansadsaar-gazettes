New Delhi, the 8th December, 2023 


S.O. 5374(E).—WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 


(Railway Board), vide number S.0.382 (E), dated the 2lth March, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 22nd March, 2023, (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Eastern Dedicated 
Freight Corridor in the District of Purba Bardhaman in the State of West Bengal;
