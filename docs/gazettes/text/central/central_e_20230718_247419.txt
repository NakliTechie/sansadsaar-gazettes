S.O. 3228(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
(Amendment) Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of 'Barabanki-Akbarpur 
(161 Km) Doubling” as a “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land, in Barabanki, Ayodhya and 
Akbarpur District in the State of Uttar Pradesh, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose 
with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
