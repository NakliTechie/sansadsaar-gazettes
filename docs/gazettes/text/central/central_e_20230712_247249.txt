आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा |

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th July, 2023 


S.O. 3069(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendment in the Gazette Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways 
published vide 8.0. 2094(E), dated- 03-05-2023, is required for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four 
laning, etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH-161BB in the stretch of land from Km. 0.00 to 
Km. 12.05 in the district of KAMAREDDY in the State of TELANGANA, hereby declares its intention to acquire 


such land. 


In the Schedule to the said Notifications, the Mandal/Taluk is notified as “Madnoor” for the Villages 
Madnoor, Wadifathepur, Sonala, Thadi Hipperga, Mahalsapur, Limbur, Hasantakli, Pedda Takli, and Sirpur, but the 
Villages Limbur, Hasantakli, Pedda Takli, and Sirpur pertains to “Dongli” Mandal Kamareddy District, Banswada 
Revenue Division and the Villages Madnoor, Wadifathrpur, Sonala, Thadi Hipperga, and Mahalsapur pertains to 


“Madnoor” Mandal/Taluk, Kamareddy District, Banswada Revenue Division. 


Change of Name of the Mandal for Madnur-Rudrur-Bodhan Section of NH-161BB in the State of 


Telangana. 


Due to the formation of New Mandal Dongli vide the Kamareddy District Gazette No. 114, 
dated: 26-09-2023, the Villages Limbur, Hasantakli, Pedda Takli, and Sirpur are coming under Dongli 
Mandal/Taluk, Kamareddy District, Banswada Division. Hence, a corrigendum is required with the above- 
mentioned changes.
