स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण विभाग, झारखंड राज्य सरकार, नेपाल हाउस, दोरंदा, रांची--834002, झारखंड को संबोधित नोटिस

प्रति किंगपिन कॉलेज ऑफ नर्सिंग, सेवा मिशन अस्पताल, चिल्दग रोड, गोंदली पोखर, अनागरा, रांची--835403 
(झारखंड) को भी भेजी गई थी। जारी किए गए नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुकतता के संबंध में परिषद्‌

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F.No. 8-36/8476-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Kingpin College of Nursing situated at Sewa Mission Hospital, Childag Road, Gondli Pokhar, 
Anagara, Ranchi — 83503, Jharkhand is recognized by State Government of Jharkhand and Jharkhand Nurses & 
Registration Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-36/8476-INC dated 6/09/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section |4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Chief Secretary, Department of Health and Family Welfare, 
Government of Jharkhand, Nepal House, Doranda, Ranchi — 834002, Jharkhand was issued. Copy of the notice dated 
6/09/2022 was also enclosed to Kingpin College of Nursing situated at Sewa Mission Hospital, Childag Road, 
Gondli Pokhar, Anagara, Ranchi — 83503, Jharkhand. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested 
to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council received a letter dated 0/I0/2022 from the institution. Thereafter Indian Nursing 
Council issued a letter dated 09/0/2023. No action, as required, has been taken by the institution. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
0/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run B.Sc(N) 
Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3)(b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Kingpin College of Nursing situated at 
Sewa Mission Hospital, Childag Road, Gondli Pokhar, Anagara, Ranchi — 83503, Jharkhand shall be entitled 
to be registered only in the State of Jharkhand subject the orders of State government of Jharkhand. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
