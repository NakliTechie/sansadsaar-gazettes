MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th March, 2023 


S.O. 085(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.0.70 (E), dated 0.0.2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary No.l62, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 
956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH45A on the stretch of land from Km. 0 to Km. 29 (Deputy Collector Office Villianur 
Puducherry) in the district of PUDUCHERRY in the state of PONDICHERRY; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The New Indian Express” and 
“Dailythanthi” both dated 04.02.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any persons by the competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
