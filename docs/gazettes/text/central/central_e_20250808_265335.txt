MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th August, 2025 


G.S.R. 541(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 9C of the Mines 
and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following further amendment in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Mines 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 
633(E), dated the 147 August, 2015, namely:- 


In the said notification, in paragraph II, for serial number (i) and the entries relating thereto, the 
following entries shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(i) Director General, National Mineral Exploration Trust - Member Secretary, ex officio;’. 


[F. No.43/1/2025-M.1] 
SANJAY LOHITYA, Addl. Secy.

G.S.R.100(E), dated the 7" February, 2022.
