No. PR / 76 / 14 / DD / 96 / 14 / DC / 554 / 17. - In terms of the provisions of Section 21B ( 3 ) of the Chartered 
Accountants Act , 1949 read with Rules 18 ( 17 ) and 19 ( 1 ) of the Chartered Accountants ( Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases ) Rules , 2007 , the Disciplinary Committee has held 
CA. ( Ms. ) Bhavya Garg ( Membership No. 124759 ) , Prayag Chand Hariram , Ralles Road , AMRAVATI 
444601 , guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clause ( 7 ) of Part I of the Second Schedule 
to the aforesaid Act and consequently ordered for removal of the name of aforesaid CA. ( Ms. ) Bhavya Garg 
( Membership No. 124759 ) from the Register of Members for a period of 01 ( one ) year , which is also upheld by the 
Appellate Authority vide its order pronounced on 3rd November , 2022. In pursuance thereof and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub - section ( 2 ) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act , read with Regulation 18 of the Chartered 
Accountants Regulations , 1988 , it is hereby notified that the name of said CA. ( Ms. ) Bhavya Garg ( Membership 
No. 124759 ) , shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 01 ( one ) year with effect from 
16th December , 2022 .
