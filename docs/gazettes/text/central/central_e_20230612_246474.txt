INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F.No. 8-38/776-INC.— :— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Central India College of Nursing situated at G.E. Road, Dewada Chowk, Kopedih Road, 
Gram Dewada, Distt. Rajnandgaon 4944, Chhattisgarh was recognized by State Government of Chhattisgarh 
and Chhattisgarh Nursing Council to conduct Post Basic Diploma in Psychiatric Nursing Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-38/776-INC dated 6/09/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of Indian 
Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary, Department of Health & Family Welfare, Government of 
Chhattisgarh, R.N. S4-8, Mahanadi Bhawan Mantralaya, New Raipur — 492002, Chhattisgarh was issued. Copy of 
the notice dated 6/09/2022 was also enclosed Central India College Of Nursing situated at G.E. Road, Dewada 
Chowk, Kopedih Road, Gram Dewada, Distt. Rajnandgaon 4944, Chhattisgarh. In the notice issued, the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
Central India College of Nursing. Even the institution Central India College of Nursing has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
0/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run Post 
Basic Diploma in Psychiatric Nursing Course. 

Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic Diploma in 
Psychiatric Nursing from Central India College Of Nursing situated at G.E. Road, Dewada Chowk, Kopedih 
Road, Gram Dewada, Distt. Rajnandgaon 4944, Chhattisgarh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State 
of Chhattisgarh subject to orders of state government of Chhattisgarh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
