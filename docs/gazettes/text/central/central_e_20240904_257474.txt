MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 


(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 24th September, 2024 


S.O. 492(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 293 (E) dated the |2th March, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 2th March, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Daulatabad Goods Siding project in the 
District of Aurangabad in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Paper “Lokmat” "Lokmat 
Times" & “Lokamt Samachar” dated 0 October, 2023, under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in Talesamand village, certain objections were received and the same have been considered by 


the competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 


report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 


the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 


shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
