9.0. 96(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport And Highways number S.O.2039 (E) dated 2.05.2024 published in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of 
the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
(Widening/Two-Laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and operation of NH-347B on the stretch of land from 
K.m. 0+000 to K.m. 35+560 in (Thikri-Anjad-Road) (Slip Road) in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. Hereby 
declares its intention to acquire Such Land. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Raj 
Express” 8 June 2024 and “The Times of India” 9 June 2024 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances,
