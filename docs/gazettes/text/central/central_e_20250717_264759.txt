MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th July, 2025 


F. No. A-35013/03/2018-Ad-III/144.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri Nikhil Pavan 
Kalyan, IAS (OD:2012) an officer belonging to Odisha Cadre of Indian Administrative Service as Director 
of Census Operations/Director of Citizen Registration, Odisha with effect from 16.06.2025 (FN) for a period 
upto 31.12.2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation under the Central Staffing 
Scheme. 


2. The Headquarters of Shri Nikhil Pavan Kalyan, IAS (OD:2012) will be Bhubaneswar (Odisha).
