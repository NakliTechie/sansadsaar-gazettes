PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 247 December 2024 


No. 35 /2024-25 
Subject: Import of Menthol under Advance Authorisation/DFIA - reg 


F. No. 01/89/180/Misc-3/AM-04/PC-2[A] / Vol.-IflI / E-141—In exercise of powers conferred 
under paragraph 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, the Director General of Foreign 
Trade hereby makes the following amendments to Handbook of Procedures (HBP) and Standard Input- 
Output Norms (SION) with immediate effect. 


2. The SION/Adhoc norms for import of Menthol, suspended vide Public Notice No. 48/2023 dated 7 
March 2024, are re-instated and shall now be covered under the following ITC(HS) codes read with 
Supplementary Note No. | of Chapter 29, ITC(HS), 2022 Schedule —I (Import Policy): 


Item Description 


906 11 10 --Menthol:--- Natural Menthol 
906 11 90 --Menthol:--- Other 


3. The RAs and NCs may now consider the cases for issuance and fixation of Advance 
Authorization/DFIA and Adhoc norms as per policy and procedure. 


Effect of this Public Notice: 


SION/Adhoc norms in respect of Menthol, now covered under ITC(HS) codes 29061110 and 
29061190, have been re-instated with immediate effect.
