MINISTRY OF POWER 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th March, 2025 


S.O. 522(E).—In accordance with Section 3(3) of Tribunal Reforms Act, 202l, a Search-cum-Selection 
Committee is hereby constituted as follows for making recommendations to the Central Government for the appointment 
to the post of Technical Member for Electricity in the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL) :- 


(i) Hon’ble Mr. Justice K.V. Viswanathan Judge of Supreme Court - Chairperson 
(ii) Chairperson, APTEL - Member 
(iii) Secretary to the Government of India, Ministry of New & Renewable Energy - Member 
(iv) Secretary to the Government of India, Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas - Member 


(v) Secretary to the Government of India, Ministry of Power - Member-Secretary 


2. The Search-cum-Selection Committee shall recommend a panel of two names for appointment to the post of 
Technical Member of APTEL. 


3. The Search-cum-Selection Committee shall determine the procedure for making its recommendations.
