का.आ. 1770(अ).-- केन्द्रीय सरकार ने रेलवे अधिनियम 1989 (1989 का 24) (जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त 
अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 20क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई थी, भारत सरकार ने रेल मंत्रालय (रेल 
बोर्ड) की अधिसूचना संख्या का. आ. 2480 (A). जो भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग ii, खंड 3, उप-खंड ii तारीख 
04/06/2025 में प्रकाशित की गई थी और उक्त अधिसूचना के उपाबध्द में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को, राजस्थान राज्य के जयपुर 
जिले में विशेष रेल परियोजना खातीपुरा जयपुर के उपग्रह स्टेशन के रूप में नई टर्मिनल सुविधा के विकास हेतु के निष्पादन, 
अनुरक्षण,प्रबन्ध और प्रचालन के लिए अर्जित करने को आश्य की घोषणा की थी। 
और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20क की धारा (4) के अधीन दैनिक समाचार पत्र, अर्थात्‌

और सक्षम अधिकारी द्वारा इस परियोजना में तय समय में प्राप्त आपत्ति का निस्तारण किया जा चुका है

S.O. 1770(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railway 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 2480(E), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section — 
3, Sub-section (ii), dated June 2025 and issued under section under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 7 


intention to acquire the land specified in the Scheduled annexed of the said notification for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely “Khatipura Development 
of the New Terminal Facility as Satellite Station to Jaipur” in the District -Jaipur in the State of Rajasthan. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily newspapers, 
namely, Dainik Bhaskar and Rajasthan Patrika (Hindi) dated 07 August 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections received within the stipulated time period were resolved by competent 
authority and accordingly. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
