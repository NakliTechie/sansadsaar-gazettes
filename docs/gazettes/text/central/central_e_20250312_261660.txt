मार्च 2024 (जिसे ईसमें ईसके पश्चात उक्त अधिनियम कह गया हे) द्वारा उक्त अधिसुचना से उपाबध्ध अनुसूची में विनिद्रिष्ट

घोषणा करती हे। 


और उक्त अधिसुचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20क की उप-धारा (4) के अधीन दैनिक समाचार पत्र 
“संदेश” (गुजराती), “युगना ऐंधाण” (गुजराती) और “टाईम्स नेशन“ (अंग्रेजी) में तारीख 7 मार्च 2024 को प्रकाशित किया 
गया था। 


और जंबुसर में ०३ आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई थी और उस पर विचार किया, तथा सक्षम प्राधिकारी द्वारा उन्हे अनुज्ञात कर 
दिया गया है।

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]0th March, 2025 


S.O. I44(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 375 (E), dated the 5th March, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part I, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the I5th March, 2024 and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of l989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Jambusar-Samani Gauge Conversion 
Project (Western Railway) in the District of Bharuch in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely 
the “Sandesh” and “Yugna Aendhan’(Gujarati) & “Times Nation” (English), dated (7॥ March, 2023 under 
sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, 


And, whereas, 03 objections in Jambusar were received and same have been considered and disallowed by 
the Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been passed. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
