MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 4th February, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 581(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number. S.O 3748 (A) dated 11 August 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, PartlI, 
section 3, Sub-section (1) dated the 14 August 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 20-A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project,namely 
“Wardha- Nanded (via Yevatmal Pusood)” New BG Railway Line in the district of Nanded in the state of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Udyacha Marathwada” Dated 13 December 2025 And “Dainik The Waraqu -E Taza Urdu Daily Nanded” Dated 147 
December 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, the objections received have been considered and action taken accordingly by the competent authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 


to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 


the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette; the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 


shall vest absolutely in the Central Government Free from all encumbrances.
