INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd July, 2024 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


F.No. 8-8/805-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section l4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Siddhi Vinayak Paramedical & Nursing College situated at NH-75 Jhansi Road Village 
Gandhari, Distt. Datia 47566l, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and 
Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. I8-l8/I5I56-INC dated 5/l/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya 
Pradesh Room No. 24I, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 5/l/2022 was also enclosed to Siddhi Vinayak Paramedical & Nursing College situated 
at NH-75 Jhansi Road Village Gandhari, Distt. Datia 47566, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Siddhi Vinayak Paramedical & Nursing College. Even the institution, Siddhi Vinayak Paramedical & Nursing 
College has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
06/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run Post 
Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic B.Sc(N) from Siddhi Vinayak Paramedical 
& Nursing College situated at NH-75 Jhansi Road Village Gandhari, Distt. Datia 47566, Madhya Pradesh, shall 
be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to orders of state government of Madhya 
Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secretary 


Indian Nursing Council
