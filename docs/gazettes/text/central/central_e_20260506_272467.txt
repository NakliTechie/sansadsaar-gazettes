MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 


Guntur, the 6th May, 2026 


S.O. 2277(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by sub-section () of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that the land, 
the brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed here to, is required for construction of Road Over Bridge 
In lieu of Level Crossing No.03 at Km 3/25-27 between Guntur and Nambur in Guntur District of Andhra Pradesh, 
hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the 
aforesaid purpose under sub-section (l) of Section 20-(D) of the said Act within a period of thirty (30) days from the 
date of publication of this notification in the official gazette. 


Every such objection, shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely Revenue Divisional Officer Guntur, 
Guntur District in writing and shall be set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority and Revenue Divisional 
Officer, Guntur of Guntur District shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal 
practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub section (II) of section 20-(D) of the said act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land under this notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested persons at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
