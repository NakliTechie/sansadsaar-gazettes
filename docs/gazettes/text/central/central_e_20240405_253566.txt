MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th April, 2024 


S.O. 629(E).—Whereas, Jammu and Kashmir Peoples Freedom League (JKPFL) has been declared as an 
unlawful association, vide notification number S.O. |44(E) dated the 5" March, 2024, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii). 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 5 read with sub-section (]) 
of section 4 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of 967), the Central Government hereby 
constitutes the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Tribunal consisting of Justice Neena Bansal Krishna, Judge, High 
Court of Delhi, for the purpose of adjudicating whether or not there is sufficient cause for declaring the Jammu and 
Kashmir Peoples Freedom League (JKPFL) as an unlawful association.
