NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2026 


S.O. 4427(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (2) and (3) of section 3 of the 
Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986), the Central Government hereby makes the following order 
further to amend the River Ganga (Rejuvenation, Protection and Management) Authorities Order, 2016, 
namely: - 


1. (1) This Order may be called the River Ganga (Rejuvenation, Protection and Management) Authorities 
(Second Amendment) Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. In the River Ganga (Rejuvenation, Protection and Management) Authorities Order, 2016- 
(1) in paragraph 3, in sub-paragraph (1), - 

(1) after clause (a), the following clause shall be inserted, namely - 


“(aa) “active flood plain” means such area of River Ganga or its tributaries which comes under water due 
to floods corresponding to a one in five-year return period; ”; 


(11) for clause (1), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(l) flood plain” means such area of River Ganga or its tributaries which comes under water on either side 
of it due to floods corresponding to its greatest flow or with a flood of frequency once in hundred years and 
includes active flood plain, regulatory zone (area of flood plain covered by floods between one in five-year

the one in twenty five-year return period and one in one hundred-year return period), ’’; 


(2) in paragraph 4, for clause (ix), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(ix) the bank of River Ganga or its tributaries and their flood plains shall be maintained to reduce pollution
