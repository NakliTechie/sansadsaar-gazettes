MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd July, 2023 


S.O. 2887(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. No: 90(E) Dated:0/0/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the additional land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH205 in the stretch of land from Km. 274.800 to Km. 338.030 (New Balaji Colony) in 
the district of Tirupati in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India” (English) Dated: 
25.0.2023 and “Prajasakthi” (Telugu) dated 29.0.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


No objections have been received from persons interested in the said lands within twenty-one days from the 
date of publication of 3A Notification in the official Gazette object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub section (l) of section 3C of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
