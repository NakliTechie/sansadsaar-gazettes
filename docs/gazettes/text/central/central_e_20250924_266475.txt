MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 23rd September, 2025 

S.O. 4344(E).— Whereas, by the notification by Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number SO No.370 (E), Dated. 21.01.2025 Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II Section 3 
Sub section (1), Gazette No.366, dated. 22.01.2025 (herein after referred to as said notification) and issued under sub 
section (1) of section 20 (A) of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of the Special Railway 
Project, “Doubling of Railway line between Dornakal and Bhadrachalam stations” in the District of Bhadradri 
Kothagudem in the state of Telangana. 

And whereas an extract of this notification was published in the daily newspapers ‘All in one, The New Indian 
Express, The Hindu, Mana Telangana & Vividha’ on 29" January, 2025 with the approval of Sunil Kumar Verma, 
Chief Engineer, Construction-IV, South Central Railway, Secunderabad, under sub -section (4); Section 20A of this 
Act. 

And Whereas, Sub Divisional Magistrate/Revenue Divisional Officer and Competent Authority (L.A), 
Kothagudem have not received any objections for Karukonda village of Laxmidevipalli Mandal of Bhadradri 
Kothagudem District of Telangana State. 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20(E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 

Now therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands mentioned in 
the Schedule have been annexed, to be obtained here for the purpose mentioned above; 

Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20(E) of this Act, the Central Government, hereby declares 
on publication of notification in the Official Gazette, that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government is from all encumbrances.
