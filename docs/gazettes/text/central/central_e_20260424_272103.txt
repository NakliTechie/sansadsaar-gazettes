BUREAU OF INDIAN STANDARDS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 10th April, 2026 


F. No. BS XI/04/03/2025-26.— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 39 read with section 5 of the 
Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Bureau, with previous approval of the Central Government, 
hereby makes the following regulations further to amend the Bureau of Indian Standards (Advisory Committee) 
Regulations, 2018, namely:— 


1. (1) These regulations may be called the Bureau of Indian Standards (Advisory Committee) Amendment 
Regulations, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.

following shall be substituted, namely:—
