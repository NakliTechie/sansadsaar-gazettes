Subject: Extension in Minimum Import Price (MIP) Condition on import of Soda Ash covered under Chapter 
28 of ITC (HS) 2022, Schedule-I (Import Policy)- reg 


5.0. 2952(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 and Section 5 of Foreign Trade (Development & 
Regulation) Act, 992, read with paragraph |.02 and 2.0] of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time to 
time, and in continuation of Notification No. 46/2024-25 dated 30.2.2024, the Central Government hereby extends the 
Minimum Import Price (MIP) condition on 03 HS Codes of Soda Ash (Disodium carbonate) 2836200, 28362020 and 
28362090 under Chapter 28 of the ITC (HS), 2022, Schedule -I (Import Policy) for a further period of six months i.e. 
from 0.07.2025 upto 3.2.2025. 


Effect of the Notification: 


Minimum Import Price (MIP) @ Rs. 20.08 per MT on import of Disodium Carbonate (Soda Ash) has 
been extended upto 3.2.2025.
