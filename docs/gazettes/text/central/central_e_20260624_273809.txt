New Delhi, the 24th June, 2026 


S.R.O. 10(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(2) of section 1 of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby appoints the 24" day of 
June, 2026 as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to serial number 16 and 
the entries relating thereto in the Schedule to the said Act, relating to the Reserve and Auxiliary Air Forces 
Act, 1952 (62 of 1952), shall come into force.
