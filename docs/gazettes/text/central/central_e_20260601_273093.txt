MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2026 
S.O. 2793(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 966(E) Dated:20/04/2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(॥) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of |956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/ two lane with paved shoulder/ four laning. etc., ), maintenance, 


management and operation of NH-353C in the stretch of land from Km. 67.000 to Km. 92,000 in the district of 
JAYASHANKAR BHUPALPALLY in the state of TELANGANA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India” (English) 
and “Namaste Telangana” (Telugu) daily newspapers, both dated 24/04/2026, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 


same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 


its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 


the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely 


in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
