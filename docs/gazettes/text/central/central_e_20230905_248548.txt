S.O. 3915(E).—Whereas, the Central Government, vide notification number S.O. 2444 (E), dated the 18" 
September, 2014 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part HI, section 3, sub-section (ii) has extended the 
provisions of the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978 (33 of 1978) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act) to the metropolitan area of Nagpur City in the State of Maharashtra.
