है : 


जबकि , एस्टर स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग नंबर 21 / A , डोडाबाईलाकेरे हेसरघाटा रोड़ , शिवकोटा येलहंका , जिला बैंगलोर 
- 560089 , कर्नाटक , कर्नाटक राज्य सरकार और कर्नाटक नर्सिंग काउंसिल द्वारा जीएनएम पाठ्यक्रम संचालित करने के 
लिए मान्यता प्राप्त है । 


वहीं , प्रमुख सचिव , स्वास्थ्य और परिवार कल्याण विभाग , कर्नाटक सरकार , कमरा नम्बर 105 , प्रथम तल , 
विकास सौदा , बंगलौर - 560001 , कर्नाटक को भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत 
मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए एक नोटिस सं . 18-15 / 14543- आईएनसी दिनांक 04/05/2022 जारी किया गया था । नोटिस 
दिनांक 04/05/2022 की एक प्रति एस्टर स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग नंबर 21 / A , डोडाबाईलाकेरे हेसरघाटा रोड़ , शिवकोटा 
येलहंका , जिला बैंगलोर - 560089 , कर्नाटक को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की 
उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-15 / 14543 - INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Aster School of Nursing situated at No. 21 / A , Doodabylakere Hessaraghata Road , Shivakota 
Yelahanka , Distt . Bangalore - 560089 , Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and Karnataka 
Nursing Council to conduct GNM course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-15 / 14543 - INC dated 04/05/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family Welfare Department , Room 
No. 105 , 01st Floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore – 560001 , Karnataka was issued . Copy of the notice dated 04/05/2022 
was also enclosed to Aster School of Nursing situated at Doodabylakere Hessaraghata Road , Shivakota 
Yelahanka , Distt . Bangalore - 560089 , Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Aster School of Nursing . Even the institution Aster School of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
15/11/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Aster School of Nursing situated at No. 
21 / A , Doodabylakere Hessaraghata Road , Shivakota Yelahanka , Distt . Bangalore - 560089 , Karnataka shall be 
entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject to the orders of State government of Karnataka .
