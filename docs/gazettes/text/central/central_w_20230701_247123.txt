08. This matter is taken up through Hybrid arrangement (video conferencing/physical mode). 


This interim application has been filed by the Collector and District Election Officer, Balasore to allow the 
District Election Officer to open the strong room forthwith, remove the EVMs and VVPATs for any future election or 
any other purpose and to allow transportation of EVMs to other States/manufacturing companies or to the warehouse 
of Election Commission of India as per the norms/guidelines of the Election Commission of India. 


Mr. Gopal Kumar Agarwal, learned Standing Counsel appearing for the Election Commission of India- 
petitioner submitted that the EVMs and VVPATs are not the subject matter of the election dispute and if those are 
released in favour of the petitioner, the election petition proceeding will not be hampered in any way.

Even though the interim application has been filed since 26.0.202 and copy of the same has been handed 
over to the election petitioner but no objection has been filed. 


Learned counsel for the election petitioner is present so also Mr. Pitambar Acharya, learned Senior Advocate 
appearing for the respondent No.|. 


Considering the submissions made by the learned counsel for the petitioner and after going through the 
averments taken in the interim application, the prayer is allowed. The District Election Officer is allowed to open the 
strong room, remove the EVMs and VVPATs for any future election or any other purpose and also allowed for 
transportation of EVMs to other States/manufacturing companies or to the warehouse of Election Commission of 
India as per the norms/guidelines of the Election Commission of India. 


Accordingly, l.A. is disposed of.

ELPET No. 2 of 209 


09 Heard Mr. Sidharth Prasad Das, learned counsel for the election petitioner, Mr. Pitambar Acharya, Learned 
Senior Advocate appearing for the respondent No.! and Mr. Gopal Agarwal, learned Standing Counsel appearing for 
the Election Commission of India. 


No other counsel, who have entered appearance for some of the respondents are present. 


The election petition was admitted on 07.02.2020 and summons were directed to be issued to the respondents 
by special messenger, cost of which was directed to be assessed and deposit be made by .02.2020 and notice was 
directed to be made returnable within a week. The matter was then listed on 3.03.2020 and on that day, learned 
counsel for the election petitioner submitted that he will file an affidavit treating the summons received by the 
daughter of the respondent No.|2 as sufficient service on respondent No.l2 since she is living jointly with the 
addressee as reported in the S.R. 


Summons on respondent No. 8 was returned unserved with postal endorsement 'unknown'. Since such 
summons issued to respondent No. 8 through special messenger was also returned unserved with report that the 
addressee was not present in the given address and nobody has signed as a witness in the summons, learned counsel 
for the election petitioner was directed to take fresh steps for issuance of summons on respondent No. 8 for which 
speed post requisites were directed to be filed along with the present correct address by 20.03.2020. 


Today, the matter has come on being mentioned by the learned Standing Counsel for the Election 
Commission of India to pass necessary orders in LA. No. 2 of 202l, however, the Registry has reported that service 
affidavit regarding sufficiency of service of summons on respondents Nos. l0 and [2 has not filed, A.D. of summon 
on respondent No. 4 returned with a signature not legible and summon on respondent No. 8 returned unserved with 
postal remark 'unknown’. No steps have been taken by the learned counsel for the election petitioner. No mention 
memo has been filed for listing this matter after !3.03.2020. In the meantime, more than three years have passed. 


Today, learned counsel for the election petitioner submitted that he has no instruction in the matter. 
In view of such submission, the election petition stands dismissed.
