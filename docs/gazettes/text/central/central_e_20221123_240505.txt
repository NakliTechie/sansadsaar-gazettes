G.S.R. 842(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A of the Government Savings 
Promotion Act, 873 (5 of 873), the Central Government hereby makes the following Scheme further to 
amend the Senior Citizens’ Savings Scheme, 209, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement.- 
(l) This Scheme may be called the Senior Citizens’ Savings (Amendment) Scheme, 2022. 
(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the Ist day of October, 2022. 

2. In the Senior Citizens’ Savings Scheme, 209, in paragraph 5,- 


(a) in sub-paragraph (lA), for the words and figures, “on or after the ।* day of April, 2020”, 
the following words, figures and brackets “between l“ April, 2020 to 30" September, 2022 
(both days inclusive)” shall be substituted; 


(b) after sub-paragraph (A), the following sub-paragraph shall be inserted, namely:- 


“(IB) The deposit made under this Scheme on or after the ॥7 day of October, 2022 shall 
bear interest at the rate of 7.6 per cent. per annum.”.

retrospective effect being given to this amendment Scheme.
