MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 27th April, 2026 


S.O. 237(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for the 
execution of the Special Railway Project i.e “Construction of Tenali Traction Sub-Station in the Vijayawada-Ongole 
section for up-gradation of the Electric Traction System from lx25kV to 2x25KV to meet the 3000MT loading target” 
at Pinapadu Village ,Tenali Mandal, Guntur District in the state of Andhra Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section () of Section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection, shall be made to the Competent Authority(LA), namely, Sub- Collector, Tenali, Guntur 
District in writing and shall be set out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority Sub- Collector, Tenali of 
Guntur District shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority for Land 
Acquisition(CALA) thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition under sub section (ii) of section 20(D) of the said 
act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority (CALA).
