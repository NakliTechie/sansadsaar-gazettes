INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F.No. 18-32/15110-INC).—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Nabadwip School and College of Nursing situated at 77/9, Sarkar Para Road, Ward 16, 
Nabadwip, Distt. Nadia 741302, West Bengal is recognized by State Government of West Bengal and West Bengal 
Nursing Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-32/15110-INC dated 03/10//2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 
of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to Chief Secretary (Health &FW) Department of Health & Family 
Welfare, Government of West Bengal, Swasthya Bhawan, 04" Floor, B Wing, GN — 29, Sector — V, Bidhan Nagar, 
Kolkata 700091, West Bengal was issued. Copy of the notice dated 03/10/2022 was also enclosed to Nabadwip 
School and College of Nursing situated at 77/9, Sarkar Para Road, Ward 16, Nabadwip, Distt. Nadia 741302, 
West Bengal. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Nabadwip School and College of Nursing. Even the institution Nabadwip School and College of Nursing has 
not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
01/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 
declares that with effect from 23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) Nabadwip School 
and College of Nursing situated at 77/9, Sarkar Para Road, Ward 16, Nabadwip, Distt. Nadia 741302, West 
Bengal shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of West Bengal subject to the orders of State government of 
West Bengal. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
