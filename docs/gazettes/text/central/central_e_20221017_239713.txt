MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
( Department of Telecommunications ) 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 13th October , 2022 

G.S.R. 781 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Indian Telegraph Act , 
1885 ( 13 of 1885 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian 
Telegraph Rules , 1951 , namely : 


1. Short title and commencement . These rules may be called the Indian Telegraph ( Amendment ) 
Rules , 2022 . 

2 . 


They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

In the Indian Telegraph Rules , 1951 ( hereafter referred to as the said rules ) , in rule 525 , in 
sub - rule ( 2 ) , in clause ( v ) , after item ( p ) , the following item shall be inserted , namely : 


" ( q ) for operation and maintenance , first line maintenance and utilisation of Bharat Net ( Except 
State led model under Phase - II ) through Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited , operational expenditure charges 
for said operation and maintenance , first line maintenance of Bharat Net Phase - I network shall be funded 
by Universal Service Obligation Fund for a period of two years . " 


3. In rule 526 of the said rules , for the words , brackets and letters " ( o ) and ( p ) of clause ( v ) " , the 
words , brackets and letters " ( o ) , ( p ) and ( q ) of clause ( v ) " shall be substituted .
