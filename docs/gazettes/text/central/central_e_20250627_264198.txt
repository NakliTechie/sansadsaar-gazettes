MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
(Department of Telecommunications) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th June, 2025 


G.S.R. 423(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section |0 read with clause (ca) of 
sub-section (2) of section 35 of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 997 (24 of 997), the Central 
Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (Salary, 
Allowances and other conditions of Service of the officers and employees) Rules, 2002, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement. — (|) These rules may be called the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (Salary, 
Allowances and other conditions of Service of the officers and employees) Amendment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (Salary, Allowances and other conditions of Service of the officers 
and employees) Rules, 2002 (hereinafter referred to as the principal rules), - in rule 4, in sub-rule (), in the proviso, for 
clause (d), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(d) the officers and employees of the Authority, while on official foreign tours, shall be entitled to allowances 
in accordance with the instructions of the Ministry of External Affairs issued vide order No. 
Q/FD/695/03/2000, dated the 25th September, 2023, as amended from time to time, as admissible to the Central 
government officers drawing equivalent pay;”. 


3. Schedule II of the principal rules shall be omitted.
