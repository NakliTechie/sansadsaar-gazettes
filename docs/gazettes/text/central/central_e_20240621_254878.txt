[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 2Ist June, 2024 

S.O. 2420(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(i) of section 204 of the Railways 
Act, 989(24 of 989) (hereinafter referred in as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, this land of Village- Pithampur Tehsil-Pithampur, District- Dhar, the brief description of 
which has given in the schedule annexed here to, is required for execution of Special Railway Project for Indore- 
Dahod new BG railway line and platform for goods transportation in Dhar district of Madhya Pradesh state. Declares 
its intention to acquire such land.in the State of Madhya Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land: 

Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for a foresaid purpose under 
sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act: 

Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Sub-Divisional Officer &Land 
Acquisition Officer, Tehsil Pithampur District-Dhar (M.P.), in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and 
may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections.
