New Delhi, the 24th June, 2026 


S.O. 3404(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport

maintenance, management and operation of NH-30A in the stretch of land from |९7.  [4.450 to Km.2.40 
(Urga-Pathalgaon Section) in the District of Raigarh in the State of Chhattisgarh.

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3C, considered 
and settled the same appropriately;

has submitted its report to the Central Government;

powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby
