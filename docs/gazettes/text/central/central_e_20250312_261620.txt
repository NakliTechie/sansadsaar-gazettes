NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the !2th March, 2025 


S.O. 72(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O. 4857(E) dated the 08 November 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 08 November 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under 
sub-section (/) of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (/ lof 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
new BG line project from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) in district & Tehsil jhabua 
Village Nawagaon in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers — Free press & “Dainik Bhaskar 
> dated 3 November, 2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Nawagaon village objections were received, considered and the objections have been resolved and 
competent Authority have passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the 
Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub- 
section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
