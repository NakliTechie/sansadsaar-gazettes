INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-32/5582-INC: —In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Pragati Nursing College And Pragati Nursing School situated at Vill - Kalaram, P.S.- 
Phansidewa, P.o.- Rangapani, Distt. Darjeeling 734434 West Bengal is recognized by State Government of West 
Bengal and West Bengal Nursing Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-32/I5582 (5489) dated 27/03/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 
of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the Additional Chief secretary (Health & FW), Government of West 
Bengal, Swasthya Bhawan, 4" floor, B- Wing, GN-29, Sector —V, Bidhan Nagar, Kolkata — 70009l, West Bengal was 
issued. Copy of the notice dated 27/03/2024 was also enclosed to Pragati Nursing College And Pragati Nursing 
School situated at Vill - Kalaram, P.S. - Phansidewa, P.o.- Rangapani, Distt. Darjeeling 734434 West Bengal. In 
the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Pragati Nursing College and Pragati Nursing School Even the institution, Pragati Nursing College and 
Pragati Nursing School has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
04/09/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Pragati Nursing College And Pragati 
Nursing School situated at Vill - Kalaram, P.S. - Phansidewa, P.o.- Rangapani, Distt. Darjeeling 734434 West 
Bengal shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of West Bengal. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
