MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th April, 2023 


99.0. 1969(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (21 of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer resources 
relating to the Immediate Payment Service Switch, Automated Teller Machine Switch, Unified Payments Interface 
Switch and Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunications Systems, being Critical Information 
Infrastructure of ICICI Bank, and the computer resources of its associated dependencies, to be protected systems for 
the purpose of the said Act and authorises the following personnel to access the protected systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the ICICI Bank authorised in writing by the ICICI Bank to access the 
protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have been 
authorised in writing by the ICICI Bank for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing by the 
ICICI Bank on case to case basis. 


2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
