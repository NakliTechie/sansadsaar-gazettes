MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd April, 2025 


S.O. 579(E).—Whereas, Awami Action Committee (AAC) has been declared as an unlawful association, 
vide notification number S.O. 5(E), dated the I" March, 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii). 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 5 read with sub-section (]) 
of section 4 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, !967 (37 of 967), the Central Government hereby 
constitutes the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Tribunal consisting of Justice Sachin Datta, Judge, High Court of 
Delhi, for the purpose of adjudicating whether or not there is sufficient cause for declaring the Awami Action 
Committee (AAC) as an unlawful association.
