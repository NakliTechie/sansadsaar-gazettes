असाधारण, भाग I, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा पंजाब राज्य के बरनाला जिले में लुधियाना-बर्ठिडा

आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

को "हिंदुस्तान टाइम्स" और "अजीत" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st December, 2023 
9.0. 5131(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 3125 Dated:13.07.2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation for 


Ludhiyana-Barnala-Bathinda Greendfield Highway of NH754AD on the stretch of land from Km 34 to Km 64 in the 
district of Barnala in the state of Punjab. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hindustan Times” and “Ajit” both 
dated 16.07.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the District Revenue Officer 
Barnala has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the District Revenue Officer Barnala and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
