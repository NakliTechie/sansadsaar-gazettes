NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 2nd November , 2022 

S.O. 5344 ( E ) .— In pursuance of Department of Personnel & Training's Order No.36 / 02 / 2022 
EO ( SM - I ) ( 2 ) dated 17th October 2022 , the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub - section ( 1 ) of Section7 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act , 2016 ( 11 of 2016 ) read with Rule 9 of 
Bureau of Indian Standards Rules , 2018 ; hereby extend the charge of present incumbent Shri Pramod 
Kumar Tiwari , IAS ( AM : 1991 ) , as Director General Bureau of Indian Standards ( in the rank and pay of 
Additional Secretary to the Government of India ) beyond 24th October 2022 for a period up to 
23rd May 2024 or until further orders , whichever is earlier .
