5.0. 3045(E).— Whereas, the Director (Printing), Directorate of Printing, New Delhi, an attached Office of

of Printing, in terms of the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 965; 


And whereas, the post of Director (Printing), Directorate of Printing had fallen vacant on the 5" February, 
202l, consequent upon the completion of deputation period of then incumbent and is likely to be vacant for some 
more time; 


And whereas, Shri Sanjay Singh, Deputy Secretary (PSP), Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs has been 
given additional charge of the Director (Printing) vide Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs’ Office Order 
No. A-22028/02/204-PSP-I dated 03/07/2023; 


And whereas, there is an urgent need to make an arrangement to provide for the performance of the functions 
of the appointing authority, disciplinary authority and appellate authority hitherto exercised by the Director (Printing) 
for smooth functioning in the Directorate of Printing in the absence of a regular incumbent to the post of Director 
(Printing), Directorate of Printing. 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-rule (2) of Rule 9 and sub-rule (2) of Rule I2 of the Central Civil 
Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 965, the President hereby authorizes Shri Sanjay Singh, Deputy 
Secretary (PSP) in the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to exercise powers of the appointing authority, 
disciplinary authority and appellate authority vested in the Director (Printing) in respect of Group ‘B’, Group ‘C’ and 
Group ‘D’ posts (upgraded to Group ‘C’ as Multi-Tasking Staff) in the Directorate of Printing till a regular 
appointment is made to the post of Director (Printing) in Directorate of Printing or until further orders, whichever is 
earlier.
