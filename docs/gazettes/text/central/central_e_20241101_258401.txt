S.O. 4778(E).—In pursuance of sub-rule (b) of rule 3 of the Passport (Entry into India) Rules, 
950, the Central Government hereby designates Darranga Land Check Post of Tamulpur District 
of Assam State as an authorized Immigration Check Post for entry into/exit from India with valid 
travel documents for all classes of passengers.
