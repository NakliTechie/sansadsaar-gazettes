INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 25th April, 2024 


F.No. 8-8/4768-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, |947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Khajuraho Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at Chhatarpur, Village Kadari Post Basari 
Chhatarpur, Distt. Chhatarpur 4705, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh 
and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM and B.Sc (N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. l8-8/l4768-INC dated 23/02/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24I, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 23/02/2023 was also enclosed to Whereas Khajuraho Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at 
Chhatarpur, Village Kadari Post Basari Chhatarpur, Distt. Chhatarpur 4705, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice 
issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of 
the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Khajuraho Institute of Nursing Sciences. Even the institution Khajuraho Institute of Nursing Sciences has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
28/09/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 29/l/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM _ and B.Sc (N) 
Khajuraho Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at Chhatarpur, Village Kadari Post Basari Chhatarpur, Distt. 
Chhatarpur 4705, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh 
subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh
