S.O. 2769(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the Prevention 
of Corruption Act, 1988 (Act 49 of 1988), the Central Government, with the concurrence of the 
Hor’ble Chief Justice of High Court of Karnataka, hereby designates the XLIX Additional City Civil and 
Sessions Judge, Bengaluru City as Special Judge for trial of offences specified in Sub-section (1) of Section 
3 of the said Act, in connection with NIA case RC-28/2023/NIA/DLI, being investigated by National 
Investigation Agency.
