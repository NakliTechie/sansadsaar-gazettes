सा.का.नि. 543(अ).--- भारतीय वायुयान अधिनियम, 2024 (2024 का 16) की धारा 34 की अपेक्षा के अनुसार 
भारत सरकार के नागर विमानन मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना, संख्या सा.का.नि. 466(अ), तारीख 11 जुलाई, 2025, द्वारा भारत 
के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग 1, खंड 3, उपखंड (i) में भारतीय वायुयान नियम, 2025 का प्रारूप प्रकाशित किया गया था, और 


उन सभी व्यक्तियों से, जिनके इससे प्रभावित होने की संभावना है, उक्त अधिसूचना अंतर्विष्टकरने वाले राजपत्र की प्रतियां जनता 
को उपलब्ध कराए जाने की तारीख से तीस दिन की अवधि का अवसान होने से पहले आपत्तियां और सुझाव आमंत्रित 
किए गए थे;

MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th August, 2025 


G.S.R. 543(E).— Whereas, the draft of the Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2025 was published, as required by 
section 34 of the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024 (16 of 2024), in the notification of the Government of India in 
the Ministry of Civil Aviation, vide number G.S.R. 466(E), dated the 11" July, 2025, in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-Section (i), inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be 
affected thereby, before the expiry of the period of thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Official Gazette 
containing the said notification were made available to the public; 


And whereas, the said draft rules were made available to the public on the 14% July, 2025; 


And whereas, considering the requests received from the stakeholders for extending the timeline of sending 
comments on the said draft rules and larger implications of the said rules, the Central Government hereby extends the 
timeline of sending objections or suggestions on the draft rules upto 19th August, 2025.
