THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA, NEW DELHI 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st December, 2023 
(Chartered Accountants) 


F. No. PR/139/2017/DD/187/2017/DC/1265/2020.—In terms of the provisions of Section 21B(3) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rule 18(17) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held CA. 
Vijay Kumar Chopra(Membership No. 138740), 24, Tribhovan Park Near Acher Bus Depot, Jawahar Chowk, 
AHMEDABAD(Gujarat) 380005, guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Item (7) of Part I 
of Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in terms of 
Rule 19(1) of the aforesaid Rules, ordered for removal of name of CA. Vijay Kumar Chopra(Membership No. 
138740) from the Register of Members for a period of 06(Six) months and also imposed a fine of Rs. 1,00,000/- 
(Rupees One Lakh) only. Aggrieved by Order of the Committee, the Respondent preferred an Appeal in terms of 
Section 22G of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and the Appellate Authority in appeal no. 09/ICAI/2023 vide its 
order dated 87 November, 2023 has upheld the impugned order of the Disciplinary Committee. In pursuance thereof 
and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified 
under Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 1988, that the name of said CA. Vijay Kumar 
Chopra(Membership No. 138740), shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 06(Six) months 
with effect from 11 December, 2023. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secy.
