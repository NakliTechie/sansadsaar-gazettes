MINISTRY OF PARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd July, 2026 


F. No. 8//2026-ME.— WHEREAS, in pursuance of the provisions of Section 2 of the Salary and 
Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, 977 (No. 33 of 977), the Chairman of the 
Council of States has recognized Shri Mallikarjun Kharge as the Leader of the Opposition in the Council of 
States with effect from 26" June, 2026. 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 9 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby notifies Shri Mallikarjun Kharge as the Leader of the Opposition in the Council of 
States with effect from 267 June, 2026 for the purpose of the said Act. 


NIKUNJA BIHARI DHAL, Secy.
