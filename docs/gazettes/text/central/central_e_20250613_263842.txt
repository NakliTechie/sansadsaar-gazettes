उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है), द्वारा उक्त अधिसूचना से उपाबध्द अनुसूची मे विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को, मध्यप्रदेश राज्य के धार

और पिथामपुर के मध्य से (204.76 किमी), स्पेशल रेलवे प्रोजेक्ट के निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए

अर्थात “राज एक्सप्रेस एवं फ्री प्रेस” में तारीख 06 दिसंबर, 2024 को प्रकाशित किया गया था। 


और, माण्डनकुआ गाँव में आक्षेप प्राप्त हुए और उन पर विचार किया गया और आक्षेप का निदान किया गया है तथा 
सक्षम अधिकारी द्वारा तदनुसार आदेश पारित कर दीये गये हैं।

[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the | lth June, 2025 


S.O. 2645(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O. 563(E) dated the 29 November, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 
3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 29 November, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub- 
section () of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 ( of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), 
the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
new BG line project from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) 204.76 km in 
Jhabua district in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers — “Free Press” & 
“Raj Express” dated 06 December, 2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Mandankua village no objections were received and competent Authority have passed the 
orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
