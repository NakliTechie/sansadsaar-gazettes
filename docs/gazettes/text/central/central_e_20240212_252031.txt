S.O. 628(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 3242 Dated:9/07/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NHI6 in 
the stretch of land to be acquired with or without structure falling within the stretch for construction of 
SAGARMALA LA (Slip Toll Plaza) of Chillakuru Mandal of Tirupati District for Construction of 2 lane to 4 lane 
road from Chillakuru cross road to East Kanupur from Km. 0.000 to Km. 23.77] including 6 lane flyover and 
approaches on NH-!6 and 6 laning of East Kanupur to port south Gate in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Praja Shakthi (Telugu)” and “The 
Hans India (English)” both dated 0-08-2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition has received objections filed under Section 3-C, 
considered and settled the same appropriately by CALA; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in the exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
