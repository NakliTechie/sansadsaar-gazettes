G.S.R. 144(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (b) and (c) of sub- section (2) of section 141 
of the Border Security Force Act, 1968 (47 of 1968) and in supersession of the Border Security Force, Engineering 
(Civil) Combatant (Group ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2013, except as respects things done or omitted to be done 
before such supersession, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of 
recruitment to the posts of Inspector (Works) and Sub-Inspector (Works) in the Border Security Force in the Ministry 
of Home Affairs, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Home Affairs, Border Security 
Force, Inspector (Works) and Sub-Inspector (Works), Combatant (Group ‘B’ Post Cadre), Recruitment Rules, 
2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix.- The number of posts, their classification and level

these rules. 


Method of recruitment, age-limit, educational qualifications etc.- The method of recruitment, age-limit, 
qualifications and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the

Medical standard for promotion.- Notwithstanding anything contained in these rules, only those persons who 
are in medical category SHAPE-I, shall be eligible for promotion to the posts specified in column (1) of the 
Schedule. 


Medical standard for direct recruits.- Only those persons who are medically fit and physically fit as specified 
by the Ministry of Home Affairs guidelines dated the 20" May, 2015 on recruitment of medical examination 
in the Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles, as per the scheme prescribed by the Central Government 
as amended from time to time shall be eligible for appointment to the posts specified in column (1) of the 
Schedule under the provisions of these rules. 


Physical Standard for direct recruits.- The minimum physical standard required for the candidates shall be 
the following, namely:- 


(1) Height : 


Men — 165 centimeters relaxable by five centimeters for hill tribes and adivasis including Mizos and 
Nagas. 


Women — 157 centimeters.

of age. 


Women - Not applicable. 

(3) Weight : 
Men - Proportionate to height and age as per medical standards. 
Women - According to height but not less than forty-six kilograms. 


Ineligibility of non-citizen of India.- No person who is not a citizen of India shall, except with the prior 
permission of the Central Government, reasons to be recorded in writing, be appointed or employed under 
these rule : 


Provided that nothing contained in these rules shall debar the appointment or employment of a subject 
of Nepal or Bhutan under these rules. 


Other conditions of service.- (1) The persons appointed to the Cadre shall be subject to the Border Security 
Force Act, 1968 (47 of 1968) and the Border Security Force Rules, 1969, in respect of other conditions of service 
not specially provided for in these rules. 


(2) The person appointed on deputation from other Department shall have to wear the uniform prescribed 
for the combatised post against which they are appointed and have to undergo basic training as prescribed by the 
Director General from time to time on appointment on deputation : Provided that this rule shall not apply to the 
post of Sub- Inspector (Works). 


Liability of service. - Any person appointed under these rules shall be liable to serve anywhere in India or abroad. 


Superannuation.- (1) The person appointed under these rules shall retire from service on the afternoon of the last 
day of the month in which he or she attains the age of sixty years or as prescribed by the Central Government 
from time to time: 


Provided that the person whose date of birth is the first day of a month shall retire from service on the 
afternoon of the last day of the preceding month on attaining the age of sixty years. 


(2) No person shall be granted extension in service beyond the age of retirement of sixty years.
