THE UNION TERRITORY OF LADAKH RESERVATION 
(AMENDMENT) REGULATION, 2025 
No. | OF 2025 
Promulgated by the President in the Seventy-sixth Year of the Republic of India. 


A Regulation further to amend the Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Act, 2004 
in its application to the Union territory of Ladakh. 


In exercise of the powers conferred by article 240 of the Constitution read with 
34 of 209. sub-section (2) of section 58 of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 209, 
the President is pleased to promulgate the following Regulation made by her:— 


l. (J) This Regulation may be called the Union territory of Ladakh 
Reservation (Amendment) Regulation, 2025. 


(2) It extends to the whole of the Union territory of Ladakh. 


(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by 
notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

2. In the Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Act, 2004 (hereinafter referred to 
as the principal Act), in section 3, in sub-section (/), for the first proviso, the 
following proviso shall be substituted, namely:— 


“Provided that the total percentage of reservation shall in no case exceed 
85%, excluding reservation for Economically Weaker Sections:”. 


3. In the principal Act, in section 5,— 
(a) in sub-section (/), the second proviso shall be omitted; 


(6) after sub-section (4), the following sub-section shall be inserted, 
namely:— 


“(5) Nothing contained in this section shall apply to Economically 
Weaker Sections and filling up of vacancies for such Sections shall be 
in such manner as may be prescribed.”. 


4. In the principal Act, in section 6, for the first proviso, the following proviso 
shall be substituted, namely:— 


“Provided that the total percentage of reservation shall in no case exceed 
85% of the available vacancies, excluding reservation for Economically 
Weaker Sections:”. 


5. In the principal Act, in section 9, in sub-section (/), for the first proviso, the 
following proviso shall be substituted, namely: — 


“Provided that the total percentage of reservation shall in no case exceed 
85%, excluding reservation for Economically Weaker Sections:”’.
