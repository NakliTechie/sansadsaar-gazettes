MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2025 


S.O. 3631(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 2012 (37 of 2012), 
the Central Government hereby nominates Dr. Ashok Kumar Mahapatra to be member of Institute Body of All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhopal in place of Dr. Sunil Malik and for that purpose makes the following amendment 
in the notification of Government of India, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Number Z-28016/177/2019- 
PMSSY.IV dated the 08" May, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii) vide S.O. 
2163(E) dated the 11" May, 2023: 


In the said notification, against serial number 9 and the entry relating thereto, the following to be substituted, 
namely:- 


9. Dr. Ashok Kumar Mahapatra 


The term of Dr. Ashok Kumar Mahapatra as member of the Institute Body of AIMS Bhopal shall be governed by 
the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956. 


[F.No. Z-28016/177/2019-PMSS Y.IV(8037928)] 
ANKITA MISHRA BUNDELA, Jt. Secy.
