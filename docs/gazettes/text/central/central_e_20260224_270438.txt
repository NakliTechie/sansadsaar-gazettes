MINISTRY OF CHEMICALS AND FERTILIZERS 
(Department of Pharmaceuticals) 
(ACADEMIA AND RESEARCH DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th February, 2026 


G.S.R. 4I(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 30E of the National Institutes of 
Pharmaceutical Education and Research Act, 998 (3 of 998), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules to amend the Council (National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research) Rules, 2022, namely: — 


. Short title and commencement.—() These rules may be called the Council (National Institute of Pharmaceutical 
Education and Research) Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Council (National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research) Rules, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as 
“the said rules’), in rule 7,— 


(i) for sub-rule (4), the following sub-rules shall be substituted, namely:— 


“(4) In case of an emergent situation warranting immediate action by the Council, the Chairperson may, 
for reasons to be recorded in writing, take such action as may be deemed necessary. 


[भाग ave 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(4A) Any such action taken under sub-rule (4) shall be placed, as early as possible, in the next meeting 
of the Council for its ratification and the decision of the Council shall prevail.”; 


(ii) for sub-rule (5), the following sub-rules shall be substituted, namely:— 


“(5) When any item of business or issue requires decision of the Council, if the Chairperson so directs, 
may be circulated to the members at their official email addresses as a draft resolution, which shall be 
deemed to have been passed by the Council, if it is approved by majority of the members. 


(5A) Any resolution so deemed to have been passed under sub-rule (5) shall be placed at the next 
meeting of the Council for ratification.”. 


3. In the said rules, in rule 8, for the word and figure “rule 47, the word and figure “rule 5” shall be substituted. 


4. In the said rules, for the words “National Institute” wherever they occur, the words “National Institutes” shall be 
substituted.
