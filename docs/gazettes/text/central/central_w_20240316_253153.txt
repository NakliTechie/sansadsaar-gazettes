O.N. 57.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala, 202l for 24-Kollam 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/KL- 
LA/202, dated 2"° March, 202]; and 

WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at 
an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 

WHEREAS, the result of the election for 24-Kollam Assembly Constituency of Kerala, 202 was declared 
by the Returning Officer on 02™ May, 202] and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election Expenses was 
0i* June, 202; and 

WHEREAS, the Commission having regard to the then prevailing pandemic situation of Covid-l9, decided 
to condone any incidence of late submission of account by candidate till 3I* July, 202; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report of the DEO, Kollam, under sub-rule (l) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96l, received vide his letter no. EL-306232/202l, dated 0.0.2022 stating that Remanan, a 
contesting candidate from 24-Kollam Assembly Constituency of General Election to Legislative Assembly of 
Kerala, 202 has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses, as required under law; and 

WHEREAS, on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Kollam a Show-Cause notice No. 
76/KL-LA/202/CEMS-II, dated 20" January, 2023 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, [96] by the Election Commission of India to Remanan, for not lodging the account of Election 
Expenses; and 

WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, !96!, Remanan was directed to submit a representation in writing to the Commission explaining the 
reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his election expenses with the District Election 
Officer, Kollam District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Kollam District, in his report no. EL-306232/202l; dated 
7.02.2023 has reported that the said Show Cause Notice was served to Remanan on 08.02.2023; and 

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Kollam in his Supplementary Report dated 2.05.2023 received 
through CEO, Kerala vide letter No. EL/2/202-ELEC; dated 03.].2023 on account of the candidate has reported 
that Ramanan has not submitted any representation or statement of account of election expenses. Further, after 
receipt of the said notice, Remanan has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of 
India, for failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95 provides that:- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 
(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act, and 
(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 
the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and 
any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 
WHEREAS, on basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Remanan has failed to 
lodge the account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95], the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Remanan, resident of — Ratheesh Bhavanam, Pavithram Nagar, 
Kallumthazham, Kollam and a contesting candidate in 24- Kollam Assembly Constituency of Kerala, 202, to be 
disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House of the Parliament or the Legislative 
Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three years from the date of this 
order.
