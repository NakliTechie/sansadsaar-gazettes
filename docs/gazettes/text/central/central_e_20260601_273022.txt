ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist June, 2026 

O.N. 23(E).—WHEREAS, the seat of Smt. Sunetra Ajit Pawar, in the Council of States, elected by the 
elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Maharashtra, has become vacant on 06.05.2026 by the reason of her 
resignation, before the expiration of her term of office which was up to and including 04.07.2028; and 

WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 147, sub-section (1) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1951 (43 of 1951), the Election Commission hereby- 
(A) calls upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Maharashtra to elect, before 20" June, 2026 

and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder, a person 


for the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the Council of States; 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 08" June, 2026 (Monday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 09" June, 2026 (Tuesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 


(c) the 117 June, 2026 (Thursday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 18" June, 2026 (Thursday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 20" June, 2026 (Saturday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election.
