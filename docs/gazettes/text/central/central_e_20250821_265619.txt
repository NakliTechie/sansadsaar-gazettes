The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 
2lst August, 2025 and is hereby published for general information:— 


THE INDIAN INSTITUTES OF MANAGEMENT 
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025 


No. 3] oF 2025 
[2lst August, 2025.] 


An Act further to amend the Indian Institutes of Management Act, 207. 

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-sixth Year of the Republic of India 
as follows:— 

l. (J) This Act may be called the Indian Institutes of Management 
(Amendment) Act, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by 
notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

2. In section 4 of the Indian Institutes of Management Act, 207 (hereinafter 
referred to as the principal Act),— 


(i) for sub-section (/), the following sub-section shall be substituted, 
namely:— 


“(Z) Every Institute shall be a body corporate by the same name as 
mentioned in column (5) of the Schedule.”; 


(ii) after sub-section (JA), the following sub-section shall be inserted, 
namely:— 


“(/B) On and from the date of commencement of the Indian 
Institutes of Management (Amendment) Act, 2025, all the provisions of 
this Act shall apply to the Indian Institute of Management, Guwahati.”. 


3. In section 39 of the principal Act, in sub-section (/), after clause (f), the 
following clause shall be inserted, namely:— 


“(g) until the first Board of the Indian Institute of Management, 
Guwahati is constituted under this Act, all the powers and functions which 
may, by or under the provisions of this Act, be exercised or performed by or 
on behalf of such Board on the commencement of the Indian Institutes of 
Management (Amendment) Act, 2025, shall be exercised and performed by 
such person or persons as the Central Government may direct in this behalf.”. 


4. In the Schedule to the principal Act, after Sl. No. 2 and the entries relating 
thereto, the following SI. No. and entries shall be inserted, namely:—
