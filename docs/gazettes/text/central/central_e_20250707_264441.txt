S.O. 3028(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Proposed Road Over Bridge in lieu of existing Level Crossing Gate No.6 at

the State of Telangana in the District of Hanumkonda for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 


effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
