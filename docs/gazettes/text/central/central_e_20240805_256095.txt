NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th August, 2024 


(The Gazette notification in this record published on 10.06.2024 vide S.O. 2225(E) may be treated as cancelled 
in view of some clerical mistakes. This Notification may be read in lieu of above-mentioned cancelled 
Notification). 


S.O. 3127(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
(Amendment) Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of 'Chord Line Connecting MFJ 
(NER)-JNU-MGWN Bypassing JNU (Jaunpur Jn.) 1.5 Km" and "Chord Line Connecting DJD (NER)-SHG- 
BWI Bypassing SHG (Shahganj Jn.) 5.7 Km" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land, in Jaunpur 
District in the State of Uttar Pradesh, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the 
date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
