फा. सं. भा.बी.वि.वि.प्रा /लशाधारण बीमा/प्रशुल्‍ल्क/3/207/2024--यह अधिसूचना बीमा अधिनियम, 7938 की 
धारा 64यूएलए की उप-धारा (7) के अनुसार जारी की जाती है, जो अधिनियम की छोड़ी हुई धारा 64यूसी, के अंतर्गत 
पहले की प्रशुल्क सलाहकार समिति द्वारा अधिसूचित सभी प्रशुल्कों की अधिसूचना को निरस्त करने के लिए व्यवस्था करती 
है, जिनका प्रचलन में होना जारी है। 
4.यह ध्यान देने योग्य है कि वर्ष 2006 में, प्रशुल्क सलाहकार समिति द्वारा प्रशुल्कों को वापस लेने के अनुसरण में, भारतीय 
बीमा विनियामक और विकास प्राधिकरण (“प्राधिकरण”) ने आईआरडीए अधिनियम, 4999 की धारा 44 की उप-धारा 
(2) के खंड () के अंतर्गत अपने में निहित शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए अधिसूचना संदर्भ. 034/आईआरडीए/डी- 
टैरिफ़/दिसंबर-06 दिनांक 4 दिसंबर 2006 जारी की कि संबंधित प्रशुल्कों के अंतर्गत अग्नि, इंजीनियरिंग, मोटर, कर्मकार 
प्रतिकर और बीमा के अन्य वर्ग जैसे व्यवसाय के कुछ वर्गों के लिए लागू सामान्य विनियमों (प्रीमियमों/प्रीमियम रेटिंग से 


संबंधित को छोड़कर अन्य), शर्तों, निबंधनों, खंडों, वारंटियों, पालिसी और पृष्ठांकन वाक्य-रचना का अनुसरण अगले 
आदेशों तक जारी रहेगा।

INSURANCE REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
Hyderabad, the 20th March, 2024 
F. No.: IRDAI//Gen Insurance/Tariff/3/207/2024— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub section () 
of section 64 ULA of the Insurance Act, 938, which provides for de-notification of all tariffs notified by the erstwhile 


Tariff Advisory Committee under the omitted section 64UC of the Act, and which continue to be in force. 


[भाग WI—ave 4] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


. It may be noted that in the year 2006, pursuant to withdrawal of tariffs by the Tariff Advisory Committee, the 
Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (“Authority”) in exercise of the powers vested in it under 
clause (i) of sub section (2) of section 4 of the IRDA Act, 999, issued notification Ref.034/IRDA/De-Tariff/Dec-06 
dated 4th December, 2006 that the general regulations (other than those relating to premiums/ premium rating), terms, 
conditions, clauses, warranties, policy and endorsement wordings applicable to certain classes of business such as 
Fire, Engineering, Motor, Workmen’s Compensation and other classes of insurances under the relevant Tariffs shall 


continue to be followed until further orders. 


2. Now, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (l) of Section 64 ULA of the Insurance Act, 938, the 


Authority hereby notifies that all the following prevailing tariffs : 


a) Fire Insurance Tariffs (other than All India Fire Tariff, 200l which was already de-tariffed vide 
notification dated 28" December, 2020), namely 
i. All India Fire Tariff 
ii. Industrial All Risks Tariff 
iii. Consequential Loss (Fire) Tariff 
iv. Petro-chemical Tariff 
v. List of Hazardous Goods 
b) Motor, namely All India Motor Tariff 


c) Engineering Insurance Tariffs, namely; 


i. Contractors All Risk Insurance 
ii. Contractors Plant and Machinery Insurance 
iii. Machinery Breakdown Insurance 
iv. Electronic Equipment Insurance 
Vv. Civil Engineering Completed Risks Insurance 
Vi. Erection All Risk/Storage Cum Erection Insurance 


Vil. Loss of profit (MB & BLOP) Insurance 
Vill. Boiler and Pressure Vessels Insurance 
ix. Deterioration of Stocks-(potato) Insurance 
d) Miscellaneous, namely Workmen’s Compensation Insurance Tariffs. 
e) Marine, namely Tea Tariff 


stand entirely de-notified and no longer in force effective 0.04.2024. 


3. It is however clarified that no insurer shall at any time withdraw or discourage the use of or decline to offer to any 


customer any of the tariff products which have been in existence prior to this notification. 


4. It is hereby further notified that effective 0.04.2024, the coverage of risks coming within the scope of the de- 
notified tariffs stated in paragraph 2 above shall be subject to the IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024 and 


Master Circular (Guidelines) on products and procedures in general insurance business, as specified by the Authority.
