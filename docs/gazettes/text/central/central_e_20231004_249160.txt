MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(RAILWAY BOARD) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th September, 2023 


9.0. 4337(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.2562 (E), dated 77 June, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 9" June, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor 
in the District of Gaya in the State of Bihar; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification for inviting objections from public thereby was 
published in daily newspapers namely “Hindustan” and “Prabhat Khabar”, dated 20" June, 2023 under sub- 
section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 

AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (.), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified 
in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
