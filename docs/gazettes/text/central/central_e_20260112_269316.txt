MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |2th January, 2026 


S.O. 64(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number 5.0. 490(E), dated the 23 July, 2025 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part Il, Section 
3, Sub — section (ii), dated the 23 July, 2025 and issued under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 
(24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of the Special Railway 
Project, namely, 4™ LINE BETWEEN BAREJADI - NANDEJ (GERATPUR) - SANAND (38.20 KM) IN THE DISTRICT OF 
AHMEDABAD in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local news papers, 
namely, The Indian Express (English), dated the 26 July, 2025, Sandesh (Gujarati) dated the 26 July, 2025 and 
Rajasthan Patrika (Hindi) dated the 26 July, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has not received any objection under section 20D from any persons; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
