New Delhi, the 2nd June, 2023 


S.O. 2559(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 5057(E), dated the 2] * October, 2022, published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 28" October, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor in the district of Gaya in the State of Bihar; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers Hindustan and 
Prabhat Khabar, dated the 0!*' November, 2022 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received and the same have been considered and accordingly orders 
have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (L) of section 20E of the said Act the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the Railways Act, 989, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
