MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the l0th June, 2026 


S.O. 300I(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 955 (0 
of 955), the Central Government hereby makes the following order further to amend the Natural Gas and Petroleum 
Products Distribution (Through Laying, Building, Operation and Expansion of Pipelines and Other Facilities) Order, 
2026, namely:— 


l. (4) This Order may be called the Natural Gas and Petroleum Products Distribution (Through Laying, Building, 
Operation and Expansion of Pipelines and Other Facilities) Second Amendment Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Inthe said order, under clause 7, in sub-clause (b), the following shall be inserted: 


“provided that such communication shall not be required where the CGD entity has maintained records that it has 
provided such notice in any of the following manner: (i) notice under sub-clause (4) of clause 5, (ii) written 
communication has already been issued to the residents welfare association, apartment owners association, 
cooperative housing society, facility management agency or similar organizations in the housing area, by whatever 
name called, with a request to publicize the same to all households, (iii) a communication has been delivered by 
hand to the address, (iv) notice in the form of posters, flyers, pamphlets or any other form of handout have been 
delivered to the relevant address as well as distributed within the housing area including the housing society, housing 
area where the relevant address of a household is situated, or (v) notice has already been issued by text message or 
telephonically or by recorded voice message, that supply of LPG to the relevant address shall be stopped.”
