objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further inquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections.

land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the
