MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th March, 2023 


S.O. 54(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section | of the 
Employees’ State Insurance Act, 948 (34 of 948), the Central Government hereby appoints the 
Ist day of April, 2023, as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, namely,— 


(i) sections 38 to 43 and sections 45A to 45H of Chapter IV; 
(ii) sections 46 to 73 of Chapter V; and 
(iii) sections 74, 75, sub-sections (2) to (4) of section 76, 80, 82 and 83 of Chapter VI, 


shall come into force in all the areas of Valsad district, in addition to the already notified areas of the 
said district, in the State of Gujarat.
