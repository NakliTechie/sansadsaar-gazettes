MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th January, 2026 


S.O. 295(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for the 
Development, Maintenance, Management, and Operation of Greenfield High-Speed Corridor (66.80 km, NH-6) from 
Mawlyngkhung (near Shillong) in Meghalaya to Panchgram (near Silchar) in Assam, in the stretch of land from 
design km 35+250 to Km 66+405 in the district of West Jaintia in the State of Meghalaya, hereby declares its 
intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, The Deputy 
Commissioner(Revenue), West Jaintia Hills District, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
