MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 25th July, 2024 


9.0. 3065(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), vide number S.O.145 (E), dated the 87 January, 2024, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 117 January, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, 
Western Dedicated Freight Corridor in the District of Palghar in the State of Maharashtra; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“The Times of India” and “Punya Nagri”, dated the 10" February, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
