S.O. 5029(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH-5l6W for 
construction of Slip Toll Plaza & Missing Sy.Nos. at East Kanupur, Tikkavaram,Chintavaram, 
Udathavaripalem,Kalavakonda & Thamminapatnam villages of Chillakur Mandal of Tirupati District for construction 
of 4-lane road from Chillakur cross road to Trupu Kanupur from Km. 0.000 to Km. 23.770 and 6-lane road fromTrupu 
Kanupur to Port South Gate from Km. 23.770 to Km. 35.05 in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH, hereby declares its 
intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, CALA & Sub-Collector, Gudur in 
writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making
