sub-section (3) of section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 20l6 (I of 206), the Central Government, after

interest, hereby makes the following order further to amend the Polyester Staple Fibres (Quality Control) Order, 
2022, namely: - 


Short title and commencement: (l) This order may be called the Polyester Staple Fibres (Quality Control) 
Amendment Order, 2023. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
