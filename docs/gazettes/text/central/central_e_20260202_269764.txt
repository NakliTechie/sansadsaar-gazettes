S. 0. 465(E).—_ WHEREAS, sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (35 
of 2020) (hereinafter referred to as “the said Code”) empowers the Central Government to issue a notification 
in the Official Gazette appointing the date or different dates from which the different provisions of the said 
Code shall come into force; 


AND WHEREAS, section 104 of the said Code repeals— 
(a) the Trade Unions Act, 1926 (16 of 1926); 
(b) the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 (20 of 1946); and 
(c) the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947), 


and empowers the Central Government to issue a notification along with the notification under sub-section 
(3) of section 1 of the Code, appointing the date or dates as stated therein; 


AND WHEREAS, the Central Government in the Ministry of Labour and Employment, vide 
notification number 5.0. 5320 (E), dated 21" November, 2025 [published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii)], exercised its powers under sub-section (3) of section 1 of 
the said Code, appointing the 217 day of November, 2025 as the date on which the provisions of the said 
Code shall come into force; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in partial modification of the said notification dated 217 November, 2025, 
and in exercise of the powers under section 104 read with sub-section (3) of section 1 of the said Code, the 
Central Government hereby specifies that the provisions of— 


(a) the Trade Unions Act, 1926 (16 of 1926); 
(b) the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 (20 of 1946); and 
(c) the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947), 


stood repealed with effect from the date appointed in the notification dated 217 November, 2025 issued under 
sub-section (3) of section 1 of the said Code.
