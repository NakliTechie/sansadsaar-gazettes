MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 
(Department of Personnel and Training) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th September, 2023 


G.S.R. 659(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) read with clause (d) of sub- 
section(2) of section 27 of the Right to Information Act, 2005 (22 of 2005) , the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules to amend the Central Information Commission, Group ‘A’ posts (Under Secretary, Senior 
Principal Private Secretary and Principal Private Secretary) Recruitment Rules, 20l6, namely:- 


(.) These rules may be called the Central Information Commission, Group ‘A’ posts (Under Secretary, Senior 
Principal Private Secretary and Principal Private Secretary) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) These rules shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. In the Schedule to 
the Central Information Commission, Group ‘A’ posts (Under Secretary, Senior Principal Private Secretary 
and Principal Private Secretary) Recruitment Rules, 20l6, against serial number | relating to the post of 
Under Secretary in column(l), under the sub-heading ‘Promotion’, after Note 2, the following Note shall 
be inserted, namely:- 


“Note 3.-The eligibility service shall continue to be six years for persons holding the feeder posts on regular 
basis.”
