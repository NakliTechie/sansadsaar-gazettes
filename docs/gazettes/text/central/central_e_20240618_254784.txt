S.O. 2355(E).—In pursuance of sub-rule (b) of rule 3 of the Passport (Entry into India) Rules, 950, the 
Central Government hereby designates Kollam Seaport of Kerala State as an authorized Immigration Check Post for 
entry into/exit from India with valid travel documents for all classes of passengers.

S.O. 2356(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (2) of Clause 2 of the Foreigners Order
