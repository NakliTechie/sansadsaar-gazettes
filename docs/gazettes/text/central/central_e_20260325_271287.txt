New Delhi, the 25th March, 2026 


5.0. 593(E)— Whereas the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) was established vide the 
Resolution of the Government of India in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers No. 33/7/97-PL.I dated 29th August, 
997, inter-alia, to fix prices and notify the changes therein, if any, of bulk drugs and formulations, monitor the prices 
of non-scheduled drugs and formulations and oversee the implementation of the provisions of the Drugs (Price Control) 
Order (DPCO). 


2. And whereas the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers vide S.0.394 (E) dated the 30th May, 203, in exercise of 
the powers conferred by Section 3 and 5 of Essential Commodities Act, 955 has delegated the powers in respect of 
specified paras of the DPCO, 203, including para 32 of the said order to be exercised by the NPPA on behalf of the 
Central Government. 


3. And whereas an application was received from M/s Wockhardt Limited seeking exemption from the provisions of 
DPCO, 20I3 under para 326) of the said order in respect of the formulation “Nafithromycin Tablets — Immediate 
release film-coated tablets — each film-coated tablet containing Nafithromycin 400 mg”, which was duly approved 
by the Office of Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (India) under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 940 and 
Rules thereunder. Further, the Patent Office, Government of India has granted Patent to M/s Wockhardt Limited for 
an invention titled “Pharmaceutical compositions” under the provisions of the Patents Act, 970 (Patent No. 4539 
and Date of Grant: 23.2.2022).

4. And whereas the NPPA in its 44" meeting dated 24.03.2026 noted that M/s Wockhardt Limited meets the 
requirement of para 320) of DPCO, 203 and the formulation is covered under patent granted. Accordingly, the 
Authority decided that exemption may be granted to M/s Wockhardt Limited under para 32(i) of DPCO, 203 for 
their formulation “Nafithromycin Tablets — Immediate release film-coated tablets — each film-coated tablet 
containing Nafithromycin 400 mg”. 


5. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers delegated under para 32 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 20I3 vide 
S.0.394(E) dated 30th May, 203 issued by the Government of India in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, M/s 
Wockhardt Limited is exempted from the provisions of DPCO, 20l3 under para 326) of the said order in respect of 
the formulation “Nafithromycin Tablets — Immediate release film-coated tablets — each film-coated tablet 
containing Nafithromycin 400 mg” for a period of five years from the date of commencement of its commercial 
marketing by the manufacturer in the country or expiry of the Indian patent, whichever may be earlier. 


6. M/s Wockhardt Limited shall inform NPPA, the date of commercial marketing of the formulation “Nafithromycin 
Tablets — Immediate release film-coated tablets — each film-coated tablet containing Nafithromycin 400 mg” in 
the country and the Price to Retailer (PTR) and Maximum Retail Price fixed by the company in respect of the said 
formulation by issuing a price list in Form V under DPCO, 203. 


7. M/s Wockhardt Limited, after the expiry of period of exemption granted under para 32(i) of DPCO, 203, shall 
follow the notified ceiling price or seek retail price approval three months before the expiry of the exemption, if 
applicable, for the formulation “Nafithromycin Tablets — Immediate release film-coated tablets — each film-coated 
tablet containing Nafithromycin 400 mg”.
