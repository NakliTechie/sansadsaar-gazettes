INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the l9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-8/I5432-INC: — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Aadhar College of Nursing situated at Banswara Road Sailana, Sailam, Distt. Ratlam 457550 
Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration 
Council to conduct GNM, B.Sc(N), PB.B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-8/I5432-INC (52736) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh Room No. 24l, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Distt. 
Bhopal 462004 Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Aadhar 
College of Nursing situated at Banswara Road Sailana, Sailam, Distt. Ratlam 457550 Madhya Pradesh. In the 
notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Aadhar College of Nursing Even the institution Aadhar College of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/03/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies Le., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM, B.Sc.(N), PB.B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM, B.Sc(N), PB.B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) from Aadhar 
College of Nursing situated at Banswara Road Sailana, Sailam, Distt. Ratlam 457550 Madhya Pradesh shall be 
entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
