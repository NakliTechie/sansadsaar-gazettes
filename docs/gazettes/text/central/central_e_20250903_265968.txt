BRIC SECRETARIAT RECRUITMENT RULES- 2025 


l. SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT 
These Rules may be called “BRIC Secretariat Recruitment Rules-2025”. 


They shall come into force from the date of notification by the BRIC with the approval of the Government of 
India. 


2. OBJECTIVES 
i. To meet manpower requirements of the BRIC Secretariat by recruiting competent employees in terms of 
requisite capabilities, skills, qualifications, aptitude, merit and suitability with a view to fulfil the 
BRIC’s objectives. 
ii. To attract, select and retain the best talent available keeping in view the changing needs of the BRIC 
iii. To establish an objective and reliable system of selection. 
iv. To integrate growth opportunities of the employees with fulfilment of the BRIC’s objectives. 
v. To provide for a system, which is fair, objective and transparent in matters of recruitment of employees. 
vi. To ensure compliance with the relevant Government policies and directives on recruitment. 


3. DEFINITIONS & ABBREVIATIONS 
In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall mean as under 
i. Age: Age means the legal age as mentioned in the birth certificate or matriculation/Class-X certificate 
or equivalent to matriculation/Class-X certificate of the candidate as specified in these rules. 

ii. Annexures: shall mean the annexures attached to the Schedules of these rules. 

iii. Approving Authority: shall be as specified at Clause 9 of these Rules. 

iv. Appointing Authority: shall be as specified at Clause 9 of these rules and shall be that authority who is 
holding charge in regular capacity under these rules. 

v. BRIC: shall mean the ‘Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council’, a registered Society under the 
Society’s Registration Act l860 set up by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Ministry of Science 
and Technology, Government of India. 

vi. Bye Laws: Shall mean the BRIC Bye Laws framed and amended from time to time having the financial 
concurrence of the Integrated Finance Division (IFD) of DBT and approval of the Minister-in charge of 
the Department of Biotechnology, Ministry of Science and Technology. 

vii. Candidate: means the person/member of staff who is eligible for consideration of any of the positions 
specified in the Schedules of these rules. 

viii. Competent Authority: Minister- in charge of Department of Biotechnology, Ministry of Science and 
Technology or any such authority to whom such power is so delegated.

months, unless stayed by orders of any Court or decided otherwise by the Government. Efforts shall be 
made to complete the exercise in the minimum period. 

ix. In case of shortage of staff, due approval of Department of Expenditure would be obtained for creation 
of additional posts.

0. PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION 

i. A candidate on first appointment by direct recruitment will be on probation for a period of two years 
from the date he/she assumes charge of the post. 

ii. Notwithstanding anything contained in the Rules, the probation period of a staff member may be 
terminated or extended for a period not exceeding one year with the approval of the Appointing 
Authority. 

iii. If the probation is terminated, at the end of the normal probationary period or at the end of the extended 
period: 

a) The services of the probationer, if he was directly recruited, will be terminated forth with, and 
b) If the probationer was an employee of the BRIC, he/she will be reverted back to his earlier post/ 
grade subject to his/her lien being maintained as per the rules. 

iv. There shall, in each case of satisfactory completion of probation, be a written order to that effect 
confirming successful completion of probation period. The order shall specify the date on which the 
probation is deemed to have been satisfactorily completed. In the absence of such an order, the 
probation period will not be deemed to have been confirmed; meaning thereby that confirmation 
following the probation period is not automatic but shall be done by a positive act with due process after 
the Confirmation Committee formed with the approval of the Appointing Authority recommends the 
same. In any case, such a committee shall always be framed within 30 days of completion of probation 
period or extended period of probation. The committee shall make its recommendations within 04 
weeks of its constitution.

PERIOD OF DEPUTATION 
i. The period of deputation shall be subject to a minimum of three years, which can be extended up to a 
maximum period as per the extant orders of DoPT, only after the written consent of the incumbent and 
lending organization. The terms and conditions for tenure of deputation shall be as applicable in terms 
of extant Govt. of India instructions issued from time to time. 

ii. During the period of deputation, the candidate’s term shall be regulated by the Conduct Rules of the 
borrowing organisation i.e. BRIC. 

iii. The borrowing organization shall be responsible for making any such contribution as mandated by the 
lending organization. 

iv. The lien of the candidate appointed on deputation shall be retained in the lending organization, till the 
period of deputation. On the expiry of the period of deputation the candidate shall be repatriated back 
to his/her parent department immediately. 

v. Posts filled by Deputation shall be exempt from the purview of Reservation Rules. 


LIABILITY OF OFFICERS TO SERVE IN INDIA OR ABROAD 

All the employees who are appointed under these Rules shall be liable to serve anywhere in the country or abroad. 
The reasons for serving away from the BRIC Secretariat be recorded in writing and approval of the Hon’ble 
Minister, DBT may be obtained. 


DISQUALIFICATION: Any of the persons mentioned below would stand disqualified for the 
appointment/service: 

i. who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, or 

ii. who is having a spouse living, has entered in or contracted a marriage with any other person; 


Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible — under the 
Personal Law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and there are other grounds 
for so doing, exempt any person from the operation of the rule. 


iii. whose character and antecedent has been objected to by the District Magistrate/ Collector/ whose 
identity has not been established by the Police.

i. withheld information, given false information or provided false certificates/testimonials so as to secure

ii. lied under oath, 
iii. has been convicted of a criminal offence under Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and 
awarded punishment under any of the provisions of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS). 
iv. indulged in activities which are prejudicial to the interests of the STATE.
