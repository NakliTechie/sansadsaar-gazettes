RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 25th August 2026 
Payments Regulatory Board (Amendment) Regulations, 2025 


F. No. CO. DPSS. BD. No. S496/ 02-01-019/2026-2027 .—— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) 
read with clauses (a) of sub-section (2) of Section 38 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (51 of 2007), 
the Reserve Bank of India hereby makes the following amendment to the Payments Regulatory Board Regulations, 
2025 namely — 


1. Short Title & Commencement 
(1) These Regulations may be called the “Payments Regulatory Board (Amendment) Regulations, 2025”. 
(1) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Amendments to the Payments Regulatory Board Regulations, 2025 
In the Payments Regulatory Board Regulations, 2025, 

(1) In Regulation 4, sub-regulation (1) shall be omitted; and 

(1) Regulation 7 shall be omitted. 


SAURABH NATH, Chief General Manager.
