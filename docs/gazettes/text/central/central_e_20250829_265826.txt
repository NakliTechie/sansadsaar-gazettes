MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th August, 2025 


S.R.O. 2(E)— Whereas, the Government of Maharashtra has vide its notification number Ganab- 
2722/CR-56/Japuk(29) dated the 047 October, 2024 and TLC 2024/CR-46/M-0 dated 08" October, 2024, 
altered the name of the existing district of Ahmednagar to district of Ahilyanagar; 


And whereas, the Ahmednagar Cantonment is located within the district of Ahilyanagar; 


And whereas, the Cantonment Board, Ahmednagar has determined to alter the name of the existing 
Ahmednagar Cantonment to Ahilyanagar Cantonment; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3 read with sub- 
section (l) of section 259 of the Cantonments Act, 2006 (4I of 2006), the Central Government, as determined 
by the Cantonment Board, Ahmednagar, hereby declares the Ahmednagar Cantonment to Ahilyanagar 
Cantonment with effect from date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
