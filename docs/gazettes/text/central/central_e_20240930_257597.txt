(Public Notice) 

New Delhi, the 27th September, 2024 
No. 25/2024-2025 


Subject: Allocation of quantity 584 MT of Sugar by EU for export from India under TRQ for the year 
2024-25 (October 2024 to September 2025) —regarding. 


F. No. 02/9/7/060/AM23/EC/E-33997.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraphs 2.04 of the 
Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby allocates quantity of 584] MT for export of 
Sugar to EU from India under TRQ for the year 2024-25 (October 2024 to September 2025). 


2. As per Notification No. 3/205-20 dated 20.04.205, export of sugar (HS Code !700000) to EU under TRQ is 
'Free' subject to the conditions notified in the 'Nature of Restrictions’ in the above notification. 


3. Certificate of Origin, if required, for preferential export of sugar to EU shall be issued by Additional Director 
General of Foreign Trade, Mumbai on the recommendation of Agriculture and Processed Food Products Export 


Development Authority (APEDA) regarding entity and quantity for which eligible. Other certification requirements, if 
any, prescribed specifically for the export of sugar to EU would continue to be followed. 


4. The quota will be operated by Agriculture and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority 
(APEDA), New Delhi as the implementing agency for export of TRQ items to EU. 


5. The reporting requirement as notified vide Notification No. 3/20i5-2020 dated 20.04.20I5 read with 
Notification No. 20 dated 07.09.20I5 would be followed. 


Effect of this Public Notice: The quantity of 584] MT Sugar to be exported to EU from India under TRQ for the year 
2024-25 (October 2024 to September 2025) has been notified.
