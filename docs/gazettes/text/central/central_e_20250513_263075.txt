में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को मध्य प्रदेश राज्य में विशेष रेल परियोजना अर्थात “खंडवा-अकोला आमान परिवर्तन (एमजी से बीजी)”

अत: अब केंद्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी के उक्त रिपोर्ट की प्राप्ति तथा उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उप- 
धारा (1) द्वारा प्रदत शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि इससे उपाबध्द अनसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि पूर्वोक्त 
प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी. 

और यह कि केंद्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उप-धारा (2) के अनुसरण में यह घोषणा करती है 


कि आधिकारिक राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशित होने पर इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विलंगमो से 
मुक्त होकर केंद्रीय सरकार में आत्यांकित रूप से निहित हो जाएगी.

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 9th May, 2025 


S.O. 2127(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 971 (E) dated the 19" February 2025 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 25 February 2025 (here in after referred to as the 
said notification) and issued under sub-section(1) of section 20(A) of the Railways Act, 1989(24 of 1989), 
the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the 
said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion (MG to BG) in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the extract of the said Act was published under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act in the daily newspapers namely "Dainik Bhaskar (Hindi)" and "Patrika (Hindi)" by Hemant Kumar 
Bhagoria, Chief Engineer/Construction Organisation, Secunderabad on 28-02-2025. 


And no objection was received before the competent authority, that is, the Sub-Divisional Officer 
(Revenue), Nepanagar, from the villages of Mohana (Usarni), Usarni, Shekhapur Raiyat, Tukaithad, Khadki, 
Pangri, Mohangarh, Sawali, Kardali, Rangai and Khaknar in the district of Burhanpur. 


And, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted his report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
here by declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule 
annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
