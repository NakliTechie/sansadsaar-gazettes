S.O. 4843(E).—Whereas the Central Government, in exercise of the power conferred by sub section (l) and 
clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act 986 
(29 of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 issued in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 435 (E), dated the 3" February, 207; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest 
to amend the said notification; 


And whereas sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, !986 provides that whenever it 
appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for 
amending the notification number S.O. 435 (8), dated the 3" February, 207; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) and clauses (v) and (xiv) of 
sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986), read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of 
the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 435 (8), dated the 3" February, 
207, namely:-
