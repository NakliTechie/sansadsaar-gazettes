अपनी रिपोर्ट दे दी है;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd September, 2023 


S.O. 498(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways number S.0.503(E), dated 26 October 2022, has been published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3(A) of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Land Acquisition for 
building (widening/four lanning,etc.) Maintenance management and operation of NH-748 on the stretch of land 
from design chainage km. 84.00 to km. 95.620 (existing chainage Km. 84.00 to Km. 9777) (Goa/Karnataka 
border to Mollem section) in the state of Goa. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ The Goan ” dated 03//2022; 
“Bhanagar Bhuin” and “Goan Varta” त्वार्त 03//2022, under sub-section (3) of section 3(A) of the said Act; 
And whereas, objections have been received and the same have been considered by the competent authority; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 3(D) of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3(D) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub - section (2) of section 3 (D) of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
