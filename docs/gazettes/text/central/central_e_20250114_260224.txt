द्वारा यथा संशोधित अखिल भारतीय आयुर्विज्ञान संस्थान अधिनियम, 1956 (1956 का 25) की धारा 4 (छ) द्वारा प्रदत्त

करती है।

द्वारा शासित होगा।

MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th January, 2025 


S.O. 251(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4(g) of the All India Institute of Medical 
Sciences Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 
2012 (37 of 2012), the Central Government hereby notifies Shri Jugal Kishore and Shri Rajkumar 
Roat, elected by the Members of Lok Sabha to serve as Members of the Institute Body of All India Institute 
of Medical Sciences, Vijaypur (Samba), Jammu. 


The term of office of members shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All 
India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956.
