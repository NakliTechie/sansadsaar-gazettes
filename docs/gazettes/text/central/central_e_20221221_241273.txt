MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL , PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 
( Department of Personnel and Training ) 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 21st December , 2022 

S.O. 5973 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 10 of the 
Central Vigilance Commission Act , 2003 ( 45 of 2003 ) , the President is pleased to authorize Shri Praveen 
Kumar Srivastava , Vigilance Commissioner , Central Vigilance Commission , to act as the Central Vigilance 
Commissioner , in the vacancy to be caused due to completion of the tenure of Shri Suresh N. Patel , Central 
Vigilance Commissioner , with effect from the date of occurrence of vacancy , i.e. , 25th day of December , 
2022 , until the appointment of the Central Vigilance Commissioner .
