MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th November, 2025 


S.O. 5428(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highway, 5.0. 468 Dated:27 March 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening /four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
NE-7 in the stretch of land from Km. 74.87 to Km. 64.5 (Joint Collector, Chittoor & CALA, Bangalore Chennai 
Expressway, Collectorate Compound, Ambedkar Bhavan, 2nd Floor, Chittoor) in the village namely Thimmaiahpalle 
of Gudipala Taluk in the district of Chittoor in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Andhra Prabha" in Telugu 
Language and "The Hindu" in English both dated 3.04.2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग ए---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
