NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th March, 2024. 


S.O. 58(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH965 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 59.000 to Km. !7.000 (Sub Divisional Officer Malshiras Division Akluj) in the district of 
SOLAPUR in the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land.This notification is 
issued pursuant to the order passed by Hon’ble High Court, Bombay in Civil Writ Petition No.457 of 2023, wherein it 
was observed that the notifications, issued earlier under Section 3-A and 3-D of the Act of 956, the subject land 
being City Survey No. 359 (old Gat No. 534) situated at Village Velapur, Taluka- Malshiras, District -Solapur was 
not mentioned. The High Court further observed that it is open to the Central Government to proceed to issue a fresh 
notification under Section 3-A of the Act of !956 in respect of the subject land and mention of the said land in the 
Award already declared will not be considered as an impediment. The land owner upon publication will then file an 
undertaking and hand over vacant and peaceful possession. However, before taking possession and prior to possession 
being handed over, the joint measurement will be carried out.

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Sub divisional Officer Malshiras 
Division Akluj in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 
The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 


inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
