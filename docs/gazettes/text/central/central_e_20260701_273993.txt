MINISTRY OF STEEL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2026 


5.0. 359(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3, 4, |5A and 5B of the Collection of 
Statistics Act, 2008 (7 of 2009) read with rules 5 and 7 of the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024, the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of 
Steel, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S. 0. 868 (E), 
dated the 25th April, 2025, namely:- 


In the said notification, in clause (a), in the Table, - 


i. against serial number | relating to statistics officer, in column 2 under the heading “Designation with address 
and e-mail”, for the words and brackets "Deputy Director (Statistics Division)", the words and brackets "Joint 
Director (Statistics Division) or Deputy Director (Statistics Division)" shall be substituted; 


il. against serial number 2 relating to adjudicating officer, in column 2 under the heading “Designation with 
address and e-mail”, for the words and brackets "Deputy Director (Statistics Division)", the words and 
brackets "Joint Director (Statistics Division) or Deputy Director (Statistics Division)" shall be substituted.
