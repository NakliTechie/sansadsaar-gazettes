NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the |5th July, 2025 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 324I(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O 2450 € dated 25" June 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub- 
section (ii), dated the 25" June 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section () 
of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (/ of 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely Manmad-Jalgaon 4" Line in the district of Jalgaon & Nashik in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for Sub Divisional Officer/Pachora in 
daily newspapers namely “Dainik Sakaal” & “Dainik Deshonnati” Dated 24-04-2025 under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received. Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of Section D of the said act; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
