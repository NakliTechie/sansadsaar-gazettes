INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 

WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-18 / 14562 - INC.– In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas Florence College of Nursing situated at Ratikhera Guna Road , Distt . Ashoknagar 473338 , Madhya 
Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to 
conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-18 / 14562 - INC dated 08/06/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( Health & FW ) , Government of Madhya Pradesh , 
Room No. 241 , Mantralaya ( Vallabh Bhawan ) Area Hills , Bhopal- 462004 , Madhya Pradesh was issued . Copy of the 
notice dated 08/06/2022 was also enclosed to Florence College of Nursing situated at Ratikhera Guna Road , Distt . 
Ashoknagar – 473338 , Madhya Pradesh . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Florence College of Nursing , Madhya Pradesh . Even the institution Florence College of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
17/11/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 
declares that with effect from 08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Florence 
College of Nursing situated at Ratikhera Guna Road , Distt . Ashoknagar 473338 , Madhya Pradesh shall be 
entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of Madhya 
Pradesh .
