MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 


(SEZ DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th December, 2023 


S.O. 5166(E).—Whereas, M/s. Kanai Technology Parks Private Limited (formerly M/s. Velankani 
Technology Parks Private Limited) had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 
2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a Special Economic Zone for Electronic Hardware and 
Software including Information Technology Enabled Services SEZ at Sriperumbudur, Kanchipuram District, Chennai, 
in the State of Tamil Nadu; 


And, whereas, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of 
the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area of 57.46715 hectares at the 
above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number 5.0. 2118(E) dated 11" 
December, 2007; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Kanai Technology Parks Private Limited has now proposed to de-notify the entire 
area of 57.46715 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Tamil Nadu has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal 
vide letter No. 4512188/MIB.1/2023 dated 317 July, 2023. Further, the land parcels after de-notification will conform 
to Land Guidelines/master plans of the Government of Tamil Nadu; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Madras Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of entire area of 57.46715 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by first proviso to rule 8 of the Special Economic 

Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except as respects things done or 
omitted to be done before such rescission.
