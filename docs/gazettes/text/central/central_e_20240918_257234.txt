MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 


New Delhi, the 8"" September, 2024 
G.S.R. 579 (E).- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 98 of the Railways 


Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to 
amend the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage of Passengers) Rules, 2000 (hereinafter referred 
to as the said rules), namely:- 


lL. 


() These rules may be called the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage of Passengers) 
Second Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


In Chapter II of the said rules,- 

(a) sub-rule (2) of rule 3 shall be omitted; 

(b) in rule 4, for the words, brackets and figures “sub-rule (2) of rule 3”, the words, 
brackets and figures “sub-rule (3) of rule 3” shall be substituted; 

(c) in sub-rule (4) of rule 5A, for the words and figure “proviso to rule 3”, the words, 
brackets and figures “proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 3” shall be substituted. 

In Chapter VII of the said rules,- 

(a) in sub-rule (l) of rule 24, for the words and figure “proviso to rule 3”, the words, 
brackets and figures “proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 3” shall be substituted; 

(b) in sub-rule () of rule 25, for the words and figure “proviso to rule 3”, the words, 
brackets and figures “proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 3” shall be substituted;

(c) in sub-rule (3) of rule 25, for the words and figure “proviso to rule 3”, the words, 
brackets and figures “proviso to sub-rule (3) of rule 3” shall be substituted.
