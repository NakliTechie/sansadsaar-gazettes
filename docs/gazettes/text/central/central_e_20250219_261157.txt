S.O. 88(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas vide 8.0. No. 976(E) dated the 28 February 2024, published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette of India 
No. 93 dated 29 February 2024, issued under Sub-Section (.) of Section-3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that 
notification for the purpose of laying “Kandla -Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, Section :- Pipavav to IHB Limited 
Dumad” for the transportation of LPG in Tehsil Bhavnagar, District Bhavnagar in the State of Gujarat by IHB Limited;

And whereas, copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the concerned land owners upto 
237 March 2024; 


And whereas, Shri Avalabhai Khatarabhai Baria, Competent Authority and Deputy Collector (Retd.), Kandla - 
Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, IHB Limited, Vadodara (Gujarat) has under Sub-Section-(.) of Section-6 of said Act, 
submitted his report to the Central Government; 


And Whereas, the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire Right of User therein;

notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline;

Government hereby directs that Right of User in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration, in [HB Limited, free from all encumbrances.
