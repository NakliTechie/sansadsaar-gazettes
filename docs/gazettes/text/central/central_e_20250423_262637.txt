S.O. 1835(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by clause 37A of section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989, the 
Central Government hereby In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge In lieu of Level Crossing 
No. 219 at Km. 567/6-7 in between Raichur - Yermaras Stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land 
in the State of Karnataka in the District of Raichur for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
