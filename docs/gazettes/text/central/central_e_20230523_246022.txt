MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd May, 2023 


S.O. 2253(E).— Whereas by the notification of the government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.O. 862(E) dated 237 Febuary 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II 
Section 3, Sub-Section(ii) issued under sub-section(l) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act,) the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said Notification for building (widening/six lining,etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 8 on the stretch of land from Km. !42.400 to 22.l00 (Gurgaon —Kotputli-Jaipur 
section) in district Jaipur in the state of Rajasthan. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Rajashtan Patrika” and “Samachar 
Jagat” both dated l March 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 3 D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted 
its report to the central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the central Government here by declares that the land 
specified in the said schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and further, in pursuance of sub-section 
(2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government here by declares the on publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette, the land specified in the said schedule shall vest absolutely in the central Government, free from all 
encumbrance.
