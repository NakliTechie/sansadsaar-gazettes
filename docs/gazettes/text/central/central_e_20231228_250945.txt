EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE CORPORATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th December, 2023 


No. N-4704/I/TamilNadu/2023-P&D:- In pursuance of powers conferred by Section 46(2) of 
the Employees’ State Insurance Act, 948 (34 of 948), read with Regulation 95-A of the Employees’ 
State Insurance (General) Regulations, 950, the Director General has fixed the 04/2/2023 as the date 
from, which the medical benefit as laid down in the said Regulation 95-A and the Tamil Nadu Employees' 
State Insurance (Medical Benefit) Rules, !955 shall be extended to the families of insured persons in all the 
areas of Pudukottai district, in addition to the already notified areas in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


DEEPAK JOSHI, Insurance Commissioner(P&D)
