MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2026 
5.0. 5248(E).— In partial modification of the Government of India Notification No. 8.0. 2505(E) dated 
05.06.2025, published in the Extraordinary Gazette of India dated 8.06.2025, and in pursuance of clause (a) of 
Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), 
(hereinafter called the said Act), the Government of India hereby authorizes Shri Rengith George, Deputy Collector 
(LR), Kasaragod, Government of Kerala in place of Shri Shajahan 5., Deputy Collector, Government of Kerala to 
perform the functions of the Competent Authority for Kochi-Koottanad-Bengaluru-Mangalore Pipeline (KKBMPL) 


Project of M/s GAIL (India) Limited in the State of Kerala for the period from the date of issue of this notification till 
0.08.2027 under the said Act, on deputation basis. 


2. This notification will be effective from its date of issue.
