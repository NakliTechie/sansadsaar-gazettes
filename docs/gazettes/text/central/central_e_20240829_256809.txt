S.O. 3699(E).— In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition 
of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter called the said Act), the Government of India hereby 
authorizes Shri Shivam Agnihotri, Senior Operations Manager, M/s. Indian Oil Corporation Limited IOCL), Western 
Region Pipelines, Rajkot in place of Shri P.V. Mehta, Deputy Manager (Operations), M/s. Indian Oil Corporation 
Limited (IOCL), Western Region Pipelines to perform the functions of Competent Authority under the Petroleum and 


Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 for Dahej - Koyali R LNG Pipeline of 
M/s. Indian Oil Corporation Limited in the State of Gujarat.
