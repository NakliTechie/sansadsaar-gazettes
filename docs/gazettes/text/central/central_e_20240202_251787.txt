9. प्रतिष्ठान प्रशासन- विज्ञान और प्रौद्योगिकी विभाग, विज्ञान और प्रौद्योगिकी मंत्रालय प्रतिष्ठान का प्रशासनिक मंत्रालय 
होगा। 

10. ऐसे विषय जिनके संबंध में कोई उपबंध नहीं किया गया है - शासी बोर्ड के सदस्यों और कार्य परिषद के सदस्यों की 
सेवा के निबन्धन और शर्त संबंधी मामले, जिनके लिए इन नियमों में कोई स्पष्ट उपबंध नहीं किया गया है, केन्द्रीय 
सरकार के विनिश्चयनार्थ अग्रेषित किए जाएँगे। 


11. शिथिलन शक्ति-जहाँ केन्द्रीय सरकार का मत है कि जहां ऐसा करना आवश्यक या समीचीन है वहाँ वह, लिखित में दर्ज 
किए जाने वाले कारणों से आदेश द्वारा, शासी बोर्ड के सदस्यों और कार्य परिषद के सदस्यों के संबंध में इस नियमावली 
के उपबंधों में से किसी उपबंध में छूट दे सकेगी।

DRAFT RULES 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) These rules may be called the Anusandhan National Research 
Foundation (qualifications, experience, honorarium, allowances, and other terms and conditions of service of 
Members of the Governing Board and Executive Council) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Definitions. — (1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, — 
(a) ‘Act’ means the Anusandhan National Research Foundation Act, 2023 (25 of 2023); 


(b) “President” means a president of the Governing Board referred to in clause (a) of sub-section 
(2) of section 5 of the Act; 


(2) Words and expressions used and not defined in these rules but defined in the Act shall have the same 
meanings as assigned to them in the Act.

3. Qualifications and experience of Members of Governing Board. — (1) The Members to be nominated or 
appointed under clause (a) of sub-section (3) of section 5 of the Act shall be Members of the Prime Minister’s 
Science, Technology and Innovation Council. 


(2) The Members to be nominated or appointed under clause (b) of sub-section 3 of section 5 of the Act shall 
be a person who has previously occupied or currently occupies a position of leadership at a business 
organisation or an industry. 


(3) The Member to be nominated or appointed under clause (c) of sub-section 3 of section 5 of the Act shall 
possess minimum period of ten years of experience in the field of humanities and social sciences. 


(4) The Members to be nominated or appointed under clause (d) of sub-section 3 of section 5 of the Act shall 
hold the position of head of a national laboratory or research/academic institution engaged in scientific and 
technological research and development, or senior scientist or senior faculty member in such institution 
with a minimum period of twenty years of experience. 


(5) The Members to be nominated or appointed under clause (e) of sub-section 3 of section 5 of the Act shall 
possess a doctorate degree or other specialised educational qualification in one or more of the following 
fields, —

4. Term of office of Members of Governing Board. — The Members of the Governing Board shall ordinarily 
hold office for a period of three years from the date of appointment, or such other period as determined by the 
President, and shall be eligible for reappointment. 


5... Allowances for Members of the Governing Board appointed. — (1) Every Member of the Governing Board 
appointed under sub-clause (3) of section 5 of the Act, attending the meeting of the Governing Board, shall be 
entitled to allowances and expenses in accordance with the following:— 


(a) Where a Member is required to undertake outstation travel for the purpose of attending a meeting of the 
Governing Board, or for performing the functions of the Governing Board, such Member shall be 
entitled to — 


(i) air fare by the highest class available; 


(ii) accommodation expenses at actuals, subject to ceiling amounts as may be approved by the 
Governing Board from time to time; and 


(iii) all other aspects of such travel shall be regulated as per the guidelines or instructions as may be 
approved by the Governing Board from time to time.
