भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड () में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा मध्य प्रदेश राज्य के छतरपुर जिले में अनुविभागीय अधिकारी

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd April, 2025 


S.O. 1583(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 2613(E) Dated:03/07/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of NH No- 43-Ext- Gulganj 
— Amanganj — Pawai — Katni Road {KM 0+00 to KM 163.85} in the stretch of land from design KM 0+00 to KM 77+00 
in the (Sub Divisional Officer Revenue Tehsil - Bijawar) Chhatarpur district in the State of Madhya Pradesh; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Deshbandhu Sagar” dated 13- 
Aug-2024 and “Dainik Bhaskar Sagar” dated 14-Aug-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received no objections filed under Section 3-C; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
