S.O. 2200(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by subsection (l) of section 20A of the Railway Act 
2008, ( of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied for the public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for execution. 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project of "Pushkar-Merta (Katyasani) New BG line 
Project (Special Rail Project)" (for sub division Pushkar of Ajmer district) in the state of Rajasthan, hereby declares 
its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to competent authority, namely, the Sub divisional officer Pushkar, Ajmer 
district in the state of rajasthan in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give 
the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such 
objection and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall befinal. 


The land plans and other details of land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by 
the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
