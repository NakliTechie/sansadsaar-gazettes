New Delhi, the 28th January, 2025 
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Model Bye-Laws and Governing Board of Insolvency Professional 
Agencies) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


F. No. IBBI/2024-25/GN/REGI7.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 96, 204, 205 read 
with section 240 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 206 (3! of 206), the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of 
India hereby makes the following regulations further to amend the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Model 
Bye-Laws and Governing Board of Insolvency Professional Agencies) Regulations, 20l6, namely: - 

l. (.) These regulations may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Model Bye-Laws and Governing 
Board of Insolvency Professional Agencies) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Model Bye-Laws and Governing Board of Insolvency Professional 
Agencies) Regulations, 206, in the Schedule, in para VI, in clause 2A - 


(i) in the proviso to sub-clause (3), for the words “forty-five”, the word “ninety” shall be substituted. 
(ii) in sub-clause (5), for the word “fifteen”, the word “ninety” shall be substituted.
