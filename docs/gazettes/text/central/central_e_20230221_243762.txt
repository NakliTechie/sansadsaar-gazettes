MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 20th February , 2023 

G.S.R. 113 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause ( b ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 156 of the 
Indo - Tibetan Border Police Force Act , 1992 ( 35 of 1992 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
to amend the Indo - Tibetan Border Police Force , General Duty Cadre ( Group Group ' C ' Posts ) Recruitment Rules , 
2022 namely : - 


1. Short title and commencement . – ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Indo - Tibetan Border Police Force , 
General Duty Cadre ( Group ' C ' Posts ) Recruitment Amendment Rules , 2023 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 


2. In the Indo - Tibetan Border Police Force , General Duty Cadre ( Group ' C ' Posts ) Recruitment Rules , 2022 , in the 
Schedule to against serial number 3 relating to the post of Constable ( General Duty ) : 

( i ) in Column ( 6 ) , - after the entries , the following Note shall be inserted , namely : 

“ Note 1 : The upper age - limit shall be relaxable upto five years for the candidates of the first batch of Ex 
Agniveers ” ; 


“ Note 2 : The upper age limit shall be relaxable upto three years for the candidates of Ex - Agniveers . ” ; 

( ii ) in Column ( 7 ) , - after clause ( ii ) , the following Note shall be inserted , namely : 

“ Note : Ex - Agniveers shall be exempted from Physical Efficiency Test " : 
( iii ) in Column ( 10 ) , after clause ( ii ) , the following Note shall be inserted , namely ; 
“ Note : - Ten per cent of the vacancies shall be reserved for Ex - Agniveers . " .

P. S. DANGWAL , Dy . Secy .

vide G.S.R. No. 847 ( E ) , dated 25th November 2022 .
