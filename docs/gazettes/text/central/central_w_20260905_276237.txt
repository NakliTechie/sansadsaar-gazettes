ORDER 
New Delhi, the |2th August, 2026 


O.N. 05.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Haryana, 2024 for 78- 
SohnaAssembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification 
No.464/ECI/HAR-LA/2024, dated 50 September, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at 
an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him/her or his/her 
election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the above-mentioned election from78-SohnaAssembly Constituency of Haryana, 
was declared by the Returning Officer on 8 October, 2024 and hence the last date for lodging the account of 
Election Expenses was 7 November, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report dated |.!.2024 submitted by DEO, Gurugram forwarded by the Chief 
Electoral Officer, Haryana vide his letter no. 6/Elect-Exp./GELA-HR-2024/Elec-2025/2AE-692 dated 05.02.2025, 
under sub-rule (l)of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, !96,Sh.Ataullah Khan a contesting candidate from 
78-SohnaAssembly Constituency has failed to lodge account of election expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Gurugram a Show-Cause notice 
No. 76/HR-LA/Exp. Account/2024/CEMS-I, dated 30" April, 2025was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, !96l by the Election Commission of India to Sh.Ataullah Khan for not lodging of his 
account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96l, Sh.Ataullah Khanwas directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, Gurugram within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, theDistrict Election Officer,Gurugram vide his letter nos. Elec-2025/020 and Elec-2026/725 
dated .06.2025and 07.05.2026 respectively has informed that the notice was duly served and received 
bySh.Ataullah Khan’s Brother (Sh. Najar Mohd.) on 07.05.2025 and notice was again served to his Father(Sh. 
Kayam Khan) on 09.04.2026; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Gurugram in his Supplementary Report, vide its letter no. Elec- 
2026/725 dated 07.05.2026 has reported that Sh.Ataullah Khanhas not submitted account of election expenses. 
Further, after receipt of the said notice,Sh.Ataullah Khan has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the 
Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section |0 A of the Representation of the People Act, 95] provides that: - 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, the Election Commission shall, by order published 
in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three 
years from the date of the order.”’; 


WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh.Ataullah Khan 
has failed to lodge account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Sh.Ataullah Khan, H.No. 79, Village Chharoda P.O. Tauru, 
District Nuh, Haryana, a contesting candidate from 78-Sohna Assembly Constituencyof Haryanain General 
Election to Legislative Assembly, 2024 to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House 
of the Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of 
three years from the date of this order.
