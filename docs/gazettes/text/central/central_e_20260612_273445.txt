Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


5.0. 3051(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 
1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (No.11 of 2008), the Central 
Government hereby notifies “PH-30: - Elimination of LC-11, LC-14 & LC-15 by providing subway by push 
through method at Rly. Kms 685/10-11, 695/09-10 & 698/16-17 resp. between BD-CNDB section” Railway 
project under Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Amravati District in the State of Maharashtra, for 
providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 


Official Gazette.
