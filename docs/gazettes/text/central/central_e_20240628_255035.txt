MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[South Eastern Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 28th June, 2024 


S.O. 2490(E).— Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (South 
Eastern Railway/Construction Organization), number S.0.5410(E), dated the 217 December, 2023 published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 217 December, 2023 and issued under sub 
section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989)(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, decided its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the notification number 
S.0.5410(E), dated the 217 December, 2023 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely Dumetra Link-C line connection with Bisra station (2.3 Km.) doubling in the district of 
Sundargarh in the State of Odisha. 


And, whereas, the substance of notification number 5410(E), dated the 21% December, 2023 was published in the 
daily newspapers, namely ‘Sakal’ (Odiya Edition), dated 15" January, 2024 and ‘Navbharat’ (Hindi Edition), dated 16" 
January, 2024 under section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas, three number of petitions/objections were received against notification number S.0.5410(E), dated 
the 217 December, 2023 and same have been considered and disallowed by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
the report to the Central Government;

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on the publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest 


absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
