MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
( Food Safety and Standards Authority of India ) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 21st December , 2022 

F. No. STD / SP - 18 / T ( VeganFoods ) . The following draft of certain regulations , further to amend the Food 
Safety and Standards ( Vegan Foods ) Amendment Regulations , 2022 , which the Food Safety and Standards Authority 
of India proposes to make with previous approval of the Central Government , in exercise of the powers conferred by 
clause ( v ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 92 read with sections 16 of the Food Safety and Standards Act , 2006 ( 34 of 
2006 ) is hereby published as required by the said sub - section ( 1 ) of section 92 of the said Act for the information of all 
persons likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby given that the said draft regulations shall be taken into 
consideration after the expiry of the period of sixty days from the date on which copies of the Official Gazette in 
which this notification is published are made available to the public . 


Objections and suggestions , if any , may be addressed to the Chief Executive Officer , Food Safety and 
Standards Authority of India , FDA Bhawan , Kotla Road , New Delhi - 110002 
or sent on email at 
regulation@fssai.gov.in . 


Objections and suggestions , which may be received from any person with respect to the said draft regulations 
before the expiry of period so specified , shall be considered by the Food Authority .

1. These regulations may be called the Food Safety and Standards ( Vegan Foods ) Amendment 
Regulations , 2022 .
