INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F. No. 8-8/4985-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Maharana Pratap School Of Nursing situated at Amar Pura Road, Near Ratibad, 
Vill.- Amla, Tehsil- Huzur, Distt.- Bhopal, Pin Code- 462044, Madhya Pradeshis recognized by State 
Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-8/l4985-INC (52822) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section l4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, |947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Madhya Pradesh Room No. 24l, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was 
issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Maharana Pratap School Of Nursing situated at 
Amar Pura Road, Near Ratibad, Vill.- Amla, Tehsil- Huzur, Distt.- Bhopal, Pin Code-462044, 
Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Maharana Pratap School Of Nursing. Even the institution, Maharana Pratap School Of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
23/02//2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially thoserelating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Maharana Pratap School Of Nursing 
situated at Amar Pura Road, Near Ratibad, Vill.- Amla, Tehsil- Huzur, Distt.- Bhopal, Pin Code- 462044, 
Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 

Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
