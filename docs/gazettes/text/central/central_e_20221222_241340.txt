S.O. 6019 ( E ) .— In exercise of powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government , hereby 
makes the following amendment in the Schedule of the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways , number S.O.342 ( E ) , dated the 25th January 2022 published in 
the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) , dated the 25th January 2022 for 
building ( widening / six - laning , etc. , ) maintenance , management and operation of National Highway No. 16 , 
in the stretch of Package - II ( 6 - laning of existing 4 - lane road from Kalaparru ( Design Km . 1050.680 ) to 
Chinna Avutupalli ( Design Km . 1076.480 ) of NH - 5 ( new NH - 16 ) including 6 - lane Hanuman Junction 
bypass from ( Existing Km . 1055.680 to Km . 1060.800 ) ( Design Ch . 0.000 to Ch . 6.720 ) , ( Total Design 
Length - 27.400 Km . ) in the District of Krishna in the State of Andhra Pradesh , namely :

the village name “ Kanumolu ( K. Seetharam Puram ) " , the village name “ Kanumolu ( Perikeedu ) " shall be
