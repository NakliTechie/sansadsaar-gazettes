MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st February, 2025 


S.O. 955 (E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O No. 2096 (E) Dated:24/05/2024, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening/Two lane/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH-930D Bamni-Rajura- 
Warur Road-Dewada-Lakkadkot up to M.S. Border on the stretch of land from Km. 19/000 to Km. 51/985 in 
CHANDRAPUR District in the state of MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Sakal” and “Times of India” both 
dated 15.06.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And Whereas no objection has been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
