NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 3Ist July, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) 


59.0. 4353(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 294 (E) dated 2i* April 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 47 May 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (.) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Central Railway- 
Upgradation work of Electric Traction System for Bhusawal-Khandwa Section to meet 3000 MT loading target” 
in the district of Khandwa in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue) & 
Land Acquisition Officer, Khandwa in daily newspapers namely “Dainik Bhaskar” & “Dainik Nayi Duniya” dated 
5-05-2026 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, no objections were received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
