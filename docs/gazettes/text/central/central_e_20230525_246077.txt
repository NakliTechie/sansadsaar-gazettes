MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2023 


S.O. 2287(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas vide 8.0. No. 4940(E) dated the I9th October, 2022, published in the Extraordinary Gazette of India No. 
4728 dated 20th October, 2022, issued under sub-section (l) of section-3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that 
notification for the purpose of laying “Kandla -Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, Section:- Pipavav to IHB Limited 
Dumad” for the transportation of LPG in Tehsil Anand, District Anand in the State of Gujarat by IHB Limited; 


And whereas, copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the concerned land owners up to 


04 January, 2023. 


And whereas, the Competent Authority Shri Avalabhai Khatarabhai Baria, Competent Authority and Deputy 
Collector (Retd.), Kandla Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, IHB Limited, Gujarat has under sub-section (l) of 


Section-6 of said Act submitted report to the Central Government; 


And Whereas, the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 


land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire Right of User therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section-6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 


notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of Section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that Right of User in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 


Central Government, vest on the date of publication of the declaration, in IHB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


IHB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section 0 of the P & MP Act, 


962 and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would lie against the Central Government on any matter relating to the 


pipeline.
