transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s XL XERGI Power Private Limited for its 
400 MW Solar Power Project in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan”. 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/79/2024-PG dated 20.08.2024

transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s XL XERGI Power Private Limited for its 
400 MW Solar Power Project in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan”. 


M/s XL XERGI Power Private Limited had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspapers The 
Times of India (in English) dated 13.09.2024, Dainik Bhaskar (in Hindi) dated 13.09.2024, Jalte Deep (in Hindi) dated

observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within 2 Months from the date of publication. 
Subsequently, M/s XL XERGI Power Private Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 14.07.2025 declaring that no 
observation/representation was received within 2 Months from the date of Publication in the official gazette of 
Government of India and newspaper publications.
