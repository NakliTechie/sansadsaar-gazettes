MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th November, 2025 


S.O. 5485(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is 
required for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH 748A in the stretch of land from Km. 89+900 to Km. 36+l00 (NHAI Bagalkot) in the district 
of BELAGAVI in the state of KARNATAKA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(l) of section 3C of the said Act. 

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition 
Office, C/o Venktesh L Benkatti, Plot No.L-l6, Sector-63A, Near Kendriya Vidyalaya, Brundaavan Sector, 
Navanagr, Bagalkot-5870 in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after 
hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 

Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall 
be final. 

The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and 
can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
