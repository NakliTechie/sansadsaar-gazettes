NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the lth July, 2023 


S.O. 3074(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of !956) (hereafter refried to as the said act). The central Government, after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the schedule below, is required for 
building (widening/2-Laning with paved shoulder), maintenance, management and operation of the National Highway 
No. NH-346 in the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 96+877 Km. (Chanderi-Pichhore-Dinara-Road) (Tehsil- 
Pichhore) Additional in the District Shivpuri, in the state of Madhya Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under section () of section 
3C of the said act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the sub divisional officer (Revenue) 
and land Acquisition Officer Pichhore, District Shivpuri, in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the 
competent authority shall give the objector and all such objections and after asking such further enquiry, if any, as the 
competent authority all such objections and after such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub section (l) of section 3C of the said act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
