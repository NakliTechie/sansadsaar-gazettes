फा.सं.18-729 / 2000- आईएनसी . – भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 ( 1947 का XLVIII ) की धारा 
14 के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है : 


जबकि , मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग , हनुमंथनगर , गुट्टेपल्या , बिन्नमंगला बिन्नमंगला बैंगलोर नेलामंगला 
562123 , कर्नाटक ( " कॉलेज " ) को कर्नाटक सरकार और कर्नाटक राज्य नर्सिंग परिषद ने जीएनएम पाठ्यक्रम चलाने के 
लिए मान्यता प्रदान की थी । 


जबकि , मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग ने मल्लिगे मेडिकल सेंटर का अपने मूल अस्पताल के रूप में उल्लेख करके 
कर्नाटक राज्य नर्सिंग परिषद और कर्नाटक राज्य सरकार से मान्यता प्राप्त की थी । 


जबकि , भारतीय नर्सिंग परिषद ( " परिषद " ) द्वारा वैध मूल अस्पताल के विवरण के संबंध में मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ 
नर्सिंग द्वारा प्रस्तुत गलत जानकारी और एक नोटरीकृत उपक्रम के माध्यम से गलत जानकारी प्रदान करने , बिना प्राधिकरण 
के दस्तावेजों का अवैध रूप से उपयोग करने और अनुचित लाभ कमाने के लिए तथ्यों को गलत तरीके से प्रस्तुत करने के 
संबंध में एक शिकायत प्राप्त हुई थी । 


जबकि परिषद ने मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग से पत्र दिनांक 02.07.2021 के माध्यम से स्पष्टीकरण मांगा था । 
मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग ने उपरोक्त पत्र दिनांक 02.07.2021 के जवाब में सहायक दस्तावेजों के साथ दिनांक 
29.07.2021 को एक ईमेल भेजा । तत्पश्चात , परिषद् ने मल्लिगे स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग को एक अतिरिक्त पत्र दिनांक 
13.08.2021 जारी किया जिसमें उन्हें मूल अस्पताल , ट्रस्ट के सदस्यों और मल्लिगे मेडिकल सेंटर के आईटीआर के मुद्दे पर 
इसके अतिरिक्त स्पष्ट करने की आवश्यकता थी । परिषद ने मल्लिगे मेडिकल सेंटर प्राइवेट लिमिटेड को अलग से पत्र दिनांक

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 14th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-729 / 2000 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Government of Karnataka and Karnataka State nursing Council had granted recognition to Mallige 
School of Nursing , Hanumanthanagar , Guttepalya , Binnamangala Binnamangala Bangalore Nelamangala - 
562123 , Karnataka ( “ College " ) for running GNM course . 


Whereas , the Mallige School of Nursing had obtained recognition from the Karnataka State nursing Council 
and Karnataka state government by mentioning Mallige Medical Centre as its parent Hospital . 


Whereas , a complaint was received by Indian Nursing Council ( “ Council " ) with regards to incorrect 
information furnished by Mallige School of Nursing with respect to details of valid parent hospital and providing false 
information by way of a notarised undertaking , illegally using documents without authorisation and misrepresenting 
facts to make undue gains . 


WHEREAS , the Council had sought clarification from Mallige school of nursing vide letter dated 
02.07.2021 . The Mallige School of Nursing sent an email dated 29.07.2021 along with supporting documents in 
response to the aforesaid letter dated 02.07.2021 . Thereafter , Council issued another letter dated 13.08.2021 to 
Mallige School of Nursing requiring them to clarify further on the issue of parent hospital , members of the trust and 
ITR of Mallige medical centre . The Council also issued separate letter dated 13.08.2021 to Mallige Medical Centre 
Pvt . Ltd. asking for their comments on it being the parent hospital of Mallige School of Nursing . The Mallige School 
of Nursing sent an email dated 15.08.2021 to the Council in response to the letter dated 13.08.2021 with some other 
supporting documents . Further , Mallige Medical Centre Pvt . sent a letter dated 19.08.2021 in reply to Council letter 
dated 13.08.2021 . 


Whereas , the Council had issued show cause notice / clarification dated 27.01.2022 to Mallige school of 
Nursing . That 15 days were given to the Mallige school of Nursing to respond . However , the said college has failed to 
send any reply / comments to the said show cause notice . 


Whereas , the Council had sent a letter dated 06.04.2022 to the State government of Karnataka asking them 
for their remarks / comments to the allegations regarding obtaining recognitions from the state nursing council and 
suitability from the Council on the basis of false information for several years , furnishing false information by way of 
a notarised undertaking , illegally using documents without authorisation and misrepresenting facts to make undue 
gains amounting to serious offence under relevant laws .

Hence , therefore , the recognition of Mallige school of Nursing for conducting GNM course is hereby 
withdrawn from the academic session 2022-2023 in terms of Section 14 ( 3 ) -b of the Act .

of Nursing shall be entitled to be registered only in the state of Karnataka subject to orders of the State government 
of the Karnataka .
