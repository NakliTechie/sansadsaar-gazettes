MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd April, 2025 


9.0. 572(E).— Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of petroleum products from Barauni-Kanpur Pipeline (BKPL) to Mirzapur ToP should be laid by 
Indian Oil Corporation Limited in tehsil Sadar of Mirzapur District. 


And whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, it is 
necessary to acquire the right of user in the land described in the schedule annexed to this notification. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub section () of section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in land ) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein; 


Any person interested in the land described in the said schedule may, within twenty—one days from the date on 
which the copies of this notification, as published in the Gazette of India, are made available to the General Public, 
object in writing to the acquisition of the right of user therein or laying of the pipeline under the land to Sh. Arvind 
Kumar Mishra, Competent Authority, Pipeline Connectivity for Mirzapur ToP from Barauni-Kanpur Pipeline (BKPL), 
Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Eastern Region Pipelines, Adalpura(First Floor, Shreya Lawn), Chunar, Mirzapur- 
23304, Uttar Pradesh.
