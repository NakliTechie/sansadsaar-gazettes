MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th September, 2024 


S.O. 4127 (E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, S.O. 2044(E) Dated:21/05/2024 , published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (11) issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH217 in the stretch of land from Km. 15.07 to Km. 40.84 
(Dainadubi - Darugiri) in the district of East Garo Hills in the state of MEGHALAYA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Shillong 
Times” and “Salantini janera” both dated 6th June, 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, 
considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the 
competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise 
of the powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the 
land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all 
encumbrances
