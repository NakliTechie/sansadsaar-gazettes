S.O. 2972(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Double line between Rampurhat — Bhagalpur via 
Dumka” in Howrah, Asansol and Malda Division as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Birbhum District 
of West Bengal, Dumka And Godda Districts of Jharkhand, Banka and Bhagalpur Districts of Bihar for providing 


national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
