MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th May, 2023 
No. 07/2023 
Subject : Amendment in Export Policy of broken rice under HS Code 1006 40 00. 


S.O. 2273(E).—The Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 of 
the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), as amended, read with Para 1.02 and 2.01 
of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, hereby incorporate the Policy Conditions for broken rice against ITC (HS) code 
1006 40 00 of Chapter 10 of Schedule 2 of the ITC (HS) Export Policy, as under:- 


ITC HS Codes Description Export Policy Policy Condition 


1006 4000 Only for Prohibited Export will be allowed on the basis of permission 


broken rice granted by the Government of India to other 
countries to meet their food security needs and 
based on the request of their Government. 


2. Effect of this Notification: 


The export policy of broken rice is prohibited; however, export will be allowed on the basis of permission granted by 
the Government of India to other countries to meet their food security needs and based on the request of their 
Government.
