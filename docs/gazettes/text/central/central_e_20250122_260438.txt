NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 2th December, 2024 


S.O. 378(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 938 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 38 at 
Km. 295/20-24 in between Kolanoor and Peddapalli stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in 
the State of Telengana in the District of Peddapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
