ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th August, 2023 


O.N. 40(E).— WHEREAS, the seat of Shri Hardwar Dubey, in the Council of States, elected by the elected 
members of the Legislative Assembly of Uttar Pradesh, has become vacant on 26.06.2023 by the reason of his death, 
before the expiration of his term of office which was up to and including 25..2026; and 


WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 47, sub-section () of Section 39 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95 (43 of 95), the Election Commission of India hereby- 


(A) calls upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Uttar Pradesh to elect, before 9" September, 
2023 and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder, a 
person for the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the Council of States; 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 05" September, 2023 (Tuesday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 06" September, 2023 (Wednesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(c) the 08" September, 2023 (Friday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 5'" September, 2023 (Friday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 9" September, 2023 (Tuesday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election.
