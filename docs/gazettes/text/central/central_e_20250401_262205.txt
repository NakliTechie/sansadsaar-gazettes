LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist April, 2025 


F. No. LAFEAS-CB I09//2025-CB-I.—The following paragraph published in the Lok Sabha Bulletin-Part 
II dated 28 March, 2025, is hereby published for general information:- 


“No. 2345 Committee Branch 


Amendment to the Directions by the Speaker, Lok Sabha 
(Tenth Edition ) 


In pursuance of Rule 389 of the Rules of Procedure and Conduct of Business in Lok Sabha (Seventeenth 
Edition), the Speaker has issued the following new Direction :- 


DIRECTION A 


“Daily Allowance. 2A. A Member shall be entitled to receive daily allowance for attending the Session of the 
House: 


Provided that no Member shall be entitled to receive the aforesaid allowance unless a 
Member signs the register, maintained for this purpose by the Secretariat of the House of the 
People, on all the days (except intervening holidays for which no such signing is required) 
of the Session of the House: 


Provided further that the signature shall include attendance marked through any electronic 


or biometric means approved by Speaker and register shall include physical register or 
e-register generated through Software maintained for this purpose.” 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, Secy. General
