WESTERN RAILWAY ( CONSTRUCTION ORGANIZATION ) 

NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai , the 23rd March , 2023 

S.O. 1420 ( E ) .— Whereas , by the notification of the Government of India , in the Ministry of Railways 
( Railway Board ) , number , S.O 5347 ( E ) dated the 17th November 2022 published in Gazette of India , 
Extraordinary , Part II , section 3 Sub - section ( ii ) , dated the 16th November 2022 ( hereinafter referred to as the 
said notification ) and issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 20 ( A ) of the Railway Act , 1989 ( 24 of 1989 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution , maintenance , 
management and operation of Special Railway Project , namely Nimach - Ratlam Doubling Project in the State of 
Madhya Pradesh . 


And whereas , the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely " Dainik 
Bhaskar ( Hindi ) " & " Raj Express ( Hindi ) " on 06th December 2022 by Upper Collector and Competent Authority , 
Ratlam ( M.P. ) under sub section ( 4 ) of section 20A of the said act ; 


And whereas , for Karoda village of Ratlam District , no objection received to the Upper Collector and 
competent Authority , Ratlam ( M.P. ) ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section 20E of the said Act , the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government ; 


Now therefore , upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 20E of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ;
