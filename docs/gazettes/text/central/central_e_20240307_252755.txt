S.O. 1121(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 3 of the 
Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986), read with paragraph 20 of the notification of the Government of 
India in the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation 
(erstwhile Ministry of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation) vide number S.O. 3187(E), 
dated the 7" October, 2016 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), the 
Central Government hereby includes the following experts nominated by the State of Uttar Pradesh as Members 
in the State Ganga Committee for the State of Uttar Pradesh, namely:- 


i. Shri Raman Kant, Founder, Neer Foundation, Meerut - Member; 
ii. Shri Farooq Rehman Khan, State Program Director, WaterAid India, Uttar Pradesh - Member; 
iii. Shri Suresh Babu, Director, Rivers Wetlands and Water Policy Division, W.W.F., India - Member; 


iv. Dr. Radha Krishan Dixit, Associate Professor/ Ganga Activist, K.A.P.G. College, Kasganj, Uttar Pradesh - 
Member; 


v. Shri Avdesh Kumar, Rashtriya Mantri, Ganga Samagra - Member. 


2. The nominated members shall hold office for a term of two years from the date of publication of this 
notification. 


3. The allowances, if any, of the nominated experts shall be governed by the rules of the State Government.
