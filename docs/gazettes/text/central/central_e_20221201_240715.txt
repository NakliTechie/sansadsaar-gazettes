NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st December, 2022 


S.O. 5578(E).—Whereas the Central Government in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, 
declared its intention to acquire the additional land specified in the Schedule annexed to the notification bearing No. 
5.00. 3592 (E) dated. 01.08.2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3 issued under sub- 
section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) for 
building (widening / four laning etc) maintanance, management and operation of Bye pass Road on NH-59 near 
Berhampur Town from Ratanpur to Raghunathpur through the villages Ratanpur, Jagadalpur, Krupasindhupur, 
Nimakhandi, Borigam, Jhadankuli, Badagumula, Badakusasthali, Narendrapur, Laxmipur & Raghunathpur from 
Km 336/350 to 349/910 Km (13.56 Km.) including 4 lane ROB in the State of Odisha through EPC mode. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ THE SAKALA” and “THE 
DHARITRI” both dated 01.09.2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas No objection has been received within the statutory period of the aforesaid notification. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
