ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 10th November , 2022 

O.N. 70 ( E ) .– WHEREAS , the seat of Shri Mulayam Singh Yadav , in the House of the People , 
elected from 21 - Mainpuri Parliamentary Constituency in the State of Uttar Pradesh has become vacant on 
10th October , 2022 by the reason of his death ; and 

2 . 

WHEREAS , a bye - election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused ; 

3 . 

NOW , THEREFORE , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 149 , and Sections 30 
and 56 of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 ( 43 of 1951 ) , the Election Commission of India 
hereby : 


( A ) calls upon the said 21 - Mainpuri Parliamentary Constituency in the State of Uttar Pradesh to 
elect , before the 10th December , 2022 ( Saturday ) , and in accordance with the provisions of 
the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder , a person for the purpose of filling 
the said vacancy in the House of the People ; 


( B ) appoints , with respect to the said election : 

( a ) the 17th November , 2022 ( Thursday ) , as the last date for making nominations ; 

( b ) the 18th November , 2022 ( Friday ) , as the date for the scrutiny of nominations ; 


( c ) the 21st November , 2022 ( Monday ) , as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures ; 

( d ) 

the 5th December , 2022 ( Monday ) , as the date on which a poll shall , if necessary , be 
taken ; 


( e ) the 10th December , 2022 ( Saturday ) , as the date before which the election shall be 
completed ; and 


( C ) fixes the hours from 07:00 am to 06:00 pm as the hours during which the poll shall , if 
necessary , be taken on the date specified above , for the election .
