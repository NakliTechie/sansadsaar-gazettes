MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2023 


S.O. 10(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH127B in 
the stretch of land from Km. 0.00 to Km. 36.635 (Office of the Deputy Commissioner (Revenue Branch), East Garo 
Hills District, Williamnagar) in the district of East Garo Hills in the state of MEGHALAYA, hereby declares its 
intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Deputy Commissioner (I/c 
Revenue) in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
