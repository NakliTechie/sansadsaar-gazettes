MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 


NOTIFICATION 


New Delhi, the 12th June, 2026 


S.O. 3086(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 1274(E) dated 11/03/2026 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 
National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Construction of Toll 
Plaza maintenance, management and operation of National Highway 166F in the stretch of land Km 0 to Km 


23.447 in the District of Raigad in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.0.1274(E) dated 11/03/2026, has been published in 
“Dainik Krushival” and “Dainik Aapal Mahanagar” Both are Dated 18/03/2026; under sub-section (3) of section 


3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
