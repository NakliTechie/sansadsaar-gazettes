NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 31st March , 2023 

S.O. 1620 ( E ) .— In exercise of the power conferred by clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway Amendment 
Act , 2008 , Ministry of Railways notifies Development of Freight & Coaching Terminal at Bijwasan Project as a 
Special Railway Project for acquisition of balance 0.8075 Hectare land ( 9 Bigha - 12 Biswa ) in Village- Bhartal , 
District - South West , Delhi State , for providing National infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the official gazette .
