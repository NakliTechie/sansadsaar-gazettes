G.S.R. 193. – In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution , the 
President hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the post of Deputy Superintendent 
of Police ( Training / Instructor ) in the Central Detective Training Institutes , Bureau of Police Research and 
Development , Ministry of Home Affairs , namely : 


Short title and commencement.- ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Bureau of Police Research and 
Development , Central Detective Training Institutes , Deputy Superintendent of Police ( Training / Instructor ) , 
Recruitment Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. Initial Constitution . The persons holding the post mentioned in the Schedule on the date of commencement 
of these rules on regular basis shall be deemed to have been appointed under these rules and the service rendered by 
them in the said post on regular basis before the said commencement shall be taken into account for deciding their 
period of probation , seniority , pensionary benefits and eligibility for promotion to the next higher grade . 


3 . 

Number of posts , classification and level in the pay matrix.- The number of post , its classification and the 
level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in Columns ( 2 ) to ( 4 ) of the Schedule annexed to these 
rules . 


4. Method of recruitment , age - limit , qualifications , etc. – The method of recruitment , age limit , qualifications 
and other matters relating thereto shall be as specified in Columns ( 5 ) to ( 13 ) of the said Schedule . 

5. Disqualifications . - No person , - 

( a ) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living ; or 

( b ) who , having a spouse living , has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person ; 
shall be eligible for appointment to the said post :
