S.O. 403 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 37 of the National Food 
Security Act , 2013 ( 20 of 2013 ) , the Central Government , on being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient so to 
do , hereby amends the nutritional standards specified in Schedule - II to the said Act . In the National Food Security 
Act , 2013 , for Schedule II , the following Schedule shall be substituted , namely : 

SCHEDULE II 


The nutritional standards as specified in the table below shall be provided to 

( i ) persons in the age group or physiological group under column ( 1 ) of the said Table , and shall be met by 
providing the type of meal as specified in column ( 2 ) thereof , in accordance with the Integrated Child 
Development Services Scheme ' and 


( ii ) children in the lower and upper primary classes under column ( 1 ) of the said Table , and shall be met by 
providing the type of meal as specified in column ( 2 ) thereof , under the PM Poshan Scheme .
