New Delhi , the 12th January , 2023 

S.O. 207 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub - section ( 1 ) of 
section 4 of the Mines and Minerals ( Development and Regulation ) Act , 1957 ( 67 of 1957 ) , the Central 
Government hereby rescinds the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Mines number 
S.O. 2307 ( E ) , dated the 18th May , 2022 , published in Gazette of India , Part II , section 3 , sub - section ( ii ) , 
dated the 18th May , 2022 , except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such rescission , with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification .
