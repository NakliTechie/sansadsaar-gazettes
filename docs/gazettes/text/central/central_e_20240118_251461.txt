MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th January, 2024 


S.O. 252(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 49H of the Wild Life 
(Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972), the Central Government hereby designates the following as the ports of entry 
and exit for the purposes of Chapter VB of the said Act for repatriation of red sanders from Nepal and makes the 
following amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Environment, Forest and 
Climate Change, number S.O. 3547(E) dated 87 August 2023, namely:— 


In the said notification,—after serial number 12 and the entries relating thereto, the following serial 
numbers and entries shall be inserted, namely:- 


“13. Ruxaul; 
14. Panitanki; 
15. Jogbani; 
16. Sanauli; 
17. Nepalgunj; 
18. Banbasa.”. 


[F. No. WL-1/11/2023-WL] 
BIVASH RANJAN, Addl. Director General of Forests (WL) and Director, Wild Life Preservation
