PUBLIC NOTICE 


New Delhi, the 28th February, 2024 
No. 45/2023 


Subject: Amendments in Standard Input Output Norms (SION) (-83] for export of Stainless Steel Seamless 


Tubes/ Pipes (Cold Finished) 


F. No. -0/8/62/00002/AM22/DES-II.—In exercise of powers conferred under Paragraph .03 and 2.04 


of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade makes the 
following amendments in Standard Input Output Norms (SION) C-83! for export of Stainless Steel Seamless Pipes/ 
Tubes (Cold Finish): 


SI. No. Export Item Quantity Item of import Quantity 

| Stainless Steel Seamless | MT Seamless Stainless-Steel .0 MT 
Tubes/Pipes (Cold Finish) Tubes/Pipes (Hot Finished) 

Note: 


l. The imported seamless stainless-steel pipe/tubes shall be hot-finished/ mother hollow having an outside diameter 


(OD) not less than 65mm and above. 


2. Exporters have to provide a specific grade of pipes to be imported as input and such grade must correspond with 
the grade of export pipes/tubes. 


3. The export weight per meter of cold-finished pipes/tubes shall not exceed 50% of the weight per meter of 
imported hot-finished / mother hollow consumed as an input to produce the cold finish pipe/tube for export. 


4. Pre-Import condition shall be mandatory while issuing the duty-free licenses. 


2. Effect of this Public Notice: 
Standard Input Output Norms (SION) appearing under (-83] is amended as above with immediate effect.
