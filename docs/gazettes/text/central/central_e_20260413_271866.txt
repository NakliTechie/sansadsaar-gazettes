objector the opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allowor 


disallow the objections. 
Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final.
