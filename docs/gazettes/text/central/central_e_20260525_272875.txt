NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th May, 2026 


S.O. 2645(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways. 8.0.2323 (E) Dated 26/05/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 3, 
Sub-Section (ii) Issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with pave shoulder/four laning etc.) 
Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 61 SPUR on the stretch of land from Km. 
13+880 to Km. 69+820 Junnar-Ghodegaon Taleghar Section of Bankarphata-Junnar Taleghar Road in the 
district of Pune in the State of Maharashtra. 

And whereas the substance of the said notification . S.0.2323 (E) Dated 26/05/2025 has been published in 
"Dainik Kesari” And “Dainik Katib” both dated 14/03/2026 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately: 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
