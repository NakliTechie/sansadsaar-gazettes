INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the l0th March, 2025 


F.No.8-05/30 -INC: In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Gautam Institute of Nursing situated at Chandi — Hilsa Road, P.o.- Jaitpur, Vill.- Saiyad 
Bahri, Tehsil - Tharthari, Distt. Nalanda 80307 Bihar is recognized by State Government of Bihar and Bihar 
Nurses Registration Council to conduct ANM and GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-05/l3ll0-INC (58304) dated 20/05/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section l4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the Principal Secretary (H&FW) Department of Health, 
Government of Bihar, Vikas Bhawan, New Secretariat, Bihar was issued. Copy of the notice dated 20/05/2024 was 
also enclosed Gautam Institute of Nursing situated at Chandi — Hisla Road, P.o.- Jaitpur, Vill.- Saiyad Bahri, 
Tehsil - Tharthari, Distt. Nalanda 80307 Bihar In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Gautam Institute of Nursing. Even the institution Gautam Institute of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
26/02/2024 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM and GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
26/l0/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM and GNM from Gautam Institute of Nursing 
situated at Chandi - Hisla Road, P.o.- Jaitpur, Vill.- Saiyad Bahri, Tehsil - Tharthari, Distt. Nalanda 80307 
Bihar shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Bihar. 


Lt. Col. (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
