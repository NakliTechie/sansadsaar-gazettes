rules to amend the Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital Region and 
Adjoining Areas (Allowance payable to the Member, other than ex officio Member of the Sub- 
Committees) Rules, 2023, namely:- 


l.() These rules may be called the Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital 
Region and Adjoining Areas (Allowance payable to the Member, other than ex officio Member of 
the Sub-Committees) Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

Areas (Allowance payable to the Member, other than ex officio Member of the Sub-Committees) 
Rules, 2023, in rule 2,-
