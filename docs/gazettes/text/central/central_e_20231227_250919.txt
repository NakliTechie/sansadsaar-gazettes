THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA, NEW DELHI 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th December, 2023 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 

No. PPR/1/S/09/DD/1/S/INF/09/DC/42/09: In terms of the provisions of Section 21B(3) of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rule 18(17) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held 
CA. Ravindernath Ch (Membership No. 204494), 5/3RT L I G H, Flat No. 202, Vijayanagar Colony, 
HYDERABAD (Telangana)-500057, guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clause (1) of 
Part II and Clause (7) of Part I of the Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act. After affording an opportunity of hearing 
in terms of Rule 19(1) of the aforesaid Rules, the Disciplinary Committee ordered for PERMANENT removal of the 
name of aforesaid CA. Ravindernath Ch (Membership No. 204494) from the Register of Members and also 
imposed a fine of Rs. 5,00,000/-(Rupees Five Lakh only). Aggrieved by Order of the Committee, the Respondent 
preferred an Appeal in terms of Section 22G of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, wherein an interim stay was 
granted vide Order dated 25" February, 2012. However, the Appellate Authority vide its Order dated 5" September, 
2012 dismissed the appeal and upheld the impugned Order of the Disciplinary Committee. In compliance of the same, 
the Respondent had deposited a demand draft of Rs. 5,00,000/-(Rupees Five Lakhs only) in favour of ICAI against the 
fine imposed upon him in the matter. Aggrieved by the said Order, CA. Ravindernath Ch (Membership No. 
204494) filed a Writ Petition bearing no. 554/2013, before the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi, wherein an interim stay 
was grated on 187 February, 2013. However, the said Writ Petition was dismissed by the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi 
vide its Order dated 247 May, 2023, on account of non-prosecution. In pursuance thereof and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified under Regulation 18 of the 
Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988 that the name of said CA. Ravindernath Ch (Membership No. 204494), 
shall stand PERMANENTLY removed from the Register of Members with effect from 27" December, 2023. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secy.
