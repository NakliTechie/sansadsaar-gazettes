आयुक्त (भूमि प्रबंधन) को 'दिल्ली विकास अधिनियम, 1957' [जिसे 'जन विश्वास (प्रावधानों में संशोधन) अधिनियम, 2026'

(Adjudicating Officer) rach करती है; और दिल्‍ली विकास प्राधिकरण के प्रधान आयुक्त (भूमि प्रबंधन) को 'अपीलीय 
प्राधिकरण' (Appellate Authority) नियुक्त करती है, जो निर्णायक अधिकारी द्वारा पारित आदेशों के खिलाफ अपील की 
सुनवाई करेंगे।

MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 10th August, 2026 


F. No. K-11011/07/2026-DD-II.— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) 
of section 31AA of the Delhi Development Act, 1957 (61 of 1957), the Central Government hereby appoints 
the Commissioner (Land Management), Delhi Development Authority, as the Adjudicating Officer for 
adjudication of matters under the relevant provisions of the Delhi Development Act, 1957, as amended by 
the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 and the Principal Commissioner (Land Management), 
Delhi Development Authority, as the Appellate Authority to hear appeals against the orders passed by the 
Adjudicating Officer. 


SURYA NARAYAN JHA, Under Secy.
