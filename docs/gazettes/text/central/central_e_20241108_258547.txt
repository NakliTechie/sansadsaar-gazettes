MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[South Eastern Railway (Construction Organization) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 8th November, 2024 


S.O. 4862(E).—Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (South 
Eastern Railway/Construction Organization), number S.0.2472(B), dated the 257 June, 2024 published in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 27" June, 2024 and issued under sub section (1) of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989)(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, 
decided its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the notification number S.0.2472(E), 
dated the 25" June, 2024 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
Bondamunda(Dumetra)- Ranchi(Hatia) doubling (158.50 Km.) in the district of Sundargarh in the State of Odisha; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers, namely ‘Sakal’ 
(Odiya Publication), dated 26" July, 2024 and ‘Navbharat’ (Hindi Publication), dated 19" July, 2024 under section (4) 
of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no petition/objection has been received from any person by the competent authority during 
hearing of objection under section 20D of the said Act; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on the publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
