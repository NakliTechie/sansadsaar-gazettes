सा.का.नि. 545 (अ).--- जबकि माननीय प्रधानमंत्री ने लाल किले से ड्रोनों के प्रचालन और मरम्मत के 
लिए महिला स्वयं सहायता समूह को वित्तीय सहायता और प्रशिक्षण प्रदान करने के लिए “ड्रोन की उड़ान” पहल 
की घोषणा की है; 


और जबकि उद्योग और सरकार ने देखा कि उभरते ड्रोन स्वामियों के पास पासपोर्ट नहीं है; 


और जबकि ड्रोन नियमावली, 2024 में अपेक्षित है कि पंजीकरण (फॉर्म डी-2) और पंजीकरण समाप्त 
करने अथवा हस्तांतरण (फॉर्म डी-3) के लिए इच्छुक व्यक्ति के पास भारतीय पासपोर्ट हो; 

और जबकि गृह मंत्रालय (आंतरिक सुरक्षा), कृषि और किसान कल्याण मंत्रालय और विभिन्न हितधारकों 
के साथ विधिवत परामर्श के पश्चात, जिन्होंने ऐसी अपेक्षा से छूट दिये जाने पर सहमति व्यक्त की थी, क्‍योंकि 


इससे व्यापार करने की सुगमता में मदद मिलेगी, ड्रोन संबंधी अवसर आम जनों तक पहुंचेंगे और जन सेवा प्रदान 
करने में ड्रोन अनुप्रयोगों के प्रवेश में वृद्धि होगी;

MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2lst August, 2024 
Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2024 


G.S.R_ 55(E).—Whereas the Hon’ble Prime Minister from the red fort announced “Drone Ki Udaan”- an 
initiative to provide financial support and training to women’s self-help group for operating and repairing drones; 


And whereas the industry and government saw that the emerging drone owners do not own a passport; 


And whereas the Drone Rules, 202! require an individual to possess an Indian Passport for registration 
(Form D-2) and de-registration or transfer (Form D-3); 


And whereas after due consultation with the Ministry of Home Affairs (Internal Security), the Ministry of 
Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and various stakeholders who conveyed their consent on dispensation of such 
requirement as it would contribute to ease of doing business, democratise the opportunity of drones and increase in 
penetration of drone applications in public service delivery; 


[भाग I—ave 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas in Public Interest, the Central Government has been dispensed with the Condition of previous 
publication as required under the provisio to Section 4 of the Aircraft Act, 934 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5, sub-section (2) of section 0 and sections 
l0A, l0B and 2A of the Aircraft Act, 934 (22 of 934), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
further to amend the Drone Rules, 202, namely:— 


(.) These rules may be called the Drone (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: In Form D-2 to the Drone Rules, 202! (hereinafter referred to as Principal Rules), under Part A 
relating to details of individual (if applicable), for the entry against serial number 5, the following entries shall be 
substituted, namely :— 


“5. the Indian passport number (for individual) or proof of identity and proof of address that is, a Voter’s ID 
card, Ration Card or Driving License issued by the Government” 


3. In Form D-3 to the Principal Rules, 202!, under Part B relating to Transferor or Owner details, for the 
entry against serial number 6, the following entries shall be substituted, namely :— 


“6. the Indian passport number (for individual) or proof of identity and proof of address that is, a Voter’s ID 
card, Ration Card or Driving License issued by the Government” 


4. In Form D-3 to the Principal Rules, 202, under Part C relating to Transferee details (if applicable), 
sub-part relating to Details of individual (if applicable) , for the entry against serial number 8, the following entries 
shall be substituted, namely :-— 


“[]6, Indian passport number (for individual) or proof of identity and proof of address that is, a Voter’s ID 
card, Ration Card or Driving License issued by the Government”.
