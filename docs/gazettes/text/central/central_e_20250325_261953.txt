MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th March, 2025 
F. No. 2022/E&R/(3)/9.—It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view the operational 
requirements, Central Government has decided that with respect to the new chord line between Katangikhurd 
(KTKD) station of West Central Railway and Jhalwara (JLW) station of South East Central Railway, the jurisdiction 
from ESP CH:0.00 to ESP CH: 78.26m (SRJ of 22B) will lie with Jabalpur division of West Central Railway and 
from ESP CH:78.26m (SRJ of 22B) to ESP CH:!50.00m will lie with Bilaspur division of South East Central 
Railway. 


Above jurisdiction will come into force with effect from the date of commissioning of chord line.
