INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, |5th February, 2024 


F.No. 8-8/248-INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Sanjivani College of Nursing situated at Indore Ahmedabad Road, Tirla, Distt. Dhar — 45400, 
Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration 
Council to conduct GNM, B.Sc (N) and Post Basic B.Sc (N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-8/248l-INC dated 0/04//2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section |4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24], Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 0/04/2022 was also enclosed to Sanjivani College of Nursing situated at Indore Ahmedabad Road, 
Tirla, Distt. Dhar — 45400, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit 
their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Sanjivani College of Nursing, Madhya Pradesh. The Sanjivani College of Nursing had sent letter with regard to 
notice. Thereafter the Council issued an email dated 20/06/2022 to the Sanjivani College of Nursing. However no 
action, as required, has been taken by the Sanjivani College of Nursing till date. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
0/06/202 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 30/05/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM, B.Sc (N) and Post 
Basic B.Sc (७) Sanjivani College of Nursing situated at Indore Ahmedabad Road, Tirla, Distt. Dhar — 45400, 
Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State 
government of Madhya Pradesh.
