competent Authority;

submitted its report to the Central Government;

conferred by the sub-section (]) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said
