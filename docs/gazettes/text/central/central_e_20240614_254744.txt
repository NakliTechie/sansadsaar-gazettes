iority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after 
ing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by 
r, either allow or disallow the objections.

inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
