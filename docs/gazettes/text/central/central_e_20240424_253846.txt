MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th April, 2024 


S.O. 1778(E).— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Housing and Urban 
Affairs vide number 5.0. 2065 (E) dated the 25" June, 2019 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) dated the 25" June, 2019 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) the Central 
Government made alteration to the metro alignment of Pune Metro Line-3 Hinjewadi- Shivajinagar in respect of Pune 
Metropolitan Area; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that further alteration is necessary for the construction 
and maintenance of the Metro Railways to which this alignment relates; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 32 of the 
Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978 (33 of 1978), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the said notification, namely:- 


In the said notification,— 
(a) In the table, - 


(i) for the words and figure "Pune Metro Line-3-Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar", the words and figure "Pune Metro 
Line-3- Maan-Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar" shall be substituted; 


(ii) for the words "Civil Court", the words "District Court, Pune" shall be substituted. 
(b) In the Schedule, — 


(i) for the words, figure and brackets "Pune Metro Line-3 (Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar)", the words, figure and 
brackets "Pune Metro Line-3 (Maan-Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar)" shall be substituted; 


(ii) for the words "CIVIL COURT", the words "District Court, Pune" shall be substituted.
