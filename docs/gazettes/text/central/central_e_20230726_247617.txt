MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th July, 2023 


S.O. 3344(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (2! of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer resources 
relating to the Core Banking Solution, Real Time Gross Settlement, National Electronic Fund Transfer and Unified 
Payments Interface Switch, being Critical Information Infrastructure of the IDBI Bank Ltd., and the computer 
resources of its associated dependencies, to be protected systems for the purpose of the said Act and authorises the 
following personnel to access the protected systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the IDBI Bank Ltd. authorised in writing by the IDBI Bank Ltd. to access 
the protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have been 
authorised in writing by the IDBI Bank Ltd. for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing by the 
IDBI Bank Ltd. on case to case basis. 


2: This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
