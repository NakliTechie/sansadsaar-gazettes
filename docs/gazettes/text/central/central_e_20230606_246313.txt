कच्चा तेल के परिवहन के लिए पाइपलाइन बिछाने का प्रस्ताव है। पारादीप नुमालीगढ़ कच्चा तेल पाइपलाइन

New Delhi, the 5th June, 2023 


5.0. 2450 (E).—Whereas Numaligarh Refinery Limited, is now proposed to lay Paradip Numaligarh Crude Oil 


Pipeline (PNCPL) for transportation of Crude Oil from Paradip (Odisha) to Numaligarh (Assam). The proposed ROU 
of Paradip Numaligarh Crude Oil Pipeline (PNCPL) traverses in the same route that of Indradhanush Gas Grid 
Limited’s Guwahati-Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL) for 22.26 Km. in Majuli (Assam) District. 


Numaligarh Refinery Limited proposes to make use of about 22.26 Km. common ROU corridor of 
Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited’s Guwahati - Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL), which was acquired initially 
by Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited published in Central Gazette vide, 8.0. 336(E) Dated 20 January, 202] published 
in the Central Gazette No. 305 dated 22 January, 202l, S.O. 4394(E) Dated 04 October, 202 published in the Central 
Gazette No. 4042 dated 22 October, 202, S.O. 3947(E) Dated 2 August, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 
3773 dated 23 August, 2022, 8.0. 4886(E) Dated 3 October, 2022 published in the Central Gazette No. 4676 dated 
4 October, 2022. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 


Government hereby directs that the right of user in the lands as specified in the schedule appended to this notification 
for laying the pipeline shall, Jointly vest on the date publication of the declaration, in the Indradhanush Gas Grid 
Limited for laying the Guwahati - Numaligarh Natural Gas Pipeline (GNPL) as well as for laying Paradip Numaligarh 


Crude Oil Pipeline (PNCPL) by Numaligarh Refinery Limited for 22.26 Km. in Majuli (Assam) District free from all 
encumbrances.
