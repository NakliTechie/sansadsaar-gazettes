New Delhi, the 29th February, 2024 


S.O. 956(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 4535 Dated:6/0/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH965 in 
the stretch of land from Km. 76 to Km. 226 (New collector office , Pune) in the district of PUNE in the state of 
MAHARASHTRA

dated Newspaper /262.30// //2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby
