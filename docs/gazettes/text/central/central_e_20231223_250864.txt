G.S.R. 94 (E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24

occasion of Khartargachha Millennium, Founder Jaincharya Shri Jineshwar Suri) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
