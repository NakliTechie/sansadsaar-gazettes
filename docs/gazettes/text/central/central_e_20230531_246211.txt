MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(Department of Higher Education) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd March, 2023 


S.O. 236I(E).— Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 30 of the 
National Institutes of Technology, Science Education and Research Act, 2007 (29 of 2007) (herein after referred to as 
the ‘Act’), the Central Government vide Notification No.S.0.893 (E), dated the [ (ha April, 2008, published in the 
Gazette of India, established ‘The Council’ for all the Institutes specified in the ‘First Schedule’, ‘Second Schedule’ 
and ‘Third Schedule’ of the Act. 


2. And whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred under clause (j) of sub-section (2) of section 30 of the 
Act, the following Members of Parliament have been chosen as the members of the National Institutes of Technology, 
Science Education and Research Council by the Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha, respectively: 


(i) Shri Ghanshyam Tiwari, Member, Rajya Sabha; 
(ii) Shri Shankar Lalwani, Member, Lok Sabha; and 
(iii) Shri Praveen Kumar Nishad, Member, Lok Sabha. 


3. The term of office of, vacancies among, and allowances payable to members of Council and the 
functions of the Council shall be as specified under section 3 and section 32, respectively, of the Act.
