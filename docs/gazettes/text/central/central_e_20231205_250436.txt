And whereas, Central Electricity Authority (CEA), Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter

Subsequently, M/s TEQ Green Power XI Private Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 07.10.2023 declaring that 
no observation/representation was received within Two months from the date of Publication in the official gazette of 
Government of India.

Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of
