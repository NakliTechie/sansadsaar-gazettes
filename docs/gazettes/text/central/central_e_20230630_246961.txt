MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2023 


S.O. 2860(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the lands, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is 
required for building (Widening/Four laning/Six Laning etc), maintenance, management and operation of proposed 
Bangalore - Chennai Expressway in the stretch of lands from Design Chainage Km. 0/000 to Km. 11/450 in the 


Districts of Bangalore Rural in the State of Karnataka, hereby declares its intention to acquire such lands. 


Any person interested in the said lands may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
Notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such lands for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition Officer & 
Competent Authority, Bangalore - Chennai Expressway, No. 678/3, Neerubhavi Kempanna Layout, Hebbal, 
Bangalore 560 024, Karnataka, in writing, and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, 


either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 


final. 


The land plans and other details of the lands covered under this Notification are available and can be 


inspected by the interested persons at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
