ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the !4th August, 2024 


O. N. 64(E).— WHEREAS, the seat of Shri Vivek Thakur, in the Council of States, elected by the elected 
members of the Legislative Assembly of Bihar, has become vacant on 04.06.2024 by the reason of his election to the 
8" Lok Sabha, before the expiration of his term of office which was up to and including 09.04.2026; and 


WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 47, sub-section (l) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95 (43 of 95), the Election Commission hereby- 


(A) calls upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Bihar to elect, before 06" September, 2024 
and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder, a person for the 
purpose of filling the said vacancy in the Council of States; 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 2* August, 2024 (Wednesday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 22™ August, 2024 (Thursday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 


(c) the 27" August, 2024 (Tuesday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 03" September, 2024 (Tuesday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 067 September, 2024 (Friday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election.
