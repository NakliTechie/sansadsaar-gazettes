NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th February, 2024 


S.O. 538(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 26 of the 
Anusandhan National Research Foundation Act, 2023 (25 of 2023), the Central Government hereby 
appoints the Secretary to the Government of India in the Department of Science and Technology as the 
interim Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Anusandhan National Research Foundation with effect from 
the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette for a period of six months or till further 
orders, whichever is earlier.
