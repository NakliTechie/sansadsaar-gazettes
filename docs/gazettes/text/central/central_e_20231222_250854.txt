MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 22nd December, 2023 


9.0. 5437(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.2796 (E), dated the 267 June, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 287 June, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway 
Project, namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor in the District of Ludhiana in the State of Punjab; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification for inviting objections from public thereby was 
published in daily newspapers, namely “The Tribune” and “Ajit”, dated 267 July, 2023 under sub-section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 
AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 


authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
