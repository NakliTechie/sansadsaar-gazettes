विचार किया जा सकेगा और नियंत्री कंपनी भारत में या भारत के बाहर निगमित हो सकती है:", शब्द रखे जाएंगे।

MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l4th May, 2025 


G.S.R. 3l(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 35 of the Offshore Areas Mineral 
(Development and Regulation) Act, 2002 (7 of 2003), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to 
amend the Offshore Areas Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2024, namely:— 


I. () These rules may be called the Offshore Areas Mineral (Auction) Amendment Rules, 2025. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Offshore Areas Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2024, in Schedule I, in the Explanation, in clause (), for the portion 
beginning with the words “an applicant is a subsidiary” and ending with the words “company may also be considered”, 
the words “an applicant is a subsidiary company, the net worth of the holding company of such subsidiary company 
may also be considered and the holding company may be incorporated in India or outside India” shall be substituted.

DINESH MAHUR, Jt. Secy.
