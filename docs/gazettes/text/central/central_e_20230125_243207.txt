DELHI DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 25th January , 2023 

Transfer of land parcel measuring 14.95 Acres allotted by DDA to AIIMS for construction of Trauma center to 
L & DO / MOHUA for implementation of Master Plan of AIIMS 


S.O. 423 ( E ) .– Delhi Development Authority hereby transfers 14.95 acres of land , allotted by DDA to AIIMS , 
New Delhi , for construction of Trauma Center , to Land and Development Office / MoHUA ( Central Government ) 
under Section 22 ( 4 ) of the Delhi Development Act , 1957 for the purpose of implementation of the Master Plan of 
AIIMS , New Delhi . The land was placed at the disposal of DDA by Central Government under section 22 ( 1 ) of Delhi 
Development Act , 1957 on terms and conditions specified in Authority Resolution no . 114 dated 06.05.1961 . 
The transfer of said land will be subject to the following terms and conditions : - 


The land parcel already stands allotted to AIIMS , and therefore , no fresh lease premium shall be charged . 
This allotment was earlier made by charging No - Profit , No - Loss ( NPNL ) / Institutional rates . Therefore , the 
Annual Ground Rent would be fixed on the basis of current NPNL / Institutional Rates . 


3. The following conditions laid down at Sl . No. 8 ( vi ) of DDA's Resolution No. 114 dated 06.05.1961 will be 
strictly complied with : - 

a . Due observance of municipal bye - laws ; 

b . Use of the land for the purpose for which it is allotted ; 

c . Construction within the stipulated time ; 


d . Requiring permission before transferring any interest in the land ; 

e . Sharing with the Government fifty per cent of unearned increase ( UEI ) on transfer of land ; 

f . Obligation to pay ground rent regularly ; and 

g . Re - entry and forfeiture of premium in part or in full , if any , of the above conditions is not 
fulfilled . 


4. The outstanding dues , including arrears of Ground Rent , from the date of allotment of land by DDA to 
AIIMS to the date of transfer of the said land to Central Government are to be paid by AIIMS to DDA .
