MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th August, 2024 


S.O. 3277(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, 1425(E) Dated:15/03/2024 , published in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 
3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH367 in the stretch of land from Km 70.00 to Km 


71.500 (Bhanapur to Gaddankeri section) in the district of GADAG in the state of KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Vijayvaani” and “Vijay 


Karnataka” both dated 11-05-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 5 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered 


and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent 


authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid 


purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in 


the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
