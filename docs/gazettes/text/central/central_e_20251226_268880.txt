S.O. 6041(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways

section (1) of section 20D of the said Act.

(254.4 Km), in writing, and shall set out grounds thereof and competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity 
of being heard, either in person or through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after 
making such further inquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections.
