NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist February, 2023 


S.O. 467(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.0.l48(E) dated 4" March 2022 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 76 on the stretch of land from Km. 29.03 to Km. 0.620 (Kadapa to Renigunta 
section) in the district of Annamayya in the State of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “THE HANS INDIA” and 
“ANDHRA JYOTHI” both dated 767 March 2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent Authority; 


Govt. of Andhra Pradesh restructured / formed new Districts from |3 Districts to 26 Districts and out of these 
Districts; Annamayya is one of the new Districts. Accordingly, Central Government authorized Joint Collector, 
Annamayya as Competent Authority for Land Acquisition for the villages falling in Annamayya District from 
erstwhile Y.S.R Kadapa District by the Notification of Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.O. 2455(E) dated 30th May 2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
Section-3, Sub-section (ii), dated 30th May 2022. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
