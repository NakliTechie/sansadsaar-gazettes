MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th September, 2023 


S.O. 439(E)—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4l (EK) Dated:0/03/2023, 3075 (E) Dated:/07/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, 
management and operation of NH56E in the stretch of land from Km. 2.!25 to Km. 26.937 (Bowdara - Vizianagaram 
Section) in the district of VIZIANAGARAM in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Andhra Prabha” dated 2.03.2023 
and 4.07.2023 and “The Hindu” 2.03.2023 and 4.07.2023 respectively, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
