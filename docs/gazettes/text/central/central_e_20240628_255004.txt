MINISTRY OF PARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th June, 2024 


No. F. 8/I/2024-MEW—WHEREAS, in pursuance of the provisions of Section 2 of the Salary and 
Allowances of Leaders of Opposition in Parliament Act, !977 (No. 33 of 977), the Speaker of the House of the 
People has recognized Shri Rahul Gandhi as the Leader of the Opposition in the House of the People with effect from 
9 June, 2024. 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 9 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby notifies Shri Rahul Gandhi as the Leader of the Opposition in the House of the People with effect 
from 97 June, 2024 for the purposes of the said Act. 


UMANG NARULA, Secy.
