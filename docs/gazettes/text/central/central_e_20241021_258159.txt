NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 2]st October, 2024 


S.O. 459(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways vide 
no. S.O. 2898 (E) Dated 22 July 2024 Published In Gazette of India Extraordinary, part II, section 3, sub section (ii), 
Vide No 276] dated the 22 July 2024, (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (]) 
of section 20(A) of the Railway (Amenbnent) Act, 2008 (ll of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government, declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project Namely, 
“Godda-Pirpainti New B.G. Rail Line”in the District of Bhagalpur in the State of Bihar. 


[भाग W—ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण ] 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers Namely," Hindustan, 
dated 25 July 2024& Prabhat Khabar", dated 25 July 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas, no objection was received and the same has been considered by the competent authority 
accordingly orders have been passed. 


And, whereas in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
