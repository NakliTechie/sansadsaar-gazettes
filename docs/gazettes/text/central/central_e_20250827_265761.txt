MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[East Coast Railway (Construction)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Bhubaneswar, the 27th August, 2025 
Notice Under Section 20E of the Railway Act, 1989 


5.0. 3934(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (East 
Coast Railway, Construction Organisation), Notification number, 5.0. 90 (E). dated the 37 January, 2025 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the the 37 January, 2025 (herein after referred 
to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 
(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 


[भाग ए---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Special Railway Project, namely, “3™ & 4 Line in between Visakhapatnam (VSKP)- Gopalapatnam (GPT) 
(15.31 Km.)” in Visakhapatnam district in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely "Sakshi" 
in Telugu (Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 05" February, 2025, and "The Times of India" in English (Visakhapatnam 
Edition) dated 06" February, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within the 
specified period; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
