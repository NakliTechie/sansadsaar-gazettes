NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th March, 2023 


8.0. 537(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 3800 (E), dated the | 07 August, 2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part IL, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 2™ August, 2022 and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, [989 (24 of l989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Western Dedicated Freight Corridor in the district 
of Raigad in the State of Maharashtra; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers namely, 
“The Times of India”, dated the 2" August, 2022 and “Lokmat”, dated the a August, 2022 under sub-section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, one objection has been received by the Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been 
passed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (]) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
