New Delhi, the 23rd July, 2025 


G.S.R. 489(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 8A read with clause (ca) of 
sub-section (2) of section 75 of the Disaster Management Act, 2005 (53 of 2005), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules, namely:- 


. Short title and commencement. - (l) These rules may be called the Disaster Management National Crisis 
Management Committee (Procedure) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Definitions. -() In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, - 
(a) “Act? means the Disaster Management Act, 2005 (53 of 2005); 
(b) “Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the National Crisis Management Committee referred to in 


sub-section (2) of section 8A of the Act. 


(2) Words and expressions used herein and not defined in these rules but defined in the Act shall have the 
meanings respectively assigned to them in the Act. 


3. Procedure to be followed by National Crisis Management Committee. - () The Chairperson of the National 
Crisis Management Committee shall preside over the meetings of the National Crisis Management Committee: 


Provided that in the case of his inability to preside over any meeting of the National Crisis Management 
Committee, he shall nominate one of Members of the National Crisis Management Committee to preside over the 
meeting. 


(2) The Chairperson of the National Crisis Management Committee shall decide the date, time and place and 
manner of holding a meeting of the National Crisis Management Committee. 


(3) The Chairperson of the National Crisis Management Committee may designate an officer from the Central 
Government as Convener.
