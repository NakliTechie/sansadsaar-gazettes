2, In order to provide benefits to the beneficiaries under the Scheme conveniently, the Ministry and Department

3. Where Aadhaar authentication fails due to poor biometrics of the beneficiaries or due to any other reason, the 
following remedial mechanisms shall be adopted, namely:- 


(a) in case of poor fingerprint quality, iris scan or face authentication facility shall be adopted for authentication 
and the Ministry/Department through its Implementing Agency shall make provisions for iris scanners or 
face authentication along with finger print authentication for delivery of benefits in seamless manner; 


(b) in case the biometric authentication through fingerprints or iris scan or face authentication is not successful,

(c) in all other cases where biometric or Aadhaar One Time Password or Time-based One-Time Password

letter whose authenticity can be verified through the Quick Response code printed on the Aadhaar letter and

the Ministry/Department through its Implementing Agency.
