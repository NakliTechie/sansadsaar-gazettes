The functions of the Committee will be to advise the Ministry of Power in matters relating to the 
progressive use of Hindi for Official work and allied issues falling within the frame-work of the Official 
Language policy laid down by the Government of India. 


Tenure 


The tenure of the Committee will be three years from the date of its composition, provided that:- 
A Member of Parliament nominated to the Committee shall cease to be a member of the Committee 


as soon as he/she ceases to be a Member of Parliament. 


Official members of the Committee shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue 


of which they are the members of the Committee. 


Any mid-term vacancy shall be filled up by the concerned member's successor in office, who shall 


be a member of the Committee for the remaining term of three years. 


General 


The Headquarter of the Committee shall be at New Delhi but it may hold its meeting at any other 
station also. 


Travelling and Other Allowances 


The Non-Official Members will be paid travelling and daily allowance for attending the meeting of 
the Committee as per the guidelines contained in the Office Memorandum No. |/20034/4/86-OL(A-2) 
dated 22nd January, 987 of Department of Official Language and in accordance with the prescribed rates 
and rules amended from time to time by the Government of India.
