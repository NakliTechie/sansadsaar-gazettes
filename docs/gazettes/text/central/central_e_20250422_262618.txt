MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd April, 2025 


S.O. 86(E).— WHEREAS the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), 
clauses (५) and (xiv) of sub-section (2), and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 
of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, issued a notification to declare 
an Eco-Sensitive Zone around the Bir Mehas Wildlife Sanctuary, Punjab in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide S.O. 350(B), dated the 6" February, 207; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public 
interest to amend the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides that whenever 
it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for amending 
the notification number S.O. 350(B), dated the 6" February, 207; 


Now, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub-section 
(2) and sub-section(3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 
5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, !986, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide 5.0. 350(E), dated the 6" February, 207, namely:-

The Monitoring Committee shall, based on the actual site-specific conditions, scrutinise the activities covered

the Eco-sensitive Zone, except for the prohibited activities specified in the Table under paragraph 4 thereof, 
shall be scrutinised by the Monitoring Committee based on the actual site-specific conditions and referred to 
the concerned regulatory authorities. 


The Member-Secretary of the Monitoring Committee or the Collector or the Deputy Conservator of Forests 
shall be competent to file complaint under section 9 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986, against any 
person who contravenes the provisions of this notification. 


The Monitoring Committee may invite representative or expert from the Department, representative from the 
industry associations or the stakeholders to assist the committee in its deliberations depending on the 
requirements on case to case basis. 


The Monitoring Committee shall submit the action taken report annually of its activities for the period up to

The Central Government may give such directions in writing, as it deems fit, to the Monitoring Committee for 
effective discharge of its functions.”.
