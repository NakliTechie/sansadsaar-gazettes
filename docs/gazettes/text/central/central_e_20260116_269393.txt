G.S.R. 40(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of 
section 24 of the Coinage Act, 20l(/] of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, 
namely:— 


. Short title and commencement.— (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative 
Coin on the occasion of Diamond Jubilee Celebrations (Sixty years) of Chennai Petroleum Corporation 
Limited) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin — The coin of Sixty Rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government to commemorate the occasion of Diamond Jubilee 
Celebrations (Sixty years) of Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited.
