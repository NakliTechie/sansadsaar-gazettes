भुवनेश्वर, 15 सितम्बर, 2025 
रेलवे अधिनियम, 1989 की धारा 20ई के तहत सूचना 

का.आ. 4163(अ).-- केन्द्रीय सरकार ने रेल अधिनियम, 1989 (1989 का 24) (जिसके पश्चात अधिनियम कहा 
गया है) की धारा 20 (क) की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण भाग Il, खंड 3, उपखंड (ii) तारीख 
28 मार्च 2025, में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (पूर्व तट रेलवे) की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 1521(अ) 
28 मार्च 2025 द्वारा उक्त अधिनियम से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट को ओड़ीशा राज्य के सम्बलपुर जिले में विशेष रेल 


परियोजना अर्थात, “सरला-शासन के बीच तीसरी और चौथी लाइन (8 किमी)” के निष्पादन अनुरक्षण प्रबंध और प्रचालना 
के प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी । 


और, उक्त अधिसूचना का सार, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20क की अधीन दैनिक स्थानीय समाचार पत्रों अर्थात 
“प्रमेय” (ओड़ीआ- भूबनेस्वर संस्करण) और “द टाइम्स आंफ़ इंडिया” (अंगेजी - भूबनेस्वर संस्करण) तारीख 12 अप्रैल 2025, 
को प्रकाशित किया गया था।

लिए अर्जित की जाएगी ।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[East Coast Railway (Construction)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Bhubaneswar, the 15th September, 2025 
NOTICE UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT, 1989 


S.O. 4163(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(East Coast Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number, 5.0. 1521(E), dated 28" March, 2025 published 
in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated: 10 September, 2024 (here in after 
referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989)(here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely, “३37 and 4 Line between Sarla-Sason (8 Km)” in Sambalpur District 
in the State of Odisha . 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely 
“PRAMEYA” in Odia (Bhubaneswar Edition), and “The Times of India” in English (Bhubaneswar Edition), dated: 
12 April, 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 


passed in terms of section 20 D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to 


shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
