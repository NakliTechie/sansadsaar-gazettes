MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th August, 2024 


S.0. 3186 (E)—. Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 5186 (E), dated 7 December, 2023 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I], section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 3 (A) of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Land Acquisition for 
improvement and widening of NH-366 from Km. 11/300 to Km. 11/400 at St. Andrew 


Junction, Vasco in the State of Goa. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in The Times of India 
dated 15/12/2023; Sanatan Prabhat dated 15/12/2023 and Bhaangarbhuin, dated 15/12/2023 
under sub-section (3) of section 3 (A) of the said Act; 


And whereas, in response to the said notification and publication in the local news papers, the 
Competent Authority was in receipt of objections from persons interested in the land proposed for 


acquisition and the same have been considered by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3 (D) of the said Act, the competent 


authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3 (D) of the said Act, the Central Government 


hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3 (D) of the said Act, the Central

encumbrances.
