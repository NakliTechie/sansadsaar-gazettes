Mumbai, the 10th July, 2023 


S.O. 3035(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause 37A of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Extension of Harbour line between 
Goregaon to Borivali (7.08 km) and stabling lines (16 Nos.) under MUTP IIIA Project” as a Special 
Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Maharashtra, for providing national infrastructure for 
a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
