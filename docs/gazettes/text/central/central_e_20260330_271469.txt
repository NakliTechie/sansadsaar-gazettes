NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th March, 2026 


S.O. 648(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the third proviso to First Schedule of the Code on 
Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby notifies the Ist day of April, 2026, as the date on 
and from which the contribution from the employers and employees of establishments in the entire areas of the 
Kakching district in the State of Manipur shall be payable under section 29 and any benefits under Chapter IV relating 
to the Employees State Insurance Corporation are provided by the Corporation to the employees of such 
establishments.
