सूचनाओं के अनुसार ऐसी सूचना का अगोपनीय अंश भी साथ में प्रस्तुत करना अपेक्षित है। 


ऐसी प्रस्तुतियों को प्रत्येक पृष्ठ के शीर्ष पर स्पष्ट रूप से "गोपनीय" या "अगोपनीय" के रूप में चिह्टिनत किया जाना 
चाहिए। ऐसे चिहनों के बिना प्राधिकरण को किया गया कोई भी प्रस्तुतीकरण प्राधिकरण द्वारा "अगोपनीय"

A. Product under consideration (PUC). 


3. The product under consideration in the present investigation is Thiram in any form including its technical and

fungal diseases. In addition, it is used as an animal repellent to protect fruit trees and ornamentals from damage 
by rabbits, rodents, and deer. Thiram has been used in the treatment of human scabies, as a sunscreen, and as a 
bactericide applied directly to the skin or incorporated into soap.

Like article.

the applicant is being treated as like article to the product under consideration imported from the subject 
country. 


Subject country. 


The subject country in the present investigation is the European Union. 


Period of investigation (POD. 


The period of investigation (POI) for the present investigation is from |*' January 2023 to I*t December 2023 (a 
period of !2 months). The injury period for the investigation will cover the periods 2020-2l, 202l-22 and

Domestic industry and standing. 


The application has been filed by the Swarup Chemical Private Limited. Support letter has been filed by Amba 
Chem Industries. It has been submitted that Sudama Chemtech Pvt. Ltd is the one other producer of the product 
in India. 


Based on the information available, the production of the applicant accounts for a major proportion of the

country nor to any importers in India. 


In view of the above, the Authority prima facie considers that the applicant constitutes "domestic industry" 
within the meaning of Rule 2(b) of the Rules and the application satisfies the criteria of standing in terms of 
Rule 5(3) of the Rules. 


Normal value, export price and dumping. 


Normal value. 


The applicant has claimed that there is no published information regarding the selling price of the product in 
the domestic market in the European Union. The applicant has also submitted that there are restrictions 
imposed in the European Union on the sale of the product and therefore, it is unclear if there are sufficient sales 
of the product under consideration in the domestic market of the European Union. The applicant has, therefore, 
proposed the normal value based on its cost of production with reasonable addition for administrative, selling 
& general costs and for profits. The normal value proposed by the applicant has been considered for initiation 
of the investigation. 


Export Price 


The applicant has adopted the CIF import price into India as reported by the DGCI&S published data. 
However, the Authority has considered DG Systems transaction-wise data. Since the information is on CIF 
basis, adjustments have been made on account of ocean freight, marine insurance, commission, inland freight, 
port expenses, and bank charges to arrive at ex-factory export price. 


Dumping margin 


Considering the normal value and the export price as determined above, the dumping margin has been 
determined. It is noted that the dumping margin is significant and above the de minimis level. Thus, there is 
sufficient prima facie evidence that the normal value for the product under consideration is higher than the 
price at which it is exported from the subject country indicating that the product originating or exported from 
the subject country is being dumped in the Indian market by the exporters.

loss. The applicant has claimed that despite no demand-supply gap, the volume of subject imports has
