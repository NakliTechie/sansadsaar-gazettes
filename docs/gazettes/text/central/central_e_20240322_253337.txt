NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st March, 2024 
S.O. 1526(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 2012 (37 of 2012), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the notification of Government of India in 


Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, File Number Z-28016/65/2017-PMSSY-IV dated the 31st October, 2018 
published in the Gazette of India Part II, section 3, Sub-Section (ii) vide SO 6409(E) dated the 31st Dec, 2018: 


In the said notification, for serial number 6, 7 and 8 and the entry relating thereto, the following should be 
substituted, namely:- 


“6. Dr. Sangita Reddy, Joint Managing Director - Apollo Hospitals” 
“7. Secretary, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India” 
“8. Chief Secretary, Government of Andhra Pradesh” 


The terms of office of member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956.
