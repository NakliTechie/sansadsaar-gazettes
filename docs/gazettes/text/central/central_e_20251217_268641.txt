S.O. 5839(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railway (South

passed in terms of section 20D of the said Act;

submitted its report to the Central Government.

by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose.
