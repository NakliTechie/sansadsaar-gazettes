Prayagraj, the 23rd February, 2026 


S.O. 93(E) — Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(North Central Railway Engineering Department, notification no. S.O 498(E) dated 3 October 2025 
published in Gazette of India, extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub section (), notification no. 4767 dated 
29 October 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section () of clause 
20A of section 2 of the Railways Act 989 (24 of 989) and Railways Amendment act 2008 (l lof 2008) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the Land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of special Railway 
Project in district- Palwal, state - Haryana namely, “Construction of 02-lane road over bridge in lieu of level 
crossing No. 560 at km. 467/2-4 in Agra-Palwal section in Agra Division of North Central Railway”. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily news paper namely, HINDUSTAN 
TIMES DATED 3 November 2025 & AMAR UJALA, AGRA / MATHURA Dated 2 November 2025 
under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received in this regard. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the Land specified in the schedule 
annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
