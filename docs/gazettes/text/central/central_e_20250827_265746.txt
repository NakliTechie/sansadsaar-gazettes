गुजरात, दमन और दीव एवं दादरा और नगर हवेली के रूप में नियुक्त करते हैं ।

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th August, 2025 
F.No. A-35013/01/2016-Ad.III/199.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri Sujal Jayantibhai Mayatra, 
IAS (GJ:2011) an officer belonging to Gujarat Cadre of Indian Administrative Service as Director of Census 
Operations /Director of Citizen Registration, Gujarat, Daman & Diu and Dadra & Nagar Haveli with effect from 
21.8.2025 (FN) for a period of upto 31.12.2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation 
under the Central Staffing Scheme. 
2. The Headquarters of Shri Sujal Jayantibhai Mayatra, [AS (GJ:2011) will be Gandhinagar (Gujarat).
