अतः अब केन्द्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से उक्त रिपोर्ट के प्राप्त होने पर और रेल अधिनियम, 1989 ब रेलवे 
(संघोधन) की धारा ड की उप-धारा (1) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि इसमें उपबद्ध 
अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी 


यह और कि, केन्द्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20 ड की उप-धारा (2) के अनुसरण में यह और घोषणा 
करती है कि इस अधिसूचना के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित होने पर इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विल्लंगमों से 
मुक्त होकर केन्द्रीय सरकार में आत्यंतिक रूप से निहित हो जाएगी।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 6th February, 2025 


9.0. 631(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North 
Western Railway Construction), 8.0. 5100(E) dated 11 November, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section-3, Sub section (ii) (hereinafter referred to as the said notification), under sub section (i) of section 20A 
of the Railways Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance and 
operation of the Special Railway Project, namely “Churu-Sadulpur (57.82 km) Doubling project” in District Churu in 
the state of Rajasthan. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification were published in daily local newspapers “Rajasthan 
Patrika” and “Danik Bhaskar” dated 03 December, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said act; 


And whereas, the competent authority has disallowed the objections after hearing under sub section (2) of the 
section 20D of the act. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub section (1) of section 20E of the said act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, Further in pursuance of sub section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, that land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
