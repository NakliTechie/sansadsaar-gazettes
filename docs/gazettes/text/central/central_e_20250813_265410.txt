NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |3th August, 2025 
S.O. 3740(E).— In pursuance of sub-sections (l) and (2) of Section 54 and Section 55 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act, 202 (I of 202), the Central Government hereby constitutes the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports, 
consisting of a Presiding Officer and two Members, namely:- 


(i) Justice Ashish Jitendra Desai 


- Presiding Officer 
Former Chief Justice, Kerala High Court 


Gi) Shri Chirravuri Viswanath 


- Member 
Former Secretary, Department of Consumer Affairs, and 
Former Member, National Consumer Dispute 
Redressal Commission (NCDRC) 
(ii) Dr. Sanjeev Ranjan - Member 


Former Secretary, Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, and 
Technical Member, National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) 
2: The head office of the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports shall be at Mumbai. 


The Tariff Authority for Major Ports constituted under Section 47A of the (erstwhile) Major Port Trusts Act, 963 
shall cease to exist from the date of constitution of the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports. 


The Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports shall come into force from the date of publication of the notification, in 
the official Gazette.
