नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि के अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है।

आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा। 


ऐसी प्रत्येक आपत्ति सक्षम प्राधिकारी अर्थात जिला भूमि अर्जन पदाधिकारी, सीतामढ़ी बिहार को लिखित रूप में प्रस्तुत 
की जायेगी और उसके आधार अधिकथित किये जाएगें औरसक्षम प्राधिकारी, आपत्तिकर्त्ता को व्यक्तिगत रूप में या किसी विधिक 
पेशेवर द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आपत्तियों की सुनवाई के पश्चात्‌ तथा और ऐसी जाँच करने के पश्चात्‌, यदि 
कोई हो, जिसे सक्षम प्राधिकारी आवश्यक समझें,आदेश द्वारा या तो आपत्तियों को अनुज्ञात या अननुज्ञात कर सकेगा।

NOTIFICATION 
Mahendrughat, 8th November, 2023 

S.O. 4953(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of Section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the schedule below, is required for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project namely, Sitamarhi-Sheohar New Rail Project, in 
the district of Sitamarhi in the State of Bihar, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 

Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days form the date of publication of this notification 
in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of 20D of the said Act. 

Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority i.e. District Land Acquisition Officer, 
Sitamarhi, Bihar in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 

Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 

The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
