MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th September, 2024 


S.O. 4323(E).— Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transport of the petroleum through Diwanpura village, Taluka & District- Mehsana in Gujarat, pipeline from 
drill site LNLF to LNC#93 should be laid by the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd. 


AND WHEREAS it appears that for the purpose of laying such pipeline it is necessary to acquire the Right 
of User in land described in the Schedule annexed to this notification; 


NOW, THERFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section ([) of section-3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in the Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein. 


[भाग I—ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


PROVIDED THAT ANY person interested in the land may within 2[ days from the date of this notification, 
object to the laying of the pipeline under the land to the Competent Authority, Oil and Natural Gas Corporation 
Limited, Land Acquisition Department, KDM Bhavan, Palavasana, MEHSANA- 38400, GUJARAT; 


AND every person making such objection shall also state specifically whether he wishes to be heard in 
person or by legal practitioner.
