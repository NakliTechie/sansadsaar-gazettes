MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
(Office of Registrar General, India) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd August, 2026 


5.0. 4301(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Census Act, 1948 (37 of 1948) read 
with rule 6A and 6D of the Census Rules, 1990, the Central Government hereby declares that- 


(1) The conduct of Census of the population shall be undertaken in the Union territory of Ladakh and the 
snow bound non-synchronous areas of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the snow-bound non- 
synchronous areas of the States of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, from 1‘ September, 2026 to 30 September, 
2026 with a revisional round from 1‘ October, 2026 to 5" October, 2026; 


(ii) For the conduct of the above Census, there shall be an option for self- enumeration from 17" August, 
2026 to 31" August, 2026 just before the start of house to house population enumeration; 


(iii) The reference date for the Census in the Union territory of Ladakh and snow bound non-synchronous 
areas of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of the States of 
Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand shall be 00.00 hours of the first day of October, 2026; and 


(iv) The details of snowbound non-synchronous areas of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir as well 
as the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of the States of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand may be accessed by 
visiting the website- https://censusindia.gov.in/census. website/data/CGAZ.
