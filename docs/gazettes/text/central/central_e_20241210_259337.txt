S.O. 5338(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred by 37A of Section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) 
Act 2008, General Manager/ East Central Railway as approved to declare “Construction of Rail Over Rail 
(ROR) line between Patratu - Tokisud (Km 7.2)” as Special Railway Project. Therefore, “Construction of 
Rail Over Rail (ROR) line between Patratu - Tokisud (Km. 7.2)” Project is declared special Railway Project in 
the State of Jharkhand.
