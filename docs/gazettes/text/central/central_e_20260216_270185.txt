MINISTRY OF EARTH SCIENCES 
(National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |6th February, 2026 


G.S.R. 34(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) of section 70 of the Information 
Technology Act, 2000 (amended 2008), the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) hereby declares the following systems 
and their underlying computer resources of the National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF), 
to be Protected System for the purpose of said Act. 


(a) Arunika HPC System 


Further, as per sub-section (2) of Section 70 of IT Act 2000 (amended 2008), personnel authorized to have role- 
based access to these Protected Systems: - 


a) Designated NCMRWF and MoES employees authorized by head of NCMRWF to access the Protection 
System; 


b) NVMRWE and MOoES authorized team members of contractual Managed Service Provider or third-party 
vendor(s) (if any) who have been given need-based access; 


c) External consultants, regulators, government officials, auditors and stakeholders authorized by NCMRWF and 
MoES on case-to-case basis. 


2. The Notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in official Gazette.
