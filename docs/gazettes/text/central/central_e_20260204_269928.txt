Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely Land Acquisition Officer & Revenue

objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow 


the objections.

interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
