MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th September, 2024 


S.O. 4129(E).—The President is pleased to accept the resignation of Shri Arvind Kejriwal, Chief Minister, 
National Capital Territory of Delhi along with his Council of Ministers with immediate effect. He will, however, 
continue to act as the Chief Minister, National Capital Territory of Delhi, till the new Chief Minister is sworn in.
