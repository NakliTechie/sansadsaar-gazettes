DRAFT RULES 


l. Short title and commencement. - () These rules may be called the Inland Vessels (Special Category 
Vessels: Wooden, GRP/FRP and Similar Materials) Rules, 2025. 


(2) These rules shall come into force on the date of their final publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Definitions. - () In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires - 

(a) “Act” means the Inland Vessels Act, 202] (24 of 202); 

(b) “classification society” means a recognized organization within the meaning of clause (zo) of section 3 
of the Act, which is a member of International Association of Classification Societies. 

(c) "fair season" means the period showing fair conditions of wind and sea as may be specified by the 
Directorate General of Shipping, India or by such other authority as may be prescribed by Central 
Government; 

(d) “foul season” means the period showing foul conditions of wind and sea as may be specified by the 
Directorate General of Shipping, India or by such other authority as may be prescribed by Central 
Government. 


(2) Words and expressions used and not defined in these rules but defined in the Act, shall have the 
meanings respectively assigned to them in the Act. 


3. Scope and application— (]) The following mechanically propelled inland vessels shall be classified as 
special category vessel for the purposes of these rules, on the basis of their construction material- 
(a) vessel constructed, whether wholly or substantially (40% or more of the structural weight) of 
wood; 


(b) vessel constructed, whether wholly or substantially (40% or more of the structural weight) of glass- 
reinforced plastic (GRP), fibre-reinforced plastic (FRP), or other analogous composite materials, 
including but not limited to high-speed crafts, landing crafts, hovercrafts, hydrofoils, and small 
waterplane area twin hull (SWATH) vessels;
