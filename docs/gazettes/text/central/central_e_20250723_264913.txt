MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd July, 2025 


S.O. 3374(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 8A of the Disaster 
Management Act, 2005 (53 of 2005), the Central Government hereby constitutes a National Crisis Management 
Committee, consisting of the following members, for the purposes of said Act, namely: - 


(a) Cabinet Secretary - Chairperson; 
(b) Union Home Secretary - Member; 
(c) Defence Secretary - Member; 
(d) Secretary (Co-ordination), Cabinet Secretariat - Member; 


(९) Member and Head of Department, the National Disaster Management Authority - Member. 


2; The National Crisis Management Committee shall be the apex committee for dealing with situation of a major 
disaster, which has national ramifications. 


3. The Chairperson of the National Crisis Management Committee may co-opt any expert or any officer either 
from the Central Government or the State Government or any organisation, depending on the nature of the crisis, to 
assist the Committee in performing its functions during the threatening disaster situation or emerging disaster situation 
or disaster. 

4. The National Crisis Management Committee shall evaluate preparedness for the purpose of responding to any 
threatening disaster situation or emerging disaster situation or disaster and give directions, where necessary, for 
enhancing such preparedness. 


5. The National Crisis Management Committee shall co-ordinate and monitor actions of the concerned Ministries 
or Departments of the Government of India, State Governments, National Authority, State Authorities, Governmental 
and Non-Governmental Organisations in relation to disaster response. 


6. The National Crisis Management Committee shall give such directions as may be necessary for the proper co- 
ordination and monitoring of disaster response in the country.
