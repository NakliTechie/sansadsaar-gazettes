INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-18/14891-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Shri Ravindranath Tagore Institute of Nursing Professional Studies situated at 119/5 Village 
Morghadi Sanawad, Morghadi, Distt. Khandwa 450554 Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of 
Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/14891-INC (52845) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and Family Welfare, 
Government of Madhya Pradesh Room No. 241, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Distt. Bhopal 462004 
Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to, Shri Ravindranath Tagore 
Institute of Nursing Professional Studies situated at 119/5 Village Morghadi Sanawad, Morghadi, Distt. Khandwa 
450554 Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations 
to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
Shri Ravindranath Tagore Institute of Nursing Professional Studies Even the institution Shri Ravindranath 
Tagore Institute of Nursing Professional Studies has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 07/03/2023 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., clinical and 
teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run B.Sc. (N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 21/05/2024 
any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Shri Ravindranath Tagore Institute of Nursing 
Professional Studies situated at 119/5 Village Morghadi Sanawad, Morghadi, Distt. Khandwa 450554 Madhya 
Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
