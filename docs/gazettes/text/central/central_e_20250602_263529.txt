Il FUNCTIONS 

The objective of the Hindi Advisory Committees of various Ministries and Departments is to advise on the 
implementation of the constitutional provisions relating to official language, provisions of the Official Languages Act 
and Rules, decisions of the Central Hindi Committee and directions/instructions issued by the Ministry of Home 
Affairs (Department of Official Language) and to promote progressive use of Hindi in the working of the 
Ministry/Department concerned. If any Hindi Advisory Committee suggests changes in the Official Language Policy 
or any instructions/instructions issued in connection therewith, the Ministry/Department shall refer such suggestions 
to the Department of Official Language and shall not act on those suggestions without obtaining the consent of the 
Department of Official Language. 
It TENURE 
l. The tenure of the Samiti will be ordinarily for 3 years from the date of its constitution. 
2. Ifa person is nominated as Member in the middle of the term, he will hold the office for the residual term of the 
Samiti. 
3. In special circumstances, the tenure of the Samiti may be curtailed or enhanced by the Ministry. 


4. Member of Parliament who are the members of Committee shall cease to be members as soon as they cease to be 
Member of Parliament. 


5. Ex-officio members of the Samiti shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue of which they 
are members of the Samiti. 


IV GENERAL 


The Headquarters of the Samiti shall be at New Delhi. All correspondences related to the Samiti will be addressed to 
the Joint Secretary (Incharge, Official Language), Room No. 50, Ministry of Heavy Industries, Udyog Bhawan, New 
Delhi-00. 


0 T.A. AND OTHER ALLOWANCES

the provisions in the “Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowance & Pension) Act, 954” and amendments issued 
from time to time and rules made thereunder vide Department of Official Language Office Memorandum No. 
II/20034/04/2005-0.L (Policy-2), dated 03.02.2006 for participating in meetings of the Samiti. 


(b) Keeping in view the high level status of the Committee, Travelling Allowance/Daily Allowance to other non- 
official members of the Samiti, will be paid as per the guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language 
O.M. No.II/22034/04/86-OL(A-2) dated 22.0.987 and in accordance with the revised/prescribed rates and rules as 
amended from time to time by the Government of India.
