FOOD SAFETY AND STANDARDS AUTHORITY OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd November, 2026 


F. No. SS-TOSP20(M)/4/2026-Standard-FSSAI —The following draft of certain regulations further to amend 
the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 208, which the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India 
proposes to make with previous approval of the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub- section 
(2) of section 92 read with section 23 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (34 of 2006) is hereby published as 
required under sub-section (l) of section 92 of the said Act for the information of all persons likely to be affected 
thereby, and notice is hereby given that the said draft regulations shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of the 
period of sixty days from the date on which the copies of the Gazette containing this notification are made available to 
the public; 


Objections or suggestions, if any, may be addressed to the Chief Executive Officer, Food Safety and 
Standards Authority of India, Food and Drug Administration Bhawan, Kotla Road, New Delhi-!!0002 or may be sent 
by email at sp-packaging @fssai.gov.in. 


Objections or suggestions which may be received with respect to the said draft regulations within the period 
specified above shall be considered by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. 


Draft Regulations 
l. These regulations may be called the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Amendment Regulations, 2026. 
2. In the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 208, - 


(() In regulation (4) relating to specific requirements for primary food packaging, in sub regulation (4) relating to plastic 
materials intended to come in contact with food products, after Clause (e) the following clauses shall be inserted, namely; 


(f) | Recycled Polyethylene (PE) and Polypropylene (PP) produced by Chemical recycling process as defined in 
IS 4534, via the monomer route, may be used for food-contact materials, only when the output (end 
product) is considered equivalent to virgin material and meets the criteria applicable to virgin material. 


(g) Recycled polymer derived exclusively from food-grade packaging material may be used in the outermost 
layer of multilayer packaging material intended for food contact, provided that the inner layers possess 
adequate barrier properties to prevent migration of substances from the outermost layer into the food and 
also shall fulfil the already prescribed migration limits for packaging materials.
