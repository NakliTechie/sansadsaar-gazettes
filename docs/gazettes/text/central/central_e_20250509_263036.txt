MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 97 May, 2025. 


S.O. 28(E).—WHEREAS the Central Government is satisfied that public interest so requires 
that the services of the industry engaged in the processing or production or distribution of fuel gases (coal 
gas, natural gas and the like), which is covered under item 29 of the First Schedule to the Industrial Disputes 
Act, 947 (4 of 947), to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, the Central Government has declared the said industry to be public utility service 
for the purposes of the said Act for a period of six months with effect from the |20 June, 2024 vide 
notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and Employment, number S.O. 225(E), 
dated the 2th June, 2024; 


AND WHEREAS, the said declaration could not be extended at any time; 


AND WHEREAS, the Central Government is of the opinion that public interest requires that the said 
industry is declared as public utility service for the purposes of the said Act for a period of six months; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (vi) of clause (n) of section 
2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 947 (4 of 947), the Central Government being satisfied that public interest 
so requires, hereby declares the services engaged in the processing or production or distribution of fuel gases 
(coal gas, natural gas and the like) to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act for a period 
of six months from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
