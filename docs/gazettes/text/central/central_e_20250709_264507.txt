NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th July, 2025 
5.0. 3056(E).— Whereas the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and 
(2) of section |4 of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 956 (33 of 956), constituted the Ravi and Beas Waters 
Tribunal for verification and adjudication of certain matters of the Punjab Settlement, referred therein, vide notification 
of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Water Resources, vide number S.O. 69(E) 


, dated the 274 April, 986, and the said Tribunal forwarded its report to the Central Government on the 30% January, 
987; 


And whereas, further references have been made by the Central Government to the said Tribunal on the 97 
August, |987, requiring explanation and guidance on the report aforesaid, in terms of sub-section (3) of section 5 of the 
said Act; 

And whereas, considering the enormous exercise undertaken by the said Tribunal in completing its further 
report, the Central Government extended from time to time the period of making the said report, till the 5 day of 
August, 2025; 


And whereas, the Central Government, keeping in view the exigencies of the work involved, as pointed out by 
the said Tribunal, considers it necessary to extend the said period for one year from the 57 day of August, 2025; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to sub-section (3) of section 5 of the Inter- 
State River Water Disputes Act, 956 (33 of 956), the Central Government hereby further extends the period, within 
which the said Tribunal may forward its report on the references so made, to the Central Government till the 5" day of 
August, 2026, and for that purpose makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of 
India in the erstwhile Ministry of Water Resources number S.O. 905(E), dated the 5" August, 2003, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the last paragraph, for the words, figures and letters “till the 57 day of August, 2025”, 
the words, figures and letters “till the 5" day of August, 2026” shall be substituted.
