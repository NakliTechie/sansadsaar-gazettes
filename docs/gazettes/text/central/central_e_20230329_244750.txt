NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 28th March , 2023 

S.O. 1452 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways number S.O. 5842 ( E ) , dated the 14th December , 2022 , published in the Gazette 
of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government 
declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building ( widening / four - laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and operation of NH - 06 on the stretch of

land from Km . 606 to Km . 649 ( Amravati Maharashtra / Gujarat Border section ) in the district of 
NANDURBAR in the state of MAHARASHTRA . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Punya Nagari 
( Marathi ) ” and “ The Indian Express ( English ) " both dated 14 , Jan. 2023 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 
3A of the said Act ; 

And whereas No objections have been received to the competent Authority ; 

And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
