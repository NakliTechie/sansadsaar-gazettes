NOTIFICATION 
Visakhapatnam, the 5th September, 2026 
NOTICE UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT, 1989 


S.O. 4960(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (East Coast Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number, S.0.1527(E). dated 247 
March, 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, section-3 Sub-section (ii), dated 27" 
March, 2026 (herein after referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A 
of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, 
“Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of closing of manned LC No ML-352 at Km 654/11-13 
between Baruva (BAV) — Mandasa Rd. (MMS)(Baruva Yard) on the Howrah — Visakhapatnam Main 
Line of Visakhapatnam Division (Erstwhile Khurda Road Division)” in the Srikakulam district state of 
Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely 
"Andhra Jyothi" in Telugu (Srikakulam Edition) on dated 02™ July, 2026 and “The Times Of India” in 
English (Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 18" April, 2026 under sub- section (4) of section 20A of the said Act 


And whereas, no objection has been received from any person by the competent authority within the 
stipulated period of 30 days under section 20A of the said act; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule 
annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
