MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th November, 2024 


S.O. 5190(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 79A of the information Technology Act 2000 
(21 of 2000) the Central Government hereby notifies Cyber Forensics Laboratory, Navy Cyber Group, Naval Hgs, 
Ministry of Defence (Navy), 4th Floor Chanakya Bhawan, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi as Examiner of Electronic 
Evidence within India, with the following scope, namely: - 


(a) Computer (Media) Forensics excluding Floppy Disk Drive; 
(b) Mobile Devices Forensics. 


With aforesaid notification, earlier published gazette notification No. S.O. 4167(E) dated 29" August, 2022, 
Extraordinary, Part II- Section 3 - Sub-section (ii), is rescinded by central Government.
