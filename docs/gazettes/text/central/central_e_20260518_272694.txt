8७.0. 2540(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (East 
Coast Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number S.0.538I(E), dated 25th Nov, 2025 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 25th Nov, 2025 (here in after referred to as

annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, measurement and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely, “Talcher-Bimalagarh New B.G. Rail Link Project” under Khurda Road Division of East 
Coast Railway in the State of Odisha. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in 02 daily local newspapers namely 
“SAMBAD” in Odia (Bhubaneswar Edition), and “THE TIMES OF INDIA“ in English (Bhubaneswar Edition) Dated 
4th January, 2026 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of section 20 D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
