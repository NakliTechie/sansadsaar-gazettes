G.S.R. 427(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (3) of section 2] of the 
Mental Healthcare Act, 207 (l0 of 20I7), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, to amend the 
Mental Healthcare (Central Mental Health Authority and Mental Health Review Boards) Rules, 208 namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.- (l) These rules may be called the Mental Healthcare (Central Mental 
Health Authority and Mental Health Review Boards) Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Mental Healthcare (Central Mental Health Authority and Mental Health Review Boards) 
Rules, 208 (hereinafter referred to as the said Rules), in Chapter III, -- 

i. in the heading, for the words “Provisional Registration” the word “Registration” shall be substituted; 

li. after rule 2, the following rule shall be inserted, namely:- 


“2A, Procedure for Permanent Registration of mental health establishments by Central Authority. — 


l. A mental health establishment shall submit an application for permanent registration in Form-C as 
specified in the regulations made in exercise of the power under sub-section (2) of section 66 of the Act 
along with the specified fees. 


2. The Central Authority shall on being satisfied that the mental health establishment fulfills the standards 
and all requirements specified under sections 65 and 66 of the Act, grant permanent registration certificate 
in Form - CA to such mental health establishment. 


3. Where a Permanent Registration Certificate has been issued to any mental health establishment prior to 
publication of this notification, such certificate shall remain valid and be considered as permanent 
registration certificate for the purpose of this rule.” 


3. In the said rules, after Form-C, the following Form shall be inserted, namely:-
