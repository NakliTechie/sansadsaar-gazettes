New Delhi, the 19th September, 2024 


S.O. 4041(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, 98.0. 4536 (E) Dated the 16th October 2023 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Public 
purpose of building (widening/six/eight laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of Mumbai - Vadodara Expressway Spur to NH-NF4 in the stretch of land from 
Km. 18.925 to Km. 45.400 (Maharashtra Section) in the district of Thane in the state of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in 
“Sakal”(Marathi) and “The Free Press Journal”’(English) both dated 30th November 
2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under 
Section 3-C, considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be 
acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, 
the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free 
from all encumbrances.
