G.S.R. 336(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section(2)

rules, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.- (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative 
Coin to commemorate on the occasion of 300" Birth Anniversary of Ahilyabai Holkar) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of Three Hundred Rupees denomination shall only be coined at the 
Mint for issue under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of 300" Birth Anniversary 
of Ahilyabai Holkar.
