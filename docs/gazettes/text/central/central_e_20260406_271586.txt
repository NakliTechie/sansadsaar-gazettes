5.0. 739(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 4856 (E) dated the 2! October 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
IL, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 2] October 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section (l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Parli— Parbhani Doubling project” 
in the District of Parbhani in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Paper " Lokmat " 
"Lokmat Times"&"Dainik Bhaskar" dated 29 November 2025, under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said 
Act, 


And whereas, in Porwad and Singnapur villages no objections were received and accordingly orders have 
been passed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20(E). of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
