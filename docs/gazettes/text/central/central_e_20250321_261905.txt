INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


New Delhi, the l0th March, 2025 
F.No.8-579/2000 -INC: In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:-— 


Whereas, Grace School of Nursing situated at P Box १२०.।।, Machilipatnam, Distt. Krishna, 52002 
Andhra Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Andhra Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh Nurses Registration 
Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. l8-579/2000-INC (5627) dated 03/05/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Andhra Pradesh 5th Floor, Ground Floor, Room No. 57, A.P. Secretariat, Velegapudi, Amaravathi 522503, Andhra 
Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 03/05/2024 was also enclosed to Grace School of Nursing situated at 
P Box १४०.।।, Machilipatnam, Distt. Krishna, 52002 Andhra Pradesh In the notice issued, the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Grace School of Nursing. Even the institution, Grace School of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/2/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc. (N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
26/0/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Grace School of Nursing situated at 
P Box १२०.।, Machilipatnam, Distt. Krishna, 52002 Andhra Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the 
State of Andhra Pradesh. 


Lt. Col. (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
