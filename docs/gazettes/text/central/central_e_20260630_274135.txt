NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th June 2026 
No. 2/2026-27 


Subject: Amendment to Notification No. 65/2025-26 for extension of timelines under Component II of 
Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation (RELIEF) Intervention - reg. 


5.0. 355(E).—In exercise of powers conferred under Sections 3 and 5 of the Foreign Trade 
(Development and Regulation) Act, 992, read with paras .02 and 2.0] of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, 
as amended from time to time, the Central Government hereby amends Notification No. 65/2025-26 dated 
9.03.2026 relating to the Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation (RELIEF) under the 
Export Promotion Mission (EPM). 


2: In Para 7.2 of Annexure to Notification No. 65/2025-26 of the eligibility and validity criteria under 
Component IT, the eligibility period shall stand extended upto 30th September 2026 for shipments meant 
for delivery or transshipment under the intervention, to enhance utilization and facilitate trade resilience. 


3. All other provisions of Notification No. 65/2025-26 dated 9.03.2026 (as amended from time to time) 
shall remain unchanged. 


Effect of this Notification: The eligibility timelines under Component II of the EPM RELIEF intervention 
are extended up to 30th September 2026 to support Indian exporters and mitigate logistics challenges arising 
out of the continuing West Asia Crisis.
