PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 9th March, 2025 
No. 5/2024-25 
Subject: Extension of the last date for filing Annual RoDTEP Return (ARR) for Financial Year 2023-24 - reg 


F. No. 0/6/80/66/AM24/PC-3.—In exercise of powers conferred under paragraph .03 and 2.04 of the 
Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time, the last date for filing Annual RODTEP Return (ARR) as 
provided under Para 4.94 of Handbook of Procedure through Public Notice No. 27 dated 23.0.2024 for the financial 
year 2023-24 is extended from 3].03.2025 to 30.06.2025. Similarly the applicable grace period is also extended from 
30.06.2025 to 30.09.2025. 


Effect of the Public Notice: The last date for filing ARR is extended by three (3) months for RoDTEP availed for 
exports of Financial Year 2023-24. 


SANTOSH KUMAR SARANGT, Director General of Foreign Trade Ex-officio Addl. Secy.
