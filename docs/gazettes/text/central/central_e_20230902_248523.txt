LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2023 


No. 37/1/(XIID)/2023/T. —The following order by the President dated the 2nd September, 2023 is published 
for general information: - 


“In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by clause (1) of article 85 of the Constitution, I 
hereby summon the Lok Sabha to meet at New Delhi on Monday, the 18th September, 2023 at 
11.00 A.M. 


September 2, 2023 Droupadi Murmu, 
PRESIDENT” 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, Secy. General
