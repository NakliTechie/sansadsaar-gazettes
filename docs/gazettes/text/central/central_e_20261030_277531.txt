G.S.R. 959(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 
24 of the Coinage Act, 2011(11 of 2011), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on 
the occasion of 250" Janmotsava of Sri Muttusvami Diksita) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin — The coin of Two Hundred Fifty Rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for 
issue under the authority of the Central Government to commemorate the occasion of 250" Janmotsava of Sri 
Muttusvami Diksita. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin— The denomination of the coin specified in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely: -
