MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(SEZ Division) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2024 


S.O. 3639(E).—WHEREAS, M/s. Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation, a state industrial 
promotion organization in the State of Gujarat had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 
(28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a sector-specific Special Economic Zone for Apparel 
Sector at Ahmedabad in the State of Gujarat; 

AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of 
section 4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area of 38.043 
hectares at the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Numbers S.O. 
543(E) dated 0" April, 2007 respectively; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation has now proposed for de-notification 
of 7.6264 hectares from the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Gujarat has given its approval to the proposal vide letter No. 
IC/INFRA/NOC/697670 dated 04.02.202. Further, after de-notification, the land parcels would be utilized towards 
the creation of infrastructure which would sub-serve the objective of the SEZ as originally envisaged; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Kandla Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of an area of 7.6264 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that the requirements under sub-section (8) of 
section 3 of the said Act and other related requirements are fulfilled; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in the exercise of the powers conferred by the first proviso to sub-section (l) of 
section 4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005, and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 
2006, the Central Government hereby de-notifies an area of |7.6264 hectares, thereby making the resultant area as 
20.449 hectares. The T.P. numbers and the area for de-notification are given below in the table, namely:-
