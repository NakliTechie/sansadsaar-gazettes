S.O. 3578(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3Aof the National 
Highways Act, 956(48 of 956), (here in after referred to as the said Act), the central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is 
required for Construction of (4-lane highway with paved shoulder) green field Highway No.73IA in the stretch of 
land from Km. !2.950 to Km. 47.200 & Left limb (Chitrakoot bypass length 8.300km) in the District of 
Chitrakoot in the State of Uttar Pradesh, here by declares its intention to acquired such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under —section (l) of 
section3Cofthesaid Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority , namely , the additional Sub-Divisional 
Magistrate , Karvi, Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the competent authority shall 
give the objector and opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objection and after making such further enquire, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, 
either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
