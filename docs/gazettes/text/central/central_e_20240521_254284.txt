F. No. 6/7/2023-DGTR.—Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act, !975 as amended from time to time 
(hereinafter referred to as the ‘Act’) and the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Duty or 
Additional Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 995 (hereinafter referred to as the

has issued the Preliminary Finding Notification No. 607/2023 dated 26.04.2024 in respect of the anti-dumping 
investigation concerning imports of “Poly Vinyl Chloride Paste Resin” (hereinafter referred to as the “product under 
consideration’ or the ‘PUC’ or the ‘subject goods’) originating in or exported from China PR, Korea RP, Malaysia, 
Norway, Taiwan & Thailand (hereinafter referred to as the ‘subject countries’) 


The Authority notes that, in the Duty Table appended in paragraph 46, the use of the word ‘Any’ in Row 
numbers 4, 7, l0, l3, 6 and 8 against the Column no. 4 (Country of origin) could lead to ambiguous interpretation. 
Also, the specific exclusions from the product under consideration (PUC), as mentioned in Paragraph 4 of the said 
findings, were inadvertently left out from being mentioned below the said Duty Table. 


In view of the above, the paragraph 46 of the Preliminary Finding Notification No. 6क्‍7/2023 dated 
26.04.2024 stands substituted as mentioned below: 


46. In terms of provision contained in Rule !2 and Rule I3 read with Rule 4(d) of the Rules, the Authority 
recommends imposition of provisional anti-dumping duty equal to the lesser of margin of dumping and the margin of 
injury, so as to remove the injury to the domestic industry. Accordingly, the Authority recommends imposition of
