9.0. 4406(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 28I5 Dated:8/07/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (here in after 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for Building (widening / two/ Four laning etc.) Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH752-C in the stretch of land from Km. 33+00 to 43+500 Km. (Pachore to Shujalpur Road) 
(Shujalpur-Bypass) Taluka Shujalpur in the district of SHAJAPUR in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Nai Duniya” dated 02 August 
2024 and “Free Press ” dated 04 August 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
