Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) 
(Fifth Amendment) Regulations, 2026. 


No. IBBI/2026-27/GN/REG155.— In exercise of the powers conferred under clauses (aa) and (t) of sub-section (1) 
of section 196 read with section 240 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016), the Insolvency and 
Bankruptcy Board of India hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Insolvency and Bankruptcy 
Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) Regulations, 2016, namely: - 


1. (1) These regulations may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process 
for Corporate Persons) (Fifth Amendment) Regulations, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) 
Regulations, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as ‘the principal regulations’), for regulation 4D, the following regulation 
shall be substituted, namely:- 


“4D. Project-wise records and fund utilisation. 


(1) Where a corporate debtor has any real estate project, the interim resolution professional or the resolution 
professional, as the case may be, shall operate a separate bank account for each real estate project. 


(2) The interim resolution professional or the resolution professional, as the case may be, shall place before 
the committee, at such intervals as may be considered appropriate by the committee, a project-wise statement 
of receipts and payments, and such other financial information relating to such real estate project as may be 
required by the committee. 


(3) The interim resolution professional or the resolution professional, as the case may be, shall ensure that, in 
respect of every real estate project of the corporate debtor, the fund utilisation requirements provided under 
the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 are complied with during the corporate insolvency 
resolution process.” 


3. In the principal regulations, in regulation 4E, the following explanation shall be inserted as under:- 


“Explanation - For the purposes of this regulation, the committee may either decide to grant approval in 
respect of each request made by an individual allottee, to the resolution professional, or grant an in-principle 
approval, authorising the resolution professional to process all such requests as and when they are received 
during the corporate insolvency resolution process.” 


4. In the principal regulations, after regulation 4E, the following regulations shall be inserted, namely:- 
“AF. Identification and exclusion of real estate projects. 


(1) Where the corporate debtor has more than one real estate project, the resolution professional shall place in 
the first meeting of the committee, following project-wise details of all real estate projects of the corporate 
debtor - 


(a) RERA registration details; 


(b) stage of completion;

(c) status of possession and occupancy; 
(d) number of completed and incomplete units; and 
(e) such other information as considered necessary for assessment of such real estate project. 


(2) The committee may, after considering the information placed before it under sub-regulation (1), by a vote 
of not less than sixty-six per cent of voting share, recommend to the Adjudicating Authority along with the 
reasons to be recorded, for its consideration, the exclusion from the corporate insolvency resolution process 
of any real estate project. 


(3) Pursuant to approval of the committee under sub-regulation (2), the resolution professional shall make an 
application along with the reasons recorded by the committee before the Adjudicating Authority. 


4G. Technical and cost-to-complete assessment of real estate projects. 


(1) Where a corporate debtor has any real estate project, the resolution professional shall, with the prior 
approval of committee, appoint one or more suitably qualified and independent professionals, in accordance 
with sub-regulation (3) of regulation 27, to undertake a project-wise technical and cost-to-complete 
assessment of such project. 


(2) The assessment referred to in sub-regulation (1) shall, to the extent applicable, include - 
(a) physical status of construction and percentage of completion of the project; 
(b) status of approvals, registrations, permissions and regulatory compliances relating to the project; 
(c) details of inventory, including sold and unsold units; 
(d) estimated cost required for completion of the project and its phases; 
(e) milestone-wise funding requirements; 
(f) realistic timelines for completion and delivery of the project; 
(g) material risks and constraints affecting completion of the project; and 
(h) such other information as considered necessary. 


(3) The resolution professional shall obtain the assessment referred to in sub-regulation (1) before issuance of 
the invitation for expression of interest under regulation 36A. 


(4) The assessment shall be incorporated in the information memorandum and made available to prospective 
resolution applicants. 


(5) The costs incurred for conducting the assessment under this regulation shall form part of the insolvency 
resolution process costs."
