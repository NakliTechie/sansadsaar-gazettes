New Delhi, the 20th September, 2023 


S.O. 4I50(E)—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways

stretch of land from Km. 30 to Km. 8l (Sub Divisional Officer Betul) in the district of BETUL in the state of 
MADHYA PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act.

such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections.
