New Delhi, the 29th November, 2024 


S.O. 5148(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), Vide S.O. No. 3578 (E) dated 22* AUGUST 2024 the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of (Widening/two lane with paved 
shoulder/ four laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of NH731A (RAM VAN GAMAN PATH) on the 
stretch of land from Km. 112.950 to Km. 147.200 And Left limb (Chitrakoot by pass length 8.300 km) (Competent 
authority/Additional Sub Divisional Magistrate, Karvi) in the district of Chitrakoot in the state of UTTAR PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “AAJ ” and “ THE TIMES OF 
INDIA. both dated 04* SEPETEMBER 2024 , under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
