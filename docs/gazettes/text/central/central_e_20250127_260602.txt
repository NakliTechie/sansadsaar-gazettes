भाग ॥, खंड 3, उप-खंड (ii), (इसके बाद उक्त अधिसूचना के रूप में संदर्भित) में प्रकाशित, 989 (4989 का 24) (इसके 
बाद उक्त अधिनियम के रूप में संदर्भित), केंद्र सरकार ने विशेष रेलवे परियोजना अर्थात ईब फ्लाई ओवर रेल लाइन 
(झारसुगुडा जिला) के ओडिशा राज्य के निर्माण के निष्पादन के लिए उक्त अधिसूचना से जुड़ी अनुसूची में निर्दिष्ट भूमि के 
अधिग्रहण के अपने इरादे की घोषणा की।

राउरकेला एवं आरूप झारसुगुडा में दिनांक 22.09.2024 को प्रकाशित किया गया था। जबकि कोई भी आपत्ति इस संबंध

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 22nd January, 2025 
(under section 20 (E) of Railways Act, 989) 


9७.0. 455(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) Notification No. 3564 dated 2.09.2024 published in the Gazette of India Ordinary Part-II, Section 3, 
Sub section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as said notification) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as said act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of Special Railway Project, namely IB 
Flyover New Rail Line Project (09km) in Jharsuguda district of Odisha. 


And whereas the substance of the 20A notification under sub-section (), under sub section (4) of section: 
20A of the said act was published in the daily newspapers Utkal Mail Rourkela, Times of India Bhubaneswar, Kalinga 
Samachar Rourkela and Aaroop Jharsuguda on dated: on 22.09.2024 respectively. Whereas no objections were 
received in the stipulated period from the land losers to the Competent Authority and whereas, in pursuance of sub- 
section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted his report to the Central
