MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]lth March, 2024 
G.S.R. 74(E).— WHEREAS the draft of certain rules further to amend the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 
989, was published, as required by sub-section (l) of section 2!2 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 988 (59 of 988), vide 
notification of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, number G.S.R. 99(E), dated the 27" December, 2023, 
in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), inviting objections and suggestions from all 


persons likely to be affected thereby before the expiry of the period of thirty days from the date on which copies of the 
Gazette containing the said notification were made available to public; 


AND WHEREAS copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were made available to the public on 
the 27" December, 2023; 


AND WHEREAS, objections and suggestions received in respect of the said draft rules have been duly 
considered by the Central Government; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(l) of section l0 of the Motor 
Vehicles Act, 988 (59 of 988), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 989, namely:- 


. Short title and commencement.-(l) These rules may be called the Central Motor Vehicles (Sixth Amendment) 
Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 989, in rule 38, in sub-rule (4), in clause (0), after the words “a non-greasy 
base,” the words and figure “a tube of Feracrylum of l% gel,” shall be inserted.
