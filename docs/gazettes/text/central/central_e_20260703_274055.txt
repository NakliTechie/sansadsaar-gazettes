MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist July, 2026 
G.S.R. 553(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 read with sub-clause (11) of clause 
(g) of section 2 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (16 of 2016) and in pursuance of the 
amendment made to section 68 of the said Act under section 2 read with serial number 70 of the Schedule to the Jan 
Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 


to amend the National Capital Territory of Delhi Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (General) Rules, 2016, 
namely:— 


1. (1) These rules may be called the National Capital Territory of Delhi Real Estate (Regulation and Development) 
(General) Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Capital Territory of Delhi Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (General) Rules, 2016, 
in rule 32, in sub-rule (1), in the Table, the following row shall be omitted, namely: - 


‘Punishable with imprisonment under section 68. ten per cent. of the estimated cost of the plot, apartment or 


building, as the case may be.”
