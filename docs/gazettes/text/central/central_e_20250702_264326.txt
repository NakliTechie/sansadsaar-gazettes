MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
(Department of Telecommunications) 


ORDER 
New Delhi, the 2 July, 2025. 


S.O. 2962 (E).— Whereas the Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by sub-section 
(l) of section 58 of the Telecommunications Act, 2023 (44 of 2023) (the said Act), made the 
Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024 with effect from 26" June, 2024 for the purpose 
of crediting of sums of money to Digital Bharat Nidhi which were payable towards Universal Service 
Obligation under licenses granted on or after the 26™ June, 2024; 


And whereas, the Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024 came in force on 
26" June, 2024 and was in force till the expiry of a period of ten months from the said date; 


And whereas, the said period of ten months expired on the 2570 April, 2025; 


And whereas, the provisions of section 3 of the said Act in accordance with which authorisations are 
to be granted have not yet come in force and the rules, which are necessary to give effect to the said 
provisions, have also not yet been made; 

And whereas, difficulty may arise in crediting sums of money payable towards Universal Service 
Obligation Fund under the licenses granted on or after 26" April, 2025 to the Digital Bharat Nidhi, which 
may hinder in achieving the objectives under section 25 of the said Act; 

And whereas, it is expedient to remove the said difficulty; 

And whereas, paragraph 2 of the Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024 is self- 
contained, which provides that the sums of money payable towards Universal Service Obligation under 
licenses granted on or after the 26" June, 2024, shall be credited to the Digital Bharat Nidhi till such time the 
said Order is in force and all actions that may be taken by the Central Government in respect of licenses 
granted under the Indian Telegraph Act, 885 (3 of l885) on or after the 26" June, 2024 towards Universal 
Service Obligation shall be deemed to be actions taken under the said Act. 

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 58 of the said Act, 
except as respects things done or omitted to be done on and from the 267 April, 2025 till the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the Central Government hereby makes the following 
Order to amend the Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024, namely: — 


l. (.) This Order may be called the Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Amendment Order, 
2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In Telecommunications (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024, in paragraph |, for sub- 
paragraph (2), following sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely:— 


“(2) 7 shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.”
