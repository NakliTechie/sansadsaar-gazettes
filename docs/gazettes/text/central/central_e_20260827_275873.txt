MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 27th August, 2026 


S.O. 4783(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number,S.O.769(E), dated the 11.02.2026, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-Section (ii),gazette No.731 dated the 13.02.2026 and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, management 
and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, “Construction of Ghanpur TSS in Secunderabad -Kazipet section 
for up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from 1x25KV to 2x25 KV traction system to meet 3000MT loading 
target ” in Ghanpur (stn) Village & Mandal in Jangaon District in the state of Telangana. 


[भाग ए---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the “Velugu 
(Telugu)”, “Sakshi (Telugu)”, “The Hindu (English)”, “Vaartha(Hindi)” and “The Munsif Daily(Urdu)” dated 19 
February, 2026 with the approval of Mothilal Bhookya, Chief Engineer/Con-II, South Central Railway, Secunderabad 


And, whereas, Competent authority for LA Ghanpur (Stn)) received objections U/s 20D and after completion of 
enquiry disallowed the objections under subsection (2) of section 20D of Railways Act 1989. And, whereas, in pursuance 
of sub-section (1) of section 20E of this Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
