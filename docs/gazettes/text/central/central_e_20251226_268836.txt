सा.का.नि. 924(अ).--- चूंकि पहचान स्थापित करने के लिए आधार संख्या का इस्तेमाल करने से लोगों को सब्सिडी, 
लाभ और सेवाएं सरल एवं निर्बाध रूप में मिल जाती हैं, पहचान स्थापित करने के लिए कई तरह के दस्तावेजों की जरूरत 
नहीं पड़ती, प्रक्रिया आसान हो जाती है तथा पारदर्शिता और दक्षता में वृद्धि होती है| 


और चूंकि इलेक्ट्रॉनिकी एवं सूचना प्रौद्योगिकी मंत्रालय, भारत सरकार ने भारतीय विशिष्ट पहचान प्राधिकरण 
(यूआईडीएआई) से परामर्श के उपरांत अपने पत्र eF.No.22(7)/2025-Cyberlaws दिनांक 42 नवंबर, 2025 के माध्यम 
से संसदीय कार्य मंत्रालय (एतद्पश्चात्‌ उक्त मंत्रालय के रूप में संदर्भित), भारत सरकार (एतद्पश्चात्‌ उक्त सरकार के रूप में 
संदर्भित) के अंतर्गत राज्य सभा सचिवालय को, सुशासन के लिए आधार अधिप्रमाणन (समाज कल्याण, नवाचार, ज्ञान) 
नियम, 2020 (एतद्पश्चात्‌ उक्त नियम के रूप में संदर्भित) के नियम 3 के उप-नियम (॥) के अंतर्गत निर्धारित प्रयोजनों के 
लिए अनुमति दी थी कि उक्त सरकार को अधिप्रमाणित करने और आधार संख्या धारक की पहचान स्थापित करने हेतु 
अधिप्रमाणन के दौरान आधार संख्या का उपयोग करने तथा आधार (वित्तीय और अन्य सहायिकियों, लाभों और सेवाओं का 
लक्ष्यित परिदान) अधिनियम, 2046 (एतद्पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम के रूप में संदर्भित) की धारा 4 की उपधारा (4) के खंड 
(ख) के उपखंड (ii) के साथ पठित उक्त नियमों के नियम 5 के तहत उसको अधिसूचित करने की अनुमति दी जाए|

MINISTRY OF PARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th December, 2025 


G.S.R. 924(E).— Whereas the use of Aadhaar number to establish identity enables individuals to receive 
subsidies, benefits and services in a convenient and seamless manner, obviates the need for multiplicity of documents 
to establish identity, simplifies processes and promotes transparency and efficiency: 


And whereas the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, after consultation 
with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), had allowed vide its letter eF.No.22(7)/2025-Cyberlaws 
dated 2' November, 2025 to the Rajya Sabha Secretariat under Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Ministry), Government of India ( hereinafter referred to as the said Government) for the purposes 
prescribed under sub-rule () of rule 3 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, 
Knowledge) Rules, 2020 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules) that the said Government may be allowed to perform 
authentication and be permitted the use of Aadhaar number during authentication for establishing identity of Aadhaar 
number holder and notify the same under rule 5 of the said rules read with sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section 
(4) of section 4 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 206 
(hereinafter referred to as the Act). 


And whereas, the Aadhaar authentication shall be performed for Aadhaar authentication, on a voluntary basis, by 
Rajya Sabha Secretariat, to establish the identity of users for registration in Sampurna Digital Sansad (SDS) portal and 
for availing of the services as annexed, (hereinafter referred to as the said purpose) as prescribed under sub-rule () of 
rule 3 of the said rules and the performance of Aadhaar authentication for the said purpose shall be on voluntary basis 
and that the Rajya Sabha Secretariat shall perform the Aadhaar authentication only for Usage of digital platforms to 
ensure good governance under Sampurna Digital Sansad (SDS) Project [hereinafter referred to as the use case(s)]. 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of rule 5 of the said rules read with sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) 
of section 4 of the Act, the said Ministry hereby notifies the following, namely:— 


l. — (.) Rajya Sabha Secretariat, as provided in the Act, shall obtain the consent of the Aadhaar number holder for the 
purpose of authentication herein. 


(2) As per sub-rule (2) of rule 3 of the said rules, Aadhaar authentication is on voluntary basis, the Rajya Sabha 
Secretariat shall inform to the Aadhaar number holder of alternate and viable means of identification and shall not deny 
any service to the Aadhaar number holder for refusing to, or being unable to, undergo Aadhaar authentication, namely:— 


List of Services: 
(a) Member Services & Entitlements 


e Member services (IT, TA/DA and Medical claims), payroll and advances, pension processing, 
accommodation services. 


(b) Health & Welfare 
e Linking of CGHS cards of MPs, staff and dependents, medical claim. 
(c) Identity & Access Management 
e —_eSign service for digital signing of official documents, Identity and access service 
(d) Administrative & Staff Services 
e Staff onboarding, service record linking, estate services, HRMS and training 
(e) Attendance & Access Control 
e Attendance for staff and MPs, House sessions, verification for visitor passes. 
(f) Financial & Compliance 
e PFMS integration service and payment gateway service. 


2. This notification shall come into effect from the date of its publication in the official Gazette.
