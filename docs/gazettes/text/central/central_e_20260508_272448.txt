Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 2338(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (0२०.।| of 2008), the 
Central Government hereby notifies “Chalisgaon Rail Over Rail Flyover (0.75 Kms)” as Railway project 
under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Jalgaon District in the 
State of Maharashtra, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
