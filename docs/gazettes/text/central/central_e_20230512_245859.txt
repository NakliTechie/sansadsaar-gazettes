स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण), मध्य प्रदेश राज्य सरकार, कमरा संख्या 241, मंत्रालय (Geos भवन) we हिल्स,

नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 11th May, 2023 


F. No. 18-18/14701-INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVHUI of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Satpura Nursing Coed College situated at Pandupiapriya, Patalkot Pandupipatirya, Distt. 
Chhindwara — 480559, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya 
Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM & B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/14701-INC dated 27/07/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 241, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 27/07/2022 was also enclosed to Satpura Nursing Coed College situated at Pandupiapriya, Patalkot 
Pandupipatirya, Distt. Chhindwara — 480559, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was 
requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Satpura Nursing Coed College, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Satpura Nursing Coed College has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
30/12/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.6., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 
declares that with effect from 16/11/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM & B.Sc(N) Satpura 
Nursing Coed College situated at Pandupiapriya, Patalkot Pandupipatirya, Distt. Chhindwara — 480559, 
Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State 
government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
