MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 


ORDER 
New Delhi, the 23rd February, 2024 


S.O. 854(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by sub-sections (3) and (5) of section 30 of the Tea Act, 
953 (29 of 953), the Central Government hereby makes the following order further to amend the Tea (Marketing) 
Control Order, 2003, namely:— 


l. (l) This order may be called the Tea (Marketing) Control (Amendment) Order, 2024. 
(2) It shall come into force with effect from the Ist April, 2024. 


2. In the Tea (Marketing) Control Order, 2003, (hereinafter referred to as the said Order), in paragraph 2, for 
sub-paragraph (l), the following sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely:- 


‘(l) Every registered tea manufacturer shall, on and from the date of commencement of the Tea 
(Marketing) Control (Amendment) Order, 2024, sell- 


(i) not less than fifty per cent. of total tea manufactured in a calendar year, excluding tea 
mentioned in clause (ii); and 


(ii) one hundred per cent. of dust grades tea manufactured in a calendar year in its manufacturing 
units located in the geographical area of States of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Himachal Pradesh, 
Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttarakhand and West Bengal, 


through public tea auctions, held under the control of organiser of tea auction licensed to do so under this order: 
Provided that this paragraph shall not apply to a Mini Tea Factory. 


Explanation.—For the purpose of this paragraph, the “dust grades of tea” shall include all grades listed as dust grades 
in the Indian Standard IS 454-986’. 


3. In the FORM-E of said order, in Part-I, in serial number 0, after clause (vii) and the entries relating 
thereto, the following clause and entries shall be inserted, namely:- 


“(viil) Quantum of dust grades of tea manufactured during the month (in kgs.) 


Total Dust grades of tea manufactured Total Dust grades of tea used for manufacturing of 
packet tea, instant tea, tea bags, aromatic tea, green 
tea, quick brewing black tea and organic tea 
marketed directly by the manufacturer or exported 
directly (refer paragraph 2(2) of the Tea 
(Marketing) Control Order, 2003)
