असाधारण, भाग I, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा तमिलनाडु राज्य के अरियालुर जिले में NH45C 
के कि.मी. 80/400 से कि.मी.100/000 (विक्रवंडी-कुंभभोणम-तंजावुर सेट) तक के भू-खण्ड के निर्माण (चौड़ीकरण/पेव्ड 
शोल्डर सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन के लिए वह भूमि अपेक्षित 
है जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है ; 


और जबकि उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3ए की उपधारा (3) के तहत दिनांक 20.05.2023 
को "दीनाथंती" और "द हिंदू" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया है;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st August, 2023 


9.0. 3707(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.2078(E) Dated:03.05.2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary No. 1990, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 
1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH45C in the stretch of land from Km. 80.400 to Km. 100.000 in the district of 
Ariyalur in the State of TAMIL NADU; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published both in “Thinathandhi” and “The 
Hindu” dated 20.05.2023 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And Whereas No objection have been received from the land owner or Interested person by the Competent 
Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
