S.O. 309(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH347C in the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to Km. 36+400 (Khalghat-Kasrawad-Khargone-Bistan-MH- 
Border-Road) in the district of Khargone in the state of MADHYA PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Sub Divisional Officer 
(Revenue) and land Acquisition Officer Kasrawad, District Khargone, in writing and shall set out the grounds 
thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal 
practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections.
