और जबकि, इस अधिसूचना के साथ संलग्न अनुसूची में उल्लेखित गांवों के सर्वेक्षणों में से, जो आपत्तिया सक्षम 
प्राधिकारी द्वारा निर्धारित समयावधि के भीतर प्राप्त हुई थी, उन सभी पर विचार किया गया और सक्षम प्राधिकारी द्वारा

(North Western Railway, Construction organization), Notification number, 5.0. 3932 (8) dated 27 August, 2025 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 27" August, 2025 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008 ([] of 2008) and Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification 
for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely "Luni- 
Samdari-Bhildi Doubling Project of the District Balotra in the state of Rajasthan. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Rajasthan 
Patrika ” and “Dainik Bhaskar” dated !" September, 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, out of the surveys of the villages mentioned in the schedule annexed with this notification, all 
the objections were received within the stipulated time period by Competent Authority and accordingly, the same was 
disposed by Competent Authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed
