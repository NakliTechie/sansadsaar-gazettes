S.O. 3217(E).— Ministry of Power vide Notification No. 42/5/2023-R&R dated 03.01.2024 has 
made the appointment of Shri Jagdish Kumar as Chief Electrical Inspector and Shri Anand Kumar as 
Electrical Inspector for Patna Metro Rail Project (PMRP), under sub-section (1) of section 162 of the 
Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) read with Qualifications, Powers and Functions for Chief Electrical 
Inspector and Electrical Inspectors Rules, 2006 notified on 17.8.2006 and as amended on 16.05.2016. 


2. In para 1 of the aforesaid Notification, the present serving organisation of both the Officers mentioned 
as Patna Metro Rail Corporation Limited may be read as “Delhi Metro Rail Corporation Limited.”
