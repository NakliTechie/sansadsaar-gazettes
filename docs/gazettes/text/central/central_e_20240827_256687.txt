NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 26th April, 2024 
Notification under section 20 (E) of Railways Act, 989 


S.O. 3620(E)—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
( Railway Board)Notification No. 8.0. 70E dated 30.2.2024 ,published in the Gazette of India Ordinary, Part-II, 
Section 3, Sub section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as said notification ) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as said act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of Special Railway Project, 
namely Jharsuguda - Bilaspur 47 line (206 Km) in Jharsuguda district of Odisha.
