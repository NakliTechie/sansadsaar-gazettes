मापविज्ञान ( पैकबंद वस्तुएं ) नियम , 2011 में और संशोधन करने के लिए निम्नलिखित नियम बनाती है , अर्थात :

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 29th December , 2022 

G.S.R. 910 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) read with clauses ( j ) and ( q ) of 
sub - section ( 2 ) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act , 2009 ( 1 of 2010 ) , the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology ( Packaged Commodities ) Rules , 2011 , namely : 

1 . 

( 1 ) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology ( Packaged Commodities ) ( Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 
( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Legal Metrology ( Packaged Commodities ) ( Amendment ) Rules , 2022 , in rule 1 , in sub - rule ( 2 ) , for the 
figures , letters and words " 1st day of January , 2023 " , the figures , letters and words " 1st Day of February , 2023 ” 
shall be substituted .
