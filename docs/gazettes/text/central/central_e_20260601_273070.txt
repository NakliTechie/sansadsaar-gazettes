5.0. 2783(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Major Upgradation of Somnath Railway Station 
in Bhavnagar Division of Western Railway under Umbrella Work” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition 
of land in the State of Gujarat, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
