Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 


S.O. 4137(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 171 at Km 
390/5-6 at Rayalacheruvu Station yard" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra 
Pradesh in the District of Ananthapur for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the 
date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
