NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist July, 2026 
G.S.R. 554(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 read with sub-clause () of clause (g) of 
section 2 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 206 (6 of 206) and in pursuance of the amendment 
made to section 68 of the said Act under section 2 read with serial number 70 of the Schedule to the Jan Vishwas 


(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend 
the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (General) Rules, 206, namely: — 


I. () These rules may be called the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (General) 
Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (General) Rules, 206, in rule 
33, in sub-rule (I), in the Table, the following row shall be omitted, namely: - 


“Punishable with imprisonment under] ten per cent. of the estimated cost of the plot, apartment or building, as 
section 68. the case may be.”
