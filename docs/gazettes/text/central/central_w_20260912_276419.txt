ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the !2th February, 2026 


O.N. 07.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Lok Sabha of Odisha, 2024 was held in pursuance of the 
Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/EPS/2024/(6) dated 29" April, 2024. And 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the above-mentioned elections for 03-Sambalpur Parliamentary Constituency of 
Odisha was declared by the Returning Officer on 4 June, 2024 and hence the last date for lodging the account of 
Election Expenses was 47 July, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the District Election Officer, Sambalpur, Odisha report dated NIL forwarded by the 
Chief Electoral Officer, Odisha vide their EC-CON-COE-003-2024-2756 (Elec.) dated 3“ July, 2024, under sub- 
tule () of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 96, Sh. Sriram Pandey, a contesting candidate from 03- 
Sambalpur Parliamentary Constituency of Odisha has failed to lodge his account of election expenses, as required 
under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Sambalpur , a Show-Cause notice 
No. 76/ODISHA/PC-03/2024/CEMS-IV, dated 04..2024 was issued under sub-rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct 
of Elections Rules, 96l by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Sriram Pandey, for not lodging of his account 
of Election Expenses; and, 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub-rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, [96], Sh. Sriram Pandey, was directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, Sambalpur within 20 days from the date of receipt of the Notice; and 


Whereas, District Election Officer, Sambalpur vide his letter no. 2990/Elec dated 7.02.2025 informed that 
the said notice was served to his son on 24.0.2025, and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Sambalpur in his Supplementary Report, vide its letter no. 
2990/Elec. dated 7.02.2025 has reported that Sh. Sriram Pandey, has not submitted his account of election 
expenses. Further, after receipt of the said notice, Sh. Sriram Pandey, has neither furnished any reason nor 
explanation to the Commission of India, for failure to lodged the account as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section |0 A of the Representation of the People Act, 95 provides that :- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or under 
this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, the Election Commission shall, by order published in the 
Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of 
three years from the date of the order.” 


WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. Sriram Pandey has 
failed to lodge his account of election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95, the Election 
Commission of India hereby declares Sh. Sriram Pandey resident of At- Junush Patna, PO- College Square, PS- 
Mal Godown, Dist- Cuttack, a contesting candidate from 03-Sambalpur Parliamentary Constituency of General 
Election to the Lok Sabha of Odisha , 2024, to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either 
House of the Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a 
period of three years from the date of this order.
