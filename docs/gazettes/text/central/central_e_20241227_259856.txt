INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-11/14792-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of IndianNursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Vardan School Of Nursing situated at Behind Pargi Petrol Pump, By Pass Road, Fatepura, 
Distt.- Dahod, Pin Code- 389172, Gujarat is recognized by State Government of Gujarat and Gujarat Nursing 
Council to conduct ANM and GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-11/14792-INC (53875) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the Commissioner (Health), & Ex-Officio Secretary 
(Public Health & Family Welfare) Block No. 05, 1st Floor Old Sachiwalay, Dr. Jivraj Bhavan, Sector No. 10, 
Gandhinagar — 382010, Gujarat was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Vardan 
School Of Nursing situated at Behind Pargi Petrol Pump, By Pass Road, Fatepura, Distt.-Dahod, 
Pin Code- 389172, Gujarat. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Vardan School of Nursing. Even the institution, Vardan School Of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 

Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 


23/08/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially thoserelating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run ANM and GNM 


Course. 

Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM and GNM from Vardan School Of Nursing 
situated at Behind Pargi Petrol Pump, By Pass Road, Fatepura, Distt.- Dahod, Pin Code- 389172, Gujarat shall 
be entitled to be registered only in the State of Gujarat. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
