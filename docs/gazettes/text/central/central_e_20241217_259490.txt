(3) The Central Government on being satisfied with the plan and the estimation submitted under sub- 
paragraph (2), may make an order under sub-rule (2) of rule 3A of the rules for disbursement of amount to 
the Central Pollution Control Board or the State Pollution Control Board, as the case may be, for the purpose 
of restoration of damage. 


(4) The Fund Manager, on receipt of order under sub-paragraph (3), shall make arrangements for 
disbursement of amount as per the said order.’’.
