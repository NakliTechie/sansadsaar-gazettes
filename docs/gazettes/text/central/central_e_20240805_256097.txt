MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur , 35T July , 2024 
S.O. 37(E).— Notification under Section 20(E) of the Railways Act, 989 Whereas by the notification of 
the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway Board) Notification No. 620 dated 2.04.2024 
published in the Gazette of India Ordinary, Part-II, Section 3, sub-section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (l) of 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for execution of Special Railway Project of construction of IB Flyover of 9.0 KM length for Railway 
traffic connecting IB station and Brundamal station in Jharsuguda District of Odisha. 

And, whereas the substance of notification १२०.620 dated 2.04.2024 under sub section (4) of section: 20A of the 
said Act was published in the daily Newspapers, Aaroop, Bharat Darshan, New Indian Express & Hiranchal in Odia 
Edition on dated 25.06.2024 whereas no objections have been received in the stipulated period from the land losers, 
and whereas, in pursuance of sub section (l) of section: 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted 
his report to the Central Government. Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and 
in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (l) of the section: 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 
and, further in pursuance of sub section (2) of section: 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all Encumbrances.
