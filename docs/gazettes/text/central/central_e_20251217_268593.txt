S.O. 584(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 4550(E). Dated:07/0/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of NH753F in 
the stretch of land from Km. 0 to Km. 8.6 (Sub Divisional Officer Mangaon) in the district of RAIGAD in the state 
of MAHARASHTRA

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest
