S.O. 99(E).—Whereas, the Central Government is of the opinion that the ancient and historical 
monuments specified in the Schedule to this notification have ceased to be of national importance. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 35 of the Ancient Monuments and 
Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 958 (24 of 958), the Central Government hereby gives notice of its intention 
to declare that the monuments specified in the Schedule to this notification have ceased to be of national importance 
for the purposes of said Act. 


Any objection or suggestions, may be made within a period of two months from the date of issue of 
this notification, by any interested person in the said monuments; to the Director General, Archaeological Survey of 
India, 24 Tilak Marg, New Delhi-i000]. 


The objections or suggestions, if any, received from any person with respect to this notification within the 
period so specified shall be taken into consideration by the Central Government.
