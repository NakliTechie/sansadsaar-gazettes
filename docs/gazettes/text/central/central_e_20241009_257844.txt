Patna, the 16th September, 2024 


S.O. 4384(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred under clause 374 of Section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008, General Manager/East Central Railway has approved to declare "New line 
between Aurangabad Terminal and Anugrah Narayan Road (12.906 km) of East Central Railway", as 
Special Railway Project. Therefore, "New line between Aurangabad Terminal and Anugrah Narayan Road 
(12.906 km) of East Central Railway” project is declared Special Railway Project in the State of Bihar.
