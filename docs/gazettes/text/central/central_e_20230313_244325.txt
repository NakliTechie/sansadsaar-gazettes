S.O. 1179 ( E ) .— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways , S.O. 6150 ( E ) , dated 29 December , 2022 published in Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , 
Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highways Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ( widening / six - laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and 
operation of National Highway No. 79 on the stretch of land from Km . 34 + 400 to Km . 50 + 600 ( Kishangarh - 
Chittorgarh section ) in the District of Ajmer in the State of Rajasthan ; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ Daink Bhaskar " and " Panjab 
kesri " both are dated the 14.01.2023 under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of said Act ; 


And whereas , objections have been received and same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent authority , namely the sub divisional officer , Bhinai ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS

New Delhi , the 13th March , 2023
