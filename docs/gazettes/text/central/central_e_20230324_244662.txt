MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT , FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 17th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 215 ( E ) .- In exercise of powers conferred by sub - section ( 4 ) of section 4 read with 
section 35 of the National Green Tribunal Act , 2010 ( 19 of 2010 ) , the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules further to amend the National Green Tribunal ( Practices and Procedure ) Rules , 2011 , 
namely : 


1. Short title and commencement :-( 1 ) These rules may be called the National Green 
Tribunal ( Practices and Procedure ) Amendment Rules , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the National Green Tribunal ( Practices and Procedure ) Rules , 2011 , in sub - rule ( 1 ) of rule 3 , 
the proviso shall be omitted . 


[ F. No. 17 ( 52 ) / 2017 - PL / NGT ] 

Dr. SUJIT KUMAR BAJPAYEE , Jt . Secy . 


Note : The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , 
Sub - section ( 1 ) , vide number G.S.R. 296 ( E ) , dated the 4th April , 2011 and was last amended vide 
G.S.R. 1473 ( E ) , dated the 1st December , 2017 .
