MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd February, 2024 


S.O. 848(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Bharatiya 
Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints the Ist day of July, 
2024 as the date on which the provisions of the said Sanhita, except the provisions of the entry relating to 
section 106(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, in the First Schedule, shall come into force.
