F. No. DP-6//2024-DP.—The Hon’ble Supreme Court, vide its judgement dated 7.0.2025 in Writ 
Petition (Civil) No. 405/2023 (Jane Kaushik & Ors. Vs. Union of India), directed the Central Government to 
constitute an Advisory Committee to be chaired by Hon’ble Ms. Justice Asha Menon, former Judge of the Delhi High 
Court, to conduct an incisive study and recommend a viable equal opportunity policy and other measures for 
transgender persons. 


2. Pursuant to the aforesaid directions, the Central Government notified the composition, mandate, tenure, etc., of 
the Advisory Committee in the Gazette of India (Extraordinary) dated 23.2.2025. 
3. Now, in pursuance of the Hon’ble Supreme Court’s order dated 06.02.2026 in Miscellaneous Application 


No.96 of 2026 in Writ Petition (Civil) )२०.405 of 2023, the Central Government has decided to include Ms. Aparna 
Mehrotra, Senior Associate, Centre for Law and Policy Research, Bengaluru, as a Member of the Advisory 
Committee. A copy of the revised composition of the Committee is enclosed.
