MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th March, 2025 


S.O. 397(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.O. 2[3] (E), dated 29/05/2024, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section (I) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. 36 in the stretch of land from Km. 0.000 to Km. 28.000 and stretch of Tuljapur Bypass Design 
Chainage Km. 0.000 to Km. !.593 (Tuljapur-Ausa Section) in Taluka Dharashiv (Osmanabad) & Tuljapur District 
Dharashiv (Osmanabad). 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in "Lokmat Samachar " and " Dainik 
Sakal " both dated 30/07/2023, under sub- section (3) of section 3(A) of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person to the competent authority 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3(D) of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (.) of section 3(D) of the said, Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3(D) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
