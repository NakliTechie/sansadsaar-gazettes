MINISTRY OF FINANCE

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l6th December, 2025 


S.O. 5807(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy 
Act, 993 (5 of 993), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the 
Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services number S.0.83(E), dated the 5 March, 
207, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary , Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) , dated the 5" March, 207, 
namely :-- 


In the said notification, in the Table:- 

(i) against serial number 2 in column (4), for the entries, the following entries shall be substituted, namely: - 

“All districts of West Bengal (except Kolkata City, districts of Howrah, Hoogly, South 24 Parganas, Darjeeling, 
Jalpaiguri, Coochbehar, Uttar Dinajpur, Dakshin Dinajpur, Malda, Murshidabad, Birbhum, Alipurduar and 
Kalimpong) and Union territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands”; 

(ii) against serial number 3 in column (4), for the entries, the following entries shall be substituted namely: - 
“Kolkata city covering the areas of the following police stations namely - Shyampukur, Jorabagan, Burtolla, 
Burrabazar, Posta, Jorasanko, Amherst Street, and area of any other Police Station which may be created in 
future in Kolkata City and District of South 24 Parganas of West Bengal”; 


(iii) against serial number 4 in column (4), for the entries, the following entries shall be substituted namely: - 


“Darjeeling, Jalpaiguri, Coochbehar, Uttar Dinajpur, Dakshin Dinajpur, Malda, Murshidabad, Birbhum, 
Alipurduar and Kalimpong districts of West Bengal.”.
