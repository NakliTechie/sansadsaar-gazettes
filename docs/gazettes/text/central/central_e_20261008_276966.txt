MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th October, 2026 


9.0. 5525(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for Interchange at starting point of Ahmedabad-Dholera Expressway and way side amenities (section of NH-75!) in 
the District of Ahmedabad in the State of Gujarat, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made in writing to the Competent Authority, namely, Additional Special Land 


Acquisition Officer, | st floor, Disaster Management Building, Collector office Compound, Subhash Bridge Circle, 
Ahmedabad, and shall set out the grounds thereof. The Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after 
making such further inquiry, if any, as it considers necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land proposed to be acquired under this notification are available for 
inspection by the interested persons at the office of the aforesaid Competent Authority.
