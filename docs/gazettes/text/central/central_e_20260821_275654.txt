9.0. 4623(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (7) of section 3 of the 
Telecommunications Act, 2023 (44 of 2023), the Central Government hereby notifies the following 
telecommunication services, in respect of which the authorised entities shall ensure verifiable biometric based 
identification of users to whom such services are provided, in accordance with the Telecommunications (User 
Identification) Rules, 2026, namely:— 


(a) wireless access services; and 


(b) internet telephony service through mobile user terminals.
