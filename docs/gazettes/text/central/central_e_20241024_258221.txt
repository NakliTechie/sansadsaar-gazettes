MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th October, 2024 


S.O. 4654(E).—_ WHEREAS the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) 
clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub- section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 
(29 of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, issued a notification to declare 
an Eco Sensitive Zone around Mayureshwar Supe Wildlife Sanctuary, Maharashtra in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 2938(E), dated the 28" August, 2020; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public 
interest to amend the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides that whenever 
it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for amending 
the notification number S.O. 2938(E), dated the 28" August, 2020; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule 
(4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Environment and Forest and Climate 
Change, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 2938(E), 
dated the 287 August, 2020:- 


In the said notification, for paragraph 5 and 6, the following paragraph shall be substituted, namely:-

following persons, namely: -
