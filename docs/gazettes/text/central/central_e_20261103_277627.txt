NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd November, 2026 


9.0. 6003(E).— It is proposed by Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited to lay a pipeline for transportation of natural 
gas from Duliajan to Sectionalizing Valve Station-14 of the Guwahati-Numaligarh Pipeline. The proposed Right of 
User (RoU) of the natural gas pipeline from Duliajan to Sectionalizing Valve Station-14 of the Guwahati—Numaligarh 
Pipeline passes through the same route as the pipeline sections of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited, namely, 
CTF Geleki to Jorhat CTF, CTF Geleki to DJP Oil Pipeline, DJP to Lakwa CTF and GGS-I Lakwa to CTF Moran, for 
a length of 37.745 km in the districts of Sivasagar and Jorhat, Assam. 


Indradhanush Gas Grid Limited proposes to utilize the common Right of User (RoU) corridor of 
37.745 km in the districts of Sivasagar and Jorhat, Assam, along the pipeline sections of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation 
Limited, namely, CTF Geleki to Jorhat CTF, CTF Geleki to DJP Oil Pipeline, DJP to Lakwa CTF and GGS-I Lakwa to 
CTF Moran, which was originally acquired by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited. The said RoU has already 
been acquired by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited and published in the Gazette of India vide Notification No. 
5.0. 55(E) dated 5th January, 2012, published in Gazette of India No. 46 dated 11th January, 2012; Notification No. 
S.O. 2458(E) dated 9th October, 2012, published in Gazette of India No. 2027 dated 12th October, 2012; Notification 
No. 8.0. 2459(E) dated 15 October, 2012, published in Gazette of India No. 2028 dated 12th October, 2012; 
Notification No. 8.0. 2379(E) dated 27th September, 2012, published in Gazette of India No. 1991 dated 6th October, 
2012; Notification No. 8.0. 2370(E) dated 12th October, 2011, published in Gazette of India No. 1977 dated 14th 
October, 2011; Notification No. S.O. 207(E) dated 23rd January, 2010, published in Gazette of India No. 167 dated 
29th January, 2010; Notification No. 8.0. 208(E) dated 23rd January, 2010, published in Gazette of India No. 168 
dated 29th January, 2010; Notification No. S.O. 206(E) dated 23rd January, 2010, published in Gazette of India No. 
166 dated 29th January, 2010; and Notification No. 8.0. 204(E) dated 23rd January, 2010, published in Gazette of 
India No. 164 dated 29th January, 2010.
