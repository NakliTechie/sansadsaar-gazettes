S.O. 269(E).— In Exercise of power conferred under clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) 
Act 2008, General Manager/East Central Railway, hereby accords approval to declare the project titled 
“3rd and 4th Line between Danapur - Patna & 3rd Line from Rajendra Nagar - Patna Saheb and 3rd & 4th line 
From Patna Saheb -Fatuha (29.8 KM)"under,Danapur Division of East Central Railway,as a Special Railway 
Project. 


Accordingly, the said project “3rd and 4th Line between Danapur - Patna & 3rd Line from Rajendra 
Nagar - Patna Saheb and 3rd & 4th line From Patna Saheb -Fatuha (29.8 KM)",underDanapur Division of East 
Central Railway,is hereby declared as a Special Railway Project in the State of Bihar.
