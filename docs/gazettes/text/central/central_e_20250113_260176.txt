New Delhi, the 13th January, 2025 


S.O. 203(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of 
Business) Rules, 1961, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred 
and Seventy-ninth Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force at once. 


2: In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE, 
under the heading “MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE (SWASTHYA AUR PARIVAR 
KALYAN MANTRALAYA)”, under the sub-heading “C. DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH RESEARCH 
(SWASTHYA ANUSANDHAN VIBHAG)”, after existing entry 10, the following entry shall be inserted, 
namely: - 


“11. Matters related to Health Technology Assessment.”. 


Droupadi Murmu 
President, 


[F. No. 1/21/14/2023-Cab.]
