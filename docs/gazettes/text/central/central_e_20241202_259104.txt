MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th November, 2024 


S.O. 59(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 79A of the information Technology Act 2000 
(2 of 2000) the Central Government hereby notifies Cyber Forensic Laboratory, Indian Computer Emergency, 
Response Team (CERT-In), Electronics Niketan, 6 CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi as Examiner of 
Electronic Evidence within India, with the following scope, namely: - 


i. Computer (Media) Forensics including Drone Storage Media and excluding Floppy Disk Drive 
ii. Mobile Devices Forensics 
iii. CCTV Forensics Media Recovery (excluding Video Authentication and Enhancement) 


iv. Cloud Forensics will be carried out from premises of Cyber Forensic Laboratory, CERT-In using 
access authentication tokens and credentials recovered from seized exhibits. 


With aforesaid notification, earlier published gazette notification No. S.0.3568(E) dated 9" October, 2020, 
Extraordinary, Part II- Section 3 - Sub-section (ii), is rescinded by central Government.
