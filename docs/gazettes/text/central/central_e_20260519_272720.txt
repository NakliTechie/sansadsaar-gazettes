MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th May, 2026 
S.O. 2570(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.0.4552 (E) Dated:07/10/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (two lane with paved shoulder etc.), maintenance, management and 


operation of National Highway No-244 in the stretch of land from design Km. 12.850 to Km. 20.300 (Goha Link Road 


- 2.016 Km) in district of Doda in the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Daily Excelisor" (English) and "Amar 
Ujala" (Hindi) both dated 29.10.2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 


same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 


its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 


the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely 


in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
