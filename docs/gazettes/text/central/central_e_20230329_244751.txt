पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 5559 ( इ ) दिनाँक 30th नवंबर 2022 , जो भारत के राजपत्र , 
असाधारण , भाग II , खण्ड 3 , उपखंड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , तेलंगाना राज्य के सूर्यापेट जिले के एनएच -930 P

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 28th March , 2023 

S.O. 1451 ( E ) .– Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , S.O N.O 5559 ( E ) dated 30th November 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part 
II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 
1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Widening to 2/4 lane with Paved 
Shoulders of Junction at Hyderabad ORR , Valigonda - Thorrur Section ( Junction at NH30 ) of NH - 930P from 
Km 82.375 to Km . 94.650 and Km.95.640 to Km . 99.900 ( RDO - Suryapet ) , in the district of SURYAPET in the 
state of TELANGANA . 


[ भाग II– खण्ड 3 ( ii ) ] 

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ Namasthe Telangana " 
( Telugu ) and " The Hindu " ( English ) both dated 22 January 2023 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said 
Act ; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
