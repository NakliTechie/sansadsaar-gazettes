Patna, the 6th May, 2026 


S.O. 2249(E).—The Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of section 20A 
of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act, being satisfied that the land, as brief 
description of which is set out in the Schedule annexed hereto, is required to be acquired for public purpose at Mauza - 
Khesmi, Circle- Topchanchi, District - Dhanbad in Jharkhand State for "Special Railway Project- Eastern Dedicated 
Freight Coridor” in connection with construction of Gomoh to Pradhan-Khanta section. The land left over under 


acquisition, which is required to be affected for construction, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made in writing to the Competent Authority, that is, the District Land Acquisition 
Officer, Dhanbad and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give an opportunity of being 
heard to the objector either in person or through a legal practitioner and After hearing all such objections and making 
such further inquiry, if any, as the competent authority may consider necessary, he may, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections.
