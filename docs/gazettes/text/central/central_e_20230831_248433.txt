NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 30th August, 2023 


Subject: Cancellation of a part of outstanding stock of Non-interest bearing Government of India IDBI Special 
Security, 2024. 


F.No.4(4)-B(W&M)/208.—Government of India, hereby notifies cancellation of Non-interest bearing 
Government of India IDBI Special Security, 2024 (hereinafter briefly described as “the Special Security”) for an 
aggregate amount of Rupees one thousand five hundred crore only (%,500 crore) [nominal]. 


2. Date of Cancellation 
The date of cancellation of the Special Security by the Government of India shall be the August 30, 2023. 
3. Outstanding Stock 


The outstanding stock of the Special Security shall be Rupees Eight Hundred Seventy Eight crore and Seventy Three 
lakhs only (Rs. 878.73 crore) [nominal] after cancellation. 


By order of the President of India
