ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th March, 2024 


O.N. 39(E).—Whereas, the President of India has, by notification issued under Section l2 of the 
Representation of the People Act, !95(43 of 95) and published in the Gazette of India on 04th March, 2024, has 
been pleased to call upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Jharkhand, to elect 02 (two) members to 
the Council of States to fill the vacancies in that House caused due to the retirement of the sitting members on 03rd 
May, 2024 on the expiration of their term of office; 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-section () of Section 39 and Section 56 of the said Act, the Election 
Commission hereby- 


(A) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the ]lth March, 2024 (Monday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 2th March, 2024 (Tuesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(०) the l4th March, 2024 (Thursday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 2lst March, 2024 (Thursday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 23rd March, 2024 (Saturday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(B) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be 
taken in the above-mentioned State on the date specified above for the election.
