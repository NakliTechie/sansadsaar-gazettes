ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th September, 2025 


O.N. 48 (E).— WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly, 2023 for 06- 
Davangere North Assembly Constituency of Karnataka was held in pursuance of the Election Commission 
of India Notification No. 464/KT-LA/2023, dated 3" April, 2023; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95l, every contesting 
candidate at an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with 
the District Election Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be a true copy of the 
account kept by him/her or his/her election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the election for 06- Davangere North Assembly Constituency of 
Karnataka, 2023 was declared by the Returning Officer on 3" May, 2023 and hence the last date for 
lodging the account of Election Expenses was |2™ June, 2023; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report dated 05.08.2024 of District Election Officer, Davangere under sub- 
tule (l) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, [96] received through the Chief Electoral Officer, 
Karnataka vide his letter no. DPAR/08/Chuvebha(Addl CEO-)2023; dated 3.08.2024 stating that Sh. 
Keerthi Kumar. K.S, a contesting candidate from 06- Davangere North Assembly Constituency in 
General Election to Legislative Assembly of Karnataka, 2023 has failed to lodge the account of his election 
expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Davangere a ‘Show Cause 
Notice’ No. 76/KT-LA/34/2023/CEMS-II, dated 20" September, 2024 was issued under sub rule (5) of 
Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 96l by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Keerthi 
Kumar. K.S, for not lodging of the account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said ‘Show Cause Notice’ and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 96, Sh. Keerthi Kumar. K.S was directed to submit representation in 
writing to the Commission explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account 
of his election expenses with the District Election Officer, Davangere District within 20 days from the 
date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Davangere in his report no. ELNCR/57/2024-25; dated 
28.03.2025 has reported that the candidate Sh. Keerthi Kumar. K.S. was not residing in the given address 
and his whereabouts was not known, hence the said ‘Show Cause Notice’ was pasted on the wall of the 
house and a mahazar was prepared 27..2024; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Davangere in his Supplementary Report no. 
ELNCR/57/2024-25; dated 03.07.2025 on account of the candidate, received through Chief Electoral 
Officer, Karnataka letter No. DPAR/08/Chuvebha (Addl.CEO-l) 2023; dated 0.07.2025 has reported that 
Sh. Keerthi Kumar. K.S has not submitted any representation or statement of account of election 
expenses. Further, after receipt of the said notice, Sh. Keerthi Kumar. K.S has neither furnished any 
reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of India for failure to lodge the account of his election 
expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95! provides that:- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 


the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified 
and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.’’; 


WHEREAS, on basis of the facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. 
Keerthi Kumar. K.S has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses and has no good reason or 
justification for the failure to do so; and
