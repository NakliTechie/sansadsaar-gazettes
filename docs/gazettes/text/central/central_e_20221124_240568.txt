हो जाने के पश्चात्‌ कि तमिलनाडु राज्य के कड्डलोर जिले में NH45A के कि.मी. 67 से कि.मी. 723.8 (नया कलेक्टर 


कार्यालय भवन कुड्टालोर) तक के भू-खण्ड के निर्माण (चौड़ीकरण/पेब्ड atest सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का बनाना आदि),

गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है । 


कोई भी व्यक्ति, जो उक्त भूमि में हितबद्ध है, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3ग की उप-धारा (॥) के अधीन पूर्वोक्त 


प्रयोजन के लिए ऐसी भूमि के उपयोग पर राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से इक्कीस दिन के भीतर अपनी 
आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा |

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th November, 2022 


S.O. 5490(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule 
below, is required for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH45A in the stretch of land from Km. 67 to Km. 23.8 (New Collector 
Office Building Cuddalore) in the district of CUDDALORE in the state of TAMIL NADU, hereby declares 
its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under 
sub-section(]) of section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special District Revenue 
Officer in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector 
an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such
