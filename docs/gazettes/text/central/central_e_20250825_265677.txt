MINISTRY OF HEALTH ANDFAMILY WELFARE 
(Department of Health and Family Welfare) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd August, 2025 


S.O. 3867(E).— In pursuance of sub clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the 
Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 20l6, (as 
amended) read with rule 5 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, 
Knowledge) Rules, 2020, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Department of Health & Family Welfare, 
having been authorized by the Central Government, hereby notifies that the National Board of Examination 
in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) is allowed to perform Aadhar authentication, on voluntary basis, for verifying 
identity of candidates during various stages of national level medical examinations conducted by National 
Board of Examination in Medical Sciences (NBEMS), using Yes/No or/and eK YC authentication facilities. 


National Board of Examination in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) shall adhere to all relevant 
provisions of the Act, the rules and regulations made thereunder, and directions issued by the Unique 
Identification Authority of India.
