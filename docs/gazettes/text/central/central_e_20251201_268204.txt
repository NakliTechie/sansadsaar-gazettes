MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 


[East Coast Railway (Construction)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Bhubaneswar, the Ist December, 2025 
Declaration Under Section 20E of the Railway Act, 989 


9.0. 5538(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway 
(East Coast Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number S.0.3645(E), dated 7th August, 2025 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 8th August, 2025 (here in after referred 
to as the said notification) and issued under section (l) of section 204 of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, measurement and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely, “Talcher-Bimalagarh New B.G. Rail Link Project” under Khurda Road Division of East 
Coast Railway in the State of Odisha. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in 02 daily local newspapers namely 
“SAMBAD” in Odia (Bhubaneswar Edition), and “NEW INDIAN EXPRESS“ in English (Bhubaneswar & Sambalpur 
Edition) Dated 30th August, 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of section 20 D of the said Act. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
