NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 22nd October, 2026 


S.O. 5776(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 989, the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 228 at Km. 626/2-3 between Lingiri — Yadgir stations" as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Karnataka in the District of Yadgir for providing National Infrastructure 
for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
