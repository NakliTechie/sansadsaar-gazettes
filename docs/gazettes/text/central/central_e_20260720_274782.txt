NOTIFICATION 
Gorakhpur, the 20th July, 2026 


S.O. 3973(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of Section 20A of the Railway Act, 
1989 (24 of 1989) & Railway Amendment Act 2008 (11 of 2008) (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, situated at District-Gonda of the state 
of Uttar Pradesh, the brief description of which has given in the Schedule annexed hereto, is required for the 
execution of the special railway project, namely Katra & Tikri Stations (15.35 Km.) Doubling Project Special 
Railway Project of North Eastern Railway, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under Section 20D subsection (1) of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority/Special Land Acquisition Officer 
District-Gonda Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall set out the grounds there of, and the competent authority shall give 
the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, 
either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order by the Competent Authority under subsection (2) of Section 20D of the said Act shall be final 
u/s 20D sub-section (3). 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available, and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
