का.आ. 5144 ( अ ) . – केन्द्रीय सरकार ने , राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 1659 ( अ ) दिनांक 6 अप्रैल , 2022 , जो भारत के राजपत्र , 
असाधारण , भाग II , खण्ड 3 , उपखंड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , द्वारा उत्तराखंड राज्य के हरिद्वार जिले में राष्ट्रीय 
राजमार्ग सं०-344 BG " उत्तर प्रदेश राज्य के सहारनपुर जिले में , दिल्ली - सहारनपुर देहरादून ( ग्राम - हलगोया ) से शुरू 
होकर उत्तराखंड राज्य के हरिद्वार जिले में राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग 334 ( पुराना रा ० रा ० 58 ) के ( ग्राम - अतमलपुर बोंगला ) तक ,

आदि ) , अनुरक्षण , प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के 
अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 4th November , 2022 

S.O. 5144 ( E ) .— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , number S.O.1659 ( E ) dated 6th April , 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , 
Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ( widening / four - laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and 
operation of National Highway No. 344BG " Development of six lane access control spur to Haridwar starting at its 
junction with Delhi - Saharanpur - Dehradun Ecnomic Corridor ( Vill . - Halgoya ) in Saharanpur ( UP ) and terminating on 
NH - 334 ( Old NH - 58 ) in Bahadrabad bypass ( Vill . - Atmalpur Bongla ) in District Haridwar ( Uttarakhand ) in the state 
of Uttar Pradesh & Uttarakhand under Bharatmala Pariyojana " in the stretch of land from Km . 19.060 to Km . 50.700 
in the district of HARIDWAR in the state of UTTARAKHAND . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Dainik Jagran " and " Hindustan 
Times " both dated 27th April , 2022 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 

And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
