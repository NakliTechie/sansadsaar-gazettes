MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th December, 2025 


5.0. 575(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4387(E) Dated:26/09/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for "Construction/upgradation to 2 laning with paved shoulders",Maintenance, 
management and operation of NH-l27B in the stretch of land from Km. 0.00 to Km. 63.224 (Phulbari-Goeragre 
Section) in the district of West Garo Hills in the state of MEGHALAYA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Shillong Times” dated 04-0- 
2025 and “The Salantini Janera” dated 04-0-2025 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
