MINISTRY OF POWER 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30" June, 2023 


G.S.R. 467(E):— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (2) of Section 76 of the 
Electricity Act, 2003 (Act 36 of 2003), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, to amend the 
National Electricity Plan Notification Rules, 2004, namely:- 


l. (l) These rules may be called the National Electricity Plan Notification (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Inthe National Electricity Plan Notification rules, 2004, for rule 3, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 


“3. Notification of draftNational Electricity Plan.- (l) A draft National Electricity Plan prepared under the 
provisions of sub-section (4) of Section 3 of the Act shall be published on the website of the Authority for 
inviting suggestions and objections of licensees, generating companies and general public and notice to this 
effect shall be published in at least two daily newspapers; 


(2) For inviting suggestions and objections, a period of sixtydays, from the date of publication of such draft 
Plan, shall be provided by the Authority.” 


(3) The final National Electricity Plan shall be notified on the website of the Authority.
