MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th May, 2026 


S.O. 2613(E).— In pursuance of clause (b) of sub-section (3) of section 79 of the Information 
Technology Act, 2000 (21 of 2000), read with clause (d) of sub-rule (1) of rule 3 of the Information 
Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021, the Central Government 
being the appropriate Government hereby notifies Joint Secretary (Films), Ministry of Information and 
Broadcasting, for the purpose of issuing notice to the intermediaries in relation to any unlawful information 
as per clause (ii) of sub section (1B) of section 7 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952.
