MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th May, 2026 
S.O. 2484(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (5) of section 34 of the Occupational Safety 
Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (37 of 2020) and in supersession of the notification of Government of India 
in the Ministry of Labour and Employment No. 4013(E), dated the 197 of September 2024, except as respects things 
done or omitted to done before such supersession, the Central Government hereby appoints, the Director General of 
Mines Safety in the Directorate General of Mines Safety, Dhanbad, to be the Chief Inspector-cum-Facilitator of Mines 


for all the territories to which the said Code extends with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
official gazette, until further orders.
