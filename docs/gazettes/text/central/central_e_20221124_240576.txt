परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 5062 (अ) तारीख "07.12.2021" , जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) F प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा राजस्थान राज्य के प्रतापगढ़ जिले में राष्ट्रीय 
राजमार्ग संख्या -113 निम्बाहेडा दाहोद खण्ड के कि.मी. 72+000 से कि.मी. 83+000 तक निर्माण (चौड़ा करने/दो लेन 
पवेद MIST का बनाने आदि ), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची 
में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "31.12.2021 " 
को "राजस्थान पत्रिका " और "दैनिक जय कांठल" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ; 


और आक्षेप प्राप्त हुए थे और सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उन पर विचार कर लिया है और आक्षेपों को अननुज्ञात कर दिया 


है;

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th November, 2022 


8.0. 5479(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways,S.O. 5062(E) date 07.12.2021 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 
1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane 
with paved shoulder etc.) for Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 113 
Section of Nimbahera-Dahod from Km. 72+000 to Km. 83+000 in the District of Pratapgarh in the State of 
Rajasthan; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Rajasthan Patrika” and 
“Dainik jai Kanthal” both dated 31.12.2021 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by 
the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
