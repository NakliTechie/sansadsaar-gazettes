(चौड़ीकरण/पेग्ड Mest सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस 
अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

जबकि सक्षम प्राधिकारी को धारा 3-ग के तहत दायर आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई है, जिस पर विचार किया गया है और 
उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

रिपोर्ट दे दी है;

S.O. 1657(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 2822(E) Dated:18/07/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part वी, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building "(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance,

management and operation of NH275 and Re-construction of Major Bridge at Km 173.600 across Lakshmanathirtha 
River along with approaches land in the stretch from Km. 171.30 to Km. 176.40 Hunsur Town Limits (Bantwal - 
Mysore Section) in the district of MYSURU in the state of KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Prajavani” and “The Time of 
India” both dated 24.07.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
