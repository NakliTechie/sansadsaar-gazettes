Subject: Extension in Import Period for Yellow Peas under ITC(HS) Code 07300 of Chapter 07 of ITC (HS) 
2022, Schedule —I(Import Policy) —reg. 


S.O. 3946 (E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 and Section 5 of Foreign Trade (Development 
& Regulation) Act, 992, read with paragraph |.02 and 2.0l of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023, as amended 
from time to time, and in continuation to Notification Nos. 50/2023 dated 08.2.2023, 6/2023 dated 23.02.2024 and 
04/2023 dated 05.04.2024 and 2/2024-25 dated 08.05.2024, the Central Government hereby extends Import Policy 
Conditions for Yellow Peas under ITC(HS) Code 07300 of Chapter 07 of ITC(HS), 2022, Schedule —I (Import 
Policy) from 3i" October 2024 to 3 December 2024. All other terms and condition remain the same as in the 
referred notifications. 


Effect of the Notification: Import of Yellow Peas under ITC(HS) Code 07300 is “Free” without the MIP 
condition and without Port Restriction, subject to registration under online Import Monitoring System, with immediate 
effect for all import consignments where Bill of Lading(Shipped on Board) is issued on or _ before 
3* December 2024. 


This is issued with the approval of the Minister of Commerce & Industry.
