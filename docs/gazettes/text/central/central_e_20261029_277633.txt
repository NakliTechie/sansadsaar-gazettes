MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[ Western Railway (Construction Organization) ] 
CORRIGENDUM NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 29th October, 2026 


S.O. 5908(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board ) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No.498(E) of dated 9.06.2026 Extra Ordinary- part II- 
Section-3 -Sub-section (i) (here in after referred to as the said notification under subsection(]) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention 
to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Miyagam Karjan — lakodra Fly Over Project in 
District Vadodara in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “Divya 
bhaskar” dated 25th June 2026 and “The Indian Express” dated 25th June 2026 under subsection (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, objection has been received & resolved by the competent authority. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority Dabhoi & 
Savali has submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
