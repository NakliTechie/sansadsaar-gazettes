Objects of the Fund The objects of the Fund shall be: 


to administer and apply the moneys of the Fund for conservation, maintenance, promotion, protection, 
preservation and upgradation of monuments protected or otherwise; 


to conduct studies relating to the artistic, scientific and technical problems involved in the conservation, 
maintenance, promotion, protection, preservation and rehabilitation of cultural and natural heritage; 


to impact training to staff and specialists at all levels in the fields of identification, protection, conservation, 
preservation and rehabilitation of cultural heritage both tangible and intangible as well as the natural heritage; 


protection and promotion of artistic endeavour in all its forms, particularly innovative experiments in arts; 


the promotion and reinforcement of oral and other intangible forms of cultural expression and recording 
documentation of forms that are dying out or are threatened with extinction; 


the promotion and reinforcement of inter-disciplinary research in areas such as interaction between 
traditional values and development process in the cultural domain that make up the contemporary dynamics 
of cultural change; 


the construction of additional space to accommodate new and special galleries in existing museums and 
construction of new museums; 


the formulation of local, municipal or regional strategies of cultural development seen as integral to the 
overall development of our society; 


the establishment or reinforcement of institutions, structure and facilities whose purpose is cultural or artistic, 
including mechanisms available for support of such activities; 


the training of specialists in cultural activities, with a particular emphasis on creation of a cadre of cultural 
managers and administrators; 


to supply of equipment to governmental and non-governmental organizations with a view to maintain, 
promote, protect and preserve the cultural and natural heritage; 


to promote international cultural cooperation in particular exchanges which may promote the development of 
indigenous expertise and human resources as also the activities which are within the ambit of Cultural 
Exchange Programmes entered into between India and other countries; 


to provide low interest or interest free loans for projects and activities relatable to any of the aforesaid 
objects.

Additional endowments: 


The Council may accept any endowment, donation crowd funding or other contributions in augmentation of 
the Fund or towards actualization of any of its objects. 


The Council may also accept endowments, donations or other contributions for any special purpose 
connected with any of the Schemes not inconsistent with or calculated to impede the due working of the 
provisions of the Scheme. 


It would be possible for a donor to the Fund to indicate a project along with any specific location/aspect for 
funding and also an agency for the execution of the project while making donations to Fund subject to 
general policy guidelines and rules, if any, in this behalf. The Council or the Committee in case the power to 
accept endowments, donations or other contributions have been delegated to it, shall respect the choice of the 
donor to the extent possible. When a donor’s project covers a World Heritage Site the matter would need to 
be specifically considered keeping in view the need to take care of the environment surrounding the site, as 
also the fact that adequate funds for it might already be available from conventional sources. 


Eligibility of financial assistance: Any State Government or voluntary organization with a known record of 
service in the field of art and culture and registered under the Societies Registration Act 1860 (21 of 1860) or 
registered as Public Trust at least for the last two years will be eligible for applying for financial assistance 
from the Fund for a specific project. Individuals are not entitled to financial assistance under this Scheme. 


Application to Council: Applications for financial assistance from or under the Fund from voluntary 
organizations shall be in the Form specified in Appendix annexed to this Scheme. 


The Financial assistance or the loans under the Fund shall be decided by the Committee taking into 
consideration the project, the status of the executing body as also the wishes of the donors: Provided that 
there should be no upper limit of financial assistance under the Fund. 


Consideration of Application: All applications for financial assistance or the loans from the Fund shall be 
considered and disposed of by the Committee and the Council shall be informed about such decisions: 


Provided that before any application is considered, the President of the Committee, if he thinks it necessary 
to do so, refer such application to the State Government concerned or any other body for expressing its views 
thereon. 


Power to stop financial assistance: The President of the Committee, if he thinks it necessary to do so, and for 
reasons to be recorded in writing, may withhold or reduce any undisbursed financial assistance, made under 
the Scheme. 


Conditions of financial assistance : It shall be the condition of financial assistance to any voluntary 
organization that the voluntary organization that the voluntary organization or as the case may be, maintain a 
separate account of the financial assistance received, render a full account of the financial assistance within 
the stipulated time, shall furnish utilization certificate at the time specified in this behalf, shall make available 
audited accounts of the financial assistance received within the stipulated time and assist the officers of the 
Fund or such other Officers who may be so authorized in this behalf in the inspection of the programmes 
executed or books maintained by such organizations. 


Duration of Membership: 


A member of the Council or Committee shall cease to be a Member if he dies or resigns his membership or 
becomes of unsound mind or insolvent or is convicted of a criminal offence involving moral turpitude. 


The nominations of non-official members on the Council and the Committee shall be done by the Central 
Government in the Ministry of Culture 


A non-official Member shall be appointed for a period of three years. 

A member shall be eligible for re-nomination also.

31. Audit of accounts: 


1. Accounts of all moneys and properties and of income and expenditure of the Fund shall be maintained by the

li. A statement of the annual accounts of the Fund shall be approved by the Council and after the same has been

month of June by the Member Secretary of the Council and after approval of the Council, presented to the 
Government of India. This report shall be laid on the table of the house of Parliament.

out of interest accrued on the Corpus Fund and out of the contributions of the donors. 


2. Assistance under the National Culture Fund will be given to Government or non-Government organizations 
to foster India’s contemporary culture as well as cultural heritage and to bring both within the reach of the 
largest possible number of its citizens. 


3. Assistance under the Fund is not available to those Government or non-Government organizations who are 
already on receipt of financial assistance from attached/sub ordinate offices or autonomous organizations 
under the Ministry of Culture under any scheme. 


4. The Fund could accept projects relating to preparatory assistance, technical cooperation including studies of 
problems on heritage protection, provision of experts, supply of equipment, emergency assistance, training 
and support for promotional activities. 

5. NCE shall strive to keep in readiness a large number of projects requiring funding and shall, from time to 
time, renew, upgrade and add to them. The donors would be free to choose any projects or suggest new ones
