परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3499(आ) तारीख 29 जुलाई, 2025, जो भारत के

135ए के जौनपुर से अकबरपुर खण्ड के किमी0 139.000 से किमी0 179.200 (पैकेज-॥॥) के 4 लेन में चौड़ीकरण एवं 
उन्नयन कार्य एवं अम्बेडकरनगर बाईपास के निर्माण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध

रिपोर्ट दे दी है;

पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जन किया जाना चाहिए ;

New Delhi, the Ist April, 2026 


S.O. 1676(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 3499(E) Dated 29/07/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part H, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction (Widening/4-Lane etc) maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. 135A in the stretch of land from Km. 139.00 to Km. 179.20 for Widening to 4 lane from Km. 
139.00 to Km. 179.200 Pkg-II (Jaunpur — Akbarpur section) and Akbarpur bypass of NH-135A in the State of Uttar 
Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Aaj" and "The Times of India" 
both dated 12th August, 2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 


conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
