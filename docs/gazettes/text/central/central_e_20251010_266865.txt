MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l0th October, 2025 
S.O. 4635(E).— Whereas the Central Government is satisfied that public interest so requires that the services 
of the industry engaged in the Iron Ore Mining, which are covered under item 6 of the First Schedule to the Industrial 


Disputes Act, 947 (4 of 947) hereinafter referred to as the said Act, to be a public utility service for the purposes of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, the Central Government has declared the said industry to be public utility service for the purposes 
of the said Act for a period of six months with effect from the 4™ April, 2025, vide notification of the Government of 
India in the Ministry of Labour and Employment, number 5.0. 699(E), dated the ' April, 2025; 


And whereas, the Central Government is of the opinion that public interest requires the extension of the public 
utility service status to the said industry for a further period of six months. 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to sub-clause (vi) of clause (n) of section 2 
of the Industrial Disputes Act, |947 (/4 of 947), the Central Government hereby declares the services of the industry 
engaged in the Iron Ore Mining to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act for a period of six months 
with effect from the 4" October, 2025.
