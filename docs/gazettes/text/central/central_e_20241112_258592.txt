NOTIFICATION 

Kolkata, the 25th September, 2024 


S.O. 4877(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of Two Additional Loading Lines at 
Karamtola Station for capturing additional stone loading” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in 
Sahibganj district in the State of Jharkhand, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from 
the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette. 


[F. No. W(6)336/0/4 Vol-IV/Karamtola Station] 
VIKRAM GUPTA, Principal Chief Engineer
