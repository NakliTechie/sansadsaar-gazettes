New Delhi, the 25th August, 2025 
S.O. 3870(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways S.O. No.2658 Dated:3/06/2025 , published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the

submitted its report to the Central Government;

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
