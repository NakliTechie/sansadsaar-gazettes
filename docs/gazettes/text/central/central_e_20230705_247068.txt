MINISTRY OF AYUSH 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd June, 2023 


G.S.R. 479(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (k) of sub-section (2) of section 54 of the 
National Commission for Indian System of Medicine Act, 2020 (/4 of 2020), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules, to amend the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (Submission of List of the 
Medical Practitioners) Rules, 202], namely:— 


l. Short title and commencement.—(l) These rules may be called the National Commission for Indian System of 
Medicine (Submission of List of the Medical Practitioners) (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (Submission of List of the Medical Practitioners) 
Rules, 202] in rule 3, in sub-rule (2), the words “and forward two hard copies of the same by speed post" shall be 
omitted.
