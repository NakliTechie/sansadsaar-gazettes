CORRIGENDUM NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2024 
Case No. CVD/SSR-09/2023 


Subject : Sunset review investigation of countervailing duty concerning imports of Welded Stainless-Steel Pipes 
and Tubes originating in or exported from China PR and Vietnam. 


F. No. 7/23/2023-DGTR.--Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act, 975 as amended from time to time 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Act") and the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of 
Countervailing Duty on Subsidized Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 995 thereof, as amended from 
time to time, as amended from time to time, thereof, the Designated Authority has issued the Final Finding 
Notification No. 7/23/2023 dated 5.06.2024 in respect of the anti-subsidy investigation concerning imports of 
“Welded Stainless-Steel Pipes and Tubes” originating in or exported from China PR and Vietnam. 


The Authority notes that in row no. 6 against column no. 6 of the Duty Table in paragraph 39, the description was 
inadvertently mentioned as ‘Any producer other than 2 to 4 above’ instead of ‘Any producer other than 3 to 5 above’. 
In view of the above, the paragraph 39 of the Final Finding Notification No. 7/23/2023 dated 5.06.2024 stands 
substituted as mentioned below: 


39. Accordingly, the Authority recommends continued imposition of definitive anti-subsidy duties on the import of 
the subject goods, originating in or exported from China PR and Vietnam as indicated in Col 7 of the duty table below, 
for a period of five years from the date of notification to be issued in this regard by the Central Government.
