New Delhi, the 3rd December, 2024 


F. No. HQ-3079/0/2024-AUTH-II(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (2) of 
section 54 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 20l6 (/8 
of 206), the Unique Identification Authority of India hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the 
Aadhaar (Authentication and Offline Verification) Regulations, 202!, namely:— 


. Short title and commencement. — (/) These regulations may be called the Aadhaar (Authentication and Offline 
Verification) Second Amendment Regulations, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Aadhaar (Authentication and Offline Verification) Regulations, 202],— 


(a) inregulation 2, in sub-regulation (), in clause (0), for the words “Requesting entity”, the words “requesting 
entity” shall be substituted; 


(b) in regulation 2,— 


(i) in the short title, for the words “Requesting Entities and Authentication Service Agencies”, the 
words “requesting entity and Authentication Service Agency” shall be substituted; and 


(ii) in sub-regulation (6), for the words “the Authority may:”, the following words shall be substituted, 
namely:—
