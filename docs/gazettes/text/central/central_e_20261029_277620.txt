5.0. 5911(E).— In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the projects of- 


1. “Provision of 2-lane ROB (at Railway Cost) in lieu of LC no. 42 at KM 44.466 between KUP- Malerkotla 
Stations, on Duri-Ludhiana section near Malerkotla” 


And 


2. “Provision of 2-lane ROB (at Railway Cost) in lieu of LC no. 43 at KM 45.657 between Malerkotla- 
Himmatana Stations on Duri-Ludhiana section near Malerkotla.” 


In Ambala Division of Northern Railway as a "Special Railway Projects" for acquisition of approximately 3.7815 
hectares of land in Malerkotla District in the State of Punjab, for providing national infrastructure for public purpose 
with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
