S.O. 490(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 5 of the Inland 
Vessels Act, 202] (24 of 202) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, in relation 
to the central database as defined in clause (f) of section 3 of the said Act, hereby directs that,- 


(a) the competent authority as specified under section 6 of the said Act, to appoint officers to maintain 
the central database for inland vessels in accordance with the provisions of section 22 of the said 
Act and the Inland Vessels (Central Database and Allied Matters) Rules, 2024; and 

(b) such officer or officers, as specified in the said rules, update the central database in accordance 
with the provisions of sub-section (3) of section 4 of the said Act.
