6 . 

Superannuation . -Every person appointed under these rules shall retire from service on the afternoon of the 
last day of the month in which he or she attains the age of sixty years or as prescribed by the Central Government 
from time to time : 


Provided that a person whose date of birth is on the first day of the month shall retire from service in the 
afternoon of the last day of the preceding month on attaining the age of sixty years : 

Provided further that no person shall be granted extension in service beyond the prescribed age of retirement 
on superannuation .
