S.O. 3673(E).— In pursuance of clause () of article 239 of the Constitution, the President hereby 
directs that the Administrator (Lieutenant Governor) of the Union territory of Puducherry shall, subject to 
the control of the President and until further orders, exercise the powers and discharge the functions of the 
State Government under sub-section (4) of section I5 of the Mines and Minerals (Development and 
Regulation) Act, 957 (67 of 957) within the said Union territory.
