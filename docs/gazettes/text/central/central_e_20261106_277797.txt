S.O. 6088(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board ), number, S.O 595 (E) dated the 2lst September, 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part-II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 2lst September, 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) 
and issued under sub-section () of section 20(A) of the Railway Act,l989 (24 of 989), the Central Government 
declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of 
execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mudkhed — Dhone doubling 
project in the District of Kamareddy in the State of Telangana. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “THE TIMES 
OF INDIA, HINDI MILAP and SAKSHI” dated 26 September 2026 under sub section (4) of section 20Aof the 
said Act; 


And whereas, for Gidda & Uppalavai Villages of Kamareddy district, no objections were received and the 
same have been considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed;
