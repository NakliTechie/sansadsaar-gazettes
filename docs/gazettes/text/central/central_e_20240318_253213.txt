आवश्यकताओं को ध्यान में रखते हुए, केंद्र सरकार ने निर्णय लिया है कि दक्षिण पूर्व रेलवे के खड़गपुर और चक्रधरपुर

(हावड़ा से) खड़गपुर-आदित्यपुर खंड में की जाए। 

उपरोक्त परिवर्तन तत्काल प्रभाव से लागू होगा।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th March, 2024 


No. 2024/E&R/1(3)/4.—It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view the 
operational requirements, Central Government has decided to change the jurisdictional boundary between 
Kharagpur and Chakradharpur divisions of South Eastern Railway from existing Km. 233.880 (From 
Howrah) to revised Km. 233.200 (From Howrah) in Kharagpur- Adityapur section . 


The above change will come into force with immediate effect.
