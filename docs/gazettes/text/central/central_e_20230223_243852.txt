फा . सं . 18-29 / 14539 आईएनसी . - समय - समय पर यथासंशोधित भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 
( 1947 का XLVIII ) की धारा 14 के अधीन प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्द्वारा निम्नलिखित 
आदेश जारी करती है : 


जबकि सुब्बासक्ति इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ पैरामेडिकल साइंसेज , सत्यमंगलम , कुलथली , जिला करूर - 639120 
( तमिलनाडु ) एएनएम पाठ्यक्रम संचालित करने के लिए तमिलनाडु राज्य सरकार तथा तमिलनाडु नर्सेज एंड मिडवाइव्स 
काउंसिल द्वारा मान्यता प्राप्त है । 


वहीं , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए सचिव ( स्वास्थ्य एवं 
परिवार कल्याण ) , स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण विभाग , तमिलनाडु सरकार , चौथा तल , फोर्ट सेंट जॉर्ज , सचिवालय , 
चेन्नई – 600009 , तमिलनाडु को संबोधित नोटिस संख्या 18-29 / 14539 - आईएनसी दिनांक 04.05.2022 जारी की गई थी । 
दिनांक 04.05.2022 को जारी नोटिस की एक प्रति सुब्बासक्ति इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ पैरामेडिकल साइंसेज , सत्यमंगलम , 
कुलथली , जिला करूर - 639120 ( तमिलनाडु ) को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी किए गए नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग 
संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


ले . कर्नल ( डॉ . ) सर्वजीत कौर , सचिव 
[ विज्ञापन- III / 4 / असा . / 636 / 2022-23 ] 


New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-29 / 14539 - INC.– In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Subbasakthi Institute of Paramedical Sciences situated at Sathiyamangalam , Kulthali , Distt . 
Karur – 639120 , Tamilnadu is recognized by State Government of Tamilnadu and Tamilnadu Nurses & Midwives 
Council to conduct ANM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-29 / 14539 - INC dated 04/05/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Secretary ( H & FW ) , Department of Health and Family Welfare , 
Government of Tamilnadu , 04th Floor , Fort . St. George , Secretariat , Chennai - 600009 , Tamilnadu was issued . Copy 
of the notice dated 04/05/2022 was also enclosed to Subbasakthi Institute of Paramedical Sciences situated at 
Sathiyamangalam , Kulthali , Distt . Karur – 639120 , Tamilnadu . In the notice issued , the State Government was 
requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Subbasakthi Institute of Paramedical Sciences . Even no action , as required , has been taken by the institution . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
05/07/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 
declares that with effect from 08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Subbasakthi 
Institute of Paramedical Sciences situated at Sathiyamangalam , Kulthali , Distt . Karur - 639120 , Tamilnadu 
shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Tamilnadu subject to the orders of State government of 
Tamilnadu . 


Lt. Col ( Dr ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
