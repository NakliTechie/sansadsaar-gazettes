परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3746 (अ) तारीख 23/08/2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग 11 खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा महाराष्ट्र राज्य के सोलापुर जिले में 
NH-166 के कि.मी. 248.600 से कि.मी. 331.200 (सांगली सोलापूर सेक्क्षन) के निर्माण तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के 
निर्माण (चौड़ा करने/चार लेन का बनाने, आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध 
अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख 23/09/2023 को 
“दैनिक दिव्य मराठी (मराठी)” और "दैनिक इंडियन एक्सप्रेस (इंग्रजी)” दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

रिपोर्ट दे दी है ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th March, 2024 


9.0. 1237(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 3746 (E) dated the 23, August 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-166 New on the stretch of land from Km 248.600 to Km 331.200 (Sangali - Solapur Section) in the 
district of SOLAPUR in the state of MAHARASHTRA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Daily Divya Marathi (Marathi)” 
and “Daily Indian Express (English)” both dated the 23/09/2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
