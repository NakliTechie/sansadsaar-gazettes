National Highways Act,l956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication 
of this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objections to the use of such land for the aforesaid

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the SDO/LAO in the 
State of Maharashtra, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after 
hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either Allow or disallow the objections.
