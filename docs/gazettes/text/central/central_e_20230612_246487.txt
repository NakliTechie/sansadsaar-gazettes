INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/463-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, |947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Nisha Institute of Nursing Science and Research Centre situated at Sonkatchh Jharkheda 
Nursingad Byabera Road NH 2, Shayampur, Distt. Sehore 46666l, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State 
Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM & B.Sc(N) 
Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-8/463-INC dated 22/09//2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24!, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 22/09//2022 was also enclosed to Nisha Institute of Nursing Science and Research Centre situated at 
Sonkatchh Jharkheda Nursingad Byabera Road NH 2, Shayampur, Distt. Sehore 46666l, Madhya Pradesh. In 
the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Nisha Institute of Nursing Science and Research Centre, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Nisha Institute 
of Nursing Science and Research Centre has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
5/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 
declares that with effect from 23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM and B.Sc(N) Nisha 
Institute of Nursing Science and Research Centre situated at Sonkatchh Jharkheda Nursingad Byabera Road 
NH 22, Shayampur, Distt. Sehore 46666, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of 
Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh.
