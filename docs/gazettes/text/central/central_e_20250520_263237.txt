NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20" May, 2025 


S.O. 2239 (E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Commissions of Inquiry 
Act, 952 (60 of 952), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the notification 
of the Government of India in the Ministry of Home Affairs number S.O. 2424 (E), dated, the 4" June, 
2023, namely: - 


In the said notification, for paragraph 3, the following paragraph shall be substituted namely: 


“3. The Commission shall submit its report to the Central Government as soon as possible but not 
later than the 20" November, 2025.”
