Divisional Officer, Ramachandrapuram, namely in writing and shall be set out the grounds there of and the Competent 
Authority & Land Acquisition officer, Revenue Divisional Officer, Ramachandrapuram, shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may. after hearing all such objections and after 
making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition(CALA) thinks necessary, by 
order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub section (2) of section 20-D of the said act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land under this notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested persons at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority (CALA).
