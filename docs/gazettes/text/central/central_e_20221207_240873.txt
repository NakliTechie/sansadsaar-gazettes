MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 18th October , 2022 

S.O. 5703 ( E ) .- In exercise of the power conferred by section 4 ( e ) of the All India Institute of 
Medical Sciences Act , 1956 ( 25 of 1956 ) , as amended by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
( Amendment ) Act , 2012 ( 37 of 2012 ) , the Central Government hereby nominates Sh . Rajib Ranjan , 
Nalanda , Bihar , to be member of Institute Body of All India Institute of Medical Sciences , Patna in place of 
Dr. N.K. Arora , Executive Director , International Clinical Epidemiology Network ( INCLEN ) , and for that 
purpose makes the following amendment in the notification of Government of India , Ministry of Health & 
Family Welfare , F. No. Z - 28016 / 177 / 2019 - PMSSY - IV dated the 18th May , 2020 published in the Gazette 
of India Part II , Section 3 , Sub - Section ( ii ) Vide SO 1824 ( E ) dated 10th June , 2020 : 


In the said notification , for serial number 9 and the entry relating thereto , the following serial 
number and entry should be substituted , namely : 

" 9. Sh . Rajib Ranjan , Nalanda , Bihar " 

The terms of office of the member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the 
All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act , 1956 . 


[ F. No. Z - 28016 / 126 / 2020 - PMSSY - IV ] 
NILAMBUJ SHARAN , Economic Advisor
