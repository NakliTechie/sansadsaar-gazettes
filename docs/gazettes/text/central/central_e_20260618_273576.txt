ORDER 
New Delhi, the 18th June, 2026 


S.O. 3216(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 read with sections 3 and 11 of the Immigration 
and Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order to amend the 
Immigration and Foreigners Order, 2025, namely: — 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) This Order may be called the Immigration and Foreigners (Amendment) 
Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Immigration and Foreigners Order, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said Order), in paragraph 2, 
sub-paragraph (1), after clause (a), the following clause shall be inserted, namely: - 


‘(aa) “Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder” means the Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder as defined in 
clause (ee) of sub-section (1) of section 2 of the Citizenship Act,1955 (57 of 1955)’. 


3, In the said Order, in paragraph 14, in sub-paragraph (2), after the words “after obtaining necessary permit”, the 
words “or without permit, as the case may be,” shall be inserted. 


4. In the said Order, in the Third Schedule, in the Appendix, for the entries under the heading “RAJASTHAN”, the 
following entries shall be substituted, namely: -—
