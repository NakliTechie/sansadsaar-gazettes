3. On 07.08.2021 at 11.32 hrs a " Stop Other Service Message " ( Gas Leakage ) was received about the major LPG 
leakage incident in Kasturba Hospital Compound , Mumbai . Immediately , Fire Brigade team with fire appliances rushed to 
the spot . On arrival Assistant Divisional Fire Officer Sh . K.D. Ghadigaonkar took a round for sizing up the situation of LPG 
leakage and noticed and learnt from Station Officer Sh . V.C. Vishwasrao and team members that a huge amount of LPG was 
severely leaking from the damaged drain valve of L.P. Gas Tank , having capacity of 10 MT , behind the hospital . The fire 
brigade team comprising of Station Officer Sh . V.C. Vishwasrao , D.M. Jadhav , Khopade and their members at the site had 
prepared three hose lines i.e. , two hose lines from the Courtyard hydrants and one small hose line from fire engine in the 
form of spray and pressed into operation of reducing , controlling the leakage of LPG and restricting the LPG vapours - air 
mixture below the explosive range and barring LPG Air mixture within the periphery of LPG Tank by adopting all the 
necessary safety precautionary measures . Assistant Divisional Fire Officer immediately , ordered to prohibit the use of electric 
and electronic items , matchsticks and other flammable items and carried out operation with minimum required fire brigade 
personnel in the vicinity of the site . Assistant Divisional Fire Officer , immediately confirmed from Station Officer and 
doctors regarding the evacuation and shifting of patients from the adjoining hospital building No. 148 to the place of safety . 
Assistant Divisional Fire Officer along with the team executed complete safe evacuation of 500-600 persons , patients , 
hospital staff , including surrounding residents all around the incident site with the help of Police to avoid loss of lives due to 
catastrophic effect of LPG leakage . All the sources of ignition from nearby vicinity were closed with the help of police , and 
the movement of vehicles was stopped with the help of traffic police . Simultaneously quick communication and coordination 
was made with the HPCL technical expert team for urgent aid of Emergency Response Vehicle and empty LPG Tankers for 
the decanting & degassing of remaining 5 MT of LP Gas from the leaking LPG Tank by requesting Disaster and Fire Control 
Room to create green corridor with the help of traffic police for urgent additional aid . Under the guidance of Assistant 
Divisional Fire Officer , the sealing agent from the HAZMAT vehicle was applied to reduce the flow of LPG , but due to 
excessive pressure and huge flow of LPG , it was not possible to seal entire place due to severe frost burn injuries to his team . 
However , by taking additional care and proper use of safety rubber gloves the sealing agent was applied , thereby reducing 
and controlling the flow of LPG leakage . The operation of five hose lines of water spray was continued to reduce intensity of 
flow of LPG leakage and to minimize and restrict the formation of LPG cloud within the limit of explosive range around the 
LPG Tank , thereby keeping it away from any source of ignition . After arriving of mobile tankers at around 1900 hrs , process 
of decanting of gas was completed in the presence of technical expert team of HPCL and empty LPG tank was filled with the 
water as per SOP of HPCL and finally the entire premises was declared safe . Decantation & Degassing of LPG Bullet was

Lol . The compliance report of the Institution was verified by UGC . UGC , vide its letter No.10-3 / 2022 ( CPP - I / DU ) dated 
10.10.2022 , conveyed that the Deemed to be University has fulfilled the conditions of Lol and recommended that the 
Ministry may consider granting permission to the Deemed to be University to start an Off - Campus Centre at Bowrampet 
Village , Medchal Malkajgiri District , Greater Hyderabad from the academic year 2022-2023 . The recommendation of UGC 
was approved by Chairman , UGC . 


9. Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 of the UGC Act , 1956 , the Central Government , 
on the advice of the UGC , do hereby permits Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation , Vijayawada , Andhra Pradesh to 
start an off - campus centre at Bowrampet Village , Medchal Malkajgiri District , Greater Hyderabad from the academic year 
2022-2023 . 


10. The said off - campus shall be reviewed by the Commission after a period of five years from the date of 
Notification by the Government . This permission is subject to the compliance of the UGC ( Institutions Deemed to be 
Universities ) Regulations , 2019 and other relevant Norms / Rules / Regulations / Directions / Notifications issued by the 
Central Government , UGC and other Statutory Councils , from time to time .
