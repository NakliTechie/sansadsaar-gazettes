MINISTRY OF HEAVY INDUSTRIES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th April, 2025 


F. No. 16/1/2017-HE&MT.—Following amendment in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry 
of Heavy Industries, No. 16/1/2017-HE&MT, dated the 25" January 2022 related to Guidelines of the Scheme On 
“Enhancement of Competitiveness in the Indian Capital Goods Sector- Phase-II” is being made namely: 


In the said notification, Para 11.4 may be substituted as: 


"MHI shall grant the “Certificate of Project Completion” to the PIO after satisfactory evaluation of the project 
completion report. The Screening Committee chaired by JS/AS, HE&MT shall act as the final authority for 
sanctioning the “Certificate of Project Completion" after the project completion report is submitted 
independently by the Project Review and Monitoring Committee (PR&MC) and National Research 
Development Corporation (NRDC) for the projects under the Scheme.’ 


2. The notification shall come into effect from the date of its publication in the Gazette.
