MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 14th August, 2023 


G.S.R. 694(E).-- In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Home Affairs, Narcotics Control Bureau (Junior 
Intelligence Officer) Group ‘B’ post Recruitment Rules, 2022, namely:-- 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Home Affairs, Narcotics Control 
Bureau (Junior Intelligence Officer) Group ‘B’ post Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2)They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Ministry of Home Affairs, Narcotics Control Bureau (Junior Intelligence Officer) Group ‘B’ post 
Recruitment Rules 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said Rules),-- 


(i) in the opening paragraph, for the words “Junior Intelligence Officer”, the words “Sub-Inspector’” shall be 
substituted; 


(ii) in rulel, in sub-rule(1), for the brackets and words “(Junior Intelligence Officer)”, the brackets and words 
“(Sub-Inspector)” shall be substituted; 


(iii) in the schedule,-- 


(a) in column(1), for the words “Junior Intelligence Officer’, the words “Sub-Inspector” shall be 
substituted; 


(b) in column(2), for the figures and brackets “195*(2022)”, the figures and brackets “295*(2023)” shall 
be substituted.

PRASHANT S. LOKHANDE, Jt. Secy.
