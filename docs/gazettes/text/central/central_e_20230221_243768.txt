प्रबंधन आयोग अधिनियम , 2021 ( 2021 का 29 ) की धारा 25 की उपधारा ( 2 ) के खंड ( क ) द्वारा प्रदत्त 
शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , निम्नलिखित नियम बनाती है , अर्थात् :

( 2 ) ये राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तारीख से प्रवृत्त होंगे । ।

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 21st February , 2023 

G.S.R. 116 ( E ) . – In exercise of the powers conferred by clause ( a ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 25 of the 
Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital Region and Adjoining Areas Act , 2021 ( 29 of 2021 ) , 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules , namely : 


1. Short title and commencement.- ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Commission for Air Quality Management in 
National Capital Region and Adjoining Areas ( Other Associated Members of the Commission ) Rules , 2023 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. Other Associated Member of Commission . 

The Commission constituted under section 3 of the Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital 
Region and Adjoining Areas Act , 2021 ( 29 of 2021 ) , may co - opt a representative of the following Ministries not 
below the rank of Joint Secretary to the Government of India only on a need basis as other Associate Members for the 
purpose of clause ( h ) of sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3 of the said Act , namely : 

( a ) a representative of the Ministry of Heavy Industries ; 

( b ) a representative of the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy ; 

( c ) a representative of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare ; 

( d ) a representative of the Department of Revenue , Ministry of Finance ; 

( e ) a representative of the Department of Economic Affairs , Ministry of Finance ; 

( f ) a representative of the Ministry of Earth Sciences ; 

( g ) a representative of the Ministry of Science and Technology ; 

( h ) a representative of the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying , Ministry of Fisheries , Animal 
Husbandry and Dairying ; and 

( i ) a representative of the Ministry of Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises .
