MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, 29 November, 2024 


S.O. 5157(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Rail Over Rail Double Line Flyover at Gooty (12 Km)" as a Special Railway 
Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Ananthapuramu for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
