New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


S.O. 2548(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways Act, 
956(48 of 956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of the Schedule 
annexed here to as the competent authorities to perform the functions of such authorities under the said Act with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land from Km. 0 to 
Km. 69.608 of the NH28A ( Piprakothi-Motihari-Raxaul upto New ICP) for building (widening / four-laning, etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation in the State of BIHAR.
