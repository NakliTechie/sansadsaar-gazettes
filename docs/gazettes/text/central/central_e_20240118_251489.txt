New Delhi, the 18th January, 2024 


S.O. 278(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, $.0.2918 (E) Dated 04 July 2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/2Lanning with Paved shoulder etc) Maintenance, 
management and operation of (Chanderi-Pichhore-Dinara Road) NH-346 in the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 
96+877 Km. in the district of Shivpuri in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. Hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” Dated the 26 
July 2023, Wednesday and “Free Press” Dated 26 July 2023, Wednesday under sub-section (3) of section 3A of 
the said Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
