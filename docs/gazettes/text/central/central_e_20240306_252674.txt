MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th March, 2024 


S.O. 076(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (2! of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer resources 
relating to the Central Know Your Client Records Registry Application and Repository, and Security Interest Records 
Registry Application and Repository, being Critical Information Infrastructure of the Central Registry of 
Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India, and the computer resources of their associated 
dependencies, to be protected systems for the purposes of the said Act and authorises the following persons to access 
the protected systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security 
Interest of India authorised in writing by the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and 
Security Interest of India to access the protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have been 
authorised in writing by the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security 
Interest of India for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing by the 
Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India on case to case 
basis. 


2: This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
