और जबकि, वैश्विक स्तर पर व्याप्त संकट को देखते हुए, केंद्र सरकार ने ब्यूरो के परामर्श से आपूर्ति श्रृंखला में 
व्यवधान का संज्ञान लिया है और आश्वस्त है कि मोर्फोलीन की उपलब्धता सुनिश्चित करने के लिए, उक्त आदेश के संचालन 
को अस्थायी रूप से निलंबित करना आवश्यक और समीचीन है; 


अब, इसलिए भारतीय मानक ब्यूरो अधिनियम 2016 (2016 का 11) की धारा 25 की उपधारा (3) के साथ

MINISTRY OF CHEMICALS AND FERTILIZERS 
(Department of Chemicals And Petrochemicals) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 2nd April, 2026 


S.O. 1722(E).— Whereas, the Central Government has notified the Morpholine (Quality Control) Order, 
2020 vide S.O. 1893 (E), dated the 16™ June, 2020 (hereinafter to referred as the said Order) in the public 
interest under the powers conferred by section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016); 


And whereas, in view of the exigencies prevailing globally, the Central Government, in consultation with the 
Bureau, has taken cognizance of the supply chain disruption and is satisfied that to ensure availability of the 
Morpholine, it is necessary and expedient to suspend the operation of the said order temporarily; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 read with sub-section (3) of the section 25 
of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), and being of the opinion that it is necessary so to do in the 
public interest, the Central Government, after consultation with the Bureau of Indian Standards, hereby makes the 
following amendment to the said Order, namely:- 


In the Morpholine (Quality Control) Order, 2020, in paragraph 2, the following proviso shall be 
inserted, namely:- 


“Provided that to prevent any disruption of supply chain of the said goods or article, the conformation 
to such Indian Standard shall not be applicable for the period from the 1‘ April, 2026 to 1" July, 2026”.
