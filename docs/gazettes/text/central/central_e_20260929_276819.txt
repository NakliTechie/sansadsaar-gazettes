S.O. 535(E).— In exercise of the power conferred by sub-section(|) of section 20A of the Railways Act,

Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under

Eluru District, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority Revenue Divisional Officer, 
Eluru, Eluru District shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and 
may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections.
