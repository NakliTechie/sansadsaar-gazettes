NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 22nd September, 2025 


S.O. 4336(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 3438 (E) dated the 30th July, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub section(ii), dated the 30th July, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section(1) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said act), the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely "Proposed siding connectivity to Kodla Cement Plant of M/s. Shree Cement Ltd, with extension from dead 
end of their existing Gati Shakti Multi-Modal Cargo Terminal (GCT) served by Sulehalli station on SC-Wadi section" 
in the State of Karnataka. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely "Prajavani" 
dated the 13th August 2025 and "Deccan Herald" dated 13th August 2025 under sub section(4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 


And No objections received from Any land Owners; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority haa submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20L of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances:
