का.नि.आ. 18 ( अ ) . – छावनी भूमि प्रशासन नियम , 2021 में संशोधन करने के लिए कतिपय नियमों के साथ 
प्रारुप भारत के राजपत्र , असाधारण , भाग II , खंड -4 में का.नि.आ. 10 ( अ ) तारीख 24 जून , 2022 द्वारा प्रकाशित किया 
गया था जो छावनी अधिनियम , 2006 ( वर्ष 2006 का 41 ) की धारा 346 द्वारा यथाअपेक्षित है जिसमें उन सभी व्यक्तियों 
जिनको इससे प्रभावित होने की संभावना थी , उनको उस तारीख से जब उक्त अधिसूचना सहित शासकीय राजपत्र की 
प्रतियां प्रकाशित की गई थी को सार्वजनिक रूप में उपलब्ध कराया गया था जिसमें तीस दिवसों की अवधि समाप्त होने से 
पहले आपत्तियां और सुझाव मांगे गए थे । 

और जबकि , उक्त राजपत्र की प्रतियां तारीख 18 जुलाई , 2022 को सार्वजनिक रूप में उपलब्ध कराई गई थीं ।

MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 1st November , 2022 

S.R.O. 18 ( E ) . – Whereas the draft containing certain rules to amend the Cantonment Land 
Administration Rules , 2021 were published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 4 , vide 
S.R.O. 10 ( E ) , dated the 24th June , 2022 , as required by section 346 of the Cantonments Act , 2006 ( 41 of 
2006 ) inviting objections and suggestions from all the persons likely to be affected thereby , before the 
expiry of period thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Official Gazette containing the said 
notification was published were made available to the public ; 


18th 

And whereas , the copies of the said Official Gazette were made available to the public on dated the 
July , 2022 ; 


1 . 


And whereas , the objections and suggestions received from the public in respect of said draft rules 
have been duly considered by the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by section 346 of the Cantonments Act , 2006 
( 41 of 2006 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the Cantonment Land 
Administration Rules , 2021 , namely : 

( 1 ) These rules may be called the Cantonment Land Administration ( Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2 . 

In the Cantonment Land Administration Rules , 2021 , after rule 39 , the following rule shall be 
inserted , namely : 


" 39A . Conversion of rights on class B - 3 land into freehold.- The Central Government may any time 
formulate or amend a policy to convert existing rights on class B - 3 land into freehold . " . 


Note : The Cantonment Land Administration Rules , 2021 were published in the Gazette of India , 
Extraordinary , Part II , Section 4 , vide notification number S.R.O. 24 ( E ) , dated the 
1st December 2021 .
