and consequently ordered for removal of name of CA. Thiruveedhi Parthasaradhi (Membership No. 03477) from

time his name be removed for a period of additional l(one) month. Since the Respondent has already paid the imposed 
fine within stipulated time, in pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, read with Regulation 8 of the Chartered 
Accountants Regulations 988, it is hereby notified that the name of CA. Thiruveedhi Parthasaradhi (Membership 
No. 03477) shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 03(Three) months with effect
