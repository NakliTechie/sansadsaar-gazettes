S.O. 2500(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Proposed Road Over Bridge in lieu of existing Level 
Crossing Gate No.369 at KM.529/36 —530/02 at BADAMPUDI Station Yard" as a Special Railway Project 
for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Eluru for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
