Scheme for Rebate of State and Central Taxes and Levies on Export of Apparel/Garments and Made-ups 
(RoSCTL) 

F. No. 204/0/2023-TTP.—In pursuance of the decision of the Government of India to rebate all embedded 
State and Central Taxes and Levies on export of garments and made-ups to enhance competitiveness of these sectors, 


the Ministry of Textiles has notified continuation of Scheme for Rebate of State and Central Taxes and Levies 
(RoSCTL) upto 3l* March 2026 vide notification No. 205/l/2020-TTP dated 08.02.2024. 


2. The rates of RoSCTL Scheme were notified vide notification no. 4/26/206-IT (Vol.ID dated 8.3.209 and the 
rate of Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers (FIBC) as per notification dated 08.03.209 was ‘NIL’.
