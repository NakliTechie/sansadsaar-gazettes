F. No. 370142/24/2020-TPL number S.O. 2227(E), dated the 6" July, 2020, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, sub-section (ii), namely:-

dated 13" August 2018”, the letters, figures, and words “F. No. 13/1/2017-INF dated 11" October, 2022” 

shall be substituted.
