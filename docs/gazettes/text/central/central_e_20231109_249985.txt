MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2023 


S.O. 4869(E).—Whereas the Central Government is satisfied that the public interest so requires 
that the services of the industry engaged in Food stuffs, which is covered under item 6 of the First Schedule 


to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947), to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said 
Act; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (vi) of clause (n) of section 2 of 
the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947), the Central Government hereby declares the services 
engaged in the ‘Food Stuffs’ to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act, for a period of 
six months with effect from the date of publication of this notification.
