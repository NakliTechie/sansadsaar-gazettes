MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th November, 2023 


G.S.R. 826(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6 and 25 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 
1986 (29 of 1986), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Environment 
(Protection) Rules, 1986, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Environment (Protection) Fifth Amendment Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2: In the Environment (Protection) Rules, 1986, in Schedule-I, serial number 115 and the entries relating 


thereto, shall be omitted. 
[F. No. Q-17012/1/2019-CPW | 
NARESH PAL GANGWAR, Addl. Secy. 


Note.—The principle rules were published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide

G.S.R. 436(E), dated 14" June, 2023.
