NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the | [0 December, 2025 


S.O. 5747(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (Railway Board), Number S.O. 5555(E), dated 24th December, 2024 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under sub- section (I) of Section 20A of 
the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred in as the said act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to he said notification for the 
Purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, 
Adraj Moti-Vijapur Gauge Conversion Project (Western Railway) in the District of Mahesana in the 
State of Gujarat; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely 
the The Times of India” (English), “Divya Bhaskar” & ), Young Leader ( hindi) dated 6th January,2025 
under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been passed; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the lands specified in the Schedule Annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid 
purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government. Hereby declared that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all 
encumbrances.
