S.O. 2732(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 204 of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the

notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose

practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority for Land Acquisition (CALA) thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections;

final;
