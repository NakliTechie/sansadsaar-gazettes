1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in notification of the government 
of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways , S.O. 4086 ( E ) dated 31.08.2022 published in the Gazette of 
India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( II ) , 31.08.2022 namely : 


In the schedule to the said notification , against serial no . 1 in column 2 for the entry Special District Revenue 
Officer ( LA ) , NHAI , Vellore the entry Special District Revenue Officer ( LA ) , NHAI , Krishnagiri shall be substituted . 


As per State Government order no.5680 / HV1 / 2022-7 , dated 30.12.2022 Special District Revenue Officer ( LA ) , 
NHAI , Krishnagiri is the proposed new CALA for the work of Widening of existing 2 lane to 4 lane with paved 
shoulder including strengthening of carriageway from Km 80 / 0 - Km 125/0 of Salem Tirupathur - Vaniyambadi Road 
of NH - 179A in the district of KRISHNAGIRI in the state of Tamil Nadu .
