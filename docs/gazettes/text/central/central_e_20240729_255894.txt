SUPREME COURT OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th July, 2024 


G.S.R. 457(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Article 145 of the Constitution, and all other 
enabling provisions in this behalf, the Supreme Court with the approval of the President, hereby makes the following 
rules further to amend the Supreme Court Rules, 2013 namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Supreme Court (Amendment) 
Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the 1“ August, 2024; 


2. In the Supreme Court Rules, 2013 in order I, for rules 1, 2 and 3 the following rules shall be substituted 
namely:- 


1. “Subject to any order by the Chief Justice, the working hours of the offices of the Court shall be from 
10:00 A.M. to 05:00 P.M. 


2. The offices of the Court shall remain closed on Gazetted holidays and on the second and fourth 
Saturdays of each month.

3. During vacations and holidays, the offices of the Court shall remain open for such times as the Chief 
Justice may direct”.
