S.O. 2768(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section (5) read with Note 5(d)(ii) of Schedule V 
[Table: Sl. No. 7] of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025), the Central Government hereby specifies the business 
(other than the business specified in Note 5(d)(i) of the said Schedule), which is engaged in the infrastructure sub-sectors 
mentioned in the Updated Harmonised Master List of Infrastructure sub-sectors, in the notification of the Government 
of India in the Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs number फ्र०.।३/॥/2025-00, dated the 
9 September, 2025, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section l, as a business for the purposes of 
Schedule V [Table: Sl. No. 7] of the said Act. 


2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
