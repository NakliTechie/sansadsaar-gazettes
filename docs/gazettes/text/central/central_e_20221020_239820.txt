S.O.4949 ( E ) .– Whereas , by the notification of the Government of India , in the Ministry of 
Railways ( Railway Board ) , number , S.O 2506 ( E ) dated the 9th May , 2022 published in Gazette of India , 
Extraordinary , Part II , section 3 Sub - section ( ii ) , dated the 1st June , 2022 ( hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification ) and issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 20A of the Railways Act , 1989 ( 24 of 1989 ) and 
Railways ( Amendment ) Act 2008 ( 11 of 2008 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution , maintenance , management and operation of Special Railway 
Project , namely Nagbhir Nagpur Gauge Conversion Project ( 106 Km ) ( including proposed rail 
connectivity from Umrer BG Yard to existing WCL Yard at Umrer ) with electrification in the District of 
Nagpur , Bhandara , Chandrapur in the State of Maharashtra . 


And whereas , the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“ Dainik Jeevan Chakra ” Dated 17th June , 2022 and “ Dainik Mahasagar ” Dated 17th June , 2022 under sub 
section ( 4 ) of section 20A of the said Act ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section 20 E of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now therefore , upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 20 E of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 20 E of the said Act , the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette ; the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all 
encumbrances .
