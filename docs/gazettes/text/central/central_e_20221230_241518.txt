G.S.R. 913 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Indian Telegraph Act , 
1885 ( 13 of 1885 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian 
Telegraph Rules , 1951 , namely : - 

( 1 ) These rules may be called the Indian Telegraph ( Fifth Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2 . 

In the Indian Telegraph Rules , 1951 ( hereafter referred to as the said rules ) , in rule 525 , in sub - rule 
( 2 ) , in clause ( iii ) , after item ( e ) , the following item shall be inserted , namely : - 


3 


“ ( f ) ( i ) Capital Expenses for Upgrading existing 2,343 Left Wing Extremism Phase - I sites from 2G 
to 4G mobile services and Operating Expenses for a period of five years from the date of 
commissioning of upgradation of the said sites to 4G mobile services shall be funded by the Fund 
on actual cost , discovered by the Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited through its tender process . 


( ii ) . Operating Expenses [ including satellite bandwidth and carriage charges for Very Small 
Aperture Terminal ] for Left Wing Extremism Phase - I 2G sites during the extended period beyond 
the contractual period of five years incurred by the Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited shall be funded 
by the Fund , as per cost discovered by the Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited through tender or 
272,400 per month per site , whichever is lower and the extension shall be up to a period of twelve 
months from the date of approval of the Central Government or commissioning of 4G sites as 
referred to in sub - item ( f ) ( i ) , whichever is earlier . ” .
