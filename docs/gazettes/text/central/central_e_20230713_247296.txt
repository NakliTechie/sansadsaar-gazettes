MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 13th July, 2023 
S.R.O. 20(E).—In pursuance of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Aadhaar 
(Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 (“Act’’) read with rule 5 of the 
Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020, the Department 
of Defence, Ministry of Defence, has been authorised by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology vide 
their letter no eF. No. 13(2)/2020-EG-II(Vol-I), dated 13/06/2023, for performance of Aadhaar authentication on a 
voluntary basis, for verifying the identity of the ticket buyer on the online Aamantran portal, using Yes/No or/and e- 
KYC authentication facility, for issuance of e-tickets for national events like Republic Day Parade and Independence 

Day Celebrations. 


(i) The Aamantran Portal has been launched to facilitate e-invites to the dignitaries/ guests and e-tickets for 
the residents for National events. This digital initiative will ensure proper validation of identity through 
automated system and will provide an additional layer of security. 


(ii) Department of Defence will adhere to the guidelines laid down by the Government with respect to use of 
Aadhaar authentication. 


(iii) This notification shall come into effect from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


[F. No. D(inv)/2023/RDC/IMS/Tickets)]
