NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th May, 2026 


S.O. 2534(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 87 of the Occupational 
Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (37 of 2020), the Central Government hereby notifies for general 
information that the Directorate General of Factory Advice Service and Labour Institutes shall be known as the 
Directorate General Occupational Safety and Health with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
