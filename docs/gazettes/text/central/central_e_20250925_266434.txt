MINISTRY OF MINES 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 25th September, 2025 


5.0. 4368(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 16 read with 
section 17 and sub-section (3) of section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central 
Government, after consulting the Bureau of Indian Standards, is of the opinion that it is necessary so to do in the 
public interest and hereby makes the following Order to amend the Refined Zinc (Quality Control) Order, 2025, 


namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement - (1) This Order may be called the Refined Zinc (Quality Control) 
Amendment Order, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Refined Zinc (Quality Control) Order, 2025, in paragraph 1, for sub-paragraph (2), the following sub- 
paragraph shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(2) This Order shall come into force with effect from 17.04.2026.” 
[F. No. 02/7/2025-ES] 
SHAKIL ALAM, Economic Advisor
