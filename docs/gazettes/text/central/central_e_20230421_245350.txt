NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai , the 21st April , 2023 

S.O. 1834 ( E ) . – Whereas , by the notification of the Government of India , in the Ministry of Railways 
( Railway Board ) , number , S.O5652 ( E ) dated the 5th December 2022 published in Gazette of India , Extraordinary , 
Part II , section 3 Sub - section ( ii ) , dated the 30th November 2022 ( hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and 
issued under sun - section ( 1 ) of section 20 ( A ) of the Railway Act , 1989 ( 24 of 1989 ) and Railway ( Amendment ) Act , 
2008 ( 11 of 2008 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution , maintenance , management 
and operation of Special Railway Project , namely Baramati – Lonand Via Phaltan New Broad - Gauge Line Project 
in the district of Pune in the State of Maharashtra . 


And whereas , the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers " Dainik Samachar " 
( Marathi ) , " Jan - Pravas ( Marathi ) ” , and “ Hindustan Times ( English ) " on 14th December 2022 and in the daily 
News Paper “ The Economic Times ( English ) ” on 15th December 2022 by SDO Baramati under sub section ( 4 ) of 

section 20A of the said Act ; 


And whereas , in 12 villages of Baramati Taluka No objection were received ; 

And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section 20E of the said Act , the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government ; 


Now therefore , upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 20 E of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 20 E of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette , the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances .
