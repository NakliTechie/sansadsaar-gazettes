NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th August, 2024 


S.O. 3703(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways, number S.O. 3904(E), dated the 04th September 2023 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-—section (ii), dated 04th September 2023 and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, Haryana Orbital Rail 
Corridor Project in the district Nuh, in the State of Haryana; 

And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, “Hindustan Times” dated the 2th September 2023, 
and “Dainik Jagran” dated the 2th September 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, 06 objections have been received and the same was considered and resolved as per rule by the Competent Authority; 
And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 
Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in the exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the said 
Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 
And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the 
official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
