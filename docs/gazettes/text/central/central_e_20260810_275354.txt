F. No M-12043(11)/56/2025-OMC-PNG.—The Government of India had notified the Resolution

2. Whereas, it appeared to the Central Government that, it is necessary to review the existing framework for grant of

security and market efficiency, and to take appropriate measures for aligning the framework with emerging priorities

issues related to implementation of the provisions of the said Resolution dated 08.11.2019

06.08.2025 issued by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas and keeping in view, the public interest, ease of 
doing business, bringing in clarity wherever required, simplifying compliance and monitoring burdens, opening more 
retail outlets in remote areas to serve underserved consumers, facilitating ecosystem conducive for marketing of 
transportation fuel by authorised entities etc.
