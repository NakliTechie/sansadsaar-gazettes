MINISTRY OF HEAVY INDUSTRIES 

ORDER 

New Delhi , the 28th October , 2022 

Subject : Extension in timelines for the implementation of the Electrical Equipment ( Quality Control ) 
Order , 2020 - reg . 


S.O. 5083 ( E ) .— In pursuance to the gazette notification of the Electrical Equipment ( Quality 
Control ) Order , 2020 vide S.O. No. 4044 ( E ) dated 11th November , 2020 and subsequent Order dated 18th 
October , 2021 , the date of enforcement of the Order is hereby extended for six months i.e. from 11th 
November 2022 to 10th May 2023 .
