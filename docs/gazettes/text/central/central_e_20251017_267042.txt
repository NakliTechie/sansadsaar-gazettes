New Delhi, the 17th October, 2025 


S.O. 4741(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S8.0.5585(E) dated 27/12/2024, 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building 
(widening /Two lane with paved shoulder/Four laning etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. NH 753 in the stretch of land from Mansar to Salaikhurd Km 0/00 to km 44/00 in the 
state of Bhandara in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 5585(E) dated 27/12/2024 has been published in 
“Bhandara Patrika” and “Gramin Samachar” Both Dated 22/01/2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 19 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
