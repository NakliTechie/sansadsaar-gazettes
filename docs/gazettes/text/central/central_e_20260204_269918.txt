उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उप-धारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह समाधान हो जाने 
के पश्चात्‌ कि उड़ीसा राज्य के केन्द्रपाडा जिले में NH53 के कि.मी. 43.45 से कि.मी. 54.2 (मार्शाघई, केंद्रपाड़ा) तक के भू-

प्रयोजन के लिए वह भूमि अपेक्षित है जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd February, 2026 


S.O. 537(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NHS53 in the 


[भाग U—ate 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


stretch of land from Km. 43.5 to Km. 54.2 (Marsaghai, Kendrapara) in the district of KENDRAPARA in the state of 
ODISHA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Tahasildar, Marsaghai in writing and 
shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, 
either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further 
enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
