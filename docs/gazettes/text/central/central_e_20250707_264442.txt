section 2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 947 (4 ofl947), the Central Government, being of the opinion that in the 
public interest requires extension hereby extends the period specified in the notification number S.O. 58 (E), dated 
the 297 January, 2025 for a further period of six months from the 30" July, 2025 during which the services engaged in
