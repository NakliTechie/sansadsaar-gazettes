MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 24th August, 2026 


S.O. 4922(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), vide number S.0.3045(E), dated the 27 July, 2025, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 09" July, 2025, (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of special railway project, namely, “Western 
Dedicated Freight Corridor” in the district of Palghar in the State of Maharashtra; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification was published in dailies, namely, “The Times of India” 
and “Danik Pudhari”, dated the 0570 September, 2025, under sub-section (4) of section 204 of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within the 
specified period; 


[भाग ए---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government, hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2) of section 20(E) of the said Act the Central Government further declares that on publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central 
Government free from all encumbrances.
