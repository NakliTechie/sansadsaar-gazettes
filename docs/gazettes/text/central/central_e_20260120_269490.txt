No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/296.—In exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section () of section 
30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 992 (5 of 992), the Board hereby makes the following 
regulations to further amend the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible 
Securities) Regulations, 202, namely: - 


l. These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and Listing of Non- 
Convertible Securities) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026. 


2. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


3. Inthe Securities and Exchange Board of (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities) Regulations, 202], 


({) in regulation 2, sub-regulation (), after the clause (gg) and before the clause (hh), the following clause 
shall be inserted, namely, - 


“(gga) “retail individual investor” means an individual investor who applies or bids for debt securities for 
a value of not more than two lakhs rupees;” 


(2) in regulation 3, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely, - 


“Provided that nothing contained in this regulation shall preclude the issuer from offering an incentive in 
the form of additional interest or a discount to the issue price to senior citizens, women, serving and 
retired defence personnel, widows and widowers of defence personnel, retail individual investors or any 
other category of investors as may be specified by the Board from time to time:
