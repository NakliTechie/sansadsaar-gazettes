MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 2lst February, 2024 


No. 2022/E&R/(3)/5.—It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view the 
operational requirements, Central Government has decided to revise the jurisdiction of Pune division and 
Solapur division of Central Railway. 


The jurisdiction of Manmad Jn. - Daund Jn. Section from KM 490.20 to KM 270.00 is transferred 
from Solapur Division to Pune Division of Central Railway. The new divisional boundary between Solapur 
and Pune division will be at KM 270.00 on Pune-Daund-Solapur section. 


The changes in the above jurisdiction will come into effect from I“ April’2024.
