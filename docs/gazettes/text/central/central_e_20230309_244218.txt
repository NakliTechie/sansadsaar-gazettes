परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.1480 (आ). तारीख 30.03.2022, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग ॥, खण्ड 3, उपखंड () में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा पंजाब राज्य के कप्रथला जिले में रिंग रोड 
(जालंधर बाईपास) के कि.मी. 23.5 से कि.मी.25.5 ( डी सी कार्यालय कप्रथला), रिंग रोड (जालंधर बाईपास) के 
कि.मी. 27 से कि.मी.29.5 ( जिला राजस्व अधिकारी कपूरथला) तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के निर्माण (चौड़ा करने / चार 
लेन का बनाने, आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का 
अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जन किया जाना चाहिए ;

इस अधिसूचना के राजपत्र में प्रकाशन पर, उक्त अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विल्लंगमों से मुक्त होकर आत्यन्तिक रूप 
से केन्द्रीय सरकार में निहित हो जाएगी ।

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th March, 2023 


S.O. 1086(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways S.O. No. 1480(E) dated 30.03.2022 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of Ring Road on the stretch of land from Km 23.5 to Km 25.5 (District Revenue Officer Kapurthala), Ring 
Road on the stretch of land from Km 27 to Km 29.5 (District Revenue Officer Kapurthala) in the district of 
KAPURTHALA in the state of PUNJAB; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Times of India” and “ Ajit” both 
dated 17.04.2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
