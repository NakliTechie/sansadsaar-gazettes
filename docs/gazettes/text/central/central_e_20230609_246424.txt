S.O. 2546(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for Construction 
of National Highway No. l20 (Two Lane with Paved shoulder, etc), maintenance, management and operation of NH 
no.- !20 (Bikramganj-Dumraon section) in the stretch of land from Km. 20.665 to km 29.080 in the District of 
Rohtas in the state of Bihar, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, District Land Acquisition Officer, 
Rohtas in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an
