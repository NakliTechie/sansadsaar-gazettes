No. S-604/05/2023-LW(B).—In exercise of Power Conferred by Section- 5 of Contract Labour (R&A) 
Act, 970, the Central Advisory Contract Labour Board hereby Constitutes a Committee to go into the question of 
abolition of Contract Labour in the Trolley Retrievers working at Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International and

The term of reference of Proposed Committee would be as follows. 


(.) To study the working of Contract Labour system in the Job/Works of TROLLEY RETRIEVERS at Netaji Subhas 
Chandra Bose International and Domestic Airport of which abolition of Contract Labour system has been sought in 
Writ Petition No. !0007 (७) of 203 in the Hon’ble High Court of Calcutta (Kolkata). 


(2) The Committee has to make suitable recommendation whether or not the employment of Contract Labour in 
TROLLEY RETRIEVERS Job/Works in Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International and Domestic Airport Kolkata as 
mentioned above be prohibited keeping in view the provision of Section (0) of Contract Labour (Regulation and 
Abolition) Act, 970. 


(3) The report of Committee must be Submitted within 0l month positively as the case is related to contempt and 
board is duly bound to decide it within 06 weeks time, as directed by the Hon’ble High Court Kolkata in W.P. No. 
0007 of 203. 


(4) The Head Quarter of Committee will be at Kolkata.
