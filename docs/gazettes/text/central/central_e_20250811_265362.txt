MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |lth August, 2025 

S.O. 3655(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3] of the Contract Labour (Regulation and 
Abolition) Act, 970 (37 of 970), the Central Government, after consultation with the Central Advisory Contract 
Labour Board, hereby exempts the Coal Block allotted to Andhra Pradesh Mineral Development Corporation Limited 
namely Suliyari Coal Block of 5 MTPA (Opencast) being developed in District Singrauli, Madhya Pradesh from the 
applicability of notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour number S.0.2063(E), dated the 2“ 
June, 988, published in the Gazette of India, Part I], Section 3, Sub-section (ii), in respect of employment of contract 
labour in the works specified in the Schedule to the said notification against serial numbers | to 3, for a period of three 
years with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, subject to the following 
conditions, namely: - 


(i) The interest of the workmen engaged in the above mentioned works shall be protected; 


(ii) Such workmen shall be paid wages and other benefits as per the recommendations of the High-Powered 
Committee appointed by the Coal India Limited, Ministry of Coal, Government of India and at the rates 
notified by the Coal India Limited from time to time; 


(iii) The Central Advisory Contract Labour Board shall have the right to inspect the establishment to ensure 
and check whether such wages and benefits are given to such workers in Andhra Pradesh Mineral 
Development Corporation Limited namely the Suliyari Coal Block of 5 MTPA (Opencast) being 
developed in District Singrauli, Madhya Pradesh; 


(iv) The management of Andhra Pradesh Mineral Development Corporation Limited shall ensure that 
whenever there is a change of contract, existing contract workmen who are working may be given 
preference in employment by the incoming contractor, subject to the satisfactory performance of duties; 


(v) The workmen working in jobs ancillary or incidental to coal mining works specified against serial 
numbers | to 3 of the Schedule to the said notification number 5.0. 2063(E) dated the 2]st June, 988 
shall get wages as determined by the High-Powered Committee appointed by the Coal India Limited, 
Ministry of Coal, Government of India and at the rates notified by the Coal India Limited from time to 
time;
