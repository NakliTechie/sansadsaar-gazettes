NOTIFICATION 


8.0. 252(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), vide number S.O. 9(E), dated the 07 March, 2025, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated l] March, 2025, (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, 
“Western Dedicated Freight Corridor” in the district of Thane in the State of Maharashtra; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers, namely, “The 
Times of India” and “Punya Nagari’”, dated the 02™ April,2025, under sub-section (4) of section 204 of the said 


Act; 


And whereas no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within the 


specified period; 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government.

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government, hereby declares that the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose, and in pursuance of 
sub-section (2) of section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government further declares that on publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central 
Government free from all encumbrances.
