का.आ. 5997 ( अ ) .– केन्द्रीय सरकार ने पेट्रोलियम और खनिज पाइपलाइंस ( भूमि मे उपयोग के अधिकार का 
अर्जन ) अधिनियम , 1962 ( 1962 का 50 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 की 
उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के पेट्रोलियम और प्रकृतिक गैस मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या 
का.आ. 1056 ( अ ) . तारीख 09 मार्च , 2022 , भारत के राजपत्र सं ० 1024 , तारीख 10 , मार्च , 2022 एवं 
का . आ . 3914 ( अ ) . तारीख 20 सितम्बर , 2021 , भारत के राजपत्र सं ० 3590 , तारीख 22 सितम्बर , 2021 में प्रकाशित 
की गई थी । इस अधिसूचना से संलग्न अनुसूची में विनीर्दिष्ट भूमि में बिहार राज्य में “ हल्दीया - बरौनी पाइपलाइन सिस्टम्स 
परियोजना " हेतु जिला पटना में कच्चा तेल के परिवहन हेतु इंडियन ऑयल कॉर्पोरेशन लिमिटेड द्वारा पाइपलाइन बिछाने के 
प्रयोजन के लिए उपयोग के अधिकार के अर्जन के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ।

पाइपलाइन बिछाने के लिए अपेक्षित है , उसमें उपयोग के अधिकार के अर्जन करने का विनिश्चय किया है ;

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 13th December , 2022 

S.O. 5997 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of the India in the Ministry of Petroleum 
and Natural Gas S. O. No. 1056 ( E ) , Dated 09 March , 2022 , published in the Gazette of the India No. 1024 
Dated 10 March , 2022 , and S. O. No. 3914 ( E ) , Dated 20 September , 2021 , published in the Gazette of the India No. 
3590 Dated 22 September , 2021 , issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
( Acquisition of Right of User in Land ) Act , 1962 ( 50 of 1962 ) , ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that 


[ भाग II— खण्ड 3 ( ii ) ] 

3 

notification for the purpose of laying " Haldia - Barauni Pipeline Systems Project " for the transportation of Crude Oil in 
Patna District in the state of Bihar by Indian Oil Corporation Limited . 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public up to 25 March 2022 . 
And whereas the Competent Authority has under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 6 of the said Act submitted report 
to the Central Government ; 


And whereas the central Government , after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline , has decided to acquire right of user therein ; 


Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 6 of the said Act , the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline ; 


And further , in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 4 ) of section 6 of the said Act , the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall , instead of vesting in the 
Central Government , vest on the date publication of the declaration , in Indian Oil Corporation Limited , free from all 
encumbrances . 


भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 


Indian Oil Corporation Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section 10 of the 
P & MP Act , 1962 and no suit , claim or legal proceeding would lie against the Central Government on any matter 
relating to the pipeline .
