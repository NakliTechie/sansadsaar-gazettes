THE LAKSHADWEEP TOURISM TRADE (REGISTRATION, SAFETY 
AND FACILITATION) REGULATION, 2026 
No. 17 oF 2026 
Promulgated by the President in the Seventy-seventh Year of the Republic 
of India. 


A Regulation to provide for the registration, facilitation and regulation of persons 
engaged in tourism trade in the Union territory of Lakshadweep; to ensure safety 
of tourists, quality of tourism services, transparency in charges, responsible 
and sustainable use of fragile island and marine ecosystems and for matters 
connected therewith or incidental thereto. 


In exercise of the powers conferred by article 240 of the Constitution, the 
President is pleased to promulgate the following Regulation made by her:— 


CHAPTER I 
PRELIMINARY 


1. (4) This Regulation may be called the Lakshadweep Tourism Trade 
(Registration, Safety and Facilitation) Regulation, 2026. 


(2) It extends to the whole of the Union territory of Lakshadweep.

2. (1) This Regulation shall apply to tourism trade carried on for monetary 
consideration or in relation to travel, stay, recreation or tourism activities in the

for the time being in force.

all or any provisions of this Regulation, including—

facilities not operated for commercial tourism;

(c) occasional community events, festivals, fairs or temporary 
non-commercial services; and

necessary.

obligations relating to tourist safety, cleanliness, environmental protection, 
emergency reporting, consumer transparency or compliance with any other law.

CHAPTER II 
AUTHORITIES, REGISTER AND FACILITATION MECHANISM 


4. (1) The Administrator may notify one or more designated authorities for 
registration, inspection, adjudication, appeal, revision, safety audit, standards or any 
other function under this Regulation. 


(2) The designated authority shall maintain, preferably through an online 
system, a Tourism Trade Register containing details of registered tourism service 
providers, category endorsements, validity, suspension, cancellation, blacklisting 
and such other particulars as may be prescribed. 


(3) The Tourism Trade Register shall be accessible in such manner as may be 
prescribed. 


(4) The Administration shall integrate applications, renewals, payments, 
certificates, inspection reports and public verification through a single-window or 
digital system.

5. (1) The Administrator may, if deemed necessary, by notification, constitute 
a Tourism Trade Facilitation and Safety Committee for advising the Administration 
on tourism standards, safety protocols, capacity management, grievance trends, 
sustainable tourism practices and ease of doing business. 


(2) The Administrator may nominate representatives from tourism, police,

CHAPTER III 
REGISTRATION, CERTIFICATE, RENEWAL AND ADVERSE ACTION 


6. (1) No person shall carry on tourism trade in the Union territory unless 
registered or exempted under this Regulation. 


(2) A person carrying on more than one category of tourism trade may be 
issued a single certificate with separate category endorsements. 


(3) No separate registration shall be required for the same person and 
establishment, unless a separate endorsement is required for safety, consumer 
protection, environmental or public interest reasons. 


(4) A registered person shall not operate outside the category, area, island, 
vessel, premises or activity endorsed in the certificate. 


(5) The existing tourism service providers shall apply for registration or 
endorsement within six months from the commencement of the relevant provisions 
of this Regulation or within such extended period as may be notified. 


(6) A person who has applied within the time specified under sub-section (5) 
and whose application is pending shall not be treated as a defaulter until such 
application is decided or deemed to have been accepted under this 
Regulation, subject to compliance with safety restrictions applicable to high-risk 
activities. 


7. (1) An application for registration or endorsement shall be made to the 
designated authority in such form, manner along with such fee as may be prescribed. 


(2) Any defect in an application shall be communicated to the applicant within 
fifteen working days of receipt, failing which the application shall be treated as 
complete for the purpose of computing the disposal period. 


8. (1) Every complete application shall be disposed of within— 
(a) forty-five days for low-risk categories; 
(b) sixty days for high-risk categories; 


(c) sixty days for tourism vessels including live-aboard vessels, yachts 
and any other category of vessel as may be notified. 


(2) In case where the application is not disposed of within the period specified 
in sub-section (/), the application shall be deemed to have been accepted and the 
designated authority shall issue the certificate or endorsement within seven working 
days.

CHAPTER IV 
DUTIES AND CATEGORY-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS 
15. Every registered tourism service provider shall— 


(a) comply with the provisions of this Regulation, rules, standards, 
guidelines, certificate conditions issued thereunder and all applicable laws; 


(6) provide services honestly, safely and without coercion, touting or 
overcharging; 


(c) display registration details, approved or declared tariffs, cancellation 
or refund terms, safety warnings and grievance contacts; 


(d) issue bills, receipts or digital invoices for all payments; 
(e) maintain such records as may be prescribed; 
(f) employ or engage only qualified personnel; 


(g) maintain hygiene, cleanliness, waste-management and 
environmental safeguards; 


(A) immediately report any accident, missing tourist, serious illness, 
safety incident, environmental incident or law and order incident to the 
designated authority and other concerned authorities; 


(i) not misrepresent permits, facilities, marine life sightings, safety level, 
certification, location, itinerary, accessibility or environmental claims; 


(7) ensure that tourists are not taken to prohibited, restricted or unsafe 
areas; 


(k) comply with such code of conduct as may be prescribed. 


16. (J) No person shall operate an accommodation unit for tourists unless 
registered or exempted under this Regulation. 


(2) The operator shall maintain adequate facilities, hygiene, safety, fire and 
emergency arrangements, guest records, display information and complaint 
redressal mechanism. 


(3) The operator shall comply with reporting, identity verification and security 
requirements of a foreigner under the law for the time being in force. 


(4) Where an accommodation unit is classified, graded or approved by the 
Central Government or any authority recognised by the Central Government, such 
classification, grading or approval may be accepted for the purposes of this 
Regulation. 


(5) Classification, grading or quality-rating of accommodation units may be 
recognised for transparency and quality assurance in such manner as may be 
prescribed which, by itself, shall not create any entry barrier or be treated as a 
condition for registration or renewal under this Regulation. 


(6) A registered operator intending to close or discontinue the business during 
the validity period of registration shall intimate the designated authority at least 
fifteen days before such closure. 


(7) Homestays, bed and breakfast establishments, community-based 
accommodation and other small accommodation units shall comply with such 
necessary and simplified norms as may be prescribed.
