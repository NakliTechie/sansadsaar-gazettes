विशेष रेलवे परियोजना के क्रियान्वयन, रखरखाव, प्रबंधन एवं संचालन के लिए, उक्त अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में 
विनिद्रिष्ट भूमि को गुजरात राज्य में जिला खेड़ा में पश्चिम रेलवे पर वडोदरा डिवीजन के वडोदरा-गेरतपुर खंड के बीच

परियोजना के निष्पादन के लिये आवश्यक है ऐसी भूमि के अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा करती है;

किया गया था;

NOTIFICATION 


Mumbai, the 23rd September, 2025 

S.O. 4335(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No.489 of dated 06.08.2025 Extra Ordinary part II 
Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, namely, CONSTRUCTION OF 2 LANE ROB 
IN LIUE OF LC NO.257 AT KM 4268-20 BETWEEN SECTION VADODARA-GERATPUR OF 
VADODARA DIVISION ON WESTERN RAILWAY in District ANAND in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “ The Indian 
Express” dated 3" August 2025. “Nirmal Gujarat ” dated 3 August 2025 under subsection (4) of section 204 of 
the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 


shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
