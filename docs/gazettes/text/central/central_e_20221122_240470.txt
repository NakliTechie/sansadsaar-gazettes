MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd November, 2022 


8.0. 5424(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, number $.O. 270] (E), dated 3.06.2022 , published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3(A) of the 
National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening/four-laning, etc), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No ३6 
on the stretch of land from Km. 505.550 to Km. 567.000 (Ganjur to Ralga Section) in Chakur & Ahmadpur 
Taluka in District of Latur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in "Punyanagari” and 
“Lokmat Samachar “both dated 05.07.2022, under sub- section (3) of section 3(A) of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by 
the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3(D) of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3(D) of the said, Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3(D) of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
