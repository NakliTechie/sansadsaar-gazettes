PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS REGULATORY BOARD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l0th April, 2024 
File No. PNGRB/COM/I-PPPL (4)/2024 Vol-I (E-5022).—In exercise of the powers conferred by 
Section 6! of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006 (9 of 2006), the Petroleum and Natural 
Gas Regulatory Board, hereby makes the following regulations, to further amend the Petroleum and Natural Gas 


Regulatory Board (Determination of Petroleum and Petroleum Products Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Regulations, 
200, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement 


(.) These regulations may be called the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Determination of 
Petroleum and Petroleum Products Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Second Amendment Regulations, 
2024. 

(2) These shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. In the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Determination of Petroleum and Petroleum Products 

Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Regulations, 200, - 

(I) in regulation 4, after the fourteenth proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: - 
"Provided also that the transitional period may on the completion of the extended transition period till 
३3].03.2024, be further extended up to, and inclusive of 30.06.2024"; 

(2) in regulation 5, after the thirteenth proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: - 
"Provided also that the determination of the petroleum and petroleum products pipeline transportation 
tariff under this regulation shall be benchmarked against the goods tariff table of the railways as 
applicable on the date of the coming into force of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board 
(Determination of Petroleum and Petroleum Products Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Second Amendment 
Regulations, 2024 and if the goods tariff table of the railways is revised during the further extended 


period of transition, the Board may consider the benchmarking of the petroleum and petroleum products 
transportation tariff against such revision."
