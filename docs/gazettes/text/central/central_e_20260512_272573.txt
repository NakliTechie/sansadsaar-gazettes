sub-section (3) of section 2 of the said Code, namely:- 


a. Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) 
b. Additional Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) 


८. Deputy Chief Labour Commissioner (Central), Regional Labour Commissioner (Central), Assistant Labour 
Commissioner (Central) and Labour Enforcement Officer (Central) in the office of Chief Labour 
Commissioner (Central), Headquarters 


d. Deputy Chief Labour Commissioner (Central), Regional Labour Commissioner (Central), Assistant Labour 
Commissioner (Central) and Labour Enforcement Officer (Central) in all regions.
