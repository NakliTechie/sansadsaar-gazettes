NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th February, 2026 


G.S.R. 46(E).— The draft of the following rules to amend the Gas Cylinders Rules, 20!6 which the Central 
Government proposes to make, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 7 of the Explosives Act, 884 
(4 of 884), is hereby published as required by sub-section (l) of section 8 of the said Act, for information of all 
persons likely to be affected thereby; and notice is hereby given that the said draft shall be taken into consideration after 
Thirty (30) days from the date on which the copies of this notification as published in the Official Gazette are made 
available to the public; 


Objections or suggestions, if any, to these draft rules may be sent to the Director (Explosives), Department for 
Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Room No. 252 ‘A’, Udyog Bhavan, 
New Delhi — !000! or Email: expl-dipp@nic.in; within the period specified above. 


The objections or suggestions which may be received from any person with respect to the said draft rules, within the 
period so specified will be considered by the Central Government. 


Draft rules 


l. Short title and commencement.—({l) These rules may be called the Gas Cylinders (Amendment) 
Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their final publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Gas Cylinders Rules, 206 (hereinafter referred to as said rules), Rule 48 shall be omitted; 


3. In the form-C of the said Rules, under the heading DOCUMENTS REQUIRED TO BE SUBMITTED WITH 
THIS APPLICATION FOR A LICENCE IN FORM ‘E', 'F' & 0, clause (vi) shall be omitted.; 


4. In the form-F of the said Rules, in condition 8, after the clause (iv), the following shall be inserted, namely:- 


“Provided further that for the Industrial gas Cylinders Higher stacking and lesser gangway may be permitted 
by the licensing authority if cylinders are packed in certified structured packing (racks, cages) with adequate 
straps, clips or interlocks to ensure integrity of cylinder, its fittings and proper manoeuvring of cylinders are 
ensured with these certified structured packing. The loading unloading procedure shall comply with the 
national or international codes.”;
