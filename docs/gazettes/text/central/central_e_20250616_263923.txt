G.S.R. 386 (E).— Whereas the draft of certain rules further to amend the Gas Cylinder Rules, 
2016, were published, vide notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry 
(Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) number, G.S.R. 771 (E), dated the 16" December, 
2024 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), inviting objections and 
suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby before the expiry of a period of thirty days from 
the date on which the copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were made available to the public; 


And whereas, the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public on the 167 
December, 2024; 


And whereas no objection and suggestions received from the public within the said period; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 7 of the Explosives Act, 1884 (4 of 
1884), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Gas Cylinders Rules, 
2016, namely:- 


1. Short titled and commencement- (1) These rules may be called the Gas Cylinders (Amendment) 
Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

‘(xlia)“Micro and Small Enterprises” means an enterprise defined in clause (h) and clause 
(m) of section 2 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006; 

(b) after clause (xlvii), the following clause shall be inserted namely:- 
(xlviia) “Startup” means an entity registered with the Department for Promotion of Industry 
and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry and as defined in clause (a) of 
paragraph 1 of the Notification number G.S.R. 127(E), dated the 197 February, 2019, and as 
amended from time to time. 

(०) after clause (li), the following clause shall be inserted namely:- 
“(lia) “women entrepreneur” meansan enterprise owned and controlled by a woman having 
a minimum financial interest of 26 percent of the capital; ””.
