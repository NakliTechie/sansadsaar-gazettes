ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 22nd August, 2025 


O.N. 54(E)—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Karnataka, 2023 for 
50-Yelahanka Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification 
No. 464/KT-LA/2023, dated 3" April, 2023; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him/her or his/her 
election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the election for 50-Yelahanka Assembly Constituency of Karnataka, 2023 was 
declared by the Returning Officer on 3" May, 2023 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election 
Expenses was [27 June, 2023; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report dated 23.06.2023 and 0.04.2024 of District Election Officer, Bengaluru 
Urban under sub-rule () of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, !96 received through the Chief Electoral 
Officer, Karnataka vide his letter dated 27.06.2023 and 2.04.2024 stating that Sh. B. Lakshmaiah, a contesting 
candidate from 50-Yelahanka Assembly Constituency in General Election to Legislative Assembly of Karnataka, 
2023 has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Bengaluru Urban, a ‘Show-Cause 
Notice’ No. 76/KT-LA/08/2023/CEMS-II, dated 23" September, 2024 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 96l by the Election Commission of India to Sh. B. Lakshmaiah, for not lodging of the 
account of Election Expenses; and 

WHEREAS, through the above said ‘Show Cause Notice’ and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96, Sh. 8. Lakshmaiah was directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his election expenses with the 
District Election Officer, Bengaluru Urban District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Bengaluru Urban in his report no. AC/ELN/PR/75/2024-25; 
dated 28.03.2025 has reported that the said ‘Show Cause Notice’ was served to Sh. B. Lakshmaiah on 7.03.2025; 
and 

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Bengaluru Urban in his Supplementary Report no. 
AC/ELN/PR/74/2024-25; dated 09.07.2025 on account of the candidate received through Chief Electoral Officer, 
Karnataka letter No. DPAR/24/Chuvebha(Addl.CEO-) 2023; dated l!.07.2025 has reported that Sh. B. Lakshmaiah 
has not submitted any representation or statement of account of election expenses. Further, after receipt of the said 
notice, Sh. B. Lakshmaiah has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of India for 
failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95] provides that:- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses within the time and in the manner required by or under this 
Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 


the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such 
person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 

WHEREAS, on basis of the facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. B. Lakshmaiah 
has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; 
and 

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Sh. B. Lakshmaiah, resident of Guni Agrahara village, Shivakote 
post, Hesaraghatta Hobli, Bengalore-560089 and a contesting candidate from 50-Yelahanka Assembly 
Constituency of General election to Legislative assembly of Karnataka, 2023, to be disqualified for being chosen as 
and for being a member of either House of the Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a 
State or Union Territory for a period of three years from the date of this order.
