७.0, 3697(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section

construction of (Widening two / four laning etc,) Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH73lA (RAM VAN GAMAN PATH) on the stretch of land from Km 0.000 to Km 35.000 (competent 
authority/ Chief Revenue Officer, Pratapgarh) in the district of PRATAPGARH in the state of UTTAR 
PRADESH.

“Times of India” both dated 77 September 2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
