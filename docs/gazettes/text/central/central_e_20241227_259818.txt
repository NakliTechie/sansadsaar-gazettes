INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-02/5321-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Florence Nightingale School of Nursing situated at Chipurupalli Road, Rajam, Distt. 
Srikakulam 532127 Andhra Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Andhra Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh 
Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-02/5321-INC (53843) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Andhra Pradesh 5th Floor, Ground Floor, Room No. 157, A.P. Secretariat, Velegapudi, Amaravathi 522503, Andhra 
Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Florence Nightingale School of 
Nursing situated at Chipurupalli Road, Rajam, Distt. Srikakulam 532127 Andhra Pradesh. In the notice issued, 
the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the 
nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Florence Nightingale School of Nurisng. Even the institution, Florence Nightingale School of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
19/09/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Florence Nightingale School of Nursing 
situated at Chipurupalli Road, Rajam, Distt. Srikakulam 532127 Andhra Pradesh shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Andhra Pradesh.
