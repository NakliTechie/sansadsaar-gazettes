का.आ. 1340 ( अ ) . – केन्द्रीय सरकार ने , राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. , जो भारत के राजपत्र , असाधारण , भाग II , खण्ड 3 , 
उपखंड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , द्वारा आंध्र प्रदेश राज्य के नंदयाल जिले में राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग 340 बी के कि.मी. 0 से 
कि.मी. 56.2 ( सोमयाजुलापल्ली से दून सेक्शन ) तक के भूखण्ड पर निर्माण , ( चौड़ीकरण / पेव्ड शोल्डर सहित 2 - लेन / 4 - लेन 
का बनाना आदि ) अनुरक्षण , प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन 
करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 21st March , 2023 

S.O. 1340 ( E ) .— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , numbers S.O. 4279 ( E ) Dated 13.09.2022 and S.O.4614 ( E ) Dated 30.09.2022 , published in the Gazette 
of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National 
Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ( widening / two lane 
with paved shoulder / four laning etc. ) , Maintenance , management and operation of National Highway 340B on the 
stretch of land from Km . 0 to Km . 56.2 ( Somayajulapalli to Done Section ) in the district of Nandyal in the State of 
ANDHRA PRADESH . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Andhra Prabha ( Telugu ) " and 
" The Hindu ( English ) " both dated 21.09.2022 ; and Andhra Prabha ( Telugu ) " and " The Hindu ( English ) " both dated 
20.10.2022 under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
