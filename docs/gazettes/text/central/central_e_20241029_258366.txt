S.O. 4752(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for

(Pachpadra- Bagundi Section) in the stretch of land from Km. 73.600 to Km. 95.700 in the District of Balotra in the 
State of Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act.

writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being
