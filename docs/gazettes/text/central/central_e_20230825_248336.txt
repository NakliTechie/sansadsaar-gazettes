THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th August, 2023 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. PR/200/2017/DD/217/2017/DC/989/2019.—In terms of the provisions of Section 21B(3) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rules 18(17) and 19(1) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of 
Investigations of Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee 
has held CA. Vishal Dhingra (Membership No. 408383), House no. 1054, FF, Shakti Khand 4, Distt Ghaziabad, 
INDIRAPURAM 201 010, guilty of Professional/Other Misconduct falling within the meaning of Item (4) of Part II 
of the Second Schedule and Item (2) of Part IV of the First Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently ordered for 
removal of the name of aforesaid CA. Vishal Dhingra (Membership No. 408383), from the Register of Members for a 
period of 05(Five) years. In pursuance thereof and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 
of the aforesaid Act, read with Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988, the name of said CA. 
Vishal Dhingra (Membership No. 408383), was removed from the Register of Members for a period of 05(Five) years 
with effect from 221 October, 2021. Aggrieved by Order of the Committee, the Respondent preferred an Appeal in 
terms of Section 22G of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and the Appellate Authority in appeal no. 43/ICAI/2022 
vide its order dated 1 August, 2023 had reduced the period of removal of name from 05(Five) years to 03(Three) 
years. Accordingly, in pursuance of the said order of the Appellate Authority, the notification dated 227 October, 
2021, wherein the name of CA. Vishal Dhingra (Membership No. 408383) was removed from the Register of 
Members for a period of 05(Five) years is hereby modified to the extent that the name of CA. Vishal Dhingra 
(Membership No. 408383) stands removed for a period of 03 (Three) years only w.e.f. 22" October, 2021.
