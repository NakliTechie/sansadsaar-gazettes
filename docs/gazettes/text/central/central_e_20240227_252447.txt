MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th February, 2024 

S.O. 930(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 2756(E), dated the 9" June, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 23' June, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Western Dedicated Freight 


Corridor in the District of Navsari in the State of Gujarat; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“The Times of India” and “Sandesh”, dated the 3070 August, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 
AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 


authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), and in pursuance of sub- 


section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands 


specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and
