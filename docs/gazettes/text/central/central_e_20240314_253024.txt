PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 4th March, 2024 
No. 5 /2023 
Subject: Amendments in Para 4.4 and 4.06 of the Handbook of Procedures 2023. 


F. No. 0/94/80/084/AM24/PC-4.—In exercise of powers conferred under Paragraph |.03 and 2.04 of the 
Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby makes the 
following amendments in the provisions of Para 4.4 and 4.06 of the Handbook of Procedures 2023: 


(A) Under Para 4.4 of HBP 2023, new sub-para (iii) is added as mentioned below: 


(iii) Ad-hoc Input Output Norms may also be decided in a rule based IT environment without reference to Norms 
Committee. However, a certain percentage of cases as flagged by the RMS may be referred to Norms Committee for 
validation / review. 


(B) A new sub para 4.06 (vii) of HBP 2023 is inserted as below: 


4.06(vii) In cases where ad-hoc norms have already been arrived at by Norms Committee, the Norms Committee may 
recommend Notification of SION on a case to case basis. 


Effects of this Public Notice: Para 4.06 and Para 4.4 of the Handbook of Procedures 2023 have been amended to 
streamline and automate the process of fixation of Norms and Notification of new SIONs under Advance 
Authorisation Scheme, for ease of doing business and trade facilitation.
