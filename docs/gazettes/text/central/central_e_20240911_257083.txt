G.S.R. 565(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20i(/! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:- 


. Short title and commencement.-(|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of Diamond Jubilee Year of Official Language) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.-The coin of seventy-five rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of Diamond Jubilee Year of Official Language. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.-The denomination of the coin mentioned in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely: -
