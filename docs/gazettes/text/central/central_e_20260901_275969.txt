S.O. 4858(E).— Whereas M/s TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd., the applicant with its registered Office 
at C/o Tata Power Renewable Energy Limited, Corporate Centre, A Block, 34 Sant Tukaram Road, Carnac Bunder, 
Mumbai-400009, India, has applied for authorization under Section 164 of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of 
overhead transmission line under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s 
TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd. for its 288 MW Wind Power Project at Koppal, Karnataka”. 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/59/2025-PG dated 11.06.2025 
had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for the overhead line covered under the 
transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd. for 
its 288 MW Wind Power Project at Koppal, Karnataka”. 


M/s TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd. had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspapers 
Rajasthan Patrika (Hindi) dated 17.10.2025, The New Indian Express (English) dated 17.10.2025, Vijayavani (Kannada) 
dated 17.10.2025 and Weekly Gazette of India dated 27.12.2025 for the general public to make 
observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within 2 Months from the date of publication. 
Subsequently, M/s TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd. has submitted an affidavit dated 24.06.2026 declaring that no 
observation/representation was received within 2 Months from the date of publication of public notice. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so established 
or maintained for laying of electric lines under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing 
connectivity to M/s TATA Power Renewable Energy Ltd. for its 288 MW Wind Power Project at Koppal, Karnataka”. 
The following overhead line is covered under this scheme:
