NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 4th July, 2023 


S.O. 2937(E).—Whereas, M/s. Odisha Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation, a Government of 
Odisha undertaking, had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter 


विपुल बंसल, संयुक्त सचिव 


[भाग Lave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


referred to as the said Act) to set up a sector specific Special Economic Zone for IT (Knowledge Park) at Village 
Gaudakashipur and Arisal, Tehsil: Jatni, District: Khurda in the State of Odisha; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, vide Ministry of Commerce and 
Industry Notification Number S.O. 2(E) dated 04 January, 20l notified an area of 06.260 hectares at the above 
Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Odisha Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation has now proposed for de- 
notification of 56.078 hectares from the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Odisha has given its approval to the proposal vide letter No. 
5356 dated 6" June, 2022; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Falta Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of an area of 56.078 hectares of the above the Special Economic Zone. Further, after the 
de-notification, the area shall be utilized for allotment to ITATES companies; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that the requirements under sub-section (8) of section 
3 of the said Act and other related requirements are fulfilled; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by second proviso to sub-section (.) of section 4 
of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006, the 
Central Government hereby de-notifies an area of 56.078 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone, thereby 
making the total area of the Special Economic Zone as 50.l82 hectares. The Survey numbers and area for the de- 
notification are given below in the table, namely: -
