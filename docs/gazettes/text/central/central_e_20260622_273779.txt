MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th June, 2026 


9.0. 3296(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways. S.O. 2935 (E) Dated 30/06/2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, 
Sub-Section (ii) Issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for Construction of (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.) 
Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway 548 C in the stretch of land from Km. 43.7 to 
Km. 97.2 (Satara- Mhaswad-Tembhurni Road) in the District of Satara in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 2935 (E) Dated 30/06/2025 has been published in 
"Dainik Lokmanthan” and "Dainik Pritisangam" (Marathi & English) Both are dated 13/10/2025; under sub- 
section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
