MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS , FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
( Department Of Food And Public Distribution ) 

( WAREHOUSING DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATORY AUTHORITY ) 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 10th February , 2023 

S.O. 698 ( E ) .— In pursuance of sub - clause ( ii ) of clause ( b ) of sub - section ( 4 ) of section 4 of the Aadhaar 
( Targeted Delivery of Financial And Other Subsidies , Benefits And Services ) Act , 2016 ( 18 of 2016 ) read with rule 5 
of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance ( Social Welfare , Innovation , Knowledge ) Rules , 2020 , the 
Ministry of Consumer Affairs , Food and Public Distribution , having been authorised by the Central Government , 
hereby notifies that the Warehousing Development Regulatory Authority and its regulated repositories are allowed to 
perform Aadhaar authentication , on voluntary basis , for the purposes given below , with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , namely : 


( a ) Onboarding of depositors , warehouseman and others on WDRA portal and its regulated repositories 
platform through aadhar e - KYC ; 


( b ) performing service transactions on WDRA portal and its regulated repositories . 

2. The Warehousing Development Regulatory Authority shall adhere to the guidelines with respect to use of 
Aadhaar authentication as laid down by the Central Government .
