बशर्ते कि यदि किसी विभाग में एक ही प्रोफेसर हो, तो 
विभागाध्यक्ष की नियुक्ति वरिष्ठता के क्रम में रोटेशन के आधार

इसके अलावा यह भी प्रावधान है कि यदि किसी विभाग में 
कोई प्रोफेसर नहीं है, तो विभागाध्यक्ष की नियुक्ति वरिष्ठता के 
क्रम में रोटेशन के आधार पर एसोसिएट प्रोफेसरों में से की 
जाएगी। 

इसके अलावा यह भी प्रावधान है कि यदि किसी विभाग में 
कोई प्रोफेसर या एसोसिएट प्रोफेसर नहीं है, तो संबंधित स्कूल 
का डीन उस विभाग का प्रमुख होगा। 


(क) प्रत्येक विभाग का एक प्रमुख होगा, जिसे कुलपति द्वारा

F. No. CURAJ/R/F.68/2025/696 - In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20 
read with sub-section (2) of the Section 27 of the Central Universities Act, 2009 (25 of 2009), the Executive Council of 
Central University of Rajasthan with the approval of the Visitor, hereby makes the following Statutes to amend the 
Central University of Rajasthan Statutes, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement. — () These Statutes may be called as the Central Universities (Amendment) 


Statutes (for Central University of Rajasthan), 2025 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Central Universities' Statutes (for Central University of Rajasthan), - 


(i) 


the following Statute shall be amended, namely:- 


AMENDMENT TO THE EXISTING STATUTE 40 


PROPOSAL FOR AMENDMENTS TO THE EXISTING STATUTES 


Statute 40 — Appointment of Heads of Department 


Existing 


After amendment 


(a) Each Department shall have a Head who shall be 
appointed by the Vice-Chancellor from amongst the 
Professors of the Department on the basis of rotation 
in order of the seniority. 


Provided that if a Department has only one Professor, 
the Head of the Department shall be appointed from 
amongst the Professor and the senior most Associate 
Professor by rotation in the order of seniority. 


Provided further that if a Department has no 
Professor, the Head of the Department shall be 
appointed from amongst the Associate Professors on 
the basis of rotation in order of seniority. 


Provided further that if there is no Professor or an 
Associate Professor, in a Department, the Dean of the 
School concerned shall act as the Head of that 
Department. 


(a) Each Department shall have a Head who shall be 
appointed by the Vice-Chancellor from amongst the 
Professors and the Associate Professors of the 
Department on the basis of rotation in order of the seniority. 


Provided that if a Department has only one or no Professor/ 
Associate Professor, the Head of the Department shall be 
appointed from amongst the Professor/ Associate 
Professor and the Assistant Professors having atleast 8 
years of university level experience by rotation in the order 
of seniority. 


Provided further that if there is no Professor or Associate 
Professor or the Assistant Professor having atleast 8 
years of university level experience, in a Department, the 
Dean of the School concerned shall act as the Head of 
that Department. 


Note:- The Statute 40 was published along with the Central Universities Act, 2009 as a Schedule annexed to that Act, 
which was published in the Gazette of India, No. 25 of 2009 dated 20" March2009.
