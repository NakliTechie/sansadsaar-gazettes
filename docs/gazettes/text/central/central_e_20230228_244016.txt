ROU of Paradip Numaligarh Crude Oil Pipeline ( PNCPL ) traverses in the same route that of GAIL ( India ) Limited's 
Barauni Guwahati Natural Gas Pipeline ( BGPL ) for 03.95 Km . in Alipurduar ( West Bengal ) District . 


Numaligarh Refinery Limited proposes to make use of about 03.95 Km . common ROU corridor of GAIL 
( India ) Limited’s Barauni Guwahati Natural Gas Pipeline ( BGPL ) , which was acquired initially by GAIL ( India ) 
Limited published in Central Gazette vide , S.O. 4042 ( E ) Dated 05 November , 2019 published in the Central Gazette
