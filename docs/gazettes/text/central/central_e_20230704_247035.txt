MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
ORDER 

New Delhi, the 4th July, 2023 
S.O. 2939(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 20A of the Tobacco Board Act, 1975 (Act 4 
of 1975), the Central Government hereby authorises the Tobacco Board to allow the registered traders and dealers of 
the Tobacco Board to purchase at their auction platforms in the State of Andhra Pradesh for the auctions during Crop 
Season 2022-2023, the excess flue cured virginia tobacco produced by registered growers and unauthorised flue cured 
virginia tobacco produced by unregistered growers in the State of Andhra Pradesh on the payment of normal 


applicable service charges under the said Act. 
2. Acopy of the Record of Rights in respect of tobacco crop for the year 2023 is to be furnished. 
[F. No. 5/3/2019-EP(Agri-VI)/Pt.]
