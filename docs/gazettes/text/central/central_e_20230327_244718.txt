MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Economic Affairs) 
(BUDGET DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 27th March, 2023 


Subject: Premature redemption of a part of outstanding stock of Non-interest bearing Government of India 
IDBI Special Security, 2024. 


F. No. 4(4)-B(W&M)/208.—Government of India, hereby notifies further premature redemption on Non- 
interest bearing Government of India IDBI Special Security, 2024 (hereinafter briefly described as “the Special 
Security”) by exercising “call option” at par as stipulated in para 5(ii) of its Notification F.No.4(6)-W&M/2004 dated 
September 29, 2004, for an aggregate amount of Rupees Two Hundred Fifty Five crore only (2255.00 crore) 
[nominal]. 


2. Date of Premature Redemption 


The date of premature redemption of the Special Security by the Government of India shall be 
March 3], 2023. 


3. Outstanding Stock 


The outstanding stock of the Special Security shall be Rupees Two Thousand Three Hundred Seventy Eight 
crore and Seventy Three lakhs only (2,378.73 crore) [nominal] after the exercise of call option. 


By Order of the President of India, 
ASHISH VACHHANI, Addl. Secy.
