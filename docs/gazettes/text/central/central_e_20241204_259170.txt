New Delhi, the 470 December, 2024 


S.O. 5225(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, S.O. 526l(E) Dated December, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 
of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of Ayodhya Bypass (Northern Ayodhya Bypass starting at Km 2+540 and 
ending at Km 39+928 of NH-27 and Southern Ayodhya Bypass starting at Km 2+540 and ending at Km 
53+28 of NH-27) in the stretch of land from Km !2+540 to Km 53+28] of NH-27 in district of BASTI in the 
state of UTTAR PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Indian Express” and 
“Jansatta” both dated 29 December, 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
