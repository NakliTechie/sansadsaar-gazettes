99.0. 3277(E).—In pursuance of clause 20 A of Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) Control Order, 1985, 
the Central Government hereby notifies the following specifications in respect of Phospho Gypsum (Granular) to be 
manufactured in India for a period of three years from the date of .publication in the official Gazette, namely,- 


Phospho Gypsum (Granular); 


(i) Moisture per cent. by weight , maximum 15.0 
(ii) Sodium content (as Na) per cent. by weight, maximum 0.75 (on dry basis) 
(iii) Colour Light Green 


(iv) Particle Size: Not less than 90 per cent. of the material shall pass through 4 mm IS sieve and shall be 


retained on | mm IS sieve. Not more than 5 per cent. shall pass through 1 mm IS sieve 


(v) Sulphur (as S) per cent. by weight , minimum 13.0 
(vi) Calcium Sulphate dehydrate content, percent. by weight minimum 70.0 (On dry basis) 
(vii) Flouride (as F) per cent. by weight, maximum 1.0 


(viii) Heavy metal content ( as mg per kg) maximum 


Lead (as Pb) 100.0 
Cadmium (as Cd) 5.0
