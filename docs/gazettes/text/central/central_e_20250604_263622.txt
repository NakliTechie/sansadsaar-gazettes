MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd June, 2025 


G.S.R. 368(E).— Whereas the draft rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, 1971, was published, as 
required by section 36 of the Insecticides Act, 1968 (46 of 1968) vide notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare) number G.S.R. 894 (E), 
dated the 14th December, 2023, in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I], Section-3, Sub-section (i), inviting 
objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry of a period of thirty days 
from the date on which the copies of the Gazette of India in which the said notification was published, were made 
available to the public; 


And whereas, copies of the said notification were made available to the public on 14th day of December, 2023; 


And whereas, objections or suggestions received from the public in respect of the said draft rules have been 
considered by the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36 of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
makes the following rules further to amend the Insecticides Rules, 1971, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Insecticides (First Amendment) Rules, 2025. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Inthe Insecticides Rules, 1971, herein after referred to as the principal rules, in rule 19, in sub-rule (1),- 
(1) for clause (i), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(i) The label shall have either one, two or three panels and shall be printed only in English and Hindi languages and 
the minimum size of all the label panels shall be according to the size affixed on the face of the package: 


Provided that there shall be three types of labels depending on the net content: 
(1) ultra small package -1gm or ml to 50 gm or ml;

below the brand name and it should not occupy more than twenty percent of area of the face of the panel 
and the brand name shall be in bold; 


(b) common name as such approved by Registration Committee;

Note II: A. The provisions of item (u) are in addition to the Legal Metrology(Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 
B. Additional information may be added to the Label after approval by the Registration Committee. 


C. All relevant safety pictograms (refer picture I in the THIRD SCHEDULE) of size not less than one square 
centimetre at the bottom of the label in respect of labels of minimum size. The pictograms shall be proportionately 
bigger in respect of labels of bigger size at the bottom of the right side panel. They shall be printed only once on the 
label. For small labels the pictograms on the primary pack shall be printed on primary or the secondary pack and for 
ultra small labels the pictograms shall be printed on leaflet and the words 'Please refer leaflet for safety pictograms' 
shall be mentioned on label. 


(2) for clause (iii), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(a) The ultra small or small packages shall also be packed in a secondary pack whose label shall be printed with 
indelible ink displaying all contents of clause (i) of sub-rule (1) of rule 19: 


Provided that the secondary packs may not be required for ultra or small packages whose label of primary pack 
have all contents of clause (1) of sub-rule (1) of rule 19 with indelible ink in the font sizes not less than size prescribed: 


Provided further that for ultra-small packs, multiple primary packs may be packed in one secondary pack and 
the manufacturer shall ensure leaflet and Quick Response code be securely attached with each primary pack before 
packing them in one secondary pack. 


(b) The label of the big package shall be printed with indelible ink displaying all contents of clause (i) of sub-rule 
(1) of rule 19 and the big package may also be packed in a secondary pack.” 


(3) in clause (iv), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:- 


“provided that in case of ultra small packs, composition of the formulation or insecticide may be displayed in 
short as “percentage of active ingredient and other ingredients” and the complete composition shall be printed on leaflet 
and the name and address of the manufacturer may be printed in short on label and complete address shall be printed on 
leaflet.” 


3. In the principal rules, in sub-rule (3), for the first proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted, namely: - 


“provided that for ultra-small packs, the dimensions of the said square shall be not less than 1 square 
centimeter (1.0 cm x1.0 cm).” 


4. after sub-rule (3), for the Explanation, the following Explanation shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Explanation.- For the purposes of sub-rule (1) and sub-rule (3), it is hereby clarified that,- 


(1) no registration certificate holder shall use a label or leaflet, which is not in accordance with the provisions of the 
Insecticides (First Amendment) Rules, 2025, after a period of six months from the date of commencement of the 
said Rules. 

(1) no insecticides or its package bearing a label or leaflet, which is not in accordance with the provisions of the 


Insecticides (First Amendment) Rules, 2025, shall be distributed, sold, exhibited for sale or stocked, after a 
period of thirty months from the date of commencement of the said rules and this explanation shall cease to exist 
after expiry of the said period, unless otherwise directed.” 


5. In the principal rules, in sub-rule (7) the words “ unless stated otherwise” shall be omitted.
