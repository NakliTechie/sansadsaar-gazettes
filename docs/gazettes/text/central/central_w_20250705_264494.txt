SECRETARIAT OF THE ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th May, 2025 


O.N. 8.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the Representation of 
the People Act, 950 (43 of 950), the Election Commission of India in consultation with the State Government of 
Nagaland hereby designates Smt. Bhagyashree Bhimraoji Banayat, IAS, (NL:202) as the Chief Electoral Officer 
for the State of Nagaland with effect from the date she takes over charge and until further orders in place of 
Shri Vyasan R, IAS. 


2. Smt. Bhagyashree Bhimraoji Banayat, shall cease to hold and hand over forthwith the charge of all or any 
charges of work under the Government of Nagaland, which she may be holding before such assumption of office. 


3. Smt. Bhagyashree Bhimraoji Banayat, while functioning as the Chief Electoral Officer, Nagaland shall not 
hold any additional charge whatsoever under the Government of Nagaland except that she should be designated 
Secretary to the Government in charge of Election Department in the State Secretariat. 


[F. No. 54/NL/2025-P.Admn. | 


By Order, 
RAHUL SHARMA, Principal Secy.
