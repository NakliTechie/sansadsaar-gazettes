Chapter XII — Orderly Winding Down — 
3.l Winding Down / Wind Down of the Clearing Corporation 


Winding Down of the Clearing Corporation shall mean the procedure for termination of critical/ core operations and 
services of the Clearing Corporation. 


3.2 Critical Operations and Services of the Clearing Corporation 


The Clearing Corporation shall identify the critical / core functions based on the parameters prescribed by SEBI, if 
any, which shall be continued to the extant permissible during the winding down of the Clearing Corporation. 


3.3 Winding Down of the Clearing Corporation can arise in the following situations 

3.3.. Voluntary Winding Down 

The Clearing Corporation chooses to Wind Down and exit the business as a strategic / business decision. 
3.3.2 Involuntary Winding Down can arise in following situations 


a. Insufficient or inadequate default management resources such as settlement guarantee fund of the Clearing 
Corporation 


b. Losses arising out of regulatory actions which impacts the Clearing Corporation’s ability to carry out clearing 
and settlement of trades executed on the concerned recognized stock exchange. 


c. Losses due to other factors which impact the Clearing Corporation’s ability to fulfill its obligations towards 
Members, its constituents and/ or other creditors. 


3.4 Standard Operating Procedure for Winding Down of the Clearing Corporation
