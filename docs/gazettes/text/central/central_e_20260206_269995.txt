NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 6th February, 2026 


99.0. 66I(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) Number S.O 994(E) dated the 0274 May’2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part IJ, 
section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 0274 May, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mahabubnagar — Dhone doubling 
project in the District of NANDYAL in the State of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “THE HANS 
INDIA, HINDI MILAP, SAAKSHI and ABN Andhra Jyothi” dated gt May’2025 under sub section(4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Somapuram H/o Kamaganikuntla & Dhone Villages of Dhone Mandal, Nandyal district, 
no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have 
been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances:
