Subject: Amendment to Notification No. 65/2025-26 for inclusion of Egypt and Jordan under 
RELIEF - reg. 


S.O. 2000(E).— In exercise of powers conferred under Sections 3 and 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development 
and Regulation) Act, 992, read with paras .02 and 2.0] of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to 
time, the Central Government hereby amends Notification No. 65/2025-26 dated 9.03.2026 relating to the Resilience 
& Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation (RELIEF) under the Export Promotion Mission (EPM). 


2. For the purposes of paras 6.2, 7.2 and 8.2 of the said notification, the list of eligible destinations shall stand expanded 
to include Egypt and Jordan, in addition to the countries already notified, for shipments meant for delivery or 


transshipment. 
3. All other provisions of Notification No. 65/2025-26 dated 9.03.2026 shall remain unchanged. 


Effect of this Notification: Egypt and Jordan are also included under EPM RELIEF to support exporters 
affected by continuing logistics disruptions.
