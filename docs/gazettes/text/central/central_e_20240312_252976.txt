New Delhi, the 2th March, 2024 


S.O. 30I(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building(widening/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NHI3 in the stretch of land from 
Km. 422.00 to Km. 523.600 (Chitradurga Shimoga section) in the district of CHITRADURGA in the state of 
KARNATAKA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition officer 
National Highways Bangaluru 56000! in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority 
shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing

final.
