S.O. 2579(E).—Whereas In exercise of the powers conferred by section |6 read with section l7 and

Safety (Omnibus Technical Regulation) Order, 2024, namely: - 


l. (.) This Order may be called the Machinery and Electrical Equipment Safety (Omnibus 
Technical Regulation) Amendment Order, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Machinery and Electrical Equipment Safety (Omnibus Technical Regulation) Order, 
2024, in paragraph |, — 


(a) in sub-paragraph (2), for the words “after one year from the date of its publication in the 
Official Gazette”, the words, figures and letters “on the Ist September, 2026” shall be 
substituted; 


(b) For sub-paragraph (3), the following sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely: — 


“(3) It shall apply to machines and electrical equipment listed in the first Schedule 
annexed to this Order from the Ist September, 2026: 


Provided that the assemblies, sub-assemblies and components listed in the first 
Schedule annexed to this Order shall apply from such date as may be notified by the 
Central Government in the Official Gazette”’.
