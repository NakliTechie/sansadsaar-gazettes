NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th May, 2025 


S.O. 2389(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number 8.0 716 (E) dated 11 February 2025. 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Costruction, 
maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 166H in the stretch of land Km 0.00 to km 29.600 
(Section Peth Naka-Karandwadi )(Special Land Acquition Officer-1, Sangli ) in the District of Sangli in the state of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 716 (E) dated 11 February 2025 , has been published 
in “Dainik Sakal” and “Dainik Sakal” Both Dated 25 February 2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the sai d report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
