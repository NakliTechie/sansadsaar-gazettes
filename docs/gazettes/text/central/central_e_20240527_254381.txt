a. अ. 128(अ).--लोक प्रतिनिधित्व अधिनियम, 1951 (1951 का 43) की धारा 22 की उप-धारा (1) F 
रेखांकित उपबंधों के अनुसरण में, भारत निर्वाचन आयोग एतदट्दवारा, अपनी अधिसूचना सं. 434/पुदु/2023(2), दिनांक 
24-07-2023, समय-समय पर यथा-संशोधित, में पुदुचेरी सरकार से परामर्श करके निम्नलिखित संशोधन करता है। 
निम्नलिखित सरकारी अधिकारी, जैसा कि नीचे दी गई तालिका में उल्लिखित है, पुदुचेरी संसदीय निर्वाचन क्षेत्र में सहायक 
रिटर्निंग अधिकारियों के रूप में नियुक्त किए जाते हैं। इन सहायक रिटर्निंग अधिकारियों की प्राथमिक जिम्मेदारी लोक सभा 
के साधारण निर्वाचन, 2024 में दिनांक 04-06-2024 को होने वाली मतों की गणना की प्रक्रिया में पुदुचेरी संघ राज्य-द्षेत्र 
में पुदुचेरी संसदीय निर्वाचन क्षेत्र के रिटर्निंग अधिकारी की सहायता करनी होगी।

ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th May, 2024 


O. N. 128(E).—In pursuance with the provisions outlined in sub-section (1) of section 22 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1951 (43 of 1951), the Election Commission of India, hereby introduces the 
following amendments to its notification no. 434/PUDU/2023(2), dated 24-07-2023, as amended from time to time, in 
consultation with the Government of Puducherry. The following government officers, as mentioned in the table below, 
are appointed Assistant Returning Officers in Puducherry Parliamentary Constituency. Primary responsibility of these 
Assistant Returning Officers shall be to assist the Returning Officer of the Puducherry Parliamentary Constituency in 
the Union Territory of Puducherry in the process of counting of votes in General Election to House of the People, 
2024 to be held on 04-06-2024. 


In the existing entries of the table appended to the notification no. 434/PUDU/2023(2), dated 24-07-2023, the 
following entries shall be added: - 


SI. No. Assistant Returning Officer 
14 Director of Tourism, Puducherry 
15 Director of Higher and Technical Education, Puducherry 
16 Director, Information and Technology, Puducherry. 
17 Executive Officer (Temples), Karaikal 


The designation of the aforementioned officers as Assistant Returning Officers in the Puducherry 
Parliamentary Constituency will cease to be effective upon the successful completion of the vote counting process in 
the constituency. 


[No. 434/PUDU/2023(2)] 
By Order, 
MALAY MALLICK, Principal Secy.
