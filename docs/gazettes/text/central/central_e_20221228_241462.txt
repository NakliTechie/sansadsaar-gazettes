MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 28th December , 2022 

G.S.R. 907 ( E ) . – In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) read with clauses ( a ) and ( c ) of sub 
section ( 2 ) of section 155 of the Sashastra Seema Bal Act , 2007 ( 53 of 2007 ) , the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules further to amend the Sashastra Seema Bal Combatised ( General Duty ) Group ' C ' posts 
Recruitment Rules , 2011 , namely : 


1. Short title and commencement . - ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Sashastra Seema Bal Combatised ( General 
Duty ) Group ' C ' posts Recruitment ( Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2 . 

In the Sashastra Seema Bal Combatised ( General Duty ) Group ' C ' posts Recruitment Rules , 2011 , in the 
Schedule , against Serial Number 2 relating to the post of Constable ( General Duty ) , 


( i ) in column ( 6 ) , - the Note shall be numbered as “ Note 1 ” thereof , and after Note 1 as so numbered , the following 
Notes shall be inserted , namely : 

“ Note 2 : The upper age - limit shall be relaxable upto five years for the candidates of the first batch of Ex 
Agniveers . ” ; 

“ Note 3 : The upper age limit shall be relaxable upto three years for the candidates of Ex - Agniveers " . 

( ii ) in column ( 7 ) , - The Note to sub - heading shall be numbered as Note 1 thereof and after Note 1 as so numbered , the 
following Note shall be inserted , namely : 


“ Note 2 : Ex - Agniveers shall be exempted from Physical Efficiency Test ( PET ) . " ; 
( iii ) in column ( 10 ) , after the entries , the following Note shall be inserted , namely : 


" Note : Ten percent of vacancies shall be reserved for Ex- Agniveers , for recruitment to the post of Constable 
( General Duty ) . " .
