MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
(PHRD Division) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th May, 2026 

G.S.R. 42(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section | (f) of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act, 202 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Ports, Shipping 
& Waterways (PHRD Division) G.S.R. No. 27 (8) dated 24 March, 2023, the Central Government hereby appoints 
Shri Shahir Khan, Working President, Mormugao Port & Railway Workers Union as Member representing the interests 
of employees on the Board of Mormugao Port Authority with immediate effect. The appointment of Member of the 


Board shall be subject to the provisions of the Major Port Authorities Act, 202l. The appointment is valid till the date 


of validity of check-off, or until the attainment of age of superannuation of the Members, or till further orders, whichever 
is earliest.
