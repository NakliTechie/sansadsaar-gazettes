S.O. 3278(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the 
Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.0.3987(E), dated the 
1१४ September, 2025, namely:-

the entries relating thereto, the following serial numbers and entries shall be inserted, namely:- 


“Al. Haldia (West Bengal)”.
