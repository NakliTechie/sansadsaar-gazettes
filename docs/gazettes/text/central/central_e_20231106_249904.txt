NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th November, 2023 


S.O. 4824(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (2) of section 4 of the Coffee 
Act, 1942 (VII of 1942) read with rule 3 and sub-rule (1) of rule 4 of the Coffee Rules, 1955, the Central Government 
hereby appoints Shri M. J. Dinesh as Chairman of the Coffee Board for a period of three years with effect from the 
date of publication of this notification or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
