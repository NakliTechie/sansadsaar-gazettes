East Cabin (Dhodha Village) up to Khaparadih village, Gati Shakti Multi-Modal Cargo Terminal (District Baloda 
Bazar-Bhatapara)” in the District of Baloda Bazar-Bhatapara in the State of Chhattisgarh.

“Dainik Bhaskar”, dated the 28" June, 2025 and “Hitavada” dated the 28" June, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act;

Khapradih total fifty seven objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent authrity.

submitted its report to the Central Government;

by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose.
