4. The DGFT shall specify the procedure for administering the TRQ and the one-time conversion from the 
Advance Authorisation Scheme to the TRQ Scheme through a Public Notice. 


Effect of this Notification: 
The import policy for raw sugar is amended to allow 10 Lakh MT of duty-free imports under TRQ till 31.10.2026. 


Further, a one-time option is provided for conversion of Advance Authorisations already issued under SION E-52 to 
the TRQ Scheme, in respect of the quantity of Raw Sugar actually imported thereunder up to the date of this 
Notification subject to specified conditions.
