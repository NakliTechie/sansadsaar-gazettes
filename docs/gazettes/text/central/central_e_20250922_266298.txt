MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th September, 2025 


S.O. 4250(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 2171 (8) dated 28" April, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (1) (hereafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intension to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, Y-Connection at Garhi Harsaru to avoid reversal at 
Garhi Harsuru (NEW LINE) (2.50 Km)".in village Wazirpur of Sub Tehsil Harsaru in District Gurugram of 
Haryana State. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in two newspapers “Dainik Jagran” in 
Hindi language on dated the 20" May, 2025 and “The Times of India” in English language on dated the 
20" May, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, two objections were received and the same have been considered, and dis-allowed the same as 
per rules by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the Railways Act, 1989, the Central Government hereby declares that 
the lands specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
