MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th October, 2023 


S.O. 4668(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (2) and (3) of section 10A of the 
Aircraft Act, 1934 (22 of 1934), the Central Government hereby appoints the officers of Bureau of Civil Aviation 
Security specified in column (1) of the Table below, the officers being not below the rank of Deputy Secretary to the 
Government of India or equivalent, to act as designated officers for the purposes of the said Act to adjudge the 
penalty in the manner as specified under rule 66 of the Aircraft (Security) Rules, 2023, published in the Gazette of 
India, vide GSR 596(E), dated the 9" August, 2023, in respect to the corresponding entries of severity level specified 
in column (2) of the said Table:- 


Severity level of contravention or violation or non-compliance (See 
paragraph 2 of Second Schedule of the Aircraft (Security) Rules, 
2023.) 


Deputy Director General in-charge of Level 5 to 6 
Oversight and Compliance Division 


2. In case, any act or omission causes the contravention of more than one severity level, the penalty shall be 
adjudged by the designated officer specified in column (1) corresponding to the highest severity level involved therein 
specified in column (2) of the above table. 


3. In case of vacancy of a designated officer by virtue of any reason but not limited to superannuation, leave and 
the like for the matters of his jurisdiction, the Director General, Bureau of Civil Aviation Security shall by way of an 
order nominate an officer of the same rank or higher rank of Joint Director or the Deputy Director General, as the case 
may be, to act as the designated officer, only for the period such vacancy continues.
