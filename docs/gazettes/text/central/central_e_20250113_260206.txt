S.O. 23(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways,S.O.No. 4734(E) Dated:29/0/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH |46B in the stretch of land from K.m. 0+000 to Km. 47+400 (Nasrullaganj Rehti to Budhni Road) in the 
district of SEHORE in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “HariBhumi” and “The Hitvada” 


both dated 27 November 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received by the competent authority from any person; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government;
