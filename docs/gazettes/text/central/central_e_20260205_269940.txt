WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


New Delhi, the 3rd Febuary, 2026 
F. No. 8-6/Govt./2024-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of the Indian Nursing 


Council Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:-— 


Whereas, the following institutions located in the State of Kerala were recognized by the State Government 


and the Kerala Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct nursing courses.

Health & Family Welfare, Government of Kerala, Room No- 656, South Block, 3rd Floor Government Secretariat 
Building, Thiruvananthapuram-69500l, Kerala. A copy of the notice dated 08//2024 was also sent to the Kerala 
Nurses & Midwives Council. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit its recommendations 
to the Council regarding the suitability of the nursing institutions. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any remarks or recommendation from the State Government or the 
Kerala Nurses & Midwives Council regarding the suitability of the below-mentioned institutions. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. The Indian Nursing Council records show that 
these institutions have failed to apply for statutory inspection for several years and were found deficient/unsuitable for 
conducting nursing programmes.

947, hereby declares that with effect from 29/05/2025, any person holding a recognized qualification from the
