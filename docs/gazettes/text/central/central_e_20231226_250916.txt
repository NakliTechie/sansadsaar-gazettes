S.O. 546l(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.00 428 (E) dated the 5th September, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 25th September, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (l) of section 20 A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railways (Amendment) Act 
2008 (I] of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Nagbhir - Nagpur Gauge Conversion Project (06 Km) 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(including proposed rail connectivity from Umrer BG Yard to existing WCL Yard at Umrer) with electrification in the 
District of Nagpur, Bhandara, Chandrapur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Maharashtra Times” Dated I3th October, 2023 and “DainikManjur” Dated l3th October, 2023 under sub section (4) 
of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority 
accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (.) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette; the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
