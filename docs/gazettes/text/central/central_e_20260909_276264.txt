MINISTRY OF COAL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th September, 2026 


G.S.R. 795(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 13 of the Mines and Minerals (Development 
and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Coal Blocks Allocation Rules, 2017, namely:— 


1.(1) These rules may be called the Coal Blocks Allocation (Second Amendment) Rules, 2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Coal Blocks Allocation Rules, 2017, after rule 17, the following rule shall be inserted, namely: — 


“18. Penalty.— Whoever contravenes the provisions of these rules shall be liable to penalty as per the provisions of 
section 25A and 25B of the Act and the rules made thereunder, that is, the Mines and Minerals Adjudication of Penalties 
Rules, 2026 ”. 


[F. No. PS1/1/2026-PS1 ] 
RUPINDER BRAR, Addl. Secy. 
Note. — The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1), vide notification
