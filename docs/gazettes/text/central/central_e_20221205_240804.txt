PUBLIC NOTICE 

New Delhi , the 30th November , 2022 


[ PART I - SEC.1 ] 


No. 39 / 2015-2020 

Subject : Extension of validity of Pre - Shipment Inspection Agency ( PSIAs ) –reg . 

F. No. 01 / 53 / 8 / AM20 / PSIA - 2 / IC . — In exercise of powers conferred under the paragraph 1.03 
read with paragraph 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy , 2015-20 , the Director General of Foreign Trade in 
relaxation of the provision as in Para 2.55 ( d ) of Handbook of Procedures , 2015-20 notifies as under : 


The validity of recognition of the Pre - Shipment Inspection Agency ( PSIAs ) included in Appendix 
2G of Appendices and Aayat Niryat Forms ( A & ANF ) of Foreign Trade Policy 2015-20 which are 
completing their original tenure of three years as on 03.12.2022 is extended upto 31.12.2022 . 


2. Effects of this public notice : Validity of Pre - Shipment Inspection Agency ( PSIAs ) as listed in the 
Appendix 2G of A & ANF , has been extended from 03.12.2022 to 31.12.2022 .
