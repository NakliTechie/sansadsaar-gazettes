INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 22nd February, 2023 


F. No. 18-18/14601-INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas DVS College of Nursing and Research situated at infront of K V No. 02, Girgaon, 
Maharajpura, Distt. Gwalior - 474020, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh 
and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/14601-INC dated 15/06/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 241, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 15/06/2022 was also enclosed to DVS College of Nursing and Research situated at infront of K V No. 
02, Girgaon, Maharajpura, Distt. Gwalior - 474020, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of DVS College of Nursing and Research, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution DVS College of Nursing and 
Research has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
22/09/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 
declares that with effect from 08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Se(N) from DVS 
College of Nursing and Research situated at in front of K V No. 02, Girgaon, Maharajpura, Distt. Gwalior - 
474020, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the 
orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col. (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
