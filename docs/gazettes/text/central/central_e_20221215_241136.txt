अनुसूची के स्तम्भ (3) की तत्स्थानी प्रविष्टि में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि के सम्बन्ध में अनुसूची के स्तम्भ (2) में उल्लिखित अधिकारी 


को उक्त अधिनियम के अन्तर्गत सक्षम अधिकारी के कुत्यों का निष्पादन करने के लिए WAST सक्षम प्राधिकारी के रूप में 
प्राधिकृत करती है।

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th December, 2022 
S.O. 5874(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways Act, 
1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officer mentioned in column (2) of the Schedule 
annexed here to as the Competent Authority to perform the functions of such authority under the said Act with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land specified in the 
corresponding entry in column (3) of the said Schedule relating to state, districts, taluk and villages mentioned in 


column (4), (5), (6) and (7) respectively of the said Schedule for Construction to 4 laning of (Deoria Bypass) from 


Km. 26.78 to Km. 49.05 of (NH727A) in Deoria District in the State of UTTAR PRADESH.
