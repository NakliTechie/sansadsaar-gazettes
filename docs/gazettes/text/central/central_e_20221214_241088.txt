New Delhi , the 14th December , 2022 

Subject : Corrigendum Notification to Amendment Notification dated 30.09.2022 regarding change in

Notification No. 6 / 16 / 2020 - DGTR dated 19 May , 2021 of anti - dumping investigation 
concerning imports of Phthalic Anhydride ( PAN ) originating in or exported from China 
PR , Indonesia , Korea RP and Thailand . 


F. No. 7 / 06 / 2022 - DGTR.— Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act 1975 , as amended from time 

to time ( hereinafter referred to as " the Act " ) and the Customs Tariff ( Identification , Assessment and 
Collection of Anti - Dumping Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury ) Rules 1995 , as 
amended from time to time ( hereinafter referred to as " the Rules " ) thereof , the Designated Authority has 
issued the Amendment Notification no . 7/06/2022 dated 30.09.2022 in respect of the change in the name of 
producer / exporter Aekyung Petrochemical Co. , Ltd. in the Final Finding Notification No. 6 / 16 / 2020 
DGTR dated 19 May , 2022 of Anti - dumping investigation concerning imports of Phthalic Anhydride 
( PAN ) originating in or exported from China PR , Indonesia , Korea RP and Thailand .
