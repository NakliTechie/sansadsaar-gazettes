5.0. 423(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (North Western Railway, Construction organisation), Notification number, 95.0. 307I(E). dated 
the 0™ July, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 
0 July, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (i) of section 204 of the 
Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (ll of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely “Doubling between Ajmer-Chanderiya section (78.28km)” of Ajmer Division of 
North Western Railway” in District Ajmer in the state of Rajasthan.

And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“Dainik Bhaskar” and “Rajasthan Patrika” Dated 37 July-2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, objections received within the stipulated time period were resolved by Competent 
Authority and accordingly. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare 
that the lands specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, The Central 
Government herby declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified 
in the schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
