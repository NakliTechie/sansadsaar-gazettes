New Delhi, the 6" June, 2025 


F.No. 1-CA (7)/228/2025.—In pursuance of the Regulation 159 (1) of the Chartered Accountants 
Regulations, 1988, the Council of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India is pleased to notify the 
setting up of a Branch of Southern India Regional Council in Khammam city (Telangana) with effect from 
May 27, 2025. 


The Branch shall be known as Khammam Branch of Southern India Regional Council and its 
Headquarters shall be Khammam city (Telangana). The Jurisdiction of the Branch shall besides 
Khammam city, include the following cities/towns falling within a distance of 50 kms from the city limits 
of Khammam: - 


1. Madira 
2. Wyra 
3. Satthpalli 


As prescribed under sub-regulation (3) of Regulation 159, the Branch shall function subject to the control, 
supervision and direction of the Council through Southern India Regional Council and shall carry out such 
directions as may, from time to time, be issued by the Council.
