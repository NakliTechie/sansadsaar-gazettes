NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 24th September, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) 


S.O. 5302(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 85.0 505 (E) dated the 23rd January 2026 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 4th February 2026 and S.O 4399 (E) dated the 77 
August 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), (hereinafter 
referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section () of section 20 (A) of the Railway Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project in state of Maharashtra, district 
Wardha, namely “Proposed construction of ROB & RUB (7 Nos.) in lieu of Level Crossing Gates 
between Nagpur — Itarsi and Nagpur — Ballarshah section of Nagpur Division”. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notifications was published in daily News Papers “Dainik 
Jan-Sangram” Hindi & English on 24" February 2026 and “Dainik Jan-Sangram” Hindi & English on 
20" August 2026 by Deputy Collector Wardha under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in 02 Villages Taluka Seloo no any objections related to the purpose received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
