NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd June, 2023 


G.S.R. 456(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), read with clauses (j) and (q) of 
sub-section (2), of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 ( of 20I0), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 20lI, namely:- 


l. (4) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 20], in rule 6,- 
(a) in sub-rule (l),- 
(i) in clause (a), for the proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted, namely:- 


“Provided that in the case of an electronic product, the package of such product shall declare the name of 
the manufacturer or packer or importer, as the case may be, on the package itself, and such declaration 
shall also inform the consumers to scan the QR code for the address and other related information, if such 
information is declared through the QR Code and not declared on the package itself;’ 


(ii) in clause (b), for the proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Provided that in the case of an electronic product, the package of such product shall inform the 
consumers to scan the QR code for the common or generic name of the commodity and where such 
package contains more than one product, then for the name and number or quantity of each product, if 
such information is declared through the QR Code and not declared on the package itself”;

(iii) in clause (f), for the proviso ,the following proviso shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Provided that in the case of an electronic product, the package of such product shall inform the 
consumers to scan the QR code for the size and dimension of the commodity if such information is 
declared through the QR Code and not declared on the package itself”; 


(b) in sub-rule (2), for the proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Provided that in the case of an electronic product, the package of such product shall declare the telephone 
number and e-mail address on the package itself, and such declaration shall also inform the consumers to 
scan the QR code for other related information if such information is declared through the QR Code and 
not declared on the package itself.”
