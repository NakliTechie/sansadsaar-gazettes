New Delhi, the 28th July, 2023 


S. O. 3412(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 
1961, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and Seventy- 
second Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2 They shall come into force at once. 


2: In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE, under the 
heading “MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING (SOOCHANA AUR PRASARAN 
MANTRALAYA)”,- 


a) for entry “22A. Films and Audio-Visual programmes made available by online content providers.”, the 
following entry shall be substituted, namely: - 


“22A. Films and Audio-Visual programmes/content made available by online content providers/publishers.”’; 
b) after entry 22B, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:- 
“22C. Online advertisements.”. 
DROUPADI MURMU 
PRESIDENT
