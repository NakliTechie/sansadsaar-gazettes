S.O. 3878(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that 
for the public purpose, the land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto, is 
required for execution of Special Railway Project, notification no. 2527(E) dated 06 June 2025, namely, 
“Dhandari Kalan-DDL-Remodelling to increase handling capacity and provision of Shunting neck of 
Northern Railway”, in Ludhiana district in the State of Punjab, hereby declares its intention to acquire such 
land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of 30 days from the date of publication 
of this notification in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid 
purpose under subsection 20 D of the said Act;
