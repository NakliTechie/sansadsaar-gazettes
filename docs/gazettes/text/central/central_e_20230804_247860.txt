PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 4th August, 2023 
No. 24 /2023 
Subject:- Amendment in Para 4.73 (5) of Handbook of Procedures, 2023-reg. 


F. No. 04/94/80/258/AM2/PC-4.— In exercise of the powers conferred under paragraph .03 & 2.04 of 
the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby 
makes the following amendments in Handbook of Procedure, 2023: 


The existing para 4.73(5) of Handbook of Procedures, 2023 reads as under: 
((5) Forevermark NV, Antwerp, Belgium 

The amended para 4.73(5) of Handbook of Procedures, 2023 reads as under: 
(/5) International Institute of Diamond Grading and Research (Belgium), NV 


Effect of this Public Notice: 


Para 4.73({5) of Handbook of Procedures, 2023 stands amended to modify the name of authorized laboratories for 
certification / grading of diamonds. 


SANTOSH KUMAR SARANGEF Director General of Foreign Trade
