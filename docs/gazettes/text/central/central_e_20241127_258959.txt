DEPARTMENT OF ATOMIC ENERGY 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 27th November, 2024 
G.S.R. 733(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 


President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Department of Atomic Energy (Senior Private Secretary, 
Private Secretary (Non-Secretariat) and Stenographer Grade I) Group “B’ posts Recruitment Rules, 2024, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called the Department of Atomic Energy (Senior Private 
Secretary, Private Secretary (Non-Secretariat) and Stenographer Grade I) Group ‘B’ posts Recruitment (Amendment) 
Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Department of Atomic Energy (Senior Private Secretary, Private Secretary (Non-Secretariat) and Stenographer 
Grade I) Group ‘B’ posts Recruitment Rules, 2024, in the Schedule, against Serial Number 1, relating to the post of 
Senior Private Secretary in Note 2, under column (11), for the portion beginning with the words “The marks obtained 
in the objective test or interview” and ending with the words “recommending the names for empanelment.”, following 
shall be substituted, namely:- 


“The marks obtained in the objective test and interview will be taken into consideration among other parameters 
by the Selection Committee while recommending the names for empanelment.”
