New Delhi, the 8th December, 2023 


G.S.R. 898(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (xii) of sub-section (5) of section ] read 
with section 295 of the Income-tax Act, 96] (43 of 96), the Central Board of Direct Taxes hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Income-tax Rules, 962, namely:— 


. Short title and commencement:-() These rules may be called the Income-tax Amendment (Twenty-Eighth 
Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Income-tax Rules, 962, in rule 7C, after clause (ix), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:- 
“(x) investment by way of acquiring units of POWERGRID Infrastructure Investment Trust.”.
