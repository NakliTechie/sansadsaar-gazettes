iii)

illegal. Since, the Respondent /Bank has violated the provisions of section 25F of the Act, the workman is entitled

b) Reinstatement without back wages; & 

c) Lump sum compensation instead of compensation.

Inda is an industry as comprehended under section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 947 (hereinafter referred as 
Act for the sake of brevity). Mr. Sujit Chatterjee was the senior most employee at Bolepur Branch of United Bank of 
India and was posted as Single Window Operator at the material time. On 0/05/20]4, a vacancy arose in the post of 
head cashier at Bolepur Branch of the Bank under Burdwan Regional Office due to retirement of Shri Ashish Kumar 
Das on 30/04/20I4. For the purpose of filling up the permanent vacancy in the post of Head Cashier at Bolepur

branch to the post of Head Cashier. A special pay is attached to the post of Head Cashier as per the bipartite 
settlement governing the service condition of employees. It is contended that on 0I/05/204, Sujit Chatterjee being

Branch Manager of Bolepur Branch issued an office order for entrusting the charge of Head Cashier to the senior

post of Head Cashier to Sujit Chatterjee. He accepted the offer and communicated the same in writing to the authority 
on 8/06/204. Concerned workman performed his assigned job in the capacity of Head Cashier. Branch Manager on 
02/07/204 issued a letter No. BOL/STAFF/HEAD CASHIER/299/20I4-5 inviting application from Sujit Chatterjee 
regarding his willingness for the post of Head Cashier. On the same day, workman submitted application dated 
02/07/204 expressing his willingness. Contention of the union is that Regional Office of the bank at Burdwan issued 
letter dated 08/0I/20I5 addressed to the Chief Manager, DIR, Department, Head Office of the bank for selection to 
the post of Head Cashier, expressing choice of selecting Sujit Chatterjee, seniormost Single Window Operator to the 
post of Head Cashier. However, bank held up confirmation of the workman in the post of Head Cashier. Union 
stepped in and made representation before the bank authorities at different forms pressing for confirmation of Sujit 
Chatterjee to the post. Confirmation of Head Cashier was due on 23/06/20I5 with expiry of six months of probation 
period. It is contended that regional authority of the bank in an arbitrary manner and in violation of the policy of 
selection and posting of Head Cashier, removed Sujit Chatterjee from the charge of Head Cashier and handed over the

4. Being dissatisfied with the illegal decision of the bank, an Industrial Dispute was raised. Conciliation 
proceeding which commenced on 25/08/20I5 at Calcutta was transferred to the office of Assistant Labour 
Commissioner (Central), Durgapur. Further case is that on 02/07/205, Regional Office of the bank transferred Sujit 
Chatterjee from Bolepur Branch to Bolepur-Bhubandanga Branch of the United Bank of India as Single Window

adjudicating the question as to whether the action of the management of United Bank of India in denying promotion 
to Sujit Chatterjee as Head Cashier of Bolepur Branch is legal and justified and whether he is entitled to any relief? 


5. In their statement of claim, union prayed for an order to the effect that denying confirmation of Sujit 
Chatterjee in the post of Head Cashier and posting a junior employee there is void and ab initio. It is further 
contended that the bank acted in an illegal manner in reverting the workman to his earlier post of Single Window 
Operator and prayed for payment of wages attached to the post of Head Cashier with consequential benefits from the

9. I have considered the materials in the case record, evidence adduced by the union witness (WW-I) and 
argument advanced on behalf of the employee’s union of United Bank of India. At the outset it is worthwhile to note 
that there has been a fundamental transformation in the constitution of the employer bank on account of its

amalgamation with Oriental Bank of Commerce and Punjab National Bank w.e.f. 0I/04/2020. Since United Bank of 
India is a component of amalgamation, finality of this adjudication will bind respective parties at the level of their 
present disposition. 


0. Crux of issue for adjudication in this reference is whether the management of the bank acted in a legal and 
justified manner by denying the post of permanent Head Cashier to Sujit Chatterjee at Bolepur Branch of the United 
Bank of India. In the instant case, Sujit Chatterjee has not been examined as witness despite the fact that Mr. Debashis 
Basu Chaudhury, General Secretary of the union on 05/02/209 named Sujit Chatterjee in the list of witness. It is 
therefore, unknown as to whether the person concerned had any personal grievance for not being selected to the post 
of Head Cashier of Bolepur Branch. Union has come up with a plea that Sujit Chatterjee being the seniormost Single 
Window Operator was entitled to the post of Head Cashier at the bank on retirement of Ashish Kumar Das on 
30/04/204. In course of argument, it is submitted by the union representative that the post of Head Cashier is not a 
promotional post but a special pay is attached to such post which is usually given to the seniormost employee as per 
bipartite settlement. It transpires from pleading of the union that by office order No. 33/204 dated 02/07/20I4 
(Exhibit W-6), Sujit Chatterjee was again advised to take the charge of Head Cashier of the branch until further order. 
Arrangements were made by the Branch Manager from time to time deputing various persons to function as Head 
Cashier (In-charge) during leave of Sujit Chatterjee. There was no appointment to the post of Head Cashier on 
permanent basis until 03/07/20I5. From the testimony of WW-I it is gathered that by order dated 02/07/205, Sujit 
Chatterjee along with five others were transferred to other branches of United Bank of India and copy of order is 
produced as Exhibit W-9. Without objection against order of transfer at any point of time, Sujit Chatterjee joined the 
post of Single Window Operator at Bolepur-Bhubandanga Branch of United Bank of India on 03/07/20I5. A judicial 
Notice can be taken of the fact that employment in Banking job is transferrable in nature. Mr. Achintya Kumar 
Mukherjee, union representative fairly submitted that Sujit Chatterjee had completed five years of his posting at 
Bolepur Branch of United Bank of India and his transfer was due. Decision of the management of transferring Sujit 
Chatterjee to Bolepur-Bhubandanga Branch of the bank by order dated 02/07/20]4 therefore, does not suffer from any 
illegality. Once the concerned employee has been transferred to another branch of the bank, he has no locus standi to 
claim any post in his earlier place of work by dint of his seniority in that branch. Exhibit W-l0 is a copy of order 
dated 03/07/20I5 whereby Senior Manager (Admn.) of United Bank of India appointed Smt. Ruby Chakraborty as 
Head Cashier of the Bolepur Branch of United Bank of India. The union has no right to dispute the administrative 
decision taken on 03/07/205 for filling up the post of Head Cashier after transfer of the seniormost staff concerned. 


ll. Under the facts and circumstances discussed above, I have no hesitation to hold that management of United 
Bank of India committed no illegality by appointing Smt. Ruby Chakraborty as Head Cashier of Bolepur Branch on 
03/07/205 and there was no breach of any moral principle in not considering Sujit Chatterjee for the said post when 
he joined his new place of posting. Such decision on part of the management doesn’t amount to deprivation of a 
senior employees to any promotional post which has fallen vacant. Consequently, I hold that there is no merit in the 
Industrial Dispute and the same is dismissed.
