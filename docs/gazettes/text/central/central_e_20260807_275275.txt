MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2026 


5.0. 4369(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O.No. 2919(E) Dated:08/06/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (here in 
after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for for (Widening/Four - Laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH- 146B on the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to K.m.47+400 in (Nasrullaganj-Rehati to Budhni- 
Road) (3DAdditional) Taluka Rehti in the district of Sehore in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Swadesh” Dated 18 June 
2026 and “The Time of India” dated 18 June 2026; both under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
