MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Revenue) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd July, 2024 
No. 33/2024-Customs 


G.S.R. 436(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 25 of the Customs 
Act, 1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, 
hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Revenue) No. 57/2000-Customs, dated the 8" May, 2000, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 413 (B), dated the 8" May, 2000, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the TABLE, against 81. No. 1, in Column (4), for “9.35%” wherever it occurs, 
“4.35%” shall be substituted. 


2. This notification shall come into force on the 24" day of July, 2024. 
[F. No. 334/03/2024-TRU] 
VIKRAM VIJAY WANERE, Under Secy. 


Note : The principal notification No. 57/2000-Customs, dated the 8" May, 2000 was published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 413 (8), dated the 87 May, 2000 and 
was last amended by notification No. 28/2023-Customs, dated 1* April, 2023, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 263 (E), dated the 1* April, 2023.
