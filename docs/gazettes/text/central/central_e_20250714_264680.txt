का.आ. 3209(अ).---1. जबकि केन्द्रीय सरकार की राय है कि कोलकाता महानगर में जोका से बीबीडी बाग मेट्रो 
कॉरिडोर परियोजना के एक भाग के रूप में मोमिनपुर से एस्प्लानेड (मोमिनपुर छोड़कर) तक सार्वजनिक उद्देश्य के लिए 
मेट्रो रेलवे के संनिर्माण तथा उससे संबंधित अन्य कार्यों की सुविधा एवं मेट्रो रेलवे की सुरक्षा के लिए संलग्न अनुसूची में 
उल्लिखित संरेखण के अनुसार कार्य करना आवश्यक/समीचीन है। 
और जबकि केन्द्रीय सरकार इस बात से संतुष्ट है कि भूमि, भवन, गली, सड़क, मार्ग, नाले आदि के स्वामियों या 
अधिभोगियों को होनेवाले असुविधाओं और कठिनाइयों को न्यूनतम करने के समुचित उपाय किए गए हैं तथा योजना में 
दर्शायी गई भूमि, भवन, गली, सड़क, मार्ग, नाले आदि में उपयोगकर्ता के अधिकार का अधिग्रहण मोमिनपुर से एस्प्लानेड 
(मोमिनपुर छोड़कर) मेट्रो रेलवे के निर्माण के लिए अनिवार्य है। 


और जबकि उक्त अनुसूची में उल्लिखित क्षेत्रों के प्लान का निरीक्षण, महाप्रबंधक, मेट्रो रेलवे, कोलकाता या उनके 
नामितियों के कार्यालय में किया जा सकता है।

NOTIFICATION 

Kolkata, the 14th July, 2025 


5.0. 3209(E).— 1. Whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient for 


facilitating the construction or for ensuring the safety of Metro Railway from Mominpur to Esplanade (Excluding 
Mominpur) as a part of the Joka to BBD Bag Metro Corridor project in the city of Kolkata and other works connected 
therewith for public purpose, as per the alignment mentioned in the accompanying Schedule; 


and 


And whereas the Central Government is satisfied that due care has been taken so that minimum inconvenience 
hardships are caused to the owners or occupiers of the land, building, street, road, passage, nullah etc. and the 


acquisition of the right of the user in the land, building, street, road, passage, nullah etc. shown in the plan is inescapable 
for construction of Metro Railway from Mominpur to Esplanade (Excluding Mominpur); 


And whereas the plan of the areas mentioned in the said Schedule may be inspected in the Office of the General 


Manager, Metro Railway, Kolkata or his nominees, 


1.1. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the-powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 21 of the Metro Railways 
(Construction of Works)Act, 1978 (Act 33 of 1978) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government hereby directs under clause (a) of sub-section (1) of Section 21 of the said Act that no building or 
any other construction shall be made or done above the proposed Metro alignment or on any land within the 
20.0m width on either side of center of UP and DN Line as mentioned in the accompanying Schedule, within 04 
(four) years from the date of publication of this notification; 


Every person shall be bound to comply with these directions, as specified in sub-section (5) of section 21 of the 
said Act. 


Any person who sustains any loss or damage in consequence of these directions shall be paid an amount which 
shall be determined by an order of the Competent Authority in the first instance and if the amount determined by 
the Competent Authority is not acceptable to either of the parties, the amount shall, on an appeal preferred by either 
of the parties, within sixty (60) days from the date of the order of the Competent Authority, to the Appellate 
Authority, be determined by an order of the Appellate Authority.
