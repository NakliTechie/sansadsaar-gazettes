NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th March, 2023 


S.O. 225(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the Pension Fund Regulatory and 
Development Authority Act, 203 (23 of 203), the Central Government hereby appoints Shri Deepak Mohanty, 
former Whole-time Member, Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA), to the post of 
Chairperson, PFRDA, with a consolidated salary of Rs. 4.50 lakh per month (without facility of house and car) with 
effect from the date of assumption of charge of the post till attaining the age of 65 years or until further orders, 
whichever is earlier.
