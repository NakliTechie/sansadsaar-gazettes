5.0. 4747(E).— In Exercise of power conferred under clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008, General Manager/East Central Railway has approved to declare “Bakhtiyarpur- 
Rajgir-Tilaiya Doubling (104 Km)” under Danapur Division of East Central Railway as Special Railway 
Project. Therefore “Bakhtiyarpur-Rajgir-Tilatya Doubling (104 Km)” under Danapur Division of East 
Central Railway is declared Special Railway Project in the State of Bihar.
