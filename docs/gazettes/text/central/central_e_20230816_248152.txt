शक्ति का प्रयोग करते हुए, केंद्र सरकार सड़क परिवहन मंत्रालय में भारत सरकार की अधिसूचना में निम्नलिखित संशोधन 
करती है और राजमार्गएस.ओ. क्रमांक 3996 (ई) दिनांक 05 नवंबर 2019 भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग - ll, खंड 
3, उप-खंड (1) में प्रकाशित।

(ए) जिले "पूर्वबर्धभान" में, तालुक/मंडल "मेमारी - |" के तहत गांव "शक्तिगढ़" के नाम के सामने, इसे सुधारा

S.O. 3670(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by clause (a) of Section 3 of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways 5.0. No. 3996 (E) dated the 05 November 
2019 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part — II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii). 


In the said Schedule of the said notification — 


e In the District “Purba Bardhaman”, against name of the village “Shaktigarh” under Taluk/Mandal 
“Memari — I’, shall be corrected & read under Taluk/Mandal “Bardhaman - IT” 


Note: The principal Notification was published vide S.O. No. 3996 (E) dated the 057 November 2019 in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 057 November 2019
