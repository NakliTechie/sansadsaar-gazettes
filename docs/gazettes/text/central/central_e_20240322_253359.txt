NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 19th March, 2024 


S.O. 1529(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S. O. 4520 dated the 27 October, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
section 3 Sub-section (11), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 
20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Level Crossing 
No. 259 for Construction of road under bridge (RUB) Project (For Sub Division Mandawa ) in the district of 
Jhunjhunu in the State of Rajasthan. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Rajasthan 
Patrika” and “Dainik Bhaskar” Dated 7th December, 2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received by the competent Authority within due time; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (i) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
