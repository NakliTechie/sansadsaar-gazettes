NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th December, 2024 


9.0. 5635(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Western Railway, Construction organization), Notification number S.O. 4639 (E) dated 23" October, 2024 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section-3, Sub section (ii) (herein after referred to as the said notification) 
under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act). The Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely, “Kathuwas-Narnaul Doubling Project (24.12 Km)" (for Sub Division- Narnaul in District -Mahendragarh in 
the state of Haryana” 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newpapers “Times of India 
2 and “Dainik Bhaskar” dated 28th October, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, 05 objections were received within the stipulated time period which were heard & disposed of 
by Competent Authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
