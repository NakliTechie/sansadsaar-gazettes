NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, ]9th November, 2024 
S.O. 4984(E)- Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(North Western Railway, Construction organization), Notification number 5.0. 3998 (E) dated 8" September, 
2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section-3, Sub section (ii) (herein after referred to 
as the said notification) under sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (] of 
2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act). The Central Government declared its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, Nathdwara- Devgarh Madariya Gauge 
Conversion Project, in District Rajsamand in the State of Rajasthan. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily local newspapers 
“Rajasthan Patrika” and “Dainik Bhasker” dated 287 September, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A 
of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government;

that the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, The Central Government 
herby declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
