NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the I5th June, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


5.0. 333(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 5.0 777 (E) dated 09" April2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 0‘ April 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under 
sub-section () of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (l] of 
2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Pachora — Jamner with extension up to Malkapur-Narrow Gauge 
to Broad Gauge (84.34 Kms)” in the district of Jalgaon in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for SLAO(I)UTPH.Jalgaonin the daily 
newspapers namely "Dainik Punya Nagari" and "DainikAahwan" on 02-04-2026, and in "DainikDeshonnati" 
and "DainikTarun Bharat" on 26--2025respectivelyunder sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, no objections were received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
