New Delhi, the 30" April, 2025 


S.O. 1945(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 
of the Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948 (34 of 1948), the Central Government hereby 
appoints the Ist day of May, 2025 as the date on which the following provisions of the said Act 
shall come into force in the entire area of the Hingoli and Nandurbar districts in the State of 
Maharashtra, namely: — 


(i) sections 38 to 43; 

(ii) sections 45A to 45H; 

(iii) | sections 46 to 75; 

(iv) sub-section (2) to (4) of section 76; and 
(v) sections 82 and 83.
