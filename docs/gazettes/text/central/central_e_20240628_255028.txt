THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA, NEW DELHI 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th June, 2024 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. PR/323/2016/DD/15/2017/DC/818/2018.—In terms of the provisions of Section 21B(3) of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rule 18(17) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held 
CA. Dhirendra Datta, (Membership No. 080097), 117, Saini Enclave, Vikas Marg, NEW DELHI 110092, guilty 
of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clauses (6) and (7) of Part I of Second Schedule to the 
aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in terms of Rule 19(1) of the aforesaid 
Rules, ordered for removal of name of CA. Dhirendra Datta, (Membership No. 080097) from the Register of 
Members for a period of 01(One) month. In pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified under 
Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 1988, that the name of CA. Dhirendra Datta, (Membership 
No. 080097), shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a period of 01(One) month with effect from 
28 June, 2024. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secretary
