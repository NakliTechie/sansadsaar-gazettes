MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th December, 2025 
S.O. 5843(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.1262(E) dated 197 March 2025, published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 


National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Two lane with 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


paved shoulder/Four lane etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 753M in the 
stretch of land Km 0/000 to km 28/000 Chikhali (Hatni) to Dhad in the District of Buldhana in the state of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.0.1262(E) dated 197 March 2025, has been published 
in “Dainik Matrubhumi” and “Vidarbha Matdar” Both Dated 18" April 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A 
of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
