का.आ. 4329(अ).--केन्द्रीय सरकार ने, राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम, 1956 (1956 का 48) (जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 1035(E) तारीख 28/02/2025, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) F प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा कर्नाटक राज्य के तुमकुर जिले में NH544E के 
किमी 103.200 (पूर्व किमी 103.200) से किमी 116.700 (पूर्व किमी 118.470) (एपी/केएनटी सीमा से सिरा खंड) के 
निर्माण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के 
अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "25/04/2025 को 
“Sa हेराल्ड” , 25/04/2025 को “विजया कर्नाटक” " में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2025 


S.O. 4329(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 1035(E) Dated:28/02/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building ( widening two lane with paved shoulders / four laning etc.), 
Maintenance, management and operation of NH544E in the stretch of land from Km 103.200 (Ex. Km 103.200) to 
Km 116.700 (Ex. Km 118.470) of (AP/KNT Border to Sira section) in the district of TUMKUR in the state of

“Vijaya Karnataka” dated 25/04/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
