New Delhi, the 8th May, 2026 
G.S.R. 348(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 2011(11 of 2011), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: — 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of One thousand years since the first recorded attack on the Somnath Temple and Seventy Five years of its 
reconstruction) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.— The coin of Seventy Five Rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of One thousand years since the first recorded attack on 
the Somnath Temple and Seventy Five years of its reconstruction. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin— The denomination of the coin specified in rule 2 shall conform to the
