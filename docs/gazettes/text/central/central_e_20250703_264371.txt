MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd July, 2025 


S.O. 298I(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 8.0. 376 (E). Dated:06/08/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH752H in the stretch of land from Km. 98.]75 to Km. 02 in the district of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar in the 
state of MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Lokmat” and “Lokmat Time” both dated 
7-Aug-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 
same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
