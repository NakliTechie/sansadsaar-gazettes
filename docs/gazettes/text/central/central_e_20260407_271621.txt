MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(East Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 27th February, 2026 


S.O. 754(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), notification S.O. 257 (E), dated the 05 May, 2025 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub section (l) of section 
20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said act), the Central Government, declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the propose execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project “Hazaribag-Banadag Doubling (6 Km)” as 
Special Railway Project in the State of Jharkhand.” 

And, whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
Prabhat Khabar (Hindi) The Times of India (English), dated the 9th June, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A 
of the said Act, 

And, whereas, no objections was received and the same have been considered/disallowed by the competent 
authority accordingly orders have been passed; 

And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 

And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
