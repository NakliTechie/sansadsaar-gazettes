MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th March, 2023 


S.O. 494(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. No5929(E) dated 9.2.2022, published in the Gazette of India No. 5693, Extraordinary, Part IT, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/Six-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH4 in the stretch of land from Km. 86.65 to Km. 07.09! (Karaipet to Walajahpet section) in the village 
of Kondapuram in the Taluk of Nemili (Erstwhile Arakonam) and in the village of Kadapperi in the Taluk of 
Walajahpet in the District of Ranipet (Erstwhile Vellore District) in the State of TAMIL NADU 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dinakaran” and “The Times of 
India” both dated 27.2.2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection petition was received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग व---खण्ड 3(॥)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 5 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
