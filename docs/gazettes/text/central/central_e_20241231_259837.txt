MINISTRY OF TEXTILES 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 31st December, 2024 


9.0. 5653(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Jute Packaging 
Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 1987 (10 of 1987), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry of Textiles, published in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (ii) vide number S.O. 5459(E), dated the 26" December, 2023, 
namely:- 


In the said Order, in the Preamble, in paragraph 4, for the words, figures and letters “up to the 307 June, 
2024”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 317 March, 2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier” shall be 
substituted. 


[F. No. J-7/2/2023-Jute Section] 
RAJEEV SAXENA, Jt. Secy.
