NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th February, 2024 


9.0. 929(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.47!6 (E), dated the 20th October, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 27th October, 2023, (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Eastern 
Dedicated Freight Corridor in the District of Aligarh in the State of Uttar Pradesh; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“Amar Ujala” and “Dainik Jagran”, dated the |7th November, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, one objection was received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period and the same has been considered and disposed of by the competent authority; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government, - 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified 
in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
