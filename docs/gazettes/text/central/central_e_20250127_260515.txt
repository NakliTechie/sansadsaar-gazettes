MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(Department of Higher Education) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2025 


SO. 452(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (3) of section 5 read with 
clause (c) of sub-section (1) of section 6 of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956 (3 of 1956), the Central 
Government hereby appoints Shri Vineet Joshi, Secretary, Department of Higher Education in the Ministry of 
Education as Member of the University Grants Commission for a term of three years or till such time Shri Vineet 
Joshi occupies the post of Secretary, Department of Higher Education in the Ministry of Education or until further 
orders, whichever is earliest.
