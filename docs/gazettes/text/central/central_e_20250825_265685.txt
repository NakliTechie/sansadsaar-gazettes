MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th August, 2025 


S.O. 3877(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways Published in the Gazete of India, Extraordinary, Part II Section 3, Sub section (ii) issued under sub- 
section (1) of section 3A of the national Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), Vide 
S-O- No. 5239(E) Dated:04/12/2024, 3114(E) Dated:11/07/2025, the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land spacified in the Schedule annexed to the sad notification for Construction of (Widening/two lane with 
paved shoulder/four laning etc.) Maintenance, management and operation of Major bridge on Mandakini River on 
NH76 in the stretch of land from Km. 280.461 to Km. 281.283 in the district of chitrakoot in the state of Uttar 
Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The times of India” and “Amar 
Ujala” both dated "11/12/2024" and “The times of India” and “Amar Ujala” "17/07/2025"; under sub-section 
(3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
