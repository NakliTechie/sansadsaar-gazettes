MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
METRO RAILWAY 


NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 5th August, 2025 


5.0. 3590(E).— WHEREAS, the land, building, street, road, passage or the right of user or easement 
therein, specified in the Schedule, are required for the purpose of construction of New Town Maintenance Depot of 
New Garia— DumDum Airport Metro project (Phase-1 & Phase-2) and other works connected therewith in the 
metropolitan City of Kolkata; 


AND WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that such requirement of additional lands, buildings, 
streets, roads, passages or the right of users or easement therein are required for public purpose; 


AND WHEREAS, the plan bearing drawing number RVNL/KOL/METRO/NEWTOWN DEPOT/1/2025 of 
the area specified in the Schedule, is available for inspection in the office of the Competent Authority at 39/1, Rash 
Bihari Avenue, (1‘t Floor), Kolkata-700026; 


NOW, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 7 of the Metro Railways 
(Construction of Works) Act, 1978 (33 of 1978), the Central Government hereby declares its intention to acquire the 
additional lands, buildings, streets, roads, passages or the right of users or easement therein, as specified in the 
Schedule; 


Any person interested in the said lands, buildings, streets, roads, passages or the right of users or easement 
therein, as mentioned in the Schedule may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of the substance of 
this notification, object under sub-section (1) of section 9 of the said Act to such acquisition. Every objection under 
sub-section (1) shall be made to the Competent Authority in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard either in person or by a legal practitioner 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made, by the Competent Authority under Sub Section (2) of Section 9 of the said Act shall be 
final.
