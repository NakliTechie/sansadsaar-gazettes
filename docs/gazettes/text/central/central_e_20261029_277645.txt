WHEREAS, Bherjan-Borajan-Padumoni Wildlife Sanctuary is located in the Tinsukia District of 
Assam, constituted with three disjunctive forest patches namely, Bherjan, Borajan and Padumoni. The said 
area was declared a Wildlife Sanctuary by the Government of Assam vide Notification No. FRW.47/99/46 
dated 13% October/1999 comprising an area of 7.21 sq. km. Bherjan (1.06 sq. km) and Padumoni (4.39 sq. 
km) are Reserve Forests under Dibrugarh Division while Borajan (1.76 sq. km) is a Reserve Forest under 
Digboi Division. These Reserve Forests were collectively declared as a Wildlife Sanctuary to enhance 
protection and conservation of primate population, such as the Hoolock Gibbon, Assamese Macaque, Pig- 
tailed Macaque, Capped Langur, etc.

(1)The Monitoring Committee shall monitor the compliance of the provisions of this notification.

2006 and are falling in Eco-Sensitive Zone, except for the prohibited activities as specified in the

actual site-specific conditions and referred to the concerned regulatory authority.

section 19 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 against any person who contravenes the

such direction, as it deems fit, to the Monitoring Committee for effective discharge of its functions.
