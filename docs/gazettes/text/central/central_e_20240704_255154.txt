LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd July, 2024 


No. 4/2/CI/2024.—The following paragraph published in the Lok Sabha Bulletin-Part II dated 2 July, 
2024, is hereby published for general information:- 


“No. 22] Committee Branch-I 
Amendment to the Directions by the Speaker, Lok Sabha 
(Tenth Edition) 


In pursuance of Rule 389 of the Rules of Procedure and Conduct of Business in Lok Sabha (Seventeenth 
Edition), the Speaker has made the following amendment to the Directions:- 


DIRECTION 
After clause (2), the following new clause (3) shall be added w.e.f. 28.06.2024, namely: - 


“(3) A Member shall make and subscribe oath or affirmation, as the case may be, in accordance with the form set out 
for the purpose, in the Third Schedule to the Constitution of India and shall not use any word or expression or make 
any remark as a prefix or suffix to the form of oath or affirmation.” 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, Secy. General”
