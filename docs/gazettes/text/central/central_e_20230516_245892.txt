ORDER 
Nagpur, the [20 May, 2023 


S.O. 285(E).—Whereas, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change issued a notification 
S.0.548I(E) dated ३| December, 202] mandating filling of fly ash in the mine voids and mixing of the same to the 
extent of 25% with the external dumps in all working leases located within 300 kms of radius from any Thermal 
Power Plant. 


Whereas, a safety and feasibility study is required to be undertaken for all such operational mines with the 
due permissions from DGMS and other regulatory authorities. 


Therefore, in pursuance to the Rule-58 of Mineral Conservation and Development Rules, 207, it is hereby 
directed to undertake this study within a period of 60 days. It is further directed to submit a copy of the study report 
forthwith to the respective Regional Controller of Mines under whose jurisdiction the lease is located. 


This order shall come into force on the date of its publication in the official gazette.
