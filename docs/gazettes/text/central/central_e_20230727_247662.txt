New Delhi, the 27th July, 2023 


G.S.R. 56(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (k) of sub-section (2) of section 54 of the 
National Commission for Homoeopathy Act, 2020 (5 of 2020), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
tules, to amend the National Commission for Homoeopathy (Submission of List of the Homoeopathy Practitioners ) 
Rules, 202!, namely:— 


. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the National Commission for Homoeopathy 
(Submission of List of the Homoeopathy Practitioners) Amendment Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Commission for Homoeopathy (Submission of List of the Homoeopathy Practitioners) Rules, 202], 
in rule 3, in sub-rule (2), the words “and forward two hard copies of the same by speed post” shall be omitted.
