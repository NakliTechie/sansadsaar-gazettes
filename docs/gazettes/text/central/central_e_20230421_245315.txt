MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th April, 2023 


S.O. 1818(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of the India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas vide Original $.0. No. 3901 (E) Dated 28 October 2020 and Erratum $.O. No. 5409 (E) Dated 21 
November 2022 published in the Gazette of the India Gazette No. 3438 dated 29 October 2020 and Gazette No. 5181 
dated 22 November 2022 Respectively under sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (50 of 1962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that 
notification for the purpose of laying “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project” for the transportation of LPG in 
Tehsil-Handia, District-Prayagraj in the state of Uttar Pradesh by IHB Limited. 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public up to 5 December 
2022 and whereas the Shri Rakesh Kumar Competent Authority has under sub-section (1) of section 6 of the said Act 
submitted report to the Central Government; 


And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline. 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration in THB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


THB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section 10 of the P&MP Act, 1962 and no 
suit, claim or legal proceeding would be against the Central Government on any matter relating to the pipeline.
