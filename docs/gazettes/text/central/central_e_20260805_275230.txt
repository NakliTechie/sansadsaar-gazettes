Republic of Korea and Thailand, and imported into India, the designated authority in its final findings, published in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 1 vide notification No. 7/26/2025-DGTR, dated the qe May, 2026

continued and that the cessation of the anti-dumping duty in force is likely to lead to continuation or recurrence of 
dumping and injury to the domestic industry, and has recommended continued imposition of anti-dumping duty on 


imports of the subject goods originating in or exported from People’s Republic of China and Republic of Korea.
