New Delhi, the |7th September, 2024 


S.O. 4008(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 5(e), read with Section 6 of the Post-Graduate 
Institute of Medical Education & Research, Chandigarh Act, 966 (5] of 966), the Central Government hereby 
nominates Shri Jagat Prakash Nadda, Minister of Health & Family Welfare to be a member of the Post-Graduate 
Institute of Medical Education & Research, Chandigarh in place of Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya and makes the following 
further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare 
No. V-704/2/209-INI-II dated 27.0.2020: 


In the said notification for the serial number 7 and the entries relating thereto, the following serial number 
and entries shall be substituted, namely:- 


“7, Shri Jagat Prakash Nadda, Minister of Health & Family Welfare, New Delhi.”
