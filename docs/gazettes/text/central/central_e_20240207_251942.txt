MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 7th February 2024 


S.O. 570(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.00 792 (E) dated the 30th April 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 30th April 2023 (here in after referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989(24 of 989), the Central Government declares its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion 
(MG to BG) in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar (Hindi) and Lokmat (Hindi)” on !2th May 2023 by Hemant Kumar Bhagoria, Chief Engineer-V, 
Construction Organization Secunderabad under sub section(4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Adgaon, Shivajinagar, Danapur, Soundala, Sonvadi, Hivarkhed, Parsharampur, Talegav 
kh, Malthana kh, Malthana bu, Adgaon bu Villages of akola district [72 Objections received to the Sub Divisional 
Magistrate (Revenue) and Competent Authority Akot (MH) which have been dispose of by filling case. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
