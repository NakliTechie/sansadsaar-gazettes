S.O. 2268(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 15 of the Indian 
Veterinary Council Act, 1984 (52 of 1984), read with sub-rule (5) of rule 10 of the Veterinary Council of 
India (Procedure for recognition and de-recognition of Veterinary Colleges and Veterinary Qualifications) 
Rules, 2017, the Central Government after consulting the Veterinary Council of India (VCI) hereby makes the 
following amendment in the First Schedule to the said Act, namely:— 


In the First Schedule to the Indian Veterinary Council Act, 1984, under sub-heading “DEGREES”,- 
a) serial number 92 shall be renumbered as serial number 9260); 


b) after serial number 92(i) as so renumbered and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be inserted
