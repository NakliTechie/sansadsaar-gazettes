MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Sth January, 2026 


S.O. 76(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number S.O. No. 427] (E), dated the 22™ September 2025, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 
of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ,(widening / two lane with paved shoulder/ four 
laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 353-J in the stretch of land from 
km. 3.000 to km 62.900 in the District of Nagpur in the state of Maharashtra. Hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in "Navrashtra" (Marathi) and 
"Navbharat" (Hindi) both dated 25 October 2025, under sub- section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received from any person by the Competent Authority: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said, Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
