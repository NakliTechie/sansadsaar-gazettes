S.O. 2604(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule 
below, is required for building(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,),maintenance, 
management and operation of Four Lane of Badvel to Nellore section of National Highway-67, 
Greenfield Alignment from Km 642.96 to Km 695.l00 under Package-I & from Km.695.l00 to 
km.706.20 under Package-IT in atmakur division in the district of SPSR Nellore in the state of Andhra 
Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(]) of section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, RDO Atmakur in writing 
and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after
