परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 5448(E) तारीख 11.12.2024 , जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग ॥, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) A प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा आंध्र प्रदेश राज्य के वाई एस आर कडापा जिले में 
NH-67 के कि.मी. 476/000 से कि.मी. 523/500 ( ताड़ीपटरी से मुद्दानुर सेक्शन ) तक के भूखण्ड के निर्माण,(चौड़ा 
करने / चार लेन का बनाने आदि ), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन के लिए उस लिए उस अधिसूचना से 
उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "25.12.2024 " 
को "दा हँस इंडिया (अंग्रेजी)" और "आंध्र प्रभा (तेलुगु) " दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st January, 2025 


9.0. 569(E)— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O 5448 (E) dt:11.12.2024 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Widening / four 
laning etc,) , maintenance , management and operation of National Highway No. 67 in the stretch of land from 
Km 476.000 to Km 523.500 ( Tadipatri to Muddanur Section) in Y.S.R. Kadapa district in the state of Andhra 
Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India (English) ” and 
“Andhra Prabha (Telugu)” both dated 25.12.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received from any person by the competent authority 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
