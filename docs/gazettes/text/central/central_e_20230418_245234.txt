MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st , 2023 


S.O. 1765(E).— Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of the LPG a pipeline should be laid for implementing of “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline 
Project” by the IHB Limited in the State of Gujarat. 


[भाग व---खण्ड 3(॥)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, 
it is necessary to acquire the right of user (ROU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid, and 
which is described in the schedule annexed to this notification. 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (1) of Section 3 of the Petroleum 
and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (50 of 1962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein. 


Any person who is interested in the land described in the said schedule, may submit objection in 
writing to Shri Avalabhai Khatarabhai Baria, Competent Authority and Deputy Collector (Retd.), Kandla Gorakhpur 
LPG Pipeline Project, IHB Limited, Gujarat within twenty-one days from the date on which the copies of this 
notification issued under Sub-section (1) of section 3 of the said Act, as published in the Gazette of India are made 
available to the general public.
