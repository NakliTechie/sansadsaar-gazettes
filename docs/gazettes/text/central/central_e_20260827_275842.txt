A Regulation to provide for a bench of the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir, and 
Ladakh in Ladakh and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. 
In exercise of the powers conferred by article 240 of the Constitution read with 
34 of 209. sub-section (2) of section 58 of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 209, 
the President is pleased to promulgate the following Regulation made by her:— 
I. (J) This Regulation may be called the Union territory of Ladakh (Sitting of 
Bench of the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir, and Ladakh in Ladakh) 
Regulation, 2026. 


(2) It extends to the whole of the Union territory of Ladakh. 


(3) It shall come into force on such date as the Administrator may, by 
notification in the Official Gazette, appoint. 

Explanation—For the purposes of this sub-section, the expression 
“Administrator” means the Administrator of the Union territory of Ladakh 
appointed by the President under article 239 of the Constitution.
