S.0.406 (E).—Inexercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of 
the National Highways ७९०,।956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of 
which is given in the Schedule below, is required for Construction of NewHigh Level Major 
Bridge on Katepurna River at design chainage 433/450 Bridge on Jalna-Pulgaon Section 
NH-753 C in Dist. Washim on EPC in the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its 
intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objections to the use of such land for

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the SDO/LAO 
in the State of Maharashtra, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal 
practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if 
any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either Allow or disallow the 
objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the 
said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be covered under their notification are 
available and can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent 
Authority.
