S.O. 3561(E).—Whereas the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) was established vide the 
Resolution of the Government of India in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers No. 33/7/97-PL.I dated 29th August, 
1997, inter-alia, to fix prices and notify the changes therein, if any, of bulk drugs and formulations, monitor the prices of 
non-scheduled drugs and formulations and oversee the implementation of the provisions of the Drugs (Price Control) 
Order (DPCO). 


2. And whereas the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers vide S.O.1394 (E) dated the 30th May, 2013, in 
exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 and 5 of Essential Commodities Act, 1955 has delegated the powers in 
respect of specified paras of the DPCO, 2013, including para 32 of the said order to be exercised by the NPPA on behalf of 
the Central Government. 


3. And whereas an application received from M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals Limited, for exemption from the 
provisions of DPCO, 2013 under para 32 (i) of the said order in respect of the formulation “Paracetamol Injection 
(Intramuscular) 250me¢/ml, 2ml” which was duly approved by the Office of Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation 
(India) as ‘new drug’ under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules there under. Further, the Patent Office, 
Government of India has granted Patent to M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals Limited for an invention entitled ‘Novel 
Composition Comprising Paracetamol and Process for Preparing the same” for the term of 20 years from 
9"September 2010 in accordance with the provisions of the Patents Act, 1970(Patent No. 411067and Date of Grant: 
09.11.2022). 


4. And whereas the NPPA at its 1157 meeting dated 31.07.2023noted that M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals 
Limited meets the requirement of Para 320) of DPCO 2013 and decided that exemption may be granted to M/s Troikaa 
Pharmaceuticals Limited under Para 320) of DPCO, 2013 for their formulation “Paracetamol Injection 
(Intramuscular) 250mg/ml”. 


5. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers delegated under para 32 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013 
vide S.O. 1394(E) dated 30th May, 2013 issued by the Government of India in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, 
M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals Limited is exempted from the provisions of DPCO, 2013 under para 32 (1) of the said 
order in respect of the formulation “Paracetamol Injection (Intramuscular) 250mg/ml” for a period of five years from 
the date of commencement of its commercial marketing by the manufacturer in the country or expiry of the Indian patent, 
whichever may be earlier. 


6. M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals Limited shall inform NPPA, the date of commercial marketing of the 
formulation “Paracetamol Injection (Intramuscular) 250mg/ml” in the country and the Price to Retailer (PTR) and 
Maximum Retail Price fixed by the company in respect of above said formulation by issuing a price list in Form V under 
DPCO, 2013. 


7. M/s Troikaa Pharmaceuticals Limited shall also seek retail price approval for the formulation “Paracetamol 
Injection (Intramuscular) 250mg/ml”, if applicable, three month before the expiry of the exemption granted under 
Para 320) of DPCO 2013
