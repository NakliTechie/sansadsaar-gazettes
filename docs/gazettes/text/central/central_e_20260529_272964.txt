NATIONAL LEGAL SERVICES AUTHORITY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2026 


F. No. 6(1)95-NALSA.— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3A of the Legal Services 
Authorities Act, 1987 (39 of 1987), Hon’ble the Chief Justice of India is pleased to nominate Hon'ble Smt. Justice 
Bangalore Venkataramiah Nagarathna, Judge, Supreme Court of India, as Chairman of the Supreme Court Legal 
Services Committee with effect from 29.06.2026 and makes the following amendment in its Notification No. S.O.115(E) 
dated 09.02.2000 namely:- 


In the said Notification, for the serial number (1) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be 
substituted namely: - 


1. “SMT. JUSTICE BANGALORE VENKATARAMIAH NAGARATHNA - CHAIRMAN” 
Judge, Supreme Court of India.
