9.0. 5379(E).—WHEREAS consequent upon the resignation of Mr. Justice A.M. Khanwilkar, Chairman of 
the Mahanadi Water Disputes Tribunal, the vacancy of the Chairman has occurred in the Mahanadi Water Disputes 
Tribunal, constituted by the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Water Resources, River Development 
and Ganga Rejuvenation vide notification number S.O. !4(E), dated the [20 March 208 (hereinafter referred to as 
the said notification), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section(ii), dated 2th 
March, 208. 


AND WHEREAS, the Chief Justice of India has nominated Ms. Justice Bela M. Trivedi, Judge, Supreme 
Court of India to fill the said vacancy under section 5A of the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 956 (33 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act); 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendment in the said notification, namely:-
