MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th February, 2024 


S.O. 860(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections () and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (2 of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer 
resources relating to the Information and Office Management System, being Critical Information 
Infrastructure of the National Investigation Agency, and the computer resources of their associated 
dependencies, to be protected systems for the purposes of the said Act and authorises the following persons 
to access the protected systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the National Investigation Agency authorised in writing by the 
National Investigation Agency to access the protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have 
been authorised in writing by the National Investigation Agency for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing 
by the National Investigation Agency on case to case basis. 


2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
