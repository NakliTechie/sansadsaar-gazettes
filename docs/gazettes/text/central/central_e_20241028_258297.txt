MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th October, 2024 
S.O. 4702(E).— WHEREAS the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub- 
section (l), clauses (५) and (xiv) of sub-section (2), and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 
986, issued a notification to declare an Eco-Sensitive Zone around Manjeera Wildlife Sanctuary, 


Telangana in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.0.432 
(E), dated the 05" May 207; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and 


expedient in the public interest to amend the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 


provides that whenever it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may 
dispense with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment 
(Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with 
the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 
986 for amending the notification number S.O. 432 (E), dated the 057 May 207; 


Now, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), clauses (५) and (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) and sub-section(3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read 
with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby 
makes the following amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, 
Forest and Climate Change, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii), vide number S.O. 432 (8), dated the 057 May 207, namely:-
