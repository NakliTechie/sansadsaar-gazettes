NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th December, 2022 


S.O. 5860(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, $.0.524 (E) dated 03.!.2022 published the Gazette of India, Extraordinary Part- I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section () of Section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/six-laning,etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. 7] in the stretch of land from Km. 0.000 to Km. 57.046 (Renigunta- Naidupeta section) in the 
district of Tirupati in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “PrajaSakthi"(Telugu) and “The 
Hindu” (English) both dated 06..2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent Authority;

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
