Mumbai, the 16th April, 2026 


SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUSTS) 
(AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2026 


No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/302.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 30 read 
with Sections 11 and 12 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 (15 of 1992), the Board 
hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Real 
Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, namely: — 


1. These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Real Estate Investment 
Trusts) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026. 


2. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
