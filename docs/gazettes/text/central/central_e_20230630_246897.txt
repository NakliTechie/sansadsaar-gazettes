MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Economic Affairs) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2023 


G.S.R. 472(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 and sub-section (l) and clause (a) of 
sub-section (2) of section 46 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 999 (42 of 999), and in consultation with 
Reserve Bank of India, the Central Government having considered it necessary in the public interest hereby amends 
the Foreign Exchange Management (Current Account Transactions) Rules, 2000, with effect from the 6"" May, 2023, 
namely:- 


2. In the Foreign Exchange Management (Current Account Transactions) Rules, 2000 after rule 6, the 
following rule shall be inserted and shall be deemed to have been inserted with effect from the 6" day of May, 2023 
namely: - 


“7. Use of International Credit Card while outside India.— Nothing contained in rule 5 shall apply to 
the use of International Credit Card for making payment by a person towards meeting expenses while 
such person is on a visit outside India.”’.
