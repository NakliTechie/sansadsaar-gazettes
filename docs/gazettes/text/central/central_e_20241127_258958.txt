DEPARTMENT OF ATOMIC ENERGY 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 27th November, 2024 


G.S.R. 732(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Department of Atomic Energy (Driver Cadre) Recruitment 
Rules, 2024, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement. — () These rules may be called the Department of Atomic Energy 
(Driver Cadre) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2, In the Department of Atomic Energy (Driver Cadre) Recruitment Rules, 2024, in the Schedule, against serial 
number |, relating to the post of Driver (Ordinary Grade), in column (7), under the heading ‘Essential’, for clause (iv), 
the following shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(iv) Consolidated experience of driving a light and heavy vehicle for at least three years”.
