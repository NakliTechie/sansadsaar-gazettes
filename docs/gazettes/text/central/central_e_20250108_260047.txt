MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th January, 2025 
S.O. 44(E).— WHEREAS the Government of India signed an Agreement with International Atomic Energy 


Agency (hereinafter referred to as the Agency) for the Application of Safeguards to Civilian Nuclear Facilities in India 
on 2™ day of February 2009, which came into force on " May, 2009; 


AND WHEREAS the Government of India signed an Agreement on the Privileges and Immunities for the 
Agency (hereinafter referred to as the said agreement) on 2" day of February 2009; 


AND WHEREAS the provisions of the said agreement provide for privileges and immunities to International 
Atomic Energy Agency, to its representatives and officials; 


AND WHEREAS the Agency, its assets, income and other property shall be exempt from customs duties and 
prohibitions and restrictions on imports and exports in respect of articles imported or exported by the Agency for its 
official use as per the provisions of section 8(b) of Article II of the said agreement ; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government considers it expedient to extend the necessary privileges and 
immunities provided under the United Nations (Privileges and Immunities) Act, 947 (46 of 947) to the Agency and 
to its representatives and officials; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the United Nations (Privileges and 
Immunities) Act, 947 (46 of 947), the Central Government hereby declares that the provisions of Articles I to VUI 
of the Schedule to the said Act shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to the International Atomic Energy Agency and to its 
representatives and officials, subject to following modifications, namely:- 


In the Schedule to the said Act, for the words “The United Nations” and “Secretary General” wherever they 
occur, the words “International Atomic Energy Agency” and “Director General” shall respectively be substituted.
