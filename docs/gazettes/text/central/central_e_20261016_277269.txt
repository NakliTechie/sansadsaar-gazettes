EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE CORPORATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th October, 2026 


No. N-12011/1/2023-P&D.— In exercise of the powers conferred by Rule 21 of the Social Security (Central) 
Rules, 2026 read with clause (f) of sub-section (1) of Section 32 of the Code on Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), 
the amount of funeral expenses payable for the purposes of the said clause shall be Rs.20,000/- (Rupees Twenty 
Thousand only). This notification shall come into force with effect from 08th May, 2026.
