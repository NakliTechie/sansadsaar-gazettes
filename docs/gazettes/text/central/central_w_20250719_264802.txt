यूजीसी (सम विश्वविद्यालय संस्था) विनियम, 2023 के अनुसार इसकी विशेषज्ञ समिति द्वारा आवेदन की जांच की गई। विशेषज्ञ समिति ने पाया 
कि क्रिस्टु जयंती, बेंगलुरु, कर्नाटक के एनएएसी प्रत्यायन के दूसरे और तीसरे चक्र में अंतर है, इसलिए, समिति ने यूजीसी विनियम, 2023 के 
तहत सम विश्वविद्यालय संस्था का दर्जा देने की सिफारिश नहीं की। 


4. और आगे जबकि, मंत्रालय ने दिनांक 03.06.2025 के पत्र के माध्यम से संस्था द्वारा पात्रता शर्तों को पूरा करने के संबंध में यूजीसी 
से कुछ स्पष्टीकरण का अनुरोध किया। उत्तर में, यूजीसी ने दिनांक 23.06.2025 के अपने पत्र संख्या 26-5/2023 (सीपीपी-॥/डीयू) के माध्यम से 
सूचित किया कि एनएएसी प्रत्यायन के दो चक्रों के बीच अंतराल अवधि को एनएएसी द्वारा क्रिस्टु जयंती, बेंगलुरु, कर्नाटक के मामले में माफ कर 
दिया गया।

प्रस्तुत किए गए स्पष्टीकरणों पर विचार करते हुए, एतदद्वारा क्रिस्टु जयंती, के नारायणपुरा, कोठानूर पीओ, बेंगलुरु, कर्नाटक को सामान्य श्रेणी 
के तहत एक संस्था के रूप में घोषित करता है। उक्त घोषणा निम्नलिखित शर्तों के अध्यधीन है:-- 


i, free जयंती, बेंगलुरु प्रासंगिक सांविधिक निकाय, जहां भी लागू हो, का अनुमोदन प्राप्त करने से पहले व्यावसायिक पाठ्यक्रमों 
में छात्रों को प्रवेश नहीं देगा।

No. 9-3/2025-U.3(A)—Whereas, the Central Government is empowered under Section 3 of the University Grants 
Commission (UGC) Act, |956 to declare, on the advice of the UGC, an Institution of higher learning as an Institution deemed 
to be University. 


2. And whereas, an online application dated 3.0.2023 was submitted by Bodhi Niketan Trust on UGC’s Portal for 
grant of Institution Deemed to be University status under general Category in the name of Kristu Jayanti, K. Narayanapura, 
Kothanur P O, Bengaluru, Karnataka under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 956. 


3. And whereas, UGC, vide its letter No. 26-5/2023 (CPP-I/DU) dated .04.2025, informed that the application was 
examined by its Expert committee in accordance with the UGC (Institutions deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023. The 
Expert Committee observed that there is a gap of 2nd and 3rd cycle of NAAC Accreditation of Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru, 
Karnataka, hence, the Committee did not recommend for deemed to be University status under the UGC Regulations, 2023. 


4. And further whereas, Ministry, vide letter dated 03.06.2025 requested certain clarification from UGC regarding 
fulfilment of eligibility conditions by the Institution. In response, UGC vide its letter No. 26-5/2023(CPP-I/DU) 
dated 23.06.2025 informed that the gap period between two cycles of NAAC accreditation was condoned by NAAC in case of 
Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru, Karnataka. 


5. Now, therefore, in exercise of powers conferred under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 956, the Ministry of Education, 
considering the clarifications provided by UGC, hereby declares Kristu Jayanti, K. Narayanapura, Kothanur P O, Bengaluru, 
Karnataka as an Institution deemed to be University under general category. The said declaration is subject to the following 
conditions: 


L. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall not admit students in the professional courses before obtaining the approval of 
relevant statutory body, wherever applicable. 


ii. There shall be no diversion of assets or funds/revenues of the Institution deemed to be University/or of its 
constituent teaching units, without prior permission of the UGC and Ministry of Education. 


iii. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall not engage or indulge in any activities that are of commercial and profit making 
in nature. 


iv. The academic programmes to be offered at Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall conform to the norms and standards 
prescribed by the UGC and the Statutory Councils/Bodies concerned. 


v.  Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall align its courses with National Education Policy-2020. 


vi. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall take all the required steps to get all the eligible academic courses/programmes 
rated for valid accreditation by National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and the Institute to get valid accreditation 
by National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), as the case may be, in terms of the provisions as 
contained in the UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023, as amended from time to 
time. 


vii. All the prescribed norms and procedures of the Statutory Councils concerned in the matter of admission of 
students, intake capacity of students, renewal of approval to the academic course / programme, revision of intake 
capacity of students, starting of new courses / programmes, etc. shall continue to be in force, and shall be adhered 
to by Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru. 

viii. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall submit MoA in accordance with UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) 
Regulations, 2023 to UGC. Further, as and when necessary, it shall update or revise or modify its MoA / Rules, 
as per the provisions of the prevailing Regulations. 

ix. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall follow the fee structure as per the Rules and Regulations of the UGC and relevant 
Statutory Councils. 

x.  Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall participate in annual Indian rankings issued by National Institutional Ranking 
Framework (NIRF) of this Ministry. 

xl. Kristu Jayanti, Bengaluru shall compulsorily create Academic Bank of Credits (ABC), identities of their students 
and upload their credit score in digital lockers and ensure that the credit scores are reflected in ABC Portal and 
adopt Samarth e-Gov.
