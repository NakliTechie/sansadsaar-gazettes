the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building upgradation to 2laning with paved shoulders of 
Rampachodavaram to Koyyuru the stretch of land from Km. 43.700 to Km. 3.820 (SUB COLLECTOR, 
RAMPACHODAVARAM) for Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 5l6E in the

Competent Authority;

submitted its report to the Central Government;
