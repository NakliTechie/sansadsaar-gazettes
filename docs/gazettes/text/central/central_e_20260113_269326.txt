MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Northern Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th January, 2026 
S.O. 175(E).— Whereas as by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Construction Organization), number S.O. 4105(E), dated the 10 September, 2025 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 10 September, 2025 and issued under Sub-section (1) of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of 


execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Railway Project, namely, "Connection with doubling of 
Akbarpur-Barabanki (161 Km.) Project" in District-Barabanki in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


Whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, "Dainik 
Jagran" (Hindi) and "Hindustan Times" (English), dated the 13 September, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A 
of the said Act, no objection have been received. 


Whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 

Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
