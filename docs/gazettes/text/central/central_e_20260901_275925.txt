S.O. 4832(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 349(E) Dated: 6/06/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part HI, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of National Highway No. 46 [Bhopal (Ratnagiri Tiraha) to Vidisha (Morikodi) section] in the stretch of land from 
Km. 0.000 to Km. !.900 in the district of BHOPAL in the state of MADHYA PRADESH.
