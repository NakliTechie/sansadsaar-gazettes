CENTRAL ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th September, 2023 


F. No. RA-14026(11)/4/2020-CERC.— Whereas the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms and 
Conditions for Tariff determination from Renewable Energy Sources) Regulations, 2020 (hereinafter referred as “RE 
Tariff Regulations, 2020”) were applicable for the period from 1“ July 2020 to 317 March 2023. 


Whereas the Commission vide notification No.: RA-14026(11)/4/2020-CERC dated 27" March, 2023 
extended the period of applicability of the said regulations till 30" September, 2023. 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers under clause (2) of Regulation 1 of the RE Tariff Regulations,2020 
the Commission hereby further extends the period of applicability of the said regulations till 317 March, 2024.
