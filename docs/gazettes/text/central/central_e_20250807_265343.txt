MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2025 


S.O. 3640(E)— In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 2012 (37 of 2012), 
the Central Government hereby nominates Dr. Chandra Shekhar Mahadeorao Meshram to be member of Institute 
Body of All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bibinagar in place of Dr. George D’souza, and for that purpose makes 
the following amendment in the notification of Government of India, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Number Z- 
28016/170/2018-PMSSY-IV dated the 18" May, 2020 published in the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section 
(ii) vide S.O. 1842(E) dated the 107 June, 2020: 


In the said notification, against serial number 10 and the entry relating thereto, the following to be substituted, namely: - 
10. Dr. Chandra Shekhar Mahadeorao Meshram 


The term of Dr. Chandra Shekhar Mahadeorao Meshram as member of the Institute Body shall be governed by 
the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956. 


[F.No. Z-28016/61/2024-PMSS Y-IV(8301549)] 
ANKITA MISHRA BUNDELA, Jt. Secy.
