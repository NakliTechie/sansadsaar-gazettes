MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l0th June, 2024 


G.S.R. 332(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Medical Termination of Pregnancy 
Act, 97 (34 of 97), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Medical 
Termination of Pregnancy Rules, 2003, namely:— 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the Medical Termination of Pregnancy 
(Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Inthe Medical Termination of Pregnancy Rules, 2003,— 


(a) in rule 3B, in clause (e), for the words “mental retardation”, the words “women with intellectual disability” 
shall be substituted; 


(b) in FORM E, in sub-paragraph (e), for the words “mental retardation”, the words “women with intellectual 
disability” shall be substituted.
