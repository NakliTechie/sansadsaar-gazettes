माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश ऐसी किसी भी गतिविधि में शामिल नहीं होगा जो वाणिज्यिक 
और लाभ कमाने वाली प्रकृति की हो। 


माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश में प्रदान किए जाने वाले शैक्षणिक कार्यक्रम यूजीसी और संबंधित 
सांविधिक परिषदों/निकायों द्वारा निर्धारित मानदंडों और मानकों के अनुरूप होंगे। 


संस्थान केवल वर्तमान में नए उभरते क्षेत्रों तक ही सीमित नहीं रहेगा, अपितु यूजीसी विनियमों/दिशानिर्देशों के साथ-साथ राष्ट्रीय 
शिक्षा नीति-2020 के अनुरूप अन्य क्षेत्रों में भी विस्तार करने का प्रयास करेगा। 


माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश, राष्ट्रीय प्रत्यायन बोर्ड (एनबीए) द्वारा वैध प्रत्यायन हेतु नियत 
सभी पात्र अकादमिक पाठ्यक्रमों/कार्यक्रमों को प्राप्त करने के लिए सभी अपेक्षित कदम उठाएगा और संस्थान समय-समय पर 
यथासंशोधित यूजीसी (सम विश्वविद्यालय संस्थान) विनियम, 2023 में यथा निहित उपबंधों के अनुसार राष्ट्रीय मूल्यांकन और 
प्रत्यायन परिषद (एनएएसी), जैसा भी मामला हो, द्वारा वैध प्रत्यायन प्राप्त करेगा। 

छात्रों के प्रवेश, छात्रों की प्रवेश क्षमता, शैक्षणिक पाठ्यक्रम/कार्यक्रम के अनुमोदन का नवीकरण, छात्रों की प्रवेश क्षमता में 


संशोधन, नए पाठ्यक्रम/कार्यक्रम शुरू करने आदि के मामले में संबंधित सांविधिक परिषदों के सभी निर्धारित मानदंड और 
प्रक्रियाएं प्रभावी रहेंगी और माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश, द्वारा उनका पालन किया जाएगा। 


जब भी आवश्यक हो, संस्थान प्रचलित यूजीसी विनियमों के प्रावधानों के अनुसार अपने एमओए/नियमों को अद्यतन या संशोधित 
करेगा। 


माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश, यूजीसी के नियमों एवं विनियमों और संबंधित सांविधिक परिषदों 
के अनुसार शुल्क संरचना का पालन करेगा। 

माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश इस मंत्रालय के राष्ट्रीय संस्थागत रैंकिंग फ्रेमवर्क (एनआईआरएफ) 
द्वारा जारी वार्षिक भारतीय रैंकिंग में भाग लेगा। 


माधव प्रौद्योगिकी एवं विज्ञान संस्थान, ग्वालियर, मध्य प्रदेश अनिवार्य रूप से एकेडमिक बैंक ऑफ क्रेडिट (एबीसी), अपने छात्रों 
की पहचान बनाएगा और उनके क्रेडिट स्कोर को डिजिटल लॉकर में अपलोड करेगा एवं सुनिश्चित करेगा कि क्रेडिट स्कोर एबीसी

2. Fire Operator, Late Sh. Parveen Kumar, Sh. Bijender and Sh. Manjeet, were working in Delhi Fire Service during 
the year 2023. 


3. On 09.10.2021at 07.16 hrs a call received about the fire incident at H-1433, Industrial area, Bhogarh, Narela, Delhi. 
On receipt of the call, the fire crew rushed to the spot. On arrival, they found that a devastating fire broke out in the factory 
premises which comprise of basement, ground, and two upper floors. A manufacturing disposable items, with huge storage of 
raw materials and finished goods were housed in the factory. Without wasting time, under the command and control of officer 
in-charge, Fire Operator Sh. Parveen Kumar, and two crew members started firefighting operation. During the firefighting, 
they were informed that few workers are trapped inside the building. The ground floor was full of flame and smoke, and 
adjoining factories were also in danger of exposure hazard. It was very difficult to approach the seat of fire. The normal gate 
of the building was inaccessible. In order to gain entry into the building through upper floor,Sh. Parveen along with the two 
crew members went up by using the air cage of Bronto Sky lift. During gaining the access, a blast took place in the building. 
On account of, the intensity of the blast, all crew members got severe burn injuries. They were taken to Raja Harish Chandra 
Hospital by CAT ambulance for first aid treatment and later referred to Sir Ganga Ram Hospital. Due to severe burn injuries, 
they further referred to LNJP hospital. After examination, doctor assesses the burns of Sh. Bijender and Sh. Manjeet as 50% 
2nd and 3rd degree burn. Whereas, Sh. Parveen Kumar got 60% 2nd and 3rd degree burns and declared dead on 27.10.2021 
after 19 days of treatment. Sh. Bijender and Sh. Manjeet were discharged from LNJP hospital on 03.11.2021. They showed 
exemplary courage in the line of their duty and gallant work to save life and property. Sh. Parveen Kumar sacrificed his life 
while discharging the duty. 


4. Fire Operator, Late Sh. Parveen Kumar, Sh. Bijender and Sh. Manjeet, thus displayed conspicuous gallantry, 
exemplary courage, professional expertise and devotion to duty of a very high order. 


5. These awards are made for gallantry under Rule 3 (i) of the Rules governing the award of President’s Fire Service 
Medal for Gallantry and consequently carries with it special allowance admissible under rule 5 (a) w.e.f. 09.10.2021.

New Delhi, the 29th February 2024 


No. 9-4/2023-U.3(A)—Whereas, the Central Government is empowered under Section 3 of the University Grants 
Commission (UGC) Act, 1956 to declare, on the advice of the UGC, an Institution of higher education as an Institution Deemed 
to be University. 


2. And whereas, an online application dated 29.05.2023 was received on the UGC Portal for conferment of Institution 
deemed to be University status under de-novo category to Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya 
Pradesh, under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. 


3. And whereas, while the application was under examination, UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) 
Regulations, 2023 was notified on 02.06.2023. In accordance with Clause 30 of the UGC Regulations, 2023, the Institution 
submitted an undertaking to consider their application in accordance with UGC, Regulations, 2023. 


4. And whereas, the Ministry of Education, on the advice of UGC, issued Letter of Intent (LoI) on 07.12.2023 to 
Scindia Engineering College Society, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh for fulfilment of the following conditions within a period of 
three years before grant of Institution deemed to be University status to Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, 
Madhya Pradesh under the Distinct category: 


1. The proposed Institution deemed to be University shall prepare their MoA and rules as per the UGC Regulations, 
2023. 
ii. | The Sponsoring body shall create and maintain a Corpus Fund of Rupees 25 crore in the name of the proposed 


Institution deemed to be University. 
iii. The proposed Institution deemed to be University shall align the curriculum as per the NEP-2020. 
iv. The proposed Institution deemed to be University shall increase number of regular faculty. 
v. The proposed Institution deemed to be University shall prepare Learning outcomes of all the programs. 


vi. A separate not-for-profit Society/Trust/Company shall be created in the name of proposed deemed to be 
University or Scindia Engineering College Society, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall specifically mention in its 
registration deed (MoA) that it is exclusively for running educational activities and no other activities are being 
carried out or shall be carried out in the future under it. 


vii. The Sponsoring body shall submit revised DPR (Detailed Project Report), Strategic Vision Plan and Rolling 
implementation plan accordingly. 

viii. The Sponsoring body shall submit NoC from the affiliating University as per Clause 6 (2)(a) of UGC 
Regulations, 2023. 


ix. The Sponsoring body shall submit approval of respective Statutory Councils for the existing as well as proposed 
courses. 


x. | The Sponsoring body shall submit Land ownership document in the name of the proposed deemed to be 
University or the Sponsoring body as per Clause 6(2)(d) of the UGC Regulations, 2023. 


xi. The Sponsoring body shall submit an undertaking to the effect that the proposed Institution deemed to be 
University shall abide by all the provisions of the Act, rules made under the said Act and these regulations.

Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall become compliant with the UGC 
(Institutions deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023 within a period of six years from the date of issuance 
of this Notification. 


The entire moveable & immoveable assets shall be in the name of Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, 
Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. 


There shall be no diversion of assets or funds/revenues of the Institution Deemed to be University/or of its 
constituent teaching units, without prior permission of the UGC and Ministry of Education. 


Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall not engage or indulge in any 
activities that are of commercial and profit making in nature. 


The academic programmes to be offered at Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya 
Pradesh shall conform to the norms and standards prescribed by the UGC and the Statutory Councils/Bodies 
concerned. 


The Institute shall not keep confined only to presently new emerging areas but make endeavour to expand in 
other areas in accordance with the UGC Regulations / Guidelines as well as National Education Policy -2020. 


Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall take all the required steps to get 
all the eligible academic courses/programmes rated for valid accreditation by National Board of Accreditation 
(NBA) and the Institute to get valid accreditation by National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), 
as the case may be, in terms of the provisions as contained in the UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) 
Regulations, 2023, as amended from time to time. 


All the prescribed norms and procedures of the Statutory Councils concerned in the matter of admission of 
students, intake capacity of students, renewal of approval to the academic course / programme, revision of intake 
capacity of students, starting of new courses / programmes, etc. shall continue to be in force, and shall be adhered 
to by Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. 


As and when necessary, the Institute shall update or revise or modify its MoA / Rules, as per the provisions of 
the prevailing UGC Regulations. 


Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall follow the fee structure as per the 
Rules and Regulations of the UGC and relevant Statutory Councils. 


Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall participate in annual Indian 
rankings issued by National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) of this Ministry. 


Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh shall compulsorily create Academic 
Bank of Credits (ABC), identities of their students and upload their credit score in digital lockers and ensure 
that the credit scores are reflected in ABC Portal and adopt Samarth e-Gov.
