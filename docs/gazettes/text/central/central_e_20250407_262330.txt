Kolkata, the 7th April, 2025 
S.O. 636(E).— In exercise of Power conferred by sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways Act,l989 
(24 of 989), Chapter IV-A (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the

management and operation of “Illoo- Silli Bypass line avoiding Muri Junction (5.9 Km.)” as Special Railway Project in the 
district of Ranchi in the state of Jharkhand, hereby declares its intension to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of the notification 
in the official Gazette, object, under sub-section (l) of the section 20D of the said Act to the acquisition and use of such land 
for the aforesaid purpose. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, viz. District Land Acquisition Officer, Ranchi, 
Jharkhand, in his office located at Collectorate Building, Ranchi— 83400 (Jharkhand) in writing, and shall set out the grounds 
thereof and competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under Sub-Section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans & the other details of the land covered under notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent Authority.
