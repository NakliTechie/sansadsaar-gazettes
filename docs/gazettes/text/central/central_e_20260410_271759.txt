New Delhi, the 9th April, 2026 

S.O. 1853(E).—Whereas, M/s. Tata Semiconductor Manufacturing Private Limited, has proposed under 
section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a 
Special Economic Zone (SEZ) exclusively for Electronic Hardware and Software including IT/ITES at Plot No. 255 
and 238 (part-2) [part of Survey No. 151, Dholera Village], Industrial Zone in town Planning Scheme 2A, Dholera 
Special Investment Region, Gujarat; 

AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that requirements under sub-section (8) of section 3 
of the said Act, and other related requirements are fulfilled and it has granted letter of approval under sub-section 
(10) of section 3 of the said Act for development, operation and maintenance of the above Special Economic Zone 
on 177 March, 2026;

section 4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 
2006, hereby notifies the 66.166 hectares area comprising the Survey numbers and the area given below in the table,
