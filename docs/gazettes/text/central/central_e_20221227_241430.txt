MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 26th December , 2022 

No. AV - 11012 / 7 / 2020 - A . – In pursuance of the proviso to sub - rule ( 1 ) of rule 78 of the Aircraft Rules , 1937 
and in supersession of the notification No. No. AV - 11012 / 7 / 2020- A dated 20th June , 2022 , published in the Gazette 
of India , Part I , Section I on 22nd June 2022 , the Central Government hereby directs that no person shall operate 
scheduled air transport services to / from Diu aerodrome after 31st December , 2023 unless it has been licensed by the 
Director General of Civil Aviation as required by sub - rule ( 1 ) of rule 78 of the Aircraft Rules , 1937 .
