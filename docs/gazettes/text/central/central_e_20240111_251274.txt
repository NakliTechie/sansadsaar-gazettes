MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th January, 2024 


S.O. 145(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the lands, (the brief description of which has been specified in the Schedule annexed to this 
notification) is required for execution of the Special Railway Project namely, the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor 
in the District of Palghar in the State of Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection, to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose, 
under sub-section (1) of section 20D of the said Act; 


Every objection referred to in the preceding paragraph, shall be made to the competent authority namely, Sub 
Divisional Officer, Vasai, Palghar, Maharashtra in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and such competent 
authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard either in person or by a legal practitioner and may after 
hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary 
by order, either allow or disallow the objections; 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act, shall be 
final; 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
