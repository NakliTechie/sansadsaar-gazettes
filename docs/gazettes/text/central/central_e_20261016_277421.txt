अनुज्ञप्तिधारी के निपादन के मानक) (प्रथम संशोधन) विनियम, 2026 कहा जा सकता है। 


1.2 ये विनियमन आधिकारिक worms में प्रकाशन की तिथि से लागू होंगे। 


1.3. इन विनियमों का विस्तार केंद्र शासित प्रदेश जम्मू और कश्मीर व केंद्र शासित प्रदेश लद्दाख के संपूर्ण क्षेत्र पर होगा।

No. JERC-JKL/Reg/2026/04.— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 57, Section 58, Section 59 
and clause (za) of sub-section (2) of Section 181 of the Electricity Act, 2003 (Act No. 36 of 2003), read with Section 
50 and all other powers enabling it in this behalf, and in pursuance of the Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules, 
2020, as amended from time to time, including the provisions relating to reliability of supply, the Joint Electricity 
Regulatory Commission for the UT of Jammu & Kashmir and the UT of Ladakh hereby makes the following 
Regulations further to amend the Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission for the UT of Jammu & Kashmir and the 
UT of Ladakh (Standard of Performance for the Distribution Licensee) Regulations, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as 
the “Principal Regulations”), namely:— 


1. Short title, extent and Commencement 

1.1 These regulations may be called the Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission for the UT of Jammu & 
Kashmir and the UT of Ladakh (Standard of Performance for the Distribution Licensee) (First Amendment) 
Regulations, 2026. 

1.2 These regulations shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette 

1.3 These Regulations shall extend to the whole of the UT of Jammu & Kashmir and the UT of Ladakh. 


2. Substitution of Regulation 19 of Schedule-II of the Principal Regulations: 
19. The Indices shall be computed for the licensee as a whole by stacking, for each month all the 11kV feeders 


in the supply area, excluding the agricultural feeders, and then aggregating the number and duration of all 
interruptions in that month for each feeder. The Indices would then be computed using the following 
formulae: 


a) SAIFI = ¥,(A; x N,)/N,

3. Insertion of Regulations 20 and 21 in Schedule-II of the Principal Regulations 


After Regulation 19 of Schedule-II of the Principal Regulations, the following Regulations shall be inserted, 
namely:— 


20. The Distribution Licensee shall calculate and submit to the Commission the following parameters of 
reliability of supply, namely: - 


(a) Total duration and frequency of outages per consumer in a year: 

i. System average interruption duration index (SAIDJ); and 

ii. System average interruption frequency index (55171). 

(b) The parameters in the clause (a) above shall be computed and reported: 

i. on a monthly basis, and 

ii. on a cumulative annual (year-to-date) basis, 

in accordance with the methodology specified by the Commission in the regulation 19. 


(c) For the purpose of computation of SAIDI and SAIFI, only interruptions of five (5) minutes or more shall 
be considered (as specified in the regulation 19 above), or such other minimum outage duration as may be 
specified by the Commission by an order, from time to time.

possible, for monitoring and restoring outages within 12 months from the date of notification of the Joint
