G.S.R. 469(E).— WHEREAS the proviso to section 6 of the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 
98] (74 of 98) provides that in relation to any Union territory the Central Pollution Control Board may delegate all 
or any of its powers and functions under this section to such persons as the Central Government may specify; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Pollution Control Board has delegated all its powers and functions 
vested under the said Act in respect of the Union territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands to a Committee 
vide notification number S.O. 39 (E), dated the 6 January, 992 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub- section (ii).

(Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 98] (/4 of 98), and rule 8 of the Air ( Prevention and Control of 
Pollution) (Union Territories) Rules, 983, the Central Government, after consultation with the Andaman and Nicobar 
Administration Pollution Control Committee, hereby declares the following areas as air pollution control area in the 
Union territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands namely :-
