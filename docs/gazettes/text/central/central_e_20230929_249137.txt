MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 


NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 27th September, 2023 


9.0. 4262(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.0 1793 (E) dated the 20th April 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 20th April 2023 (here in after referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section(1) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 1989(24 of 1989) and Railway (amendment) Act 2008 (11 
of 2008) (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion (MG to BG) in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar (Hindi) and Lokmat (Hindi)” on 25th April 2023 by Hemant Kumar Bhagoria, Chief Engineer-V, 
Construction Organization Secunderabad under sub section(4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Jogban, Wadali Satwai, Katkhed, Gyajudin Nagar, Daudpur, Adgaon Khurd and Kemlapur 
villages of Akola District, 43 objections received to the Sub Divisional Magistrate (Revenue) and Competent 
Authority, Akot (M.H.); Which have been disposed of by filing case. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
