SUPREME COURT OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]4th June, 2025 


G.S.R. 385(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by article 45 of the Constitution of 
India and all other enabling provisions in this behalf, the Supreme Court, with the approval of the 
President, hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Supreme Court Rules, 203 
namely:- 


l.(!) These rules may be called the Supreme Court (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 
(2) They shall come into force on the 4" day of July, 2025. 


2. In the Supreme Court Rules, 203, in Order II, for rules | to 3, the following rules shall be 
substituted, namely:- 


"|. Except during partial Court working days and on Saturdays and holidays, the offices of 


the Court, shall, subject to any order by the Chief Justice, be open daily from 0.00 A.M. 
to 5.00 P.M., but no work, unless of an urgent nature, shall be admitted after 4.30 P.M.

2. The offices of the Court shall, except during partial Court working days, be open on 
Saturdays from 0.00 A.M. to .00 P.M., but no work, unless of an urgent nature, shall be 
admitted after 2 Noon. 


3. Except on the days which are holidays both for the Court and the offices of the Court, the 
offices of the Court shall be open during partial Court working days and Christmas and New 
Year holidays of the Court at such times as the Chief Justice may direct".
