of recruitment to the post of Stenographer Grade-I in the Directorate General of Training (except Women Training), 
Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, namely :- 


. Short title and commencement.-(|) These rules may be called the Ministry of Skill Development and 
Entrepreneurship, Directorate General of Training (except Women Training) Stenographer Grade — I (Group ‘B’ 
Post) Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


3. Number of post, classification and level in pay matrix.-The number of the said post, its classification and 
level in the pay of matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the Schedule annexed to these 
rules. 


4 Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc.- The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications and 
other matters relating to the said post shall be as specified in columns (5) to (3) of the said Schedule.
