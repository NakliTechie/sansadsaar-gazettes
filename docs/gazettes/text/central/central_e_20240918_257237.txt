MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Food and Public Distribution) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th September, 2024 


S.O. 3959(E).— In pursuance of the provisions of section 7 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial 
and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 (18 of 2016), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food 
and Public Distribution, Department of Food and Public Distribution, vide number 5.0. 371(E), dated the 8" February, 
2017, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, sub-section (ii), namely :- 


In the said notification,- 


(a) in paragraph 1, in sub-paragraph (2), for the words, figures and letters “by 30" September, 2024”, the words, 
figures and letters “by 317 December, 2024” shall be substituted; 


(b) in paragraph 2, in sub-paragraph (1), for the words, figures and letters “by 30 September, 2024”, the words, 
figures and letters “by 317 December, 2024” shall be substituted. 


[F.No. 9-2/2016-PD.II] 
RAVI SHANAKAR, Jt. Secy. 


Note : The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3,

number S.O. 2236 (E), dated the 11" June, 2024.
