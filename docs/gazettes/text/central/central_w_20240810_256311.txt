O.N. 88.—WHEREAS, the General Election for 04-Tivim Assembly Constituency of Goa, 2022 was held in 
pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/GOA-LA/2022 dated 217 January, 2022; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer, an account of his election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the election for 04-Tivim Assembly Constituency of Goa, was declared by the 
Returning Officer on 10 March, 2022 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election Expenses was 9" 
April, 2022; and 

WHEREAS, as per the report submitted by the District Election Officer, North Goa and forwarded by the 
Chief Electoral Officer, Goa vide their letter no. 5-39-2022/ELEC/765 dated 18" August, 2022, Sh. Godfrey 
D’Lima, a contesting candidate from 04-Tivim Assembly Constituency of Goa, 2022, has failed to lodge account of 
his election expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said reports of the District Election Officer, a Show-Cause notice 
No. 76/GOA-LA/4/2022/CEMS-IV, dated 21" September, 2022 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Godfrey D’Lima, for not lodging of 
account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961, Sh. Godfrey D’Lima, was directed to submit his representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reason for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, North Goa within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, North Goa has submitted his report vide letter No.5-59- 
2021/ELN/Main/810 dated 30™ November, 2023 and the CEO, Goa has forwarded it vide letter No.5-39- 
2022/ELEC/1935 dated 05" February, 2024 informing that the said notice has been received by the candidate on 
30" September, 2022; and 

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, North Goa in his Supplementary Report dated 217 May, 2024 
forwarded by the Chief Electoral Officer, Goa, vide letter no. 4-1-2024/ELEC/1540 dated 247 May, 2024 has 
reported that Sh. Godfrey D’Lima has neither submitted any representation nor any statement of account of election 
expenses even after receipt of the Show Cause Notice dated 217 September, 2022 of the Election Commission of 
India; and 

WHEREAS, after receipt of the said notice, Sh. Godfrey D’Lima has neither furnished any reason nor any 
explanation to the Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the account of election expenses as prescribed 
under law; and 

WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. Godfrey 
D’Lima has failed to lodge account of election expenses and has not furnished any reason or justification for the 
failure to do so; and 

WHEREAS, Section 10 A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that:- 

“Uf the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(० has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or under 
this Act, and 

(9 has no good reason or justification for the failure, 

the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any 

such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.” 

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Sh. Godfrey D’Lima resident of H.N.1064, Thivim Limathord, 
Bardez-Goa-403101, a contesting candidate from 04-Tivim Assembly Constituency of the State of Goa in the 
General Election to the Legislative Assembly, 2022 to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of 
either House of Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a 
period of three years from the date of this order.
