NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist September, 2023 


S.O. 3862(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways, 
number S.O. 4150(E), dated the 05th September 2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii), dated 050 September 2022 and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor Project in the 
district Nuh, in the State of Haryana; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
“Times of India” dated the 08th September 2022, and “Dainik Jagran” dated the 08th September 2022 under sub- 
section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, No objections have been received and the same was considered and resolved as per rule by the 
Competent Authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in the exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
