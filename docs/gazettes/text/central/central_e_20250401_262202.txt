S.O. 554(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (West 
Central Railway), number 4933 8.0. 5329(E) dated l0th December, 2024 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 0 December, 2024 and issued under the sub-section() 
of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) and the Railways (Amendment) ACT, 2008 (No. I] of 2008) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance management and operation of a Special 
Railway Project namely, Kota — Mathura section in Bharatpur District Construction of Road Over Bridge on LC No. 
27 Project in District Bharatpur in the State of Rajasthan; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers namely, 
"THE INDIAN EXPRESS" (English), dated the 22th of December 2024 and "RAJSTHAN PATRIKA" (Hindi) dated 
the 22th of December 2024 under the sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act;

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Central Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
