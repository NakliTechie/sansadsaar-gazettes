New Delhi, the Ist July, 2025 
INCOME-TAX 


S.O. 2954(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (v) of the Explanation to section 48 of the 
Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), Central Board of Direct Taxes, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 3, sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 1790(E), dated the 
5" June, 2017, namely:- 


2: In the said notification, in the Table, after serial number 24 and the entries relating thereto, the following serial 
number and entries shall be inserted, namely: - 

“25 2025-26 376”. 
3. This notification shall come into force on the Ist day of April, 2026 and shall accordingly apply to the 


Assessment Year 2026-27 and subsequent years. 


[Notification No. 70/2025/F.No.370142/24/2025-TPL] 
SAMUEL PITTA, Dy. Secy. (Tax Policy and Legislation) 


Note: - The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, 
sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 1790(E), dated the 5" June, 2017 and was last amended vide notification 
number S.O. 2103(E), dated the 24 May, 2024.
