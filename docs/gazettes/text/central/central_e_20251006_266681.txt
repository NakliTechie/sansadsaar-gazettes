ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th October, 2025 
O. N. 44(E). —Whereas, the President of India has, by notification issued under Section 12 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1951(43 of 1951) and published in the Gazette of India on 06" October, 2025, been 
pleased to call upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir, to 
elect two members to the Council of States to fill the vacancies in that House caused due to the retirement of 
Shri Ghulam Nabi Azad and Shri Nazir Ahmed Laway on 15" February, 2021 on the expiration of their term of 
office; 
Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the said Act, the Election 
Commission of India hereby- 
(A) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 137 October, 2025 (Monday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(0) the 14 October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(०) the 16" October, 2025 (Thursday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 24 October, 2025 (Friday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(०) the 28" October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(B) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election. 

[F.No. 318/CS-J&K/3/2025(1)]
