DOR.HOL.No. S73/6-02-003/2024-25.—Whereas, the Reserve Bank of India has, in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub section (4) of section 44A of the Banking Regulation Act,!949, accorded its sanction, vide 
Order dated March 04, 2024, published on the website of Reserve Bank of India, to the amalgamation of Fincare 
Small Finance Bank Limited into AU Small Finance Bank Limited with effect from April 0, 2024; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub section (6) of section 42 of the 
Reserve Bank of India Act, 934 (2 of 934), the Reserve Bank of India hereby directs the exclusion of “Fincare 
Small Finance Bank Limited” from the Second Schedule to the said Act with effect from April 0l, 2024, as that 
bank ceased to carry on banking business with effect from that date.
