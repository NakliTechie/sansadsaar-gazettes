NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th February, 2025 

S.O. 000(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.O. 4747 (E), dated the 29th October 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 
(48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building of National Highway No. 3!9-A (Two Lane 
/Four Lane with Paved shoulder, etc), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 3l9 A 
(Mohania-Ramgarh-Chausa-Buxar) in the stretch of land from Km. 30+660 to km 65+95 in the District of Buxar in 
the state of Bihar; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Dainik Jagran and Times of India 


dated the I5th November 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 


competent authority;

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
