Mumbai, the 9nd June, 2023 


S.O. 2556(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 5422 (E) dated the 30% November, 2022 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 5 December, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification ) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway 
(Amendment) Act, 2008(11" of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Solapur - Tuljapur - Osmanabad 
New Line in the district of Osmanabad in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik Ekmat dated 
30 November 2022” and “Dainik Kulswamini Sandesh” Dated 1670 December, 2022” under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas in 3 villages, total 36 objections were received and the same have been disposed by the competent 
Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the 
Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub- 
section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further , in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
