New Delhi, the 28th April, 2023 


S.O. 960(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of proposed 
Malout & Dabwali Bypass, National Highway 7 & 9 from Km. 87.75 to Km. 80.2, and Km. 0 to Km. 7 in district 
Sri Muktsar Sahib, NH 9 on the stretch of land from Km. 29.500 to Km. 32.500 in the district muktsar sahib of the 
state of Punjab, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, District Revenue Officer 
Sri Muktsar Sahib in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector 
an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
