IV कार्य 


हिंदी सलाहकार समिति का कार्य मंत्रालय और इसके संबद्ध तथा अधीनस्थ कार्यालयों में राजभाषा संबंधी संवैधानिक 
प्रावधानों, राजभाषा अधिनियम व नियमों के उपबंधों, केंद्रीय हिंदी समिति के निर्णयों तथा गृह मंत्रालय (राजभाषा 
विभाग) द्वारा जारी किए गए निदेशों/अनुदेशों के कार्यान्वयन और संबंधित मंत्रालय/विभाग के कामकाज में हिंदी के प्रगामी 
प्रयोग को बढ़ाने के बारे में सलाह देना है। यदि हिंदी सलाहकार समिति राजभाषा नीति या उसके संबंध में जारी किए गए 
किसी निर्देश/अनुदेश में परिवर्तन करने का सुझाव देती है, तो ऐसे सुझावों पर राजभाषा विभाग की सहमति से ही अमल 
किया जा सकेगा। 


४ कार्य-अवधि 
1. समिति का कार्यकाल उसके गठन की तारीख से सामान्यत: 3 वर्ष का होगा। 


2. समिति के कार्यकाल के दौरान, यदि किसी व्यक्ति को समिति का सदस्य नामित किया जाता है तो वह उस समिति के 
कार्यकाल की शेष अवधि के लिए ही सदस्य होगा। 


3. विशेष परिस्थितियों में मंत्रालय द्वारा समिति का कार्यकाल कम या ज्यादा किया जा सकता है। 


4. जो संसद सदस्य समिति के सदस्य हैं, वे संसद सदस्य न रहने पर इस समिति के सदस्य नहीं रहेंगे।

1७ FUNCTIONS 


The function of the Hindi Salahkar Samiti is to advice the Ministry, its attached and Subordinate offices on 
implementation of the constitutional provisions relating to official language, provisions of the Official Languages Act 
and Rules, decisions of the Central Hindi Committee and directions/instructions issued by the Ministry of Home 
Affairs (Department of Official Language) and to promote progressive use of Hindi in the working of the 
Ministry/Department concemed. If the Hindi Salahkar Samiti suggests changes in the Official Language Policy or any 
Directions/instructions issued in connection therewith, then compliance on such advice can be made only with the 
consent of the Department of Official Language.

V TENURE 
1. The tenure of the Samiti will be ordinarily for 3 years from the date of its constitution. 


2. Ifa person is nominated as member in the middle of the term, he/she will hold the office for the residual term only 
of the Samiti. 


3. In special circumstances, the tenure of the Samiti may be curtailed or enhanced by the Ministry. 


4. Members of Parliament who are the members of Committee shall cease to be members as soon as they cease to be 
Member of Parliament. 


5. Ex-officio members of the Samiti shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue of which they 
are members of the Samiti. 


VI GENERAL 


The Headquarters of the Samiti shall be at New Delhi. All correspondences related to the Samiti will be addressed to 
E-Mail msmehindi@gmail.com 


शा 1.4. AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


(a) The Members of Parliament nominated in the Samiti will be paid Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance as 
per the provisions in the “Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowance & Pension) Act, 1954” and amendments issued 
from time to time and rules made thereunder vide Department of Official Language Office Memorandum No. 
II/20034/04/2005-O.L (Policy-2), dated 03.02.2006 for participating in meetings of the Samiti. 


(b) Keeping in view the high level status of the Committee, Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance to other non- 
official members of the Samiti will be paid as per the guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language 
O.M. No.I/22034/04/86-OL(A-2) dated 22 January,1987 and in accordance with the revised/prescribed rates and 
tules as amended from time to time by the Government of India.
