INCOME-TAX 


S.O. 2189(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (c) of clause (i) of sub-section (3) of 
section 194A of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Central Government hereby notifies that the Scheme 
namely the Mahila Samman Savings Certificate, 2023, made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A of the 
Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 (5 of 1873) and notified vide notification number G.S.R. 237(E) dated the 
31° March, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (i), shall be a 
Scheme framed for the purposes of said sub-clause. 


2, This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
