G.S.R. 208(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution 
and in supersession of the Department of Biotechnology, (Group ‘A’ Scientific Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2018, 
except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the 
following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the Scientific posts in the Department of Biotechnology, 
namely:- 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 9 


1. 


Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Science and Technology, 
Department of Biotechnology (Group ‘A’ Scientific posts) Recruitment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
Definitions.- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, - 
(a) “cadre controlling authority” means the Secretary in the Department of Biotechnology; 


(b) “candidate” means a person who is eligible for consideration for any post specified in the First 
Schedule; 


(c) “competent authority” means the authority specified by the Central Government as such, from time to 
time; 

(d) “Department” means the Department of Biotechnology; 

(e) “post” means a Scientific post as defined in the Scheme; 

(f) “Schedule” means a Schedule appended to these rules; and 


(g) “Scheme” means the Revised Flexible Complementing Scheme as issued by the Department of 
Personnel and Training, vide O.M. No. AB-14017/41/2013-PP(RR) dated the 13th March, 2024; 


Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix.- The number of the said posts, their classification 
and the level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in the First Schedule. 


Educational qualifications, experience, method of recruitment, etc.- (1) The educational qualification 
for the post shall be as specified in Part-I of the Second Schedule and specific qualification(s), as may be 
required for the post concerned shall be decided by the cadre controlling authority and indicated in the 
advertisement. 


(2) The candidate for the post of Scientist ‘H’ shall have the experience in research and development in 
industrial or academic institutions or an organisation of science and technology as specified in Part-II of the 
Second Schedule. 


(3) (a) The recruitment for the post of Scientist ‘C’ shall be made by direct recruitment. 


(b) The recruitment for the post of Scientist H, shall be made either by direct recruitment or on 
deputation or absorption basis. 


(c) The cadre controlling authority shall decide the particular method of appointment for each vacancy 
of Scientist H before advertising the post and shall be indicated in the advertisement. 


(d) The recruitment for the posts of Scientists ‘D’, ‘E’, ‘F’ and ‘G’ shall be made by promotion or 
upgradation under the Scheme on completion of minimum residency period in the existing grade as 
specified in sub-rule (2) of rule 8. 


(4) Any recruitment made by the method specified in sub-rule (3) shall be made on the recommendation of 
the Committees referred to in rule 9. 


(5) The committees referred to in sub-rule (4) shall make their recommendations on the basis of interview 
held with candidates for all posts filled under method of recruitment under clauses (a) and (b) of sub-rule (3) 
and on the basis of interview and evaluation of past performance of scientists in the Department for all posts 
filled by the method of recruitment under clause (d) of sub-rule (3): 


Provided that for making recruitment to the posts, the committee referred to in sub-rule (4) shall 
comply with the methodology of assessment or selection [including the number of candidates to be called 
against vacancies advertised in terms of sub-rules (1) and (2)] which has been duly approved by the 
competent authority before making advertisement for recruitment for the post. 


Age-limit and nationality.- (1) The upper age-limit for direct recruitment shall be as specified in column 
(4) of Part-II of the Second Schedule. 


(2)The upper age-limit for post in Scientist ‘H’ to be filled on deputation basis shall be fifty-eight years. 
(3)The crucial date for determining the age-limit shall be the closing date for receipt of applications from 
candidates against the advertisement. 


(4)Only an Indian citizen shall be eligible for appointment against the posts. 


Direct recruitment.- (1) The candidate possessing the educational qualification and experience as specified 
in the Second Schedule shall be eligible for appointment by direct recruitment in the manner specified in 
sub-rule (5) of rule 4, on the recommendations of the committee referred to in sub-rule (4) of rule 4.

(2)Every officer on appointment to the posts of Scientists ‘C’ and ‘H’ on direct recruitment basis shall be on 
probation for a period of one year. 


Provided that the cadre controlling authority may extend the period of probation in accordance with the 
instructions or orders issued by the Central Government from time to time in this regard. 


(3)The confirmation of the candidate appointed on probation under sub-rule (2) shall be considered by the 
Departmental Confirmation Committee specified in sub-rule (7) of rule 9. 


Deputation (including short-term contract)/absorption.- (1) The candidate from other Ministries or 
Departments of the Central Government or the State Governments or Union territories or Central 
Universities or State Universities or public sector undertakings or autonomous statutory bodies or 
institutions may be appointed on deputation basis for the posts specified in clauses (b) and (c) of sub-rule 
(3) of rule 4 in the manner specified in sub-rules (4) and (5) of rule 4 subject to the fulfillment of the 
following conditions, namely:- 


(a) the candidate possesses minimum educational qualification and experience as specified in the Second 
Schedule; 


(b) the candidate holds a post analogous to the post applied for or has worked for at least five years on 
the immediate lower post on regular basis in the parent cadre or Department; 


(c) the upper age-limit for candidates recommended for appointment on deputation shall be as per the 
provisions of sub-rule (2) of rule 5. 


(2)The period of deputation including the period of deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately 
preceding the present appointment on deputation in the same or some other organisation or department of 
the Central Government, shall ordinarily be five years. 


(3)The vacancies caused by the incumbents being away on deputation or long illness or study leave or under 
other circumstances for a duration of one year or more may be filled on deputation basis from officers of the 
Central Government possessing the educational qualifications specified for direct recruitment. 


(4)The candidate from the Central Government or the State Government or Union territory shall be eligible 
for absorption under clause (b) and (c) of sub-rule (3) of rule 4 in the manner specified in sub-rules (4) and 
(5) of rule 4. 


Promotion through the Revised Flexible Complementing Scheme.-(1) Promotion or upgradation of 
Scientists to the grade of Scientist ‘D' upto Scientist 'G' shall be as specified under the Scheme.

the stage where the Scientist failed to qualify.
