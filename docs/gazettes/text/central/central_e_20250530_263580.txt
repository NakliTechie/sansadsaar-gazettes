परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 2441(अ) तारीख 24/06/2024, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग ॥, खण्ड 3, उप-खंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा तेलंगाना राज्य के नगरकुरनूल जिले में 
कलवाकुरती-कोल्लापुर सेक्शन के NH-167K के कि.मी. 20.450 से कि.मी. 46.130 (रेवेन्यु डिविजनल ऑफ़िसर, 
नगरकुरनूल) तक के भूखण्ड पर टोल प्लाजा के निर्माण, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से 
उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी; 

और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उप-धारा (3) के अधीन तारीख 
"13 अगस्त 2024" को "द हिंदू (अंग्रेजी)" और "TT (तेलुगु)" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था; 

जबकि सक्षम प्राधिकारी को धारा 3-ग के तहत दायर आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई है, जिस पर विचार किया गया है और 
उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th May, 2025 


S.O. 2398(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 2441(E) Dated: 24/06/2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under Sub-section (1) of Section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
of Kalwakurthy-Kollapur section of NH-167K in the stretch of land from Km. 20.450 to Km. 46.130 
(Revenue Divisional Officer, Nagarkurnool) in the district of NAGARKURNOOL in the state of TELANGANA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “THE HINDU (English)” and 
“VELUGU (Telugu)” both dated 13 August, 2024; under Sub-section (3) of Section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of Sub-section (1) of Section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the Sub-section (1) of Section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of Sub-section (2) of Section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
