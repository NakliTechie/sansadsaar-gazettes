New Delhi, the 28th February, 2025 

S.O. 046(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 426(E) Dated:5/03/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-

referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building(widening/four laning etc), Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH367 in the stretch of land from Km. 33.00 to Km. 55.49 (Shirur cross to Gaddankeri section) in the district 
of BAGALKOT in the state of KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Daily kannada news paper 
Vijayavani” and “Deccan Hearald” both dated 03-05-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately;

conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land
