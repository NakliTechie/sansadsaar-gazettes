NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad,, the 12th December 2024 


S.O. 389(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 
89 at Km. 165/30-32 in between Makudi -Wirur stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of 
land in the State of Maharashtra in the District of Chandrapur for providing National Infrastructure for a 
public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
