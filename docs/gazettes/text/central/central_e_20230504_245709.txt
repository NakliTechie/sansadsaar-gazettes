S.O. 2122(E)— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 


(Railway Board), number, S.O 5422 (E) dated the 30% November, 2022 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 570 December, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification ) and issued under sun-section (1) of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 


annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 5 


Railway Project, namely Solapur - Tuljapur - Osmanabad New Line in the district of Osmanabad in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Ekmat dated 30 November 2022” and “Dainik Kulswamini Sandesh Dated 16 December, 2022” under sub 
section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in 2 villages 8 No.s of objections were received and the same have been disposed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 


report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 


the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further , in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 


hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
