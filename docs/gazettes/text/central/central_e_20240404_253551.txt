New Delhi, the 3rd April, 2024 


S.O. 64(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 
96], namely:- 


l. (I) These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and 
Seventy-sixth Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force at once. 


2. In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 96!, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE, under the 
heading “MINISTRY OF FISHERIES, ANIMAL HUSBANDRY AND DAIRYING (MATSYAPALAN, 
PASHUPALAN AUR DAIRY MANTRYALAYA)”, under the sub-heading “A. DEPARTMENT OF FISHERIES 
(MATSYAPALAN VIBHAG)”, after entry 8, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:- 


“8A. Promotion of Seaweeds cultivation and associated activities, industrial applications, marketing, import 
including exotics, quarantine, etc.”. 


Droupadi Murmu, 


PRESIDENT
