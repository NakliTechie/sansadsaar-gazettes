NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th June, 2023 


S.O. 2699(E). — In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (d) of section 2 of the Land Ports Authority of 
India Act, 2010 (31 of 2010), the Central Government hereby specifies the land port at Petrapole, located in Tehsil 
Petrapole, District 24 Parganas, West Bengal, India, Pin Code — 743405 as integrated check post measuring 100.78 
acres of land along the National Highway number 112 up to the Zero point on the International Border with 
Bangladesh as follows, namely: — 


(a) North East : 23°02°19”N and 88° 53’03” E; 
(b) North West : 23° 01°50”N and 88° 52717” E; 
(c) South East : 23° 01°56”N and 88° 52’46” E; 
(d) South West : 23° 02°14”N and 88° 52733” E. 
2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
