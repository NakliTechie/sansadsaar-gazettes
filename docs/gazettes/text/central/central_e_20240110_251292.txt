ऐसी प्रत्येक आपत्ति, सक्षम प्राधिकारी, अर्थात भूमि अधिग्रहण के सक्षम अधिकारी (देहरादून) को लिखित रूप में प्रस्तुत की

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l0th January, 2024 


S.O. 33(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of State Highway 
24 (New NH 07) in the stretch of land from km. 0.000 to km. 20.620 (Spur Bhaniyawala - Jollygrant - Rishikesh) in 
the district of Dehradun in the State of Uttarakhand; hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


[भाग ॥---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Competent Authorityof Land 
Acquisition (Dehradun) in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the 
objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
