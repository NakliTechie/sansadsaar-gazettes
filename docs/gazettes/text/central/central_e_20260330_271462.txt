New Delhi, the 30th March, 2026 


S.O. 652(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 598(E) Dated:02/04/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ( widening two lane with paved shoulders / four laning etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No - l69A in the stretch of land from 5.600 to 
Km. 76.200 (Hebri to Parkala) and from Km. 85.200 to Km 88.900 (Karavali Junction to Malpe) (Thirthahalli - Udupi 
section); in the district of UDUPI in the state of KARNATAKA;

“Udayavani” dated [2/04/2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
