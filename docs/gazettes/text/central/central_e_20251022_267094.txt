S.O. 4793(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways 
Act, 1956(48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of

under the said Act with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in 
respect of the stretch of land from Km. 19.2 to Km. 95.65 of the NH32 for building (widening / four-
