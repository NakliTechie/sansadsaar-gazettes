का.आ. 620 ( अ ) . – केन्द्रीय सरकार ने , राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का . आ . जो भारत के राजपत्र , असाधारण , भाग II , खण्ड 3 , 
उपखंड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , द्वारा बिहार राज्य के भागलपुर जिले में NH80 के कि.मी. 132.53 से 
कि.मी .132.685 ( डिस्ट्रिक्ट लैंड एक्कीजीशन ऑफिस कोर्ट कंपाउंड , कलेक्टेरियट भागलपुर ) तक के भूखण्ड पर टोल प्लाजा 
के निर्माण , अनुरक्षण , प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने 
के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3 क की उप - धारा ( 3 ) के अधीन तारीख 07.12.2022 को 
" प्रभात खबर " एवं दिनांक 19.01.2023 को " दैनिक जागरण में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

दिया है ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 8th February , 2023 

S.O. 620 ( E ) .— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways ,, published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub 
section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance , management and operation of NH80 on the stretch of land 
from Km . 132.53 to Km . 132.685 ( District land acquisition office court compound , collecteriate Bhagalpur ) in the 
district of BHAGALPUR in the state of BIHAR ; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Prabhat Khabar " dated 07.12.2022 
and " Dainik Jagran " dated 19.01.2023 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
