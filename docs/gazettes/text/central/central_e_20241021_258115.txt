MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Central Railway (Construction Organization) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
PRAYAGRAJ, the 9th October, 2024 


S.O. 4593(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of Section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989) & Railway Amendment Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government after being satisfied that for the public purpose the land situated at District Bulandshahr of the state of 
Uttar Pradesh, the brief description of which has given in the Schedule annexed hereto is required for the purpose of 
execution of the North Central Railway's Special Railway project, namely Raising of Speed to 160kmph/200kmph 
on existing New Delhi-Howrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in the district of Bulandshahr in the State of Uttar 
Pradesh, for Sub Sectioning and Paralleling Post hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Newspapers, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under 
Section 20D sub- section (1) of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely, Additional District Magistrate (E) 
Distt. Bulandshahr Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, 
either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of Section 20D of the said Act shall be final 
u/s 20D sub section (3). 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent Authority/Additional District Magistrate (E) Distt. 
Bulandshahr.
