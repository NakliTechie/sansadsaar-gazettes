INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 

WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F.No. 18-29 / 2705 - INC : – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Lakshmi Ammal School of Nursing situated at No. 17A , Varathanar Street , Chengalpattu , 
Distt . Kancheepuram – 603001 , Tamilnadu is recognized by State Government of Tamilnadu and Tamilnadu 
Nurses and Midwives Council to conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-29 / 2705 - INC / 17647 dated 22/03/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Secretary ( Health & FW ) , Department of Health and Family 
Welfare , Government of Tamilnadu , 04th Floor , Fort St. George , Secretariat , Chennai 600009 , Tamilnadu was 
issued . Copy of the notice dated 22/03/2021 was also enclosed to Lakshmi Ammal School of Nursing situated No. 
17A , Varathanar Street , Chengalpattu , Distt . Kancheepuram – 603001 , Tamilnadu . In the notice issued , the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Lakshmi Ammal School of Nursing . Even the institution Lakshmi Ammal School of Nursing has not responded 
to the notices issued . 


Whereas , Indian Nursing Council issued a letter dated 04/09/2020 informing the institution that institution 
has been considered as closed for GNM due to non - admission since two ( 2 ) years . Non - admission would ipso facto 
mean that the institution does not possess the requisite Teaching , Clinical or infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Lakshmi Ammal School of Nursing 
situated at No. 17A , Varathanar Street , Chengalpattu , Distt . Kancheepuram - 603001 , Tamilnadu shall be 
entitled to be registered only in the State of Tamilnadu subject to orders of the State government of Tamilnadu .
