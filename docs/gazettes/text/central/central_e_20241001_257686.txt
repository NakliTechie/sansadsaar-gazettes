New Delhi, the lst October, 2024 


S.O. 4306(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry 
of Road Transport and Highways, 2728-E Dated:2/07/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of NH325 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 26.8 to Km. 73.6 (SDO OFFICE SIWANA) in the district of BARMER in the 
state of RAJASTHAN 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Rajasthan

Patrika” dated 8,07.2024 and “Dainik Bhaskar” dated 7.07.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A 
of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, 
considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the 
said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
