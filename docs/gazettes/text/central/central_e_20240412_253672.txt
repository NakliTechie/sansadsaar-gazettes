MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the [20 April, 2024 


S.O. 708(E).—Whereas the Central Government is satisfied that public interest so requires that the services 
engaged in the Iron Ore Mining, which is covered under item 6 of the First Schedule to the Industrial Disputes Act, 
947 (4 of 947), to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act; 


And whereas, the Central Government has declared the said industry to be public utility service for the 
purposes of the said Act for a period of six months with effect from the 47 October, 2023 vide notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and Employment number S.O. 4424 (5), dated the !!" October, 2023; 


And whereas, the Central Government is of the opinion that public interest requires the extension of the 
status of the said industry as public utility service for a further period of six months; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to sub-clause (vi) of clause (n) of section 
2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 947 (4 of 947), the Central Government hereby issues the following order as 
follows: - 


l. Short title and Commencement. - (|) This order may be called the Public Utility Services (First Order) 2024. 
(2) It shall come into force on the 4" day of April, 2024. 


2. The Central Government hereby declares the services engaged in the Iron Ore Mining to be a public utility 
service for the purposes of the said Act for a further period of six months with effect from 4" April, 2024.
