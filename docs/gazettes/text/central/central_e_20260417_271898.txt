S.O. 932(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways 0०, 989

Any person interested in the said land may, within Thirty days from the date of publication of this notification

Bangalore and Regional Commissioner, Bengaluru for “Construction of RUB in lieu of LC No.l0 at Km: 6/200 
(Soladevanahalli village)” in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the 
objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections:
