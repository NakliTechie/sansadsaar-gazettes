मेमौजूदा नई दिल्‍ली-हावडा (कानपुर-लखनऊ सहित), मार्ग पर गतिसीमा को 160 किमी ० प्रति घण्टा/200 किमी ० प्रति

5.0. 2708(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) ofSection 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989)& Railway Amendment Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government after beingsatisfied that for the public purpose the land situated at District Hathras of the state ofUttar 
Pradesh, the brief description of which has given in the Schedule annexed hereto isrequired for the purpose of 
execution of the North Central Railway'sSpecial Railway projectnamelyRaising of Speed to 160kmph/200kmph on 
existing New Delhi-Howrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in the district of Hathras in the State of Uttar Pradesh, 
forSub Sectioning and Paralleling Post hereby declares itsintention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from thedate of publication of this notification 
in the Newspapers, raise objection to the acquisitionand use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under Section 20D 
sub- section (1) of the saidAct. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely, Sub Divisional Magistrate, 
Sasni/SLAO Hathras Uttar Pradesh in writing and shallset out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector anopportunity of being heard either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections.
