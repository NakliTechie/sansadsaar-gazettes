G.S.R. 409(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20i(! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement.— (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of Golden Jubilee of Dredging Corporation of India Limited) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.— The coin of Fifty Rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for issue under the 
authority of the Central Government to commemorate the occasion of Golden Jubilee of Dredging Corporation of India 
Limited. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.— The denomination of the coin specified in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely: -

5. Standard weight and remedy allowed.— The standard weight of the coin of denomination referred to in rule 2 to
