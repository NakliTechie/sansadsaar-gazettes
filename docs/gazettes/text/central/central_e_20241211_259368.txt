S.O. 5366(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the 
National Highways Act,l956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is 
given in the Schedule below, is required for building(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning 
etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway NH-346 in the stretch of land 
from Km. 0+000 to Km. 87+900 (Jhaekheda-Berasia-Vidisha Road) in the district of BHOPAL in 
the state of MADHYA PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid 
purpose under sub-section() of section 3C of the said Act. 

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Sub Divisional 
Officer(Revenue) and Land Acquisition officer Berasia District Bhopal in writing and shall set out

disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said

The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available
