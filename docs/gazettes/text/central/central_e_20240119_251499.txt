MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(Department of Higher Education) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th January, 2024 


F. No. N-08/0/2024.—CDN, Whereas Section 40 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities 
Act, 20l6 envisages that the Central Government shall, in consultation with the Chief Commissioner, 
formulate rules for persons with disabilities laying down the standards of accessibility for the physical 
environment, transportation, information and communications, including appropriate technologies and 
systems, and other facilities and services provided to the public in urban and rural areas. 


And Whereas, University Grants Commission (UGC) released ‘Accessibility Guidelines and 
Standards for Higher Education Institutions and Universities’ which was prepared under the supervision 
of Expert Committee constituted for the purpose after detailed public consultation including consultation 
with Chief Commissioner of Persons with Disabilities. 


Now, therefore, Ministry of Education hereby notifies the ‘Accessibility Guidelines and Standards 
for Higher Education Institutions and Universities’ and the same is available via link https:// 
www.uge.gov.in/pdfnews/8572354 Final-Accessibility-Guidelines.pdf.
