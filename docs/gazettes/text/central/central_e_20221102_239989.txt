S.O. 5096 ( E ) .— In exercise of powers conferred by clause ( a ) of section 3 of the National

authorities under the said Act with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette , in respect of the stretch of land specified in the corresponding entry in column ( 3 ) of the said 
Schedule relating to state , districts , taluk and villages mentioned in column ( 4 ) , ( 5 ) , ( 6 ) and ( 7 ) respectively 
of the said Schedule for building ( widening two / four laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and 
operation for under construction Bhagalpur bypass design chainage from km . 132.530 to 132.685 ( 155 m ) 
of the National Highway No. 80 in Bhagalpur district of the State of Bihar .
