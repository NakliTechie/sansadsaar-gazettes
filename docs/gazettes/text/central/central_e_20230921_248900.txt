THE PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS REGULATORY BOARD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th September, 2023 


F. No. PNGRB/COM/3-PPPL Tariff (4)/20I2 Vol-V (P-4397).—In exercise of the powers conferred by 
Section 6l of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006 (9 of 2006), the Petroleum and Natural 
Gas Regulatory Board, hereby makes the following regulations, to further amend the Petroleum and Natural Gas 
Regulatory Board (Determination of Petroleum and Petroleum Products Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Regulations, 
200, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement 


() These regulations may be called the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Determination of 
Petroleum and Petroleum Products Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Amendment Regulations, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on [ October, 2023. 


2. In the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Determination of Petroleum and Petroleum Products 
Pipeline Transportation Tariff) Regulations, 200, 


(a) in regulation 4, after the twelfth proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: - 


"Provided also that the transitional period may on the completion of the extended transition period till 
30.09.2023, be further extended up to, and inclusive of, 3.2.2023"; 


(b) inregulation 5, after the eleventh proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: - 


"Provided also that the determination of the petroleum and petroleum products pipeline transportation tariff under this 
regulation shall be benchmarked against the goods tariff table of the railways as applicable on the date of the coming
