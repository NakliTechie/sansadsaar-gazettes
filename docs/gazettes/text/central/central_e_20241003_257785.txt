MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organisation) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 27th September, 2024 


9.0. 432](E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(North Western Railway, Construction organization), Notification number S.O. 2380 (E) dated 20th June, 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section-3, Sub section (ii) (herein after referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 ([] of 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act). The Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely, “Bari Sadri - Nimach new Broad gauge line project” in District Chittorgarh in the State of 
Rajasthan. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily local newspapers “Rajasthan 
Patrika” and “Dainik Bhasker” dated 28th June, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, The Central Government herby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
