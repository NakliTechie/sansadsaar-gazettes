MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st December, 2025 


S.O. 6148(E).— —Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4558 Dated:07/10/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH80 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 95.7 to Km. 192.33 in the district of BHAGALPUR in the state of BIHAR 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” dated 24/12/2025, 
“Dainik Jagran” dated 24/12/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
