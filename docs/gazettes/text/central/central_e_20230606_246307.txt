MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
Western Railway (Construction Organization) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 6th June, 2023 


9.0. 2454(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, Extra Ordinary part II Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after 
referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20A of the Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification 782-E, Dated 8.04.2023 for execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of the Special Railway Project, namely Ratlam — Indore - Dr. Ambedkar Nagar - Khandwa Gauge 
Conversion Project in the State of Madhya Pradesh; 


And whereas under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, notification was published local 
newspapers, Hindi language Raj Express Indore on 25-04-2023 and published in English newspaper Free-Press Indore 
on 25-04-2023 and whereas Competent Authority has not received any objection from any person and whereas, in 
pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act; the competent authority has submitted its report to the 
central government. Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
