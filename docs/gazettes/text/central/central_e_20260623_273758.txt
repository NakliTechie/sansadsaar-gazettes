S.O. 3355(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (No.8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby appoints the 237 day of June 
2026 as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to serial number 50 and the entries relating 
thereto in the Schedule to the said Act, relating to the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development 
Authority Act, 1985 (2 of 1986), shall come into force.
