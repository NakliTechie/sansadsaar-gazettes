NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 12th December , 2022 

G.S.R. 875 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the 
Constitution , the President hereby makes the following amendments to the Indian Railway Management 
Service ( Group ' A ' ) Rules , 2022 , namely : 


4 . 


MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 


1. ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Indian Railway Management Service ( Group ' A ' ) 
( Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) 

They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

In the Indian Railway Management Service ( Group ‘ A ’ ) Rules , 2022 , ( hereinafter referred to as the 
said rules ) , in rule 2 , for clause ( f ) , the following clause shall be substituted namely : 


( Railway Board ) 


" ( f ) * Examination ' means the Indian Railway Management Service Examination conducted by the 
Union Public Service Commission . " 


In the said rule , in Rule 6 , in sub rule ( 1 ) , for clause ( a ) , the following clause shall be substituted 
namely : 


" ( a ) Fifty per cent of the vacancies in the grade of Junior Time Scale shall be filled by direct 
recruitment through Indian Railway Management Service Examination conducted by the 
Commission on the basis of the educational qualifications and age - limit specified by the 
Government in consultation with the Commission . " 


In the said rule , in Schedule II , against Serial No. 8 in Column No. 3 , for the entries relating to 
direct recruitment in Junior Time Scale ( Level 10 ) , the following entries shall be substituted 
namely : 


" Fifty per cent by direct recruitment through the Indian Railway Management Service Examination 
as per age , educational qualifications and other conditions as notified by the Central Government 
in consultation with the Commission . "
