New Delhi, the llth March, 2025 


G.S.R. 7(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 56 of the 
Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force Act, 992 (35 of 992), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
further to amend the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, General Duty Cadre (Group Group ‘C’ Posts) Recruitment 
Rules, 2022 namely :— 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, 
General Duty Cadre (Group ‘C’ Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2; In the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, General Duty Cadre (Group ‘C’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2022, in 
the Schedule, against serial number 3 relating to the post of Constable (General Duty), in column (6), for the figures 
and words “l8 to 25 years”, the words and figures “Between 8 and 23 years” shall be substituted.
