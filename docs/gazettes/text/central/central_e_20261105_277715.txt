G.S.R. 975(E).— Whereas, in the matter of import of Alloy Steel Chisel/tool and Hydraulic Rock Breaker in 
fully assembled condition (hereinafter referred to as the subject goods), originating in or exported from China PR and 
Korea RP, the designated authority, vide its final findings notification F. No. 6/8/2022-DGTR, dated the 28th March, 
2024, had recommended imposition of anti-dumping duty on imports of the subject goods; 


And whereas, on the basis of the aforesaid findings of the designated authority, the Central Government had 
imposed anti-dumping duty on the subject goods vide notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Revenue), No. !/2024-Customs (ADD), dated the 27th June, 2024, published vide number G.S.R. 
349(E) in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), dated the 27th June, 2024; 


And whereas, the designated authority, vide initiation notification F. No. 07/20/2026-DGTR, dated the 28th 
September, 2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section |, has initiated an anti-circumvention 
investigation under section 9A(lA) of the Customs Tariff Act, 975 (5] of 975) read with rules 25 and 26 of the 
Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Anti-Dumping Duty on Dumped Articles and for 
Determination of Injury) Rules, 995 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), concerning alleged circumvention of the 
anti-dumping duty imposed on import of the subject goods by imports of the product under investigation exported from 
Malaysia; 


And whereas, the designated authority has observed prima facie evidence of country-based circumvention through 
Malaysia and has recommended provisional assessment of imports of the product under investigation exported from 
Malaysia and obtaining an appropriate guarantee from the importers in accordance with sub-rule (4A) of rule 26 of the 
said rules; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (A) of section 9A of the Customs Tariff Act, 
975, read with sub-rule (4A) of rule 26 of the said rules, the Central Government, after considering the aforesaid 
recommendation of the designated authority, hereby orders that, pending the outcome of the said anti-circumvention 
investigation, imports into India of the product under investigation, namely Alloy Steel Chisel/tool and Hydraulic Rock 
Breaker in fully assembled condition, including the seven assemblies/sub-assemblies/components of hydraulic rock 
breakers as listed in the Explanation below, exported from Malaysia, shall be subjected to provisional assessment till a 
decision under sub-rule (3) of rule 27 of the said rules is taken by the Central Government. 


2. The provisional assessment shall be subject to furnishing such guarantee as the proper officer of customs deems 
fit, for payment of the anti-dumping duty, if any, that may become payable consequent upon the recommendations of 
the designated authority and the decision of the Central Government under sub-rule (3) of rule 27 of the said rules.
