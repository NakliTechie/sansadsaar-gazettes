S.O. 4828(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Central 
Government under the Railways Act, 1989 (as amended by the Railways Amendment Act, 2008), and after being 
satisfied that the land described briefly in the Schedule below is required for a public purpose, namely for the execution, 
maintenance, management, and operation of a Special Railway Project, i.e., the Dongargarh—Gondia Fourth Railway 
Line (84.10 km) in the States of Chhattisgarh and Maharashtra, the Central Government hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land, with or without structures, situated in the villages listed under Goregaon Tehsil in Gondia District of 
the State of Maharashtra, along with a brief description thereof. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of 30 days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, file objections under sub-section (1) of Section 20D of the said Act regarding the 
acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose.Every such objection shall be made in writing to the 
Competent Authority and Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue), Tirora, District Gondia, Maharashtra, stating the grounds 
thereof. The Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a 
legal practitioner, and after hearing all such objections and making such further inquiry, if any, as deemed necessary, 
may by order either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of Section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available for inspection in the office 
of the Competent Authority mentioned above and may be inspected by any interested person.
