S.O. 5540 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 4 ) of section 51A of the 
Customs Act , 1962 ( 52 of 1962 ) , the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs , on being satisfied that it 
is necessary and expedient to do so , hereby amends the notification No.19 / 2022 - Customs ( N.T. ) dated the 
30th March 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) vide 
S.O. 1512 ( E ) , dated the 30th March , 2022 , namely , - 


In the said notification , in clause 2 , for the figures , letters and word " 30th November , 2022 ” , the 
figures , letters and word " 1st April , 2023 ” , shall be substituted .
