MINISTRY OF TEXTILES

ORDER 
Kolkata, the 24th February, 2026 


S.O. 00(E).— Whereas, vide notification S.O. 2523 (E) dated 8" June 202], Jute Commissioner in exercise 
of powers conferred under Clause 6 () (i) of the Jute and Jute Textiles Control Order, 206 directed that all dealers / 
traders / agencies / stockists etc. trading with 500 kgs or more quantity of raw jute in a jute year (July-June) (kutcha and 
pucca, baled and unbaled raw jute) to apply to the office of the Jute Commissioner for registration within a period of 
30 days from the date of publication of this Order in the proforma in this office website [jutecomm.gov.in] & ‘JUTE 
SMART?’ portal. The clause 4 of the S.O. 2523 (E) dated 8" June 202] stated that “There shall be a fixed date, to be 
decided in due course, beyond which no trading/stocking of raw jute will be allowed by any trader/dealer/agency or 
stockists etc & the same will be prohibited & punishable by law for such traders / dealers / agency / stockists who do 
not register by that date.” 


2. Whereas, vide orders dated 29.06.2022, 07.07.2023, 05.03.2024 and 29.05.2025; sufficient opportunity has 
been given for the dealers/traders/agencies/stockists of raw jute to register on the Jute SMART portal. 


3. After giving consideration to factors enumerated in Clause 5 (2) and in exercise of the powers conferred on me 
by Clause 5() (i) of the Jute and Jute Textiles Control Order, 206, I ,Amrit Raj, Jute Commissioner, hereby specify 
that with effect from 0!.04.2026, the jute mills shall not purchase raw jute from the stockists / trader / dealer / agency 
which do not have a valid registration with O/o Jute Commissioner. 

4. It shall be mandatory for the Jute Mills to submit the Registration Number with Jute Commissioner of the 
supplier/broker in the Purchase Order/Rukka and consignor in the Mill Receipt return submitted through the Jute 
SMART portal i.e. https://smart.jutecomm.gov.in with effect from 0.04.2026. 


5, The list of stockists/traders who have a valid registration with O/o Jute Commissioner shall be updated 
periodically on the website of Jute Commissioner i.e. https://jutecomm.gov.in/ 


6. This Order shall come into force with effect from 0.04.2026 and shall remain valid until further orders.
