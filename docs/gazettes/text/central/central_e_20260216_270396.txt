NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the |6th February, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


5.0. 848(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S. O. 2626 (A) dated the 3th June, 2025 is published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 
3 Sub-section (ii), dated the |3th June, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section 
(.) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to 
the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely “Double chord line between Nilje & Kopar” Railway project in the district of Thane in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Pudhari 
(Marathi)” dated l4th July, 2025 and “Free press journal (English)” dated 2nd July, 2025 under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in. Village Usarghar & Bhopar in Kalyan Taluka no objections were received and the same have 
been considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
