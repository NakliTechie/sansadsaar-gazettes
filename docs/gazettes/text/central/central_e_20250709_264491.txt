NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th July, 2025 


S.O. 3060(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (ba) of Explanation to section 54EC of the 
Income-tax Act, 96 (43 of 96l), the Central Government hereby notifies bonds redeemable after five years and 
issued on or after the date of this notification, by the Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency (IREDA) (a Public 
Limited Government Company established as a Non-Banking Financial Institution), as ‘long-term specified asset’ for 
the purposes of the said section. 


2. IREDA shall utilise the proceeds from such bonds only for those renewable projects which can service the debt out 
of the project revenues without being dependent on the State Governments for the service of debts.
