MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
(Central Advisory Contract Labour Board) 
RESOLUTION 
New Delhi, the !3th September, 2024 


F. No. U-2303/02/2023-L.W(B).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5 of the Contract Labour 
(Regulation and Abolition) Act, 970, the Central Advisory Contract Labour Board hereby constitutes a Committee to 
go into the question of prohibition of contract labour in various jobs/works of extraction of salt from the lake to 
pilling/storing and operation in Guda Salt Refinery and Nawa Salt Refinery of Sambhar Salt Limited, Sambhar Lake, 
Rajasthan.

3. The terms of reference of the proposed Committee would be as follows:- 


“To study the working of contract labour system in the jobs/work of extraction of salt from the lake to pilling/storing 
and operation in Guda Salt Refinery and Nawa Salt Refinery of Sambhar Salt Limited, Sambhar Lake, Rajasthan and 
to make suitable recommendations whether or not the employment of contract labour in the said establishment be 
prohibited in the jobs/work of extraction of salt from the lake to pilling/storing and operation keeping in view the 
provisions of Section 0 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, l970.” 


4. The Headquarters of the Committee will be at Ajmer, Rajasthan. The Committee will submit its report within 
two months.
