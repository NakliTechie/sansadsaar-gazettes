0.४. 31.—WHEREAS, the Bye-Election to Legislative Assembly of Assam, 2021 for 58-Tamulpur 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No.100/AS- 
LA/3//2021 , dated 01" October, 2021; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at 
an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(iii)] भारत का राजपत्र : फरवरी 10, 2024/माघ 21, 1945 75 


WHEREAS, the result of the bye-election for 58-Tamulpur Assembly Constituency of Assam, 2021 was 
declared by the Returning Officer on 020 November, 2021 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election 
Expenses was 017 December, 2021; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report dated 01-12-2021 of the DEO, Baksa under sub-rule (1) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, received from the CEO, Assam vide his letter no. ELE-283/2021/82 dated 
18.01.2022, Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, a contesting candidate from 58-Tamulpur Assembly Constituency of Assam, 
2021 has failed to lodge account of election expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, a Show-Cause notice No. 76/AS- 
LA/1/2022/CEMS-I(Bye), dated 18 February, 2022 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, for not lodging of account of 
Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961, Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, was directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, Baksa District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Baksa District, vide his letter no. BEL/EEM/2023(Bye-Election)/5 
dated 09.03.2022 has reported that the said notice was served to Shri Yaswanta Chauhan’s father Shri Padum 
Bahadur Chauhan on 08.03.2022; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Baksa, Supplementary Report No, ELE-22/262/2023-ELE-BK- 
109085 dated 12-09-2023 and reported that Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, has not submitted account of election 
expenses. Further, after receipt of the said notice, Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, has neither furnished any reason nor 
explanation to the Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the account as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section 10 A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that:- 
“Tf the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner _ required by 
or under this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 


the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any 
such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 


WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Shri Yaswanta 
Chauhan has failed to lodge account of election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to 
do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Shri Yaswanta Chauhan, Vill. Kumbhijhar, PO- Nagrijuli, PS- 
Tamulpur, Distt. Baksa, Assam and a contesting candidate in 58-Tamulpur Assembly Constituency Bye 
Election of the State of Assam to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House of the 
Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three 
years from the date of this order.

New Delhi, the 7th Fabruary, 2024 

O.N. 48.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Karnataka, 2023 for 72-Hubballi 
Dharwad East Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 
464/KT-LA/2023, dated 13% April, 2023; and 

WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at 
an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him or by his election 
agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 

WHEREAS, the result of the election for 72 - Hubballi Dharwad East Assembly Constituency of 
Karnataka, 2023 was declared by the Returning Officer on 13 May, 2023 and hence the last date for lodging the 
account of Election Expenses was 12" June, 2023; and 

WHEREAS, as per the report of the DEO, Dharwad dated 13.06.2023 under sub-rule (1) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 received through the CEO, Karnataka vide his letter no. DPAR 09 
CHUVEBA(Addl CEO-1) 2023, dated 26.06.2023 stating that Venkappa F Siddanath, a contesting candidate from 
72 - Hubballi Dharwad East Assembly Constituency in General Election to Legislative Assembly of Karnataka, 
2023 has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses, as required under law; and 

WHEREAS, on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Dharwad a Show-Cause notice 
No. 76/KT-LA/10/2023/CEMS-II, dated 13" October, 2023 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct 
of Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Venkappa F Siddanath, for not lodging of the 
account of Election Expenses; and 

WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct 
of Elections Rules, 1961, Venkappa F Siddanath was directed to submit representation in writing to the 
Commission explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his election 
expenses with the District Election Officer, Dharwad District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; 
and 

WHEREAS, the DEO, Dharwad in his report no. Chunavane/vahi: 10 /2023-24; dated 08.11.2023 has 
reported that the said Show Cause Notice was served to Venkappa F Siddanath on 17.10.2023; and

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Dharwad in his Supplementary Report dated 08.11.2023 
received through CEO, Karnataka vide letter No. DPAR/09/Chuvebha(Addl.CEO-1) 2023; dated 09.11.2023 on 
account of the candidate has reported that Venkappa F Siddanath has not submitted any representation or statement 
of account of election expenses. Further, after receipt of the said notice, Venkappa F Siddanath has neither 
furnished any reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of India for failure to lodge the account of his 
election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that:- 

“Tf the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 

(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act; and 

(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 

the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified 


and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 

WHEREAS, on basis of the facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Venkappa F 
Siddanath has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the 
failure to do so; and

Shyandil Ashram, Dayanand Colony, Hubballi, TQ-Hubballi, Dist-Dharwad and a contesting candidate in 
72 - Hubballi Dharwad East Assembly Constituency of G.E. to LA of Karnataka, 2023, to be disqualified for being 
chosen as and for being a member of either House of the Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative 
Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three years from the date of this order.
