New Delhi , the 14th February , 2023 

G.S.R. 99 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3 of the All India Services 
Act , 1951 ( 61 of 1951 ) , read with sub - rules ( 1 ) and ( 2 ) of rule 4 of the Indian Administrative Service ( Cadre ) Rules , 
1954 , the Central Government , in consultation with the Government of Chhattisgarh , hereby makes the following 
regulations further to amend the Indian Administrative Service ( Fixation of Cadre Strength ) Regulations , 1955 , 
namely : 


These Regulations may be called the Indian Administrative Service ( Fixation of Cadre Strength ) 
Amendment Regulations , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

In the Indian Administrative Service ( Fixation of Cadre Strength ) Regulations , 1955 , in the Schedule , for the 
heading " Chhattisgarh " and the entries relating thereto , the following shall be substituted namely :
