S.O. 5477(E).—Whereas M/s Waaree Forever Energies Pvt Ltd (WFEPL), the applicant having its registered 
office at 602, 6th Floor, Western Edge-I, Western Express Highway, Borivali (East), Mumbai City, Mumbai- 400066, 
Maharashtra, India has applied for authorization under Section 164 of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of overhead 
transmission line under the transmission scheme "Providing connectivity of 700 MW to M/s Waaree Forever Energies 
Private Limited for its proposed Solar Power Plant in Solapur, Maharashtra.” 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/111/2025-PG dated 
09.12.2025 had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for the overhead line covered 
under the transmission scheme of Providing connectivity of 700 MW to M/s Waaree Forever Energies Private Limited 
for its proposed Solar Power Plant in Solapur, Maharashtra.” 


M/s Waaree Forever Energies Pvt Ltd (WFEPL) had published notice for transmission scheme in local 
newspapers Navbharat (in Hindi) dated 29.12.2025, Punyanagari (Regional language) dated 29.12.2025, Business 
Standard (in English) dated 29.12.2025 and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 04.04.2026 for the general public to 
make observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within two months from the date of publication. 
Subsequently M/s Waaree Forever Energies Pvt Ltd (WFEPL), has submitted an affidavit dated 31.08.2026 declaring 
that one observation/representation was received, which was duly examined, suitably responded to, and resolved, 
within a period of two months from the date of publication of the Public Notice in the newspaper/Official Gazette of 
the Government of India. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so 
established or maintained for laying of electric lines under the transmission scheme " Providing connectivity of 700 
MW to M/s Waaree Forever Energies Private Limited for its proposed Solar Power Plant in Solapur, Maharashtra.”
