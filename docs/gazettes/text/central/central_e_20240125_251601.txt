Mumbai, the 24th January, 2024 
S.O. 337(E).— Whereas by the Notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number 8.0. 3586(E), dated |lth August, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part II, section-3, 


Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989(240fl989)(here in after referred to as the said Act),the 
Central Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 


Annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely, Ratlam-Indore -Dr.Ambedkar Nagar - Khandwa Gauge Conversion Project in the State of Madhya 
Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily “Free press Journal” dated 
is" August, 2023 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 8th August, 2023 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A 
of the said Act. And, whereas, nil objection has been received by the competent authority.
