MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th January, 2026 


S.O. 166(E).— Whereas it appears to the Central Government that in the public interest it is necessary that for 
transportation of crude oil through the Paradip — Numaligarh crude oil pipeline project of Numaligarh Refinery Limited 
from Paradip (Odisha) to Numaligarh (Assam) via Siliguri (West Bengal), a pipeline must be laid, part of which would 
fall in the State of Assam; and 


Whereas it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying such pipeline, it is necessary to 
acquire the Right of User (२00) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid and which is described 
in the Schedule annexed hereto; 


Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section (1) of Section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (Act No. 50 of 1962), the Government of India hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the Right of User (२00) therein. 


Any person interested in the land described in the said schedule may, within 21 (twenty-one) days from the 
date on which copies of the Gazette of India containing this Notification are made available to the general public, object 
in writing to the acquisition of the Right of User (RoU) therein for laying of the pipeline under the land, to the Competent 
Authority ,Paradip — Numaligarh Crude Oil Pipeline Project, Numaligarh Refinery Limited , Government of India 
Enterprises , 122A, Christian Basti, G.S. Road, Guwahati - 781005, Assam.
