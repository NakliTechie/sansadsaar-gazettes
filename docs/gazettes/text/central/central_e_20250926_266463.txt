S.O. 4409(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Clause (a) of Section 3 of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments 
in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways 
number S.O. 51(E) dated the 13" J anuary, 2005, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii), dated the 13" J. anuary 2005 —namely: In the Schedule to the 
said Notification S.No. 51(E) for the words “District Revenue Officer, Office of Collector & District 
Magistrate, Collectorate, Madurai” at S.No. 1 and Col.No. 2 the words “Special District Revenue 
Officer, Land Acquisition, National highways, Madurai” shall be substituted and for the words 
“District Revenue Officer, Office of Collector & District Magistrate, Collectorate, Dindigul, at 
S.No. 2 and Col. No. 2 the words “Special District Revenue Officer, Land Acquisition, National 
highways, Dindigul” shall be substituted.
