MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th December, 2025 
S.O. 5679(E).— WHEREAS the Central Government established a body called the National Biodiversity 
Authority under sub-sections (1) and (4) of section 8 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (18 of 2003) vide notification 


number S.O. 1147(E), dated the 17 October, 2003, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) (hereinafter referred to as the said notification); 


AND WHEREAS the demise of the non-official member has resulted to becoming the position vacant at serial 
number 20 of the said Authority; 


WHEREAS under sub-rule (3) of the rule 7 of the Biological Diversity Rules, 2024, provides a casual vacancy 
shall be filled by a fresh nomination and the person nominated to fill the vacancy shall hold office only for the remainder 
of the term of the member in whose place such member was nominated. 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (4) of section 8 of the said 
Act read with sub-rule (1) of rule 6 of the Biological Diversity Rules, 2024, the Central Government hereby makes 
following further amendment to the notification of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Environment 
and Forests, vide number S.O. 1147 (E), dated the 17 October, 2003, namely:- 


In the said notification, under the sub-heading, "Non official members under clause (d) of sub-section (4) of 
section 8", for the serial number (20) and entries relating thereto, the following serial number and entries shall be 
substituted, namely:- 


“20) Dr. Aditya Kumar Joshi (upto 05.09.2026)".
