STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
This Bill is introduced in pursuance of article 114(1) of the Constitution of 
India to provide for the appropriation out of the Consolidated Fund of India of the 
moneys required to meet the expenditure charged on the Consolidated Fund of India 


and the grants made by the Lok Sabha for expenditure of the Central Government 
for the financial year 2026-27. 


NIRMALA SITHARAMAN. 


PRESIDENT’S RECOMMENDATION UNDER ARTICLE 117 OF THE 
CONSTITUTION OF INDIA 


[Copy of letter D.O. No. 2(14)-B(D)/2026 dated 09.03.2026 from 
Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman, Minister of Finance and Corporate Affairs to the 
Secretary-General, Lok Sabha] 


The President, having been informed of the subject matter of the Appropriation (No. 2) 
Bill, 2026 to authorise payment and appropriation of certain sums from and out of 
the Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2026-27 
recommends, under article 117(1) and (3) of the Constitution, the introduction of 


the Appropriation (No. 2) Bill, 2026 in Lok Sabha and also the consideration of the 
Bill. 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, 
Secretary-General.
