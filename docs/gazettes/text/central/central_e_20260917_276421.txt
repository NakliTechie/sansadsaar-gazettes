MINISTRY OF MINES

New Delhi, the 17th September, 2026 


5.0. 5099(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section (/) of 
section 4 of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), upon obtaining 
re-accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Education and Training of the Quality Council of 
India, the Central Government hereby notify the following agency for the purposes of the said Act, namely:— 


Sl. No. Exploration agency Category of exploration agency 
1. M/s Engeotech Consultant ‘A’ 
1. The agency shall carry out prospecting operations in compliance with the conditions specified in the 


guidelines issued vide Order No.M.VI-16/15/2021-Mines VI dated the 12" August, 2021. 


2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette and shall 
remain valid for a period of five years or till expiry or termination of the accreditation granted, whichever is 


earlier.
