5.0. 2355(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 44 of the Code on Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following Scheme, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement.- (|) This Scheme may be called the other beneficiaries and members of their 
families medical facilities Scheme, 2026. 
(2) It shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.- This Scheme shall apply to other beneficiaries and members of their families registered under 
any scheme, other than the Employees’ State Insurance Scheme, framed by the Central Government. 


3. Definitions.- In this scheme, unless the context otherwise requires- 
(a) “Code” means the Code on Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020); 


[भाग --खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(b) “Corporation” means the Employees’ State Insurance Corporation; 

(c) “person” means a beneficiary, including members of his or her family, registered under any scheme, 
other than the Employees’ State Insurance Scheme framed by the Central Government; 

(d) All other words and expressions used and not defined in this scheme shall have the meaning 
respectively assigned to them in the Code. 


4. Registration.- A person desirous of availing medical treatment and attendance under the Scheme shall be 
required to- 
(a) fill a registration form containing such particulars electronically or otherwise in respect of himself and 
members of his family as may be specified by the Corporation; 
(b) produce the identity card issued to such person by the Corporation or under any other scheme, as the 
case may be, framed by the Central Government. 


5. Benefits.- Persons registered under this scheme shall be provided medical treatment and attendance from 
under-utilised Employees’ State Insurance Hospitals. 


6. User Charges.- (.) Persons availing medical treatment and attendance under this Scheme shall be required to 
pay user charges at such rates as may be notified by the Corporation in consultation with the Central 
Government. 


(2) The user charges collected under sub-paragraph () shall be deemed to be contribution and shall form part 
of the Employees’ State Insurance Fund. 


7. Time and manner of availing Medical Facility.- The time and manner in which the facilities may be availed 
by the beneficiaries shall be such as may be specified by the Corporation from time to time. 


8. Maintenance of register and records.- The concerned hospitals shall maintain separate electronic registers 
and records of the beneficiaries availing medical treatment and attendance under this scheme and user charges 
collected from them in such form and manner as may be specified by the Corporation.
