S.O. 626 ( E ) .– Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways number S.O. 2219 ( E ) dated 14th July 2017 issued under section 05 of the National 
Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1988 ) , the Central Government has entrusted the stretch from Km 0 + 00 to 
Km 41 + 979 ( AP / Odisha Border to Narasannaapeta section ) of National Highway No. 326A in the State of 

Andhra Pradesh to the State Government of Andhra Pradesh ;
