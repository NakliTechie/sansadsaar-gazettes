Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Professionals) (Amendment) 
Regulations, 2025 


F. No. IBBI/2025-26/GN/REG23.— In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 96 and 207 
read with section 240 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 206 (3l of 206), the Insolvency and 
Bankruptcy Board of India hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Insolvency and 
Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Professionals) Regulations, 206, namely:— 


l. (!) These regulations may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency 
Professionals) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Professionals) Regulations, 206, in 
regulation 5, in clause (a), for the word “twelve”, the words and the mark “twenty-four” shall be 
substituted.
