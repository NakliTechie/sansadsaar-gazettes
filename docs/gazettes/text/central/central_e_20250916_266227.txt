Bhopal, the l6th September, 2025 


S.O. 497(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by subsection (l) of section 20A Of the railway 
Act.l989 (Railway Amendment Act.-2008) , the central Government ,after being satisfied for the public purpose 
,the land the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below ,is required for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project of “GUNA BYPASS LINE” in the district of Guna in the 
state of Madhya Pradesh ,hereby declares its intension to acquire such land . 


Any person interested in the said land may ,within a period of thirty days from the date of publication 
of this notification in the Official Gazette ,raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid 
purpose under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to competent authority , namely ,the joint Collector ,District 
Guna in writing and shall set out grounds there of ,and the competent authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard ,either in person or by legal practitioner and may after hearing all such objection and 
after making such further enquiry ,if any ,as the competent authority thinks necessary ,by order ,either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the Act. Shall be 


final. 


The land plans and other details of land covered under this notification are available and can be 


inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
