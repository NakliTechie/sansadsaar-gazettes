उप-धारा (2) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, केंद्र सरकार एतदद्वारा 01 जुलाई, 2026 को उस तारीख के रूप में

S.O. 3260(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby appoints the 17 day of July, 2026, 
as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to amendments in the Seamen’s Provident Fund 
Act, 1966 (4 of 1966), as specified against serial number 33 of the Schedule to the said Act, shall come into force.
