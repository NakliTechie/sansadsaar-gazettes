Notification No. DoR . FIN . REC . 75 / ED ( SS ) -2022 . 

I. In exercise of the powers conferred by clause ( b ) of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3 of the Securitisation and 
Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act , 2002 , the Reserve Bank of India hereby 
makes the following amendments in the Notification DNBR ( PD - ARC ) No. 05 / ED ( SS ) -2017 dated April 28 , 2017 
with immediate effect . 


( i ) In Paragraph 1 of the said notification , Rupees one hundred crore shall be substituted with Rupees three 
hundred crore with effect from the date of this Notification . 


( ii ) Paragraph 2 of the said notification , shall be substituted with the following : 

" All the ARCs , which are already registered with the Reserve Bank of India shall achieve the minimum NOF 
level of Rupees two hundred crore latest by March 31 , 2024 and Rupee three hundred crore latest by March 31 , 
2026. " 


II . In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 7 read with clause ( u ) of sub - section ( 1 ) of 
section 2 of the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act , 2002 , 
the Reserve Bank of India hereby makes the following amendment in the Notifications DNBR ( PD - ARC ) No. 07 / 
ED ( SS ) -2018 dated May 16 , 2018 and DoR . FIN . No. 08 / 26.03.001 / 2020-2021 dated March 10 , 2021 with 
immediate effect . 

( i ) Condition ( a ) of the said notifications shall be omitted .
