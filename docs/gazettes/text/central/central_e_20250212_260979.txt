है, ने गुजरात राज्य के ग्राम ओगनाज, तालुका दासक्रोई, बंगलौर शहरी जिला में सूचना प्रौद्योगिकी एवं सूचना प्रौद्योगिकी 
समर्थित सेवाओं के लिए एक क्षेत्र विशिष्ट विशेष आर्थिक जोन की स्थापना हेतु विशेष आर्थिक जोन अधिनियम, 2005 
(2005 का 28), (जिसे एतद्पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3 के अंतर्गत प्रस्ताव किया था;

New Delhi, the 12" February, 2025 


S.0.736 (E).- Whereas, M/s. Calica Constructions and Impex Private Limited, a_ private 
organisation, had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a sector-specific Special Economic Zone for Information 
Technology and Information Technology Enabled Services at Village Ognaj, Taluka Dascroi, District 
Ahmedabad in the State of Gujarat; 


And, whereas, the Central Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of 
section 4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zone Rules 2006, had notified the area of 
10.4310 hectares and 0.5059 hectares at above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and 
Industry Notification numbers S.O.1183(E) dated 087 May, 2009 and S.0.2349(E) dated 12" October, 2011; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Calica Constructions and Impex Private Limited, has now requested for 
issuance of revised notification of the above SEZ, subsequent upon consolidation of Final Plot Numbers into 
two Final Plot Nos. 394+395 by Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation; 


Now, therefore, in the exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section (1) of 
section 4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005, and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic 
Zones Rules, 2006, the Central Government hereby re-notifies an area of 10.9368 hectares, comprising the 
re-notification Original plot number and the area given below in the table, namely: -
