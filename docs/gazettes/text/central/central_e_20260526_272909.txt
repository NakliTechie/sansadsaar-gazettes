MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th May, 2026 


9.0. 266l(E).— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural 
Gas S.O.No. 3073(E) dated 09.07.2025, issued under sub-section (l) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), 
published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette of India No. 3006 dated 0.07.2025, the Government of India declared its 
intention to acquire the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that notification for the purpose 
of laying pipeline for transportation of crude oil through Paradip — Numaligarh crude oil pipeline in the state of Assam 
by Numaligarh Refinery Limited (NRL). 


And whereas copies of the said Extraordinary Gazette notification were made available to the public. 


And whereas the objections received from the public to the laying of the pipeline have been considered and 
disposed by the Competent Authority. 


And whereas the Competent Authority has, under sub-section (l) of section 6 of the said Act submitted its 
report to the Government of India. 


And whereas the Government of India after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said land 
is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of the section 6 of the said Act, the 
Government of India hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Government 
of India hereby directs that the Right of User in the land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in Government 
of India, vest, on this date of the publication of the declaration, in the Numaligarh Refinery Limited (NRL), free from 
all encumbrances.
