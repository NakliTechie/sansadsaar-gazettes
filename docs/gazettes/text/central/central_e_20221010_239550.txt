INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-02 / 2604 - INC . — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Bless College of Nursing situated at Door No. 20-3-124 - H1 , Maruthi Nagar , 
Akkarampally Road , Near Leelamahal Junction , Tirupathi , Distt . Chittor - 517501 , Andhra Pradesh 
is recognized by State Government of Andhra Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-02 / 2604 - INC / 12603 dated 25/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition 
under section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health Medical and 
Family Welfare Department , Government of Andhra Pradesh , 05th Block , Ground Floor , Room No. 157 , 
Secretariat , Velagapudi , Amaravathi - 522503 , Andhra Pradesh was issued . Copy of the notice dated 
25/02/2021 was also enclosed to Bless College of Nursing situated at Door No. 20-3-124 - H1 , Maruthi 
Nagar , Akkarampally Road , Near Leelamahal Junction , Tirupathi , Distt . Chittor – 517501 , Andhra 
Pradesh . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects 
the case of Bless College of Nursing . Even the institution Bless College of Nursing has not responded to 
the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection 
under section 13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only 
that the Council would be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct 
nursing courses . Refusal for inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the 
requisite Teaching , Clinical or infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc ( N ) from Bless College of Nursing 
situated at Door No. 20-3-124 - H1 , Maruthi Nagar , Akkarampally Road , Near Leelamahal Junction , 
Tirupathi , Distt . Chittor - 517501 , Andhra Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of 
Andhra Pradesh subject to orders of State government of Andhra Pradesh . 

Lt. Col ( Dr. ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
