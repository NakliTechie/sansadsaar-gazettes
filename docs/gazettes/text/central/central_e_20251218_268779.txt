FOOTWEAR DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT INSTITUTE 
ORDINANCE 
New Delhi, the |6th December, 2025 


F.No. FDDI/GC/83/2024.—In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 29 read with section 28 of the 
Footwear Design and Development Act. 20l7 (20 of 207), the Senate makes the following Ordinance further to 
amend the Footwear Design and Development Institute Ordinances, 209, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement. - (/) This Ordinance may be called the Footwear Design and Development 
Institute (Amendment) Ordinance, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. In the Footwear Design and Development Institute Ordinances, 209, in clause 5, in sub-clause (/),- 
(i) in item (A), for sub-items (c) and (d), the following item shall be substituted, namely: - 

"(c) School examination conducted by the National Open School with a minimum of five subjects." 
(ii) after item (B), the following item shall be inserted, namely:- 


"(BA) A candidate holding three-year full-time or distance or online diploma, after class l0, offered by the All 
India Council for Technical Education or the Board of Technical Education of any State Government or Union 
territory administration, shall be eligible for lateral entry into the second year to the Bachelor's Programmes.".
