MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th October, 2025 


S.O. 4876(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, of the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/FourSix laning etc), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 353-J in the stretch 
of land from Km. 3.000 to Km 62.900 in the District of Nagpur in the state of Maharashtra under NH(O) hereby 
declares its intention to acquire such land, 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) 
of section 3-C of the said Act,

Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the Sub Divisional Officer and Land 
Acquisition Officer, Saoner, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the said competent authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3-C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available, and can be inspected
