MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd November, 2023 


S.O. 5036(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Employees’ State 
Insurance Act, 1948 (34 of 1948), the Central Government hereby appoints the 151 day of December, 2023, as the date 
on which the provisions of the said Act, namely:— 


(i) sections 38 to 43 and section 45A to section 45H of Chapter IV; 
(ii) sections 46 to 73 of Chapter ५; and 
(iii) sections 74, 75, sub-sections (2) to (4) of section 76, 80, 82 and section 83 of Chapter VI, 
shall come into force in all areas of the Kandhamal district in the State of Odisha.
