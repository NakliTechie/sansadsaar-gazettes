INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F. No. 18-19/7975-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Savitribai Phule School/College of Nursing situated at CS 2130, E Ward, Tarabai Park, Distt. 
Kolhapur 416003, Maharashtra is recognized by State Government of Maharashtra and Maharashtra Nursing 
Council to conduct ANM and B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-19/7975-INC dated 15/11//2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the Principal Secretary (Public Health Department), Government of 
Maharashtra, 10" Floor, B Wing G T Hospital Complex Building, Mumbai — 400001, Maharashtra was issued. Copy 
of the notice dated 15/11/2022 was also enclosed to Savitribai Phule School/College of Nursing situated at CS 2130, 
E Ward, Tarabai Park, Distt. Kolhapur 416003, Maharashtra. In the notice issued, the State Government was 
requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Savitribai Phule School/College of Nursing. Even the institution Savitribai Phule School/College of Nursing 
has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
01/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.6., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 
declares that with effect from 23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM and B.Sc(N) 
Savitribai Phule School/College of Nursing situated at CS 2130, E Ward, Tarabai Park, Distt. Kolhapur 416003, 
Maharashtra shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Maharashtra subject to the orders of State 
government of Maharashtra. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
