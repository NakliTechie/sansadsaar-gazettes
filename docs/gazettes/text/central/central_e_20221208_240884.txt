NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 8th December , 2022 

S.O. 5710 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways , 4276 ( E ) , Dated 13 September 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section

Act ) , the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification 
for building ( widening / four - laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and operation of NH 752 ( D ) ( Old NH148NG ) on the stretch of 
land from Km . 0.000 to Km . 41.420 in the district of DEWAS in the state of MADHYA PRADESH ;

report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by the 
sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule 
should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ;
