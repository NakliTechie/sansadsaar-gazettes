BAR COUNCIL OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2Ist April, 2026 


F. No. BCI:D: 297/2026— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 7 read with Section 49 of the 
Advocates Act, |96l, and in compliance with the Order dated 9.02.2026 passed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court of 
India in Writ Petition (Civil) No. 226 of 2026, Dhanya Kumar Jain & Ors. v. Bar Council of India & Anr., wherein the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court has recorded as follows: 


“Since the impugned rule deprives office-bearers of the Bar Associations of High Courts, District Courts, or 
Sub-Divisional Courts from contesting the State Bar Council elections, we, prima facie, see no justification for 
creating such an artificial distinction under the Rules. Consequently, we direct the Bar Council of India to 
reconsider the provision and carry out a suitable amendment within one week.” 


Now, therefore, the Bar Council of India hereby amends the Bar Council of India Uniform Rules and 
Mandatory Guidelines for the Elections of Bar Councils, 20I6, as follows. 


This proposed amendment was placed before the Hon’ble Supreme Court by Bar Council of India along with 
IA No. 78285 of 2026 filed in W.P.(C) 39 of 2023. 


Finally, the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India vide its order dated 20.04.2026 passed in W.P. (C) No. 454/2026, 
observed that the grievance of the elected office bearers of the bar association has been adequately redressed through 
the amended provision and approved the amendment and directed Bar Council of India to publish the amendment in the 
Official Gazette of India without any delay. 


Therefore, this amendment is being published in the Official Gazette of India after approval of the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court. 


AMENDMENT 


In the Bar Council of India Uniform Rules and Mandatory Guidelines for the Elections of Bar Councils, 206, in Chapter 
Ill, for the existing Chapter वा and the provisions thereunder, the following shall be substituted, namely. 


CHAPTER III 
l. Eligibility of office-bearers of Bar Associations to contest election to a State Bar Council 
a. An advocate who is an office-bearer of a Bar Association shall be eligible to contest the election to a 


State Bar Council, provided that such advocate files an undertaking before the Returning Officer at 
the time of filing the nomination stating that, if elected as a member of the State Bar Council, the 
advocate shall not simultaneously hold more than one representative post and shall accordingly resign 
as an office-bearer of the Bar Association upon election, or in the alternative shall resign as a member 
of the State Bar Council if the advocate chooses to continue as an office-bearer of the Bar Association. 


b. If any advocate who holds a position as an office-bearer of a Bar Association is elected as a member 
of a State Bar Council, such advocate shall, within fifteen days from the date of declaration of results, 
exercise a written option to retain only one of the representative posts and shall communicate the same 
to the concerned Bar Association and the concerned State Bar Council. 


०. Upon exercise of such option, the advocate shall relinquish the other post or posts held by him or her 
and shall intimate the same in writing to the concerned Bar Association and the concerned State Bar 
Council for record and necessary action. 


d. If any advocate elected as a member of a State Bar Council fails to exercise the option and 
communicate relinquishment in terms of clause (b) within fifteen days from the date of declaration of 
results, such advocate shall be deemed to have vacated the office of Member of the State Bar Council 
upon expiry of the said period. 


2. Commencement and Applicability 


This amendment shall come into force from the date of its publication in the official gazette of India, after 
due approval from the Hon’ble Apex Court; and this shall apply to all cases where elections have not yet 
been held.
