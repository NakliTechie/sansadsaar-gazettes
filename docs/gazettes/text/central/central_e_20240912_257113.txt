MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Eastern Railway (Construction Department)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the l2th September, 2024 


S.O. 3903(E).—Whereas by the notification of The Govt. of India in The Ministry of Railways, S.O. number 
4080(E), dated the 7th September, 2023 published in the Gazette of India on !5/09/2023, extraordinary part I, section 
3, sub-section (II) issued under sub-section (.) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) read with the 
Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declaration its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed here to required for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely New BG Line from Nabadwip Ghat 
Nabadwip Dham in the district of Nadia in the State of West Bengal. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Ajkal Patrika & Telegraph Patrika 
sated the 27th September, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas any objection has not been filed within the statutory time limit 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report of the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose.

absolutely in the Central Government, free form all encumbrances.
