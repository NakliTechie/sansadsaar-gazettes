WHEREAS, the Badalkhol Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Bagicha Tehsil, Jashpur district of 
Chhattisgarh State. The Sanctuary is an important protected area known for its rich habitat consisting of a 
variety of flora and fauna including mammals, birds, amphibians, reptiles, fish, varieties of butterflies, 
insects and number of waterfalls such as Chhuri Fall, Gulu Fall adding richness to the scenic beauty of the 
natural landscape. It is spread over an area of 104.454 square kilometres. The Wildlife Sanctuary is located 
between 22°51'12" N to 23°01'53.10" N latitude and 83° 42' 58" E to 83° 58' 24.70" E longitude; 


AND WHEREAS the sanctuary consists of a mixture of moist and dry tropical deciduous forests, 
evergreen Sal forests, precious Teak forests including species such as Shorea robusta, Terminelia tomentosa, 
Anogeissus latifolia, Boswellia serrata, Bassia latifolia, Pterocarpus marsupium, Terminelia chebula, 
Diospyros melanoxylon, Sterculia urens, Cassia fistula, Terminalia belerica, Terminalia arjuna, Diospyros 
cordifolia, Ficus glomerata, Adina cordifolia, Mitragyna parvifolia, Syzygium cumini, Ficus virenes, 
Cochlospermum religiosum, Aegle marmelos, Ficus bengalensis, Ficus religiosa, Emblica officinalis, 
Schleichera oleosa etc.; 


AND WHEREAS different types of mammals are found in the sanctuary such as Elephant (Elelphas 
maximus indicus), Sloth Bear (Melursus ursinus), Common Leopard (Panthera pardus), Indian Gaur 
(Bos gaurus), Wild Boar (Sus scrofa), Spotted Deer (Axis axis), Barking Deer (Muntiacus muntjak), 
Porcupine (Hystrix indica), Hyena (Hyaena hyaena), Jackal (Canis aureus), Small Indian Civet (Viverricula 
indica) etc. 


AND WHEREAS it is necessary to conserve and protect the area, extent and boundary which is 
specified in paragraph 1 of this notification around the protected area of Badalkhol Wildlife Sanctuary as 
Eco-sensitive zone from ecological, environmental and biodiversity point of view and to prohibit industries 
or class of industries and their operations and processes in the said Eco-Sensitive Zone; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (1) and clauses (v) and 
(xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub-section (3) of Section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 
1986) read with sub-rule (3) of Rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 1986, the Central Government 
hereby notifies the area to an extent of 1 to 4 kilometre around the boundary of Badalkhol Wildlife Sanctuary 
in the State of Chhattisgarh as the Badalkhol Wildlife Sanctuary, Eco-sensitive Zone (herein after referred 
to as the Eco-sensitive Zone) details of which are as under, namely: - 


1. Extent and boundaries of Eco-sensitive Zone. — (1) the extent of Eco-Sensitive Zone varies from 
1 kilometre to 4 kilometre around the boundary of Badalkhol Wildlife Sanctuary, Chhattisgarh. The 
area of Eco-sensitive Zone is 110.08 square kilometre. Out of which 45.03 square kilometre is reserve 
and protected forests area and 65.045 square kilometre is revenue area. The extent of Eco-sensitive 
Zone in different directions is given in table below:

the competent authority in the State. 


(2) The Zonal Master Plan for the Eco-sensitive Zone shall be prepared by the State Government in such 
manner as is specified in this notification and also in consonance with the relevant Central and State laws
