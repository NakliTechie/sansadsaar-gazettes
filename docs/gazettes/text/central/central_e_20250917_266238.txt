NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th September, 2025 


S.O. 4211(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 4061(E) dated 190 September 
2024, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part H, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification 
for Construction of New High Level major Bridge on Katepurna River at design chainage 133/450 Bridge on 
Jalna-Pulgaon Section NH-753C in District Washim on EPC mode in the State of Maharashtra, hereby declare 


its intention to acquire such land. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.0.4061 (E) dated 19 September 2024, has been 
published in “Dainik Hitavada Nagpur” and “Dainik Lokmat Washim” Both Dated 02/10/2024; under sub-section 
(3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
