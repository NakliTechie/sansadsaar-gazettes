(एन एच-128ए का मौजूदाचैनेज 36.758 किमी) से गोसाई की बाजार बाईपास (एन एच-28 का मौजूदा चैनेज 
242.787 किमी) तक 8.952 किमी लंबी 4-लेन लिंक रोड का निर्माण, जिसमें एन एच 28 के 242.787 किमी पर एक

एन एच-1287ए के 2-लेन से 4-लेन तक चैड़ीकरण करना और प्रसाद संस्थान से मोहम्मदपुर जंक्शन तक एन एच-128ए को 
सुदृढ़ीकरण करना, जिसकी कुल लंबाई 40.900 किमी (कुल लंबाई 55.902 किमी) है। अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के 
लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

S.O. 3295(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport

section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of Construction of 4-Lane Link Road from Thekma village (existing chainage Km 36.758 of NH-128A) to Gosai Ki 
Bazar Bypass (existing chainage Km 242.787 of NH-28) with length of 8.135 km, including a VUP on NH-28 at Km 
242.787, along with widening of NH-128A from 2-Lane to 4-Lane from Smt. Lata Mangeshkar Junction to Prasad 
Institute for a length of 4.700 km and Strengthening of NH-128A from Prasad Institute to Mohammedpura Junction, 
covering a length of 41.350 km (total length: 54.185 km), in the State of Uttar Pradesh on EPC Mode. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Date 19 April 2026 #Hindustan 
And Hindustan Times# ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
