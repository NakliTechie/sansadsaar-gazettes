5.0. 2757(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Eastern Railway, Construction Origination), Notification number, 5.0. 953(E), dated 23 February, 2026 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated 23 February, 2026 (hereinafter referred to as 
the said notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (I of 
2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mankapur-Tikri with Y-Connection at Mankapur (Jhilahi-Tikri) 
Doubling Project (28.37 KM) in Tehsil Mankapur of Gonda District in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely "Dainik 
Jagran" and "The Times of India" Dated 28 February, 2026 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, the competent Authority has resolved all the objection received in this project against the 
notification. And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
