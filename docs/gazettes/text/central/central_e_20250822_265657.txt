NOTIFICATION 

Guntur, the 22nd August, 2025 
७.0. 3858(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Guntur: Guntur-Krishna Canal: Construction of 2-lane 
ROB in lieu of LC No. 02 at Km 1/13-15 between Guntur-Nambur stations" as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Guntur for providing National 


Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
