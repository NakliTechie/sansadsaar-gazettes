99.0. 628(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Road Transport and Highways, declared its intension to acquie the land specifed in the shedule annexed to 
the said notification bearing 8.0 230 (E) 6 May 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 
956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), for (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH22 on the stretch of land from Km 46.300 to Km 56.560 (O/o DC 
Shimla) in the district of SHIMLA in the state of HIMACHAL PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Tribune” 
(English) and “Amar Ujala (Hindi) ” both dated 2] May 2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received objection filed under Section 3C and the same have 
been considered and decided by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (]) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
