NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 8th June, 2026 
Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


5.0. 3050(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (No.l] of 2008), the Central 
Government hereby notifies Railway project “PH-30:- Elimination of LC-23 by providing subway by push 
through method in lieu of LC Gate no. 23 between Vaghli-Kajgaon Railway stations @ Kms 340/20-22 in IGP- 
BSL section” under Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Jalgaon District in the State of Maharashtra, 
for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in 


the Official Gazette.
