Mumbai, the 22nd December, 2025 


Under clause 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


8७.0. 62(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 5.0 4254 (E) dated 22" September 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section 
(l) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (I of 2008) 
(hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (l of 2008), the Central Government declares its

execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project “Odha station - Provision of 
stabling lines for handling Kumbh Mela traffic in 2027” District Nashik in the State of Maharashtra, in the 
district of Nashik in the State of Maharashtra.

Dated 0-l0-2025 & 30-09-2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act.

said notification have been received within the time limit. Hearing on the said objections received was conducted 
by the competent authority Assistant Deputy Collector & Sub-Divisional Officer, Nashik on 9//2025.

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government.

conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose.
