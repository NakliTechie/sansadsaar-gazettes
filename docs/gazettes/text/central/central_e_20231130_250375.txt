MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th November, 2023 


9.0. 5092(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 564(E) Dated 02/2/2022 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Two lane/Four lane/Widening/Toll Plaza etc.) 
Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.752G on the stretch of land from Km. 64/000 to 
Km. 0/40 Satana to Mangrul (Section — IID) in the district of NASHIK in the State of MAHARASHTRA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Deshdoot Times 
(English) ' and “Aapal Mahanagar (Marathi) ” both dated 46क्‍2/2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority Deputy Collector (Land 
Acquisition) National Highway Project, Nashik and in exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of 
section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should 
be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
