५.0. 5348(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred under section 36 of the Ancient Monuments 
and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958 (24 of 1958), the Central Government hereby makes 
correction in the notification of Government of India in the Ministry of Scientific Research and Cultural 
Affairs, S.O. 886, dated the 107 April, 1961 published at page 856 of the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3 
Sub-section(ii), on 22 April, 1961 as under: 


“In the Schedule attached to the said Notification, the Survey Plot No. 229 in respect of 
Ghrishneshwar Temple may please be read as Survey Plot No. 299.”
