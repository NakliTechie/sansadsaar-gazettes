The applicants have alleged that dumped imports of the subject goods from the subject country are 
causing material injury to the domestic industry and have requested the imposition of anti-dumping 
duties on imports of the subject goods originating in or exported from the subject country.

A. PRODUCT UNDER CONSIDERATION (PUC) 


The product under consideration in the present investigation is ‘Peroxosulphates’, also known as 
‘Persulphates’. Persulphates are a group of closely related compounds which are produced as Alkali 
Metal Salts. These include Ammonium Persulphates (APS), Potassium Persulphates (PPS), and 
Sodium Persulphates (SPS). 


The chemical formulas of the above-mentioned persulphates are (NH4)2S208, K2S208, and 
Na2S208, respectively. All three types of Persulphates have the same end-uses as an initiator and 
oxidizing agent in the textile and chemical industries. All three salts are one and the same product 
which has a ‘white crystalline powder’ appearance, is dry and soluble in water, decomposes slowly, 
and liberates oxygen while absorbing moisture. 


The unit of measurement considered in the present investigation for the product under consideration 
is Metric Tons (MT). 


The product under consideration is classified under Chapter 28 of the Customs Tariff Act, 1975 
under subheading 2833 40 00. However, the product is also imported under 2833 29 90. The customs 
classifications are indicative only, and in no way binding on the scope of the investigation. 


The applicants have contended that the three types of persulphates covered under the scope of 
product under consideration vary in terms of cost and price and may be treated as separate PCNs. 
Accordingly, following product control number (PCN) methodology is proposed: 


Type of Persulphate Code 
Ammonium Persulphate APS 
Potassium Persulphate PPS 
Sodium Persulphate SPS

B. LIKE ARTICLE 


There is no known difference between the subject goods produced by the domestic industry and the 
product under consideration imported from the subject country. The subject goods produced by the 
domestic industry are comparable to the product under consideration imported from the subject 
country in all terms including, characteristics such as physical & chemical characteristics, 
manufacturing process & technology, functions & uses, product specifications, pricing, distribution 
& marketing and tariff classification. Both products are technically and commercially substitutable 
and the consumers use them interchangeably. Therefore, for the purpose of the present investigation, 
the subject goods produced by the domestic industry are being treated as "like article" to the subject 
goods imported from the subject country.

E. DOMESTIC INDUSTRY AND STANDING 


The application has been filed by Calibre Chemicals Private Limited and UI-VR Private Limited. 
Apart from the applicants, the other known producers of the subject goods in India are Yoyo 
Chemicals, Aminisha Organics LLP, Gujarat Persalts Private Limited and Ana Industries. The 
applicants have submitted that Gujarat Persalts Private Limited and Ana Industries have stopped 
production of the subject goods. Yoyo Chemicals and Aminisha Organics LLP have supported the 
present application. 


The applicants have certified that they have not imported the subject goods from the subject country 
during the period of investigation. The applicants have related entities in the subject country. 
However, these related entities did not export the subject goods to India during the period of 
investigation. Further, UI-VR imported negligible volume of imports of the subject goods from its 
related entity in Germany. 


It is seen that both the applicants have also purchased certain quantities of the subject goods from the 
domestic market for resale, including goods imported by traders from the subject country. UI-VR 
made these purchases to maintain supplies to its existing customers when its manufacturing 
operations were affected. Calibre Chemicals made such purchases during the period when its 
production facilities for APS and SPS to meet the requirements of its existing customers and ensure 
continuity of supply. The Authority notes that these purchases were low in relation to the Indian 
demand and applicants’ own production. In view of the above, the Authority, prima facie, considers 
that the relationships and domestic purchases do not render the applicants ineligible to constitute the 
domestic industry. 


After examination of the application, the Authority notes that the applicants account for major 
proportion in the total Indian production of the like article during the period of investigation. 
Accordingly, the applicants prima facie constitute the domestic industry within the meaning of Rule 
2(b) of the Anti-Dumping Rules, 1995, and the application satisfies the requirements of Rule 5(3) of 
the said Rules. 


F. BASIS OF ALLEGED DUMPING 
Normal Value for China PR 


The applicants have claimed that China PR should be treated as a non-market economy and the 
normal value should be determined in terms of Rule- 7 of Annexure I of the Rules. The applicants 
have cited Para 8(2) of Annexure I of the Rules and has stated that the Chinese producers should be 
directed to demonstrate that market economy conditions prevail in the industry producing the subject 
goods in terms of Para 8(3) of Annexure I of the Rules. The applicants have claimed that for China 
PR, normal value should be determined in accordance with Para 7 and 8 of Annexure 1 of the Rules. 


The applicants have submitted that attempted to get evidence of the price at which the product is 
being sold by the producers globally. The applicants have proposed determination of normal value 
for China PR on the basis of the consumption price in the USA, a market economy third country. For 
this purpose, the applicants have considered the export prices of APS and SPS by UI-VR Private 
Limited and PPS by Calibre Chemicals Private Limited to their respective related parties in the USA. 
The applicants have submitted that these exports were made at profitable prices and have accordingly 
considered them as the consumption prices in the USA. The interested parties may offer their 
comments on the normal value methodology proposed by the applicants. The Authority for the 
purpose of initiation of this investigation has considered normal value for China based on price 
payable in India considering the cost of production in India after duly adjustment of selling, general 
and administrative expenses and reasonable profit. 


Export price for China 


The export price of the product under consideration has been determined by considering the CIF 
price of the product under consideration as reported in DGCIS import data. Adjustments have been 
claimed for ocean freight, marine insurance, commission, bank charges, port expenses and handling 
charges.

Dumping margin 


The normal value and the export price have been compared at ex-factory level, which prima facie 
establishes that the dumping margin with respect to the product under consideration imported from 
the subject country is not only above the de-minimis level but is significant. Thus, there is prima 
facie evidence that the product under consideration from the subject country is being dumped in the 
domestic market of India by the exporters from the subject country. 


G. INJURY AND CAUSAL LINK 


The applicants have provided prima facie evidence with respect to the injury suffered by the 
applicants due to the dumped imports. The volume of the subject imports from the subject country 
have increased over the injury period in both absolute as well as relative terms. The increase in 
imports was coupled with steep decline in import price. Imports are undercutting the prices of the 
domestic industry and causing price suppression in the domestic market. Despite increase in capacity 
and sufficient demand in the country, the applicants were unable to achieve a commensurate increase 
in production and domestic sales due to the adverse impact of the dumped imports. The applicants 
have further claimed that due to presence of subject goods, their performance has deteriorated in 
respect of profitability, ROI, cash profits. 


H. INITIATION OF ANTI-DUMPING INVESTIGATION 


On the basis of the duly substantiated written application submitted by the applicants and having 
reached satisfaction based on the prima facie evidence submitted by the applicants concerning the 
dumping of the product under consideration originating in or exported from the subject countries 
country, the consequential injury to the applicants as a result of the alleged dumping of the product 
under consideration and the causal link between such injury and the dumped imports, and in 
accordance with Section 9A of the Act read with Rule 5 of the AD Rules, the Authority, hereby, 
initiates an anti-dumping investigation to determine the existence, degree, and effect of the dumping 
with respect to the product under consideration originating in or exported from subject country and 
to recommend the appropriate amount of anti-dumping duty, which if levied, would be adequate to 
remove the injury to the domestic industry.
