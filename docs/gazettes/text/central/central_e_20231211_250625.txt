declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land situated in Tehsil Kanpur Sadar, District Kanpur Nagar in 
Uttar Pradesh State, specified in the schedule appended to that notification for the purpose of laying pipeline for the 
transportation of LPG from Kandla to Gorakhpur by the IHB Ltd for implementing the Kandla to Gorakhpur LPG

notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user of the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration, in IHB Ltd, free from all encumbrances.
