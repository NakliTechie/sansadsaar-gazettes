Election petitioner has filed a Withdrawal Application No.26 of 2022, praying that the Election Petition may 


be dismissed as withdrawn. 


On the aforesaid withdrawal application this Court passed the following order on 20.05.2022:- 


"Heard Sri Shivam Yadav alongwith Sri Saurabh Yadav, learned counsel for the Election Petitioner and Sri 
Udayan Nandan, learned counsel for the respondent - winning candidate. The Election Petitioner has filed 
a Civil Misc. Withdrawal Application No.26 of 2022 dated .05.2022. In paragraphs 2, 3 and 4 of the 
withdrawal Application the Election Petitioner has stated as under :- 


772, That the above noted petition has been filed by the petitioner challenging the Election of the respondent 
on various grounds mentioned in the petition itself, however, the petition is pending since year 20l9 and with 
the change of political circumstances and scenario, the petitioner do not wish to pursue the present petition

any further, the aforesaid decision has been taken by the petitioner without any pressure or coercion upon him 
and it is out of free will the present affidavit is being filed before this Hon'ble Court. 

3. That it is for this reason the petitioner want to withdraw the election petition, the present election petition 
therefore be allowed to be withdrawn and the same may be dismissed as withdrawn so that justice be made. 


4. That it is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to dismiss the 
aforesaid Election Petition /४०./३ of 20/9, as withdrawn without prejudice to the rights of respondents in the 
interest of justice, otherwise, the applicant shall suffer irreparable loss and injury." 


Section l09 of the Representation of the People Act, l95! provides as under :- "l09 Withdrawal of 
election petitions - 
(f) An election petition may be withdrawn only by leave of the High Court. 


(2). Where an application for withdrawal is made under sub-section (), notice thereof fixing a 
date for the hearing of the application shall be given to all other parties to the petition and 
shall be published in the Official Gazette." 


Issue notice fixing 5.07.2022 for hearing on the withdrawal application, which may also be 
published in the official gazette. 


Steps be taken by the Election Petitioner within a week.”’ 


In compliance to the aforesaid order, notices were issued and publication has also been made in the 
Government Gazette Uttar Pradesh on 04.06.2022. Copy of the Gazette has been filed by the Election - Petitioner 
alongwith compliance affidavit dated 26.07.2022 being paper No. A - 49. 


None has filed any application to oppose the aforesaid withdrawal application. 
In view of the aforesaid, the Election Petition is dismissed as withdrawn. 
Withdrawal Application No.26 of 2022 is disposed of.
