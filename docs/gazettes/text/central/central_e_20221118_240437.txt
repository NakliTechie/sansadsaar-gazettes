SMALL INDUSTRIES DEVELOPMENT BANK OF INDIA 

NOTIFICATION 

Lucknow , the 14th November , 2022 

“ Small Industries Development Bank of India Pension [ Amendment ] Regulations , 2022 " 

HRDV No. L001278933A / Staff . Gen. ( 2 ) . – In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of 
Section 52 of the Small Industries Development Bank of India Act , 1989 ( 39 of 1989 ) , the Board of 
Directors of the Small Industries Development Bank of India hereby makes the following amendments to 
the Small Industries Development Bank of India Pension Regulations , 2002 ( hereinafter referred to as 
Original Regulations ) : 

1 . 


These Regulations may be called the Small Industries Development Bank of India Pension 
[ Amendment ] Regulations , 2022 . 


These Amendments shall come into force with effect from April 01 , 2021 . 

In the Regulation 41 of the Original Regulations , for the sub - regulation ( 5 ) shall be substituted , 
namely : 


" ( 5 ) The ordinary rate of family pension shall be thirty percent of the Pay , subject to a minimum 
of Rs . 3,500 / - ( Rupees Three Thousand Five Hundred only ) per month and a maximum of Rs . 
24,495 / - ( Rupces Twenty Four Thousand Four Hundred Ninety Five only ) per month . 


Explanation : In the case of a part - time employee , the minimum and maximum amounts of the 
family pension shall be in proportion to the rate of wages applicable . " 


[ Chitra Kartik Alai ] 
Chief General Manager
