MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th May, 2025 


S.O. 208(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 298 Dated: 9/03/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-36] on the stretch of land from Km. 400.575 to Km. 465.500 (Yavatmal Wardh section) in the 
District of YAVATMAL in the state of MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Deshonnati” and “Dainik Bhaskar” 
both dated 25th March 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent authorityt; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
