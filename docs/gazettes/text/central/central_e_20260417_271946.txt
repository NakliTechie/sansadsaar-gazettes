NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 9th April, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 
5.0. 1938(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S. 0. 5276 (A) dated the 14th November, 2025 is published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 18th November, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Multitracking of Panvel-Chouk section (17.28 
Km)” and “Panvel-Chord lines between Panvel-Somtane and Panvel-Chikhli (7.54Km)” in the district of 
Raigad in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Ramprahar 
(Marathi)” and “Mid day (English)” dated 3rd December, 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections were received under section 20(D) and the same _ have _ been 
considered/disposed/disallowed by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
