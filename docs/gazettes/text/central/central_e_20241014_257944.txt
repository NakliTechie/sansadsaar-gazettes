been duly considered by the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 5 and 7 of the Explosives Act, 1884, the 
Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Explosives Rules, 2008, 
namely:-

'(18A) "discharge site" means the area immediately surrounding the fireworks used for an

c. for clause (19), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:- 


'(19) "display fireworks" means a group of manufactured fireworks to be assembled at site 
for fireworks display to produce visible or audible effects or both’; 


d. for clause (24A) the following clause shall be substituted, namely:- 


'2Q4A) "aerial fireworks" means low hazard explosives, with a lifting charge and an aerial 
function charge to cause sound, multiple sounds, light, colour and any pyro technique 
effect or combination of various effects in an assembled form ready to use at site, including 
shells and rocket, but not including fireworks item whose contrivances is of diameter less
