MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 30th November , 2022 

. 

S.O. 5563 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , S.O No. 3967 ( E ) , dated on 23rd August 2022 published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , 
Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 
of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of ( widening / Six laning with paved 
shoulder etc. , ) , maintenance , management and operation of NH - 44 in the stretch of land from Km . 9.900 to 
Km . 19.948 ( Revenue Divisional Officer , Rajendra Nagar ) Aramghar - Shamshabad section in the district of 

RANGAREDDI in the state of TELANGANA . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Namasthe Telangana " ( Telugu ) 
and “ The Hindu " ( English ) both dated 21 September 2022 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 

And whereas the competent Authority has received no objections ; 

And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
