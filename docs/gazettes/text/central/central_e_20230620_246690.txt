MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th June, 2023 


S.O. 2703(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (d) of section 2 of the Land Ports Authority of 
India Act, 2010 (31 of 2010), the Central Government hereby specifies the land port at Agartala, located in Tehsil 
Ramnagar, District West Tripura, Tripura, India, Pin Code — 799001 as integrated check post measuring 28.94 acres of 
land along the Agartala - Akhaura Road up to the Zero point on the International Border with Bangladesh as follows, 


namely: — 
(a) North East : 23°50°15.5”N and 91° 15’20.6” E; 
(b) North West : 23° 50’715.2”N and 91° 15714.9” E; 
(c) South East : 23° 50°39.5”N and 91° 15’13.0” E; 
(d) South West : 23° 50°39.6”N and 91° 15’09.7” E. 
2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
