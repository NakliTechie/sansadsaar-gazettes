S.O. 62(E).—The Central Government, exercising the powers conferred by clause (7A) of section 2 of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989), had designated the Additional District Magistrate (Finance and Revenue)/ 
Siddharthnagar as the competent authority for acquisition of land under the Act. 


Using the same authority, now Special Land Acquisition officer/Siddharthnagar is nominated competent 
authority in place of Additional District Magistrate (Finance and Revenue)/Siddharthnagar. In respect of the Special 
Railway Project of new Railway line between Khalilabad - Bahraich section in the State specified in column (4) of the 
Table below, from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette, the officer mentioned in column (2) is hereby 
authorised as the competent authority to discharge the functions as such authority under the said Act for the public 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of such Special Railway Project.
