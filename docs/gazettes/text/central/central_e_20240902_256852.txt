MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2024 


9.0. 3726(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, $.0.2550 (E), Dated:02.07.2024, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening / six-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. 44(Old.No.7) on the stretch of land from K.M. 33/130 to K.M 93/000 (Athipalli to Krishnagiri 
Section) in Thorapalli Agraharam village in Krishnagiri District in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” and “Dhina Thanthi” 
both dated 14.07.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
