NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th July, 2023 


S.O. 3151(E).—In continuation of this Department’s Notification published in the Official Gazette vide 
S.O. 4141(E) dated 15" November 2019 and in pursuance of Department of Personnel & Training order 
No. 36/01/2023 EO(SM-1) dated 13.06.2023, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by sub 
section (1) of Section 7 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016) herby appoints Shri Pramod Kumar 
Tiwari, IAS (AM:91), as Director General of the Bureau of Indian Standards by temporarily upgrading the post of 
Director General, Bureau of Indian Standards to the level of Special Secretary w.e.f. 147 June 2023 till he attains the 
extended tenure i.e. up to 237 May 2024 or until further orders whichever is earlier.
