NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th May, 2025 
No.:/2025-26 


Subject: Restoration of RoDTEP for Advance Authorisations (AAs) holders, Special Economic Zones (SEZs) 
and Export-Oriented Units (EOUs) from 0.06.2025 


S.O. 2368(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development and 
Regulation) Act, !992, read with Para .02 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, the Central Government hereby notifies 
the restoration of RODTEP for Advance Authorizations (AAs) holders, Special Economic Zones (SEZs), and Export- 
Oriented Units (EOUs) from 0.06.2025. 


2: The rates are available in Appendix 4RE including newly aligned HS codes as per the Finance Act, 2025. 
The details are available on the DGFT portal at www.dgft.gov.in, under the link 'Regulations > RODTEP’. 


Effect of this Notification: The Support under the RODTEP Scheme for exports of products manufactured from AAs, 
SEZs, and EOUs is restored with effect from 0.06.2025. 


This issues with the approval of the Minister of Commerce & Industry.
