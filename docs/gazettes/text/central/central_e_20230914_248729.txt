G.S.R. 668(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 25 of the Customs 
Act, 962 (52 of 962) and sub-section (l2) of section 3 of the Customs Tariff Act, 975 (5 of 975), the Central 
Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, hereby makes the following further 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance (Department of 
Revenue), No. 50/20!7-Customs, dated the 30" June, 207, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 785(E), dated the 30" June, 207, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, - 
(i) against S. No. 597, in column (3), for the proviso, the following provisos shall be substituted, namely: - 
“Provided that items (ii) and (iv) shall cease to have effect after 307 September, 2023. 
Provided further that items (i), (iii) and (v) shall cease to have effect after 30" September 2025.”;
