S.O. 664(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number of $.0.502(A) Dated O5February 2024 and 27 Octomber 2023, Published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under sub-section() of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act,l956(48 of l956)(hereinafter to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified int the Schedule annexed to said Notification for building (widening/Velugram Sutrakar 
etc.) maintenance,management and operation of Naitonal Highway No.848 on the stretch of land from Km.0.000 to 
Km.5.(Velugram-Sutrakar section) in District of Palghar(Thane), in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Free Press Journal" dated the 
4th February 2024 and "Pudhari"dated l4th February 2024, under sub section(3) of Section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received; objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 
same appropriately; 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section(l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section(l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
