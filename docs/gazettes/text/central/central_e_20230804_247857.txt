5.0. 3498(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway 
(Railway Board), S.O. No 2240(E) dated 22""May, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 
3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 22"May, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (i) of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution,

And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily Hindi newspaper “Dainik 
Jagran” Varansi dated 08.06.2023& English newspaper‘“Times of India” Lucknow dated 08.06.2023 under sub- 
section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, not a single objection received in the office of Competent Authority in prescribed period of 30 
days from the publication in newspaper. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, namely, Additional District Magistrate, 


(Finance & Revenue), Ghazipur, Uttar Pra desh and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 
20E of the Railways Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
