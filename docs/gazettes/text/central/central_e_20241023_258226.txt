and Industry Notification Numbers 5.0. 1464 (E) dated 87 April, 2016 respectively;

additional area of 50.8034 hectares as a part of above Special Economic Zone and the Central

2023;

(1) of section 4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special 
Economic Zones Rules, 2006, the Central Government hereby notifies an additional area of
