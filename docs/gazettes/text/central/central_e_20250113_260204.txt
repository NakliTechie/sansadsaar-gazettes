G.S.R. 3(E).—The draft of the following rules to amend the Ammonium Nitrate Rules 202, 
which Central Government proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 7 of the 
Explosives Act, 884 (4 of 884) is hereby published as required by sub-section (l) of section 8 of the 
said Act for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby, and notice is hereby given that the said 
draft shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of a period of thirty (30) days from the date on which 
the copies of the Gazette of India containing this notification are made available to the public. 

Objections or suggestions if any, to these draft rules may be sent to Director (Explosives), 
Department For Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Room No. 
252 A, Udyog Bhawan, New Delhi —00] or Email: expl-dipp @nic.in; within the period specified above;

draft rules within the period above so specified will be taken into consideration by the Central Government.
