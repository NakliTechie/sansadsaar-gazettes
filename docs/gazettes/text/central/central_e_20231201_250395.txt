9.0. 530(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O.326 Dated:3.07.2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation for 
Ludhiana-Barnala-Bathinda Greendfield Highway of NH754AD on the stretch of land from Km 34 to Km 64 in the 
district of Barnala in the state of Punjab. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hindustan Times” and “Ajit” both 
dated 6.07.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the District Revenue Officer 
Barnala has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the District Revenue Officer Barnala and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
