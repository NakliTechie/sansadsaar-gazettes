फा.सं. 18-15 / 1016- आईएनसी . – भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 ( 1947 का XLVIII ) की धारा 
14 के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है : 


जबकि , विघ्नेश्वर इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग साइंसेज , विजयनगर , हुबली , जिला धारवाड़ - 580032 , कर्नाटक 
जीएनएम कोर्स चलाने के लिए कर्नाटक राज्य सरकार और कर्नाटक नर्सिंग काउंसिल द्वारा मान्यता प्राप्त है । 


वहीं , प्रधान सचिव , स्वास्थ्य और परिवार कल्याण विभाग , कमरा नंबर 105 , पहली मंजिल , विकास सौदा , 
बैंगलोर - 560001 , कर्नाटक को भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के 
लिए एक नोटिस सं . 18-15 / 1016- आईएनसी / 12350 दिनांक 19/02/2021 जारी किया गया था । नोटिस दिनांक 
19/02/2021 की एक प्रति विघ्नेश्वर इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग साइंस , विजयनगर , हुबली , जिला धारवाड़ - 
कर्नाटक को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी 
सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 14th October , 2022 

F.No. 18 - 15 / 1016 - INC : – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at Vijaynagar , Hubballi , Distt . Dharwar 
- 580032 , Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Nursing Council to conduct 
GNM course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-15 / 1016 - INC / 12353 dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family Welfare Department , 
Room No. 105 , 01st Floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore – 560001 , Karnataka was issued . Copy of the notice dated 
19/02/2021 was also enclosed to Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at Vijaynagar , Hubballi , 
Distt . Dharwar 580032 , Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council received a letter dated 10/03/2021 from Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing Sciences . 
Thereafter the Council issued letter dated 31/03/2021 to the Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing Sciences . However no 
action , as required , has been taken by the Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing Sciences . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
26/12/2020 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run GNM 
Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Vighneshwara Institute of Nursing 
Sciences situated at Vijaynagar , Hubballi , Distt . Dharwar- 580032 , Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered 
only in the State of Karnataka subject to the orders of State government of Karnataka .
