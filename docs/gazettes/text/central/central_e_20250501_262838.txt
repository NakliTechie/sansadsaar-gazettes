G.S.R. 284(E).— WHEREAS the draft of certain rules further to amend the Explosives 
Rules, 2008, were published, vide notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Commerce and Industry (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) number, G.S.R. 30 
(E), dated thel3" January, 2025 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(0), inviting objections or suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby before the expiry of 
a period of thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Gazette containing the said notification 
were made available to the public; 


AND WHEREAS the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public on the 
3" January, 2025; 


AND WHEREAS no objection or suggestions were received from the public within the said period; 
NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Explosives Act, 884 (4 
of 884), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Explosives 


Rules, 2008, namely:- 


l. (.) These rules may be called the Explosives (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

shall be omitted;
