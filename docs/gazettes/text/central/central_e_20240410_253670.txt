S.O. 674(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.0.3729(E) dated 2.08.2023 and its Corrigendum number S.0.4866(E) Dated: 08.09.2023, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of 
Section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening/four/six-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway ४०.।6 on the 
stretch of land from Six laning of existing 4-lane road from Gundugolanu (Design Km. 023.280) to Kalaparru 
(Design Km. 050.680) of NH-5 (New NH-6) (Design Length: 27.400 Km) in the State of Andhra Pradesh under

NHDP Phase-V under Bharatmala Pariyojana on EPC Mode (Package-I) in the District of Eluru in the State of Andhra 
Pradesh hereby declares its intension to acquire such lands; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India" and "Prajasakthi" 
both dated 24-] [-2023, under sub-section (3) of Section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
