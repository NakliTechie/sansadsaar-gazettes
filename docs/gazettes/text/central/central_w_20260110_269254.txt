Mission 
To promote application of science and technology in the hydrocarbon sector (excluding upstream sector). 
The terms of reference of the Committee will be as under:— 


i To identify and promote development and development of technologies related to hydrocarbons for 
production of fuels and feed stocks for chemicals and petrochemicals. 


ii. 10 ensure optimum processing of hydrocarbons raw material for use as fuels and chemicals. 

ili. 10 ensure reduction in carbon footprint of hydrocarbon sector and energy efficient operations. 

iv. To promote application of alternate energy sources in hydrocarbon sector. 

v. To identify long term technological needs of hydrocarbon sector. 

vi. 10 facilitate collaborative research programmes among oil and gas organizations and other stake holders. 


vii. To examine and deliberate on R&D project proposals submitted by various R&D organizations and 
academic institutions for funding by OIDB through Centre for High Technology (CHT). 


viii. To formulate mechanism for demonstration and fast track commercialization of indigenously developed 
technologies. 


ix. To promote dissemination of information on latest developments in hydrocarbon sector.
