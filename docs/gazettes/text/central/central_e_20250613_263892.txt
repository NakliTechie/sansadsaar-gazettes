NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |3th June, 2025 

S.O. 2594(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number 5.0 2220 (E) dated 09 June 202] 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening 
/ four laning etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 60 Ext. in the stretch of land 
from Km 9.680 to km 70.000 ( Ahmednagar- Baramati — Phaltan section )in the district of Anmadnagar in the state 
of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Dainik Loksatta” and “ Dainik 


Shrigonda Citizen” Both Dated 3 December 2024 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 
And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 


absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
