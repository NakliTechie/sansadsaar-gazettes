लिया कि मई/जून 2024 से इंटरमीडिएट और फाउंडेशन पाठ्यक्रम की परीक्षाएं वर्ष में दो बार मौजूदा प्रथा से वर्ष में तीन 
बार मई/जून, सितंबर और जनवरी में आयोजित की जाएंगी।

परीक्षा आयोजित की जाती है, उसके पहले दिन या उससे पहले अन्य पात्रता के साथ कम से कम चार महीने की अवधि के

परीक्षा के लिए अर्थात संस्थान के अध्ययन बोर्ड के साथ पंजीकृत और इस आशय का प्रमाण पत्र प्रस्तुत करता है कि वह

THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 
[Set up by an Act of Parliament] 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the I5th April, 2024 
(Chartered Accountants) 


No. 3-CA (EXAM)/2024.—In its continuous endeavour for the benefit of students, Council of the Institute 
decided that from May/June 2024 onwards, Intermediate & Foundation course Examinations will be held thrice in a 
year in the month of May/June, September and January from the existing practice of twice a year. 


Due to more frequency of exams, students will get more opportunity to appear in the exam. 


Students who are fulfilling the eligibility criteria for Admission to the Foundation course examination i.e 
registered with the Board of Studies of the Institute for a minimum period of four months on or before the ३7 day of 
the month in which the examination is held along with other eligibility conditions specified in Regulation 25F and for 
Intermediate course Examination i.e registered with the Board of Studies of the Institute and produces a certificate to 
the effect that he has undergone a study course for a period of not less than eight months as on the first day of the 
month in which the examination is held along with other eligibility conditions specified in Regulation 28G (details at 
https://resource.cdn.icai.org/75788ca-regulations-6]309.pdf) can appear in the next eligible exam. 


It may be noted that the CA Final course examination will continue to be held twice a year in the month of 
May and November. 


Students are advised to take note of the above and act accordingly. 
CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secy.
