MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2lst July, 2025 


G.S.R. 486(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Mines and Minerals 
(Development and Regulation) Act, !957 (67 of 957), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules further to amend the Minerals (Other than Atomic and Hydro Carbons Energy Minerals) Concession 
Rules, 206, namely:— 


. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the Minerals (Other than Atomic and 
Hydro Carbons Energy Minerals) Concession (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Minerals (Other than Atomic and Hydro Carbons Energy Minerals) Concession Rules, 20l6,— 
(a) in rule 44, clause (ia) shall be omitted; 
(b) in rule 45,— 
(i) in sub-rule (7),— 


(A) for the words “and Titanium in”, the words “, Titanium, Zirconium and Caesium in” shall be 
substituted; 


(B) for the words “and Titanium sponge metal”, the words “, Titanium sponge metal, Zirconium 
sponge and Caesium metal” shall be substituted; 


(0) after sub-rule (0), the following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely:— 


“(I) The Indian Bureau of Mines shall publish average sale price of Rubidium in Indian Rupees on 
the basis of prices published by the United States Geological Survey or other reputed publications for 
Rubidium Carbonate for the latest available calendar year by multiplying such price with the 
following, namely:— 


(a) average reference rate of Reserve Bank of India for the said calendar year; and 
(b) the conversion factor of .35.”; 
(c) in rule 67,— 
(i) in sub-rule (), — 


(A) for the words “Atomic Minerals Division”, the words “Atomic Minerals Directorate for 
Exploration and Research” shall be substituted; 


(B) for the words “the State Government shall”, the words, brackets and figures “under the second 
proviso to sub-section () of section 4, the Central Government shall” shall be substituted; 


(ii) for sub-rule (3), the following sub-rule shall be substituted, namely:— 


“(3) The Central Government may revoke a notification issued under sub-rule (.) before the expiry of 
the period stated in the notification.”; 


(d) in rule 68,— 
(0) the words and letter “Part C of” shall be omitted; 


(ii) in the first proviso, for the words “State Government”, the words “Central Government” shall be 
substituted.
