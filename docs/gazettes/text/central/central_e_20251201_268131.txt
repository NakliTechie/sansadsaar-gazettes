MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
(PHRD Division) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist December, 2025 


G.S.R. 879(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section | (f) of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act, 202 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Ports, Shipping 
& Waterways (PHRD Division) G. S. R. No. 684 (E) dated 25" September 2023, the Central Government hereby 
appoints Shri Rithesh Kumar, Vice President, Canara Port Workers Union (R) (INTUC) as a member representing 
employees’ interests on the Board of New Mangalore Port Authority (NMPA) with immediate effect. 


2: The appointment of Member of the Board shall be subject to the provisions of the Major Port Authorities Act, 
202l. The appointment is valid till the date of validity of check-off, or until the attainment of age of superannuation of 
the Members, or till further orders, whichever is earliest.
