S.O. 5665(E)—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of section l4 of the Energy 
Conservation Act, 200l (52 of 200l), the Central Government, in consultation with the Bureau of Energy 
Efficiency, hereby specifies, the following norms for process and energy consumption standards for the star labelling

similar purposes, being manufactured, or imported in India shall, meet the performance requirements of the 
induction hob prescribed in IS 904:2022, measurement of standby power requirement of the induction hob 
prescribed in IEC 6230:20ll, safety requirement prescribed in IS 302-2-6 and a label to be affixed on Induction hob 
shall display star label based on the Energy Consumption matrix.
