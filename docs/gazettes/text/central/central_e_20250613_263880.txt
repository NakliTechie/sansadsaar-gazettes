NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th June, 2025 


S.O. 2638(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways, Northern Railway (Construction Organization), number, 8.0. 684(E), dated the 7 th February , 2025, 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (1) (hereafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the 
said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, “Chord 
Line Connecting DJD (NER)-SHG-BWI Bypassing SHG (Shahganj Jn.) 5.7 Km” Reg- villages Godila. Takha (West) 
& Takha (East) Of Tehsil: Shahganj, District: Jaunpur in the state of Uttar Pradesh ; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers Dainik Jagran, dated 
the 15th February 2025 , Hindustan Times dated 15th February 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said 
Act;

conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the Railways Act, 1989, the Central Government hereby declares that
