MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th October, 2025 


S.R.O. 38.—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 13 of the National Cadet Corps Act, 1948 (31 of 
1948), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to further amend the National Cadet Corps Rule, 
1948, namely :- 


1. Short title and commencement :- 
(i) These Rules may be called the National Cadet Corps (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 
(ii) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Cadet Corps Rules (0), 1948, in the schedule वा, in paragraph 2, fair sub-paragraph (0), the 
following sub paragraph shall be substituted, namely:- 


(b) Junior Division Officers 


On being first commissioned - Second Officer 
After 8 years of commissioned service - First officer 
After 15 years of commissioned Service - Chief Officer 


[F. No. 4/24/2022-D(GS-VD]
