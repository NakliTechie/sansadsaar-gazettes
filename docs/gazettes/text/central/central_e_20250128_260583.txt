MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th January, 2025 


9.0. 509(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O 712(E) dated 14 February 
2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 3A of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening / tow lane with paved shoulder/ four with earthen shoulder etc.) maintenance, management 
and operation of National Highway No.-752 H in the stretch of land Km 0.000 to km 44.900 (Sub Divisional 
Officer, Vaijapur, in the District Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 712(E) dated 14 February 2024, has been 
published in “ Dainik Lokmat” and “ Lokmat Times” Both Dated 25/02/2024 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A 
of the said Act; 


And Whereas Objections field under section 3-C considered and settled the same appropriately, 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
