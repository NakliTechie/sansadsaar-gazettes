MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Northern Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 14th August, 2023 


S.O. 3625(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 
1989 (amended through bill No. 24 of 1989), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has been given in the Schedule 
annexed hereto, is required for construction and proposed additional land for access of farmers in block section 
between Rudauli-Rozagaon section in connection with doubling of Barabanki-Akbarpur (161Km.) Project in 
District-Ayodhya in the State of Uttar Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from the date of publication of this notification 
in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition officer, 
Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the 
objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such

objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
