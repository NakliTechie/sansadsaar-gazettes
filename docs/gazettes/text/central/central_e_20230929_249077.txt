और जबकि केंद्र सरकार ने, विस्फोटक उद्योग के हितधारकों के साथ विचार-विमर्श के बाद, सार्वजनिक हित में

बंद कर दिया जाए; 


अत:, अब, विस्फोटक अधिनियम, 7884 (4884 का 4) की धारा 6 की उप-धारा (॥) के खंड (क) द्वारा प्रदत्त 
शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, केंद्र सरकार एतद्दारा पूरे देश में इलेक्ट्रिक डेटोनेटर (क्लास 6 डिविजन 3 अथवा यू एन 
क्लास 4 डिविजन ar विस्फोटक) के विनिर्माण, कब्जे और आयात पर अप्रैल, 2025 से प्रतिबंध लगाती है।

MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th September, 2023 


S.O. 4255(E).—Whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that the explosive, namely, electric 
detonator (explosive of Class 6 Division 3 or U N Class | Division |) is of a dangerous character; 


And whereas it is expedient for the security concerns and public safety to prohibit the manufacture, 
possession and import of electric detonator (explosive of Class 6 Division 3 or U N Class | Division ); 


And whereas the Central Government, after consultation with stakeholders of explosives industry, in the 
public interest has decided that the production of the said explosive shall be completely phased out by the end of 
financial year 2024-2025; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (l) of section 6 of the 
Explosives Act, 884 (4 of l884), the Central Government hereby prohibits the manufacture, possession and import of 
the electric detonator (explosive of Class 6 Division 3 or UN Class | Division |) throughout the country with effect 
from the Ist day of April, 2025.
