MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
(PHRD Division) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th August, 2025 


G.S.R. 533(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section 1 (f) of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act, 2021 and in partial modification of the Notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Ports, 
Shipping & Waterways (PHRD Division) G.S.R. No. 217 (E) dated 19" March, 2024, the Central Government hereby 
appoints Shri Tapas Kumar Biswas, 2nd Class Driver, Marine Department in place of Shri Asim Kumar Sutradhar, 
General Secretary, National Union of Waterfront Workmen (I) as Member representing the interests of employees on 
the Board of Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Authority with immediate effect. The appointment of Member of the 
Board shall be subject to the provisions of the Major Port Authorities Act, 2021. The appointment is valid till the date 
of validity of check-off, or until the attainment of age of superannuation of the Members, or till further orders, 
whichever is earliest.
