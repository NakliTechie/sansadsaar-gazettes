The BoAA is a representative body of academicians/experts from the textile field and the 
Principals/Directors of IHTs. The BoAA has been constituted by merging the Board of Studies and 
the Board of Examiners on the recommendations of the 45th Governing Body Meeting of IIHTs. 
BoAA shall be responsible for framing of curriculum &syllabus for the Diploma and Post Diploma 
programs conducted by various IIHTs (Indian Institute of Handloom Technology) across the Union 
of India. The Board is also responsible for the smooth conduct of examinations at all these ITHTs. 


The BoAA should work to develop an interface between the IIHTs and the Handloom Industry. 
They shall revise the syllabus from time to time taking into account the demands and needs of the 
Handloom and Textile Industry. 


The Chairman BoAAjointly with the Controller of Examination shall recommend the successful 
candidates ofII[HTs to the Chairman Governing Body for issuing the Diploma and Post Diploma 
Certificates. 


The Chairman, BoAA jointly with Controller of examinations shall issue Semester examination 
marksheets and consolidated marksheets of Diploma program after following due procedures.
