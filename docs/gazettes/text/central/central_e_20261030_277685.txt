NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 29th October, 2026. 
Under clause 37A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 


S.O. 5937(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of extended loop in UP & 
DN direction at Kedgaon station between Pune — Daund section” as Railway project under Central 
Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Pune District of Maharashtra State, 
for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette.
