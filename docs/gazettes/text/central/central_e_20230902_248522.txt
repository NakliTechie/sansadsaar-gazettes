NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2023 


No. RS. 1/3/2023-L.—The following Order by the President dated the 2" September, 2023, is published for 
general information:- 


“In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by clause (1) of article 85 of the Constitution, I hereby 
summon the Rajya Sabha to meet at New Delhi on Monday, the 18" September, 2023 at 11.00 A.M. 


September 2, 2023 Sd/- 
PRESIDENT”
