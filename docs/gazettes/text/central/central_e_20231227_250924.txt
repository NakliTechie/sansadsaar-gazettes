G.S.R. 97(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the

Toxicology Recruitment Rules, 202]. namely:-

the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 


“General Central Service, Group B Gazetted, Non-Ministerial. “
