MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[South Eastern Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 28th April, 2026 


S.O. 222(E).— Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Railways (South Eastern Railway/Construction Organization), number 942, S.0.986(E), dated 274 May 
2025 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (॥) herein after referred to as the 
said notification issued under sub-section (l) of section 204 of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989)(herein 
after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intension to acquire the land specified 
in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely ‘Buramara-Chakulia (59.96 km) new line’, pertains to 6.20 Km section in 
the District of Mayurbhanj in the State of Odisha. 


And, whereas, the substance of said notification number 942, S.0.986(E), dated 274 May 2025 was 
published in the daily newspapers, namely ‘SAMBAD’ (Odiya) published on 5.05.2025 and ‘Times of 
India’ (English) Bhubaneswar Edition on |5.05.2025 under section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas objections were received within the schedule time period and disposed of by the 
Competent Authority under section 20D of the said Act; 


As such, the corrigendum in amendment of said notification number 942, S.0.986(E), dated 27 
May 2025 issued under section 20A has been notified in Gazette of India vide number 2038, S.O. 2(E), 
dated 27" April, 2026. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted his report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on this publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
