3. Conditions of License 
No cultivator shall be granted license unless he/she satisfies that:

Central Bureau of Narcotics/ Narcotics Commissioner to the cultivators. 
4. Maximum Area 


(i) All the cultivators eligible under clause 2 above will be issued license of 0.10 Hectare in one plot only. This

(ii) Cultivators will be permitted to take on lease, land belonging to others, to make up the licensed area, if they so 
desire.

(i) A minimum qualifying yield (MQY—M) of 5.9 Kg Morphine/ Hectare of Morphine in opium tendered should

following year i.e. 2026-27. 


(ii) Morphine content of opium tendered during 2025-26 may become the basis for payment for the crop year 2025- 
26, if the Government decides to do so in this regard. 


(iii) Cultivators who had fully ploughed back their entire poppy during crop year 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25 
would not be entitled for license in the crop year 2026-27, if they have also ploughed back their crop fully in 
the crop year 2025-26. 


6. Condonable Limit 


If the area actually cultivated is up to 5% in excess of the licensed area, such excess cultivation may be 
condoned.

the Crop Year 2025-26, shall provide in the Application Form No. 1, the details of owner of the plot, survey 
number etc of the plot in which they are intending to cultivate opium poppy crop as well as any other details 
as may be suitably and reasonably notified by the Narcotics Commissioner. 


(ii) These General Licensing Conditions are without prejudice to the right of the Narcotics Commissioner/ Deputy 
Narcotics Commissioner to issue/ withhold a license whenever it is deemed proper to do so in accordance with 
the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 and the Rules made thereunder. 


(ili) The license shall be subject to the condition that any field may be taken over for any research that may be 
conducted by the Government directly or in collaboration with any specialized Institution or Agency for 
obtaining opium gum or poppy straw without extraction of opium. The cultivator whose field is selected for 
research shall be considered for license for the next year, if he/ she has tendered the stipulated MQY-M and is 
otherwise eligible. The area taken over for research will not be taken into account while calculating the average 
yield. 


(iv) For the purpose of making payment of opium tendered by the cultivator, on the basis of analysis by the 
Government Opium and Alkaloid Works, Neemuch or Ghazipur, either on the quantity of opium tendered by 
a farmer calculated at 70° consistency or on the morphine content basis, if government decides to do so.
