G.S.R. 24(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section I5 of the Government Savings 
Promotion Act, 873 (5 of 873), the Central Government hereby makes the following Scheme to further 
amend the Government Savings Promotion General Rules, 208, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement. — () These rules may be called the Government Savings Promotion 
General (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the official gazette. 


2. Inthe Government Savings Promotion General Rules, 208, in the Schedule I, under Fee to be 


charged for services, the words and figures “(b) Cancellation or change of nomination — %50” shall 
be deleted.
