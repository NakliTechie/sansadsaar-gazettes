F. No. N-16/1/2021-PLAN.—As per Government of India’s Resolution No. 17015/7/2018-RI Cell 
dated 27.03.2019 regarding National Commission for Safai Karamcharis (NCSK), the President is pleased to

31.03.2025 (i.e. the tenure of the Commission).
