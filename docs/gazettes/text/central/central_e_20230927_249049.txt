MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FORESTS AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th September, 2023 


S.O. 4245(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(l), clause (v) and clause (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986), read with sub-rule 
(4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate 
change, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, section 3, sub-section(ii), vide S.O. number 272 (E), 
dated the 27" August, 204, namely :— 


In the said notification,— 


(i) in paragraph 2, in sub-paragraph (), for the portion beginning with the words “The State Government 
shall” and ending with the words “the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate change’, the 
following shall be substituted, namely:- 


“The State Government shall, within a period of two years with effect from 26.09.2023, in accordance 
with this notification prepare a Zonal Master Plan for the purpose of the Eco-sensitive Zone”; 


(ii) in paragraph 3, in sub-paragraph (0), the words “approved by the Ministry of Environment, Forests and 
Climate Change”, shall be omitted.
