MINISTRY OF TEXTILES 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 28th June, 2024 


S.O. 2500(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section (.) of Section-3 of the Jute Packaging 
Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 987 (0 of 987), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry of Textiles, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii) vide number S.O. 5459(E), dated the 26" 
December, 2023, namely:- 


In the said Order, in the Preamble, in paragraph 4, for the words, figures and letters “up to the 30 June, 
2024”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 30" September, 2024 or until further orders, whichever is earlier” shall 
be substituted. 


[F. No. J-7/2/2023-Jute Section] 
PRAJAKTA L. VERMA, Jt. Secy. 


Note: The principal Order was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) 
vide number S.O. 5459(E), dated the 267 December, 2023.
