New Delhi, the !2th September, 2023 


S.O. 4039(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below is required for 
building (Upgradation and reconstruction Two/Four-Laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway No.548D in the stretch of land from Km. 204.500 to Km. 208.775 (Starting From Moha to Sautada) in 
Patoda Taluka and District Beed in the State of Maharashtra. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Subdivisional Officer, Patoda in 
writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making 
such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order either allow or disallow the 
objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority
