New Delhi, the 25th August, 2026 


5.0. 4730(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 9.0. 2926(E) Dated 8th June,2026 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (Two lane/Four lane/Widening/Toll Plaza etc.) Maintenance, management 
and operation of National Highway No.752G on the stretch of land from Km. 64/000 to Km. 0/40 Satana to 
Mangrul (Section — IID) in the district of NASHIK in the state ofp MAHARASHTRA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Lokmat Times (English)” 
and “Dainik Maharashtra Times (Marathi)” both dated 3/06/2026; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority Deputy Collector (Land Acquisition) 
National Highway Project, Nashik and in exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the 
said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
