5. Seniority.—() The relative seniority of members of the cadre shall be fixed by the Authority and unless

commencement of these rules. 


(2) All members of the cadre included in the initial formation of the cadre in any grade shall rank senior to all

(3) The inter-se seniority of persons appointed to the same grade on direct recruitment basis shall be determined

selection shall rank senior to those appointed as a result of a subsequent selection. 


(4) The inter-se seniority of persons appointed to the same grade on promotion basis shall be determined by their 
relative seniority in the lower grade from which they are promoted. 


(5) In the case of a person who is initially appointed on deputation and absorbed later, his seniority in the grade in 
which he or she is absorbed shall normally be counted from the date of absorption, if he or she has, however, been 
holding already (on the date of absorption) the same or equivalent grade on regular basis in his or her parent department, 
such regular service in the grade shall also be taken into account in fixing his/her seniority, subject to the condition that 
he or she shall be given seniority from — 

- the date he or she has been holding the post on deputation, (or)

department, whichever is earlier.

8. Medical facilities. — The officials of the Authority, both serving and retired, shall be entitled to medical 
facilities as specified in Schedule-II:

or public sector undertakings, shall be entitled to medical facilities.

appointed prior to the date of introduction of the National Pension System in the Authority, shall have the option either 
to continue with the Contributory Provident Fund or subscribe to the National Pension System: 
Provided that the officials appointed on or after the date of introduction of the National Pension System shall 
subscribe to the National Pension System. 


(2) The officials of the Authority appointed on deputation, shall continue to be governed by the Provident Fund 
Scheme as are applicable to them in their parent organisation: 


Provided that the Authority shall recover contribution towards Provident Fund from such officials and remit 
the amount alongwith employer’s contribution to the lending organisation and any loss of interest on account of late 
remittance shall be borne by the Authority. 


20. Travelling allowance. — The officials of the Authority while on tours, including outside India, shall be entitled 
to allowances as are applicable to the officers of the Central Government drawing the pay and allowances in 
corresponding level, from time to time. 


2i. Gratuity. — The officials of the Authority, other than those on deputation, shall be eligible for gratuity in 
accordance with the provisions of the Payment of Gratuity Act, !972 (39 of 972). 

22. Group insurance. — The officials of the Authority, other than those on deputation, shall be entitled to group 
insurance scheme formulated by the Authority. 

23. Pension and other retirement benefits. — The officials of the Authority, other than those on deputation, shall 


not be eligible for pension: 


Provided that the officials of the Authority, excluding the Chairperson, Members and those appointed on 
deputation, appointed on or after the I*t January, 2004 shall be governed by the National Pension System or 
the Unified Pension Scheme regulated under the provisions of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development 
Authority Act, 20I3 (23 of 203). 


24. Leave. — The Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 972, as amended from time to time shall mutatis mutandis 
apply to the officials of the Authority. 
25. Disciplinary proceedings and impositions of penalties. — In the matter of conduct, discipline and imposition 


of penalties, the Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, !964 and the Central Civil Services (Classification, 
Control and Appeal) Rules, [965 and the instructions issued by the Central Government from time to time, 
shall mutatis mutandis apply to the officials of the Authority. 

26. Other conditions of service. — Other conditions of service of the officials of the Authority for which no 
specific provision has been provided, shall be governed by such rules as are from time to time applicable to the 
officers of the Central Government drawing the pay and allowances in corresponding levels.

SCHEDULE-II 
(See rule 8) 
MEDICAL FACILITIES 
(A) OUT-PATIENT TREATMENT 
(!) The Authority shall prepare and maintain a panel of authorised medical practitioners area wise with 
registration number of the concerned registered medical practitioner. 


(2) The out-patient treatment shall be taken from the authorised or notified medical practitioner from the panel 
maintained by the Authority. 


(3) Ifan employee is receiving treatment from the outpatient department of an empanelled hospital or Central 
Government Health Scheme recognised hospital, the attending physician may be treated as deemed 
authorised or notified medical practitioner for the Authority. 


Explanation.-. For the purposes of this paragraph ‘authorised or notified medical practitioner’ means a registered 
medical practitioner having a degree recognised under the National Medical Commission Act, 209 (30 of 20I9) or a 
registered medical practitioner holding degree or diploma in Ayurveda or Unani or Siddha or Homoeopathy of not less 
than four years duration from a recognised university or statutory Board or Council or faculty of Indian Medicine and 
Homoeopathy and equivalent, specified in the Schedules of the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine 
Act, 2020 (4 of 2020) and the National Commission for Homeopathy Act, 2020 (5 of 2020), as the case may be. 


(4) The officials of the Authority and their family members including dependents shall be entitled for the 
reimbursement of medical expenses on actual basis for non-hospitalisation cases, on production of 
prescription from the authorised or notified medical practitioner along with the original bills or cash memos 
of medicines and tests. 


Explanation. — For the purposes of this paragraph, ‘family’ has the same meaning as assigned to it in the Central 
Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 944. 


(5) Reimbursement in case of treatment during foreign visit shall be in accordance with the Central Services 
(Medical Attendance) Rules, !944, as amended from time to time. 


(B) IN-PATIENT TREATMENT 
(!) The Authority shall prepare and maintain a panel of hospitals or health care organisations or diagnostic 
centres by executing Memorandum of Understanding with each of these organisations already included in 
the panel of the Central Government Health Scheme and are willing to provide in-patient or out-patient 


treatment to the officials of the Authority as per the rates specified by the Ministry of Health and Family 
Welfare, from time to time. 


(2) The officials of the Authority and their family members including dependents shall get medical treatment 
in any of the empanelled hospital or diagnostic centre on the advice of authorised or notified medical 
practitioner. 


(3) The rates admissible for treatment and tests or investigations including hospital accommodation, package 
rates for specified medical or surgical procedures, nursing home facility, etc. shall be as per the rates 
specified by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, from time to time. 


(4) The officials of the Authority shall be entitled for in-patient treatment at the empanelled hospitals. 


(5) Incase of retired employees, their entitlements shall be determined in accordance with their pay drawn at 
the time of retirement. 


(6) In the case of an emergency, the officials of the Authority may directly go to any private or recognised 
private hospital or diagnostic centre and the charges paid for medical expenses, shall be reimbursed, as per 
the rates specified by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: Provided that where rates are not specified, 
the expenses incurred shall be reimbursed on actual basis. 


(7) The empanelled hospitals or diagnostic centres, shall charge the rates approved for the package rate. 


(8) The credit facility shall be given in emergency cases to the officials of the Authority undertaking treatment 
in these hospitals or diagnostic centres and the bill shall be reimbursed as per approved rates by the 
Authority: 


Provided that in case empanelled hospitals or diagnostic centres insist for immediate payment of cost for 
treatment, hospital accommodation, medicines, various tests, the Authority may sanction advance payment 
of ninety per cent. of the approximate expenses as per package rates specified by the Government and release 
the amount to the empanelled hospital or diagnostic centre through cheque or digital mode.
