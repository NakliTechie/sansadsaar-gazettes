Man-made heritage sites.- Buildings, structures, artefacts, areas and precincts of historical, architectural,

(b) the local authorities shall draw up plans for the segregation of solid wastes into biodegradable and non-

(c) the biodegradable material shall be recycled preferably through composting or vermiculture;
