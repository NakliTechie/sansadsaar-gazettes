S.O. 1422(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) and (2) of section 16 read with sub-

makes the following Order, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) This Order may be called the Footwear made from All Rubber and all 
Polymeric Material and its Components (Quality Control) Order, 2024. 


(2) It shall come into force with effect from the Ist day of August, 2024.
