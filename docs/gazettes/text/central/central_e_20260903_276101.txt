(SEZ Division) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist September, 2026 


5.0. 4888(E).—Whereas, M/s. Infosys Limited, had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic 
Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up an Information Technology and 
Information Technology Enabled Services Special Economic Zone at Plot No. 38, Old Mahabalipuram Road, 
Sholinganallur, Kancheepuram District, Tamil Nadu; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, had notified an area of 5.37 hectares at 
the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number S.O. 277(E) dated 
6* January, 2020; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Infosys Limited has now proposed for de-notification of 0.0792 hectares from the 
above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Tamil Nadu has given its approval to the proposal vide letter 
No.3535798/MIB. /2026-2 dated 7'" June, 2026; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Madras Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of an area of 0.0792 hectares of the above the Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that the requirements under sub-section (8) of 
section 3 of the said Act and other related requirements are fulfilled;

Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006, the Central 
Government hereby de-notifies an area of 0.0792 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone, thereby making the
