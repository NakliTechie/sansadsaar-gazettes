परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. का.आ.492(अ) तारीख 05/02/2024, जो भारत के

the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH17 in the stretch of land from Km. 5.873 to Km. 6.34 (DC Office, Dhubri) in the district of DHUBRI 
in the state of ASSAM

Sentinel” both dated 14.02.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;

settled the same appropriately;
