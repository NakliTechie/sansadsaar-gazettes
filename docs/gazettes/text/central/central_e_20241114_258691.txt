MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Central Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Prayagraj, the 24th October, 2024 


S.O. 4948(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North Central 
Railway, Construction department), Notification number, 5.0. 270 (E) dated the I5th June, 2024 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3 Sub Section (ii), dated lIth July, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) 
and issue under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act. 2008 (/ lof 2008) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed the said 
notification for the purpose of Design, Supply, Erection, Testing & Commissioning of Sub-Sectioning & Paralleling Post (SSP) 
of Special Railway Project, namely Special Railway Project of Raising of Speed to 60kmph/200kmph on existing 
New Delhi-Hawrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in District Bulandshahr of the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers "Amar Ujala" and ''Times 
of India" Dated 3lth July, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, all the objections received in this project have been disposed of by the Competent Authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its report the 
Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
