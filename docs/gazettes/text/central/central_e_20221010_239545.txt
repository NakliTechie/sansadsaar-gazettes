INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-709 / 2000 - INC . — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Aishwarya School of Nursing situated at No. 1A , Survey No. 84 , Anubhavnagar , 
Mudalapalya , Distt . Bangalore - 560072 , Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka 
and Karnataka State Nursing Council to conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice No. 18-709 / 2000 - INC / 12350 dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) , Health and 
Family Welfare Department , Room No. 105 , 1st floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore - 560001 , Karnataka was 
issued . Copy of the notice dated 19/02/2021 was also enclosed to Aishwarya School of Nursing situated at 
No. 1A , Survey No. 84 , Anubhavnagar , Mudalapalya , Distt . Bangalore – 560072 , Karnataka . In the 
notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as 
respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Aishwarya School of Nursing . Even the institution Aishwarya School of Nursing has not responded to 
the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection under 
section 13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only that 
the Council would be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct 
nursing courses . Refusal for inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the 
requisite Teaching , Clinical or infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 
declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from 
Aishwarya School of Nursing situated at No. 1A , Survey No. 84 , Anubhavnagar , Mudalapalya , Distt . 
Bangalore - 560072 , Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject 
to orders of State Government of Karnataka .
