Government of India, in the erstwhile Ministry of Agriculture, (Department of Agriculture and 
Cooperation), dated the 37 February, 1997, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii), vide number 5.0. 79(E), dated the 47 February, 1997, namely:-

substituted, namely:-

General (Plant protection), Indian Council of
