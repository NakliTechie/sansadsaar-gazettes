CORAM: AMIT BORKAR, J. 
DATED: APRIL 24, 2023 
P.C.: 
1. On the last occasion, this Court passed following order: 


“1. Perusal of earlier orders indicate that the petitioner has consistently remained absent. This Court 
by order dated 7 February 2020 notified that the petitioner intends to withdraw the election 
petition. Thereafter, on 28" February 2020, none appeared for petitioner and, therefore, the 
matter is kept for dismissal. 


2. Since the petitioner thereafter has remained absent, stand over to 24 April 2023. To be shown 
under the caption for dismissal.” 


2. Today, none appears for the petitioner. It appears that the petitioner is not interested in prosecuting the 
election petition. 


3. The Apex Court in P. Nalla Thampy Thera v. B.L. Shankar reported in AIR 1984 SC 135 has laid down 
parameters for dismissal of Election Petition. On consideration of the conduct of the petitioner on earlier occasions, it 
appears that the petitioner is not interested in prosecuting the election petition. 


4. The election petition, therefore, stands dismissed for non-prosecution. No costs. 


5. In view of dismissal of the election petition, all interim/ interlocutory applications stand disposed of. 


AMIT BORKAR, J.
