NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 23rd December, 2025 


Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 605(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 4254 (E) dated 22" September 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
IL, section 3 Sub-section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section (l) of 
section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (ll of 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (! of 2008), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, “Devlali - Provision of C&W examination and stabling 
facilities for Kumbh Mela 2027” District Nashik in the State of Maharashtra, in the district of Nashik in the State 
of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for SLAO/SDO NASHIK daily 
newspapers namely “Dainik Aapla Mahanagar (English)” & “Dainik Saimat (Marathi)” Dated 0l-0-2025 & 
30-09-2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And objections have been received in this village. The objections of the affected account holders on the said 
notification have been received within the time limit. Hearing on the said objections received was conducted by the 
competent authority Assistant Collector & Sub-Divisional Officer, Nashik on 9/l/2025. Therefore, stage-wise 
action has been taken and the demand of the account holders has been accepted. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
