5.0. 676(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 33 of the “Tribhuvan” Sahkari University Act,

University Statutes, 2025, namely:— 


Ii, 


Short title and commencement. - (!) These Statutes may be called as the “Tribhuvan” Sahkari University 
(Amendment) Statutes, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
In the “Tribhuvan” Sahkari University Statutes, 2025 :— 


(a) in statute 8, in substitute to (2), for serial number (vii) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall 
be substituted, namely:— 


“(vii) Two officers of the Joint Secretary and above level from the Ministry of Cooperation - Members, 
ex-officio;”; 


(b) in statute 9, in substitute to (2),— 
(0) after serial number (vii) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be inserted, namely:— 
“(viia) Representative of National Dairy Development Board, Gujarat - Member;”; 
(0) for serial number (viii) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted, namely:— 


“(viii) One representative from one amongst Karnataka Cooperative Milk Producers’ Federation 
Limited and Tamil Nadu Cooperative Milk Producers’ Federation Limited, by rotation, for three 
years - Member;”; 


(iii) serial numbers “(vi) and (vii)” occurring after serial number (viii) shall be renumbered as serial 
numbers “(ix) and (x)” respectively.
