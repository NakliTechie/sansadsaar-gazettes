आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा ।

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 6th February , 2023 

S.O. 600 ( E ) .— In exercise of powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highways Act , 
1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government , after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose , the land , the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below , is required for building 
( widening / two lane with paved shoulder / four laning etc. , ) , maintenance , management and operation of NH75 in the 
stretch of land from Km 91.401 to Km118.6 ( Office of the Special Land Acquisition Officer , NH - 48 Nelamangla 
Hassan Section K R S Agrahara Kunigal , Tumkur ) , NH75 in the stretch of land from Km 40.001 to Km63.6 ( Office of 
the Special Land Acquisition Officer , NH - 48 Nelamangla Hassan Section K R S Agrahara Kunigal , Tumkur ) in the 
district of RAMANAGARA in the state of KARNATAKA , hereby declares its intention to acquire such land .. 


Any person interested in the said land may , within twenty - one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette , object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub - section ( 1 ) of 
section 3C of the said Act . 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority , namely , RAMANAGARA „ Special Land 
Acquisition Officer ( Km 40.001 to Km63.6 NH75 ) in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard , either in person or by a legal practitioner , and may , 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry , if any , as the Competent Authority thinks 
necessary , by order , either allow or disallow the objections . 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final . 
The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority .
