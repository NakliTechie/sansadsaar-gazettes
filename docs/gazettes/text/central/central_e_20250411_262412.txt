NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, SRINAGAR 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th February, 2025 
F. No. Admn/39/2025-EDP (E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 26 of the 
National Institutes of Technology, Science Education and Research Act, 2007 (29 of 2007), with the approval of the 


Visitor of the National Institute of Technology, Srinagar, the Board of the National Institute of Technology, Srinagar, 
hereby makes the following Statutes to amend the National Institute of Technology, Srinagar Statutes, 20]7, namely:— 


l. (l)_ These Statutes may be called the Statutes of the National Institutes of Technology, Srinagar 
(Amendment) Statute, 2025. 


(2) They shall apply to the National Institute of Technology, Srinagar. 
(3) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Institute of Technology, Srinagar Statutes, 20!7 (hereinafter referred to as the principal 
Statutes), in Statute 4, after sub-statute (iv), the following sub-statute shall be inserted, namely— 


“(v) In the event of occurrence of any vacancy in the office of the Chairperson of Board of Governors by 
reason of expiry of his or her tenure, death, resignation or otherwise or in the event of the Chairperson being 
unable to discharge his or her functions owing to absence, illness or any other cause, the Chairperson of an 
Institute of National Importance, may discharge the functions assigned to the Chairperson under Section 6 
of the Act, for a period of six months or till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever is earlier, 
with the approval of the Visitor and in case of expiry of tenure, the Visitor may extend the term of the 
incumbent Chairperson for a period of six months or till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever 
is earlier.” 


3. In the principal Statutes, in Statute 7, sub-statute (5) shall be omitted.
