G.S.R. 740(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (6) of section 195 read with 
section 295 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Central Board of Direct Taxes hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Income-tax Rules, 1962, namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the Income-tax Amendment (Twenty-fifth 
Amendment), Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force with effect from the 17 day of January, 2024.

(Systems)”, the words and brackets “or the Director General of Income-tax (Systems)” shall be
