**For every one percent increase in the oil content beyond 46% in Shea Nut, the permitted quantity for its import may

permitted quantity for its import may increase by 50 kgs on pro-rata basis. 


Note I - For import item Shea Nuts, Customs Authority to draw samples for every bill of entry and test the same for 
its oil content. The Customs Authority to endorse on the Bill of Entry the oil content of each consignment. Regional 
Authority to redeem authorizations based on the weighted average of oil content so endorsed by the Customs Authority. 
However, clearance of import consignments should not be held back till the results are obtained, but to be allowed upon 
customs examination & drawing of samples. 


Note 2 - This SION will be valid till 3].03.2027 and will need to be re-assessed to reflect dynamic pricing structure of 
the industry. 


Effect to this Public Notice: Standard Input Output Norm (SION) E-25 for the export of Shea Stearine has been 
amended with revised import entitlements.
