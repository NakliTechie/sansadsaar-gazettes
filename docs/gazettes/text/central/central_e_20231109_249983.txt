NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2023 


S.O. 4879(E).— Whereas the Central Government is satisfied that the public interest so requires 
that the services of the industry engaged in the ‘processing or production or distribution of fuel gases (coal 
gas, natural gas and the like)’ which is covered under item 29 of the First Schedule to the Industrial 
Disputes Act, 947 (4 of 947), to be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (vi) of clause (n) of section 2 of 
the Industrial Disputes Act, 947 (4 of 947) the Central Government hereby declares the services 
engaged in the ‘processing or production or distribution of fuel gases (coal gas, natural gas and the like)’ to 
be a public utility service for the purposes of the said Act, for a period of six months with effect from the 
date of publication of this notification.
