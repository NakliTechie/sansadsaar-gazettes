MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 
(Department of Personnel and Training) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |3th July, 2023 


G.S.R. 502(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by proviso to clause (3) of article 320 of the 
Constitution, the President hereby makes the following regulations further to amend the Union Public Service 
Commission (Exemption from Consultation) Regulations, 958, namely:- 


l. (.) These regulations may be called the Union Public Service Commission (Exemption from Consultation) 
Amendment Regulations, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2, In the Union Public Service Commission (Exemption from Consultation) Regulations, 958, in Schedule II, 
for item 6 and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted, namely:- 


“6. All Group ‘A’ and Group ‘B’ posts in pay level up to level 3A in the Pay Matrix filled by the method of 
deputation or deputation (including short term contract)”. 


[F. No. 3908/03/2023-Estt.(B)] 
MANOJ KUMAR DWIVEDI, Addl. Secy.
