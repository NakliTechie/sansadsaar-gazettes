परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 1596(E) तारीख 02/04/2025, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग I, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा कर्नाटक राज्य के कलबुर्गी (गुलबर्गा) जिले में 
NH167N के कि.मी. 0.00 से कि.मी.15.85 (मिरियान तेलंगाना/कर्नाटक सीमा-चिंचोली खंड के पास) के निर्माण 
(चौड़ीकरण/4-लेन का बनाना आदि),अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में 
विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "06/05/2025 को 
“थे हिंदू [इंग्लिश] , 06/05/2025 को “विजया कर्नाटक” " में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2025 
S.O. 4324(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 1596(E) Dated:02/04/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and 


operation of NH167N in the stretch of land from Km. 0.00 to Km. 15.85 (Near Miriyan Telangana/Karnataka Border- 
Chincholi Section) in the district of Kalaburagi (Gulbarga ) in the state of KARNATAKA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” dated 06/05/2025 , 
“Vijaya Karnataka” dated 06/05/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
