S.O. 520(E).— WHEREAS the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provision) Act, 2023 
(l8 of 2023) inter alia amends the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) for 
decriminalising the offences under the said Act; 

AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and 
expedient in the public interest to amend the Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022; 

AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 
provides that whenever it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do 
so, it may dispense with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the 
Environment (Protection) Rules, 986;

to dispense with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the 
Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for amending the Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022;
