New Delhi , the 14th February , 2023 

S.O. 682 ( E ) . In exercise of powers conferred by clause ( a ) of section 3 of the National Highways 
Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in notification of 
the government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways , S.O. 1345 ( E ) dated 25.03.2021 , 
published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( II ) , 25.03.2021 namely : 


In the schedule to the said notification , against serial no . 1 in column 6 for the entry " Taluk Krishnagiri " 
the entry " Taluk Barugur " shall be substituted .
