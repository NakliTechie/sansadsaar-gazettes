the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No 716 on the stretch of land from Km. 101.620 to Km. 121.935 (Kadapa to 
Renigunta section) in the district of Tirupati in the State of ANDHRA PRADESH.
