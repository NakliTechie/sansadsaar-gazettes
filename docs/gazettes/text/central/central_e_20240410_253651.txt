New Delhi, the l0th April, 2024 


S.O. 678(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways number S.O 527 Dated:/2/2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-33 in the stretch of land from Km. 4.900 to Km. 93.000 (Mahagama to Hansdiha-Road) in the 
district of DUMKA in the state of JHARKHAND 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Swet Patra” 25 January, 2024 
Thursday: and “Times of India 25 January, 2024 Thursday under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
