7. Meetings of the Committee.—(l) The Committee shall meet at least twice in a year and may hold 
additional meetings, as and when required, to be convened at the discretion of the Chairperson; 


(2) The Chairperson may, convene special meetings of the Committee at any time; 


(3) The key recommendations of the Committee shall be documented and disclosed in such manner as 
specified by the Central Government. 


8. Functions of the National Level Steering Committee.—(l) The Committee shall recommend decisions 
relating to normative allocations to States; 


(2) The Committee shall recommend policy measures on matters requiring inter-ministerial consultation, 
including convergence initiatives and frameworks; 


(3) The Committee shall provide high-level strategic oversight for the effective implementation of this Act; 


(4) The Committee shall review the performance of the States and recommend corrective or supportive 
measures; 


(5) The Committee shall facilitate coordination among the Ministries and Departments of the Central 
Government, and between the Central Government and State Governments, for effective convergence and 


implementation of the provisions of this Act; 


(6) The Committee shall advise on standards, guidelines, monitoring systems, digital and geospatial public 
infrastructure necessary for implementation of the provisions of this Act; and 


(7) The Committee shall perform such other functions as may be assigned to it by the Central Government.
