NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist March, 2024 


S.O 974(E).— WHEREAS the Government of India signed an Agreement for establishment of a Universal 
Postal Union Regional Office at New Delhi, India on the 9" July, 2023; 


AND WHEREAS Articles 2, 3, 4 and Article 5 and the Annexure I of the said Agreement provide for 
privileges and immunities to the Universal Postal Union (UPU) Regional Office, its representatives and officials; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government considers it expedient to extend the necessary privileges and 
immunities provided under the United Nations (Privileges and Immunities) Act, 4947 (46 of 947) to the UPU 
Regional Office, New Delhi, its representatives and officials; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the United Nations (Privileges and 
Immunities) Act, !947 (46 of 947), the Central Government hereby declares that the provisions of section | of 
Article I, sections 2,3,4,5,6,7 and section 8 of Article II, section 9 of Article III and clauses (a), (b), (०), (d) and (e) of 
section |8 and section 20 of Article V and clauses (a), (b), (c), (9), (e) and (f) of section 22 and section 23 of Article 
VI of the Schedule to the said Act shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to the UPU Regional Office, New Delhi, India, its 
representatives and officials.
