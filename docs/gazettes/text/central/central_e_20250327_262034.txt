Subject: Amendment in Appendix-4J of Handbook of Procedures (HBP-2023) 


F. No. 01/94/180/075/AM24/PC-4.— In the exercise of powers conferred under Paragraph 
1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director 
General of Foreign Trade hereby amends the Appendix-4J of Handbook of Procedures 2023 as 
follows: 


“The entry at Serial No. 13 (Walnut in any form) in Appendix-4J has been deleted.” 


Effect of the Public Notice: The item ‘Walnut in any form’ has been removed from Appendix- 
AJ for ease of doing business.
