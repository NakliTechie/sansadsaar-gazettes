हो जाने के पश्चात्‌ कि महाराष्ट्र राज्य के नासिक जिले में |॥1953 के कि.मी. 104 से कि.मी.121.57 (नया प्रशासनिक

शोल्डर सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन के लिए वह भूमि अपेक्षित 
है जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है।

प्रयोजन के लिए ऐसी भूमि के उपयोग पर राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से इक्कीस दिन के भीतर अपनी 
आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा | 


ऐसी प्रत्येक आपत्ति सक्षम प्राधिकारी, अर्थात्‌ उपजिल्हाधिकारी (भूमि अधिग्रहण) को लिखित रूप में प्रस्तुत की

विधिक पेशेवर द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आपत्तियों की सुनवाई के पश्चात्‌ तथा ऐसी और जाँच करने के

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th December, 2024 


S.O. 5482(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH953 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 104 to Km. 121.57 (New Administrative Building, Collector Office Premises, Near CBS Old 
Mumbai Agra Road) in the district of NASHIK in the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Deputy Collector (Land 
Aquisition) in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 
The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
