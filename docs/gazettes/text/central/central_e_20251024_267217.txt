NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th October, 2025 


S.O. 4861(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 15 of the Architects Act, 
1972 (20 of 1972), the Central Government, after consultation with the Council, hereby makes the following 
amendments further to amend the notification of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Education and 
Social Welfare, vide number G.S.R. 68(E), dated the 217 February, 1973, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), dated the 217 February, 1973, namely: — 


In the said notification, under the heading “U.S.A.”, in serial number 8(b), after entry number 37, the following 
entry shall be inserted, namely:— 


“38. Master of Architecture from School of Architecture, Planning and Preservation, University of 
Maryland, USA.”. 


2: This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
