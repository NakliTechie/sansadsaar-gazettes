ANNEXURE 
Mandate and key responsibilities of the Internal Committee (IC) 


1. Receiving Complaints: The IC is responsible for receiving complaints of sexual harassment from employees. On 
receipt of the complaint, the Committee shall send one of the copies received from the aggrieved woman to the 
respondent within a period of seven working days. [Rule 7(2)] 


il. Conciliation Report: In case of settlement, the IC shall record and forward the report to the employer. Also copies 
of the report shall be provided to both the parties (Section 10 (2&3). 


iil. Inquiry Process: Conducting a fair and timely inquiry into the complaints received, which shall be completed within 
a period of 90 days. [Section 11(4)]. 


iv. Recommendations: Submitting findings and recommendations (including interim measures) of the inquiry to the 
employer for appropriate action. (Section 12). 


v. Submission of Inquiry Report: On completion of the inquiry, the IC shall submit a report of its findings to the 
employer within a period of ten days from the date of completion of the inquiry. [Section 13(1)] 
V1. Confidentiality: Maintaining strict confidentiality throughout the process. (Section 16 & 17). 
Vii. Annual Reports: To prepare Annual Reports in the manner as prescribed under Rule 14 and submit to the employer 


in each Calendar Year (Section 21).
