MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th February, 2025 


S.O. 657(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.4599 (E) dated 22th October 
2024, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of 
section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (Two Lane with paved shoulder /Four Lane,etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway NH-752E in the stretch of land Km 00+000 to km 55+937 ”Paithan-Shirur” (Dy.Collector,Land Acquisition, 
Minor Irrigation, Beed) in the District of Beed in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 4599 (E) dated 22th October 2024 has been 
published in “Lokmat Times” and “Dainik Lokmat” Both Dated 26th October 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 
3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


6 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
