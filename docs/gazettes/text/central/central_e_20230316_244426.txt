MINISTRY OF TEXTILES 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 28th February , 2023 

G.S.R. 194 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by section 22 of the National Jute Board Act , 2008 ( 12 
of 2009 ) , the CentralGovernment hereby makes the following amendment in Section 11of National Jute Board 
Regulations , 2012 published vide the Notification number G.S.R. No.241 ( E ) dated the 20th March , 2012 of Ministry 
of Textiles , Government of India , in the Gazette ofIndia , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , sub - Section ( i ) , as under : 


" Provided that in cases where the Board has to depute its officers to participate in delegations , meetings , 
seminars , exhibitions , fairsand other activities abroad , it shall be competent for the Secretary , N J B with the 
prior approval of Secretary ( Textiles ) andChairman NJB to depute the officers if ; 


( a ) the delegation , meeting , seminar , exhibition , fair or the activity - in which participation is sought has been 
approved by the Board ; 


( b ) the budget provision exists for incurring expenditure thereto ; and 

( c ) the consolidated annual list of activities abroad which the Boardwould like to participate for promotion and 
also the number ofpersons to be deputed from the Board is approved by the Board . "
