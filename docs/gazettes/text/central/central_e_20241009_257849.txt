MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(East Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 24th September, 2024 


S.O. 4383(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(East Central Railway), number S.O. 95 (E), dated the 09 March, 2024 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-ii, section 3, Sub-section (2), dated the 09 March, 2024 and issued under sub-section () 
of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land in the schedule annexed to the said notification for 
the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Projects namely, 
“New BG line between Jhajha & Batia (20 Km), Rail Project” in Jamui District in the state of Bihar. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local 
newspaper, namely, Times of India (Patna Edition) of dated 22 March, 2024, Dainik Jagran (Bhagalpur 
Edition) of dated 23 March, 2024 and Prabhat Khabar (Patna Edition) of dated 23 March, 2024 under sub- 
section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objection were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders 
have been passed in terms of section 20 D of the said act; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 205 of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in the pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Govt. 
Hereby declares that on publication of the notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
