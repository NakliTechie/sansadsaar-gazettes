5.0. 4508(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways Act,l989 
(24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act,) the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public

Special Railway Project, doubling project between Boridand-Surajpur (79.90 Km) (Chhattisgarh) situated in 
Chhattisgarh State in District — Surajpur and Korea in the state of Chhattisgarh, hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may within a period of 30 days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section () of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely concerned Sub Divisional Officer 
(Revenue), Surajpur, Bhaiyathan and Baikunthpur, Chhattisgarh in writing and shall set out grounds thereof and the 
competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal 
practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary by order either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said act shall be 
final. The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by 
the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
