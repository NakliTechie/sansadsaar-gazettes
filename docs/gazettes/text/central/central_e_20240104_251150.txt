MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist January, 2024 


G.S.R. 19(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines (Drawing officer, 
Assistant Drawing officer and Senior Technical Assistant (Drawing)) Recruitment Rules, 2019, namely: — 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines 
(Drawing officer, Assistant Drawing Officer and Senior Technical Assistant (Drawing)) Recruitment (Amendment) 
Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2 In the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines (Drawing Officer, Assistant Drawing Officer and Senior 
Technical Assistant (Drawing) Recruitment Rules, 2019, in the Schedule, against serial number | relating to the post 
of Drawing officer, in column (11), - 


(i) under the sub-heading “Promotion”, for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be substituted; 


(ii) after sub-heading “Promotion” and the entries relating thereto and before Note, the following Note shall be 
inserted, namely: - 


“Note 1: The eligibility service shall continue to be three years in respect of incumbents holding the feeder post 
of Assistant Drawing Officer in level 7 in the pay matrix (Rs. 44900-142400) on regular basis on the date of 
notification of the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines (Drawing officer, Assistant Drawing officer 
and Senior Technical Assistant (Drawing)) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023.”; 


(1) the “Note” shall be numbered as “Note 2”. 
[F. No. 22/1/2023-M. IIT] 
SANJAY LOHIYA, Addl. Secy.
