लोक प्रयोजन के लिए यह भूमि जिसका संक्षिप्त विवरण उनके नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है। राजस्थान राज्य के अजमेर 
जिले में विशेष रेल परियोजना “पुष्कर-मेड़ता (कत्यासनी) नयी बड़ी रेल लाइन परियोजना (विशेष रेल परियोजना)” के 
सन्दर्भ में एतद द्वारा ऐसी भूमि का अधिग्रहण करने का अपना इरादा घोषित करता है। 


उक्त भूमि में रुचि रखने वाला कोई भी व्यक्ति, अधिकारिक राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से 
तीस दिनों की अवधी के भीतर, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20डी की उप धारा (4) के तहत उपरोक्त उद्देश्य के लिये ऐसी 
भूमि के अधिग्रहण और उपयोग संबंध में आपत्ति कर सकेगा।

5.0. 426(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by subsection (l) of section 20A of the Railway Act 
2008, (l of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied for the public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for execution. 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project of "Pushkar-Merta (Katyasani) New BG line 
Project (Special Rail Project)" in the stretch of land in Tehsil Pushkar of district of Ajmer in the state of 
Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of seven days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to competent authority, namely, the Sub divisional officer Pushkar in 
writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of 
being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such objection and after making such 
further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by 
the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority
