G.S.R. 607(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 25 read with section 
469 of the Companies Act, 20I3 (8 of 203), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to 
amend the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (Form of Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 208, 
namely:- 


l. (l) These rules may be called the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (Form of Annual Statement of 
Accounts) Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: In the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (Form of Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 208, 
in rule 5, in sub-rule (2), for the words “one Member’, the words “the chief executive officer” shall be substituted.
