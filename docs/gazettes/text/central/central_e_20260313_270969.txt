असाधारण भाग ॥, खंड 3, उपखंड (ii) तारीख 27 नवम्बर 2025 में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (रेलवे बोर्ड ) की 
अधिसूचना का. आ. 5458 (अ) द्वारा उक्त अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को राजस्थान राज्य के जिला जैसलमेर

परियोजना के निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन हेतु अर्जित करने के लिए अपने आशय कि घोषणा कि थी और उक्त 
अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20 क की उपधारा (4) के अधीन स्थानीय दैनिक समाचार पत्रों राजस्थान पत्रिका 
एवं दैनिक नवज्योति में तारीख 04 दिसंबर 2025 को प्रकाशित किया गया था :

NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 12th March, 2026 


S.O. 1307(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Western Railway, Construction organisation), Notification number 8.0. 5458(E) dated 27 November, 2025 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section-3, Sub section (ii) (hereinafter referred to as the said notification), 
under sub section (i) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the 
said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely “Ramdevra - Pokran New Railway line via Bhairav Gufa - kailash Tekri (13.380km)” in the District Jaisalmer 
of the State of Rajasthan.”. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification were published in daily local newspapers “Rajasthan 
Patrika” and “Danik Navjyoti” dated 04 December , 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said act; 


And whereas, objections received within the stipulated time period were resolved by Competent Authority 
accordingly. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, The Central Government herby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
