INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 


New Delhi, the 25th April, 2024 


WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


F.No. 8-34/5370-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Saharanpur Hospital and Nursing College situated at VPO Sansarpur, Tehsil — Behat, Distt. 
Sahranpur - 24729 Uttar Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh Nurses & 
Midwives Council to conduct GNM and B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-3/I5370-INC dated 27/06/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under section I4 
of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (H&FW), Department of Health and Family 
Welfare, Government of Uttar Pradesh, 05" Floor, Vikas Bhawan, Janpath Market, Vidhan Sabha Road, Hazratganj, 
Lucknow — 22600! Uttar Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 27/06/2023 was also enclosed to Saharanpur 
Hospital and Nursing College situated at VPO Sansarpur, Tehsil - Behat, Distt. Sahranpur - 24729 
Uttar Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Saharanpur Hospital and Nursing College. Even no action, as required, has been taken by the institution. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 20/0/2023 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., clinical and 
teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 22/09/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM and B.Sc(N) from 
Saharanpur Hospital and Nursing College situated at VPO Sansarpur, Tehsil — Behat, Distt. Sahranpur - 
24729 Uttar Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Uttar Pradesh subject to the orders of 
State government of Uttar Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) Sarvjeet Kaur, Secy.
