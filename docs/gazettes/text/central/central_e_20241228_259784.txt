MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 7th December, 2024 
(Notification under secton 20 (E) of Railways Act, 989, Date: 7.2.2024) 


S.O. 5628(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (Railway Board) Notification No. 3569 dated 2.09.2024 published in the Gazette of India 
Ordinary Part-II, Section 3, Sub section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as said notification) under sub-section 
() of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as said act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for execution of Special Railway Project, namely Jharsuguda Bilaspur 4th line (206 Km) in 
Raigarh district of Chhattisgarh. 


And whereas the substance of the 20A notification under sub-section (), under sub section (4) of 
section: 20A of the said act was published in the daily newspapers Jankarm, Ispat Times and Naveen 
Kadam on dated: on 22.09.2024 respectively. Whereas no objections were received in the stipulated period 
from the land losers to the Competent Authority and whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 
20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted his report to the Central Government. Now, 
therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of the Section 20 (E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose, and 
further in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all Encumbrances.
