MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 2nd March, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 

S.O. 7(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S. 0. 587 (E) dated the 23" December, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 267 December, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (l) of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 
2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified 
in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation 
of Special Railway Project, namely “Daund - Manmad Doubling Project (247.50 Km)” in the district of Ahilyanagar 
in the State of Maharashtra. 

And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Nava Maratha” 
and “Loksatta” Dated 2.0.2026 under sub section (4) of section 20 A of the said Act; 

And whereas, in Tandulwadi village no objections were received and the same have been considered by the 
competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 

Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
