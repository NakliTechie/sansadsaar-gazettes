NATIONAL LEGAL SERVICES AUTHORITY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th May, 2025 


F. No. 6(1)95-NALSA.—In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3A of the Legal Services Authorities 
Act, 1987 (39 of 1987), Hon’ble the Chief Justice of India is pleased to nominate Hon'ble Mr. Justice Abhay 
Shreeniwas Oka, Judge, Supreme Court of India, as Chairman of the Supreme Court Legal Services Committee with 
immediate effect and makes the following amendment in its Notification No. S.0.115(E) dated 09.02.2000 namely: - 


In the said Notification, for the serial number (1) and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be substituted 
namely: - 


1. “MR. JUSTICE ABHAY SHREENIWAS OKA — CHAIRMAN” 
Judge, Supreme Court of India.
