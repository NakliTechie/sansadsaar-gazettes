MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3lst May, 2024 


S.O. 257(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.472(E) dated 2* Septembet 2023, 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building 
(Bypass / widening / four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.45A, in the 
stretch of land from Km.0 to Km.29 (Viluppuram — Puducherry — Cuddalore - Nagapattinam Section) in district of 
Villupuram in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "The New Indian Express" and 
"Thina Thanthi" both dated the 97 December 2023 under Sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
