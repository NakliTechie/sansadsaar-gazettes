MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2026 
5.0. 2343(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 40 of the 
Code on Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby notify that the provisions of section 39 of 
the Code, shall not apply in relation to vacancies which carry a total remuneration (wages as defined in the said Code) 


of less than eleven thousand rupees per month.
