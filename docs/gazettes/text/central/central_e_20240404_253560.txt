अधिकारी (१) (यूटीपीएच) जलगांव, जिला दैनिक सामाचार पत्रों अर्थात “दैनिक देशोन्नती” और “दैनिक पुण्यनगरी” में

पारित कर दिये गए हैं |

सरकार को प्रस्तुत कर दी है। 


अतः अब केंद्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से उक्त रिपोर्ट के प्राप्त होने पर और उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ड की 
उपधारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषण करती है कि इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची मे विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि 
पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी | 

और यह कि केंद्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम कि धारा 20ड कि उप-धारा (2) के अनुसरण में यह घोषणा करती है 
कि राजपत्र मे इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशित होने पर इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विलंगमों से मुक्त होकर 
केंद्रीय सरकार मे आत्यांकित रूप से निहित हो जाएगी |

S.O. 647(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 438] (E) dated l9th September 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the !9th September 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and 
issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 
2008 (I] of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management 
and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Pachora-Jamner with Extension upto Malkapur-Narrow Gauge 
to Broad Gauge in the district of Jalgaon in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for SLAO(I)UTPH.Jalgaon daily 
Marathi newspapers namely “Dainik Deshonnati” & “Dainik Punyanagri” Dated 24-0i-2024 under sub section 
(4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority 
accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
