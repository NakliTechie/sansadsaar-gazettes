Subject: Suspension of SIONs pertaining to food products-reg. 


F.No. 01/85/00137/AM-26/DES.VI—In exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraph 1.03 and 2.04 of 
the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby suspends 
Standard Input Output Norms (SIONs) mentioned at Annexure “A” to this Public Notice with immediate effect. 


2 The entities engaged in export of products under SIONs in Annexureto this Public Notice may apply for

asapplicable

immediate effect.
