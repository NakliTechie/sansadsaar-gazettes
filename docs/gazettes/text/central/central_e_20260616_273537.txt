Kolkata, the l6th June, 2026 


S.O. 34(E).— whereas by the notification of The Govt. of India in The Ministry of Railways number 
9.0. 5980(E), dated the 24th December, 2025 published in the Gazette of India, extraordinary part II, section 3, sub- 
section (II) issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declaration its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed 
here to required for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project, 
namely Naihati-Ranaghat 3rd Line in the district of Nadia in the State of West Bengal. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Eisamay Partika & Statesman 
Partika" on | January, 2026 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


Subsequently, 67 objection petitions received by this office after Publication of 20A Notification. All petitions 
are disposed of u/s 20(D) of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 by conducting proper hearing after servicing the 
notices. 


And whereas in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report of the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this declaration in the Officials Gazette, the land specified in the said schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free form all encumbrance.
