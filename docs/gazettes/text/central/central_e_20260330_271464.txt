MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th March, 2026 


S.O. 649(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the third proviso to First Schedule of the Code on 
Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby notifies the Ist day of April, 2026, as the date on 
and from which the contribution from the employers and employees of establishments in the entire areas of the West 
Khasi Hills, South West Khasi Hills, Eastern West Khasi Hills and East Jaintia Hills in the State of Meghalaya shall 
be payable under section 29 and any benefits under Chapter IV relating to the Employees State Insurance Corporation 
are provided by the Corporation to the employees of such establishments.
