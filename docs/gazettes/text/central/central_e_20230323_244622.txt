S.O. 1392 ( E ) .– In pursuance of the provisions of section 7 of the Aadhaar ( Targeted Delivery of Financial and 
Other Subsidies , Benefits and Services ) Act , 2016 ( 18 of 2016 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following 
further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Consumer Affairs , Food and 
Public Distribution , Department of Food and Public Distribution , number S.O. 371 ( E ) , dated the 8th February , 2017 , 
namely : - 

In the said notification , - 


( a ) in paragraph 1 , in sub - paragraph ( 2 ) , for the words , figures and letters “ by 31st March , 2023 ” , the words , 
figures and letters “ by 30th June , 2023 shall be substituted ;

in paragraph 2 , in sub - paragraph ( 1 ) , for the words , figures and letters “ by 31st March , 2023 ” , the words , 
figures and letters “ by 30th June , 2023 ” shall be substituted .
