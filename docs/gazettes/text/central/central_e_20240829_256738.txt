NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th August, 2024 


LAFEAS-TO20/2/2024-Table Office-RSS.— Shri Beedha Masthan Rao Yadav, an elected Member of the 
Council of States (Rajya Sabha), representing the State of Andhra Pradesh, resigned his seat in the Rajya Sabha and 
his resignation has been accepted by the Chairman, Rajya Sabha w.e.f. 29th August, 2024.
