5. Royalty shall be paid at the aforementioned rates on the “Well Head Price” of crude oil, casing head condensate and 
natural gas in all cases, subject to the following conditions: 


(1) “Well Head Price” shall be arrived at by allowing a fixed ad-valorem deduction of 20% of the sale price in case 
of nomination regime and 15% of the sale price in case of other than nomination regime, towards post well head 
costs. 


(2) Royalty shall be computed on ex-royalty basis under all regimes”.
