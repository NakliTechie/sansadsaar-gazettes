NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th September, 2024 


S.O. 403(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 5 of the Mines Act, 952 
(35 of 952), the Central Government hereby appoints Shri Ujjwal Tah, Director General of Mines Safety, in the 
Directorate General of Mines Safety Organisation, to be the Chief Inspector of Mines for all the territories to which 
the said Act extends, with effect from 2.09.2024(FN), until further orders.
