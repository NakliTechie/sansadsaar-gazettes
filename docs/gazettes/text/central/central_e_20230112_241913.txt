MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th January, 2023 


S.O. 208(E).— In pursuance of the second proviso to sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Mines and 
Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby notifies 
the M/s. FCI Aravali Gypsum and Minerals India Limited, a Central Government Company, for the 
purposes of the second proviso to sub-section (1) of section 4 of the said Act: 


Provided that the M/s. FCI Aravali Gypsum and Minerals India Limited shall make over the data 
generated by it, in respect of the prospecting operations undertaken by it, to the concerned State 
Government. 

2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
