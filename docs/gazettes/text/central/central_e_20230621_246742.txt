9.0. 2733(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 5530 (E) published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section 
(ii), dated the 57 December, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sun-section (1) of 
section 20 A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of 
execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Solapur — Tuljapur - 
Osmanabad New Line” in the district of Solapur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Swarajya” dated 08.03.2023 and “Dainik Agrini Varta” dated 06.03.2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, in 09 villages 91 Nos. of objections were received and the same have been disposed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
