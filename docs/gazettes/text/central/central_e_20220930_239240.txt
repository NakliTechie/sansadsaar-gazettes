S.O. 4625 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 of the Tobacco Board Act , 1975 
( 4 of 1975 ) , read with rules 3 and 4 of the Tobacco Board Rules , 1976 , the Central Government hereby

( Tobacco Industry Development Control ) , number S.O. 5417 , dated the 17th December , 1975 , namely :
