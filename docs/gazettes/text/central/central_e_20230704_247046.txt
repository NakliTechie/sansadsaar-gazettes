MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th July, 2023 


S.O. 2924(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport And Highways number S.O. 1472 (E) dated 
28.03.2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for (Widening/Two-Laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH-347B on the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 34+560 Km. in (Thikri to Anjad-Road) (Tehsil- 
Anjad Bus-Lay) in the District of Barwani, in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik 
Bhaskar” 30 May 2023 Tuesday; And “Free Press” 30 May 2023 Tuesday; under sub-section (3) of 
section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the Said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances,
