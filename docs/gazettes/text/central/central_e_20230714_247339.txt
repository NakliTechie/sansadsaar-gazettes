Category I or a Category II Alternative Investment Fund and is regulated under the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 20!2, made under the Securities and Exchange 
Board of India Act, 992 (5 of 992) or regulated under the International Financial Services Centres
