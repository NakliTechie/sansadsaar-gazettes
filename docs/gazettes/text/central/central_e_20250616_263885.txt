S.O. 2690(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (b) of clause (46A) of section 0 of the 
Income-tax Act, 96! (43 of 96l), (hereinafter referred to as “the Income-tax Act”), the Central Government hereby 
notifies the ‘Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission’, Bengaluru (PAN: AAAGKOI2L) (hereinafter referred to 
as “the assessee”), a commission constituted under ‘The Electricity Act, 2003’ (No. 36 of 2003), for the purposes of 
the said clause. 


2. This notification shall be effective from the assessment year 2025-26, subject to the condition that the assessee 
continues to be a commission constituted under ‘The Electricity Act, 2003’ (No.36 of 2003) with one or more of the 
purposes specified in sub-clause (a) of clause (46A) of section 0 of the Income-tax Act.
