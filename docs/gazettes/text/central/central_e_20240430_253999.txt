New Delhi, the 30th April, 2024 


G.S.R. 266(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 6 of the Bhopal 
Gas Leak Disaster (Processing of Claims) Act, 985 (2 of 985), the Central Government hereby extends 
the tenure of Shri Justice Anil Verma, a sitting Judge of the High Court of Madhya Pradesh to hold 
concurrent charge of the post of Commissioner for the Welfare of the Victims of Bhopal Gas Leak Disaster, 
for a period of one year with effect from 30.04.2024 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
