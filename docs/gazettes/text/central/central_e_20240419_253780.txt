New Delhi, the l9th April, 2024 


S.O. 747(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 503(E) Dated:05/02/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (here in after referred 
to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for for (Widening/Four - Laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and operation of NH- !46B 
on the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 47+400 K.m. in (Nasrullaganj-Rehati to Budhni-Road) (3DAdditional) 
Taluka Rehti in the district of Sehore in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Patrika” Dated I5 February 2024 
and “Pradesh Time” dated 6 February 2024 ; both under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 
And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 
Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 


conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
