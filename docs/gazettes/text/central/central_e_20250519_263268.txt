Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors to 
Corporate Debtors) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


No. IBBI/2025-26/GN/REG25.— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (t) of sub-section () of 
section 96, section 240 read with clause (e) of section 2 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 20I6 (३3] of 206), 
the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the 
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate 
Debtors) Regulations, 20|9, namely:- 


l. (.) These regulations may be called the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process 
for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors to 
Corporate Debtors) Regulations, 209, after regulation 7A, the following regulation shall be inserted, namely: - 


“]78. Non-submission of repayment plan 


Where no repayment plan has been prepared by the debtor under section l05 of the Code, the resolution 
professional shall file an application, with the approval of creditors, before the Adjudicating Authority 
intimating the non-submission of a repayment plan and seek appropriate directions.”
