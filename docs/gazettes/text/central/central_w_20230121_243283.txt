S.R.O. 51. — In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Ministry of Defence , Signal Intelligence Directorate , Integrated Defence Staff Headquarters 
( Deputy Foreign Language Adviser , Foreign Language Examiner and Translator ) Recruitment Rules , 2017 except as 
respects things done or omitted to be done before such suppression , the President hereby makes the following rules 
regulating the method of recruitment to the posts of Deputy Foreign Language Adviser , Foreign Language Examiner 
and Translator in Signal Intelligence Directorate , Integrated Defence Staff Headquarters , Ministry of Defence namely : - 
1. Short title and commencement.- ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence , Signal Intelligence 
Directorate , Integrated Defence Staff Headquarters ( Deputy Foreign Language Adviser , Foreign Language Examiner 
and Translator ) Recruitment Rules , 2023 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette .
