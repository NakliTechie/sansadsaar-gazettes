MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 12th September, 2024 


5.0. 3927(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 4264 (E) dated the 28 September 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part I, section 3 Sub-section (1), dated the 29 September 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed 
to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely- Itarsi -Nagpur Balanced 3rd line in the district of Nagpur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely "Dainik Arvi 
Times” and “Dainik Navprabhat” Dated 27.10.2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received and sorted the same and have been considered by the competent 
Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the central government free from all encumbrances.
