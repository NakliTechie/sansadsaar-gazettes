Coast Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number, 5.0. 543 (E), dated the 26 November, 2025

and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, “Construction of Railway 3rd line Between Khurda Road- 
Vizianagaram (363 Kms) of East Coast Railway” in Srikakulam district in the state of Andhra Pradesh.

jyothi" in Telugu (Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 8' December, 2025, and "The Times of India" in English 
(Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 9t* December, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of section 20D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

declares that on publication of the notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here
