ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 18th June, 2026 


O.N. 80.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Haryana, 2024 for 14-Pehowa 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/ECI/HAR- 
LA/2024, dated 5" September, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him/her or his/her 
election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the above-mentioned election for 14-Pehowa Assembly Constituency of Haryana, 
2024 was declared by the Returning Officer on 80 October, 2024 and hence the last date for lodging the account of 
Election Expenses was 7 November, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report submitted by DEO, Kurukshetra report dated 09.11.2024 forwarded by 
the Chief Electoral Officer, Haryana vide his letter no. 6/Elect-Exp./GELA-HR-2024/Elec-2025/2AE-692 dated 
05.02.2025, under sub-rule (1) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, Sh. Kuldeep Singh a contesting 
candidate from 14-Pehowa Assembly Constituency of state of Haryana has failed to lodge account of election 
expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Kurukshetra a Show-Cause 
notice No. 76/HR-LA/Exp. Account/2024/CEMS-I, dated 30% April, 2025 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 
of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Kuldeep Singh for not lodging 
of his account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct 
of Elections Rules, 1961, Sh. Kuldeep Singhwas directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, Kurukshetra District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Kurukshetra vide his letter no. Elec-2025/442 dated 11.06.2025 
has informed that the notice was duly served and received by Sh. Kuldeep Singh’s son (name Yugraj) on 
14.05.2025; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Kurukshetra in his Supplementary Report, vide its letter no. Elec- 
2025/443 dated 13.06.2025 has reported that Sh. Kuldeep Singhhas not submitted account of election expenses. 
Further, after receipt of the said notice, Sh. Kuldeep Singh has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the 
Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section 10 A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that: - 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, the Election Commission shall, by order published in 
the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three 
years from the date of the order.’’; 


WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. Kuldeep Singh 
has failed to lodge account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; and 

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Sh. Kuldeep Singh, 488, Murtzapur, Haryana, a contesting 
candidate for 14-Pehowa Assembly Constituency of Haryana in General Election to Legislative Assembly, 2024 to 
be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House of the Parliament or the Legislative 
Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three years from the date of this 
order.

ORDER 
New Delhi, the 14th July, 2026 


O.N. 84.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Rajasthan, 2023 for 
47-Amer Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification 
No.464/RJ-LA/2023, dated 30" October, 2023; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at 
an election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the above-mentioned election from 47-Amer Assembly Constituency of 
Rajasthan, 2023 was declared by the Returning Officer on 37१ December, 2023 and hence the last date for lodging the 
account of Election Expenses was 27 January, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report submitted by DEO, Jaipur report dated 04.01.2024 forwarded by the Chief 
Electoral Officer, Rajasthan vide his letter no. F.3(1)(5)EEM/ELECTION/Candidates’ Exp./433 dated 16.01.2024, 
under sub-rule (1) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, Sh. Vinod Jat a contesting candidate from 47- 
Amer Assembly Constituency of state of Rajasthan has failed to lodge his account of election expenses, as required 
under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Jaipur a Show-Cause notice No. 
76/RJ-LA/47/2023/CEMS-I, dated 19 March, 2024 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Vinod Jat for not lodging of account of Election 
Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961, Sh. Vinod Jat was directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission explaining 
the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District Election 
Officer, Jaipur within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Jaipur vide his letter no. 
Ele./L.Gen.Ele./2024/2094 dated 13.04.2024 has informed that the notice was duly served and received by Smt. 
Badam Choudhary, wife of Sh. Vinod Jat on dated 03.04.2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the supplementary report submitted by DEO, Jaipur dated 13.08.2024 forwarded by the 
Chief Electoral Officer, Rajasthan vide his letter no. F.3(1)(5)EEM/ELECTION/Candidates’ Exp./8511 dated 
21.08.2024, has reported that Sh. Vinod Jat submitted his incomplete account of election expenses, as required under 
law, and submitted the same beyond the prescribed time limit; and 


WHEREAS, The Election Commission of India directed the DEO, Jaipur, vide its letter dated 02.01.2025 
and reminder dated 25.03.2025, to ask the candidate to submit his complete accounts of election expenditure, 
including vouchers, registers, details of bank accounts, etc., as prescribed under the law; and 


WHEREAS, as per the second supplementary report submitted by DEO, Jaipur dated 19.03.2025 
forwarded by the Chief Electoral Officer, Rajasthan vide his letter no. F.3(1)(5)EEM/ELECTION/Candidates’ 
Exp./2023/1490 dated 01.04.2025, has reported that Sh. Vinod Jat still has not submitted his incomplete account 
of election expenses, as required under the law, even after personally receiving the letter/notice on 10.03.2025; and 


WHEREAS, due to the delayed submission and incomplete of the accounts, the Election Commission of 
India vide its letter no. 76/Raj-LA/40-49/2023/CEMS-1, dated 30.01.2026 directed Sh. Vinod Jat to submit complete 
accounts of election expenditure, including all supporting vouchers, and also to explain the reasons for the delay in 
filing the said accounts before the District Election Officer; and

WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Jaipur in his third Supplementary Report, vide its letter no. F 
election/2026/1199 dated 01.04.2026 reported that Sh. Vinod Jat has not submitted the account of his election 
expenses in the manner required as he failed to submit vouchers of election expenses, as required under law. Further, 
after receipt of the Commission’s letter dated 30.01.2026 (received by his wife Smt. Badam Choudhary), Sh. Vinod 
Jat has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the 
account of his election expenses in the manner as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section 10 A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that:- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or under 
this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 


the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such 
person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 


WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. Vinod Jat has 
failed to lodge his account of election expenses in the manner required under law and has no good reason or 
justification for the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares Sh. Vinod Jat, 43, Gram Naradpura, Tehsil-Jamwaramgarh, 
District-Jaipur, Rajasthan, a contesting candidate from 47-Amer Assembly Constituency of Rajasthan in General 
Election to Legislative Assembly, 2023 to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House 
of the Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of 
three years from the date of this order.
