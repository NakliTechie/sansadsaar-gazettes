MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th January, 2025 


S.O. 542(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, of the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/Four-Six laning etc), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 53 (Old NH.6) in 
the stretch of land from Km. 508.00 to Km. 544.200 (Bhandara-Nagpur Section) in the District of Nagpur in the state 
of Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land, 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(l) of section 3C of the said Act, 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the Deputy Collector and Land 
Acquisition Officer, V.LD.C. No. 2, Nagpur, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the said competent 
authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, 
by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final.
