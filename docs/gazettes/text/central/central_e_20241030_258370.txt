CORRIGENDUM NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th October, 2024 
(Case No - AD (OJ) - 17/2023) 


Subject: Anti-dumping investigation concerning imports of “Cellophane Transparent Film” originating in or 
exported from China PR. 


1. F. No. 6/16/2022-DGTR.—Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act,1975 as amended from time to time 
(hereinafter referred to as the "Act") and the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Duty or 
Additional Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 1995, as amended from time to time, 
thereof, the designated Authority has issued the final finding notification no. 6/18/2023-DGTR dated 06.08.2024 in 
respect of the anti-dumping investigation undertaken on subject product “Cellophane Transparent Film.” 


2. In para no.120 of the final findings, ‘Shandong Henglian New Material’ may be read as ‘Shandong Henglian 
New Materials Co., Ltd.’ 


3. The other text of the final finding shall remain unchanged.
