MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2025 


S.O. 6069(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of 3A of the National Highway Act, 
956 (48 of 956) (hereafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building Construction 
of a (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,) with paved shoulder configuration, maintenance, 
management and operation of National Highway No.752G in the stretch of land from Km. 2/427 to Km. 64/000 
(Pimpalner to Satana Road Section-2) in the district of NASHIK in the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares 
its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Deputy Collector (Land 
Acquisition) National Highway Project, Nashik in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after 
hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, 
by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
