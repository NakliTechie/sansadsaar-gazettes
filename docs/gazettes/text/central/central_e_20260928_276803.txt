MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 28th September, 2026 


S.O. 535(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), G.S.R. 900 (E) dated the 6" December 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3, Sub-section (ii), No. 8l4 dated 6" December 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section () of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 
2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely 5th & 6th line between Borivali -Virar on Western Railway , under 
MUTP IIIA in the district of Thane in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Punya 
Nagari” (Marathi) dated 2‘ January 2026 and “Hindustan Times” (English), under sub section (4) of section 20 A of 
the said Act. And whereas, no objections were received under Section 20 (D) of the said act, and the same have been 
considered by the Competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed. And whereas, in pursuance of sub- 
section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (/) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government, free from all encumbrances.
