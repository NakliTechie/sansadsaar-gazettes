MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Financial Services) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th July, 2025 


G.S.R. 54(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3] of the National Bank for 
Financing Infrastructure and Development Act, 202] ([7 of 202), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules further to amend the National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development General 
Rules, 2022, namely: - 


I. (I) These rules may be called the National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development General 
(Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development General Rules, 2022, after rule 9, the 
following shall be inserted, namely: - 


“]0, Concessional rate of fees for Government Guarantee.— ([) The concessional rate of fees for 
Government guarantees applicable to the Institution shall be 0.l per cent, for borrowings from multilateral 
institutions, sovereign wealth funds, and such other foreign institutions as may be specified, by notification, 
by the Central Government: 


Provided that the Central Government may reduce such concessional rate of fees for Government guarantee, 
in respect of borrowings to be availed by the Institution for the purposes mandated to the Institution by the 
Central Government, and the Institution shall submit a request to the Central Government including the 
details of the borrowings, the parties involved, and the intended use of the borrowed funds. 


(2) The guarantee fee shall be payable by the Institution at the time of securing the Government guarantee 
and thereafter every year, on the * of April. 


(3) The Government guarantee fees shall be subject to other conditions imposed by the Central Government." 
[F. No. 5/0/202-IF-I-Part(2)] 
MANOJ MUTTATHIL AYYAPPAN, Jt. Secy.

January, 2022 and subsequently amended vide notification number G.S.R. 580(E), dated the 5th July, 2022.
