MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 12th May, 2023 


S.O. 2607(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred by clause 7A of section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) Act 
2008, the district land acquisition officer/Sitamarhi for Sitamarhi District and the district land acquisition 
officer/Sheohar for Sheohar District as nominated “Competent Authority for land acquisition (CALA)” by State 
Government, are hereby authorized to performed the function of such authority under the said Act in the area of 
special Railway Project “Sitamarhi-Sheohar new line project (28 km)” in the district of Sitamarhi and Sheohar, 
Bihar state respectively with effect from the date of publication in the Gazette of India. 


2. In exercise of power conferred by clause 20 F of section 3 of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008, the 
Divisional Commissioner, Tirhut Division/Muzaffarpur as nominated “Arbitrator” by state Government, is hereby 
authorized to performed the function of such authority under the said Act in the area of Special Railway Project 
“Sitamarhi-Sheohar New Line Project (28 km)” in the district of Sitamarhi and sheohar, Bihar State with effect 
from the date of publication in the Gazette of india.
