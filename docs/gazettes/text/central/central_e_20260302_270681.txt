MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 27th February, 2026 


S.O. 22(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (Railway Board), number, S.O. 3935 (E) dated the 26 August 2025 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 26 August 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section () of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely “Parbhani— Parli Doubling project” in the District of Beed in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Paper " Lokmat 
Times” “Lokmat Samachar” & “Lokmat" dated 06 September 2025, under sub section (4) of section 20A 
of the said Act, 


And whereas, in Jalalpur,Vaijwadi and Parli Vaijnath villages, objections were not received and 
accordingly report have been passed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग avs 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 9 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 20(E). of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.

“Parbhani — Parli Doubling Project” in Jalalpur, Vaijwadi and Parli Vaijnath Villages of Beed District, 
Under Sub- Divisional Officer & Land Acquisition officer Parli Vaijnath and Competent Authority for 
Land Acquisition, Parli Vaijnath; for Land Acquisition in the state of Maharashtra. 
Brief description of the land to be acquired with or without structures, falling within the Special Railway 
Project “Parbhani — Parli Doubling project” in the State of Maharashtra.
