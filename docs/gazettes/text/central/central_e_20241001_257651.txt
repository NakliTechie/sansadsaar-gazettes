MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the lst October, 2024 


S.O. 4300(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Road Transport and Highways, 4429(E) Dated:0/0/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening to 2 laning with paved shoulder/four laning etc...) Maintenance, management and 
operation from km.70.700 to km.234.567 of the National Highway No. 30 (Bhadrachalam to Kunta) of 
the district of Alluri Sitharama Raju in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” and 
“Andhra Prabha” both dated 07.2.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, 
considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of 
the powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all 
encumbrances.
