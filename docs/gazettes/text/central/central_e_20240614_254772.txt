MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l4th June, 2024 


S.O. 238(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the Sub-Section (i) of Section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for "Development to 4/6-lanning of NH-3l (New NH-0) with both sides service roads from Km. 569.258 to 
Km. 58.030 (End of AH-02 from Shivmandir to Sevoke Army Cantonment) in the districts of Darjeeling and 
Jalpaiguri, West Bengal on EPC Mode", hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (i) of 
Section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Special Land Acquisition 
Officer, Teesta Barrage Project, Rabindranagar, Siliguri, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal

Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 
Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of Section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected
