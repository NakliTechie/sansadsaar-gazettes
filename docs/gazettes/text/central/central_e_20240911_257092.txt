S.O. 3892(E).— The Central Government, exercising the powers conferred by clause (7A) of section 2 of the 
Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), had designated the Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Dumariyaganj as the competent 
authority for acquisition of land under the Act. 


Using the same authority, now Additional District Magistrate (Finance and Revenue)/Siddharthnagar is 
nominated competent authority in place of Sub-Divisional Magistrate/Dumariaganj. In respect of the Special Railway 
Project of new Railway line between Khalilabad - Bahraich section in the State specified in column (4) of the Table 
below, from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette, the officer mentioned in column (2) is hereby 
authorised as the competent authority to discharge the functions as such authority under the said Act for the public 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of such Special Railway Project.
