New Delhi, the 3th January, 2025 


S.O. 260(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4(g) of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 956 (25 of 956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 20i2 (37 of 202), 
the Central Government hereby notifies Shri Trivendra Singh Rawat and Adv. Chandra Shekhar, elected by the 
Members of Lok Sabha to serve as Members of the Institute Body of All India Institute of Medical Sciences, 
Rishikesh.

Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 956.
