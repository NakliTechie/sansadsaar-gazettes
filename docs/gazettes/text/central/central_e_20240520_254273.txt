MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South East Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 6th May, 2024 
Notice under Section 20E of the Railway Act, 1989 


S.O. 2019(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (South 
East Central Railway), number S.O. 3145(E), dated 12th July, 2023 the published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-ii, section 3. Sub-section (ii), No. 3016 dated the 14th July, 2023 and issued under sub- section (1) 
of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (herein after referred as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely Gauge Conversion of Kendri- 
Dhamtari and Abhanpur-Rajim branch line (67.20 Km)-2nd Material Modification to Raipur-Titlagarh Doubling on 
SEC Railway in Dhamtari District in the state of Chhattisgarh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
Navbharat (Raipur Edition) of dated 26th July, 2023, Nayiduniya (Raipur Edition) of dated 26th July, 2023, and The 
Times Of India (Raipur Edition) of dated 26th July, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, 


And whereas, no objection has been received by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub- section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in the pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Govt. hereby declares 
that on publication of the notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
