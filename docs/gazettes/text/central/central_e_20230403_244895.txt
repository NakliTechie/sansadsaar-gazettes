MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 


( Railway Board ) 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 28th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 264 ( E ) —―In exercise of the powers conferred by section 198 of the Railways Act , 1989 ( 24 of 1989 ) , 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Dedicated Freight Corridor Railway 
General Rules , 2018 , namely : 


1. Short title and commencement . - ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Dedicated Freight Corridor Railway General 
( Amendment ) Rules , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Dedicated Freight Corridor Railway General Rules , 2018 , in rule 156 , for sub - rule ( 1 ) , the following 
sub - rule shall be substituted , namely : 


" ( 1 ) The speed of trains over non - interlocked facing points shall not exceed thirty kilometers an hour in any 
circumstances and the speed over turn - outs and crossovers shall not exceed its permissible speed or thirty 
kilometers an hour whichever is less , unless otherwise prescribed by approved special instructions , which may 
permit a higher speed . " 


[ F. No. 2022 / Safety ( A & R ) / 19 / 27 ] 

JAYA VARMA SINHA , Member ( O & BD ) and Ex - Officio Secy . 

Note : The principal rules were published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part - II , section 3 , Sub - section ( 1 ) 
vide notification No. G.S.R. 765 ( E ) dated 10th August , 2018 and were further amended vide G.S.R.822 ( E ) 
dated 6th November , 2019 .
