SECRETARIAT OF THE ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd March, 2025 


O.N. 12.— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 13A of the Representation of the 
People Act, 1950 (43 of 1950), the Election Commission of India in consultation with the Administration of Union 
Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands hereby designates Shri L. Kumar, IAS (AGMUT: 2012) as the Chief 
Electoral Officer for the Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands with effect from the date he takes over 
charge and until further orders in place of Shri Balwan Singh Jaglan, IAS. 


2. Shri L. Kumar shall cease to hold and hand over forthwith the charge of all or any charges of work under 
the Administration of Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands, which he may be holding before such 
assumption of office. 

3. Shri L. Kumar while functioning as the Chief Electoral Officer, Andaman and Nicobar Islands shall not 
hold any additional charge whatsoever under the Administration of the Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar 
Islands except that he should be designated Secretary to the Administration of the Union Territory of Andaman and 
Nicobar Islands in charge of Election Department in the Union Territory Secretariat. 


[F. No. 154/A&N/2024-P.Admn.] 
By Order, 
RAHUL SHARMA, Principal Secy.
