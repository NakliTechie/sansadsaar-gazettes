प्रस्तुत कर दी है। 

अत: अब केन्द्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से उक्त रिपोर्ट के प्राप्त होने पर और उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20 'ड' की 
उपधारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि इससे उपाबध्द अनुसूची मे विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि पूर्वोक्त 
प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी। 


और यह कि केंद्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम कि धारा 20 'ड' कि उपधारा (2) के अनुसरण मे यह घोषणा करती है कि 
राजपत्र मे इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशित होने पर इस से उपाबध्द अनुसूची मे विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विलंगमो से मुक्त होकर केन्द्रीय 
सरकार मे आत्यांकित रूप से निहित हो जाएगी।

Mumbai, the 7" January, 2025 


S.O. 36(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 4660(E) dated the 25th October 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 25th October 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and 
issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (i] of 2008) (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed 
to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely new BG line project from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) in Dhar 
district Tehsil Dhar Village Tirla in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers — “Free Press, 
Times Nation” & “Raj Express” dated 2nd November, 3rd November 2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act. 


And whereas, in Tirla village objections were received, considered and the objections have been resolved and 
competent Authority have passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
