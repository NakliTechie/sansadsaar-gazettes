वहीं , परिषद् ने इस मामले पर समग्र विचार किया है । भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम 1947 की धारा 13

वास्तविक अर्थ यह होगा कि संस्थान के पास अपेक्षित शिक्षण , नैदानिक या आधारभूत सुविधाएं नहीं हैं ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 

WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F.No. 18-1447 / 2002 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , St. Mary's Hospital & Institute of Bio - Medical Technology situated at Mettukudisai Village , 
Tehsil Arni , Distt . Tiruvannamali – 632315 , Tamilnadu is recognized by State Government of Tamilnadu and 
Tamilnadu Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-1447 / 2002 - INC / 11906 dated 09/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Secretary , Department of Health and Family Welfare , 
Government of Tamilnadu , 04th Floor , Fort St. George , Secretariat , Chennai – 600009 , Tamilnadu was issued . Copy of 
the notice dated 09/02/2021 was also enclosed to St. Mary's Hospital & Institute of Bio- Medical Technology 
situated at Mettukudisai Village , Tehsil Arni , Distt . Tiruvannamali – 632315 , Tamilnadu . In the notice issued , the 
State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution . 


Whereas , the Council received a letter dated 23/02/2021 from St. Mary's Hospital & Institute of Bio 
Medical Technology . Thereafter the Council issued letter dated 16/03/2021 to the St. Mary's Hospital & Institute 
of Bio - Medical Technology . Institution submitted online application for inspection under Section 13 of INC Act , 
1947. The application was rejected by INC as compliance was not tenable . The institution was supposed to submit 
compliance . Instead of compliance , institution has submitted a letter dated 10/11/2021 challenging the powers of INC . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection under section 
13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only that the Council would 
be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct nursing courses . Refusal for 
inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the requisite Teaching , Clinical or 
infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from St. Mary's Hospital & Institute of Bio 
Medical Technology situated at Mettukudisai Village , Tehsil Arni , Distt . Tiruvannamali - 632315 , Tamilnadu 
shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Tamilnadu subject the orders of State government of 
Karnataka . 


Lt. Col. ( Dr. ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
