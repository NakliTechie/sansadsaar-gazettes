government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, 8.0. 1029(E) dated 01.03.2023 for the 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (II), 02.03.2023 namely:-

21.03.2022, against serial no. 1 in column 2 for the entry “District Revenue Officer, Krishnagiri” the entry
