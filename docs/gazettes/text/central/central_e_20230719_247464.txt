MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture, and Farmers Welfare) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th July, 2023 


S.O. 3240(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 955 (0 
of 955), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Fertiliser (Inorganic, 
Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order,!985, namely:- 


l. This Order may be called the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) (Sixth) Amendment Order, 
2023. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


l. In the Fertiliser Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order, 985 ( hereinafter referred to as the said Order), 
in Schedule IV,- 


(a) inserial number 9 relating to “Fermented Organic Manure”,- 
(i) imentry (iv), for the figures “<20”, the figures and words “upto 30” shall be substituted; 
(ii) imentry (vi), for the figures “6.5-8.0”, the figures “6.5-8.4” shall be substituted; 
(iii) after serial number 9, the following Note shall be inserted , namely:- 


“Note:- Every manufacturer shall categorically indicate on bags that Fermented Organic Manure 
shall be added 5-25 days prior to sowing of crop in the field.’ 


(b) in serial number 0 relating to “Liquid Fermented Organic Manure”’;- 

(i) imentry (iv), for the figures “<20” , the figures and words “upto 30” shall be substituted; 
(ii) in entry (v), for the figures “6.5-8.0”, the figures “6.5-8.4” shall be substituted; 

(iii) after serial number |0, the following Note shall be inserted , namely:- 


“Note;-Every manufacturer shall categorically indicate on container that Liquid Fermented Organic 
Manure shall be added 5-25 days prior to sowing of crop in the field”
