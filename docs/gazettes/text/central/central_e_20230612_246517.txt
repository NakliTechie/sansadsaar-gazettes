INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F.No. 8-5/4788-INC).— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Poorvika School of Nursing situated at Byanandahalli Near Janapriya Township, 
Kadabagere Post, Bengaluru — 560064, Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and Karnataka 
Nursing Council to conduct GNM course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-5/l4788-INC dated 26/l0/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 
of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Principal Secretary, Health and Family Welfare Department, Room 
No. 05, 0* Floor, Vikas Souda, Bangalore — 56000, Karnataka was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/0/2022 
was also enclosed to Poorvika School of Nursing situated at Byanandahalli Near Janapriya Township, 
Kadabagere Post, Bengaluru — 560064, Karnataka. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Poorvika School of Nursing. Even the institution Poorvika School of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
27/04/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Poorvika School of Nursing situated at 
Byanandahalli Near Janapriya Township, Kadabagere Post, Bengaluru — 560064, Karnataka shall be entitled to 
be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject to the orders of State government of Karnataka. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
