PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 7" February, 2025

Subject: Amendments in conditions of the Standard Input Output Norms (SION) at E-36 
for export of Wheat Flour - regarding. 


F.No. 0/85/7/0004/ DES.VI .— exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraph 
.03 & 2.04 of Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director 
General of Foreign Trade hereby makes the following amendments: 


For the SION E-36 as amended in Public Notice No. 62/205-20 dated 23.03.2023, note 3 added 
by P.No 25/2023 dated 47 August 2023, is amended as under: 
Note 3: Export of ‘Wheat Flour (Atta) with Millets’ is also allowed subject to the conditions that: 


[भाग L—ave I] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


a. The export item will contain a minimum of 60% Wheat Flour (Atta) & a minimum of 45% 
Millets in addition to other ingredients (if any). 

b. Import entitlement of Wheat under the Advance Authorization for such export items, i.e. 
‘Wheat Flour Atta with Millets’ will be calculated in proportion to the %age content of 
Whole Wheat Flour (Atta) in the export item, i.e. for export of every | Kg of Whole Wheat 
Flour (Atta), import of .07 kg of Wheat will be allowed. 

c. Millets & other ingredients being added to the export item will be domestically sourced. 

d. The export description in the SB will clearly indicate the %age content of Whole Wheat 
Flour, %age content of Millets and other ingredients being added. 

e. All other conditions specified earlier in Public Notice No. 38/20I5-20 dated 25..2022 
and Public Notice No. 62/205-20 dated 23.03.2023 will continue to be in force. 


Effect to this Public Notice: Standard Input Output Norm (SION) E-36 is being amended 
to allow export of Wheat Flour (Atta) with Millets. 


SANTOSH KUMAR SARANGI, Director General of Foreign Trade & Ex-officio Addl. Secy.
