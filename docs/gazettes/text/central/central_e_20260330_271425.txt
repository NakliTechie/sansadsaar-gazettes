S.O. 655(E)— WHEREAS, the Central Government has notified the Linear Alkyl Benzene (Quality 
Control) Order, 2022 vide S.O. 648 (E), dated 5" April, 2022 (hereinafter to referred as the said Order) in the public 
interest under and the powers conferred by section 6 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 206 (।] of 206). 


And whereas, in view of the exigencies prevailing globally, the Central Government, in consultation with 
Bureau, has taken cognizance of the supply chain disruption and is satisfied that to ensure availability of the Linear 
Alkyl Benzene, it is necessary and expedient to suspend the operation of the said Order temporarily; 


NOW, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section |6 read with sub-section (3) of section 25 of 
the Bureau of Indian Standards Act 206 ([] of 20l6), and being of the opinion that it is necessary so to do in the 
public interest, the Central Government after consultation with the Bureau of Indian Standards, hereby makes the 
following amendment to the said Order, namely :- 


In the Linear Alkyl Benzene (Quality Control) Order, 2022, in paragraph 2, the following proviso shall be 
inserted, namely: - 


“Provided that to prevent any disruption of supply chain of the said goods or article, the conformation to such 
Indian Standard shall not be applicable for the period from the Ist April, 2026 to Is July, 2026"
