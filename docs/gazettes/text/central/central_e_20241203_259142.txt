NOTIFICATION 


Prayagraj, 29th November, 2024 


S.O. 5204(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North 
Central Railway, Construction department), Notification number, S.O. 2709 (E) dated the I5th June, 2024 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3 Sub Section (ii), dated 0 July, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as 
the said notification) and issue under sub-section (l) of section 204 of the Railways (Amendment) Act. 2008 (/ lof 
2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed the said notification for the purpose of Design, Supply, Erection, Testing & 
Commissioning of Traction Sub-Station (TSS) & Sub-Sectioning & Paralleling Post (SSP) of Special Railway 
Project, namely Special Railway Project of Raising of Speed to 60kmph/200kmph on existing New Delhi- 
Hawrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in District Hathras of the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers "Amar Ujala" 
and "Times of India" Dated 3lth July, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, all the objections received in this project have been disposed of by the Competent Authority;

And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances. 


And, that all persons interested in the above mentioned land in pursuance of sub-section (04) of section 20(F) 
of the said Act by the Central Government are hereby informed that they may, personally or through an agent or legal 
practitioner, be notified by the competent authority Sub Divisional Magistrate, Sasni/SLAO Hathras, can submit 
his claim in the next 20 days from the date of publication of this notification in the office of the Competent Authority.
