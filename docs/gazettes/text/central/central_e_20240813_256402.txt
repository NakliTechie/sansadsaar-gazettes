S.O. 345I(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 


(Railway Board), number S.O 398 (9), dated |8th Jul, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II,

section 20 A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of a Special Railway Project namely, “EMU Maintenance Facilities i-e. 
Bhivpuri-Karjat EMU Carshed and POH Facilities” in Tahsil Karjat, District- Raigad in the state of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, under sub-section (4) of section 20 A of the said Act, the substance of said notification has 
been published in the daily local newspapers namely, “The Free Press Journal” (English), dated 20-07-2023 and 
“LOKMAT” (Marathi) dated 20-07-2023 and whereas objections have been received under section 20(D) and the 
same have been considered/disposed/disallowed by the Competent Authority. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Central Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub — section () of the section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified 
in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
