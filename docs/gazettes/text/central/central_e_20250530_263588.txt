S.O. 243(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Clause (a) of Section 3 of the National Highways 
Act 956 (48 of 956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the Notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways No. 8.0. 2733 (E) dated 8th November, 200, 
Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii), dated 8th November, 20I0, 
namely:— 


In the Schedule to the said Notification S.O. 2733 (E) 


(i) Against Serial number (a) for the words “Special District Revenue Officer (Land Acquisition), National 
Highways, Ramanathapuram”, the words “Special District Revenue Officer, Land Acquisition, National Highways, 
Pudukkottai” shall be substituted. 


(ii) Against Serial number (b) for the words “Special District Revenue Officer (Land Acquisition), National 
Highways, Ramanathapuram”, the words “Special District Revenue Officer, Land Acquisition, National Highways, 
Ramanathapuram- |” shall be substituted.
