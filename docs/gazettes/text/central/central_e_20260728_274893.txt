MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th July, 2026 


9.0. 4173(E).—Consequent on the commissioning of the Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport, 
Bhogapuram, Andhra Pradesh with effect from 00:01 hours of 17th August, 2026 and in pursuance of the decision taken 
at the time of grant of Site Clearance for this project on 27.01.2016, the Central Government hereby notifies that the 
scheduled commercial operations at the Civil Enclave at Visakhapatnam Airport (INS Dega) will be closed for 30 years 
[which shall, for these purposes, not include use for Airport Activity at times of national emergency or (at any time) by 
aircraft owned or operated by or for the Indian Air Force or other Armed Forces of India or Police or any such other 
authorities or for transportation of dignitaries by special Government owned, leased or hired VIP aircraft], once the 
operations are commenced at Bhogapuram International Greenfield Airport. The International Air Transport Code 
“VTZ” for the existing Visakhapatnam airport is also hereby transferred to Bhogapuram International Greenfield Airport 
from 00:01 hours of 17th August, 2026. 


Non-Scheduled Commercial operations may however continue to be operated at the Civil Enclave at 
Visakhapatnam Airport (INS Dega).
