2. समिति के कार्य-काल के दौरान, यदि किसी व्यक्ति को समिति का सदस्य नामित किया जाता है तो वह उस समिति 
के कार्यकाल की शेष अवधि के लिए ही सदस्य होगा। 


3. विशेष परिस्थितियों में मंत्रालय द्वारा समिति का कार्यकाल कम या ज्यादा किया जा सकता है। 
4. जो संसद सदस्य समिति के सदस्य हैं, वे संसद सदस्य न रहने पर इस समिति के सदस्य नहीं रहेंगे। 


5. समिति के पदेन सदस्य उस समय तक सदस्य बने रहेंगे जब तक कि वे अपने उन पदों पर हैं जिनके नाते वे समिति के

FUNCTIONS 


The functions of the Committee will be to render advice with regard to the implementation of the provisions 


relating to Official Language contained in the constitution, Official Language Act and Rules, and policy decisions of 
the Kendriya Hindi Samiti and instructions issued by Department of Official Language, Ministry of Home Affairs 
relating to official language and also in regard to the progressive use of Hindi in the Ministry.

TENURE 
.The tenure of the Committee will be ordinarily for 3 years from the date of its constitution. 


2.If a person is nominated as Member in the middle of the term, he/she will hold the office for the residual term 
of the Committee. 


3.In special circumstances, the tenure of the Committee may be curtailed or enhanced by the Ministry. 


4.Member of Parliament who are the members of the Committee shall cease to be members as soon as they 
cease to be the Member of Parliament. 


5.Ex-officio members of the Committee shall continue as members as long as they hold office by virtue of 
which they are members of the Committee. 


GENERAL 
The Headquarters of the Committeeshall be at New Delhi. 
TRAVELLING AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


The Members of the Parliament nominated in the Committee, will be paid Travelling Allowance/Daily 
Allowance as per the provisions in the “Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowance & Pension) Act, 954” and 
amendments issued from time to time and rules made thereunder vide Department of Official Language Office 
Memorandum No. II/20034/04/2005-O.L.(Policy-2), dated 03 February, 2006 for participating in meetings of 
the Committee. 


Travelling Allowance/Daily Allowance to other non-official members of the Committee, will be paid as per the 


guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language O.M. No.II/22034/04/86-O.L. (A-2) dated 
22..987 keeping in view the high-level status of the Committee and in accordance with the prescribed rates 
and rules as amended from time to time by the Government of India.
