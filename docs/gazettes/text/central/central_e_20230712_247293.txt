परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 4620 तारीख 30/09/2022, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग I, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) F प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा हरियाणा राज्य के यमुनानगर जिले में 
Greenfield Highway के कि.मी. 55.037 से कि.मी.71.168 (जिला कार्यालय, यमुना नगर) तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के 
निर्माण (चौड़ा करने/चार लेन का बनाने, आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध 
अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

इंडिया" को "अर्थ प्रकाश" और "15-10-2022" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ; 


और आक्षेप प्राप्त हुए थे और सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उन पर विचार कर लिया है और आक्षेपों को अननुज्ञात कर 
दिया है ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th July, 2023 


S.O. 3103(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4620 Dated:30/09/2022 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of Greenfield Highway in the stretch of land from Km. 55.037 to Km. 71.168 (DISTRICT Office, 
YAMUNA NAGAR) in the district of YAMUNANAGAR in the state of HARYANA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “7JIMES OF INDIA” and “ARTH 
PARKASH” both dated /5-10.2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
