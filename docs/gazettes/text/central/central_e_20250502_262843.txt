MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd May, 2025 
S.O. 1998(E).—In continuation to this Ministry’s Notification No. 5.0. 5417(E) dated the 
21% December, 2023; Notification No. S.O. 4184(E) dated the 24" September, 2024; and Notification 
No. S.O. 5650(E) dated the 31% December, 2024; the President is pleased to extend the tenure of the Finance 


Commission constituted for the Union Territories of Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Dadra and Nagar Haveli 


and Daman and Diu, Ladakh and Lakshadweep by three months i.e. up to 30.06.2025.
