MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2024 


S.O. 373(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is 
required for building (widening of 2/4 lane with paved shoulder etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of National Highway No 652 in the stretch of land from Km. 00+000 to Km. 30+982 of Tuljapur to Akkalkot of 
National Highway No 652 in the district of Dharashiv in the State of Maharashtra Hereby declares its intention 
to acquire land. 


Any person interested in the said lands may, within twenty-one (2) days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Dy. Collector Land Acquisition 
M.P. No.2 Dharashiv in the state of Maharashtra, in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the competent 
authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested persons at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
