MINISTRY OF CHEMICALS AND FERTILIZERS 
(Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th May, 2024 


S.O. 2115(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 6 of the Chemical Weapons 
Convention Act, 2000 (34 of 2000), the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers number S.O. 1490(E), dated the 
137 September, 2006 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), namely:- 


In the said notification, for serial numbers 2 and 4 and the entries relating thereto, the following serial 
numbers and entries shall respectively be substituted, namely:— 


"2. Ms. Indra Mallo - Director (Joint Secretary, National Authority Chemical 
Weapons Convention) 

4. Shri Deepankar Aron -Director (Joint Secretary, Department of Chemicals and 
Petrochemicals)”. 


[F. No. C.I-40015/4/2020-CHEM.I-CPC] 
DEEPANKAR ARON, Jt. Secy. 


Note:- The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) vide number S.O. 1490 (8), dated the 13" September, 2006 and was last amended vide notification number 
5.0. 401 (8), dated the 317 January, 2024.
