S.O. 3250(E).— In Exercise of Power conferred by clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) Act 
2008, General Manager/East Central Railway has approved to declare “3rd & 4th line work between Katihar - 
Barauni & Bachhwara — Goldinganj (via Shahpur Patoree) in Sonpur Division of East Central Railway” 
as Special Railway Project. 


Therefore, “3rd & 4th line work between Katihar - Barauni & Bachhwara - Goldinganj 
(via Shahpur Patoree) in Sonpur Division of East Central Railway” is declared as Special Railway Project in the 
State of Bihar.
