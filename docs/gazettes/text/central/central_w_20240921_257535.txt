S.R.O. 52.—In exercise of powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Defence Research and Development Organisation Technical Cadre Recruitment Rules, 2000, 
except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the 
following rules regulating the method of recruitment to Group ‘A’, Group ‘B’ and Group ‘C’ technical posts in the 
Defence Research and Development Organisation, Ministry of Defence, namely: — 


l. Short title and commencement.—(]) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Defence Research 
and Development Organisation Technical Cadre Recruitment Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. Definitions.—In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires,— 

(a) “Assessment Board” means a Board constituted under sub-rules (l) and (4) of rule 5; 

(b) “cadre” means Defence Research Technical Cadre; 

(c) “category” means category of the cadre; 

(d) “Chairperson, DRDO” means the Chairperson of the Defence Research and Development 

Organisation; 

(e) “Government” means the Government of India; 

(f) “grade” means a grade of the cadre; 

(g) “Schedule” means schedule annexed to these rules; and 

(h) “Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes” shall have the same meanings as assigned to them 


respectively in clauses (24) and (25) of article 366 of the Constitution.

3. Categories, grades, authorised strength and its review.—(l) The posts and grades included in the cadre, 
their Level in pay matrix and classification shall be as specified in the First Schedule. 
(2) Authorised permanent strength of the various categories in the cadre shall be such as may, from time 
to time, be determined by the Government. 
(3) The Government may make temporary additions to or deletions from the strength of various 
categories as deemed necessary from time to time. 
(4) The Government may include in the cadre any post other than those included in the First Schedule 
or exclude from the cadre a post included in the said Schedule. 
(5) The Government may appoint any officer whose post is included in the cadre under sub-rule (4), to 
the appropriate grade of the cadre in a temporary capacity or in a substantive capacity, as it may be deemed 
fit. 
(6) Notwithstanding anything contained in the foregoing sub-rules, the authorised strength of category- 


B shall, at no time, exceed sixty per cent. of the total authorised strength of the cadre. 


4. Future maintenance.—(l) All new posts shall be created only in the lowest grades of category-A and 
category-B as specified in the First Schedule. 


(2) Vacancies in any grade due to retirement, resignation etc. shall revert to the post of Technician ‘A’ 
in category-A and to the post of Senior Technical Assistant ‘B’ in category —B in the ratio of 40:60, subject 
to the sanctioned strength of posts in each category. 


(3) No vacancy shall arise in a grade on promotion of a person from that grade to the next higher grade 
and on promotion from one grade to the next grade, the post held by an individual in the lower grade shall 
stand upgraded automatically and shall be personal to the individual. 


(4) Direct Recruitment: (a) Direct recruitment shall be made only in the lowest grades of category-A 
and category-B for which the qualifications and age limits shall be as laid down in the Second Schedule. 


(b) Candidates possessing the higher qualifications viz., Master of Science, Doctor of Philosophy, Bachelor 
of Technology, Bachelor of Engineering Degrees etc. in any subject or discipline shall not be considered for 
recruitment to both categories. 


(c) Candidates with Diploma or Bachelor’s Degree in Science shall be eligible for recruitment to the lowest 
grade of category-A provided such candidates fulfil the educational qualification requirements for 
recruitment to the lowest grade of category-A, as laid down in the Second Schedule. 


(5) Departmental Competitive Examination (DCE): (a) Fifty per cent. of the vacancies for 
recruitment in the lowest grade of category-A and twenty-five per cent. of the vacancies in the lowest grade 
of category-B shall be filled through Departmental Competitive Examination from amongst departmental 
employees with minimum three years regular service in Defence Research and Development Organisation 
and possessing qualifications prescribed in the Second Schedule but without any age limit, failing which the 
unfilled vacancies may be filled by direct recruitment. 


(b) Possession of higher qualifications like Master of Science, Doctor of Philosophy, Bachelor of 
Technology, Bachelor of Engineering degrees etc. by departmental candidates is not a bar for appearing to 
recruitment to the lowest grades of category-A and category-B through Departmental Competitive 
Examination, provided such candidates possess the qualifications prescribed in the Second Schedule for 
recruitment to that grade. 


(6) For filling up the posts through direct recruitment or through Departmental Competitive 
Examination, the choice of specialisations, subject to sub-rules (4) and (5), shall be at the discretion of the 
appointing authority depending upon the functional requirement. 


(7) The composition of the Selection Board for direct recruitment or recruitment through Departmental 
Competitive Examination to the lowest grades of category-A and category-B is as given in the Fourth 
Schedule. 


5. Limited Flexible Complementing Scheme.—(]) (a) Promotions from one grade to another grade shall be 
made under the merit-based Limited Flexible Complementing Scheme, from amongst eligible personnel in the cadre 
and the names of the eligible candidates are recommended by Eligibility Screening Committee for Defence Research 
Technical Cadre Assessment, which will recommend list of eligible candidates for Defence Research Technical Cadre 
Central Assessment, based on approved eligibility norms. 


(b) Constitution of Eligibility Screening Committee for Defence Research Technical Cadre Assessment shall 
be as specified in the Sixth Schedule.
