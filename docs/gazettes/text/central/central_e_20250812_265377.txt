NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 11th August, 2025 


S.O. 3664(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of 2-lane Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 332 
at Km. 464/24-26 between Veeravalli - Nuzvid stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State 
of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Krishna for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from 
the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
