PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 5th January, 2025 
No. 39/2024-25 
Subject: Procedure for export of certified organic products. 


F. No. 0/9/80/90/AM5/Export Cell.—In exercise of the powers conferred under para .03 and 2.04 of 
the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023 as amended time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby lays down the 
following procedure for export of certified organic products, in supersession of earlier Public Notice No. 73 (RE- 
203)/2009-204 dated 8..204 and Public Notice No. 0/20i5-2020 dated 05.05.205: 


(i) “Organic Products” for export shall only be certified as such if Produced, Processed, Packed and 
labelled as per the standards laid down in the document “National Programme for Organic 
Production (NPOP)”, available on the website of APEDA www.apeda.gov.in as amended from time 
to time. 


(ii) A product will be allowed to be exported as “Organic Product” only when accompanied by a 
Transaction Certificate (TC) issued by a Certification Body accredited by the National Accreditation 
Body (NAB) for Organic Products under the National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP) 
of the Department of Commerce. 


(iii) The 8" edition of the NPOP shall come into force with effect from l80 days from the date of 
issuance of this notification. 


2. Effect of this Public Notice: Procedure for export of Certified Organic Products has been notified. This 


supersedes the earlier Public Notice No. 73 (RE-203)/2009-20I4 dated 8..20I4 and Public Notice No. 0/205- 
2020 dated 05.05.205.
