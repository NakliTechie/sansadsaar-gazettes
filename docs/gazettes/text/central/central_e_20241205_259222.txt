Subject: Export of Broken Rice to Senegal and Gambia through National Cooperative Exports Limited 
(NCEL) -reg 

S.O. 525I(E).—In exercise of powers conferred under Section 3 read with Section 5 of the Foreign Trade 
(Development & Regulation) Act, !992, read with Para |.02 and 2.0] of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended 
from time to time, the Central Government hereby amends Notification No. 46 dated 30.I].2023 read with 
Notification No. 5/2024-25 dated 05.06.2024 for the export of Broken Rice to Senegal and Gambia through NCEL, 
as under: - 


The time period for Export of Broken Rice (ITC(HS) 0064000) through NCEL to Senegal and Gambia 
for the quantity(s) notified vide Notification No. 46 dated 30..2023 read with Notification No. 5/2024-25 dated 
05.06.2024 has been extended by 2 months up to 3lst January 2025. 


Effect of the Notification: Time period for Export of Broken Rice to Senegal and Gambia through NCEL has been 
extended for 2 months up to 3lst January 2025.
