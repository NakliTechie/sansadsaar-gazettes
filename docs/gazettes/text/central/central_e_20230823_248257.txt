New Delhi, the 23rd August, 2023 


S.O. 3778(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 69 of the Representation of the People Act, 
95 (43 of 95), the Central Government, after consulting the Election Commission of India, hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Conduct of Elections Rules, [96[, namely:— 


.(.) These rules may be called the Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Conduct of Elections Rules, 96] (hereinafter referred to as the said Rules), after rule 8, the following rule 
shall be inserted, namely:— 


“I8A. Voters on election duty to cast his vote at facilitation centre.— Notwithstanding anything in this Part, a 
voter on election duty shall receive his postal ballot, record his vote thereon in accordance with this Part and 
return the same at the facilitation centre as specified, in writing, by the returning officer.”. 


3. In rule 24 of the said Rules, in sub-rule (2), in clause (c), for the words “any gazetted officer”, the words “any 
Group A or Group B officer” shall be substituted.

defined in clause (r) of section 2 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 20l6 and flagged” shall 
be substituted; 


(b) inclause (e), for the words and figures “above 65 years”, the words and figures “above 80 years” shall 
be substituted. 


5. In Form 3D to the said Rules, in paragraph (5),— 
(a) for the words “any Gazetted Officer’, the words “any Group A or Group B officer” shall be substituted; 
(b) the following shall be inserted at the end, namely:— 


“A voter on election duty who opts for postal ballot shall receive his postal ballot, record his vote 
thereon and return the same at the facilitation centre as specified, in writing, by the returning officer.”.
