MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th March, 2024 


S.O. 1531(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 2012 (37 of 2012), 
the Central Government hereby nominates Dr. Poonam Khetrapal Singh, to be member of Institute Body of All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences, Bathinda, Punjab in place of Dr. Ashok Panagariya, Professor Emeritus, Sawai Man 
Singh Medical College, Jaipur, Rajasthan, and for that purpose makes the following amendment in the notification of 
Government of India, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Number Z-28016/170/2018-PMSSY-IV dated the 30th 
January, 2020 published in the Gazette of India Part II, section 3, Sub-Section (ii) vide SO 616(E) dated the 7th Feb, 
2020: 


In the said notification, for serial number 9 and the entry relating thereto, the following should be substituted, 
namely:- 


“9. Dr. Poonam Khetrapal Singh;” 


The terms of office of member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956.
