का.आ. 1010(अ).--केन्द्रीय सरकार ने पेट्रोलियम और खनिज पाइपलाइन (भूमि में उपयोग के अधिकार का 
अधिग्रहण) अधिनियम 1962 (1962 का 50) (जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया) है की धारा 3 की 
उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के पेट्रोलियम और प्राकृतिक गैस मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का. 
आ. 4348 (८) दिनांक 19 -11-2019 द्वारा उस अधिसूचना से संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि में केंद्र सरकार ने 
परियोजना KG-DWN-98/2 के लिए जी. मूलापोलेम एच/ओ जी. वेमावारम(वी) पर स्थित लैंड फॉल पॉइंट से आंध्रप्रदेश 
राज्य के ईस्ट गोदावरी जिले के आई. पोलावरम मंडल में गोगुल्लंका एच/ओ गुथिनादीवी (वी) तक पाइपलाइन बिछाने के 
उद्देश्य से उस अधिसूचना से जुड़ी अनुसूची में निर्दिष्ट भूमि में उपयोगकर्ता का अधिकार हासिल करने के अपने इरादे की

बिछाई जानी चाहिए।

MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th February, 2024 


S.O. 1010(E).—Whereas by Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas S.O. No. 4348 (E) dt.19-11 -2019 issued under Sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipe Lines (Acquisition of Right of uses in Land) Act 1962 (50 of 1962) (here in after referred to as the said 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the Right of User in the land specified in the Schedule 
appended to that Notification for the purpose of laying pipeline for Project KG-DWN-98/2 from land fall point at 
G. Moolapolem H/o G. Vemavaram(V) to Gogullanka H/o Guthinadeevi (५) of I. Polavaram Mandal of East 
Godavari District in Andhra Pradesh State a pipeline should be laid by the ONGC, Eastern Offshore Asset, Kakinada, 
East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas copies of the said Gazette Notification were made available to the public from 03.01.2020. 


And whereas no objection have been received from the public to lying of the pipeline by the Competent 
Authority. 


And whereas the Competent Authority has under Sub-section (1) of section 6 of the said Act, submitted 
report to the Central Government. 


And whereas the Central Government after considering the said report, decided to acquire the Right of 
User in the lands specified in the Schedule. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the Right of User on the land specified in the Schedules appended to this 
Notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline: 


And further in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section (4) of Section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the Right of Users on the land for laying of pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vest, on this date of the publication of this declaration, in the ONGC, EOA, Kakinada Asset, free 
from encumbrances.
