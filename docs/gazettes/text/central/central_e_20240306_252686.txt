NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th March, 2024 


S.O. 08(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.4588 (E) Dated:9/0/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building & widening to two lane with hard shoulder, maintenance, 
management and operation of Ukhrul-Taloi-Tadubi Road Pkg.-I (including Ukhrul Bypass) on NH-!02A in the stretch 
of land from De. Ch. 0.000 Km (Ex. Ch. 537.850 Km on NH-202) to De. Ch. 6.57] (Ch. 9.940 on Ex. Ukhrul-Taloi- 
Tadubi Road) under the jurisdiction of Deputy Commissioner, Ukhrul in the district of UKHRUL in the state of 
MANIPUR; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Sanghai Express (English 
Edition)” and “Huetyen Lanpao (Manipuri Edition)” both dated 9/] [/2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received no objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
