New Delhi, the 26th February, 2024 


S.O. 897(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and

as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management 
and operation of NH766C in the stretch of land from Km. 0.000 to Km. 37.850 (Except Km.24.45 to Km.27.20) of

settled the same appropriately;

submitted its report to the Central Government;
