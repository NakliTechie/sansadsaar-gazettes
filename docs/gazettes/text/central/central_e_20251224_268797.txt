MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th December, 2025 


5.0. 5989(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number S.0.473(E) Dated: 27 January 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part वी, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 
(48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for (widening / four-laning, etc.), Maintenance, management 
and operation of NH321G in the stretch of land from Km. 51+600 to Km. 75+700 (Agra-Jalesar-Etah Road) 
(Package-2) in the district of ETAH in the state of UTTAR PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hindustan (Hindi)” And 
“Hindustan Times (English)” both dated “07 September 2025”; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
