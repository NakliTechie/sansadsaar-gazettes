NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 20th March , 2023 

S.O. 1288 ( E ) .— In exercise of powers conferred by clause ( a ) of section 3 of the National 
Highways Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) , the Central Government hereby authorize the officer mentioned in 
column ( 2 ) of the Schedule annexed hereto as the competent authority to perform the functions of such 
authority under the said Act with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette , in respect of the stretch of land specified in corresponding entry in column ( 3 ) of the said Schedule 
relating to State , District , Taluka and Village mentioned in column ( 4 ) , ( 5 ) , ( 6 ) and ( 7 ) respectively of the 
said Schedule from Km . 57.500 to Km . 98.250 of the National Highway No.160 for building 
( widening / four - laning , etc. ) maintenance , management and operation of the National Highway No. 160 in 
the State of MAHARASHTRA .
