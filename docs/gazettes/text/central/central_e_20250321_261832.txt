MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |lth March, 2025 


S.O. 344(E).—In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter called the said 
Act), the Government of India hereby authorizes Shri Rishabh Verma, Deputy Collector, 
Government of Uttar Pradesh in place of Shri Praveen Yadav, Deputy Collector, Government of 
Uttar Pradesh to perform the functions of Competent Authority under the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962, on deputation basis, for all Gas Pipeline 
Projects of M/s GAIL (India) Limited in the State of Uttar Pradesh for a period of Three Years. 


This notification will be effective from the date of its issue.
