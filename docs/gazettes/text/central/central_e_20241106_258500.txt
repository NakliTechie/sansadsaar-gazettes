New Delhi, the 6th November, 2024 


No. 37/1/(II1)/2024/T.—The following order by the President dated the 6th November, 2024 is 
published for general information: - 


“In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by clause (1) of article 85 of the Constitution, I hereby 
summon the Lok Sabha to meet at New Delhi on Monday, the 257 November, 2024 at 11.00 A.M. 


November 6, 2024 
DROUPADI MURMU, President” 


P. C. TRIPATHY, Addl. Secy.
