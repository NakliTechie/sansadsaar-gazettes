MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |7th May, 2024 


9.0. 2006(E).— Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of LPG in the State of Gujarat, a pipeline should be laid for implementing Kandla — Gorakhpur 
LPG pipeline (KGPL) project by IHB Limited. 


And whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, it is 
necessary to acquire the right of user in the land described in the schedule annexed to this notification. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub section () of section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein; 


Any person interested in the land described in the said schedule may, within twenty—one days from the date 
on which the copies of this notification, as published in the Gazette of India, are made available to the General Public, 
object in writing to the acquisition of the right of user therein for laying of the pipeline under the land to Shri Gaurav 
Jerambhai Prajapati, Retired Deputy District Development Officer, Competent Authority, IHB Limited, Kandla 
Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project, Ahmedabad (Gujarat).
