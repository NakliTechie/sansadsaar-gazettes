और, चूंकि आपत्तियां प्राप्त हुईं और उन पर सक्षम प्राधिकारी द्वारा विचार किया गया तथा उन्हें अस्वीकृत कर

और, चूंकि, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उपधारा () के अनुसरण में सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने अपनी रिपोर्ट 
केन्द्रीय सरकार को प्रस्तुत कर दी है; 


अतः अब सक्षम प्राधिकारी की उक्त रिपोर्ट प्राप्त होने पर तथा उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 208 की उपधारा (॥) 
द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए केन्द्रीय सरकार घोषणा करती है कि इसके साथ संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि 
पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी; 


और, इसके अतिरिक्त, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उपधारा (2) के अनुसरण में, केन्द्रीय सरकार एतट्दारा 
घोषित करती है कि इस अधिसूचना के राजपत्र में प्रकाशन पर, इसके साथ संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी 
भारग्रस्तताओं से मुक्त होकर पूर्णतः केन्द्रीय सरकार में निहित हो जाएगी।

Mumbai, the 6th December, 2024 


S.O. 5283(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), Number 5.0. 598(E), dated 77 December, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under sub- section () of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 
989) (hereinafter referred in as the said act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to he said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Vijapur-Ambliyasan Gauge Conversion Project (Western Railway) 
in the District of Mahesana in the State of Gujarat; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the 
“Indian Experess”, The Times of India” (English), “Divya Bhaskar” & “Gujarat Samachar (Gujarati) dated 6" 
December ,2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the Competent 
Authority and accordingly orders have been passed; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule Annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declared that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
