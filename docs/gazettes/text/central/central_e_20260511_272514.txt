New Delhi, the llth May, 2026 


F. No. A-5003/354/2024-E-III.— In continuation of the erstwhile Ministry of Irrigation and Power's 
Resolution No. FC-47(2)/72, dated the l8th April, 972 constituting the Ganga Flood Control Board (hereinafter 
referred to as the said resolution), the Central Government hereby makes the further amendments in the said resolution, 
namely: - 


2. In the said resolution, 


(i) in paragraph 2, against serial number 20, for the words "Chairman, Ganga Flood Control Commission", the words 
"Chairman, Ganga Flood Control Commission cum Member (Floods)" shall be substituted; 


(ii) after paragraph 2, the following paragraph shall be inserted, namely: 


॥2,] The Ganga Flood Control Board shall meet at such intervals as may be necessary for the effective discharge 
and execution of its mandatory functions: 


provided that the Board shall meet at least once in every calendar year.".
