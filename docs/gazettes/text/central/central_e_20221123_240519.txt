S.O. 5452 ( E ) .- In exercise of powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government , after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose , the land , the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below , is required for building 
widening , two / four lane with paved shoulders etc . , ) , maintenance , management and operation of National Highway 
No.167B in the stretch of land from Km 22.300 to Km 58.635 ( Mydukur to Porumamilla section ) in the district of 
Y.S.R. ( Kadapa ) in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH , hereby declares its intention to acquire such land .
