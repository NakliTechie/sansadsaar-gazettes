MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |lth March, 2026 


G.S.R. 74(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 4 of the Van (Sanrakshan 
Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 980 (69 of 980), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to further 
amend the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Rules, 2023, namely: — 


() These rules may be called the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Amendment Rules, 2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Rules, 2023, in rule 9, for sub-rule (2), the following shall be substituted, 
namely,- 


“((2) In addition to every proposal verified in the field by the Divisional Forest Officer concerned, field inspection 
shall be simultaneously undertaken for every proposal that involves more than forty hectares of forest land by the 
Conservator of Forests or Chief Conservator of Forests or Regional Chief Conservator of Forests holding charge of the 
concerned forest circle.".
