New Delhi, the 11th March, 2025 


S.O. 1129(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 41 of the Haj Committee 
Act, 2002 (35 of 2002), the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendment in the Schedule to the 


said Act, namely:— 


In the said Schedule, under the heading relating to ZONE-V, for the words “UNION TERRITORIES OF 
DAMAN AND DIU AND DADRA AND NAGAR HAVELI”, the words “UNION TERRITORY OF DADRA AND 
NAGAR HAVELI AND DAMAN AND DIU” shall be substituted. 

[F. No. HAJ-11/1/2022-HAJ-MoMA] 
CPS BAKSHI, Jt. Secy.

132(B), dated 4th February, 2003 and 8.0. 3834(E), dated 27" October, 2020.
