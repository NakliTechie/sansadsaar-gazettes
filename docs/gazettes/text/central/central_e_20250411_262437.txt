q. एक पुस्तकालयाध्यक्ष जिसकी नियुक्ति इसी प्रयोजन हेतु गठित चयन समिति की संस्तुति पर कार्यकारी परिषद द्वारा की 
जाएगी और वह विश्वविद्यालय का पूर्णकालिक बैतनिक अधिकारी होगा। 

2. पुस्तकालयाध्यक्ष के चयन के उद्देश्य हेतु केन्द्रीय विश्वविद्यालय अधिनियम, 2009 के परिनियम 8 के खंड(2) के उपबंधों 
के अनुसार एक चयन समिति गठित की जाएगी। 


3. इस प्रकार नियुक्त पुस्तकालयाध्यक्ष कुलपति के प्रशासनिक पर्यवेक्षण और अनुशासनात्मक नियंत्रण के अधीन कार्य करेगा। 


4. यदि पुस्तकालयाध्यक्ष को सरकार या किसी अन्य संगठन/संस्था से प्रतिनियुक्ति पर नियुक्त किया जाता है, तो उसकी सेवा 
की शर्तें विश्वविद्यालय के प्रतिनियुक्ति नियमों द्वारा शासित होंगी।

ORDINANCES-I 
For Appointment, Tenure, terms, and conditions of 
THE OFFICERS OF THE UNIVERSITY 
(THE LIBRARIAN) 
(Under Section 9 and |7 and Statute 9) 


In exercise of the powers vested in Section 28 (2) of the Central Universities Act, 2009 and for the purpose of 
implementing Sections 9 and ॥7 and Statute 9, the Executive Council hereby makes the following Ordinances for 
appointment and other terms and conditions of Librarian in Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University (A Central 
University). 


Appointment of the Librarian 


l. There shall be a Librarian who shall be appointed by Executive Council on the recommendation of the Selection 
Committee constituted for the purpose and he/she shall be a whole-time salaried officer of the University. 


2. There shall be constituted a Selection Committee in accordance with the provisions of clause (2) of Statute I8 
of the Central Universities Act, 2009 for the purpose of selection of the Librarian. 


3. The Librarian so appointed shall work under the administrative supervision and disciplinary control of the 
Vice-Chancellor. 


4. Incase the Librarian is appointed on deputation from the Government or any other Organization/Institution, 
the terms and conditions of his/her service shall be governed by the Deputation Rules of the University. 


Provided that the Librarian appointed on deputation may be repatriated earlier than the stipulated period by the Executive 
Council on the recommendation of the Vice-Chancellor.

Tenure of the Librarian 


l. The person appointed as the Librarian shall hold the office as the Librarian until he/she attains the age of sixty- 
two years. 


Provided that where the librarian is appointment on deputation, his/her tenure shall be as provided in the terms of 
deputation. 


Acting Librarian 


l. Incase of vacancy in the office of the Librarian, duties of the office of Librarian shall be performed (in addition 
to his own duties) by such officer or Professor of the University, as may be nominated by the Vice-Chancellor 
for such period as may be specified by the Vice-Chancellor: 


Provided that where the Librarian is absent on leave or duty or is unable to perform his duties for any other reason, the 
Vice-Chancellor may, for such duration of absence or inability make arrangement for the office in the same manner as 
in the case of vacancy in the office or, if he seems fit, assign the duties of the office to a suitable person holding a senior 
administrative position under the Librarian. 


Terms and Conditions 


l. The Librarian shall be appointed on such scale of pay as prescribed by the University Grants Commission, and 
shall be entitled to such leave, leave salary, allowances, provident fund, and other benefits as prescribed by the 
University from time to time for the administrative staff of the University. 


2. The Librarian shall be provided with an unfurnished residential accommodation for which rent, or license fee 
shall be payable at the usual rate. 


3. The librarian shall be the administrative head of the University Library (located in any campus of the 
University) and shall be responsible for the general management thereof. 


4. The Librarian shall be the Member-Secretary of the Library Committee and of the Committee of Management 
of the Library System of the University. 


5. Subject to the provisions of the Act, Statutes and Ordinances, the Librarian shall exercise such powers and 
perform such duties as may be assigned to him/her from time to time by the Executive Council or by the 
Vice-Chancellor.
