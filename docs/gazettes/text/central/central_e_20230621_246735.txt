MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th June, 2023 


F. No. 2022/E&R/I(3)/l0pt.—It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view 
the operational requirements, Central Government has decided to revise the jurisdiction of Rangiya, 
Alipurduar and Tinsukia Divisions of Northeast Frontier Railway. The revised jurisdictions amongst 
these Divisions will be as under: 


(i) A portion of Rangiya-Murkongselek section, from chainage 290/0 Km (between 
Silanibari-North Lakhimpur block section) to chainage 450/0 Km (Murkongselek), is 
transferred from Rangiya Division to Tinsukia Division of Northeast Frontier Railway. The 
new boundary between Rangiya Division and Tinsukia Division of Northeast Frontier 
Railway will now be at 290/0 Km on Rangiya-Murkongselek Section. 


(ii) Jurisdiction of Dangtal station is transferred from Alipurduar division to Rangiya 
division. Accordingly, existing boundary between Rangiya division & Alipurduar division 
will now be at 242.000 km. Further, Basugaon station of Alipurduar Division is nominated 
as the new interchange point between Alipurduar and Rangiya Division. 


The changes in the above jurisdiction will come into effect from Ist July 2023. 


PRADEEP KUMAR GARG, Secy.
