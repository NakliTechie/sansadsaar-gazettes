MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 10th August, 2023 


S.O. 3581(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 3 of the 
Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in 
the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub —section (ii), vide number S.O. 6071 (E ), dated the 277 
December, 2022, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, - 


(A) In the Hindi version, in column (2) of first row, the words, “गुजरात तटीय जोन प्रबंध प्राधिकरण का नाम और 
संपर्क SA” is substituted with the word, “Ward”, 


(B) In both Hindi and English version, - 


(a) against serial number 5, in column (2), for the words “Water Resources Department”, the words “Public 
Works Department” shall be substituted; 


(b) against serial number 11, in column (2), for the words “Shri Sushant S. Naik”, the words “Dr. Sushant S. 
Naik” shall be substituted; 


(c) against serial number 13, in column (2), for the word “Director”, the words “Director and Exofficio 
Joint Secretary” shall be substituted.
