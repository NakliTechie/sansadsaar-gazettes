manufacturer in accordance with the ceiling price specified in column (5) of the above table as per

price list, if any, as furnished by the manufacturer, on a conspicuous part of the premises where he carries

The manufacturers of above said scheduled formulations shall furnish quarterly return to the NPPA, in
