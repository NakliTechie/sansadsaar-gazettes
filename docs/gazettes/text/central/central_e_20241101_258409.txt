S.O. 4780(E).—Whereas, the Commission of Inquiry (hereinafter called the said Commission) was 
constituted on 6"" October, 2022 vide notification number S.O. 4742(E) under section 3 of the Commissions of Inquiry 
Act, 952 (60 of 952), to examine the matter of according Scheduled Castes status to new persons, who claim to 
historically have belonged to Scheduled Castes but have converted to religion other than those mentioned in the 
Presidential Orders issued from time to time under article 34 of the Constitution, and matters related thereto; 


And whereas, the said Commission was required to submit its report and recommendations on or before the 
0" October, 2024; 


And whereas, the said Commission had requested to renew the tenure of the Commission for finalizing its 
reports and recommendations; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Commissions of Inquiry Act, 952 
(60 of 952), the Central Government hereby extend the tenure of the Commission, for a period of one year with 
effect from I' October, 2024 to 0" October, 2025.
