दिनांक 10.01.2023 को भारत के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित wast 177 (ई), संख्या 169 अतिरिक्त, भाग 2, धारा 3, 
उप-धारा (1) और राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम 1956 (1956 का 48) की धारा 3 ए की उप-धारा (1) के तहत जारी किया

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 10th October, 2023 


S.O. 4403(E).— Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.177(E), dated 10.01.2023 published in Gazette of India, No. 169 Extra-ordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
sub-section (ii) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways Act 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the lands specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 4, in the stretch of land from Km 106/800 to Km. 134/439 (Walajapet / Ranipet to 
Andhra Pradesh Border / Tamil Nadu Border Section) District of Vellore and Ranipet in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dinakaran” and “The Times of 
India” both dated 22.01.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; And whereas no objection petition 
was received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
