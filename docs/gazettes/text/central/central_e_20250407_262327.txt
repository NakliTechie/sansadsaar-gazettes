MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Revenue) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th April, 2025 
No. 02/2025-Central Excise 


G.S.R. 220(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944 (1 of 1944) 
read with section 147 of Finance Act, 2002 (20 of 2002), the Central Government being satisfied that it is necessary 
in the public interest so to do, hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government 
of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) No. 05/2019-Central Excise, dated the 6" July, 2019, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 488(E), dated 
the 6" July, 2019, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, - 
(i) against SI. No. 1, in column (4), for the entry, the entry “Rs. 13 per litre” shall be substituted; 
(ii) against SI. No. 2, in column (4), for the entry, the entry “Rs. 10 per litre” shall be substituted. 
2. This notification shall come into force on the 8" day of April, 2025. 
[F.No. 190349/6/2025-TRU] 
DHEERAJ SHARMA, Under Secy. 


Note. - The principal notification No. 05/2019-Central Excise, dated the 6" July, 2019 was published in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number 488(E), dated the 6" July,2019 and 
last amended vide notification No. 03/2022-Central Excise, dated the 30" June, 2022, vide number 
G.S.R. 491(E), dated 30" June, 2022.
