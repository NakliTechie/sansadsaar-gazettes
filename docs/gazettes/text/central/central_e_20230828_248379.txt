NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2023 


G.S.R. 63(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Central Indirect Taxes and Customs Directorates Staff 
Car Driver (Special Grade) Recruitment Rules, 2023, namely: - 


l. ({) These rules may be called the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs Directorates Staff Car Driver 
(Special Grade) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the official Gazette. 

2. Inthe Central Indirect Taxes and Customs Directorates Staff Car Driver (Special Grade) Recruitment Rules, 


2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule |, in sub-rule ((), णि the words “Central Indirect Taxes and 
Customs”, the words “Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs” shall be substituted. 


3. In the said rules, in rule 2, in sub-rule (.), for item (xiii), the following item shall be substituted, namely: - 
“(xiii) Directorate General of Human Resource Development”. 
4. In the said rules, in the Schedule, in column (l!), for the Note, the following Note shall be substituted, 
namely, - 
“Note: Where juniors who have completed their qualifying or eligibility service are being considered for promotion, 
their seniors shall also be considered provided they are not short of the requisite qualifying or eligibility service by 
more than half of such qualifying or eligibility service or two years, whichever is less, and have successfully 
completed their probation period for promotion to the next higher grade along with their juniors who have already 
completed such qualifying or eligibility service”.
