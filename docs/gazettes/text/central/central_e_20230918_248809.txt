LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th September, 2023 


No. 14/1/23-EMB. —The Speaker, Lok Sabha is pleased to notify the new building of Parliament 
situated in the precincts of the Parliament House on Plot Number 118, New Delhi, east of the existing 
Parliament House with Raisina road to the south and Red Cross road to the north, to be, hereon, designated 
as the Parliament House of India. 


SIDDHARTH MAHAJAN, Jt. Secy.
