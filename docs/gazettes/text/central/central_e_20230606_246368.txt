S.O. 2483(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the Sub-Section (i) of Section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for "Widening and Strengthening of existing NH-l6B from Km 25.500 to Km 4.000" in connection with 
the “Preparation of Detailed Project Report for Rehabilitation and Up-gradation to 2-Lane with Paved 
Shoulders Configuration of Nandakumar-Contai-Digha-Chandaneshwar Road (0.000 Km. to 90.775 Km.) 
along with Contai Bye-pass (3.30 Km.) (Total Length 94.075 Km.) of NH-l6B in the District- Purba 
Medinipur in the State of West Bengal", hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one (2) days from the date of publication of this 
notification, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (l) of section 3C of the said 
Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition Officer, Office of 
the District Magistrate & Collector, Purba Medinipur, Village-Ganapatinagar, P.O. Uttar Sonamui, P.S. Tamluk,
