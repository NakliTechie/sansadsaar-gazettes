५.0. 4578(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of Section 20A of the Railway Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, an area of land measuring more or less 0.93 acre (0.37652 hectare), the brief description of which 
has been given in the Schedule annexed hereto, is required for construction of Approach Road from N.H. |0 to 
Tunnel No. 4 and 5 for the execution of the Special Railway Project, namely, Sivok-Rangpo New BG Rail Line 


Darjeeling District in the state of West Bengal, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within Thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 


under sub-section () of Section 20D of the said Act; 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition 
Officer, Darjeeling, West Bengal in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by 


order, either allow or disallow the objection; 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of Section 20D of the said Act shall be 


final;
