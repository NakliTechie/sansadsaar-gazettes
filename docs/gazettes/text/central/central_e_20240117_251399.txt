1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, S.O. 2753 (E) dated 15" June, 2022, published 
in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ID), dated 15" June, 2022 namely: -

replaced by “Ganwaria”.
