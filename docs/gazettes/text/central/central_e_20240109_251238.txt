NOTIFICATION 
Gorakhpur, the 9th January, 2024 


S.O. 96(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway ( North 
Eastern Railway, Construction Orgination ),Notification Number S.O.No 5085(E) dated 297 November, 2023 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 297 November, 2023 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act 
989 (24 OF 989 ) & Railways Amendment Act 2008 (/ of 2008 ) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance,management and operation of the Special Railway Project 
namely new railway line between Anand Nagar To Ghughali Via Maharajganj Section (52.70 Km) Of North Eastern 
Railway, in Maharajganj District of the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And wher hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Jagran” Gorakhpur dated 05.2.2023 & “Times of India” Gorkahpur dated 05.2.2023 under sub-section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas the competent Authority has recived no objection in the project against the notification. And, 
whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, namely, Deputy Land Acquisition 
Officer (CALA) Maharajganj, Uttar Pradesh and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20E 
of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall 
be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
