MINISTRY OF CULTURE 
(Archaeological Survey of India) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 10th October, 2023 


G.S.R. 729(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to further amend the Ministry of Culture, Archaeological Survey of India, 
Private Secretary Recruitment Rules, 2023, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Culture, Archeological Survey of India, Private Secretary (Group ‘B’ 
post), Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Ministry of Culture, Archaeological Survey of India, Private Secretary Recruitment Rules, 2023,-- 
(a) Inthe Schedule,-- 


(ii) In column (11), for the existing entry under Note 1 of Deputation, the following entry shall be 
substituted, namely:-- 


The departmental Officers in the feeder category who are in the direct line of promotion will not be 
eligible for consideration for appointment on deputation or absorption. Similarly, Deputatists shall not 
be eligible for consideration for appointment by promotion.
