Subject : Re - designation of the post of Heads of LSA field Units of DoT – regarding . 

No. 9-5 ( 10 ) /2021-Estt.-This refers to the Department of Telecommunications notification no . 9-5 ( 1 ) / 2016 
Estt . dated 23.02.2017 . The erstwhile 34 TERM Cells were restructured into 22 Licensed Service Area ( LSA ) field 
Units each headed by HAG + / HAG Level Officers . Heads of LSA field Units report to Director General Telecom , an 
apex level post approved by the Cabinet .
