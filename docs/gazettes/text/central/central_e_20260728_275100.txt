NOTIFICATION 
Gorakhpur, the 28th July, 2026 


S.O. 4185(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (North Eastern Railway, Construction Origination), Notification number, S.O. 1898 (E) date 
13 April 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated 13 
April 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A 
of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely Khalilabad-Bahraich New Line Railway project (240.66 km.) in Tehsil Khalilabad of 
Santkabirnagar District in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
"Hindustaan" , “Aamarujala” and ''The Times of India" Dated 23 April 2026 under sub section (4) 
of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, the competent Authority has resolved all the objection received in this project 
against the notification. And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all 
encumbrances.
