New Delhi, the 3rd November, 2022 


G.S.R. 810(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 (13 of 
1885), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian Telegraph Rules, 1951, 


namely:— 
1. (1) These rules may be called the Indian Telegraph (Amendment) Rules, 2022. 
(2) These shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

and figures “for a period of five years from the 27" March, 2017”, the words and figures, “for a period upto the 
317 March, 2025” shall be substituted.
