MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2025 


S.O. 6073(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.3893(E) dated 25/08/2025 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 
National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening /2- 
Laning with paved shoulder/four laning), maintenance , management and operation of the National Highway No. 
60 SPUR in the stretch of land from Km. 2+928 to Km 63+378 Bhimashankar- Taleghar — Wada — Rajgurunagar 
Road in the District of Pune, in the State of Maharashtra

And whereas the substance of the said notification $.0.3893(E) dated 25/08/2025 has been published in 
“Business Standard” and “Dainik Sandhya” Both Dated 13/09/2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
