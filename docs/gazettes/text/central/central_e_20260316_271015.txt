MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l6th March, 2026 


S.O. 373(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule 
below, is required for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, 
management and operation of Greenfield Expressway in the stretch of land from Km. 290 to Km. 369 (New 
Collector Office Building, Aurangabad Road, Ahmednagar) in the district of AHMEDNAGAR in the state 
of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(l) of section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Dy Collector (Land 
Acquisition No.l) Ahmednagar in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority 
shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall 
be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and 
can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
