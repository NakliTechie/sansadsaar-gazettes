G.S.R. 32(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20i(! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.-(|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin to 
commemorate on the occasion of BIRTH CENTENARY OF MOHAMMED RAFI) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of One Hundred Rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of BIRTH CENTENARY OF MOHAMMED 
RAFI. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.- The coin of the above denomination mentioned in rule 2 shall conform to 
the following dimensions and composition, namely: -
