G.S.R. 595(E).— — In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and 
in superssession of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Group ‘B’ Posts, Recruitment Rules, 
208, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the 
following rules to regulate the method of recruitment to the posts of Section Officer, Private Secretary, Stores Officer, 
Section Officer (Fire), Assistant Section Officer, Personal Assistant, Assistant Engineer (Civil), Security Supervisor 
and Transport Supervisor in the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, namely: - 


. SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT. - (l) THESE RULES MAY BE CALLED THE MINISTRY OF 
ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY, NON-SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNICAL (GROUP ‘B’ POSTS) 
RECRUITMENT RULES, 2025.

tules. 
3. Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix. — The number of the posts, their classification and
