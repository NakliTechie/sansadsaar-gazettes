S.O. 3161(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 1 of the 
Finance Act, 2024 (8 of 2024), the Central Government hereby appoints, — 
(a) the 17 day of October, 2024, as the date on which the provisions of sections 13 of the said Act shall come 
into force; 
(b) the 17 day of April, 2025, as the date on which the provisions of sections 11 and 12 of the said Act shall 
come into force.
