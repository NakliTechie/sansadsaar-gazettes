CENTRAL ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION 
CORRIGENDUM 
New Delhi, the 28th September, 2023 


F. No. L-1/250/2019/CERC.—In the notification No. L-1/250/2019/CERC dated 07.02.2023 
regarding the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (Sharing of Inter-State Transmission 
Charges and Losses) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2023, published in the Gazette of India 
Extraordinary (Part-III, Section-4, No. 149) on 01.03.2023, the following corrections are made: 


1. The words “under Step-1” in the last column of the first row of Table I and Table II under sub- 
clause (f) of Clause (2) of Regulation 13 shall be deleted. 


2. Inthe last line of sub-clause (f) of Clause (1) of Annexure III, the words “subclause (b) of Clause 
(2) of Regulation 15” shall be substituted with the words “sub-clause (a) of Clause (2) of 
Regulation 15”. 


3. Inthe formula under sub-clause (b)(i) under Clause (2) of Annexure-III, the expanded meaning of 
“T’ shall be read as under: 


“T” is the number of time blocks in a month for which T-GNA has beengranted 


4. In the formula under sub-clause (b)(Gi) of Clause (2) of Annexure-III, the expanded meaning of 
the word “Waiver” shall be read as under: 


“Waiver (%) = 100 X (sum of SDRTG for the time blocks in the month for which T-GNArg has 
been granted) / (total number of time blocks for which T-GNAgg has been granted in the month 
X 0.3 X T-GNArg)”
