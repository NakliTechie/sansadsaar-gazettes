MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2023 


S.O. 2284(E).—Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public 
interest that for the transportation of the LPG a pipeline should be laid for implementing 
of “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project” by the IHB Limited in the state of Gujarat. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, 
it is necessary to acquire the right of user (ROU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be 
laid, and which is described in the schedule annexed to this notification. 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (l) of Section 3 of the Petroleum 
and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central 
Government hereby declares its intention to acquire the right of user therein. 


Any person who is interested in the land described in the said schedule, may submit objection in 
writing to Shri. Mayankkumar R. Patel, Competent Authority and Prant Officer (Rural), Vadodara, Kandla- 
Gorakhpur LPG pipeline (KGPL), Dist. Vadodara (GJ) within twenty-one days from the date on which the 
copies of this notification issued under Sub-section () of section 3 of the said Act, as published in the 
Gazette of India are made available to the general public.
