S.O. 287(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/four laning/Six-laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of Greenfield alignment of Ismailabad 
(Gangheri) - Narnaul National Highway !52D in the stretch of land from Km 43.900 to Km 85.800 in the district of 
Charkhi Dadri in the state of Haryana, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, District Revenue Officer, Charkhi 
Dadri in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity 
of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making 
such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
