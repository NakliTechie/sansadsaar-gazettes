MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th January, 2023 
S.O. 231(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 31 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and 
Abolition) Act, 1970 (37 of 1970), the Central Government, after consultation with the Central Advisory Contract 
Labour Board, hereby exempts the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER), 
Chandigarh from the applicability of notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and 
Employment number S.O. 3161(E), dated the 12" December, 2014, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 


Part II, Section 3, sub-section (ii), dated the 12" December, 2014, in respect of employment of contract labour 
involved in the works of- 


(i) sanitation services (cleaning, sweeping and dusting); 
(ii) security services (Watch and ward of the institute); and 
(ili) catering services to admitted patients, 


in the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh, for a further period of one 
year with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette subject to the following 
conditions, namely:- 


(a) the hospital management shall ensure that the contract workers are paid wages and provided other facilities 
equal to the lowest paid regular worker performing similar work in the establishment in terms of sub-clause 
(a) of clause (v) of sub-rule (2) of rule 25 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Rules, 1971; 
and 


(b) the Board will have the right to inspect the establishment to ensure and check whether such wages and 
benefits are given and whether the above said conditions are implemented by the establishment.
