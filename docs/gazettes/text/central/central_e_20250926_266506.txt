परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 5.0. 2442(5.) तारीख 30/05/2025, जो भारत के 
राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) F प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा पंजाब राज्य के बर्ठिडा जिले में NH254

“थे ट्रिब्यून” , 07/06/2025 को “जगबानी” " में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

रिपोर्ट दे दी है ;

की उपधारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि उक्त अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का

New Delhi, the 26th September, 2025 


59.0. 4405(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 242(E.) Dated:30/05/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH254 in the stretch of land from Km. 32.300 to Km. 55.650 in the district of BATHINDA in the state of 
PUNJAB 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Tribune” dated 07/06/2025 , 
“Jagbani” dated 07/06/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
