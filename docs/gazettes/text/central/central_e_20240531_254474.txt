MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st May, 2024 
S.O. 2154(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred under Rule 16(1) of the Petroleum Rules, 2002, and in 
continuation of the notification Government of India, Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways S.O. 1413(E) dated 
30" March 2021, the Central Government, in consideration of the necessity to import petroleum in public interest, 
hereby extends the notification for Magdalla Port (Hazira), Surat {Reliance Industries Ltd., Shipping & Offshore 
Division Terminal- Gas Jetty-1, Gas Jetty-2 and EDC Jetty} in Gujarat for import of petroleum into India by sea in 


accordance with operations permitted in Navigational Safety at Ports Committee (NSPC) certificate number 
NSPC/050/2022 dated 30.05.2022 for a period from from 01.06.2024 to 31.05.2027.
