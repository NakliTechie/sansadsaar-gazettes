NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 14th October , 2022 

G.S.R. 776 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses ( d ) and ( e ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act , 2011 ( 11 of 2011 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules , namely : - 


One Hundred Rupees 


1. Short title and commencement.- ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Coinage ( Issue of commemorative coin on the 
occasion of 90th INTERPOL General Assembly ) Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of One Hundred Rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of " 90th INTERPOL General Assembly " .
