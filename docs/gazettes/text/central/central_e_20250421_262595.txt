S.O. 1812(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of Section 20A of the 
Railways Act,1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, 
after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has been

Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date 
of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and 
use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (1) of section 20D of the said Act.
