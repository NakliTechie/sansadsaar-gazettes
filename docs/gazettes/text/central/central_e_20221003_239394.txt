S.O. 4662 ( E ) .- In exercise of powers conferred by clause ( a ) of section 3 of the National Highways Act , 
1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) , the Central Government hereby authorizes the officer mentioned in column ( 2 ) of the Schedule 
annexed here to as the competent authority to perform the functions of such authorities under the said Act with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , in respect of the stretch of land specified in 
the corresponding entry in column ( 3 ) of the said Schedule relating to state , districts , taluk and villages mentioned 
in column ( 4 ) , ( 5 ) , ( 6 ) and ( 7 ) respectively of the said Schedule for construction of new major bridge RET from 
k.m. 149.988 to 150.087 on National Highway No. 26 in the State of ODISHA .
