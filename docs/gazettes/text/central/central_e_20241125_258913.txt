MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


S.O. 5048(E).—In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition 
of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter called the said Act), the Government of India hereby 
authorizes Shri Akhilesh Kumar Jain, Additional Collector, District - Guna, Government of Madhya Pradesh in place 
of Shri Vivek Kumar Raghuvanshi, Deputy Secretary (Personnel) General Administration Department, Bhopal, 
Government of Madhya Pradesh to perform the functions of Competent Authority under the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962, on additional charge basis, for all Natural Gas Pipeline 
Projects of M/s GAIL (India) Limited in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


2: This notification will be effective from the date of its issue.
