MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Maligaon, the 28th July, 2026 


5.0. 4201(E).— The Central Government, by the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of 
Railways (N.F. Railway, Number S.O. 4768(E), dated 21st October, 2025, issued under sub-section (1) of Section 
20A of the Railways Act, 2008 and published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section 
(11), dated 10th June, 2025, had declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification for the public purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special 
Railway Project namely, "Construction of 4 Lane Road Over Bridge at Level Crossing No. NN-1 (Manned) at 
Km_-2/2-3 between NJP-ABFC" in the district of Jalpaiguri in the State of West Bengal. 


And whereas, the Competent Authority, under sub-section (1) of Section 20E of the said Act, has submitted 
its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to this notification shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that, for the purposes of the provisions of this Act, the land specified in the Schedule annexed to this 
notification shall, upon publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, vest absolutely in the Central 
Government free from all encumbrances.
