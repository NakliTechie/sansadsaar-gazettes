NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2lst November, 2024 


O.N. 9.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the Representation of the 
People Act, 950 (43 of 950), the Election Commission of India in consultation with the Govt. of N.C.T. of Delhi 
hereby designates Ms. R Alice Vaz, IAS (AGMUT: 2005) as the Chief Electoral Officer for the N.C.T. of Delhi 
with effect from the date she takes over charge and until further orders in place of Sh. P. Krishnamurthy, IAS. 


2: Ms. R Alice Vaz shall cease to hold and hand over forthwith the charge of all or any charges of work under 
the Govt. of N.C.T. of Delhi, which she may be holding before such assumption of office. 
3. Ms. R Alice Vaz while functioning as the Chief Electoral Officer, N.C.T. of Delhi shall not hold any 


additional charge whatsoever under the Government of N.C.T. of Delhi except that she should be designated 
Secretary to the Government in charge of Election Department in the State Secretariat. 


[F. No. 54/DL/2024-P.Admn. ] 
By Order, 
RAHUL SHARMA, Principal Secy.
