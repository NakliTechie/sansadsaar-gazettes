MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(SEZ DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th March, 2024 
S.O. 67(E).—Whereas, M/s. Information Technology Park Limited had proposed under section 3 of the 
Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a Special Economic 


Zone for Information Technology and Information Technology Enabled Services at Sy. No. 80 (part), Sadamangala 
Village, Sadamangala Industrial Area, Whitefield, Bengaluru, in the State of Karnataka; 


And, whereas, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 4 of 
the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area of .5] hectares at the above 
Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number S.O. 030(E) dated 30" 
March, 207; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Information Technology Park Limited has now proposed to de-notify the entire area 
of .5] hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Karnataka has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal 
vide letter No. CI 60 SPI 2023 dated 57 October, 2023. Further, de-notified land will be utilized towards creation of 
IT infrastructure (Non-SEZ); 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Cochin Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of entire area of .5] hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by first proviso to rule 8 of the Special Economic 
Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except as respects things done or 
omitted to be done before such rescission.
