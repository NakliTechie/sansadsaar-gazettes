MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 12th December, 2024 


S.O. 380(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No.42 at 
Km.289/14-16 in between Peddapalli and Raghavapuram stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land 
in the State of Telangana in the District of Peddapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
