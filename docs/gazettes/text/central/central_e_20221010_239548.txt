फा.सं. 18-05 / 10679 आईएनसी . – भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 ( 1947 का XLVIII ) की 
धारा 14 के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्द्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती 
है : 


जबकि , स्वामी विवेकानंद नर्सिंग स्कूल , माधव विला , चकाई रोड , सिमुलतला , जमुई 811316 , बिहार , बिहार 
राज्य सरकार और बिहार नर्स पंजीकरण परिषद् द्वारा मान्यताप्राप्त है ।

वहीं , प्रमुख सचिव , स्वास्थ्य और परिवार कल्याण विभाग , बिहार सरकार , विकास भवन , नया सचिवालय , 
पटना - 800015 , बिहार को भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम , 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के 
लिए एक नोटिस सं . 18-05 / 10679- आईएनसी / 11909 दिनांक 09/02/2021 जारी किया गया था । नोटिस दिनांक 
09/02/2021 की प्रति स्वामी विवेकानंद नर्सिंग स्कूल माधव विला , चकाई रोड , सिमुलतला , जमुई - 811316 , बिहार 
को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी 
सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था । 


जबकि , परिषद् को स्वामी विवेकानंद नर्सिंग स्कूल के मामले में राज्य सरकार से कोई सिफारिश नहीं मिली है । 
यहां तक कि संस्था स्वामी विवेकानंद नर्सिंग स्कूल ने भी जारी नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 

WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F.No. 18-05 / 10679 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Swami Vivekanand Nursing School situated at Madhav Villa , Chakai Road , Simultala , 
Jamui – 811316 , Bihar is recognized by State Government of Bihar and Bihar Nurses Registration Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-05 / 10679 - INC / 11909 dated 09/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family Welfare 
Department , Government of Bihar , Vikas Bhawan , New Secretariat , Patna – 800015 , Bihar was issued . Copy of the 
notice dated 09/02/2021 was also enclosed to Swami Vivekanand Nursing School situated at Madhav Villa , Chakai 
Road , Simultala , Jamui – 811316 , Bihar . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Swami Vivekanand Nursing School . Even the institution Swami Vivekanand Nursing School has not responded 
to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
23/01/2020 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run GNM 
Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Swami Vivekanand Nursing School 
situated at Madhav Villa , Chakai Road , Simultala , Jamui - 811316 , Bihar shall be entitled to be registered only in 
the State of Bihar subject to orders of the State government of Bihar .
