MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th November, 2022 


S.O. 555(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH-43 in the stretch of land from 
Km. 356.867 to Km. 357.93 (Jainagar Railway Level Crossing AB-72) (Office of the Sub division Officer (Revenue) 
Surajpur, Distt-Surajpur (C.G) in the Tehsil Latori in the District of SURAJPUR in the State of CHHATTISGARH, 
hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within 24 days from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(!) of section 3C of the 
said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Sub Divisional Officer(Revenue) 
Surajpur Dist. Surajpur (Chhattisgarh) in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority 
shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing 
all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by 
order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
