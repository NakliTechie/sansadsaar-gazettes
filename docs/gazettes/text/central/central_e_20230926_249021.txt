MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT 
[Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan)] 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2]st September, 2023 


G.S.R. 688(E).—The following draft of certain rules to amend the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 
207, which the Central Government proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (I) and (2) 
of section 00 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 206 (49 of 20I6), is hereby published as required by 
sub-section (l) of the said section, for the information of all persons likely to be affected thereby; and notice is hereby 
given that the said draft rules shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of a period of thirty days from the date 
on which the copies of the Official Gazette in which this notification is published are made available to the public; 


Objections and suggestions, if any, may be addressed to Shri Ram Charan Meena, Under Secretary to the 
Government of India, Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), Room No I! A, 5th 
Floor, Pandit DeenDayalAntyodaya Bhawan, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi, !l0003 or by email at 
rcmeena.79 @ gov.in 


The objections and suggestions, which may be received from any person with respect to the said draft rules 
before the expiry of the period specified above, will be considered by the Central Government. 
DRAFT RULES 
l. Short title and extent.- (l) These rules may be called the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (Amendment) 
Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 207, in rule (l5), in sub-rule (l), after clause (h), the 
following clause shall be inserted, namely: — 


“(i) Guidelines on accessible and inclusive piped water supply for persons with disabilities & for other 
population groups as specified in the notification of the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Department of Drinking Water 
and Sanitation, Government of India, vide number CDN-07/58/202]-Coordination-DDWS, dated the 970 
August 2023” 


(ii) Accessible Standards for Community Toilet in Rural Areas as specified in the notification of the 
Ministry of Jal Shakti, Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Government of India, vide number No. 
S-802/5/202-SBM-DDWS, dated the 4" August 2023.
