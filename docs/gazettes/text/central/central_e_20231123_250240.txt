S.O. 5038(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3 and 4 of the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 
(7 of 2009), as amended in 2017 (21 of 2017), read with rules 5 and 7 of the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011, the 
Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation hereby directs Collection of Statistics on manufacturing and 
related activities through a statistical survey, hereinafter referred to as ‘Annual Survey of Industries 2022-23’, as per 
the details mentioned in the Schedule-I. 


2. The Ministry further appoints the persons as also their assignees in office, mentioned in the Schedule II as 
Statistics Officers in respect of the geographical units mentioned thereof with reference to the aforesaid direction. 
Each Statistics Officer shall immediately submit an undertaking in the prescribed format, namely, Form-I appended 
to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011, to the Additional Director General, Field Operations Division, National 
Sample Survey Office, New Delhi. 


3. The persons authorized by each statistics officer, after submission of undertaking in the prescribed format, 
namely, Form-I appended to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011 to the Statistics Officer, would be engaged in the 
geographical jurisdiction of the Statistics Officer for verification of information furnished by informants, for 
inspecting relevant records, and for seeking clarifications, as may be necessary, from informants. In accordance with 
these duties, each person authorized would carry a photo identity card and a letter of authorization issued by the 
concerned Statistics Officer. The Statistics Officer shall keep the undertakings of the authorized persons in his safe 
custody and forward a certificate of having obtained the above undertakings to the Office of the Additional Director 
General, Field Operations Division, National Sample Survey Office, New Delhi.

4. The statistics collected in Part I of Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) schedule after due verification and 
scrutiny, will be processed by officials working at the Office of the Deputy Director General, Industrial Statistics 
Wing, Kolkata and the statistics collected in Part II of the ASI schedule thereof will be processed by officials 
working at the Labour Bureau. Subject to the provisions of the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 and the Collection 
of Statistics Rules, 2011, the unit level data and the reports, generated from the data through statistical techniques, 
may be kept in custody and disseminated by the Industrial Statistics Wing and the Computer Centre of this Ministry 
and the Labour Bureau under the Ministry of Labour and Employment to the users within and outside the 
Government, the States/UTs and the public at large as per policy guidelines of the Government. 


5. All persons engaged in any activity in respect of collection of statistics, processing of data and dissemination 
with respect to ASI 2022-23 are governed by the provisions under the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 and the 
Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011. 


6. Following powers have been delegated to the Statistics Officers: 


(a) A Statistics Officer is empowered to depute/authorize officials to collect statistics in respect of the 
aforementioned direction, to verify any information furnished by informants on the basis of business records and 
other records and to seek clarifications from any owner or occupier or person in-charge of a factory or establishment 
or management, as the case may be, at the premises where such records are kept. A Statistics Officer is also 
empowered to depute/authorize officials under the administrative control of any State Government or Union 
territory Administration, which is willing to collect statistics from any of those factories/establishment for which the 
Statistics Officer has not deputed/authorized any official under his administrative control. 


(b) A Statistics Officer is empowered to give one month notice or for the period deemed fit to each of the informant 
for furnishing return in the format, specified for the purpose of collection of statistics. 


(c) A Statistics Officer is empowered to provide to any informant, the facility of furnishing return in electronic form 
through a web-portal for certain period, to be specified by him in the notice to be issued to the informant. 


SCHEDULE-I 
Subject and purpose for collection of statistics: 


1. Statistics relating to growth, composition and structure of organized manufacturing sector comprising 
activities related to manufacturing processes, repair services, gas and water supply and cold storage is collected 
through Annual Survey of Industries 2022-23. 


Geographical area for collection of statistics: 
2; The Annual Survey of Industries 2022-23 is conducted in the whole of India. 
Method of data collection: 


3. A Notice will be issued by each Statistics Officer to informants under his jurisdiction, indicating therein the 
date by which, the officer or office to whom, the unit or units for which, and the formats and the manner in which 
information is required to be furnished, including submission of information in electronic form through a web-portal 
designed for the purpose and made available to informants up to a certain date, specified in the notice. 


Nature of informants for whom data may be collected: 


4. The informants are owners or occupiers or persons in-charge, of factories registered under the Factories Act, 
1948 or of establishments registered under the Beedi & Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act,1966 or the 
units registered under any of the following Acts/Boards/Authority viz., Companies Act, 2013, Shops and Commercial 
Establishments Act, Societies Registration Act, Cooperative Societies Act, Khadi and Village Industries Board, 
Directorate of Industries (District Industries Centre) who would be issued notice by Statistics Officer for furnishing 
information under the aforementioned direction. 


5. A Statistics Officer may require furnishing of consolidated information in respect of two or more factories or 
establishments under a single management operating within a State or Union Territory, in case information is not 
separately available for individual factories or establishments, through a notice issued to the concerned owner or 
occupier or person in-charge of the management. 


Period during which collection of statistics may be completed: 


6. The date for submission of information by each informant would be mentioned in the notice and would 
generally fall during November 2023 to October 2024.
