MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2024 


SO. 945 (E). —Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.04(E), Dated: 05.03.2024, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening / four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. 844 on the stretch of land from Km. 0/400 to Km. 53/800 (Hosur-Royakotta - Dharmapuri 
Section) in Krishnagiri District in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “THE HINDU” (English) dated 
22.03.2024 and “DINAKARAN” (Tamil) dated 22.03.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent authority Under 3C; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
