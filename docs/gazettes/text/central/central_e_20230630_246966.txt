परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 1184 तारीख 13/03/2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा बिहार राज्य के भागलपुर जिले में NH8O के 
कि.मी. 95.7 से कि.मी.192.33 (मुंगेर-मिर्जाचौकी सेक्शन में फोर लेन सड़क निर्माण) तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के निर्माण

विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "19.03.2023" 
को "दैनिक जागरण" और "हिन्दुस्तान टाइम्स" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2023 


S.O. 2842(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, 1184 Dated:13/03/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 
(48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four- 
laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH80 in the stretch of land from Km. 95.7 to Km. 
192.33 ( Widenning/Four Laning of Munger to Mirzachauki ) in the district of BHAGALPUR in the state 
of BIHAR 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Jagran” and 
“Hindustan Times” both dated 19.03.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by 


the competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 


has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 


declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 


Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
