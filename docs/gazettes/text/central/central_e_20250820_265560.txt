New Delhi, the 20th August, 2025 


F. No. HQ-3073//2020-AUTH.II Part (l)/Comp. No. 2972(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-sections (l) and (2) of section 54 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and 
Services) Act, 206 (/8 of 206), the Unique Identification Authority of India hereby makes the following regulations 
to amend the Aadhaar (Payment of Fees for Performance of Authentication) Regulations, 2023, namely:— 


l. Short title and commencement.—(l) These regulations may be called the Aadhaar (Payment of Fees for 
Performance of Authentication) Amendment Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the principal regulations, in regulation 3, the following shall be amended, namely— 
2.l add sub-regulation 2A of after sub-regulation 2 of regulation 3 as — 


Where a requesting entity has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding or agreement with 
the Authority for performing authentication with update regarding the status as to whether any 
Aadhaar number previously submitted has been subsequently omitted or deactivated or re- 
activated, for any response subsequently made by the Authority by way of such update in respect 
of performance of authentication of Aadhaar numbers, a fee as specified in sub regulation (2) 
shall be chargeable to such requesting entity for every such update: 


Provided that no such fee shall be charged, where the requesting entity provides such 
information to the Authority which is relevant for deactivation of Aadhaar numbers. 


Provided further that the Authority may, where such update is in the interests of performance 
of its functions and exercise of its powers, including the maintenance of information of Aadhaar 
number holders in CIDR, updating of their current details for continued accuracy in CIDR, 
processing of information of Aadhaar numbers and inquiry into CIDR, by order, waive such 
fee, to such extent as such order may specify”. 


2.2 sub-regulation 3 of regulation 3 may be read as — 
The fee specified in sub-regulations (/), (2) and (2A) .................. 
2.3 sub-regulation 4 omitted.
