MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st May, 2024 


S.O. 2040(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act,1956 (48 of 1956) (hereafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building(widening/2 / 4 laning with paved shoulder), maintenance, management and operation of the NH-730S in the 
stretch of land from K.m. 0+000 to 40+140 K.m. (Maharajganj-Nichlaul-Thuthibari Road)in — the District 
Maharajganj,state of UTTAR PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Deputy Land Acquisition 
Officer (S.S.), Maharajganj in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give 
the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, 
eitherallow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 
The land plans and other details of the land covered under this nitification are available and can be inspected 
by by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
