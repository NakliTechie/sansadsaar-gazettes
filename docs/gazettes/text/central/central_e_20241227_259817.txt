INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-/5647-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Ranchhodbhai Harmanbhai Patel Institute Of Nursing Education And Research Centre situated 
at Shraddha Hospital Campus, Borsad- Singlav Road, Borsad, Distt.- Anand, Pin Code- 388540, Gujarat is 
recognized by State Government of Gujarat and Gujarat Nursing Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-l/I5647-INC (45582) dated 26/2/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section l4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the Commissioner (Health), & Ex-Officio Secretary 
(Public Health & Family Welfare) Block No. 05, Ist Floor Old Sachiwalay, Dr. Jivraj Bhavan, Sector No. 0, 
Gandhinagar — 38200, Gujarat was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/2/2023 was also enclosed to Ranchhodbhai 
Harmanbhai Patel Institute Of Nursing Education And Research Centre situated at Shraddha Hospital Campus, 
Borsad- Singlav Road, Borsad, Distt.- Anand, Pin Code- 388540, Gujarat. In the notice issued, the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the 
case od Ranchhodbhai Harmanbhai Patel Institute of Nursing Education And Research Centre Even the 
Institution Ranchhodbhai Harmanbhai Patel Institute of Nursing Education and Research Centre has not 
responded tothe notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/03/2023 would showthat the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies L.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc (N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Ranchhodbhai Harmanbhai Patel Institute 
Of Nursing Education And Research Centre situated at Shraddha Hospital Campus, Borsad- Singlav 
Road, Borsad, Distt.- Anand, Pin Code 388540, Gujarat shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of 
Gujarat.
