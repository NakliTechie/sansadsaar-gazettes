MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 5th March, 2026 


5.0. 75(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 5.0. 363(E), dated the 23nd January, 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 23nd January, 2026 and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from 
Ix25KV to 2x25 KV traction system to meet 3000MT loading target - Construction of Nekonda TSS in Kazipet- 
Dornakal section in Nekonda Village & Mandal in Warangal District in the state of Telangana. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the “Sakshi 
(Warangal Main) (Telugu)”, “Velugu,(Telugu)” “The Hindu(English)”, and “Vaartha (hindi)” dated 29th January, 2026 
with the approval of Suneel Kumar Verma, Chief Engineer, Construction-IV, South Central Railway, Secunderabad. 


And, whereas, objections were received and same have been considered and after completion of enquiry disallowed by 
the Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been passed And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 
section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
