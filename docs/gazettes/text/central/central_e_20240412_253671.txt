New Delhi, the l0th April, 2024 


S.O. 697(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Number 8.0. 59i(E) Dated 07 December 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 
956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of National Highway No. 248 in the stretch of land from Km. 82.000 to Km. 24.000 
(Raebareli-Allahabad Section) in the district of Raebareli, in the state of UTTAR PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Hindi “Jansatta” and English “The 
Indian Express” both dated 30 December 2023, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall
