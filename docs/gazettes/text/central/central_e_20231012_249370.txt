MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF FOREIGN TRADE) 
PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 2th October, 2023 
No. 33/2023 
Subject: Suspension of SION E-25 for export product Shea Stearine. 


F. No. HRARNORMREVIW00003853AM23/NC-VI.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Paragraph 
.03 & 2.04 of Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade 
hereby suspends the SION E-25 for the export of “Shea Stearine” with immediate effect. 


2: An exporter desirous of obtaining Advance Authorisation for export of above said product may apply under 
Para 4.07 of Handbook of Procedures, 2023 till SION E-25 is revised based on revised production-consumption data 
submitted by the exporting firms. 


Effect to this Public Notice: Standard Input Output norm(SION) appearing under E-25 is suspended with 
immediate effect till new SION is established based on revised production and consumption data submitted by the 
exporting firms. 

SANTOSH KUMAR SARANGI, Director General of Foreign Trade & 
Ex-officio Addl. Secy.
