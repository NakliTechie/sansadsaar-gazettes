G.S.R. 293(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6, 8 and 25 of the Environment (Protection) 
Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central 
Government, after having dispensed with the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the said 
tule in public interest, hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 
206, namely:- 


l. (.) These rules may be called the Bio-Medical Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 206, in rule |l, in sub-rule (), after the words "Departments of 
Health," the word "Ayush," shall be inserted. 


3.  Inrule I2 of the said rule, in sub-rule (6), after the words "District Health Officer," the words "representative, as 
nominated by the State or Union Territory Department of Ayush," shall be inserted.
