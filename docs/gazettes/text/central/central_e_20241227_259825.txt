INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-02/2146-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Narayana School of Nursing situated at Near RTC Complex, Podugu Narayanamma, Beside 
Judge Bunglowa Srikakulam, Distt. Srikakulam 532001 Andhra Pradesh is recognized by State Government of 
Andhra Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-02/2146-INC (53845) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Andhra Pradesh Sth Floor, Ground Floor, Room No. 157, A.P. Secretariat, Velegapudi, Amaravathi 522503, Andhra 
Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Narayana School of Nursing situated 
at Near RTC Complex, Podugu Narayanamma, Beside Judge Bunglowa Srikakulam, Distt. Srikakulam 532001 
Andhra Pradesh In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Narayana School of Nursing. Even the institution, Narayana School of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
19/09/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Narayana School of Nursing situated at 
Near RTC Complex, Podugu Narayanamma, Beside Judge Bunglowa Srikakulam, Distt. Srikakulam 532001 
Andhra Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Andhra Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
