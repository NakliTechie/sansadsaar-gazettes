Bhubaneswar, the 5th August, 2025 
Notice under Section 204 of the Railways Act-,l989 


S.O. 3586(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 989 (24/89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act.), the Central Government, after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the Land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto is required for 
the execution of Special Railway Project, namely, “Flyover from Rajathagarh to Machapur (Rajathgarh to 
Radhakishorepur (5.77km) and Radhakishorepur to Machapur (5.2km) (Total-0.89 km)” in Cuttack district in the 
state of Odisha, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of the 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (l) of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Flyover from Rajathagarh to Machapur 
(Rajathgarh to Radhakishorepur (5.77km) and Radhakishorepur to Machapur (5.!2km) (Total-!0.89 km) in writing, 
and shall set out grounds thereof and competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either 
in person or through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further 
inquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of 20D of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
