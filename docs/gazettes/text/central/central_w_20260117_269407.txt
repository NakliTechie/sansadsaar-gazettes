AWARD 
(Passed on this 167 day of October - 2025.) 


As per letter dated 19/09/2024 by the Deputy Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) Jabalpur, Government 
of India, Ministry of Labour, New Delhi, the reference is made to this Tribunal under section-10 of Industrial 
Disputes Act, 1947 as per reference number SH-1(75)/2022 dt. 19/09/2024. The dispute under reference related to :- 


“Whether the action of the management of Sub Area Manager, Badra Sub Area, South Eastern 
Coalfields Ltd., Anuppur (MP) in terminating the services of Shri Ramesh Kumar, Pump Operator 
w.e.f. 26-12-2022 is legal and justified? If not, what relief the workman Shri Ramesh Kumar is 
entitled to?” 


After registering a case on the basis of the reference, notices were sent to the parties and served. Mr. Neeraj 
Kewat learned Counsel filed his Vakalatnama for management. The workman side never appeared. They did not file 
even any statement of claim. None of the parties filed any pleadings or evidence. 


Since, the initial burden to prove its case is on the workman side in which he fails. Hence, holding the 
claim of the workman union not proved, the reference stands answered against the workman union.
