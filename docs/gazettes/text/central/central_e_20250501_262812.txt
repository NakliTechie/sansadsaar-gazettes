S.R.O. 6(E)— In exercise of powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the President 
hereby make the following rules further to amend the Canteen Stores Department, Ministry of Defence (Group ‘A’ 
posts) Recruitment Rules, 1988, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Canteen Stores Department, Ministry of Defence (Group ‘A’ posts) 
Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2, In the Canteen Stores Department, Ministry of Defence (Group ‘A’ posts) Recruitment Rules, 1988 (hereinafter 
referred to as the principal rules), for rule 2, the following rule shall be substituted, namely: - 


“2. Number of posts, classification, and level in pay matrix. - The number of said posts, their classification 
and level in the pay matrix attached thereto, shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the aforesaid Schedule.
