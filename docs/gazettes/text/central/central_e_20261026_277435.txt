के लिए निम्नलिखित नियम बनाती है, अर्थात्‌:-- 


1. संक्षिप्त शीर्षक और प्रारंभ. -- (1) इन नियमों का संक्षिप्त नाम राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग फीस (दरों का अवधारण और संग्रहण) 
(पांचवां संशोधन) नियम, 2026 है। 
(2) ये 01 नवंबर, 2026 को प्रवृत्त होंगे।

इस प्रकार पुनःसंख्यांकित उप-नियम (3ख) से पूर्व, निम्नलिखित उप-नियम अंतःस्थापित किया जाएगा, अर्थात्‌:--

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th October, 2026 


G.S.R.. 917(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 9 of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 
1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the National Highways Fee 
(Determination of Rates and Collection) Rules, 2008, namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) These rules may be called the National Highways Fee (Determination of 
Rates and Collection) (Fifth Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the 017 day of November, 2026. 


2. In the National Highways Fee (Determination of Rates and Collection) Rules, 2008, in rule 9, after sub-rule (3), 
the existing sub-rule (3A), (3B) and (3C) shall be renumbered as sub-rule (3B), (3C) and (3D) and the following sub- 
tule shall be inserted, before sub-rule (3B) as so renumbered, namely:— 


“(3A) A person who owns a mechanical vehicle, with valid functional FASTags registered for non-commercial 
purposes and resides within a distance of twenty kilometres from a fee plaza may, at his option, obtain either a monthly 
pass under sub-rule (3) or Concessional Local Pass under this rule for such vehicle for commuting through the such fee 
plaza, which shall be valid only for that specific fee plaza, on payment of — 


(a) rupees one thousand and five hundred per annum, where the fee plaza is situated outside the limits of a 
municipal or local town area; and 


(b) rupees three thousand per annum, where the fee plaza is situated within the limits of a municipal or local town 
area: 


Provided that the rates specified under this sub-rule may be revised by the Central Government annually on 
every 17 April of the year: 


Provided further that no such Concessional Local Pass shall be issued for commuting on a national highway 
or expressway on which fee is levied based on the closed user fee collection system: 


Provided also that no such Concessional Local Pass shall be issued if a service road or alternative road is 
available for use by such driver, owner or person in charge of such mechanical vehicle.”.
