3. The terms of reference of the proposed Committee would be as follows: - 


“To study the working of contract labour system in the jobs/work of aerobridge operation and 
maintenance in International Airport Pvt. Ltd. Mumbai and to make suitable recommendations whether or 
not the employment of contract labour in the said establishment be prohibited in the jobs/works of 
aerobridge operation and maintenance keeping in view the provisions of Section 10 of the Contract 
Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.” 


4. The Headquarters of the Committee will be at Mumbai. The Committee will submit its report within two 
months.
