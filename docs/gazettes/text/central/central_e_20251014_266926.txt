New Delhi, the 14th October, 2025 


S.O. 4661(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the 
Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby designates Begumpet 
Airport, Hyderabad in Telangana State as an authorized Immigration Post on a temporary basis for entry 
into/exit from India with valid travel documents for the participants of the “Wings India-2026’ event from 
26 January to 02 February, 2026.
