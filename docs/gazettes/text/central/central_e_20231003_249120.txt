New Delhi, the 3rd October, 2023 


S.O. 4298(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan 
Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (18 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints the 
3rd day of October, 2023 as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to serial 
number 29 and the entries thereto in the schedule to the said Act relating to the Cable Television Networks 
(Regulation) Act,1995, shall come into force.
