hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and 
Listing of Non-Convertible Securities) Regulations, 202[, namely — 


l. These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and Listing of Non- 
Convertible Securities) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2024.
