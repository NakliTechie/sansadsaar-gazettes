MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th April, 2026 


5.0. 1755(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 5A of the Suppression of 
Unlawful Acts against Safety of Civil Aviation Act, 1982 (66 of 1982), the Central Government, for the purposes of the 
said Act, hereby confers to the officers in the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police to Deputy Inspector General of 
Police, National Investigation Agency, Ministry of Home Affairs, the powers of arrest, investigation and prosecution 
exercisable by a police officer under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023). 


2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.

(1) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, उक्त अधिनियम के प्रयोजनों हेतु राष्ट्रीय अन्वेषण अभिकरण, गृह मंत्रालय के
