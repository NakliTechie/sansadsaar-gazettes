New Delhi, the 21st April, 2025 


S.O. 1806(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Offshore Areas Mineral 
(Development and Regulation) Act, 2002 (17 of 2003), the administering authority hereby makes the following 
amendment in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Mines, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number 5.0. 4819(E), dated the 57 November 
2024, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, in serial number 7, in column (2), under the heading Longitude 


for the figures and letter “94°02'52.975"E”, the figures and letter “94°07'52.975"E” shall be substituted. 


[F. No. AA/6//2024-MinesVI] 
VIVEK KUMAR BAJPAI, Administering Authority 


Note.-The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 4819(B), dated the 57 November 2024.
