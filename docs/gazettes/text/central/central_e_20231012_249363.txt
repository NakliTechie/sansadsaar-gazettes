9.0. 4444(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 3382(E) dated the 287 July 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II,

section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 287 July, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under 
sub-section (l) of section 204 of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (ll of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the 
said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely Daund-Manmad doubling project in the district of Ahmednagar in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Loksatta” & 
“Samachar” dated 2570 Aug. 2023 and corrigendum was published in “Nagar Times” & Sarvamat” dated 307 
Aug. 2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Akolner & Ghospuri villages no objections were received and competent Authority have 
passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
