ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th April, 2023 


O.N. 5(E).— WHEREAS, the seat of Sh. Santokh Singh Chaudhary in the House of People, elected from 
4-Jalandhar (SC) Parliamentary Constituency in the State of Punjab has become vacant on 14" January, 2023 by 
the reason of his demise; and 


WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Sections 149, 30 and 56 of the Representation of the People Act, 
1951 (43 of 1951), the Election Commission of India hereby- 


(A) calls upon the said 4-Jalandhar (SC) Parliamentary Constituency in the State of Punjab to elect, before 
the 157 May, 2023 (Monday) and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and 
orders made thereunder, a person belonging to any of the Scheduled Caste, whether of that State or of any 
other State, for the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the House of the People; 


(B) appoints, with respect to said election:- 
1. the 207 April, 2023 (Thursday), as the last date for making nominations; 
ii. the 21* April 2023 (Friday), as the date for the scrutiny of nominations; 
iil. the 24" April, 2023 (Monday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
iv. the 10 May, 2023 (Wednesday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
V. the 157 May, 2023 (Monday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 8.00 A.M. to 6.00 P.M., as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above, for the election.
