MINISTRY OF RAILWAY 
[Western Railway (Construction Organization) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 7th December, 2024 


S.O. 5284(E).—Where as by the Notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number 8.00. 3779(E), dated 23th August, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part II, 
section-3, Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (240f1989)(here in after referred to as the said 
Act),the Central Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification 2418 (E)date 21 June 2024 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of land to be 
acquired with or without structure for the Special Railway Project, New BG Line project From Dahod - Indore Via, 
Jhabua, Sardrapur, dhar and Pithampur (204.76km) in District Dhar, Tehsil Pithampur Village Akollya in the state of 
Madhya Pradesh. And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily “Free press Journal” 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


dated 28/06/2024 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 28/06/2024 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the 
said Act. And, whereas, nil objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power 
conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20 (0) of the said Act, the Central Government here 
by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
