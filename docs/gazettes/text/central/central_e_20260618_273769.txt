अधिनियम की धारा 20 डः के अनुसरण में यह घोषणा करती है इस अधिसूचना के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित होने पर इससे उपबद्ध 
अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विल्लंगनो से मुक्त होकर केंद्रीय सरकार में आत्यंतिक रूप से निहित हो जाएगी।

S.O. 3236(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, In the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board) number S.O. 5682(E), dated the 08 December 2025 and number S.O. 393(E), dated the 28 January 2026 
published in the Gazette of India (Extra-Ordinary) Part-II, section -3, sub-section (ii) , herein after referred to as the 
said notification) under subsection (1) of 20A of the Railway act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for execution of Special Railway Project, namely Kharsia - Naya Raipur - Parmalkasa 57 and 67 line 
(278.00 km.) in the state of Chhattisgarh. 


And, whereas the substance of the said notification 20A was published as per section 20A sub-section (4) in 
the daily Newspapers, Dainik Bhaskar (Hindi) Raipur Edition dated 24.12.2025, Haribhumi (Hindi) Raipur Edition 
dated 24.12.2025, Time of India (English) Raipur Edition dated 24.12.2025 and published in Central Chronicle Raipur 
(English) dated 14.02.2026, Dainik Bhaskar Raipur dated 14.02.2026, Navbharat Raipur dated 14.02.2026 and 
Haribhoomi Raipur dated 14.02.2026. 


And whereas, objections were received within the set time period and disposed of by the Competent Authority 
under section 20D of the said Act; And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said act, the 
Competent Authority has submitted his report to the central Government; Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said 
report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of the section 20E of the 
said act 1989, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be 
acquired for the aforesaid purpose; And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified 
in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all Encumbrances.
