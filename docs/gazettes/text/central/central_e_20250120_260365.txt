MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th January, 2025 
S.O. 334(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, S.O. 4080(E) Dated 9.09.2024 , published in the Gazette of India No. उउबी Dated 
9.09.2024, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act, 956 (48 of I956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Land acquisition 


for forming By-pass to Tiruvaiyaru town from km 55/926 to km 62/680 of NH 226E (New NH 36) in the district of 
THANJAVUR in the state of TAMIL NADU 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Daily Thanthi” and “The Times 
of India” both dated 26th November 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
