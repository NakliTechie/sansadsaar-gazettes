MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 5th, September, 2023 


S.O. 397I(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the 
Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “ Construction/Rebuilding of 
Bridge no—7/A (Jalangi Bridge)”, between Krishnanagar & Bahadurpur station of Krishnanagar — 
Lalgola section in Sealdah Division, as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Nadia & 
Murshidabad District in the State of West Bengal, for providing national infrastructure for a public 
purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette. 


[F. No. 78-W/D/L/Jalangi Bridge] 
P. K. SHARMA, Chief Administrative Officer/Con.
