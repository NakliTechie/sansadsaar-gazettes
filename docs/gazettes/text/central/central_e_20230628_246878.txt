New Delhi, the 28th June, 2023

Waterways Authority of India Act, l985 (82 of 985), the Central Government hereby appoints Shri Rathendra

Authority with immediate effect, until further orders. Shri P.L. Haranadh, Chairperson, Paradip Port Authority will 
cease to be the Member of [WAI Board.
