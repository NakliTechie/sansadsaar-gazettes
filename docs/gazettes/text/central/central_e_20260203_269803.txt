MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 2nd February, 2026 


5.0. 484(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 5950(E), dated the 22nd December, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the 22nd December, 2025 and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of 
the Railways Act, l989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Up-gradation work of Electric Traction 
System from Ix25KV to 2x25 KV traction system to meet 3000MT loading target - Construction of Bonakalu TSS in 
Khammam - Kondapalli section in Bonakal Village & Mandal in Khammam District in the state of Telangana. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the “Nava 
Telangana” (Telugu),”’Sakshi (Khammam Main)” (Telugu), “The Hans India” (English), “The Hindu” (English), & 
“Swathantra Vaartha” (Hindi) dated 26th December, 2025 with the approval of Suneel Kumar Varma, Chief Engineer, 
Construction-IV, South Central Railway, Secunderabad. 


And, whereas, NO objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the Competent 
Authority and accordingly orders have been passed And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the 
said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
