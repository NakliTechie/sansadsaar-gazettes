NOTIFICATION 
Bhubaneswar, the l0th September, 2024 
Notice under Section 20E of the Railways Act, 989 


S.O. 3878 (E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (East 
Coast Railway, Construction Organisation), Notification number, S.O. 985(E) Dated the 29th February 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 29th February 2024 (herein 
after referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 
(24 of 989) on dtd 29.02.2024 (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, "Dhanmandal - Chandikhol Chord Rail 
Link Project (5.960 Km.)". 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely 
"PRAGATIBADI" in Odiya (Bhubaneswar Edition) on dated 6th June, 2024, and “SAMBAD KALIKA” in English 
(Bhubaneswar Edition) Dated 6th June, 2024 under sub- section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of section 20 D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
