S.O. 4992(E).— Whereas, M/s. Bihar Industrial Area Development Authority has proposed under section 3 of 
the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a Multi-Sector 
SEZ at Nawanagar, District-Buxar in the State of Bihar;

the said Act, and other related requirements are fulfilled and it has granted the letter of approval under sub-section (10) 
of section 3 of the said Act for development, operation and maintenance of the above Special Economic Zone on 97 
August, 2024;

4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006,
