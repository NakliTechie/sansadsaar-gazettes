INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd July, 2024 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


F.No. 8-8/8862-INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:-— 


Whereas, Amey Institute of Nursing situated at A/63, Shashtri Colony, A- Block, Distt. Bhind 47400, 
Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration 
Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-!8/8862-INC dated 2/09/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section [4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya 
Pradesh Room No. 24], Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 2/09/2022 was also enclosed to Amey Institute of Nursing situated at A/63, Shashtri 
Colony, A- Block, Distt. Bhind 47400 Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested 
to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Amey Institute of Nursing. Even the institution, Amey Institute of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
7/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Amey Institute of Nursing situated at A/63, 
Shashtri Colony, A- Block, Distt. Bhind 47400, Madhya Pradesh, shall be entitled to be registered only in the 
State of Madhya Pradesh subject to orders of state government. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secretary 
Indian Nursing Council
