INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-5/53-INC. — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Dhanvantri Vidyaniketan School of Nursing situated at Malligenahalli, Distt. Shimoga 577204 
Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Nurses Registration Council to conduct 
GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-I5/I53-INC (47365) dated 26/2/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, |947 addressed to The Principal Secretary, Health and Family Welfare 
Department, Room No. 05, I* Floor, Vikas Souda, Bangalore 56000l Karnataka was issued Copy of the notice dated 
26/2/2023 was also enclosed to Dhanvantri Vidyaniketan School of Nursing situated at Malligenahalli, Distt. 
Shimoga 577204 In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Dhanvantri Vidyaniketan School of Nursing Even the institution Dhanvantri Vidyaniketan School of Nursing 
has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
8/04/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Dhanvantri Vidyaniketan School of 
Nursing situated at Malligenahalli, Distt. Shimoga 577204 Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the 
State of Karnataka.
