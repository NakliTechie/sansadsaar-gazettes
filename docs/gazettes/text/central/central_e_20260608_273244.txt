MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Economic Affairs) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th June, 2026 


F. No. 13/20/2014-NS. — It is hereby notified that the deposits made under the Senior Citizens 
Welfare Fund, announced in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs) Notification 
No. G.S.R. 322(E) dated 18 March, 2016, shall with effect from 15 April, 2025 to 31%" March, 2026, bear 
interest at 6.85% (Six point eight five percent). This rate will be in force with effect from 17 April, 2025.
