MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 26th June, 2023 


S.O. 2795(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.0.4162 (E), dated the a” september, 2022, published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 06" September, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the 
said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor in the district 
of Aurangabad in the State of Bihar; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely, 
“Dainik Jagran” and “Prabhat Khabar” dated the 10" September, 2022 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received and the same have been considered by the Competent 
Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
