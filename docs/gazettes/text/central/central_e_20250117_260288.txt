MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17" January, 2025 


S.O. 315(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (h) of section 2 of the Cable 
Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995 (7 of 1995), and in supersession of the notification number 
5.0. 718(E), dated the 29" September, 1994, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 29" September, 1994, except as respects things done or omitted to be 
done before such supersession, the Central Government hereby notifies, the following registering 
authorities for the purposes of the said Act, namely :- 


(i) Section Officer in the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting dealing with digital addressable 
systems for local cable operator; and 


(ii) Additional Secretary or Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting for 


multi-system operator.
