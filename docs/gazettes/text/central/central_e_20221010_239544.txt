14 के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् एतद्द्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है :

दिनांक 22/03/2021 की एक प्रति श्री बाबा इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग साइंसेज , देसाई पार्क , अरिहंत कॉलोनी के पास , गडग 
रोड , हुबली , जिला , धारवाड़ - 580020 , कर्नाटक को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान 
की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-758 / 2001 - INC . In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences situated at Desai Park , Near Arihant Colony , Gadag 
Road , Hubli , Distt . Dharwad – 580020 , Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka and 
Karnataka State Nursing Council to conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-758 / 2001 - INC / 13659 dated 22/03/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) , Health and 
Family Welfare Department , Room No. 105 , 1st floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore - 560001 , Karnataka was 
issued . Copy of the notice dated 22/03/2021 was also enclosed to Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences 
situated at Desai Park , Near Arihant Colony , Gadag Road , Hubli , Distt . Dharwad - 580020 , 
Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to 
the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the casc 
of Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences . Even the institution Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences 
has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences has 
not taken admissions for GNM Course for the last 3 academic year . The College has also refused the 
Council from conducting statutory inspection . The regulations of the Council provides that if any institution 
has not taken admission for 2 consecutive year , the said institution shall be treated as closed . Also , Refusal 
for statutory inspection under section 13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the 
basis of inspections only that the Council would be in a position to decide about the suitability of the 
nursing institution to conduct nursing courses . 


Now therefore , the Council in terms of the regulations , declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 
the Shri Baba Institute of Nursing Sciences shall be treated as closed institutions subject to orders of 
State government . 


Lt. Col ( Dr ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
