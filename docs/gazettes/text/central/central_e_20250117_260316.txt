New Delhi, the 17 th January, 2025 

S.O 325(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 8 of the Cable 
Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995 (7 of 1995), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
further amendments in the notification of the Government of India the Ministry of Information and 
Broadcasting, number S.O. 2693 (E), dated the 5“ September, 2013, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (1), dated the 60 September, 2013, namely:-

“16. DD Assam General Entertainment (Regional)”;

“21h. DD Odia General Entertainment (Regional); 

22. Sansad TV — 1 HD News and Current Affairs (Lok Sabha); 
23. Sansad TV — 1 News and Current Affairs (Lok Sabha); 
24. Sansad TV — 2 HD News and Current Affairs (Rajya Sabha)”;

substituted, namely:-
