Under Section 20E of the Railways Act, 1989 


5.0. 2203(E).— Whereas, under sub-section (1) of Section 20-D of the Railways Act, 1989, the Central 
Government issued a notification published in the Gazette of India (Extraordinary), Part वी, Section 3, Sub-section (II) 
dated 3 March 2026, through the Ministry of Railways, North Central Railway. 


Through the said notification, it was declared that the land specified in the schedule annexed thereto is required 
for a special railway project, namely the construction of a Railway Over Bridge at Lalorkala in Morena district of 
Madhya Pradesh, for the purposes of survey, management, and operation. 


Asummary of the said notification, as required under sub-section (4) of Section 20-A of the Act, was published 
on 03 March 2026 in the newspapers Dainik Bhaskar and Times of India. 


Objections were received by the competent authority. The objections stated that for the land proposed to be 
acquired, Survey Nos. 1696, 1697, 1699, 1700, 1701, 1702, 1703, 1704 were earlier recorded as 1/4 share, but in Samvat 
years 2041 to 2055, they have been recorded as 33/24 and 1/24 respectively. 


Smt. Basanti Devi Mandil, wife of Shri Ramgopal Mandil, through her advocate Shri Sunil Kumar 
Kulshreshtha, submitted an objection stating that her land is being acquired for the Railway Over Bridge. In her Survey 
No. 1629/1, area 0.063 hectare (110 x 67 = total 7370 sq. ft.), out of which a portion measuring 10 = 10 (total 100 
sq. ft.) has already been sold. 


However, the objector did not clearly specify which exact portion of the land had been sold. In such a situation, 
the objection is not considered valid. 


Under Section 20-B of the Railways Act, 1989, the Tehsildar was directed to carry out demarcation of the 
survey numbers. As per the Tehsildar’s report, after demarcation, no change was found in the affected survey numbers. 


Accordingly, the competent authority has submitted its report in compliance with sub-section (1) of Section 
20-D of the said Act.
