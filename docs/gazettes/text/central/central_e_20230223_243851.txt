INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-05 / 9525- INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Maa Mundeshwari Paramedical & Nursing Training Sansthan situated at Village - Odar , PO 
- Odar , PS – Sonhan , Distt . Kaimur ( Bhabhua ) – 821108 , Bihar was recognized by State Government of Bihar 
and Bihar Nurses & Registration Council to conduct ANM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-05 / 9525 - INC / 11919 dated 09/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) Department of Health , Government 
of Bihar , Vikas Bhawan , New Secretariat , Bihar was issued . Copy of the notice dated 09/02/2021 was also enclosed 
Maa Mundeshwari Paramedical & Nursing Training Sansthan situated at Village - Odar , PO – Odar , PS - 
Sonhan , Distt . Kaimur ( Bhabhua ) - 821108 , Bihar . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Maa Mundeshwari Paramedical & Nursing Training Sansthan . Even the institution Maa Mundeshwari 
Paramedical & Nursing Training Sansthan has not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection under section 
13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only that the Council would 
be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct nursing courses . Refusal for 
inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the requisite Teaching , Clinical or 
infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 
declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Maa 
Mundeshwari Paramedical & Nursing Training Sansthan situated at Village - Odar , PO Odar , PS – Sonhan , 
– 
Distt . Kaimur ( Bhabhua ) - 821108 , Bihar shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Bihar subject to 
orders of state government of Bihar .
