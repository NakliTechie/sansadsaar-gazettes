Subject: - Imposition of Minimum Export Price (MEP) on Export of Honey. 


S.O. 5636 (E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with Section 5 of the 
Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), read with Para 1.02 and 
2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time, and in amendment of the 
earlier Notification No. 75/2023 dated 14.03.2024, the Central Government hereby extends the 
condition of Minimum Export Price of Natural Honey under ITC(HS) code 04090000 of 
Schedule-II (Export Policy), as under:- 


Policy condition 


Subject to a Minimum Export Price (MEP) of US$ 
2000 F.O.B per Metric Ton (PMT), till 
31°:December, 2025. 


2. Effect of this Notification: 


Minimum Export Price (MEP) on Natural Honey is extended beyond 317 December, 2024 till 31* 
December, 2025.
