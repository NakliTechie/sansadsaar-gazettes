MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th March, 2025 


S.O. 408(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O. 29 Dated:3/0/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH27 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 0 to Km. 287.86 (Muzaffarpur-Purniya Section) in the district of MUZAFFARPUR in the state 
of BIHAR; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hindustan” and “The Times of India” 
both Newspaper dated 8.0.2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has not received objections under Section 3-C, 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in 
the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
