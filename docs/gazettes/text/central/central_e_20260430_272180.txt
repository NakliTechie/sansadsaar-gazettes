7. उक्त नियमों में, नियम 35 के स्थान पर निम्नलिखित रखा जाएगा, अर्थात्‌:-- 


“35. भारत के विदेशी नागरिक कार्डधारक के रूप में रजिस्ट्रीकरण का निरस्तीकरण.-- 
(1) जहाँ भारत के विदेशी नागरिक कार्डधारक के रूप में रजिस्ट्रीकरण को निरस्त करने का आदेश 


किया गया है, वहाँ वह व्यक्ति जिसका रजिस्ट्रीकरण निरस्त किया गया है या वह कोई अन्य व्यक्ति जिसके पास संबंधित 
व्यक्ति को जारी किया गया भारत के विदेशी नागरिक कार्ड भौतिक रूप में है, केंद्रीय सरकार द्वारा लिखित सूचना के माध्यम 
से अपेक्षित किए जाने पर, उक्त कार्ड की मूल प्रति, ऐसे व्यक्ति को तथा ऐसी अवधि के भीतर, जो सूचना में विनिर्दिष्ट की

(2) भारत के विदेशी नागरिक कार्ड परिदत्त किए जाने पर, उसे निरस्त कर दिया जाएगा और यदि कार्ड परिदत्त नहीं किया 
जाता है, तो केंद्रीय सरकार यह निदेश दे सकेगी कि उसे निरस्त माना जाएगा। 


(3) जहाँ किसी व्यक्ति के संबंध में भारत के विदेशी नागरिक कार्डधारक के रूप में रजिस्ट्रीकरण को निरस्त करने का आदेश 
किया गया है जिसे इ-ओ सी आई रजिस्ट्रीकरण जारी किया गया है, वहाँ केंद्रीय सरकार यह निदेश दे सकेगी कि इसे निरस्त 
माना जाएगा।

नागरिक कार्डधारक के रूप में रजिस्ट्रीकरण के निरस्तीकरण का उल्लेख करते हुए, उपयुक्त प्रविष्टि करेगा।”

G.S.R. 323(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 18 of the Citizenship Act, 1955 (57 
of 1955), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Citizenship Rules, 
2009, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) These rules may be called the Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 
2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: In the Citizenship Rules, 2009 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 3, the following 
proviso shall be inserted, namely: - 


“Provided that the applicant in such cases shall note that the minor child cannot at any time hold the 
passport of any other country while also holding the Indian passport.”

(i) in the opening portion, the words “in duplicate” shall be omitted; 


(ii) in clause (a), sub-clause (ii) shall be omitted. 


In the said rules, for rule 33, the following rule shall be substituted, namely:— 


“33. Registration as Overseas Citizen of India cardholder and maintenance of register of 
persons under section 7A.— (1) Every person, who is registered as an Overseas Citizen of India 
cardholder under section 7A shall be issued an Overseas Citizen of India card in physical form or an 
electronic Overseas Citizen of India (०-०९) registration containing the details as in Form XXIX. 


(2) The issuing authority shall maintain a record of the persons registered as Overseas citizen of 
India cardholder as in Form XXX in electronic form.”. 


In the said rules, for rule 34, the following rule shall be substituted, namely :— 


“34. Declaration of renunciation of Overseas Citizen of India card— (1) A declaration of 
renunciation of the card registering a person as an Overseas Citizen of India cardholder under section 
7C shall be made in Form XXXI electronically on the designated online portal, namely, 
https://ociservices.gov.in to the Indian Mission or Post concerned or the Foreigners Regional 
Registration Officer concerned and in case of Overseas Citizen of India card issued in physical form, 
the original of the Overseas Citizen of India card shall also be surrendered to the Indian Mission or 
Post concerned or the Foreigners Regional Registration Officer concerned. 


(2) On receipt of the declaration of renunciation of the card registering as an Overseas Citizen of 
India cardholder and surrender of the original Overseas Citizen of India card (in cases where the 
Overseas Citizen of India card has been issued in physical form), the Indian Mission or Post 
concerned or the Foreigners Regional Registration Officer concerned shall issue an acknowledgment 
in Form XXXII electronically on the designated online portal, namely, https://ociservices.gov.in 
and remove the name of the declarant from the record maintained in electronic form under sub-rule 
(2) of rule 33.”. 


In the said rules, for rule 35, the following rule shall be substituted, namely:— 


“35. Cancellation of registration as Overseas Citizen of India cardholder — (1) Where an order 
has been made cancelling the registration as an Overseas Citizen of India cardholder, the person 
whose registration has been cancelled or any other person in possession of the Overseas Citizen of 
India card in physical form issued to the person concerned shall, when required by notice in writing 
by the Central Government, deliver the said card in original to such person and within such period 
as may be specified in the notice. 


(2) On the Overseas Citizen of India card being delivered, it shall be cancelled and in case the card 
is not delivered, then the Central Government may direct that it shall be treated as cancelled. 


(3) | Where an order has been made cancelling the registration as an Overseas Citizen of India 
cardholder in respect of a person who has been issued an e-OCI registration, the Central Government 
may direct that the e-OCI registration shall be treated as cancelled. 


(4) The issuing authority shall make an appropriate entry in the record maintained in the 
electronic form under sub-rule (2) of rule 33 stating the cancellation of registration as Overseas 
Citizen of India cardholder.”.
