EMPLOYEES ' STATE INSURANCE CORPORATION 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 26th September , 2022 

F. No. N - 17011 / 1 / 2022 - P & D. - In pursuance of powers conferred by Section 46 ( 2 ) of the Employees ' State 
Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , read with Regulation 95 - A of the Employees ' State Insurance ( General ) 
Regulations , 1950 , the Director General has fixed the 01/10/2022 as the date from , which the medical benefit as laid 
down in the said Regulation 95 - A and the Tamil Nadu Employees ' State Insurance ( Medical Benefit ) Rules , 1955 
shall be extended to the families of insured persons in the entire area of Krishnagiri , Tiruchirappalli districts ( in 
addition to the already notified areas in the districts ) and the entire area of Perambalur district in the State of 
Tamil Nadu 


S.RAVICHANDRAN , Insurance Commissioner ( P & D )
