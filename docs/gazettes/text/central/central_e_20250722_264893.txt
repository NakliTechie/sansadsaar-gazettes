S.O. 3368(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Coal number

Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 25" September 2024, the Central 
Government gave notice of its intention to acquire 557.49 hectares (approximately) or 1377.57 acres (approximately) 
land and all rights in or over such land specified in the Schedule appended to that notification; 


And whereas the competent authority in pursuance of section 8 of the said Act has made his report to the 
Central Government;

Government of Jharkhand, is satisfied that the lands measuring 557.49 hectares (approximately) or 1377.57 acres 
(approximately) and all rights in or over such land as described in Schedule appended hereto should be acquired; 

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 9 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the land measuring 557.49 hectares (approximately) or 1377.57 acres (approximately) 
and all rights in or over such land as described in Schedule are hereby acquired.
