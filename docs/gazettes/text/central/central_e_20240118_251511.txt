S.O. 283(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.0.562(E) dated 03.02.2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (Four/Six-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No-966(Greenfield) in the stretch of the land from Km |4.400 to Km 2.006 including land for 
Trumpet interchange with NH66 at Km. !2!.006 (Vazahayoor to Iringaullur Section) of NH-966(Greenfield) in the 
district of Kozhikode in the state of Kerala. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Mathrubhumi” dated 07.02.2023 
and “The Hindu ” dated 07.02.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 


Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
