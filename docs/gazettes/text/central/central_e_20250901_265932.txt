विनियम 159(1क) के अनुसरण में तारीख 13 अगस्त, 2025 से गोंदिया जिला (महाराष्ट्र) में पश्चिमी भारत प्रादेशिक 
परिषद्‌ की एक शाखा की स्थापना करने की अधिसूचना जारी करती है।

THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2025 


No. 1-CA (7)/235/2025.—In pursuance of the Regulation 159 (1A) of the Chartered Accountants 
Regulations, 1988, the Council of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India is pleased to notify the setting up of 
a Branch of Western India Regional Council in Gondia District (Maharashtra) with effect from August 13, 2025. 


The Branch shall be known as Gondia District Branch of Western India Regional Council and its Headquarters shall 
be at Gondia. The Jurisdiction of the Branch besides the following places shall cover whole District of Gondia: -
