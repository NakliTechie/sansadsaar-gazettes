MINISTRY OF CHEMICALS AND FERTILIZERS 
(Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 14th May, 2025 
H Acid (Quality Control) Amendment Order, 2025 


S.O. 2144(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 
(11 of 2016), the Central Government, after consulting the Bureau of Indian Standards, is of the opinion that it is 
necessary or expedient so to do in the public interest, hereby makes the following amendment to the H Acid (Quality 
Control) Order, 2024, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) This Order may be called the H Acid (Quality Control) Amendment 
Order, 2025. 

(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 
2: In the H Acid (Quality Control) Order, 2024, in paragraph 1, for sub-paragraph (2), the following sub-paragraph 


shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(2) This order shall come into force on the 137 day of August, 2025.” 


[F. No. C-II-18012/11/2024-Chem.II-CPC] 
DEEPANKAR ARON, Jt. Secy. 


Note: The principal Order was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 
the 14th November, 2024, vide number S.O. 4925(E), dated the 14th November, 2024.
