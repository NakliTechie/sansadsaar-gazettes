NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2026 


S.O. 2737(E)— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, number 5.0. No. 5854 (E), dated the 7" December 2025, published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Jam Bypass on NH- 
44 (Old NH-7) in village Jamb, Taluka-Samudrapur, District of Wardha, in the State of Maharashtra.

Hitavada" (English) both dated 3" January 2026, under sub- section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received from any person by the Competent Authority: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 3(D) of the said, Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3(D) of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
