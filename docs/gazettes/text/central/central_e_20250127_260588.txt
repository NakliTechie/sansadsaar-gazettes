MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2025 


S.O. 489(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 507(E) Dated: 05/02/2024 & S.0.3479(E) Dated: 4/08/2024, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for upgradation to two laning with hard 
shoulders of National Highway No 02A in the stretch of land from De. Ch. Km 78.250 to Km 26.250 (Shangshak to 
Tengnoupal Section Pkg.-3) in the district of Kamjong in the state of MANIPUR; 


And whereas the substance of the said notifications has been published in “Sangai Express (English Edition)” 
and “Sangai Express (Manipuri Edition)” both dated 3/02/2024, “Sangai Express (English Edition)” and “Sangai 
Express (Manipuri Edition)” both dated 26/08/2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
