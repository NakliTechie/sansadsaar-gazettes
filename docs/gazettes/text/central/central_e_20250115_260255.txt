S.O. 29(E).—Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (South Eastern 
Railway/Construction Organization), number S.O.54l0(E), dated the 2l‘' December, 2023 published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 2‘' December, 2023 and issued under sub section () of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989)(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, 
decided its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the notification number $.0.540(E), dated the 
2* December, 2023 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Ranchi 
(Hatia)-Bondamunda (Dumetra) doubling (58.50Km.) in the district of Sundargarh in the State of Odisha. 


And, whereas, the substance of notification number 540(E), dated the 2“' December, 2023 was published in the 
daily newspapers, namely ‘Sakal’ (Odiya Edition), dated 5" January, 2024 and ‘Navbharat’ (Hindi Edition), dated 6" 
January, 2024 under section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
the report to the Central Government and recommended for issue of 20E Gazette Notification; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on the publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
