S.O. 4990 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 read with clause ( b ) of section 2 of the Live 
stock Importation Act , 1898 ( 9 of 1898 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendment in 
the notification of the Government of India , in the Ministry of Agriculture , Department of Animal Husbandry , 
Dairying and Fisheries , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , section 3 , sub - section ( ii ) , vide 
number S.O. 1495 ( E ) , dated the 11th June , 2014 , namely : 


In the Schedule to the said notification , in paragraph 1 , in proviso , for word , letters and figures " 30th June 
2022 " , the word , letters and figures " 31st March 2023 " shall be substituted .

GIRJA NAND SINGH , Jt . Secy . 


EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM 

By substituting the period referred to in the proviso to paragraph 1 of the Schedule to the notification vide number 
S.O. 1495 ( E ) , dated the 11th June , 2014 , from “ 30th June 2022 " to " 31st March 2023 ” , the interest of no persons shall 
be adversely affected .
