G.S.R. 575(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3 of the All

Nagaland hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian Forest Service (Pay) Rules, 206 
namely:- 


l. (i) These rules shall be called the Indian Forest Service (Pay) Third Amendment Rules, 2025.
