5.0. 4672(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (No.l of 2008), the

project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Pune District of 
Maharashtra State, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
