S.O. 38(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S. O. 074 (A) dated the 06th March, 2024 is published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 06th March, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely Kalyan (Ambivali)-Murbad New Railway Line in the district of Thane in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Navrashtra 
(Marathi)” dated 0" April, 2024 and “Mid-Day (English)” dated 0 April, 2024 under sub section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in. Village Potgaon, Nandeni, Pasheni, Shastrinagar, Murbad in Murbad Taluka no 
objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been 
passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
