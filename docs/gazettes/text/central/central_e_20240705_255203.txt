MINISTRY OF MINORITY AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd July, 2024 


G.S.R. 369(E). —In exercise of the powers conferred by section 44 of the Haj Committee Act, 2002, (35 of 
2002), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the Haj Committee Rules, 2002, namely:— 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the Haj Committee (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Haj Committee Rules, 2002 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 5,— 


(i) in sub-rule (2), in clause (a), in sub-clause (ii), for the words “Ministry of External Affairs”, the words 
“Ministry of Minority Affairs ” shall be substituted; 


(ii) for sub-rule (4), the following sub-rule shall be substituted, namely: — 


“(4)The Ministry of Minority Affairs in the Central Government shall request Union Ministries of External 
Affairs, Home Affairs, Finance (Department of Revenue) and Civil Aviation to nominate one officer each, 
not below the rank of the Joint Secretary to the Government of India, serving in the respective Ministries, as 
ex- officio members of the Committee as specified in clause (iii) of section 4.”; 


3. In the said rules, in rule l, in sub-rule (), in the second proviso, for the words “Ministry of External Affairs”, the 
words “Ministry of Minority Affairs” shall be substituted. 


4. In the said rules, in rule 27, in sub-rule (2), in clause (b), for the words “Ministry of External Affairs”, the words 
“Ministry of Minority Affairs” shall be substituted.

CPS BAKSHI, Jt. Secy.
