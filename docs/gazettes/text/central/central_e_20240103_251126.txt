MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT 
(Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd January, 2024 

G.S.R. 07(E).— Whereas a draft of certain rules to amend sub-rule (.) of rule 5 of the Rights of Persons 
with Disabilities Rules, 20l7 were published, as required by sub-section (.) of section 00 of the Rights of Persons 
with Disabilities Act, 20l6 (49 of 206), vide notification of the Government of India the Ministry of Social Justice 
and Empowerment, Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities number G.S.R. 688 (E), dated the 2* 
September 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, section 3, sub-section (i) inviting objections 
and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry of thirty days from the date on which 
the copies of the Official Gazette in which this notification is published are made available to the public; 


And whereas, copies of the said notification were made available to the public on the 28 th September, 2023; 


And whereas, the objections and suggestions received from the public in respect of the said notification have 
been considered by the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections () and (2) of section l00 of the Rights 
of Persons with Disabilities Act, 206 (49 of 206), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further 
to amend the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 20!7, namely:- 


(.) These rules may be called the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 207, in rule I5, in sub-rule (l), after clause (h), the following 
clause shall be inserted, namely:- 


“(i) guidelines on accessible and inclusive piped water supply for persons with disabilities and for other 
population groups with access challenges, as specified in the notification of the Department of Drinking Water 
and Sanitation, Government of India vide notification number CDN-07/58/202-Coordination-DDWS, dated 
the 2nd August, 2023.” 


“(j) accessibility standards for community toilets in Rural areas, as specified in the notification of the Department 
of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Government of India vide notification number S-!802/5/202l-SBM-DDWS 
dated the 470 August, 2023.”
