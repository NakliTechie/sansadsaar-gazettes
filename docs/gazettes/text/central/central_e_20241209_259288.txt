MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th December, 2024 


G.S.R. 757(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 4B, read with section 98 of the Railways 
Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Vice 
Chairman (Selection and Appointment) rules, 2007, namely:- 


l. (l) These rules may be called the Vice Chairman (Selection and Appointment) Amendment Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Vice chairmen (Selection and Appointment) Rules, 2007, in rule 4, for sub-rule (.), the following sub-rule 
shall be substituted, namely:- 


“() The Vice Chairman shall be selected by a Search-cum-Selection Committee comprising of the following 
members, namely: - 


(a) Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Railway Board as Chairperson; 


(b) Member (Infrastructure), Railway Board and ex-officio Chairman, Rail Land Development Authority, 
as Member; 


(c) Director General (Human Resources), Railway Board, as Member; and 
(d) A person of eminence and expert in the relevant field, who is not in the service of Indian Railways or 
its Public Sector Undertaking, to be nominated by the Central Government, as Member.” 
[F. No. 2024/LML-I/2/4] 
PANKAJ TYAGI, Executive Director (Land and Amenities) 
Note : The Vice Chairman (Selection and Appointment) Rules, 2007 were published in the Gazette of India, 


Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide G.S.R. number 5(E) dated the 47 January, 2007 and 
was last amended vide G.S.R. number 636(E), dated the 4" September, 2009.
