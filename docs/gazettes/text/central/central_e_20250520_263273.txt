MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th May, 2025 


9.0. 2256(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways,S.O. No.l0l0(E) Dated:27/02/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 
956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of (widening/two lane with paved 
shoulder/four laning etc.,) Maintenance, management and operation of Newly declared NH-70] in the stretch of land 
from Km. 3+000 to Km. 2i+420 (Rafiabad-Kupwara-Chowkibal-Tangdhar-Chamkot Road)(Package-) in 
the district of KUPWARA in the state of JAMMU & KASHMIR (U.T.). 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Kashmir Images” dated 
05/03/2025; and “UQAB” dated 05/03/2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
