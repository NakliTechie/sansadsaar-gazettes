NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th March, 2024 

S.O. 1528(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 2012 (37 of 2012), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the notification of Government of India in 
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, File Number Z-28016/65/2017-PMSSY-IV dated the 31st Oct, 2018 published 
in the Gazette of India Part I, section 3, Sub-Section (1) vide SO 6411(E) dated the 31st Dec, 2018: 

In the said notification, for serial number | and the entry relating thereto, the following should be substituted, 
namely:- 

“Member under clause (aa) of Section 4: 

“1. Vice Chancellor, Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University, Lucknow;” 

The terms of office of member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956.
