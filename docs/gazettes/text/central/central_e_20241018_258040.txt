ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th October, 2024 


O. N. 90(E).—WHEREAS, the seat of Shri Rahul Gandhi, in the House of the People elected from 04- 
Wayanad Parliamentary Constituency, has become vacant on 8" June, 2024, consequent upon his resignation; and 


2. WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 


3. NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 49, Section 30 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95 (43 of 95), the Election Commission of India hereby- 


(A) calls upon the said 04-Wayanad Parliamentary Constituency in the State of Kerala, to elect, before 
257 November, 2024 (Monday) and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules 
and orders made thereunder, a person for the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the House of the 
People, 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election:- 
(a) the 25 October, 2024 (Friday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(0) the 28 October, 2024 (Monday), as the date for the Scrutiny of nominations; 


(c)the 30°" October, 2024 (Wednesday), as the last date for the withdrawal of 
candidatures; 


(d) the 3 November, 2024 (Wednesday), as the date on which the poll shall, if 
necessary, be taken; 


(e) the 25 November, 2024 (Monday), as the date before which the election shall be 
completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 07:00 A.M. to 06:00 P.M., as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be 
taken on the date specified above for the election.
