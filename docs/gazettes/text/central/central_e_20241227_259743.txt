INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F.No. 18-36/13434-INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVI of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Dhanbad School Of Nursing situated at Asarfi Hospital Baramuri, Bisunpur, Polytechnic, 
Dhanbad, Distt.- Dhanbad, Pin Code- 828130, Jharkhand is recognized by State Government of Jharkhand and 
Jharkhand Nurses & Registration Council to conduct ANM,GNM, B.Sc(N) and Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-36/13434-INC (46143) dated 26/12/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to Chief Secretary, Department of Health and Family 
Welfare, Government of Jharkhand, Nepal House, Doranda, Ranchi — 834002, Jharkhand was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 26/12/2023 was also enclosedto Dhanbad School Of Nursing situated at Asarfi Hospital Baramuri, 
Bisunpur, Polytechnic, Dhanbad, Distt.- Dhanbad, Pin Code- 828130, Jharkhand. In the notice issued, the 
State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the 
nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the 
case of Dhanbad School of Nursing. Even the institution Dhanbad School of Nursing has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/03/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies whichconstitute the institution is unable to run ANM, 
GNM, B.Sc(N) and Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM, GNM, B.Sc(N) and Post Basic B.Sc(N) from 
Dhanbad School Of Nursing situated at Asarfi Hospital Baramuri, Bisunpur, Polytechnic, Dhanbad, Distt.- 
Dhanbad, Pin Code- 828130, Jharkhand shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Jharkhand. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
