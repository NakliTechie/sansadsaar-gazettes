New Delhi, the 20th November, 2023 


No. AV-02/7/2020-A.—In pursuance of the proviso to sub-rule (l) of rule 78 of the Aircraft Rules, 937 and in 
supersession of the notification No. AV-02/7/2020- A dated 26th December, 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Part I, 
Section I on 27th December, 2022, the Central Government hereby directs that no person shall operate scheduled air transport 
services to/from Diu aerodrome after 3lst December, 2024 unless it has been licensed by the Director General of Civil Aviation as 
required by sub- rule () of rule 78 of the Aircraft Rules, 937.
