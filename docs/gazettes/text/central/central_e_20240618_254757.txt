वाली सभी शक्तियों का प्रयोग राज्य सरकारों तथा संघ राज्यक्षेत्रों के प्रशासनों द्वारा भी किया जाएगा।

New Delhi, the |8th June, 2024 


9.0. 2353(E).— Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (3) of section 3 of the 
Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of |967), the Central Government declared the Liberation Tigers of 
Tamil Eelam (LTTE), as an unlawful association vide notification number S.O. 983 (E), dated the [47 May, 2024, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section-3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 44 May, 2024. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) 
Act, 967 (37 of 967) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby directs that all powers 
exercisable by it under section 7 and section 8 of the said Act shall also be exercised by the State Governments and the 
Union territory Administrations in relation to the above said unlawful association.
