NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2th October, 2023 


S.O. 4472(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, being S.0.9l9(E), dated New Delhi, the 26" April , 2023, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A (in respect of R.S. 
plots) of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of l956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NHI6A on the stretch of land from 
Km 45.5 to Km 229.00 (Kharagpur-Bardhaman-Moregram Economic Corridor) in the district of BIRBHUM in the 
state of WEST BENGAL. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Bartaman” dated 0.06.2023 ; and 
“The Statesman” dated 9.05.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A (in respect of R.S. plots) of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections are found to have been received, and as such the question of hearing and disposal 
of objection dose not arise; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
