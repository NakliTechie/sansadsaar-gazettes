MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th September, 2026 
S.O. 543(E).— The following order by the President is published for general information. 


ORDER 


In exercise of the powers conferred by article 263 of the Constitution, the President hereby makes the following 
order further to amend the Inter-State Council Order, 990, namely:- 


l. (I) This order may be called the Inter-State Council (Amendment) Order, 2026. 
(2) It shall come into force at once. 

Di In the Inter-State Council Order, 990, in clause 2 for the proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted, 
namely:- 
“Provided that— 


(i) where a proclamation issued by the President under article 356 of the Constitution of India in 
relation to any State is in force, the Governor of that State shall be invited to attend the meetings 
of the Council; or 

(ii) where an order issued by the President under article 239AB of the Constitution of India in relation 
to the National Capital Territory is in force, the Lieutenant Governor of the National Capital 
Territory shall be invited to attend the meetings of the Council; or 

(iii) where an order issued by the President under section 5 of the Government of Union Territories 
Act, 963 (20 of 963) in relation to the Union territory of Puducherry is in force, the Lieutenant 
Governor of the Union territory of Puducherry shall be invited to attend the meetings of the 
Council; or 

(iv) where an order issued by the President under section 73 of the Jammu and Kashmir 
Reorganisation Act, 209 (34 of 209) in relation to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir 
is in force, the Lieutenant Governor of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir shall be invited 
to attend the meetings of the Council”.
