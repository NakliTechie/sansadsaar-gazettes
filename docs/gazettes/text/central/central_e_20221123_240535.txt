NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd November, 2022 


99.00, 5460(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, [956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of NH353J on the stretch of land from Km. 5.73 to Km. 35.08 (Administrative 
Building Tahsil Karyalaya Saoner District Nagpur) in the district of NAGPUR in the state of 
MAHARASHTRA; 

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and 
“Navrashtra” both dated 25/]2/202; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by 
the competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (]) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
