EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE CORPORATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th May, 2023 


No. N-17011/1/Lakshadweep/2023-P&D.—In pursuance of powers conferred by Section 46 (2) of the 
Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948 (34 of 1948), read with Regulation 95-A of the Employees’ State Insurance 
(General) Regulations, 1950, the Director General has fixed the 01/05/2023 as the date from, which the medical 
benefit as laid down in the said Regulation 95-A and the Lakshadweep Employees' State Insurance (Medical Benefit) 
Rules, 2023 shall be extended to the families of insured persons in all the areas of Kavaratti, Agatti and Minicoy 
Islands in the Union Territory of Lakshadweep. 


DEEPAK JOSHI, Insurance Commissioner(P&D)
