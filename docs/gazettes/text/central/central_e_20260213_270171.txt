Secunderabad, the |3th February, 2026 


S.O. 783(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for the 
execution of the Special Railway Project, “Up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from lx25kV to 2x25KV to 
meet 3000 MT loading target — Construction of Padugupadu TSS in Ongole — Gudur Section” in Padugupadu village, 
Kovur Mandal in SPSR Nellore District in the state of Andhra Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such 
land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section (l) of Section 20D of the said Act.

writing and shall be set out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority Revenue Divisional Officer, Nellore shall

objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition(CALA) thinks

Any order made by the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition under sub section (ii) of section 20(D) of the
