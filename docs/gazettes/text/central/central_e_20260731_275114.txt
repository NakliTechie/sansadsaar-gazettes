MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 27th July, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 


5.0. 4242(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O. 6014 (E) dated 23rd December 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 26th December 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), 
the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
“Central Railway- Upgradation work of Electric Traction System for Pachora-Badnera Section to meet 3000 
MT loading target” in the district of Buldhana in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for Sub-Divisional Officer, Malkapur 
in daily newspapers namely “Dainik Shyamtak” & “Dainik Matrubhumi” Dated 07-04-2026 under sub section (4) 
of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, objections were received. Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 
passed in terms of Section D of the said act. 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all cumbrances.
