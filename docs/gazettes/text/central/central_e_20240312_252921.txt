New Delhi, the 2th March, 2024 


G.S.R. 76(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, l955 
(40 of 955), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Lubricating Oils and 
Greases (Processing, Supply and Distribution Regulation) Order, 987, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement. - (l) This Order may be called the Lubricating Oils and Greases 
(Processing, Supply and Distribution Regulation) Amendment Order, 2024. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Lubricating Oils and Greases (Processing, Supply and Distribution Regulation) Order, !987 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Order), after clause 9, the following clause shall be inserted, namely: - 


“QA. Application for inclusion under PART B of Schedule I.- (!) Any company other than those 
specified under PART A of Schedule I, carrying on the business of a processor and fulfilling the following 
criteria may make an application to the Central Government in Form (-3 for inclusion of its name in PART 
B of the said Schedule, namely: - 


(i) minimum ten years of continuous experience in the field of manufacturing and sales of 
lubricating oil, or grease or both, under the same company name with its own brand name in India; 


(ii) average sales volume of 50,000 Kilo Litres per year in the last three financial years, with 
minimum average sales volume of l0,000 Kilo Litres per year in the last three financial years each in 
automotive and industrial segment of lubricating oil or grease, or both; 


(iii) having National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) 
accredited laboratory for testing/certification of lubricating oils and greases; and 


(iv) an approved vendor for minimum two Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) in India with 
the average sales volume of 2000 Kilo Litres per year in the last three financial years to the OEMs. 


(2) On receipt of an application under sub-clause (l), the Central Government after making such 
enquiry as it considers necessary, shall cause the name of such company to be inserted under PART B of 
Schedule I or may refuse the same.”.
