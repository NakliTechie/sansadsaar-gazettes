Hon'ble Mahesh Chandra Tripathi, J. 

(Civil Misc. Application No. 16 of 2024 filed on behalf of Tribhuwan Ram-applicant/election petitioner) 


1. Heard Sri N.K. Pandey and Ms. Divya Shukla, learned counsel for the applicant/election petitioner and Sri Bharat 
Singh Pal, Advocate holding brief of Sri K.R. Singh, learned counsel for the first respondent. 


2. This application had been filed by the applicant/petitioner with request to permit the petitioner to withdrawn the 
present election petition on the ground that with the changed political circumstances and scenario, the petitioner does 
not wish to pursue the election petition any further and the same may be dismissed as withdrawn. The said decision had 
been taken by the petitioner without any pressure or coercion upon him. 


3. The matter was taken up on 19.1.2024 and on the said date, the Court had proceeded to pass following order:- 


"1. Heard Sri N.K. Pandey, learned counsel for the applicant/petitioner and Sri K.R. Singh, learned counsel for the first 
respondent. 


2. Civil Misc. Application u/s 109, 110 OF Representation of People Act, 1951 has been filed by the 
applicant/petitioner with a request to permit the petitioner to withdraw the present election petition on the basis of 
averments contained in paragraph 9 and 10 of the application. 


3. Ihave heard both the parties and perused the record. 


4. Looking into the provisions contained under Sections 109 and 110 of Representation of People Act, since the 
application for withdrawal is made for this Election Petition for seeking leave of this court, therefore, it is necessary 
that notice of this withdrawal application be given to the parties and be published in the Official Gazette. In the present 
matter in spite of notice only the first respondent is contesting and Sri K.R. Singh, learned counsel who is representing 
the first respondent has no objection in case the withdrawal application is allowed. Accordingly, the petitioner is 
directed to take steps within five days and the office is also directed to ensure that notice of publication be supplied to 
Sri N.K. Pandey, learned counsel for the petitioner by 23.2.2024 so that the publication may be done up to 7.2.2024. 


5. Put up this matter on 8.2.2024 at 3.30 P.M." 


4. On the matter being taken up today, Sri N.K. Pandey, learned counsel for the petitioner has filed an application 
supported by the affidavit of the petitioner dated 07.02.2024 indicating therein that pursuant to the aforesaid order 
dated 19.01.2024, the notice of the withdrawal of the election petition was published in the official Gazette of the 
State of Uttar Pradesh on 30.02.2024 and the relevant extract of the official Gazette has been brought on record as 
Annexure No.1 to the affidavit. The affidavit filed today is taken on record. 


5. Sri N.K. Pandey, learned counsel for the petitioner in this backdrop submits that copy of the notice of the 
withdrawal of the election petition has already been served upon learned counsel for the returned candidate. He 
further submits that pursuant to the aforesaid order, he has also taken steps on 23.01.2024 for service upon the 
respondent Nos. 2 to 13 and the petitioner has complied with the conditions required for withdrawal of the election 


174 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : APRIL 27, 2024/VAISAKHA 7, 1946 [PART II—SEc. 3(i1i)] 
petition. It is, therefore, requested that in the interest of justice, this Court may dismiss the Election Petition No. 07 of 
2019 as withdrawn. 

6. Learned counsel for the first respondent has no objection, in case the election petition is permitted to be withdrawn. 


7. In compliance of the aforesaid order, the notice was issued to the respondent Nos. 2 to 13 and as per office report 
dated 08.02.2024, the notices are deemed to be sufficient upon the respondent Nos. 2 to 13. Further, the publication 
was also made in the official Gazette of Uttar Pradesh on 03.02.2024 and therefore, I do not find any reason not to 
permit the present election petition as dismissed as withdrawn. 


8. Accordingly, the withdrawal application is allowed and the present election petition is dismissed as withdrawn.
