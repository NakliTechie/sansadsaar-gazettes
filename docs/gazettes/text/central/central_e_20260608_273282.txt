MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th June, 2026 


S.O. 2926(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,l956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway No.752G in the stretch of land from Km. 64/000 to Km. 0/40 (Satana to Mangrul Phata Part-IID in 
the district of NASHIK in the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Deputy Collector (Land 
Acquisition) National Highway Project, Nashik in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
