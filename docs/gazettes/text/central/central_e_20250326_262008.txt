MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th March, 2025 


S.O. 434(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/2-Lane with paved shoulder/4 Laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway No.548A in the stretch of land from Km 33+400 to 43+600 (Third Section) in the district of Raigad in 
the state of MAHARASHTRA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objections to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(|) of section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Sub-Divisional Officer and 
Land Acquisition Officer Alibag District-Raigad, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either Allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
