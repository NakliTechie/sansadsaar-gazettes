NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th June, 2026 


G.S.R. 447(E).— Whereas the draft of the Gas Cylinders Rules, 20l6 were published, as required under sub- 
section (l) of section 8 of the Explosives Act, 884 (4 of 884), vide notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Commerce and Industry (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade), number G.S.R. 46(E), 
dated 24 February, 2026 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) dated the 
25"February, 2026, inviting objections or suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby, before the expiry 
of a period of thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Gazette containing the said notification was made 
available to the public; 

And whereas, the copies of the Gazette said notification were made available to the public on the 25""February, 
2026; 

And whereas, objections and suggestions received in respect of the said draft rules, have been duly considered 
by the Central Government; 

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 and 7 of the Explosives Act, 884 (4 of 884), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Gas Cylinders Rules, 206, namely: - 
l. Short title and commencement.—_() These rules may be called the Gas Cylinders (Second Amendment) Rules, 


2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Gas Cylinders Rules, 206 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), rule 48 shall be omitted. 


3. In the said rules, in FORM C, under the heading “DOCUMENTS REQUIRED TO BE SUBMITTED WITH 
THIS APPLICATION FOR A LICENCE IN FORM E, or E and D or F or G or H”, item (v7) shall be omitted. 


4. Inthe said rules, in FORM F, after condition 8, the following condition shall be inserted, namely: - 


“8A. For industrial gas cylinders, higher stacking and lesser gangway may be permitted by the licensing authority, if 
cylinders are packed in certified structured packaging (racks, cages) with adequate straps, clips or interlocks to ensure 
the integrity of the cylinder, its fittings and proper maneuvering of cylinders are ensured with these certified structured 
packaging and loading and unloading procedure shall comply with the national or international codes.”.
