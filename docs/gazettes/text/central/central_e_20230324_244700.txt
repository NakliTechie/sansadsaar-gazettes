NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 24th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 221 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section 1 ( f ) of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act , 2021 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Ports , Shipping 
& Waterways ( PHRD Division ) No. G.S.R. 629 ( E ) dated 16th August , 2022 , the Central Government hereby appoints 
the following persons on the Board of V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority as Members representing the interests of 
the employees of the Major Port Authority : 


[ PART II - SEC . 3 ( 1 ) ] 


Name of Member appointed 

Shri S. Balakrishnan , President , Tuticorin Port Trust Democratic Staff Union ( CITU ) , Tuticorin 

Shri V. Sathyanarayanan , General Secretary , Tuticorin Port Mariners and General Staff Union ( HMS ) , 
Tuticorin . 


2. The Appointment of Members of the Board shall be subject to the provisions of the Major Port Authorities Act , 
2021. The appointment is valid till the date of validity of check - off , or until the attainment of age of superannuation of 
the Members , or till further orders , whichever is earliest .
