INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the |lth May, 2023 


F.No. 8-8/4404-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Dayal Nursing College situated Shivpuri Link Road, Distt. Gwalior — 474009, Madhya Pradesh 
is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct 


M.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-!8/l4404-INC dated 05/08/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24], Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 05/08/2022 was also enclosed to Dayal Nursing College situated at Shivpuri Link Road, Distt. 
Gwalior — 474009, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Dayal Nursing College, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Dayal Nursing College has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
04/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 6/l/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of M.Sc(N) Dayal Nursing 
College situated at Shivpuri Link Road, Distt. Gwalior — 474009, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh.
