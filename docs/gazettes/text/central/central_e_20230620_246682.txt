9.0. 2695(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (9) of section 2 of the Land Ports Authority of 
India Act, 2010 (31 of 2010), the Central Government hereby specifies the land port at Sutarkandi, located in Tehsil 
Karimganj, District Karimganj, Assam, India, Pin Code — 788712 as integrated check post measuring 88.56 acres of 
land along the National Highway number 37 up to the Zero point on the International Border with Bangladesh as 
follows, namely: — 


(a) North East : 24° 52719.83”N and 92° 15’0.42” E; 
(b) NorthWest =: 24° 52717.24”N and 92° 14’59.42” E; 
(c) South East > 24° 52716.23”N and 92° 1571.4” E; 
(d) South West =: 24° 52714.97”N and 92° 14’59.51” E. 


2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
