New Delhi, the 26th September, 2025 


S.O. 4379(E)— Whereas, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 
of the Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, 958 (28 of 958) had declared entire State of Manipur, 
excluding the areas falling under the jurisdiction of the 3 (thirteen) Police Stations of 5 districts, as 
‘disturbed area’ for a period of six months with effect from 0.04.2025 vide Notification S.0.538 (E) 
dated 30.03.2025. 


And whereas, a further review of the law and order situation in the state of Manipur has been 
undertaken. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 of the Armed Forces (Special 
Powers) Act, 958 (28 of 958), the entire State of Manipur, excluding the areas falling under the 
jurisdiction of the following 3 (thirteen) Police Stations of 5 districts, is declared as ‘disturbed area’ for a 
period of six months with effect from 0.0.2025, unless withdrawn earlier:-
