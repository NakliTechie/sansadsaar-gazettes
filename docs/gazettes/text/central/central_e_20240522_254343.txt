F. No.6//2023-DGTR.—Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act, 975, as amended from time to time and

the following correction in the final finding notification issued vide notification no. 6//2023-DGTR dated 28" March, 
2024 in respect of the subject original anti-dumping Investigation.

duty table at para 9 in the said notification shall be substituted to read as “8309 90 90”.
