New Delhi, the 8th December, 2023 


9.0. 5233(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 1301(E) Dated:20/03/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of 2 lane ROB and its approaches in lieu of LC.No.31 at Km.2/100 
(Railway Chainage 33/900-1000) on NH-326A on EPC mode of Maintenance, management and operation in the 
district of SRIKAKULAM in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” and “Andhra Jyothi” 
both dated 24.03.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
