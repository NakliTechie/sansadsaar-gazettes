S.O. 4645(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 3 of Prevention of 
Corruption Act, 1988 (Act 49 of 1988), the Central Government, with the concurrence of the Chief Justice of High 
Court of Delhi, hereby appoints the Court of Additional Sessions Judge-03, Patiala House Courts, New Delhi as 
Special Judge, for trial of offences specified in clauses (a) & (b) of Sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the said Act, in 
connected offence to NIA case RC-30/2021/NIA/DLI, being investigated by National Investigation Agency.
