MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department For Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 30th August, 2024 


59.0. 370(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (/) and (2) of section 6 read with sub- 
section (3) of section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 20l6 ([] of 206), the Central Government, after 
consulting the Bureau of Indian standards, is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do in the public 
interest, hereby makes the following Order to amend the Footwear made from All Rubber and all Polymeric 
Material and its Components (Quality Control) Order, 2024, namely:- 


l. (l) This Order may be called the Footwear made from All Rubber and all Polymeric Material and its 
Components (Quality Control) Amendment Order, 2024. 


(2)  Itshall come into force from the date of its publication in the official Gazette. 


2. In the Footwear Made from All Rubber and all Polymeric Material and its Components (Quality Control) Order, 
2024, in paragraph 2, for the fifth proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted namely: 


“Provided also that the stock of footwear without Bureau of Indian Standards Mark, manufactured or 
imported prior to the commencement of this Order, shall be permitted to be sold or displayed or offered to be 
sold for a period upto the 3*July, 2026”.
