MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 9th December , 2022 

S.O. 5759 ( E ) .- Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , S.O N.O 1220 ( E ) dated 21s¹ March 20222 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , 
Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of ( widening / two lane with paved shoulder / four 
laning , etc. , ) , Toll Plaza Maintenance , management and operation of NH - 930P on the stretch of land from 
Km . 82.375 to Km . 94.650 and Km . 95.640 to Km . 99.900 ( RDO - Suryapet ) , Junction at Hyderabad ORR 
( Interchange at Gowrelly ) Valigonda - Thorrur - Nellikudur - Mahabubabad - Yellandu - Kothagudem junction at 
NH - 930P in the district of SURYAPET in the state of TELANGANA . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " Namasthe Telangana " ( Telugu ) 
and “ The Hindu ” ( English ) both dated 17 May 20222 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 

And whereas the competent Authority has received no objections from any person ; 

And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
