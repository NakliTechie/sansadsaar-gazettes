G.S.R. 65(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 39 read with clause (a), (b), (c ) and 
(d) of sub-section 2 and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Boilers Act, 2025 ([2 of 2025) and in 
supersession of the Central Boilers Board (Nomination of Members) Rules, 2008, except as respects things 
done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement. — (l) These rules may be called the Central Boilers Board 
(Nomination of Members) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2, Definitions. - In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires,- 

(a) "Act" means the Boilers Act, 2025 (2 of 2025); 

(b) "Board" means the Central Boilers Board constituted under section 3 of the Act; 


(c) “Technical Adviser” means the Technical Adviser appointed under sub-section () of section 4 
of the Act; 


(d) "member" means a member of the Board. 


3. Chairperson, ex officio. — The Secretary to the Government of India incharge of the Department of 
the Central Government having administrative control of the Board shall be the Chairperson, ex officio as 
per clause (a) of the sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Act. 


4. Member-Secretary, ex officio. - The Technical Advisor shall be the Member- Secretary, ex officio 
as per clause (d) of the sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Act. 


3: Manner of nomination of other members. — (i) a senior technical officer conversant with the 
inspection and examination of boilers to be nominated by the Government of each State (other than Union 
territory) as per clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Act; 


(ii) Equal number of other members nominated in sub-rule (i) above, shall be nominated as per 
clause (c) of sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Act in the following manner :- 


(a) one member to represent Ministry of Heavy Industries to be nominated in consultation with 
that Ministry; 


(b) one member to represent Ministry of Steel to be nominated in consultation with that Ministry; 


(c) one member to represent Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas to be nominated in 
consultation with that Ministry; 


(d) one member to represent Ministry of Labour and Employment to be nominated in consultation 
with that that Ministry; 


(e) one member to represent Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises to be nominated in 
consultation with that Ministry; 


(f) one member to represent Central Electricity Authority to be nominated in consultation with 
Ministry of Power; 


(g) one member to represent the Bureau of Indian Standards to be nominated in consultation with 
that Bureau; 


(h) one member to represent Union territories to be nominated in consultation with Ministry of 
Home Affairs;

(i) one member from Confederation of Indian Industry or Federation of Indian Chamber of 
Commerce and Industry or The Associated Chambers of Commerce and Industry of India; 


(j) one member from Boilers Manufacturers Association; 

(k) one member from Boiler components Manufacturers Association; 

(l) four members to represent boilers and boiler components manufacturing industry; 

(m) two members to represent National Laboratories; 

(n) two members to represent Engineering Consultancy Organisations or Inspection Agencies; 
(o) four members to represent boiler user industry; and, 


(p) not more than five members to represent any other interest which, in the opinion of the Central 
Government, ought to be represented on the Board. 


Term of office of nominated members. — () Every member nominated under rule 5 shall ordinarily 
hold office for a period of three years from the date of publication of the notification in the Official 
Gazette for constitution of the Central Boilers Board unless otherwise directed by the Central 
Government. 
(2) A nominated member may resign his office by a letter addressed to the Chairman of the Board.
