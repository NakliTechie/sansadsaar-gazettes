इस समिति का कार्य राजभाषा विभाग, गृह मंत्रालय द्वारा निर्धारित नीतियों के अनुसार खाद्य प्रसंस्करण उद्योग मंत्रालय तथा इसके

2. Functions of the Committee 


The function of the Committee will be to advise on matters relating to progressive use of Hindi in the Ministry of 


Food Processing Industries and all Autonomous Academic Intuitions under its administrative control in accordance with 
policies laid down by the Department of Official Language. 


3. Tenure 
The term of the Committee will be for three years from the date of its reconstitution. 
provided that :— 


(a) A Member of Parliament who is a member of this Committee shall cease to be a Member of the Committee as 
soon as he ceases to be a Member of Parliament. 


(b) Ex-officio members of the Committee shall continue as member as long as they hold office by virtue of which 
they are members of the committee. 


(c) Ifa vacancy arises in the Committee due to resignation, death etc. of a member, the member appointed in that 
capacity shall hold office for the residual term only. 


4. General 


The headquarters of the Committee shall be in New Delhi. In pursuance of decision taken during 315 meeting of 


Kendriya Hindi Samiti constituted under the chairmanship of Honorable Prime Minister, held on 06.09.2018 and according to 
O.M. No. 14013/01/2019 dated 21.01.2019 of Department of Official Language, MHA, meeting of Hindi Advisory Committee 
should be held in Delhi.

No. 10-9/2024-U.3(A)—Whereas, Vels Institute of Science Technology and Advanced Studies (VISTAS) (Deemed 
to be University), Tamil Nadu submitted an online application on UGC Portal to start an off-campus Centre at Velan Nagar, 
Periyapalayam Road, Manjankaranai Village, Uthukkottai, Thiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu as per the UGC (Institutions 
deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023. 


2. And whereas, UGC, vide its letter No.40-8/2025 (CPP-I/DU) dated 13.08.2025, informed that the application was 
examined through its Standing Committee. The Committee, after presentation of the Institution and based on the documents 
submitted, recommended for issuance of Letter of Intent (Lol) to Vels Institute of Science Technology and Advanced Studies 
(VISTAS) (Deemed to be University), Tamil Nadu with certain conditions. 


3. And further whereas, the Ministry, on the advice of UGC, issued Letter of Intent (Lol) dated 09.09.2025 to Vels 
Institute of Science Technology and Advanced Studies (VISTAS) (Deemed to be University), Tamil Nadu for fulfilment of 
certain conditions within a period of 3 years before starting an off-campus at Velan Nagar, Periyapalayam Road, Manjankaranai 
Village, Uthukkottai, Thiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu. 


4. And whereas, the Registrar, Vels Institute of Science, Technology and Advanced Studies (VISTAS) (Deemed to be 
University), Tamil Nadu, vide letter No. VISTAS/R/MoE/085/2025 dated 15.09.2025, submitted compliance report in respect 
of fulfilment of the conditions of the Lol. The same was forwarded to UGC for verification and further advice. In response, 
UGC, vide letter dated 27.01.2026, informed that the compliance report was accepted by the Standing Committee and 
recommended for final approval in the matter. 


5. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, the Ministry of 
Education, on the advice of UGC, hereby accords approval to Vels Institute of Science, Technology and Advanced Studies 
(VISTAS) (Deemed to be University), Tamil Nadu to start an off-campus Centre at Velan Nagar, Periyapalayam Road, 
Manjankaranai Village, Uthukkottai, Thiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu subject to the condition that admissions under 
professional courses shall be made only after obtaining approval from the respective Regulatory Councils/Bodies. 


6. All other conditions mentioned in the earlier Notification(s) of this Ministry as well as the Rules / Regulations of 

UGC and other Statutory Councils, issued from time to time, shall continue to be adhered by Vels Institute of Science 
Technology and Advanced Studies (VISTAS) (Deemed to be University), Tamil Nadu.
