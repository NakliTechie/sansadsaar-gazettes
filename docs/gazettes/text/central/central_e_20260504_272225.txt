2. राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग प्राधिकरण (भर्ती, वरिष्ठता और पदोन्नति) विनियम, 2024 (जिन्हें इसके पश्चात उक्त विनियम 
कहा जाएगा), के विनियम 12 में, उप-विनियम (1) में, खंड (ड़) के लिए निम्नलिखित खंड प्रतिस्थापित किया जाएगा, 
अर्थात्‌:- 
“(ड़) खोज-सह-चयन समिति के माध्यम से या प्रतिनियुक्ति आधार पर भरे जाने वाले पदों के लिए पात्रता निर्धारण 
की महत्वपूर्ण तिथि विस्तारित तिथि सहित, यदि कोई हो, आवेदन प्राप्त करने हेतु निर्दिष्ट अंतिम तिथि होगी: 


बशर्ते सीधी भर्ती के माध्यम से भरे जाने वाले पदों के लिए, आवश्यक शैक्षणिक योग्यता निर्धारण की 


महत्वपूर्ण तिथि, नियुक्ति प्राधिकारी की मंजूरी के साथ विज्ञापन में निर्दिष्ट की जाएगी, जो ऐसे विज्ञापन के संबंध 
में आवेदन प्राप्त करने की अंतिम तिथि से छह महीने से अधिक नहीं होगी।"

“(12) पदोन्नति के प्रयोजन हेतु रिक्ति वर्ष कैलेंडर वर्ष होगा और पदोन्नति की पात्रता निर्धारित करने हेतु निर्णायक

NATIONAL HIGHWAYS AUTHORITY OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th May, 2026 
F. No. ADMIN-11012/59/2025 (E-293027).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 35 read with 
section 9 of the National Highways Authority of India Act, 1988 (68 of 1988), the National Highways Authority of 


India hereby makes the following regulations, to amend the National Highways Authority of India (Recruitment, 
Seniority and Promotion) Regulations, 2024, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These regulations may be called the National Highways Authority of 
India (Recruitment, Seniority and Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the National Highways Authority of India (Recruitment, Seniority and Promotion) Regulations, 2024 
(hereinafter referred to as the said regulations), in regulation 12, in sub-regulation (1), for clause(e), the following clause, 
shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(e) the crucial date for determination of eligibility for posts to be filled through Search-cum-Selection 
Committee or on deputation basis shall be the last date including the extended date, if any, specified for the 
receipt of applications: 


[भाग पा--खण्ड 4] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Provided that for the posts to be filled through direct recruitment, the crucial date for determination 
of essential educational qualifications shall be as specified in the advertisement, with the approval of the 
Appointing Authority, which shall, not be later than six months from the last date of receipt of applications in 
respect of such advertisement.” 


3. In the said regulations, in regulation 13, for clause (12), the following clause, shall be substituted, namely: - 


“(12) | The vacancy year for the purpose of promotion shall be the calendar year and the crucial dates for 
determining the eligibility for promotion shall be the 17 January and 1‘ July of the vacancy year, as the case 
may be.” 


4. In the said regulations, in the schedule for the posts of Chief General Manager (Finance and Accounts), General 
Manager (Finance and Accounts), Deputy General Manager (Finance and Accounts), Manager (Finance and Accounts) 
and Assistant Manager (Finance & Accounts), for the words “in an organisation of repute”, wherever they occur in 
column (11), the words “or Goods and Services Tax related matters” shall be substituted.
