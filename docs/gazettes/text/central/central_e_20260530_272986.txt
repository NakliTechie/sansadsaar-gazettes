NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 30th May, 2026 


Act 
2008 (34 of 2008), the Central Government, in supersession of the notifications of the Government of India, in the 
Ministry of Home Affairs, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide 
number S.O. 4259(E) and 4260(E), dated the 25" November, 209, except as respects things done or omitted to be done 
before such supersession, hereby designates Court of Additional Sessions Judge, Jagdalpur named Special Court (NIA), 
Jagdalpur, as the Special Court for the purpose of sub-section (l) of section |! of the said Act, exclusively for the trial 
of the Scheduled Offences investigated by the National Investigation Agency in consultation with the Hon’ble Chief 
Justice of the High Court of Chhattisgarh, Bilaspur and the Government of Chhattisgarh. 


The jurisdiction of the Special Court for National Investigation Agency mentioned above shall extend over 
Civil District Bastar (Jagdalpur), Dakshin Bastar (Dantewada), Kondagaon and Uttar Bastar (Kanker) of the State of 
Chhattisgarh.
