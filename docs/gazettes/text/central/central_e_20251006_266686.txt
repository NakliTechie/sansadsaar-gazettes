ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th October, 2025 

O. N. 42 (E).—_WHEREAS, the seat of Shri Sanjeev Arora, in the Council of States, elected by the elected 
members of the Legislative Assembly of Punjab, has become vacant on 0.07.2025 by the reason of his resignation, 
before the expiration of his term of office which was up to and including 09.04.2028; and 

WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 

NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 47, sub-section (l) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95! (43 of 95), the Election Commission hereby- 
(A) calls upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Punjab to elect, before 28th October, 2025 

and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder, a person 


for the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the Council of States; 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 3" October, 2025 (Monday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 4"" October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 


(c) the 6" October, 2025 (Thursday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 24" October, 2025 (Friday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 28" October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election.
