पश्चयात उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 20 (क) की उप धारा (1) के अधीन जारी, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, 
भाग 1, खण्ड 3, उप खण्ड-(1), दिनांक 5 मार्च, 2024 में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (उत्तर पश्चिम रेलवे 
निर्माण विभाग) की अधिनियम संख्या का आ. 1059 (अ) दिनांक 4 मार्च, 2024 (जिसे इसके पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिसूचना कहा 
गया है), द्वारा उक्त अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनूसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को, गुजरात राज्य के महेसाणा जिले में विशेष रेल 
परियोजना, अर्थात “तरंगा हिल -अंबाजी-आबू रोड नई (116.65 कि.मी.) बड़ी रेल लाइन की विशेष रेल परियोजना” के 
निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के प्रयोजन के लिए अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की 
घोषणा की गई थी 

और, उक्त अधिसूचना का सार अधिनियम की धारा 20 अ की उपधारा (4) के अधीन दैनिक समाचार पत्रों, 
अर्थात “गुजरात समाचार" में तारीख 10 मार्च, 2024 और “संदेश में तारीख 10 मार्च, 2024 को और टाइम्स ऑफ

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 27th November, 2024 
9.0. 5103(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North 
Western Railway Construction), S.O. No. 1059 (A) dated 4 March , 2024 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 5 March , 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 


said notification for execution, maintenance and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely "Taranga Hill —Abu 
Road Via Ambaji (116.65 km ) NEW BG LINE Project in district Mehsana in the State of Gujarat . 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Gujarat 
Samachar “and “Sandesh” and Times of India on date 10 March ,2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 

And, whereas, no objection were received by the Competent Authority within due time. 

And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 

Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
