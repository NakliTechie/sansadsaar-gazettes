NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th June, 2025 


S.O. 2829(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (0) of clause (46A) of section l0 of the 
Income-tax Act, 96l (43 of 96]), (hereinafter referred to as “the Income-tax Act”), the Central Government hereby 
notifies “Food Safety and Standards Authority of India” (PAN: AAAGFO0023K) (hereinafter referred to as “the 
assessee”’), an authority constituted under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (34 of 2006), for the purposes of the 
said clause. 


2. This notification shall be effective from the assessment year 2026-2027, subject to the condition that the 
assessee continues to be an authority constituted under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (34 of 2006) with one 
or more of the purposes specified in sub-clause (a) of clause (46A) of section 0 of the Income-tax Act.
