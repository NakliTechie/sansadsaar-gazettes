NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]8th September, 2024 


S.O. 3968(E) .— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, (32 Dated:0/0/2024 , published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section 
(I) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH63 in the stretch of land from Km. 280.08 to 
Km. 375.45 (SLAO office 4th Main Road, 5th Cross, Opposite Gangaparameshwari 
Kalyana Mantappa, Vidyanagara, Ballari) in the district of BALLARI in the state of 
KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in 
“Samyuktha Karnataka” Kannada Edition and “The Hindu” English Edition both dated 08th 
February, 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under 
Section 3-C, considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, 
the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and 
in exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (I) of section 3D of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be 
acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official 
Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central 
Government, free from all encumbrances.
