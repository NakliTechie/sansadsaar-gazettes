का.आ. 1040(अ).--- जहॉकि भारत सरकार, रेल मंत्रालय (रेलवे बोर्ड) द्वारा अधिसूचना संख्या 5.0. 
5296(E), दिनांक 19.11.2025, जो भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, APTI, धारा 3, उप-धारा (ii), राजपत्र अंक 5125, 
दिनांक 20.11.2025 में प्रकाशित हुई ( जिसे आगे “उक्त अधिसूचना” कहा गया है ), रेलवे अधिनियम, 1989 (1989 का 
24) की धारा 20/ की उप-धारा (1) के अंतर्गत जारी की गई थी, जिसके द्वारा केंद्र सरकार ने आंध्र प्रदेश राज्य के 
एनटीआर ज़िले के दो ग्रामों में कोंडापल्ली एवं रायनापाडु स्टेशनों के बीच कि.मी. 573/17-19 पर स्थित समपार संख्या 
147 के स्थान पर सड़क ऊपरी सेतु (आरओबी) के निर्माण के प्रयोजनार्थ, उक्त अधिसूचना से संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट 
भूमि का अधिग्रहण करने की अपनी मंशा घोषित की थी। 


और जहाँकि उक्त अधिसूचना का एक अंश, रेलवे अधिनियम, 1989 की धारा 20/ की उप-धारा (1) के अंतर्गत, 
उप मुख्य अभियंता/निर्माण (सड़क सुरक्षा परियोजनाएँ), सिकंदराबाद की स्वीकृति से, दिनांक 25 नवम्बर, 2025 को 
दैनिक समाचार-पत्रों “Sakshi”, “Andhra Jyothy” तथा “Deccan Chronicle” में प्रकाशित किया गया था।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 23rd February, 2026 


S.O. 1040(E).—whereas, by the notification by Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O.No. 5296(E). Dated 19.11.2025 Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II 
Section 3 Sub section (ii), Gazette Issue No: 5125, dated. 20.11.2025 (herein after referred to as said notification) and 
issued under sub section (1) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), the Central Government declares 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose construction 
of the Road Over Bridge (ROB) in lieu of Level Crossing (LC) No. 147 at Km.573/17-19 between Kondapalli and 
Rayanapadu Stations in 02 villages in NTR District in the state of Andra Pradesh. 


And whereas an extract of this notification was published in the daily newspapers "Sakshi", "Andhra Jyothy" 
and "Deccan Chronicle" on 25th November, 2025 with the approval of the Deputy Chief Engineer/Construction Road 
Safety Projects, Secunderabad, under sub-section (1); Section 20A of this Act. 


And whereas, any objection has not been filed within the stipulated time limit. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20 (E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 


report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands mentioned in 


the Schedule have been annexed. To be obtained here for the purpose mentioned. 


Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of this Act, the Central Government hereby declares on 
publication of notification in the Official Gazette. That the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 


absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
