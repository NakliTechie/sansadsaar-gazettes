NOTIFICATION 


New Delhi, the 8th January, 2024 


S.O. 89(E).— Whereas by the notification of the government of India in the Ministry of Railways No. S.O. 
3269 (E) dated July21,2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, part II, section 3, Sub-section 2 (ii) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) issued under sub-section (1) of section 20 A of the Railways Act,1989 
(24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of the “Special Railway Project”, namely 
“Chandigarh — Baddi Broad guage New Railway Line” , in the District Solan of Himachal Pradesh. 


10 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And whereas under Sub Section (4) of Section 20 A of the said Act, the substance of the said notification has 
been published in "The Tribune"(English) dated 1970 August, 2023 and in "Amar Ujala" (Hindi) dated 197 
August,2023 respectively; 


And whereas no objections have been received under section 20(D) and the same has been 
considered/disposed by the competent authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section (1) of the section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of the sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act. the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
