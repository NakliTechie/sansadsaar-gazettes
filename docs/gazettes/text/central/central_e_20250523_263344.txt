New Delhi, the 23rd May, 2025 


5.0. 23I(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 43 of the Prevention of 
Money-laundering Act, 2002 (5 of 2003) and in consultation with the Chief Justice of High Court of Chhattisgarh, the 
Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in 
the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) number S.O. 372(E), dated the Sth February, 206, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 3, sub-section (ii), dated the 5th February, 206, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, against serial number 5 relating to the State of Chhattisgarh, for the entries in 
columns (3) and (4), the following entries shall respectively be substituted, namely:- 


"Court of VI Additional Sessions, Judge, Raipur. The entire State of Chhattisgarh.".
