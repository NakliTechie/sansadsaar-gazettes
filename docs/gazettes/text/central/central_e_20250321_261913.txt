INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the l0th March, 2025 


F.No. 8-05/6249 -INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, ANM School of Nursing situated at M J K Hospital, Distt. West Champaran 845438 Bihar is 
recognized by State Government of Bihar and Bihar Nurses Registration Council to conduct ANM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-05/6249-INC (5723) dated 03/05/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the Principal Secretary (H&FW) Department of Health, 
Government of Bihar, Vikas Bhawan, New Secretariat, Bihar was issued. Copy of the notice dated 03/05/2024 was 
also enclosed ANM School of Nursing situated at M J K Hospital, Distt. West Champaran 845438 Bihar In the 
notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
ANM School of Nursing. Even the institution ANM School of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/2/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 26/0/2024 
any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from ANM School of Nursing situated at M J K Hospital, 
Distt. West Champaran 845438 Bihar shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Bihar. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
