CENTRAL POLLUTION CONTROL BOARD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th May, 2023 


F. No. CP-99/133/2021-PCP-HO-CPCB-HO.—In exercise of the power conferred by Section 4, Clause (4) 
of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (Act No. 6 of 1974) and Section 6 of the Air (Prevention 
and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 (Act No. 14 of 1981), and with the concurrence of Ministry of Environment, 
Forest & Climate Change (MoEF&CC), Government of India, the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) hereby, 
extends the nomination of Chairman and Member Secretary of Ladakh Pollution Control Committee (LPCC) with 
effect from 25.03.2023 for a further period of 01 year or sanction of the post of Chairman and Member Secretary, 
whichever is earlier. 


NARESH PAL GANGWAR, Chairman 
[ADVT.-HI/4/Exty./124/2023-24] 


Note: The Principal Notification was notified vide Notification No. F.No. C-603/Ladakh PCC/Legal-2021, dated 
25.03.2021, and subsequently amended vide Notification No. F. No. C-603/Ladakh PCC/Legal 2021, Dated 
07.03.2022 and Notification F. No. C-603/Ladakh PCC/Legal 2021, Dated 03.06.2022.
