मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना सं. का.नि.आ. 72 तारीख 25 जुलाई, 2008 को उन बातों के सिवाय अधिक्रांत करते हुए जिन्हें ऐसे 
अधिक्रमण से पूर्व किया गया है या करने का लोप किया गया है, घोषणा करती है कि इसमें अनुसूची में वर्णित भूमि, जो

अधिनियम की धारा 7 के खंड (ग) में पहले से ही विनिर्दिष्ट निर्बंधनों को अधिरोपषित करना जारी रखना आवश्यक है जिससे

MINISTRY OF DEFENCE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th September, 2025 


S.R.O. 17(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Works of Defence Act, 1903 
(7 of 1903), and in supersession of the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Defence 
number S.R.O. 72, dated the 25" July, 2008, published in Gazette of India, Part II, section 4, dated the 
30" August, 2008, except as respects, things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the Central 
Government hereby declares that it is necessary to continue to impose restrictions already in place, as 
specified in clause (c) of section 7 of the said Act upon the use and enjoyment of the land described in the 
Schedule herein, being land lying in the vicinity of the Defence Research Development Laboratory, Bothli 
Nagpur in the State of Maharashtra, in order that the said land may be kept free from buildings and other 
obstructions. 


2. The said restrictions shall come into force with effect from the date of publication of this notification 
in the Official Gazette. 


3. A sketch plan of the said land may be inspected in the office of the Collector, Nagpur, Maharashtra. 


SCHEDULE 


All the land comprised in the area lying within the distance of one hundred meters from the crest of 
the outer parapet of the boundary wall of the Defence Research Development Laboratory, Bothli Nagpur, in 
the State of Maharashtra.
