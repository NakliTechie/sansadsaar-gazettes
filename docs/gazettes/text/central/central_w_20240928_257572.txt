P.C. 
. The proceedings are listed in pursuance of a praecipe application as moved by Mr. Shinde, learned counsel for 


respondent no. 2. 


2. This election petition challenges the election of respondent no. 3 to the Lok Sabha from the Lok Sabha Constituency 
no. 36-Shirur, District Pune, in Parliamentary elections held in the year 20l9. Admittedly, the term of the Lok Sabha relevant 
to such election expired on 6 June, 2024. Thereafter, fresh elections were held in the month of May, 2024, the results of 
which were declared on 4 June, 2024, in which respondent no. 3 had again contested from the same constituency 
and is stated to have been re-elected to the Lok Sabha. It is, therefore, stated that the present proceedings have now 
becomeacademic/infructuous. 


3, It is on such backdrop respondent no. 2 has moved this Court by such application dated 9 June, 2024 setting out the 
urgency. It is stated that for the purpose of the present proceedings, respondent no. 2 had stored and secured 4622 Ballot 
units, 23!! Control Units and 7242 VVPAT machines, which are stored in a godown with police security. It is stated that 
now the State Assembly elections would be held in the month of November, 2024 and for such purpose, respondent no. 2 
requires the aforesaid materials. It is hence submitted that appropriate orders be passed considering that the petition has now 
become infructuous. 


4. Although the cause-list was notified listing the present proceeding, however, none represents the petitioner, and 
possibly in view of the fact thatthe petition itself has become infructuous. 


5. From the record it appears that on the last occasion, the Court had heard the proceedings on 4 March, 2020 on the 
Application (L) No. 3 of 209 filed by respondent no. l, which came to be allowed in terms of prayer clauses(a) and (b). 
Also, the proceedings were not moved by the advocate for the petitioner for any further orders to be passed on the 
proceedings and more particularly, considering the nature of the challenge as raised in regard to which observations have been 
made by the Court in order dated 4 March, 2020. 


6. In the aforesaid circumstances, as now the term of the Lok Sabha relevant to the 209 election and subject matter of 
the present proceedings itself has come to an end, as also fresh Lok Sabha elections were held in May, 2024, results of 
which being already declared, learned counsel for respondent no. 2 would be correct in submitting that the present proceedings 
have become infructuous. The petition is accordingly disposed of as infructuous.

7. Respondent no. 2 is hence free to use the Ballot units, Control Units and VVPAT machines as per the procedure 
followed by it for other elections, as they are no more required for the purpose of present proceedings. 


8. Disposed of. No costs.
