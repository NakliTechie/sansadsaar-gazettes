9.0. 454I(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No. !796(E), 2496(E) and 3040(E) of dated. 
20.04.2023, 06.06.2023 And !0.06.2023, Extra Ordinary part II Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after referred to as 
the said notification) under sub-section (I) of section 20A of the Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway 
Project, namely, Anand-Godhra doubling Project in District Kheda in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “Sandesh” dated 
i8" June 2023 & 287 June 2023, “Divya Bhaskar” dated 297 July 2023, “The Indian Express” dated 8"" June 2023, 
28" June 2023 & 29" July 2023 under subsection (4) of section 20A of the said Act; And, whereas, no objection has 
been received by the competent authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
