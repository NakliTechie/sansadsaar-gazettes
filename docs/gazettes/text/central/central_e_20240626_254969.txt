राज्य में विशेष रेल परियोजना अर्थार्तू खंडवा-अकोला के मध्य आमान परिवर्तन कार्य हेतु (एमजी से बीजी) परियोजना के निष्पादन, 
अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए आपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की थी;

मुख्य इंजीनियर-४/निर्माण संगठन/सिकंदराबाद द्वारा प्रकाशित किया गया था;

Secunderabad, the 25th June, 2024 


S.O. 2462 (E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 1648 (E) dated the 080 April 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the O8th April 2024 (here in after referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section(1) of section 20(A) of the Railways Act, 1989(24 of 1989), the Central Government declares its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion (MG 
to BG) in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar (Hindi)” on 08-06-2024 and “Patrika (Hindi)” on 21-06-2024 by Hemant Kumar Bhagoria, Chief Engineer- 
V, Construction Organization Secunderabad under sub section(4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Amgaon (Forest village), Dantpahari (Forest village), Khadki, Mohangarh, Ratnapur and 
Usarni (Forest village) (06) Villages of Burhanpur district no Objections received to the Sub-Divisional Officer 
(Revenue), Nepanagar. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
