के अर्जन करने के लिए अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी;

जिला-अहमदाबाद, राज्य-गुजरात -382440 ने केन्द्रीय सरकार को अपनी रिपोर्ट दे दी है |

पाइपलाइन बिछाने के लिये अपेक्षित है, उसमे उपयोग के अधिकार का अर्जन करने का विनिश्चय किया है;

MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th February, 2023 


S.O. 980(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas, published Vide Gazette No. 4073 PART HU—Section 3 Sub-section (ii) dated 22nd OCTOBER 202], 
5.00. No. 4427(E) dated 2Ist October, 202] issued under sub-section (i) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land situated in Taluka - Viramgam 
District Ahmedabad in Gujarat State, specified in the schedule appended to that notification for the purpose of laying 
pipeline for the transportation of petroleum product from Kandla in the State Gujarat to Gorakhpur in the Uttar 
Pradesh by the IHB Ltd. for implementing the “Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project” 


And whereas the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the concerned public upto 
30.05.2022. And whereas, Ms. Tamanna Zalodiya, Competent Authority, Sub Divisional Magistrate, Sanand, Dist. 
Ahmedabad, State — Gujarat- 382]]0 has under sub-section (/) of section 6 of the said Act, has submitted his report to 
the Central Government. 


And whereas, the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline has decided to acquire right of user therein. 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline. 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of this declaration in THB Limited, free from all encumbrances. 


THB Limited shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of section 0 of the P&MP Act, 962 
and no suit, claim or legal proceedings would lie against the Central Government on any matter relating to the 
pipeline.
