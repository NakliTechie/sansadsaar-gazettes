S.O. 6080(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, 4655(E) Dated 03 October 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part IJ, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 


the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH752D (Spur) on the stretch of land from Km. 47.700 to Km. 86.500 (Ujjain - Badnawar SEction) in 


the district of UJJAIN in the state of MADHYA PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and 
“Free Press” both dated 09 October 2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby

vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
