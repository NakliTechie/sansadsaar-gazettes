G.S.R_266 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to clause ( 3 ) of article 320 of the 
Constitution , the President hereby makes the following regulations further to amend the Union Public Service 
Commission ( Exemption from Consultation ) Regulations , 1958 , namely : 


2 . 


MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL , PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 

( Department of Personnel and Training ) 

NOTIFICATION 


Short title and commencement : - ( 1 ) These regulations may be called the Union Public Service Commission 
( Exemption from Consultation ) Second Amendment Regulations , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

In the Union Public Service Commission ( Exemption from Consultation ) Regulations , 1958 , in Schedule II , 
after item 6B and the entries relating thereto , the following shall be inserted , namely : 


" 6C . Appointment on deputation basis to the post of Deputy Superintendent of Police ( Level - 10 ) and 
Additional Superintendent of Police ( Level - 11 ) in National Investigation Agency , for a period of two years " .
