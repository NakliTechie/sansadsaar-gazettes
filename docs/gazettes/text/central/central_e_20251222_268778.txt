intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Taranga Hill —Abu Road Via Ambaji 
(116.65 km ) new BG line Project district Banaskantha in the State of Gujarat”. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Gujarat 
Samachar”, “Sandesh” and “Times of Inidia” dated 18" July 2025 under sub section (4) of section 204 of the said Act; 


And whereas, in the survey of the villages mentioned in the schedule annexed to this notification, no objection 
has been received within the prescribed time period in the office of the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition and 
Sub-Divisional Officer, Danta. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
