MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th August, 2023 


S.O. 38I4(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways, 
number S.O. 275(E), dated the !2th May 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated 2th May 2023 and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 
of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor Project in the district Gurugram, in 
the State of Haryana; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
“Times of India” dated the 29th May, 2023, and “Danik Jagran” dated the 29th, May, 2023 under sub-section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, No objection has been received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in the exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
