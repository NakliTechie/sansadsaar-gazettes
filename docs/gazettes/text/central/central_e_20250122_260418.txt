NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 12th December, 2024 


S.O. 408(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 
1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing 
No. 366 at Km. 524/18-20 in between Chebrolu-Badampudi stations" as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Eluru for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
