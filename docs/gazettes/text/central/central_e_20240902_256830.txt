MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(TRADE NEGOTIATIONS MULTILATERAL DIVISION) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2024 


No. K-44033/1/2020-TPD(TNM).—In modification of Gazette Notification of even number dated 1“ 
February 2021, it is hereby notified for the general information that Shri Darpan Jain, Joint Secretary, Ministry of 
Commerce & Industry, Department of Commerce shall discharge the functions of the Designated Authority with 
immediate effect, in place of Shri Anant Swarup, then Joint Secretary, for the purpose of: 


i) The Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Countervailing Duty on Subsidised Articles and 
for Determination of Injury) Rules, 1995, as amended; 


ii) The Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and Collection of Anti-dumping duty on Dumped Articles and for 
Determination of Injury) Rules, 1995, as amended; 


iii) The Customs Tariff (Identification and Assessment of Safeguard Duty) Rules, 1997 as amended and Customs 
Tariff (Transitional Product Specific Safeguard Duty) Rules, 2002, as amended; and 


iv) Safeguard Measures (Quantitative Restrictions) Rules, 2012, as amended.
