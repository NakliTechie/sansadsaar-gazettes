read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 949, Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies that ‘The

meaning of the said Act and all the provisions of the said Act applicable to The Ananthasayanam Co-operative Bank 


Ltd., Trivandrum, Kerala shall cease to apply to it, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before this 
notice.
