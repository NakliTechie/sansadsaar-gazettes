MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 17th January, 2024 


S.O. 191(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number 8.0 3745 (E), dated 4th September, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section - 3, Sub- Section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 20 A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of a Special Railway Project namely, “Airoli-Kalwa elevated corridor 
project under MUTP-III” in Tahsil & District- Thane in the State of Maharashtra. 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, under sub-section (4) of section 20 A of the said Act, the substance of said notification has 
been published in the daily local newspapers namely, “The Free Press Journal” (English), dated 08th November, 2023 
and “LOKMAT” (Marathi) dated 08th November, 2023 and whereas objections have been received under section 
20(D) and the same have been considered/disposed/disallowed by the Competent Authority. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Central Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub — section (1) of the section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified 
in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
