New Delhi , the 15th December , 2022 

S.O. 5878 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 3 ) of section 1 of the 
Employees ' State Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , the Central Government hereby appoints the 1st day of 
January , 2023 , as the date on which the provisions of the said Act , namely , 

sections 38 to 43 and sections 45A to 45H of Chapter IV ; 

sections 46 to 73 of Chapter V ; and 

( iii ) 

sections 74 , 75 , sub - sections ( 2 ) to ( 4 ) of section 76 , 80 , 82 and 83 of Chapter VI , 

shall come into force in all the areas of Sehore , Shajapur and Guna districts , in addition to the 
already notified areas of the said districts , in the State of Madhya Pradesh .
