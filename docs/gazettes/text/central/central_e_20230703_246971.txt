MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l8th May, 2023 


S.O. 2874(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by Rule 4(2) of the Railway Servants (Hours 
of Work and Period of Rest) Rules, 2005 under Section 36 of the Indian Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989), 
the Central Government hereby notifies the Joint Secretary, Industrial Relations Division in the Ministry of 
Labour & Employment as the Appellate Authority to hear Appeals under the said Rules. This notification 
will take effect from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
