NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 30th September , 2022 

S.O. 4726 ( E ) .– Whereas by notification of Government of India in the Ministry of 
Petroleum and Natural Gas , number S.O. 3150 ( E ) dated 06-07-2022 , issued under sub - section ( 1 ) 
of Section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines ( Acquisition of Right of User in Land ) Act , 
1962 ( 50 of 1962 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Government of India had declared 
its intention to acquire the Right of User in land , specified in the Schedule appended to that 
notification for transportation of natural gas through M / s Pipeline Infrastructure Limited's ( PIL ) 
Silvassa Spur Pipeline from Tap off point in Vill : Zaroli , Taluka : Umargam , Dist : Valsad , Gujarat 
to M / s Reliance Industries Limited's ( RIL ) Silvassa Manufacturing Division in Vill : Kharadpada , 
Silvassa , U.T. of Dadra & Nagar Haveli ; 


And whereas , the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public 
by dt . 11-07-2022 to 26-07-2022 ; 

And whereas , no objection received from the public to the laying of the said pipeline ; 

And whereas , the Competent Authority has under sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 6 of the said 
Act , submitted report to the Government of India ; 


And whereas , the Government of India , after considering the said report and on being 
satisfied that the said land is required for laying the said pipeline , has decided to acquire the 
Right of User therein , free from all encumbrances ; 


[ भाग II— खण्ड 3 ( ii ) ] 


Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 6 of the 
said Act , the Government of India hereby declares that the Right of User in the land , specified in 
the Schedule , appended to this notification , is hereby acquired for laying the said pipeline ; 


And further , in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 4 ) of Section 6 of the said 
Act , the Government of India hereby directs that the Right of User in the said land for laying the 
pipeline shall , instead of vesting in the Government of India , vest on the date of publication of 
the declaration , in M / s . Pipeline Infrastructure Ltd , free from all encumbrances .
