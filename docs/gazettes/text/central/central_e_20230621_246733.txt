MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 20th June, 2023 


S.O. 2732(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 9(E), dated the 29" December, 2022, published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the ane January, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of |989) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project namely, Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor in the district of Etawah in the State of Uttar Pradesh; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers “Amar Ujala” and 
“Dainik Jagran”, dated the a5" January, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, one objection was received and the same has been disposed of as per rule by the 
competent authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 20E of the said Act the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 
Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 


conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Railways Act 989, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
