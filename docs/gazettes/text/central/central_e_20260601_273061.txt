NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the Ist June, 2026 


S.O. 2784(E).—Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways published 
in the Gazette of India dated the l0th October, 2025, under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 
(24 of 989), the intention of the Central Government to acquire certain lands for the public purpose of construction 
of Luni—-Samdari—Bhildi Doubling Railway Project (Special Railway Project) in District Banaskantha in the State of 
Gujarat was declared; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas during the course of Joint Measurement Survey (JMS) and verification by the District Inspector 
of Land Records (DILR), it has been observed that certain survey numbers situated in Deesa Taluka, District 
Banaskantha, Gujarat, required for the said project were not included in the aforesaid notification and additional land 
is required in some of the already notified survey numbers. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989, the Central Government hereby declares its intention to acquire the lands specified in the Schedule below for 
the said public purpose. 


Any person interested in the land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, file objections, if any, to the Competent Authority, namely Sub Divisional 
magistrate/Prant Officer Deesa, District Banaskantha, Gujarat in writing, as provided under sub-section (l) of section 
20D of the said Act. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
