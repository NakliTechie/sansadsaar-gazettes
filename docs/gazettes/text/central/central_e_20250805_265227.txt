NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 3 lst July, 2025 


5.0. 3589(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 3.0. number 06 (E), dated the 03" January 2025 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary part II, section 3, sub-section (II) dated 06" January 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Notification) issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Act), the Central Government declaration its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed hereto the said notification is required for the purpose of execution of the Special Railway Project, New BG 
Line between Errupalem-Nambur stations via Amaravathi in 02 villages in Khammam District in the state of 
Telangana. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily newspapers Eenadu Daily 
News Paper (Ushodaya Enterprises Pvt., Ltd.) and “The New Indian Express” on 20th January, 2025 with the 
approval of P.V.S. Prasad, Chief Engineer, Construction-I, South Central Railway, Secunderabad, under sub -section 
(4); Section 20A of this Act. 


And Whereas, Competent Authority, Khammam and Special Deputy Collector (LA), IPT & Railways, have 
objections received for Kesireddypally and Yerrupalem Villages of Yerrupalem Mandal of Khammam District of 
Telangana State. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of 20 (E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands mentioned 
in the Schedule have been annexed. To be obtained here for the purpose mentioned above; 


Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of this Act, the Central Government, hereby declares 
on publication of notification in the Official Gazette, that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central government is from all encumbrances.
