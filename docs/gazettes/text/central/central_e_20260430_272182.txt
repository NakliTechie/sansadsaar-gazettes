जिसको और जिससे लक्षद्वीप संघ राज्य क्षेत्र के एंक्रोट, अमीनी, कड़मठ, कल्पेनी, चेतलात और heat ATT के संपूर्ण क्षेत्रों

निगम द्वारा कर्मचारी राज्य बीमा निगम से संबंधित अध्याय 4 के अधीन फायदे प्रदान किये जाएंगे।

MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th April, 2026 


5.0. 2144(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the third proviso to First Schedule of the Code on 
Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby appoints the Ist day of May, 2026, as the date on 
and from which the contribution from the employers and employees of establishments in the entire areas of the 
Andrott, Amini, Kadmat, Kalpeni, Chetlat, and Kiltan Islands in the Union territory of Lakshdweep shall be payable 
under section 29 and any benefits under Chapter IV relating to the Employees State Insurance Corporation will be 
provided by the Corporation to the employees of such establishments.
