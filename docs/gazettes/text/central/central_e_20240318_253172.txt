MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th March, 2024 
S.O. 462(E).—Whereas, Muslim Conference Jammu and Kashmir (Sumji faction) (MCJK-S) has been 
declared as an unlawful association, vide notification number S.O. 935(E) dated the 28" February, 2024, published in 


the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii). 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 5 read with sub-section (]) 
of section 4 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of 967), the Central Government hereby 
constitutes the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Tribunal consisting of Justice Neena Bansal Krishna, Judge, High 
Court of Delhi, for the purpose of adjudicating whether or not there is sufficient cause for declaring the Muslim 


Conference Jammu and Kashmir (Sumji faction) (MCJK-S) as an unlawful association.
