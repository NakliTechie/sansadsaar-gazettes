New Delhi, the 27th September, 2023 


S.O. 425I(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 955 
(40 of 955), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Fertiliser (Inorganic, 
Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order, 985, namely: -- 


l. ({) This Order may be called the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) (Eighth Amendment)( Order), 
2023

(i) for the words “one year’’ the words “six months” shall be substituted 
(ii) for the figure “2022” the figure “2023” shall be substituted.
