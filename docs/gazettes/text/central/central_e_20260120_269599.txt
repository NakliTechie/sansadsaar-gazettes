NOTIFICATION 
Gorakhpur, the 20th January, 2026 


S.O. 319(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway 
(Railway Board), S.O. No 4627(E) dated 10-10-2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part- 
2™4 Section 37 Sub Section (ii) on dated 10-10-2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under 
Railway Act 1989(24 of 1989) and sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Amendment Act, 2008 
(11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance and 
operation of the Special Railway Project between Anand Nagar To Ghughali via Mahrajganj Section, New 
Railway Line Project, District Maharajganj of the state of Uttar Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily Hindi newspapers 
"Dainik Jagran" Gorakhpur October, 19 2025 & Daily English newspaper "The Times of India" 
Lucknow October, 19 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, and any objection not 
received in the office of Competent Authority by any Land Owner/Intrested Person. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land 
Acquisition officer, (S.S) Mahrajganj, and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 
20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed here to shall be acquired for a foresaid purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all 
encumbrances.
