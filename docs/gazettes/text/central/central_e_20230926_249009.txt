MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th September, 2023 


S.O. 4232(E).—Whereas the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 of the 
Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, 958 (28 of !958) had declared 8 districts and 2] Police Stations in 5 other 
districts of the state of Nagaland as ‘disturbed area’ for a period of six months with effect from 0.04.2023 vide 
Notification S.O.l423(E) dated 24.03.2023. 


And whereas a further review of the law and order situation in the state of Nagaland has been undertaken. 


Now, therefore, Dimapur, Niuland, Chumoukedima, Mon, Kiphire, Noklak, Phek and Peren districts in 
Nagaland and the areas in Nagaland falling within the jurisdiction of i) Khuzama, Kohima North, Kohima South, 
Zubza and Kezocha police stations in Kohima District; it) Mangkolemba, Mokokchung-I, Longtho, Tuli, Longchem 
and Anaki ‘C’ police stations in Mokokchung District; iii) Yanglok police station in Longleng District; iv) Bhandari, 
Champang and Ralan police stations in Wokha District; and v) Ghatashi, Pughoboto, Satakha, Suruhuto, Zunheboto 
and Aghunato police stations in Zunheboto District, are declared as ‘disturbed area’ under Section 3 of the Armed 
Forces (Special Powers) Act, 958 for a period of six months with effect from 0!.0.2023, unless withdrawn earlier.
