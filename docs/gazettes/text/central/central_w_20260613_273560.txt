S.R.O. 8.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the provisio to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Integrated Headquarters Ministry of Defence(Navy), Naval Armament Inspection Organisation, 
Technical Supervisory Staff (Mechanic and Ammunition and Explosive) Group ‘B’ Recruitment Rules, 207, in so far 
as they relate to the post of Chargeman (Mechanic) and Chargeman (Ammunition and Explosive), except as respects 
things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the following rules regulating 
the method of recruitment to the posts of Chargeman (Mechanic) and Chargeman (Ammunition and Explosive), in 
Navy, Ministry of Defence, namely:— 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l)These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence (Navy), Naval 
Armament Inspection Organisation, Chargeman (Mechanic) and Chargeman (Ammunition and Explosive), Group “B’ 
posts, Recruitment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 

2; Application.— These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (]) of the schedule annexed to these 
rules. 

3. Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix. The number of the said posts, their classification 


and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc.— The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications 
and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to ((3) of the said Schedule.
