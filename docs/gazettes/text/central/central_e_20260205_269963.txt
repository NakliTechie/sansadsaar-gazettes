फा. सं. 8-20/ सरकार / 2024-आईएनसी:--समय-समय पर यथासंशोधित भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 
4947 (4947 का XLVIII) की धारा 44 के अधीन प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ एतद्द्वारा 
निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है:- 


जबकि, मणिपुर राज्य में स्थित निम्नलिखित संस्थानों को राज्य सरकार तथा मणिपुर नर्सेज एंड मिडवाइव्स काउंसिल 
द्वारा नर्सिंग पाठ्यक्रम संचालित करने के लिए मान्यता दी गई थी। 


वहीं, भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 4947 की धारा 44 तहत मान्यता वापस लेने हेतु अपर मुख्य सचिव 
(स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण) स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण विभाग, मणिपुर सरकार, कमरा नं. 477 मणिपुर सचिवालय, साउथ 
ब्लॉक, इम्फाल-795004, मणिपुर को एक नोटिस संख्या 48-20 / सरकार / 2024-आईएनसी (63690) दिनांक 08.44.2024 को 
जारी किया गया था। दिनांक 08.44.2024 को जारी नोटिस की एक प्रति मणिपुर ato एंड मिडवाइव्स काउंसिल को भी भेजी 
गई थी। जारी किए गए नोटिस में, राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थानों की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में अपनी सिफारिशें परिषद को 
प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था। 


चूंकि, निम्नलिखित संस्थानों की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को राज्य सरकार या मणिपुर ado एंड मिडवाइव्स 
काउंसिल से कोई टिप्पणी या सिफारिश प्राप्त नहीं हुई है।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


New Delhi, the 3rd Febuary, 2026 
International Financial Services Centres Authority (Fund Management) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026 


F. No. 8-20/Govt./2024 - INC— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section |4 of the Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, the following institutions located in the State of Manipur were recognized by the State Government 
and the Manipur Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct nursing courses. 


Whereas, a notice no. !8-20/Govt./2024-INC (63690) dated 08..2024 for withdrawal of recognition under 
Section l4 of the Indian Nursing Council Act, 947, was issued to Additional Chief Secretary (Health & FW) 
Department of Health & Family Welfare, Government of Manipur, Room [77 Manipur Secretariat, South Block, 
Imphal-79500l, Manipur. A copy of the notice dated 08..2024 was also sent to the Manipur Nurses & Midwives 
Council. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit its recommendations to the Council 
regarding the suitability of the nursing institutions. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any remarks or recommendation from the State Government or the 
Manipur Nurses & Midwives Council regarding the suitability of the below-mentioned institutions. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. The Indian Nursing Council records show that 
these institutions have failed to apply for statutory inspection for several years and were found deficient/unsuitable for 
conducting nursing programmes. 


Now therefore, the Council, exercising powers under Section l4(3)(b) of the Indian Nursing Council Act, 
947, hereby declares that with effect from 07.08.2025, any person holding a recognized qualification from the 
following institutions shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Manipur. 

List of Institutions: 
. Royal School Of Nursing, Irdeo Complex, Wangbal, Thoubal Thoubal 79538, Manipur (ANM) 
2. Manipur Nursing Institute, Tentha Thongkhong Bazar Wangjing, Distt. Thoubal 79548, Manipur (ANM) 
3. Female Health Worker Training, School, Near District Hospital, Distt. Bishnupur 79526, Manipur (ANM) 
4. Siroy School Of Nursing, Sagolband Tera Loukrakpam, Leikai, Manipur (GNM)
