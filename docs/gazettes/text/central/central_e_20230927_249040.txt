G.S.R. 699(E).-- In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20i(! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.- (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of Centenary Year of Kaivalyadhama) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of one hundred rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for issue under 
the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of “Centenary Year of Kaivalyadhama”. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.- The coin of the denomination referred to in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely: -
