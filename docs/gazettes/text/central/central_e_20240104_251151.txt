MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist January, 2024. 


G.S.R. 20(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Chief Editor, 
Senior Editor, Editor, Assistant Editor and Technical Assistant (Publication), Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts Recruitment 
Rules, 2020, namely: — 


. Short title and commencement.—() These rules may be called the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, 
Chief Editor, Senior Editor, Editor, Assistant Editor and Technical Assistant (Publication), Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts 
Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2 In the Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Chief Editor, Senior Editor, Editor, Assistant Editor and 
Technical Assistant (Publication), Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts, Recruitment Rules, 2020 in the Schedule, against serial 
number 3 relating to the post of Editor, in column (), - 


(i) under the sub-heading “Promotion”, for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be substituted; 


(ii) after sub-heading “Promotion” and the entries relating thereto and before Note, the following Note shall be 
inserted, namely: - 


“Note I: The eligibility service shall continue to be three years in respect of incumbents holding the feeder post of 
Assistant Editor in level 7 in the pay matrix (Rs. 44900-!42400) on regular basis on the date of notification of the 
Ministry of Mines, Indian Bureau of Mines, Chief Editor, Senior Editor, Editor, Assistant Editor and Technical 
Assistant (Publication), Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2023.”; 


(iii) the “Note” shall be numbered as “Note 2”.
