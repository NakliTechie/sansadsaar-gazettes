S.O. 2232(E).—Whereas the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 202] (4 of 
202) (hereinafter referred to as the Act) came into force on 25" May, 202], in terms of Section (2) of the Act. 


AND WHEREAS sub-section () of section 22 provides that every State Government shall, by notification, 
within six months from the date of commencement of this Act, constitute a State Council to be called the State Allied 


and Healthcare Council for exercising such powers and discharging such duties as may be laid down under this Act. 


AND WHEREAS due to the difficulty in finding adequate experienced professionals in the specific 
category in Allied and Healthcare professions as per the provisions of the Act by the States/UTs and due to the 
Legislative/statutory formalities/procedures, the State Allied and Healthcare Councils could not be constituted by 
State Governments within stipulated period and because of that difficulties have arisen regarding compliance with the 


said provisions of section 22 of the Act. 


AND WHEREAS the stipulated period was extended to two year from the date of commencement of Act by 
Order viz., the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions 4" (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2022, 


dated 6.i.2022. 


AND WHEREAS, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 69 of the Act, the Central Government 


hereby makes the following Order, to remove the above said difficulties, namely: 
l, Short title and commencement: 


(l) This Order may be called the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions 57 
(Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2023.

Professions Act, 202, constitute State Allied and Healthcare Councils.
