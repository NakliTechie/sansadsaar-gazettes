परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 4148 तारीख 20/सितंबर/2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग I, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) A प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा मध्य प्रदेश राज्य के उज्जैन जिले में राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग 
क्रमांक 75200 (पुराना 148 एन जी ) के कि.मी. 29.800 से कि.मी. 72.000 (उज्जैन गरोठ ग्रीनफ़ील्ड अलाइनमेंट) तक के

अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

2024 " को "दैनिक भास्कर" और "फ्री प्रैस" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st December, 2023 


S.O. 5129(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4148 Dated:20/September/2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH752D (Old 148 NG) in the stretch of land from Km. 29.800 to Km. 72.000 (Ujjain Garoth Greenfield 
Allignment) in the district of UJJAIN in the state of MADHYA PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and “Free 
press” both dated 27 September 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
