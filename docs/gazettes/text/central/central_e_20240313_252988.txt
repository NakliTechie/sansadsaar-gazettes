MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th March, 2024 


S.O. 1328(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 49E of the Wild 
Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972), the Central Government hereby designates Shri Gobind Sagar 
Bhardwaj, Additional Director General of Forests, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change as 
the Management Authority for the purpose of discharging the functions and exercising the powers under 
the Act till the 15 August 2027 or until further orders, whichever is earlier. 


[F. No. WL-1/9/2023-WL]
