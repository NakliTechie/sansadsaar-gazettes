Secunderabad, the 26th October, 2026 


5.0. 5806(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number,S.O.l602(E), dated the 25.03.2026, published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-Section (ii),gazette )२०.537 dated the 26.03.2026 and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, “Construction of Jammikunta TSS in Ramagundam- 
Kazipet section for up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from lx25KV to 2x25 KV traction system to meet 
3000MT loading target ” in Saidabad Village & Jammikunta Mandal in Karimnagar District in the state of Telangana. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely the 
“NavaTelangana (Telugu)”, “Sakshi (Telugu)”, “Deccan Chronicle (English)” and “Hindi Milap (Hindi)” dated 0 
April, 2026 and 02 April,2026 with the approval of Mothilal Bhookya, Chief Engineer/Con-I, South Central Railway, 
Secunderabad 


And, whereas, objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the Competent Authority 
and accordingly orders have been passed And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, 
the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (.) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
