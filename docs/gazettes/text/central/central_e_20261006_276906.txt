5.0. 5471(E).—Whereas M/s SAEL Industries Limited having its registered address at H. NO. 44, Model 
Town, Guruharsahai, Firozpur, Punjab-152022, India has applied for authorization under Section 164 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003 for laying of overhead transmission line under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing 
Connectivity to M/s SAEL Industries Limited for Its 300 MW Solar Power Plant at YSR Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh.” 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/17/2025-PG (MoP) dated 
20.01.2025 had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for the overhead line covered 
under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for Providing Connectivity to M/s SAEL Industries Limited for 
Its 300 MW Solar Power Plant at YSR Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh.” 


M/s SAEL Industries Limited had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspapers Swatantra 
Vaartha (in Hindi) dated 11.03.2025, Prajashakti (in Telugu)) dated 11.03.2025, The Times of India (in English) dated 
11.03.2025 and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 12.04.2025 for the general public to make 
observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within two Months from the date of publication. 
Subsequently, M/s SAEL Industries Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 12.06.2026 declaring that no 
observation/ representation was received within two months from the date of Publication in the Newspaper and 


official Gazette of Government of India. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity Act, 
2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so 
established or maintained for laying of electric lines under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for 
providing Connectivity to M/s SAEL Industries Limited for Its 300 MW Solar Power Plant at YSR Kadapa, Andhra 


Pradesh.” The following overhead line is covered under this scheme: 


1. Generation pooling station of M/s SAEL Industries Limited (located in village: Diguva Kalvatala, Mandal: 
Peddamudium, District: YSR Kadapa Andhra Pradesh) to Kurnool- III PS (ISTS S/s) 220 kV S/c line on D/c 
and M/c towers [Line length is about 26.5 km out of which about 1-2 km (approx..) from Kurnool — HI PS 
end will be implemented on Mult circuit towers].
