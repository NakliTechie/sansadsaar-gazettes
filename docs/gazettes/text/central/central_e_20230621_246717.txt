S.O. 2728(E).—Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that coal is likely to be obtained from the

containing details of the area of land described in the Schedule may be inspected at the Office of the Collector, 
District -Raigarh (Chhattisgarh) or at the Office of the Coal Controller, Ministry of Coal, Scope Minar, 5 Floor, 
Core — II, Laxmi Nagar, Delhi — 110092 or at the Office of the South Eastern Coalfields Limited ( Land and Revenue 
Department), Seepat Road, Bilaspur - 495006 (Chhattisgarh) or General Manager (Exploration Division ), Central 
Mine Planning and Design Institute Limited, Gondwana Place, Kanke Road, Ranchi-834 031, Jharkhand ; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Coal Bearing Areas 
(Acquisition and Development) Act, 1957 (20 of 1957), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government hereby gives notice of its intention to prospect for coal from the land described in the aforesaid 
Schedule ; 


Any person interested in the land described in the above mentioned schedule may -- 


(i) | claim compensation under Section 6 of the said Act for any damage caused or likely to be caused by 
any action taken under sub section (3) of Section 4 of the said Act, or 


(ii) claim compensation under sub-section (1) of Section 13 of the said Actin respect of prospecting 
license ceasing to have effect or under sub-section (4) of Section 13 of the said Act, for mining lease 
ceasing to have effect and deliver all maps, charts and other documents relating to the land to show 
the expenditure incurred in respect of items specified in clauses (i) to (iv) of sub-section (1) of 
section 13 of the said Act, 


to the Officer-In-Charge or Head of the Department (Revenue), South Eastern Coalfields Limited, Seepat Road, 
Bilaspur -495006 (Chhattisgarh) within a period of ninety days from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
