MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 
8.0. 4156(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of new Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 409 at Km. 597/17-19 at Dwarapudi yard with 2.50m Road Under Bridge" as a Special 
Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of East Godavari for 
providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this 


notification in the Official Gazette.
