MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 20th March, 2024 


9.0. 490(E).—Whereas the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 202] (/4 of 
202) (hereinafter referred to as the Act) came into force on 25 "May, 202], in terms of Section (2) of the Act. 

AND WHEREAS sub-section () of section 22 provides that every State Government shall, by notification, 
within six months from the date of commencement of this Act, constitute a State Council to be called the State Allied 
and Healthcare Council for exercising such powers and discharging such duties as may be laid down under this Act. 

AND WHEREAS due to ongoing elections and the difficulty in finding adequate experienced professionals 
in the specific category in Allied and Healthcare professions as per the provisions of the Act by the States/UTs and 
also due to the Legislative/statutory formalities/procedures, the State Allied and Healthcare Councils could not be 
constituted by State Governments within stipulated period and because of that difficulties have arisen regarding 
compliance with the said provisions of section 22 of the Act. 

AND WHEREAS the stipulated period was extended to three years from the date of commencement of Act 
by Order viz., the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions 6'"(Removal of Difficulties) Order, 
2023, dated 02..2023. 


AND WHEREAS, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 69 of the Act, the Central Government 
hereby makes the following Order, to remove the above said difficulties, namely: 


l. Short title and commencement: 


(l) This Order may be called the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions 
7'"(Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2024. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the official Gazette. 


2. It is hereby clarified that all the State Governments/Union Territories shall, as soon as may be but within three 
years and six months from the date of commencement of the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare 
Professions Act, 202, constitute State Allied and Healthcare Councils.
