MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2023 


9.0. 5506(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.24(E), Dated 4 March 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Widening/Two laning etc.,) Maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No.205 (New NH-42) on the stretch of land for the work "Construction of 2-lane ROB 
with approaches in lieu of existing LC No.4lE at Km.86/l00 (Railway chainage at Km.64/5-6) of NH-205 under Setu 
Bharatam in the State of Andhra Pradesh under NH(O) for the year 2022-23 on EPC Mode" in the district of Sri Satya 
Sai in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ANDHRA PRABHA” and “HANS 
INDIA” both dated 6 March 2023 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
