MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE 

( Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare ) 


ORDER 

New Delhi , the 30th September , 2022 

S.O. 4638 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act , 
1955 ( 10 of 1955 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the 
Fertiliser ( Inorganic , Organic or Mixed ) ( Control ) Order , 1985 , namely : 


1. ( 1 ) This Order may be called the Fertiliser ( Inorganic , Organic or Mixed ) ( Control ) ( Fifth ) 
Amendment ) Order , 2022 

( 2 ) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Fertiliser ( Inorganic , Organic or Mixed ) ( Control ) Order , 1985 , ( hereinafter referreed to as 
principal Order ) , in clause 20C , 


( a ) in sub - clause ( 4 ) for the words " two years " the words " three years " shall be substituted ; 

( b ) in sub - clause ( 5 ) , for the word , figures and letters " 30th September , 2022 ” , the word , letters and 
figures " 31st March , 2023 " shall be substituted .

in Form G - 3 of the Principal Order , in the portion of the area marked as ** occurring after item 5 , for 
the words , figures and letters " upto 22nd day of February , 2023 " , the words , figures and letters " upto 
22nd day of February , 2024 " shall be substituted .
