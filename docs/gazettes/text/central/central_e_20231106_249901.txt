MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3lst October, 2023 


S.O. 483(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas, Published in the Gazette No. 289 dated 25.05.2023 vide S.O.No. 2283(E) dated 08.05.2023, Part-II, 
Section-3, Sub Section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipeline 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land Situated in Tehsil- Vadodara, District- 
Vadodara in Gujarat State, specified in the Schedule appended to that notification for the purpose of laying pipeline 
for transportation of the LPG from Kandla in the State of Gujarat to Gorakhpur in the State of Uttar Pradesh by the 
IHB Limited for implementing the "Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project". 


And whereas copies of the said Extra Ordinery Gazzate notification were made available to the public upto 
06/07/2023. 


And whereas Shri Mayankkumar R. Patel, Prant Officer, Vadodara (Rural) and Competent Authority, 
Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline (KGPL) Project, Dist. Vadodara, Gujarat has under sub-section () of section 6 of 
the said Act, has submitted his report to the Central Government; 


And wheras, the Central Government after considering the report and on being satisfied that the said land is 
required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


Now therefore, in excercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in excercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vests on the date of publication of the declaration, in IHB Limited free from all encumbrances. 


THB Limited, shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section l0 of the P&MP Act, 962 
and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would lie against the central Government on any matter relating to the pipeline.
