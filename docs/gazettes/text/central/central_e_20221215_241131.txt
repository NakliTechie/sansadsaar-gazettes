MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th December, 2022 


S.O. 5865(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 3840, dated 6.08.2022, S.O. 
4849, dated 2.0.2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under 
sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for building (widening / Two-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway 
no. NH-44A on the stretch of land from Km 62+800 to 98+250 Km. in the district of Jammu, Reasi, Rajouri & 
Poonch in the State of (UT) Jammu & Kashmir. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Jagran” dated 29 Oct. 
2022 and “Panjab Kesari” dated 29 Oct. 2022 and “State Times” dated 03 Nov. 2022 and “Hind Samachar” dated 
03 Nov. 2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent authority; 
Or 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
