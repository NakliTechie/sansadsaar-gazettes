INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 22nd February, 2023 


F. No. 8-36/925-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Radha Govind School of Nursing situated at Radha Govind Nagar, Lalki Ghati, Ramgarh, 
Distt. Ramgarh — 82922, Jharkhand is recognized by State Government of Jharkhand and Jharkhand Nurses & 
Registration Council to conduct ANM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. [8-36/l925-INC dated 09/02/202] for withdrawal of Recognition under section |4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Chief Secretary, Department of Health and Family Welfare, 
Government of Jharkhand, Nepal House, Doranda, Ranchi — 834002, Jharkhand was issued. Copy of the notice dated 
09/02/202 was also enclosed to Radha Govind School of Nursing situated at Radha Govind Nagar, Lalki Ghati, 
Ramgarh, Distt. Ramgarh — 82922, Jharkhand. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council received online application for inspection under Section !3 of INC Act, 947. The 
application was rejected by INC as compliance was not tenable. The institution was supposed to submit compliance. 
No compliance received from Radha Govind School of Nursing. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
24/0/2020 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run ANM 
Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3)(b) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Radha Govind School of Nursing situated 
at Radha Govind Nagar, Lalki Ghati, Ramgarh, Distt. Ramgarh — 82922, Jharkhand shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Jharkhand subject the orders of State government of Jharkhand. 


Lt. Col. (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
