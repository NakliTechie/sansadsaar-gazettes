G.S.R. 402(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (iii) of sub-section (2) of section

the Metro Railways (Operation and Maintenance) Act, 2002 (60 of 2002), the Central Government hereby 
makes the following rules to amend the Metro Railways (Procedure of Claims) Rules, 2017, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called the Metro Railways (Procedure of 
Claims) Amendment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

tules).-

(b) in sub-rule (3), for the words “eighty thousand”, the words “one lakh sixty thousand” shall be 
substituted. 


(2) Inrule 18 of the principal rules, for the words “five lakh”, the words “eight lakh” shall be substituted.
