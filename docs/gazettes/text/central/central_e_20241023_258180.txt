MINISTRY OF TEXTILES 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 23rd October, 2024 


S.O. 4634(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 read with section 7 and sub-section (3) of 


section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 20l6 (Il of 20i6) and in supersession of the Medical Textiles 
(Quality Control) Order, 2023, the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary so to do in the public interest 
and after consulting the Bureau of Indian Standards, hereby makes the following Order, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement - (l) This Order may be called the Medical Textiles (Quality Control) 
Order, 2024. 

(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. Application — This Order shall apply to the goods or articles specified in column (2) of the said Schedules, but 

shall not apply to such goods or article meant for export and items specified in Schedule-A to this order that 
are manufactured by self help groups. 
Explanation — For the purpose of this para, self help groups means small informal group of 0 to 20 
individuals, who are homogenous with respect to social and economic background and come together 
voluntarily for promoting savings habit among members and for a common cause to raise and manage resources 
for the benefit of group members and in hilly tracts or regions and predominantly tribal dominated areas where 
communities are dispersed, smaller groups of minimum five members are also formed into self help groups. 

3. Compulsory use of Standard Mark — The goods or article specified in column (2) of the said Schedules shall 
conform to the corresponding Indian Standard specified in column (3) thereof and shall bear the Standard Mark 
under a licence from the Bureau in accordance with Scheme-I of Schedule-II to the Bureau of Indian Standards 
(Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 208. 

4. Certification and enforcement authority — The Bureau of Indian Standards and the Textiles Committee, 
Ministry of Textiles shall be the certifying and enforcing authority in respect of the goods or article specified 
in column (2) of the said Schedules.
