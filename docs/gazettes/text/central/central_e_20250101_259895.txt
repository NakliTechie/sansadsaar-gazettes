RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 6th December, 2024 

DoR. RET.REC.53/2.0.00/2024-25.— In exercise of the powers conferred under the sub-section () of 
Section 42 of the Reserve Bank Act, 934 and sub-section (.) of Section l8 of the Banking Regulation Act, 949 (0 
of 949), read with Section 56 thereof, and in partial modification of the earlier notification 
DOR.RET.REC.34/2.0.00/2022-23 dated May 04, 2022, the Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies that the 
average Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) required to be maintained by every bank shall be 4.25 per cent of its net demand 


and time liabilities effective from the reporting fortnight beginning December 4, 2024 and 4.00 per cent of net 
demand and time liabilities effective from fortnight beginning December 28, 2024.
