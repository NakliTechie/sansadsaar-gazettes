MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 

Kolkata, the 24th August, 2026 


S.O. 4683(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Eastern 
Railway), S.O. No. 5124(E) dated 28th October, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II- Section-(3)- 
Sub Section (ii) on 12th November, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (1) of section 
20A of the Railways Act, 1989, (as amended vide the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification, is required for the purpose of execution, maintenance and operation of the Special Railway Project 
namely, “Howrah Division - 40 Nos Elimination of Level Crossing of DFC route by ROB/RUBs on Indian Railway - 
DKAE-KAN HBC Chord line” in the Purba Bardhaman District in the State of West Bengal; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely, 
“Anandabazar Patrika” (Bengali edition), “Prabhat Khabar” (Hindi edition) and “The Times of India" (English edition) 
on 26th November, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection on behalf of Interested Persons were received by the Competent Authority within 
the stipulated period of 30 days under section 20A of the said act; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of said the report of the Competent Authority in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
Schedule given below shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule given below 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
