S.O. 756(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport And Highways number S.O. 299 dated 04.07.2023 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of 
section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
(Widening/Four-Laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and operation of NH-346 on the stretch of land from Total

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Dainik Bhaskar" and ''The 
Times of India" Both dated; 22 September 2023 Thursday; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the Said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall
