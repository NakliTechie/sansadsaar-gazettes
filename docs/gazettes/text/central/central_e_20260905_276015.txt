S.O. 497(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 and section 7A of the Census Act, 948 
(37 of 948) read with rules 6A and 6D of the Census Rules, 990, the Central Government hereby defers the 
houselisting operations for Census 2027 for the State of Manipur and for that purpose, hereby makes the following 


amendment in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Home Affairs number S.O. 99(E), dated 


the 7 January, 2026 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 
7" January, 2026. 


In the said notification, after the words “in all States and Union territories in India”, the words and brackets 
“(except the State of Manipur till further declaration)” shall be inserted.
