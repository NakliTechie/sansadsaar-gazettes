S.O. 5(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956(48 of 956), (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for Building (Widening/two lane with paved shoulder/ four laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of 
NH73A in the stretch of land from Km. 74.700 to Km. !2.950 (Sub Divisional Magistrate, Sirathu (Judicial), 
District HQ-Manjhanpur in the district of KAUSHAMBI in the state of Uttar Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to 
acquired such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under —section (l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority , namely , Competent Authority / Sub 
Divisional Magistrate, Sirathu (Judicial), District HQ-Manjhanpur in the district of KAUSHAMBI in writing and 
shall set out the grounds there of and the competent authority shall give the objector and opportunity of being heard, 
either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objection and after making such further 
enquire, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary , by order , either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final.
