MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th February, 2026 


G.S.R. 145(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 9C of the Mines and 
Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
further amendment in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Mines published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 633(E), dated the 14th August, 2015, 
namely:- 


In the said notification, in paragraph (ID), after serial number (a) and the entries relating thereto, the following 
shall be inserted namely:- 


“(aa) Additional Secretary or Joint Secretary, in-charge of Exploration, Ministry of Mines - Member, 
ex officio;”. 


[F. No. 11/8/2015-M.]] 
FARIDA M NAIK, Jt. Secy. 


Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section 
(i), vide number G.S.R. 633(E), dated the 14th August, 2015 and was amended, vide numbers G.S.R.100(E), 
dated the 7th February, 2022 and G.S.R. 541(E), dated the 8" August, 2025.
