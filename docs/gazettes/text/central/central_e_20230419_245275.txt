F. No. CEA-PS-13-17(23)/6/2022-PSPM_ Division.—Whereas M/s. Jabalpur Transmission Company 
Limited, the applicant with its registered office at Unit No. 101, First Floor, Windsor, Village Kole Kalyan Off CST 
Road, Vidyanagari Marg, Santacruz (East), Mumbai, Maharashtra — 400098, India, has applied for authorization under 
Section 164 of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of electric line under the transmission scheme “Diversion work of 
Jabalpur pool — Bina 765kV S/C Line from location No 80/7 to Location No 86/0 falling under submergence area of 
Banda irrigation project.” 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter no. 1 1/2/2010-PG dated 25.11.2010 had 
granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 and Ministry of Power, Government of India 
also granted the approval under Section — 164 of Electricity Act, 2003 dated 05.06.2013 to M/s. Jabalpur 
Transmission Company Limited . 


M/s Jabalpur Transmission Company Limited has published notice for transmission scheme in Hindi 
newspapers (Dainik Jagran, Swadesh Jyoti) dated 16.05.2022, English newspaper (The Hitavada and Business 
Standard) dated 16.05.2022 and in Weekly Gazette of India (Hindi and English) dated 02.07.2022 to 08.07.2022 for 
the public to make observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within60 days from the date of 
publication. Subsequently M/s Jabalpur Transmission Company Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 12.10.2022 
declaring that no objection has been received from public within 60 days of publication of Public Notice in 
newspapers / Gazette of India. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity 
Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so 
established or maintained for laying of electric lines under the transmission scheme “Diversion work of Jabalpur pool 
— Bina 765kV S/C Line from location No 80/7 to Location No 86/0 falling under submergence area of Banda 
irrigation project.” The following overhead line is covered under this transmission scheme:
