MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th August, 2025 
S.O. 3902(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building “Upgradation to 2 laning with paved shoulders”, maintenance, management and operation of NH-27 in the 


stretch of land from Km. 53.6 to Km. 72.35 (Darugire to Karonggre Section) in the district of East Garo Hills in the 
state of MEGHALAYA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, The Deputy Commissioner, EGH in 
writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being 
heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further 
enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
