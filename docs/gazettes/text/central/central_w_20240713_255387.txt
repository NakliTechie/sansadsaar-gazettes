MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD & PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(DEPARTMENT OF FOOD & PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION) 


New Delhi, the 4th July 2024 
Subject- Procurement target for wheat required in connection with public stockholding for food security purposes. 


No. 7 (1)/2024-Py.I /2024-25— Whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that is necessary and expedient to 
set procurement targets for crops procured at Minimum Support Price (MSP) in connection with public stockholding for food 
security purposes, especially for meeting the food requirements of urban and rural poor in India. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the power conferred by para 2(c)&(d) of Section 3 of the Essential Commodities 
Act, 1955 read with section 22 of the National Food Security Act 2013, as amended from time to time, the Central Government 
hereby notifies the following procurement targets in respect of wheat for the Rabi Marketing Season (RMS) 2024-25 as 
follows:— 


Crop Procurement target [Figures in Metric Ton) 
Wheat 2,66,00,000 


The above mentioned table reflects the procurement target for wheat for Rabi Marketing Season 2024-25. The actual 
procurement may be less or more than this estimate depending upon the prevailing situation and the need to ensure food 
security.
