S.O. 2209(E).— The Central Government, under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), by notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Railways No. 
538 dated 04.02.2026 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II-Section 3-Sub-section (ii) 509 dated 
04.02.2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification), hereby, acquires the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification, for development of Special Railway Project namely, Godda to Pirpainti New B.G. Railway Line 
in District Godda in the State of Jharkhand. Under the Railway Line Construction Project, the Government of India had 
declared its intention to acquire the land from KM 63.394 (Mauza -Khuthari, Anchal-Mahagama) to KM 81.764 (Ithari 
,Anchal-Meherma)of Godda district for the purpose of the section. 


And the gist of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers i.e. "Prabhat Khabar" dated 
04.02.2026 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


Objections were received under section 20 'D'. After due hearing, they were considered by the competent 
authority. Orders were passed accordingly. 


And in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, the Central Government, on receipt of the said report from the competent authority and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20(E) of the said Act, hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And this Central Government, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20(E) of the said Act, hereby declares 
that on the publication of this notification in the Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
