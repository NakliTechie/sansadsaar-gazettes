28/03/2026 , “Divya Bhaskar” dated 28/03/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;

settled the same appropriately;

submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the
