Corrigendum to Notification No. S.O. 1997(E), published on 01/05/2023 


Mumbai, the 19th May, 2023 


S.O. 2236(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number 8.0. 1228(E), dated 14th March, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part II, section-3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated 14th March, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (1) of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, 
has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, Rajkot-Kanalus doubling of Broad 
Gauge single line in District Jamnagar in the State of Gujarat; 


Therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power conferred by 
sub- section (1) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
