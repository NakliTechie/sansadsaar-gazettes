अधिनियम, 7887 के अंतर्गत सार्वजनिक अवकाश घोषित कर दिया था जो मध्य प्रदेश से राज्य सभा के लिए चल रहे 
उप-निर्वाचन के लिए अभ्यर्थिताएं वापस लेने के लिए नियत की गई अंतिम तिथि भी है; और 


यत:, आयोग ने मामले पर विचार करने के बाद, उक्त निर्वाचन के लिए अभ्यर्थिताएं वापस लेने की अंतिम तिथि

ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20h August, 2024 


O.N. 86(E).—WHEREAS, the Commission, vide its Notification No. l00/CS-MP/08/2024 (]), 
dated 4" August, 2024, had notified the schedule for bye election to the Council of States from Madhya Pradesh; and 


WHEREAS, the last date for withdrawal of candidatures in respect of State of Madhya Pradesh for the above 
election was fixed as 26" August, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, the Government of Madhya Pradesh vide notification dated 4.08.2024 declared 26.08.2024, a 
public holiday under N. I. Act, 88] which is also the date fixed for last date of withdrawal for candidatures for the 
ongoing bye election to the Council of States from Madhya Pradesh; and 


WHEREAS, the Commission, after taking into consideration the matter, has decided to re-schedule the last 
date for withdrawal of candidatures for the said election to 27.08.2024; 


Now, therefore, the Commission, in exercise of its powers conferred under Section 39 of the Representation 
of the People Act, 95l, hereby directs that the following amendment shall be made in its Notification No. l00/CS- 
MP/08/2024 (), dated 44° August, 2024, namely; 


For the existing entries in sub clause (c) of clause (B), the following entry shall be substituted: 
“(c) the 27" August, 2024 (Tuesday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures;” 
[F. No. 00/CS-MP/08/2024 ()] 
By Order, 
SUMAN KUMAR DAS, Secy.
