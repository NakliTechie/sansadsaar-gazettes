New Delhi, the 3rd March, 2025 


S.O. 082(E).—Whereas by a notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas S.O. No. 40 (E) Dated 9th September 2024, issued under sub-section () of section 3 of the Petroleum 
and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), published in the Extra Ordinary Gazette of India dated the 20th September 2024, the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to that notification for 
the purpose of laying pipeline in the State of Gujarat for transportation of Crude Oil/Gas/Water from AKHOL EPS 
TO NAGRA END POINT (40" OIL Line), Kathana # 3l TO Kathana GGS (4" Flow Pipeline), Kathana # 50 
TO Kathana # 27 (4"' Flow Pipeline), Padra # (34 To Padra # 66 (4"' Flow Pipeline), Padra # 70 TO Wellpad # 
25A (4" Flow Pipeline), Padra # 3-8 TO Padra # GGS (8"' & 4" Flow Pipeline), Padra # 56 TO Padra # 32 
(4" Flow Pipeline), Padra # 32 TO Padra GGS (4" Flow Pipeline), Padra # 45 TO Padra # 02-7-8 (4&8"' 
Oil-Gas Pipeline), Padra#22 TO Padra GGS (4 & 8" Oil-Gas Pipeline), एक्ग्क्षीउे] TO Padra#22 (4 & 8" Oil- 
Gas Pipeline), NAGRA END POINT TO LAXMIPURA T-POINT (20'' TRUNK PIPELINE), should be laid by 
Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd. 


And whereas copies of the said Extra Ordinary Gazette notification were made available to the public up to 
9 October 2024. 


And whereas the Competent Authority has under sub-section () of section 6 of the said Act submitted report 
to the Central Government;

And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the pipeline, has decided to acquire right of user therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule appended to this 
notification is hereby acquired for laying the pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Central Government, vest on the date of publication of the declaration, in Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd free 
from all encumbrances. 


Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd, shall be exclusively liable for any compensation in terms of Section 0 
of the P&MP Act, 962 and no suit, claim or legal proceeding would lie against the Central Government on any 
matter relating to the pipeline.
