MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMER'S WELFARE 
(Department of Agriculture and Farmer's Welfare) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th May, 2026 
Establishment of New Central Integrated Pest Management Centre (CIPMC) at Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh 


S.O. 2755(E).—The President is pleased to establish a new Central Integrated Pest Management Centre at 
Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. This office shall work under Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage, 
Faridabad, an attached office under the administrative control of Department of Agriculture & Farmer's Welfare. The 
above office will perform activities as per the mandate and objectives of Integrated Pest Management cum Locust 
Division, Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage.
