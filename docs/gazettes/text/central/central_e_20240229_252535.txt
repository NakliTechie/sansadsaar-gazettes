MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th February, 2024 


S.O. 978(E).—Whereas, it appears to the Central Government, that it is necessary in the public interest that 
for the transportation of the LPG in the state of Uttar Pradesh, Pipeline should be laid for implementing Kandla to 
Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline Project by the IHB Limited. 


And Whereas, it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying the said pipeline, it is 
necessary to acquire the right of user (ROU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid, and 
which is described in the Schedule annexed to this notification. 


[भाग IL—ve 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 5 


Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub section () of Section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipeline (Acquisition of Right of user in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), the Central Government hereby 
declares its intention to acquire the right of user there in. 


Any person who is interested in the land described in the said schedule, may submit objection in writing to 
Shri Somdatt Maurya, Competent Authority (UP), Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG Pipeline, IHB Limited, Indane Bottling 
Plant, Panki, Kanpur-208020 within twenty one days from the date on which the copies of this notification issued 
under Sub-section (l) of Section 3 of the said Act, as published in the Gazette of India, are made available to the 
general public.
