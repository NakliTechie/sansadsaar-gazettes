S.O. 4562(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (6) of Section 20F of the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby appoints the 
Arbitrator mentioned in column (2) of the table below to perform the functions as such authority under the said Act, 
with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the Special Railway 
Project of (1) “Kherwadi Railway Station — Development of circulating area of Goods Shed in UP direction for 
holding passengers during Kumbh Mela traffic in 2027” (2) “Devlali — Provision of C&W examination and 
stabling facilities for Kumbh Mela 2027” (3) “Odha Station — Provision of stabling lines for handling Kumbh 
Mela traffic in 2027” (4) “PH-53 — Provision of 2 Nos. 6 M wide Foot Over Bridge with ramp at Kasbe Sukene 
Station for Kumbh Mela 2027” in the state of Maharashtra relating to district mentioned in column (3) of the said 
Table for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of such special railway project.
