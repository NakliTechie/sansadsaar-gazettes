NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th May, 2026 


S.O. 2275(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 503(E) Dated:04//2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter

annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation for Widening 
and Strengthening of existing 2-lane to 2-lane with paved shoulder from Km. 239/280(Koksara) to Km. 294/280 
(Nabarangpur) of NH26 in Kalahandi & Nabarangpur District in the state of ODISHA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” dated 20/03/2026 , 
“Odisha Bhaskar” dated 20/03/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 
same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
