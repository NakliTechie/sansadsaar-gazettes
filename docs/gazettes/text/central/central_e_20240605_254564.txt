अधिनियम 1934 (1934 का 30) की धारा 29 की उप धारा (2) की अपेक्षानुसार, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग 2, 
खण्ड- 3, उप-खण्ड (i) में अधिसूचना संख्या सा.का.नि. 835 (अ) तारीख 14 नवंबर, 2023 द्वारा प्रकाशित की गई थी,

और केंद्रीय सरकार को उक्त प्रारूप नियमों पर जनता से कोई आपत्ति और सुझाव प्राप्त नहीं हुए;

1. (1) इन नियमों का संक्षिप्त नाम कैल्शियम कार्बाइड (संशोधन) नियम, 2024 है। 
(2) ये नियम राजपत्र में इनके अंतिम रूप से प्रकाशन की तारीख को प्रवृत्त होंगे।

G.S.R. 312(E).— Whereas, the draft rules to amend the Calcium Carbide Rules, 1987, were published as 
required by Sub-Section (2) of Section 29 of the Petroleum Act 1934 (30 of 1934), vide notification number G.S.R. 
835(E), dated the 147 November, 2023, in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), 
inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby before the expiry of forty-five days 
from the date on which the notification was made available to the public; 


And whereas the said notification was made available to the public on the 147 November, 2023; 


And whereas no objection and suggestions were received from the public on the said draft rules by the 
Central Government; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 4 of the Petroleum Act, 1934, the Central 
Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Calcium Carbide Rules, 1987, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Calcium Carbide (Amendment) Rules, 2024 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their final publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Calcium Carbide Rules, 1987 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in Rule 4, in the proviso, the 
words “in quantities” shall be omitted. 


3. Inrule 26 of the said rules, for the proviso, the following proviso shall be substituted namely:- 
“Provided that no licence shall be required for storage of calctum carbide — 
(a) temporarily within port limits under such conditions as the Conservator of the port may specify, 
(b) while in transit under the custody of railway administration or road transport agency; 
(c) in the possession of the Defence Forces of the Union”. 

4. Inrule 27 of the said rules, in sub-rule (1), the following proviso shall be inserted namely:- 
“Provided that calcium carbide- 


(a in any quantity not exceeding five kilograms shall be stored in prescribed receptacles each containing not 
more than one kilogram; and 


(b) in any quantity exceeding five kilograms but not exceeding two hundred kilograms shall be stored,- 


(i) in receptacles stored in a dry and well ventilated storage shed, which may form a part of or attached to a 
building that it is separated there from by a substantial partition and the only means of access to it is from 
outside air; 


(ii) due precautions shall be taken to prevent unauthorised persons from having access to the receptacles; 


(iii) the receptacles shall be kept securely closed except while taking out carbide there from.”
