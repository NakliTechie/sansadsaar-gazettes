NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th December, 2024 
No. 86/2024-Customs (N.T.) 


S.O. 5446(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by proviso to sub - section (l) of Section 65 of the 
Customs Act, 962 (52 of 962), the Central Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest 
so to do, hereby specify the manufacturing processes and other operations in relation to a class of goods that shall not 
be permitted in a warehouse, as mentioned in column (2) of the table below: - 


SI. No. Manufacturing processes and other operations in relation to a class of goods in a warehouse 


Goods imported for solar power generation projects which supply electricity. 


Explanation.- For the purpose of SI.No.l, the restriction is applicable only when electricity is resulting from the 
manufacturing processes and other operations in relation to the warehoused goods under section 65 of 
Customs Act 962(52 of 962).
