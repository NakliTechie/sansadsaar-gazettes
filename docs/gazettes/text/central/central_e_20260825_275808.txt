MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[South Eastern Railway(Construction Organization) ] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 24th August, 2026 


S.O. 4708(E).— Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(South Eastern Railway/Construction Organization), number 4510, S.0.4639(E), dated 10" October 2025 in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) herein after referred to as the said notification 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989)(herein after referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government, declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Bangriposi- 
Gorumahisani (85.60Km.) new line”, in the District of Mayurbhanj in the State of Odisha. 


And, whereas, the substance of said notification number 4510, $.0.4639(E), dated 10 October 2025 was 
published in the daily newspapers, namely ‘SAMBAD?’ (Odiya) on 04.12.2025 and ‘Times of India’(English) 
Bhubaneswar Edition on 05.12.2025 respectively under section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas, no objection was received by the competent authority within the period prescribed under 
section 20D of the said Act; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted his report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on this publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
