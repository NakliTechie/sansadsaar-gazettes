MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th December, 2025 


G.S.R. 902(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (6) of section 6 and clause 
(a) of sub-section (7) of section 7 of the Territorial Waters, Continental Shelf, Exclusive Economic Zone and other 
Maritime Zones Act, 976 (80 of 976), the Central Government hereby extends the Customs Act, 962 (52 of 962) 
and Customs Tariff Act, !975 (5] of I975) to the continental shelf of India and the exclusive economic zone of India 
with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, for the following purpose, namely:— 


(a) the reconnaissance, exploration or production of minerals under operating rights granted under the 
Offshore Areas Mineral (Development and Regulation) Act, 2002 (7 of 2003) in the continental shelf 


of India or the exclusive economic zone (EEZ) of India, and; 


(b) the supply of any goods in connection with any of the activities mentioned in clause(a).
