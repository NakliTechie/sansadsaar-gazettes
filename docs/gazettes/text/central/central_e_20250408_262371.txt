MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th April, 2025 


S.O. 1664(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (a) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 1956(48 of 1956), (here in after referred to as the said Act), the central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for Construction of Widening of existing carriageway 2-lane with paved shoulder and slope protection works , 
sliding/sinking zones, along NH-10 From Km 64.000 to Km 80.000 ( Singtam to Ranipoll) in the District of Gangtok 
State of Sikkim, hereby declares its intention to acquired such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under —section (1) of section 3C of the 
said Act. 


10 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the District Collector, Gangtok, in the state of 
Sikkim in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the competent authority shall give the objector and 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objection and after 
making such further enquire, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be inspected by 
the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
