MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th July, 2025 


S.O. 3534(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3] of the Contract Labour (Regulation and 
Abolition) Act, 970 (37 of 970), the Central Government, after consultation with the Central Advisory Contract 
Labour Board, hereby exempts the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh from the 
applicability of the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and Employment number 
5.0. 36(E), dated the 2" December, 204, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated the 2" December, 20I4, in respect of employment of contract labour involved in the works of,- 


(i) sanitation services (cleaning, sweeping and dusting); 
(ii) security services (watch and ward of the institute); and 


(iii) catering services to admitted patients, 


in the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh, for a further period of two years with 
effect from 3" January, 2024 to 2" January, 2026 subject to the following conditions, namely: - 


(a) the hospital management shall ensure that the contract workers are paid wages and provided other facilities 
equal to the lowest paid regular worker performing similar work in the establishment in terms of sub-clause 
(a) of clause (v) of sub-rule (2) of rule 25 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Rules, !97; and 


(b) the Board shall have the right to inspect the establishment to ensure and check whether such wages and benefits 
are provided to such workers and whether the above said conditions are implemented by the establishment. 


Explanatory Memorandum: It is certified that no person shall be adversely affected by giving retrospective affect to 
this notification.
