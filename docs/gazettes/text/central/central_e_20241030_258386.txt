G.S.R. 679(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 57, read with sections 30, 30A, 4l, 4IA, 
53, 54, 56, sub-section (3) of section 98 and sub-section (2) of section 58 of the Customs Act, 962 (52 of 962), the 
Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs hereby makes the following amendments in the Sea Cargo Manifest and 
Transshipment Regulations, 208, namely:- 


I. Short title and commencement — (l) These regulations may be called the Sea Cargo Manifest and Transshipment 
(Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Sea Cargo Manifest and Transshipment Regulations, 208,- 
(l) In the TABLE after FORM-XII: 
i. against Sr. No. 4, in column (3), for the entry, the entry “5..2024” shall be substituted; 
il. against Sr. No. 5, in column (3), for the entry, the entry “30..2024” shall be substituted; 
iii. against Sr. No. 6, in column (3), for the entry, the entry “5.0.2025” shall be substituted.
