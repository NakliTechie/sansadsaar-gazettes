New Delhi, the 4th October, 2023 


S.O. 4344(E).—In reference to Notification Nos. 2/4()/2023/TO(B) dated the 3" January, 2023 
and 2/4(4)/2023/TO(B) dated the 29" March, 2023, the Supreme Court of India passed an order dated 
22.08.2023 in Criminal Appeal No. 250] of 2023 {Arising out of SLP(Crl.) No. 644 of 2023} remanding 
the matter to the High Court of Kerala for reconsideration of the application filed by Shri Mohammed 
Faizal P.P., MP seeking suspension of his conviction. 


2. Now, the Hon’ble High Court of Kerala at Ernakulamvide its order dated 03.0.2023 in Crl. M. 
Appl. No. /2023 in Crl. A. No. 49 of 2023, has rejected the prayer of Shri Mohammed Faizal P. P., MP 
for suspension of the order of conviction. 


3. In view of order dated 03.0.2023 of the Hon’ble High Court of Kerala, Shri Mohammed Faizal 
P.P., Member of LokSabha representing the Lakshadweep Parliamentary Constituency of the Union 
Territory of Lakshadweep stands disqualified from the membership of LokSabha from the date of his 
conviction, i.e. ™ January, 2023 in terms of provisions of Article 02()(e) of the Constitution of India 
read with Section 8 of the Representation of the People Act, 95] subject to further judicial 
pronouncements.
