MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
CORRIGENDUM 
Secunderabad, the 9th February, 2026 


5.0. 690(E).— In the 7A notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Railways published in the 


Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) Dated 17 December 2025 vide S.O. 5533(E) in 
gazette No.5345 dated 01.12.25, regarding “Up-gradation work of Electric Traction System from 1x25kV to 2x25KV 
traction system to meet 3000 MT loading target- Construction of Venkatagiri TSS in Gudur -Tirupati section in 
the of State of Andhra Pradesh”, the Government of Andhra Pradesh has issued a Gazette Notification vide 
G.O.Ms.No.526 dated 30.12.2025, restructuring the Districts and Revenue Divisions. 


As per the revised jurisdiction, Venkatagiri Mandal, which earlier fell under Gudur Revenue Division, has now 
come under the jurisdiction of Srikalahasti Revenue Division of Tirupati District. Accordingly, the Ministry of 
Railways hereby issues the following corrigendum. 


S.No. Published as To be read as
