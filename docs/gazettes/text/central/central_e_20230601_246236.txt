परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3686(अ) तारीख 04.08.2022, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा हरियाणा राज्य के अम्बाला जिले में अम्बाला- 
शहजादपुर-नारायणगढ़- कालाअम्ब राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग संखया 72 के कि.मी. 0.000 से कि.मी.32.996 तक के राष्ट्रीय 
राजमार्ग के निर्माण (चौड़ा करने / चार लेन का बनाने, आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधत और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से 
उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "07.08.2022 " 
को "द ट्रिब्यून (अंग्रेजी)" और "अमर उजाला (हिंदी)" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था; 


और आक्षेप प्राप्त हुए थे और सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उन पर विचार कर लिया है और आक्षेपों को अननुज्ञात कर दिया 
है;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 


NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st June, 2023 

S.O. 2376(E).—In Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways S.0.3686(E) dated 04.08.2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
Ambala-Shahjadpur-Naraingarh-Kalaamb Road Section of NH-72 land from Km. 0.000 to Km. 32.996 in the District 
of AMBALA in the state of HARYANA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Tribune (English) and “Amar 
Ujala (Hindi)” both dated 07.08.2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall
