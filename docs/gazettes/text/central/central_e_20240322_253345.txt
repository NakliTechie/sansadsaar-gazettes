S.O. 533(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences 
Act, 956 (25 of 956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 202 (37 of 202), 
the Central Government hereby nominates Vice Chancellor, Visva-Bharati University, Shantiniketan, West Bengal to 
be member of Institute Body of All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Kalyani, West Bengal in place of Prof. (Dr.) 
Sonali Chakravarti Banerjee, Vice Chancellor, University of Calcutta, West Bengal, and for that purpose makes the 
following amendment in the notification of Government of India, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Number Z- 
2806/65/207-PMSSY-IV dated the I5th July, 209 published in the Gazette of India Part II, section 3, Sub-Section 
(ii) vide SO 2758(E) dated the Ist August, 209: 


In the said notification, for serial number | and the entry relating thereto, the following should be substituted, 
namely:- 


“Member under clause (aa) of Section 4: 
“| Vice Chancellor, Visva-Bharati University, Shantiniketan, West Bengal” 


The terms of office of member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 956.
