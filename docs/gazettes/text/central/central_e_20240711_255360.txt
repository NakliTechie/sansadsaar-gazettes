NOTIFICATION 


Prayagraj, the l0th June, 2024 


S.O. 27I(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (l) of Section 204 of the Railway Act, 


989 (24 of 989) (Hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose the land situated at District Aligarh of the state of Uttar Pradesh, the brief description of which has 
given in the Schedule annexed hereto is required for the purpose of execution of the North Central Railway Special 
Railway project, namely Raising of Speed to 60 kmph/200 kmph on existing New Delhi-Howrah route (incl. 
Kanpur-Lucknow) in the district of Aligarh in the State of Uttar Pradesh, for sub sectioning and paralleling post 
hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from the date of publication of this notification 
in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (l) of Section 20D of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely, Special Land Acquisition 
Officer(J.O.) Distt. Aligarh Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after 
hearing such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the Competent Authority thinks necessary by 
order either allow or disallow the objections. Any order by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of 
Section20D of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent Authority.
