MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th October, 2024 


S.O. 4827(E).—The Competent Authority approves the following amendments in the 
Para 3 (Appointment, Tenure and Service Conditions of the Chairperson and Members) of the earlier 
notification No. 465 dated 10th May, 2006, with effect from the date of this notification:- 


2. Inthe said Notification- 
(i) In Serial No.3.9, the following entries shall be substituted: 


"The Chairperson of the National Statistical Commission will be entitled for an honorarium 
of = 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand) per month. Each Member of the National Statistical 
Commission, except ex-officio Member, will be entitled for an honorarium of = 40,000/- (Rupees 
Forty Thousand) per month. The future revisions in the honorarium shall be made, as and when 
required, by the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI) with the approval of 
the Department of Expenditure (DoE), Ministry of Finance (MoF)." 


Gi) In Serial No. 3.11, the following entries shall be substituted: 


"While undertaking tours, including local travel, in connection with the work of the 
Commission, the Chairperson or Members of the National Statistical Commission will be entitled 
to traveling allowance, daily allowance and for reimbursement of other travel costs, such as stay in 
a hotel guest house etc., as admissible from time to time to a Secretary to the Government of India 
under the traveling allowance rules of the Government of India." 


(iii) In Serial No.3.12, the following entries shall be substituted: 


"The Chairperson or Members of the National Statistical Commission will be eligible for 
reimbursement of bills relating to residential telephone / mobile phone / broadband as admissible 
from time to time to a Secretary to the Government of India. An undertaking would also be 
submitted by the Chairperson / Member that he/she has not claimed reimbursement of such bills or 
expenditure from any other source/ organisation" 


(iv) After Serial No. 3.16, Serial No. 3.17 shall be inserted as follows: 


"The Rule 3 of the Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, as amended from time to time, as 
applicable to a Government servant as well as the Code of Professional Ethics, as amended from time to time,
