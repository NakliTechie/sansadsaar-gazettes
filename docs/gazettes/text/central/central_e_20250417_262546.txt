S.O. 776(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.420(E) dated 2577 September 
2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of 
section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (two lane with paved shoulder/four with earthen shoulder etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 753M in the stretch of land Km 0.000 to km 63.900 Sub Divisional 
Magistrate, Bhokardan, in the district Jalna in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 420(E) dated 250 September 2024, has been 
published in “Dainik Lokmat” and “Lokmat Times” Both Dated 03/0/2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land

vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
