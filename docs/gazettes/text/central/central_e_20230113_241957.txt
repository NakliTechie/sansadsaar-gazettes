MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th January, 2023 


S.O. 241(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Department of Road Transport And Highways number S.O. 3540 (E) dated 29.07.2022 published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for (Widening/Two-Laning, 
etc.) Maintenance, management and operation of NH-45 on the stretch of land from Km. 0+000 to 160+000 Km. in 
(Kundam to Shahpura-Section-Road) (Kundam-Corrigendam) in the district of Jabalpur, in the state of 
MADHYA PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” 20 August 
2022 Saturday; And “Free Press” 20 August 2022 Saturday; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received From Any person By The Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the Said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
