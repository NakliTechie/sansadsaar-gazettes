following rules further to amend the Income-tax Rules, 2026, namely: — 
. (l) These rules may be called the Income-tax (Eighth Amendment) Rules, 2026. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), after the rule 89, the following rule 
shall be inserted, namely: — 


“89A. Conditions for the merger of registered non-profit organisation for the purposes of sections 352(4) [Table: 
SI. No. 8] and 354A.— () For the purposes of sections 352(4) [Table: SI. No. 8] and 354A of the Act, the conditions 
for the merger of registered non-profit organisation with any other registered non-profit organisation having same or 
similar objects shall be the following, namely: — 


(a) the registered non-profit organisation registered under section 332 shall not be merged with the registered 
non-profit organisation provisionally registered under section 332; 


(b) the registered non-profit organisation provisionally registered under section 332 shall not be merged with 
the registered non-profit organisation registered under section 332; 


(०) there is no tax, interest, penalty, fine or any other sum (hereinafter referred to as the outstanding demand) 
payable in consequence of any order passed under the Act in respect of the merging registered non-profit 
organisation where such outstanding demand is not in appeal before any appellate authority; 


(d) in a case where an appeal is pending, the outstanding demand for the merging registered non-profit 
organisation shall be paid by the merged registered non-profit organisation upon the said appeal attaining 
finality; 


(e) the objects of the merged registered non-profit organisation shall not be amended within a period of five 
years from the end of the financial year in which the merger takes place; 


(f) all the assets and liabilities of the merging registered non-profit organisation shall become the assets and 
liabilities of the merged registered non-profit organisation from the date of the merger; 


(g) the merger shall not result in any direct or indirect benefit to any related person as referred to in section 
337(() [Table: Sl. No. 2] of the merging or the merged registered non-profit organisation; 


(h) all the obligations of the merging registered non-profit organisation shall be the obligations of the merged 
registered non-profit organisation including the following: — 


(i) application of the income of the merging registered non-profit organisation shall be given effect to by 
the merged registered non-profit organisation as per section 34]; 


(ii) application of the accumulated income of the merging registered non-profit organisation shall be given 
effect to by the merged registered non-profit organisation as per section 342; 


(iii) the merged registered non-profit organisation shall be liable to pay tax under section 334 in respect of 
the specified income of the merging registered non-profit organisation;
