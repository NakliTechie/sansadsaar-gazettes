NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th October, 2025 
Notice under Section 20E of the Railways Act-, 989 


5.0. 4608(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (East 
Coast Railway, Construction Organisation), Notification number, 5.0. 89(E), dated the 3" January, 2025 published in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the the 37 January, 2025 (herein after 
referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 
989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely, “Flyover between Pendurthi (PDT) and Simhachalam North 
(SCMN) to avoid surface crossing at Simhachalam (7.35 Km) .” in Visakhapatnam district in the state of Andhra 
Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely "Andhra 
Jyothi" in Telugu (Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 05 February, 2025, and "The New Indian Express " in English 
(Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 05 February, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 204 of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received; Competent Authority has heard the objections and orders have been 


passed in terms of section 20D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 


specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 


to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
