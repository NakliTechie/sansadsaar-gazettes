Subject: Amendment in Appendix 2X of FTP, 2023 - reg. 


F. No. 01/89/180/Misc-39/AM-05/PC-2[A]./E- 1425.—In exercise of powers conferred under paragraph 
1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby makes 
amendments in the list of countries exempted from testing for presence of Azo Dyes in Textiles and Textile Articles 
under Appendix 2X of FTP, 2023, in supersession of Public Notice No. 10/2015-2020 dated 15.05.2016. The 
updated Appendix-2X is reproduced herewith:- 


Appendix -2X 


Testing of Textiles and Textile Articles for presence of Azo Dyes will not be required for imports originating from the 
following countries.
