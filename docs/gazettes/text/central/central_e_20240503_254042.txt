NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 3rd May, 2024 


S.O. 895(E).—Where as by the Notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number 8.0. 4509 (E), dated l4th October, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part 
II, section-3, Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989(240fl989)(here in after referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 


Annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely, Ratlam-Indore -Dr.Ambedkar Nagar - Khandwa Gauge Conversion Project in the State of Madhya 
Pradesh. 


And where as, the substance of the said notification was published in daily “Free press Journal” dated 20 Oct, 
2023 and “Nai Duniya’&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 20 Oct, 2023 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act. 
And, where as, Nil objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, where as, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power 
conferred by sub-section (.) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government here 
by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
