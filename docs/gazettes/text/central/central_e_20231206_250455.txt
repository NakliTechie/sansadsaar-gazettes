सा.का.नि. 880(अ).--केन्द्रीय सरकार, कर्मचारी राज्य बीमा अधिनियम, 1948 (1948 का 34) की धारा 95 
द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, कर्मचारी राज्य बीमा निगम के साथ विचार-विमर्श करने के पश्चात्‌, कर्मचारी 
राज्य बीमा (केन्द्रीय) नियम, 1950 में और संशोधन करने के लिए उक्त धारा की उप-धारा (1) द्वारा यथा अपेक्षित 
निम्नलिखित प्रारूप नियम उन सभी व्यक्तियों की सूचना के लिए प्रकाशित करने का प्रस्ताव करती है जिनकी उससे 
प्रभावित होने की संभावना है, तथा Wage नोटिस दिया जाता है कि उक्त प्रारूप नियमों पर राजपत्र में प्रकाशित होने की 
तारीख के तीस दिनों के पश्चात्‌ विचार किया जाएगा |

भवन, TH मार्ग, नई दिल्‍ली-110001 को भेजे जाएं (dm.khare@nic.in)|

होने पर केन्द्रीय सरकार द्वारा उन पर विचार किया जाएगा।

MINISTRY OF LABOUR OF EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th December, 2023 


G.S.R 880(E).—The following draft of certain rules further to amend the Employees' State Insurance 
(Central) Rules, 1950 which the Central Government, after consultation with the Employees’ State Insurance 
Corporation, proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by section 95 of the Employees’ State Insurance 
Act, 1948 (34 of 1948), is hereby published as required by sub-section (1) of the said section, for information of all 
persons likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby given that the said draft rules will be taken into consideration 
after thirty days from the date of publication in the Official Gazette; 


The objections and suggestions may be addressed to Shri Dheerendra Mohan Khare, Under Secretary 
(SS.1), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Shram Shakti Bhawan, Rafi Marg, New Delhi-110001 
(Email id-dm.khare @ nic.in); 


Any objection or suggestion, which may be received from any person in respect of the said draft rules within 
the period specified above, will be considered by the Central Government. 


DRAFT RULES 
1. (1) These rules may be called the Employees’ State Insurance (Central) Amendment Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of its final publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Employees' State Insurance (Central) Rules, 1950, in rule 29, the sub-rule (2), in the proviso, for the 
words “rupees five crores”, the words “rupees twenty-five crore” shall be substituted.
