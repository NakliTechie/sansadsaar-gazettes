NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 29th September, 2025 


S.O. 4439(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(North Western Railway, Construction organisation), Notification number, 5.0. 2859(E) dated the 247 June, 2025 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 25" June, 2025 (hereinafter 
referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (I 
of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Special Railway Project Luni-Samdari-Bhildi Doubling Project 
(Special Railway Project) in Jodhpur Division of Rajasthan State. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Rajasthan 
Patrika" Dated 29" June 2025 & "Dainik Bhasker" Dated 30" June 2025, under sub section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, No objections were received within the stipulated time period by Competent Authority and 
accordingly orders have been passed. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declare that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, The Central Government herby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
