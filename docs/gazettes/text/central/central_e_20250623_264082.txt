NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23" June, 2025 


S.O. 279(E).— Whereas, M/s. Neogen Properties Private Limited had proposed under section 3 
of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a 
Special Economic Zone for Apparel at Thumkunta and Gollapuram Villages, Hindupur Mandal, Anantapur 
District, in the State of Andhra Pradesh; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section 
() of section 4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area 
of 4.65 hectares at the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification 
Numbers S.O. 966 (E) dated 3" June, 2007 respectively; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Neogen Properties Private Limited has now proposed to de-notify the entire 
area of 4.65 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Andhra Pradesh has given No Objection Certificate to 
the proposal vide letter No. 47805/Infra/A3/206 dated 257 March, 2025. After de-notification, the land 
parcel will conform to Land Use Guidelines/master plans of the State Government; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Visakhapatnam Special Economic Zone has 
recommended the proposal for de-notification of entire area of 4.65 hectares of the Special Economic 
Zone;

Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except
