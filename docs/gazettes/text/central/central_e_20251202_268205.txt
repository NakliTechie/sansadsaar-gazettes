Solar Power Park in Bikaner, Rajasthan.

two months from the date of publication. Subsequently, M/s Soltown Infra Pvt Ltd has submitted an affidavit dated 
17.10.2025 declaring that no observation/ representation was received within two months from the date of Publication 
in the official Gazette of Government of India.
