MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 21st May, 2024 


S.O. 2037(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "New BG line between Manuguru — Ramagundam stations (207.80 Km)" as a 
Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in the Districts of Bhadradri Kothagudem, 
Mulugu, Jayashankar Bhupalpally and Peddapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 
effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
