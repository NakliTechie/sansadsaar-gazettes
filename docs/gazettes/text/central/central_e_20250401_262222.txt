MOTILAL NEHRU NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, ALLAHABAD 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th February, 2025 


F. No. A-II/I-H/4/2023—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 26 of the National 
Institutes of Technology, Science Education and Research Act, 2007 (29 of 2007), with the approval of the Visitor of 
the Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad, the Board of the Motilal Nehru National Institute of 
Technology, Allahabad, hereby makes the following Statutes to amend the Motilal Nehru National Institute of 
Technology, Allahabad Statutes, 20[7, namely:— 


(I) These Statutes may be called the Statutes of the Motilal Nehru National Institute of 
Technology, Allahabad (Amendment) Statute, 2025. 


(2) They shall apply to the Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad. 
(3) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad Statutes, 20l7 (hereinafter referred to as the 
principal Statutes), in Statute 4, after sub-statute (iv), the following sub-statute shall be inserted, namely— 


“(v) In the event of occurrence of any vacancy in the office of the Chairperson of Board of Governors by reason of 
expiry of his or her tenure, death, resignation or otherwise or in the event of the Chairperson being unable to discharge 
his or her functions owing to absence, illness or any other cause, the Chairperson of an Institute of National 
Importance, may discharge the functions assigned to the Chairperson under Section 6 of the Act, for a period of six 
months or till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever is earlier, with the approval of the Visitor and in 
case of expiry of tenure, the Visitor may extend the term of the incumbent Chairperson for a period of six months or 
till the nomination of a regular Chairperson, whichever is earlier.” 


3. In the principal Statutes, in Statute 7, sub-statute (5) shall be omitted.
