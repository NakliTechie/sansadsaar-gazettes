S.O. 3305(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan 
Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby appoints the 1st

the Presidency Small Cause Courts Act, 1882 (15 of 1882), as mentioned against serial number 3 of the 
Schedule to the said Act, shall come into force.
