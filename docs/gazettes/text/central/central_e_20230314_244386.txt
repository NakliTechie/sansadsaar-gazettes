NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 14th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 188 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section 1 of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act , 2021 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Ports , Shipping 
& Waterways ( PHRD Division ) No.G.S.R.268 ( E ) dated 04th April , 2022 , the Central Government hereby appoints 
Shri Agrim Kaushal , Economic Adviser , Ministry of Ports , Shipping & Waterways , in place of Shri Lucas L. 
Kamsuan , Former Joint Secretary , Ministry of Ports , Shipping & Waterways , as an Ex - officio Member of the Board of 
V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority with immediate effect and until further orders .
