MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th September, 2026 
G.S.R. 868(E).— The following draft of the Motor Vehicles (Registration and Functions of Vehicle Scrapping 
Facility) Rules, 202l, which the Central Government proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by section 
59(4) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 988 (59 of 988), is hereby published as required by sub-section (l) of section 22 
of the said Act for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby; and notice is hereby given that the said draft 


rules shall be taken into consideration after the expiry of thirty days from the date on which the copies of this notification 
as published in the Official Gazette, are made available to the public; 


Objections and suggestions to these draft rules, if any, may be sent to the Additional Secretary (Transport), 
email: comments-morth @ gov.in, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Transport Bhawan, Parliament Street, New 
Delhi-!0 OOL. 


The objections or suggestions which may be received from any person in respect of the said draft rules before 
the expiry of the aforesaid period will be considered by the Central Government.

Vehicle Scrapping Facility) Amendment Rules, 2026.
