परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3642 (अ),दिनांक: - 14.08.2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र,

कि.मी. 75.000 से कि.मी. 199.000 तक नया प्रस्तावित बैंगलोर - मायडाकुरु (कडपा) - अमरवती/ विजयवाड़ा ग्रीन 
फील्ड आर्थिक गलियारा निर्माण (4-लेन/6-लेन का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना 
से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "05.11.2023 " 
को "प्रजा शक्ति" और "दा हंस इंडिया" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ; 


जबकि सक्षम प्राधिकारी को धारा 3-ग के तहत दायर आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई है, जिस पर विचार किया गया है और 
उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जन किया जाना चाहिए ;

New Delhi, the 2nd February, 2024 


S.O. 443(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O. 3642 (E) Dated:- 14.08.2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building of four/six laning of Newly proposed Bangalore - Mydakuru 
(Kadapa) - Amarvathi/Vijayawada Greenfield Corridor in the stretch of land from Km. 75.000 to Km. 199.000 in the 
district of Y. S. R. Kadapa in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Prajasakti” and “The Hans India” 
both dated 05.11.2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
