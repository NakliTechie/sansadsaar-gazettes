MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th July, 2025 
F. No. 2025/E&R/I(3)/2.— It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view the operational 


requirements, Central Government has decided to transfer the Muzaffarpur Jn.-Samastipur Jn. section from Sonpur 
division to Samastipur division of East Central Railway. 


The new jurisdictional boundary between Sonpur and Samastipur divisions on Muzaffarpur — Hajipur section 
will be between Muzaffarpur - Ramdayalu Nagar stations at KM 50.000. The existing jurisdictional boundaries between 
Sonpur and Samastipur divisions at Muzaffarpur - Kaparpura stations at KM 92.800, Muzaffarpur - Jubbasahani stations 
at KM 0.744 and Samastipur — Karpurigram stations at KM 36.820 will stand abolished as they will fall under continuous 
jurisdiction of Samastipur division. 


The above change will come into effect from |*t September 2025.
