S.O. 248(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4(g) of the All India Institute of Medical 
Sciences Act, 956 (25 of 956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 
202 (37 of 202), the Central Government hereby notifies Dr. Shivaji Bandappa Kalge and Shri Anurag

Institute of Medical Sciences, Bilaspur.
