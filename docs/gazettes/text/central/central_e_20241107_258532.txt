MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(S.E.C. Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 6th November, 2024 


Notice under Section 20 A of the Railways Act 1989 

S.O. 4842(E).— In exercise of Powers conferred by sub section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act 1989 
(24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said act),the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, 
the land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto is required for execution of Special Railway 
project, namely Gevraroad To Pendraroad (135.000 Km) in the state of Chhattisgarh, hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land; Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of 30 days for the date of publication of 
this notification in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of section 20D of the said act; Every objection shall be made to the competent Authority, namely, Additional 
Collector, Korba, Collectorate office, Korba , Chhattisgarh in writing ,and shall set out ground thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal 
practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections; Any order made by the Competent 
Authority under sub-section(2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final; The land plans and other details of the land 
covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the 
Competent Authority.
