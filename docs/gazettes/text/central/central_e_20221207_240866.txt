New Delhi , the 7th December , 2022 

S.O. 5693 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) , clause ( v ) and clause ( xiv ) of sub 
section ( 2 ) and sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3 of the Environment ( Protection ) Act , 1986 ( 29 of 1986 ) read with sub - rule 
( 4 ) of rule 5 of the Environment ( Protection ) Rules , 1986 , the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , 
published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part - II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) , vide S.O. number 2171 ( E ) , dated 
the 27th August , 2014 , namely : 

In the said notification , 

( 1 ) 


in paragraph 2 , in sub - paragraph ( 1 ) , for the words , " Central Government in the Ministry of Environment , 
Forests and Climate Change " , the words " Competent Authority of the State Government " shall be 
substituted ; 


in paragraph 3 , in sub - paragraph ( 10 ) , for the word and letters , “ Ministry of Environment , Forests and 
Climate Change , SESZMC , the word and letters " Competent Authority of the State Government , the 
SESZMC " shall be substituted .
