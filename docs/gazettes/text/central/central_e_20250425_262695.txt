THE INSTITUTE OF COST ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th April, 2025 


F. No. CWR()/2025.— Whereas the draft Regulations further to amend the Cost and Works Accountants 
Regulations, |959, was published, as required by sub-section (3) of section 39 of the Cost Accountants Act, 959 
(23 of 959), in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part III, Section 4, dated the 8 October, 2024, vide notification 
number CWR(l) 2024, dated the 7 October, 2024, inviting objections and suggestions from persons likely to be 
affected thereby, before the expiry of thirty days from the date on which the said Gazette containing the notification was 
made available to the public; 


And whereas the copy of the said Gazette was made available to the public on the 87 October, 2024; 


And whereas the objections and suggestions received from the public on the said draft regulations have been 
considered by the Council of the Institute; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 39 of the Cost Accountants 
Act, 959, the Council, with the approval of the Central Government, hereby makes the following regulations further 
to amend the Cost and Works Accountants Regulations, 959, namely:- 


l. (l) This regulation may be called the Cost and Works Accountants (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
