S.O. 1786(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.4737(E) dated 17 October 2025 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 
National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Building (Upgradation of 
Four lane/Two lane with paved shoulders configuration of Kalyan-Murbad-Malshej Ghat Nirmal Road NH-61 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 15/200 to Km. 38/00 And 58/00 to 84/00 (Design Length-48.58 Km) on EPC mode in the 
district of Thane in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.0.4737(E) dated 17 October 2025, has been published in 
“Sanmishra” and “The Free Press Journal” Both Dated 1 November 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
