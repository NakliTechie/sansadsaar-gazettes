MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th March, 2024 


S.O. 1172(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the Sub-Section (i) of Section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for "Construction of New Bridge at Norghat over River Haldi along with approaches including LA in 
connection with Rehabilitation and Upgradation To 2-Lane with Paved Shoulders Configuration of 
Nandakumar-Contai-Digha-Chandaneshwar Road (0.000 Km To 90.775 Km) along with Contai Bye-Pass 


(3.30km) (Total Length — 94.075 Km) of NH-116B in the State of West Bengal", hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one (21) days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(1) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition Officer, 
Office of the District Magistrate & Collector, Purba Medinipur, Village-Ganapatinagar, P.O. Uttar Sonamui, P.S. 
Tamluk, District- Purba Medinipur, PIN-721648 in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, 
after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
