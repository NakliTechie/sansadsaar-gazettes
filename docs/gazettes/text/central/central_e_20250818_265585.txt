नियम 5 और 7 के साथ पठित सांखियकी संग्रहण अधिनियम, 2008 (2009 का 7) तथा 2017(2017 का 21) एवं जन 
विश्वास (प्रावधानों का संशोधन) अधिनियम, 2023 संख्या 18, 2023 में यथा संशोधित की धारा 3 और 4 द्वारा प्रदत्त 
शक्तियों का उपयोग करते हुए, एतद द्वारा एक सांख्यिकीय सर्वेक्षण, जिसे इसके आगे (वार्षिक औद्योगिक सर्वेक्षण 2024-

उसमें दी गई भौगोलिक इकाइयों के संबंध में नियुक्त करता है। प्रत्येक सांख्यिकी अधिकारी, न्‍्यायनिर्णायक अधिकारी और 
अपीलीय प्राधिकारी, निर्धारित प्रारूप, अर्थात्‌ सांख्यिकी संग्रहण नियम, 2024 के साथ संलग्न weal, में अपर

रखा गया है वहां पर स्पष्टीकरण प्राप्त करनें के लिए कर्मचारियों को प्रतिनियुकत/प्राधिकृत करने के लिए

MINISTRY OF STATISTICS AND PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2025 
5.0. 3784(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3 and 4 of the Collection of Statistics Act, 
2008 (7 of 2009), as amended in 2017 (21 of 2017) and through THE JAN VISHWAS (AMENDMENT OF 
PROVISIONS) ACT, 2023 NO. 18 OF 2023 in 2023, read with rules 5 and 7 of the Collection of Statistics Rules, 
2024, the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation hereby directs Collection of Statistics on 
manufacturing and related activities through a statistical survey, hereinafter referred to as “Annual Survey of 
Industries 2024-25’, as per the details mentioned in the Schedule-I. 


2. The Ministry further appoints the persons as also their assignees in office, mentioned in the Schedule-II as 
Statistics Officers, Adjudicating Officer in Schedule-III and Appellate Authority in Schedule-IV in respect of the 
geographical units mentioned thereof with reference to the aforesaid direction. Each Statistics Officer, Adjudicating 
Officer and Appellate Authority shall immediately submit an undertaking in the prescribed format, namely, Form-I 
appended to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024, to the Additional Director General, Field Operations Division, 
National Statistics Office, Delhi. 


3: The persons authorized by each Statistics Officer, after submission of undertaking in the prescribed format, 
namely, Form-I appended to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024 to the Statistics Officer, would be engaged in 
the geographical jurisdiction of the Statistics Officer for verification of information furnished by informants, for 
inspecting relevant records, and for seeking clarifications, as may be necessary, from informants. In accordance with 
these duties, each person authorized would carry a photo identity card and a letter of authorization issued by the 
concerned Statistics Officer. The Statistics Officer shall keep the undertakings of the authorized persons in his safe 
custody and forward a certificate of having obtained the above undertakings to the Office of the Additional Director 
General, Field Operations Division, National Statistics Office, Delhi. 


4. The statistics collected in Part-I of Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) schedule after due verification and 
scrutiny, will be processed by officials working at the Office of the Additional Director General, Enterprise Survey 
Division, National Statistics Office, Kolkata and the statistics collected in Part-II of the ASI schedule thereof will be 
processed by officials working at the Labour Bureau. Subject to the provisions of the Collection of Statistics Act, 
2008 as amended in 2017 (21 of 2017) and through THE JAN VISHWAS (AMENDMENT OF PROVISIONS) ACT, 
2023 NO. 18 OF 2023 in 2023 and the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024, the unit level data and the reports, 
generated from the data through statistical techniques, may be kept in custody and disseminated by the Enterprise 
Survey Division and the Data Informatics and Innovation Division of this Ministry and the Labour Bureau under the 
Ministry of Labour and Employment to the users within and outside the Government, the States/UTs and the public at 
large as per policy guidelines of the Government. 


5. All persons engaged in any activity in respect of collection of statistics, processing of data and dissemination 
with respect to ASI 2024-25 are governed by the provisions under the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 as amended 
in 2017 (21 of 2017) and through THE JAN VISHWAS (AMENDMENT OF PROVISIONS) ACT, 2023 NO. 18 
OF 2023 in 2023 and the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2024. 


6. Following powers have been delegated to the Statistics Officers: 


(a) A Statistics Officer is empowered to depute/authorize officials to collect statistics in respect of the 
aforementioned direction, to verify any information furnished by informants on the basis of business records 
and other records and to seek clarifications from any owner or occupier or person in-charge of a factory or 
establishment or management, as the case may be, at the premises where such records are kept. A Statistics 
Officer is also empowered to depute/authorize officials under the administrative control of any State 
Government or Union territory Administration, which is willing to collect statistics from any of those 
factories/establishment for which the Statistics Officer has not deputed/authorized any official under his 
administrative control.

(b) A Statistics Officer is empowered to give one month notice or for the period deemed fit to each of the 
informant for furnishing return in the format, specified for the purpose of collection of statistics. 


(c) A Statistics Officer is empowered to provide to any informant, the facility of furnishing return in electronic 
form through a web-based application/portal for certain period, to be specified by him in the notice to be issued 
to the informant. 


7. Powers of the adjudicating officer- An adjudicating officer appointed under section 15A of the Act to hold an 
inquiry and impose penalty, shall also have the powers to- 


(1) call for oral or written evidence on oath or on affidavit from parties including from any other person who 
has been summoned; 


(1) peruse or inspect relevant documents, records or copies thereof; 


(ii) summon and enforce the attendance of any person acquainted with the facts and circumstances of the case. 
Any appropriate agency including the District Administration shall render such assistance on receiving a 
reference regarding enforcing the attendance of any person while discharging the duties of the adjudicating 
officer appointed under the Act. 


8. Appeal against the order of adjudicating officer- 


(1) The appropriate Government may appoint any of its officers, above the rank of adjudicating officer, as an 
appellate authority under the provisions of the Act. 


(2) Whoever aggrieved by the order passed by the adjudicating officer under this Act, may prefer an appeal to the 
appellate authority, within thirty days from the date of receipt of order, in Form-II. 


(3) An appeal may be admitted even after the expiry of the period of thirty days if the appellant satisfies the 
appellate authority that he had sufficient cause for not preferring the appeal within that period. In case the appeal has 
been preferred against the order of the adjudicating officer, the operation of the order of the adjudicating officer 
shall be kept in abeyance, till the disposal of the appeal. 


(4) Whenever, such an appeal is received, the appellate authority shall hold a hearing, either in person or virtual or 
by any other admissible mode, as may be decided by the appellate authority. 


(5) The appellate authority shall issue a notice of hearing to the parties, including persons acquainted with the facts 
and circumstances of the case, by name, date, venue and mode of hearing, along with the relevant documents of the 
receipt on appeal, at least fifteen days before the scheduled date of hearing. 


(6) The notice of hearing may also provide for submission of written communication, which may be relevant for 
deciding the appeal, by the parties, if they so desire at least five days before the date of hearing. 


(7) The notice of hearing shall be served in the following modes, namely: — 
(i) by speed post or registered post; 
(1) by electronic mail in case electronic address is available; 

and proof of deliveries shall be kept on the record of the case file. 


(8) The parties may be present in person or through his duly authorised representative by the specified mode of 
hearing as decided by the appellate authority including video conferencing, if the facility of video conferencing is 
available, at the time of hearing of the case. 


(9) The appellate authority may, after giving the parties to the appeal an opportunity of being heard, pass such order 
as he may think fit. 


SCHEDULE-I 
Subject and purpose for collection of statistics: 


1. Statistics relating to growth, composition and structure of organized manufacturing sector comprising 
activities related to manufacturing processes, repair services, gas and water supply and cold storage is collected 
through Annual Survey of Industries 2024-25. 


Geographical area for collection of statistics: 
2. The Annual Survey of Industries 2024-25 is conducted in the whole of India. 
Method of data collection: 


3. A Notice will be issued by each Statistics Officer to informants under his jurisdiction, indicating therein the 
date by which, the officer or office to whom, the unit or units for which, and the formats and the manner in which

information is required to be furnished, including submission of information in electronic form through a web based 
application/portal designed for the purpose and made available to informants up to a certain date, specified in the notice. 


Nature of informants for whom data may be collected: 


4. The informants are owners or occupiers or persons in-charge, of factories registered under the Factories Act, 
1948 or of establishments registered under the Beedi & Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act,1966 or the 
units registered under any of the following Acts/Boards/Authority viz., Companies Act, 2013, Shops and Commercial 
Establishments Act, Societies Registration Act, Cooperative Societies Act, Khadi and Village Industries Board, 
Directorate of Industries (District Industries Centre) who would be issued notice by Statistics Officer for furnishing 
information under the aforementioned direction. 


5: A Statistics Officer may require furnishing of consolidated information in respect of two or more factories or 
establishments under a single management operating within a State or Union Territory, in case information is not 
separately available for individual factories or establishments, through a notice issued to the concerned owner or 
occupier or person in-charge of the management. 


Period during which collection of statistics may be completed: 


6 The date for submission of information by each informant would be mentioned in the notice and would 
generally fall during August, 2025 to July, 2026 


Reference period: 


7. Information is required to be furnished for the financial year commencing from 1* April 2024 and ending on 
31“March 2025 or for the Accounting Year of a unit ending on any date between 1% April 2024 and 31" March 
2025. 


Nature of information to be collected: 


8. The information required relates to assets and liabilities, receipts, expenses, input items (indigenous and 
imported), products and by-products, distributive expenses, employment and labour cost, working days, man-days 
worked, absenteeism, labour turnover, man-hours worked, earning and social security benefits. 


Language in which information is to be furnished by informant: 
9. An informant shall furnish information in the prescribed formats, either in Hindi or in English. 
Obligation of informant: 


10. An owner or occupier or person in-charge of a factory or establishment or management, as the case may be, 
shall furnish information in the manner and by the date mentioned in the notice received by him from the concerned 
Statistics Officer. He should also furnish relevant records for inspection, and answer questions in relation to the 
information sought, as may be required by the Statistics Officer or a person authorized by him. 


Nature of business records and other records which may be inspected: 


11. Business records of a unit, such as balance sheet, profit and loss account, muster rolls, attendance registers, 
labour register, pay rolls, Director’s report or any other document in support of the information furnished may be 
inspected by the Statistics Officer or a person authorized by him. 


The manner of inspection: 


12. The concerned Statistics Officer or a person authorized by him may verify the information furnished under the 
aforementioned direction on the basis of business records and other records of the unit and seek clarifications, at the 
premises where such records are kept, from the concerned owner or occupier or a person in-charge of a factory or 
establishment or management, as the case may be, or a person authorized by him of the unit.
