उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 20 (क) की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3,

अधिसूचना संख्या का. आ. 196(अ) दिनांक 30 दिसंबर, 2024 (जिसे इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है) द्वारा उक्त 
अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनूसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को, उत्तर प्रदेश राज्य के हाथरस जिले के सादाबाद तहसील में विशेष रेल 
परियोजना, अर्थात्‌ मौजूदा नई दिल्ली-हावडा (कानपुर-लखनऊ सहित), मार्ग पर गतिसीमा को 160 किमी ० प्रति घण्टा/200

See, टेस्टिंग एवं कमीशनिंग हेतु अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की गई थी; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार अधिनियम की धारा 20 अ की उपधारा (4) के अधीन दैनिक समाचार पत्रों, अर्थात्‌ 
"हिन्दुस्तान" और "दि टाइम्स ऑफ इण्डिया" में तारीख 22 जनवरी, 2025 को प्रकाशित किया गया था; और, इस परियोजना में 
प्राप्त सभी अक्षेपों का निस्तारण सक्षम प्राधिकारी द्वारा कर दिया गया है और सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ड 
की उपधारा (1) के अनुसरण में केन्द्रीय सरकार को अपनी रिपोर्ट सौंप दी है ;

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Central Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Prayagraj, the 17th March, 2025 


9.0. 1379(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North Central 
Railway, Construction department), Notification number, S.O. 196(E) dated the 30th December, 2024 published in Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3 Sub Section (ii), dated 13th January, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issue under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act. 2008 (1 lof 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed the said notification for the purpose of Design, Supply, Erection, Testing & Commissioning of Traction Sub-Station 
(TSS) & Sub-Sectioning & Paralleling Post (SSP) of Special Railway Project, namely Special Railway Project of Raising 
of Speed to 160kmph/200kmph on existing New Delhi-Hawrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in District Hathras of 
the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers "Hindustan" and ''The 
Times of India" Dated 22 January, 2025 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, all the objections received in this project have been disposed of by the Competent Authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its report the 
Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances. 


And, that all persons interested in the above mentioned land in pursuance of sub-section (04) of section 20(F) of the 
said Act by the Central Government are hereby informed that they may, personally or through an agent or legal practitioner, 
be notified by the competent authority Sub Divisional Magistrate, Sasni/SLAO Hathras, can submit his claim in the next 
20 days from the date of publication of this notification in the office of the Competent Authority.
