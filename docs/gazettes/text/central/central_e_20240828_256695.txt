99.0. 3637(E).—In pursuance of sub-rule (1) of rule 4 of the Notaries Rules, 1956 and in supersession of 
notification of the Government of India in Ministry of Law and Justice, Department of Legal Affairs number 
S.0.3191(E), dated the 3% September, 2019 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1), the Central Government hereby designates the following officers in the Department of Legal Affairs, 
Ministry of Law and Justice as competent authority, namely :- 


1. Smt. Renu Pande, Deputy Legal Adviser, for the purpose of rule 13 of the said rules in relation to notaries 
appointed by the Central Government; and 


2s Shri Rajeev Kumar, Deputy Secretary, for the purpose of the said rules, in relation to the notaries appointed 
by Central Government, except rule 13.
