NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd February, 2026 
S.O. 551(E).— In pursuance of clause (d) of rule 2 of the Sustainable Harnessing of Fisheries in the 
Exclusive Economic Zone Rules, 2025, the Central Government hereby authorises the following officers to 
perform the functions as Authorized Officer under the said rules, namely: — 


(a) officer of the rank of Chief Petty Officer or equivalent and above of the Indian Navy; and 
(b) subordinate officer of the rank of Adhikari and above of the Indian Coast Guard.
