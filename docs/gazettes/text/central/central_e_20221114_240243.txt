Income - tax Act , 1961 ( 43 of 1961 ) , the Central Board of Direct Taxes hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Finance , Department of Revenue , Central Board of Direct Taxes 
No.61 / 2022 dated the 10th June , 2022 , published in Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) 
vide number S.O. 2693 ( E ) dated the 10th June , 2022 , namely : 

In the said notification , in the SCHEDULE , 

( 1 ) SI . No. 2259 - Sl . No. 2286 and the entries relating thereto shall respectively be omitted ; 

( II ) 

Sl . No. 2592 - Sl . No. 2612 and the entries relating thereto shall respectively be omitted .
