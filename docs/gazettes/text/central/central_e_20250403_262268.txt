S.O. 598(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NHI69A in the stretch of land from 5.600 
to Km 76.200 (Hebri to Parkala) and from Km 85.200 to Km 88.900 (Karavali Junction to Malpe) (Thirthahalli - 
Udupi section) in the district of UDUPI in the state of KARNATAKA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such 
land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Assistant Commissioner and 
Special land acquisition officer Kundapura - 57620l in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner,
