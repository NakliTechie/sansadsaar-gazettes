NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th January, 2024 


S.O. 74(E).— In pursuance of sub-rule (b) of rule 3 of the Passport (Entry into India) Rules, 950, the 
Central Government hereby designates Kamarajar Seaport of Tamil Nadu State as an authorized Immigration 
Check Post for entry into/exit from India with valid travel documents for all classes of passengers.

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th January, 2024 


S.O. 75(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (2) of Clause 2 of the Foreigners Order 
948, the Central Government hereby appoints Foreigners Regional Registration Officer, Chennai as the “Civil 
Authority” for the purposes of the said Order for the Immigration Check Post located at Kamarajar Seaport in the 
State of Tamil Nadu with effect from 57 January 2024.
