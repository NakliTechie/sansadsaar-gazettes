(1) में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (पश्चिम रेलवे निर्माण संगठन) की अधिसूचना सं. 2171(अ) दिनांक 


11.05.2023 में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (पश्चिम रेलवे निर्माण संगठन) की अधिसूचना में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि 
को गुजरात राज्य के पंचमहाल जिले में आणंद - गोधरा दोहरीकरण परियोजना नाम की विशेष रेल परियोजना के 
निष्पादन के लिये आवश्यक है, ऐसी भूमि के अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की गई थी; 


और, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20क की उप-धारा (4) के अधीन स्थानीय दैनिक समाचार “गुजरात समाचार” में 
दिनांक 15.07.2023, और “इंडियन एक्सप्रेस” में दिनांक 16.07.2023 को प्रकाशित किया गया था; 
और, सक्षम प्राधिकारी को किसी भी व्यक्ति का कोई आक्षेप प्राप्त नहीं हुआ है ; 


और, सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20 इ की उप-धरा (1) के अनुसरण में अपनी रिपोर्ट केंद्र 
सरकार को सौंप दी है;

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 20th November, 2023 


9.0. 4991(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No. 2171(E) of dated 11.05.2023 Extra Ordinary part II 
Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention 
to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, Anand-Godhra Doubling Project Project in 
District Panchmahal in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “Gujarat 
Samachar” dated 15" July 2023. “The Indian Express” dated 167 July 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; and whereas, no objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report of the Central Government; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
