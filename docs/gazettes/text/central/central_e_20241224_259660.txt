Sardrapur, dhar and Pithampur (204.76km) in District Dhar, Tehsil Sardarpur Village Chinchodiya in the state

Journal” dated 05" July. 2024 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 05" July. 2024 under sub-section (4) of 
Section 20A of the said Act. And, whereas, nil objection has been received by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power 
conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose.

here by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule
