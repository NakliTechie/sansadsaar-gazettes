MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th June, 2023 


G.S. 1२. 451(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 32 read with sub-sections (1) and (2) of 
section 11 of the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990 (25 of 1990), the Central Government 
hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) 
Stenographer Posts Recruitment Rules, 2019, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Stenographer 
Posts (Amendment) Recruitment Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2... Inthe Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Stenographer Posts Recruitment Rules, 2019, 
in the Schedule, against serial number 1, relating to the post of Principal Private Secretary, in Column 
(11), for the words , “Private Secretary or Reporter in level 7 (Rs.44900-142400) in the pay matrix with 
seven years regular service in the grade”, the words “Private Secretary or Reporter in level 7 
(Rs.44900-142400) in the pay matrix with nine years regular service in the grade” shall be substituted. 


[F. No. A-12018/7/2018-BAP] 
VIKRAM SAHRAY, Jt. Secy.
