NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the Ist March, 2024 


S.O. 999(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (46) of section l0 of the Income-tax Act, 96] 
(43 of 96), the Central Government hereby notifies for the purposes of the said clause, ‘Uttar Pradesh Real Estate 
Regulatory Authority’ (PAN AAAGU067IE), an Authority constituted by the State Government of Uttar Pradesh, in 
respect of the following specified income arising to that Authority, namely:- 


(a) Amount received as Grant-in-aid or loan/advance from Government; 


(b) Fee/penalty received from builders/developers, agents or any other stakeholders as per the 
provisions of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 206; 


(c) Fee received under Right to Information Act, 2005; and 

(d) Interest earned on bank deposits. 
2: This notification shall be effective subject to the conditions that Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory 
Authority,- 

(a) shall not engage in any commercial activity; 

(b) activities and the nature of the specified income shall remain unchanged throughout the financial 

years; and 
(c) shall file return of income in accordance with the provision of clause (g) of sub-section (4C) of 


section |39 of the Income-tax Act, 96]. 


3. This notification shall be deemed to have been applied for the assessment years 202-2022, 2022-2023 and 
2023-2024 relevant for financial years 2020-202, 202-2022 and 2022-2023, respectively.
