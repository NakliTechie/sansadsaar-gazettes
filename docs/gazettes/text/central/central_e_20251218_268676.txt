Functions of The Committee 


The functions of the Committee are to render advice regarding implementation of the provisions Acts/Rules 
relating to Official Language contained in the Constitution, Policy decisions of the Kendriya Hindi Samiti, 
instructions issued by the Ministry of Home Affairs/Department of Official Language and also regarding the 
Progressive use of Hindi in the Ministry of Culture and its Attached/Subordinate Offices/Autonomous Institutions. 


Tenure 
The tenure of the members of the Samiti shall ordinarily be three years from the date of its constitution 
provided that: 


(a) | Members of Parliament who are the members of the Committee shall cease to be members as soon as 
they cease to be Members of Parliament. 


(0). Ex-officio Members of the Committee shall continue as Members as long as they hold the office by 
virtue of which they are Members of the Committee. 


(c) If any vacancy arises on the Committee due to resignation or death of a Member, the Member 
appointed in that capacity shall hold the office for the residual term of three years. 


(d) Tenure of the Committee can be extended or curtailed in the special circumstances. 
TRAVELLING AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


As per Department of Official Language’s Office Memorandum No. II/20034/4/2005-O.L (Policy-2) dated 
03.02.2006. Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance to Non Official Members of the committee will be paid as 
under:- 


(a) The Members of Parliament nominated in this committee will be paid travelling Allowance and Daily allowance 
for attending the meetings of the Committee as per the provisions of the Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowance 
and Pension), Act, 954 and Rules made thereunder and the amendments issued from time to time. 


(b) Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance to other Non-Official Members of the Committee will be paid as per 
guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language’s Office Memorandum No. II/2/22034/4/86-O.L (A-2) 
dated 22 January, !987 and in accordance with the prescribed rates and rules of the Government of India as amended 
from time to time.
