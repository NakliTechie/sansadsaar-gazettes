the Government of India in the Ministry of Home Affairs (Office of the Registrar General, India) number 
S.O. 9(E), dated the has January, 2020, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated the 97 January, 2020, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such 
supersession, the Central Government hereby declares that the houselisting operations of the Census of India 2027 
shall take place between I* April, 2026 and 30" September, 2026 in all States and Union territories in India during 
the period of thirty days specified by each State and Union territory. There shall also be an option for self- 
enumeration which shall be conducted in fifteen days’ time period just before the start of house to house houselisting 
operations of thirty days.
