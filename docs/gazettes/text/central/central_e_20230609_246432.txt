S.O. 2551(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3D of the National Highways 
Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the central governemnt: hereby makes the following 
amendment in the notification of the Government of India in Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, number 
S.0.1128(E), dated 10 March 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary: - 


In the said notification, against the 3A notification "SO. no 2145(E) dated 01 st June 2022" the entry 
"SO. no 2145(E) dated 09th May 2022" shall be substituted and name of paper publication "Times of India" to be 
removed in second para i.e "And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "The Hindu" 
and “varthabarathi” dated 01.06.2022, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;". 


3A Notification SO no Date was wrongly notified in 3D notification and The name of paper publication was 
not mentioned properly.
