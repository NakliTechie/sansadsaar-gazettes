9.0. 5958(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 3749(E) Dated:10/07/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-

referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
NH116A in the stretch of land from Km. 0 to Km. 56.500 in the district of Paschim Medinipur in the state of WEST 


BENGAL 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” dated 
06/08/2026 , and “Dainik Statesman” dated 06/08/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received no objection filed under Section 3C of the said Act, 
within stipulated period of time specified in the said Act.
