INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the ]]th May, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/4434-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Nandvandan College of Nursing situated at Mandla Road, Near By Gour Tiraha, Jabalpur, 
Distt. Jabalpur — 48200l, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya 
Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct Post Basic B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-8/4434-INC dated 3/08/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24!, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 3/08/2022 was also enclosed to Nandvandan College of Nursing situated at Mandla Road, Near By 
Gour Tiraha, Jabalpur, Distt. Jabalpur — 48200, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Nandvandan College of Nursing, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Nandvandan College of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
04/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies L.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 6/l/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic B.Sc(N) and 
M.Sc(N) Nandvandan College of Nursing situated at Mandla Road, Near By Gour Tiraha, Jabalpur, Distt. 
Jabalpur — 48200, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject 
to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
