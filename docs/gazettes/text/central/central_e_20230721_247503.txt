MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 10th July, 2023 


G.S.R. 526(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (g) of sub-section (2) of section 60, read with 
section 198 of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further 
to amend the Indian Railways (Open Lines) General Rules, 1976, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Indian Railways (Open Lines) General 
(Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Indian Railways (Open Lines) General Rules, 1976, (hereinafter referred to as said rules), for rule 
5.16, the following rule shall be substituted, namely: - 


“5.16 Shunting during reception of trains- When signals have been taken ‘off for an incoming train on to a 
line which is not isolated, no shunting movement shall be carried out towards points over which the incoming 
train is to pass except under special instructions for identified stations where frequent shunting movements 
take place, and where such points are protected by a Stop Signal or by a Shunt Signal with the precautions to 
be observed while performing shunting that- 


a) shunting shall be carried out under supervision of authorised competent railway servant; and 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


b) rake or load should be fully on air brake; and 
c) the maximum speed during shunting operations shall not exceed 15 kmph” 
3. In the said rules, in rule 8.05, for sub-rule (3), the following sub-rule shall be substituted, namely:- 


“8.05 (3) When signals have been taken ‘off’ for an incoming train on to a line which is not isolated, no 
shunting movement shall be carried out towards points over which the incoming train is to pass except as 
permitted by rule 5.16 of these rules.” 


4. In the said rules, in rule 8.10, for sub-rule (2), the following sub-rule shall be substituted, namely:- 


“8.10 (2) When signals have been taken ‘off’ for an incoming train on to a line which is not isolated, no 
shunting movement shall be carried out towards points over which the incoming train is to pass except as 
permitted by rule 5.16 of these rules.”
