25 सितंबर 2025 (जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है) द्वारा उक्त अधिनियम से उपावद्ध अनुसूची में 
विनिर्दिश भूमि को मध्य प्रदेश राज्य में विशेष रेल परियोजना अर्थात 'खंडवा-अकोला आमान परिवर्तन (एमजी से बीजी) के 
निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की थी.

अर्थात "दैनिक भास्कर (हिंदी) व पत्रिका (हिंदी) में हेमंत कुमार भगोरिया, मुख्य इंजीनियर/निर्माण संगठन, सिकंदराबाद 
द्वारा दिनांक 16.10.2025 को प्रकाशित किया गया था |

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 6th February, 2026 


S.O. 580(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O 4372 (E) dated the 25"September 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 
3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 25"September2025 (here in after referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub- 
section(1) of section 20(A) of the Railways Act, 1989(24 of 1989), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion (MG to BG) in the 
State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the extract of the said Act was published in the daily newspapers, namely, "Dainik Bhaskar 
(Hindi) and Patrika (Hindi)" on 16.10.2025 by Hemant Kumar Bhagoria, Chief Engineer/Construction Organization, 
Secunderabad, under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act. 


And, no objections were received from the villages of Ratnapur, Amullakhurd, Khaknar Kala, Rangai and 
Tukaithad in Burhanpur district before the competent authority, namely, the Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue), 


Nepanagar. 


And, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 20D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 


report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, the Central Government, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20E of the said Act, hereby declares that the land specified 
in the Schedule appended hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
