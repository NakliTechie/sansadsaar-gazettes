M/s Serentica Renewables India Private Limited (SRIPL) had published notice for transmission scheme in 
local newspapers The New Indian Express (in English) dated 03.08.2024, Rajasthan Patrika (in Hindi) dated

Gazette of India dated 19.10.2024 for the general public to make observations/representations on the proposed

received within 2 Months from the date of Publication in the official gazette of Government of India and newspaper 
publications.
