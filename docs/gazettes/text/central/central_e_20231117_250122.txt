S.O. 4958(E).—In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines

and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 for Ennore - Thiruvallur - 


Bengaluru - Puducherry - Nagapattinam — Madurai - Tuticorin Natural Gas Pipeline Project of M/s. Indian 
Oil Corporation Limited in the State of Tamil Nadu. 


2. This notification will be effective from the date of its issues.
