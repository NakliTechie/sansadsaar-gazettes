S.O. 296(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O 353 (E) dated the 20th March, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 3 
Sub-section(ii), dated the 20th March, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub- 
section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mudkhed — Dhone 
doubling project in the District of Medak in the State of Telangana. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “THE HINDU, 
HINDI MILAP and MANA TELANGANA” dated 08th May 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas, for Kamaram, Mirzapally, Akkannapet & Jhansi Lingapur Villages of Medak Revenue Division, 
Medak district, no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority accordingly 
orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances:
