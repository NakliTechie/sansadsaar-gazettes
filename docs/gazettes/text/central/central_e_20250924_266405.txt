MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2025 


G.S.R. 706(E).—Whereas the use of Aadhaar number to establish identity enables individuals to receive 
subsidies, benefits and services in a convenient and seamless manner, obviates the need for multiplicity of documents 
to establish identity, simplifies processes and promotes transparency and efficiency; 


And whereas, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, after 
consultation with the Unique Identification Authority of India, had allowed vide its letter no. eF.No. !3(2)/2020-EG-II 
(Vol-IID), dated the 29" July, 2025 to the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, for the purposes specified under 
sub-rule (l) of rule 3 of the Aadhaar Authentication for the Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, 
Knowledge) Rules 2020, herein after referred to as the said rules as per the provisions of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) 
of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and 
Services) Act, 20l6, herein after referred to as the said act, read with Rules, that the Railway Recruitment Control 
Board may be allowed to perform authentication and be permitted the use of Aadhaar number during authentication 
for establishing identity of Aadhaar number holder; 


And whereas, the Aadhaar authentication shall be performed on a voluntary basis for verification of 
candidates and examination personnel including invigilators, scribes, and supporting staff at various stages of the 
recruitment process until final joining, and that Railway Recruitment Control Board shall perform the Aadhaar 
authentication only for transparent recruitment purpose conducted by the Railway Recruitment Control Board using 
Yes/ No or/and Electronic- Know Your Customer (eK YC) authentication facilities; 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Act read 
with rule 5 of the Good Governance (Social, Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020, the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Recruitment Control Board) hereby notifies the following, namely: — 


l. ({) The Ministry of Railways shall obtain the consent of the Aadhaar number holder for the purpose of 
authentication herein as per the provisions of the Act.

(2) Aadhaar authentication is on voluntary basis as accordance with sub-rule (2) of rule 3 of said rules, the 
Ministry of Railways shall inform to the Aadhaar number holder of alternate and viable means of 
identification and shall not deny any service to the Aadhaar number holder for refusing to, or being 
unable to, undergo Aadhaar authentication, namely:- 


(a) Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card. 
(b) Driving License issued by the Licensing Authority under the Motor Vehicles Act, 988. 


2. This notification shall come into on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
