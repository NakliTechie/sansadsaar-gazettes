INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-02/0753-INC: — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Gnaneswari School of Nursing situated at H. No 3-9-572, Road No 3, Sri Balaji Nagar, Opp.- 
Saharastates, Mansoorabad, L.B. Nagar, Hyderabad, Distt. Medak 500068 Telangana is recognized by State 
Government of Telangana and Telangana State Nurses Midwives Auxiliary Nurse Midwives & Health visitors 
Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-02/l0753INC (52849) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section l4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Room No. 359, D- Block, Telangana Secretariat, Hyderabad — 500022, Telangana was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Gnaneswari School of Nursing situated at H. No 3-9- 
572, Road No 3, Sri Balaji Nagar, Opp.- Saharastates, Mansoorabad, L.B. Nagar, Hyderabad, Distt. Medak 
500068 Telangana. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to 
the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the 
case of Gnaneswari School of Nursing. Even the institution Gnaneswari School of Nursing has not responded 
to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
05/08/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 
i.e., clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to 
run GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Gnaneswari School of Nursing situated 
at H. No 3-9-572, Road No 3, Sri Balaji Nagar, Opp.- Saharastates, Mansoorabad, L.B. Nagar, Hyderabad, 
Distt. Medak 500068 Telangana. Shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Hyderabad. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
