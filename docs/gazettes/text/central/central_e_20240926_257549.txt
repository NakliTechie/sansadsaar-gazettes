New Delhi, the 26th September, 2024 


S.O. 4238(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (() of section 70 of the National 
Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 202 (4 of 202]), the Central Government, after consultation 
with the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Profession, hereby makes the following amendment in the 
Schedule to the said Act, namely:- 

In the said Schedule, against serial number 7, in column (2), under sub-heading “Other Care Professionals”, 
in column (3), after item (iii) and the entries relating thereto, the following item and entries shall be inserted, namely: - 


“(iv) Acupuncture Professionals 2230, 3230”.
