ex-factory price for the 580gm ‘Type-A & ‘Type-B’ Jute Bags which are as follows: 


Description Bag Type Price/MT Price per 100 bags 
B-Twill Sacking Type A Rs. 221407.00 Rs. 12841.59 
(580 gms) 
Type B Rs. 221407.00 Rs. 12841.59 
(Without Linkage) 


2. Type A & Type B bags of the size 94cm x 57 cm/HD., 580 gm mass per bags, ends per dim, pecks per 
dm., as specified in the BIS Specification IS: 16186:2014 respectively with additional identification marking 
as per extant orders/instructions, shall be purchased or sold during the month of June, 2026. 


3. Every bag is to be printed/marked with emblem to specified in the PCSO issued by the O/o Jute 
Commissioner, Kolkata with the name of the manufacturing mill printed on it as well as BIS certification 
marking. The average minimum breaking strength shall be as provided in the aforesaid specification of BIS. 


4. The price fixed under this Notification shall be exclusive of GST which shall be paid in addition to the ex- 
factory price by the purchaser.
