साथ पठित उपधारा 11 द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए अधिसूचित करती है कि भारतीय नौसेना में

New Delhi , the 21st December , 2022 

S.R.O. 24 ( E ) . In exercise of the powers conferred by section 184 read with sub - section 11 of the 
Navy Act , 1957 ( 62 of 1957 ) , the Central Government hereby notifies , that Agniveers enrolled in the Indian 
Navy shall form a distinct rank different from any other existing rank and the junior most rank in the Indian 
Navy .
