CABINET SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th July, 2023 


S.O. 3223(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 
1961, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and Seventy- 
first Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force at once. 
2. In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE,- 


(a) under the heading “MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
(ELECTRONIKI AUR SOOCHANA PRAUDYOGIKI MANTRALAYA)”, for entry ‘2A. Promotion 
of Digital Transactions including Digital Payments.’, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:- 


“2A. Promotion of Digital Transactions excluding Digital Payments.”; 


(b) under the heading “MINISTRY OF FINANCE (VITTA MANTRALAYA)”, under sub-heading “E. 
DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES (VITTIYA SEWAYEN VIBHAG)”, after entry 8, the 
following entry shall be inserted, namely:- 


“8A. Promotion of Digital Payments.”. 
DROUPADI MURMU, 
PRESIDENT
