MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 20th June, 2024 


G.S.R. 341(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 20P of the Railway Act, 1989(24 of 1989), the 
Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:- 


1. Short title, commencement and application:-(1) These rules may be called the Land Acquisition(Special 
Railway Projects)(Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


2. Inthe Land Acquisition( Special Railway Projects) Rules, 2016,- 


(i) in rule 4, in sub-rule(2), for the words “a nationalized bank’, the words and brackets “a Scheduled 
commercial bank(Public and Private) notified from time to time by the Reserve Bank of India as Agency bank”, shall 
be substituted; 


(ii) in rule 5, in sub-rule(1), for the words “a nationalized bank”, the words and brackets “a Scheduled 
commercial bank (Public and Private) notified from time to time by the Reserve Bank of India as Agency bank’’, shall 
be substituted.
