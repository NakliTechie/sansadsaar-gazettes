the Central Government declared its intention to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project 
namely, 


Dabhoi — Samlaya Gauge Conversion Project in District Vadodara in the State of Gujarat. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “the 
indian express” and “ divya bhaskar ” dated 4970 October 2025 under subsection (4) of section 204 of 
the said Act; 


And, whereas, objection has been received & resolved by the competent authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
Dabhoi - Samlaya has submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
