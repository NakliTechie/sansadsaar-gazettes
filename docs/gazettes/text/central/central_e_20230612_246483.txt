(1947 का >(1४/॥) की धारा 14 के अधीन प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ wager 
निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है:- 


जबकि, संस्कार इंटरनेशनल बी.एससी. नर्सिंग कॉलेज, गंगानगर फाटक के पास, सरस डेयरी रोड, हनुमानगढ़ 
जंक्शन, हनुमानगढ़-335512 (राजस्थान) राजस्थान राज्य सरकार तथा राजस्थान उपचर्या परषिद्‌ द्वारा बी.एससी. 
(नर्सिंग) पाठ्यक्रम संचालित करने के लिए मान्यता प्राप्त है। 


वहीं, भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए प्रधान 
सचिव (स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण), स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण विभाग, राजस्थान सरकार, सरकारी सचिवालय, 
मुख्य भवन, जयपुर-302005, राजस्थान को संबोधित नोटिस संख्या 18-27/5574-आईएनसी दिनांक 12.08.2022 को 
जारी किया गया था। दिनांक 12.08.2022 को जारी नोटिस की एक प्रति संस्कार इंटरनेशनल बी.एससी. नर्सिंग कॉलेज, 
गंगानगर फाटक के पास, सरस डेयरी रोड, हनुमानगढ़ जंक्शन, हनुमानगढ़-335512 (राजस्थान) को भी भेजी गई थी। 
जारी किए गए नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत 
करने का अनुरोध किया गया था।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F. No. 18-27/5574-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Sanskar International B.Sc Nursing College situated at Near Ganganagar Fatak, Saras Dairy 
Road, Hanumangarh Junction, Hanumangarh — 335512, Rajasthan is recognized by State Government of 
Rajasthan and Rajasthan Nursing Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-27/5574-INC dated 12/08/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the Principal Secretary (Health & FW) Department of Health & 
Family Welfare, Government of Rajasthan, Govt. Secretariat, Main Building, Jaipur - 302005, Rajasthan was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 12/08/2022 was also enclosed to Sanskar International B.Sc Nursing College situated at 
Near Ganganagar Fatak, Saras Dairy Road, Hanumangarh Junction, Hanumangarh - 335512, Rajasthan. In 
the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, serious allegations have been raised against the Sanskar International Nursing College with 
regard to issuance of invalid and fake certificate in Community Health integrated course. It amounts to as serious 
offence on behalf of the college and the student. It is brought to our attention that an FIR has already been lodged 
against the concermed parties. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any response from the State Government as respects the case of 
Sanskar International B.Sc Nursing College, Rajasthan. Even the institution Sanskar International B.Sc Nursing 
College has not responded to the notices issued. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 
declares that with effect from 22/12/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) to Sanskar 
International B.Sc Nursing College situated at Near Ganganagar Fatak, Saras Dairy Road, Hanumangarh 
Junction, Hanumangarh — 335512, Rajasthan shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Rajasthan subject 
to the orders of State government of Rajasthan. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
