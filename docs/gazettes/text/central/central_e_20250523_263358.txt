S.O. 2306(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred by 37A of Section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008, 
General Manager/ East Central Railway has approved to declare “New Railway Line between Charhi — Chapri Station 
(13.771 KM)” as Special Railway Project. Therefore, “New Railway Line between Charhi — Chapri Station (13.771 
KM) Project is declared special Railway Project in the State of Jharkhand.
