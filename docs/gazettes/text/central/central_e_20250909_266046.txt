5.0. 4084(E).— Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section (1) of Section 
20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (Act No. 24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), after being satisfied, 
declares that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is provided in the Schedule annexed 
hereto, is required for the purpose of construction of a new railway line (13.77 km) between Chapri and Charhi 
Stations in the State of Jharkhand, and the same shall be acquired. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within thirty days from the date of publication of this notification 
in the Official Gazette, raise objections under sub-section (1) of Section 20D of the said Act regarding the acquisition 
and use of such land for the above purpose. 


Every such objection shall be made in writing to the competent authority, Hazaribagh, State of Jharkhand, 
stating the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall provide the objector an opportunity of being heard, 
either personally or through a legal practitioner. After hearing all such objections and making such further inquiry, if 
any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, it shall, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Every such objection shall be addressed in writing to the competent authority, i.e., the Land Acquisition 
Officer, Hazaribagh, stating the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall provide the objector an opportunity 
of being heard, either personally or through a legal practitioner. After hearing all such objections and making such 
further inquiry, if any, as the competent authority deems necessary, it shall, by order, either allow or disallow the 


objections.
