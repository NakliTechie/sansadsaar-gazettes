New Delhi, the 30th May, 2025 


S.O. 2388(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.4483 (8) dated I!" October 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (two lane with paved shoulder/ four lane, etc.) maintenance, management and operation of National Highway 
No. 753 M in the stretch of land from Km 0/000 to km 28/000 “Chikhli (Hatni) to Dahad “ in the District of Buldhana 
in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification , has been published in “Dainik Ashia Express Buldhana" 
dated on 3" December 2024 and “Dainik Prashnkal* dated on 4 December 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 
3A of the said Act; 


And Whereas Objections field under section 3-C considered and settled the same appropriately, 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
