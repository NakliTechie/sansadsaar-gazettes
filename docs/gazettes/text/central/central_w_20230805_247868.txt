हनमकोंडा, वारंगल से हैदराबाद के आसपास के क्षेत्र में स्थानांतरित करने के लिए दिनांक 07.07.2022 को एक प्रस्ताव प्रस्तुत किया गया।

1... भूमि समवत विश्वविद्यालय/प्रायोजक निकाय के नाम पर पंजीकृत होनी चाहिए। 


ii, विश्वविद्यालय अनुदान आयोग (समवत विश्वविद्यालय संस्थान) विनियम, 2016 और 2019 की अपेक्षानुसार हैदराबाद में 
वास्तविक और शैक्षणिक अवसंरचना का सृजन किया जाना चाहिए। 


४. समवत विश्वविद्यालय, परिसर के स्थानांतरण के संबंध में छात्रों, संकाय और गैर-शिक्षण कर्मचारियों की सम्मति प्रस्तुत करेगा। 
५४. समवत विश्वविद्यालय तेलंगाना राज्य सरकार से अनापत्ति प्रमाणपत्र प्राप्त कर प्रस्तुत करेगा।

अध्यधीन होगा। जब तक वारंगल परिसर को केंद्र सरकार द्वारा समवत विश्वविद्यालय के ऑफ-कैंपस के रूप में घोषित नहीं किया

No. 9-3/2017-U.3(A)—Whereas, the Central Government is empowered under Section 3 of the University Grants 
Commission (UGC) Act, 1956 to declare, on the advice of the UGC, an Institution of higher learning as an Institution deemed 
to be University. 


2. And whereas, in exercise of powers conferred under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, this Ministry, vide Notification 
No.9-3/2017-U.3(A) dated 29.11.2019, on the advice of UGC, declared Chaitanya Degree College (Autonomous), 
Hanamkonda, Warangal, Talangana as an Institution deemed to be University in the name of Chaitanya Institute of Science & 
Technology, Warangal, Telangana under general category for a provisional period of five years with prospective date. 


3. And further whereas, this Ministry, vide Notification No.9-3/2017-U.3(A) dated 29.11.2019, rechristened the name 
of Chaitanya Institute of Science & Technology (Deemed to be University), Hanamkonda, Warangal to Chaitanya (Deemed to 
be University), Hanamkonda, Warangal. 


4. And whereas, the President and Chancellor of Chaitanya (Deemed to be University), Warangal Telangana submitted 
a proposal on 07.07.2022 for shifting of Chaitanya (Deemed to be University) from Hanamkonda, Warangal to the vicinity of 
Hyderabad. 


5. And whereas, UGC, vide its letter No.41-1/2020 (CPP-I/DU) dated 18.11.2022 recommended that the Ministry may 
accord ‘In principal’ approval to Chaitanya (Deemed to be University), Warangal for shifting of their main campus from 
Hanamkonda, Warangal to the vicinity of Hyderabad with certain conditions. 


6. And whereas, this Ministry, on the advice of UGC, vide letter No. 9-3/2017-U.3(A) dated 29.12.2022, accorded ‘In 
principle approval’ to Chaitanya (Deemed to be University), Hanamkonda, Warangal for shifting of the main campus from 
Hanamkonda, Warangal to the vicinity of Hyderabad subject to the fulfilment of the following conditions: 


i. The land at Hyderabad for Deemed to be University shall not be less than available at Warangal. 
ii. The land should be registered in the name of Deemed to be University/Sponsoring Body. 


iii. The physical and academic infrastructure should be created at Hyderabad as required in the UGC (Institutions 
Deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2016 and 2019. 


iv. The Deemed to be University will submit willingness of the students, faculty and non-teaching staff with regard 
to shifting of the campus. 


v. | The Deemed to be University will submit NOC from the State Government of Telangana. 


vi. Since the Deemed to be University is also proposing to use Warangal campus for the Deemed to be University, 
the Warangal campus can be treated as Off-campus of the Deemed to be University. However, this shall be 
subject to the approval of the Warangal campus as off-campus by the Central Government as per the provisions 
of the UGC (Institutions Deemed to be University) Regulations, 2019. Till such time the Warangal campus is 
declared as an off-campus of the Deemed to be University by the Central Government, no academic activities 
shall be undertaken at Warangal campus after the campus is shifted to Hyderabad. 


vii. The "In Principal" approval shall not be treated as 'Final approval’ and can be withdrawn at any point of time by 
the Central Government. 


7. And whereas, the Institution, vide letters dated 30.03.2023 and 21.04.2023 submitted compliance report in respect 
of the above conditions. The compliance report and the clarifications of the Institution were considered by the Commission in 
its 570th meeting (Item No. 2.08) held on 27.06.2023 in which the following resolution was passed: 


“The Commission considered and decided to recommend that the Ministry may consider approving the shifting of 
campus of Chaitanya deemed to be university from Warangal to Hyderabad. The Commission further decided to
