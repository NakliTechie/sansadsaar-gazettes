MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 26th September, 2024 


S.O. 4234(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) the Central Government hereby notifies “Manmad-Indore New Line {New line 
between Manmad to New Dhule and Nardana to Dr. Ambedkar Nagar (309.43 Kms)” as Railway 
project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Nashik & Dhule 
District of Maharashtra State, Badwani, Khargone, Dhar & Indore District of Madhya Pradesh State, for 
providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette.
