के अधीन जारी और तारीख 02 जनवरी 2024 को भारत के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित अधिसूचना द्वारा राष्ट्रीय राजधानी क्षेत्र 
दिल्‍ली की विधान सभा के निर्वाचित सदस्यों से यह अपेक्षा की है कि 27 जनवरी 2024 को श्री सुशील कुमार गुप्ता की

क्षेत्र दिल्‍ली से उस सदन के लिए एक सदस्य निर्वाचित कर दें;

ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd January, 2024 
O.N. 05(E).—Whereas, the President of India has, by notification issued under Section 12 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1951(43 of 1951) and published in the Gazette of India on 02nd January, 2024, has 
been pleased to call upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of the National Capital Territory of Delhi, 
to elect 1 (one) member to the Council of States to fill the vacancy in that House caused due to the retirement of Shri 
Sushil Kumar Gupta on 27th January, 2024 on the expiration of his term of office; 
Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the said Act, the Election 
Commission of India hereby- 
(A) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 9th January, 2024 (Tuesday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 10th January, 2024 (Wednesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(c) the 12th January, 2024 (Friday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 19th January, 2024 (Friday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 23nd January, 2024 (Tuesday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 
(B) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 


on the date specified above for the election. 


[F. No. 318/CS-DL/2/2024(1)]
