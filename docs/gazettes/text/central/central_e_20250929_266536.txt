NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 29th September, 2025 


S.O. 4438(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Sultanganj-Katuria via Asarganj, 
Tarapur & Belhar New Line” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Bhagalpur, Munger 
& Banka District in the State of Bihar, for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
