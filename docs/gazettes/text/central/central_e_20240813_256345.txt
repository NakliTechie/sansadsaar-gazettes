S.O. 3453(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section | of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (8 of 2023), hereinafter referred to as the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby appoints the 3 day of August, 2024 as the date on which the amendments to the Prevention of Money- 


laundering Act, 2002 (5 of 2003) as specified in column (5) against serial number 34 of the Schedule to the said Act, 
shall come into force.
