S.O. 28(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,l956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH-NES in the 
stretch of land from Km. 475.7 to Km. 500.6 (Samba) in the district of SAMBA in the UT of JAMMU & KASHMIR, 
hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act.
