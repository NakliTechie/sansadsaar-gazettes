MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 
(Department of Personnel and Training) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th December, 2023 


G.S.R. 875(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (A) of section 3 of the All 
India Services Act, 95] (6 of 95), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Indian Forest Service (Pay) Rules, 206, namely: — 


l. (0) These rules may be called the Indian Forest Service (Pay) Amendment Rules, 2023. 
(ii) They shall be deemed to have come into force on the Ist day of January, 206. 


2. In the Indian Forest Service (Pay) Rules, 206, in rule 5, for sub-rule (0), the following sub-rule shall be 
substituted, namely:- 


“(|0) On promotion from one grade to another in the Service, a member of the Service shall have an option 
to get his pay fixed in the Level of the higher post either from the date of his promotion or from the date on 
which he subsequently earns an increment in the lower scale in the manner provided in the relevant sub-rule 
above; in the latter case, on the date of promotion, a member of Service shall be placed at the next higher cell 
in the Level to which he or she is promoted with further re-fixation to be done in the manner provided in 
relevant sub-rule above with effect from the Ist day of January, or the Ist day of July, the date of accrual of 
next increment in the lower scale.” 

[F. No. 402/0/2023-AIS-I] 

J. ASHOK KUMAR, Director 

Explanatory Memorandum: 


The Central Government has decided to amend sub-rule 0 of rule 5 of the Indian Forest Service (Pay) Rules, 
206 which provides for the fixation of pay of IFS officers on their promotion from one grade to another. The Indian 
Forest Service (Pay) Rules, 206 are being amended accordingly with effect from the 7 January, 206. 


It is certified that no member of the Indian Forest Service is likely to be adversely affected by giving 
retrospective effect to these rules.
