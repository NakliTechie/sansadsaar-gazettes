S.O. 4780(E).—In pursuance of Sub—Section (5) of Section 3G of the National Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 
1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the order of Government of India in the 
Ministry of Road Transport & Highways issued vide No. NHAI/LA/RR/97/5.11/Part-B dated 31.12.2001. In the table 
of the aforesaid order against Sl. No.4 relating to designation of officer in column (2), for the entry “Deputy 
Commissioner’, Chitradurga” the entry Additional Deputy Commissioner, Chitradurga, shall be substituted.
