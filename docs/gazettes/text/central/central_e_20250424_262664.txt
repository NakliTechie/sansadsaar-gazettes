NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 22nd April, 2025 


S.O. 852(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub section () of section 20(A ) of the Railway Act, 
989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred as the said act) , the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto is required for execution of 
Special Railway Project, namely for Up-gradation of existing (52569 Traction System to 2x25KV OHE with AT 
feeder arrangement between Jharsugda-Raigarh, Raigarh-Champa, Champa-Bilaspur, Tilda-Bilaspur, Tilda-Durg, 
Mundikota-Nagpur, Bilaspur-Pendra Road-Anuppur & Raipur-Lakholi sections over SEC Railway to meet 3000MT 
loading target in the state of Chhattisgarh and Odisha over SEC railway, hereby declares its intention to acquire such 
land. 


Any person interested in the said land may within a period of 30 days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub 
section () of section 20 (D) of the said act. 


Every objection shall be made to the competent authority namely, Sub Divisional Magistrate (Revenue) Kota, 
Chhattisgarh in writing and shall set out grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal practitioner and may after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks necessary by order either allow or 
disallow the objections.
