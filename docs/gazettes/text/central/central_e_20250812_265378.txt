Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of 2-lane Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 338 at Km. 472/18-20 between Nuzvid - Vatlur stations" as a Special Railway Project for

Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
