S.O. 1351(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 11 of the 
Anusandhan National Research Foundation Act, 2023 (25 of 2023), the President of the Governing Board of 
the Anusandhan National Research Foundation hereby appoints Dr. Shivkumar Kalyanaraman as the Chief 
Executive Officer of the said Foundation for a period of three years with effect from the forenoon of 
17 March, 2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
