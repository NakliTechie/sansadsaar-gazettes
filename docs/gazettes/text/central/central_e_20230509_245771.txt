NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2023 


S.O. 2138(E).—Whereas, M/s. NSL SEZ (Hyderabad) Pvt. Ltd. (formerly, M/s. Topnotch Infrastructure 
Private Limited), had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act) to set up a sector specific Special Economic Zone for Information Technology and 
Information Technology Enabled Services at Sy. No. 1/1, Plot No. 6 IDA Uppal, Ranga Reddy District, in the State of 
Telangana; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area of 14.50 hectares and 
de-notified an area of 10.72 hectares at the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry 
Notification Numbers S.O. 782 (E) dated 187 May, 2007 and S.O. 1858 (E) dated 04" May, 2018, respectively; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government further approved the request of change of name from M/s. 
Topnotch Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. to M/s. NSL SEZ (Hyderabad) Pvt. Ltd. vide letter dated 197 February, 2009; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. NSL SEZ (Hyderabad) Pvt. Ltd. has now proposed for inclusion of additional area 
of 2.19 hectares as a part of above Special Economic Zone and the Central Government after considering the said 
proposal approved and conveyed it on 017 May, 2023; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by second proviso to sub-section (1) of section 4 
of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006, the
