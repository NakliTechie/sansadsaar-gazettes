अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 20 'ए' के खंड (1) प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह समाधान हो जाने के पश्चात

महाराष्ट्र को लिखित मे किया जाएगा और उसके आधार उपवर्णित करेगा और सक्षम प्राधिकारी आक्षेपकर्ता को व्यक्तिगत 
रूप से या विधि व्यवसायी के माध्यम से सुनवाई का अवसर प्रदान करेगा और ऐसे आक्षेपों कि सुनवाई करने तथा ऐसी जांच

Under clause 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 


S.O. 5034(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has been given in schedule

Railway Project, namely “Bhusawal-Badnera-Wardha 377 & 47 Line (313 Kms)” Railway line in the 


district of Amravati in the State of Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication 
of this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the 
aforesaid purpose under sub-section (1) of section 20D of the said Act.

and Land Acquisition Officer, Chandur Railway, Maharashtra in writing and shall set out the grounds 
thereof, and the Competent Authority shall give the objector the opportunity of being heard, either in person 
or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, 
as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act
