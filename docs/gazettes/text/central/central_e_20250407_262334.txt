NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 30th November, 2024 


S.O. 1639(E).— In Exercise of Power conferred by 37A of Section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) 
Act 2008, General Manager/ East Central Railway has approved to declare “Permanent diversion of the fire 
affected Dhanbad to Chandrapura line by Dhanbad - Ispatnagar via Bhuli, Tetulmari, Nichitpur, Matari, Telo 
including Y-connection at Chandrapura (28 Km)” as Special Railway Project. Therefore, “Permanent 
diversion of the fire affected Dhanbad to Chandrapura line by Dhanbad - Ispatnagar via Bhuli, Tetulmari, 
Nichitpur, Matari, Telo including Y-connection at Chandrapura (28 Km)” Project is declared special Railway 
Project in the State of Jharkhand.
