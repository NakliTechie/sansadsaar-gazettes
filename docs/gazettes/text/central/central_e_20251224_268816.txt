के कि.मी. 180.000 से कि.मी.241.000 (ASAT से दहीकोंडा AAA) तक के भूखण्ड के (चौड़ीकरण/पेग्हड शोल्डर सहित

विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

दण्डकारण्य समाचार (अंग्रेजी) एवं कर्मक्रांति (हिन्दी) में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th December, 2025 


5.0. 5995(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4734(E) Dated:17/10/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for Raod (Widening/with paved shuolder Two-Four laning etc), Maintenance, 
management and operation of NH-30(Old NH-43) in the stretch of land from Km. 180.000 to Km. 241.000 (Bedma to 
Dahikonda Section) in the District of Kondagaon in the state of Chhattisgarh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published on the dated 26.10.2025 in 
"Dandkarnay Samachar" (English) and "Karamkranti" (Hindi) ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 


the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
