INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the !0th March, 2025 


F. No. 8-4/592l-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Royal Institute of Paramedical Sciences situated at Darind Nagabal Ganderbal, Ganderbal, 
Distt. Ganderbal 920 Jammu & Kashmir is recognized by State Government of Jammu & Kashmir and Jammu 
& Kashmir State Paramedical & Nursing Council to conduct GNM and B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. l8-l4/592l-INC (56357) dated 09/05/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section |4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to the secretary (Health & Medical Education), Government 
of Jammu 
& Kashmir Room No. 08, Civil Secretariat Srinagar — !8000] was issued. Copy of the notice dated 09/05/2024 was 
also enclosed to Royal Institute of Paramedical Sciences situated at Darind Nagabal Ganderbal, Ganderbal, 
Distt. Ganderbal 9i20l Jammu & Kashmir In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit 
their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Royal Institute of Paramedical Sciences. Even the institution, Royal Institute of Paramedical Sciences has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
0/I/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM 
and B.Sc. (N) Course. 

Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section l4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
26/0/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM and B.Sc(N) from Royal Institute of 


Paramedical Sciences situated at Darind Nagabal Ganderbal, Ganderbal, Distt. Ganderbal 920l Jammu & 
Kashmir shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Jammu & Kashmir.
