MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th May, 2025 


G.S.R. 293(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 418 and section 469 read 
with section 466 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
to amend the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (Recruitment, Salary and other Terms and Conditions of 
Service of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 2020, namely :- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (Recruitment, Salary and other 
Terms and Conditions of Service of Officers and other Employees) Amendment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


2. In the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (Recruitment, Salary and other Terms and Conditions of 
Service of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 2020, in Schedule-I, against serial number 18 relating to the post of 
Stenographer Grade-IIL- 


(a) under column (1), for the entries “Stenographer (1406-17, the entries “Stenographer Grade-I]T” shall be 
substituted; 


(b) under column (6), for the words and figures “Between 18 and 25 years of age”, the words and figures 
“Between 18 and 27 years of age” shall be substituted; 


(०) under column (6), for Note 1, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Note 1 : Relaxable for Government Servants upto forty years in accordance with the instructions or orders 
issued by the Central Government.” ; 


(d) under column (6), for Note 2, the following shall be substituted, namely :- 

“Note 2 : The crucial date for determining the age limit shall be as advertised by the Staff Selection 

Commission.”; 

(e) under column (7), for the words and figures “Transcription : 40 minutes on computer”, the words and 
figures “Transcription : 50 minutes on computer” shall be substituted.
