PUDUVAI BHARATHIAR GRAMA BANK 
(Head Office: Saram) 
NOTIFICATION 
Puducherry, the 20th May, 2024 


No. PBGB/HRM/SR/03/2024.—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 30 of the Regional Rural 
Bank Act, 976 (2 of 976) the Board of Directors of Puduvai Bharathiar Grama Bank after consultation with 
Indian Bank being the Sponsor Bank and the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development and with the 
previous sanction of the Central Government hereby makes the following regulations to amend the Puduvai Bharathiar 
Grama Bank (Officers and Employees) Service Regulations, 20]0, namely:- 


l. (.) These regulations may be called Puduvai Bharathiar Grama Bank Service (Amendment) Regulations, 
2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In regulation 2 of the Puduvai Bharathiar Grama Bank (Officers and Employees) Service Regulations, 
200 (hereinafter referred to as the said regulations) in sub-regulation (), for clause (2), the following clause 
shall be substituted, namely:- 


(g) “Competent Authority” means: 
(i) the Chairman in respect of Officer Scale —II, IV and V; 
(ii) the General Manager in respect of Officer Scale-I and II; and 


(iii) an Officer not below the rank of Scale-IV in respect of employees relating to Group ‘B’ Office 
Assistant (Multipurpose) and Group ‘C’ Office Attendant (Multipurpose) as decided by the 
Board. 


3. In regulation 50 of the said regulations, for clauses (i) and (ii), the following clauses shall be 
substituted, namely:- 


(i) the Board, where the Chairman is Competent Authority; 
(ii) the Chairman, where the General Manager is Competent Authority; and 


(iii) the General Manager, where the Competent Authority is decided by the Board under sub-clause 
(iii) of clause (g) of sub-regulation (l) of regulation 2. 


RANJITH KUMAR K, Chairman
