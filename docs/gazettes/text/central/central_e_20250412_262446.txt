गई थी;

था;

और, सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ड़ की उप-धारा () के अनुसरण में, अपनी रिपोर्ट केंद्रीय

अतः, अब, केंद्रीय सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से उक्त रिपोर्ट के प्राप्त होने पर और उक्त अधिनियम, की धारा 
20 की उप-धारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है की इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में 
विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जित की जाएगी; 


और इसके अतिरिक्त केन्द्रीय सरकार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20३ की उप-धारा (2) के अनुसरण में यह 
घोषणा करती है कि इस अधिसूचना के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित होने पर इससे उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी 
विल्लंगमों से मुक्त होकर, पूर्ण रूप से केन्द्रीय सरकार में निहित हो जाएगी।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the lith April, 2025 


S.O. 706(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway 
(North Western Railway Construction), S.O. No. 862 (A) dated 8 February, 2025 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 8 February , 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 2008 (l! of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for execution, maintenance and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely "Taranga Hill 
—Abu Road Via Ambaji (!6.65 km ) NEW BG LINE Project District-Mehsana in the State of Gujarat. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “ “Sandesh” 
and Divyabhaskar “on date 25 February, 2025 and 26 February, 2025 respectively under sub section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection were received by the Competent Authority within due time. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
