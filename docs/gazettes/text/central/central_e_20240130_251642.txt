NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th January, 2024 


G.S.R. 75(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6 and 25 of the Environment (Protection) 
Act, 986 (29 of 986), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Environment 
(Protection) Rules, !986, namely:— 


l. ({) These rules may be called the Environment (Protection) Amendment Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, in Schedule — I, in serial number 2 relating to Man-made Fibre, under 
heading ‘Environmental Standards for Man-made Fibre Industry’, under sub-heading ‘A. Effluent Standards’ in 
‘note’, after the words “Filament Yarn Units”, the words “except production from continuous spun yarn technology” 
shall be substituted.
