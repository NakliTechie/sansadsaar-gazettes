MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th July, 2023 


S.O. 3379(E).—Whereas the Central Government, in exercise of the power conferred by sub section (.) and 
clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 
of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, issued a notification in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, sub-section (ii), vide number S.O 555(E), dated the 7" February, 
205; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest 
to amend the said notification; 


And whereas sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, !986 provides that whenever it 
appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for 
amending the notification number S.O 555(B), dated the 7" February, 205; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) and clauses (v) and (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the 
Environment (Protection) Rules, !986, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide notification number 8.00 555(E), dated the 
7" February, 205, namely:- 


In the said notification,- 


(i) in paragraph 2, for sub-paragraph (]), the following sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely:- 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


“(]) The State Government shall, prepare a Zonal Master Plan, for the purpose of the Eco-sensitive 
Zone, in consultation with local people and in accordance with this notification, within a period of two 
years from the date of publication of this amendment notification”. 

(ii) in paragraph 3, in sub-paragraph (|]), the words, “approved by the Ministry of Environment, Forests 
and Climate Change”, shall be omitted; 

(iii) in paragraph 5, in sub-paragraph (3), after the words, “Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate 
Change”, the words “or the State Environment Impact Assessment Authority, Goa, as the case may 
be,” shall be inserted.
