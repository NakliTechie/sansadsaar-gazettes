for PERMANENT removal of the name of aforesaid CA. Siva Prasad Pulavarthi (Membership No. 204076) from 
the Register of Members and also imposed a fine of Rs. 5,00,000/-(Rupess Five Lakhs only). Aggrieved by Order of

wherein an interim stay was granted vide Order dated 25" February, 20!2. However, the Appellate Authority vide its 
Order dated 57 September, 202 dismissed the appeal and upheld the impugned Order of the Disciplinary Committee. 
In compliance of the same, the Respondent had deposited a demand draft of Rs. 5,00,000/-(Rupees Five Lakhs only) 
in favour of ICAI against the fine imposed upon him in the matter. Aggrieved by the said Order, CA. Siva Prasad 
Pulavarthi (Membership No. 204076) filed a Writ Petition bearing no. 553/203, before the Hon’ble High Court of 
Delhi, wherein an interim stay was granted vide Order dated 8' February, 203. However, the said Writ Petition was 
dismissed by the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi vide its Order dated 247 May, 2023, on account of non-prosecution. In 
pursuance thereof and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is 
hereby notified under Regulation l8 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 988 that the name of 
CA. Siva Prasad Pulavarthi (Membership No. 204076), shall stand PERMANENTLY removed from the 
Register of Members with effect from 277 December, 2023.
