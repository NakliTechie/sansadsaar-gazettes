Subject: - Amendment in Export Policy of Non-basmati rice under HS Code 0063090. 


S.O. 3838(E).— The Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 
of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 992 (No. 22 of 992), as amended, read with Para |.02 and 
2.0I of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, hereby in relaxation of Notification No.20/2023 dated 20" July, 2023 allows 
export of non-basmati white rice under any of the following conditions: 


i. The Notification No.20/2023 [S.O. 3249(E)] dated 20" July, 2023, was published in the Gazette of India at 
2:57:0! hours on 20.07.2023. Therefore, Para 2 (iii) of Notification No.20/2023 [S.O. 3249(E)] dated 20" July, 2023 
is amended as under: 


“where Non-basmati rice consignment has been handed over to the Customs before 2:57:0! hours on 
20.07.2023 and is registered in Customs system or where Non-basmati rice consignment has entered the 
Customs Station for exportation before 2:57:0l hours on 20.07.2023 and is registered in the electronic 
systems of the concerned Custodian of the Customs Station with verifiable evidence of date and time 
stamping of these commodities having entered the Customs Station prior to 2:57:0l hours on 20.07.2023. 
The period of export shall be upto 30.0.2023. 


ii. Export duty is paid before 2:57:0] hours on 20.07.2023. 


2, For removal of doubts, wherever the words/phrase “before this Notification” appears in the Notification 
No.20/2023 [S.O. 3249(E)] dated 20" July, 2023, the same shall mean “before 2:57:0l hours on 20.07.2023” 


3. Effect of this Notification: 

In relaxation of Notification No.20/2023 dated 20" July, 2023 export of non-basmati white rice is allowed 
when export duty is paid before 2:57:0] hours on 20.07.2023. Para 2 (iii) of Notification No.20/2023 dated 20" July, 
2023 is amended to specify the date and time of effect of the Notification No.20/2023 dated 20" July, 2023.
