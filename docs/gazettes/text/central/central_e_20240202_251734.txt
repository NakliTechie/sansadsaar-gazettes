NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2nd February, 2024 


S.O. 420(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (21 of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer resources 
relating to the Mutual Fund System and its Database, being Critical Information Infrastructure of the Computer Age 
Management Services Limited, and the computer resources of their associated dependencies, to be protected systems 
for the purposes of the said Act and authorises the following persons to access the protected systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the Computer Age Management Services Limited authorised in writing by 
the Computer Age Management Services Limited to access the protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have been 
authorised in writing by the Computer Age Management Services Limited for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing by the 
Computer Age Management Services Limited on case to case basis. 


2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
