ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
DIRECTION 
New Delhi, the 20th March, 2024 


O.N. 7I(E).— Whereas, Section 6lA of the Representation of the People Act, 95, provides that the giving 
and recording of votes by voting machines in such manner as may be prescribed, may be adopted in such constituency 
or constituencies as the Election Commission of India may, having regard to the circumstances of each case, specify; 


2; Whereas, as per the proviso to Rule 49A of the Conduct of Elections Rules, !96, a printer with a drop box 
of such design, as may be approved by the Election Commission of India, may also be attached to voting machine for 
printing a paper trail of the vote, in such constituency or constituencies or parts thereof as the Election Commission of 
India may direct; 


3. Whereas, the Election Commission has considered the circumstances in all the Parliamentary 
Constituencies of Lok Sabha, all the Assembly Constituencies of Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Odisha 
& Sikkim at the current general elections, 2024 and in 26 Assembly Constituencies (as per Annexure-) in 3 
States at the current bye-elections, announced vide the Election Commission’s Press Notes No. ECI/PN/23/2024 
& ECI/PN/24/2024 both dated 6", March 2024, and is satisfied that sufficient number of Electronic Voting 
Machines (EVMs) and Printers for printing Paper Trail [Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail(VVPAT)] are available for 
taking the poll in all the Parliamentary Constituencies and all the Assembly Constituencies of Andhra Pradesh, 
Arunachal Pradesh, Odisha and Sikkim and in 26 Assembly Constituencies (as per Annexure-l) of 3 States. The 
polling personnel are well trained in efficient handling of EVMs and ‘VVPAT Printers’ and the electors are also fully 
conversant with the operation of the EVMs and the VVPAT Printers; 


4. Now, therefore, the Election Commission of India, in exercise of its powers under the said Section 6IA of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95, and Rule 49A of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 96l, hereby specifies all 
the Parliamentary Constituencies of the Lok Sabha, all the Assembly Constituencies of the legislative 
assemblies of Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Odisha & Sikkim at the current General Elections, 2024 
and 26 Assembly Constituencies (as per Annexure-l) in 3 states at the current bye-election, as per the 
schedule announced by the Election Commission on 6" March, 2024, as the constituencies in which the votes, 
shall be given and recorded by means of EVMs and VVPAT printers in the manner prescribed, under the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96l, and the supplementary instructions issued by the Election Commission from time to time on 
the subject. 


5. The Election Commission also hereby approves the design of EVMs and VVPAT Printers as developed by 
the Bharat Electronics Ltd., Bangalore and Electronics Corporation of India Ltd., Hyderabad, which shall be attached 
to the said machines, to be used for the giving and recording of votes in all the Constituencies.
