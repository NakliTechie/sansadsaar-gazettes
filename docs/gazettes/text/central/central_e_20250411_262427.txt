NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10 April, 2025 
S.O. 1694(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Doubling between Aurangabad and Parbhani stations (177.29Km)" as a 
Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Maharashtra in the Districts of Aurangabad, Jalna and 
Parbhani for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this 


notification in the Official Gazette.
