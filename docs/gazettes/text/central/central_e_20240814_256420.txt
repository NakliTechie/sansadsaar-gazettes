INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd July, 2024 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


F.No. 18-18/4897-INC:—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Granthamm College of Nursing situated at Modi Kheria Mau Road Bada Gaon Morar 
Gwalior, Distt. Gwalior 474006, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and 
Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/4897-INC dated 22/09/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya 
Pradesh Room No. 241, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 22/09/2022 was also enclosed to Granthamm College of Nursing situated at Modi Kheria 
Mau Road Bada Gaon Morar Gwalior, Distt. Gwalior 474006, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Granthamm College of Nursing. Even the institution, Granthamm College of Nursing has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
16/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run Post 
Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic B.Sc(N) from Granthamm College of 
Nursing situated at Modi Kheria Mau Road Bada Gaon Morar Gwalior, Distt. Gwalior 474006, Madhya 
Pradesh, shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to orders of state government. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secretary 
Indian Nursing Council
