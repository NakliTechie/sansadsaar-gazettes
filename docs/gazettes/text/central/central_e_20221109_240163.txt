AADHAAR (ENROLMENT AND UPDATE) (TENTH AMENDMENT) 
REGULATIONS, 2022 
(No. 6 of 2022) 


No. HQ-16027/1/2022-EU-I- HQ (No. 6 of 2022).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (a) of 
sub-section (2) of Section 54 read with Section 23(2)(a) of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other 
Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act 2016(18 of 2016) the Unique Identification Authority of India, hereby makes 
the following regulations to further amend the Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) Regulations, 2016 (No.2 of 2016), 


namely:- 


[भाग वा---खण्ड 4] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Part I 
PRELIMINARY 


1 . Short title and commencement.— (1) These regulations may be called the Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) 
(Tenth Amendment) Regulations, 2022 (No. 6 of 2022). 


(2) These regulations shall come into force with effect from the date of publication of this notification. 
Part II 


2. Amendment of Regulation 16. - In Regulation 16 of the principal Regulation, for the words “in accordance with 
update procedure specified in this Chapter”, the words “in accordance with update procedure as specified by the 
Authority from time to time” shall be substituted. 


3. Insertion of new Regulation 164. — After Regulation 16 of the principal Regulation, the following new Regulation 
shall be inserted, namely:- 


“16A. Update of Documents. — Aadhaar number holders may, on completion of every 10 years from the date of 
enrolment for Aadhaar, update their supporting documents in Aadhaar, at least once, by submitting Proof of Identity 
(POD and Proof of Address (POA) documents as specified under Aadhaar Enrolment and Update Regulation 10 
hereinabove, so as to ensure continued accuracy of their information in the Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR), 
in such manner as may be specified by the Authority from time to time.” 


Dr. SAURABH GARG, Chief Executive Officer
