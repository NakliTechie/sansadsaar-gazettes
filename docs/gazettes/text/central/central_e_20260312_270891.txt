New Delhi, the 12th March, 2026 


G.S.R. 175(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (c), (f), (0), (i) and 
(s) of sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011, namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement— (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (General) Third 
Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011, rules 16, 21 and 21A shall be omitted. 


[F. No. I-9/1/2026-W&M] 
ANUPAM MISHRA, Addl. Secy.
