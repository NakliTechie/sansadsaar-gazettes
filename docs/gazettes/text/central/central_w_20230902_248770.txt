ORDER 
New Delhi, the 25th July, 2023 


O.N. 134.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala, 2021 for 112- Ranni 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/KL- 
LA/2021, dated 12'* March, 2021; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the election for 112- Ranni Assembly Constituency of Kerala, 2021 was declared by 
the Returning Officer on 0200 May, 2021 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election Expenses was 01% 
June, 2021; and 


WHEREAS, the Commission having regard to the then prevailing pandemic situation of Covid-19, decided to 
condone any incidence of late submission of the account by candidate till 31* July, 2021; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report of District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta under sub-rule (1) of Rule 89 of 
the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 received through the CEO, Kerala vide his letter no. EL1/2/2021-ELEC, dated 
24.08.2021 stating that Aji B. Ranni, a contesting candidate from 112- Ranni Assembly Constituency in General 
Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala, 2021 has failed to lodge his account of election expenses, as required 
under law; and 


WHEREAS, on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta, a Show-Cause 
Notice No. 76/KL-LA/2021/CEMS-II, dated 06" October, 2021 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Aji B. Ranni, for not lodging the account of 
Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 1961, Aji B. Ranni was directed to submit his representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his Election Expenses with the 
District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta in his report no. El-266504/2020; dated 
22.11.2021 reported that in the absence of Aji B. Ranni, the Tahsildar & ERO of Ranni LAC pasted the notice on the 
wall of his house and got the signature of two witnesses on 30.10.2021; and 


WHEREAS, the candidate has represented to the Commission vide his e-mail dated 19" October, 2022 and 
has requested to grant him a few days more time to submit the account of his election expenditure; and 


Accordingly vide letter no. 76/KL-LA/2021/CEMS-II; dated 02" March 2023 the Commission has forwarded 
his representation to the CEO, Kerala to obtain the remarks of District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta and submit 
Supplementary Report as to whether the candidate has lodged the account of his election expenses and a copy of 
which also marked to the District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta as well as the candidate Sh. Aji B. Ranni. 


WHEREAS, the candidate Aji B. Ranni vide his e-mail dated 24" April, 2023 sent only Abstract Statement of 
election expenditure of Rs.1,36,256/- spent on various items without accompanied by the vouchers related to election 
expenditure as required under Rule 86 (2) of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, Candidate Registers & Affidavits 
etc.; and 


WHEREAS, the Commission vide his letter no. 76/KL-LA/2021/CEMS-II; dated 12" June, 2023 directed Aji 
B. Ranni to comply with the Rule 86(1) to (4) of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 and to submit the Day to Day 
Account Register, Cash Register, Bank Register, Abstract Statement and accompanied by expenditure Vouchers with 
the District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta within 25 days of issue of letter and also requested District Election 
Officer, Pathanamthitta to submit a Supplementary Report indicating as to whether the defaulting candidate has 
fulfilled the requirements as desired or not; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Pathanamthitta in his Report no. E1-266504/2020; dated 
07.07.2023 informed that Aji B. Ranni has not submitted the account of his election expenses as prescribed under 
law; and 


WHEREAS, Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 provides that:- 
“Tf the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act, and

(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, 


the Election Commission shall, by order published in the Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and 
any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the order.”’; 


WHEREAS, on basis of the facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Aji B. Ranni has 
failed to lodge his account of election expenses in the prescribed manner and has no good reason or justification for 
the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the

three years from the date of this order.
