NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 5th December, 2024 


S.O. 5286(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (373) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 989 
(24 of 989) the Central Government hereby notifies “Babupeth — Sasti New Rail Line (3.4 km)” as Railway 
project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Chandrapur to 
Babupeth railway station located on Wardha (Sewagram) - Ballarshah section, for providing national 
infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
