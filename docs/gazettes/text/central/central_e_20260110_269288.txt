NOTIFICATION 
Bengaluru, the 10th January, 2026 


5.0. 159(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20 A of the Railway Act 1989 
(24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government (Ministry of Railways, South Western 
Railway, Construction Organisation, Bengaluru Cantonment) after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, 
the brief description of which has been given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for the execution of the 
Special railway project, for Traffic Facilities and capacity Augmentation works (umbrella works) 2023-24 Freight 
Terminal development at Sasalu station yard in between Mayakonda and Chikkajajur stations at Ch: 287/774 - 
289/717 in the state of KARNATAKA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within Thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section (1) of Section 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority and Assistant Commissioner and Land 
Acquisition Officer, Chitradurga Sub-Division, Chitradurga, land acquisition for Traffic Facilities and capacity 
Augmentation works (umbrella works) 2023-24 Freight Terminal development at Sasalu station yard in between 
Mayakonda and Chikkajajur stations in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, 
either allow or disallow the objections: 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of Section 20 D of the said Act shall 
be final: 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid Office of the Competent Authority and Assistant Commissioner and Land 
Acquisition Officer, Chitradurga Sub-Division, Chitradurga.
