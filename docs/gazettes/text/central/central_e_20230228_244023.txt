Mumbai , the 28th February , 2023 

S.O. 914 ( E ) .– In exercise of the powers conferred by clause 37A of Section 2 of the Railways ( Amendment ) 
Act , 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “ Gauge Conversion of Dabhoi Samalaya ( 46.45 Km . ) including 
Samalaya Railway fly over bridge ( 8.40 Km . ) and Lotana bye pass line ( 7.55 Km . ) " as a Special Railway Project for 
acquisition of land in the State of Gujarat , for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from 
the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette .
