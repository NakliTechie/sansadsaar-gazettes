New Delhi, the 28th November, 2024 

INCOME-TAX 


S.O. 52(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the fifth proviso to section l94N of the Income-tax 
Act, 96l (43 of 96l) (hereafter in this notification referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after 
consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, hereby specifies that the provisions of section 94ीप of the said Act shall 
not apply to Foreign Representations duly approved by the Ministry of External Affairs of the Government of India 
including Diplomatic Missions, agencies of the United Nations, International Organisations, Consulates and Offices 
of Honorary Consuls which are exempt from paying taxes in India as per the Diplomatic Relations 
(Vienna Convention) Act 972 (43 of 972) and the United Nations (Privileges and Immunities) Act 947 
(46 of 947). 


2D The notification shall be deemed to have come into force with effect from the 7 day of December, 2024.
