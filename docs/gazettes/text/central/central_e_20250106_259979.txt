NOTIFICAION 
New Delhi, the Ist January, 2025 


G.S.R. 12(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (b) and (०) of sub- 
section (2) of section 141 of the Border Security Force Act,1968 (47 of 1968), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following rules to amend the Ministry of Home Affairs, Border Security Force,(Combatised Stenographers Cadre), 
Group ‘A’ and Group ‘B’ Posts, Recruitment Rules, 2019, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. -(1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Home Affairs, Border Security 
Force, (Combatised Stenographers Cadre), Group ‘A’ and Group ‘B’ Posts, Recruitment (Amendment) 
Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Ministry of Home Affairs, Border Security Force, (Combatised Stenographers Cadre), Group ‘A’ and 
Group ‘B’ Posts, Recruitment Rules, 2019, - 


(a) inrule 5, for the words “fifty-seven years” wherever they occur, the words “sixty years” shall be 
substituted ; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


(b) in the Schedule, - 


(i) against serial number 2 relating to the post of Assistant Commandant (Personal Secretary), 
in column (11),- 


(A) under the heading “By Promotion”, for the words “three years”, the words “five years” shall be 
substituted; 


(B) after the heading “By Promotion” and the entries relating thereto, the following note shall be inserted, 
namely: - 


“Notel: The eligibility service for the incumbents holding the post of Inspector (Personal 
Assistant) on regular basis on the date of commencement of the Ministry of Home Affairs, 
Border Security Force, (Combatised Stenographers Cadre), Group ‘A’ and Group ‘B’ Posts, 
Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2025 shall be three years of regular service in the grade”; 


(C) _ the Note shall be numbered as “Note 2” thereof; 


(ii) against serial number 3 relating to the post of Inspector (Personal Assistant), in column (11), 
in Note 3, for the figures and word “52 years”, the words “fifty-six years” shall be substituted; 


(iii) against serial number 4 relating to the post of Sub-Inspector(Steno), in column (11) in Note 3, 
for the figures and word “52 years”, the words “fifty-six years” shall be substituted.
