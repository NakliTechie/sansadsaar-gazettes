पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उप-धारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह समाधान 
हो जाने के पश्चात्‌ कि राजस्थान राज्य के चूरू जिले में राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग - 709 विस्तार के कि.मी. 0.00 से कि.मी. 5.450

वह भूमि अपेक्षित है जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की 
घोषणा करती है।

प्रयोजन के लिए ऐसी भूमि के उपयोग पर राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से Sahat दिन के भीतर अपनी 
आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा |

और उसमें उसके आधार अधिकथित किए जाएंगे और सक्षम प्राधिकारी, आपत्तिकर्ता को व्यक्तिगत रूप में या किसी विधिक 
पेशेवर द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आपत्तियों की सुनवाई के पश्चात्‌ तथा ऐसी और जाँच करने के पश्चात्‌, यदि कोई

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th June, 2024 


S.O. 2436(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building two 
lane with paved shoulder configuration Rajgarh bypass from km 0.00 to km 5.50 & Pilani Bypass from km. 36.50 
to km. 42.850 maintenance, management and operation of NH- 709 Extn in the District of Churu in the State of 
Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, SDM in writing and shall set out the 
grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or 
by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the 
Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
