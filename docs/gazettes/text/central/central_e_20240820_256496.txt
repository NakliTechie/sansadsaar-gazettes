MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(Department of Higher Education) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th August, 2024 


G.S.R. 512(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (2) of section 44 of the 
Architects Act, 1972 (20 of 1972), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the 
Council of Architecture Rules, 1973, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.-(1) These rules may be called the Council of Architecture (Amendment) 
Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Council of Architecture Rules, 1973 (hereinafter referred to as principal rules), in rule 22A,- 


(a) In sub-rule (1), for the words 10 file the nomination to contest the election in respect of vacancy 
in”, the words “to be nominated by” shall be substituted; 


(b) In sub-rule (3), for the words “file nomination”, the words “be nominated” shall be substituted. 


3. In the principal rules, in rule 22B, in sub-rule (2), for the word “applications”, the word “applicants” shall be 
substituted.
