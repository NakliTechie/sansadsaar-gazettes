Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby authorises the Director General, Medical Commissioner, 
Insurance Commissioner, Additional Commissioner, Regional Director Grade ‘A’/Director, Deputy Medical 
Commissioner, Dean, Director (Medical), Medical Superintendent, Regional Director Grade ‘B’/Joint Director, Deputy 
Director, Assistant Director or Manager Grade-I, Inspector-cum-Facilitator or Social Security Officer or Manager 
Grade-Il or Superintendent of the Corporation to institute in the name of the Corporation, suits and other legal 
proceedings necessary in the interest of the Corporation and to defend any such proceedings instituted against the 
Corporation in all courts or tribunals or any other authority as may be specified including those established under the 
said Code.
