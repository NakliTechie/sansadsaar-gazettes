MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd December, 2022 


S.O. 644(E).— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas, number $.O. 3770 (E) dated the 04" August 2022, issued under sub-section (l) of section (3) of the 
Petroleum & Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act,!962 (50 of 962) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the right of user in the land, specified in the 
Schedule appended to that notification where a pipeline was laid for transportation of natural gas from the sources in 
the State of Andhra Pradesh of M/s Reliance Industries Limited, to various consumers in the country by M/s Reliance 
Gas Transportation Infrastructure Limited, now known as Pipeline Infrastructure Limited. 


And whereas, the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public on the 2" August 
2022; 


And whereas no objection received from the public for acquisition of right of user for the said pipeline had 
been considered and disposed by the Competent Authority: 


And whereas the Competent Authority has under sub-section () of section (6) of the said Act, submitted 
report to the Central Government for acquisition of Right of User in lands appended to the Schedule; 


And whereas the Central Government, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for the pipeline laid, has decided to acquire the right of user therein; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 6 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the right of user in the land specified in the Schedule, appended to this 
notification, are hereby acquired; 


And, further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby directs that the right of user in the said land shall instead of vesting in the Government of India, 
vest, on the date of the publication of the declaration, in M/s Reliance Gas Transportation Infrastructure Limited, now 
known as Pipeline Infrastructure Limited, free from encumbrances.
