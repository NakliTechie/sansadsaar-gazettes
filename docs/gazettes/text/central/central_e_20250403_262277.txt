सरकार, रेल मंत्रालय (रेलवे बोर्ड) में प्रकाशित, का.आ. संख्या 2870(ई), तारीख 48 जुलाई, 2024 (जिसे इसमें इसके पश्चात उक्त

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 2nd April, 2025 


S.O. 573(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S$.0.2870(E) dated the 8" of July, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3, sub-section (ii) (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (i) of section 
20(A) of the Rialways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereafter referred as in as the said Act), Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Rail Over Rail (ROR) double line 
between Guntakal West — Mallappa Gate in the State of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Sakshi 
(Telugu) and The Hans India (English)” on 30th July, 2024 by P. V. Sai Prasad, Chief Engineer-I, Construction 
Organization, Secunderabad under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Chippagiri and Doulathapuram villages of Kurnool district, certain objections were 
received by the Revenue Divisional Officer and Competent Authority for Land Acquisition, Pattikonda (AP) which 
have been rectified. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances:
