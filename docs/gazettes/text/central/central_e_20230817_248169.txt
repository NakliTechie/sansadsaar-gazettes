S.O. 368(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Culture 


(Archaeological Survey of India) number S.O. 262(E), dated the 050 May, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 06" May, 2023, the Central Government gave two months' 
notice of its intention to declare the Kapileswar Temple, Kapileswar, District Khurda, State Odisha specified in the 
Site Plan and the Schedule annexed to the said notification to be an ancient monument of national importance and a 
copy of the said notification was affixed in a conspicuous place near the said Kapileswar Temple, as required by sub 
section (l) of section 4 of the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 958 (24 of 958); 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And whereas, copies of the aforesaid notification were made available to the public on the 06" May, 2023; 


And whereas, no objection has been received from the public to the making of such declaration by the 
Central Government; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 4 of the said Act, the 
Central Government hereby declares that the Kapileshwar Temple, District Khurda, State Odisha, specified in the 
schedule and site plan annexed hereto, to be an ancient monument of national importance.
