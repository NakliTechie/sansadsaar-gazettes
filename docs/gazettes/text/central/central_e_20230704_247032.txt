G.S.R. 477(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section |2 read with clause (e) of sub-section (2) of

with the approval of the Central Government, hereby makes the following regulations regulating the method of 
recruitment to the post of Director in the Khuda Bakhsh Oriental Public Library, Patna, namely : — 


l. Short title and commencement. — () This regulation may be called the Ministry of Culture, Khuda Bakhsh 
Oriental Public Library, Patna, Director, Recruitment Regulation, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication of the Official Gazette. 


2. Number of post, classification, level in the pay matrix. — The number of post, its classification, level in the pay 
matrix attached thereto, shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the Schedule annexed to these regulation. 


3. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc. - The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications and 
other matters relating thereto, shall be as specified in columns (5) to (3) of the aforesaid Schedule. 


4. Disqualification. — No person, - 


(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, or
