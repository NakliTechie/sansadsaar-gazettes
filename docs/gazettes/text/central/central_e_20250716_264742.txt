New Delhi, 14th July, 2025 


PNGRB/Fin/8-OC(1)/2018 (P-3264):—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (g) of sub-section (2) 


of section 61 read with clause (g) of section 11 of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006 (19 of 
2006), the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board hereby makes the following regulations, further to amend the 
Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Levy of Fee and Other Charges) Regulations, 2007, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement: 


(1) These regulations may be called the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Levy of Fee and Other 
Charges) Amendment Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board (Levy of Fee and Other Charges) Regulations, 2007:— 


(a) in regulation 3, in table, S. No. 12, S. No. 13 and Note, shall be substituted as under:
