MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Western Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 16th June, 2023 


9.0. 2666(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.0 4609 (E) dated the 30 September 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 30 September 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20 (A) of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the 
said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, 
namely Nimach-Ratlam Doubling Project in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar (Hindi)” & “Malav Darshan (Hindi)” on 12 October 2022 by Sub Divisional Officer and Competent 
Authority, Nimach (M.P.) under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said act; 


And whereas, for Hingoriya and Jamuniyakalan village of Nimach District, no objection received to the Sub 
Divisional Officer and competent Authority, Nimach (M.P.); 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
