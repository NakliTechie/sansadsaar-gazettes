MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th June, 2024 


S.O. 2409(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 23 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 
(29 of 1986), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the notification of the Government 
of India in the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change number S.O. 2340(E), dated the 16" June, 2021, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-ll, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), namely:- 


In the said notification,— 


(1) in paragraph 1, for the words “private laboratories”, the words “laboratories or institutes as environmental 
laboratories” shall be substituted; 


(1) for paragraph 3, the following paragraph shall be substituted; namely:— 

“3. The recognition of laboratory shall be granted for a period of three years”; 
(iii) paragraph 4 shall be omitted; 
(iv) for paragraph 7, the following paragraph shall be substituted, namely:— 


“7. (1) The recognised laboratory shall participate in an all inter-laboratory comparison exercise conducted 
by the Central Pollution Control Board and a proficiency testing programme conducted by the proficiency 
testing provider certified under ISO/IEC: 17043, covering all the parameters under recognition, within two 
years from the date of recognition. 


(2) The Central Pollution Control Board shall make available the records and reports of the exercise in the 
public domain. 


(3) The recognised laboratory shall pay the applicable fee for the conduct of the exercise and the 
programme.”. 


[F. No. Q-15018/13/2020-CPW] 
NARESH PAL GANGWAR, Addl. Secy. 


Note.— The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part ll, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) vide number S.O. 2340(B), dated the 16th June, 2021.
