G.S.R. 25(E). — In exercise of the powers conferred by section 32 of the Tobacco Board Act, 
975 (4 of 975), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Tobacco 
Board Rules, 976, namely: — 
l. Short title and commencement. — (l) These Rules may be called the Tobacco Board (Amendment) 
Rules, 2025. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Tobacco Board Rules, 976 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 33, for sub- 
rules(5),(6) and (7), the following sub-rules shall be substituted, namely :-

“(5) Every certificate of registration granted under section 0 shall be in Form 2 and shall be valid 
for a block period of three years specified in such certificate. 
(6) The renewal of certificate of registration shall be for a block period of three years, at a time, 
endorsed on such certificate. 
(7) Every certificate of registration or renewal of registration shall be valid only for the block 
period for which it was granted. 
(8) The fee to be paid for the area covered under the certificate of registration as prescribed under 
sub-rule (4) shall be as per the criteria for registration or renewal of registration laid down by the 
committee constituted under sub-rule (2) of rule 33A.”. 
3. In the said rules, in rule 34N, for sub-rules (2) and (3), the following sub-rules shall be substituted, 
namely: - 
“(2) The licence obtained for operation of a barn has to be renewed for every block period of three 
years and unless so renewed, shall cease to be effective on the expiry of the block period of three 
years for which it is granted. The application for such renewal shall be made in the same manner as 
that for fresh application. 
(3) Every application for licence or for renewal for licence for operation of a barn shall be in Form 
25 and shall contain all the particulars specified therein and shall also be accompanied by a fee of 
rupees ten per year for every barn.”
