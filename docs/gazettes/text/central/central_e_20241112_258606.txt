MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2th November, 2024 

S.O. 4886(E)— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 2307(E) Dated:4/06/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified 
in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning 
etc.,), Maintenance, management and operation of NH - 63, 4 & 2/8 in the stretch of land from Km 35.650 to 
36.450 Ankola-Gooty Road on NH 63 passing through Hubballi City, from Km 249.00 to 250.050 Old Pune- 
Bangalore Road on NH4 and from km 93.800 to 93.350 (Ranichennamma Circle) in the district of DHARWAD 
in the state of KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Times of India” and “Samyukta 


Karnataka” both dated 30-06-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (I) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 


specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
