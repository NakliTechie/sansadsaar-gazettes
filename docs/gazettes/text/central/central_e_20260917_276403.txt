5.0. 506(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (Railway Board), number, $.0.3224(E), dated the 4th June, 2026 published in Gazette of India vide 
Gazette )२०.३।0] Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-Section (ii), dated the l8th June, 2026 and issued 
under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for the Purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special 
Railway Project namely, “Construction of Gooty TSS in Guntakal —Kadapa section for Up-gradation work of 
Electric Traction System from x25kV to 2x25KV to meet 3000 MT loading target ” in Chetnepalli village & 
Gooty Mandal in Ananthapuramu District in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily newspapers namely 
the “Sakshi (Ananthapur Main)” (Telugu) and Deccan chronicle” (English), dated 23 and 24" of june, 2026 
with the approval of H.K. Bhagoria, Chief Engineer, Construction-I, South Central Railway, Secunderabad. 


And, whereas, NO objections were received and same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority and accordingly orders have been passed And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () 


of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the Said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 


that the lands specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the
