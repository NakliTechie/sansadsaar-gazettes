NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |2th May, 2026 

S.O. 2440(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4879(E) Dated: 24/0/2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of Building (widening/four-laning, etc.) Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH 55 Widening and Strengthening to 2L+PS including LA of Balikuda-Mulakani from Km. 38.00 to 
Km. 353.45 in the district of Jagatsinghpur in the state of ODISHA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “PRATIDINA” dated 3/03/2026, 
“SAMAJA” dated 3/03/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
