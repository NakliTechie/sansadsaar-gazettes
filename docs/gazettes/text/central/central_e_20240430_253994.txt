NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 30th April, 2024 


S.O. 874(E)—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of ROB in lieu of LC No. 23 between 
Jammikunta — Bisugirsharif stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in 
the District of Karimnagar for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
