Bilaspur, the 23rd December, 2025 
NOTIFICATION UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT 989 

5.0. 5964(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, In the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) S.O. number 4630(E), dated 09/0/2025, published in the Gazette of India (Extra-Ordinary) Part-II 
section 3 sub section (ii), (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under subsection () of 20A of the railway 
act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the central Government declared its intention to acquire 
the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of Special Railway Project, namely 
“Bilaspur Division - Construction of RUB in lieu of LC No. 305 at Km. 60/9-2 ROB Yard. (Box pushing 
method).” in the state of Chhattisgarh. 

And, whereas, the substance of the said notification Number 4630(E), dated 09/0/2025, was published in the 
daily newspapers, "Navin Kadam” (Raigarh), (Hindi) & “Central Chronicle” (Raipur), (English) on 0‘‘ November, 
2025 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act; 

And whereas, no objection was received within the set time period. 

And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted his report to the Central Government; 

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section I) of Section 20E of the said Act, 989, the Central Government hereby declares that the 
lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 

that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall 

vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances,
