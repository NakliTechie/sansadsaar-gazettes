G.S.R. 64(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section llC of the Mines and Minerals 
(Development and Regulation) Act, !957 (67 of 957), the Central Government hereby makes the following further 
amendments in the First Schedule to the said Act, namely:- 


In The First Schedule to the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 957,- 
(i) in PART A, in item I, for the word "Coal", the words "Coal, including Coking Coal" shall be substituted; and

"3A. Coking Coal.".
