New Delhi, the l6th March, 2023 
99.0. 362(E).—In pursuance of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Aadhaar 
(Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 206, l8 of 20l6 and read with rule 
5 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020,, the 
Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying (Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying), having 
been authorised by the Central Government, hereby notifies that the Animal Welfare Board of India is allowed to 


perform Aadhaar authentication, on voluntary basis, for “AWBI Online Portal’ (www.awbi.gov.in) for the following 
purposes namely:- 


(i) The online Public Grievance Redressal and addressing the complaints of cruelty towards animals; 


Gi) The processing of applications for recognition and renewal of recognition of Animal Welfare 
Organisations or Gaushalas or Non-governmental organizations and the like working across the country 
for the welfare of animals; 


(iii) The processing of applications for providing Financial assistance to recognised Animal Welfare 
Organisations or Gaushalas or Non-governmental organizations or Society for Prevention of Cruelty to 
Animals or State Government under various schemes namely:- 


(a) regular and rescue Cattle Grant; 


(b) scheme for provision of shelter houses for looking after the animals;

(0) scheme for provision of ambulance services to animals in distress; 

(d) scheme for birth control and immunization of stray dogs; and 

(e) scheme for relief to animals during natural calamities and unforeseen circumstances; 
(iv) For processing of applications under performing animal (Registration) rules, 200I.- 

(a) animal owner registration and its renewal; 


(b) pre-shoot or post-shoot permission for the use of performing animals in Film or Ad-film or TV 
serials etc. 


(c) registration of race horses or polo ponies and its renewal; 

(d) registration of performing animals to be used in circuses and its renewal; 
(e) registration of performing animals in events or dog shows; 

(f) registration of elephants as performing animals in safari rides; 


(v) The processing of applications for the nomination of Honourary Animal Welfare representatives (formerly 
known as Honourary Animal Welfare Officer); 


(vi) The processing of applications for the authorisation as Colony Animal Care Taker; and 


(vii) The humane education or training for creating awareness in the society for treating the animals with kindness 
and compassion. 


2. The Animal Welfare Board of India shall adhere to the guidelines with respect to use of Aadhaar authentication as 
laid down by the Central Government.
