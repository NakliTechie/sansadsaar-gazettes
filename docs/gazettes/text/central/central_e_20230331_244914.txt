MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th March, 2023 


S.O. 1536(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number 8.00. 5512 (E), dated the 247 November, 2022 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub — section (ii), dated the 28" November, 2022 and issued under sub-section (1) 
of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexure to the said notification for 
the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, Western 
Dedicated Freight Corridor in district of Rewari in the State of Haryana; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily English newspapers “The 
Indian Express” New Delhi dated the 167 December, 2022 and Hindi newspaper “Dainik Bhaskar” Rewari dated the 
16" December, 2022 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, six objections have been received and the same have been considered and disposed off by the 
Competent Authority; 


And where as, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted his report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority namely, Chief Revenue officer Rewari 
district Rewari state of Haryana and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the 
Railways Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be 
acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
