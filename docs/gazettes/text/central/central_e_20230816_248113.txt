WHEREAS under the provisions of article 82 and article 70(3) of the Constitution, as amended by the Constitution

was deferred by Presidential Orders issued in terms of section I0A of the Delimitation Act, 2002 vide S.O. 283-286, dated 087 
February, 2008;

respect of all States, except Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur and Nagaland, was published vide notification number 
S.0.382(E), dated !9th February, 2008;

circumstances that led to the deferring of the delimitation exercise in the State of Assam had ceased to exist, was pleased to rescind 
the notification number S.O. 283(E), dated the 8th February, 2008 so as to readjust the division of the State of Ass am into territorial 
constituencies for the purpose of elections to the House of the People and to the State Legislative Assembly; 


AND WHEREAS the Election Commission was requested to conduct the delimitation of parliamentary and assembly 
constituencies in the State of Assam as per section 8A of the Representation of the People Act, !950 (43 of 950);
