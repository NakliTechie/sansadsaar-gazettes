MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 2nd August, 2023 


5.0. 3470(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 8.00 1874 (E) dated the 25th April 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 25th April 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued 
undersub-section (1) of section 20 (A) of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway (amendment) Act 2008 (11 
of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), theCentral Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management 
and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Khandwa Akola Gauge Conversion (MG to BG) in the State of 
Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Bhaskar (लावा)? & “Patrika (Hind1)” on Sth May 2023 by Hemant Kumar Bhagoria Chief Engineer-V, Construction 
Organization Secunderabad under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Khodari village of Burhanpur District, 02 objection received to the Sub Divisional 
Magistrate (Revenue) and competent Authority, Nepanagar (M.P.); Which have been disposed of by filing case. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declaresthat on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
