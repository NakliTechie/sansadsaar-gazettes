NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 
S.O. 4981(E).— In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of “Construction of 3rd line between 
Varanasi - Shivpur (5.83 km)” in Lucknow Division of Northern Railway as a "Special Railway Project" for 
acquisition of land, in Varansi District in the State of Uttar Pradesh, for providing national infrastructure for a public 
purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
