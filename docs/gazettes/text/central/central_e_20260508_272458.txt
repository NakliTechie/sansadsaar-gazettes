5.0. 2375(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section () of section 4 of 
the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 957 (67 of 957), and consequent upon accreditation 
provided by the National Accreditation Board for Education and Training of Quality Council of India, the Central 
Government hereby notifies M/s HITTITES Exploration under “Category ‘A’ Exploration Agency”, which shall carry 
out prospecting operations in compliance with the conditions specified as per the guidelines for notification of accredited 


private exploration agencies issued by the Government of India, Ministry of Mines, vide order number M.VI- 
6/5/202-Mines VI, dated the 2th August, 202]. 


2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette and shall remain in 
force for a period of five years from the date of such notification or till the expiry or termination of the accreditation 


granted, whichever is earlier.
