in the Offices and Stations of All India Radio , Prasar Bharati ( Broadcasting Corporation of India ) under the Ministry 
of Information and Broadcasting , namely : 


MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING

Rules , 2023 .

and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns ( 5 ) to ( 13 ) of the aforesaid Schedule .
