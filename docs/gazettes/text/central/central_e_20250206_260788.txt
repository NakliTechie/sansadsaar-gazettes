G.S.R. 9(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20i(! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.- () These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin to 
commemorate the occasion of commissioning of JAMMU — SRINAGAR RAILWAY LINE) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of One Hundred Rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government on the occasion of commissioning of JAMMU — SRINAGAR 
RAILWAY LINE. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.- The coin of the denomination mentioned in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely: -
