MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th October, 2023 
S.O. 4377(E).— Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (.) and (3) of section 3 of the 
Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of 967), the Central Government has declared the Jammu and 
Kashmir Democratic Freedom Party (JKDFP), to be an unlawful association under the said Act vide notification 


number S.O. 4348(E) dated the 5th October, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section-3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated the 5" October, 2023. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) 
Act, 967, the Central Government hereby directs that all powers exercisable by it under section 7 and section 8 of the 
said Act shall also be exercised by the State Governments and the Union territory administration in relation to the 
above said unlawful association.
