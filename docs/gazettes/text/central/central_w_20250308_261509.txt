No. 9-5/2025-U.3(A)—Whereas, C.M.R. Educational Society, Hyderabad, Telangana had submitted an online 
application on the UGC Portal for grant of Institution deemed to be University status under general category to Malla Reddy 
Engineering College, Maisammaguda, Gundlapochampally, Medchal, Malkajgiri, Telangana in the name of Malla Reddy (MR) 
under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 956. 


2. And whereas, UGC, vide its letter No.4-5/2023 (CPP-I/DU) dated 0.0.2025, informed that the application was 
examined through its Expert Committee in accordance with UGC (Institution deemed to be Universities) Regulations 2023. The 
Expert Committee unanimously recommended for the conferment of Institution deemed to be University status under general 
category to Malla Reddy Engineering College, Medchal, Malkajgiri, Telangana in the name of Malla Reddy (MR) with certain 
condition. 


3. And whereas, the recommendations of the UGC Expert Committee were considered and approved by the 
Commission in its 586" meeting (Item No. 2.4) held on 23.2.2024. 


4. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 956, the Ministry of 
Education, on the advice of UGC, hereby declares Institution deemed to be University status under general category to Malla 
Reddy Engineering College, Maisammaguda, Gundlapochampally, Medchal, Malkajgiri, Telangana in the name of Malla 
Reddy (MR), Hyderabad, Telangana. This approval is further subject to the following conditions: — 


i. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall follow the UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 
2023 and its amendments, from time to time. 


ii. _ All the undertakings given by the sponsoring body shall be strictly followed by the deemed to be University. 


iii. There shall be no diversion of assets or funds/revenues of the Institution deemed to be University/or of its 
constituent teaching units, without prior permission of the UGC and Ministry of Education. 


iv. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall not engage or indulge in any activities that are of commercial and profit 
making in nature. 


v. | The academic programmes to be offered at Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall conform to the norms and 
standards prescribed by the UGC and the Statutory Councils/Bodies concerned. 


vi. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall align its course with National Education Policy-2020. 


vii. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall take all the required steps to get all the eligible academic 
courses/programmes rated for valid accreditation by National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and the Institute to 
get valid accreditation by National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), as the case may be, in terms 
of the provisions as contained in the UGC (Institutions Deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023, as 
amended from time to time. 


viii. All the prescribed norms and procedures of the Statutory Councils concerned in the matter of admission of 
students, intake capacity of students, renewal of approval to the academic course / programme, revision of intake 
capacity of students, starting of new courses / programmes, etc. shall continue to be in force, and shall be adhered 
to by Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad. 


ix. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall submit MoA in accordance with UGC (Institutions Deemed to be 
Universities) Regulations, 2023 to this Ministry and UGC. Further, as and when necessary, it shall update or 
revise or modify its MoA / Rules, as per the provisions of the prevailing Regulations. 


x. | Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall follow the fee structure as per the Rules and Regulations of the UGC and 
relevant Statutory Councils. 


xi. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall participate in annual Indian rankings issued by National Institutional 
Ranking Framework (NIRF) of this Ministry. 


xii. Malla Reddy (MR), Hyderabad shall compulsorily create Academic Bank of Credits (ABC), identities of their 
students and upload their credit score in digital lockers and ensure that the credit scores are reflected in ABC 
Portal and adopt Samarth e-Gov.
