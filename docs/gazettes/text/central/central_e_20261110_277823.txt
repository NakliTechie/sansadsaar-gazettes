S.O. 6143(E).— Whereas M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited, the applicant with its registered 
address at Building 7A, Level 5, DLF Cyber city, Gurugram, Haryana — 122002 has applied for authorization under Section 164 of 
the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of overhead transmission line under the transmission scheme, “Transmission system for providing 
connectivity to M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited for its 300 MW Solar power project (along with 110 MW 
solar power project of M/s Green Infra Clean Solar Farms Private Limited) in Barmer, Rajasthan”. 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/15/2026-POWER GRID(MOP) dated 
09.01.2026 had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for the overhead line covered under the 
transmission scheme, “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited 
for its 300 MW Solar power project (along with 110 MW solar power project of M/s Green Infra Clean Solar Farms Private Limited) 
in Barmer, Rajasthan”. 


M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited had published notice for transmission scheme in local 
newspapers, Hindustan Times (in English) dated 06.02.2026, Rajasthan Patrika (in Hindi) dated 07.02.2026 and in Weekly Gazette 
of India dated 14.03.2026 for the general public to make observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within two 
months from the date of publication. Subsequently, M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited has submitted an 
affidavit dated 04.08.2026 declaring that 02 nos. observation/representation was received within two months from the date of 
Publication in the official gazette of Government of India and within 2 months from the date of newspaper publications and the same 
have been answered by M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited, and that no further representations were received. 


M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology Private Limited had submitted an Undertaking dated 03.08.2026 stating that the 
route of the proposed overhead transmission line(s) is located outside the Revised Great Indian Bustard (GIB) Priority Area of 
Rajasthan and Gujarat, as referred to in the judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India dated 19.12.2025 in Writ Petition (Civil) 
No. 838 of 2019; the applicant has further undertaken to comply with all directions of the Hon’ble Supreme Court as contained in 
the said judgment as well as with any subsequent directions issued by the competent authorities in pursuance of the above order by 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court, and has also stated that the transmission line alignment has been optimized, in such a way that they share 
maximum common stretch to the extent possible. 


And now the applicant has requested to confer upon him, all the powers under section 164 of the Electricity Act, 2003, 
which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of telegraph lines and posts 
for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so established or maintained for laying of electric 
lines under the transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s Green Infra Clean Wind Technology 
Private Limited for its 300 MW Solar power project (along with 110 MW solar power project of M/s Green Infra Clean Solar Farms 
Private Limited) in Barmer, Rajasthan”. The following overhead line is covered under this scheme:
