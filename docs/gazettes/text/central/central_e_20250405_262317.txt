The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 
5th April, 2025 and is hereby published for general information:— 
THE MUSSALMAN WAKF (REPEAL) ACT, 2025 
No. 5 oF 2025 
[5th April, 2025.] 
An Act to repeal the Mussalman Wakf Act, 923. 


BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-sixth Year of the Republic of India 
as follows:— 


. () This Act may be called the Mussalman Wakf (Repeal) Act, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by 
notification in the Official Gazette, appoint. 


2. (/) The Mussalman Wakf Act, 923 is hereby repealed.
