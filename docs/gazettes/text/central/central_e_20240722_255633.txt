MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 15th July, 2024 


S.O. 2897(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (373) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Bypass line between Pindyal(Nashkal) - Hasanparthi stations (24.55 Km)" as 
a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in the Districts of Jangaon and 
Hanumakonda for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of 


this notification in the Official Gazette.
