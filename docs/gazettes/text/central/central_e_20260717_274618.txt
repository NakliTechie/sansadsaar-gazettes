रजिस्ट्रीकरण अधिनियम, 1860 (1860 का 21) के अधीन पंजीकृत एक सोसाइटी थी, तथा उस क्षमता में उसने राष्ट्रीय 
डोप-रोधी नियम, 2021 विरचित किए थे, जो विश्व डोप-रोधी एजेंसी द्वारा समय-समय पर अंगीकृत एवं संशोधित किए 
जाने वाले विश्व डोप-रोधी संहिता के अधीन अपनी बाध्यताओं के अनुसरण में डोप-रोधी गतिविधियों तथा डोप-रोधी ढांचे 
के अंतर्गत आने वाले व्यक्तियों पर लागू प्रक्रियाओं का विनियमन करते हैं; 


और जबकि, खेलों के लिए राष्ट्रीय डोप-रोधी बोर्ड की स्थापना नहीं होने तक और उक्त बोर्ड तथा राष्ट्रीय डोप- 
रोधी एजेंसी द्वारा उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 30 एवं 31 के अधीन विनियम नहीं बनाए जाने तक, किसी भी प्रचालानात्मक 
ara के अभाव में डोप-रोधी गतिविधियों के क्रियान्वयन में कठिनाई उत्पन्न हो सकती है, जिससे उक्त अधिनियम के उपबंधों 
के प्रभावी कार्यान्वयन में बाधा आ सकती है; 


पूर्वोक्त वर्णित कठिनाई उक्त अधिनियम के उपबंधों को प्रभावी करने में उत्पन्न हुई है और उक्त कठिनाई को दूर 
करना समीचीन है;

अधिसूचित नहीं कर दिए जाते |

G.S.R.. 638(E).— Whereas, the National Anti-Doping Agency was, prior to its reconstitution under the said 
Act, a society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (21 of 1860) and had, in that capacity, framed the 
National Anti-Doping Rules, 2021 governing anti-doping activities, and procedures applicable to persons within the 
anti-doping framework, pursuant to its obligations under the World Anti-Doping Code as adopted and amended from 
time to time by the World Anti-Doping Agency; 


And whereas, until such time as the National Board for Anti-Doping in Sports is established and such Board 
and the National Anti-Doping Agency makes regulations under sections 30 and 31 of the said Act, a difficulty may 
arise in carrying out anti-doping activities in the absence of any operative framework, which may impede the effective 
implementation of the provisions of the said Act; 


And whereas, the aforementioned difficulty has arisen in giving effect to the provisions of the said Act and it 
is expedient to remove the said difficulty; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 33 of the National Anti-Doping 
Act, 2022 (15 of 2022), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order, namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) This Order may be called the National Anti-Doping (Removal of 
Difficulties) Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the 17" day of July, 2026, and shall be in force till such time regulations are notified by 
the National Board for Anti-Doping in Sports under section 30 and by the National Anti-Doping Agency under section 
31 of the said Act. 


2. Continued applicability of the National Anti-Doping Rules, 2021.— (1) The National Anti-Doping Rules, 2021 
adopted by the National Anti-Doping Agency, which were applicable immediately prior to the commencement of the 
said Act, shall continue to apply for all matters covered by its provisions until such time that regulations on the same 
subject matter are notified under sections 30 or 31 of the said Act, as the case may be.
