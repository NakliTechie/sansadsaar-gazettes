New Delhi, the 14th March, 2026 


S.O. 1362(E).—In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of “Rajpura bypass line for DFCCIL 
from New Shambhu station to Kauli station (13.46 km) of Northern Railway” as a "Special Railway 
Project” for acquisition of approximately 49.383 Hectare of land, in district Patiala of the state Punjab, for providing 
national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 


official gazette.
