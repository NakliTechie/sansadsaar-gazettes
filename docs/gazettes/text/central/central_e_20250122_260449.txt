NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 12th December, 2024 


S.O. 399(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 187 at Km. 
262/38-263/2 in between Singarayakonda — Tanguturu stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land 
in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Prakasham for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose 
with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
