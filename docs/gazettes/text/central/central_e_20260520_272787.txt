Mumbai, the |2th May, 2026 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 2585(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 3368 dated 25 July 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 
3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 25 July 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section 
() of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (Il of 2008) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely “Additional Hotgi Bye Pass line (5.85 Km) of Central Railway” in the district of 
Solapur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for Deputy Collector Land Acquisition 
No (3) Solapur in daily Marathi newspapers namely “Dainik Sanchar” & “The Times of India” dated 02.08.2025 
under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in 04 villages of South Solapur 49 objections were received and the same have been disposed 
by the competent Authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
