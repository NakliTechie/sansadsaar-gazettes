NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd April, 2026 


S.O. 2006(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, SO No.6004(E) Dated:24/2/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I], Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of additional structure / bridges (widening), maintenance, management 
and operation of NH48 in the stretch of land from Km. 230.680 to Km. 250.000 in Taluka Mangrol & Kamrej, District 
Surat in the state of GUJARAT, 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ The Times of India” and “Gujarat 
Samachar” both dated 20.0!.2026; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
