MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th August, 2026 


S.O. 4755(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, of the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building ,(widening/ four laning etc) maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 7 (New 
National Highway No. 44) in Ramtek Tahasil, in the stretch of land from km. 652.000 to km 723.000 (Jabalpur- 


Nagpur Section) in the Nagpur District, in the state of Maharashtra, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(l) of section 3C of the said Act, 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the Deputy Collector, Land 
Acquisition (General), Nagpur Maharashtra, in the in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the said 
competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks 


necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections.
