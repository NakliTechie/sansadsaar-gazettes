MINISTRY OF EXTERNAL AFFAIRS 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 27th October, 2023 


S.O. 4709(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the United Nations (Security Council) 
Act, 947 (43 of 947), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order to amend the Implementation of 
the United Nations Security Council Resolution on Mali Order, 2023 namely : - 


l. (i) This Order may be called the Implementation of the United Nations Security Council Resolution on Mali 
(Amendment) Order, 2023. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Implementation of the United Nations Security Council Resolution on Mali Order, 2023, in Paragraph 3, 
under the heading “A. Individuals”, paragraphs MLi.00l, MLi.002, MLi.003, MLi.004, MLi.005, MLi.006, MLi.007 
and MLi.008 and the entries relating thereto shall be omitted. 


[F. No. U.II/52/32/2022] 
PRAKASH GUPTA, Jt. Secy. 


Note — The principal Order was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), 
vide notification number S.O. 857(E), dated the 237 February, 2023.
