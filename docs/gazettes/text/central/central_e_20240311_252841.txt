G.S.R. 70(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 98 of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989), 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage 
of Passengers) Rules, 2000, namely:- 


Short title and commencement.— () These rules may be called the Railways (Opening for Public Carriage of 
Passengers) Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
