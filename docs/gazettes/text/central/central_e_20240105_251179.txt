S.O. 71(E).—Whereas, certain modifications which the Central Government proposed to make in the Master 
Plan for Delhi-2021/ Zonal Development Plan of Zone-O regarding the area mentioned hereunder were published in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, as Public Notice vide No. 8.0. 536 (E) dated 09.02.2022 by the Delhi 
Development Authority in accordance with the provisions of Section 44 of the Delhi Development Act, 1957 (61 of 
1957) inviting objections/ suggestions as required by sub-section (3) of Section 11-A of the said Act, within thirty 
days from the date of the said notice. 


2. Whereas, 01 objection/suggestion received with regard to the proposed modification has been considered by the 
Board of Enquiry and Hearing, set up by the Delhi Development Authority and also approved in the meeting of Delhi 
Development Authority. 


3. Whereas, the Central Government has, after carefully considering all aspects of the matter, decided to modify the 
Master Plan for Delhi-2021/ Zonal Development Plan of Zone-O.
