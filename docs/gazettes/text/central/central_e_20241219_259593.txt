PRELIMINARY NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 9th December, 2024 


S.O. 5495(E).—.In exercise of the power conferred by sub-section(l) of section 20A of the 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred in as the said Act), the Central Government after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which was given in the schedule 
annexed hereto, is required for execution of the Special Railway Project, namely, Construction of ROB in 
lieu of LC 23 between Jammikunta and Bisgirsheriff stations in Karimnagar District in the state of 
Telangana, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land: 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of 
publication of this notification in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land 
for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Revenue Divisional 
Officer, Huzurabad, Karimnagar District, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the competent 
authority Revenue Divisional Officer, Huzurabad, Karimnagar District shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks necessary, by 
order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (ID) of section 20(D) of the said act 
shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
