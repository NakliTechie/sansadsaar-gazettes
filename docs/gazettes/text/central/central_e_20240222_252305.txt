practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections; Any order made by the Competent 
Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final; The land plans and other details of the 
land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office 
of the Competent Authority.
