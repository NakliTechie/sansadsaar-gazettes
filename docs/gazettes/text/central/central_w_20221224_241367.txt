No. 309 - Pres / 2022 - The President is pleased to award the Tatrakshak Medal for Gallantry to Commandant Kunal 
Chandrakant Naik ( 0637 - V ) on the occasion of the Independence Day , 2022 . 


CITATION 

Commandant Kunal Chandrakant Naik ( 0637 - V ) joined the Indian Coast Guard on 27 December 2004 . 

2. The officer is a Qualified Flying Instructor and is having more than 2200 hrs of flying to his credit . On 13 Sep 21 , 
around 2030 hrs , Air Enclave Porbandar was tasked to rescue 07 fishermen stranded at sea off Diu . The fishermen were
