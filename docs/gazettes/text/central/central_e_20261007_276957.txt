RESOLUTION 
New Delhi, the 7th October, 2026 


F. No. 27-9/20i2-UU (Pt.3)—In terms of sub-section (l) of Section 2] of Chapter III of the 
Auroville Foundation Act, 988, the Government of India hereby re-constitutes the Auroville International 
Advisory Council as follows: 


l. Shri Hasmukh P. Rama, Chancellor and Founder President, AURO University, Surat. 


2. Dr. David Frawley, Founder and Director, American Institute of Vedic Studies, Santa Fe, 
New Mexico. 


3. Dr. Kiran Vyas, Founder of Tapovan, Open University of Yoga & Ayurveda. 
4. Shri Kottamasu Venkateswara Rao, Chairman & Director, Tata International Singapore Pte. Ltd. 


2. The Chairman of the Council shall be elected by the members of the Council from among themselves. 


3. The term of office of the members shall be for a period of four years from the date of this Resolution. The 
members shall serve in an honorary capacity. 


4. Secretary, Auroville Foundation shall be the Ex-Officio Secretary of the Auroville International Advisory 
Council. 


GOVIND JAISWAL, Jt. Secy.
