MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3 March, 2025 

S.O. 20(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) and in pursuance of the proviso to sub- paragraph (6) of paragraph 3 and 
proviso to clause (c) of paragraph 5 of the notification of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry 
of Environment and Forests, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii), vide number 8.0. 533(E), dated the l4th September, 2006, the Central Government considering it 
necessary and expedient, hereby extends the term of the Chairman and Members of the State Level 
Environment Impact Assessment Authority, Meghalaya and the term of the Chairman and Members of the 
State Level Expert Appraisal Committee, Meghalaya for a period up to 2l“' October, 2025 or till the re- 
constitution of the State Level Environment Impact Assessment Authority and the State Level Expert 
Appraisal Committee, as the case may be, whichever is earlier, and for that purpose makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Environment, Forests and 
Climate Change number S.O. 920 (E), dated the 22"! April, 2022, namely:- 


In the said notification,— 


(a) in paragraph 2, for the words “three years”, the words “three years and six months” shall be 
substituted; 
(b) in paragraph 6, for the words “three years”, the words “three years and six months” shall be 


substituted.
