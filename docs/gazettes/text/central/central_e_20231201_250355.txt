NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 30th November, 2023 


F. No. J-9/11/2019-CPU.—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 10 and 
sub-section (2) of section 15 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (35 of 2019) and in view of DoP&T’s 
order no. 36/01/2023-EO(SM-I)(2) dated 31 August, 2023 and the Gazette Notification of even number 
dated 257 September, 2023, the Central Government, in partial modification of this Department’s 
notification of even number dated 29" July, 2020, assigns the additional charge of Chief Commissioner in 
the Central Consumer Protection Authority[CCPA] to Shri Rohit Kumar Singh, Secretary in the 
Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India in place of Ms. Nidhi Khare, Special Secretary in 
the Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India for smooth functioning of the Central Consumer 
Protection Authority [CCPA].
