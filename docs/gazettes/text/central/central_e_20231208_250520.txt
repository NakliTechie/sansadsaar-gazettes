MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th December, 2023 


S.O. 5238(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 988) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) and in supersession of the Notification 
9.0. 008 (E) dated [77 September 2002, the Central Government has vide Notification No 8.0. 3946 (E) dated 50 
September,2023 constituted a permanent authority to be known as the Central Empowered Committee, (hereinafter 
referred to as the Committee) for the purposes of monitoring and ensuring compliance of the orders of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in the matter Writ Petitions (Civil) No. 202//995 and 7/996, covering the subject matters of 
Environment and Forest & Wildlife, and related issues arising out of the said orders and to suggest measures and 
recommendations generally to the State as well as Central Government, for the more effective implementation of the 
Act and other orders of the Court. 


2, Accordingly, following are hereby appointed to this committee with immediate effect for a period of three years 
or till they attain the age of Sixty-Six years or till any other order of the Central Government, whichever is 
earlier: 


) Sri Siddhant Das, Chairman 

2) Sri Chandra Prakash Goyal, Member 
3) Sri Sunil Limaye, Member 

4) Dr J.R. Bhatt, Member 


5) Ms Banumathi G, Assisstant Inspector General of Forests, Minsitry of Environment, Forest & Climate 
Change- Member Secretary 


3: The members of the Committee are appointed in their personal capacity and the Committee shall function under 
the administrative control of the Central Government in the Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change 
with its headquarters at Delhi. 


4. The salaries and allowances payable to, and the other terms and conditions of service of, the Chairperson and 
Members, will be prescribed separately, provided that neither the salary nor perks and the other allowances nor 
the other terms and conditions of service of the Chairperson or a Member shall be varied to his disadvantage 
after his appointment.
