NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 27th February, 2025 


S.O. 063(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 69 (E) dated the 04 April 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 04 April 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (I) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution,

maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Ankai Bypass Line project in the District 
of Nashik in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Paper "Navbharat" & 
"Lokmat Times" dated 30 August 2024, under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, 


And whereas, in Ankute & Kusur villages no objections were received and accordingly orders have been 
passed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of 20 (E) of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government, 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose, 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
