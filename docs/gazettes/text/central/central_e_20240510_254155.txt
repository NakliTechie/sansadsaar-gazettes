MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
(Wireless Planning and Coordination Wing) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th May, 2024 


G.S.R. 27(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4 and 7 of the Indian Telegraph Act, 885 
((3 of I885) and sections 4 and 0 of the Indian Wireless Telegraphy Act, 933 ([7 of 933), the Central Government 
hereby makes the following rules to amend the Use of Low Power and Very Low Power Short Range Radio 
Frequency Devices (Exemption from Licensing Requirement) Rules, 208, namely:- 


. Short title and commencement.- () These rules may be called the Use of Low Power and Very Low Power Short 
Range Radio Frequency Devices (Exemption from Licensing Requirement) Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Use of Low Power and Very Low Power Short Range Radio Frequency Devices (Exemption from Licensing 
Requirement) Rules, 208, in rule 3, 


(i) Table-VII shall be omitted; 


(ii) in Table-IX, against SI.No.l4, for the entry under column(2), the entry “24.00-24.25 GHz” shall be 
substituted. 


3. The Equipment Type Approvals (ETAs) issued for specific models in it prior to publication of this Notification 
shall continue to be valid till the life time of such models.
