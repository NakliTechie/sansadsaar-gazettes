पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3323 दिनांक 21/07/2022, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग 11, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा कर्नाटक राज्य के गदग जिले में NH367 के

आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

दिया है;

रिपोर्ट दे दी है;

की उपधारा (1) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि उक्त अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का 
पूर्वोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए अर्जन किया जाना चाहिए;

S.O. 5756(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 5.00 3323 dated 21/07/2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-

section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter

annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four laning, etc,) Maintenance, management and operation 
of NH367 on the stretch of land from Km. 52.8 to Km. 71.00 (Bhananpur - Gaddankeri Section) in the district of 
GADAG in the state of KARNATAKA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Vijayvaani and “Samyukta 
Karnataka” both dated 01/08/2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
