O.N. 52.—WHEREAS, the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Haryana, 2024 for 32- Gohana 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464/ECI/HAR- 
LA/2024, dated 5h September, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be true copy of the account kept by him/her or his/her 
election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the above-mentioned election for 32- Gohana Assembly Constituency of Haryana, 
2024 was declared by the Returning Officer on 8 October, 2024 and hence the last date for lodging the account of 
Election Expenses was 77 November, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report submitted by DEO, Sonipat report dated 08..2024 forwarded by the Chief 
Electoral Officer, Haryana vide his letter no. 6/Elect-Exp./GELA-HR-2024/Elec-2025/2AE-692 dated 05.02.2025, 
under sub-rule () of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections Rules, 96l, Sh. Dinesh Kumar a contesting candidate 
from 32- Gohana Assembly Constituency of state of Haryana has failed to lodge account of election expenses, as 
required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, Sonipat a Show-Cause notice No. 
76/HR-LA/Exp. Account/2024/CEMS-I, dated 307" April, 2025 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the 
Conduct of Elections Rules, 96l by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Dinesh Kumar for not lodging of his 
account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96l, Sh. Dinesh Kumar, was directed to submit representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer, Sonipat within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Sonipat vide his letter no. Elec-2025/564 dated 9.06.2025 has 
informed that the notice was duly served and received by Sh. Dinesh Kumar’ mother (name Smt. Vidya Devi), on 
30.05.2025; and 


WHEREAS, the District Election Officer, Sonipat in his Supplementary Report, vide its letter no. Elec- 
2025/564 dated 9.06.2025 has reported that Sh. Dinesh Kumar has not submitted account of election expenses. 
Further, after receipt of the said notice, Sh. Dinesh Kumar has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to the 
Election Commission of India, for failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law; and 


WHEREAS, Section l0 A of the Representation of the People Act, 95 provides that:- 
“If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person- 


(a) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses, within the time and in the manner required by or under 
this Act, and 


(b) has no good reason or justification for the failure, the Election Commission shall, by order published in the 
Official Gazette, declare him to be disqualified and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years 
from the date of the order.”’;

WHEREAS, on the basis of facts and available records, the Commission is satisfied that Sh. Dinesh Kumar 
has failed to lodge account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so; and 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section l0A of the Representation of the People Act, 95, the Election 
Commission of India hereby declares Sh. Dinesh Kumar, Village and Post Office Anwali, Tehsil Gohana, District 
Sonepat, a contesting candidate for 32-Gohana Assembly Constituency of Haryana in General Election to 
Legislative Assembly, 2024 to be disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House of the 
Parliament or the Legislative Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three 
years from the date of this order.

dated 7 May, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act, 95, every contesting candidate at an 
election shall, within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate, lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his/her election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him/her or his/her 
election agent under Section 77 of the said act; and 


WHEREAS, the result of the election for 43- Patiala Parliamentary Constituency of Punjab, 2024 was 
declared by the Returning Officer on 047 June, 2024 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election 
Expenses was 4" July, 2024; and 


WHEREAS, as per the report dated 05.07.2024 of DEO, Patiala under sub-rule (l) of Rule 89 of the Conduct 
of Elections Rules, 96! received through the CEO, Punjab vide his letter no. Elec-2024/R-7458, dated 22.07.2024 
stating that Sh. Paramjit Singh S/o Tarlochan Singh, a contesting candidate from 43- Patiala Parliamentary 
Constituency of State of Punjab has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses, as required under law; and 


WHEREAS, on the basis of the said report of the District Election Officer, a ‘Show-Cause Notice’ No. 76/PB- 
LS/I3/2024/CEMS-I, dated 3[* January, 2025 was issued under sub rule (5) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of Elections 
Rules, 96 by the Election Commission of India to Sh. Paramjit Singh S/o Tarlochan Singh, for not lodging of the 
account of Election Expenses; and 


WHEREAS, through the above said ‘Show Cause Notice’ and under sub rule (6) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules, 96, Sh. Paramjit Singh S/o Tarlochan Singh was directed to submit representation in writing to 
the Commission explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his election 
expenses with the District Election Officer, Patiala District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice; and 


WHEREAS, the DEO, Patiala has reported that the said ‘Show Cause Notice’ was served to Sh. Paramjit 
Singh S/o Tarlochan Singh on 06.02.2025; and

Punjab vide his letter no. Elec-2025/R-8097, dated 25.0.2025 has reported that Sh. Paramjit Singh S/o Tarlochan 
Singh has not submitted any representation or statement of account of election expenses. Further, after receipt of the
