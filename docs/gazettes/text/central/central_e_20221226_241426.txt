उप - धारा ( 1 ) के दूसरे परंतुक द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , मुख्य आयुक्त दिव्यांगजन के परामर्श से , कार्य की

सुरक्षा के परिचालन संबंधी पदों को उक्त धारा के प्रावधानों से एतदद्वारा छूट प्रदान करती है ।

MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT 
[ Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities ( Divyangjan ) ] 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 16th December , 2022 

S.O. 6055 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 34 of 
the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act , 2016 ( 49 of 2016 ) , the Central Government , in consultation with the Chief 
Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities , having regard to the nature and type of work , hereby exempts the 
operational posts of Executive , Technical , Motor Transport and Security in Intelligence Bureau under Ministry of 
Home Affairs from the provisions of the said section .
