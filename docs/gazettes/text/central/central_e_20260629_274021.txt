NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 3rd June, 2026 


5.0. 3498(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, 5.0. 2299 (E) dated the 23 May 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, 
section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 20 May 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub- 
section (l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) - 
Ankai Doubling project” in the District of Nashik in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Paper " Lokmat Times” 
“Navbharat” & “Navarashtra" dated 04 June 2025, under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act, 


And whereas, in Saygaon village, objections were not received and accordingly report have been passed by the 
Competent Authority;

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20(E). of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
