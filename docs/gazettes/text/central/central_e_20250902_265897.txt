New Delhi, the 2nd September, 2025 


S.O. 4015(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 1271 Dated: 19th March, 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for for the public Purpose of building (widening/six/eight-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of Mumbai - Vadodara Expressway Spur to NH-NE4 in the stretch of land from Km. 18.925 
to Km. 45.400 (Maharashtra Section) in the district of Thane in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Punya Nagari”(Marathi) and “The 
Free Press Journal”(English) both dated 05th April 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that 
on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in 
the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
