MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th July, 2023 


S.O. 3343(E)—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 70 of the 
Information Technology Act, 2000 (21 of 2000), the Central Government hereby declares the computer resources 
relating to the Core Banking Solution and Unified Payments Interface Switch, being Critical Information 
Infrastructure of the Paytm Payments Bank Ltd., and the computer resources of its associated dependencies, to be 
protected systems for the purpose of the said Act and authorises the following personnel to access the protected 
systems, namely:— 


(a) any designated employee of the Paytm Payments Bank Ltd. authorised in writing by the Paytm 
Payments Bank Ltd. to access the protected system; 


(b) any team member of contractual managed service provider or third-party vendor who have been 
authorised in writing by the Paytm Payments Bank Ltd. for need-based access; and 


(c) any consultant, regulator, Government official, auditor and stakeholder authorised in writing by the 
Paytm Payments Bank Ltd. on case to case basis. 


2 This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
