INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the l0th March, 2025 


F. No. 8-24/4742-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Kuntala Devi School of Nursing situated at Po- Debatra Colony, Samanta Marg, Nayagarh, 
Distt. Nayagarh 752069 Odisha is recognized by State Government of Odisha and Odisha Nurses & Midwives Council 
to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-24/4742-INC (58398) dated 5/05/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 
4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Odisha, 
Secretariat Building, Bhubaneswar — 7500, Odisha was issued. Copy of the notice dated 5/05/2024 was also enclosed 
to Kuntala Devi School of Nursing situated at Po- Debatra Colony, Samanta Marg, Nayagarh, Distt. Nayagarh 
752069 Odisha. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
Kuntala Devi School of Nursing. Even the institution, Kuntala Devi School of Nursing has not responded to the 
notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 29/0/2024 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., clinical and 
teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section |4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 26/0/2024 
any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Kuntala Devi School of Nursing situated at Po- Debatra 
Colony, Samanta Marg, Nayagarh, Distt. Nayagarh 752069 Odisha shall be entitled to be registered only in the State 
of Odisha. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
