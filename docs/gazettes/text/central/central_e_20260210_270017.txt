S.O. 689(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the President 
hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 96l, 
namely: - 


6 () These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and Eighty- 
fourth Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force at once.

heading “MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS (PATTAN, POT PARIVAHAN AUR 
JALMARG MANTRALAYA)”, for the entry “l0. Organisation and maintenance of mainland islands and 
inter-island shipping services.”, the following entry shall be substituted, namely: - 


“_]0, Organisation, development and maintenance of ports along with shipping services in the islands, except 


to the extent delegated to the Union Territory Administrations.”
