G.S.R. 66(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clauses | and 2 of Clause 24 of the Rules and 
Regulations of the Memorandum of Association of Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural Industrialization (MGIRD, 
and with the approval of the Central Government, the Executive Council of Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural 
Industrialization hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the Group ‘A’, ‘B’ and ‘C’ 
posts in the Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural Industrialization (MGIRIJ) as under:- 


l. Short title and commencement. —() These rules may be called the Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural 
Industrialization Group ‘A’, ‘B’ & ‘C’ Officers and Staff Recruitment Rules, 2024. (2) They shall come into force on 
the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.— These rules shall apply to the posts specified in column (l) of the Schedule annexed to these rules. 


3. Number of posts, classification and pay band, grade pay or pay scale. - The number of the said posts, their 
classification and the pay band, grade pay or pay scale attached thereto shall be as specified in column (2) to (4) of the 
said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age limit and other qualifications.-The method of recruitment to the said posts, age 
limits and qualifications and other matters relating thereto shall be as specified in columns (5) to (3) of the said 
Schedule. 


5. Initial constitutions. — () Group ‘A’, ‘B’ and ‘C’ Officers and Staff working on regular establishment in MGIRI 
on the date of commencement of these rules shall be deemed to have been duly appointed to the corresponding posts 
which they were holding on regular basis on that date. (2) The inter-se seniority of officials who are appointed at the 
initial constitution shall be determined as per the date of their regular appointment to respective grades subject to the 
condition that their inter-se seniority within their respective grades shall not be disturbed. (3) The regular continuous 
service of officers mentioned in Sub-rule (l) prior to their appointment at the initial constitution under these rules, 
shall count for the purpose of probation period, the qualifying service for promotion, confirmation and pension.
