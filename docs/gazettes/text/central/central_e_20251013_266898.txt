NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the ]3th October, 2025 


S.O. 4648(E).— In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of “Construction of 3rd & 4th Line between 
Ghaziabad—Moradabad, Moradabad-Roza and Roza-Sitapur (402.78 km)” as a ''Special Railway Project" for 
acquisition of 994.07 Hactare of land in Districts Ghaziabad, Hapur, Amroha, Moradabad, Rampur, Bareilly, 
Shahjahanpur, Lakhimpur Kheri, Hardoi and Sitapur in the State of Uttar Pradesh for providing national infrastructure 
for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
