NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th May, 2025 


S.O. 2031(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 151 of the Finance Act, 2017 (7 of 2017), 
the Central Government hereby appoints the 9th day of May, 2025 as the date on which sections 152 and 153 of 
the said Act, relating to amendments in the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (51 of 2007), shall come 
into force.
