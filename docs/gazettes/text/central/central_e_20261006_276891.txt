MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Food and Public Distribution) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 6th October, 2026 


5.0. 5461(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 (10 
of 1955) read with clauses 7 and 16 of the Sugar (Control) Order, 2025, the Central Government hereby makes the 
following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, 
Food and Public Distribution, Department of Food and Public Distribution, number S.O. 4165(E), dated the 28th July, 
2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part वी, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 28th July, 2026, 


namely:— 
In the said notification, in paragraph 1,— 


for the words and figures "no dealer of sugar shall hold any stock for a period exceeding thirty days from the date of 
receipt of such stock and shall not keep sugar in stock at any time and in any place throughout the country in excess of 
2000 quintals, except in case of Kolkata and its extended metropolitan areas, where the stock shall not exceed 4000 
quintals ", the words and figures " no dealer of sugar shall hold any stock for a period exceeding fifteen days from the 
date of receipt of such stock and shall not keep sugar in stock at any time and in any place throughout the country in 
excess of 1000 quintals, except in case of Kolkata and its extended metropolitan areas and the State of Assam, where 


the stock shall not exceed 2000 quintals" shall be substituted. 
2. This order shall come into force on the 15" day of October, 2026. 
[F. No. 1(2)/2026-SP-I] 
ASHWINI SRIVASTAVA, Jt. Secy.

3rd September, 2026.
