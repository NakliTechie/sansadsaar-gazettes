19.04.2023 में प्रकाशित भारत सरकार के रेल मंत्रालय (पश्चिम रेलवे निर्माण संगठन) की अधिसूचना में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि 
को मध्य प्रदेश राज्य के खण्डवा जिले मे रतलाम- इंदौर- डा. अम्बेडकर नगर- खंडवा आमान परिवर्तन परियोजना नाम की 
विशेष रेल परियोजना के निष्पादन के लिये आवश्यक है, ऐसी भूमि के अर्जन करने के आशय की घोषणा की गई थी । 


और, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20(क) की उप-धारा (4) के अधीन उक्त अधिसूचना का सार स्थानीय समाचार 
पत्रों में से ग्राम मोरघड़ी के लिए, हिन्दी भाषा के समाचार पत्र राज एक्सप्रेस इन्दौर में दिनांक 06-05-2023 को तथा 
अंग्रेजी भाषा के समाचार पत्र फ्री-प्रेस इन्दौर में दिनांक 06-05-2023 एवं ग्राम मोरटक्का के लिए हिन्दी भाषा के समाचार 
पत्र दैनिक भास्कर इन्दौर में दिनांक 28-04-2023 को तथा अंग्रेजी भाषा के समाचार पत्र टाइम्स ऑफ इंडिया इन्दौर में 
दिनांक 28-04-2023 को प्रकाशित किया गया था और सक्षम प्राधिकारी को किसी भी व्यक्ति का कोई आक्षेप प्राप्त नहीं

NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, 6th June, 2023 


S.O. 2455(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, Extra Ordinary part II Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after 
referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act 1989 (24 of 1989) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification 1790-E Dated 19-04-2023 for execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of the Special Railway Project, namely Ratlam — Indore - Dr. Ambedkar Nagar - Khandwa Gauge 
Conversion Project in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And under sub-section (4) of section 20(A) of the said Act, out of local newspapers, for Morghari village in 
Hindi language newspaper Raj Express Indore on 06-05-2023 and published in English newspaper Free-Press Indore 
on 06-05-2023 and for Mortakka village in Hindi language newspaper Dainik Bhasker Indore on 28-04--2023 and 
published in English newspaper Times of India Indore on 28-04-2023 and whereas the Competent Authority has not 
been received any objection from any person and whereas , in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20(E) of the 
said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to the central government . 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances 


The Land Plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person aforesaid Office of the Competent Authority.
