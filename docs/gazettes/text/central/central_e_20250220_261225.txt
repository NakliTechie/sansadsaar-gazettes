Mumbai, the 20th February, 2025 


S.O. 93(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number, S.O.l36(E) dated the 07/0I/2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub- 
section (ii), dated the 07/0/2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section () of 
section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (l! of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the 
purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely new BG line project 
from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) in Dhar district Tehsil Dhar Village Chikliya 
in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers Dainik bhaskar and 
Times Nation” dated 5/0/2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Chikliya village objections were received, considered and the objections have been resolved 
and competent Authority have passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report 
to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
