2017) andin partial modification of the notifications of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of 
Revenue), No. 35/2020-Central Tax, dated the 3% April, 2020 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I,

G.S.R. 31062), dated the 1" May, 2021 and No. 13/2022-Central Tax, dated the 5" July, 2022, published in the
