सौंप दी है। 

अत: अब केंद्र सरकार, सक्षम प्राधिकारी से रिपोर्ट प्राप्त होने पर और उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उप-धारा (1) 
द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, यह घोषणा करती है कि यहां संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि उपरोक्त उद्देश्य के 
लिए अधिग्रहण किया जाएगा | 

अतः यह की केंद्र सरकार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 20ई की उप-धारा (2) के अनुसरण में, यह घोषणा करती है कि 
आधिकारिक राजपत्र में अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन पर, यहां संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि सभी विल्लग्रो से मुक्त हो कर केंद्र 
सरकार में निहित हो जाएगी।

NOTIFICATION 
Bhubaneswar, the 5th June, 2025 
NOTICE UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT, 1989 


S.O. 2495(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (East 
Coast Railway, Construction Organisation), Notification number, 5.0. 825 (E). dated the 17" February, 2025 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 17" February, 2025 (herein after referred 
to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) 
(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely, “Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of closing of manned LC No ML-400 
at Km 714/3-5 between Naupada (NWP) - Kotabommali (KBM) on Main Line of Waltair Division” in district- 
Srikakulam in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely "SAKSHI" 
in Telugu (Srikakulam & Visakhapatnam Edition) on dated 1*t March, 2025 and 'THE NEW INDIAN EXPRESS” in 
English (Visakhapatnam Edition) dated 17 March, 2025 under sub- section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
