NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 


S.O. 4133(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 44 at Km. 
285/24-26 between Peddapalli - Raghavapuram stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the 
State of Telangana in the District of Peddapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
