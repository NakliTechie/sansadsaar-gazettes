MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(Directorate General of Foreign Trade) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st March, 2024 
No. 65/2023 
Subject: Export of Onions (under HS code 0703 10 19) to UAE. 


S.O. 1113(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 of the Foreign Trade 
(Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), as amended, read with Para 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign 
Trade Policy, 2023 and in accordance with the provision contained in Para 3 of Notification No. 49/2023 dt. 
07.12.2023, the Central Government permits export of 14,400 MT of onions (under HS code 0703 10 19), with a 
quantity ceiling of 3600 MT quarterly, to UAE through National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL). 


Ds Effect of the Notification: 


Export of 14,400 MT of onions (under HS code 0703 10 19), with a quantity ceiling of 3600 MT quarterly, to UAE 
through National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL) is notified.
