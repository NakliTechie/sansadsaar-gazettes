New Delhi, the 20th June, 2026 


5.0. 3258(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2026 (8 of 2026), the Central Government hereby appoints the 17 day of July, 2026, 
as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to amendments in the Merchant Shipping Act, 
2025 (24 of 2025), as specified against serial number 79 of the Schedule to the said Act, shall come into force.
