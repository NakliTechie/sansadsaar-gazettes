G.S.R. 754(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 12 of the Unified

Council Rules, 1998, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Central Waqf 
Council (Group 'A', 'B' and 'C' Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2014, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Central Waqf Council (Group 'A’, 'B' and 'C' 
Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial Posts) Recruitment Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

" By deputation (including short-term contract).”;
