जारी, भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग 2, खण्ड 3, उप-खण्ड (ii), तारीख 05 दिसंबर 2022 में प्रकाशित भारत

इसके पश्चात उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है), द्वारा उक्त अधिसूचना से sag अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को महाराष्ट्र 
राज्य के सोलापूर जिले में विशेष रेल परियोजना अर्थात “सोलापुर उस्मानाबाद वाया तुलजापुर नई रेलवे लाइन” की विशेष

घोषणा की थी।

वार्ता” में तारीख 06 मार्च, 2023 को प्रकाशित किया गया था।

NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai , the 25th July, 2023 


8.0. 3337(E)— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 5530 (E) published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section 
(ii), dated the 57 December, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section (1) of 
section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) & Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely Solapur — Tuljapur - Osmanabad New Line in the district of Solapur in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Dainik 
Swarajya dated 08" March, 2023” and “Dainik Agrini Varta Dated 67 March, 2023” under sub section (4) of section 
20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in 01 villages 46 No’s of objections were received and the same have been disposed by the 


competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 


report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 


the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 


hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
