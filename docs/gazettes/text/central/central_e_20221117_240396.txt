MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi the 7th November, 2022 


S.O. 537I(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 240(E) dt: 27.05.2022 published in Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Widening/four 
laning etc,) , maintenance , management and operation of National Highway No. 67 in the stretch of land 
from Km. 476.000 to Km. 523.500 (Tadipatri to Muddanur Section) in Y.S.R. Kadapa district in the state of 
Andhra Pradesh; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu (English) ” and 
“Prajasakthi (Telugu)” both dated 02.06.2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received from any person by the Competent Authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
