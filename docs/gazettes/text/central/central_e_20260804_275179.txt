MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th August, 2026. 


S.O. 4317(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the second proviso to sub-section (1) of section 4 
of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), upon obtaining accreditation from 
the National Accreditation Board for Education and Training of the Quality Council of India, the Central Government 
hereby notifies the following agency for the purposes of the said Act, namely:— 


SL No Exploration agency Category of exploration agency 


M/s Minedge Global Private Limited 


1. The agency shall carry out prospecting operations in compliance with the conditions specified in the guidelines 
issued vide Order No. M.VI-16/15/2021-Mines VI dated the 12" August, 2021. 


2, This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette and shall remain 
valid for a period of five years or till expiry or termination of the accreditation granted, whichever is earlier.
