( CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS ) 

No. PR / 194 / 13 - DD / 225 / 13 / DC / 587 / 2017. - In terms of the provisions of Section 21B ( 3 ) of the Chartered 
Accountants Act , 1949 read with Rules 18 ( 17 ) and 19 ( 1 ) of the Chartered Accountants ( Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases ) Rules , 2007 , the Disciplinary Committee has held 
CA. Ganapathy Sivaprakash ( Membership No. 024556 ) , No. 5 ( Old No. 2 ) , 5th Cross Street , West Shenoy Nagar , 
CHENNAI - 600 030 , guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clauses ( 6 ) , ( 7 ) and ( 8 ) of Part 
I of Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently ordered for removal of name of CA. Ganapathy 
Sivaprakash ( Membership No. 024556 ) from the Register of Members for a period of 02 ( Two ) years . In pursuance 
of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 2 ) of 
Section 20 of the aforesaid Act , read with Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 1988 , the name of 
CA. Ganapathy Sivaprakash ( Membership No. 024556 ) was removed from the Register of Members for a 
period of 02 ( Two ) years with effect from 30th September , 2022. Aggrieved by Order of the Committee , the 
Respondent preferred an Appeal in terms of Section 22G of the Chartered Accountants Act , 1949 and the Appellate 
Authority vide its order pronounced on 11th October , 2022 had modified the period of removal of name from 02 ( two ) 
years to 01 ( one ) year . Accordingly , in pursuance of the said order of the Appellate Authority , the notification dated 
30th September , 2022 wherein the name of CA. Ganapathy Sivaprakash ( Membership No. 024556 ) was removed 
from the Register of Members for a period of 2 ( two ) years is hereby modified to the extent that the name of CA. 
Ganapathy Sivaprakash ( Membership No. 024556 ) stands removed for a period of 01 ( one ) year instead of 
02 ( two ) years w.e.f. 30th September , 2022 .
