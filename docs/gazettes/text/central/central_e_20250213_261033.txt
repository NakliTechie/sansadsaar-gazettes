MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3th February, 2025 


S.O. 778(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways number S.O. 2320(E) Dated: 4 June 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building Maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway No. 94 in the stretch of land from Km. !32.300 to Km. 42.000 (Land slide treatment from Km 29.090 to 
Km 29.20 and 33.750 to Km 33.850) in the district of Uttarkashi in the state of Uttarakhand. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hindustan” (Hindi) and “The 
Times of India” (English) both dated 05 Jul 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


[भाग I—ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
