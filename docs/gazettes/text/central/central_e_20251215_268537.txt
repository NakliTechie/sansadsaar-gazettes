MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |5th December, 2025 


S.O. 5790(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O.No. 842 (E), Dated:7/02/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH-l63G in the stretch of land from Km. 284.285 to Km. 285.409 and Km. 294.738 to Km. 296.397 in the district 
of NTR in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” dated 
28/02/2025 , “Prajashakti” dated 0/03/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received in the matter. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
