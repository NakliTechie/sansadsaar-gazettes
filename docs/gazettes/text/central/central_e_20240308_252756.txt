NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 6th March, 2024 


Notification Under Section 20 E of the Railway Act, 1989 


S.O. 1164(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, In the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board) number S.O. 20 (E), dated the 01 January 2024, published in the Gazette of India (Extra-Ordinary) 
Part-II section 3 sub section (ii), hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under subsection (1) of 20A of the 
railway act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for execution of Special Railway Project, 
namely BILASPUR — USLAPUR FLY OVER PROJECT (10.40 Km), District — Bilaspur in the state of Chhattisgarh. 


And, whereas the substance of the said notification 20 A was published as per section 20A sub section (4) in 
the daily Newspapers, Haribhumi (Hindi) Bilaspur Edition, Nai Duniya (Hindi) Bilaspur Edition and The Hitwad 
(English) Raipur Edition on 31.01.2024 whereas, Tifra village land looser objections received within the set time 
period and disposed of by the Competent Authority under section 20D of the said Act,

And, whereas, in pursuance of subsection (1) of section 20E of the said act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted his report to the central Government; Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent 
Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub section (1) of the section 20E of the said act 1989, the 
central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; And, further, in pursuance of subsection (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all Encumbrances.
