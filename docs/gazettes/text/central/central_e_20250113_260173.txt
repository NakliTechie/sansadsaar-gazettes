S.O. 97(E).—Whereas on the publication of the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of

land and all rights in or over the land described in the schedule appended to the said notification (hereinafter referred 
to as the said land) vested absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances under Sub-Section (l) of
