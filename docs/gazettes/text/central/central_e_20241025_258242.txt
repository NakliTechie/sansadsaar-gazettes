MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th October, 2024 


9.0. 4665(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport and Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 28II (A) Dated 8 July 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ID) issued under sub-section () of section 
3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building 
(widening / four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.752C in the stretch 
of land from Km. 0+000 to 40+500 Km. Jirapur to Susner MP-RJ-Border- Road in the District-Rajgarh, State of 
Madhya Pradesh. Hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in ““Naiduniya” and “Free Press” 
both dated 29 July 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
