समाधान हो जाने के पश्चात्‌ कि महाराष्ट्र राज्य के नागपूर जिले में नागपूर शहर के चारो ओर बाहरी रिंग रोड के कि.मी. 
34.000 से कि.मी. 62.035 तक के भूखंड (फेटरी-भरतवाडा-नारा-भिलगांव-पावनगांव-धारगांव सेक्षन), के निर्माण 
(चौड़ा करने/चार लेन का बनाने आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंध और प्रचालन के निमार्ण के लोक प्रयोजन के लिए वह भूमि 
अपेक्षित है, जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया गया है, ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा 
करती है। 

कोई व्यक्ति, जो उक्त भूमि में हितबद्ध है, उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3ग की उप-धारा (1) के अधीन पुर्वोक्त 
प्रयोजन के लिए ऐसी भूमि के उपयोग पर राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से इक्कीस दिन के भीतर आक्षेप 
कर सकेगा।

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi the 14th August, 2023 
S.O. 3634(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, of the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening /Four / laning, etc.) of Outer Ring Road around Nagpur City, from Km. 34.000 to Km. 62.035 (Fetri- 


Bharatwada-Nara-Bhilgaon-Pawangaon-Dhargaon Section) in district Nagpur, the State of Maharashtra, hereby 
declares its intention to acquire such land, 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section(1) of section 3C of the said Act, 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, the Deputy Collector Land 
Acquisition (General) Nagpur, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the said competent authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final.
