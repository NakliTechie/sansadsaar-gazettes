MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 9th April, 2026 


5.0. 2252(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred under clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act, 2008, the General Manager, East Central Railway, hereby accords approval to declare the project 
titled “ New BG Rail line between Pariya (Km 0.00) and Chatra (Km 75.l) along with Y connection at Kastha 
(4.95Km)” under East Central Railway as a Special Railway Project. 


Accordingly, the said project “New BG Rail line between Pariya (Km 0.00) and Chatra (Km 75.l) along 
with Y connection at Kastha (4.95Km)” under East Central Railway is hereby declared as a Special Railway Project in 
the State of Bihar and Jharkhand.
