MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th May, 2025 


S.O. 2308(E).— Whereas by notification of Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural 
Gas, number S.O. l086(E) dated 28-02-2025, issued under sub-section () of Section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Government of India had declared its intention to acquire the Right of User in land, specified in the Schedule appended 
to that notification for transportation of Gaseous Ethylene from Hazira Manufacturing Division (HMD) to Dahej 


[भाग ave 3(i)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Manufacturing Division (DMD) of M/s. Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) in Gujarat, an extension pipeline (HDPL 
Extn.2) to the existing Hazira — Dahej Natural Gas Pipeline (HDPL) should be laid by M/s. Reliance Industries Limited; 


And whereas, the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public from 
2-03-2025 to 7-03-2025; 


And whereas, the objections received from the public to the laying of the said pipeline have been considered 
and disallowed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, the Competent Authority has under sub-section () of Section 6 of the said Act, submitted report 
to the Government of India; 


And whereas, the Government of India, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the said pipeline, has decided to acquire the Right of User therein, free from all encumbrances; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 6 of the said Act, the 
Government of India hereby declares that the Right of User in the land, specified in the Schedule, appended to this 
notification, is hereby acquired for laying the said pipeline; 


And further, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of Section 6 of the said Act, the Government 
of India hereby directs that the Right of User in the said land for laying the pipeline shall, instead of vesting in the 
Government of India, vest on the date of publication of the declaration, in M/s. Reliance Industries Limited, free from 
all encumbrances.
