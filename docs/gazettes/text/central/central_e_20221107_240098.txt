MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
( Department of Commerce ) 

( DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF FOREIGN TRADE ) 


S.O. 5206 ( E ) .– In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 & 9 of FT ( D & R ) Act , 1992 , read with 
paragraph 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy , 2015-2020 , as amended from time to time , the Central 
Government hereby amends the Policy Condition No. 7 ( ii ) of Chapter 27 of Schedule - I ( Import Policy ) of ITC 
( HS ) , 2022 : 


Existing Policy Condition 

The importer can apply for registration not earlier than 
60th day and not later than 15th day before the 
expected date of arrival of import consignment . The 
Automatic Registration Number shall remain valid for 
a period of 75 days . Importer shall have to enter the 
Registration Number and expiry date of Registration in 
the Bill of Entry to enable Customs for clearance of 
consignment . 

Effect of the Notification : 


Revised Policy Condition 

The importer can apply for registration not earlier 
than 60th day and not later than 5 days before the 
expected date of arrival of import consignment . The 
Automatic Registration Number shall remain valid for 
a period of 75 days . Importer shall have to enter the 
Registration Number and expiry date of Registration in 
the Bill of Entry to enable Customs for clearance of 
consignment . 


The Policy Condition No. 7 ( ii ) of Chapter- 27 of Schedule - I ( Import Policy ) of ITC ( HS ) , 2022 has been
