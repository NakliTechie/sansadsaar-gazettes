STAT के खण्ड (अ) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, भारत सरकार के सड़क परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की 
अधिसूचना संख्या का. आ. 1467 (अ), तारीख 28 मार्च, 2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3,

208.00 तक पढ़ा जाये।

विधिक पेशेवर द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आपत्तियों की सुनवाई के पश्चात्‌ तथा ऐसी और जाँच करने के 
पश्चात्‌, यदि कोई हो, जिसे सक्षम प्राधिकारी आवश्यक समझे, आदेश द्वारा या तो आपत्तियों को अनुज्ञात कर सकेगा या

S.O. 2204(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of Section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956), the following amendments after passing a speaking order in the notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.1467 (E) dated the 28 March, 
2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), ) dated the 28 March, 2023 
namely:- 


In the said notification, 


In the schedule of the said notification, instead of Km 208.815 to Km 208.00, read Km 202.815 to 
Km 208.00. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Sub Divisional Officer (C), 
Mandi in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 
disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
