ETHYLENE DICHLORIDE (QUALITY CONTROL) AMENDMENT ORDER, 2025 


S.O. 1126(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 read with sub-section (3) of section 25 of 
the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central Government, after consulting the Bureau of 
Indian Standards, being of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do in the public interest, hereby makes 
the following Order further to amend the Ethylene Dichloride (Quality Control) Order, 2021, namely: - 


1. (1) This order may be called the Ethylene Dichloride (Quality Control) Amendment Order, 2025. 
(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Ethylene Dichloride (Quality Control) Order, 2021, in paragraph 1, for sub-paragraph (2), the following 
sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely: — 


(2) It shall come into force on the 12" day of September, 2025.” 
[F. No. PCT-5901 1/48/2024-PC.TECH.-CPC]
