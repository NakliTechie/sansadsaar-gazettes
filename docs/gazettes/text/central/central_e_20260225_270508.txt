5.0. 1021(E).— Whereas M/s Solarcraft Power India 7 Private Limited (SPI7PL) having its registered address 
at 109, First Floor, Rishabh IPEX Mall, IP Extension Patparganj, Delhi — 110092, India has applied for authorization 
under Section 164 of the Electricity Act, 2003 for laying of overhead transmission line under the transmission scheme 
“Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s Solarcraft Power India 7 Private Limited for its proposed Wind 
Power Project in Kalaburagi District, Karnataka.” 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/117/2024-PG dated 26.12.2024 
had granted prior approval under section 68(1) of the Electricity Act, 2003 for the overhead line covered under the 
transmission scheme “Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s Solarcraft Power India 7 Private Limited 
for its proposed Wind Power Project in Kalaburagi District, Karnataka.” 


M/s Solarcraft Power India 7 Private Limited had published notice for transmission scheme in local newspapers 
Dakshin Bharat Rashtramat and Navbharat (both in Hindi) dated 06.10.2025, The New Indian Express and The Free 
Press Journal (both in English) dated 06.10.2025, Kannada Prabha (in Kannada) dated 06.10.2025, Saamana (in Marathi) 
dated 06.10.2025, and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 18.10.2025 for the general public to make 
observations/representations on the proposed transmission route within 2 Months from the date of publication. 
Subsequently, M/s Solarcraft Power India 7 Private Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 23.01.2026 declaring that 
no observation/ representation was received within two months from the date of Publication in the Newspaper/official 
Gazette of Government of India.

Act, 2003, which the telegraph authority possess under the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 with respect to the placing of 
telegraph lines and posts for the purpose of a telegraph established or maintained by Government or to be so established
