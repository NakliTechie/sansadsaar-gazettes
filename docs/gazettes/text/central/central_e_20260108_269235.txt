MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th January, 2026 


S.O. 139(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 3431 (E) Dated:25/07/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(11)] 


(1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for construction of Bridges at 23rd km of NH-220 in the stretch of land from Km. 22.082 to Km. 
22.524 in the district of SARAIKELA KHARSAWAN in the state of JHARKHAND. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Prabhat Khabar” and “Dainik 
Jagran” dated 24/11/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
