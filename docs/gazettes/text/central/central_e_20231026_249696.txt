The anti-dumping duty imposed under S.N. 93 to S.N. 99 under this notification shall not apply, if — 
the goods are imported by a manufacturer of goods, other than those making jute sacking bags; 


the importer follows the procedure set out in the Customs (Import of Goods at Concessional Rate of Duty or 
for Specified End Use) Rules, 2022; and 


the importer furnishes an undertaking to the Deputy Commissioner of Customs or the Assistant 
Commissioner of Customs, as the case may be, stating as under: - 


the intended use for imported goods; 
that the imported jute sacking cloth shall not be used for conversion to jute sacking bag; and 


that in case he fails to comply with sub-clause (b), he shall pay an amount equal to the duty leviable, on the 
goods imported, under this notification.”;
