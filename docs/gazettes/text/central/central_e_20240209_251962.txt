MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 8th February, 2024 


S.O. 587(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (North 
Western Railway, Construction organisation), Notification number, S.O. 488] (E) dated the 097 November, 2023 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 097 November, 2023 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (Il of 2008) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely special railway project of "Taranga Hill - 
Abu Road via Ambaji New BG Line (6.654 Km)" in District Banaskantha in the state of Gujarat. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely —Gujarat 
Samachar, —Sandesh and —The Times of India Dated 027 December-2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


And whereas, No objections were received by competent Authority within due time; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग IL—ve 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
