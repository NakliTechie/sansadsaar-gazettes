New Delhi, the 16th July, 2025 


F. No. A-35013/02/2020-Ad-III/147.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri J. Nivas, IAS 
(AP: 2010) an officer belonging to Andhra Pradesh Cadre of Indian Administrative Service as Director of 
Census Operations/Director of Citizen Registration, Andhra Pradesh with effect from 20.06.2025 (FN) for a 


period upto 31.12.2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation under the Central 
Staffing Scheme.
