MINISTRY OF COAL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2023. 


G.S.R. 394(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) and clauses (d), (da), (db) and (dc) of 
sub-section (2) of section 13 of the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the 
Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Coal Blocks Allocation Rules, 2017, 
namely:— 


1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Coal Blocks Allocation (Amendment) 
Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Coal Blocks Allocation Rules, 2017, in rule 5, in sub-rule (5), in clause (c), in sub-clause (11), the words 
“in the form of bank guarantee” shall be omitted. 


[F. No. 11022/01/2015-CA-IIT] 
M. NAGARAJU, Addl. Secy.

dated the 18" May, 2020.
