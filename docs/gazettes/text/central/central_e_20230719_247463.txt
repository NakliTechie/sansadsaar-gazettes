MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Economic Affairs) 
(BUDGET DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 19th July, 2023 


Subject : Cancellation of a part of outstanding stock of Non-interest bearing Government of India IDBI Special 
Security, 2024. 


F. No. 4(4)-B(W&M)/2018.—Government of India, hereby notifies cancellation of Non-interest bearing 
Government of India IDBI Special Security, 2024 (hereinafter briefly described as “the Special Security”) for an 
aggregate amount of Rupees one thousand sixty four crore and twenty seven lakhs only (1064.27 crore) [nominal]. 

2. Date of Cancellation 
The date of cancellation of the Special Security by the Government of India shall be the March 31, 2022. 
By Order of the President of India, 


ASHISH VACHHANT, Addl. Secy.
