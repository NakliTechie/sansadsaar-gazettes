MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2026 


5.0. 2736(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, number S.O. 634 (E), dated 06.02.2026 , published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub- section () issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 
(48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc), 
maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No 36] in the stretch of land from Km. 320.580 to 
Km. 400.575 (Mahagaon-Yavatmal Section) in village Jawla & Kosdani Taluka Arni District Yavatmal in the State 
of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in " Sakal" and " Lokmat Samachar" 
both dated 08.03.2026, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said, Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
