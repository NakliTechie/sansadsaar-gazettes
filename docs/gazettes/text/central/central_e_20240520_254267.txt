फाइल संख्या एनए-39/10/2024-पोषण प्रशासन.---जबकि खाद्य एवं पोषण बोर्ड (एफएनबी) की स्थापना कृषि 
मंत्रालय (खाद्य विभाग) के अंतर्गत 1964 में की गई थी और बाद में, वर्ष 1993 में केन्द्र सरकार के एक विभाग की सभी 
शक्तियों के साथ इसे महिला एवं बाल विकास मंत्रालय को हस्तांतरित कर दिया गया था। खाद्य एवं पोषण बोर्ड 
(एफएनबी) इस मंत्रालय की एक तकनीकी शाखा थी जिसकी केन्द्र में राष्ट्रव्यापी व्यवस्था और चार क्षेत्रीय कार्यालय तथा 


फरीदाबाद, मुंबई, कोलकाता एवं चेन्नई में चार गुणवत्ता नियंत्रण प्रयोगशालाएं और 30 राज्यों/संघ राज्य क्षेत्रों में 43 
सामुदायिक खाद्य तथा पोषण विस्तार इकाइयां थीं। पोषण बोर्ड (एफएनबी) मुख्यालय,अपने चार क्षेत्रीय कार्यालयों के 
माध्यम से सामुदायिक खाद्य तथा पोषण विस्तार इकाइयों (सीएफएनईयूएस), जो समुदाय के लिए पोषण विस्तार 
कार्यक्रम संचालित करती थीं, को तकनीकी सहायता के साथ-साथ प्रचालन (लॉजिस्टिक) सहायता उपलब्ध कराता था। 


जबकि, भारत सरकार के प्रधान आर्थिक सलाहाकर द्वारा सरकारी निकायों के युक्तिकरण पर नंबर, 2020 में 
तैयार की गई रिपोर्ट में एफएनबी को हमेशा के लिए बंद करने की अनुशंसा की गई थी। 


जबकि, मंत्रिमंडल की दिनांक 06.4.2023 को आयोजित बैठक में लिए गए निर्णय के अनुपालन में इस मंत्रालय 
ने निम्नलिखित कार्रवाई की है:-

MINISTRY OF WOMEN AND CHILD DEVELOPMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th May, 2024 


F.No. NA-39/10/2024-NUTRITION ADMN.-Whereas Food and Nutrition Board (FNB) was set up in 1964 
under the Ministry of Agriculture (Department of Food) and was later transferred to the Ministry of Women and Child 
Development in 1993 with all the powers of a Central Government Department. Food and Nutrition Board, was a 
technical wing of the Ministry with a countrywide set-up at the Centre having four Regional Offices and four Quality 
Control Laboratories (QCL) located at Faridabad, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai with 43 Community Food and 
Nutrition Extension Units (CFNEUs) located in 30 States/UTs. FNB Headquarter, through its four Regional Offices, 
provided the technical as well as logistic support for the functioning of CFNEUs which conducted Nutrition Extension 
programmes for the Community. 


Whereas, the report prepared by Principal Economic Adviser (Pr.EA) to the Government of India in November, 
2020 on Rationalization of Government Bodies recommended closing down the FNB. 


Whereas, in compliance of the decision taken by the Cabinet in its meeting held on 06.04.2023, the Ministry has 
taken the following action:-

Out of 147 employees of FNB, 33 employees opted and were allowed voluntary retirement under Special 
Voluntary Retirement Scheme (SVRS). Further, 7 Group ‘A’ technical officers of FNB have been sent on 
deemed deputation to NIPCCD for Nutrition Vertical while 61 Group ‘B’& Group ‘C’ Technical Staff of 
FNB have been sent on deemed deputation to The Food Corporation of India. The remaining 46 Non- 
Technical Staff have been placed on the rolls of Surplus Cell created in the Ministry. 


Three CFNEUs located at Mumbai, Jaipur and Bhubaneswar have been transferred to Directorate of Estate, 
under MoHUA. 


Further, four immovable properties located at Faridabad, Chennai, Navi Mumbai and Kolkata are under 
process for transfer. Out of these four properties, Central Food Testing Laboratory (CFTL), Faridabad and 
Regional Food Testing Laboratory (RFTL), Chennai are owned by FNB/Ministry and are being transferred to 
FCI/ Department of Food and Public Distribution. While, RFTL, Navi Mumbai and RFTL, West Bengal are 
leased properties and will be leased out to FCI/ Department of Food and Public Distribution after obtaining 
NOC from States of Maharashtra and West Bengal. All three RFTL and one CFTL have been handed over to 
FCI on ‘as is where is' basis. 


Whereas the activities of the Food and Nutrition Board have been closed vide Gazette Notification dated 


16.06.2021. 


Whereas, the dissolution/closure of FNB have been approved vide MWCD order No. NA-39/9/2020/ 


NUTRITION ADMN(e-878 16) dated 30.11.2023. 


In view of the above, FNB stands closed w.e.f. 01.12.2023.
