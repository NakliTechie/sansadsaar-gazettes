बोटाड पीपल्स को-ऑपरेटिव बैंक लिमिटेड, बोटाड, पर लागू नहीं होंगे।

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the [70 January, 2024 


DoR.MON. No. &5972/ 2.2.26/2023-24.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 
36 A (2) read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 949, Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies 
that ‘The Botad Peoples Co-operative Bank Ltd., Botad’ has ceased to be a co-operative bank within the 
meaning of the said Act and all the provisions of the said Act applicable to The Botad Peoples 
Co-operative Bank Ltd., Botad shall cease to apply to it, except as respects things done or omitted to be 
done before this notice. 


JAYANT KUMAR DASH, Executive Director
