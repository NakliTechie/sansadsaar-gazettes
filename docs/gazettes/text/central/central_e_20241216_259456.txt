S.O. 5436 (E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of Section 3 of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby 
authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of schedule annexed here to as the competent authority to perform the 
function of such authorities under the said Act with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land _ specified in the corresponding entry in 
column (3) of the said schedule relating to state, district, Taluk, and villages mentioned in column (4), (5), 
(6) and (7) respectively of the said schedule for Project management, operation including construction of 
the National Highway 105 (New NH-527B) (Darbhanga - Jainagar) in the state of Bihar for up-gradation to Two 
Lane/Four Lane with Paved shoulder configurations as specified in the Schedule given below.
