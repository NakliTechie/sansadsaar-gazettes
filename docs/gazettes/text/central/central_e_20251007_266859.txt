MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(S.E.C. Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Bilaspur, the 3rd October, 2025 


Notice under Section 20 A of the Railways Act 1989 


S.O. 4574(E).— In exercise of Powers conferred by sub section (1) of section 20A of the Railways 
Act 1989 (24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said act),the Central Government, after being satisfied that 
for the public purpose, the land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto is 
required for execution of Special Railway project, namely “Bilaspur Divn.: Construction of RUB (by 
BOX Pushing method) in lieu of manned LC No.354 at Km.694/21-23 bet. JAIRAMNAGAR- 
AKALTARA.” in the state of Chhattisgarh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; Any person 
interested in the said land may, within a period of 30 days for the date of publication of this notification in 
the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section 
(1) of section 20D of the said act; Every objection shall be made to the competent Authority, namely, Sub 
Divisional Officer, Janjgir, SDM office, Janjgir , Chhattisgarh in writing ,and shall set out ground thereof 
and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or

enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the 
objections; Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section(2) of section 20D of the said 
Act shall be final; The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available 
and can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
