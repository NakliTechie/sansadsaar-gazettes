NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 5th February, 2025 
Notice Under Section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 


S.O. 856(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 
(24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act,) the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the land brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto is required for the execution of 
Special Railway Project, Boridand-Surajpur Doubling project (79.90 Km) situated in Chhattisgarh State in District — 
Manendragarh-chirimiri-Bharatpur, Surajpur and Koriya in the state of Chhattisgarh, hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may within a period of 30 days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every objection shall be made to the Competent Authority namely concerned Sub Divisional Officer 
(Revenue), Manendragarh, Baikunthpur and Surajpur, Chhattisgarh in writing and shall set out grounds thereof, and 
the competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal
