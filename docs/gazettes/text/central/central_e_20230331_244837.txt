New Delhi , the 31st March , 2023 
No. 21 / 2023 - Customs ( N.T. ) 

S.O. 1567 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause ( b ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 1 of 
the Finance Act , 2023 ( 08 of 2023 ) , the Central Government hereby appoints -

section 135 of the said Act shall come into force ;

said Act shall come into force .
