S.O. 389(E).— Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 30 of the National 
Institutes of Technology, Science Education and Research Act, 2007 (29 of 2007) (herein after referred to as the ‘Act’), 
the Central Government vide Notification No.S.0.893 (8), dated the 7" April, 2008, published in the Gazette of India, 
established ‘The Council’ for all the Institutes specified in the ‘First Schedule’, ‘Second Schedule’ and ‘Third Schedule’ 
of the Act. 


2 And whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred under clause (j) of sub-section (2) of section 30 of the Act, 
the following Members of Parliament have been chosen as the members of the National Institutes of Technology, 
Science Education and Research Council by the Lok Sabha: 


(i) Shri Shashank Mani, Member, Lok Sabha; and 
(ii) Shri Rakesh Rathor, Member, Lok Sabha. 


3. The term of office of, vacancies among, and allowances payable to members of Council and the functions of
