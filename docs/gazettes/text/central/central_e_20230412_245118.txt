सा.का.नि. 286 ( अ ) . – खादी और ग्रामोद्योग आयोग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 61 ) की धारा 26 द्वारा प्रदत्त 
शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , केंद्र सरकार एतद्वारा खादी और ग्रामोद्योग आयोग नियम , 2006 में संशोधन करने के लिए 
निम्नलिखित नियम बनाती है , नामत : 

संक्षिप्त नाम और प्रारंभ.- ( 1 ) इन नियमों को खादी और ग्रामोद्योग आयोग ( संशोधन ) नियम , 2023 कहा जाएगा । 
( 2 ) ये नियम सरकारी राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तारीख से लागू होंगे । 

2. खादी और ग्रामोद्योग आयोग नियम , 2006 में नियम 5 के उप - नियम ( 2 ) के बाद , निम्नलिखित उप - नियम अंतर्विष्ट 
किया जाएगा , नामत :

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 11th April , 2023 

G.S.R. 286 ( E ) . – In exercise of the powers conferred by section 26 of the Khadi and Village Industries 
Commission Act , 1956 ( 61 of 1956 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the Khadi 
and Village Industries Commission Rules , 2006 , namely : 

1 . 


Short title and commencement .- ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Khadi and Village Industries 
Commission ( Amendment ) Rules , 2023 . 


( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Khadi and Village Industries Commission Rules , 2006 , after sub - rule ( 2 ) of rule 5 , the following sub 
rule shall be inserted , namely : - 


" ( 3 ) ( a ) If the Chairman dies or resigns or is removed from office or for any other reason unable to perform 
the duties of his office , the Government may designate one of the members appointed under clause ( a ) of sub 
section ( 2 ) of section 4 to be the Chairman until a Chairman is appointed by the Government under sub 
section ( 3 ) of section 4 of the Act ; 


( b ) If the Chairman is unable to discharge his functions owing to absence , illness or any other reason , the 
Government may designate one of the members appointed under clause ( a ) of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 4 to 
be the Chairman until the regular Chairman resumes his duties . "
