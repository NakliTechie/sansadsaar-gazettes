G.S.R. 70(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of the 
Coinage Act, 20II(! of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:- 


l. Short title and commencement.- () These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of 2800" Nirvan Kalyanak of Parshvanath Bhagwan) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.- The coin of eight hundred rupees denomination shall only be coined at the Mint for issue 
under the authority of the Central Government to commemorate on the occasion of 2800" Nirvan Kalyanak of 
Parshvanath Bhagwan.
