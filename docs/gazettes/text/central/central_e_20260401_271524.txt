MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 1st April, 2026 


5.0. 1694(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (6) of section 4A of the Carriage 
by Air Act, 1972 (69 of 1972), and pursuant to rule 24 of Chapter III of the Third Schedule to the said Act, the 
Central Government, having regard to the provisions of the said Act, and being satisfied that it is necessary 
or expedient to give effect to the limits of liability as revised by the depository, hereby makes the following 
provision in the said Third Schedule with effect from the 287 December, 2024, namely:- 


1. Inrule 21, in sub-rule (1), for the words "one lakh twenty- eight thousand eight hundred twenty-one 
", the words " one lakh fifty-one thousand eight hundred eighty" shall be substituted; 


2. Inrule 22, - 


(a) in sub-rule (1), for the words " five thousand three hundred forty- six ", the words "six thousand 
three hundred three" shall be substituted; 


(b) in sub-rule (2), for the words " one thousand two hundred eighty- eight ", the words "one 
thousand five hundred nineteen" shall be substituted; and 


(c) in sub-rule (3), for the words " twenty-two ", the words "twenty-six" shall be substituted. 
[F. No. AV-11027/1/2025-A-MOCA] 
ASANGBA CHUBA AO, Jt. Secy. 


Note : The liability limits contained in rules 21 and 22 of the Third Schedule to the Carriage by Air Act, 
1972 were earlier revised vide notification number 5.0. 987 (E), dated the 02™ March, 2020.
