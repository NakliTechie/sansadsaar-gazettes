Subject: Extension in Minimum Import Price (MIP) Condition of specific items covered under Chapter 48 of 
ITC HS, 2022, Schedule -I (Import Policy)-regarding. 


5.0. 2170(E). —: In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 and Section 5 of Foreign Trade (Development 
& Regulation) Act, 1992, read with paragraph 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, as amended from time 
to time, and in continuation of Notification No. 26/2025-26 dated 22.08.2025, Notification No. 69/2025-26 dated 
31.03.2026 read with Policy Circular No. 04/2025-26 dated 03.09.2026, the Central Government hereby extends the 
Minimum Import (MIP) condition of specific items covered under Chapter 48 of ITC HS, 2022, Schedule -I (Import 
Policy), for a period up to 30.09.2026 with all other terms and conditions remaining same as in the Notification No. 
26/2025-26 dated 22.08.2025. 


Effect of the Notification: Minimum Import Price (MIP) @ INR 67,220 per MT on Cost, Insurance, and Freight 
(CIF) value, imposed on import of Virgin Multi-layer Paper Board (VPB) under ITC (HS) Codes 48059100, 
48059200, 48059300, 48109200 and 48109900 has been extended for the period up to 30.09.2026. 


This is issued with the approval of the Minister of Commerce & Industry.
