4. Main components of the Scheme are: Assistance for conduct of National Championships both for Men and 
Women in Senior, Junior and Sub Junior age categories for meeting expenses towards Boarding & Lodging, Hiring of 
FOP/Equipment, Accessibility Audit, Accessible Transportation, Payment of Technical Officials and Prize Money; 
Assistance for National Games towards sports equipment, Timing, Scoring and Result (TSR) services, remuneration to 
Technical officials and technical conduct; Allocation of funds for grassroot development; Assistance for hosting of 
international tournaments in India for meeting expenses towards Boarding, lodging & transportation, Rent of playfields, 
Cost of consumable equipment, Certificates, medals and awards, Prize money, Payment of remuneration and travel to 
Technical Officials/Referees/ Umpire/Jury, Hosting / competition sanction fee, Uniform for technical officials/ referees/ 
Umpire/Jury, Dope testing and any other statutory expenses, Event insurance, Expense on electricity incurred during 
the conduct of the international event, Accessibility Audit and Accessible Transportation; Assistance for engagement 
of High Performance Directors, Foreign Coaches, Foreign Experts, National Chief Coach, other Coaches; Assistance to 
NSFs for engagement of support personnel such as sports medicine doctors, physiotherapists, Psychologist etc; 
Assistance for National Camps/Training in Academies; Training of the Elite athletes at the Olympic Training Centres; 
Assistance towards Travel from place of stay to national camp/foreign training/ competition & back; Assistance towards 
boarding & lodging etc. for national camps and non-camp days dietary allowance for focus group athletes; Assistance 
towards Field of play charges for camps / Championships; Assistance towards Sports kit for campers; Assistance 
towards Medical Insurance for campers; Assistance towards air travel for foreign training / competition exposures; 
Assistance towards deputation of referees/ judges/ umpires/Technical officials for competitions abroad; Assistance 
towards boarding & lodging abroad and payment of out of pocket allowances; Assistance towards Ceremonial Dress 
and Kit Charges for multi-sports events; Assistance towards transportation, hiring of equipment, infrastructure, 
consumables; Assistance towards training of Sports Scientists, coaches and support personnel; Expenditure on dope 
control; Purchase of equipment; Assistance for indigenous sports for global outreach; Assistance for Cultural Exchange 
and International Cooperation; Support for engagement of administrative manpower; Assistance towards Medical 
Assessment and sports science protocols; Engagement of Project Management Units (PMUs) in the Department for 
managing specific issues relating to planning, coordination with national and international stakeholders, having bearing 


on international standing of the country in the comity of sporting nations. 


5. Proposals of NSFs for training, coaching, participation in international events, hosting of international sports 
events, engagement of coaches and support personnel etc are considered in meetings of the Annual Calendar of Training 


and Competitions (ACTC), chaired by Secretary (Sports).
