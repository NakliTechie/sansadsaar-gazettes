NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th February, 2026 

S.O. 674(E).— Whereas, M/s. Cognizant Technology Solutions India Private Limited, had proposed under 
section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a 
Special Economic Zone for Information Technology and Information Technology Enabled Services at Survey 


No. 115/1, 115/24, 115/25, 115/26 and 155/30, Nanakramguda Village, Serilingampally Mandal, Ranga Reddy 
District, in the State of Telangana; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, had notified an area of 2.56 hectares at 
the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number S.O. 3463(E) dated 
870 November, 2016; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Cognizant Technology Solutions India Private Limited has now proposed to de-notify 
the entire area of 2.56 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Telangana has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal vide 
letter No.1 780/ITE&C/Prom/2015 dated 2™ September, 2024. Further, after de-notification, such de-notified area will 
conform to land use guidelines/master places of the State Government; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Visakhapatnam Special Economic Zone 145 
recommended the proposal for de-notification of the entire area of 2.56 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in the exercise of the powers conferred by the first proviso to rule 8 of the Special Economic 
Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except for things done or omitted to 
be done before such rescission.
