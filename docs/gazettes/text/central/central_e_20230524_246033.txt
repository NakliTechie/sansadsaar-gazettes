MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(CENTRAL RAILWAY) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 22nd May 2023 

99.0. 2266(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 5146 (E) dated the 7" November, 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 77 November, 2022 (herein after referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway (Amendment) 
Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely “Wardha —Yavatmal- Nanded New Railway line” in 
the district of Washim in the State of Maharashtra; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Daily 
Matrubhumi”, “ Daily Vidarbha Matdar” Dated 157 February, 2022 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas no objection were received and accordingly the orders have been passed by Competent 
Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 


declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
