F. No. 18-20/Govt./2024-INC.— In the Withdrawal Order of the Indian Nursing Council published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part III - Section 4, No. 88, dated February 5, 2026, the following corrections are 
hereby made: 

On Page 1, in the Hindi version, the date "नई दिल्‍ली 3 जनवरी, 2026" shall be read as "नई दिल्‍ली, 3 फरवरी, 


2026". (Correspondingly, on Page 2 in the English version, the typographical error "3rd Febuary, 2026" shall be read 
as "3rd February, 2026"). 


On Page 1 (Hindi version) and Page 2 (English version), the heading 
"अंतरराष्ट्रीय वित्तीय सेवा केंद्र प्राधिकरण (निधि प्रबंधन) (संशोधन) विनियम, 2026" and “International Financial

be treated as deleted.
