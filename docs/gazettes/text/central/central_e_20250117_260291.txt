G.S.R. 65 (E)— In exercise of the powers conferred under sub-sections (1) and (2) of 


section 22 of the Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995 (7 of 1995), the Central 
Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Cable Television Networks 
Rules, 1994, namely:- 


1. 


Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called the Cable Television 
Networks (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


In the Cable Television Networks Rules, 1994, (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in 
rule 2, - 


(i) after clause (aa), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:- 
“(ab) "designated portal" means the Broadcast Seva Portal or any other portal notified 
by the Central Government;”. 


(11) for clause (e), the following clause shall be substituted, namely: - 
“(e) “registering authority” means the registering authority notified under clause (h) 
of section 2 of the Act;” 

In the said rules, for rule 3, the following rule shall be substituted, namely: - 


“3. Application for registration as a local cable operator in India. - (1) Every application 
for registration as a local cable operator shall be made online and may be renewed after 
every five years. 


(2) The application for registration or renewal of registration shall be made online to the 
registering authority in Form 1. 


(3) Every application for registration or renewal of registration shall be accompanied by- 
(i) a processing fee of rupees five thousand only ; and 
(1) the requisite documents mentioned in Form 1; and, 
(iii) declaration in Form 2. 


(4) The processing fee for registration or renewal of registration shall be paid as per the 
option provided in the designated portal. 


(5) | The application for renewal of registration by a cable operator under rule 5 shall be 
made at least ninety days before the expiry of registration.

reasons furnished by the applicant, if such application is made before the expiry of 
registration.”. 


In the said rules, for rule 5, the following rule shall be substituted, namely: 

“5. Registration of local cable operator: - (1) On receipt of application for registration or 
renewal of registration as the case may be for local cable operator, complete in all respects, 
certified by the applicant and verified in an automated manner, the registering authority, may 
(a) if deemed necessary, undertake further verification on specific applications, selected on 
predetermined criteria to ensure that the applicant fulfils eligibility criteria under rule 2A and 
the requirements of rule 3; and 

(b) subject to the requirements of rule 3 and terms and conditions specified in rule 5A, issue 
certificate of registration or renewal of registration, as the case may be, in Form 3. 

(2) | Where the registering authority is satisfied that registration or renewal of registration 
cannot be granted, it shall inform the applicant of the reasons for such refusal. 

(3) The applicant may file an appeal to the Central Government as referred to in second 
proviso to sub-section (5) of section 4 of the Act against the refusal of grant or renewal of 
registration online within a period of thirty days of receipt of communication of such refusal. 
(4) The registration granted to local cable operators shall be valid throughout the 
territory of India.” 

In the said rules, in rule 11C, after sub-rule (2), the following proviso shall be inserted

“Provided that the applicant may prefer an appeal against the order of the registering
