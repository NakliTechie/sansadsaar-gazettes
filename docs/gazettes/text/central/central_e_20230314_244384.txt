New Delhi , the 14th March , 2023 

G.S.R. 187 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section 1 of Section 3 of the Major Port 
Authorities Act , 2021 and in supersession of the Notification of the Government of India , Ministry of Ports , Shipping 
& Waterways ( PHRD Division ) No.G.S.R.630 ( E ) dated 16th August , 2022 , the Central Government hereby appoints 
Shri Binod Kumar , IAS , Principal Secretary , Transport Department , Government of West Bengal , in place of 
Shri Rajesh Kumar Sinha , IAS , Former Principal Secretary , Transport Department , Government of West Bengal , as a 
Member of the Board of Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Authority under Section 3 ( 1 ) ( c ) ( i ) of Major Port Authorities 
Act , 2021 with immediate effect and until further orders .
