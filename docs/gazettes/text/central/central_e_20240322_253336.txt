Subject: - Amendment in export policy of Onions. 


S.O. 1541(E).: — S.O. (E): In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 of the Foreign 
Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), as amended, read with Para 1.02 and 2.01 of the 
Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, the Central Government herebyamends Notification No. 49 dt. 07.12.2023 regarding 
Export Policy of Onions in Chapter 07 of Schedule 2 of the ITC (HS) Export Policy, as under: 


0703 10 19 Prohibited till 31.03.2024] Prohibited until further orders. 


Dy Provisions under Para-3 of Notification No. 49 dt. 07.12.2023 remains unchanged. 


3. Effect of this Notification: 


Export prohibition on export of onions under HS Code 0703 10 19 valid till 31% March, 2024 is extended until further 
orders.
