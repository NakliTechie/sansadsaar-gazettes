MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd November, 2023 


S.O. 5033(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 1 of the Employees’ State 
Insurance Act, 1948 (34 of 1948), the Central Government hereby appoints the lstday of December, 2023, as the date 


on which the provisions of the said Act, namely:- 
(i) sections 38 to 43 and section 45A to section 45H of Chapter IV; 
(ii) sections 46 to 73 of Chapter V; and 


(11). sections 74, 75, sub-sections (2) to (4) of section 76, 80, 82 and section 83 of Chapter VI, 


shall come into force in all areas of Mainpuri district, in addition to the already notified areas of the said district and in 


all the areas of Basti district, in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


[F. No. S-38013/15/2023-SS-I]
