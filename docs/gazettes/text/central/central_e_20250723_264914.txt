S.O. 3378(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 read with sub-section (3) of section 25 of 
the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central Government being of the opinion that it is necessary 
so to do in the public interest, after consulting the Bureau of Indian Standards, hereby withdraws the Acetic Acid 
(Quality Control) Order, 2019, and for the said purpose, rescinds the notification of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers issued vide number S.O. 2791(E), dated the 5" August, 2019, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), with effect from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette. 


[F. No. C-II-13012/3/2021-CHEM. I-CPC] 
DEEPANKAR ARON, Jt. Secy.
