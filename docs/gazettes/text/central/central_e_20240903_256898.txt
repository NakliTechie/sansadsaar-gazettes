writing and shall set out the grounds thereof Competent Authority shall give the obector an opportunity of being hard, 
either in personor by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further 
enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objectins. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final.
