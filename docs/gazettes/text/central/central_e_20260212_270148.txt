MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |2th February, 2026 

S.O. 75(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder of Tura Bypass From Km. 0.00 To 29.030 Km.), maintenance, management 
and operation of Connecting Tura Bypass in the stretch of land from Km. 0 to Km. 29.030 (West Garo Hills) in the 
district of West Garo Hills in the state of MEGHALAYA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Additional Deputy Commissioner 
(Revenue) West Garo Hils, Tura in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give 
the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 

Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
