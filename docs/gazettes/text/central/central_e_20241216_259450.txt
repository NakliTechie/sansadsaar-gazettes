MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
(Office of the Registrar General, India) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th December, 2024 
No. A-35013/02/2018-Ad-III/1735.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri Karma R. Bonpo, 
IAS (SK:2010) an officer belonging to Sikkim Cadre of Indian Administrative Service as Director of Census 
Operations/Director of Citizen Registration, Sikkim with effect from 26.11.2024 (FN) for a period of upto 31.12.2025 


or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation under the Central Staffing Scheme. 


2. The Headquarters of Shri Karma R. Bonpo, IAS (SK:2010) will be Gangtok (Sikkim).
