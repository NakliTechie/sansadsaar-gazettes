New Delhi, the 24th August, 2026 


S.O. 466(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 95.00. 4729 (E) Dated:!7/l0/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder /four-laning, etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of Realignment of stretch of land from Km. 234 to Km. 235.08 of the NH-38 for permanent 
Rectification of Black spots TN-(02)-44, by Construction of Left Main Carriageway Realignment proposal on near 
Eppothumvendran Seven Eye Bridge in Madurai to Tuticorin section of NH-38 (Old NH -45B) in the state of Tamil 
Nadu, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” 
dated 3/l/2025 and “Daily Thanthi” dated 3//2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
