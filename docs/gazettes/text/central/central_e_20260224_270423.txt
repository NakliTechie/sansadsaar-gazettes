MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 23rd February, 2026 


S.O. 959(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (373) of Section 2 of The Railways Act. 1989 
(24 of 1989) the Central Government hereby notifies the Railway project namely “Proposed construction of Road 
Over Bridge in lieu of manned level Crossing LC No: 94 in Bahanaga Bazar railway station Yard of 
Kharagpur Division” under South Eastern Railway as Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Balasore 
District in the state of Odisha, for eliminating the level crossings as a part of improving safety of Train/Road users and 


for providing national infrastructure for public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette.
