MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th August, 2026 


S.O. 444(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH 80 in the stretch of land from Km. [90.0000 
Km. 25.00 (including Sahibganj Bypass), and from Km. 260.00 to Km. 283.00 in the district of SAHIBGANJ in the 
state of JHARKHAND, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, District Land Acquisition Officer, 
Sahibganj in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector an 
opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and 
after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow 
the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
