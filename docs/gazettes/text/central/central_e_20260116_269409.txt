MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l6th January, 2026 
G.S.R. 4(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) read with clause (b) of sub-section (2) 


of section 60 of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, further 

to amend the Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket and Refund of Fare) Rules, 205, namely:— 

l. (4) These rules may be called the Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket and Refund of Fare) Amendment 
Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force with effect from 6" day of January, 2026. 


2. In the Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket and Refund of Fare) Rules, 205 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
tules), in rule 6, after sub-rule (3), the following sub-rules shall be inserted, namely: — 
“(4) In the case of Vande Bharat Sleeper Express, if a confirmed ticket is presented by the passenger or his 
representative for cancellation, the refund of fare shall be made after deducting cancellation charges from the fare 
as follows:— 
(a) if the ticket is presented for cancellation more than seventy-two hours in advance of the scheduled 
departure of the train, the cancellation charge shall be twenty five per cent. of the fare. 
(b) ifthe ticket is presented for cancellation between seventy-two hours and upto eight hours before the 
scheduled departure of the train, the cancellation charge shall be fifty per cent. of the fare. 
(c) if the ticket is presented for cancellation in less than eight hours of the scheduled departure of the 
train, no refund shall be granted. 
(5) Notwithstanding anything contained in these rules, wherever cancellation charges have been mentioned, the 
cancellation charges mentioned in sub-rule (4) shall be applicable in the case of Vande Bharat Sleeper Express 
tickets.”. 
3. In the said rules, in rule 8, after sub-rule (7), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 

“Provided that in case of Vande Bharat Sleeper Express, no refund of fare shall be admissible on the 
tickets in case the ticket is not cancelled or TDR not filed online upto eight hours before the scheduled 
departure of the train.”. 

4. In the said rules, in rule I], - 
(a) insub-rule (J), in clause (i), for the words “forty-eight hours”, the words “seventy-two hours in case of 
Vande Bharat Sleeper Express and Amrit Bharat II Express or forty-eight hours in case of other trains, 
as the case may be” shall be substituted. 
(b) in sub-rule (2), in clause (i), for the words “forty-eight hours”, the words “seventy-two hours in case of 
Vande Bharat Sleeper Express and Amrit Bharat II Express or forty-eight hours in case of other trains, 
as the case may be” shall be substituted. 
5. In the said rules, after rule 23, the following rules shall be inserted, namely:— 
“24. Application of other provisions for Vande Bharat Sleeper Express. — Except as otherwise provided in these 
rules, the provisions of rules |, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, l0, ll, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, I7, 8, 9, 2, 22 and rule 23 shall be 
applicable as per status of the ticket whether confirmed ticket or wait-listed ticket, as the case may be. 
25. Provisions for Amrit Bharat II Express. - The reserved ticket of Amrit Bharat II Express shall be governed 
by the rules as are applicable to the reserved ticket of Vande Bharat Sleeper Express: 
Provided that the unreserved ticket of Amrit Bharat II Express shall be governed by the provisions of 
tule 5 of these rules.”.
