No. PR/G/382/207/DD/2/208/DC/433/202.—In terms of the provisions of Section 2!B(3) of the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 949 read with Rule 8(7) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of Investigations of 
Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee has held CA. 
Joydeep Roy (Membership No. 30346), Annapurna Bhawan, Patra Para Balitikuri Kalitala, HOWRAH(West 
Bengal) 73, guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clauses (7) & (8) of Part I of 
Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in terms of Rule 
9(]) of the aforesaid Rules, ordered for removal of name of CA. Joydeep Roy (Membership No. 30346) from the 
Register of Members for a period of 02(Two) years and also imposed a fine of Rs. 25,000/-(Rupees Twenty Five 
Thousand) only to be paid within 90 days of receipt of the Order and in case of default in payment of fine within 
stipulated time, his name be removed for an additional period of !(One) month. Since the Respondent has failed to 
pay the imposed fine within stipulated time, in pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified under 
Regulation 8 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations 988, that the name of CA. Joydeep Roy (Membership No. 
30346), shall stand removed from the Register of Members for a consolidated period of 25(Twenty Five) 
months [02(Two) years plus additional 0](one) month] with effect from 6" April, 2024.
