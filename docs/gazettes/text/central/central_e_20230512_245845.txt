वहीं, भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 4947 की धारा 44 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए प्रधान सचिव 
(स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण), मध्य प्रदेश राज्य सरकार, कमरा संख्या 244, मंत्रालय (acy भवन) we few, 
भोपाल-462004, मध्य प्रदेश को संबोधित नोटिस संख्या i8-78 / 44559--आईएनसी दिनांक 27.07.2022 को जारी किया गया 
था। दिनांक 27.07.2022 को जारी नोटिस की एक प्रति प्रीति इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग एंड पैरामेडिकल साइंस, 760, स्नेह 
नगर, कमला नहरू नगर वार्ड, जबलपुर शहर, जिला जबलपुर-482002 (मध्य प्रदेश) को भी भेजी गई थी। जारी किए 
गए नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का 
अनुरोध किया गया था। 


चूंकि, प्रीति इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग एंड पैरामेडिकल साइंस, मध्य प्रदेश के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को राज्य सरकार 
से कोई अनुशंसा प्राप्त नहीं हुई है। यहां तक कि संस्था यानी प्रीति इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग एंड पैरामेडिकल साइंस ने भी 
जारी किए गए नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the ]lth May, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/4559-INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Preeti Institute of Nursing & Paramedical Science situated at 760, Sneh Nagar, Kamla Nehru 
Nagar Ward, Jabalpur City, Distt. Jabalpur — 482002, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of 
Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-l8/l4559-INC dated 27/07/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24!, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 27/07/2022 was also enclosed to Preeti Institute of Nursing & Paramedical Science situated at 760, 
Sneh Nagar, Kamla Nehru Nagar Ward, Jabalpur City, Distt. Jabalpur — 482002, Madhya Pradesh. In the 
notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Preeti Institute of Nursing & Paramedical Science, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Preeti Institute of 
Nursing & Paramedical Science has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
30/2/202l would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 6//2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) Preeti Institute of 
Nursing & Paramedical Science situated at 760, Sneh Nagar, Kamla Nehru Nagar Ward, Jabalpur City, Distt. 
Jabalpur — 482002, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject 
to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh.
