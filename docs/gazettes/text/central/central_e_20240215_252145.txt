New Delhi, the 5th February, 2024/Magha 26, 945 (Saka) 
The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 
i5th February, 2024 and is hereby published for general information: — 
THE CONSTITUTION (SCHEDULED CASTES AND 
SCHEDULED TRIBES) ORDERS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2024 
No. 6 oF 2024 

[5th February, 2024.] 


An Act further to amend the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 950 and 
the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 950 to modify the list of 
Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in relation to the State of Odisha. 


BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fifth Year of the Republic of India as 
follows:— 


. This Act may be called the Constitution (Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) Short title. 
Orders (Amendment) Act, 2024. 


2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,— Definitions. 


(a) “Scheduled Castes Order” means the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) 
C.O. 49. Order, 950; 


(b) “Scheduled Tribes Order” means the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) 
C.O. 22. Order, 950.
