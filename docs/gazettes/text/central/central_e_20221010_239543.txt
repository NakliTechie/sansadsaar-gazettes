सोसायटी , आनंद नगर रोड़ , ओल्ड हुबली , जिला धारावर - 580024 , कर्नाटक को भी भेजी गई थी । जारी नोटिस में राज्य 
सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद् को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का अनुरोध किया गया था । 


जबकि , राजीव गांधी स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग के मामले में परिषद राज्य सरकार से कोई सिफारिश नहीं मिली है । और 
राजीव गांधी स्कूल ऑफ नर्सिंग संस्थान ने भी जारी नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है । 


वहीं , परिषद् ने इस मामले पर समग्र विचार किया है । भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद् अधिनियम 1947 की धारा 13 
के तहत वैधानिक निरीक्षण से इनकार करना एक गंभीर मुद्दा है । केवल निरीक्षणों के आधार पर ही परिषद् नर्सिंग कोर्स 
संचालित करने के लिए नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के बारे में निर्णय लेने की स्थिति में होगी । निरीक्षण से इंकार करने का 
वास्तविक अर्थ यह होगा कि संस्थान के पास अपेक्षित शिक्षण , नैदानिक या आधारभूत सुविधाएं नहीं हैं ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F. No. 18-696 / 2000 - INC . In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Rajiv Gandhi School of Nursing situated at Hihgland , Al - Ameen Educational 
Society , Anand Nagar Road , Old Hubli , Distt . Dharwar - 580024 , Karnataka is recognized by State 
Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Nursing Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-696 / 2000 - INC / 12356 dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition 
under section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family 
Welfare Department , Government of Karnataka , Room No. 105 , 1st floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore 
560001 , Karnataka was issued . Copy of the notice dated 19/02/2021 was also cenclosed to Rajiv Gandhi 
School of Nursing situated at Hihgland , Al - Ameen Educational Society , Anand Nagar Road , Old 
Hubli , Distt . Dharwar – 580024 , Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to 
submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects 
the case of Rajiv Gandhi School of Nursing . Even the institution Rajiv Gandhi School of Nursing has 
not responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Refusal for statutory inspection 
under section 13 of Indian Nursing Council Act 1947 is a serious issue . It is on the basis of inspections only 
that the Council would be in a position to decide about the suitability of the nursing institution to conduct 
nursing courses . Refusal for inspection would ipso facto mean that the institution does not possess the 
requisite Teaching , Clinical or infrastructural facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
25/06/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Rajiv Gandhi School of Nursing 
situated at Hihgland , Al – Ameen Educational Society , Anand Nagar Road , Old Hubli , Distt . Dharwar 
– 580024 , Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject to orders of 
the State government of Karnataka .
