(Civil/Mechanical/Electrical/Construction/Armament/Artist/Charts) and Draughtsman (Civil/ Mechanical/ Electrical/ 
Construction/Armament/Artist/ Charts); and the Integrated Headquarters Ministry of Defence (Navy), Drawing Office 
Staff Group ‘C’ posts Recruitment Rules, 2014, in so far as they relate to the posts of Draughtsman (Civil), 
Draughtsman (Mechanical), Draughtsman (Electrical), Draughtsman (Construction) and Draughtsman (Cartographic), 
except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the following 
rules regulating the method of recruitment to the posts of Draughtsman(Civil), Draughtsman (Mechanical), 
Draughtsman (Electrical), Draughtsman (Construction), Draughtsman (Cartographic) and Draughtsman (Artist-cum- 


Charts) in the Naval Headquarters, Ministry of Defence, namely :- 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Naval Headquarters, 
Draughtsman (Civil), Draughtsman (Mechanical), Draughtsman (Electrical), Draughtsman (Construction), 


Draughtsman (Cartographic) and Draughtsman (Artist-cum-Charts), Group ‘C’ Posts Recruitment Rules, 2024. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. Application.-These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (1) of the Schedule annexed to these rules.

in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule.

a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or

Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal

any person from the operation of this rule.

by order and for reasons to be recorded in writing, relax any of the provisions of these rules with respect to any class or 
category of persons. 


7. Saving.-Nothing in these rules shall affect reservation, relaxation of age-limit and other concessions required 
to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, the Other Backward Classes, Ex-servicemen and other 
special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued by the Central Government from time to time in this 
regard.

S.R.O. 50.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Navy Group ‘C’ ‘Non-Industrial Posts’ (Hospital Staff) Recruitment Rules, 1981, in so far as they 
relate to the posts of Junior Physiotherapist, Clinical Research Assistant, Medical Social Worker, Junior Dietician, 
Laboratory Technician (Hospitals), Radiographer (Hospitals), Compounder (Qualified), Laboratory Assistant Grade - 
II (Hospitals) and Midwife, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the 
President hereby makes the following rules, regulating the method of recruitment to the posts of Junior 
Physiotherapist, Junior Dietician, Clinical Research Assistant, Medical Social Worker, Pharmacist, Laboratory 
Technician (Hospitals), Radiographer (Hospitals), Laboratory Assistant Grade -II (Hospitals) and Midwife in the 
Naval Headquarters, Ministry of Defence, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.- (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Naval 
Headquarters, Hospital Staff (Group ‘C’ posts), Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.- These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column(1) of the Schedule annexed to 
these rules. 


3. Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix.- The number of the said posts, their classification 
and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc.- The method of recruitment, age-limit, 
qualifications and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the 
said Schedule.

5. Disqualification.-_ No person, -

(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, 


shall be eligible for appointment to the said posts: 


Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal 
law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for so 
doing, exempt any person from the operation of this rule. 


6. Power to relax.- Where the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do, 
it may, by order and for reasons to be recorded in writing, relax any of the provisions of these rules with 
respect to any class or category of persons. 


ts Saving.- Nothing in these rules shall affect reservation, relaxation of age-limit and other concessions 
required to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, the Other Backward Classes, the ex- 
servicemen and other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued by the Central 
Government from time to time in this regard.
