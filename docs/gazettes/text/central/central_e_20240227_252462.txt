NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th February, 2024 


S.O. 93(E).— WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.3260 (E), dated the 20th July, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 2Ist July, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Western Dedicated Freight 
Corridor in the District of Palghar in the State of Maharashtra; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “The 
Times of India” and “Dainik Punyanagari”, dated the 22nd August, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of 
the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), and in pursuance of sub- 
section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,- 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
