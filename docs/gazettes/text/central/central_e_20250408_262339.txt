5.0. 644(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (ba) of Explanation to section 54EC of the 
Income-tax Act, 96 (43 of 96l), the Central Government hereby notifies that bonds redeemable after five years 
and issued on or after 0l“ day of April, 2025, by the Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited
