MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway(Construction Organization) | 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 30th December, 2024 


S.O. 5639(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railway (North Western Railway Construction), S.O. No. 4864 (E) dated 08 November , 2024 published 
in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 8 November , 2024 
(hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
2008 ( of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance and 
operation of the Special Railway Project, namely "Taranga Hill Abu Road Via Ambaji (6.65 km ) NEW 
BG LINE Project District- Mehsana in the State of Gujar 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely 
“Divya bhaskar” and Sandesh on date 22 November, 2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said 
Act; 


And, whereas, no objection were received by the Competent Authority within due time. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent 
Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the lands specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all 
encumbrances.
