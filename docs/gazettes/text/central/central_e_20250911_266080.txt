S.O. 4114(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 644 dated 07th February 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) and 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Corrigendum of the same was published vide 8.0. 2249 (E) dated 20th May, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 
notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH734 in the stretch 
of Moradabad Ring Road(PKG-1) from Km. 0+000 to Km. 33.724 in the district of MORADABAD in the state of 
UTTAR PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Hindustan Times" and " Amar 
Ujala" both dated 04th June 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person, by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 


vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
