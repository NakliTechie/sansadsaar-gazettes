G.S.R. 36(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of 
the Coinage Act, 20lI(/] of 20I), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: — 


l. Short title and commencement.— (|) These rules may be called the Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the 
occasion of the Centenary Year of Shri Ram College of Commerce) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Denomination of coin.— The coin of One Hundred Rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for issue under 
the authority of the Central Government to commemorate the occasion of the Centenary Year of Shri Ram College of 
Commerce. 


3. Dimension and composition of coin.— The denomination of the coin specified in rule 2 shall conform to the 
following dimensions and composition, namely:-

REVERSE:
