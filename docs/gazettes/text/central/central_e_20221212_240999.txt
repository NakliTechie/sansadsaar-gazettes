पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क परिवहन 
और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 3055(अ) दिनाँक 05 जुलाई 2022, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग ||, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा तेलंगाना राज्य के महबूबनगर जिले में महबूबनगर-

अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने 
आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

New Delhi, the 12th December, 2022 


S.O. 5795(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O NO. 3055(E) dated on 05th July 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part IJ, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning, 
etc.,), Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of Mahabubnagar-Chincholi road section of NH- 
167N(Package-1) on the stretch of land from Km. 0.00 to Km. 47.20 {Additional Collector (Revenue), 
Mahabubnagar} in the district of MAHABUBNAGAR in the state of TELANGANA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Namasthe Telangana”(Telugu) and 
“The Hindu”(English) both dated 28 July 2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
