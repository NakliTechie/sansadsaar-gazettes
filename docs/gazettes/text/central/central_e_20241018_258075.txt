S.O. 4587(E).— In pursuance of Section 3 of the National Commission for Women Act, 990 
(20 of 990), the Central Government hereby nominates Smt. Vijaya Kishore Rahatkar as Chairperson of the 
National Commission for Women (NCW) for a period of three years from the date of assumption of charge 
of the post, or till the age of 65 years, or until further orders, whichever is the earliest.
