MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 

(Northern Railway) 
(CONSTRUCTION ORGANIZATION) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 3rd April, 2024 
S.O. 607(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Construction Organization), number 5.0. 584(E), dated the 07 December, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated the 07 December, 2023 and issued under Sub-section () of 
section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of 


execution of the Special Railway Project, namely, Roza-Sitapur doubling project for Remodeling of Roza yard 
in the District of Shahjahanpur in the State of Uttar Pradesh,; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily local newspapers, namely, 
Hindustan and Amar Ujala (Hindi), dated the !4 December, 2023 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, two objections have been received under sub section — 20(D) and the same have been 
consider/disallowed by the competent authority.

Whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
