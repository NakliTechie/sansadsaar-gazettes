9.0. 2312(E).—Whereas the Joint Secretary to the Government of India, specially empowered under 
sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the Conservation of Foreign Exchange and Prevention of Smuggling Activities Act, 
1974 (52 of 1974) issued Order No. PD-12001/10/2024-COFEPOSA dated 06.03.2024 under the said sub-section 
directing that Mr. Mar Meed Salim alias Md. Salim, S/o Mar Meed Sultan, R/o House No.8, Maggin Street, Kyauk 
Myaung, Tamse township, Yangon, Myanmar be detained and kept in Adarsh Kendriya Kara, Beur Jail, Patna with a 
view to preventing him from smuggling goods, abetting the smuggling of goods, engaging in transporting or 
concealing or keeping smuggled goods, harbouring persons engaged in smuggling goods or in abetting the smuggling 
of goods in future. 


2. Whereas the Central Government has reasons to believe that the aforesaid person has absconded or has been 
concealing himself so that the Order cannot be executed. 


3. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (1) of Section 7 of the said 
Act, the Central Government hereby directs the aforesaid person to appear before the Director General of Police, 
Government of Bihar, Patna within 7 days of the publication of this Order in the Official Gazette.
