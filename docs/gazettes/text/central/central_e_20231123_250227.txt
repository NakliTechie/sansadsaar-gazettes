No. 45/2023 
Subject: - Imposition of Minimum Export Price on export of Onions. 


99.0. 503(E).—In supersession of Notification No.42/2023 dated 28/0/2023 [S.O. 479(E)] and in exercise 
of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, !992 
(No. 22 of 992), as amended, read with Para |.02 and 2.0l of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, the Central 
Government hereby amends the Export Policy of Onions in Chapter 07 of Schedule 2 of the ITC (HS) Export Policy, 
as under: 


Tariff item Unit |Item description |Export Policy condition 
HS Code Policy 
0703 0 9 KG _ |Onions: Free |Subject to a Minimum Export Price (MEP) of US $ 800 F.O.B. 
per Metric Ton (MT), till 3 December, 2023.” 


2. The Notification will come into immediate effect. 
3. The following consignments of onions will be allowed to be exported without MEP: 


i. Where onions consignment has been handed over to the Customs before 297 October, 2023 (the date from which 
the Notification No.42/2023 dated 28/0/2023 [S.O. 479(E)] came into effect) and is registered in their system / 
where onions consignment has entered the Customs station for exportation before this Notification and is registered 
in the electronic systems of the concerned Custodian of the Customs Station with verifiable evidence of date and 
time stamping of these commodities having entered the Customs Station prior to 29" October, 2023 (the date from 
which the Notification No.42/2023 dated 28/0/2023 [S.O. 479(E)] came into effect). The period of export shall be 
upto 30" November, 2023. 


ii. Export duty has been paid before the issuance of this Notification. Such export duty is non - refundable. 
4. Effect of this Notification: 


Export of onions is ‘Free’. Minimum Export Price (MEP) of US $ 800 F.O.B per Metric Ton (MT) is imposed till 
3[7 December, 2023. Notification No.42/2023 dated 28/0/2023 [S.O. 479(B)] is superseded.
