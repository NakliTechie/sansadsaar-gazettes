फायरमैन के पदों पर भर्ती की पद्धति का विनियमन करने के लिए निम्नलिखित नियम बनाते हैं, अर्थात :-

लिडिंग फायरमैन और फायरमैन (समूह TW’ पद) भर्ती नियम, 2025 है। 


(2) ये राजपत्र में उनके प्रकाशन की तारीख को प्रवृत्त होंगे।

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th April, 2025 


S.R.O. 15.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession the Directorate of Artillery (Class III and Class IV posts) Recruitment Rules, 1973, in so far as they 
relate to the posts of Leading Hand Fire, Fire Engine Driver, Fireman Grade I and Fireman Grade II, except as 
respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the following rules 
regulating the method of recruitment to the posts of Leading Fireman and Fireman, in the Directorate General of 
Artillery, Ministry of Defence, namely:- 


1, Short title and commencement. (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence,
