ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the I4h August, 2024 


O. N. 83(E).— WHEREAS, the seat of Dr. K. Keshava Rao, in the Council of States, elected by the elected 
members of the Legislative Assembly of Telangana, has become vacant on 05.07.2024 by the reason of his 
resignation, before the expiration of his term of office which was up to and including 09.04.2026; and 


WHEREAS, a bye-election is to be held for the purpose of filling the vacancy so caused; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of Section 47, sub-section () of Section 39 and Section 56 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95 (43 of 95), the Election Commission hereby- 


(A) calls upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Telangana to elect, before 06" September, 
2024 and in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and of the rules and orders made thereunder, a person for 
the purpose of filling the said vacancy in the Council of States; 


(B) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 2* August, 2024 (Wednesday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 2274 August, 2024 (Thursday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 


(c) the 27" August, 2024 (Tuesday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 03 September, 2024 (Tuesday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 06" September, 2024 (Friday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(C) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
on the date specified above for the election.
