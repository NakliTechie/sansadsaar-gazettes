Secunderabad, the 16rh February, 2024 


S.O. 796(E).— in exercise of the power conferred by sub-section (I) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989) (hereafter referred as in as the said Act), central government after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for execution of 
the special railway project, Mudkhed - Dhone Doubling in Jogulamba Gadwal district in the state of Telangana, 
hereby declares its intention to acquire such land: 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub- section (1) of section 20D of the said act: 

Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely Special Dy.Collector(LA), JLIP, 
Jogulamba Gadwal district in writing and shall be set out the grounds thereof and the competent authority Special 
Dy.Collector(LA), JLIP, Jogulamba Gadwal district shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in 
person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any 
as the competent authority thinks necessary, by, order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub section (II) of section 20(D) of the said act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land under this notification are available and can be inspected by the 
interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority. 


Description of lands in acquisition: — Doubling of track between Mahabubnagar - Dhone Section within the limits of 
Jogulamba Gadwal District jurisdiction for passenger and freight facility.
