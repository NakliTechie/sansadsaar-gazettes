ORDER 
New Delhi, the 16th December, 2025 


5.0. 5837(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 
1955 (10 of 1955), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Fertiliser 
(Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Order,1985, namely:- 


1. This Order may be called the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) (Control) Thirteen Amendment 
Order, 2025 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette 


2. In the Fertiliser ( Inorganic, Organic or Mixed ) ( Control) Order, 1985, (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Order), in the clause 38,- 


(i) in sub-clause 1 shall be renumbered as sub-clause “(1)” thereof; 
(1) in sub-clause (1) as so numbered, for the word “ten” the word “fourteen “ shall be substituted; 
(iii) sub-clause (4) shall be omitted. 
[F. No. 2-2/2024 Fert Law]

S.O. 5838(E).— In pursuance of sub-clause (1) of clause 38 of the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or 
Mixed) (Control) Order, 1985, and in supersession of the notification of Government of India in the Ministry 
of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare number S.O. No. 5415 (9), dated the 2274 December, 2023, the Central 
Government hereby constitutes the Central Fertiliser Committee consisting of the following members, 
namely:- 


1 Agriculture Commissioner, DA&FW Chairman; 
2 Joint Secretary, INM Member; 
3 DDG, NRM, ICAR Member; 
4 Joint Secretary, DoF Member; 
5 Advisor, Trade, DA&FW Member;

12 Director, Agriculture, Uttar Pradesh Member; 
13 Director, Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh Member; 
14 DG, Fertilizer Association of India Member; 
15 DS, INM, DA& FW Member- Convener. 
2. The term of the office of the Committee shall be five years from the date of publication of this 
notification in the official Gazette. 
3. At least eight members including chairman shall be present to fulfill the quorum in the meeting. 
4. The members of the Committee shall be entitled to draw travelling and daily allowances at the rate 


specified below while attending the meeting of the Committee, namely:- 


(1) an official member of the Committee shall be entitled to draw traveling and daily allowances at the rate 
applicable to the service to which he belongs; 


(1) a non-official member shall be entitled to draw travelling and daily allowances in accordance with the 
orders issued by the Central Government from time to time. 


5. The committee shall be provided with such members of staff, as may be necessary, by the Central 
Government for carrying out its functions under the Order.
