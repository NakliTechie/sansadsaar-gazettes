NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 18th January , 2023 

No. N - 17011 / 1 / HP / 2023 - P & D. - In pursuance of powers conferred by Section 46 ( 2 ) of the Employees ' 
State Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , read with Regulation 95 - A of the Employees ' State Insurance ( General ) 
Regulations , 1950 , the Director General has fixed the 01/01/2023 as the date from , which the medical benefit as laid 
down in the said Regulation 95 - A and the Himachal Pradesh Employees ' State Insurance ( Medical Benefit ) Rules , 
1977 shall be extended to the families of insured persons in the entire area of " Chamba , Kullu , Hamirpur , Kinnaur 
and Lahaul & Spiti " district in the State of Himachal Pradesh . 


DEEPAK JOSHI , Insurance Commissioner ( P & D )
