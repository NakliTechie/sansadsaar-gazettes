ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th October, 2025 
O.N. 46(E). — Whereas, the President of India has, by notification issued under Section 2 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 95(43 of 95) and published in the Gazette of India on 06" October, 2025, been 
pleased to call upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir, to 
elect one member to the Council of States to fill the vacancy in that House caused due to the retirement of Shri Mir 
Mohammad Fayaz on 0" February, 202 on the expiration of his term of office; 
Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the said Act, the Election 
Commission of India hereby- 
(A) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 3"* October, 2025 (Monday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 4 October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(c) the 6' October, 2025 (Thursday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 247 October, 2025 (Friday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 28" October, 2025 (Tuesday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; and 


(B) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 


on the date specified above for the election.
