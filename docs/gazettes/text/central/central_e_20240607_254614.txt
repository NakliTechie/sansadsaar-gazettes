NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th June, 2024 


No. N-10/12/2013-PPC/PBRB.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 9 and sub- 
section (5) of section 11 read with clauses (c) and (d) of sub-section (2) of section 33 of the Prasar Bharati 
(Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990 (25 of 1990), the corporation hereby makes the following regulations to 
amend the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) (Senior Administrative and Allied posts) Recruitment 
Regulations, 2021, namely:- 


1. Short title and Commencement.- (1) These regulations may be called the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting 
Corporation of India) (Senior Administrative and Allied Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Regulations, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) (Senior Administrative and Allied posts) Recruitment 
Regulations, 2021, in the Schedule,- 


(i) against serial number 1, relating to the post of Deputy Director of Administration (Headquarters), for the 
entries under column 11, the following entries shall be substituted, namely:- 


“Deputation (including short-term contract or absorption): 


Officers of the Central Government, State Governments, Union territories Administrations, autonomous bodies or 
statutory organisations or public sector undertakings, - 


(a)(i) holding analogous post on a regular basis in the parent cadre or department; or 


(1) with five years’ service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on a regular basis in level-10 
(Rs. 56100-177500) in the pay matrix or equivalent, in the parent cadre or department; or 

(iii) with seven years’ service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on regular basis in level-9 
(Rs. 53100-167800) in the pay matrix or equivalent, in the parent cadre or department; or 

(iv) with eight years’ service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on regular basis in level-8 
(Rs. 47600-151100) in the pay matrix or equivalent, in the parent cadre or department; and
