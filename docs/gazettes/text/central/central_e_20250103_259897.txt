S.O. 48(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (46) of section l0 of the Income-tax Act, 96] 
(43 of 96), the Central Government hereby notifies for the purposes of the said clause, ‘The Commissioners for the 
Rabindra Setu, Kolkata’ (PAN AABTT2734P), a body established under the Howrah Bridge (Amendment) Act, 965 
(West Bengal Act XII of 965), in respect of the following specified income arising to that body, namely: 


(a) Proceeds from Taxes of Municipalities / Municipal Corporation and Railways; 


(b) Miscellaneous income like Rental and Maintenance charge, income for laying optical fibre cable, 
way leave rent, Damage cost recoverable; and 


(c) Interest earned on bank deposits.

Kolkata’ :- 
(a) shall not engage in any commercial activity; 
(b) activities and the nature of the specified income shall remain unchanged throughout the financial 
years; and 
(c) shall file return of income in accordance with the provision of clause (g) of sub-section (4C) of 
section |39 of the Income-tax Act, 96l. 
3. This notification shall be deemed to have been applied for assessment years 209-2020 to 2023-2024 


relevant for the financial years 20!8-209 to 2022-2023 respectively.
