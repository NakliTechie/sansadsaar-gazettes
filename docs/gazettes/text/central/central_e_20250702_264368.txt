(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers’ Rights Rules, 2003 (hereinafter referred to as the 
principal rules), for rule 20, the following rules shall be substituted, namely:- 


"20. The method of appointment of officers and other employees of the Authority.- () The Authority 
shall recruit and make appointments to the posts of officers specified in the Fourth Schedule annexed to these 
rules. 


(2) The number of posts, their classification and their pay level attached thereto shall be as per the 
service conditions specified in the Seventh Schedule annexed to these rules. 


20A. Initial Constitution.- () Personnel holding the posts specified in Column () of 


the Seventh Schedule, immediately before the commencement of these rules on a regular basis shall be 
deemed to have been appointed under the provisions of these rules. 


(2) The regular continuous service of personnel referred in sub-rule (l) in the respective grade 
immediately before the commencement of these rules shall count for the purpose of the probation period, 
qualifying service for promotion, confirmation and pension in the service.”
