INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-31 / 13610 - INC . – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , M.S. Institute of Nursing situated at 421 , Bhakhamau , Post - Basaha , District Lucknow 226026 , 
Uttar Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council to 
conduct Post Basic B.Sc ( N ) Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-31 / 13610 - INC dated 04/05/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of Indian 
Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) , Department of Health and Family Welfare , 
Government of Uttar Pradesh , 05th Floor , Vikas Bhawan , Janpath Market , Vidhan Sabha Road , Hazratganj , Lucknow 
- 226001 Uttar Pradesh was issued . Copy of the notice dated 04/05/2022 was also enclosed to M.S. Institute of 
Nursing situated at 421 , Bhakhamau , Post Basaha , District Lucknow - 226026 , Uttar Pradesh . In the notice 
issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of 
the nursing institution . 


- 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
M.S. Institute of Nursing . Even no action , as required , has been taken by the institution . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 19/07/2021 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , clinical and 
teaching facilities . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 declares 
that with effect from 08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic B.Sc ( N ) from 
M.S. Institute of Nursing situated at 421 , Bhakhamau , Post Basaha , District Lucknow- 226026 , Uttar Pradesh 
shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Uttar Pradesh subject to the orders of State government of 
Uttar Pradesh .
