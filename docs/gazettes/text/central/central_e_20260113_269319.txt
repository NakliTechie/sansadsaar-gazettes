MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 13th January, 2026 


5.0. 187(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 
(11 of 2016), the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do in the public interest, 
after consulting the Bureau hereby makes the following Order to amend the Electrical Accessories (Quality Control) 
Order, 2023, namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) This order may be called the Electrical Accessories (Quality Control) 
Amendment Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the said order, in Table-1, the last entry pertaining to Indian Standard “7809 (Part 3/ Sec 1): 1986” in column (2) 
and corresponding entry in column (3) i.e., “Specification for Pressure sensitive adhesive insulating tapes for electrical 
purposes- Part 3 requirements or individual materials — Sec 1 plasticized polyvinylchloride tapes with non- 
Thermosetting adhesive” shall be omitted.

SANJIV, Jt. Secy.
