MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


S.O. 4978(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), Vide S.O. No. 247 (E) dated 2°t JUNE 2024 the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of 
(Widening/two lane with paved shoulder/ four laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of 
NH73]A in the stretch of land from Km 74.700 to Km 2.950 (Special Land Acquisition Officer Banda) 
in the district of CHITRAKOOT in the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “AAJ ” and “ THE 
TIMES OF INDIA both dated 29" JUNE 2024, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 
And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered 
and settled the same appropriately; 
And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
