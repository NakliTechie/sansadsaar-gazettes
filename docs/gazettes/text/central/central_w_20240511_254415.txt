S.R.O. 18.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the President 
hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the posts of Principal Technical Officer 
(Mechanical), Principal Technical Officer (Ammunition and Explosives) and Principal Technical Officer (Design) of 
the Naval Armament Inspectorate Cadre in the Indian Navy, Ministry of Defence, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Indian Navy Naval 
Armament Inspectorate, Principal Technical Officer (Mechanical), Principal Technical Officer (Ammunition and 
Explosives) and Principal Technical Officer (Design), Group ‘A’ Posts, Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2: Application.—These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (1) of the Schedule annexed to these 
rules. 
3: Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix._The number of the said posts, their classification 


and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc—The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications 
and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the said Schedule. 


3D: Disqualification No person,- 
(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or 
(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, 
shall be eligible for appointment to the said posts : 


Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal 
law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for so doing, exempt 
any person from the operation of this rule. 


6. Power to relax.— Where the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do, it 
may, by order and for reasons to be recorded in writing and in consultation with the Union Public Service 
Commission, relax any of the provisions of these rules with respect to any class or category of persons. 


7. Saving.—Nothing in these rules shall affect reservation, relaxation of age-limit and other concessions required 
to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, the Other Backward Classes, the Ex-servicemen and 
other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued by the Central Government from time to time 
in this regard.

S.R.O. 19.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Army), Defence Staff Headquarters, Signal 
Intelligence Directorate (Group ‘A’ and Group ‘B’ posts) Recruitment Rules, 2013, in so far as they relate to the posts 
of Director Signal Intelligence (Research), Principal Research Officer, Senior Research Officer, Research Officer, 
Junior Research Officer and Data Entry Operator Grade ‘D’, except as respects things done or omitted to be done 
before such supersessions, the President hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the 
post of Director Signal Intelligence (Research), Principal Research Officer, Senior Research Officer, Research 
Officer, Junior Research Officer and Data Entry Operator Grade ‘D’ in the Defence Staff Headquarters, Signal 
Intelligence Directorate, Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Army), namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement.-(1) These rules may be called the Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of 
Defence (Army), Defence Staff Headquarters, Signal Intelligence Directorate, Director Signal Intelligence (Research), 
Principal Research Officer, Senior Research Officer, Research Officer, Junior Research Officer and Data Entry 
Operator Grade ‘D’ (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.-These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (1) of the Schedule annexed to these 
rules. 
3: Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix.-The number of the said posts, their classification 


and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc.- The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications 
and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the said Schedule. 


2D. Disqualification.—No person, — 
(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or 
(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, 
shall be eligible for appointment to the said posts : 


Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal 
law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for so doing, exempt 
any person from the operation of this rule.

S.R.O. 20.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 
supersession of the Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Air), Senior Programmer, Programmer, Data 
Processing Assistant and Data Entry Operator Grade 'D' Recruitment Rules, 2013, except as respects things done or 
omitted to be done before such supersession, the President hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of 
recruitment to the posts of Principal System Analyst, Senior System Analyst, System Analyst, Data Processing 
Assistant and Data Entry Operator Grade 'D' in the Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of Defence (Air), namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement.-(1) These rules may be called the Integrated Headquarters of Ministry of 
Defence (Air), Principal System Analyst, Senior System Analyst, System Analyst, Data Processing Assistant and 
Data Entry Operator Grade 'D' (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Posts) Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.- These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (1) of the Schedule annexed to 
these rules. 


3. Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix.-The number of the said posts, their classification 
and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule. 


4. Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc.-The method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications 
and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the said Schedule.
