MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |9th February, 2026 


5.0. 952(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 62 read with sub-section (4) of section 25 of 
the Motor Vehicles Act, 988 (59 of 988), the Central Government hereby makes the following Scheme, to amend the 
Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme, 2025, namely: — 


l. Short title and commencement - (|) These Scheme may be called the Cashless Treatment of Road Accident 
Victims (Amendment) Scheme, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme, 2025, in paragraph |, in sub-paragraph (), for 
the words “Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme”, the words, brackets and letters “Prime Minister - 
Road Accident Victims’ Hospitalisation and Assured Treatment (PM-RAHAT) Scheme” shall be substituted.
