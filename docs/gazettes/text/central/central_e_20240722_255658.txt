MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
( Department of Commerce) 
(SEZ Section) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th July, 2024 


S.O. 2903-(E). — Whereas, M/s. Electronics Corporation of Tamil Nadu (ELCOT) Ltd, 
had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up an Information Technology and Information 
Technology Enabled Services Special Economic Zone at Viswanathapuram Village, Hosur 
Taluk, Krishnagiri District, in the State of Tamil Nadu; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of section 4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones 
Rules 2006, had notified an area of 70.01 hectares at the above Special Economic Zone vide 
Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number S.O. 1155(E) dated 47 May, 2009; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Electronics Corporation of Tamil Nadu (ELCOT) Ltd has now 
proposed for de-notification of 18.9535 hectares from the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Tamil Nadu has given its approval to the 
proposal vide letter No.5418/MIE-2/2022-2 dated 2377 September, 2022; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Madras SEZ has recommended 
the proposal for de-notification of an area of 18.9535 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government is satisfied that the requirements under 
sub-section (8) of section 3 of the said Act and other related requirements are fulfilled; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by second proviso to sub- 
section (1) of section 4 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and in pursuance of rule 8 of 
the Special Economic Zones Rules, 2006, the Central Government hereby de-notifies an area 
of 18.9535 hectares, thereby making resultant area as 51.0565 hectares. The Survey numbers 
and the area for de-notification are given below in the table, namely: - 


Table for De-notification area 


Survey No. Total area (in hectares) 


189535 
Total notified area of the SEZ after above de-notification 51.0565
