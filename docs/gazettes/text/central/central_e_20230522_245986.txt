MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd May, 2023 


S.O. 2241(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 49E of 
the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972), the Central Government hereby designates Dr. 
Satya Prakash Yadav, Additional Director General of Forests, Ministry of Environment, Forest 
and Climate Change as the Management Authority for discharging the functions and exercising 
the powers under the Acct till the 29" day of February, 2024 or further order, whichever is earlier.
