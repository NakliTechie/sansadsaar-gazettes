NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 20th February, 2025 


S.O. 944(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.0.26(E) dated the 5/0/2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 
3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 5/0I/2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under sub-section 
() of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (/ of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification 
for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely new BG line 
project from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) in Dhar district Tehsil Dhar Village 
Gyanpura in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers Dainik bhaskar ,Raj 
express and Free press General” dated 2!/0i/2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Gyanpura village objections were received, considered and the objections have been resolved 
and competent Authority have passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its report to 
the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (l) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
