MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Consumer Affairs) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th August, 2023 


S.O. 3564(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Tribunals 
Reform Act, 202, the Central Government hereby appoints Justice Amreshwar Pratap Sahi, a retired Chief 
Justice of Madras High Court, to the office of the President, National Consumer Disputes Redressal 
Commission (NCDRC), with effect from the forenoon of 2" August, 2023, for a period of four years or till 
he attains the age of seventy years or until further orders, whichever is the earliest. 


2. The terms and conditions of this appointment shall be regulated in terms of the provisions of the 
Tribunals Reform Act, 202], the Tribunal (Conditions of Service) Rules, 202 and other instructions on the 
matter, if any, issued by the Central Government from time to time.
