MINISTRY OF YOUTH AFFAIRS AND SPORTS 
(Department of Sports) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th September, 2026 


99.0. 5007(E).— Whereas, section 6 of the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 (25 of 2025) provides that 
the Central Government shall, on the recommendations of the National Sports Board, notify a National Sports 
Election Panel; 


And whereas, the National Sports Board has not yet been constituted under the said Act; 


And whereas, in the absence of the constitution of the National Sports Board, a difficulty has arisen in giving 
effect to the provisions of section 6 of the said act relating to the notification of the National Sports Election Panel; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38 of the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 
(25 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following Order to remove the aforesaid difficulty, namely: — 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l) This Order may be called the National Sports Governance (Removal of 
Difficulties) Order, 2026. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 
2: For the purposes of section 6 of the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 (25 of 2025), and until the National 


Sports Board is constituted under the said Act, the Central Government may notify the National Sports Election Panel 
without the recommendations of the National Sports Board.
