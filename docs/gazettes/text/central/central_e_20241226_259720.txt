झाबुआ, सरदारपुर, धार और पिथामपुर के मध्य से (204.76 किमी), मध्य प्रदेश राज्य के धार जिले में रानीपुरा गाँव,

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 26th December, 2024 


S.O. 5568(E)—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O. 4650(E) dated the 24" October, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 247 October 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred 
to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed 
to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway 
Project, namely new BG line project from Dahod to Indore (through Jhabua, Sardarpur, Dhar and Pithampur) 
in Dhar district Tehsil Dhar Village Ranipura in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily News Papers — “Free Press, 
Times Nation, Dainik Bhaskar” & “Raj Patrika” dated 20 November, 77 November 2024 under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in Ranipura village objections were received, considered and the objections have been 
resolved and competent Authority have passed the orders accordingly; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
