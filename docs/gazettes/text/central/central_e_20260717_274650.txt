F.No. 8-02/Govt./2024 - INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of the Indian 
Nursing Council Act, !947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, the following institutions located in the State of Andhra Pradesh were recognized by the State 
Government and the Andhra Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct nursing courses. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-02/Govt./2024 - INC (65477) dated 26..2024 for withdrawal of recognition 
under Section 4 of the Indian Nursing Council Act, 947, was issued to the Principal Secretary, Health Medical and 
Family Welfare Deptt., Government of Andhra Pradesh, Sth Block, Ground Floor, Room No. |57, A.P. Secretariat, 
Velagapudi, Amaravathi 522503, Andhra Pradesh. A copy of the notice dated 26..2024 was also sent to the Andhra 
Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit its 
recommendations to the Council regarding the suitability of the nursing institutions. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any remarks or recommendation from the State Government or the 
Andhra Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council regarding the suitability of the below-mentioned institution. 

Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. The Indian Nursing Council records show that 
these institutions have failed to apply for statutory inspection for several years and were found deficient/unsuitable for 
conducting nursing programme. 

Now therefore, the Council, exercising powers under Section 4(3)(b) of the Indian Nursing Council Act, 
947, hereby declares that with effect from l.09.2025, any person holding a recognized qualification from the
