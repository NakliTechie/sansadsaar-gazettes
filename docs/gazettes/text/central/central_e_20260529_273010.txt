MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th May, 2026 


5.0. 2692(E).—In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines 
(Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter called the said Act), the Government of 
India hereby authorizes Smt. Ritu Hemnani, Joint Collector, Government of Chhattisgarh in place of Smt. Ritu 
Hemnani, Deputy Collector, Government of Chhattisgarh to perform the functions of Competent Authority under 
the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962, on deputation basis, for a period 
of Two years, for all Natural Gas Pipeline Projects of M/s GAIL (India) Limited in the State of Chhattisgarh. 


2, This notification will be effective from the date of its issue.
