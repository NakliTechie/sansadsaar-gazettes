7. The monthly data on production (quantity and value), import, export, installed capacity, sales and other related data 
of chemical sector would be collected from the informants. 


Language in which information is to be furnished by informant: 
8. Every informant shall furnish information in the prescribed format in English. 
Obligation of informant: 


9. An owner or manager of a unit shall furnish information in the prescribed manner and by stipulated date every 
month as mentioned in the notice received by him from the Statistics Officer. He should also furnish relevant records 
for inspection, and answer questions in relation to the information sought, as may be required by the Statistics Officer 
or a person authorised by him. 


Nature of business records and other records which may be inspected: 


0. Business records of a unit, any other legal document in support of the information furnished by the unit may be 
inspected by the Statistics Officer or a person authorised by him. 


The manner of inspection: 


ll. The Statistics Officer or a person authorised by him may verify the information furnished by the units on the basis 
of business records of the unit and seek clarifications from the concerned owner or manager or a person authorised by 
the management of unit.
