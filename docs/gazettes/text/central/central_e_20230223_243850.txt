INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-05 / 12134 - INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Medical Training Sansthan situated at Jaso Road , Sasaram , Buxar 802101 , Bihar was 
recognized by State Government of Bihar and Bihar Nurses & Registration Council to conduct ANM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-05 / 12134 - INC / 12367 dated 19/02/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( H & FW ) Department of Health , 
Government of Bihar , Vikas Bhawan , New Secretariat , Bihar was issued . Copy of the notice dated 19/02/2021 was 
also enclosed Medical Training Sansthan situated at Jaso Road , Sasaram , Buxar – 802101 , Bihar . In the notice 
issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of 
the nursing institution . 


And whereas , the Under Secretary , Government of Bihar , Department of Health , has revoked the recognition 
granted to Medical Training Sansthan for running ANM Course vide letter dated 19/03/2021 . 


Hence , therefore , the recognition of Medical Training Sansthan for conducting ANM Course is hereby 
withdrawn as the said institution does not have necessary permissions from the state government . 


Lt. Col ( Dr ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
