ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th June, 2024 


O.N. 154(E).—Whereas, the President of India has, by notification issued under Section 12 of the 
Representation of the People Act, 1951(43 of 1951) and published in the Gazette of India on 060 June, 2024, has been 
pleased to call upon the elected members of the Legislative Assembly of Kerala, to elect 03 (three) members to the 
Council of States to fill the vacancies in that House caused due to the retirement of the sitting members on 017 July, 


2024 on the expiration of their term of office; 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of Section 39 and Section 56 of the said Act, the Election 


Commission hereby- 
(A) appoints, with respect to the said election; 
(a) the 13" June, 2024 (Thursday), as the last date for making nominations; 
(b) the 147 June, 2024 (Friday), as the date for the scrutiny of the nominations; 
(c) the 187 June, 2024 (Tuesday), as the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures; 
(d) the 250 June, 2024 (Tuesday), as the date on which a poll shall, if necessary, be taken; and 
(e) the 28" June, 2024 (Friday), as the date before which the election shall be completed; 


and 


(B) fixes the hours from 09:00 am to 04:00 pm, as the hours during which the poll shall, if necessary, be taken 
in the above-mentioned State on the date specified above for the election.
