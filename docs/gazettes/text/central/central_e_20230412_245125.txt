New Delhi, the 10th April, 2023 


F. No. 46/12/2020-R&R.—In terms of Section 3(2) of the Tribunal Reforms Act, 2021 and relevant provisions 
of Tribunal (Conditions of Service) Rules, 2021, the President hereby appoints Mr. Justice Ramesh Ranganathan to 
the post of Chairperson of the Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL) in the salary of Rs. 250000/- (fixed) per 
month, for a period of 04 years with effect from the forenoon of the 2nd December 2022, or till attaining the age of 
70 years, whichever is earlier.
