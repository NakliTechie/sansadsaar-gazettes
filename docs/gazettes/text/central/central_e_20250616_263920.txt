and Highways, 695 Dated:27/0/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to

“Chhatarpur Bhraman Daily Panna” both dated 27 March 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas No anyone objection filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
