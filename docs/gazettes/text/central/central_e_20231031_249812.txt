MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the, 27th October, 2023 


59.0. 4749(E).—Whereas the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), 
clause (v) and clause (xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 
986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, issued a notification in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 2!69 (E), dated the oH 
August, 204; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest 
to amend the said notification; 


And whereas sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides that whenever it 
appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


And whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, !986 for 
amending the notification number S.O. 269 (E), dated the 97 August,204; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l), clause (v) and clause (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule 
(4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate 
Change, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide notification 
number S.O. 269 (E), dated the yi he August, 20i4, namely:- 


In the said notification,- 
(a) in paragraph 2, for sub-paragraph (), the following sub-paragraph shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(]) The State Government shall, prepare a Zonal Master Plan, for the purposes of the Eco-sensitive 
Zone, in consultation with local people and in accordance with this notification, within a period of two 
years from the 26" October, 2023”. 


(b) in paragraph 3, in sub-paragraph (0), the words “and approved by the Ministry of Environment, 
Forests and Climate Change”, shall be omitted.
