New Delhi, the 6th November, 2022 


S.O. 5337(E).— Whereas by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Number SO 3569(E) dated 29" July 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3,

annexed to the said Notification for building (widening/ two laning with paved shoulder/four laning etc.), maintenance,

stretch of land from Km 62.500 to Km 90.000 in the district of BANKURA in the State of WEST BENGAL.

INDIA” both dated 9” August 2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas all the objections under sub section (.) of Section 3C have been received and the same have been 
considered and disallowed by the competent Authority under Sub Section (2) of Section 3C;

submitted its report to the Central Government;

conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
