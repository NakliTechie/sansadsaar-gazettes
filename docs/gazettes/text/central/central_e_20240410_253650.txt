MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 10th April, 2024 


S.O. 1680(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways number S.O. 77 (E) dated the 03rd January, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-Section (ii) vide No 71 dt. 5th January 2024, issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four—six 
laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.516F on the stretch of land from Km 0 
to Km 12.250 (Samalkot to Achampeta Junction,) in the district of Kakinada (earlier EAST GODAVARI) in the state 
of ANDHRA PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India” (English) and “Praja 
Sakthi” (Telugu) both dated 11.01.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the 
same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
