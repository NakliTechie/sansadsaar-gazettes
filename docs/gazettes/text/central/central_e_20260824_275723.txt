MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th August, 2026 


S.O. 4654(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 320(E) Dated:5/06/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH47 in the stretch of land from Km. 3.55 to Km. 34.] in the district of HARDA in the state of MADHYA 
PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar ” dated 
20/06/2026, “Free Press” dated 20/06/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority and Sub divisional Officer (R) has received objections filed under 
Section 3-C, considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority Sub divisional Officer (R) and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
