NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th July, 2025 


F. No. A-35013/02/2016-Ad-ITI/145.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri M. Sundaresh Babu, 
IAS (KN:2012) an officer belonging to Karnataka Cadre of Indian Administrative Service as Director of 
Census Operations/Director of Citizen Registration, Tamil Nadu & Puducherry with effect from 23.06.2025 
(FN) for a period upto 31.12.2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation under 
the Central Staffing Scheme. 


2. The Headquarters of Shri M. Sundaresh Babu, IAS (KN:2012) will be Chennai (Tamil Nadu).
