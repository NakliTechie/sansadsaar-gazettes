NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th March, 2023 


G.S.R. 230(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 


President hereby makes the following rules to amend the Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Directorate of 
Enforcement, Assistant Director of Enforcement, Group ‘A’ post, Recruitment Rules, 2020, namely: - 


l. 


Short title and commencement. - (l) These rules may be called the Ministry of Finance, Department of 
Revenue, Directorate of Enforcement, Assistant Director of Enforcement, Group ‘A’ post, Recruitment 
(Amendment) Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


In the Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue, Directorate of Enforcement, Assistant Director of 
Enforcement, Group ‘A’ post, Recruitment Rules, 2020, in the Schedule, in Column (3), after the entries, the 
following Note shall be inserted, namely:- 


“Note:- Consultation with the Union Public Service Commission not necessary while appointing an officer on 
deputation basis for a period of one year with effect from 3'" February, 2023.”
