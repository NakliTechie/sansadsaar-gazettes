नियुक्ति समिति द्वारा दिनांक 18.04.2025 के क्रमांक 36/01/2025-ईओ(एसएम-1) द्वारा श्री

नियुक्ति। 


(ii) श्री पंकज कुमार मिश्रा द्वारा सदस्य वित्त, परमाणु ऊर्जा आयोग के पद का प्रभार छोड़ने पर कैबिनेट 
की नियुक्ति समिति द्वारा दिनांक 25.04.2025 के संख्या 36/01/2025-ई (एसएम-1) द्वारा 
सुश्री सीमा जैन, सदस्य वित्त (अंतरिक्ष आयोग) को उक्त पद का अतिरिक्त प्रभार नियमित पदधारी 
की नियुक्ति तक या अगले आदेश तक, जो भी पहले हो, सौंपा जाना और 


(iii) डॉ. के. कस्तूरीरंगन, सदस्य, परमाणु ऊर्जा आयोग का निधन।

NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 6th May, 2025 


No.: AEC-1(1)/2025/5833.—The constitution of the Atomic Energy Commission notified vide 
No. 1(1)/2024/15029 dated October 21, 2024, stands revised consequent to the 


(i) Appointment of Shri Vumlunmang Vualnam, Secretary, Ministry of Civil Aviation as Secretary, Department 
of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance vice Shri Manoj Govil, upon his appointment as Secretary 
(Coordination), Cabinet Secretariat by the Appointments Committee of the Cabinet vide No. 36/01/2025- 
EO(SM-1) dt. 18.04.2025, 


(ii) Assignment of additional charge of the post of Member Finance, Atomic Energy Commission to Ms. Seema 
Jain, Member Finance (Space Commission) upon relinquishment of the charge of the post by Shri Pankaj 
Kumar Mishra, by the Appointments Committee of the Cabinet vide No. 36/01/2025-EO(SM-1) dt. 
25.04.2025 till the appointment of a regular incumbent or until further orders, whichever is earlier and 


(iii) Passing away of Dr. K. Kasturirangan, Member, Atomic Energy Commission.
