MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th November, 2022 


S.O. 552(E).— Whereas by notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways,S.0.no.438(A),Dated 4 September 2022,published in the Gazette of India,Extraordinary,Part II,section 3 
,sub section (ii) issued under sub-section (I) of section 3A of the National Highway Act,l956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter to as the said Act),the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction (widening/four/two laning with paved shoulder,etc), 
maintenance, management or operation of National Highway No.353 J on the stretch of land from km 0.000 to 53.79 
(Morshi Chandurbazar Achalpur ) in the district of Amravati in the state of Maharashtra, hereby declare its intention 
to acquire such land. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ Navbharat” and “Deshonnati" 
both dated 20.09.2022 under sub section (3) of the section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas No objections have been received. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now ,therefore ,upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub section (l) of section 3D of the said Act ,the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further ,in pursuance of sub section (2) of section 3D of the said Act ,the Central Government hereby 


declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government,free from all emcumbrances.
