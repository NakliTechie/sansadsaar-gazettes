MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Revenue) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th September, 2023 


G.S.R. 655(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred under section 3 of the Tribunal Reforms Act, 2021 
(33 of 2021), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Tribunal (Conditions of 
Service) Rules, 2021, namely:- 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Tribunal (Conditions of Service) Second Amendment Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Tribunal (Conditions of Service) Rules, 2021 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), after Rule 7, the 
following rule shall be inserted, namely:- 


“7A Retirement or resignation from parent service on appointment as Chairperson or Member.— 
Where the person appointed as a Chairperson or a Member is a serving Judge of the Supreme Court or a 
High Court or a serving Member of an organised service, he shall either resign or obtain voluntary 
retirement from his parent service before joining the Tribunal.” 


3. In the said rules, for rule 13, the following rule shall be substituted, namely:- 


“13. Pension, Provident Fund and Gratuity.— Pension, Provident Fund and gratuity shall not be admissible for 
the service rendered in the Tribunal.”
