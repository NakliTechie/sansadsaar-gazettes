(DIRECTORATE GENERAL OF FOREIGN TRADE) 
PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 24th March, 2026 


No. 53/2025-26 


Subject: Extension in the validity of TRQ Authorisation for import of gold under India-UAE CEPA 
(Tariff Head 708) in FY 2025-26 till 30.06.2026 - reg. 


F. No. 0/89/80/35/AM-25/PC-2(A) /E-43340.—In view of the prevailing geopolitical 
developments affecting global trade and logistics, and with a view to facilitating importers, the Director General of 
Foreign Trade, in exercise of the powers conferred under paragraphs |.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, 
as amended from time to time, hereby extends the validity of TRQ Authorisations issued in the financial year 2025-26, 
for import of Gold under CTH 708 issued under the India- UAE CEPA, from the existing validity of 3.03.2026 up to 
30.06.2026. 


2. No separate application, composition fee, amendment or endorsement shall be required to avail this 
automatic extension of TRQ Authorisations. 


Effect of this Public Notice: The validity of TRQ Authorisations for import of Gold under CTH 708 issued under 
the India-UAE CEPA, issued in the financial year 2025-26, stands automatically extended up to 30.06.2026.
