प्राप्त नहीं हुई । हालांकि , संस्था द्वारा आवश्यकता के अनुसार कोई कार्रवाई नहीं की गई है ।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-26 / 11703 - INC.– In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Radiance Institute of Nursing , Pahuwind Bhikhiwind , Patti Tran - Taran 143303 , Punjab is 
recognized by State Government of Punjab and Punjab Nurses and Registration Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-26 / 11703 - INC dated 09/10/2019 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of Indian 
Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( Health & FW ) , Government of Punjab , Room No. 314 , 
03rd Floor , Mini Secretariat , Sector – 09 , Chandigarh 160009 , Punjab was issued . Copy of the notice dated 
09/10/2019 was also enclosed to Radiance Institute of Nursing , Pahuwind Bhikhiwind , Patti Tran Taran 
143303 , Punjab . In the notice issued , the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 
Radiance Institute of Nursing , Punjab . However no action , as required , has been taken by the institution . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 09/04/2019 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , clinical and 
teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run as nursing institution . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 declares 
that with effect from 19/06/2021 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Radiance Institute of 
Nursing , Pahuwind Bhikhiwind , Patti Tran Taran 143303 , Punjab shall be entitled to be registered only in the 
State of Punjab subject to orders of the State government of Punjab .
