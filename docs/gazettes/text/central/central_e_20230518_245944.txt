New Delhi, the 17th May, 2023 


S.0.2219 (E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Clause (e) of Section 4 of the All India Institute of 
Medical Sciences Act, 1956 (25 of 1956) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences (Amendment) Act, 
2012 (37 of 2012), the Central Government hereby nominates Dr. A K Bisoi, to be member of Institute Body of All 
India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhubaneswar in place of Dr. S.K. Acharya, Executive 
Director, Fortis Hospital and for that purpose makes the following amendment in the notification of Government of 
India in Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, File Number Z-28016/177/2019-PMSSY-IV dated the 187 May, 2020 
published in the Gazette of India Part II , section 3 , Sub-Section (ii) vide SO 1822 (E) dated the 10" June, 2020: 


In the said notification, for serial number 9 and the entry relating thereto, the following serial number and 
entry should be substituted, namely :- 


“9. Dr. A K Bisoi” 


The terms of office of the member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956.
