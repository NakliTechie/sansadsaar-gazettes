S.O. 5020(E).— Whereas the Ministry of Power, Government of India, vide Gazette Notification No. CG- 
DL-E-11092024-257066 (S.O. 3875(E)) dated 10" September 2024, granted authorization under Section 164 of the 
Electricity Act, 2003 to M/s Halvad Transmission Limited, the applicant with its registered office at C-105, Anand 
Niketan, New Delhi — 110021, for the transmission scheme: "Transmission system for evacuation of additional 7 GW 
of RE power from Khavda RE Park under Phase III Part A". The following overhead transmission lines are covered 
under this scheme: 


1. KPS2 (GIS) — Halvad 765 kV D/C line 
li. LILO of Lakadia — Ahmedabad 765 kV D/C line at Halvad 


And whereas, M/s Halvad Transmission Limited published corrigendum to the earlier public notices for the 
above transmission scheme in local newspapers, Indian Express (in English) dated 22" November 2025, Rajasthan 
Patrika (in Hindi) dated 2274 November 2025, Kutch Mitra (in Gujarati) dated 2274 November 2025 and Sandesh (in 
Gujarati) dated 227 November 2025, and in Weekly Gazette of India dated 27" December 2025 for the general public 
to make observations/ representations on the proposed transmission route within two months from the date of 
publication. Subsequently, M/s Halvad Transmission Limited has submitted an affidavit dated 31.03.2026 declaring 
that 60 nos. of representations were received within two months from the date of Publication of the Corrigendum in 
the official Gazette of Government of India and the same have been answered by M/s Halvad Transmission Limited 
and that no further representations were received within the 2 months’ notice period.
