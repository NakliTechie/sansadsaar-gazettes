INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-18/14979-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Mittal College Of Nursing situated at Mahoba Road Khop Chhatarpur Mp, Chhatarpur, 
Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 471001, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh 
and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/14979-INC (52825) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Madhya Pradesh Room No. 241, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was 
issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Mittal College Of Nursing situated at Mahoba 
Road Khop Chhatarpur Mp, Chhatarpur, Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 471001, Madhya Pradesh. In the 
notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government asrespects the case 
of Mittal College Of Nursing. Even the institution, Mittal College Of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Due to non-admission to GNM course since 
2020-2021 Indian Nursing Council has considered GNM program as closed. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Mittal College Of Nursing situated at 
Mahoba Road Khop Chhatarpur Mp, Chhatarpur, Distt.- Chhatarpur, Pin Code- 471001, Madhya Pradesh 
shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
