NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l6th April, 2025 


9.0. 74(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.032(E) Dated:28 February 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH-i49B in the stretch of land from Km.!2.000 to Km.38.200 (Office of Sub Divisional Officer 
(Revenue) and Sub Divisional Magistrate Korba, District Korba in the State of Chhattisgarh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Patrika” and “Times of India” both 
Newspaper dated 05 March 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
