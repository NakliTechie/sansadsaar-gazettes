INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F. No. 8-3/5765-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Dr. A.K. Memorial College of Nursing situated at Tamsa Marg Meeranpur, Akbarpur, Distt. 
Ambedkar Nagar 22422 Uttar Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Uttar Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh 
Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct ANM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-3I/l5765-INC (49648) dated 6/0i/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Government of Uttar Pradesh, 57 Floor, Room No. 56, VikasBhawan, Janpath Market, Vidhan 
Sabha Road, Hazratganj, Lucknow -22600] Uttar Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 6/0/2024 was also 
enclosed to Dr. A.K. Memorial College of Nursing and Hospital situated at Tamsa Marg Meeranpur, Akbarpur, 
Distt. Ambedkar Nagar 22422 Uttar Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit 
their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Dr. A.K. Memorial College of Nursing and Hospital. Even the institution Dr. A.K. Memorial College of 
Nursing and Hospital has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
9/07/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Dr. A.K. Memorial College of Nursing 
And Hospital situated at Tamsa Marg Meeranpur, Akbarpur, Distt. Ambedkar Nagar 22422 Uttar Pradesh 
shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
