S.O. 2376(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred under Rule 16(1) of the Petroleum Rules, 2002, the 

Central Government, in consideration of the necessity to import petroleum and gas in public interest, hereby notifies 

Chhara Port {M/s Simar Ports Private Limited (SPPL)} in Gujarat for import of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) into 

India by sea in accordance with operations permitted in Navigational Safety at Ports Committee (NSPC) certificate 
number NSPC/62/2024 dated 03.10.2024 for a period of three year from 19.05.2025 to 18.05.2028.
