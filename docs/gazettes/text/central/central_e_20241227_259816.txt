जिला मुरादाबाद-244604 (उत्तर प्रदेश) को भी भेजी गई थी। जारी किए गए नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की

संस्था यानी एस डी कॉलेज ऑफ नर्सिंग ने भी जारी किए गए नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F.No. 8-3/5386-INC: — In exercise of the powers conferred under Section |4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, S D College of Nursing situated at Opposite Mandi Samiti, Thakurwara, Distt. Moradabad 
24460 Uttar Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Uttar Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh Nurses & Midwives 
Council to conduct B.Se(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-3I/I5386-INC (53868) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Government of Uttar Pradesh, 57 Floor, Room No. 5l6, VikasBhawan, Janpath Market, Vidhan 
Sabha Road, Hazratganj, Lucknow -22600! Uttar Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also 
enclosed to S D College of Nursing situated at Opposite Mandi Samiti, Thakurwara, Distt. Moradabad 24460 
Uttar Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the 
Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of S D College of Nursing. Even the institution S D College of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
28/03/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc. (N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from S D College of Nursing situated at 
Opposite Mandi Samiti, Thakurwara, Distt. Moradabad 24460 Uttar Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered 
only in the State of Uttar Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
