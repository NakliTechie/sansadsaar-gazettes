MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
Western Railway - Construction Organization 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 3rd August, 2023 


S.O. 3496(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. l680(E), dated 7th April, 2023 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part I, 
section-3, Sub-section (ii), dated 8th April, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under sub-section (l) 
of section 204 of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification 
for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely, Rajkot-Kanalus doubling 
of Broad Gauge single line in District Jamnagar in the State of Gujarat; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily “The Indian Express” dated 2th 
April, 2023 and “Divya Bhaskar” dated !2th April, 2023 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act; And, 
whereas, Nil objection has been received by the competent authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the power 
conferred by sub- section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
