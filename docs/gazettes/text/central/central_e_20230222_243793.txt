NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

S.O. 811 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 31 of the Contract Labour ( Regulation and 
Abolition ) Act , 1970 ( 37 of 1970 ) , the Central Government , after consultation with the Central Advisory Contract 
Labour Board , hereby exempts the Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port , Kolkata ( erstwhile Kolkata Port Trust ) from the 
applicability of notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and Employment number S.O. 
959 ( E ) , dated the 7th July , 2005 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part - II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) , 
dated the 7th July , 2005 , in respect of employment of contract labour in the works of sleeper , renewal of railway 
tracks , repairing , restoration , laying and linking of tracks for a further period of two years with effect from the date of 
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , subject to compliance with the conditions specified under sub 
clause ( a ) of clause ( v ) of sub - rule ( 2 ) of rule 25 of the Contract Labour ( Regulation and Abolition ) Central 
Rules , 1971 .
