S.O. 3648(E).— Whereas, the Director (Printing), Directorate of Printing, New Delhi, an 
attached Office of Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India, New Delhi has been 
declared as the appointing authority and has also been conferred with the powers of disciplinary authority 
and appellate authority in respect of certain Group ‘B’, Group ‘C’ and Group ‘D’ posts (upgraded to Group 
‘C’ as Multi-Tasking Staff) in the Directorate of Printing, in terms of the Central Civil Services 
(Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965; 


And whereas, the post of Director (Printing), Directorate of Printing had fallen vacant on the 150 
February, 2021, consequent upon the completion of deputation period of then incumbent and the post is 
held on additional charge since then; 


And whereas, Shri Manoj Kumar Sharvar, Director (PSP), Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs 
has been given additional charge of the Director (Printing) vide Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs’ 
Office Memorandum No. A-22012/2/2025-Admn-I-MoHUA (EFS-9212041) dated 10.06.2026; 


And whereas, there is an urgent need to make an arrangement to provide for the performance of the 
functions of the appointing authority, disciplinary authority and appellate authority hitherto exercised by 
the Director (Printing) for smooth functioning in the Directorate of Printing in the absence of a regular 
incumbent to the post of Director (Printing), Directorate of Printing. 


Now, therefore, in pursuance of sub-rule (2) of Rule 9 and sub-rule (2) of Rule 12 of the Central 
Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, the President is pleased to authorize Shri 
Manoj Kumar Sharvar, Director (PSP) in the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to exercise powers of 
the appointing authority, disciplinary authority and appellate authority vested in the Director (Printing) in 
respect of Group ‘B’, Group ‘C’ and Group ‘D’ posts (upgraded to Group ‘C’ as Multi-Tasking Staff) in 
the Directorate of Printing till a regular appointment is made to the post of Director (Printing) in 
Directorate of Printing or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
