MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th February, 2025 


S.O. 048(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 53I9(E) Dated:09/2/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification building (widening/two laning etc.,), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 205 (Km.9/6 of New NH-7]) on the stretch of land for "Construction of 2 Lane 
ROB and its approaches in lieu of Level Crossing No.95 at Km.83/600 (Railway Chainage 45/500-600) on NH- 
205" in the district of Annamayya in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The HANS INDIA” and “Praja 
Sakthi” both dated 8/2/2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
