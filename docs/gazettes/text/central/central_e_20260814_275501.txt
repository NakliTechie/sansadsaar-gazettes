MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the l4th August, 2026 


S.O. 454(E) .— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 2476(E) Dated: 3/05/2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of 
National Highway No. |46 [Bhopal (Ratnagiri Tiraha) - Vidisha (Morikodi) section] in the stretch of land from Km. 
0.800 to Km. |.000 and Km. |.900 to Km. 4.200 in the district of BHOPAL in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in news paper "Dainik Bhaskar" and 
(Free Press" both dated 24.05.2026; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has not received any objections under Section 3-C; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
