INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-18 / 12883 - INC.– In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas Sardar Patel School of Nursing situated at Panchayat Bhurimati , Vill Ambakua , Teh Ranapur , Distt . 
Jhabua - 457993 , Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh 
Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-18 / 12883 - INC dated 24/06/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary ( Health & FW ) , Government of Madhya Pradesh , 
Room No. 241 , Mantralaya ( Vallabh Bhawan ) Area Hills , Bhopal – 462004 , Madhya Pradesh was issued . Copy of the 
notice dated 24/06/2022 was also enclosed to Sardar Patel School of Nursing situated at Panchayat Bhurimati , Vill 
Ambakua , Teh – Ranapur , Distt . Jhabua - 457993 , Madhya Pradesh . In the notice issued , the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Sardar Patel School of Nursing , Madhya Pradesh . Even the institution Sardar Patel School of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
15/11/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) of Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 
declares that with effect from 08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Sardar Patel 
School of Nursing situated at Panchayat Bhurimati , Vill Ambakua , Teh Ranapur , Distt . Jhabua - 457993 , 
Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to the orders of State 
government of Madhya Pradesh . 


Lt. Col ( Dr ) SARVJEET KAUR , Secy .
