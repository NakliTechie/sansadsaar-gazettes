New Delhi, the Ist September, 2023 


S.O. 3892(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (3) of section 7 of the 
Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 952 (9 of 952), the Central Government hereby 
makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Labour and 
Employment number S.0.433(E), dated the 29th May, 205, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3, sub-section(ii), namely:— 


In the said notification, in paragraph 4, after the words “Proceeds arising out of’, the words “sale or 
redemption of investment in equities and related investments,” shall be inserted and shall be deemed to have been 
inserted with effect from the 24" day of August, 2023.
