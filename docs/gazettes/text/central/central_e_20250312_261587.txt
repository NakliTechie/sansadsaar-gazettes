MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2th March, 2025 


S.O. I50(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways vide S.O.No. 2735 (E) dated 2.07.2024 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for 4 lanes access controlled (New NH-!63G) Green Field Highway from Warangal to 
Khammam of length !08.24 Km. for the stretch of land from Km. !40.00 to Km. 40.300 & Km. 42.400 to Km. 
53.200 (Office of Revenue Divisional Officer cum CALA, Narsampet) in the District of Warangal in the State of 
TELANGANA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Mana Telangana” and “The Times 
of India” both dated 25.07.2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
