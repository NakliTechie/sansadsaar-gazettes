NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th August, 2024 


9.0. 356(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 82! Dated:26/04/2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (six-laning), maintenance, management and operation of MH/KN Border 
(Nimbal Village, Aland Taluk, Gulbarga District) to KN/TS Border (Singnodi Village, Raichur Taluk and District) as 
a part of Surat - Chennai Economic Corridor under Bharatmala Pariyojana in the stretch of land from Km. 2.000 to 
Km. 23.450 and Km. 26.000 to Km. 97.000 in the District of Gulbarga (Kalaburagi) in the State of KARNATAKA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” and “Samyukta 
Karnataka” both dated 23.05.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received no objections under Section 3C of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
