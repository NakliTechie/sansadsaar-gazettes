G.S.R. 155(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of session 435D read with section 
435E of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 (44 of 1958), the Central Government, hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Shipping, dated the 2 September, 
2020, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) vide number G.S.R.543 (E), 
dated the 2" September, 2020, namely:-
