NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad,, the 12th December , 2024 


S.O. 388(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 79 at Km. 195/23-25 in between Sirpur Kaghaznagar station " as a Special Railway Project 
for acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in the District of Komaram Bheem Asifabad for providing 
National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette.
