INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F. No. 18-18/15036-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Choudhary Ramcharanlal Sahu Nursing Institute situated at Itwara Bazar, Kandeli, Distt. 
Narsinghpur 487001 Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh 
Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM, B.Sc(N), and PB.B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/15036-INC (52763) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh Room No. 241, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Distt. 
Bhopal 462004 Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Choudhary 
Ramcharanlal Sahu Nursing Institute situated at Itwara Bazar, Kandeli, Distt. Narsinghpur 487001 Madhya 
Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as 
respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Choudhary Ramcharanlal Sahu Nursing Institute Even the institution Choudhary Ramcharanlal Sahu 
Nursing Institute has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
28/03/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM, B.Sc(N) and PB.B.Sc(N)Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM, B.Sc(N) and PB.B.Sc(N) from Choudhary 
Ramcharanlal Sahu Nursing Institute situated at Itwara Bazar, Kandeli, Distt. Narsinghpur 487001 Madhya 
Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
