New Delhi, the 9th June, 2025 


5.0. 253(E).— In pursuance of sub-rule (b) of rule 3 of the Passport (Entry into India) Rules, 950, the 
Central Government hereby designates Nathu La Check Post located in the East Sikkim District of Sikkim State as an 
authorized Immigration Check Post on a temporary basis for entry into/exit from India with valid travel documents for 
the pilgrims of Kailash Manasarovar Yatra 2025. 


[F. No. 25022/28/2025-Imm]
