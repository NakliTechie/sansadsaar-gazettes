परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 4128 तारीख 11/09/2025, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग 1, खण्ड 3, उपखंड (ii) A प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा महाराष्ट्र राज्य के कोल्हापूर जिले में NH-166 के 
कि.मी. 67.140 से कि.मी.145.300(रत्वागिरी-कोल्हापूर सेक्शन) तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के निर्माण (चौड़ा करने / चार

अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

"टाईम्स ऑफ इंडिया (अंग्रेजी) और "दैनिक लोकमत " (मराठी) दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

New Delhi, the 6th February, 2026 


S.O. 623(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4128 Dated:11/09/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-

annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH-166 in the stretch of land from Km. 67.140 to Km. 145.300 (Ratnagiri-Kolhapur Section ) in the district of 
KOLHAPUR in the state of MAHARASHTRA. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "Daily Lokmat" in Marathi dtd 
09.10.2025 And "Daily Times Of India" in English dtd 09.10.2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 

And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 

Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 


conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
