INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 7th October , 2022 

F.No. 18 - 11 / 14022 - INC : – In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Panchamrut Institute of Nursing situated at K P Residency to Abjibapa Green Road , Ahmedabad 
382350 , Gujarat is recognized by State Government of Gujarat and Gujarat Nursing Council . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-02 / 14022 - INC / 14209 dated 09/04/2021 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to the Commissioner ( Health ) , & Ex - Officio Secretary ( Public Health & 
Family Welfare ) Block No. 05 , 1st Floor Old Sachiwalay Dr. Jivraj Bhavan , Sector No. 10 , Gandhinagar – 382010 , 
Gujarat was issued . Copy of the notice dated 09/04/2021 was also enclosed to Panchamrut Institute of Nursing 
situated at K P Residency to Abjibapa Green Road , Ahmedabad - 382350 , Gujarat . In the notice issued , the State 
Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution . 


Whereas , the Council received a letter dated 08/06/2021 from Panchamrut Institute of Nursing . Thereafter the 
Council issued letter dated 08/07/2021 to the Panchamrut Institute of Nursing . However no action , as required , has 
been taken by the Panchamrut Institute of Nursing . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 06/09/2020 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , clinical and 
teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run B.Sc ( N ) Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 25/06/2022 any 
person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc ( N ) from Panchamrut Institute of Nursing situated at 
K P Residency to Abjibapa Green Road , Ahmedabad - 382350 , Gujarat shall be entitled to be registered only in 
the State of Gujarat subject to orders of State government of Gujarat .
