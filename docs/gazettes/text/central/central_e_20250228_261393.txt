New Delhi, the 28th February, 2025 

S.O. 042(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways Act, 
956(48 of 956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of the Schedule 
annexed here to as the competent authorities to perform the functions of such authorities under the said Act with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land specified in 
the corresponding entry in column (3) of the said Schedule relating to state, districts, taluk and villages mentioned 
in column (4), (5), (6) and (7) respectively of the said Schedule for building (maintenance, management and 
operation) for construction of Ramgarh bypass, ROB and LHS at LC-95 on Alwar-Nuh Section of NH-248A in the 
State of Rajasthan.
