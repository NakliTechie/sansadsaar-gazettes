MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS 
( Department of Telecommunications ) 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 21st October , 2022 

G.S.R. 793 ( E ) .- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the Indian Telegraph Act , 
1885 ( 13 of 1885 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian 
Telegraph Rules , 1951 , namely : - 

( 1 ) These rules may be called the Indian Telegraph ( 2nd Amendment ) Rules , 2022 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 

2. In the Indian Telegraph Rules , 1951 , in rule 525 , in sub - rule ( 2 ) , in clause ( v ) , for item ( p ) , the 
following item shall be substituted , namely : - 

" ( p ) For hiring or restoration or capacity enhancement of International Bandwidth for internet 
connectivity to Agartala from Bangladesh Submarine Cable Company Limited Bangladesh via Cox 
Bazar or Kuakata , Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited to be supported for a period mentioned in the 
Agreement from the actual date of hiring or restoration or capacity enhancement of the aforesaid 
bandwidth . " .
