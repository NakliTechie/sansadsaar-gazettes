NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 5th August, 2025 


S.O. 3605(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level 
Crossing No.16 at Km. 15/20-22 between Nidamanuru and Uppaluru stations" as a Special Railway Project 
for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Krishna for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
