आज़ाद सिपाही (हिंदी) और टाइम्स ऑफ़ इंडिया (इंग्लिश) दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st January, 2026 


S.O. 337(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 540 Dated:30/01/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (11) 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH75 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 130.6 to Km. 147.54 in the district of PALAMU in the state of JHARKHAND; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Azad Sipahi (Hindi) and Times of 
India (English) dated 09.02.2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled the same 
appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
