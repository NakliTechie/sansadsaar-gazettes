S.O. 4617(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 4 of the Marine Products 
Export Development Authority Act, 1972 (13 of 1972), read with rules 3 and 4 of the Marine Products Export 
Development Authority Rules, 1972, the Central Government hereby appoints the following persons as members of the 
Marine Products Export Development Authority and for that purpose makes the following further amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Department of Commerce 
S.O. number 4072 (8), dated the 26" August, 2022, namely:- 


In the said notification, for serial numbers 3 and 4, and the entries relating thereto, the following serial numbers 
and entries shall respectively be substituted, namely: - 


“3, Shri Hibi Eden, Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha - Member 
4. Shri Balashowry Vallabhaneni, Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha = - Member”.
