MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th March, 2024 


S.O. 1389(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 1729(E) Dated 13.04.2023, published in the Gazette of India No.1649 dated 13.04.2023, 
Extraordinary Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for Widening from two lane to four lane 
with paved shoulder in km 60/650-73/2 and strengthening in km 60/650 - 61/8, 62/8-63/4, 65/2-67/2, 67/6-70/0, 71/2- 
71/450 and 71/750 — 73/2 and only wearing course at km.61/8-62/8, 63/4-65/2, 67/2-67/6, 70/0-71/2 including 
widening of minor bridges, construction of additional bridge at km.64/2, widening and reconstruction of culverts, 
construction of retaining walls and drain in built up areas in Musiri - Namakkal road (NH 381B) in the district of 
Namakkal in the state of Tamil Nadu 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The New Indian Express” in 
English Language dated 01.08.2023 and “Dinakaran” in Tamil Language dated 30.07.2023; under sub-section (3) of 
section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
