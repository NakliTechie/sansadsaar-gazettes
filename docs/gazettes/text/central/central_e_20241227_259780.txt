INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F.No. 18-04/15821-INC: —In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Niru Bora Institute of Health & Science Education situated at Vill: Gasbari, Ward No.8, 
Morigaon, Distt. Morigaon 782105 Assam is recognized by State Government of Assam and Assam Nurses 
Midwives & Health Visitor Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-04/15821(53861) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 
of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Assam, C.M 
Block 3rd Floor Assam Secretariat, Dispur, Guwahati, Assam -781006. Assam was issued. Copy of the notice dated 
26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Niru Bora Institute of Health & Science Education at Vill: Gasbari, Ward No.8, 
Morigaon, Distt. Morigaon 782105 Assam In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their 
recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Niru Bora Institute of Health & Science Education. Even the institution, Niru Bora Institute of Health & 
Science Education has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
23/08/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 


clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Niru Bora Institute of Health & Science 
Education situated at Vill: Gasbari, Ward No.8, Morigaon, Distt. Morigaon 782105 Assam shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Assam. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
