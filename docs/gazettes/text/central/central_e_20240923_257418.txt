MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 
७.0. 4161(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of Road Over Bridge in lieu of Level Crossing No. 455 at Km. 
693/12-14 between Gullipadu - Narsipatnam Road stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the 
State of Andhra Pradesh in the District of Anakapalli for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with 


effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
