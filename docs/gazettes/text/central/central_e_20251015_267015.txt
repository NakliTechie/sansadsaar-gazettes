S.O. 4687(E).—Whereas, by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (South 
Eastern Railway/Construction Organization), number 2996, S.0.3063(E), dated the 9" July, 2025 published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 9 July, 2025 and issued under sub 
section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989)(herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government, decided its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the notification number 
2996, S.O. 3063(E), dated the 9" July, 2025 for execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special 
Railway Project, namely ‘Construction of Nalpur-Bauria ROB in lieu of LC २०.।8 at Km. 23/7-9’ in the district of 
Howrah in the State of West Bengal. 


And, whereas, the substance of notification number 2996, 5.0. 3063(E), dated the 9" July, 2025 was 
published in the daily newspapers, namely ‘Bartaman’ (Bengali Edition), dated 6™ July, 2024 and ‘’The Telegraph’ 
(English Edition), dated 6" July, 2024 under section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And, whereas, no petition/objection was received against notification number 2996, S.0.3063(E), dated 
the 9" July, 2025 for consideration of the competent aurhority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted the report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on the publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
