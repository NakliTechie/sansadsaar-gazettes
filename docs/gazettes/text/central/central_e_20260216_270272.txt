Packaging Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 1987 (10 of 1987), the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry 
of Textiles, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (i1) vide number 
5.0. 1830(E), dated the 227 April, 2025, namely:- 


In the said Order, in the Preamble, in paragraph 4, for the words, figures and letters “up to the 30" 
June, 2025”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 30 June, 2026 or until further orders, whichever is 
earlier” shall be substituted.
