(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986,

interest to amend the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS, sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides that whenever 
it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS, the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with 
the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for 
amending the notification number S.O. 3595(E), dated the 30" November, 206;

amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change,
