Mumbai, the 23rd April, 2024 


S.O. 1793(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause 37A of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act. 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Construction of 2 lane Road Over Bridge (ROB) 
in lieu of LC No. 267 at Km 441/16-18 between section Vadodara-Geratpur of Vadodara Division on Western 
Railway.” as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Gujarat, for providing national 
infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
