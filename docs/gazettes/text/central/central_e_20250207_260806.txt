G.S.R. 120 (E).- In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of sub-section (1) read 
with sub-section (2) of section 7 of the Customs Act, 1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Board of Indirect 
Taxes and Customs hereby makes the following further amendment in the notification of the Government 
of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) No. 61/94-Customs (N.T.) dated the 217 November, 
1994, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 
828 (E), dated the 21" November, 1994, namely :- 

In the said notification, in the Table, against serial number 6 relating to the State of Gujarat, in 
column (3), after the entry at (d) and corresponding entry in column (4), the following item and entries shall 
be inserted, namely :- 


“(e) Rajkot Unloading of imported goods and the loading of export goods or any class 
lof such goods.".
