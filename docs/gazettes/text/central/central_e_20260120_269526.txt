NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 20th January, 2026 


S.O. 290(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for the 
Development, Maintenance, Management, and Operation of Greenfield High-Speed Corridor (66.80 km, NH-6) from 
Mawlyngkhung (near Shillong) in Meghalaya to Panchgram (near Silchar) in Assam, in the stretch of land from 
design km 66+220 to km 66+ 340 & km 66+405 to km 47+ 250 in the district of East Jaintia in the State of 
Meghalaya, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, The Deputy Commissioner 
(Revenue), East Jaintia Hills District, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority 
shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing 
all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by 
order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
