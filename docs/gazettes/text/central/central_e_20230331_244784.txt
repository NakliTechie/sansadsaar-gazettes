S.O. 1529 ( E ) . – In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 4 ) of section 51A of the

No.19 / 2022 - Customs ( N.T. ) dated the 30th March 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , 
Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) vide S.O. 1512 ( E ) , dated the 30th March , 2022 , namely , -

be inserted : 


b . In the said notification , in para 2 , for the words , ` 1st April , 2023` , the words ` 1st May , 2023` shall 
be substituted .
