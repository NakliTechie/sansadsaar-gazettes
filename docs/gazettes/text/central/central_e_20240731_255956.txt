New Delhi, the 31st July, 2024 
No. 19/2024-Central Excise 


G.S.R. 466(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944 
(1 of 1944) read with section 147 of the Finance Act, 2002 (20 of 2002), the Central Government, on being 
satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, hereby makes the following further amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), No. 18/2022-Central 
Excise, dated the 19" July, 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), 
vide number G.S.R. 584 (E), dated the 19" July, 2022, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, - 
(i) against S. No. 1, for the entry in column (4), the entry “Rs. 4600 per tonne” shall be substituted; 


2. This notification shall come into force on the 1“ day of August, 2024.
