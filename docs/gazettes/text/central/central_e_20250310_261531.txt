hereby appoints Shri N. Yuvaraj, IAS, Secretary to Govt. of Andhra Pradesh, Infrastructure & Investment 
Department, in place of Shri S. Suresh Kumar, IAS, Secretary to Govt. of Andhra Pradesh, Infrastructure & 
Investment Department,, Govt. of Andhra Pradesh, as a representative of State Govt. on the Board of 
Visakhapatnam Port Authority under Section 3 (1)(c)G) of Major Port Authorities Act, 2021 with immediate 


effect and until further orders.
