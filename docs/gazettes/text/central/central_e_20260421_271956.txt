MINISTRY OF FINANCE

(CENTRAL BOARD OF INDIRECT TAXES AND CUSTOMS) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st April, 2026 
No. 01/2026 — Central Tax 


G.S.R. 302(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (6) of section 39 of the Central Goods 
and Services Tax Act, 2017 (12 of 2017), the Commissioner, on the recommendations of the GST Council, hereby 
extends the due date for furnishing the return in FORM GSTR-3B for the month of March, 2026 till the twenty-first 
day of April, 2026, for the registered persons who are required to furnish return under sub-section (1) of section 39 read 
with clause (1) of sub-rule (1) of rule 61 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017. 


2. This notification shall come into effect from 20th day of April, 2026.
