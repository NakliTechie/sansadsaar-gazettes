INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd July, 2024 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 


F.No. 18-18/7901-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, B M School of Nursing situated at Near Pedi School Khai Road, Bhopal, Distt Vidhisha 
464001, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses 
Registration Council to conduct B.Sc(N), Post Basic B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-18/7901-INC dated 22/06/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of Madhya 
Pradesh Room No. 241, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. 
Copy of the notice dated 22/06/2022 was also enclosed to B M School of Nursing situated at Near Pedi School Khai 
Road, Bhopal, Distt Vidhisha 464001, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested 
to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of B M School of Nursing. Even the institution, B M School of Nursing has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
13/09/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
B.Sc(N), Post Basic B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
08/09/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N), Post Basic B.Sc(N) and M.Sc(N) Course from 
B M College of Nursing situated at Near Pedi School Khai Road, Bhopal, Distt Vidhisha 464001, Madhya 
Pradesh, shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh subject to orders of state government 
of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
