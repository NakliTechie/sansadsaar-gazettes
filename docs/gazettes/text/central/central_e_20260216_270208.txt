MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th February, 2026 


S.O. 825(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways. S.O. 5859 (E) Dated 17/12/2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, section 3, Sub- 
Section (ii) Issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of National 
Highway 965 DD on the stretch of land from Km. 75.100 to Km. 88.100 in the district of Raigad in the State of 
Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 5859 (E) Dated 17/12/2025 has been published in 
"Dainik Ratnagiri Times" and "Dainik Aple Samrajya" both are dated 01 /01 /2026; under sub-section (3) of section 
3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
