New Delhi, the 16th February, 2026 
S.O. 813(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, S.O. 1169 (E) Dated 12 March 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 


referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of National

Highway No.752G on the stretch of land from Km. 64/000 to Km. 101/140 Satana to Mangrul (Section - III) in the

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Maharashtra Times 
(Marathi)” and “Deshdoot Times (English)” both dated 25 March 2025 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received 1 objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority Deputy Collector,(Land Acquisition) 
National Highway Project, Nashik and in exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the 
said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
