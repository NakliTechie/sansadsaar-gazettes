NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3lst March, 2026 
No. 74/2025-26 
Subject: Continuation of RODTEP Scheme beyond March 3, 2026 


S.O. 857(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development 
and Regulation) Act, 992, read with Para |.02 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to 
time, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the Remission of Duties and Taxes 
on Exported Products (RODTEP) Scheme: 


The RoDTEP Scheme shall continue for all eligible export products for a further period of six months 
with effect from April 0l, 2026, to September 30, 2026. 


The rates and value caps as notified in Appendix 4R and Appendix 4RE, as applicable on March 3], 
2026, shall continue unchanged during the aforesaid period. 


The other terms and conditions governing the Scheme shall remain unchanged. 


Effect of this Notification: Eligible exports made during the period from 0.04.2026 to 30.09.2026 shall 
continue to be entitled to RODTEP benefit at the rates and value caps in force as on 3.03.2026, subject to 
the existing terms and conditions of the Scheme.
