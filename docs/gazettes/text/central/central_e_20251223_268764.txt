LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd December, 2025 


F. No. 37/1/(VI)/2025/T.—The following order by the President dated the 23rd December, 2025 
is published for general information:- 


“In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sub-clause (a) of clause (2) of article 85 
of the Constitution, I hereby prorogue the Lok Sabha. 


December 23, 2025 Droupadi Murmu, 
PRESIDENT” 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, Secy. General
