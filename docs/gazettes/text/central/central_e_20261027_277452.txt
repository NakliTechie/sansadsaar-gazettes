MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[East Coast Railway (Construction)] 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th October, 2026 
NOTICE UNDER SECTION 20E OF THE RAILWAY ACT, 989 


5.0. 5795(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Railways (East Coast 
Railway, Construction Organization), Notification number, S.O. 3865(E) dated the 227 January, 2026 published in 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 287 January, 2026 (herein after referred to 
as the said notification) and issued under sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein 
after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its decision to acquire the land specified in the 
schedule annexed to the said notification and also annexed hereto, for the purpose of execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely “3rd line between Nirgundi — Barang (22 Km) & 
Khurda Road - Vizianagaram (363 Kms) of newly sanctioned 3rd line between Bhadrak-Vizianagarm project (525Km)” 
under Khurda Road and Waltair Division of East Coast Railway in the State of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.” in the 
district of Khordha of Odisha state. 

And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspapers namely "Dharitri" 
in Odiya (Bhubaneswar Edition) on dated 3" February, 2026, and 'The Times of India’ in English (Bhubaneswar 
Edition) dated 3" February, 2026 under sub- section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 

And whereas, no objections were received; Competent Authority has communicated the same in terms of 
section 20D of the said Act. 

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 

Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of the notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed here to 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.

Brief description of land to be acquired, with or without structures, falling within the Special Railway Project,

3rd line between Bhadrak-Vizianagarm project (525Km)” under Khurda Road and Waltair Division of East Coast
