Subject : Export of Non-basmati White Rice (under HS Code 10063090) to Comoros, Madagascar, Equatorial 
Guinea, Egypt and Kenya through National Cooperative Exports Limited (NCEL). 


S.O. 5183(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Section 3 read with section 5 of the Foreign Trade 
(Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 (No. 22 of 1992), as amended, read with Para 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign 
Trade Policy, 2023 and in accordance with the provision contained in Para 2 (iv) of Notification No. 20/2023 dated 
20.07.2023, export of following quantity of Non-basmati White Rice (under HS Code 10063090) to Comoros, 
Madagascar, Equatorial Guinea, Egypt and Kenya is permitted through National Cooperative Exports Limited 
(NCEL) : - 


SI. No. Country Name Quantity

2 Madagascar 50,000 MT
