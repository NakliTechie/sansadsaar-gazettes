. Short title and commencement.—(|) These regulations may be called the Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) 
Second Amendment Regulations, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) Regulations, 206 (hereinafter referred to as the principal regulations), 
in regulation 27, a sub-regulation (4) shall be inserted after sub-regulation (3), namely:— 


“(4) Where the Authority is satisfied that the biometric information captured at the time of enrolment for 
Aadhaar or at the time of biometric update, is mixed with the biometric information of another individual, such 
Aadhaar number shall be omitted in the manner as specified by the Authority.” 


3. In the principal regulations, in regulation 28,— 
(a) in sub-regulation (l), a sub-clause (ca) shall be inserted after sub-clause (c), namely:— 


“(ca) Aadhaar number holder was aged five years or above but was enrolled without capturing core 
biometric information and later biometric update has also been carried out.” 


(b) in sub-regulation (), a sub-clause (ea) shall be inserted after sub-clause (e), namely:— 
“(ea) 


(i) Where the Authority receives information regarding the death of an Aadhaar number holder including the 
Aadhaar number of the deceased individual, wherever available, the said information shall be verified in a 
manner as prescribed by the Authority, and upon such verification, the Aadhaar number shall be deactivated. 


(ii) In order to ensure continued accuracy of information in the CIDR or to verify the information pertaining 
to the death of the Aadhar number holder received under sub-regulation (i), the Authority: 


A. — shall seek information from the Registrar General of India or Chief Registrar for a State appointed 
under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, /969, or 


B. may seek information from any other entity in the Aadhaar ecosystem; or 
C. may receive information from any individual.” 


(c) in sub-regulation (2), the word “temporarily”, wherever it occurs, shall be omitted. 


4. In the principal regulations, in regulation 30, in sub-regulation (), a proviso shall be inserted after the second 
proviso, namely: — 


“Provided further that the same shall not be required in case of deactivation of Aadhaar number of a 
deceased person under clause (ea) of sub-Regulation (7) of regulation 28.”
