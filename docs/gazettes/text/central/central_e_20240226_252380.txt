S.O. 856(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S. O. 204 (A) dated the 4h May, 2023 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I], 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 4% May, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in

the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely Kalyan (Ambivali)-Murbad New Railway Line in the district of Thane in the 
State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Sakal 
(Marathi)” and “Active Time (English)” Dated 16" May, 2023 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in. Village Potgaon, Ghorle, Nandani, Pasheni, Devpe, Shastrinagar, Malinagar, Murbad in 
Murbad Taluka no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority 
accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
