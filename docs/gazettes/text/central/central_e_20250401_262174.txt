S.O. 540(E).—Whereas the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by 
section 3 of the Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, 958 (28 of 958) had declared 8 districts and 2] 
police Stations in 5 other districts of the state of Nagaland as ‘disturbed area’ for a period of 6 months with 
effect from I“ April, 2025 vide notification number 5.0. 536(E) dated 30" March, 2025. 


And whereas, in November, 2024, the Government of Nagaland created the district of 'Meluri' by 
carving it out of 'Phek' district, which has been declared a ‘disturbed area’ under the aforementioned powers. 


Now therefore, in partial modification of notification number S.O. 536 (E) dated 30" March, 
2025, the district 'Meluri' is also declared as ‘disturbed area’ under section 3 of the Armed Forces (Special 
Powers) Act, 958 (28 of 958) for a period of 6 months with affect from * April, 2025 unless withdrawn 
earlier.
