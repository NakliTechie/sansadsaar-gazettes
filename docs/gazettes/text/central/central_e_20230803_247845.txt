आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा |

S.O. 3490(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH419 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 50.800 to Km. 65.326 (Dhanbad) in the district of DHANBAD in the state of 
JHARKHAND, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Project Name:- (Widening two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc. Maintenance, Management and
