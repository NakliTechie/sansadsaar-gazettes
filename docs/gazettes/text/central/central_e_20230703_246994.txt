S.O. 2899(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 8 read with sub-section (1) 
of section 10 of the Competition Act, 2002 (12 of 2003), the Central Government hereby, appoints Smt. Ravneet Kaur

charge of the post or till the date of attaining the age of sixty-five years or until further orders, whichever is the 


earliest.
