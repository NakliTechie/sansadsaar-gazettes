पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उप - धारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की संख्या 3825 ( अ ) दिनांक 16th अगस्त 2022 जो भारत के राजपत्र , असाधारण , 
भाग- II , खण्ड -3 , उपखंड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , द्वारा पश्चिम बंगाल राज्य के पश्चिम मेदिनीपुर जिले में NH116A के 
किमी . 0 + 000 से किमी . 56 + 500 ( इकोनॉमिक कॉरिडोर का खड़गपुर - बर्धमान - मोरग्राम खण्ड ) तक के भू - खण्ड के 
निर्माण ( चौड़ीकरण / पेव्ड शोल्डर सहित 2- लेन / 4 - लेन का बनाना आदि ) , अनुरक्षण , प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन 
के लिए उक्त अधिसूचना के साथ संलग्न अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अधिग्रहण करने के अपने इरादा की घोषणा की थी ; 


और जबकि धारा 3 ग की उप धारा ( 1 ) के तहत सभी आपत्तियां प्राप्त कर ली गई हैं और उन पर सक्षम प्राधिकारी 
द्वारा धारा 3 ग की उप धारा ( 2 ) के तहत विचार और अस्वीकृत कर दिया गया

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 12th December , 2022 

S.O. 5796 ( E ) .— Whereas by the Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , Number SO 3825 ( E ) dated 16th August 2022 published in Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , 
Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said Notification for building ( widening / two laning with paved shoulder / four laning etc. ) , 
maintenance , management and operation of NH - 116A ( Kharagpur - Bardhaman - Moregram Section of Economic 
Corridor ) in the stretch of land from Km . 0 + 000 to Km . 56 + 500 in the district of PASCHIM MEDINIPUR in the State 
of WEST BENGAL . 


And whereas the substance of the said Notification has been published in “ BARTAMAN ” and “ TIMES OF 
INDIA " both dated 03rd September 2022 under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 


And whereas all the objections under sub section ( 1 ) of Section 3C have been received and the same have 
been considered and disallowed by the competent Authority under Sub Section ( 2 ) of Section 3C ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of Section 3D of the said Act , the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
