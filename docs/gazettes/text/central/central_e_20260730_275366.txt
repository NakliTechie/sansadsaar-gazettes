Functions of The Samiti 


The functions of the Samiti will be to render advice with regard to the implementation of the provisions 
contained in the Constitution, Official Language Act and Rules relating to Official Language Hindi, decisions of the 
Kendriya Hindi Samiti and directions/instructions issued by the Department of Official Language and to promote the 
Progressive use of Hindi in the functioning of the Ministry of Minority Affairs. 


Tenure of the Samiti 


The tenure of the Samiti shall ordinarily be three years from the date of its constitution, provided that: 


A. 


B. 


General 


A member of Parliament who is a member, of the Samiti, ceases to be a member of the Samiti as soon 
as he/she ceases to be a Member of Parliament. 


The Ex-officio Members of the Samiti shall remain members of the samiti as long as they hold their 
offices. 


If a vacancy arises in the Samiti due to death or resignation etc. of a member, the member nominated 
on the vacancy shall hold office for the remaining period of the Samiti. 


If a person is nominated as a member of the Samiti in the middle of its tenure, he/she shall be member 
of the Samiti for the remaining period. 


The Headquarters of the Samiti shall be at New Delhi, but if considered necessary, it may hold its meetings at 
any other place also. 


TRAVELLING ALLOWANCES AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


Travelling allowances and Daily Allowances would be paid to the non-official members of the Samiti for 
participating the meetings as per the instructions contained in the Deptt. of Official Language, Ministry of Home 
Affairs, Govt. of India in O.M. No. 11/20034/4/86-0L (A-2) dated 22.1.1987 and as per the rates prescribed by Govt. 
of India from time to time.
