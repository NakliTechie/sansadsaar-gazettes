Bilaspur, the 7th August, 2026 
Notice under Section 20E of the Railway Act, 989 


S.O. 4435(E).— Whereas, the Central Government had declared its intention to acquire the lands specified in 
the schedules annexed to the notifications issued under sub-section (.) of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 
989) (hereinafter referred to as 'the said Act')—specifically, the notification of the Ministry of Railways (South East 
Central Railway), Government of India, No. 8.0. 27(E) dated 30th April 2026, published in the Gazette of India 
(Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii)) on 30th April 2026, covering areas in Taluka Arjuni Morgaon of 
Gondia District, Maharashtra; and the notification of the Ministry of Railways (South East Central Railway), 
Government of India, No. S.O. 2623(E) dated 25th May 2026, published on 25th May 2026, covering areas in Taluka 
Sadak Arjuni of Gondia District, Maharashtra—for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management, and operation 
of the special railway project, namely, the "Gondia-Balharshah (Chandafort) Doubling (240 kilometers)."
