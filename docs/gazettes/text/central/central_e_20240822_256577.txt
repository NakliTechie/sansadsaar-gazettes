New Delhi, the 22nd August, 2024 


S.O. 3557 (E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry 
of Road Transport and Highways, 2388 Dated: 20/06/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the 
National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH45A in the stretch of land from Km. 29 to Km. 67 (New Collector Office 
Building, Cuddalore) in the district of CUDDALORE in the state of TAMIL NADU 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Daily Thanthi” 
and “The New Indian Express” both dated 12/07/2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, 
considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the 
competent authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise 
of the powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for 
the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the 
land specified in the said Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all 
encumbrances.
