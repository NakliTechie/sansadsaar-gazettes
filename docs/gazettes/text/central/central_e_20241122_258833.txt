New Delhi, the 22nd November, 2024 


9.0. 5013(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 2393 Dated:20/06/2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NH63 in the stretch of land from Km. 86 to Km. 124.3 (Armoor - Jagtial - Mancherial Section) in the 
district of JAGTIAL in the state of TELANGANA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” English daily 
and “Namaste Telangana” Telugu daily both dated 6th July, 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said 
Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately;

And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers
