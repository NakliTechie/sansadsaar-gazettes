New Delhi, the Ist June, 2023 


S.O. 2387(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways Act, 
956(48 of 956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of the Schedule 
annexed here to as the competent authorities to perform the functions of such authorities under the said Act with effect 
from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land specified in the 
corresponding entry in column (3) of the said Schedule relating to state, districts, taluk and villages mentioned in 
column (4), (5), (6) and (7) respectively of the said Schedule for building (widening/four lane etc.), maintenance 
management and operation development of Newly declared NH-727AA to 2/4-Lane Standard Highway from Bettiah 
on NH-727 connecting Patjirwa, Pakhnaha in the state of Bihar, Pipraghat and terminate at Sewrahi Jn. with NH-730 
in the State of Uttar Pradesh.
