MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Revenue) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th March, 2026 


S.O. 397(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 50 read with sub-sections (l) and (3) of 
section 4 of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 973 (46 of 973) and sub-sections (3), (4) and (5) of section 49 of 
the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 999 (42 of 999), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of Government of India, Ministry of Finance number S.O. 2565 (E), dated the 30th 
September, 204, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table,- 
(a) against serial No. 3, in column (3), for the entry, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:- 
"Cases involving an amount or value in excess of rupees two crore but not exceeding rupees ten crore"; 
(b) against serial No. 4, in column (3), for the entry, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:- 
"Cases involving an amount or value in excess of rupees two crore but not exceeding rupees ten crore”. 
[F. No. K-022/80/20]-Ad.ED] 
RAJEEV LOCHAN, Under Secy. 


Note : The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 2565(E), dated the 30th September, 204.
