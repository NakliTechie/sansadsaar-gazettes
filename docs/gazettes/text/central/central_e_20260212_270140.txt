F. No. 01/94/180/258/AM21/PC-4.— In exercise of the powers conferred under paragraph 1.03 & 2.04 of the 
Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby makes 
the following amendment with immediate effect. 


2. The amendments in Para 4.73 (19) of HBP-2023 is as under: 


Para No. Existing Para Amended Para 


Para 4.73 (19) | GIA Laboratory, DMCC, Dubai, UAE GIA Laboratory, FZCO, Dubai, UAE 


Effect of this Public Notice: The suffix of the GIA Laboratory, DMCC, Dubai, UAE listed at S.No.19 of Para 4.73 of 
the HBoP-2023 has been changed from “DMCC” to “FZCO”.
