MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th December, 2025 


5.0. 5845(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, S.O. 1167 (E) dated 12.03.2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/Six-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation in respect of the stretch of the land from km. 13.000 to km. 43.700 of the New National Highways NH-148NA 
extension of DND-Faridabad-Ballabgarh-Bypass junction with Delhi-Vadodra Expressway near KMP interchange in 
the stretch of land in the District of Faridabad in the state of Haryana. 


[भाग ए---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Hari Bhoomi” and “The Hans” both 
dated 27.03.2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 


the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
