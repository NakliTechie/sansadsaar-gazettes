S.O. 4872(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by clause (a) of Section 3 of the National Highways 
Act, 1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby authorizes the officers mentioned in column (2) of the

with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, in respect of the stretch of land 
from Km. 0.00 to Km. 42.000 of the 2 lane with Paved shoulder Greenfield Highway for building, maintenance, 
management and operation in the State of UTTARAKHAND.
