INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the llth May, 2023 


F. No. 8-8/452-INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section |4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas Dr. S P Singh Institute of Science & Management situated at 45/45A, 46-A, 68-A, Samanvaya 
Nagar, A Wadhpuri Bhel Distt. Bhopal — 462022, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya 
Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. |8-8/l452-INC dated 27/07/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to Principal Secretary (Health &FW), Government of Madhya Pradesh, 
Room No. 24, Mantralaya (Vallabh Bhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was issued. Copy of the 
notice dated 27/07/2022 was also enclosed to Dr. S P Singh Institute of Science & Management situated at 45/45A, 
46-A, 68-A, Samanvaya Nagar, A Wadhpuri Bhel Distt. Bhopal — 462022, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, 
the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the 
nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Dr. S P Singh Institute of Science & Management, Madhya Pradesh. Even the institution Dr. S P Singh 
Institute of Science & Management has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
07/0/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) of Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 
declares that with effect from 6/l/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of Post Basic B.Sc(N) Dr. S P 
Singh Institute of Science & Management situated at 45/45A, 46-A, 68-A, Samanvaya Nagar, A Wadhpuri Bhel 
Distt. Bhopal — 462022, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh 
subject to the orders of State government of Madhya Pradesh. 


Lt. Col (Dr.) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
