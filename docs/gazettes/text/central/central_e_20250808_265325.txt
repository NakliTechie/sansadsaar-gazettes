NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 4th August, 2025 
Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 3647(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) the Central Government hereby notifies “Doubling Between Puntamba — Sainagar 
Shirdi (16.50 Km)” as Railway project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for 
acquisition of land in Ahilyanagar District of Maharashtra State for providing national infrastructure for a 
public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
