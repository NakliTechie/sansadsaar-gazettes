Mumbai, the 26th June, 2024 


S.O. 2465(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 1989 
(24 of 1989) the Central Government hereby notifies “Bhusawal -Khandwa 37 & 47 line Project (131 Kms)” as 
Railway project under Central Railway, under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Jalgaon District 
of Maharashtra State, Burhanpur & Khandwa District of Madhya Pradesh State, for providing national infrastructure 
for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
