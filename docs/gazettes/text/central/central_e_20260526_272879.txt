Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 2647(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The Railways Act, 989 (24 
of 989) (hereinafter referred to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (No.I of 2008), the Central Government hereby 
notifies “New BG Line between Jalna-Khamgaon Project (62 Kms)” as Railway project under Central Railway, 
under “Special Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Jalna & Khamgaon District in the State of Maharashtra, 
for providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in 
the Official Gazette.
