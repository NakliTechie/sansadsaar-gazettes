99.0. 5236(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () read with clauses (५) and (xiv) of sub- 
section (2) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986), and sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the 
Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the 
notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide notification number S.O. 260 (E), dated the 
3i° May, 202, namely:- 


In the said notification, - 
() in paragraph 2, - 


(a) in sub-paragraph (), for the words, “approved by the Central Government in the Ministry of 
Environment and Forests’, the words “approved by the Competent Authority of the State 
Government for its effective implementation” shall be substituted; 


(b) for sub-paragraph (8), the following paragraph shall be substituted, namely:- 


“(8) Pending the preparation of the Zonal Master Plan for Eco-sensitive Zone and approval thereof 
by the Competent Authority of the State Government, all new constructions and other developmental 
activities shall be referred to the Monitoring Committee constituted under paragraph 4 of this 
notification.”. 


Gi) in paragraph 4, - 
(a) in sub-paragraph (2),- 
(i) in item (c), the words “to be nominated by the Government of India” shall be deleted; 


(ii) in item (g), the words “to be nominated by the Ministry of Environment and Forests, 
Government of India” shall be deleted. 


(b) in sub-paragraph (4), for the words, “Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, for prior 
clearances under the provisions of the said notification”, the words “Ministry of Environment, 
Forest and Climate Change or the State Environment Impact Assessment Authority, Gujarat, as the 
case may be, for prior clearances under the provisions of the said notification.” Shall be substituted.
