l. Short title and commencement.- (l) This order may be called the Vegetable Oil Products Production and 
Availability (Regulation) Amendment Order, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2 In the Vegetable Oil Products Production and Availability (Regulation) Order, 20] (hereinafter referred to as 
the said order), for paragraph 2,the following shall be substituted, namely:—“2. Definitions.-() In this order, unless the 
context otherwise requires,- 


(a) “Act” means the Essential Commodities Act, 955(0 of l955);

(g) “nodal officer” means officer as defined in clause (da) of section 2 of the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 (7 
of 2009); 


(h) “producer” means a person or firm or company engaged in the business of producing any vegetable oil product 
or any solvent-extracted vegetable oil, either in his own factory or in the factory of any other person, acting

(i) “registration certificate” means the registration granted to a producer under paragraph 4 of this order.

notified by the Government from time to time, blended (blends of two or more different types of vegetable 
oils); 

(2) Words and expressions used but not defined in this order and defined in the Essential Commodities Act, 
955 (l0 of 955) or the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (34 of 2006) or rules or regulations made

respectively”.
