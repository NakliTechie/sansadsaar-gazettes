Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 987 (0 of 987), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry of Textiles, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, sub-Section (ii) vide number 8.0. 830(E), dated the 227५ April,

2025”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 3 December, 2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier’ shall 
be substituted.
