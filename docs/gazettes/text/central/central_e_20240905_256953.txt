रख-रखाव, प्रबंध और प्रचालन के प्रयोजन के लिए उक्त अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि को अर्जित करने

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 

Jaipur, the 5th September, 2024 
S.O. 383(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North 
Western Railway Construction), S.O. No. 249] (E) dated 03 July, 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 04 July 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) under 
sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 2008 ( of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the 
Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said 


notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely " Dobh 
Bahali-Bhiwani (42.30 km ) doubling Project in the district of Rohtak in the state of Hayrana”. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Amar ujala” 
“Hari Bhumi” and “Dainik Bhaskar” dated July ,2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection were received by the Competent Authority within due time. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
