MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 25th October, 2024 


S.O. 4680(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of 
Road Transport and Highways, 2058-E Dated:21/05/2024, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation of NH325 in the stretch of land from 
Km. 73.6 to Km. 103.3 (SDO OFFICE JALORE) in the district of JALORE in the state of RAJASTHAN 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Rajasthan 
Patrika” dated 31.05.2024 and “Dainik Bhaskar” dated 31.05.2024 “ under sub-section (3) of section 3A 
of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 7 


settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
