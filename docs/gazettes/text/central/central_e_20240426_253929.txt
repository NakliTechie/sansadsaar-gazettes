MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th April, 2024 


S.O. 808(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.0.58 (E) Dated the 05th February 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/six/eight laning, etc.), maintenance, management 
and operation of Mumbai - Vadodara Expressway Spur to NH-NFE4 in the stretch of land from Km. 8.925 to Km. 
45.400 (Maharashtra Section) in the district of Thane in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Lokmat”(Marathi) and “The 
Free Press Journal”(English) both dated 22th February, 2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
