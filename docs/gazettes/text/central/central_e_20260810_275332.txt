MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th August, 2026 


G.S.R. 717(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (a) and (b) 
of sub-section (2) of section 165 of the Assam Rifles Act, 2006 (47 of 2006), the Central Government hereby 
makes the following rules to amend the Assam Rifles, Rifleman/Riflewomen (General Duty), Group ‘C’ 
combatised post, Recruitment Rules, 2023 namely: - 


1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called the Assam Rifles, 
Rifleman/Riflewomen (General Duty), Group ‘C’ combatised post Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2: In the Assam Rifles, Rifleman/Riflewomen (General Duty), Group ‘C’ combatised post Recruitment 
Rules, 2023, in the Schedule, 
(a) in column (6), for the entries, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 
“Between 18 and 23 years of age. 


Note 1: The crucial date for determination of the age-limit shall be as advertised by the Staff 
Selection Commission or the concerned Nodal Force. 


Note 2: The upper age-limit shall be relaxable upto five years for the candidates of the first 
batch of Ex-Agniveers. 


Note 3: The upper age-limit shall be relaxable upto three years for the candidates of Ex- 
Agniveers.”; 


(b) in column (7), for the entries, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 

“(a) | Educational qualification: Matric or equivalent from a board recognised by the 
Central Government or State Government or equivalent Army qualification in case of Ex- 
Army personnel. 

(b) Physical and Medical Standard: Physical and medical standard for recruitment to the 
post of Rifleman/ Riflewomen (General Duty) shall be applicable as per Scheme prescribed 
by the Central Government from time to time. 

(c) The candidate must qualify Physical Efficiency Test and written examination as 
prescribed for Rifleman/ Riflewomen (General Duty) as specified in the advertisement for 


recruitment. 


Note: Ex-Agniveers shall be exempted from written examination,Physical 
Standard Test and Physical Efficiency Test”. 


(c) in column (10), for the entries, the following shall be substituted, namely: - 
“By direct recruitment. 


Note 1: Fifty per cent. of the vacancies shall be reserved for Ex-Agniveers for recruitment 
to the post of Rifleman/Riflewomen (General Duty) in every recruitment year.

Note 2: In the first phase, the recruitment shall be conducted by the Nodal Force for fifty 
per cent. vacancies earmarked for Ex-Agniveer or as per the instructions issued by the 
Ministry of Home Affairs from time to time. 


Note 3: In the second phase, recruitment shall be conducted through open examination 
as per the existing provisions against the remaining fifty per cent. of the vacancies 
along with the unfilled vacancies of the first phase or as per the instructions issued by the 
Ministry of Home Affairs from time to time. 


Note 4: The existing provisions of reservation for various categories shall be applicable for 
both phases. 


Note 5: Five per cent. of the vacancies of overall General Duty personnel shall be reserved 
for Riflewomen (General Duty) till such time that the overall strength of Riflewomen 
(General Duty) does not exceed total five per cent. strength of General Duty cadre”.
