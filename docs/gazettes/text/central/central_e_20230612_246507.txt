INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th June, 2023 


F.No. 8-75/2360-INC).— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 947 (XLVI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Ambigara Chowdaiah College of Nursing situated at No. 8/2, Chokkanahalli, Jakkur Post, 
Hegdenagar, Yelahanka Hobli, Bangalore — 560064, Karnataka is recognized by State Government of Karnataka 
and Karnataka Nursing Council to conduct B.Sc(N) course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-5/2360-INC dated 9/09/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to Principal Secretary, Health and Family Welfare Department, Room 
No. l05, 0I* Floor, Vikas Souda, Bangalore — 56000l, Karnataka was issued. Copy of the notice dated 9/09/2022 
was also enclosed to Ambigara Chowdaiah College of Nursing situated at No. 8/2, Chokkanahalli, Jakkur Post, 
Hegdenagar, Yelahanka Hobli, Bangalore — 560064, Karnataka. In the notice issued, the State Government was 
requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Ambigara Chowdaiah College of Nursing. Even the institution Ambigara Chowdaiah College of Nursing has 
not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
5/02/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3)(b) declares that with effect from 
23/02/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) from Ambigara Chowdaiah College of 
Nursing situated at No. 8/2, Chokkanahalli, Jakkur Post, Hegdenagar, Yelahanka Hobli, Bangalore — 560064, 
Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject to the orders of State government 
of Karnataka.
