MINISTRY OF FINANCE

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd October, 2025. 


S.O. 4830(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (e) of section 450 of the 
Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Central Government hereby specifies the 
Municipal Debt Securities, having the meaning assigned to it in the Securities and Exchange Board 
of India Act, 1992 (15 of 1992) or the rules or regulations made thereunder, to be as security under 
this section for the purposes of “repo” and “reverse repo”.
