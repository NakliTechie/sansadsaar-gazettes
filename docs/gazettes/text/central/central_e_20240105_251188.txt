New Delhi, the 5th January, 2024 


No. N-16/1/2021-PLAN.—As per Government of India’s Resolution No.17015/7/2018-RI Cell dated 
27.03.2019 regarding National Commission for Safai Karamcharis (NCSK), the President is pleased to appoint

tenure of the Commission).
