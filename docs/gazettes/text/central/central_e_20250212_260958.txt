MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 12th February, 2025 


S.O. 739(E).—The President is pleased to accept the resignation of Ms. Atishi, Chief Minister, 
National Capital Territory of Delhi along with her Council of Ministers with immediate effect. She will, 
however, continue to act as the Chief Minister, National Capital Territory of Delhi, till the new Chief Minister 
is sworn in.
