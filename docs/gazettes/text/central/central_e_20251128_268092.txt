Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire

Widening of existing Highway 60, 2/4-laning with Paved shoulder, in the stretch of land, from NH-60 Package 
I, Starting from Village Phaltan at the Junction of Phaltan-Baramati Road Km 30+430 To Dahiwadi Km 
340+250 District of Satara in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O.553(E) dated 29th November 2024, has been 
published in“The Times of India (English)” Dated 26 December 2024 And “Dainik Sakal” & “Dainik 
Punyanagari(Marathi)” Dated 25 December 2024 And “Dainik Sakal” & “Dainik Punyanagari” Dated 03 
January 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
