5.0. 909(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
Act, 1989 the Central Government hereby notifies "३४ & 47 Line between Guntakal — Wadi stations 
(230.00 Km)" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in the 
Districts of Ananthapuramu, Kurnool and in the State of Telangana in the District of Narayanpet and in the 
State of Karnataka in the Districts of Raichur, Yadgiri, Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
