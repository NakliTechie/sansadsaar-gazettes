Oral Order 

52 / Dated : 01.12.2022 

It is submitted by learned counsel , Mr. Arvind Kumar Lall that he is representing election 
petitioner- Smt . Nirmala Devi and on her instruction , earlier a withdrawal petition was filed . After filing of 
the withdrawal petition , notice for withdrawal has been duly published in the official gazette of Election 
Commission of India under Section 111 of the Representation of People Act , 1951 and no objection has 
been received so far . 


Under the circumstance , instant petition is dismissed as withdrawn . 

Let a copy of this order be communicated to Chief Election Commissioner , New Delhi and 
Chairman of Rajya Sabha . 


On the prayer of learned counsel , security amount of Rs.2000 / - deposited at the time of filing of 
election petition in terms of Section 111 of the Representation of People Act , 1951 is permitted to be 
withdrawn . 


I.A. No. 6416 of 2022 filed for withdrawal of election petition , is hereby , allowed . 
All the other I.As. are accordingly disposed of . 


Smt . Nirmala Devi
