New Delhi, the 2th November, 2024 


S.O. 4887(E)— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4068 Dated:9/09/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (]) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of NHI30CD in the stretch of land from Km. 396.8 to Km. 42].! (Vizianagaram) in the district of 
VIZIANAGARAM in the state of ANDHRA PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Praja Sakti” and “Times of 
India” both dated 30/09/2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
