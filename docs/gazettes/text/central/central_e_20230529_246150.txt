MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th May, 2023 


G.S.R. 395(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by section 32 of the Metro Railways (Operation and 
Maintenance) Act, 2002 (60 of 2002), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, further to amend the 
Metro Railways (Carriage and Ticket) Rules, 2014, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Metro Railways (Carriage and Ticket) Amendment Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Metro Railways (Carriage and Ticket) Rules, 2014, in rule 4, in sub-rule (2), after clause (g), the 
following proviso shall be inserted, namely:- 


Provided that a person duly authorised in this behalf by a hospital registered under the Transplantation of 
Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994 (42 of 1994), may take along, human organs or tissue or both, for the 
purpose of organ or tissue transplant.
