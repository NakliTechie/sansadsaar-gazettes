MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th June, 2026 


S.O. 3040(E).— Whereas, M/s Renaissance Designbuild Private Limited, had proposed under section 3 of the 
Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a Special Economic 
Zone for Information Technology and Information Technology Enabled Services at Plot No. 47, Koorgally Industrial 
Area, Village Koorgally, Hobli, Ilwala, Talauka Mysore, District Mysore in the State of Karnataka; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, had notified an area of 10.118 hectares at 
the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number S.O. 11(E) dated 
47 January, 2011; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s Renaissance Designbuild Private Limited has now proposed to de-notify the entire 
area of 10.118 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Karnataka has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal vide 
letter No.CI 12 SPI 2022 dated 13% November, 2025. Further, after de-notification, such de-notified area will conform 
to land use guidelines/master places of the State Government; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Cochin Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of the entire area of 10.118 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in the exercise of the powers conferred by the first proviso to rule 8 of the Special Economic 
Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except for things done or omitted to 
be done before such rescission.
