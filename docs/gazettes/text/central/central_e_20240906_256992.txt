MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th September, 2024 


S.O. 3842(E).—In partial modification of Government of India notification no. S.O. 89l(E) dated 
02.05.2024 published in the Extraordinary Gazette of India dated 02.05.2024 and in pursuance of clause (a) of Section 
2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (50 of 962), (hereinafter 
called the said Act), the Government of India hereby authorizes Smt. Sujata Gandhe, Deputy Collector (General), 
Wardha, Government of Maharashtra to perform the functions of Competent Authority for Mumbai — Nagpur — 
Jharsuguda Natural Gas Pipeline Project (MNJPL) of M/s GAIL (India) Limited in the State of Maharashtra under the 
Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962, on deputation basis, for a period 
of three years, instead of her earlier authorization on additional charge basis. 


2, This notification will be effective from the date of its issue.
