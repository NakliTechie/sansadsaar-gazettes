“Transmission system for providing connectivity to M/s Bhadla Three SKP Green Ventures Private Limited for its 300 
MW Renewable Power Park in Phalodi, Rajasthan.” 


And whereas, Ministry of Power, Government of India vide its letter No. 25-17/17/2026-POWER GRID
