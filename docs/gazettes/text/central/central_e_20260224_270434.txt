MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th February, 2026 


S.O. 968(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways number 8.0. 608I(E) dated 29/2/2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (L) of section 3A of the National Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening / two lane with paved shoulder/ four laning etc.) 
maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.-60 SPUR on the stretch of land from Km. 
2+928 to Km. 63+378 Bhimashankar —Taleghar-Wada- Rajgurunagar Road in the District of Pune in the state 
of Maharashtra. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S.O. 608I(E) dated 29/2/2025 has been published in 
“Business Standard (English)”“Janprawas (Marathi)”both are Dated 09//2026 under sub-section (3) of section 
3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections filed under section 3-C, Considered and settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
