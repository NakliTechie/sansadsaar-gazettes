New Delhi, the l3th October, 2025 


५.0. 4650(E).— In exercise of the power conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act-2008, the Central Government hereby notifies the project of “Construction of 3rd & 4th Line between Delhi - 
Panipat - Ambala (98 km) in Delhi Division of Northern Railway” as a "Special Railway Project" for acquisition 
of land in Sonipat, Panipat, Karnal, Kurukshetra and Ambala Districts in the state of Haryana; Districts North Delhi, 
North West Delhi and Central Delhi in the National Capital Region, New Delhi for providing national infrastructure for 
a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the official gazette.
