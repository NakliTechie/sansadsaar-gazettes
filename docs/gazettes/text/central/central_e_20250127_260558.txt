MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2025 


9.0. 479(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O 4799 (E) dt: 05..2024 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Widening / four 
laning etc,), maintenance , management and operation of National Highway No. 76G in the stretch of land from Km 
0.000 to Km 60.000 ( Muddanur to B.Kothapalli Section) in Sri Sathya Sai district in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India (English) ” and 
“Andhraprabha (Telugu)” both dated 4..2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections has been received from any person by the competent authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
