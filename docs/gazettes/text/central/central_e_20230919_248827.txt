New Delhi, the 19th September, 2023 


S.O. 4124(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Jan Vishwas 
(Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (18 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints the 2270 September, 
2023 as the date on which the provisions of the said Act, in so far as it relates to serial number 3, and the entries 
relating thereto in the Schedule to the said Act, relating to the Boilers Act,1923 (5 of 1923) shall come into force.
