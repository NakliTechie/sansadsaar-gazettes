MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th March, 2024 


S.O. 425(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act,!956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH367 
in the stretch of land from Km 70.00 to Km 7I.500 (Bhanapur to Gaddankeri section) in the district of GADAG in the 


state of KARNATAKA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 

Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 

Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, Special Land Acquisition officer 
National Highways Dharwad - | in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall 
give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all 
such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, 
either allow or disallow the objections. 

Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 
final. 

The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
