THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTRTED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 
(Set up under an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 4th February, 2026 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. 29-CA/Law/D-355/2026.—A Division Bench of the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi in 
CHAT.A.REF 2/2018 under Section 21(6)(c) of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 has ordered on 
23.12.2025 that the name of Shri Kishan Gupta, Chartered Accountant (M. NO. 084077), Permanent 
Address: H - 108, 2nd Floor, Opp Hotel Marina, Cannaught Place, New Delhi-110001 and Professional 
Address: 215, Gulmohar Enclave, New Delhi - 110049 be removed from the Register of Members for a 
period of one year after finding him guilty of “Other Misconduct” under Section 22 read with Section 21 of 
the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949. 


Now, therefore, pursuant to the said order of the Hon’ble Delhi High Court dt. 23.12.2025 and in 
terms of sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the said Act read with Regulation 18 of the Chartered 
Accountants Regulations, 1988, it is hereby notified that the name of Shri Kishan Gupta, Chartered 
Accountant (M. No. 084077), stands removed from the Register of Members for period of one year w.e.f. 
the date of publication of this notification.
