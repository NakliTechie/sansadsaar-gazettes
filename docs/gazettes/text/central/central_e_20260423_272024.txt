MINISTRY OF COOPERATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd April, 2026 

S.O. 2048(E).— In pursuance of statute 8 of the “Tribhuvan” Sahkari University Statutes, 2025, read 
with sub-section (2) of section 22 of the “Tribhuvan” Sahkari University Act, 2025 (11 of 2025), the Central 
Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the Government of India, 
Ministry of Cooperation, dated 16" July, 2025 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O. 3247(E), dated the 167 July, 2025, namely: — 


In the said notification, in paragraph 2, under the heading “Joint Secretary and above level officer 
from the Ministry of Cooperation”, for serial number 3 and the entry relating thereto, the following 
serial number and entry shall be substituted, namely: — 


“3. Two officers not below the rank of Joint Secretary from the Ministry of Cooperation, out of 
which one shall be an officer who is dealing with the Cooperative Policy and Parliament and the 
other one shall be an officer who is dealing with the Cooperation to Prosperity.” 


[F. No. K-17/1/2025-MoC] 
ANAND KUMAR JHA, Jt. Secy. 


Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 3247(E), dated the 16th July, 2025.
