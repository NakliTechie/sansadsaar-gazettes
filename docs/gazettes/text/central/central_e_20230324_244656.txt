MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th March, 2023 
S.O. 422(E).—Whereas the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 3 of the 
Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act, [958 (28 of 958) had declared the Tirap, Changlang and Longding districts in 
Arunachal Pradesh and the areas falling within the jurisdiction of Namsai and Mahadevpur police stations in Namsai 


district of Arunachal Pradesh, bordering the State of Assam as ‘disturbed area’ vide Notification S.0.4623(E) dated 
30.09.2022. 


And whereas a further review of the law and order situation in the state of Arunachal Pradesh has been 
undertaken. 


Now, therefore, Tirap, Changlang and Longding districts in Arunachal Pradesh and the areas falling within 
the jurisdiction of Namsai, Mahadevpur and Chowkham police stations in Namsai district of Arunachal Pradesh, 
bordering the State of Assam, are declared as ‘disturbed area’ under Section 3 of the Armed Forces (Special Powers) 
Act, 4958 for a period of six months with effect from 0.04.2023, unless withdrawn earlier.
