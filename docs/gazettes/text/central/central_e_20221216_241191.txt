MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 16th December , 2022 

S.O. 5903 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , S.O. No. 249 ( E ) , Dt.18.01.2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , 
Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) 
( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for ( Widening / two / four lanning with paved shoulder ) , Maintenance , 
management and operation of National Highway No. 927D in the stretch of land from Km . 72 + 200 to Km . 107 + 500 
( Dhoraji - Jamkandorna - Kalavad - Jamnagar Section ) in the District of RAJKOT in the state of GUJARAT . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “ Gujarat Samachar ” Dt 23.01.2022 
and " The Times of India " Dt . 09.02.2022 ; under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
Deputy Collector , Dhoraji ; 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Deputy Collector , Dhoraji has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Deputy Collector , Dhoraji and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
