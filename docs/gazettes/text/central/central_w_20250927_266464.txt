2. Functions of the Committee : 


The function of the Committee will be to advise the Central Government regarding the progressive use of Hindi in the 
Ministry of Law and Justice (Legislative Department and Department of Legal Affairs) in relation to the implementation of the 
principles relating to official language as mentioned in the Constitution, the Official Languages Act, 963 and the rules made 
thereunder, the policy decisions of the Central Hindi Committee and the instructions issued in relation to the official language 
by the Ministry of Home Affairs (Department of Official Language). 


3. TENURE OF TERM: 
l. The tenure of the Committee shall ordinarily be 3 years from the date of its constitution. 


2. During the tenure of the Committee, if a person is nominated as a member of the Committee, she/he shall be a 
member only for the remaining period of the tenure of that Committee. 


3. The tenure of the Committee may be reduced or extended by the Ministry under special circumstances. 


4. A Member of Parliament who is member of the Committee shall cease to be the member of this Committee if 
she/he ceases to be the Member of Parliament. 


5. An ex officio member of the Committee shall continue to be the member so long as she/he holds the office by 
virtue of which she/ he is the member of the Committee. 


4. General : 


The headquarter of the Committee will be in New Delhi, but the Committee can hold its meetings at any other place 
if necessary. 


5. Travelling Allowance and Other Allowances : 


(a) The MPs nominated to the Committee shall be paid TA/DA for attending the meetings of the Committee as per 
the provisions of Salaries, Allowances and Pension of Members of Parliament Act, 954 and the related 
amendments issued from time to time and the related rules made thereunder. 


(b) In view of the high level nature of the Committee, the other non-official members of the Committee shall be 
paid travelling allowance/daily allowance as per the guidelines issued by Department of Official Language and 
as per the rates and rules as amended/prescribed by the Government of India from time to time.
