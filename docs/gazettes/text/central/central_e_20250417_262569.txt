CORRIGENDUM NOTIFICATION 


Case No. SG-01/2024 


Subject: Preliminary findings in the Safeguard Investigation concerning imports of “Non- 
Alloy and Alloy Steel Flat Products”. 


F. No. 22/01/2024-DGTR 


1. 


Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, as amended from time to time and the 
Customs Tariff (Identification and Assessment of Safeguard Measures) Rules, 1997, as 
amended from time to time, the Director General hereby makes the following corrections 
to the Preliminary Findings dated 18 March 2025 issued vide notification No. 
22/01/2024-DGTR, in respect of the subject safeguard investigation: 


a. At paragraph 253 of the said Preliminary Findings, the words “Paragraph I of 
Annexure to” may be read as “Rule 8(i) of’. 


b. At paragraph 311 of the said Preliminary Findings, the words “Annexure to” may be 
read as “Rule 6 of’.
