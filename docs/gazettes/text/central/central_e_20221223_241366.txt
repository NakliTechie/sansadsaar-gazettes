NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 23rd December , 2022 

No. A - 60011 / 22 / 2022 - NDIAC . The President is pleased to appoint Justice Hemant Gupta , former Judge of 
the Supreme Court of India , as Chairperson , New Delhi International Arbitration Centre ( NDIAC ) , in the salary of 
Rs . 2,50,000 / - per month ( fixed ) ( level 18 of Pay Matrix ) , with effect from 23rd December , 2022 for a period of 
03 years , or till his attaining the age of 70 years , whichever is earlier , by temporary up - gradation of the post of 
Chairperson , NDIAC to the level 18 of the Pay Matrix , initially for 2 years .
