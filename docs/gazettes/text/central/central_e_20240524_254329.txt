NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th May, 2024 
INCOME-TAX 


S.O. 2103(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (v) of the Explanation to section 48 of the 
Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in 
the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), Central Board of Direct 
Taxes published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, sub-section (ii) number S.O. 1790(E), 
dated the 5" June, 2017, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Table, after serial number 23 and the entries relating thereto, the following 
serial number and entries shall be inserted, namely:- 


Table 
81. No. Financial Year Cost Inflation Index 
(1) (2) (3) 
“24 2024-25 363” 


2. This notification shall come into force with effect from the 1st day of April, 2025 and shall accordingly apply 
in relation to the assessment year 2025-26 and subsequent assessment years.

2571(E), dated the 12 June, 2023.
