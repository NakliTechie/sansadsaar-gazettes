NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 28th April, 2025 


SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT TRUSTS) 


(SECOND AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2025 


No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2025/243-In exercise of the powers conferred under section 30 read with sections 


11 and 12 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 (15 of 1992), the Board hereby makes the following 


regulations to further amend the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 


2014, namely: — 


1. 


These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Infrastructure Investment 


Trusts) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2025. 


2: 


3. 


They shall be deemed to have come into force on April 2, 2025. 


In the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, in 


regulation 18, in sub-regulation (4), in the proviso, the words and number “and (v)” shall be substituted with the words, 


numbers and symbol “, (५), (vi), (vii) and (viii)”.
