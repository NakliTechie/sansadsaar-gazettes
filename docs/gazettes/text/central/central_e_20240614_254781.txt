New Delhi, the 14th June, 2024 
S.O. 2331(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 1079(E). Dated: 06 March 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 


(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction (widening/2-laning with paved shoulder), maintenance, 


[भाग 1---खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 27 


management and operation of the National Highway No. NH-727B in the stretch of land from Km. 35+200 to 37+300 
km. (Tamkuhiraj to Salempur Road) in the District Gopalganj, Anchal Bhore, in the state of Bihar on EPC mode. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Aaj (Hindi)” and “Times of 
India (English)” both dated 13 March 2024 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And Whereas no objections have been received; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
