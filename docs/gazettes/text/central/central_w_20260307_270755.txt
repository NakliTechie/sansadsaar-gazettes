ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the I5th January, 2026 


O. N. 2.—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the Representation of the 
People Act, 950 (43 of 950), the Election Commission of India in consultation with the Government of Punjab 
hereby designates Smt. Anindita Mitra, IAS (PB:2007) as the Chief Electoral Officer for the State of Punjab with 
effect from the date she takes over charge and until further orders in place of Shri Sibin C., IAS. 


2 Smt. Anindita Mitra shall cease to hold and hand over forthwith the charge of all or any charges of work 
under the Government of Punjab, which she may be holding before such assumption of office. 


3. Smt. Anindita Mitra while functioning as the Chief Electoral Officer, Punjab shall not hold any additional 
charge whatsoever under the Government of Punjab except that she should be designated Secretary to the 
Government in charge of Election Department in the State Secretariat.
