New Delhi, the 8th October, 2024 


S.O. 4556(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3 and 4 of the Collection of 
Statistics Act, 2008 (7 of 2009), as amended in 2017 (21 of 2017), read with rules 5 and 7 of the Collection 
of Statistics Rules, 2011, the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation hereby directs Collection 
of Statistics on Capital Expenditure Outlook, Capital Expenditure Intention, Investment Activity Outlook 
and Strategies for large private sector industries through a statistical survey, hereinafter referred to as 
‘Forward Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Capex Investment Intentions’, as per the details 
mentioned in the Schedule-I. 


2: The Ministry further appoints the persons as also their assignees in office, mentioned in the 
Schedule II as Statistics Officers in respect of the geographical units mentioned thereof with reference to

the aforesaid direction. Each Statistics Officer shall immediately submit an undertaking in the prescribed 
format, namely, Form-I appended to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011, to the Additional Director 
General, Field Operations Division, National Sample Survey Office, New Delhi. 


3. The persons authorized by each Statistics Officer, after submission of undertaking in the 
prescribed format, namely, Form-I appended to the Collection of Statistics Rules, 2011 to the Statistics 
Officer, would be engaged in the geographical jurisdiction of the Statistics Officer for pursuing data 
collection through web-based interface in CAPI. 


4. The statistics collected in Forward Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Capex 
Investment Intentions schedule, will be processed by officials working at the Office of the Additional 
Director General, Enterprise Survey Division, Kolkata. The unit level data of the survey will not be 
published by the Ministry. 


5. All persons engaged in any activity in respect of collection of statistics, processing of data 
and dissemination with respect to Forward Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Capex Investment 
Intentions are governed by the provisions under the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 and the Collection of 
Statistics Rules, 2011. 


6. Following powers have been delegated to the Statistics Officers: 


a. A Statistics Officer is empowered to depute/authorize officials to pursue 
collection of statistics in respect of the aforementioned direction. 


b. A Statistics Officer is empowered to give notice to each of the informant for 
furnishing information in the format, specified for the purpose of collection of statistics. 
SCHEDULE-I 


Subject and purpose for collection of statistics: 


1. Statistics relating to Capital Expenditure Outlook, Capital Expenditure Intention, 
Investment Activity Outlook and Strategies for large private sector industries is collected through Forward 
Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Capex Investment Intentions. 


Geographical area for collection of statistics: 


2; The Forward Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Capex Investment Intentions is 
conducted in the whole of India except the villages in Andaman and Nicobar Islands which are difficult to 
access. 


Method of data collection: 


3. A Notice will be issued by each Statistics Officer to informants under his jurisdiction, 
indicating therein the date by which, the officer or office to whom, the unit or units for which, and the 
formats and the manner in which information is required to be furnished, including submission of 
information in electronic form through web based interface in Computer Assisted Personal Interview 
(CAPI) designed for the purpose and made available to informants up to a certain date, specified in the 
notice. 


Nature of informants for whom data may be collected: 
4. Enterprises that are registered with the MCA under the Companies Act, 2013. 
Period during which collection of statistics may be completed: 


5. The date for submission of information by each informant would be mentioned in the 
notice and would generally fall during October, 2024 to March, 2025. 


Reference period: 


6. Information is required to be furnished on provisional Capital Expenditure to be incurred by 
the enterprise in 2024-25 and Capital Expenditure intentions, Investment Activity Outlook and Investment 
Activity Strategy for 2025-26. 


Nature of information to be collected: 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण है 


A; The information required relates to Buying / selling capacity, Preferred / Alternative 
sectors, Preferred States/Cities, Investment strategies, Capital expenditure, Challenges and Goals of the 
industries in the given period. 


Language in which information is to be furnished by informant: 
8. An informant shall furnish information in the prescribed formats, in English. 
Obligation of informant: 


9. An owner or occupier or person in-charge of a company or enterprise or management, as the 
case may be, shall furnish information in the manner and by the date mentioned in the notice received by 
him from the concerned Statistics Officer. He should also furnish relevant records as may be required by 
the Statistics Officer or a person authorized by him for reference. 


Nature of business records and other records which may be inspected: 


10. The business records and other records will not be inspected upfront. However, in stray cases, 
official business records may require to be referred.
