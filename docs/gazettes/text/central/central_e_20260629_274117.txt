Bengaluru, the 29th June, 2026 


S.O. 3487(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Central Government (Ministry of Railway, South 
Western Railway, Construction Organisation, Bengaluru Cantonment) S.O. 4668(E) dated: 4.0.2025 published in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under Section 2, Clause 20A of Railway 
Act, 989 (24 of 989) (herein referred to as the said act) the Ministry of Railway, South Western Railway, 
Construction Organisation, Bengaluru Cantonment declare its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule 
annexed to the said notification for in lieu of level crossing No.56 for construction of two lane ROB with service 
road in the district of Tumakuru in the state of Karnataka. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in Vijaya Karnataka News Paper 
dated 30.0.2025 and in Deccan Herald News Paper dated 30.0.2025, The Hindu News Paper dated 30.0.2025

submitted its report to the Ministry of Railway, South Western Railway, Construction Organisation, Bengaluru
