WI. 18-31 / 15366-आईएनसी:-समय-समय पर यथासंशोधित भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 1947 (1947 का 
XLVI) की धारा 44 के अधीन प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ एतद्द्वारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती 
है :- 


जबकि, प्रतीक सिंह इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग एंड पैरामेडिकल साइंसेज, पोस्ट देवरा बाजार, ग्राम करौंदा मसीना,

कौंसिल द्वारा एएनएम पाठ्यक्रम संचालित करने के लिए मान्यता प्राप्त है।

लखनऊ-226001, उत्तर प्रदेश को संबोधित नोटिस संख्या 18-31 / 15366-आईएनसी (54180) दिनांक 27.03.2024 को जारी किया 
गया था। दिनांक 27.03.2024 को जारी नोटिस की एक प्रति प्रतीक सिंह इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ नर्सिंग एंड पैरामेडिकल साइंसेज, पोस्ट 
देवरा बाजार, ग्राम करौंदा मसीना, तहसील नौघर, जिला सिद्धार्थ नगर-272207 (उत्तर प्रदेश) को भी भेजी गई थी। जारी किए

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 19th November, 2024 


F.No. 18-31/15366-INC: —In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVIII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Prateek Singh Institute of Nursing And Paramedical Sciences situated at Post Deora Bazar, 
Vill.- Karaunda Masina, Tehsil - Naughar, Distt. Siddharth Nagar 272207 Uttar Pradesh is recognized by State 
Government of Uttar Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh Nurses & Midwives Council to conduct ANM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-31/15366-INC (54180) dated 27/03/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section 14 of Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to the principal Secretary, Department of Health and 
Family Welfare, Government of Uttar Pradesh, 5" Floor, Room No. 516, VikasBhawan, Janpath Market, Vidhan 
Sabha Road, Hazratganj, Lucknow -226001 Uttar Pradesh was issued. Copy of the notice dated 27/03/2024 was also 
enclosed to Prateek Singh Institute of Nursing And Paramedical Sciences situated at Post Deora Bazar, Vill.- 
Karaunda Masina, Tehsil - Naughar, Distt. Siddharth Nagar 272207 Uttar Pradesh. In the notice issued, the 
State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing 
institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Prateek Singh Institute of Nursing And Paramedical Sciences. Even the institution Prateek Singh Institute of 
Nursing And Paramedical Sciences has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
27/09/2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 1.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 
ANM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
21/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of ANM from Prateek Singh Institute of Nursing And 
Paramedical Sciences situated at Post Deora Bazar, Vill.- Karaunda Masina, Tehsil - Naughar, Distt. Siddharth 
Nagar 272207 Uttar Pradesh shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Uttar Pradesh.
