INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the I5th February, 2024 


F. No. 8-I/I5555-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, |947 (XLVIII of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Navkar Institute of Nursing situated at Giriver Pride, Opp. Hotel Safari, Beside New Odhav 
Over Bridge, Ahmedabad, Distt. Ahmedabad 38245, Gujarat is recognized by State Government of Gujarat and 
Gujarat Nursing Council to conduct B.Sc(N) & Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-/5555-INC dated 6/05/2023 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 4 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 947 addressed to the Commissioner (Health), & Ex-Officio Secretary (Public Health & 
Family Welfare) Block No. 05, [7 Floor Old Sachiwalay, Dr. Jivraj Bhavan, Sector No. 0, Gandhinagar — 38200, 
Gujarat was issued. Copy of the notice dated 6/05/2023 was also enclosed to Navkar Institute of Nursing situated 
at Giriver Pride, Opp. Hotel Safari, Beside New Odhav Over Bridge, Ahmedabad, Distt. Ahmedabad 38245, 
Gujarat. In the notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as 
respects Suitability of the nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Navkar Institute of Nursing. Even the institution, Navkar Institute of Nursing has not responded to the notices 
issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
08/I2/2022 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., 
clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute that the institution is unable to run 


B.Sc(N) & Post Basic B.Sc(N) Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section !4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
04/08/2023 any person holding a recognized qualification of B.Sc(N) & Post Basic B.Sc(N)) from Navkar Institute 
of Nursing situated at Giriver Pride, Opp. Hotel Safari, Beside New Odhav Over Bridge, Ahmedabad, Distt. 
Ahmedabad 38245, Gujarat shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Gujarat subject to orders of state 
government. 


Lt. Col (Dr) SARVJEET KAUR, Secy.
