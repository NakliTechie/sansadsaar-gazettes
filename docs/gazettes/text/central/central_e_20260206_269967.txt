MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th February, 2026 


S.O. 6(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O 2659 (E) dt: |3.06.2025 published in Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part वी, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956 (48 of |956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Widening / four 
laning etc,), maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No.67 in the stretch of land from 
Km 56.200 to Km 563.055 (Peddasettipalli Village) in the district of YSR Kadapa in the state of Andhra Pradesh. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hans India (English)” and 
“Andhra Prabha(Telugu)” both dated 22.07.2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the competent authority 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
