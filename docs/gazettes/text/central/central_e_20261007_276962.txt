NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th October, 2026 


G.S.R. 869(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (e) of sub-section (2) of section 30 of the 
India International Arbitration Centre Act, 2019 (17 of 2019), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules to amend the India International Arbitration Centre (Number of Posts and Recruitment of Registrar, Counsels 
and other Officers and Employees) Rules, 2022, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the India International Arbitration Centre (Number of Posts and Recruitment of 
Registrar, Counsels and other Officers and Employees) Amendment Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the India International Arbitration Centre (Number of Posts and Recruitment of Registrar, Counsels and other 
Officers and Employees) Rules, 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), rule 12 shall be renumbered as sub- 
rule (1) thereof and, - 


(a) in sub-rule (1) as so renumbered, for the words “other conditions of service, shall be regulated”, the words 
“other conditions of service for which no express provision has been made in these rules, shall be regulated”, 
shall be substituted; 


(b) after sub-rule (1) as so renumbered, the following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely: - 


“(2) Any officer or employee, may by giving notice of forty-five days in writing, resign from his office, or 
the Centre may, by giving notice of forty-five days, for reasons to be recorded in writing, direct any officer 
or employee to relinquish his office.”.
