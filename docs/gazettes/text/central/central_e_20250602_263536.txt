THE LADAKH AUTONOMOUS HILL DEVELOPMENT 
COUNCILS (AMENDMENT) REGULATION, 2025 


No. 2 OF 2025 
Promulgated by the President in the Seventy-sixth Year of the Republic of India. 


A Regulation further to amend the Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Councils 
Act, 997. 


In exercise of the powers conferred by article 240 of the Constitution read with 
34 of 209. sub-section (2) of section 58 of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 209, 
the President is pleased to promulgate the following Regulation made by her:— 


l. (J) This Regulation may be called the Ladakh Autonomous Hill 
Development Councils (Amendment) Regulation, 2025. 


(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by 
notification in the Official Gazette, appoint. 


Short title and 
commencement. 


2 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA EXTRAORDINARY _ [Part Il— Sec. ] 


Amendment of 2. In section 4 of the Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Councils 
oo ne Act, 997, after sub-section (/), the following sub-sections shall be inserted, 


namely:— 


“(IA) Not less than one-third of the total number of seats in the Council 
shall be reserved for women and such seats may be allotted by rotation to 
different territorial constituencies. 


(/B) The rotation of constituencies reserved for women referred to in 
sub-section (/A) shall be made on the basis of serial number allotted to each 
constituencies by notification in the Official Gazette.” 


DROUPADI MURMU, 
President. 


DR. RAJIV MANI, 
Secretary to the Govt. of India.
