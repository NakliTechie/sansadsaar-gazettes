PUBLIC NOTICE 
New Delhi, the 7th March, 2024 
No. 48/2023 
Subject: Import of Menthol covered under ITC(HS) codes: 290600, 3003902, 3302590 and 3302400 
under Advance Authorisation/DFIA - reg 


F. No. 04/89/80/Misc-3/AM-04/PC-2[A]/Vol.-III/E-4l.—In exercise of powers conferred under 
paragraph .03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby 
makes the following amendments to Handbook of Procedures (HBP) and Standard Input-Output Norms (SION) with 
immediate effect. 


2. The input Menthol covered under ITC(HS) codes 290600, 3003902, 330I2590 and 330i2400 stands 
suspended from Standard Input Output Norms and/or Adhoc Norms, wherever it appears in any SION and/or adhoc 


norms, pending review of such SION and/or Adhoc Norms. 


3. Till such review is undertaken, no Advance Authorisation and/or DFIA shall be issued for Menthol covered 
under ITC(HS) codes 290600, 3003902, 3302590 and 3302400. 


Effect of this Public Notice: 


Menthol covered under ITC(HS) codes 290600, 3003902!, 330!2590 and 330i2400 stands suspended 
from SION/Adhoc norms with immediate effect.
