ELECTION COMMISSION OF INDIA 

ORDER 

New Delhi , the 14th March , 2023 

O.N. 47. - WHEREAS , the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala , 2021 for 39- Mankada 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464 / KL 
LA / 2021 , dated 12th March , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 , every contesting candidate at 
an election shall , within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate , lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act ; and 


WHEREAS , the result of the election for 39- Mankada Assembly Constituency of Kerala , 2021 was 
declared by the Returning Officer on 02nd May , 2021 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election 
Expenses was 01st June , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , the Commission having regard to the then prevailing pandemic situation of Covid - 19 , decided 
to condone any incidence of late submission of account by candidate till 31st July , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , as per the report dated 04.06.2021 of the DEO , Malappuram under sub - rule ( 1 ) of Rule 89 of 
the Conduct of Elections Rules , 1961 received through the CEO , Kerala vide his letter no . EL1 / 2 / 2021 - ELEC , dated 
24.08.2021 stating that M. Ali , a contesting candidate from 39- Mankada Assembly Constituency in General Election 
to Legislative Assembly of Kerala , 2021 has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses , as required under 
law ; and 


WHEREAS , on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer , Malappuram a Show - Cause notice 
No. 76 / KL - LA / 2021 / CEMS - II , dated 06th October , 2021 was issued under sub rule ( 5 ) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules , 1961 by the Election Commission of India to M. Ali , for not lodging of the account of Election 
Expenses ; and 


WHEREAS , through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule ( 6 ) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules , 1961 , M. Ali was directed to submit a representation in writing to the Commission explaining the 
reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of his election expenses with the District Election 
Officer , Malappuram District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice ; and 


WHEREAS , the DEO , Malappuram in his report no . P1 / 9752 / 2022 ; dated 06.10.2022 has reported that the 
said Show Cause Notice was served to M. Ali on 09.11.2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , the District Election Officer , Malappuram in his Supplementary Report on account of the 
candidate dated 02.11.2022 has reported that M. Ali has not submitted any representation or statement of account of 
election expenses . Further , after receipt of the said notice , M. Ali has neither furnished any reason nor explanation to 
the Election Commission of India for failure to lodge the account of his election expenses as prescribed under law ; 
and 


WHEREAS , Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 provides that : 

" If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person 

( a ) has failed to lodge an account of election expenses , within the time and in the manner required by or 
under this Act , and 


( b ) has no good reason or justification for the failure , 

the Election Commission shall , by order published in the Official Gazette , declare him to be 
disqualified and any such person shall be disqualified for a period of three years from the date of the 
order . " ; 


WHEREAS , on basis of the facts and available records , the Commission is satisfied that M. Ali has failed to 
lodge the account of his election expenses and has no good reason or justification for the failure to do so ; and 


NOW , THEREFORE , in pursuance of Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 , the 
Election Commission of India hereby declares M. Ali , resident of - Mylappuram Veedu , Perinthattiri PO , Malappuram 
District- 676507 and a contesting candidate in 39- Mankada Assembly Constituency of Kerala , 2021 , to be 
disqualified for being chosen as and for being a member of either House of the Parliament or the Legislative 
Assembly or the Legislative Council of a State or Union Territory for a period of three years from the date of this 
order .

ORDER 

New Delhi , the 14th March , 2023 

O.N. 51. – WHEREAS , the General Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala , 2021 for 45 Tirur 
Assembly Constituency was held in pursuance of the Election Commission of India Notification No. 464 / KL 
LA / 2021 , dated 12th March , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , as per Section 78 of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 , every contesting candidate at 
an election shall , within 30 days from the date of election of the returned candidate , lodge with the District Election 
Officer an account of his election expenses which shall be a true copy of the account kept by him or his election agent 
under Section 77 of the said act ; and 


WHEREAS , the result of the election for 45 - Tirur Assembly Constituency of Kerala , 2021 was declared by 
the Returning Officer on 02nd May , 2021 and hence the last date for lodging the account of Election Expenses was 
01st June , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , the Commission having regard to the then prevailing pandemic situation of Covid - 19 , decided 
to condone any incidence of late submission of account by candidate till 31st July , 2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , as per the report dated 04.06.2021 of the DEO , Malappuram , under sub - rule ( 1 ) of Rule 89 of 
the Conduct of Elections Rules , 1961 , received through the CEO , Kerala vide his letter no . EL1 / 2 / 2021 - ELEC , dated 
24.08.2021 stating that Moideen Valiyakath , a contesting candidate from 45 - Tirur Assembly Constituency in 
General Election to Legislative Assembly of Kerala , 2021 has failed to lodge the account of his election expenses , as 
required under law ; and 


WHEREAS , on basis of the said report of the District Election Officer , Malappuram a Show - Cause notice 
No. 76 / KL - LA / 2021 / CEMS - II , dated 06th October , 2021 was issued under sub rule ( 5 ) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules , 1961 by the Election Commission of India to Moideen Valiyakath , for not lodging of the account of 
his Election Expenses ; and 


WHEREAS , through the above said Show Cause Notice and under sub rule ( 6 ) of Rule 89 of the Conduct of 
Elections Rules , 1961 , Moideen Valiyakath was directed to submit a representation in writing to the Commission 
explaining the reasons for not lodging the account and also to lodge the account of election expenses with the District 
Election Officer , Malappuram District within 20 days from the date of receipt of the notice ; and 


WHEREAS , the DEO , Malappuram in his report no . P1 / 9752/2022 ; dated 06.10.2022 has reported that the 
said Show Cause Notice was served to Moideen Valiyakath on 06.11.2021 ; and 


WHEREAS , the District Election Officer , Malappuram in his Supplementary Report on account of the 
candidate dated 02.11.2022 has reported that Moideen Valiyakath has not submitted any representation or statement 
of correct account of election expenses . Besides , after receipt of the said notice , Moideen Valiyakath has neither 
furnished any reason nor explanation to the Election Commission of India , for failure to lodge the account of his 
election expenses as prescribed under law ; and 

WHEREAS , Section 10A of the Representation of the People Act , 1951 provides that : 

" If the Election Commission is satisfied that a person
