MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th March, 2024 


S.O. 582(E).—Whereas, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (l) and (3) of 
section 3 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of 967), the Central Government declared 
the Jammu and Kashmir Peoples Freedom League (JKPFL), as an unlawful association vide notification 
number S.O. 44(E) dated the | 57 March, 2024, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, 
Section-3, Sub-section (ii) dated the | 5" March, 2024. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42 of the Unlawful Activities 
(Prevention) Act, 967 (37 of 967) (herein after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government 
hereby directs that all powers exercisable by it under section 7 and section 8 of the said Act shall also be 
exercised by the State Governments and the Union territory administrations in relation to the above said 
unlawful association.
