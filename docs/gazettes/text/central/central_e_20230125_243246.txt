95- क के साथ पठित कर्मचारी राज्य बीमा अधिनियम 1948 , ( 1948 का 34 ) की धारा 46 ( 2 ) द्वारा प्रदत्त

95 - क तथा मध्य प्रदेश कर्मचारी राज्य बीमा ( चिकित्सा हितलाभ ) नियम , 1954 में निर्दिष्ट चिकित्सा हितलाभ

EMPLOYEES ' STATE INSURANCE CORPORATION 


[ PART III - SEC.4 ] 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 18th January , 2023 

No. N - 17011 / 1 / MP / 2023 - P & D : In pursuance of powers conferred by Section 46 ( 2 ) of the Employees ' 
State Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , read with Regulation 95 - A of the Employees ' State Insurance ( General ) 
Regulations , 1950 , the Director General has fixed the 01/01/2023 as the date from , which the medical benefit as laid 
down in the said Regulation 95 - A and the Madhya Pradesh Employees ' State Insurance ( Medical Benefit ) Rules , 
1954 shall be extended to the families of insured persons in the entire area of Sehore , Shajapur and Guna ( in 
addition to the already notified areas in the districts ) in the State of Madhya Pradesh . 

DEEPAK JOSHI , Insurance Insurance Commissioner ( P & D )
