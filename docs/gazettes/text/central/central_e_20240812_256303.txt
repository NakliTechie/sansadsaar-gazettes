NOTIFICATION 
Patna, the 23rd July, 2024 


S.O. 3248(E).—In Exercise of Power conferred under clause 7A of Section 2 of the Railway (Amendment) 
Act 2008, the District Land Acquisition Officer/Aurangabad, as nominated by State Government, is hereby authorized 
as Competent Authority to perform the function of such authority under the said Act in the area of the Special Railway 
Project “Construction of Bulb line for Nabinagar (Ankorha) (7.49 km)”, in the District of Aurangabad, Bihar 
State with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Gazette of India.
