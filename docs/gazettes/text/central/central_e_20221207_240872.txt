MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT , FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 


NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 7th December , 2022 

S.O. 5694 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) , clause ( v ) and clause ( xiv ) 
of sub - section ( 2 ) and sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3 of the Environment ( Protection ) Act , 1986 ( 29 of 1986 ) 
read with sub - rule ( 4 ) of rule 5 of the Environment ( Protection ) Rules , 1986 , the Central Government 
hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the Government of India , Ministry of 
Environment , Forest and Climate Change , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part - II , 
Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) , vide S.O. number 2167 ( E ) , dated the 27th August , 2014 , namely : 

In the said notification , 


( A ) in paragraph 2 , sub - paragraph ( 1 ) , for the words , " Central Government in the Ministry of 
Environment , Forests and Climate Change " , the words " Competent Authority of the State 
Government " shall be substituted ; 


( B ) in paragraph 3 , for sub - paragraph ( 10 ) , for the words , " Ministry of Environment , Forests and 
Climate Change " , the words " Competent Authority of the State Government , the " shall be 
substituted .
