MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department of Commerce) 
(SEZ DIVISION) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th May, 2023 


S.O. 237(E)—WHEREAS, M/s. Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation, a state industrial promotion organization 
in the State of Gujarat had proposed under section 3 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act) to set up a sector specific Special Economic Zone for Biotechnology Sector at Biotech Park, Savli GIDC Estate, 
Village Manjusar, District Vadodara in the State of Gujarat; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 4 of the 
said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, notified an area of 5.8098 hectares at the above Special 
Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number 5.0. 2706 (E) dated 277 October, 2009; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s. Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation has now proposed to de-notify the entire area of 
5.8098 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Gujarat has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal vide letter No. 
IC/INFRA/SEZ/NOC/755292 dated 77 October, 202]; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Kandla Special Economic Zone has recommended the proposal for 
de-notification of entire area of 5.8098 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by first proviso to rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 
2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except as respects things done or omitted to be done before 
such rescission.
