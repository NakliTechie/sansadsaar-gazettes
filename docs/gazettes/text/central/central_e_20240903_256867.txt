NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 2nd September, 2024 
S.O. 375l(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 4877 (E) dated the 22 May 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 20nd May 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and issued under 
sun-section (l) of section 20 A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) and 
Railway (Amendment) Act (I! of 008), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of 
Special Railway Project, namely Construction of Rapid Loading System (RLS) Railway Tracks with Railway 
Siding at Majri Area Amalgamated Ekona I & II OCM in the district of Chandrapur in the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Navbharat” 
& “The Hitvada” on dated 07/07/2024 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act. 


And whereas, in 04 villages, l6 objections were received and the same have been considered by the 
competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20 E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section () of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose. 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20 E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
