MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department For Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 12th June, 2026 
5.0. 3038(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) and (2) of section 16 read 
with sub-section (3) of section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central 
Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do in the public interest and after 


consulting the Bureau, hereby makes the following Order further to amend the Footwear made from 
Leather and other Materials (Quality Control) Order, 2024, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) This Order may be called the Footwear made from Leather and 
other Materials (Quality Control) Amendment Order, 2026. 
(2) It shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 
2. In the Footwear made from Leather and other Materials (Quality Control) Order, 2024, in clause 2, — 
(a) in the third proviso, for figures, letters and word “ध३1१ July, 2026”, the figures, letters and word “31 
July, 2027” shall be substituted; 
(5) after the third proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 
“Provided also that nothing in this Order shall apply to the import of goods or articles up to 4500 pairs in a 
year by manufacturers of leather and footwear products for the purposes of research and development and 
non-commercial use, subject to the following conditions, that it shall, — 
(a) not be sold commercially; 
(6) be marked and embossed with the words ‘NOT FOR SALE’; 
(c) be disposed as scrap; 
and the manufacturer shall maintain year-wise records and shall be furnished to the Government as and 
when required.”.
