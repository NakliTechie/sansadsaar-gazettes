5.0. 457(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 006 (E) dated the 02ndApril, 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, section 3 Sub-section(ii), dated the 02™ April, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued 
under sub-section(l) of section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989), the Central Government declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, 
maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Mahabubnagar — Dhone doubling project 
in the District of Mahabubnagar in the State of Telangana. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely“THE HANS 
INDIA, THE HINDU, HINDI MILAP, MANA TELANGANA and REHNUMA (Urdu)” dated 6thApril 2025under 
sub section(4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, for Kodur, Kotakadra, Jannappapally, Dharamapur, Ramachandrapuram, Rekulampalle, 
Puttapalli and Venkatayapally villages of Mahabubnagar district,no objections were received and the same have been 
considered by the competent Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted 
its report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;
