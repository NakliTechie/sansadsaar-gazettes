NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, 3th June 2025 
Under clause 37A of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


S.O. 2696(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of The 
Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) the Central Government hereby notifies “Multitracking of 
Panvel-Chouk section (7.28 Km)” as Railway project under Central Railway, under “Special 
Railway Project” for acquisition of land in Raigad District of Maharashtra State for providing 
national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this 


notification in the Official Gazette.
