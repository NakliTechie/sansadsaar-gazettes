S.O. 3744(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of section 4 of the National Forensic 
Sciences University Act, 2020 (32 of 2020), the Central Government hereby notifies that the campuses established at 
Bhopal (Madhya Pradesh), Dharwad (Karnataka), and Guwahati (Assam) shall include as the campuses of the 
National Forensic Sciences University.
