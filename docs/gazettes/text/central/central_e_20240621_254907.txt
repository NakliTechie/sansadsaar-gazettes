S.O. 247(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act, 956(48 of 956), (here in after referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required 
for Building (Widening/two lane with paved shoulder/ four laning etc.) maintenance, management and operation of 
NH73IA in the stretch of land from Km.74.700 to Km. 2.950 (Special Land Acquisition Officer Banda) in the 
district of CHITRAKOOT in the state of Uttar Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquired such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under —section (l) of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority / Special Land Acquisition Officer Banda in 
the district of CHITRAKOOT in writing and shall set out the grounds there of and the Competent Authority shall give 
the objector and opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such

The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
