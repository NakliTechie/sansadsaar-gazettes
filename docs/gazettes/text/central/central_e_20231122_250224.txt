MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd November, 2023 


S.O. 5006(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. No. 382(E) Dated 7th July, 2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No. 07 on the stretch of land from Km 83.750 to Km 9.l00 (Mahesh Khunt- 
Sonbarsa Raj- Saharsa-Madhepura-Purnea Section) in the district of MADHEPURA in the state of BIHAR. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Jagran” and “The Times 
of India” both dated 29th July 2023, under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objection has been received from any person by the Competent Authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
