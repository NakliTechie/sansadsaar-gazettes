MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 12th December, 2025 
Under clause 20E of the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 


5.0. 5797(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number. S.O 5198 (A) dated 29 November 2024 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
PartII, section 3, Sub-section (1) dated the 29 November 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 20-A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred 
to the Railway (Amendment) Act 2008 (11 of 2008), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and 
operation of Special Railway Project,namely “Wardha- Nanded (via Yavatmal Pusood)” in the district of Nanded 
in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “The times of 
Gaurav” And “Godarir Samachar” Dated 30th January 2025 under sub section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And, the objections received have been considered and action taken accordingly by the competent authority. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette; the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government Free from all encumbrances.
