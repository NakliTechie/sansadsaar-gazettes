F. No. 6 / 13 / 2021- DGTR.– Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act , 1975 as amended from time 

to time ( hereinafter referred to as the " Act " ) and the Customs Tariff ( Identification , Assessment and 
Collection of Anti - dumping Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury ) Rules , 1995 , as 
amended from time to time , thereof , the designated Authority has issued the final finding notification no . 
6/13/2021 dated 23.09.2022 in respect of the anti - dumping investigation undertaken on the subject product 
“ Stainless - Steel Seamless Tubes and Pipes . ”

Zhejiang Yinlai Steel Tube Co. , Ltd. is a limited liability company . During the period of investigation ,

the company is a part of Zhejiang Group , therefore Authority has proceeded with the group treatment
