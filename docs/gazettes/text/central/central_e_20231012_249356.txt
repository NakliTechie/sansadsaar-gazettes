S.O. 4446(E).— Whereas, the Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), 
clauses (५) and (xiv) of sub-section (2) and sub-section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29

206; 


And Whereas the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public 
interest to amend the said notification; 


And whereas sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, !986 provides that whenever it 
appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the requirement of 
notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for amending the 
notification number S.O. 2275 (E), dated the “ July, 206;

(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the 
Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the erstwhile Ministry of 
Environment and Forests, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) 
number S.O. 2275 (8), dated the [7 July, 206, namely:- 


In the said notification, in paragraph 5, for serial numbers (a) and (b) and the entries relating thereto, the 
following serial numbers and entries shall be substituted, namely:-
