MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 30th May, 2025 


9७.0. 273 (E)—WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 


(Railway Board), vide number S.0.4379 (E), dated the 26th September, 2024 published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 9th October, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and issued under sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, Western Dedicated 
Freight Corridor in the District of Anand in the State of Gujarat; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “The 
Times of India” and “Sandesh”, dated 24th October, 2024, under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within the 
specified period; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government;

NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (), and in pursuance of sub-section 
(2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government, - 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 


(li) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
