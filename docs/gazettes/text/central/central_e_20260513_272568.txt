S.O. 2467(E).— In Exercise of Power conferred by clause 37A of section 2 of the Railway 
(Amendment) Act 2008, General Manager/ East Central Railway has approved to declare “Koderma — 
Barkakana (Arigada) Doubling Project (133.3282 Km)” as Special Railway Project. 


Therefore, “Koderma — Barkakana (Arigada) Doubling Project (133.3282 Km)” project is declared 
Special Railway Project in the state of Jharkhand.
