S.O. 5607(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for building 
(widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH544 in the 
stretch of land from Km. 270 to Km. 342 " Perambra Village Chalakudy Taluk (former Mukundapuram Taluk) " in 
the district of THRISSUR in the state of KERALA, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land.

notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section(l) of 
section 3C of the said Act.
