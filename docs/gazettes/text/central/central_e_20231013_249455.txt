5.0. 4503(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of section 3 of the National Highways Act, 
1956 (48 of 1956), the central government hereby makes the following amendments in the notification of the 
Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways number S.0.3596(E) dated 11th August 2023, 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II- Section 3-Sub-section(ii), dated the 11th august 2023, 
namely:- 


In the said notification, - 


1. Against Notification & Schedule, for the Notification “construction of toll plaza maintenance, management 
and operation of NH309A” shall be substituted by “up gradation (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning 
etc.) maintenance, management and operation of NH309A”. 


Foot note:- The principal notification was published vide S.0.3596(E) dated 11th August 2023 in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II- Section 3-Sub-section(ii), dated the 11th august 2023
