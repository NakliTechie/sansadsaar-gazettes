New Delhi, the 8th April, 2026 
S.O. 83(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (3) of article 77 of the Constitution, the President 


hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 96], 
namely: - 


l. 


() These rules may be called the Government of India (Allocation of Business) (Three Hundred and Eighty- 
fifth Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force at once. 


In the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, !96l, in THE SECOND SCHEDULE, under the 
heading “MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION (UPBHOKTA 
MAMLE, KHADYA AUR SARVAJANIK VITARAN MANTRALAYA)”, under the sub-heading “B. 
DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION (KHADYA AUR SARVAJANIK VITARAN 
VIBHAG)”,- 


(i) entry 4 relating to ‘Directorate of Vanaspati, Vegetable Oils and Fats.’ shall be omitted; 
(ii) entry 5 relating to ‘Directorate of Sugar, New Delhi.’ shall be omitted; 


(iii) entry 8 relating to ‘Matters relating to the Development Council of Sugar Industry, New Delhi.’ shall 
be omitted; and 


(iv) after entry 23, the following entry shall be inserted, namely: - 


“24. Directorate of Sugar and Vegetable Oils (DSVO).”. 


Droupadi Murmu 
President
