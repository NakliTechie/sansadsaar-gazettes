MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the !8th February, 2025 
S.O. 882(E).—Whereas it appears to the Central Government that in the public interest it is necessary that 
for transportation of crude oil through the Paradip—Numaligarh crude oil pipeline project of Numaligarh Refinery 


Limited from Paradip (Odisha) to Numaligarh (Assam) via Siliguri (West Bengal), a pipeline must be laid, part of 
which would fall in the State of Assam; and 


Whereas it appears to the Central Government that for the purpose of laying such pipeline, it is necessary to 
acquire the Right of User (RoU) in the land under which the said pipeline is proposed to be laid and which is 
described in the Schedule annexed hereto; 


[भाग ave 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Section (l) of Section 3 of the Petroleum and 
Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 962 (Act No. 50 of 962), the Government of India 
hereby declares its intention to acquire the Right of User (RoU) therein. 


Any person interested in the land described in the said schedule may, within 2] (twenty-one) days from the 
date on which copies of the Gazette of India containing this Notification are made available to the general public, 
object in writing to the acquisition of the Right of User (RoU) therein for laying of the pipeline under the land, to the 
Competent Authority (Assam) of the aforementioned project under Numaligarh Refinery Limited, Shri Sazzad Alam, 
ACS, Joint Secretary to the Government of Assam , Industries & Commerce Department, at the Project Office of 
Numaligarh Refinery Limited, 22A, Christian Basti, G.S. Road, Guwahati - 78!005, Assam.
