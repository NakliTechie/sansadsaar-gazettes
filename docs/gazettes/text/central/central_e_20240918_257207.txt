F. No. 8.2506/0/08/Law(HQ)/-—I, Ujjwal Tah, Chief Inspector of Mines, by virtue of powers conferred 
on me under Section 75 of the Mines Act, 952 (35 of 952) and in supersession of all the notifications published 
earlier in the Gazette of India on the subject, do hereby, generally authorize all officers of this Directorate who are 
sub-ordinate to the Chief Inspector of Mines and who are appointed as Inspector of Mines under Sub-Section () of 
the Section 5 of the said Act, to institute prosecutions for any offence under the said Act, against the owner, agent or 
manager of any mine situated within their respective Inspection Jurisdiction as declared under Sub-Section (3) of 
Section 6 of the said Act.
