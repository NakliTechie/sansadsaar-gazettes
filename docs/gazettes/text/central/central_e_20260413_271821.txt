MINISTRY OF PARLIAMENTARY AFFAIRS 
ADDENDUM NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 13th April, 2026 


G.S.R. 281(E).— In partial modification of the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of 
Parliamentary Affairs, number G.S.R. 924(E), dated the 19th December, 2025, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide CG-DL-E-26122025-268836, No. 838, dated the 26th December, 
2025, the following shall be substituted in place of Para 1(2) : 


1(2) — Asper sub-rule (2) of rule 3 of the said rules, Aadhaar authentication shall be on a voluntary basis. The Aadhaar 
number holder shall be informed of alternate and viable means of identification, and no service shall be denied to the 
Aadhaar number holder for refusing to, or being unable to, undergo Aadhaar authentication, namely:— 


List of Services: 

(a) Member Services & Entitlements 

* Member services (IT, TA/DA and Medical claims), payroll and advances, pension processing, accommodation 
services. 


(b) Health & Welfare 
¢ Linking of CGHS cards of MPs, staff and dependents, medical claim. 


(c) Identity & Access Management 
* eSign service for digital signing of official documents, Identity and access service 


(d) Administrative & Staff Services 
* Staff onboarding, service record linking, estate services, HRMS and training 


(e) Attendance & Access Control 
¢ Attendance for staff and MPs, House sessions, verification for visitor passes. 


(f) Financial & Compliance 
¢ PFMS integration service and payment gateway service. 


2: In such cases wherein the Aadhaar number holder refuses to, or is unable to, undergo Aadhaar authentication, 
the Aadhaar number holder may choose to use alternate and viable means of identification for the purposes specified 
above in para 1(2). The following alternate and viable means of identification shall also be recognized, namely: — 


(i) Permanent Account Number (PAN); 
(i1) Voter Identity Card.
