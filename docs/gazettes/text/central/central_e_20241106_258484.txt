MINISTRY OF POWER 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 6th September, 2024 

No.25/6/2023-R&R.—In terms of section 76 (6) read with section 89(l) of the Electricity Act, 2003, the 
President hereby appoints Shri Harish Dudani, Former Principal Judge (Family Court), Delhi High Court as Member, 


Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) on consolidated pay of Rs. 5,00,000/- per month (without house 
and car) for a period of five years w.e.f. 6" August 2024, or till he attains the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier.
