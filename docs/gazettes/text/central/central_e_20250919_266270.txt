5.0. 4234(E).—Whereas, M/s Gopalan Enterprises (India) Private Limited, had proposed under section 3 of 
the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) to set up a Special 
Economic Zone for Information Technology/Information Technology Enabled Services at villages Mahadevapura & 
Kaggadasapura, K R Puram, Whitefield, Bangalore, Karnataka; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 
4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, had notified an area of 4.2903 hectares 
at the above Special Economic Zone vide Ministry of Commerce and Industry Notification Number 5.0. 5(E), 
8.0. 52(E) and 5.0. 53(E) dated 47 May, 2009; 


AND, WHEREAS, M/s Gopalan Enterprises (India) Private Limited, has now proposed to de-notify the 
entire area of 4.2903 hectares of the above Special Economic Zone; 


AND, WHEREAS, the State Government of Karnataka has given No Objection Certificate to the proposal 
vide letter No. CI 28 SPI 2023 dated 5" July, 2025. Further, after de-notification, such de-notified area will conform 
to land use guidelines/master places of the State Government; 


AND, WHEREAS, the Development Commissioner, Cochin Special Economic Zone has recommended the 
proposal for de-notification of the entire area of 4.2903 hectares of the Special Economic Zone; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in the exercise of the powers conferred by the first proviso to rule 8 of the Special 
Economic Zones Rules 2006, the Central Government hereby rescinds the above notification except for things done or 
omitted to be done before such rescission.
