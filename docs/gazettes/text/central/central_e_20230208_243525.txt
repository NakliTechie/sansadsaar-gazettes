NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 8th February , 2023 

G.S.R. 87 ( E ) . In exercise of powers conferred by section 22 of the Metro Railways ( Operation and 
Maintenance ) Act , 2002 ( 60 of 2002 ) , the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to amend the 
Opening of Metro Railways for Public Carriage of Passengers Rules , 2013 , namely : - 


MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS 


1. Short title and commencement . – ( 1 ) These Rules may be called the Opening of Metro Railways for Public 
Carriage of Passengers ( Amendment ) Rules , 2023 . 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette . 


3 . 


2. In the Opening of Metro Railways for Public Carriage of Passengers Rules , 2013 , ( hereinafter referred to as the 
said rules ) , in rule 4 , in sub - rule ( 1 ) , after clause ( e ) , the following clause shall be inserted , namely : 


4 . 


" ( ea ) Certificate or Report that the system is fit for revenue operation from the independent safety assessor or 
consultant after review of the systems including rolling stock , track , traction and signalling ) in the case of 
Regional Rapid Transit System ( RRTS ) ; " . 

In the said rules , in rule 24 , for sub - rule ( 8 ) , the following sub - rule shall be substituted , namely : 

" ( 8 ) The type of rolling stock already running on a section of the metro railway after approval by the Central 
Government , may be introduced on a new section of the metro railway or any section of the metro railway in 
any other city or metropolitan area with the sanction of the Commissioner . " . 


In the said rules , in the Schedule , in FORM XV , in item 7 , the words " based on Terrestrial Trunked Radio 
specifications " shall be omitted .
