MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the llth July, 2023 
S.O. 3082(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, !956 (48 of 956) (hereafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building(widening/Two-Laning with paved shoulder), maintenance, management and operation of the National 
Highway No. NH-552 Extn. in the stretch of land from Km. 70+525 to 78+400 Km. (Mihona-Lahar-Daboh- 
Bhander-Bypass-Road) (Bhander-Bypass) Additional in the District DATIA, in the State of 


MADHYA PRADESH, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section () of 


section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, the Sub Divisional Magistrate 
Bhander, Datiya, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the Competent Authority shall give the objector 
an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections 
and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or 


disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (l) of section 3C of the said Act shall be 


final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 


by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
