S.R.O. 12.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the provision to the article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules to amend Ministry of Defence, Army Office Superintendent (other than 
Military Engineering Service or College of Military Engineering or Engineer Centers) Group ‘B’ Posts Recruitment 
Rules, 2013, namely; - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Ministry of Defence, Army Office Superintendent. 


(Other than Military Engineering Service or College of Military Engineering or Engineer Centre’s) Group ‘B’ Posts, 
Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2; In the Ministry of Defence, Army Office Superintendent (other than Military Engineering Service or College 
of Military Engineering or Engineer Centre’s) Group ‘B’ Posts Recruitment Rules, 2013, in the Schedule: - 


(1) in column (4), for the heading and entries relating thereto, the following column and entries shall be substituted, 
namely: - 


“Level in pay matrix 
(4) 
Level-6 (Rs. 35400-112400) 


in the pay matrix”; 


(2) in column (11), - 
(a) under the sub-heading “promotion”, - 


(i) #09 the words, letters and figure “Pay Scale of Rs. 5200-20200 with the grade pay of Rs. 2400 in 
PB-1”, for the words, figures, brackets and letters “Level-4(Rs. 25500-81100) in the pay matrix” shall 
be substituted; 


(ii) For the Note 2, the following note shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Note 2; For the purpose of computing minimum qualifying service for promotion, the service rendered on a 
regular basis by an officer prior to 17 January, 2016 the date from which the revised pay structure based on 
the 77 Central Pay Commission recommendations has been extended, shall be deemed to be service rendered 
in the corresponding level in the pay matrix extended based on the recommendation of the Commission.”; 


(b) under the sub-heading “Deputation”, - 


(i) in clause (a), in sub-clause(ii) for the words, letters and figure “Pay Scale of Rs. 5200-20200 with the 
grade pay of Rs. 2400 in Pay Band-1”, the words, figures, brackets and letters “Level-4 (Rs. 25500-81100) in the pay 
matrix “shall be substituted; 


(ii) for the Note, the following note shall be substituted, namely: - 


“Note; For the purpose of appointment on deputation basis the service rendered on a regular basis by an officer 
prior to 1“ January, 2016, the date from which the revised pay structure based on the 77 Central Pay 
Commission recommendations has been extended, shall be deemed to be service rendered in the corresponding 
pay in the pay matrix extended based on the recommendation of the Commission except where there has been 
merger of more than one pre-revised scale of the pay into one grade with a common grade pay or pay scale, 
and where this benefit will extend only for the post for which that grade pay or pay scale is the normal 
replacement grade without any upgradation.”
