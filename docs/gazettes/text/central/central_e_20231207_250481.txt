G.S.R. 883(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 156 of the 
Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force Act, 1992 (35 of 1992), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
to amend the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, Water Wing (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Technical Posts) Recruitment Rules, 
2019 namely :— 


1. Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, 
Water Wing (Group ‘A’ and ‘B’ Technical Posts), Recruitment Amendment Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
