NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th January, 2023 


S.O. 77(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, number $.0.4308(E) dated 14th September, 2022 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (11) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening /6-laning etc.,) maintenance, management and 
operations of National Highway No. 65, in the stretch for “6-laning of existing 2-lane 0) Nandigama Bypass from 
Km. 220.700 to Km. 226.600 (Design Chainage from Km. 220.700 to Km. 227.480) and (ii) Kanchikacherla Bypasses 
from Km. 238.200 to Km. 245.400 (Design Chainage from Km. 238.200 to Km. 245.760) on NH-65 under NHDP 
Phase-IV on EPC Mode” in the District of NTR in the State of Andhra Pradesh; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” and 
“Prajashakti” both dated 29-09-2022 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
