MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th April, 2026 


G.S.R. 35(E).— WHEREAS the draft of certain rules further to amend the Gas Cylinders Rules, 206, were 
published as required under section |8 of the Explosives Act, 884 (4 of 884) vide notification of the Government of 
India in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, vide number, 
G.S.R. 03 (E), dated the 3"! February, 2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub 
section (i), inviting objections or suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby before the expiry of a period 
of thirty days from the date on which the copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were made available to 
the public; 


AND WHEREAS, the copies of the said notification were made available to the public on the 
47 February, 2026; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections or suggestions were received from the public within the said period; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 7 of the Explosives Act, 884, 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Gas Cylinders Rules, 206, namely: 


l. Short title and commencement.—_(]) These rules may be called the Gas Cylinders (Amendment) Rules, 2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. Inthe Gas Cylinders Rules, 206 (hereinafter referred to as the principalrules), in rule 3, in sub-rule (5), after 
the words, “touching Indian port” the words, “or cylinders and valves imported by establishments of the 
Ministry of Defence, Department of Space and its Public Sector Undertakings for their own use” shall be 
inserted. 

3. का the principal rules, in rule 32, to sub-rule (), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:- 

“Provided that the permission shall not be required in respect of,- 


(a) empty cylinders without valves; 


(b) valves and LPG regulators, imported from the foreign manufacturing plants approved by the 
Chief Controller and having valid import licence in Form-D.”. 


4. Inthe principal rules, in rule 48, in sub-rule (3), in clause (b), after the words, “Liquified Petroleum Gas”, the 
words, “or other flammable gases” shall be inserted.
