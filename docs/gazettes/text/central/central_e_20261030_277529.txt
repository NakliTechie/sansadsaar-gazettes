S.O. 5913(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Census Act, 1948 (37 of 1948) read 
with rules 6A and 6D of the Census Rules, 1990, the Central Government hereby declares that in all States and Union 
territories of India except the State of Manipur (till further declaration), the States of Goa, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, 
Uttarakhand, the Union territory of Ladakh and the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of the State of Himachal Pradesh 
and the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir— 


(i) the conduct of census of the population shall be undertaken from the 25" January, 2027 to the 28" February, 2027 
with a revisional round from the 1t March, 2027 to the 5 March, 2027; 


(ii) for the conduct of the said census, there shall be an option for self-enumeration from the 10“ January, 2027 to the 
247 January, 2027, just before the start of the house to house population enumeration; 


(iii) the reference date for the said census shall be 00:00 hours of the 17 day of March, 2027.
