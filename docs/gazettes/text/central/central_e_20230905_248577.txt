MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(RAILWAY BOARD) 


NOTIFICATION 


New Delhi, the 4th September, 2023 


S.O. 3950(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 


989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, the lands, (the brief description of which has been specified in the Schedule annexed to this 
notification) is required for execution of the Special Railway Project namely, the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor 
in the District of Hapur in the State of Uttar Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 


notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection, to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose, 
under sub-section (l) of section 20D of the said Act; 


Every objection referred to in the preceding paragraph, shall be made to the competent authority namely, 


Additional District Magistrate (Land Acquisition), Joint Organisation, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh in writing and shall 
set out the grounds thereof and such competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard either in 
person or by a legal practitioner and may after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if 
any, as the competent authority thinks necessary by order, either allow or disallow the objections;
