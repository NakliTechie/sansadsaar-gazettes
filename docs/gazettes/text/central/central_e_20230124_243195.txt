NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 24th January , 2023 

S.O. 385 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 3 ) of section 1 of the Employees ' State 
Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , the Central Government hereby appoints the 1st day of February , 2023 , as the date 
on which the provisions of the said Act , namely , — 

( i ) 

sections 38 to 43 and sections 45A to 45H of Chapter IV ; 

( ii ) 

sections 46 to 73 of Chapter V ; and 

( iii ) sections 74 , 75 , sub - sections ( 2 ) to ( 4 ) of section 76 , 80 , 82 and 83 of Chapter VI , 

shall come into force in all the areas of Aizawl district , in addition to the already notified areas of the said district , in 
the State of Mizoram .
