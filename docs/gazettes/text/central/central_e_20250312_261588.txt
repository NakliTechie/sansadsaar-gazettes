New Delhi, the 12th March, 2025 


S.O. 1140(E).—In reference to the Gazette Notification No. 8.0. 4716(A), dated 29th October 2024, 
published in the Official Gazette, regarding land acquisition for "Construction of ROB 34-C at Railway Kilometre 
35/2-3 at Saraichandi Yard" as Special Railway project in Prayagraj district of Uttar Pradesh state under the Railways 
Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), as amended by the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008, it is hereby notified that: 


Due to a typographical error, the survey number mentioned at S.No. 34 in the said notification was 
incorrectly recorded as "166". The correct survey number should be read as "466". 


All concerned authorities, landowners, and stakeholders are requested to take note of this correction. The 
other contents of the original notification remain unchanged.
