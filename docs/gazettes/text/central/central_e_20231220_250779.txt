Mumbai, the 20th December, 2023 


9.0. 5398(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board) (Published in the Gazette of India, under notification No. 60l(E) of dated 2!.2.2022 Extra Ordinary part II 
Section-3 Sub-section (II)) (here in after referred to as the said notification) under subsection(I) of section 20A of the 
Railway Act 989 (24 of 989) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) the Central Government declared its intention 
to be acquired the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for execution, maintenance, 
management and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely, Nadiad-Petlad Gauge Conversion Project in 
District Anand in the State of Gujarat; 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily local newspaper “Gujarat 
Samachar” dated 2*' December, 2022. “The Indian Express” dated 2* December, 2022 under subsection (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; and whereas, no objection has been received by the competent authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (I) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report of the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto 
shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
