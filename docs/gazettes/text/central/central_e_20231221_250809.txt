MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 21st December, 2023 


No. A-35013/01/2018-Ad.III/838.—The President is pleased to appoint Shri Lalit Jain, IAS 
(HP:2011) an officer belonging to Himachal Pradesh Cadre of Indian Administrative Services as Director 
of Census Operations /Director of Citizen Registration, Haryana with effect from 07.12.2023 (FN) for a 
period of upto 31.12.2025 or until further orders, whichever is earlier, on central deputation under the 
Central Staffing Scheme. 


2 The Headquarters of Shri Lalit Jain, IAS (HP:2011) will be Chandigarh (Haryana).
