S.O. 1691(E).— Whereas, M/s Shyamaraju & Company (India) Private Limited, had proposed under Section

section 4 of the said Act read with rule 8 of the Special Economic Zones Rules 2006, had notified/de-notified the 
following areas at above Special Economic Zone as per the details given below in the table: -

CI 171 SPI 2023 dated 17" December, 2025. Further, after de-notification, the land parcels would be utilized for
