NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 24th March, 2025 
F. No. CO.DOR.RG.No.S8257/2.22.904/2024-25.— In exercise of the powers conferred under 
Section 36 A (2) read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 949, Reserve Bank of India hereby 
notifies that “The Cuddalore and Villupuram District Central Co-operative Bank’s Employees Cooperative 
Bank Ltd., Cuddalore — 60700], Tamil Nadu’ has ceased to be a co-operative bank within the meaning of the 
said Act and all the provisions of the said Act applicable to “The Cuddalore and Villupuram District Central 
Co-operative Bank’s Employees Cooperative Bank Ltd., Cuddalore — 60700l, Tamil Nadu’ shall cease to 


apply to it, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before this notice.
