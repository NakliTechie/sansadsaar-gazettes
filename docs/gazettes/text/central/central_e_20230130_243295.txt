New Delhi , the 25th January , 2023 

S.O. 452 ( E ) .– In exercise of powers conferred by Clause ( e ) of Section 4 of the All India Institute of 
Medical Sciences Act , 1956 ( 25 of 1956 ) as amended by All India Institute of Medical Sciences ( Amendment ) Act , 
2012 ( 37 of 2012 ) , the Central Government hereby nominates Sh . Desh Deepak Verma , retired IAS , to be member of 
Institute Body of All India Institute of Medical Sciences , Gorakhpur in place of Dr. Ambrish Mithal , 
Endocrinologist , Medanta and for that purpose makes the following amendment in the notification of Government of 
India in Ministry of Health & Family Welfare , File Number Z - 28016 / 170 / 2018 - PMSSY - IV dated the 30th January , 
2020 published in the Gazette of India Part II , section 3 , Sub - Section ( ii ) vide SO 615 ( E ) dated the 7th February , 
2020 : 


In the said notification , for serial number 10 and the entry relating thereto , the following serial number and 
entry should be substituted , namely : 

“ 10. Sh . Desh Deepak Verma , retired IAS ” 

The terms of office of the member shall be governed by the provisions contained in Section 6 of the All India 
Institute of Medical Sciences Act , 1956 .
