MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[Eastern Railway (Construction Department)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Kolkata, the 24th April, 2024. 


S.O. 878(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 the Central Government hereby notifies “Pakur/Nagarnabi to Godda new BG line” as a 
Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in Godda, Dumka and Pakur Districts in the State of Jharkhand, for 
providing national infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the 
Official Gazette.
