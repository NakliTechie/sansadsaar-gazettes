G.S.R. 241(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944 (1 of

of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), No. 18/2022-Central Excise, dated 
the 19" July, 2022, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (1), vide number 
G.S.R. 584 (8), dated the 19" July, 2022, namely:-
