MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2025 


S.O. 4326(E).— — Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 78(E) Dated:/02/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four-laning/six-laning etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH4 in the stretch of land from Km. 3.800 to Km. 37.000 (Special 
District Revenue Officer(LA), NH, Tiruvallur) in the village namely Poonamallee in the district of TIRUVALLUR in 
the state of TAMIL NADU 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Hindu” and “Dinamani” both 
dated 04.03.2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no objections have been received from any person by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


[भाग U—aete 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
