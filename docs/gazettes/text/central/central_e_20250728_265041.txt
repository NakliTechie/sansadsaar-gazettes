MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Financial Services) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th July, 2025 


5.0. 3470(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 173 of the General Insurance Business 
(Nationalisation) Act, 1972 (57 of 1972), the Central Government hereby frames the following scheme further to amend 
the General Insurance (Employees’) Pension Scheme, 1995, namely: - 


1. (1) This scheme may be called the General Insurance (Employees’) Pension (Amendment) Scheme, 2025. 

(2) This scheme shall be deemed to have come into force on the 21st day of December, 2023. 
2: In the General Insurance (Employees’) Pension Scheme, 1995 (hereinafter referred to as the said scheme), in 
paragraph 2, - 


(i) in sub-paragraph (9), in clause (i) after the words “Chairman-cum-Managing Director” at both the places 
where they occur, the words “and Executive Director” shall be inserted; 


(ii) in sub-paragraph (k), in the proviso, after the words “Chairman-cum-Managing Director” wherever they 
occur, the words “or Executive Director’ shall be inserted. 


3. In the said scheme, in Appendix-I, against serial number (i) relating to the category of employees, after the 
words “Chairman-cum-Managing Director” at both the places where they occur, the words “and Executive 
Director” shall be inserted.
