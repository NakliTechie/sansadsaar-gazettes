THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 
(Set up by an Act of Parliament) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 28th April, 2023 
(CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) 


No. PPR/P/259/2017/DD/243/TAMC/INF/2017/DC/1100/2019.—In terms of the provisions of Section 
21B(3) of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 read with Rule 18(17) of the Chartered Accountants (Procedure of 
Investigations of Professional and Other Misconduct and Conduct of Cases) Rules, 2007, the Disciplinary Committee 
has held CA. Goutam Ghanty (Membership No. 060511), Agrani Lane, Mahiskapur, Plot Benachity, 
DURGAPUR (West Bengal)- 713213, guilty of Professional Misconduct falling within the meaning of Clause (1) of 
Part II of Second Schedule to the aforesaid Act and consequently after affording an opportunity of being heard in 
terms of Rule 19(1) of the aforesaid Rules, imposed a fine of Rs. 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs) only to be paid 
within a period of 01 month and in case of default in payment of fine within stipulated time, his name be removed for 
a period of 1(One) month. Since the Respondent has failed to pay the imposed fine within stipulated time, in 
pursuance of the aforesaid Order of the Disciplinary Committee and in exercise of the powers conferred by sub- 
section (2) of Section 20 of the aforesaid Act, it is hereby notified under Regulation 18 of the Chartered Accountants 
Regulations 1988, that the name of said CA. Goutam Ghanty (Membership No. 060511), shall stand removed for 
a period of 01 (one) month with effect from 28" April, 2023. 


CA. (Dr.) JAI KUMAR BATRA, Secy.
