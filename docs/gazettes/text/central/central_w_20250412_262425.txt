राष्ट्रपति सचिवालय 
नई दिल्‍ली, दिनांक 26 जनवरी 2025 
सं. 2 -प्रेज/2025.--राष्ट्रपति महोदया, निम्नलिखित कार्मिकों को असाधारण वीरतापूर्ण कार्यों के लिए "कीर्ति चक्र" प्रदान करने का 
अनुमोदन करती हैं :-- 
1. आईसी-83449 वाई मेजर मनजीत, दि पंजाब रेजिमेंट, 22 राष्ट्रीय राईफल्स 
(पुरस्कार की प्रभावी तारीख 25 अप्रैल 2024) 


25 अप्रैल 2024 को मेजर मनजीत ने सोपोर जिले के एक गाँव में दो विदेशी आतंकवादियों की गतिविधियों का पता लगाने के लिए 
एक विश्वसनीय स्रोत से प्राप्त सटीक मानवीय खुफिया जानकारी के आधार पर सतर्कता से उनका पीछा किया। 


संभावित लक्ष्य क्षेत्र का पता लगाने के बाद, अधिकारी ने प्रारम्भिक घेरा स्थापित किया, जिसके दौरान आतंकवादियों ने प्रतिरोध किया 
और 25 अप्रैल 2024 की रात भर में घेरा तोड़ने के कई प्रयास किए। 26 अप्रैल 2024 को प्रातः 0530 बजे, एक आतंकवादी प्राकृतिक आवरण 
का लाभ उठाते हुए पास के बगीचे में घुस गया। अधिकारी ने बगीचे की ओर जाने वाली दीवार का सहारा लेते हुए उस आतंकवादी को, जो बगीचे 
के प्रवेश द्वार पर स्थित एक गौशाला की ओर बढ़ने का प्रयास कर रहा था, उसकी जगह पर ही ढेर कर दिया। 


स्थिति की गंभीरता को भांपते हुए अधिकारी ने गौशाला में फंसे एक नागरिक और दो बच्चों को सुरक्षित निकाला। निकासी के बाद, उन 
पर भारी गोलीबारी के बावजूद, मेजर मनजीत आतंकवादी की ओर रेंगते हुए आगे बढ़ते गए। जैसे ही आतंकवादी ने स्काउट पर ग्रेनेड फेंकने का 
प्रयास किया, अधिकारी ने उसे कड़ी मुठभेड़ में मार गिराया। 


असाधारण नेतृत्व क्षमता का प्रदर्शन और अद्वितीय वीरता दिखाने के लिए मेजर मनजीत को “कीर्ति चक्र” से सम्मानित किया जाता है। 
2. 15240290 एफ नायक दिलवर खान, दि तोपखाना रेजिमेंट, 28 राष्ट्रीय राईफल्स (मरणोपरांत) 
(पुरस्कार की प्रभावी तारीख 23 जुलाई 2024) 


23 जुलाई 2024 को नायक दिलवर खान कुपवाड़ा जिले की लोलाब घाटी के घने जंगलों में घात लगाकर बैठी पार्टी का हिस्सा थे। 
रात 2350 बजे उनकी पार्टी ने दो आतंकवादियों की गतिविधि देखी, जिनमें से एक बहुत ही निकट था। उनकी पार्टी ने आतंकवादी पर फायर 
किया और आतंकवादी ने बदले में उन पर आक्रमण किया। 


अपनी टीम पर मंडरा रहे गंभीर खतरे को भांपते हुए, नायक दिलवर खान ने अपनी व्यक्तिगत सुरक्षा की पूरी तरह से परवाह न करते 
हुए, भारी गोलीबारी के बीच आतंकवादी पर झपट्टा मारा और उससे हाथापाई करने लगे। इस दौरान दूसरा आतंकवादी दूर से अंधाधुंध फायरिंग 
करता रहा | इस वीरतापूर्ण कार्यवाही के दौरान नायक दिलवर खान गंभीर रूप से घायल हो गए। बावजूद इसके, अपने घावो के कारण वीरगति 
प्राप्त करने से पहले, उन्होंने आतंकवादी को पकड़ कर रखा और नजदीक से फायर कर उसे मार गिराया । 


अडिग साहस, व्यक्तिगत सुरक्षा की परवाह न करने और अद्वितीय वीरता का प्रदर्शन करने के लिए, नायक दिलवर खान को “कीर्ति 
चक्र (मरणोपरांत)” से सम्मानित किया जाता है।

डिवाइसेज (आई.ई.डी) को निष्क्रिय करने में महत्वपूर्ण योगदान दिया। 02 जून 2024 को मेजर आशीष दहिया ने पुलवामा जिले के एक गाँव में 
एक सुनियोजित अभियान का नेतृत्व किया। 


प्रारंभिक तलाशी के दौरान, आतंकवादियों ने अंधाधुंध फायरिंग और ग्रेनेड फेंककर भागने का प्रयास किया, जिस पर मेजर आशीष 
दहिया ने सटीकता के साथ जवाबी कार्यवाही करते हुए भागते हुए आतंकवादी को गंभीर रूप से घायल कर दिया। भीषण गोलीबीरी के बीच, 
एक आतंकवादी ने ग्रेनेड फेंका, जिससे उनके साथी को स्प्लिंटर लगकर चोटें आईं। मेजर आशीष दहिया ने अपनी सुरक्षा की पूरी तरह से

No. 2-Pres/2025.— The President is pleased to approve the award of “Kirti Chakra” to the under mentioned personnel 
for the acts of conspicuous gallantry:— 


1. IC-83449Y MAJOR MANJIT, THE PUNJAB REGIMENT, 22 RASHTRIYA RIFLES 
(Effective date of Award: 25 April 2024) 


On 25 April 2024, Major Manjit assiduously trailed movement of two foreign terrorists in a village in Sopore district 
through responsive human intelligence as received from a credible source. 


On localizing the probable target complex, the officer established the initial cordon during which the terrorists 
retaliated and made several attempts to break cordon throughout the intervening night of 25 April 2024. On 26 April 2024 at 
0530 Hour, one of the terrorists entered adjoining orchard favoring natural cover. The officer taking the cover of a boundary 
wall leading to orchard, pinned down the terrorist who was trying to shift to a cowshed at the entry of the orchard. 


On sensing the severity of the situation, the officer evacuated a trapped civilian and two children from the cowshed. 
Post evacuation, in spite of heavy volume of fire directed onto him, Major Manjit started crawling towards the terrorist. On 
noticing the movement of terrorist who was trying to lob a grenade at the scout, the officer engaged him in a fierce firefight 
and eliminated him. 


For displaying conspicuous gallantry and exhibiting outstanding leadership Major Manjit is awarded “KIRTI 
CHAKRA”. 


2. 15240290F NAIK DILWAR KHAN, THE REGIMENT OF ARTILLERY, 28 RASHTRIYA RIFLES 
(POSTHUMOUS) 


(Effective date of Award: 23 July 2024) 


Naik Dilwar Khan was part of an ambush in dense jungles of Lolab Valley, Kupwara district on 23 July 2024. At 2350 
hours, his party observed two terrorists, one at very close distance. His ambush party fired at the terrorist, who in-turn charged 
towards them. 


Sensing grave threat from the closing-in terrorist to his team, showing complete disregard to personal safety, 
Naik Dilwar Khan, though under heavy volume of fire, lunged and grasped the terrorist, engaging him in hand-to-hand combat, 
while the other terrorist continued to fire indiscriminately from distance. During this gallant action, Naik Dilwar Khan was 
grievously injured. Undeterred by his injuries, he did not let go of the terrorist and killed him by firing at point blank range 
before succumbing to his wounds. 


For displaying indomitable courage, utter disregard to personal safety and gallantry of highest order, Naik Dilwar 
Khan is awarded “KIRTI CHAKRA (POSTHUMOUS)”.

3. IC-83753H MAJOR SATENDER DHANKAR, THE ARMOURED CORPS, 4 RASHTRIYA RIFLES

in Doda District, resulting in elimination of three foreign terrorists. 


On receipt of input regarding presence of three terrorists in a Dhok (mud house) in impenetrable jungles in Doda 
district. On receiving the input, officer displayed immaculate tactical acumen and quickly established cordon of the target area. 


Terrorists after sensing the cordon, opened indiscriminate fire on the cordon party. The Officer immediately brought 
accurate fire and eliminated the first fleeing terrorist. Appreciating grave danger to his buddy, he pushed his buddy out of the 
line of fire, exposing himself to heavy fire, during which, one bullet even grazed past his thigh. 


Exhibiting indomitable spirit and courage with utter disregard to his safety, officer crawled ahead and brought effective 
fire on terrorists thus disabling terrorists from bringing fire on own party. 


Maintaining tactical composure, the officer crawled to Rocket Launcher operator under heavy fire and re-sited him 
which helped in eliminating the second terrorist and grievously injuring the third terrorist. 


For displaying exemplary leadership and undaunted courage in face of enemy fire, Major Satender Dhankar is awarded 
“SHAURYA CHAKRA”.

2. FUNCTIONS OF THE COMMITTEE 


The Samiti will advise on the matters relating to progressive use of Hindi in official work of the Ministry of Personnel, 
Public Grievances and Pensions in accordance with the policies and guidelines as laid down by the Kendriya Hindi Samiti and 
Department of Official Language Ministry of Home Affairs in this regard from time to time. 


3. TENURE OF THE COMMITTEE 
The tenure of the Samiti will ordinarily be three years from the date of its reconstitution, provided that :— 


(a) the Members of the Parliament who are members of the Samiti shall cease to be the members of the Samiti as soon 
as they cease to be the Members of the Parliament ; 


(b) the Members of the Parliament who are the members of the Samiti by the virtue of their being members of the 
Committee of Parliament on Official Language shall cease to be the members of the Samiti as soon as they cease 
to be the Members of the Committee of the Parliament on Official Language ; 


(c) Ex-officio members of the Samiti shall continue to be the members of the Samiti as long as they hold the office by 
virtue of which they are the members of the Samiti; and 


(d) if any vacancy arises in the Samiti owing to death of a member or owing to tendering of resignation by a member 
or other reasons, the member so appointed against that vacancy shall hold office for the residual term of the Samiti. 


4. HEADQUARTERS OF THE COMMITTEE 
The Headquarters of the Samiti will be at New Delhi. 
5. TRAVELLING AND OTHER ALLOWANCES 


I. The Members nominated in the Samiti, will be paid Travelling Allowance/Daily Allowance as per the provisions 
in the ‘Members of Parliament (Salary, Allowances and Pension) Act,1954’ and the rates and rules as 
amended/fixed from time to time vide Department of Official Language OM No. 1 1/20034/34/2005-O.L.(Policy), 
dated 03.02.2006 for participating in the meetings of the Samiti. 


Il. For attending the meetings of the Samiti, Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance will be admissible to the 
Non-Official members as per the guidelines contained in the Department of Official Language Office 
Memorandum No. 1 1/20034/4/86-O.L.(A-2) dated 22 January, 1987 and the rates and rules revised by Govt of 
India from time to time.
