MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS

NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 2]st June, 2024 


S.O. 2425(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number, S.O 806 (E) dated the 2277 February 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), dated the 2974 February 2022 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification ) and 
issued under sub-section (.) of section 20A of the Railway Act, 989 (24 of 989) and Railway (Amendment) Act, 
2008 (I of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management 


and operation of Special Railway Project, namely Borvihir (Dhule) - Nardana New Line in the district of Dhule in 
the State of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in for Dhule SDO daily Marathi 
newspapers namely “Dainik Punya Nagari” & “Dainik Sakaal”, Dated 9-l0-2023 under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, objections were received and sorted the same and have been considered by the competent 
Authority accordingly orders have been passed; 


[भाग ए--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 205 of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central government free from all encumbrances.
