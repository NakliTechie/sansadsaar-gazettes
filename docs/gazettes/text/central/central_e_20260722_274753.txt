MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 22nd July, 2026 


S.O. 4014(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, 2412(E) Dated:12/05/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part 
II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 
(48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH 844 in the stretch of land from Km. 10.4 to 
Km. 53.80 (Hosur-Royakotta-Dharmapuri Section) in the district of KRISHNAGIRI in the state of TAMIL 
NADU 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in The Hindu (English) and 
Dhina Thanthi (Tamil) dated:27.05.2026 under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been received and the same have been cnsidered and disallowed by the 
Competent Authority Under 3C; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 
has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 
Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
