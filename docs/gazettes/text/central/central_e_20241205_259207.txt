NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th November, 2024 


F. No. PLI-04//2024-PLI-DOP.—In exercise of powers conferred vide Rule 3 of Post Office Life 
Insurance Rules (20I) and on the basis of Actuarial Valuation of the assets and liabilities of Post Office Life 
Insurance Fund (POLIF) as on 3i.03.2024, the Director General Postal Services is pleased to declare a simple 
Reversionary Bonus on the Postal Life Insurance Policies on their becoming claims, due to death or maturity at the 
following rates:- 


Type of Insurance Policy Rate of Bonus 
i. Whole Life Assurance (WLA) Rs. 76/- per thousand of sum assured 
ii. Endowment Assurance (EA) Rs. 52/- per thousand of sum assured 
(including Joint Life & Children Policies) 

iii. Anticipated Endowment Assurance (AEA) Rs. 48/- per thousand of sum assured 

iv. Convertible Whole Life Assurance (CWLA) Whole Life bonus rate would be applicable, but on 
conversion, Endowment Assurance bonus rate will be 
applicable 

v. Terminal Bonus Rs. 20/- per sum assured of Rs. 0,000/- subject to 
maximum of Rs. !000 for Whole Life Assurance and 
Endowment Assurance policies with term of 20 years 
or more. 

2. The rates of Bonus mentioned above for the Financial Year 2025-26 will be applicable from 0.04.2025. 

3. Interim Bonus at the rates mentioned above will also be payable for all claims arising due to maturity or 

death until future valuation is completed. 
4. The amount of Bonus involving a fraction of 50 paisa or more shall be rounded off to the next higher Rupee 


and fraction below 50 paisa shall be ignored.
