No. 10-4/2026-U.3(A)—Whereas, Malla Reddy (MR), Deemed to be University, Telangana submitted an online 
application on the UGC Portal for establishment of an off-campus centre at Krishnapuram Village, Renigunta Mandal, Tirupathi 
as per the UGC (Institutions deemed to be Universities) Regulations, 2023. 


2. And whereas, UGC, vide its letter No. 41-5/2026 (CPP-I/DU) dated 12.05.2026, informed that the application was 
examined through its Standing Committee. The Committee, after presentation of the Institution and based on the documents 
submitted, recommended for issuance of Letter of Intent (Lol) to Malla Reddy (MR), Telangana for fulfilment of certain 
conditions. 

3. And whereas, the Ministry, on advice of UGC, issued letter of Intent (Lol) dated 01.06.2026 to Malla Reddy (MR), 
Deemed to be University, Telangana for fulfilment of certain conditions within a period of three years before establishment of 
an off-campus centre at Krishnapuram village, Renigunta Mandal, Tirupathi. 

4. And further whereas, the Registrar, Malla Reddy (MR), Telangana, vide letter dated 02.06.2026, submitted the 
compliance report in respect of the conditions stipulated in the Lol. The same was forwarded to UGC for verification and 
further advice. Further, UGC, vide letter dated 03.07.2026, informed that the compliance report was accepted by the Standing 
Committee. 

5. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, the Ministry of 
Education, on the advice of UGC, hereby accords approval to Malla Reddy (MR), an Institution deemed to be University, 
Telangana to start an off-campus Centre at No. 6, Krishnapuram village, Renigunta Mandal, Tirupathi. 

6. All other conditions mentioned in the earlier Notification(s) of this Ministry as well as the Rules / Regulations of 
UGC and other Statutory Councils, issued from time to time, shall continue to be adhered by Malla Reddy (MR), Deemed to 
be University, Telangana.
