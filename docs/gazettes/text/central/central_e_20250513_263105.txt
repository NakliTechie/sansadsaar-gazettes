MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(SOUTH CENTRAL RAILWAY) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 2nd May, 2025 


S.O. 23(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Proposed Construction of Road under Bridge (RUB) in lieu of Level 
Crossing No. 63A at KM. 379/22-24 between Warangal (WL) and Chintalpalli (CLE) stations" as a Special Railway 
Project for acquisition of land in the State of Telangana in the District of Warangal for providing National 
Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
