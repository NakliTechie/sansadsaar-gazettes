S.O. 5008(E).—In exercise of Power confirmed by clause (a) of section 3 of National Highways Act 1956 
(48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby make the following amendments in the Notification of the Government 
of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 1932 (E) dated the 26.04.2022, Published in the 
Gazette of India. 


In the Preamble to the said notification— 


For the words and figures “Sub Divisional officer, Dharashiv”, the words, figures and “Deputy Collector Land 
Acquisition, (Medium Project No.2) Dharashiv”, shall be substituted. 


Footnote: 
1. The Principal Notification was published vide 8.0. 1932 (E) dated the 26.04.2022, in the Gazette of India. 
2. The amendment Notification was published vide 8.0. 1236 (E) dated the 15.03.2023, in the Gazette of India.
