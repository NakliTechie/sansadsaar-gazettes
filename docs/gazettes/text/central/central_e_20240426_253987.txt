NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 26th April, 2024 


S.O. 843(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 402] (E) Dated 2/09/2023, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, 
Sub-Section (ii) Issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening / two lane with pave shoulder / four laning etc.) 
Maintenance, management and operation of National Highway No. 965 C on the stretch of land from Km. 48+375 to 
Km. 79+533 Pandharpur to Sangola Road (Section - II)(Dy. Collector Land Acquisition No. !! Krushna Khore, 
Solapur ) in the district of SOLAPUR in the State of MAHARASHTRA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in "The Indian Express (English)" date 
30/0/2023 and "Sakal (Marathi)" date 26/0/2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately: 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority in exercise of the powers conferred 
by the sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in 
the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
