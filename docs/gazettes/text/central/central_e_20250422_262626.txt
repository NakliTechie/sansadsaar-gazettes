NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 22nd April, 2025 
S.O. 1834(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge In lieu of Level Crossing No. 217 at Km. 
558/6-7 in between Marichethal - Raichur Stations" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of 
Karnataka in the District of Raichur for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the 


date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.
