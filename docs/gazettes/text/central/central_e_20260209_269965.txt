G.S.R. I5(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Ex-servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services 
and Posts) Rules,l979, namely: - 


. Short title and commencement: - 


(.) These rules may be called the Ex-servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services and Posts) Amendment Rules, 
2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Ex-servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services and Posts) Rules, 979, in rule 2, in clause (c), in sub- 
clause (i), for the portion beginning with the words "who has served" and ending with the words "Indian Union, and", the 
following shall be substituted, namely:- 


"who has served in any rank whether as a combatant or non-combatant in the Regular Army or Navy or Air Force 
of the Indian Union or Military Nursing Service of the Indian Union, and".
