RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 
NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 12th December, 2025 


F. No. CO.DOR.SOG.No.S7046/09.08.024/2025-2026.— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (0) 
of sub-section (2) of section 58 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Central Board of the Reserve 
Bank of India, with the previous sanction of the Central Government hereby further to amend the Reserve Bank of India 
Scheduled Banks’ Regulation, 1951, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement. — (1) This Regulation may be called the Reserve Bank of India Scheduled Banks' 
(Amendment) Regulations 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the 157 day of December, 2025. 


2. In the Reserve Bank of India Scheduled Banks' Regulations, 1951, — 
(a) in sub-regulation (i) of regulation (6), the words “and the monthly return furnished under the third proviso to 
that sub-section shall be in such form as may be required by the Bank” shall be omitted. 


(b) in the ANNEXURE, — 
(A) in the Form 'A', — 
(1) in the second line of the Form, for the words “Statement of position at the close of business on 
Friday @ the”, the words “Statement of position at the close of business on the last day of the 
fortnight @ the” shall be substituted.”; 


(ii) in item II, in sub-item (b), for the brackets and words “(other than from Reserve Bank of India, 
Industrial Development Bank of India, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development 
and Export-Import Bank of India)” the brackets and words “(other than from the Reserve Bank 
of India, the Exim Bank, the National Housing Bank, the National Bank, the Small Industries 
Bank, the National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development or the other 
development financial institution)” shall be substituted.”; 


(iii) after item C, in the footnote, for the footnote@, the following footnote shall be substituted, 
namely: — 
“@ Where last day of any fortnight is a public holiday under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 
1881 (26 of 1881) for one or more offices of a scheduled bank, the return shall give the preceding

“(vil) National Housing Bank 
(ix) Small Industries Bank 
(x) National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development 
(xi) other development financial institution.”.
