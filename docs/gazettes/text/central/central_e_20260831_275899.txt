5.0. 4807(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 803(E) Dated:08/04/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for construction of Toll Plaza Maintenance, management and operation 
of NHI60A in the stretch of land from Km. 0.000 to Km. I56.000 in the district of PALGHAR in the state of 
MAHARASHTRA

27/05/2026 , “Dainik Navshakti ” dated 28/05/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;

vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
