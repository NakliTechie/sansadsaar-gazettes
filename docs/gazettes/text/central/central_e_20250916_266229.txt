MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(S.E.C Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the l5th Septermber, 2025 
Notice under Section 204 of the Railways Act, 989 


S.O. 498(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways 
Act, 989 (24 of 89) (herein after referred as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied 
that for the public purpose, the land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed hereto, is 
required for the execution of Special Railway Project of Railway line from Chirimiri to Nagpur road halt in 
Chhattisgarh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; 


Any persons interested in the said land may Within a period of 30 days from the date of publication 
of this notification in the official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid 
purpose under sub-section (l) of Section 20D of the said Act. 


Every objection shall be made to the competent Authority, namely Additional Collector (Land 
Acquisition Officer), District Manendragarh — Chirmiri - Bharatpur (Chhattisgarh) in writing and shall set 
out grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, 
either in person or through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making 
such further enquiry, if any as the competent Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow 
the objections; 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said act 
shall be final. The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and 
can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
