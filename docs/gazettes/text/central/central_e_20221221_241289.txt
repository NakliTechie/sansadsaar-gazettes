का.आ. 5957 ( अ ) . – केंद्रीय सरकार ने , राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उपधारा ( 1 ) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का . आ . 5354 ( अ ) तारीख 23 दिसम्बर , 2021 , जो भारत के 
राजपत्र , असाधारण , भाग II , खण्ड 3 , उपखण्ड ( ii ) में प्रकाशित की गई थी , द्वारा गुजरात राज्य के वडोदरा जिले में 
प्रस्तावित वडोदरा - मुंबई एक्सप्रेस वे के डिजाइन चैनेज 324 + 500 कि.मी. से 380 + 000 कि.मी. ( वडोदरा - मुंबई ) तक के 
भुखण्ड के निर्माण ( छह / आठ लेन का बनाने , आदि ) , अनुरक्षण , प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध 
अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 20th December , 2022 

S.O. 5957 ( E ) .– Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways , S.O. 5354 ( E ) dated 23.12.2021 , published in Gazette of India , Extraordinary , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section 
( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to 
as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to 
the said notification for the public purpose of building ( Six / Eight laning , etc. ) , maintenance , management and 


4 


operation of Proposed Vadodara - Mumbai Expressway , on the stretch of land from design chainage Km . 324 + 500 to 
Km . 380 + 000 ( Vadodara - Mumbai ) in the district Vadodara in the state of Gujarat ; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " The Indian Express ” and “ Sandesh ” 
both dated 7th January 2022 , under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 

And whereas No objections have been received by the Competent Authority ; 

And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
