New Delhi , the 13th January , 2023 

S.O. 260 ( E ) .— Consequent upon his conviction by the Court of Sessions , Kavaratti , Lakshadweep in 
Sessions case no . 01/2017 , Shri Mohammed Faizal P.P. , Member of Lok Sabha representing the 
Lakshadweep Parliamentary Constituency of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep stands disqualified from 
the membership of Lok Sabha from the date of his conviction i.e. 11 January , 2023 in terms of the 
provisions of Article 102 ( 1 ) ( e ) of the Constitution of India read with Section 8 of the Representation of the 
People Act , 1951 .
