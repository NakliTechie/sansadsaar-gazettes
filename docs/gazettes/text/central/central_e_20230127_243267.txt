NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th January, 2023 


G.S.R. 60(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l)read with clauses (j) and (q) of sub- 
section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (l of 200), the Central Government hereby makes the 
following rules further to amend the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 20, namely:- 


l. (.) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment (Amendment) 
Rules, 2023. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) (Amendment) Rules, 2022, in rule l, in sub-rule (2), for the 
figures, letters and words “7 day of February, 2023”, the figures, letters and words “]* Day of April, 2023” shall be 
substituted.
