MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 

Mumbai, the 21st October, 2024 


9.0. 4627(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), numbers’ 998(A) dated 08th March 2022 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
section 3, Sub-section (ii) dated the 21 May 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under 
sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railway (Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said 
Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land specified in the schedule annexed to the said 
notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of Special Railway Project, namely 
“Wardha (Sewagram) - Ballarshah 3rd line” in the district of Chandrapur in the state of Maharashtra. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Aadi 
Manav” And “Maha Vidarbha ”Dated 04°J uly 2024 under sub section (4)of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objections were received and the same have been considered by the competent Authority 
accordingly order shave been passed; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section(1) of section 20 E of the said act, the Central Government here by declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose; 


And Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government here by 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette; the land specified in the schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government Free from all encumbrances.
