MINISTRY OF FINANCE 
(Department of Revenue) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 27th October, 2025 
No. 30/2025-Customs (ADD) 


G.S.R. 793(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (5) of section 9A of the Customs 
Tariff Act, 1975 (51 of 1975) read with rules 18, 20, 23, 29 and 31 of the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment 
and Collection of Anti-dumping Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 1995, the Central 
Government, hereby makes the following amendment in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance 
(Department of Revenue), No. 66/2021-Customs (ADD), dated the 11" November, 2021, published in the Gazette of 
India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number 8.0. 790(E), dated the 117 November, 2021, 
namely: - 


In the said notification, in the Table, against S. No. 1, in column (7), for the entry, the entry ‘1,296’ shall be 
substituted. 


2. This notification shall come into force with immediate effect. 


[F. No. CBIC-190354/234/2021-TRU] 
DHEERAJ SHARMA, Under Secy. 


Note: The principal notification No. 66/2021-Customs (ADD), dated the 117 November, 2021, was published in the

11" November, 2021.
