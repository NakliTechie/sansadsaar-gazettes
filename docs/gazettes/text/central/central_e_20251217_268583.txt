की आवश्यकता को समाप्त करता है, प्रक्रियाओं को सरल बनाता है और पारदर्शिता एवं दक्षता को बढ़ावा देता है: -

ई-केवाईसी अधिप्रमाणन सुविधाओं का उपयोग करते हुए एनआरए पोर्टल पर रजिस्ट्रीकरण सहित परीक्षा के विभिन्न चरणों 
के दौरान, आवेदकों, लोक परीक्षा पदाधिकारियों और लोक परीक्षाओं में शामिल अन्य हितधारकों की पहचान स्थापित करने

MINISTRY OF PERSONNEL, PUBLIC GRIEVANCES AND PENSIONS 
(Department of Personnel and Training) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 7th December, 2025 


S.O. 5835(E).— WHEREAS the use of Aadhaar number to establish identity enables individuals to receive 
subsidies, benefits and services in a convenient and seamless manner, obviates the need for multiplicity of documents to 
establish identity, simplifies processes and promotes transparency and efficiency; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government has issued a notification in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O 273 (8), dated the 5" May, 2025 allowing the National 
Recruitment Agency to perform Aadhaar authentication, on voluntary basis, for establishing the identity of applicants, 
public examination functionaries and other stakeholders involved in public examinations, during various stages of 
examination, including registration on the portal of NRA, using Yes/No or/and e-K YC authentication facilities; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government has considered it necessary and expedient in the public interest to 
supersede the said notification; 


AND WHEREAS the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, after 
consultation with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAID), has consented to allow the National Recruitment 
Agency to perform authentication and be permitted the use of Aadhaar number during authentication for establishing 
identity of Aadhaar number holder and notify the same under rule 5 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance 
(Social Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules) read with sub-clause (ii) 
of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits 
and Services) Act, 206 (hereinafter referred to as the Act); 


AND WHEREAS the Aadhaar authentication shall be performed for usage of digital platforms to ensure good 
governance and enablement of innovation and the spread of knowledge as referred to under sub-rule (l) of rule 3 of 
the said rules and the performance of Aadhaar authentication for the said purpose shall be on voluntary basis and that 
the said National Recruitment Agency shall perform the Aadhaar authentication for usage of digital platforms to ensure 
good governance and enablement of innovation and the spread of knowledge; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of 
the Act read with rule 5 of the said rules and in supersession of the notification issued vide S.O 2] 73 (E), dated the 
5 May, 2025, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the Central Government 
hereby notifies that the Aadhaar authentication shall be on voluntary basis and the National Recruitment Agency shall,— 


(a) obtain the consent of the Aadhaar number holder for the purpose of authentication herein; 


(b) 70 deny any service to the Aadhaar number holder for refusing to, or being unable to, undergo Aadhaar 
authentication; and 


(c) inform the Aadhaar number holder of the following alternate and viable means of identification, 
namely:— 


(0) PAN Card; 

(ii) Driving Licence; 

(iii) Passport; 

(iv) Photo Identity card issued by Government office or recognised institution; 


(v) Voter Identity Card. 


2. This notification shall come into force from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
