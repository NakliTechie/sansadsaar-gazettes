Definitions. - () In this rules, unless the context otherwise requires,- 


“banked” means the number of carbon credit certificates remaining with an obligated entity after the 
completion of compliance for the respective compliance year; 


“compliance year” means the financial year as referred in column (5) of the Schedule appended to these 
rules; 


“greenhouse gases emission intensity targets” means targets in terms of tCO2- per equivalent output or 
product as referred in column (5) of the Schedule appended to these rules; 


“Schedule” means the Schedule appended to these rules. 


(2) The words and expressions used herein and not defined but defined in the Environment Protection Act, 
986 (29 of 986), the Energy Conservation Act, 200 (52 of 200), the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) 
and the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, 2023, shall have the same meanings respectively as assigned to them 
in those Acts and under the said Scheme. 


Greenhouse gases emission intensity targets. — () The greenhouse gases emission intensity targets shall 
be calculated as per the methodology described in the detailed procedure as provided under the said Scheme. 


(2) The greenhouse gases emission intensity targets, for the specified trajectory period shall be as given in 
the Schedule. 


Responsibilities of the obligated entities. — The obligated entity shall, - 


(l) achieve the greenhouse gases emission intensity targets in the respective compliance year as per the 
Schedule; 


(2) meet its greenhouse gases emission intensity target under paragraph |! and detailed procedure under 
the said Scheme; 


(3) surrender the banked carbon credit certificates or carbon credit certificates purchased in the respective 
compliance year equivalent to the shortfall to comply with the greenhouse gases emission intensity 
target; 


(4) register on the portal under Indian Carbon Market Framework; 
(5) submit all the documents in the manner as stipulated in the detailed procedure under the Scheme: 


Explanation: For the removal of the doubts, it is hereby clarified that the obligated entities fail to 
submit the documents, the shortfall shall be calculated as the carbon credit certificate to be purchased 
as provided in the formula under the paragraph 5 of these rules, where achieved greenhouse gas 
emission intensity shall be considered as baseline greenhouse gas emission intensity; 


Issuance of Carbon Credit Certificates. — (!) Bureau shall issue the carbon credits certificate as per the 
clause (c) of sub-paragraph 2 of paragraph 5 of the Scheme.

(2) The carbon credit certificates to be issued to the obligated entities under the said Scheme, shall be 
determined as per the following formula, namely,- 


(greenhouse gases emission intensity target for that compliance year — greenhouse gases emission 
intensity achieved in that compliance year) multiply by unit of equivalent product produced in that 
compliance year is equal to number of carbon credit certificates to be issued for the respective compliance 
year; 


(3) The number of carbon credit certificates to be purchased by the obligated entities to meet the shortfall 
under the said Scheme, shall be determined as per the following formula, namely,- 


(greenhouse gases emission intensity achieved for that compliance year — greenhouse gases 
emission intensity target in that compliance year) multiply by unit of equivalent product produced in that 
compliance year is equal to number of carbon credit certificates to be purchased for the respective 
compliance year; 


(4) The carbon credit certificates issued or purchased may be banked by the obligated entities as provided in 
the detailed procedure under the said Scheme. 


Environmental compensation. — (l) In case an obligated entities fail to comply with or contravene any 
provisions of the paragraph 4 of these rules; the Central Pollution Control Board shall impose environmental 
compensation Order on such obligated entities for the shortfall in the respective compliance year which shall 
be equal to twice of the average price at which carbon credit certificate is traded during the trading cycle of 
such compliance year. 


Explanation: for the purposes of these rules, it is hereby clarified that the average price shall be determined 
by the Bureau. 


(2) No Order for imposition of the environmental compensation shall be made unless the obligated entities 
have been given reasonable opportunity of being heard. 


(3) Environmental Compensation shall be paid within the 90 days from the day of such imposition of the 
Order. 


Provided that obligated entity who fails to pay the environmental compensation shall be liable to pay penalty 
under the Environment Protection Act, 986. 


(4) The fund collected under the Environmental Compensation shall be maintained in a separate account and 
utilised for the purposes of the said Scheme on the recommendation of the National Steering Committee for 
Indian carbon market and subsequent approval of the Central Government.
