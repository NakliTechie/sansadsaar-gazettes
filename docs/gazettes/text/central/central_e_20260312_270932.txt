MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 11th March, 2026 


5.0. 1303(E).—Whereas by notification of Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural 
Gas, number S.O. 4224(E) dated 17-09-2025, issued under sub-section (1) of Section 3 of the Petroleum and Minerals 
Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (50 of 1962) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act),the 
Government of India had declared its intention to acquire the Right of User in land, specified in the Schedule appended 
to that notification for transportation of Liquid Ethane from Dahej Manufacturing Division in Gujarat to Parvat, where 
it will be hooked-up with the existing pipeline facilities of DNEPL, interconnecting with RIL-Hazira Manufacturing 
Division should be laid by M/s Reliance Ethane Pipeline Limited; 


And whereas, the copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public from 25-09-2025 to 
08-10-2025; 


And whereas, the objections received from the public to the laying of the said pipeline have been considered 
and disallowed by the Competent Authority; 


And whereas, the Competent Authority has under sub-section (1) of Section 6 of the said Act, submitted report 
to the Government of India; 


And whereas, the Government of India, after considering the said report and on being satisfied that the said 
land is required for laying the said pipeline, has decided to acquire the Right of User therein, free from all encumbrances; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 6 of the said Act, the 
Government of Indiahereby declares that the Right of User in the land, specified in the Schedule, appended to this
