MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 4th February, 2025 
S.O. 66(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 435 X of the Merchant Shipping Act, 
958 (44 of 958) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, being satisfied that it is 
impracticable to comply with the requirements of the rules made under Part XVA (Fishing Boats) of the said 
Act, hereby exempts the Indian fishing boats of less than 24 meters in length from the requirements of the 
Merchant Shipping (Indian Fishing Boats Inspection) Rules, [988, subject to the condition that such fishing 
boats carry the equipment prescribed in the following rules and regulations and the same are maintained in 


efficient condition, namely:- 


l. The Merchant Shipping (Fire Appliances) Rules, 990; 
2. The Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Collisions at Sea) Regulations, 975; 


3. The Merchant Shipping (Life Saving Appliances) Rules, [99]; and 


4. The Merchant Shipping (Distress and Safety Radio Communication) Rules, 995.
