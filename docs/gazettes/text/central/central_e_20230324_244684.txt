NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 24th March , 2023 


[ PART II SEC . 3 ( ii ) ] 


S.O. 1427 ( E ) .— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub - section ( 3 ) of section 1 of the Employees ' State 
Insurance Act , 1948 ( 34 of 1948 ) , the Central Government hereby appoints the 1st day of April , 2023 , as the date 
on which the provisions of the said Act , namely , 


( i ) sections 38 to 43 and sections 45A to 45H of Chapter IV ; 

( ii ) sections 46 to 73 of Chapter V ; and 

( iii ) sections 74 , 75 , sub - sections ( 2 ) to ( 4 ) of section 76 , 80 , 82 and 83 of Chapter VI , 


shall come into force in all the areas of Kanchipuram , Tirupattur , Tiruvannamalai and Viluppuram districts , in 
addition to the already notified areas of the said districts , and in all the areas of Kallakurichi district , in the State of 
Tamil Nadu .
