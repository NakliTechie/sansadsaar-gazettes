आदेश द्वारा शिथिल कर सकेगी।

S.R.O. 56.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution, the 
President hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the post of Assistant Divisional 
Fire Officer (Group ‘B’, Non-Gazetted) in Naval Headquarters, Ministry of Defence, namely: - 


l. Short title and commencement.- (l) These rules may be called the Naval Headquarters, Ministry of 
Defence (Group ‘B’ (Non-Gazetted), Fire Fighting Staff Recruitment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Application.- These rule shall apply to the posts as specified in column (]) of the Schedule annexed to these 
rules.
