MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 9th December, 2024 


S.O. 5336 (E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway (North 
Western Railway Construction), 8.0. No. 4635 (A) dated 07 October, 2024 published in Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii) on dated 23 October, 2024 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) under sub-section (i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 2008 (/ of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government, declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the 
said notification for execution, maintenance and operation of the Special Railway Project, namely "Taranga Hill —Abu 
Road Via Ambaji (6.654 km ) NEW BG LINE Project in District- Sabarkantha in the State of Gujarat . 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers namely “Gujarat 
Samachar “and “Divya bhaskar’” and “Times of India” and Sandesh on date 26 October, 2024 under sub section (4) of 
section 20A of the said Act; 


And, whereas, no objection was received by the Competent Authority within due time. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government. 


Now therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
