का.आ. 3761(अ).--केन्द्रीय सरकार ने, राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम, 1956 (1956 का 48) (जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है) की धारा 3क की उपधारा (1) के अधीन जारी की गई भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 2440(E) तारीख 24/06/2024, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Il, खण्ड 3, THES (1) में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा कर्नाटक राज्य के विजयपुर जिले में |॥15488 के 
कि.मी. 0.000 से कि.मी.102.310 (मुर्रम से विजयापुर सेक्शन) के निर्माण (चौड़ीकरण/पेव्ड शोल्डर सहित 2-लेन/4-लेन 
का बनाना आदि), अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधत और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन 
करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख " 02-07-2024 " 
को "दैनिक HAS समाचार पत्र संयुक्ता" और "SHAT हेराल्ड" दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ;

उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

New Delhi, the 3rd September, 2024 


9.0. 3761(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 2440(E) Dated:24/06/2024 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (1) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), Maintenance, 
management and operation of NH548B in the stretch of land from Km. 0.000 to Km. 102.310 (Murrum to Vijayapur 
Section) in the district of Vijayapur in the state of KARNATAKA 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Daily kannada news paper Vijay 
karnatak” and “Deccan Hearald” both dated 02-07-2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
