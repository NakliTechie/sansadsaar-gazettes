(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or 
(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into, or contracted a marriage with any person, 


shall be eligible for appointment to the said cadre:

7. Liability to serve. - Any person appointed to any post under these rules shall be liable to serve anywhere 
in India or abroad. 


8. Power to relax. - Where the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do,

and the service rendered by them in the said post on continuous basis before the said commencement shall 
be taken into account for deciding their period of probation, seniority, pensionary benefits and eligibility 
for promotion to the next higher grade. 


0. Ineligibility of non-citizens of India. - No person who is not a citizen of India shall, except with the prior 
permission of Central Government in writing, be appointed under these rules: 


Provided that nothing contained in this rule shall debar the appointment or employment of a 
subject of Nepal or Bhutan in the Force.
