LOK SABHA SECRETARIAT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 17th January, 2025 
No. 37/1/(IV)/2025/T.—The following order by the President dated the 17th January, 2025 is 


published for general information: - 


“In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by clause (1) of article 85 of the Constitution, 
I hereby summon the Lok Sabha to meet at New Delhi on Friday, the 31st January, 2025 


at 11.00 A.M. 


January 17, 2025 
Droupadi Murmu, 


PRESIDENT” 


UTPAL KUMAR SINGH, Secy. General
