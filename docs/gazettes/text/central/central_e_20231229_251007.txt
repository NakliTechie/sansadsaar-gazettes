MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2023 

S.O. 5505(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, 3725 Dated:2/08/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part Il, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 
of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire 
the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), 
maintenance, management and operation of NH752D (Ujjain Garoth Greenfield Alignement) in the stretch 
of land from Km. 72.000 to Km. 89.200 (SDO Office Agar-Barod) in the district of AGAR MALWA in the 
state of MADHYA PRADESH

And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and 
“Free Press” both dated 29 August 2023; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas no any objection have been received from any person by Competent Authority ; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority 


has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the sub-section (i) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 


declares that the land specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said 


Schedule shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
