आदेश 


यह आदेश दिया जाता है कि इस संकल्प की प्रति, सभी राज्य सरकारों और संघ राज्य क्षेत्र प्रशासनों, भारत 
सरकार के सभी मंत्रालयों तथा विभागों, राष्ट्रपति सचिवालय, उप राष्ट्रपति सचिवालय, प्रधान मंत्री कार्यालय, मंत्रिमंडल 
सचिवालय, लोक सभा सचिवालय, राज्य सभा सचिवालय, संसदीय राजभाषा समिति, नीति आयोग, भारत के नियंत्रक 
एवं महालेखापरीक्षक, निर्वाचन आयोग, संघ लोक सेवा आयोग और संसदीय पुस्तकालय को भेजी जाए। 


यह भी आदेश दिया जाता है कि इस संकल्प को जनसाधारण की जानकारी के लिए भारत सरकार के राजपत्र में 
प्रकाशित किया जाए।

MINISTRY OF PORTS, SHIPPING AND WATERWAYS 
RESOLUTION 
New Delhi, the 20st July, 2023 


No. E-11013/2/2019-Hindi.—Government of India, in partial amendment to the Resolution published in the 
Gazette of India, Part-I, Section-1 dated 01-07 January, 2022 vide No. E- 11013/2/2019-Hindi regarding reconstitution 
of the Hindi Salahkar Samiti of Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, hereby accord its approval to nominate 
Shri Dhananjay Bhimrao Mahadik, Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha) as non-official member of the Hindi 
Salahkar Samiti of Ministyr of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, in place of Dr. Vikas Mahatme, Member of Parliament 
(Rajya Sabha), earlier nominated as a non-official member of the said Samiti by Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs, at 
serial number 23, consequent upon his retirement. 


2. The tenure of Shri Dhananjay Bhimrao Mahadik, Member of Parliament (Rajya Sabha) in this committee will 

be till the remaining term of the committee. 

3. All other conditions given in the resolution of even number dated 28.12.2021 shall remain unchanged. 
ORDER 


Ordered that a copy of this Resolution be communicated to Administrations of all States and UTs, All Ministries and 
Departments of Government of India, President’s Secretariat, Vice President’s Secretariat, Prime Minister’s office, 
Cabinet Secretariat, Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, Committee of Parliament on Official Language, 
Niti Ayog, Comptroller and Auditor General of India, Election Commission, Union Public Service Commission and 
Parliament Library. 


It is also ordered that the Resolution be published in the Gazette of India for general information. 


AGRIM KAUSHAL, Economic Advisor
