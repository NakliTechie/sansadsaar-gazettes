NOTIFICATION 
Bilaspur, the 28th November, 2025 
Notice under Section 20 A of the Railways Act 989 


5.0. 553(E).—In exercise of Powers conferred by sub section () of section 20A of the Railways Act 989 
(24 of 89) (hereinafter referred as the said act),the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed here to is required for execution of 
Special Railway project, namely “Bilaspur Division: - Elimination of Level crossing No. BK-!07 in Jhalwara yard by 
provision of Light ROB.” in the state of Madhya Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land; Any 
person interested in the said land may, within a period of 30 days for the date of publication of this notification in the 
official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section (l) of 
section 20D of the said act; Every objection shall be made to the competent Authority, namely, Sub- Divisional 
Officer (Revenue), Katni SDM office, Katni , Madhya Pradesh in writing ,and shall set out ground thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal 
practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent 
Authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections; Any order made by the Competent 
Authority under sub-section(2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final; The land plans and other details of the 
land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office 


of the Competent Authority.
