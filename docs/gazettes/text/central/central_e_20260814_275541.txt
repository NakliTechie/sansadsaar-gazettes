MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3th August, 2026 


No.2026/E&R/(3)/3.—It is hereby notified for general information that keeping in view the 
operational requirements, Central Government has decided to change the jurisdictional boundary between 
Ranchi division of South Eastern Railway and Dhanbad division of East Central Railway from existing 
98.98 Km to revised 98.55 Km towards Barkakana station on Ranchi-Barkakana section. 


The above change will come into force with immediate effect.
