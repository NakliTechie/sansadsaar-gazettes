का . आ . 844 ( अ ) . – केन्द्रीय सरकार , राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) ( जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात् उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है ) की धारा 3 क की उप - धारा ( 1 ) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , यह समाधान 
हो जाने के पश्चात् कि महाराष्ट्र राज्य के सातारा जिले में NH - 48 के कि.मी. 592.240 से कि.मी. 725.000 ( कागल 
सातारा सेक्शन ) तक के भू - खण्ड के निर्माण ( चौड़ीकरण / पेव्ड शोल्डर सहित 2 लेन / 4 - लेन / छह लेन का बनाना आदि ) , 
अनुरक्षण , प्रबन्धन और प्रचालन के लोक प्रयोजन के लिए वह भूमि अपेक्षित है जिसका संक्षिप्त वर्णन नीचे अनुसूची में दिया 
गया है , ऐसी भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है ।

आपत्ति प्रकट कर सकेगा ।

आपत्तिकर्ता को व्यक्तिगत रूप मे या किसी विधिक पेशेवर द्वारा सुने जाने का अवसर देगा और ऐसी आपत्तियों की सुनवाई

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

S.O. 844 ( E ) .— In exercise of powers conferred by sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National 
Highways Act , 1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government , after 
being satisfied that for the public purpose , the land , the brief description of which is given in the Schedule 
below , is required for building ( widening / two lane / Six lane with paved shoulder etc. , ) , maintenance , 
management and operation of NH - 48 in the stretch of land from Km 592.240 to Km 725.000 ( Kagal 
Satara section ) in the district of Satara in the state of MAHARASHTRA , hereby declares its intention to 
acquire such land . 


Any person interested in the said land may , within twenty - one days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette , object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub 
section ( 1 ) of section 3C of the said Act . 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority , namely , Copetent Authority / 
Deputy Collector , ( Land Acquistion ) No. 2 Satara in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard , either in person or by a legal

3 

practitioner , and may , after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry , if any , as the 
Competent Authority thinks necessary , by order , either allow or disallow the objections . 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3C of the said Act 
shall be final . 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and 
can be inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority .
