Transport and Highways number S.O. 3448(E), dated 25% July, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the “said notification”), 
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I], Section 3, Sub-section (ii) relating to the levy of user fee on a

"Bijnor—Kotwali Section" shall be substituted.
