MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Western Railway (Construction Organisation)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 27th August, 2025 


S.O. 3932(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways(Amendment) Act, 2008(ll of 2008)and the Railways Act, 989 (24 of 989)(hereinafter referred to as the 
said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of 
which has been given in the schedule annexed hereto, is required for execution of the “Luni-Samdari (48.53 Kms.) 
& Samdari - Bhildi (223.44 Kms.) double line project” for Sub Division Siwana of District Balotra in the state of 
Rajasthan, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act; 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Sub Divisional Officer, Siwana, 
District Balotra, Rajasthan, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give the 
objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections; 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
