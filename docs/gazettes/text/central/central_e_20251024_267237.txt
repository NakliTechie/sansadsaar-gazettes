MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(West Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Kota, the 24th October, 2025 


S.O. 4886(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(I) of section 20A of the of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred in as the said act), the Central Government after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has given in the schedule annexed hereto, 
is required for execution of the Special Railway Project, namely, Kota — Mathura section in Bundi District 
Construction of Road Over Bridge on LC No. 113 Project in District Bundi in the State of Rajasthan, hereby declares 
its intention to acquire such land : 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section(I) of section 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Sub Divisional Officer 
Keshoraipatan, Taluka Keshoraipatan, District Bundi, in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and 
may after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (IJ) of final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
