MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 2th November, 2024 


G.S.R. 698(E).—_WHEREAS the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (8 of 2023) inter alia 
amends the Environment (Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) for decriminalising the offences under the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient in the public 
interest to amend the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 206; 


AND WHEREAS sub-rule (4) of the rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 provides that 
whenever it appears to the Central Government that it is in the public interest to do so, it may dispense with the 
requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986; 


AND WHEREAS the Central Government is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to dispense with 
the requirement of notice under clause (a) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986 for 
amending the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement), Rules, 206; 


NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6, 8 and 25 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 986 (29 of 986) read with sub-rule (4) of rule 5 of the Environment (Protection) Rules, 986, the 
Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Hazardous and Other Wastes 
(Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 20i6, namely: - 


l. (J) These rules may be called Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) 
Second Amendment Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 206 (hereinafter 
referred to as the said rules), for rule 40 the following rule shall be substituted, namely:- 


‘*40. Action for contravention. - Any person who fails to comply or contravenes the provisions of these 
rules shall be liable to a penalty in accordance with the provisions of section |5 of the Act.”.
