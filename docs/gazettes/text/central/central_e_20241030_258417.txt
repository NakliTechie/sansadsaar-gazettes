Mangrove forests and littoral or supra littoral forest flora-faunal assemblage within this smallest Sunderbans island of 
5.95 sq kms. 


AND WHEREAS, The Sanctuary supports a number of vulnerable species including Gangetic Dolphin 
(Platinista gangetica), Estuarine crocodile (Crocodilus porosus), Fishing cat (Felis viverrina Bennett), Estuarine otter 
(Lutra lutra), Jackal (Coins aureus), Chital (Axis axis), Wild pig (Sus scrofa) etc.
