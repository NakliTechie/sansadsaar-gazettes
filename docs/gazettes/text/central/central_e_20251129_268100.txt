G.S.R. 877(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 4 of the Van 
(Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 980 (69 of 980), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
rules to further amend the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Rules, 2023, namely: - 


. Short title, and commencement. (|) These rules may be called the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Second 
Amendment Rules, 2025. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

"(i) the Nodal Officer of the State Forest Department of the State Government or Union territory 
Administration shall facilitate and assist the user agency in identifying degraded forest lands for 
compensatory afforestation under these rules.".
