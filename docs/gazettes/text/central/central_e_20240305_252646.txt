परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या का.आ. 4582 तारीख 49/40/2023, जो भारत के राजपत्र, 
असाधारण, भाग Ul, खण्ड 3, उपखंड () में प्रकाशित की गई थी, द्वारा झारखंड राज्य के पलामू जिले में NH75 के कि.मी. 
496.87 से कि.मी.200.9 (दलाओ पलामू) तक के राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग के निर्माण (चोड़ाकरने / चार लेन का बनाने, आदि), 
अनुरक्षण, प्रबंधन और प्रचालन के लिए उस अधिसूचना से उपाबद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का अर्जन करने के अपने 
आशय की घोषणा की थी ; 


और उक्त अधिसूचना का सार उक्त अधिनियम की धारा 3क की उपधारा (3) के अधीन तारीख "23.0.2024" 
को "खबर मंत्रा" और "टाइम्स ऑफ़ इंडिया " दोनों में प्रकाशित किया गया था ; 


जबकि सक्षम प्राधिकारी को धारा 3-ग के तहत दायर आपत्ति प्राप्त हुई है, जिस पर विचार किया गया है और 
उसका उचित रूप से निपटान किया गया;

रिपोर्ट दे दी है;

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 5th March, 2024 


S.O. 035(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 4582 Dated:9/0/2023 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub- 
section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation 
of NH75 in the stretch of land from Km. 96.87 to Km. 200.9 (DLAO PALAMU) in the district of PALAMU in the 
state of JHARKHAND 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Khabar Mantra” and “Times Of 
India” both dated 23.0.2024; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
