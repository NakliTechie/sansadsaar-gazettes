NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the 6 February, 2025 


S.O. 623(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section(i) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989(24 of 989) (here in after referred in as the said Act), the Central Government after being satisfied that for the 
public purpose, this land of Village- Jolana Tehsil-Sardarpur District- Dhar, the brief description of which has 
given in the schedule annexed here to, is required for execution of Special Railway Project for Indore- Dahod new BG 
railway line in Dhar district of Madhya Pradesh state. Declares its intention to acquire such land in the State of 
Madhya Pradesh, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land: 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for a foresaid purpose under 
sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely, Sub-Divisional Officer &Land 
Acquisition Officer, Tehsil Dhar District-Dhar (M.P.), in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and 
may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any as the competent authority thinks 
necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (ID) of section 20(D) of the said act shall be 
final. 


The Land Plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person aforesaid Office of the Competent Authority.
