S.O. 5862(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub- 
section (1) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act) vide 
5.00. No. 5348(E) dated 23rd December 2021, S.O. No. 1081(E) dated 10th March 2022 and 8.0. No. 2677(E) 
dated 10th June 2022 the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for construction of (Widening two/four laning etc.) Maintenance, management and 
operation of NH731A (RAM VAN GAMAN PATH) on the stretch of land from Km. 35.000 to Km. 64.682 
(Competent authority/Special Land Acquisition Officer (Nagar Mahapalika) in the district of PRAYAGRAJ in the 
state of UTTAR PRADESH; 


And whereas the substance of the said notifications has been published in “Hindustan” and “The Times Of 
India” both dated 5th January 2022 , “Amar wala” and “Hindustan Times” both dated 31st March 2022 and “Amar 
ujala” and “Hindustan Times” both dated 12th July 2022; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas objections have been receivedand the same have been considered and disallowed by the 
competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
