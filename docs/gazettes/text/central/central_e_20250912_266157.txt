MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(SOUTH WESTERN RAILWAY CONSTRUCTION ORGANISATION) 
NOTIFICATION 
Bengaluru, 11th September, 2025 


S.O. 4155(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-Section (1) of Section 20E of the Railway Act, 
1989 (24 of 1989), the Ministry of Railway (South Western Railway, Construction Organization, Bengaluru 
Cantonment) after being satisfied with the report of Competent Authority and Special Land Acquisition Officer, 
Tumkur vide his office communicated hereby declares that the lands described and shown in the following schedule 
and notified under 8.0. 2698(E) dated 16.06.2025 issued under sub-section (1) of Section 20A of chapter IVA of 
Railway Act, 1989 amended in 2008, shall be acquired for executing the Special Railway Project namely 
“Construction of ROB in lieu of LC No. 38 at Kyatsandra - Tumkur @ Ch: 65/300-400 (Baddihalli road, near fire 
station, Kyatsandra Village)” in the State of Karnataka. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within 30 days from the date of publication of this notification 
in the official gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (1) of 20D of the said Act: 


Every such objections shall be made to the ‘Competent Authority’, namely, Special Land Acquisition 
Officer, Tumkur - Raydurga Railway Project, Tumkur for LC No. 38 at Baddihalli Gate in writing and shall set out 
the grounds thereof and the competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in 
person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if 
any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections; 


The Competent Authority issued 20D Notice to the Land Owners and provided 30 Days period for 
objections and the hearing conducted on 25.07.2025. No objections were received and no land owners attended for 
objection hearing. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of Section 20D of the said Act shall be 
final; 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this 20E notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid Office of the Competent Authority.
