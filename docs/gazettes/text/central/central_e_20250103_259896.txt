MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 3rd January, 2024 


G.S.R.03(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (2) of section 56 of the 
Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force Act, !992 (35 of 992), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules 
to amend the Ministry of Home Affairs, Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, Armourer Cadre (Group ‘B’ and ‘C’ 
posts), Recruitment Rules, 2023, namely :— 


l. Short title and commencement.— (l) These rules may be called the Ministry of Home Affairs, Indo-Tibetan 
Border Police Force, Armourer Cadre (Group ‘B’ and ‘C’ posts), Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 2024. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. Inthe Ministry of Home Affairs, Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force, Armourer Cadre (Group ‘B’ and ‘C’ posts) 
Recruitment Rules, 2023, in the Schedule, against serial number 5 relating to the post of Constable (Armourer), in 
column (l]), for the entries, the following shall be substituted, namely:- 


“By absorption : From amongst Matriculate Constable (General Duty) of the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force with 
atleast two years regular service and have undergone the following courses organised by the Indo-Tibetan Border 
Police Force subject to passing the Conversion Test, namely:- 


(a) Basic Training; (b) Basic Armourer Course, Grade-III.”.
