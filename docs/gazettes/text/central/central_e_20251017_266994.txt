New Delhi, the [70 October, 2025 


S.O. 4729(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highways 
Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for 
the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which is given in the Schedule below, is required for 
building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of 
Realignment stretch of land from Km. 234 to Km. 235.08 of the NH38 for Permanent Rectification of Blackspots TN- 
(02)-44, by Construction of Left Main Carriageway Realignment proposal on near Eppothumvendran Seven Eye 
Bridge in Madurai to Tuticorin section of NH-38 (Old NH-45B) in the state of Tamil Nadu, hereby declares its intention 
to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within twenty-one days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, object to the use of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub-section() of 
section 3C of the said Act. 


Every such objection shall be made to the Competent Authority, namely, “Special District Revenue Officer, 
Land Acquisition, National Highways, Thoothukudi-2" in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof and the 
Competent Authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 3C of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land to be acquired under their notification are available and can be 
inspected by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the Competent Authority.
