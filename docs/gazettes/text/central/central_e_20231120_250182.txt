MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(Railway Board) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 14th November, 2023 


S.O. 4988(E).—_ WHEREAS by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.0.3259 (E), dated 20" July, 2023 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), dated 21" July, 2023 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and 


[भाग 1--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


issued under sub-section (1) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 1989 (24 of 1989) (hereinafter referred to as 
the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution of Special Railway Project, namely, “Western 
Dedicated Freight Corridor” in the District of Anand in the State of Gujarat; 


AND WHEREAS, the substance of the said notification for inviting objections from public thereby was 
published in daily newspapers namely “The Times of India” and “Divya Bhaskar”, dated 5 August, 2023 
under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


AND WHEREAS, no objections were received from the public in respect of the said notification within 
the specified period; 


AND WHEREAS, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


NOW THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1), and in pursuance of 
sub-section (2), of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government,— 


(i) upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, hereby declares that the lands specified in the 
Schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; and 

(ii) hereby declares that on publication of this notification, the lands specified in the Schedule annexed 
hereto shall vest absolutely upon it free from all encumbrances.
