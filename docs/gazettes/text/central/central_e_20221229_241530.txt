MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 29th December, 2022 


99.0. 6112(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road 
Transport and Highways, number S.0.629(E) dated 14.02.2022, published in the Gazette of India, 
Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (1) of section 3A of the National 
Highway Act, 1956 (48 of 1956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared 
its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building, 
Maintenance, Management, Operation and Construction of New Four Lane Elevated Road from Chennai 
Port to Maduravoyal (NH-4) in the State of Tamil Nadu in the stretch from KM. 0.000 to KM. 20.200 for 
the development of double decker Elevated Corridor due to construction of Roads and formation of ramps 
(Special District Revenue Officer, (LA), NH-205, NH-716B, NH-4, NH-5, Tiruvallur District, No. 3/4, Lal 
Bagathur Sastri Street, Periyakuppam, Near Thulasi Theatre, Tiruvallur — 602 001) in the district of 
CHENNALT in the State of TAMILNADU. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in the “The Times of India” 
in English and in “Dinakaran” in Tamil both dated 07.04.2022; under Sub-Section(3) of section 3A of the 
said Act; 


And whereas, Objections have been received from the persons interested in the said lands within 


the time prescribed under section 3C(1); 


And whereas, the Objection Petitions received under section 3C(1) have been enquired in detail 


and the Objections have been disallowed under section3C(Q); 


And whereas, in pursuance of Sub-Section(1) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent 


Authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the 
powers conferred by the Sub-Section(1) of Section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 


declares that the land specified in the schedule below should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the lands specified in the 


Schedule below shall vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all Encumbrances.
