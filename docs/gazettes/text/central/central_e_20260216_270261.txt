this notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid 


purpose under sub-section () of section 20D of the said Act.

Divisional Officer, Nashik Maharashtra in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the Competent 
Authority shall give the objector the opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner and 
may, after hearing such objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the Competent Authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 


final.
