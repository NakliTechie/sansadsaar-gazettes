5.0. 3406(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 95.0. 565l (E) dated 08th December 2025, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, 
Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning etc.,), 
maintenance, management and operation in the stretch of land from Km. 273.! to Km. 274.3 of NH-48 for Upgradation 
& Development of | No. at Grade Junction to Grade Separator/ Underpass/Flyover structure at 200 Feet Bypass Junction 
project at Km. 273.6 (Delhi-Jaipur-Ajmer Expressway) in the district of JAIPUR in the state of RAJASTHAN 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and "The Indian 
Express” both dated !0 December 2025; undersub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 


absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
