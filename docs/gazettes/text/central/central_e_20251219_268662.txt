MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 9th December, 2025 


S.O. 599(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highway, S.O. 4295 (E) dated 23 September 2025 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 
3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (Missing Link with paved shoulder etc.), maintenance, 
management and operation of National Highway No. 65 (New NH-S58), on the stretch of land from km 39.900 to km 
4.00 (Fatehpur - Pali Section) in district of Churu in the State of Rajasthan; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Bhaskar” and "Rajasthan 
Patrika” both dated 27 September 2025; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received no objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
