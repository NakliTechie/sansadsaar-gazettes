S.O. 674(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and 
Highways, 637(E) Dated:06/02/2026 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section

as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intension to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for building (widening/four-laning, etc.), maintenance, management and operation of NH-54 
in the stretch of land from Km. 60.600 to Km. 6!.000 in the village namely, Hatli in the district of CHAMBA in the 
state of HIMACHAL PRADESH 


And whereas the substance of the said notification S. O. 637 (E) dated 06/02/2026 has been published in “The Tribune 
> dated 4/02/2026 , “ Himachal Dastak ” dated 4/02/2026 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;

same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers conferred by 
the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 
said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
