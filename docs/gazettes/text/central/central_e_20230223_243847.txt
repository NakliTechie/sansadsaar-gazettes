फा. सं. 18-11/13452-आईएनसी.--भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 1947 (1947 का XLVIII) की धारा 
14 के तहत प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, भारतीय उपचर्या परिषद्‌ एतट्दारा निम्नलिखित आदेश जारी करती है:- 


जबकि, अम्बाजी नर्सिंग कॉलेज, मेहसाणा- अहमदाबाद राजमार्ग, गणेशपुरा, तहसील कादी, जिला मेहसाणा - 382705, 
गुजरात, गुजरात राज्य सरकार और गुजरात नर्सिंग काउंसिल द्वारा मान्यता प्राप्त है। 


वहीं, आयुक्त (स्वास्थ्य) एवं प्रमुख सचिव, स्वास्थ्य और पदेन सचिव (लोक स्वास्थ्य एवं परिवार कल्याण) ब्लॉक न. - 05, 
प्रथम तल पुराना सचिवालय, डॉ. जीवराज भवन, सेक्टर नं. 10, गांधीनगर - 382010, गुजरात को भारतीय उपचर्या 
परिषद्‌ अधिनियम, 1947 की धारा 14 के तहत मान्यता वापस लेने के लिए एक नोटिस सं. 18-11/13452- 
आईएनसी/8147 दिनांक 28/02/2022 जारी किया गया था। नोटिस दिनांक 28/02/2022 की एक प्रति अम्बाजी नर्सिंग 


कॉलेज, मेहसाणा- अहमदाबाद राजमार्ग, गणेशपुरा, तहसील कादी, जिला मेहसाणा - 382705, गुजरात को भी भेजी गई 
थी। जारी नोटिस में राज्य सरकार से नर्सिंग संस्थान की उपयुक्तता के संबंध में परिषद्‌ को अपनी सिफारिशें प्रस्तुत करने का 
अनुरोध किया गया था। 


जबकि, अम्बाजी नर्सिंग कॉलेज के मामले में परिषद राज्य सरकार से कोई सिफारिश नहीं मिली है। यहाँ तक कि अम्बाजी 
नर्सिंग कॉलेज ने भी जारी नोटिस का कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है।

INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 22nd February, 2023 


F. No. 18-11/13452-INC.—In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act, 1947 (XLVII of 1947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order:— 


Whereas, Ambaji Nursing College situated at Mehsana — Ahmedabad Highway, At — Ganeshpura, Takadi, Distt. 
Mehsana - 382705, Gujarat is recognized by State Government of Gujarat and Gujarat Nursing Council to conduct 
GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 18-11/13452-INC/8147 dated 28/02/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 addressed to The Commissioner (Health), & Ex-Officio Secretary (Public Health & 
Family Welfare), Block No. - 05, 017 Floor Old Sachiwalay, Dr. Jivraj Bhavan, Sector No. 10, Gandhinagar - 
382010, Gujarat was issued. Copy of the notice dated 28/02/2022 was also enclosed to Ambaji Nursing College 
situated at Mehsana — Ahmedabad Highway, At — Ganeshpura, Takadi, Distt. Mehsana - 382705, Gujarat. In the 
notice issued, the State Government was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects 
Suitability of the nursing institution. 

Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case of 


Ambaji Nursing College. Even the institution Ambaji Nursing College has not responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 02/06/2021 
would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e., clinical and 
teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 14(3)(b) declares that with effect from 30/05/2022 any 
person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Ambaji Nursing College situated at Mehsana — 
Ahmedabad Highway, At — Ganeshpura, Takadi, Distt. Mehsana - 382705, Gujarat shall be entitled to be 
registered only in the State of Gujarat subject to the orders of State government of Gujarat.
