INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 

New Delhi , the 22nd February , 2023 

F. No. 18-15 / 14500 - INC.— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14 of Indian Nursing Council 
Act , 1947 ( XLVIII of 1947 ) , the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues the following order : 


Whereas , Rehoboth Institute of Nursing situated at Kadusonnappanahalli , Hosur Bande , Hennur 
Bagalur Main Road , Bangalore , Distt . Bangalore - 562149 , Karnataka is recognized by State Government of 
Karnataka and Karnataka Nursing Council to conduct GNM course . 


Whereas , a notice no . 18-15 / 14500 - INC dated 01/04/2022 for withdrawal of Recognition under section 14 of 
Indian Nursing Council Act , 1947 addressed to Principal Secretary , Health and Family Welfare Department , Room 
No. 105 , 01st Floor , Vikas Souda , Bangalore – 560001 , Karnataka was issued . Copy of the notice dated 01/04/2022 
was also enclosed to Rehoboth Institute of Nursing situated at Kadusonnappanahalli , Hosur Bande , Hennur 
Bagalur Main ad , Bangalore , Distt . Bangalore - 562149 , Karnataka . In the notice issued , the State Government 
was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the nursing institution . 


Whereas , the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the case 
of Rehoboth Institute of Nursing . Even the institution Rehoboth Institute of Nursing has not responded to the 
notices issued . 


Whereas , the Council has considered the matter in its entirety . Perusal of the inspection report dated 
25/05/2021 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies i.e. , 
clinical and teaching facilities . These are serious deficiencies which constitute the institution is unable to run GNM 
Course . 


Now therefore , the Council exercising powers under section 14 ( 3 ) ( b ) declares that with effect from 
30/05/2022 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Rehoboth Institute of Nursing situated at 
Kadusonnappanahalli , Hosur Bande , Hennur- Bagalur Main Road , Bangalore , Distt . Bangalore - 562149 , 
Karnataka shall be entitled to be registered only in the State of Karnataka subject to the orders of State government 
of Karnataka .
