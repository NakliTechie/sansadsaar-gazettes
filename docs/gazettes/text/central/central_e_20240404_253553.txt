पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिनियम कहा गया है, की धारा 20क की उपधारा (1) द्वरा प्रदत्त-शक्तियों का प्रयोग करत हुए, यह

गया है, झारखंड राज्य के धनबाद जिले में विशेष रेल परियोजन ”सोन नगर - अंडाल की मल्टी ट्रैकिंग (पूर्व में बुलाया 
गया, इस्टर्न डेडिकेटेड फ्रेट कॉरिडोर)” के निष्पादन, अनुरक्षण, प्रबंध और प्रचालन के लिए अपेक्षित है, ऐसी भूमि का 
अर्जन करने के अपने आशय की घोषणा करती है; 


उक्त भूमि में हितबद्ध कोई ब्यक्ति, राजपत्र में इस अधिसूचना के प्रकाशन की तारीख से तीस दिन के भीतर, उक्त 
अधिनियम की धारा 20घ की उपधारा (1) के अधीन उपरोक्त प्रयोजन के लिए, ऐसी भूमि के अर्जनज और उपयोग के संबंध

Kolkata, the 28th March, 2024 


S.O. 1613(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by the sub-section(1) of section 20A of the Railways 
(Amendment) Act, 2008 (11 of 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, after being 
satisfied that for the public purpose, the land, the brief description of which has been given in the Schedule annexed 
hereto, is required for the purpose of execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway 
Project, namely, " Multi tracking of Son Nagar - Andal ( erstwhile called Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor )”, in the 
District of Dhanbad in the State of Jharkhand, hereby declares its intention to acquire such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of this 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition and use of such land for the aforesaid purpose 
under sub-section(1) of section 20D of the said Act; 


Every such objection shall be made to the competent authority namely, District Land Acquisition Officer, 
Dhanbad, Jharkhand in writing and shall set out the grounds thereof, and the competent authority shall give the 
objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or by legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such 
objections and after making such further enquiry, if any, as the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either 
allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be final;
