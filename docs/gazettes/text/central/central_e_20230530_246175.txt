करे, तो किसी अन्य अनुसूचित बैंक” शब्दों के स्थान पर, “किसी भी राष्ट्रीयकृत बैंक या अनुसूचित बैंकों” शब्द रखे

New Delhi, the 29" May, 2023 


S.O. 2354(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 19 of the Coconut Development Board Act, 
1979 (5 of 1979), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Coconut 
Development Board Rules 1981, namely:— 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Coconut Development Board (Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
De In the Coconut Development Board Rules, 1981, in rule 34,- 


1. in sub-rule (1), for the words “current account in the State Bank of India or any of the nationalized 
banks” the words “interest bearing Saving Bank Account in any nationalized banks or scheduled banks 
of Government of India” shall be substituted; 


ii. in sub-rule (2), for the words “with the State Bank of India or any of its subsidiaries or, if approved by 
the Central Government, with any other scheduled bank” the words “with any of the nationalized banks 
or scheduled banks” shall be substituted; and 


ili. in sub-rule (3), for the words “against the current account of the Board” the words “against the account 
maintained by the Board” shall be substituted. 


iv. The following explanation shall be inserted, namely: - 


“Explanation. - for the purposes of this rule, the expressions “nationalized bank and scheduled bank” 
shall have the same meanings as respectively assigned to them in the Banking Companies (Acquisition 
and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980 (40 of 1980) and the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 
1934)”
