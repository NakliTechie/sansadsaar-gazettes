MINISTRY OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS, FOOD AND PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION 
(Department of Consumer Affairs) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 2nd June, 2023 


S.O. 2423(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential Commodities Act, 955 (l0 of 
955), the Central Government hereby makes the following order further to amend the Removal of Licensing 
Requirements, Stock Limits and Movement Restrictions on Specified Foodstuffs Order, 206, namely:- 


l. Short Title and Commencement- 


(.) This order may be called the Removal of Licensing Requirements, Stock Limits and Movement Restrictions 
on Specified Foodstuffs(Amendment) Order, 2023 


(2) It shall come into force with immediate effect. 


2. In the Removal of Licensing Requirements, Stock Limits and Movement Restrictions on Specified 
Foodstuffs Order, 206, in clause 3, in sub-clause (2), item (i) shall be inserted namely:- 


“(i) Pulses namely tur and urad for a period up to 3“ October 2023 with following stock limits for all States 
and Union Territories: 


e Wholesaler: 200 MT for each of the pulse; 
e Retailer:5 MT for each of the pulse 


e Big chain retailers: 5 MT for each of the pulse at each retail outlet and 200 MT at depot for each of the 
pulse; 


e Millers: Stock limits will be last 3 months production or 25% of annual installed capacity, whichever is 
higher. 


[भाग त--खण्ड 3(ii)] भारत का राजपत्र : असाधारण 3 


e Importers: Importers not to hold imported stock beyond 30 days from the date of custom clearance. 


3. Respective legal entities, as above, shall declare the stocks position on the portal (fcainfoweb.nic.in/psp) of 
Department of Consumer Affairs and in case the stocks held by them are higher than the prescribed limits then they 
shall bring the same to the prescribed stock limits within 30 days of issue of this notification. 


4. It shall be ensured that pulses stock is regularly declared and updated on the portal of this Department i.e. 
Department of Consumer Affairs.
