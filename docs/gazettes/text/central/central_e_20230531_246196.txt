पर, सक्षम प्राधिकारी के कार्यो का निर्वहन करने के लिये प्राधिकृत करती है।

MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st May, 2023 


S.O. 2369(E).— In pursuance of clause (a) of Section 2 of the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition 
of Right of User in Land) Act, 1962 (50 of 1962), (hereinafter called the said Act), the Government of India hereby 
authorizes Shri. Y.H.S. Satish, Tahsildar, Government of Andhra Pradesh to perform the functions of Competent 
Authority on deputation basis under the Petroleum and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in Land) Act, 
1962 for Kakinada-Vijayawada-Nellore Natural Gas Pipeline (KVNPL) Project of M/s IMC Limited in the State of 
Andhra Pradesh. 


2. Earlier notified Competent Authority for M/s. IMC Limited in the State of Andhra Pradesh, Sri. M. Lakshmi 
Narasimham, Deputy Collector, Government of Andhra Pradesh, vide S.O. 820(E) dated 23.02.2022 published in the 
Extraordinary Gazette of India dated 23.02.2022 stands de-notified. 


3. This notification will be effective from the date of its issue.
