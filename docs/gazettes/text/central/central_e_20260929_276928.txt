PRODUCT UNDER INVESTIGATION (PUD 


The Product under Investigation (also referred to as ‘PUI’) allegedly circumventing the Anti- Dumping 
duties in force is “ Alloy Steel Chisel/tool and Hydraulic Rock Breaker in fully assembled condition, 
and 7 sub-assemblies as defined in the original investigation, exported from Malaysia. . The PUI is 
classified under Chapter 84 to the First Schedule of the Customs Tariff Act, !975 under the sub- 
headings 8409, 842, 8430 and 843. However, customs classifications are indicative only and in no 
way binding on the scope of the investigation. 


DOMESTIC INDUSTRY 


The application has been filed by M/s DOZCO (India) Pvt. Ltd. The Applicant is the sole recognizable 
domestic producer of “Alloy Steel Chisel/tool and Hydraulic Rock Breaker in fully assembled 
condition” in India. The Applicant has claimed that it is not related to any exporter or producer of the 
subject goods in the subject countries or the CUI, or any importer of the PUC in India. On the basis of 
the information available, the Authority is satisfied that the application has been made ‘by or on behalf 
of the domestic industry’ in terms of Rule 2 (b) and Rule 5(3) of the Rules.

COUNTRY UNDER INVESTIGATION (CUD 


The country under investigation in the present investigation is Malaysia, through which the subject 
goods originating in the subject countries are allegedly being rerouted to India. 


F. PERIOD OF INVESTIGATION 


The period of investigation (POI) for the present investigation is April 2025 to March 2026 (2 
Months) and the injury period will cover the periods FY 2022 - 23, FY 2023 - 24, FY 2024 - 25 and 
the POI. 


EXISTING MEASURES 


The existing anti-dumping measure, recommended vide Final Findings Notification No. 6/8/2022- 
DGTR dated 28th March 2024 and imposed by the Central Government vide Customs Notification No. 
/2024-Customs (ADD), dated 27 June 2024 is allegedly being circumvented. 


GROUNDS FOR ALLEGED CIRCUMVENTION 


. The application for anti-circumvention investigation has been filed. The applicant has furnished 


following reasons/ evidence seeking anti-circumvention investigation: 


a) There is significant change in the pattern of trade whereby imports from Malaysia have increased 
significantly after the imposition of duty. It has been claimed that there is no due cause or 
justification for these imports other than the duty in force. 


b) The Applicant has submitted that circumvention of the product under consideration has taken place 
through (i) importation in unassembled/incomplete forms, (ii) alteration in the name of the product, 
(॥) and country-based circumvention. 


c) There is no known producer in Malaysia undertaking full-fledged production activities. It has been 
claimed that exports of of “Alloy Steel Chisel/tool and Hydraulic Rock Breaker in fully assembled 
condition” consigned from Malaysia are substantially produced in China & Korea and thereafter 
processed in Malaysia and then exported to India as goods originating in Malaysia. 


d) It is pertinent to note that Malaysia is neither considered a traditional market for importation of the 
PUI nor does it have producers or manufacturers of the said product. Accordingly, the sudden surge 
in imports from Malaysia cannot be ascribed to normal market conditions. The subject countries 
like China PR and Korea RP are rerouting exports through Malaysia, despite having no production 
capacity, reflecting a clear shift in trade patterns post-duty. 


e) The imports from Malaysia are at dumped prices and are undercutting the prices of the domestic 
industry and consequently adversely impacting the domestic industry by undermining the remedial 
effects of the existing anti-dumping measure. 


. The Applicant has requested retrospective imposition of Anti-dumping duty for circumventing products 


i.e. PUI from the date of initiation of investigation.

The Authority on the basis of the prima facie evidences provided by the Applicant, notes that there is 
change in the pattern of trade wherein imports have commenced from Malaysia and increased to a 
significant level since the imposition of duty without any sufficient cause or economic justification and 
known full-fledged production activity and undermining of the remedial effects of the existing anti- 
dumping measure. The Authority, after due examination and based on the facts available, has therefore 
considered it appropriate to limit the scope of the present investigation to the alleged country-based 
circumvention through Malaysia in terms of Rule 25(2)(c) of the Rules. 


INITIATION OF ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION INVESTIGATION 


. The Governments of the subject countries and of Malaysia, the country under investigation, were 


intimated through their Embassies in India prior to the initiation of the investigation in terms of 
Rule 5(5) of the AD Rules, 995. 


. On the basis of the duly substantiated application by the Applicant regarding imports from Malaysia 


and having satisfied itself on the change in pattern of trade and the undermining of the remedial effect 
of the existing AD measure, the Authority hereby initiates an Anti-Circumvention investigation in 
accordance with Section 9A (lA) of the Act and Rule 25 & 26 of the Rules to consider extension of 
the existing anti-dumping duty imposed on imports from China PR and Korea RP to imports of the 
subject goods exported from Malaysia from the date of initiation of this investigation in accordance 
with the Rule 27 (3) of the Rules. 


. In terms of Rule 26 (4A) of the Rules, the Authority recommends to the Central Government that 


imports of the Product Under Investigation covered by the present investigation be provisionally 
assessed and that an appropriate guarantee may be obtained from the importers, till the time a decision 
under sub-rule (3) of Rule 27 of the Rules is taken by the Central Government. 


. It may be noted that, in terms of Section 9A(IA) of the Act read with Rule 28 of the Rules, in the 


event circumvention is determined, the anti-dumping duty in force may be extended to imports of the 
PUI from Malaysia from a date not earlier than the date of initiation of the present investigation.

SUBMISSION OF INFORMATION ON CONFIDENTIAL BASIS 


Where any party to the present investigation makes confidential submissions or provides 
information on a confidential basis before the Authority, it is required to simultaneously submit a 
non-confidential version of such information in terms of Rule 7(2) of the AD Rules, 995 and in 
accordance with the relevant trade notices issued by the Authority in this regard. 


Such submissions must be clearly marked as "confidential" or "non-confidential" at the top of 
each page. Any submission which has been made to the Authority without such markings shall be 
treated as "non-confidential" information by the Authority, and the Authority shall be at liberty to 
allow other interested parties to inspect such submissions. 


The non-confidential version of the information filed by the interested parties should essentially 
be a replica of the confidential version with the confidential information preferably indexed or 
blanked out (where indexation is not possible) and such information must be appropriately and 
adequately summarized depending upon the information on which confidentiality is claimed. 


The confidential version shall contain all information which is by nature confidential and/or other 
information which the supplier of such information claims as confidential. For information which 
is claimed to be confidential by nature or the information on which confidentiality is claimed 
because of other reasons, the supplier of the information is required to provide a good cause 
statement along with the supplied information as to why such information cannot be disclosed. 


The Authority may accept or reject the request for confidentiality on examination of the nature of 
the information submitted. If the Authority is satisfied that the request for confidentiality is not 
warranted or if the supplier of the information is either unwilling to make the information public 
or to authorize its disclosure in generalized or summary form, it may disregard such information.
