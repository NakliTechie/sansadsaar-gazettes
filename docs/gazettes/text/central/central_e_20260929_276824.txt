F. No. 6/03/2025-DGTR.— ।. Having regard to the Customs Tariff Act, 975, as amended from 
time to time (hereinafter also referred to as the “Act”), and the Customs Tariff (Identification, Assessment and 
Collection of Anti-Dumping Duty on Dumped Articles and for Determination of Injury) Rules, 995, as 
amended from time to time (hereinafter also referred to as the “Rules”), the Designated Authority (hereinafter 
also referred to as the “Authority”) issued final findings vide Notification No. 6/03/2025-DGTR dated 28th 
April 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the “final findings”) in the anti-dumping investigation concerning imports 
of “Low Ash Metallurgical Coke” (hereinafter referred to as the “subject goods’) originating in or exported 
from Australia, China PR, Colombia, Indonesia, Japan and Russia (hereinafter referred to as the “subject 
countries”), recommending imposition of anti-dumping duty on imports of the subject goods from the subject 
countries. On the basis of the said recommendation, the Department of Revenue has imposed anti-dumping duty 
vide Notification No. 8/2026-Customs (ADD) dated 27th July 2026 


2. The Authority, in paragraph 26 of the final findings, excluded from the scope of the product under consideration 
ultra-low phosphorous metallurgical coke for use in ferroalloy manufacturing and LAM Coke of size 20-40 mm 
for use in blast furnaces up to [30 m3 for pig iron manufacturing, subject to actual user conditions. The Authority 
further clarified in paragraphs 326 and 327 of the final findings that the said exclusions have been granted on 
actual user condition basis and that such imports shall be made without payment of anti-dumping duty only if 
imported by the actual user. The note below the Duty Table appended to paragraph 333 of the final findings 
accordingly provides that the said exclusions are subject to imports by the actual user and furnishing of an 
appropriate and legally enforceable undertaking before the Customs authorities regarding bona fide imports and 
end use, and in the case of LAM Coke of size 20-40 mm, a valid certificate issued by the concerned State

Pollution Control Board or Central Pollution Control Board certifying the capacity of the blast furnace of the 
user. 


In order to provide clarity to the Customs authorities and to ensure that the benefit of the exclusions is extended 
only to eligible actual users for the specified end use, the Authority considers it appropriate to specify that the 
actual user condition shall be implemented by Customs in accordance with the Customs (Import of Goods at 
Concessional Rate of Duty or for Specified End Use) Rules, 2022. 


Accordingly, the note below the Duty Table appended to paragraph 333 of the final findings shall be substituted 
and read as under: 


“*Low Ash Metallurgical Coke that is, Metallurgical Coke having ash content below 76% excluding the 
following: 


a. ultra-low phosphorous metallurgical coke with phosphorous content up to 0.030% with size upto 30 
mm with 5% size tolerance for use in ferroalloy manufacturing. This exclusion is subject to actual user 


condition satisfying Note / given below, 


b. semi-coke or soft coke; 


८. LAM Coke of size 20-40 mm (with a mean size of approximately 30 mm + 2 mm) for use in blast 
furnaces up to 430 m? for pig iron manufacturing. This exclusion is subject to actual user condition 
satisfying Note I given below. 


Note I: The exclusions at (a) and (c) above shall be available only where the goods are imported by the actual 
user for its own consumption in the specified end use, subject to furnishing of an appropriate and legally 
enforceable undertaking before the Customs authorities regarding bona fide imports and end use. In the case 
of the exclusion at (c) above, the actual user shall also furnish a valid certificate issued by the concerned State 
Pollution Control Board or Central Pollution Control Board, as applicable, certifying the capacity of the blast 
furnace of the user, and the exclusion shall not be applicable for any other use or any other furnace size. The 
actual user condition would be implemented by Customs at the time of clearance of goods at the Customs port 
by following the Customs (Import of Goods at Concessional Rate of Duty or for Specified End Use) Rules, 
2022.” 


The above does not alter the scope of the product under consideration, the exclusions, or the anti-dumping duty 
recommended in the final findings, and only clarifies the manner of implementation of the actual user condition 


already recommended by the Authority. 


All other contents of the final findings dated 28th April 2026 shall remain unchanged.
