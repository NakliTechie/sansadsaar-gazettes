MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th June, 2026 


S.O. 2903(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, S.O. 267(E). Dated:3/06/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section () of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule 
annexed to the said notification for (widening of 2 laning with paved Shoulder etc.), maintenance, management and 
operation of National Highway No 652 in the stretch of land from Km. 50/000 to Km. 70/709 in the District of 
SOLAPUR in the state of MAHARASTRA; 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Dainik Lokmat” dated 8/07/2025 
and “4/08/2025” dated “The Times of India under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3C, considered and settled 
the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
