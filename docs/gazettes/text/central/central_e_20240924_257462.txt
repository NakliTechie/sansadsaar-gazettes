MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
(South Central Railway) 
NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 10th September, 2024 
S.O. 4175(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (37A) of Section 2 of the Railways Act, 1989 
the Central Government hereby notifies "Construction of Road Over Bridge In lieu of Level Crossing No. 147 at Km. 
573/17-19 in Rayanapadu yard" as a Special Railway Project for acquisition of land in the State of Andhra Pradesh in 
the District of NTR for providing National Infrastructure for a public purpose with effect from the date of publication 
of this notification in the Official Gazette.
