INDIAN NURSING COUNCIL 
WITHDRAWAL ORDER 
New Delhi, the 9th November, 2024 


F. No. 8-8/2477-INC:— In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4 of Indian Nursing 
Council Act, 947 (XLVHI of 947), the Indian Nursing Council hereby issues thefollowing order:— 


Whereas, Aayushman College Of Nursing situated at Chhayan Road Ranapur, Ranapur , Distt.- 
Jhabua, Pin Code- 457993, Madhya Pradesh is recognized by State Government of Madhya Pradesh and Madhya 
Pradesh Nurses Registration Council to conduct GNM Course. 


Whereas, a notice no. 8-!8/l2477-INC (52738) dated 26/02/2024 for withdrawal of Recognition under 
section l4 of Indian Nursing Council Act, !947 addressed to the principal secretary (Health & FW), Government of 
Madhya Pradesh Room No. 24!, Mantralaya (VallabhBhawan) Area Hills, Bhopal — 462004, Madhya Pradesh was 
issued. Copy of the notice dated 26/02/2024 was also enclosed to Aayushman College Of Nursing situated at 
Chhayan Road Ranapur, Ranapur ,Distt.- Jhabua, Pin Code- 457993, Madhya Pradesh. In the notice issued, 
the StateGovernment was requested to submit their recommendations to the Council as respects Suitability of the 
nursing institution. 


Whereas, the Council has not received any recommendation from the State Government as respects the 
case of Aayushman College Of Nursing. Even the institution, Aayushman College Of Nursing has not 
responded to the notices issued. 


Whereas, the Council has considered the matter in its entirety. Perusal of the inspection report dated 
2/04//2023 would show that the institution is deficient in many respects especially those relating to deficiencies 
i.e., clinical and teaching facilities. These are serious deficiencies whichconstitute that the institution is unable to 
run GNM Course. 


Now therefore, the Council exercising powers under section 4(3) (b) declares that with effect from 
2/05/2024 any person holding a recognized qualification of GNM from Aayushman College Of Nursing situated 
at Chhayan Road Ranapur, Ranapur , Distt.- Jnabua, Pin Code- 457993, Madhya Pradesh shall be entitled to 
be registered only in the State of Madhya Pradesh.
