New Delhi, the 21st March, 2023 


99.0. 1396(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) section 116 of the Multi -State 
Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 (39 of 2002), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in 
the Second Schedule of the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 (39 of 2002). 


In Second Schedule to the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002,- 


(1) after serial number 21 and the entries relating thereto, the following serial numbers and entries shall 
respectively be inserted, namely:- 


“ 22. National Cooperative Organics Limited, Anand, Gujarat. 
23. Bharatiya Beej Sahakari Samiti Limited, New Delhi. 
24. National Cooperative Exports Limited, New Delhi.”; 


(ii) the serial numbers 7, 11,13,14 and 20 and the entries relating thereto, shall be omitted.
