S.R.O. 55(E)— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section () and sub-section (4) of 
section 3 of the Cantonments Act, 2006 (4l of 2006), the Central Government, on being satisfied that for the 
administration of the Pachmarhi Cantonment, it is desirable to vary the constitution of the Pachmarhi Cantonment Board 
where the term of the members shall expire on the 297 November, 2024, hereby makes a declaration to vary the 
constitution of the Pachmarhi Cantonment Board with effect from 307 November, 2024, for a period of one year, or till 
such time the said Board is constituted under section |2 of the said Act, whichever is earlier.
