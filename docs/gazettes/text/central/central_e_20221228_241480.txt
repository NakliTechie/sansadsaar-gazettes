एस . ओ . 4300 ( ई ) दिनांक 14 सितंबर , 2022 द्वारा भारत के राजपत्र में प्रकाशित असाधारण संख्या 4119 , भाग II , खंड 
3 , उप - धारा ( ii ) राष्ट्रीय राजमार्ग अधिनियम , 1956 ( 1956 का 48 ) की धारा 3 ए की उप - धारा ( 1 ) के तहत जारी ( बाद 
में उक्त अधिनियम के रूप में संदर्भित ) , केंद्र सरकार ने निर्दिष्ट भूमि का अधिग्रहण करने के अपने इरादे की घोषणा की किमी

प्रकाशित किया गया है , दोनों दिनांक 28 सितंबर , 2022 की धारा 3 ए की उप - धारा ( 3 ) के तहत उक्त अधिनियम ,

के जवाब में कोई आपत्ति प्राप्त नहीं हुई है ।

रिपोर्ट दे दी है ;

की उपधारा ( 1 ) द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए , यह घोषणा करती है कि उक्त अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि का

MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 

NOTIFICATION 


Delhi , the 27th December , 2022 

S.O. 6076 ( E ) . – Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways , number S.O.4300 ( E ) dated the 14th September , 2022 , published in the Gazette of India , Extraordinary 
No. 4119 , Part II , Section 3 , Sub - section ( ii ) issued under sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3A of the National Highway Act , 
1956 ( 48 of 1956 ) ( hereinafter referred to as the said Act ) , the Central Government declared its intension to acquire 


[ भाग II— खण्ड 3 ( ii ) ] 

3 

the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building ( widening / two - laning , etc. ) , 
maintenance , management and operation of National Highway No.67 in the stretch of land from Km . 0/000 to Km . 
80/000 ( Nagapattinam - Thanjavur Section ) in the district of TIRUVARUR in the state of TAMIL NADU . 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in " The Hindu " and “ Thinathanthi " 
both dated the 28th September , 2022 under sub - section ( 3 ) of section 3A of the said Act ; 


And whereas no objections have been received in response to the publication made in the local dailies 
" Thinathanthi " and in " The Hindu " both dated 28th September , 2022 . 


And whereas , in pursuance of sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Competent Authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government ; 


Now , therefore , upon receipt of the said report of the Competent Authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub - section ( 1 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose ; 


And further , in pursuance of sub - section ( 2 ) of section 3D of the said Act , the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette , the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government , free from all encumbrances .
