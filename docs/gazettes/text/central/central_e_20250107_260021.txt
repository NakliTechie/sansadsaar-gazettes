S.O. 119(E).—In exercise of powers conferred by Clause (a) of Section 3 of the National 
Highways Act 1956 (48 of 1956), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendment in the 
Notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways No. S.O. 1701(E) 
dated 177 July 2008, Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub Section (ii), 
dated 17" July 2008- namely: In the Schedule to the said Notification 5.0. 1701(E) For the words and 
brackets “District Revenue Officer, Dindigul” at 18 Para,the words “Special District Revenue Officer, 
Land Acquisition, National Highways, Madurai” shall be substituted.
