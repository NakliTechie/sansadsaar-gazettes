अनुसरण में सक्षम प्राधिकारी ने अपनी रिपोर्ट केंद्रीय सरकार को प्रस्तुत कर दी है।

उपधारा (॥) द्वारा प्रदत शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए यह घोषणा करती है कि इससे उपावद्ध अनुसूची में विनिर्दिष्ट भूमि

NOTIFICATION 
Mumbai, the [70 December, 2024 


S.O. 5452(E).— Where as by the Notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways 
(Railway Board), number S.O. 394] (9), dated 3/09/2024 published in Gazette of India Extraordinary. Part II, 
section-3, Sub-section (ii), of section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 (240f989)(here in after referred to as the 
said Act),the Central Government, has declared its intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed 
to the said notification for execution, maintenance, management and operation of land to be acquired with or 
without structure for the Special Railway Project, New BG Line project From Dahod - Indore Via, Jhabua, 
Sardrapur, dhar and Pithampur (204.76km) in District Dhar, Tehsil Gandhwani Village Golpura Pada - 
Sultanpur in the state of Madhya Pradesh. And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in 
daily “Free press Journal” dated 2/09/2024 and “Nai Duniya”&“Dainik Bhaskar” dated 2[/09/2024 under sub- 
section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act. And, whereas, nil objection has been received by the competent 
authority. 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of the 
power conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20(E) of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares 
that the lands specified in the Schedule annexed here to shall be acquired for the afore said purpose. 


And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20 (E) of the said Act, the Central Government 
here by declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the 
Schedule annexed here to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
