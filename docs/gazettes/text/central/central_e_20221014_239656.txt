Whereas the Petitioner Smt . Nirmala Devi has filed an Election Petition bearing E.P No. 01 of 2016 calling 
in question the election of Sri Mukhtar Abbas Naqvi and Shri Mahesh Poddar to the Rajya Sabha as its members in 
the Biennial Election by the elected members of the Jharkhand State Legislative Assembly , held in the month of 
May / June 2016 ; 


And Whereas during the course of trial , the Election Petitioner Smt . Nirmala Devi has filed an Interlocutory 
Application bearing I.A No. 6416/2022 in this Court under section 151 of the Civil Procedure Code for withdrawal of 
Election Petition on the ground mentioned therein ; 


As such keeping in view of section 109 & 110 of the Representation of People Act , 1951 this Court has 
directed the publication of Notice that all concerned person / persons are being noticed that the instant Election Petition 
is being withdrawn and therefore , if they so desire to continue the instant Election Petition can appear as a party in this 

case ; 


Take notice that in the event of non - appearance of any interested person / persons who so ever is interested 
to contest this election petition , then he may do so by appearing within the stipulated period prescribed under the law 
failing which this Hon'ble Court shall pass appropriate Order in accordance of law ; 

Given under my hand and the seal of this Court this 9th of September , 2022 .
