आगामी 20 दिवस में सक्षम प्राधिकारी के कार्यालय में प्रस्तुत कर सकते है।

MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[North Central Railway (Construction Organization)] 
NOTIFICATION 
PRAYAGRAJ, the 8th October, 2024 


S.O. 4594(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Railway 
(North Central Railway, Construction department), Notification number, S.O. 3056 (E) dated the 3lst July, 2024 
published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3 Sub Section (ii), dated 25th July, 2024 (hereinafter 
referred to as the said notification) and issue under sub-section (.) of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) Act. 
2008 (i lof 2008) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the 
land specified in the schedule annexed the said notification for the purpose of Design, Supply, Erection, Testing & 
Commissioning of Traction Sub-Station (TSS) of Special Railway Project, namely Special Railway Project of Raising 
of Speed to 60kmph/200kmph on existing New Delhi-Hawrah route (incl. Kanpur-Lucknow) in District 
Gautambuddh Nagar of the state of Uttar Pradesh. 


And, whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in the daily Hindi newspapers "Dainik 
Bhaskar" and 'Hindustan Times" Dated 07th August, 2024 under sub-section (4) of section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, all the objections received in this project have been disposed of by the Competent Authority; 


And, whereas, in pursuance of sub-section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the report of the Competent Authority, and in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (l) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the lands specified in 
the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose;

And, further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule annexed here 
to shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances. 


And, that all persons interested in the above mentioned land in pursuance of sub-section (04) of section 20(F) 
of the said Act by the Central Government are hereby informed that they may, personally or through an agent or legal 
practitioner, be notified by the competent authority Additional District Magistrate (Land Acquisition) 
Gautambuddh Nagar, can submit his claim in the next 20 days from the date of publication of this notification in the 
office of the Competent Authority.
