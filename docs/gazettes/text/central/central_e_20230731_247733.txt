NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 31st July, 2023 


G.S.R. 566(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 read with section 74 of the Indian Post 
Office Act, 1898 (6 of 1898), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Indian 
Post Office Rules, 1933, namely: - 


1. (1) These rules may be called the Indian Post Office (Fourth Amendment) Rules, 2023. 
(2) They shall come into force with effect from the 17 day of August, 2023. 


2. In the Indian Post Office Rules, 1933, in Schedule-III, after serial number 39 and the entries relating thereto, the 
following serial number and entries shall be inserted, namely: - 


(1) (2) (3) (4) 
“40 | Netherlands 330 30.”
