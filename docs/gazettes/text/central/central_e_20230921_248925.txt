New Delhi, the 20th September, 2023 


S.O. 449(E)—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Shipping, Road 
Transport andHighways, Department of Road Transport and Highways number S.O. 49 (E) dated - 0 March 
2023, published in Gazetteof India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (.) of 
section 3A of the National HighwaysAct, !956 (48 of 956) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central 
Government declared its intention to acquire the landspecified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for 
building (widening / four-laning, etc.), maintenance, managementand operation of National Highway No. 43 Ext. 
Gulganj - Amanganj - Pawai - Katni Road {KM. 0+00 to KM. 63+85} in the stretchof land from design Km. 37+00 
to Km. 57+30 (Sub-Divisional officer revenue & land acquisition officer Tehsil - Shahnagar) inthe district of 
PANNA in the state of MADHYA PRADESH. 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “Fauladi Kalam Daily” and “Dainik 
BhaskarSatna” dated 26 March 2023;under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act;
