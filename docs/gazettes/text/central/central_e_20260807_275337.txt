NOTIFICATION 
Jaipur, the 6th August, 2026 


S.O. 4366(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of 
Railways (North Western Railway, Construction organization), Notification number, S.O. 2784(E) 
dated 0* June, 2026 published in Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3 Sub-section (ii), 
dated the 0l* June, 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification) and issued under sub- 
section (l) of Section 204 of the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (ll of 2008) (hereinafter 
referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the purpose of execution, maintenance,

management and operation of "Luni-Samdari-Bhildi Doubling Railway Project (Special Rail 
Project)" in Deesa Taluka, District Banaskantha in the State of Gujarat. 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification was published in daily newspapers 
namely "Gujarat Samachar" and "Sandesh" dated 067 June, 2026, and "Times of India" dated 
0" June, 2026 under sub-section (4) of Section 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, in respect of the surveys numbers of the villages specified in the schedule 
annexed to this Notification, no objections were received by the competent authority within the 
prescribed period. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section () of Section 20E of the said Act, the competent 
authority has submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority, and in exercise of 
the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government 
hereby declares that the land specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall be acquired for the 
aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central 
Government hereby declares that on publication of this notification in the official Gazette, the land 
specified in the schedule annexed hereto shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from 
all encumbrances.
