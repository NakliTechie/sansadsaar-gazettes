MINISTRY OF ROAD TRANSPORT AND HIGHWAYS 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 23rd September, 2025 


9.0. 4323(E).—Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport 
and Highways, 232(E) Dated:26/05/2025 , published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii) issued under sub-section (l) of section 3A of the National Highway Act, 956 (48 of 956) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to acquire the land specified in 
the Schedule annexed to the said notification for building (widening/two lane with paved shoulder/four laning 
etc.,), maintenance, management and operation of NH-69 in the stretch of land from (Design Ch. 0.00 (Existing 
Ch. Km.428.880) to Design Ch.5.400 (Existing Ch. 444.00) (Chinthamani to Andhra Pradesh Border Section) in the 
district of CHIKKABALLAPURA in the state of KARNATAKA; 


4 THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—SEC. 3(ii)] 


And whereas the substance of the said notification has been published in “The Times of India” 
dated 28/05/2025, “Vijaya Karntaka” dated 28/05/2025 ; under sub-section (3) of section 3A of the said Act; 


And whereas the Competent Authority has received objections filed under Section 3-C, considered and 
settled the same appropriately; 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (l) of section 3D of the said Act, the competent authority has 
submitted its report to the Central Government; 


Now, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the powers 
conferred by the sub-section (.) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land 
specified in the said Schedule should be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 3D of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the said Schedule shall 
vest absolutely in the Central Government, free from all encumbrances.
